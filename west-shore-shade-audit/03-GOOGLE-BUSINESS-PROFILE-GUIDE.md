## Why Google Business Profile Matters

For local home services, **Google Maps is often the #1 source of leads.** When someone searches “motorized patio screens near me” or “retractable awnings Tampa,” the map pack (3 businesses with stars) gets most of the clicks.

**Goals:**
1. Show up in more local searches
2. Get more calls and direction clicks
3. Build review count and star rating
4. Look active and trustworthy vs. competitors

## Section 5: Reviews — Most Important Section

### Current situation
- Yelp: ~13 reviews, ~4.0 stars (lower priority)
- Google: Referenced positively in some reviews — **Google is the priority**
- Website testimonials exist but aren’t tied to live Google reviews

### Review goal
- **Target:** 8–12 new Google reviews per month during install season
- **Long-term:** 100+ Google reviews with 4.7+ average

### How to get Google review link
1. Log into Google Business Profile
2. Click **Ask for reviews** (or Get more reviews)
3. Copy the short link
4. Save it — this goes on `/review` page, in texts, and in emails

### Post-install review request (use this workflow)

**Timing:** 24–48 hours after install, when customer is happy.

**Text message template:**
> Hi [Name], it's [Your name] from West Shore Shade — hope you're loving the new [screens/awnings]! If we did a good job, would you mind leaving us a quick Google review? It really helps our small business: [GOOGLE REVIEW LINK]. Thank you!

**Email template:**
> Subject: How's everything looking?
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> Thank you for choosing West Shore Shade! We hope you're enjoying your new [product].
>
> If you have 60 seconds, we'd really appreciate a Google review:
> [GOOGLE REVIEW LINK]
>
> Questions? Call us anytime at 813-252-6813.
>
> — West Shore Shade Outdoors & More

### Responding to reviews

**Positive review response template:**
> Thank you, [Name]! We loved working on your [lanai/patio] in [City]. Enjoy the shade! — West Shore Shade Team

**Negative review response template:**
> [Name], we're sorry to hear this. We take quality seriously and would like to make it right. Please call us at 813-252-6813 and ask for [Matt/office manager]. We'd appreciate the chance to resolve this.

**Rules:**
- [ ] Respond to **every** review within 24–48 hours
- [ ] Never argue publicly — invite offline conversation
- [ ] For product issues, offer service visit
