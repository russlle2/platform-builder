## Bottom Line (Read This First)

West Shore Shade is **not** invisible online. The website is modern, there is a large blog, city pages for Tampa Bay areas, Google Ads tracking, and a real showroom on US-41. That’s good.

The problem is **what happens when someone is ready to buy**:

1. **Financing link is broken** — The FINANCING button on the website goes to a dead page. For big-ticket installs ($5,000–$50,000+), this can kill deals.
2. **Address is inconsistent online** — Some places say **9885** US Hwy 41, others say **9871**. This hurts Google Maps ranking and confuses customers.
3. **Brand confusion with “West Shore Home”** — A big national company with a similar name creates trust problems in search results.
4. **Reviews aren’t being used well** — Good work exists, but the website doesn’t show live Google reviews strongly, and the `/review` page is basically empty.
5. **Lead follow-up tools are missing** — No online scheduling, no live chat, no “how did you hear about us?” on forms, no call tracking to see which ads work.

**Good news:** Most fixes don’t require a new website. Many are office/admin tasks you can drive from your desk.
