## Section 2: Google Ads Recommendations

*Share this section with whoever manages the Google Ads account.*

### What Google Ads should focus on

Google works best when people **search with intent** — they want screens or awnings now.

**High-priority keywords (campaign ideas):**

| Campaign Theme | Example Keywords |
|----------------|------------------|
| Motorized screens | motorized patio screens tampa, lanai screens near me, retractable screens tampa bay |
| Awnings | retractable awning installer tampa, motorized awning sarasota |
| Garage screens | garage door screen installation florida |
| Local + product | pool lanai screens brandon, exterior shades clearwater |
| Commercial | commercial patio shades tampa, restaurant awning installation |

**Keywords to avoid or limit:**
- “West Shore Home” (wrong company — wasted spend)
- Very broad terms like “blinds” or “windows” (wrong audience)

### Ad copy that converts

Include these elements in every ad:
1. **Free estimate**
2. **Product** (motorized screens / awnings)
3. **Location** (Tampa Bay / Gibsonton showroom)
4. **Trust** (warranty, since 2018, local)
5. **Urgency** (May sale — when active)

**Example ad:**
> Headline 1: Motorized Patio Screens — Tampa Bay  
> Headline 2: Free Estimate | May Sale — 20% Off  
> Description: Custom lanai & patio screens. Visit our Gibsonton showroom. 10-year warranty. Call 813-252-6813.

### Landing pages matter

**Don't send all ads to the homepage.**

| Ad topic | Send to this page |
|----------|-------------------|
| Tampa screens | /motorized-shades/tampa-fl |
| Sarasota awnings | /awnings or /motorized-shades/sarasota-fl |
| Garage screens | /garage-door-screen |
| Lanai screens | /lanai |
| General / brand | Homepage or /contact-us |

- [ ] Ask ads manager: “Are we using city/product landing pages or only homepage?”
- [ ] Request call tracking to measure phone leads from ads

### Why social media matters for this business

Motorized screens and awnings are **visual products.** Before/after videos sell the dream: bug-free lanais, shaded patios, push-button convenience.

**Accounts found:**
- Facebook: facebook.com/people/West-Shore-Shade/100063066139928/
- Instagram: @westshoreshade

### Content calendar (2–3 posts per week)

| Week | Post type | Example |
|------|-----------|---------|
| Week 1 | Before/after project | Side-by-side lanai install in Tampa |
| Week 2 | Promo | May sale graphic with phone number |
| Week 3 | Educational | “3 reasons Florida homeowners love motorized lanai screens” |
| Week 4 | Showroom | “Walk-ins welcome — come see fabrics in Gibsonton” |

### Video ideas (phone video is fine)

- Screen deploying with remote (15 seconds)
- Customer reaction / testimonial (with permission)
- Installer finishing a job — professional team shot
- Showroom fabric samples

### Facebook/Instagram ads (when budget allows)

**Best audiences:**
- Homeowners 35–65 in Tampa Bay counties (Hillsborough, Pinellas, Pasco, Sarasota, Manatee)
- Interests: home improvement, outdoor living, patio, pool, lanai
- **Retargeting:** People who visited westshoreshade.com but didn't fill out form

**Note:** Meta Pixel is NOT currently on the website. Ask SodaRocket to install it before running retargeting ads.

**Simple ad budget starting point:**
- $10–20/day retargeting (website visitors)
- $20–40/day local prospecting (video of installs)

## Section 7: Measuring What Works

Ask for monthly reports (from ads person or SodaRocket) on:

| Metric | Why it matters |
|--------|----------------|
| **Phone calls from Google Ads** | Often 60%+ of leads in this industry |
| **Form submissions** | Track by source if “how did you hear about us?” is added |
| **Cost per lead** | Total ad spend ÷ leads = what you pay per inquiry |
| **Google Maps insights** | Calls, directions, website clicks from GBP |
| **Close rate** | Leads → estimates → sold jobs (office tracks this) |
