Welcome—here’s what to expect

Your first visit sets the tone. We’ll listen to your goals, take comfortable digital records, and craft a personalized plan—no pressure.

1) Say hello

We’ll greet you, offer comfort items, and confirm your health history. If you’re in pain, we’ll prioritize relief first.

2) Digital records

We capture photos and X‑rays with low‑dose, high‑clarity technology so your doctor can see the full picture.

3) Personalized plan

Your doctor will walk through findings, answer questions, and outline options that fit your timeline and budget.

Save time with digital forms

Complete your forms before you arrive to shorten check‑in. Prefer paper? Arrive 10 minutes early and we’ll provide copies.

What to bring

  • Photo ID and insurance card (if applicable)
  • List of medications and allergies
  • Recent X‑rays (we can request them for you)

FAQ

How long is my first visit?
Most new patient visits take about 60–90 minutes depending on your needs.
Will I get a cleaning at my first appointment?
In most cases, yes. If gum therapy is needed, we’ll discuss the best approach first.
Do you see kids?
Absolutely! We’re a family practice and love caring for children.

Ready to book?

Choose a time that works for you—online, anytime. Prefer to talk? We’re one call away.
