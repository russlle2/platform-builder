Questions? We’re here to help

Will you tell me costs before treatment?
Yes. You’ll always receive a written estimate that explains your insurance benefits and expected co‑pay before we begin.
Do you offer payment plans?
Yes. For larger plans, we can arrange monthly payments through trusted financing partners. We’ll help you apply in‑office.
Can I use HSA/FSA?
Absolutely. We accept HSA and FSA for eligible services. We’ll provide itemized receipts for your records.
