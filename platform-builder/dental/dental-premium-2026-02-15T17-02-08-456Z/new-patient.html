Welcome—here’s what to expect

We’re thrilled to care for you. Expect a warm welcome, no rush, and a clear plan for a healthy smile.

Your first visit

  1. Conversation

    Tell us about your goals and concerns—we listen first.

  2. Thorough exam

    We’ll check teeth, gums, bite, and perform any needed X‑rays.

  3. Personalized plan

    We review findings together and create options that fit your priorities.

What to bring

  • Photo ID and insurance card (if any)
  • List of medications
  • Any previous X‑rays (optional)
