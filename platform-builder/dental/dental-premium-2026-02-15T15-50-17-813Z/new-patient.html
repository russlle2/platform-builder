Your first visit, step by step

  1. Check in

    Arrive a few minutes early. We’ll confirm details and answer insurance questions.

  2. Comprehensive exam

    Your dentist will evaluate your teeth, gums, bite, and TMJ, and review digital images as needed.

  3. Gentle cleaning

    Your hygienist customizes your cleaning and shares personalized home‑care tips.

  4. Clear plan

    We’ll explain any findings and map a plan that fits your goals and budget.

What to bring

  • Photo ID and insurance card (if applicable)
  • List of medications and medical history
  • Previous dental X‑rays (optional)
