Frequently Asked Questions

What kind of postpartum pain can Holistic Medicine help with?
Our Holistic Medicine approach can effectively address common postpartum physical pains such as lower back pain, hip discomfort, pelvic floor tension, carpal tunnel syndrome, neck and shoulder stiffness from nursing or carrying, and general body aches. We focus on identifying the root causes of your discomfort.
How is somatic healing different from physical therapy for new mothers?
While both are beneficial, somatic healing in Holistic Medicine focuses on the mind-body connection to release chronic tension and pain patterns. It helps you become more aware of your body's signals and develop self-regulation tools, complementing traditional physical therapy by addressing the neurological and emotional components of physical recovery.
Is it safe to begin Holistic Medicine sessions soon after childbirth?
Yes, our methods are gentle and can be adapted for mothers at various stages of postpartum recovery, often starting a few weeks after birth, with your doctor's clearance. We prioritize your comfort and safety, adjusting techniques to respect your body’s current capacity and healing process.
What can I expect during a typical session for postpartum physical pain?
A session typically involves a detailed discussion of your current physical experiences, gentle hands-on assessment if appropriate, and guided somatic exercises or movements. We’ll teach you practical tools for pain reduction and body awareness you can use at home, empowering you in your daily recovery.
