Frequently Asked Questions

What does Holistic Medicine mean for someone my age, especially regarding stress?
For seniors, Holistic Medicine means looking at your whole self – mind, body, and daily habits – to understand and address stress. It's about gentle, natural ways to help your nervous system find balance, rather than just treating symptoms. We focus on methods that are safe and comfortable for your stage of life.
How does nervous system regulation specifically benefit me as a senior?
Regulating your nervous system can significantly improve your quality of life. It can reduce feelings of anxiety, improve sleep, calm digestion, and even enhance your overall resilience to daily pressures. For seniors, this often means feeling more relaxed, having more energy, and enjoying activities with greater ease.
Are the treatments gentle and safe for someone with existing health considerations?
Absolutely. Our primary focus is on gentle, non-invasive approaches that are carefully adapted to your individual health profile. We will always review your medical history thoroughly to ensure every recommendation is safe, appropriate, and complements any existing care you are receiving. Your comfort and safety are our highest priorities.
What kind of commitment is required, and how quickly might I notice changes?
The commitment involves actively participating in your personalized plan, which might include simple daily practices. Changes can be gradual, but many seniors report feeling a greater sense of calm and improved sleep within a few weeks of consistent application. We'll work at a pace that feels comfortable and effective for you.
