Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly does 'nervous system regulation' mean for someone my age in Holistic Medicine?
For you, nervous system regulation in Holistic Medicine means helping your body shift out of a 'fight or flight' stress response into a more relaxed 'rest and digest' state. This can involve gentle techniques to reduce anxiety, improve sleep, and lessen physical tension, helping you feel more balanced and at ease day-to-day.
Will the approaches used be too intense or complicated for me?
Not at all. We specialize in gentle Holistic Medicine approaches tailored for seniors. Our methods are designed to be comfortable and easily integrated into your life. We always prioritize your comfort and ensure that any techniques or recommendations are well within your capabilities, never overwhelming.
How quickly might I notice changes in my stress levels or comfort with Holistic Medicine?
The experience is unique for everyone, but many clients report feeling a greater sense of calm and reduced tension after just a few sessions. Our focus is on gradual, sustainable improvements in your nervous system's well-being, helping you build resilience over time rather than seeking quick fixes.
Can Holistic Medicine complement the care I already receive from my primary doctor?
Absolutely. Our Holistic Medicine services are designed to work alongside your existing medical care. We encourage open communication with your primary physician and can collaborate to ensure our gentle stress reduction and nervous system regulation practices support your overall health plan effectively.
