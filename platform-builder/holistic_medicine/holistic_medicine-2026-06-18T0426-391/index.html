Frequently Asked Questions

How does Holistic Medicine specifically address the physical pain linked to professional burnout?
We recognize that burnout isn't just mental exhaustion; it deeply impacts your physical body. Our Holistic Medicine approach uses techniques like acupuncture, bodywork, and somatic practices to release tension stored in your muscles, fascia, and nervous system, directly targeting the pain and stiffness that often accompany prolonged professional stress.
I've tried standard treatments for my back pain, but it keeps coming back. How is your approach different?
If your pain is chronic and linked to stress, standard treatments might only offer temporary relief. Our Holistic Medicine methods look beyond the symptom to understand how your lifestyle and stress patterns contribute to your pain. We integrate Eastern principles of energy flow with Western anatomical understanding to create a more comprehensive and lasting solution for your discomfort.
Will this require a significant time commitment given my already demanding work schedule?
We understand your time is valuable. Our sessions are designed to be efficient yet effective. While consistency is key for lasting change, we work with you to create a plan that fits your schedule, focusing on practical tools and targeted treatments that yield significant results without adding more stress to your life.
What exactly is 'somatic healing' and how can it help with my stress-related physical symptoms?
Somatic healing focuses on the mind-body connection, teaching you to become aware of and release physical tension stored in your body due to stress and past experiences. For burnout, this means learning to discharge chronic muscle tightness, improve posture, and calm an overactive nervous system, leading to profound and sustainable physical relief.
