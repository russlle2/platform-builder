Common questions

Do you promise specific outcomes?

We do not promise cures. Our focus is education, risk reduction, and realistic improvements supported by a collaborative plan.

Will I need labs?

Labs are optional and used selectively to inform care when evidence suggests they will change management.

Do you coordinate with my doctor?

Yes — we prioritize communication with your existing providers to ensure safe, integrated care.
