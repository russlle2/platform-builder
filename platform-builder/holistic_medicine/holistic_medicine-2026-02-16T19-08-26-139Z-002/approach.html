An evidence-informed, patient-centered process

Our care is collaborative: we listen, clarify priorities, and co-create a plan that blends lifestyle, education, and targeted interventions when appropriate. We follow up to support progress and adapt the plan.

1 — Intake & Listening

A detailed intake explores timelines, medical history, lifestyle patterns and goals. We identify possible contributors and immediate priorities.

2 — Shared Plan

We co-design a practical plan that may include nutrition, sleep practices, stress tools, botanicals or supplements when safe. Expect clear, measurable steps and educational resources.

3 — Optional labs & focused testing (educational)

When indicated, we discuss targeted laboratory options to clarify mechanisms. Labs are used to inform decisions, not as guarantees of outcomes.

4 — Follow-ups & Coordination

Follow-up visits monitor progress, refine strategies and coordinate with other clinicians. We emphasize achievable steps and long-term resilience.

All recommendations are individualized. We emphasize education, risk/benefit discussion, and shared decision-making. We do not guarantee outcomes.
