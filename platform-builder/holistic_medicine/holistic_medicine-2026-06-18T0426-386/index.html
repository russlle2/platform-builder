Frequently Asked Questions

How does Holistic Medicine help with my specific chronic pain from illness or injury?
Our Holistic Medicine approach focuses on understanding the entire picture of your health. Instead of just treating symptoms, we work to identify underlying imbalances contributing to your chronic pain. This might involve gentle bodywork, stress reduction techniques, or subtle energy practices tailored to soothe your nervous system and support your body's natural healing capacity, helping you find more sustained comfort.
I'm new to somatic healing. Is this Holistic Medicine practice suitable for someone without prior experience?
Absolutely! Our Holistic Medicine practice is designed to be gentle and accessible for everyone, especially those new to somatic healing. We understand that reconnecting with your body after chronic illness or injury can feel daunting, so we introduce concepts and practices at a pace that feels safe and comfortable for you. There's no prior experience needed, just an openness to explore new ways of finding ease in your body.
What should I expect during my first Holistic Medicine session for recovery?
Your first Holistic Medicine session will begin with a compassionate conversation where we listen carefully to your unique experiences with chronic pain or injury, and your recovery goals. We'll explore your health history in a relaxed setting. Based on this, we'll gently introduce a personalized plan, which might include light touch, breathing exercises, or guided awareness techniques, all designed to support your physical and emotional well-being without any pressure.
Will Holistic Medicine treatments interfere with my current medical treatments for chronic illness or injury?
Our Holistic Medicine approach is designed to complement your existing medical care, not replace it. We encourage open communication with your healthcare team. We focus on supportive, gentle techniques that can enhance your overall comfort and resilience during recovery, potentially reducing side effects from other treatments and supporting your body's ability to heal. We always prioritize your safety and well-being.
