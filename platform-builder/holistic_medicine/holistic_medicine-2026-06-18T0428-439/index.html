Frequently Asked Questions

How does Holistic Medicine specifically help parents with mental fog?
We delve into factors often overlooked, like micronutrient deficiencies, stress hormone imbalances, and gut-brain axis health, which significantly impact mental clarity. By addressing these root causes, you'll experience a tangible reduction in brain fog and improved cognitive function.
Is this approach compatible with my already packed parenting schedule?
Absolutely. Our Holistic Medicine plans are designed for integration into busy lives. We focus on practical, sustainable changes that yield significant impact without adding to your overwhelm. Think targeted interventions, not endless tasks.
What kind of results can I expect for my cognitive function?
Clients typically report enhanced focus, better memory recall, reduced irritability, and a greater sense of calm and control. Imagine navigating school pickups or meal prep with a clear, composed mind instead of feeling scattered.
Will I need to take a lot of supplements with your Holistic Medicine recommendations?
Not necessarily. Our approach is highly individualized. We prioritize dietary and lifestyle interventions first. If supplements are recommended, they are precisely chosen based on your unique needs and backed by scientific evidence, not a 'more is better' philosophy.
