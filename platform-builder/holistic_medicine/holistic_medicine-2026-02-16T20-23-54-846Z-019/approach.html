How we thoughtfully build your care plan

A collaborative rhythm of intake, gentle experiments, and follow-ups. We prioritize clarity, measurable steps, and safety-first decisions.

1 — Intake: we gather the story

The first visit is a wide-angle intake: health history, medications, sleep, stressors, daily rhythms and goals. We listen for patterns and start a shared hypothesis — not a one-shot diagnosis.

2 — Personalized plan: practical & prioritized

Plans contain a short list of doable changes: two to four core actions, a ritual or two, and natural supports when appropriate. Every recommendation is given with rationale and clear expectations.

3 — Optional labs (educational)

When useful, we discuss optional laboratory tests as educational tools — to clarify a hypothesis or monitor progress. Labs are never required and always interpreted in context.

4 — Follow-ups: iterate & scale

Short, regular check-ins help us change what isn’t working and reinforce what is. We prefer short cycles of action + reflection to big sudden overhauls.

5 — Safety, integration & referrals

We operate within safety limits, consult specialists when needed, and aim to integrate with your existing medical team rather than replace it.

What a first plan might look like

  • One simple sleep ritual to test for two weeks
  • Dietary adjustment focused on regular meals and chewing practice
  • A short herbal protocol (if appropriate) with clear stop criteria
  • Check-in at 3 weeks to review & adapt

Frequently asked

Do you promise cures? No. We focus on education, measurable improvements and whole-person strategies. Expectations are discussed at intake.

Are labs required? Only when they would meaningfully inform care. Labs are optional and presented as tools, not mandates.

How long until I feel better? Timelines vary. We set near-term experiments so you can see small wins within weeks and assess next steps together.
