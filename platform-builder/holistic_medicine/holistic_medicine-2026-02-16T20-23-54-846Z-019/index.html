Our Opening Ritual

Every good plan starts with listening. We combine thoughtful intake, gentle exams, and a shared goal-setting conversation to craft your personal path to balance.

  • Deep Intake: A whole-person history.
  • Functional Lens: Looking for root contributors — sleep, stress, digestion, inflammation.
  • Collaborative Plan: Tailored lifestyle, mind-body tools, and optional labs for clarity.

What to expect

Our sessions are conversational, evidence-informed, and paced to you. Expect education, practical steps, and a plan that grows with your progress — not promises of cures.

Personalized plan

Diet, movement, sleep hygiene, stress-resilience tools, and gentle supplements when appropriate.

Optional testing

Labs are educational tools — used when they add clarity to symptoms and choices.

Follow-up rhythm

Short check-ins, deeper follow-ups, and adjustments as life changes.
