Frequently Asked Questions

How does Holistic Medicine specifically help athletes manage physical stress?
Our Holistic Medicine approach focuses on regulating your nervous system, which is often overstimulated by intense training and competition. We use techniques that encourage your body to shift into a 'rest and digest' state, promoting deeper recovery, reducing inflammation, and building resilience against future stressors. This isn't just about temporary relief; it's about foundational support for sustained activity.
I'm always active; how can I tell if my nervous system needs support?
If you're an athlete or very active, signs your nervous system might need support include persistent fatigue despite adequate sleep, difficulty winding down, increased irritability, prolonged muscle soreness, or frequent minor injuries. Our Holistic Medicine practitioners are skilled at identifying these subtle cues and creating a plan to bring your system back into balance, helping you maintain peak performance without burnout.
Will Holistic Medicine treatments interfere with my training schedule?
Absolutely not. Our Holistic Medicine plans are designed to complement and enhance your existing training, not disrupt it. We focus on supporting your body's natural recovery processes and improving your overall capacity for stress, which can actually help you train more effectively and avoid setbacks. We work with your schedule to ensure treatments fit seamlessly into your active lifestyle.
What should I expect during my first visit to for Holistic Medicine?
During your first Holistic Medicine visit, will have a friendly, in-depth conversation with you about your athletic pursuits, your current stress levels, recovery challenges, and overall health goals. We'll explore how your body is responding to your active life and discuss a personalized approach to support your nervous system and enhance your resilience. It's a collaborative space where we listen and build a plan together.
