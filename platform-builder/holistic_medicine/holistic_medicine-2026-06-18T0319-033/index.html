Frequently Asked Questions

What is Holistic Medicine, and how does it help with stress for seniors?
Holistic Medicine at considers your entire well-being – mind, body, and spirit – to address stress. For seniors, this means gentle, non-invasive methods that support your body's natural ability to calm its nervous system, rather than just masking symptoms. We aim to identify and address the root causes of your stress.
Are the treatments gentle enough for someone over 60?
Absolutely. Our primary focus is on gentle care, specifically designed with the comfort and needs of seniors aged 60+ in mind. We prioritize methods that are soothing and supportive, ensuring your experience is always comfortable and never overwhelming, promoting a sense of ease and relaxation.
How long does it take to see results in nervous system regulation?
The time it takes to notice improvements can vary, as each person's response is unique. Many of our clients report feeling a greater sense of calm and better sleep within a few sessions. Our goal is to equip you with tools and support for sustained nervous system regulation over time.
Do you work alongside my existing doctors or medications?
Yes, we believe in an integrated approach to your health. We encourage open communication with your primary care physician and other specialists. Our Holistic Medicine services are complementary and designed to support your overall health plan, never to replace necessary medical care or prescribed medications.
