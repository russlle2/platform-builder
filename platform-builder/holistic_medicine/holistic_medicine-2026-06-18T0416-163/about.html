How is Holistic Medicine different for improving sleep in seniors?

Our approach for seniors aged 60+ is especially gentle and tailored. We prioritize methods that are safe, non-invasive, and consider the unique physiological changes and medication sensitivities that can occur with age, focusing on natural remedies and lifestyle adjustments to support your body's innate ability to rest deeply.

Will I need to take strong medications to improve my sleep?

Our core philosophy in Holistic Medicine is to work with your body's natural processes. We explore natural, gentle alternatives first, such as herbal supports, dietary adjustments, and calming practices. Our aim is to help you improve sleep without relying on strong medications, though we always respect and integrate with any medical care you currently receive.

How long does it take to see improvements in my sleep?

Improvement timelines can vary for each individual, as we address the root causes of your sleep challenges rather than just symptoms. Many of our clients report feeling a noticeable difference in their ability to relax and fall asleep more easily within a few weeks, with continued improvements in sleep quality over a few months of consistent practice.

What if I have other health concerns besides sleep?

As a Holistic Medicine practice, we understand that all aspects of your health are interconnected. While our primary focus for you will be on improving sleep, we consider your overall well-being. Any other health concerns you have will inform our gentle, personalized approach to ensure that your sleep plan supports your complete health picture.
