Frequently Asked Questions

How does Holistic Medicine address physical pain during major life changes?
Major life transitions can create significant stress, often resulting in physical pain or discomfort. Our holistic medicine approach considers how your emotional and mental state impacts your body. We use techniques that help release stored tension, soothe the nervous system, and restore balance, directly addressing the physical manifestations of your evolving life circumstances.
What kind of somatic healing can I expect for my body’s response to change?
Somatic healing focuses on the body's sensations as a pathway to release accumulated stress and trauma. During your sessions, you'll be guided through gentle movements, breathwork, and focused attention to help unwind chronic tension and pain patterns. This process can help your body feel safer and more settled as you adapt to new situations.
Is this approach suitable if my pain isn't directly related to a recent life event?
Absolutely. While we specialize in supporting individuals through transitions, our holistic medicine and somatic healing methods are effective for various types of physical pain and discomfort. Often, past experiences or ongoing stressors contribute to bodily symptoms. We aim to help you understand and address these underlying factors, regardless of their immediate trigger.
What is the first step to beginning Holistic Medicine care at ?
The first step is typically an initial consultation where we listen to your unique story, understand your current physical concerns, and discuss the life changes you're navigating. This allows us to create a personalized plan for your physical pain relief and somatic healing, ensuring our approach is tailored specifically to your needs and goals.
