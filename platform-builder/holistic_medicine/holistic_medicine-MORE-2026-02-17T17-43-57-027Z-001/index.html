Common questions

Is this clinical care?

This program offers educational, integrative support. It is not a substitute for urgent or ongoing clinical treatment. We encourage coordination with your healthcare providers.

What makes a cohort different?

Cohorts emphasize habit shaping, peer accountability, and rhythm rather than one-off fixes. You get both shared learning and individualized agenda-setting.

Can I join mid-cycle?

Sometimes—we evaluate fit case-by-case and provide a tailored onboarding to catch you up.
