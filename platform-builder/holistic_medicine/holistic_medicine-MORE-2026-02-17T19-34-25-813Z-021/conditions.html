Conditions we support — educational and supportive care

We provide evidence-aware, integrative approaches to a range of chronic and recurring concerns. Our work is educational and supportive — we do not claim cures. Decisions about medications and urgent care should be made with your primary medical provider.

Important: The information here is not medical advice. If you experience severe or life-threatening symptoms (chest pain, sudden weakness, trouble breathing, thoughts of harming yourself), call emergency services or your local emergency department immediately.

Common concerns we work with

  • Sleep & Fatigue — assessment of sleep patterns, behavioral supports, and targeted interventions.
  • Digestive irregularities — symptom tracking, food patterns, and stepwise testing when needed.
  • Chronic pain — pacing, movement therapies, and layered strategies to reduce flare frequency.
  • Stress, anxiety, and mood challenges — tools for regulation, lifestyle changes, and coordinated care with prescribers when appropriate.
  • Metabolic concerns — practical habit shifts, nutrition focus, and monitoring strategies.
  • Hormone-related concerns — cycle health, peri-menopause support, and individualized tracking.

How we approach complex or overlapping issues

We prioritize clarifying which concern most limits your day-to-day. From there, we sequence steps that reduce burden quickly while building toward longer-term resilience. This includes practical tracking, selective testing, and short focused check-ins.

When to bring documentation

Bring recent labs, medication lists, symptom logs, and any notes from other clinicians. That makes the first session more productive.
