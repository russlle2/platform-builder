How we work — layered, measurable, humane

Our clinical path is organized around practical gains: what can be tested and changed in the near term, and what grows resilience over months. Membership ensures continuity so we can tune steadily rather than rush fixes.

Core principles

  • Start with one manageable change and measure it.
  • Sequence the most impactful, lowest-burden interventions first.
  • Coordinate with your other clinicians when medication or specialty care is needed.
  • Use membership continuity to try, adjust, and embed practices without repeated re-intake.

Our process

Phase 1 — Orientation
A comprehensive intake to set priorities, collect baseline data, and set 1-2 immediate goals.
Phase 2 — Targeted action
Short, frequent check-ins while you try specific practices, trackers, and tests when appropriate.
Phase 3 — Embed & sustain
Widen the focus to maintenance, social supports, and long-term markers of success.

What membership changes

Members have scheduled touchpoints, priority booking, and ongoing access to curated materials so progress is cumulative instead of multiple one-off visits.
