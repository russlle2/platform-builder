Consultation agenda & follow-up

The checklist above informs a focused intake agenda and a follow-up cadence. Below we show the logic used so you see how recommendations are built.

How areas map to agenda items

  • Sleep: sleep history, sleep hygiene, brief screening for disordered sleep.
  • Nutrition: patterns, food sensitivities, targeted labs if indicated.
  • Movement: daily activity, mobility, realistic exercise targets.
  • Stress & Mood: context, coping strategies, circadian cues.
  • Energy & Fatigue: timeline, triggers, medication review.
  • Digestive Health: bowel patterns, food response, simple testing plan.
  • Hormonal Health: cycles, symptoms, relevant labs conversation.
  • Environment: exposures, sleep/chemical considerations, immediate mitigations.

Typical follow-up cadence

Based on selections, the planner suggests an initial review at 2–6 weeks with a membership-led cadence afterwards oriented around coaching and monitoring. These are educational recommendations, not treatment guarantees.
