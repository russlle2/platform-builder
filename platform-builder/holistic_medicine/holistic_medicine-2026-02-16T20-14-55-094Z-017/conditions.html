Chronic stress & anxiety

Tools for calming the nervous system, sleep hygiene, breathwork and gentle herbal support where appropriate.

Sleep disturbances

Behavioral strategies, evening routines, and selective, safe interventions to improve sleep onset and continuity.

Digestive discomfort

Dietary patterns, mindful eating, and targeted support to address bloating, irregularity and reflux symptoms.

Inflammatory symptoms

Lifestyle, nutrition and movement approaches that reduce systemic strain; we discuss labs only when clinically useful.

Low energy & fatigue

Work-within-your-life plans to restore steadier energy through sleep, nutrition and pacing strategies.

General wellness & prevention

Practical steps for long-term resilience—movement, stress buffering, and nutrient-rich eating.
