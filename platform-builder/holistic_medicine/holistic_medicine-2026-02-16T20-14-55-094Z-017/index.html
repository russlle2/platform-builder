Questions people often ask

Do you guarantee a cure?

No—holistic care focuses on education, risk reduction, and sustainable changes. Individual results vary and we do not promise cures.

Do you work with labs?

Yes, optional testing is part of the toolbox. Tests are used to inform education and planning, never as the sole answer.

How long is a typical plan?

Most plans start with a 6–12 week intensive phase and move to maintenance depending on goals and progress.

Is this covered by insurance?

Coverage varies. We provide documentation to submit claims, but please check with your insurer for details.
