What to expect in our clinical flow

1 — Intake & listening

A deep-dive conversation: history, priorities and current challenges. We co-create the goals here.

2 — Assessment

We review relevant history and any existing labs. If additional tests could change management, we discuss options and costs.

3 — Plan

A balanced plan with lifestyle steps, optional supplements or botanicals, and clear measurement points.

4 — Follow-up

Short check-ins to adjust, plus scheduled reassessments to track progress and safety.

About labs & testing (educational)

Tests can be informative when chosen for a specific question. We discuss who benefits, what each test can and cannot tell us, and avoid unnecessary or redundant panels. Common examples include:
  • Basic metabolic and thyroid panels — to screen common contributors to fatigue.
  • Targeted nutrient testing — when signs suggest deficiency and results would change recommendations.
  • Specialized GI testing — only if symptoms or history indicate likely benefit.
Testing is optional and always discussed with transparency about cost, utility and interpretation. We never use tests to oversell treatments.

Questions people ask about our approach

Do you promise results?
No. Each body is unique. We focus on realistic expectations, educational support and stepwise change—never guarantees.
Will you recommend prescription medications?
When within scope and clinically indicated, we may discuss pharmacologic options, but our orientation emphasizes lifestyle, coordination and evidence-based integrative tools.
What about herbal safety?
Herbs are considered when evidence and safety align with your care. We always review interactions with medications and existing conditions.
