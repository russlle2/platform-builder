Understanding Burnout's Impact: A Holistic Medicine Perspective

Burnout isn't just exhaustion; it's a complex interplay of chronic stress, emotional depletion, and a sense of reduced accomplishment. Our Holistic Medicine approach for working professionals aged 30-45 examines the physiological and psychological roots of your burnout, addressing factors like adrenal fatigue, sleep disruption, and the emotional toll of workplace demands. We help you identify the specific stressors contributing to your current state, moving beyond surface-level symptoms to support a deeper, more sustainable recovery.
