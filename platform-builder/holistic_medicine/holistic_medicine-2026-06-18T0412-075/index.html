Frequently Asked Questions

How can Holistic Medicine help with my college anxiety and academic pressure?
Holistic Medicine at focuses on addressing the underlying patterns contributing to your anxiety. We provide personalized strategies to regulate your nervous system, giving you concrete methods to reduce stress responses, improve focus, and build resilience against academic pressures.
I'm already swamped with schoolwork. Will this add more to my plate?
Our goal is to simplify, not complicate. We integrate practical, actionable steps into your existing routine. The time you invest in learning to regulate your nervous system will ultimately free up mental space, improve your efficiency, and reduce the energy you currently spend battling stress.
What specific techniques does Holistic Medicine use for stress reduction?
We utilize a range of evidence-informed Holistic Medicine approaches tailored to your needs, which may include targeted breathing exercises, mindfulness practices for focus, dietary considerations, and lifestyle adjustments designed to calm your nervous system and support your mental clarity amidst academic demands.
How long until I start feeling a difference with Holistic Medicine?
Many students report feeling a greater sense of calm and control after just a few sessions as they begin implementing the strategies. Consistency is key, and we're here to support you in building habits that lead to sustained stress reduction and nervous system regulation throughout your college career.
