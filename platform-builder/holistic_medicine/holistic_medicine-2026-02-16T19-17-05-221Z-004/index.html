Questions people ask

What is an Intensive visit?
An Intensive is a longer initial visit (usually 60–120 minutes) to build a thorough history, baseline metrics, and a clear 90-day plan. Optional labs or imaging are discussed as educational tools.
Do you promise cures?
No. We focus on measurable improvements and sustainable changes. Medicine is about probability, not guarantees.
Will insurance cover visits?
Coverage varies. We provide superbills and can discuss billing options during intake.
