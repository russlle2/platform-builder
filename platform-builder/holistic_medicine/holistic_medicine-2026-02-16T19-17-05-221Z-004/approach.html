A thoughtful process — science-minded, person-centered

Our work blends clinical reasoning with practical coaching and supportive therapies. Below is how we typically partner with clients. Every step is collaborative and tailored; labs and supplements are optional and educational, never mandatory.

1
Comprehensive Intake
A deep review of history, lifestyle, medications, and goals. We listen first.
The initial visit gathers context to form a safe, prioritized plan. We ask about patterns, past interventions, and your resources for change.
2
Personalized Care Plan
Clear, practical steps you can try right away.
Plans often combine nutrition adjustments, sleep and stress tools, botanicals when appropriate, and movement — with measurable short-term goals.
3
Follow-up & Coaching
Frequent, supportive check-ins to refine the plan.
We use follow-ups to troubleshoot, support adherence, and celebrate progress. Intensive clients receive an accelerated schedule of touchpoints.
4
Coordination & Education
We collaborate with your other clinicians and provide the rationale behind recommendations.
Clear communication ensures safety — especially when herbs or supplements may interact with medications.

Optional labs — educational tools

When helpful, we may suggest targeted tests to illuminate nutrient status, thyroid, metabolic patterns, or gut ecology. Tests are selected to answer specific clinical questions and framed as learning instruments — they inform choices but do not dictate them.

Examples of optional tests
  • Comprehensive metabolic panel & thyroid panel
  • Micronutrient assessments (vitamin D, B12, ferritin)
  • Advanced functional markers (inflammation, insulin sensitivity)
  • Stool or microbiome profiles for persistent GI issues

Safety & boundaries

We do not promise cures. If you have urgent symptoms or a serious diagnosis, we will coordinate care with specialists and emergency services as needed. All recommendations are made with attention to safety and your existing medical plan.
