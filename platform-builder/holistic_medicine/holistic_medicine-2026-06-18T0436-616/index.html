Frequently Asked Questions

How does Holistic Medicine specifically address immune support for someone recovering from chronic illness?
Our Holistic Medicine approach involves a thorough assessment to pinpoint specific immune deficiencies or overactivity. We then use evidence-based interventions like targeted nutritional therapy, specific botanical medicines, and gut health protocols designed to rebalance your immune system, enhance its protective functions, and reduce inflammation, all crucial for a stable recovery.
What kind of vitality building strategies does offer for those recovering from injury?
For those recovering from injury, our vitality building strategies in Holistic Medicine focus on supporting tissue repair, reducing pain, and restoring energy. This can include optimizing nutrient intake for healing, addressing mitochondrial function to combat fatigue, and implementing gentle, restorative practices that aid your body in rebuilding strength and stamina without overexertion.
Will the Holistic Medicine treatments interfere with my current medical prescriptions for my chronic illness?
Your safety and well-being are paramount. Before recommending any Holistic Medicine treatment, we thoroughly review your current medical prescriptions and health history. We carefully select interventions that are known to be compatible and will discuss any potential interactions with you and, if appropriate, with your other healthcare providers to ensure a coordinated and safe approach to your recovery.
How long does it typically take to see improvements in immune function and vitality with Holistic Medicine after chronic illness?
The timeline for improvement in immune function and vitality with Holistic Medicine varies based on the nature and duration of your chronic illness or injury, as well as your individual response. While some individuals may notice subtle changes within weeks, a more significant and lasting impact typically unfolds over several months as your body systematically rebalances and rebuilds. We focus on sustainable, long-term improvement.
