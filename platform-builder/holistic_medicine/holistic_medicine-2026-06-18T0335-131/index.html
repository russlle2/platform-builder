Frequently Asked Questions

How does Holistic Medicine specifically address sleep issues for professionals experiencing burnout?
Our approach within Holistic Medicine goes beyond surface-level symptoms. We investigate the underlying physiological and psychological factors contributing to your professional burnout and sleep disruption. This involves examining stress hormones, circadian rhythm imbalances, and lifestyle patterns unique to your demanding schedule, creating a targeted plan for restorative rest.
What kind of commitment is required to see results in my sleep and energy levels?
Achieving lasting improvements in sleep and energy through Holistic Medicine is a process, not an overnight fix. We typically recommend an initial commitment of 8-12 weeks to establish new patterns and see significant shifts. Your personalized plan will outline actionable steps that fit within your professional life, designed for sustainable progress.
Will I need to make drastic changes to my busy work schedule?
We understand your professional life is demanding. Our Holistic Medicine strategies are designed to integrate seamlessly into your existing commitments, not overhaul them. We focus on efficient, impactful adjustments and intelligent scheduling that optimize your current routine for better rest and energy management, without adding more stress.
Is this approach evidence-based, or is it more theoretical?
Our Holistic Medicine practice is firmly rooted in evidence. We utilize principles from sleep science, neurobiology, and clinical research, alongside traditional modalities that have demonstrated efficacy. Your personalized plan will be informed by current scientific understanding of sleep, stress physiology, and restorative practices.
