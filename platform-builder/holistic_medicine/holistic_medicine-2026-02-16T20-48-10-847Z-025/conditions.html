Common concerns we support

We take an educational, whole-person view. Below are typical areas people bring to sessions; recommendations focus on lifestyle, supportive botanicals or supplements when appropriate, and targeted skills. This is not a substitute for urgent medical care.

Stress & Anxiety

Practical breathing techniques, sleep hygiene, pacing, and adaptive routines to reduce allostatic load. Sessions teach tools to manage day-to-day reactivity.

Poor Sleep

Behavioral approaches to sleep timing, stimulus control, and evening routines. We offer education on environmental factors and non-pharmacologic strategies before considering supplements.

Digestive Upset

Diet pattern mapping, mindful eating, and gentle protocols to support digestion. When needed, we discuss testing options as part of a broader plan.

Chronic Inflammation

Lifestyle and food-based steps to reduce inflammatory burden and educate around evidence for nutritional approaches. We avoid promises of reversal and focus on measurable improvements.

Low Energy & Fatigue

Assess sleep, diet, stress, and activity. Coaching emphasizes small, sustainable shifts — pacing, restorative practices, and prioritized sleep interventions.

Important: These descriptions are educational. If you have severe symptoms, acute changes, or urgent concerns, please seek immediate medical attention. Care here complements mainstream medicine and may include referrals or collaboration when appropriate.
