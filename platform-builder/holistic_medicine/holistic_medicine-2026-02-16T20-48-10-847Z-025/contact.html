A note on how we help

Our care encourages curiosity — examining sleep, movement, digestion, stress, and daily rhythms — and creating plans that fit your life. We emphasize education, measured change, and follow-up rather than promising cures. If you’re seeking urgent medical care, please call your local emergency services or primary care.

How contacting works

After you reach out we’ll confirm a brief intake call to understand goals and logistics. From there, options include a single consult, a short series of visits, or our membership for ongoing collaborative care. Labs and specialty testing are discussed as educational options, not mandatory steps.

What to expect next
  1. Quick scheduling call or email to clarify your needs
  2. Complete a short intake form (secure link)
  3. Choose between a single visit, a plan, or membership

Membership & shared care

Membership focuses on continuity — priority booking, learning resources, and quarterly reviews that adapt as life changes. Ideal for people seeking an ongoing partner for lifestyle, prevention, and symptom support.
