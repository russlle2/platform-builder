Four-step framework

1. Intake & Story

A compassionate, thorough conversation to map history, priorities, and current resources.

2. Individualized Plan

Clear, prioritized steps across sleep, nutrition, movement and stress skills — tailored and paced to your life.

3. Tools & Education

Practical lessons you can use immediately: breathwork, restorative routines, and food-based strategies.

4. Follow-ups & Tracking

Regular check-ins to adjust the plan and celebrate small wins. We emphasize measurable habits over quick fixes.

Optional: Educational Labs

When helpful for clarity, we discuss non-invasive testing (labs) as an educational tool to inform plans, not as stand-alone solutions.

Collaboration

We coordinate with your other providers when appropriate and refer for specialized care if needed.

About optional labs

Labs can help illuminate patterns (nutrient status, basic metabolic context) but are used judiciously. If considered, results inform education and individualized plans. We do not recommend testing for its own sake.

What to expect at your first visit

  1. 60–75 minute intake: story, goals, current routines
  2. Clear summary and 2–4 immediate action steps
  3. Optional supportive recommendations with rationale and follow-up plan

A note on outcomes

We focus on capacity-building and education. Individual responses vary; our aim is durable improvements in daily functioning rather than guaranteed cures.
