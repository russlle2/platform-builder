Our process — learning, planning, tending

  1. 1. Intake & listening

    A careful conversation to map concerns, goals, and context. This is education-first: we gather data to inform possibilities.

  2. 2. Personalized plan

    A collaborative plan combining lifestyle guidance, simple practices, and optional educational labs (if helpful).

  3. 3. Events & follow-ups

    Attend an event cohort for guided learning; brief touchpoints keep progress steady. No guarantees — only support and resources.

  4. 4. Optional labs & resources

    We may suggest educational testing to deepen understanding. Labs are optional and explained clearly before any recommendation.
