How we work — clear, compassionate, and evidence-minded

Our method centers on listening first, then crafting an individualized plan that you can actually follow. Common tools: education, lifestyle shifts, strategic labs, and supportive coaching.

The step-by-step intake & care flow

1
Intake & story
We map symptoms, medical history, sleep, stressors, routines and goals — so recommendations fit your life.
2
Assessment
Where useful, selective labs are discussed as educational tools to direct treatment — not as mandatory steps.
3
Plan & priorities
A practical blueprint with small, measurable steps and timelines. We focus on what to do first to gain momentum.
4
Support & follow-up
Scheduled check-ins to adjust the plan, celebrate wins, and troubleshoot barriers.
5
Integration
Sustaining change through habit design, community resources, and handed-over tools for long-term resilience.
About optional labs
Labs (bloodwork, stool, hormone panels) are offered when they will change management. They are used as educational data points, not guarantees. We review results and translate them into clear next steps if they add actionable insight.

What to expect in your first visit

  • Detailed health history and goal-setting
  • Initial recommendations you can implement immediately
  • Discussion of whether labs would be informative
Follow-ups
Typically 20–40 minutes depending on the focus. We track outcomes and refine priorities each visit.
Teamwork
We collaborate with your other providers when helpful and coordinate referrals if specialized care is needed.
