How can Holistic Medicine help me manage the physical effects of parenting stress?

Our Holistic Medicine approach focuses on understanding how stress manifests in your body, from tight shoulders to persistent fatigue. We use gentle techniques to release muscle tension, improve posture, and calm your nervous system, offering you tools to address those aches and pains that come with daily parenting demands.

I'm always short on time. How can I fit somatic healing into my busy schedule?

We understand that time is a precious commodity for parents. Our somatic healing sessions are designed to be efficient yet effective. We'll also equip you with simple, short practices you can integrate into your day, helping you find moments of release and self-care even when you're juggling many responsibilities. It's about quality over quantity.

Is Holistic Medicine safe and effective for parents with young children?

Yes, absolutely. Our Holistic Medicine practices are gentle and non-invasive, focusing on supporting your body's natural ability to heal. We tailor every session to your specific needs and comfort level, ensuring a safe and supportive experience as you navigate the unique physical and emotional demands of parenting young children.

What should I expect during my first Holistic Medicine appointment?

During your first Holistic Medicine appointment, we'll have a warm, open conversation about your concerns, daily routines, and how stress impacts your body. We'll listen attentively to your experiences as a parent. Then, Dr. Morgan Ellis will guide you through gentle assessments and introduce you to some initial techniques to begin your path toward physical ease and inner calm.
