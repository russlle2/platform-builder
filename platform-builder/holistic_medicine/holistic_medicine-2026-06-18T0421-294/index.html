Frequently Asked Questions

How does Holistic Medicine specifically address emotional healing for athletes and active people?
Our Holistic Medicine approach recognizes that physical stress, injuries, and performance pressure can lead to emotional imprints, sometimes traumatic. We use trauma-informed techniques to help your nervous system process these experiences, preventing emotional blocks from hindering your physical recovery or future performance. It's about healing the mind to heal the body.
I'm always pushing my body. Can Holistic Medicine help with the mental fatigue that comes with physical stress?
Absolutely. The mental fatigue you describe is a common side effect of sustained physical stress. Our Holistic Medicine methods, including mindfulness and specific somatic practices, are designed to help you regulate your nervous system, reduce burnout, and restore mental clarity, allowing you to train and perform with renewed focus and energy.
What kind of trauma-informed care does your Holistic Medicine practice offer for injury recovery?
Injury can be a deeply unsettling and even traumatic experience for active individuals. Our Holistic Medicine practice provides a supportive environment to process the emotional impact of injury, including fear, frustration, and grief. We use gentle, body-centered techniques to help you reconnect with your body positively, supporting psychological recovery alongside physical rehabilitation.
Will Holistic Medicine interfere with my current training or physical therapy regimen?
No, our Holistic Medicine approach is designed to complement your existing training and physical therapy. We focus on the emotional and energetic aspects of your well-being, which can actually enhance your body's ability to heal and adapt. We aim to integrate seamlessly into your overall health strategy, supporting your goals without conflict.
