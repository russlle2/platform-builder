Frequently Asked Questions

How does Holistic Medicine specifically address emotional healing for someone recovering from chronic illness or injury?
Our Holistic Medicine approach recognizes that physical setbacks often lead to emotional distress, anxiety, or even trauma. We use specialized techniques to help you process these feelings, reduce chronic stress, and rebuild your sense of self and control, moving beyond the identity of your illness or injury.
What if my physical recovery is still ongoing; can Holistic Medicine still help my emotional well-being?
Absolutely. Emotional healing is not contingent on complete physical recovery. In fact, addressing your emotional well-being concurrently can significantly support your physical healing process. Our Holistic Medicine methods can help you cope with pain, manage fatigue, and maintain a positive outlook even while your body continues to mend.
I feel like my chronic illness or injury has changed who I am. Can Holistic Medicine help me find my 'new normal'?
Yes, many individuals experience a shift in identity after a significant health event. Our Holistic Medicine care is designed to help you grieve what has been lost and empower you to redefine your strengths and capabilities. We work with you to cultivate self-compassion and build a resilient sense of self that embraces your present reality.
Is a trauma-informed Holistic Medicine approach different from general therapy?
Yes, a trauma-informed Holistic Medicine approach specifically acknowledges the potential for past and current trauma to impact your health and recovery. It means we operate with sensitivity to your experiences, create a safe space, and use strategies that help regulate your nervous system without re-traumatizing you, which is particularly vital when dealing with chronic illness or injury.
