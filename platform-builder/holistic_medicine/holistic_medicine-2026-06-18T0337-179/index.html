Frequently Asked Questions

How does Holistic Medicine specifically help parents with sleep?
Our Holistic Medicine approach looks at your unique situation as a parent – your daily stressors, energy demands, and the specific challenges young children bring to sleep. We develop a personalized plan that addresses physical, mental, and emotional factors affecting your rest, moving beyond quick fixes to create lasting changes for truly restorative sleep.
I'm already overwhelmed. Will this program add more to my plate?
Absolutely not. Our goal with Holistic Medicine is to simplify and empower you. We break down strategies into manageable steps and focus on practical solutions that integrate seamlessly into your busy family life, not add more tasks. Our coaching provides support and accountability, making it easier to prioritize your rest without feeling additional pressure.
What if my child's sleep is the main reason I can't rest?
We understand that young children often dictate parental sleep. While we don't directly coach child sleep, our Holistic Medicine approach empowers you to maximize the sleep you *do* get and build resilience for those interrupted nights. We'll equip you with strategies to manage stress, optimize your environment, and recover more effectively, even when your child's sleep isn't perfect.
How quickly can I expect to see improvements in my rest with Holistic Medicine?
Improvements vary for everyone, but many parents report feeling a shift in energy and sleep quality within a few weeks of consistently applying our Holistic Medicine strategies. Our focus is on sustainable progress rather than instant, temporary fixes, so you can build long-term habits for lasting rest and reduced stress.
