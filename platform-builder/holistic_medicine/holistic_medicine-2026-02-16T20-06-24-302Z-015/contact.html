Communication is confidential. We prioritize safety: if you’re experiencing a medical emergency, please call local emergency services immediately. The following describes usual flow for non-urgent consultations.

Frequently asked

Do you offer same-day appointments?

Occasionally. If you have an urgent concern, please indicate that in your message and we will do our best to accommodate or direct you to appropriate resources.

Will you prescribe medication?

We may discuss medications as part of a balanced plan when clinically indicated. Prescribing follows standard medical guidelines and is tailored to safety and need.

Can I request labs?

Labs are optional and used to support understanding; we will discuss the potential value and limitations before ordering.
