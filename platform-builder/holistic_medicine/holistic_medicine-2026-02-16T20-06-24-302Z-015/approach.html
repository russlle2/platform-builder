A clear framework for personalized, integrative care

Our model combines clinical assessment with practical lifestyle, nutritional, and supportive interventions. We emphasize education, shared decision-making, and measurable steps. Lab testing is optional and used as an educational tool when it will inform care.

1. Intake & Listening

Extended initial visit to gather history, goals, and priorities. We review current treatments and identify immediate, safe steps you can take.

2. Collaborative Plan

We co-create a realistic plan with 1–3 priority actions: dietary adjustments, movement, sleep strategies, and targeted botanical or supplement options when appropriate.

3. Support & Follow-up

Follow-ups focus on troubleshooting, education, and gradual progress. Plans are adjusted based on response and new information.

Educational testing — when and why

We sometimes recommend targeted labs to clarify causes or to guide nutrient supports. Testing is always optional and chosen to add actionable information. Examples include metabolic panels, thyroid, nutrient markers, and targeted inflammatory markers.

Metabolic & CBC
Baseline health and red flags
Thyroid panel
Energy, weight changes, or hair loss
Inflammatory markers
Persistent pain or systemic inflammation
Nutrient testing
Targeted nutrients when deficiency is suspected

Safety & coordination of care

We review current prescriptions and coordinate with other providers as needed. Herbal and supplement recommendations consider possible interactions and are introduced cautiously. Our priority is patient safety and integrative collaboration.

How progress is measured

We set practical goals and track symptoms, functional markers (sleep, energy, digestion), and occasionally lab trends. Progress is framed in achievable milestones rather than promises of cure.
