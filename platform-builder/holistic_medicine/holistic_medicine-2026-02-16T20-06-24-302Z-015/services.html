Session Ritual

Visits begin with a focused intake, listening to what matters most to you. We aim to provide clear teaching, one to three practical actions you can implement, and a plan for follow-up. Herbal or supplement recommendations are offered with safety in mind and documented interactions considered.

What to Expect

You will receive an individualized plan with priorities, expected timelines, and educational resources. We emphasize realistic, sustainable steps and measure progress over time.

Common questions

Do you accept insurance?
We provide superbills for many plans; direct billing varies. Check with your insurer for coverage details.
Are herbal medicines safe?
Herbals can be supportive but may interact with medications. We discuss safety and monitor changes. Recommendations are educational and individualized.
Can I bring my labs?
Yes — sharing recent labs helps. We can recommend targeted tests when clinically appropriate.
