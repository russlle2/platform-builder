Do I need to be 'more calm' to benefit?

No—your current emotional state is exactly where we begin. We meet you in the middle of the chaos, not after you've achieved 'calm.' Your messy kitchen is our starting point.

How is this different from regular therapy?

It's trauma-informed care specifically for parents managing day-to-day stress. We focus on your body's physical stress responses during typical parenting moments—not just talking about childhood experiences.

Can this help during naptime when I'm exhausted?

Yes. We teach micro-practices that take 60 seconds during moments you're already having—like waiting for the coffee to brew or while your child plays quietly.

Is this just for parents with big traumas?

No. This is for anyone feeling overwhelmed by the emotional labor of parenting young children. It's about the daily accumulation of stress, not just past experiences.
