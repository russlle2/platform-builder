How an integrative plan takes shape

We use a stepwise, collaborative process: gather context, identify priorities, try practical steps, review results. Testing is an option when it will change decisions.

Ready for change?

Intake & story

We explore medical history, lifestyle, stressors, and your priorities so recommendations fit your life rather than the reverse.

Assessment

Assessment synthesizes patterns from labs, symptoms, and habits to form hypotheses — always explained in plain language.

Plan & education

Plans include practical steps, rationale, and expected markers of progress. Education empowers you to apply and adapt interventions.

Follow-up & adaptation

Follow-ups check response, troubleshoot, and advance the plan. Medicine is iterative; we course-correct based on data and experience.

About optional labs

Labs and functional testing are tools, not defaults. We discuss when testing may clarify a diagnosis or change the plan, and we focus on tests that will alter decisions. Interpretations are educational and shared with you; any abnormal findings are communicated with recommended next steps or referrals.

What to expect at your first visit

  • A 60–90 minute intake for comprehensive context
  • Prioritized list of 2–3 initial steps you can start immediately
  • Clear plan for follow-up and indicators we'll use to gauge progress
