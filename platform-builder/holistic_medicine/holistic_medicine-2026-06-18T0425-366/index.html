Frequently Asked Questions

How can Holistic Medicine specifically help my recovery from chronic illness or injury?
Our Holistic Medicine approach focuses on your unique experience of chronic illness or injury, acknowledging that your body has been through significant stress. We address not just the site of pain, but the underlying patterns, nervous system dysregulation, and emotional impact of your condition, offering gentle, comprehensive support for your physical and somatic healing.
I'm sensitive to many treatments due to my condition. Is your approach gentle enough for me?
Absolutely. Our methods are designed with your sensitivity in mind. We prioritize gentle, non-invasive techniques that respect your body's current capacity and pace. We work collaboratively with you, ensuring every step feels safe and supportive, never pushing beyond what feels comfortable for your unique recovery journey.
Will this replace my current medical care for my chronic illness or injury?
No, our Holistic Medicine services are designed to complement and enhance your existing medical care, not replace it. We work as part of your support team, providing additional tools for pain relief, somatic regulation, and overall well-being that can greatly benefit your recovery alongside conventional treatments.
How quickly can I expect to feel a difference in my physical pain or discomfort?
Healing from chronic illness or injury is a gradual, personal process. While some clients experience immediate shifts, our focus is on sustainable, long-term improvement rather than instant fixes. We aim to equip you with lasting strategies for comfort and resilience, respecting your body's timeline for integration and recovery.
