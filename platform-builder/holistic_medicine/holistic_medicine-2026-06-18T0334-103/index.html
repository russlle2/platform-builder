Frequently Asked Questions

How does Holistic Medicine specifically help seniors with sleep?
Our Holistic Medicine approach recognizes the unique physiological changes that can affect sleep in later life. We focus on gentle, natural methods like dietary adjustments, specific relaxation techniques, and supportive botanicals that work with your body, not against it, to encourage your natural sleep rhythms.
Will I need to take strong medications for sleep with this approach?
Our primary focus in Holistic Medicine is to support your body's innate ability to sleep well without relying on strong medications. We explore natural, gentle alternatives first, aiming to address the root causes of your sleep challenges rather than just masking symptoms. Any recommendations will always prioritize your comfort and well-being.
Is Holistic Medicine safe if I'm already taking other medications?
Absolutely. Your safety is our top priority. As part of our Holistic Medicine consultations, we thoroughly review your current medications and health history to ensure that any recommendations are safe and complementary. We will always discuss potential interactions and work collaboratively with your other healthcare providers.
How long does it take to see improvements in my sleep with Holistic Medicine?
While everyone's experience is unique, many of our clients in Holistic Medicine begin to notice subtle improvements in their sleep quality within a few weeks of consistently applying their personalized plan. True, lasting restorative sleep is a gradual process, and we're here to support you every step of the way as you gently reclaim your rest.
