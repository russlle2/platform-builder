Frequently Asked Questions

Why is nervous system regulation so crucial during postpartum recovery?
Your nervous system has undergone immense changes during pregnancy and birth. Postpartum, chronic sleep deprivation, hormonal shifts, and the constant demands of a newborn can keep your body in a state of high alert. Regulating your nervous system helps reduce anxiety, improve sleep, stabilize mood, and supports your overall physical and emotional recovery, allowing you to be more present and calm for yourself and your baby.
What kind of stress reduction techniques will I learn for my postpartum period?
You will learn practical, accessible techniques designed to fit into your busy life as a new mother. These include gentle breathwork, mindful body awareness practices, targeted relaxation exercises, and strategies to identify and mitigate common postpartum stressors. Our goal is to equip you with tools you can use anytime, anywhere, to bring your system back to a state of calm.
How does Holistic Medicine specifically address the needs of new mothers?
Holistic Medicine acknowledges the interconnectedness of your physical, emotional, and mental well-being, especially during postpartum. We don't just focus on symptoms; we look at the root causes of your stress and dysregulation, considering factors like nutrition, sleep, hormonal balance, and emotional support. Our approach is personalized to ensure you receive comprehensive care that supports your whole self during this sensitive time.
Is this program suitable if I'm feeling completely overwhelmed and exhausted?
Absolutely. This program is designed specifically for new mothers who feel overwhelmed and exhausted. We start exactly where you are, offering gentle, step-by-step guidance. Our priority is to create a nurturing space where you can begin to offload some of that burden and learn strategies to replenish your energy and regulate your nervous system, even amidst the chaos of new motherhood.
