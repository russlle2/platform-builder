Myths we hear — and what really matters

Myth: One pill fixes it

Truth: Medications help many conditions, but relief is often found by layering lifestyle, nutrition, and mind-body work tailored to you.

Myth: Alternative means unproven

Truth: We use evidence-informed modalities and prioritize safety. Education and shared decision-making are core to care.

Myth: Fast cure is realistic

Truth: Sustainable change is gradual. Our membership supports follow-up, incremental progress, and coaching—never promises of a cure.
