How we work — a practical, whole-person process

Our approach is structured: intake, assessment, collaborative planning, and follow-up. We use optional, targeted testing only as an educational aid to refine recommendations.

1. Pre-Visit Intake

You complete concise forms focused on history, current priorities, sleep, diet, and stressors. This helps us use time efficiently during the visit.

2. In-Depth Visit

During a VIP Day or standard visit we explore context, triggers, and goals. The visit is educational and collaborative; we discuss evidence, trade-offs, and realistic steps.

3. Personalized Plan

You receive a prioritized, practical plan — small, measurable actions for nutrition, sleep, movement, and self-care routines. Supplements or prescriptions are considered only when helpful and safe.

4. Follow-Up & Iteration

Structured follow-ups (30–45 mins) allow troubleshooting, adjustment, and gradual progression. Our goal is sustainable change, not rapid fixes.

Optional, educational labs

Labs are discussed as tools to inform the plan, not as mandatory steps. When recommended, they are chosen selectively and explained in plain language.

Basic metabolic & thyroid
When fatigue or metabolic concerns are present, we may review bloodwork trends with you and discuss implications.
GI & microbiome panels (educational)
For persistent digestive symptoms, tests can provide additional context; results are integrated with symptom patterns and lifestyle.
Hormone snapshots
When cycle or menopausal issues are central, targeted testing can clarify timing and guide interventions.

Collaboration and safety

We coordinate with your other providers when appropriate, and we prioritize safety — discussing medication interactions and contraindications before recommending any supplement or therapy.
