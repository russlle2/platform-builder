Frequently asked — practical answers

Do you bill insurance?
We are primarily fee-for-service. We can provide a superbill for clients to submit to certain insurers; coverage depends on your plan. Payment policies are reviewed at booking.
Are labs required?
Labs are optional tools used when clinically appropriate. They are discussed in the intake and pursued only with informed consent. We focus on interpretation and practical next steps.
What if I need ongoing care?
If sustained support is indicated, we design a plan that may include regular check-ins, referrals, or community resources. The VIP Day is designed to create a clear, teachable roadmap.
Cancellation & reschedule policy
For VIP Day bookings, please notify us at least 7 days in advance to reschedule. Fees and refunds are explained during booking. Emergencies are handled case-by-case.
