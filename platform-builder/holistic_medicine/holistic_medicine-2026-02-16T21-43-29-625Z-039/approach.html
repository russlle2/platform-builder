Curiosity-led, data-informed care

I combine listening, practical education, and stepped plans so you can make decisions with clarity. Labs and supplements are tools—not mandates.

Intake — The first conversation

A guided review of history, daily rhythms, sleep, digestion, stress patterns, and any current labs or medications.

Collaborative plan

We co-create a prioritized, realistic plan with small experiments and measurable goals rather than sweeping changes.

Follow-ups & tuning

Regular check-ins to review progress, tweak supports, and troubleshoot barriers; habit momentum is the goal.

Optional labs — educational use

When helpful, we may review basic labs (nutrient levels, thyroid, metabolic markers) to inform the plan. Labs are optional and framed as one piece of the puzzle.

Principles I work by

  • Start with the least invasive, most evidence-aligned steps.
  • Teach you the "why" so practices are sustainable.
  • Measure, iterate, and avoid overcomplication.

Practical questions

Will I be given a long supplement list? Not typically. Any supplements recommended are chosen for clear purpose and reviewed regularly.
How soon will I notice changes? Small habit changes can feel different within days; sustained improvements are often seen after several weeks of consistent practice.
