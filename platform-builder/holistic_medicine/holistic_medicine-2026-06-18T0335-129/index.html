Frequently Asked Questions

How can Holistic Medicine specifically help me with sleep as a parent of young children?
Our approach acknowledges the unique stressors of parenting. We don't just offer generic sleep advice; we delve into your individual circumstances, integrating ancient wisdom and gentle practices to address hormonal imbalances, nervous system dysregulation, and emotional fatigue that often contribute to sleep issues for parents. We help you find tailored solutions for true restorative rest.
I'm already overwhelmed. Will this add more to my to-do list?
Absolutely not. Our goal is to simplify and soothe, not add burden. We focus on integrating small, impactful practices into your existing routine, rather than creating new, time-consuming tasks. Our recommendations are designed to be gentle, intuitive, and supportive, helping you ease into better sleep without adding extra pressure.
What kind of practices can I expect from a Holistic Medicine sleep consultation?
You can expect personalized guidance drawing from ancient traditions. This might include recommendations for specific calming herbs, gentle movement practices, guided relaxation techniques, dietary adjustments to support sleep, or emotional processing tools. Everything is tailored to your unique needs and capacity as a busy parent.
Is this approach suitable if I'm also dealing with other household stresses besides sleep?
Yes, entirely. We view sleep as a cornerstone of overall well-being. By addressing your sleep, you'll often find an improved capacity to manage other household stresses, emotional demands, and daily challenges. Our Holistic Medicine perspective considers your entire life context, helping you build resilience across the board.
