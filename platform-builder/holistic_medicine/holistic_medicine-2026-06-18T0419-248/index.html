Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is 'trauma-informed care' in Holistic Medicine?
Trauma-informed care in Holistic Medicine means we understand that past difficult experiences can deeply affect your current emotional and physical health. Our approach is always gentle and respectful, focusing on safety, trust, and empowerment, ensuring you feel comfortable and in control throughout your healing process, helping you maintain your vitality.
How can emotional healing specifically benefit my mental clarity as a retiree?
Emotional healing can significantly improve your mental clarity by reducing the cognitive load that unresolved emotions can create. When you process past burdens, your mind is freed to focus on the present, enhancing memory, decision-making, and overall cognitive function, allowing you to enjoy a more vibrant and clear-headed retirement.
Will I have to revisit painful memories in detail during my sessions?
Our approach prioritizes your comfort and safety. While we explore the impact of past experiences, we do so gently and collaboratively. You are always in control of what you share, and we use techniques that don't require re-traumatization, focusing instead on processing and releasing the emotional charge, helping you achieve peace and maintain vitality.
Is Sage Garden Holistic Medicine suitable for all backgrounds?
Absolutely! We are deeply committed to being an inclusive and welcoming community for everyone, regardless of your background, beliefs, or life experiences. We believe that emotional healing is a universal need, and we strive to create a safe, respectful, and supportive environment for all retirees seeking to enhance their mental clarity and vitality.
