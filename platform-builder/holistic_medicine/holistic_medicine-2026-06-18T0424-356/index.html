Frequently Asked Questions

How does Holistic Medicine specifically address chronic pain from illness or injury?
Our Holistic Medicine approach for chronic pain looks beyond the immediate symptoms. We meticulously assess how your illness or injury has impacted your entire system, including structural imbalances, nervous system dysregulation, and energetic blocks. By addressing these interconnected factors, we aim to reduce your pain at its source, promoting a more stable and comfortable physical state.
Will this approach be too intense if my body is sensitive from chronic conditions?
Absolutely not. Our methods are tailored with utmost sensitivity to your current physical state. We understand that bodies recovering from chronic illness or injury often require a gentle, nuanced touch. We prioritize your comfort and safety, gradually introducing techniques that encourage your body to release tension and find ease without overwhelming it.
How long does it typically take to feel results with somatic healing for my condition?
Recovery from chronic conditions is highly individual. While some individuals report noticeable shifts in comfort and mobility after just a few sessions, lasting results typically emerge over a series of treatments as your body re-learns healthier patterns. Our focus is on sustainable improvement, not quick fixes, ensuring the changes you experience are meaningful and enduring for your long-term well-being.
Is Holistic Medicine a complementary approach to my existing medical treatments?
Yes, our Holistic Medicine services are designed to complement your existing medical care. We respect and encourage your ongoing relationship with your doctors. Our role is to provide specialized somatic healing and pain relief that supports your body's intrinsic ability to recover and thrive, working in harmony with any treatments you are already receiving.
