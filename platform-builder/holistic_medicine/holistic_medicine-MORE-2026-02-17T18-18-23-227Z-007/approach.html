Our method

Assessment, small tests, and durable routines

We emphasize clear priorities: identify what's actionable now, test gently, and translate what works into everyday rhythms. This lowers friction and helps sustain gains.

1. Clarify

Comprehensive intake focusing on history, context and safety checks.

2. Test

Short, measurable practice trials and targeted diagnostics when needed.

3. Stabilize

Supportive follow-ups and habit design that fit daily life.

What to expect in a first visit

  • Review of symptoms and a focused safety screening
  • Baseline measures we can track (sleep, activity, symptom rating)
  • A short list of prioritized changes with clear checkpoints

How we frame progress

Improvement is often stepwise. We assess early signals of benefit and keep the plan simple: trackable outcomes, one or two practice changes, and scheduled reassessment. This reduces overwhelm and clarifies what helps.
