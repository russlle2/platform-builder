Conditions we work with

We support people living with common chronic concerns. Our work centers assessment, symptom management and sustainable self-care strategies. Care is evidence-informed and adapted to each person.

Important: This website is educational. Our team does not promise cures. Specific diagnoses and treatments are discussed during evaluation. For urgent medical needs, seek emergency care.

Sleep disruption

Behavioral strategies, timing consistency, and selective testing when indicated to clarify causes of insomnia or non-restorative sleep.

Persistent low energy

Broad review of sleep, nutrition, movement and medical contributors followed by practical pacing and targeted labs if needed.

Digestive complaints

Symptom mapping, diet patterns and gentle trials to narrow triggers; appropriate testing coordinated when indicated.

Mood and anxiety concerns

Integrated plan combining behavioral tools, social and sleep supports, and liaison with psychiatric providers when medication review is needed.

Chronic pain & activity limits

Function-first approach: pacing, graded movement and environmental adjustments to reduce daily impact and improve quality of life.
