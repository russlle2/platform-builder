How can Holistic Medicine help me with postpartum stress?

Holistic Medicine supports your postpartum stress by addressing the root causes. We focus on nervous system regulation through gentle techniques, nutritional support, and lifestyle adjustments tailored to your unique needs, helping you find a sustainable sense of calm amidst the demands of new motherhood.

Is your approach safe for new mothers, especially while breastfeeding?

Absolutely. Your safety and well-being, and that of your baby, are our top priority. All recommendations are carefully considered with your postpartum recovery and any breastfeeding in mind. We prioritize gentle, non-invasive methods and will always discuss any concerns with you openly.

What does 'nervous system regulation' mean for a new mother?

For a new mother, nervous system regulation means helping your body shift out of 'fight or flight' mode, which is common with sleep deprivation and new responsibilities, and into a more relaxed, 'rest and digest' state. This helps reduce anxiety, improve sleep quality, and enhance your ability to cope with daily stressors.

How quickly can I expect to feel a difference in my stress levels?

Every mother's journey is unique, and progress varies. Some mothers experience a sense of immediate relief and calm during our sessions, while others notice gradual, yet significant, improvements in their stress levels and overall well-being over a few weeks as they integrate new practices into their routine. We’re here to support you every step of the way.
