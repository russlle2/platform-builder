Understanding Stress in Transition: A Holistic Medicine View

Major life changes, whether expected or not, can significantly elevate stress and dysregulate your nervous system. Our Holistic Medicine approach focuses on identifying these specific stressors and their physiological impacts. We then provide precise, evidence-informed interventions to help your body and mind adapt, rather than react, to new circumstances, fostering greater internal stability.
