How we design a brief, testable plan

We emphasize clarity: a small experiment, measurement, and an agreed follow-up. The goal is learning, not promises.

1

Listen & map

An intake to gather history, rhythms, and what you’ve already tried. We map energy and symptom patterns together.
2

Design a small trial

Two to three targeted changes with simple measurements so you can tell if things move.
3

Review & adapt

A follow-up to look at results, keep what helps, and modify what doesn’t.
Practical framing:

We recommend short timelines (2–8 weeks) and measurable goals: sleep minutes, symptom count, or energy windows. These are learning aims rather than clinical guarantees.

Timeline planner

Pick target dates for each phase. Use this as a soft roadmap — you can adjust anytime.

Note: This planner is for personal planning and education. It is not a replacement for clinical scheduling or urgent care. If you have significant concerns, coordinate with your medical team.

Quick suggestions

  • Set Phase 1 for 7–14 days.
  • Use Phase 2 for a focused experiment (2–4 weeks).
  • Mark Phase 3 as a maintenance check-in or longer follow-up.
