Three-phase roadmap

Each phase is small, time-bound, and evidence-aware. Use this planner to set realistic steps for 6–12 weeks. Educational content only; if you have serious or worsening symptoms, reach out to licensed care.

Phase 1 — Stabilize (2–3 weeks)

Focus on immediate, low-risk supports: sleep window, basic hydration, short movement, and clearing medication questions with your clinician.

Phase 1 is general educational guidance. It does not replace urgent medical care.

Phase 2 — Restore (4–6 weeks)

Build stability into routines and add restorative practices like breath work, graded activity, or focused sleep hygiene.

If symptoms intensify, pause and consult a clinician. This plan is educational.

Phase 3 — Integrate (ongoing)

Adapt strategies to life stressors and transition to maintenance with periodic check-ins.

This phase focuses on sustainable routines. Not a substitute for ongoing medical management when needed.

Micro-habits to try this week

Small, concrete experiments you can do alone or with coaching support. Track for two weeks and note any changes.

  • Night anchor: 10 minutes before bed, dim lights and list three wins from today.
  • Midday reset: Two brief movement breaks (3–5 minutes) after lunch and mid-afternoon.
  • Signal snack: A protein-led snack within an hour of activity to support energy.
