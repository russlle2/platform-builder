Frequently Asked Questions

What does 'nervous system regulation' mean in Holistic Medicine for seniors?
Nervous system regulation means helping your body and mind shift from a state of stress or 'fight-or-flight' to a calmer, more relaxed state. In Holistic Medicine, we use gentle techniques tailored for seniors to encourage this balance, helping you feel more peaceful and less overwhelmed.
Is Holistic Medicine gentle enough for someone my age?
Absolutely! Our Holistic Medicine practices are specifically designed to be gentle and safe for seniors. We prioritize your comfort and well-being, using methods that support your body's natural processes without causing strain. Your care plan will always be adapted to your unique needs and preferences.
How can Holistic Medicine help with my daily stress?
Holistic Medicine offers various tools to help you manage daily stress. We can explore gentle techniques like mindful breathing, specific nutritional support, or calming herbal remedies. Our goal is to empower you with simple, effective strategies that you can use to reduce stress and promote a sense of calm whenever you need it.
Do I need to have prior experience with Holistic Medicine?
Not at all! Many of our clients are new to Holistic Medicine, and we are delighted to guide you. We explain everything clearly and ensure you feel comfortable and informed about every aspect of your care. Our inclusive environment means all backgrounds and levels of experience are warmly welcomed.
