Frequently Asked Questions

How can Holistic Medicine specifically help me as a parent dealing with constant stress?
As a parent, your body often holds stress in predictable ways like shoulder tension, headaches, or digestive upset. Our Holistic Medicine approach directly addresses these physical manifestations by gently guiding your body to release stored tension and regulate your nervous system, offering profound relief so you can feel more resilient and present.
I'm always busy with my children. How do I fit Holistic Medicine into my already packed schedule?
We understand your time is precious. Our Holistic Medicine sessions are designed to be deeply effective, providing significant relief in a focused amount of time. We also provide simple, practical techniques you can integrate into your daily life to maintain your calm and physical comfort between appointments, fitting seamlessly into your parenting routine.
Will Holistic Medicine help with my chronic back pain from lifting toddlers and carrying bags?
Absolutely. The physical demands of parenting often lead to chronic pain patterns. Our Holistic Medicine focuses on somatic healing, helping your body release the deep-seated tensions and imbalances contributing to your back pain. We work to restore natural movement and strength, giving you lasting comfort and ease for all your parenting tasks.
I feel emotionally drained from managing everything. Can Holistic Medicine help with this feeling of depletion?
While our primary focus is physical pain, the mind-body connection is central to Holistic Medicine. Releasing physical tension and calming your nervous system often has a profound positive impact on your emotional state, reducing feelings of overwhelm and depletion. By nurturing your body, you also create space for greater emotional resilience and inner peace.
