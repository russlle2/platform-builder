Frequently Asked Questions

How is your holistic medicine different when my illness is ongoing?
We focus on what you need right now, not a future 'cure.' Your emotional state affects your physical recovery, so we address both together. You'll learn practical tools to manage daily stress, connect with your body without fear, and find moments of peace even during difficult symptoms.
Do you work with people who have complex medical histories?
Absolutely. Many of our clients manage multiple conditions. We coordinate with your doctors, prioritize your emotional needs alongside physical care, and never overwhelm you with more appointments. Your care is personalized around your life, not the other way around.
What if I'm not ready to talk about my trauma?
You never have to. We start where you are—whether that's just learning to sit with discomfort or identifying what feels safe. Your pace is respected, and we focus on building emotional stability before diving deeper. Your comfort is our priority, always.
How do I know if emotional healing is right for me?
You don't need to feel 'ready' to begin. Emotional healing means finding ways to feel safer in your own body while managing your illness. If you've felt dismissed or rushed by medical providers before, this is the space where you're heard exactly as you are, without pressure to 'fix' yourself quickly.
