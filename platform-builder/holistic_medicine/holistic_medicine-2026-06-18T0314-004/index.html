Frequently Asked Questions

How is Holistic Medicine different from standard medical care?
Holistic Medicine considers the full tapestry of your life—physical, emotional, and environmental factors—unlike symptom-focused care. We address root causes of imbalance through personalized strategies that fit your unique circumstances, not generic treatment plans.
What happens during your first Holistic Medicine visit?
Your initial consultation begins with a compassionate, thorough discussion about your health history, lifestyle, and aspirations. We listen without judgment, then partner with you to design a care plan that integrates natural therapies, nutritional guidance, and practical self-care steps tailored to your life.
Do you work with insurance providers?
We don't bill insurance directly, but many clients use out-of-network benefits for Holistic Medicine services. We provide detailed billing codes to simplify reimbursement and offer flexible payment plans so care remains accessible without compromising quality.
How soon will I notice positive changes?
You'll often feel subtle shifts within weeks—like improved energy or better sleep—though deeper transformation requires time. Most clients achieve meaningful progress within 3-6 months through our collaborative, adaptive approach. We adjust your plan regularly to honor your unique pace.
