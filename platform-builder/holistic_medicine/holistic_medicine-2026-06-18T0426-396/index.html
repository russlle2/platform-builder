Frequently Asked Questions

How does Holistic Medicine at specifically address chronic pain from illness or injury?
We focus on understanding the full scope of your pain, not just where it hurts. Our methods combine approaches like targeted bodywork, movement education, and mindful awareness practices to release deep-seated tension, improve circulation, and recalibrate your nervous system. This helps reduce chronic discomfort and supports your body's natural healing processes following illness or injury.
I've tried many therapies for my recovery; what makes your Holistic Medicine approach different?
Our distinction lies in our integrative model. We don't offer a one-size-fits-all solution. Instead, we carefully assess your unique history and current physical state. thoughtfully blends modalities from both Western and Eastern traditions, specifically tailored to help individuals recovering from chronic conditions re-establish comfort and mobility, rather than just temporarily masking symptoms.
Is somatic healing suitable for someone with physical limitations due to a long-term injury or illness?
Yes, absolutely. Somatic healing is often very gentle and adaptable. It emphasizes listening to your body's current capacity and working within those boundaries. Our goal is to incrementally expand your comfort and range of motion, without pushing you into further discomfort. This approach is particularly effective for those with physical limitations, as it builds resilience from within.
How long does it typically take to see results with Holistic Medicine for chronic recovery?
The timeline for seeing results can vary greatly depending on the nature and duration of your chronic illness or injury, as well as your individual response. Many clients report feeling increased ease and improved body awareness within a few sessions. Our focus is on sustainable progress and equipping you with tools for long-term self-management, rather than quick fixes.
