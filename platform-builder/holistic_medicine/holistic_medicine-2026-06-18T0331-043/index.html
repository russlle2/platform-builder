Frequently Asked Questions

How can Holistic Medicine help with stress and nervous system regulation for someone over 60?
Our Holistic Medicine approach focuses on gentle, natural methods that respect your body's wisdom. We aim to identify and address the root causes of stress, offering personalized strategies that can calm your nervous system, improve sleep, and enhance your overall sense of peace without harsh interventions.
Is Holistic Medicine safe and appropriate for seniors with existing health conditions?
Yes, our Holistic Medicine practices are designed to be very gentle and adaptable. We always take your complete health history into account, ensuring that any recommendations are safe and complementary to your existing care. We encourage open communication with your primary healthcare providers to ensure a coordinated approach to your well-being.
What kind of techniques does use for stress reduction?
We utilize a range of gentle, evidence-informed Holistic Medicine techniques tailored to seniors. These might include mindful breathing exercises, specific nutritional guidance, gentle movement recommendations, and personalized botanical support, all aimed at calming your nervous system and reducing feelings of overwhelm. We focus on what feels right for you.
How long does it take to see results from Holistic Medicine for stress relief?
The experience of stress reduction and nervous system regulation is unique to each individual. Many clients report feeling a greater sense of calm and ease after just a few sessions. Our Holistic Medicine approach emphasizes sustainable practices that you can integrate into your daily routine, leading to lasting comfort and improved well-being over time.
