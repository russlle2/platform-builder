Frequently Asked Questions

How does Holistic Medicine specifically help with college anxiety and academic pressure?
Our Holistic Medicine approach focuses on regulating your nervous system, which is often overstimulated by academic demands. We use evidence-informed techniques to reduce physical tension, calm racing thoughts, and build your resilience, helping you navigate deadlines and social pressures with greater ease and less overwhelm.
Is Holistic Medicine a good fit if I'm already seeing a therapist for anxiety?
Absolutely! Our Holistic Medicine services can complement existing mental health support. We focus on the physiological aspects of stress and anxiety, offering practices like mindful breathing, targeted nutrition, and lifestyle adjustments that work synergistically with therapy to enhance your overall well-being and nervous system regulation.
What kind of commitment does working with Holistic Medicine at involve?
We understand your busy college schedule. Our Holistic Medicine plans are tailored to your needs and availability. We'll start with an initial conversation to understand your unique stressors, then create a manageable plan that fits into your life, focusing on practical tools you can integrate daily to support your nervous system and reduce stress.
Will Holistic Medicine help me perform better academically?
While our primary focus is on stress reduction and nervous system regulation, many students find that as their anxiety lessens and their mind becomes clearer, their ability to focus, retain information, and perform under pressure naturally improves. By cultivating a calmer state, Holistic Medicine can indirectly support your academic success.
