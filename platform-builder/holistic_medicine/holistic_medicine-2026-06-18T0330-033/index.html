Frequently Asked Questions

How does Holistic Medicine specifically address stress reduction for seniors?
Our Holistic Medicine approach focuses on gentle, individualized methods that acknowledge the unique physiological changes and life experiences of seniors. We prioritize techniques that are non-invasive and easy to integrate into your daily life, specifically targeting nervous system balance to alleviate stress and its physical manifestations.
What kind of techniques are used to regulate the nervous system?
We utilize a blend of gentle, evidence-informed practices such as mindful breathing exercises, targeted nutritional support, subtle movement recommendations, and personalized botanical remedies. The aim is to calm your sympathetic nervous system and enhance your parasympathetic response, promoting a state of relaxation and restoration.
Is this approach suitable if I'm already seeing other medical professionals?
Absolutely. Our Holistic Medicine services are designed to complement your existing healthcare regimen. We encourage open communication with your other providers to ensure a cohesive and supportive care plan. Our focus is on enhancing your overall well-being and stress resilience, working alongside your current medical team.
What can I expect in my first session for stress and nervous system support?
Your initial session will involve a comprehensive, gentle conversation about your health history, current stress levels, and specific concerns. We'll discuss your lifestyle and goals, allowing us to collaboratively develop a personalized plan that feels comfortable and effective for you, focusing on practical steps for immediate relief and long-term regulation.
