Frequently Asked Questions

How does Holistic Medicine specifically address burnout-related sleep problems for someone like me?
For working professionals aged 30-45 experiencing burnout, our Holistic Medicine approach looks at more than just sleep duration. We assess underlying factors like chronic stress, adrenal fatigue, nutritional deficiencies, and circadian rhythm disruption, integrating Eastern and Western methods to restore balance and improve sleep quality, not just quantity.
What kind of treatments can I expect from a Holistic Medicine practitioner focused on sleep?
You can expect a comprehensive assessment followed by a tailored plan. This might include personalized herbal formulations, acupuncture for nervous system regulation, dietary recommendations, stress reduction techniques, and sleep hygiene coaching, all within a Holistic Medicine framework designed to address your unique needs as a busy professional.
I've tried sleep aids before with limited success. How is this different?
Unlike conventional sleep aids that often provide temporary relief, our Holistic Medicine approach aims to resolve the root causes of your sleep disturbance. We focus on natural regulation of your sleep cycles and nervous system, empowering you with sustainable strategies rather than dependency on external substances, which is crucial for long-term well-being and managing burnout.
How long until I start seeing improvements in my sleep and energy with Holistic Medicine?
Improvements can vary depending on your individual situation and consistency with the plan. Many clients report initial shifts in sleep quality and energy within a few weeks, with more significant and lasting changes typically observed over 2-3 months as your body rebalances through our Holistic Medicine interventions.
