Three-part framework

1. Clarify context — Daily patterns, recent changes, and environmental factors that move the needle.
2. Target small experiments — One habit, one measurement, one practical adjustment for a short window.
3. Iterate and stabilize — Review data, refine rituals, and add supports as needed.

Session shapes

Sessions can be diagnostic, focused, or maintenance. We craft time and tools to match the current phase of care.
  • Diagnostic: deeper review, mapping, and a 30–60 day targeted plan.
  • Focused: short interventions and troubleshooting for a single problem.
  • Maintenance: regular review, accountability, and long-term adjustments.
