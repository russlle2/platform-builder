Our method: small, testable adjustments with clear check-ins

We call it an iterative pathway: map, try, measure, adjust. The goal is to make simple changes you can live with and judge by how they affect your day-to-day function.

Core promise
Clear actions after visits; measurable checkpoints; humane pacing.
Where we do not replace
Emergency care, specialized surgical decisions, and urgent medication management.
Map
Pattern discovery — your rhythms, triggers, and constraints.
Experiment
One or two low-burden changes to test for 7–14 days.
Measure
Simple metrics you can track — sleep logs, symptom notes, brief scales.
