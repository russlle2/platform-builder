Frequently Asked Questions

How does Holistic Medicine at specifically help seniors improve their sleep?
We focus on understanding the root causes of your sleep disturbances, which can be different as you age. Our gentle methods, drawing from ancient traditions, aim to rebalance your body's natural rhythms, quiet your mind, and create a tranquil internal environment conducive to deep, restorative sleep. We consider your overall well-being, not just the symptom of sleeplessness.
Are the Holistic Medicine approaches gentle and safe for someone over 60?
Absolutely. Our entire philosophy is built on gentleness and respect for your body's wisdom. We prioritize non-invasive, nurturing practices such as specific herbal blends, mindful breathing exercises, and subtle energetic work. Every recommendation is carefully tailored to your individual health profile and comfort level, ensuring safety and efficacy.
What kind of commitment is involved in working with for sleep improvement?
Improving sleep is a soulful process, not an overnight fix. We encourage a compassionate commitment to your well-being. Our initial consultations are thorough, and follow-up sessions are scheduled to support your progress. We ask you to engage in the practices we suggest, allowing the ancient wisdom to slowly and gently unfold its benefits in your life, leading to lasting rest.
Will I need to stop any current medications if I pursue Holistic Medicine for sleep with you?
It's essential to always consult with your primary healthcare provider regarding any changes to your medications. Our Holistic Medicine practices are designed to complement, not replace, conventional medical care. We work collaboratively to ensure your overall health and safety, integrating our gentle approaches thoughtfully alongside your existing health regimen.
