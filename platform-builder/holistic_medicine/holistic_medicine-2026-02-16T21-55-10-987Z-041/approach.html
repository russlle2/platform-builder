How a visit unfolds — transparent, educational, collaborative

We treat visits like a dialogue: careful intake, practical experiments, and short, focused follow-ups. Tests are tools — not the default.

1 — Intake

A generous conversation mapping history, current routines, and priorities. 60–90 minutes for first visits.

2 — Personalized plan

Clear, measurable steps: sleep anchors, gentle movement, nutrition experiments, and stress tools. Each plan is tailored and adjustable.

3 — Follow-ups

Short, focused sessions to iterate the plan, troubleshoot obstacles, and teach sustainable habits.

4 — Optional investigations

When indicated, selective lab tests can clarify questions. We discuss pros/cons and interpret results together — education first.

Philosophy in a sentence

We prefer small, reversible experiments and clear measuring points. The goal is to increase your confidence and capacity to manage health — not to promise instant cures.

Commonly discussed tests (educational)

  • Basic metabolic panels and blood counts — to rule out common contributors to fatigue.
  • Thyroid and hormone screens — when symptoms suggest imbalance.
  • Targeted inflammation markers — used selectively to inform a plan.
  • Stool or microbiome assessments — sometimes useful for persistent digestive issues; interpretation is nuanced.
These tests are tools to inform conversation, not one-size-fits-all answers.

Questions about the process

How soon will I feel better?
Timelines vary. Some people notice changes in days with simple habit shifts; others need weeks of practice. We set realistic expectations together.
Will you suggest supplements?
When appropriate, supplements may be offered as short-term tools. We prioritize lifestyle changes and explain why any supplement is recommended.
