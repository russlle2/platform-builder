Gentle Relief for Postpartum Physical Strain through Holistic Medicine

Your body has undergone a monumental shift, and it’s natural to feel new aches, pains, or imbalances. Our approach to Holistic Medicine focuses on easing common postpartum physical challenges like back pain, pelvic discomfort, and muscle tension. We work with you to understand your specific needs, offering tailored support to bring comfort back to your daily life as a new mother.

What specific types of postpartum physical pain does Holistic Medicine address?

Our Holistic Medicine practice addresses a range of postpartum physical discomforts, including lower back pain from carrying or feeding, pelvic floor tension, wrist and hand pain (often called 'mommy thumb'), shoulder and neck stiffness, and general fatigue-related body aches. We tailor our approach to your unique areas of discomfort.

How does somatic healing in Holistic Medicine help new mothers recover?

Somatic healing in Holistic Medicine helps you tune into your body's signals, allowing for the gentle release of physical and emotional tension stored from childbirth and the demands of new motherhood. It fosters a deeper connection to your physical self, supporting your body’s natural ability to recover and adapt, and helping you feel more present and calm.

Is Holistic Medicine safe alongside other postpartum care you might be receiving?

Absolutely. Our Holistic Medicine services are designed to complement your existing postpartum care, including medical check-ups or physical therapy. We prioritize open communication and can collaborate with your other healthcare providers to ensure a cohesive and supportive recovery plan for you.

Will your Holistic Medicine services fit my schedule as a new mother?

We understand the demands on a new mother's time. Our Holistic Medicine practice strives to offer flexible scheduling options to accommodate your needs. We encourage you to reach out and discuss your availability, and we'll do our best to find a time that works for you and your baby.
