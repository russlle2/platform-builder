How we work

A three-part process: gather, co-design, and simplify into implementable steps. Each stage is brief and focused to avoid overwhelm.

The three-stage rhythm

1 — Gather

We collect a compact history and daily rhythm snapshot to identify pattern drivers—timing, stressors, and routine frictions.

2 — Co-design

Together we prioritize a small set of experiments and practical practices tied directly to your main goal.

3 — Simplify

We produce a pared-down action packet with specific timing, measures to watch, and two follow-ups to adjust the plan as you go.

Benefits of this approach

Low friction
Small, testable changes you can keep doing.
Measurable
We track a couple of simple signals so you know if something is working.
Coordinated
We make plans that fit with other clinicians and your schedule.
Adaptive
The plan evolves based on real-world feedback rather than theory alone.
