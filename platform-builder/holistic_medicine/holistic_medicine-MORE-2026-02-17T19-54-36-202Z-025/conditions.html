Conditions we commonly work with

We offer educational, evidence-aware support across a range of non-emergency concerns. Below are areas where clients often see practical gains with structured, paced work.

Important disclaimer:

Services are educational and integrative. We do not provide emergency medical care or promise cures. If you have severe or worsening symptoms, sudden changes, or urgent health needs, seek prompt medical attention. Our work is intended to complement—never replace—your primary care team.

Stress, overwhelm & burnout
Practical pacing strategies, sleep-priority adjustments, and micro-habits to rebuild energy without adding more tasks.
Sleep difficulties
Behavioral and lifestyle tactics to support sleep opportunity and restorative patterns. Includes light exposure, timing, and gentle routine shifts.
Digestive complaints
Structured food-pattern experiments, meal pacing, and troubleshooting for common bloating and discomfort. We prioritize gentle, reversible tests.
Hormonal transitions
Lifestyle and symptom-management strategies for periods of change—peri-menopause, postpartum rhythm shifts, and other transitions. Collaborative, patient-centered care with your clinicians.
Mood variations & focus
Practical supports for daily regulation: routine structure, sleep hygiene, light and movement timing, and social rhythm tweaks.

What we do—and what we don't

Educational, not prescriptive medical care
We discuss options and craft plans that you can run under supervision of your healthcare providers where relevant.
Supportive monitoring
Short follow-ups help refine experiments and adjust pacing; objective tracking is encouraged when indicated.
