Frequently Asked Questions

How does Holistic Medicine specifically help parents dealing with daily household stress?
We focus on the unique pressures of parenting young children, addressing issues like sleep deprivation, emotional overwhelm, and constant demands. Our Holistic Medicine approach helps you regulate your nervous system, process underlying emotional triggers, and develop practical coping strategies tailored to your busy life, so you can respond more calmly to daily challenges.
I'm worried about my past affecting my parenting. Can your trauma-informed Holistic Medicine help me?
Absolutely. Our trauma-informed care acknowledges how past experiences can influence your present reactions and stress levels. We provide a safe, supportive environment within Holistic Medicine to help you gently process these experiences, understand their impact, and develop healthier response patterns, so you can parent from a place of greater calm and intention.
What kind of time commitment does working with entail?
We understand your time is precious as a parent. Our sessions are designed to be efficient yet effective. We'll discuss a personalized plan that fits your schedule, often starting with regular check-ins and then adjusting as you build confidence and implement new strategies. Our goal is to empower you with lasting tools, not create another dependency.
Will Holistic Medicine help me feel less reactive and more patient with my children?
Yes, that's a primary focus of our work. By addressing the root causes of your stress and emotional reactivity through Holistic Medicine, you'll gain a deeper understanding of your triggers. We'll equip you with techniques to manage big emotions, calm your nervous system, and respond to your children from a more grounded and patient place, fostering a more peaceful family dynamic.
