Frequently Asked Questions

How can Holistic Medicine help with my postpartum stress?
Holistic Medicine approaches postpartum stress by looking at the interconnectedness of your physical, emotional, and mental health. We focus on gentle, natural strategies to rebalance your nervous system, reduce cortisol levels, and improve sleep quality, which are all crucial for mitigating stress during this sensitive time.
Is nervous system regulation truly possible when I'm sleep-deprived with a new baby?
Yes, absolutely. While sleep deprivation is a reality with a newborn, we implement practical, evidence-based techniques that can significantly impact your nervous system's ability to cope, even with limited sleep. These might include specific breathing exercises, targeted nutritional support, or gentle botanical remedies designed to promote calmness and resilience.
Will these Holistic Medicine approaches interfere with breastfeeding or my baby's health?
Your and your baby's safety are paramount. All recommendations are carefully considered in the context of breastfeeding and your postpartum recovery. We prioritize safe, gentle, and non-invasive methods, always discussing potential interactions and ensuring our approach is compatible with your unique circumstances and any other care you are receiving.
What can I expect during my first consultation regarding postpartum stress and nervous system support?
During your initial consultation, we will have a comprehensive discussion about your health history, current postpartum experiences, and specific concerns regarding stress and nervous system regulation. This allows us to gain a full understanding of your unique needs and collaboratively develop a personalized, gentle care plan focused on supporting your well-being.
