Frequently Asked Questions

How is Holistic Medicine different for addressing my burnout and sleep issues?
Unlike conventional approaches that might only mask symptoms, our Holistic Medicine strategy identifies and addresses the interconnected physiological and psychological factors contributing to your burnout and sleep problems. We look beyond surface-level issues to optimize your entire system for lasting restorative rest, using evidence-based, science-meets-wellness methods.
I'm a busy professional; how can I realistically integrate new sleep habits into my demanding schedule?
We understand your time is valuable. Our approach focuses on efficiency and realistic implementation. We'll work with you to design strategies that fit seamlessly into your existing routine, prioritizing impact over volume. Small, consistent changes, scientifically applied, yield significant results without adding more stress to your plate.
Will I need to make drastic lifestyle changes to see results?
Our goal is sustainable improvement, not radical overhaul. We focus on targeted, impactful adjustments rather than overwhelming you with drastic changes. We'll identify the most critical areas for intervention in your specific case, ensuring that any modifications are practical, manageable, and tailored to your lifestyle and professional demands.
How quickly can I expect to feel a difference in my energy and sleep quality?
While individual results vary, many of our clients report noticeable improvements in sleep quality and daytime energy within a few weeks of implementing their personalized plan. Our focus is on long-term, sustainable improvement, but we also prioritize strategies that deliver tangible early wins to keep you motivated and feeling better sooner.
