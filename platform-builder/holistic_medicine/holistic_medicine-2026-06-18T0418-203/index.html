What does 'trauma-informed' mean in Holistic Medicine for seniors?
For seniors, 'trauma-informed' means our care at is delivered with deep respect for your life experiences, ensuring every interaction is gentle, safe, and empowering. We focus on building trust and avoiding anything that might feel overwhelming, creating a supportive environment for your emotional healing journey. Your comfort and sense of security are our top priorities.
How can Holistic Medicine help with emotions for someone over 60?
Holistic Medicine at offers gentle techniques to help you process emotions that may have lingered over the years. We focus on non-invasive methods like mindful awareness, gentle touch, and supportive conversation to foster emotional balance and inner calm. Our goal is to help you feel lighter and more at peace, without dwelling on past difficulties in a distressing way.
Is your approach suitable if I'm not comfortable talking about past difficult events?
Absolutely. Our gentle Holistic Medicine approach respects your comfort level entirely. You are never pressured to discuss anything you're not ready for. We can work with you using techniques that focus on present feelings and cultivating inner resources, rather than direct discussion of past events.
