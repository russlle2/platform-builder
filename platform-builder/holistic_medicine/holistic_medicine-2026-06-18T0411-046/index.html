Frequently Asked Questions

How does Holistic Medicine specifically help with nervous system regulation during chronic illness recovery?
Our Holistic Medicine approach gently introduces practices and insights that help re-pattern your nervous system's stress responses. This can involve techniques to reduce chronic tension, improve sleep quality, and cultivate a sense of inner calm, all of which are crucial for your body's ability to recover and adapt.
I'm experiencing ongoing fatigue from my injury; can Holistic Medicine still be beneficial for my stress levels?
Absolutely. Chronic fatigue often intertwines with an overactive stress response. Our Holistic Medicine strategies are tailored to meet you where you are, focusing on methods that don't add to your energy burden but rather support your system in finding more restful states, even with limited energy.
Is 's Holistic Medicine approach compatible with my current medical treatments?
Yes, our Holistic Medicine practice is designed to complement your existing medical care. We encourage open communication with your healthcare team and see our role as supporting your body's inherent capacity for balance and regulation alongside other treatments. Our goal is to enhance your overall well-being during recovery.
What if I'm not sure if my symptoms are related to nervous system dysregulation? Can Holistic Medicine still help?
Many symptoms experienced during chronic illness or injury recovery have a nervous system component. Our initial conversations in Holistic Medicine focus on understanding your unique experiences and how stress might be impacting your well-being. We'll explore gentle strategies that can often bring relief and improved comfort, regardless of the primary cause.
