Understanding Your Postpartum Body: A Holistic Medicine Approach

Your body has undergone incredible changes, and it's natural to experience aches, strains, and shifts in sensation. Our Holistic Medicine approach focuses on truly listening to your body's story, offering a safe space to address pelvic floor discomfort, back pain, C-section scar tenderness, and overall somatic realignment. We honor your unique experience and support your physical recovery with gentle, effective methods.

What kind of physical pains can Holistic Medicine help with during postpartum recovery?

We specifically address discomforts common to new mothers, such as lower back pain, pelvic floor tenderness, C-section scar sensitivity, wrist pain (De Quervain's tenosynovitis), neck and shoulder tension from feeding, and general body soreness. Our aim is to bring gentle, effective relief to these specific areas.

Is Holistic Medicine safe for me if I'm still bleeding or recovering from birth?

Absolutely. Our methods are gentle and tailored to your specific stage of postpartum recovery. We always conduct a thorough intake to understand your unique circumstances, including any stitches, healing wounds, or energy levels, ensuring every treatment is safe, comfortable, and supportive for your current physical state.

How is this different from traditional physical therapy for postpartum issues?

While traditional physical therapy is excellent, our Holistic Medicine approach integrates physical techniques with somatic healing, acknowledging the emotional and energetic aspects of postpartum recovery. We don't just treat the symptom; we consider how your whole being is responding to this new phase, fostering a deeper sense of comfort and integration.

How soon after giving birth can I begin Holistic Medicine sessions?

Many of our new mothers begin sessions within a few weeks postpartum, once they feel ready and have received clearance from their healthcare provider for light activity. We can start with very gentle, supportive techniques even in the early weeks, focusing on comfort and initial somatic reintegration. We'll discuss what feels right for your body during your initial consultation.
