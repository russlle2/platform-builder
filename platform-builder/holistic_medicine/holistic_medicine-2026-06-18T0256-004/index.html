Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is Holistic Medicine and how can it benefit me?
Holistic Medicine is an approach to health that considers your entire being – mind, body, and spirit – rather than just isolated symptoms. It aims to identify and address the root causes of imbalance, promoting overall well-being, enhancing your body's natural healing abilities, and empowering you with tools for long-term health.
Is Holistic Medicine suitable for all health conditions?
Holistic Medicine can complement conventional treatments and offer significant benefits for a wide range of health concerns, from chronic conditions to preventative care. We always conduct a thorough assessment to determine if our approach is appropriate for your specific situation and will openly discuss how we can best support your health goals.
How long does it take to see results with Holistic Medicine?
The timeline for seeing results can vary greatly depending on your individual health concerns, the duration of your condition, and your commitment to the recommended plan. Holistic healing is a process, not a quick fix. We focus on sustainable changes that build over time, leading to gradual yet profound improvements in your well-being.
Do you accept insurance for Holistic Medicine services?
While does not directly bill insurance, we can provide you with a detailed superbill that you can submit to your insurance provider for potential reimbursement, depending on your plan's out-of-network benefits. We encourage you to contact your insurance company directly to inquire about your coverage for Holistic Medicine services.
