How the work unfolds — a gentle sequence

1
Intake (discovery)

We map your story: symptoms, lifestyle, sleep, movement, stressors, medical history, and current medications. This is a listening, educational session where questions guide clarity.

2
Personalized plan

A concise plan framed as experiments—small, measurable changes you can try for 2–6 weeks. Plans include nutrition guidance, sleep architecture, movement, and mind-body practices when appropriate.

3
Follow-ups

Regular check-ins (25–40 mins) to review data, adjust strategies, and support habit formation. We prioritize learning and refinement over quick fixes.

4
Longer-term care

For persistent or complex patterns, we scale support—add sessions, involve collaborators, and use education-focused labs to guide choices.

Optional educational labs

Labs are offered as tools to understand patterns—not as mandates. Commonly discussed options include basic metabolic panels, thyroid function, targeted nutrient testing, and functional digestive assessments. Each is considered for its potential to inform a practical plan.

  • Basic labs (CBC, metabolic panel)
  • Thyroid hormones (TSH, free T4/T3)
  • Vitamin D, B12, iron studies when indicated
  • Targeted digestive markers when warranted

Principles that shape our work

We favor small, sustainable experiments; clear education over jargon; shared decision-making; and safety-first recommendations. Nothing is imposed—choices are collaborative.

Coordination

I collaborate with your primary care or specialists when needed. If urgent or specialist care is necessary, I will help guide next steps or referrals.

What I do not do

I do not provide emergency services, and I do not promise cures. My role is education, support, and whole-person planning.
