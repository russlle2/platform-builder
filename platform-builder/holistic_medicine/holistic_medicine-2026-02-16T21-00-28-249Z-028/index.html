Questions people ask

Do you treat chronic conditions?

We support whole-person strategies that aim to reduce symptoms and improve function through education and lifestyle-based interventions. We do not promise cures; care is individualized and often collaborative.

Will you order labs?

Optional labs are discussed as educational tools to guide personalized plans. We explain why a test may be useful and how to interpret results.

How do virtual visits work?

Virtual appointments use secure video. Many learning and coaching elements translate well online; some physical assessments may require an in-person visit.
