Frequently Asked Questions

How does Holistic Medicine specifically address emotional healing for someone recovering from chronic illness or injury?
Our Holistic Medicine approach recognizes that chronic illness and injury often bring significant emotional distress. We use trauma-informed practices like mindful awareness and gentle somatic techniques to help you process feelings such as grief, anxiety, or anger, which are common responses to enduring physical hardship. Our aim is to foster emotional regulation and a sense of internal safety during your recovery.
I'm hesitant to talk about my experiences. How does your trauma-informed Holistic Medicine ensure a safe space?
Creating a safe and non-judgmental environment is paramount in our trauma-informed Holistic Medicine. We prioritize your comfort and pace, ensuring you always feel in control. Our practitioners are trained to listen deeply and offer support without pressure, respecting your boundaries and helping you feel secure as you share your experiences related to your illness or injury.
Will Holistic Medicine clash with my ongoing medical treatments for chronic illness or injury?
Not at all. Our Holistic Medicine approach is designed to complement your existing medical care. We focus on your emotional and mental well-being, which can significantly enhance your overall resilience during physical recovery from chronic illness or injury. We encourage open communication with your medical team to ensure all aspects of your health are supported cohesively.
I feel very isolated due to my chronic condition. How does your community-focused Holistic Medicine help?
We understand that chronic illness or injury can lead to feelings of isolation. Our Holistic Medicine practice is intentionally built to be a welcoming and inclusive community. While our focus is individual care, the underlying philosophy fosters connection. We provide a space where you feel understood, seen, and supported, reducing feelings of loneliness as you navigate your unique path to recovery.
