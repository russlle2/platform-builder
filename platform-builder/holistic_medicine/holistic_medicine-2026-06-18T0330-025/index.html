Frequently Asked Questions

How can Holistic Medicine specifically help me with college stress and anxiety?
Our Holistic Medicine approach focuses on regulating your nervous system and building your resilience. We use techniques like acupuncture, herbal support, and mindful practices that are excellent for reducing the physical and mental symptoms of stress, improving focus, and promoting better sleep, all crucial for academic success.
Will Holistic Medicine interfere with my academic schedule?
We understand your demanding schedule. Our sessions are designed to be efficient and impactful. Many students find that the stress reduction benefits actually free up mental energy, making them more productive and focused in their studies, ultimately saving them time.
Is Holistic Medicine a substitute for mental health counseling?
Holistic Medicine can be a powerful complementary approach to mental health counseling. We focus on physical and energetic balance to support your overall well-being and nervous system health, which can greatly enhance the benefits of therapy. We're happy to collaborate with your existing care team if you have one.
What should I expect during my first Holistic Medicine appointment?
During your first Holistic Medicine visit, we'll have a thorough, compassionate conversation about your unique stressors, academic pressures, and health history. We'll then create a personalized plan focusing on stress reduction and nervous system regulation, which might include gentle therapies, herbal recommendations, or simple practices you can do at home.
