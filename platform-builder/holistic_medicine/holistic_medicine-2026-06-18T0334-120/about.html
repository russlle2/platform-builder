How does Holistic Medicine specifically address sleep issues during a life transition?

During significant life changes, your body and mind experience heightened demands. Holistic Medicine considers your unique physiological and psychological responses to these stressors, identifying root causes of sleep disruption rather than just surface symptoms. We then craft a personalized plan using evidence-based interventions to support your body's natural sleep cycles.

Is the approach evidence-based, or is it more anecdotal?

Our practice is firmly rooted in evidence. We integrate clinically researched nutritional science, targeted botanical medicine, and scientifically validated mind-body practices. Every recommendation is chosen for its proven efficacy in supporting sleep health, especially for individuals navigating the complexities of major life transitions.

What if I've tried other sleep solutions without success?

It's common for standard approaches to fall short when sleep issues are intertwined with significant life changes. Our Holistic Medicine framework offers a comprehensive, individualized perspective, looking beyond typical solutions to address the unique interplay of factors affecting your rest. We focus on understanding your specific circumstances to build a more effective path forward.

How long does it typically take to see improvements in sleep?

The timeline for improvement varies for each individual, as it depends on the specific nature of your life transition, the duration of your sleep challenges, and your body's unique response. However, many clients report initial improvements in sleep quality and duration within a few weeks, with more profound, sustained changes developing over several months as we refine your personalized plan.
