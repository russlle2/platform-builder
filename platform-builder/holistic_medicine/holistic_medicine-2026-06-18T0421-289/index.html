Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is Holistic Medicine, and how can it help me as a parent?
Holistic Medicine for parents means we look at your whole experience – your emotions, daily stressors, physical health, and family dynamics – rather than just isolated symptoms. We aim to support your overall well-being and help you develop resilience, so you can better manage the emotional demands of parenting and household life.
I'm not familiar with 'trauma-informed care.' What does that mean for me?
Trauma-informed care simply means we understand that past experiences can deeply influence how you react to present stressors, especially as a parent. We create a safe, respectful environment where you feel understood and empowered, helping you gently process emotions and build coping strategies without judgment or re-traumatization.
I'm already so busy. How can I possibly fit Holistic Medicine into my life?
We completely understand your time constraints. Our Holistic Medicine approach for parents focuses on practical, accessible strategies that can be integrated into your existing routines. We help you identify small, impactful changes and tools that genuinely reduce stress and increase your emotional capacity, even in short bursts.
Will Holistic Medicine help me with specific parenting challenges, like meltdowns or sleep issues?
While we don't directly provide parenting advice for specific behaviors, our Holistic Medicine focus on your emotional well-being can profoundly impact how you respond to these challenges. By reducing your stress and increasing your emotional regulation, you'll feel more equipped to navigate difficult moments with your children, leading to calmer interactions for everyone.
