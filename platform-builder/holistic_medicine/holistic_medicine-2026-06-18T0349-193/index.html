Frequently Asked Questions

Will this work if I have back-to-back exams?
Yes. We prioritize sleep for cognitive function during high-pressure periods. We'll adjust protocols to stabilize your rest before major deadlines, not after you've burned out.
Do I need to stop studying at 10 PM?
No. We build sleep-friendly habits *into* your existing routine—like a 20-minute wind-down before your final Zoom call that actually resets your nervous system.
How long until I notice better sleep?
Most students see measurable improvements in sleep continuity within 2-4 weeks, not months. We track your sleep quality with simple at-home metrics, not vague 'feelings.'
What if I've tried melatonin and it didn't work?
Melatonin addresses symptoms, not root causes. We identify *why* your sleep is disrupted (e.g., stress response patterns, circadian misalignment from late study sessions) and address it with targeted techniques you can sustain.
