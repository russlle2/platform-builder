Frequently Asked Questions

Is Holistic Medicine safe alongside my current medications?
Absolutely. We work *with* your existing care team, reviewing all medications and treatments to ensure safe, integrated support. Your safety is our priority.
What happens during a typical session?
You’ll share your health journey, goals, and concerns. We’ll discuss your lifestyle, stressors, and physical symptoms to co-create a practical, personalized plan—not a one-size-fits-all protocol.
Can Holistic Medicine help with chronic conditions?
Yes. We focus on understanding the root causes of ongoing health patterns, not just managing symptoms. Many find renewed energy and function through tailored lifestyle and natural support strategies.
Do you accept insurance?
We don’t bill insurance directly, but many plans cover holistic services. We’ll help you maximize your benefits and provide detailed receipts for reimbursement.
