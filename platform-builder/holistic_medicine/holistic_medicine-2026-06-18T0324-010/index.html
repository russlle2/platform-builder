Frequently Asked Questions

I'm overwhelmed. How can I possibly fit this into my schedule?
We start with tiny moments—like 90 seconds while waiting for coffee. Your nervous system needs consistency, not hours. We build from where you are, not where you 'should' be.
What if I can't sit still for 10 minutes?
You don't need to. We work with movement, breath, and sensory awareness in your daily routine—like noticing your feet on the floor during a phone call. Calm isn't about stillness; it's about reconnecting with your body in the moment.
How is this different from therapy or meditation apps?
Therapy helps you understand your story; we help your body remember safety. Meditation apps often ask you to 'focus' when your nervous system is already overwhelmed. We meet you in your physical experience, not your thoughts.
Do you work with people who've tried everything else?
Absolutely. Many have tried mindfulness apps or therapy without relief. When your body stays stuck in 'survival,' you need nervous system regulation—not more advice. We've helped hundreds find calm where other approaches left them stranded.
