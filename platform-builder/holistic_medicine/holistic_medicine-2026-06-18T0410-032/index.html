Frequently Asked Questions

How can Holistic Medicine specifically help my postpartum stress?
Holistic Medicine addresses the root causes of postpartum stress by supporting your body's natural regulatory systems. We focus on balancing hormones, optimizing nutrient levels, and implementing strategies that directly calm your nervous system, rather than just masking symptoms. This helps you build resilience against ongoing demands.
Is this approach safe to combine with breastfeeding or other medications?
Absolutely. Your safety and your baby's well-being are our top priorities. We carefully review your complete health history, including any medications you're taking and whether you're breastfeeding, to ensure all Holistic Medicine recommendations are safe, compatible, and beneficial for your specific situation.
What does 'nervous system regulation' actually mean for a new mom?
For a new mom, nervous system regulation means shifting your body out of a constant fight-or-flight state and into a more relaxed, restorative one. This can manifest as improved sleep, less irritability, better focus, and a greater capacity to handle the demands of motherhood without feeling constantly overwhelmed. It's about feeling more grounded and present.
How quickly can I expect to feel a difference with Holistic Medicine support?
While individual experiences vary, many new mothers report feeling a noticeable shift in their stress levels and overall well-being within a few weeks of starting their personalized Holistic Medicine plan. Consistency is key, and we'll work together to set realistic expectations and monitor your progress every step of the way.
