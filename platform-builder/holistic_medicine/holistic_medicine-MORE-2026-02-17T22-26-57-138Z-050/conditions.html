Conditions and supportive framing

We provide educational, supportive strategies that can complement medical care. We do not promise cures; instead we offer structured ways to reduce symptom burden and improve daily function, in partnership with your clinicians.

Chronic Fatigue & Low Energy

Practical sequencing: sleep stability, circadian supports, selective labs, small-step activity plans.

Digestive Disturbance

Focus on food patterns, gentle elimination trials, stress-linked motility strategies, and referral when alarm features exist.

Stress-Reactive Conditions

Breathwork, micro-habits, sleep prioritization, and pragmatic cognitive techniques to reduce reactivity.

Hormonal Transition Support

Lifestyle and nutritional framing to manage perimenopause, thyroid shifts, and metabolic changes with measurable steps.

Chronic Pain (non-acute)

Biopsychosocial approaches: pacing, movement exposure, sleep, nutrition, and coordinated care with providers.

Important: the information here is educational and supportive. It is not a substitute for medical advice. If you have new, worsening, or urgent symptoms, please seek immediate medical care or contact your primary clinician.

How we support common journeys

We start with a short evaluation to clarify safety, prioritize testing, and build a phased plan. Plans emphasize brief wins and durable routines; they are designed to be communicated to your wider care team.

When to consider our services

  • Ongoing symptoms that affect daily function
  • Desire to reduce medication burden with a clinician-informed plan
  • Need for a clear, short-term roadmap
We always review safety concerns and coordinate recommendations when needed.

Common questions

Will you stop my medications?
We do not arbitrarily stop medications. Any change is coordinated with prescribing clinicians and done cautiously with monitoring plans.
Do you provide lab orders?
When indicated and appropriate, we may suggest targeted testing. We ensure tests are purposeful and paired with clear interpretation.
