Core elements

Safety & triage

We ensure there are no urgent concerns and identify testing only when it meaningfully changes decisions.

Functional assessment

A focused look at sleep, digestion, movement, and stress to detect patterns you can act on.

Phased plan

Short experiments and a simple tracking plan so you can tell what works without guessing.

Why we emphasize short experiments

Experiments reduce risk and clarify causality. Rather than long lists of simultaneous changes, we use sequenced trials that produce usable information quickly.

Process in practice

  1. Concise intake — focused intake forms and a single 60–90 minute session.
  2. Targeted actions — one or two interventions to try for a fixed period.
  3. Quick review — short follow-up to interpret results and plan next steps.

Questions we often get

How do you measure success?
We pick a small number of relevant markers (sleep, symptom frequency, activity tolerance) and check for change over set intervals.
Will I need many visits?
Not always. Many people benefit from a short audit plus one focused follow-up. Ongoing visits are tailored to the complexity we uncover.

Ready to test something useful?

Start with a short assessment and leave with a one-page plan you can try in the next two weeks.
