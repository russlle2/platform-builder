Core pillars

Listen & Map

A broad intake that captures symptoms, routines, stressors, and supports — then a straightforward map of priorities.

Practice & Adjust

Short, testable practices we try for a week or two. We review what helps and what needs tweaking.

Support at Home

Written supports, small toolkits, or brief check-ins to hold progress without overwhelming your schedule.

Myths versus practical truth

  • Myth: Integrative means unscientific. Truth: We knit lifestyle, evidence-informed techniques, and collaboration with clinicians when needed.
  • Myth: Change requires big overhaul. Truth: Tiny, repeated experiments compound into reliable shifts over time.

Who we work with

People navigating chronic stress, intermittent symptoms, or those seeking a clearer routine around sleep, digestion, mood, and daily energy. We do not offer emergency care or guarantee cures — our role is educational and supportive.
