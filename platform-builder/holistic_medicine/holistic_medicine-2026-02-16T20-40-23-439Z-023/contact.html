What happens after you reach out

A gentle, structured path so your concerns are heard and explored.

  1. Message received: We review the details to prepare for a focused first call.
  2. Intake conversation: A guided discussion about history, lifestyle, and goals.
  3. Plan formation: Personalized recommendations (nutrition, movement, mind-body, targeted labs when educationally appropriate).
  4. Follow-up & support: Iterative check-ins to refine the plan. Care is a process, not a promise.

How contacting helps

  • Clarifies your priorities and possible next steps
  • Connects you with an evidence-informed, whole-person perspective
  • Outlines optional educational tests and referrals where useful
  • Respects your pace and personal context

We never promise cures. Our focus is education, symptom context, and practical support.
