How we partner with you

Care here follows a steady map: listen, educate, try small changes, measure, repeat. Any testing is discussed as a way to illuminate, not to promise answers.

1. Intake — Deep listening
We gather your history, patterns, relationships to food, sleep, stress, and goals. Questions are welcome; judgment is not.
2. Shared plan — Simple, teachable steps
Expect a prioritized plan you can read and follow: habits, gentle nutrition shifts, movement, sleep cues, and optional botanicals when appropriate.
3. Follow-ups — Adjust and measure
Short, frequent follow-ups keep momentum. We track outcomes and tune the plan together.
4. Optional tests & referrals
When tests help explain symptoms or ensure safety, we recommend targeted labs and partner with specialists as needed.

Intake in detail

The initial visit is a careful inventory of your lifestyle, medical history, sleep and stress patterns, and what you hope to change. We emphasize learning: you should leave with clear, practical next steps.

What a plan looks like

  • 1–3 measurable behavior changes (sleep window, evening routine, simplified meals)
  • Short breathing or movement practices to try daily
  • When appropriate, short courses of botanicals or nutrients with safety notes

Follow-up rhythm

Most people start with a 4–8 week cadence of brief check-ins to adapt the plan. Frequency is personalized.

About labs (educational)

Labs can be useful to rule out medical causes or to inform safe supplement use. Examples: basic metabolic panels, thyroid indices, targeted micronutrient checks. We discuss benefits, limits, and costs before ordering.

Questions people ask

Is this a replacement for primary care?
No. We collaborate with your primary care and specialists when needed. We supplement with lifestyle-focused, integrative strategies.
How long until I notice changes?
Some practices shift mood or sleep within days; lasting change is often seen over weeks to months. We focus on sustainable, measurable steps.
