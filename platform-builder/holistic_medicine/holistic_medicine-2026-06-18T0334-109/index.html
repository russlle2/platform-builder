Frequently Asked Questions

How does Holistic Medicine specifically help parents with sleep?
Our Holistic Medicine approach recognizes that your sleep is impacted by many factors, including your child's sleep, household stress, and your own physical and emotional well-being. We assess everything from your daily routines and diet to stress management techniques, creating a customized plan that supports your body's natural ability to achieve restorative sleep, not just for you, but for a calmer home.
Will this involve complicated changes to my already busy schedule?
We understand your time is precious! Our Holistic Medicine plans are designed to integrate seamlessly into your life. We focus on small, impactful shifts that don't add more stress. Our goal is to simplify, not complicate, helping you create sustainable habits for better rest without feeling overwhelmed by an extensive to-do list.
Is Holistic Medicine only for parents whose children don't sleep?
Not at all. While we certainly help with child sleep challenges, our Holistic Medicine focus is on *your* restorative rest as a parent. Even if your child sleeps well, the demands of parenting can lead to your own sleep debt and stress. We help you prioritize and protect your sleep, ensuring you have the energy and patience to thrive as a parent.
What kind of results can I expect from your Holistic Medicine approach?
You can expect to feel more rested, less overwhelmed, and more equipped to manage the demands of parenting. Our Holistic Medicine clients often report falling asleep more easily, waking feeling more refreshed, experiencing reduced anxiety around sleep, and having more consistent energy throughout their day. Ultimately, you'll gain practical tools to improve your well-being and the peaceful rhythm of your home.
