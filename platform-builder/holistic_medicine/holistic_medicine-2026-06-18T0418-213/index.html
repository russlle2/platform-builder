Frequently Asked Questions

What does 'trauma-informed' Holistic Medicine mean for me as a senior?
For you, 'trauma-informed' means that your care at acknowledges that past experiences, even those you might not consider 'traumatic,' can affect your emotional and physical health today. We approach your care with extra sensitivity, ensuring our methods are gentle, respectful, and designed to help you feel safe and understood, without ever pushing you to revisit painful memories unless you choose to.
How is Holistic Medicine different from traditional therapy for emotional well-being?
While both aim to support your well-being, Holistic Medicine at considers your entire self – mind, body, and spirit – in addressing emotional health. Instead of focusing solely on verbal processing, we might incorporate gentle somatic practices, nutritional guidance, or mindfulness techniques alongside talking, all tailored to your comfort and preferences. It's about a broader, integrated approach to fostering emotional calm.
Will Holistic Medicine be too intense or overwhelming for my age?
Absolutely not. Our services are specifically designed to be gentle and accessible for seniors. We prioritize your comfort and pace, ensuring that all interventions are supportive and never overwhelming. Our approach is about gradual, sustainable change and cultivating inner peace, not about intense emotional upheaval. We always work collaboratively with you to ensure your comfort and well-being.
How can Holistic Medicine help me with emotional challenges related to aging or loss?
Holistic Medicine at can provide invaluable support for emotional challenges that may arise with aging, such as grief, changes in independence, or adjusting to new life stages. We offer gentle strategies to process these feelings, build resilience, and find renewed purpose and peace. Our focus is on helping you navigate these transitions with grace, understanding, and emotional strength, fostering a sense of continued wholeness.
