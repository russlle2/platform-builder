Frequently Asked Questions

How does Holistic Medicine specifically address physical stress from training or activity?
Our Holistic Medicine approach looks beyond isolated pain points. We assess how your entire system responds to physical demands, identifying patterns of tension, compensatory movements, or nervous system dysregulation. By addressing these root causes, we can reduce current physical stress and help prevent future discomfort, optimizing your body's ability to recover and perform.
Is Holistic Medicine suitable if I'm currently dealing with an injury?
Yes, our Holistic Medicine methods are often complementary to conventional injury rehabilitation. We focus on reducing inflammation, improving tissue mobility, and calming the nervous system to support your body's healing process. Our goal is to help you manage pain, accelerate recovery, and build resilience to return to your activities safely and effectively.
What kind of somatic healing techniques are used in Holistic Medicine for active individuals?
Somatic healing in our Holistic Medicine practice involves gentle, targeted techniques that help your body release stored tension and trauma, often held unconsciously. This can include specific manual therapies, breathwork, and guided awareness exercises designed to reset your nervous system, improve body-mind connection, and enhance your overall physical comfort and performance.
How long will it take to see results from Holistic Medicine for my physical stress?
The timeline for results with Holistic Medicine varies for everyone, depending on the nature and duration of your physical stress. Many active individuals report feeling a noticeable difference in comfort and mobility within a few sessions. We work collaboratively to create a personalized plan, focusing on consistent progress and empowering you with tools for long-term self-care and resilience.
