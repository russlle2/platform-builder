How we partner with you

Think of us as a guide and lab partner: we listen, educate, and co-design an individualized plan that makes room for your life. Below is the typical flow for our intensive model.

1
In-depth intake
A comprehensive review of history, symptoms, lifestyle, and goals. This is where we gather the context to build a sensible plan.
2
Shared decision planning
We co-create a plan with clear priorities: nutrition, stress tools, movement, and sleep recommendations. The plan emphasizes education so you understand why each step matters.
3
Optional targeted labs
Labs are offered as educational tools to refine the plan—not as mandatory requirements. Examples include basic metabolic panels, inflammatory markers, or selective gut evaluations.
4
Structured follow-ups
We schedule focused follow-ups to track progress, troubleshoot, and adjust interventions. Your care is iterative and measurable.
5
Long-term maintenance
When goals are reached, we shift to maintenance strategies and as-needed check-ins to keep gains sustainable.
About labs
Labs can be helpful when they inform a change in plan. We review benefits and limitations, and interpret results with the goal of actionable steps rather than raw numbers.

A client with mid-day crashes and digestive bloating started with an intake. The plan prioritized meal timing, a short elimination protocol with follow-up, and revised sleep cues. After six weeks, energy stabilized and symptoms decreased; the plan evolved to maintenance and periodic check-ins.

FAQ

Is this an alternate to my doctor?
No. We partner with medical providers and refer when medical evaluation is a priority.
What if I can’t do labs?
Many plans start without labs; we use symptoms and responses to guide changes and only add testing when it will change the plan.
