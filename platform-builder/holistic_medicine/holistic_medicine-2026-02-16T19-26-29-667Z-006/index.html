Myths vs. What Actually Helps

We love curiosity. Let’s bust a few myths and land on practical truths.

Myth: Quick fixes heal everything

Truth: Lasting change usually needs pattern shifts, time, and education — we focus on skills you can use for life, not one-off tricks.

Myth: Tests tell the whole story

Truth: Labs can help; they are tools. We interpret them alongside your story, symptoms, and priorities.

Myth: More supplements = better

Truth: Thoughtful, minimal interventions and behavioral shifts are where most wins happen.

Frequently Asked (and cheerfully answered)

What is an "intensive" program?

An intensive is a concentrated, time-bound program with an initial thorough intake, a clear plan, and scheduled follow-ups to build momentum quickly.

Do you guarantee a cure?

No. We focus on education and whole-person plans that support better functioning. We do not promise cures.

Will I need labs?

Optional. Labs are discussed as educational tools when they can meaningfully inform decisions. They are never required to start.

How do follow-ups work?

Follow-ups are scheduled check-ins (video or phone) to review progress, troubleshoot, and refine your plan.
