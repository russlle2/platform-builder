Frequently Asked Questions

I'm always busy. How can Holistic Medicine fit into my demanding schedule?
We understand your time is incredibly valuable. Our Holistic Medicine approach focuses on practical, gentle techniques that can be integrated into your existing routine without adding more stress. We'll help you find manageable strategies that make a real difference, even with a packed calendar.
I'm not familiar with 'nervous system regulation.' Is this for me?
Absolutely! You don't need any prior knowledge. Nervous system regulation simply means helping your body move out of 'fight or flight' mode and into a state of calm. We'll explain everything in an approachable way and guide you through gentle practices to reduce your stress and feel more at ease.
How is Holistic Medicine different from other stress relief methods I've tried?
Our Holistic Medicine practice takes a comprehensive yet accessible view of your well-being. Instead of just managing symptoms, we look at the root causes of your stress and offer gentle, integrated solutions to support your entire system. This means more sustainable calm, tailored specifically for your high-performance lifestyle.
Will I have to make big changes to my lifestyle to see results?
Not at all. We believe in gentle, incremental changes that lead to lasting impact. Our Holistic Medicine approach focuses on introducing simple, effective strategies that you can comfortably incorporate. The goal is to reduce your stress, not add more pressure with overwhelming expectations.
