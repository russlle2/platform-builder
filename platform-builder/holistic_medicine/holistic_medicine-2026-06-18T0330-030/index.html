Frequently Asked Questions

How does Holistic Medicine specifically help with nervous system regulation during a life transition?
Our Holistic Medicine approach uses gentle, time-tested methods to address the root causes of nervous system dysregulation. We focus on practices that calm the sympathetic nervous system's 'fight or flight' response and activate the parasympathetic 'rest and digest' state, helping your body and mind adapt more smoothly to change.
I feel constantly anxious since my recent life change. Can Holistic Medicine truly reduce this stress?
Absolutely. Holistic Medicine views anxiety during transitions as a signal from your nervous system. We work with you to understand these signals and provide personalized support through ancient healing traditions, helping to reduce the pervasive feelings of anxiety and bring a greater sense of peace and calm into your daily life.
What kind of practices are involved in Holistic Medicine for managing stress during big life changes?
Our Holistic Medicine practice integrates various modalities tailored to your unique needs. This might include gentle breathwork, specific herbal remedies, mindful movement, dietary adjustments, and energy balancing techniques, all aimed at supporting your nervous system and reducing stress as you navigate new chapters.
Will Holistic Medicine help me feel more grounded when everything around me is changing?
Yes, feeling grounded is a primary outcome we aim for. Our Holistic Medicine methods are designed to reconnect you with your internal wisdom and body's innate ability to find stability. By regulating your nervous system, you'll cultivate an inner anchor that helps you feel more secure and present, even when external circumstances are shifting.
