Frequently Asked Questions

How does Holistic Medicine help with physical pain during a major life transition?
Major life transitions often create stress that lodges in your body, leading to physical pain and discomfort. Our Holistic Medicine approach looks beyond symptoms to understand how these changes are impacting your overall system. We use integrated techniques to release tension, reduce inflammation, and restore your body's natural equilibrium, helping you navigate your transition with greater physical ease.
What kind of somatic healing can I expect in your Holistic Medicine practice?
Somatic healing in our Holistic Medicine practice focuses on helping you become more aware of your body's sensations and how they relate to your emotional state during life changes. We might use gentle touch, guided awareness, or specific movement practices to help you release stored stress and trauma from your body, promoting a sense of calm and integration as you adapt to new circumstances.
Is this Holistic Medicine approach suitable for chronic pain or acute pain during a transition?
Yes, our integrated Holistic Medicine approach is designed to support both chronic and acute pain stemming from or exacerbated by major life transitions. We assess your unique situation to develop a personalized plan, whether your pain has been a long-standing issue now amplified by change, or a new discomfort that has emerged during a recent shift in your life.
How quickly can I expect to feel results from your Holistic Medicine for physical pain and somatic healing?
The timeline for feeling results from our Holistic Medicine for physical pain and somatic healing varies for each individual, depending on the nature and duration of your discomfort and the specifics of your life transition. Many clients report a sense of immediate relief and greater bodily awareness after initial sessions, with deeper, more lasting changes unfolding over consistent engagement with your personalized care plan.
