Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is Holistic Medicine, and how can it help with my physical pain?
Holistic Medicine at means we look beyond just your symptoms. If you're experiencing back pain or tension headaches, we consider your lifestyle, stress levels, and how your body holds pressure. Our goal is to find the underlying causes and provide gentle, effective methods for lasting relief, rather than just masking the discomfort.
I'm a busy entrepreneur. Will this require a huge time commitment?
We understand your time is valuable. Our Holistic Medicine sessions are designed to be efficient yet effective. We focus on practical, accessible techniques that you can integrate into your existing routine. The aim is to give you tools that create lasting change without adding unnecessary burden to your already full schedule.
I've never tried anything like 'somatic healing' before. Is it complicated?
Not at all. Somatic healing might sound unfamiliar, but it's a gentle process of noticing and releasing physical tension in your body. You don't need any prior experience. will guide you through simple, approachable exercises that help you connect with your body's signals and find more comfort and ease.
Can Holistic Medicine truly help with the stress I feel as a high-achiever?
Absolutely. The constant demands of high achievement often manifest as physical stress – stiff shoulders, tight jaws, or persistent fatigue. Our Holistic Medicine approach directly addresses this body-mind connection, helping you unwind deeply held tension. By releasing physical stress, you'll find greater mental clarity and resilience to tackle your professional challenges.
