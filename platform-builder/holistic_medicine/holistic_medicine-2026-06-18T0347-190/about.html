Can you help if I'm breastfeeding on demand?

Absolutely. Our protocols work *with* your feeding schedule. We teach you how to time short rest periods during lulls (like after a 2am feed) and use safe acupressure to signal your body it's time to recover without disrupting feedings.

How is this different from general sleep coaching?

We focus solely on *postpartum physiological recovery*. General sleep coaches don't address uterine healing or hormonal shifts. Our approach is backed by clinical studies on postpartum sleep cycles, not generic 'sleep hygiene' advice.

Do you use supplements?

Only if medically appropriate. We prioritize non-pharmaceutical methods first—like adjusting your evening ritual to support natural sleep hormones. If needed, we prescribe gentle, breastfeeding-safe herbs like lemon balm in precise doses.

What if I can't sleep at night when my baby sleeps?

That's our starting point. We design protocols for 'micro-rest recovery' during baby's sleep—using breathwork to lower cortisol quickly. You'll learn to rest effectively even with 20-minute windows, not wait for ideal 8-hour blocks.
