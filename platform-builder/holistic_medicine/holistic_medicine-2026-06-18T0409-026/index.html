Frequently Asked Questions

How does Holistic Medicine specifically help with chronic illness or injury recovery?
For those recovering from chronic illness or injury, stress often exacerbates symptoms and hinders healing. Our Holistic Medicine approach directly targets nervous system dysregulation, offering practices that reduce inflammation, improve sleep, and manage pain by calming your body's stress response. This creates a more supportive internal environment for your recovery.
What kind of practices can I expect for stress reduction and nervous system regulation?
You can expect gentle yet profound practices that include breathwork techniques, guided meditation, specific movement practices, and traditional herbal support. Each session is tailored to your current capacity and specific needs, focusing on bringing your nervous system back into a state of balance and ease, rather than pushing your body further.
Will these Holistic Medicine practices interfere with my current medical treatments?
Our Holistic Medicine services are designed to complement, not replace, your existing medical care. We encourage open communication with your healthcare team. Our focus on stress reduction and nervous system regulation can often enhance the effectiveness of other treatments by reducing systemic stress and improving your body's resilience, without adverse interactions.
How long until I might feel a difference in my stress levels or nervous system?
Many individuals report a noticeable sense of calm and reduced tension after just a few sessions, as your nervous system begins to respond to gentle regulation. However, chronic conditions often require consistent engagement. Your progress will be unique, but we are committed to providing ongoing support and adaptive strategies to help you cultivate lasting peace and stability.
