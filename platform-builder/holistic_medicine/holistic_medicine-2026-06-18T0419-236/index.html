Frequently Asked Questions

How does Holistic Medicine specifically address emotional healing for chronic conditions?
Our Holistic Medicine approach acknowledges that chronic illness often brings emotional strain, grief, and even trauma. We integrate therapies that help you process these complex feelings, regulate your nervous system, and build coping strategies tailored to your unique health situation. It’s about caring for your emotional well-being as much as your physical.
What does 'trauma-informed care' mean in the context of my recovery?
Trauma-informed care means we understand that your experiences with chronic illness or injury may have been overwhelming or distressing. We create a safe environment, prioritize your sense of control, and avoid practices that might re-traumatize you. Our focus is on empowering you and helping you regain a sense of agency over your body and emotions.
Will this approach interfere with my current medical treatments?
Not at all. Our Holistic Medicine services are designed to complement your existing medical care. We focus on the emotional and psychological aspects of your recovery, which can often enhance your overall well-being and ability to engage with your primary treatments. We encourage open communication with your medical team.
Is Holistic Medicine only for severe emotional distress, or can it help with general recovery challenges?
Holistic Medicine can benefit anyone experiencing the emotional impact of chronic illness or injury, whether it’s acute distress, persistent fatigue, anxiety, or difficulty adjusting to changes. Our goal is to provide tools and support to navigate all emotional challenges that arise during your recovery, promoting greater calm and resilience.
