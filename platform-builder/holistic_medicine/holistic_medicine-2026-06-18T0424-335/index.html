Frequently Asked Questions

How does Holistic Medicine help with academic stress and physical pain?
Academic stress and anxiety often lead to physical symptoms like tension headaches, back pain, or digestive upset. Our Holistic Medicine approach addresses these physical manifestations by looking at the whole picture. We use gentle, evidence-based techniques to release accumulated stress from your body, promoting relaxation and reducing pain so you can concentrate on your studies.
Is this a long-term commitment, or can I get quick relief for immediate pain?
While we aim for lasting changes, many students experience immediate relief from acute pain and tension after just a few sessions. Our goal is to equip you with self-care tools for ongoing management, making your college experience more comfortable and less physically demanding. We can tailor a plan that fits your schedule and needs, whether it's for immediate relief or sustained well-being.
Will Holistic Medicine interfere with my academic schedule or medications?
Our sessions are designed to be flexible and supportive of your busy student life. We prioritize non-invasive techniques that complement, rather than interfere with, your existing routines or prescribed medications. We always encourage open communication with your other healthcare providers to ensure a coordinated approach to your well-being.
What specific somatic techniques do you use to help with anxiety-related physical tension?
We utilize a range of gentle, science-backed somatic techniques. These might include targeted breathwork to calm your nervous system, guided body awareness exercises to release chronic muscle tension, or specific movement practices to improve posture and reduce strain from prolonged sitting. Each session is tailored to your unique needs, focusing on practical tools you can use daily.
