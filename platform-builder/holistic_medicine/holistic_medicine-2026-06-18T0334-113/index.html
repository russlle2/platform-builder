Frequently Asked Questions

What makes your Holistic Medicine approach to sleep different for seniors?
Our approach considers the specific physiological changes that can affect sleep as you age. We focus on gentle, non-invasive methods that work with your body, avoiding harsh medications that might have side effects. Your care plan is tailored to your individual needs and sensitivities, prioritizing safety and comfort.
Will I need to stop taking my current medications to receive Holistic Medicine care?
Absolutely not. We work collaboratively with your existing healthcare providers and always consider your current medications. Our goal is to complement your established health regimen, never to disrupt it. We will discuss any potential interactions transparently and ensure your care is integrated and safe.
How quickly can I expect to see improvements in my sleep with Holistic Medicine?
Improvements can vary for each individual, as our approach focuses on gradual, sustainable change. Many of our clients report feeling a greater sense of calm and small improvements in sleep quality within a few weeks, with more significant, lasting changes developing over several months as your body rebalances. Consistency is key.
What if I've tried many things for my sleep before, without success?
It's understandable to feel discouraged if previous attempts haven't yielded results. Our comprehensive Holistic Medicine evaluation looks at a broader spectrum of contributing factors than conventional approaches often do. We invite you to share your full history, and we will patiently work with you to uncover new pathways to more restful sleep.
