Frequently Asked Questions

How does holistic medicine differ from my primary care doctor?
Your primary care doctor focuses on symptom management and acute care. We take a whole-person approach, addressing root causes—like stress patterns or nutritional gaps—that affect your health. Think of us as your wellness partner, not a replacement for your medical team.
Do you accept insurance?
We don't accept insurance, but we provide detailed super bills for out-of-network reimbursement. Many clients find their FSA/HSA plans cover holistic medicine visits—ask us about billing options during your consultation.
How long until I feel better?
This varies by your unique body and goals. We begin with noticeable improvements in energy or digestion within 4-6 weeks. Your holistic medicine plan evolves with you—there's no fixed timeline, just steady progress toward your wellness vision.
What should I expect at my first visit?
You'll spend 90 minutes with Dr. Reed: we'll discuss your full health journey, review any prior tests, and create your personalized plan. No rushing. You'll leave with clear next steps and a sense of being truly heard, not just checked off a list.
