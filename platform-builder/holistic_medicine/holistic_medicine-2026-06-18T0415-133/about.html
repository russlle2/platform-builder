How does Holistic Medicine specifically help seniors with sleep challenges?

Holistic Medicine addresses sleep challenges in seniors by looking at the interconnectedness of your health. Instead of just masking symptoms, we explore underlying factors like nutrition, stress, daily rhythms, and comfort, offering gentle, natural methods to help your body rebalance and encourage deeper, more consistent sleep.

Are the treatments gentle and safe for individuals over 60?

Absolutely. Our primary focus is on gentle, non-invasive approaches that are carefully selected for their safety and effectiveness for seniors. We prioritize your comfort and well-being, always discussing potential options thoroughly to ensure they align with your health profile and preferences.

What should I expect during my first consultation for sleep improvement?

During your initial consultation, we'll have an in-depth conversation about your sleep patterns, health history, lifestyle, and specific concerns. This allows us to understand your unique situation comprehensively, forming the basis for a personalized plan aimed at improving your restorative rest.

How long does it typically take to see improvements in sleep quality?

The timeline for seeing improvements can vary, as each person's journey is unique. Many clients report noticing positive changes within a few weeks of consistently implementing their personalized plan. Our goal is sustained improvement, gently guiding your body towards more restorative sleep over time.
