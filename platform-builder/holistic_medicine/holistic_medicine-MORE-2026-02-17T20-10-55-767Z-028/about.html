Origins

We started from a simple question: how do people make small, meaningful changes that actually last? Rather than private therapy alone, cohorts create reliable practice windows and community feedback loops. Clinicians guide, peers reflect, and each person leaves with a concrete plan.

What we value

  • Clarity over jargon — clear steps, not ideas alone.
  • Respect for context — life constraints shape any plan.
  • Shared experimentation — cohorts test, measure, iterate.

Community checks

Peer sessions are moderated to keep things safe and useful. Clinical touches are educational and not a replacement for one-to-one medical care. If you need individualized clinical decisions, book a private consult.
