How the cohorts guide change

We combine brief teaching, measurable experiments, and community problem-solving so you leave with a set of small, testable habits — not a vague checklist.

Core pillars

Anchor routines
Tiny daily rituals that stabilize biological rhythms — morning light, mealtime windows, evening wind-downs.
Signal & response
Learn simple ways to notice triggers and apply one short response — breath, brief movement, or a food swap.
Friendly experiments
Small tests with clear measures so you can tell what helps without overhauling everything at once.

Typical program flow

1. Map
Two examples of baseline tracking and intake conversation.
2. Try
Weeks of focused micro-changes with group check-ins.
3. Tune
Adaptation, small calibrations, and a consolidation phase with an exit toolkit.

Who benefits most

People who want practical structure, peer accountability, and an approach that favors gradual, testable adjustments over sweeping changes. Not a substitute for urgent medical care; some participants also pursue parallel specialist evaluation.
