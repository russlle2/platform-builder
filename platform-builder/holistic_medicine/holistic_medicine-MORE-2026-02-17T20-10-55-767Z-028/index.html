What we center

Three complementary threads that shape each cohort and session.

Context-first

We begin with your day-to-day: where time is spent, what drains you, and where small shifts add up.

Practical skillwork

Skills, not sermons. Simple experiments you can do alone or with the cohort between sessions.

Shared learning

Small cohort runs let you learn with others and borrow ideas from real people in real lives.

Busting a few myths

  • Myth: You must overhaul your life to feel better.

    Truth: Small, consistent experiments are how sustainable change happens.

  • Myth: Integrative care is only for extremes.

    Truth: We support people with everyday friction just as much as complex conditions.

  • Myth: Group learning replaces personal care.

    Truth: Cohorts amplify practical habits while individual sessions personalize them.

Short case notes

Real sketches — anonymized and simplified to show what a plan looks like.

  1. Focus: Sleep consolidation.

    Plan: Two-week bedroom hygiene + 3 evening rituals. Check-in at week 2; tweak timing. Outcome: steadier sleep onset for most participants.

  2. Focus: Afternoon exhaustion.

    Plan: Small meal timing experiment, 10-minute mid-day walk, and brief screen-breaks. Outcome: improved alertness in 10/12 cohort members.
