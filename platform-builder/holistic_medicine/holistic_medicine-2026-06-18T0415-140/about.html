How does Holistic Medicine specifically address sleep issues during major life transitions?

When you're experiencing a major life change, your body's stress response can be heightened, directly impacting sleep. Our Holistic Medicine approach considers the unique physiological and psychological stressors you're facing. We look at hormonal balance, nervous system regulation, and emotional processing to create a plan that directly supports your sleep architecture during these specific periods of adjustment.

What kinds of modalities does Holistic Medicine use to improve my sleep?

Our Holistic Medicine practice integrates scientifically-backed natural modalities. This might include targeted nutritional support to balance neurotransmitters, specific botanical remedies to calm the nervous system, light therapy protocols, or personalized guidance on sleep hygiene and stress reduction techniques, all tailored to your individual needs during your transition.

I've tried other sleep solutions; how is Holistic Medicine different for someone in transition?

Unlike generic sleep advice, our Holistic Medicine approach acknowledges that your sleep disruption isn't just about a bad habit; it's often a symptom of your body and mind adapting to a new normal. We don't just mask symptoms; we work to re-establish your body's natural capacity for restorative rest by addressing the specific physiological and emotional demands of your current life phase.

How quickly can I expect to see improvements in my sleep with Holistic Medicine?

While individual results vary, many clients report initial improvements in sleep quality within a few weeks as they implement the personalized strategies from our Holistic Medicine plan. Our goal is not just immediate relief, but sustainable, long-term restorative rest, so we focus on foundational changes that build over time, helping you maintain good sleep through future life shifts.
