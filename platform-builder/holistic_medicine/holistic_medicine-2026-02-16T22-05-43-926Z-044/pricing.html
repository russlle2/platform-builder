How we explore what matters

Education begins with gentle assessment. Before a series we invite a short intake questionnaire and a one-on-one check-in so the material fits your context. Optional laboratory testing may be discussed as educational tools — not mandates — to help you and your practitioner better understand patterns in sleep, digestion, inflammation, and energy.

Note: workshops are educational and supportive. They do not replace individualized medical advice. If you have urgent medical concerns, please consult your primary care clinician.

What a series looks like

  • Week 1 — Foundations: rhythm, breathing, and sleep habits.
  • Week 2 — Gut & nutrition patterns: simple changes with measurable effects.
  • Week 3 — Stress-science: tools to calm the nervous system.
  • Week 4 — Integration: building a personalized plan and next steps.

Tiny habits we teach (start today)

  • Evening wind-down: 30-min low light, no screens
  • Micro-breaths: 4-4-6 pattern, twice daily
  • Gentle gut reset: 2-day mindful eating practice
  • Energy check-ins: brief mid-day pause
