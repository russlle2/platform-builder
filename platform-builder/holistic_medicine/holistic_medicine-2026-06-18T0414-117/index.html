Frequently Asked Questions

How does Holistic Medicine specifically address sleep issues for entrepreneurs?
Our approach acknowledges the unique pressures and irregular schedules faced by entrepreneurs. We delve beyond surface symptoms, examining how stress, demanding cognitive load, and irregular routines impact your specific sleep physiology. Our interventions are tailored to integrate seamlessly into your life, focusing on sustainable changes that support both your health and your professional output.
What kind of results can I expect from optimizing my sleep with your Holistic Medicine guidance?
Clients typically report significant improvements in sleep latency (how quickly you fall asleep), sleep efficiency (time spent actually sleeping), and overall sleep quality. This translates to enhanced daytime alertness, improved focus, better mood regulation, and a greater capacity to manage stress, directly impacting your productivity and decision-making.
Is your approach to sleep improvement evidence-based, or is it more experiential?
Our practice is firmly rooted in evidence-based Holistic Medicine. We utilize current scientific understanding of sleep physiology, neuroscience, and stress management. While we tailor interventions to your individual experience, every recommendation is backed by clinical research and a clear understanding of its potential benefits and mechanisms.
I'm already very busy. How much time will this Holistic Medicine program require from me?
We understand your time is a critical asset. Our programs are designed for efficiency and impact. While initial assessments are thorough, the strategies we implement are practical and aim to integrate into your existing routines without adding undue burden. The goal is to optimize your time for rest, making your efforts highly effective without requiring significant additional hours.
