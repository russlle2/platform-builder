Frequently Asked Questions

How does Holistic Medicine specifically address sleep issues for someone recovering from chronic illness?
Our Holistic Medicine approach looks at your entire system, not just symptoms. For recovery from chronic illness, we assess how inflammation, pain management, medication side effects, and stress responses might be disrupting your sleep. We then create a personalized plan to mitigate these factors, using natural, integrative methods to support your body's innate ability to achieve deep, healing rest.
I'm dealing with chronic pain from an injury; can you really help me sleep better?
Yes, absolutely. Chronic pain is a significant barrier to restorative sleep after an injury. We work to address pain and its impact through various Holistic Medicine strategies, which may include anti-inflammatory nutrition, targeted supplements, gentle relaxation techniques, and mindful practices. Our goal is to reduce your pain perception and calm your nervous system so your body can enter the deeper sleep stages vital for repair.
Will I need to stop any medications I'm currently taking for my chronic condition to improve my sleep?
We never advise you to stop prescribed medications without consulting your primary care provider. Our Holistic Medicine approach is complementary and supportive. We work alongside your existing medical care, identifying ways to optimize your sleep naturally while respecting your current treatment plan. We focus on enhancing your body's ability to rest, often reducing reliance on sleep aids over time, with careful communication and coordination with your other healthcare providers.
How long does it typically take to see improvements in sleep quality when recovering from a long-term health challenge?
The timeline for improving sleep during recovery from a long-term health challenge varies for everyone, as your situation is unique. However, many clients report initial improvements in sleep quality within a few weeks of consistently implementing their personalized Holistic Medicine plan. Sustained, restorative rest is a gradual process of rebuilding, and we are committed to coaching you through each phase of your transformation towards lasting better sleep.
