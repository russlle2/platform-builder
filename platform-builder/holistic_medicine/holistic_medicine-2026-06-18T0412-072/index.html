Frequently Asked Questions

How does Holistic Medicine specifically help with postpartum stress and nervous system regulation?
Holistic Medicine considers your entire being – physical, emotional, and mental – when addressing postpartum stress. We use gentle, natural methods to help balance your hormones, reduce inflammation, support sleep, and teach you practical tools to calm your nervous system, allowing you to feel more stable and less overwhelmed during this demanding time.
What kind of practices can I expect to learn for nervous system regulation?
You'll learn personalized techniques tailored to your needs, which may include specific breathing exercises to activate your 'rest and digest' response, gentle somatic movements to release tension, mindfulness practices to ground you in the present, and nutritional guidance to support your overall resilience. These are tools you can use anytime, anywhere.
Is it too late to start if my baby is already a few months old?
Absolutely not! It's never too late to prioritize your well-being. Whether you're weeks or months postpartum, your nervous system can benefit from support and regulation. We'll meet you exactly where you are and create a plan that helps you feel more centered and in control, no matter how long it's been since your baby arrived.
Will this replace my traditional postpartum care?
Our Holistic Medicine approach complements your traditional postpartum care. We focus on enhancing your stress reduction and nervous system regulation, providing an additional layer of support that works in harmony with your medical providers. We encourage open communication with your healthcare team and are happy to collaborate for your comprehensive well-being.
