Frequently Asked Questions

How does Holistic Medicine specifically address sleep issues for someone experiencing burnout?
For working professionals aged 30-45 dealing with burnout, Holistic Medicine looks at the whole picture. We don't just recommend sleep aids; we delve into your stress levels, nutrition, emotional well-being, and lifestyle to identify the root causes of your sleep disruption. By addressing these underlying factors, we help you cultivate genuine, lasting rest rather than just masking symptoms.
What kind of practices in Holistic Medicine can help improve my sleep when my mind won't switch off?
When your mind is constantly racing, we can explore practices like calming acupuncture protocols, specific herbal formulations that soothe the nervous system, and guided mindfulness techniques. These Holistic Medicine methods are designed to downregulate your stress response, making it easier for your mind to transition from work mode to deep relaxation, preparing you for restorative sleep.
I'm skeptical about trying something new; what if Holistic Medicine doesn't work for my persistent sleep problems?
It's understandable to feel skeptical, especially when you've tried many things. Our Holistic Medicine approach is deeply personalized. We start with a thorough assessment to understand your unique situation and create a plan tailored to you. Many clients find that by addressing the deeper imbalances, they finally achieve the consistent, quality sleep that traditional methods couldn't provide.
How long does it take to see results for sleep improvement with Holistic Medicine?
The timeline for sleep improvement with Holistic Medicine varies for everyone, as it depends on the chronicity and complexity of your sleep challenges and burnout. Some clients notice subtle shifts within a few weeks, while for others, it may take a few months to establish profound, consistent changes. Our focus is on sustainable progress, building lasting foundations for your sleep and overall well-being.
