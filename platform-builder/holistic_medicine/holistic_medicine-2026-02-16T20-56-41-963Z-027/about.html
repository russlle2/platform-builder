Common misconceptions

People often assume integrative means unscientific. In our practice, integrative means combining useful tools — diet, movement, targeted testing, and mindful therapies — guided by data and patient goals.

A typical pathway

Initial intake → prioritized plan with 2–3 specific tools → follow-ups for refinement. Labs when they meaningfully change decisions. No guarantees; focus on understanding and symptom reduction.

Questions people ask

How long before I see change?
Timeline varies: some behavioral changes help quickly; others need months. We set realistic expectations together.
Will I be pushed into supplements or tests?
Never. We discuss pros and cons and only recommend tests when they clarify care or guide meaningful choices.
Is this a replacement for primary care?
We collaborate with your medical team and encourage coordination for ongoing conditions and medications.
