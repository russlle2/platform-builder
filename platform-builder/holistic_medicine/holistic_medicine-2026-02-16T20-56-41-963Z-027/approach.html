How we work — a stepwise, collaborative practice

Our process centers on careful intake, evidence-informed planning and iterative follow-up. Labs and supplements are optional and always explained with risks and benefits.

  1. Intake & Narrative mapping — A 60–75 minute visit to explore history, lifestyle, goals and context. We listen more than we speak and gather what matters.
  2. Assessment & shared priorities — We synthesize findings and co-create what to address first, considering safety, feasibility and personal values.
  3. Personalized plan — Food-based strategies, movement, sleep hygiene, mind-body tools, and targeted botanicals where appropriate. No guarantees, only tailored recommendations.
  4. Optional labs — When useful, we may suggest conventional or functional testing as an educational tool to clarify mechanisms; testing is elective and explained.
  5. Follow-up & refinement — Regular check-ins to track outcomes, adjust dosing or approaches, and focus on sustainable habits.
What to expect

Education-forward visits, a collaborative tone, and a plan you can adapt. We emphasize safety and transparent options.

Follow-up cadence

Typical plans include a 4–8 week follow-up after initial changes, then monthly or as clinically indicated. We track symptoms, function and quality of life.

About optional labs

Labs can refine a plan but are not always necessary. We discuss what each test might change and the limitations of interpretation.

Example:

A patient with disrupted sleep and low daytime energy followed a staged plan combining sleep timing, brief supplement trial and breathwork; outcomes improved over several months. Results are individual and not guaranteed.

Collaboration & referrals

We partner with conventional providers and allied therapists when needed to ensure safety and comprehensive care. Your autonomy and choices guide all recommendations.
