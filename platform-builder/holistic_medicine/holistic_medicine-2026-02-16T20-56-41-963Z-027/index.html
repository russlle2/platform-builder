Common myths — and what the evidence actually says

Myth

Holistic care promises a single cure for complex problems.

Truth

Integrative medicine emphasizes assessment, individualized plans, and ongoing support rather than guaranteed cures.

Myth

Natural means safe or immediate.

Truth

Natural options can be helpful but require context, dosing, and monitoring—especially alongside other treatments.

Myth

One-size-fits-all advice works for everyone.

Truth

We build plans from history, labs (if helpful), and goals. The human system is dynamic—care must be too.

Illustrative notes (anonymized)

Stress & sleep regulation

Client A presented with chronic stress and fragmented sleep. After intake we prioritized sleep hygiene, breathwork, and a short trial of melatonin combined with evening routines. Over 12 weeks, sleep consolidated and daytime energy improved. Labs were considered but not immediately needed.

Digestive resilience

Client B struggled with bloating and irregularity. A phased plan included dietary pattern testing, fiber modulation, and microbiome-supportive habits. Where indicated, noninvasive stool markers were discussed. Symptoms reduced with tailored changes and follow-up.

Individual results vary. Any recommendations are educational and personalized; specific testing or referrals may be suggested when clinically appropriate.
