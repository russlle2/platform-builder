Myths & Realities about holistic care

There are many misconceptions about integrative medicine. Below are common myths followed by balanced perspectives to help you make informed choices.

Myth

Holistic care replaces conventional medicine.

Reality

We aim to complement standard treatments, coordinate with your providers, and focus on whole-person wellbeing.

Myth

Results are immediate.

Reality

Some changes take time. We emphasize education, gradual adjustments, and measurable steps.

Myth

One-size-fits-all solutions exist.

Reality

Care plans are individualized based on your history, goals, and any relevant diagnostics.

Common questions

Do you offer labs?

Yes — we can recommend educational labs when helpful and discuss interpretation. Labs are optional and coordinated as part of a plan.

Is this covered by insurance?

Coverage varies. Administrative staff can guide you, but some consults and supplements may be out-of-pocket.

Will I be told to stop my medications?

Never without a prescriber. We coordinate with your medical team and prioritize safety when discussing adjunctive strategies.
