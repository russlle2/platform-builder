Design notes:
- Visual depth is provided by an inline decorative SVG and CSS gradients. No external fonts or CDNs are used.
- The page emphasizes education and whole-person care; it avoids promises of cures and includes privacy and safety notes.
- Navigation labels are intentionally phrased to fit a modern/mystic tone (e.g., Offerings, Concerns, Philosophy).

Assets:
- For a repeating background accent, reference an SVG at assets/img/pattern.svg if desired. The page includes an inline SVG for decoration; adding an external pattern.svg will complement other pages.

Integration tips:
- Hook the contact form action to your secure backend endpoint at /submit-contact or modify to use your form provider.
- Ensure any intake links or lab ordering pages are secured and that private health data is transmitted via encrypted channels.

Accessibility & legal:
- Keep the disclaimers visible; this template is meant for educational/supportive consultations only.
