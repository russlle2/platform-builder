Frequently Asked Questions

How can Holistic Medicine specifically help my sleep?
Holistic Medicine addresses sleep challenges by considering the full picture of your health. Instead of just masking symptoms, we explore underlying imbalances in your body and lifestyle that might be disrupting your rest. We'll work together to gently rebalance these areas, helping your body naturally fall into a more restorative sleep pattern.
Will I have to take many new supplements or medications?
Our focus in Holistic Medicine is to support your body's natural capabilities. We prioritize gentle, non-pharmacological approaches first. This might include dietary adjustments, relaxation techniques, or exploring natural remedies if appropriate. Any recommendations will always be discussed thoroughly with you and integrated safely with your current health regimen.
Is this approach suitable for seniors with existing health conditions?
Absolutely. Holistic Medicine is uniquely suited for seniors, especially those with existing health conditions, because it emphasizes gentle, individualized care. We always take your complete medical history into account, working collaboratively with your other healthcare providers to ensure our sleep-focused strategies are safe, supportive, and complementary to your overall well-being.
How quickly can I expect to see improvements in my sleep?
Improving sleep through Holistic Medicine is a personalized process. While some individuals notice positive shifts relatively quickly, lasting and deep improvements often come from consistent practice of the gentle strategies we develop together. We'll set realistic expectations and celebrate each step forward, guiding you toward sustained, restorative rest.
