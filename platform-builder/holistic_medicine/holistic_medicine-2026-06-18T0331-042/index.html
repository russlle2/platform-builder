Frequently Asked Questions

How can Holistic Medicine specifically help my postpartum stress and nervous system?
Holistic Medicine for new mothers focuses on addressing the root causes of postpartum stress, not just the symptoms. We look at areas like nutritional deficiencies, sleep patterns, hormonal balance, and emotional processing to create a comprehensive plan that helps calm your nervous system, reduce feelings of overwhelm, and build resilience during this demanding time.
Is this approach suitable if I’m struggling with anxiety or feeling constantly on edge after giving birth?
Absolutely. Our primary focus is on nervous system regulation, which is incredibly helpful for managing anxiety and feelings of being 'on edge.' We provide gentle, non-invasive techniques and personalized guidance within Holistic Medicine that aim to shift your body from a state of fight-or-flight into one of rest and digest, helping you feel more grounded and peaceful.
What kind of commitment is required for a Holistic Medicine plan during postpartum?
We understand that as a new mother, your time and energy are precious. Our Holistic Medicine plans are designed to be flexible and integrate seamlessly into your life. We start with understanding your unique situation and then build a plan with practical, manageable steps. The commitment involves your willingness to incorporate new habits and practices, and we’re here to support you every step of the way.
Will Holistic Medicine interfere with my current medical care or breastfeeding?
Our Holistic Medicine approach is designed to complement your existing medical care. We always encourage open communication with your primary healthcare providers. When it comes to breastfeeding, we carefully select and recommend practices, herbs, and nutritional support that are safe and supportive for both you and your baby. Your well-being and your baby's are our top priority.
