Frequently Asked Questions

What if I have trouble falling asleep even after trying your tips?
It's normal to have a few nights where it doesn't click. We'll adjust your plan together, focusing on the root cause—like stress from exams or caffeine timing—not just adding more tips.
Do I need to give up coffee to sleep better?
Not at all. We'll look at when and how much you drink it, then create a plan that works with your habits. For example, switching to decaf after 2 p.m. might be all you need.
How soon will I notice better sleep?
Many students feel a difference in their energy within a week. We focus on small, consistent changes—like one new bedtime habit—to build rest without overwhelming you.
What if I'm not sure if I'm even having sleep problems?
It's common to think you're 'just tired from school,' but poor sleep affects your focus and mood. We'll help you recognize the signs and start building better rest with one simple step.
