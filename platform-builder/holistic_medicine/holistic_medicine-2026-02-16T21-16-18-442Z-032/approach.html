A gentle, structured path — intake to ongoing support

Our approach layers listening, education, and practical plans. We meet you where you are and co-create steps that fit your life. Below is the typical flow; elements are optional and adapted per person.

1 — Intake & story

Careful listening to history, priorities, and current routines. Goal-setting that is concrete and compassionate.

2 — Education & options

Discussion of evidence, risks and benefits, and lifestyle tools. Shared decision-making guides the plan.

3 — Personalized plan

Small, practical steps: rhythms, food patterns, movement, sleep practices, and stress skills — tailored to daily life.

4 — Follow-up & iteration

Regular check-ins to track progress, remove barriers, and refine the approach. Care is iterative and adaptive.

Optional educational labs: when appropriate, we review testing options purely for information and targeted guidance. Tests are discussed case-by-case — never prescribed without context.
Examples of tests we may discuss (educational)
• Basic metabolic and thyroid panels • Targeted micronutrient checks • Simple gut function markers • Sleep or circadian assessment tools

We emphasize non-invasive, low-risk options first and always orient to safety. Referrals to specialists are made when needed; our role is to integrate learning, longitudinal support, and practical habit change.
