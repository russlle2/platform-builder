Myth and truth

Holistic care is sometimes mistaken for quick fixes. Below are common misunderstandings and what to really expect.

Myth

No single pill or ritual will ‘fix’ everything instantly.

Truth

Care is an unfolding conversation. We focus on education, lifestyle supports, and coordinated plans tailored to you — with respect for evidence and limits.

FAQ

What should I expect in a first visit?

An intake focused on history, current concerns, and priorities. We discuss options, timelines, and whether labs or collaborations are educationally appropriate.

Do you order labs?

Occasionally — as educational tools to help understand patterns. Labs are optional and discussed transparently.

Is this a substitute for medical care?

No. We collaborate with your clinicians when appropriate and will refer when specialized medical care is needed.

Payment and insurance?

We offer clear pricing and can provide superbills for insurance reimbursement when relevant.
