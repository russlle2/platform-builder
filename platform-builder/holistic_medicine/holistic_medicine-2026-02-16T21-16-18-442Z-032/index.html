Case notes — anonymized, educational

Below are short, de-identified vignettes from our workshop and intake series meant for learning. They are not guarantees and do not replace medical advice.

Evening rhythm for sleep

A participant in our sleep series decreased nighttime screen time, introduced a 20-minute wind-down ritual, and tracked sleep patterns. Education around sleep stages helped set expectations; progress was incremental and individualized.

Digestive resilience

Through dietary journaling and paced breath work, someone with intermittent bloating learned to identify triggers, added gentle fiber changes, and consulted their primary care for testing when warranted.

Energy and pacing

Participants in an energy-focused series used activity mapping and micro-rests to redistribute effort across the day, supported by gentle nutritional education and check-ins.
