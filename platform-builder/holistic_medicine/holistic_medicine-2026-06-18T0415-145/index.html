Frequently Asked Questions

How can Holistic Medicine specifically help my college anxiety and sleep?
We focus on connecting the dots between your academic stress, daily habits, and sleep quality. Our holistic medicine approach addresses the root causes of anxiety-induced sleeplessness, providing you with practical tools to calm your mind, improve your sleep hygiene, and foster restorative rest, rather than just masking symptoms.
I'm on a tight student budget. Are your Holistic Medicine services affordable?
We understand that college life comes with financial considerations. We offer various session options and can discuss payment plans to ensure our holistic medicine services are accessible. Your well-being is important, and we're committed to finding a solution that works for you.
Will Holistic Medicine clash with my existing academic schedule or other commitments?
Absolutely not! We pride ourselves on being flexible and understanding the demanding nature of college life. Our holistic medicine appointments are scheduled with your academic calendar in mind, and we work with you to integrate new sleep habits seamlessly into your routine, not disrupt it.
What if I've tried everything for sleep and nothing works? Can Holistic Medicine still help me?
Many college students feel this way, and you're not alone. Our holistic medicine approach is highly personalized. We delve deeper than conventional methods, considering your unique lifestyle, stressors, and physical well-being to uncover underlying issues and craft a tailored plan to finally help you achieve consistent, restorative sleep.
