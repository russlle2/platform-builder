Frequently Asked Questions

How can Holistic Medicine help with my college anxiety and academic pressure?
Our Holistic Medicine approach provides you with practical, science-backed strategies to regulate your nervous system, process overwhelming emotions, and build coping mechanisms specifically for the demands of college. We look at your whole self, not just symptoms, helping you develop lasting resilience.
Is this approach suitable for students who've experienced past trauma?
Absolutely. Our care is deeply rooted in trauma-informed principles. We create a safe, supportive environment where you can explore past experiences and their impact on your present well-being at your own pace, without feeling re-traumatized. Your comfort and agency are always prioritized.
What kind of techniques does Holistic Medicine use for emotional healing?
We blend various evidence-informed techniques, including mindfulness practices, somatic awareness, nervous system regulation exercises, and nutritional psychology, all tailored to address the unique stressors of college life. Our goal is to equip you with practical tools for emotional self-regulation and stress reduction.
How often would I need to attend sessions, and what's the time commitment?
The frequency and duration of your sessions will be collaboratively determined based on your individual needs and academic schedule. We aim for an approach that integrates seamlessly into your college life, providing effective support without adding undue pressure. We'll discuss this in detail during your initial consultation.
