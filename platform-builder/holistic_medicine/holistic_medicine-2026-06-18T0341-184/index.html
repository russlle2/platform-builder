Frequently Asked Questions

How soon can I expect to sleep better after starting your program?
Many people notice a difference in their restfulness within 2-4 weeks. We focus on sustainable changes, not quick fixes, so you can build sleep habits that last throughout your recovery.
I’m on medications that affect sleep. Can you still help?
Absolutely. We work with your healthcare team to ensure any sleep strategies we suggest are safe with your current medications. We'll review your regimen together to find the right approach for you.
Do you use supplements or require special equipment?
No. Our sleep support is based on simple, accessible changes you can make at home. We might suggest a comfortable pillow or a calming tea, but nothing you can't easily find. You won't need a new mattress or expensive gadgets.
What if I can’t make it to an in-person appointment in ?
We offer virtual consultations for those who can't travel easily. You’ll get the same personalized guidance, just from the comfort of home. We also provide written materials and follow-up emails to support you between sessions.
