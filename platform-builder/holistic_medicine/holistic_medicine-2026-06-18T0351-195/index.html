Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly will I notice better rest?
Most clients experience deeper sleep within 2 weeks—not 'feeling better,' but measurable improvements in sleep architecture (validated via wearable data). You'll wake up with fewer 3 AM mental loops and more focus for your next big meeting.
Do you use supplements?
Only if evidence supports it. We prioritize behavioral shifts first: optimizing your sleep environment, timing, and pre-sleep habits. If needed, we recommend specific, research-backed supplements (e.g., magnesium glycinate for nervous system calm) at the exact dose and time your body needs it.
What if my job requires late nights?
We design rest strategies *around* your schedule, not against it. We'll create a 'reset window' during your natural dip in alertness (often 1-3 PM), align your light exposure to mask shift work, and build a micro-nap protocol that actually recharges you—no guilt, no burnout.
How is this different from a sleep coach?
We're trained in integrative medicine—blending Eastern restorative practices with sleep medicine. You won't get generic tips. Instead, you'll get a personalized protocol addressing *why* your stress disrupts sleep (e.g., cortisol spikes from back-to-back Zooms) and how to fix it at the root.
