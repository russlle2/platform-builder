Frequently Asked Questions

How does Holistic Medicine specifically address sleep for new mothers?
For new mothers, Holistic Medicine views sleep disruption as a sign of deeper imbalances. We look at your unique physical, emotional, and energetic state during postpartum to create a personalized plan. This might include gentle herbal support, specific nutritional adjustments, mindful movement, or practices to quiet your nervous system, all tailored to help you achieve more restorative rest.
Is this approach suitable if I'm exclusively breastfeeding or pumping?
Absolutely. Your breastfeeding journey is deeply respected and considered in every aspect of your care. Any recommendations, whether they involve herbs, supplements, or lifestyle shifts, are carefully selected to be safe and supportive for both you and your baby, ensuring no disruption to your milk supply or your baby's well-being.
How quickly can I expect to see improvements in my sleep with Holistic Medicine?
The path to deeper rest is unique for every new mother. While some clients report feeling a greater sense of calm and slight improvements within a few weeks, sustainable change often unfolds over a longer period as we gently re-educate your body and mind. Our goal is lasting, gentle restoration rather than a temporary fix.
What if my baby's sleep patterns are unpredictable? Can Holistic Medicine still help me?
Yes, even with unpredictable infant sleep, we focus on maximizing the quality of your rest whenever it occurs. Our work helps your body and mind enter deeper states of relaxation more quickly, and recover more effectively during those precious moments of rest. We also equip you with tools to manage sleep fragmentation and reduce the cumulative effects of sleep deprivation.
