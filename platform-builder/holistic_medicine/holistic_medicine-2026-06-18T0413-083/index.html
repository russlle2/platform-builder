Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is Holistic Medicine, and how can it help my stress?
Holistic Medicine looks at your whole self – your body, mind, and spirit – to understand what's causing your stress. Instead of just focusing on symptoms, we explore gentle, natural ways to support your overall well-being. This can involve simple lifestyle adjustments, relaxation techniques, and nutritional guidance to help calm your nervous system and reduce feelings of overwhelm.
I'm a senior and new to this. Is Holistic Medicine too complicated for me?
Absolutely not! Our approach is specifically designed to be gentle and accessible, especially for seniors with no prior wellness experience. We break down concepts into easy-to-understand steps and focus on practices that are comfortable and safe for you. There’s no need to feel intimidated; we’re here to guide you with patience and understanding.
How quickly can I expect to feel a difference in my stress levels?
Everyone's experience is unique, but many of our senior clients report feeling a greater sense of calm and ease after just a few sessions. Our focus is on sustainable, gentle changes that help your nervous system gradually rebalance. We'll work at your pace, ensuring you feel comfortable and supported as you begin to notice positive shifts in your well-being.
Do I need to stop taking my current medications to try Holistic Medicine?
No, you do not. Our Holistic Medicine approach is designed to complement any existing medical care you're receiving. We always recommend you continue to follow your doctor's advice regarding medications. Our role is to offer gentle, natural support to enhance your overall well-being, focusing on stress reduction and nervous system balance, in harmony with your current health routine.
