Supportive guidance

Typical areas

Our approach is educational and exploratory. We help people build sustainable routines and interpret evidence relevant to their lives. Not every path suits every person; clinical decisions are individualized.

  • Sleep disturbances — habit, environment, circadian patterns
  • Low energy & fatigue — lifestyle, pacing, nutritional patterns
  • Digestive complaints — dietary patterns, routines, symptom tracking
  • Pain & movement — pacing, movement prescription, recovery habits
  • Mood & stress — regulation practices, structure, rest cycles
  • Women's health — cycle awareness, lifestyle interactions

What we do and do not do

We provide educational assessment, collaborative planning, and cohort-supported behavior change. We do not provide emergency services, guarantee cure, or replace specialist medical care when needed.

Typical path
  1. Initial consult to map priorities (60 min)
  2. Actionable practices + cohort learning
  3. Check-ins and measure review (individual or group)
