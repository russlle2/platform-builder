Questions people often ask

      

        How is the cohort different from private care?
        
Shared learning lowers cost and increases accountability. You still get clinician time for personalization during the program.

      

      

        Are supplements required?
        
We recommend options when evidence suggests benefit; nothing is mandated. Decisions are collaborative.

      

      

        Will you order labs?
        
Labs are considered when data helps decision-making; we discuss benefits, limits, and costs before ordering.
