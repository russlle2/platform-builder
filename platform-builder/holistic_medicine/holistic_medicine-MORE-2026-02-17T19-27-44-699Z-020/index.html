Small practices that add up

A curated set of brief, evidence-aware practices you can try in daily life. These are educational suggestions — not medical directives.

  • Movement pause — three 3-minute movement breaks in your day.

  • Breathing reset — diaphragmatic breathing for 2–4 minutes (use the guided practice).
