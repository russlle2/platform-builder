How our planning works

We treat your time as the scarce resource. After the inventory, we allocate attention in the first session and design micro steps you can try between visits. The plan we recommend adapts: a short check-in if you have immediate needs, a paced series if you need skill-building.

What we’ll deliver

  • A focused agenda for the initial session
  • Three prioritized goals with clear micro-habits
  • A follow-up cadence (2-week, 6-week, 12-week suggestions)
  • Simple measures to track progress between visits
