Frequently Asked Questions

How does Holistic Medicine specifically help active individuals with physical stress?
For athletes and active people, Holistic Medicine focuses on reducing the cumulative physical stress from training and competition. We address nervous system dysregulation, which often manifests as poor recovery, persistent fatigue, or even injury susceptibility. Our methods aim to calm your system, improve adaptive capacity, and enhance your body's natural ability to restore balance.
What kind of nervous system regulation techniques can I expect to learn?
You'll learn practical, accessible techniques designed to shift your nervous system out of overdrive. This might include specific breathing exercises, targeted mindfulness practices, or somatic awareness strategies. These aren't just for downtime; they are tools you can integrate pre-workout, post-training, or during moments of high pressure to manage your internal state effectively.
Is Holistic Medicine compatible with my current training regimen?
Absolutely. Our approach in Holistic Medicine is designed to complement and enhance your existing training. We don't replace your coaches or physical therapists; instead, we provide an additional layer of support focused on systemic balance and stress reduction. This integration helps optimize your body's response to training, potentially improving performance and reducing the risk of overtraining or burnout.
How quickly can I expect to feel a difference with Holistic Medicine for stress reduction?
Many active individuals report noticing a shift in their stress levels and nervous system state within a few sessions, especially as they begin to apply the techniques consistently. Lasting changes, like improved sleep quality, reduced chronic tension, and enhanced emotional regulation, develop over time with sustained practice and commitment to the strategies we develop together.
