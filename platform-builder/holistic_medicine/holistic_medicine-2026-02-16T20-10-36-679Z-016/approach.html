Our care pathway: collaborative, educational, personalized

We use a structured yet flexible process: listening first, then assessing, learning together and adjusting as you progress. Options like labs are discussed as educational tools — not requirements.

Schedule an intake

1. Intake & Listening

A focused first visit explores history, current symptoms, medications, lifestyle and priorities. Time is made for your story — this guides everything that follows.

2. Comprehensive Assessment

Assessment includes targeted questions, review of recent records and optional non-invasive measures. Testing is considered when it meaningfully informs a plan.

3. Collaborative Plan

Together we co-create a realistic plan: dietary steps, habits, therapeutic supports, and measurable goals. We discuss risks, alternatives and expected timelines.

4. Follow-ups & Adaptation

Short follow-ups monitor progress, address barriers, and refine the plan. Our emphasis is on sustainable change and patient education.

5. Optional Labs & Tools

Labs or functional tests may be discussed as educational resources to clarify physiology. Any testing is optional and chosen with your informed consent.

6. Ongoing Self-Care Skills

We prioritize skills you can use daily: stress management, nutritional patterns, sleep hygiene, and pacing strategies to support long-term wellbeing.

Our approach emphasizes education, shared decision-making and safety. We do not promise cures — we aim to reduce symptom burden and support functional improvement through sustainable strategies.
