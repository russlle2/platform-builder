Common misunderstandings — quick clarifications
Myth: Integrative care promises quick cures.
Truth: We aim to educate, support evidence-informed choices, and guide sustainable changes rather than promise guaranteed cures.
Myth: It's all supplements and vague advice.
Truth: Nutrition, targeted testing, behavior change, and careful coordination with specialists are core elements.
Myth: You must stop conventional care.
