Frequently Asked Questions

How is your Holistic Medicine approach different for new mothers struggling with sleep?
Our Holistic Medicine approach focuses specifically on the unique physiological and emotional demands of postpartum recovery. We consider hormonal balance, nutrient depletion, stress levels, and nervous system regulation, rather than just offering generic sleep tips. Your personalized plan will be designed to support your body's natural healing and rest cycles after childbirth.
I'm breastfeeding; can I still use Holistic Medicine remedies for sleep?
Absolutely. Your safety and your baby's well-being are our top priorities. We carefully select and recommend Holistic Medicine remedies that are safe and compatible with breastfeeding, always discussing potential interactions and ensuring they support both your recovery and your milk supply. We'll prioritize gentle, nourishing approaches.
How quickly can I expect to see improvements in my sleep with Holistic Medicine care?
Recovery is personal, and everyone's body responds differently. While some new mothers notice improvements in a few weeks, sustainable change often takes time as we address underlying imbalances through Holistic Medicine. We focus on gradual, consistent progress to help you build lasting, restorative sleep patterns. Your journey is unique, and we honor that.
Do I need to stop co-sleeping or night feeding to benefit from your Holistic Medicine program?
Not at all. We understand the realities of newborn care and the choices you make for your family. Our Holistic Medicine strategies are designed to integrate into your existing life, supporting you in finding pockets of rest and improving the quality of your sleep, even amidst night waking. We work with your current situation, not against it.
