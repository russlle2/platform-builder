Frequently Asked Questions

How does Holistic Medicine at specifically address pain for seniors?
Our Holistic Medicine practice employs gentle, non-invasive techniques tailored to the physiology of individuals over 60. We focus on identifying the underlying causes of your physical pain, rather than just masking symptoms, using methods that support your body's natural capacity for comfort and ease, promoting long-term relief.
Will the somatic healing sessions be too strenuous for me?
Absolutely not. Our somatic healing sessions are designed with utmost gentleness and care, specifically for your comfort and safety. We adapt every movement and technique to your individual capacity and needs, ensuring a soothing and restorative experience that never feels strenuous, only supportive.
What kind of results can I realistically expect from your Holistic Medicine services?
Clients typically experience a significant reduction in chronic physical discomfort, improved flexibility, and a greater sense of bodily ease. Our aim is to enhance your daily quality of life, allowing you to move with more fluidity and participate in activities you enjoy, all while feeling more grounded and comfortable in your own body.
Is 's approach suitable if I have specific age-related health concerns?
Yes, our Holistic Medicine approach is highly individualized. takes a comprehensive look at your health history, including any age-related conditions, to ensure our care plan is perfectly aligned with your needs. We always prioritize your safety and well-being, integrating our treatments seamlessly with any existing medical care you may be receiving.
