Frequently Asked Questions

How does Holistic Medicine address emotional stress for athletes?
For athletes and active people, emotional stress can manifest as physical tension, delayed recovery, or performance anxiety. Our Holistic Medicine approach uses trauma-informed techniques to gently explore the roots of these emotional patterns, helping you release stored tension and cultivate a more balanced internal state. This can significantly improve your physical resilience and mental clarity.
Is Holistic Medicine suitable for someone who experiences performance anxiety?
Absolutely. Performance anxiety often stems from underlying emotional patterns or past experiences. Our Holistic Medicine practice provides tools and support to address these emotional roots, helping you reframe your relationship with pressure and competition. By fostering inner calm and self-trust, you can approach your sport with greater confidence and ease.
Can Holistic Medicine help with physical pain that doctors can't explain?
Sometimes, persistent physical pain, especially for active individuals, has an emotional component that conventional medicine might overlook. Our Holistic Medicine approach explores the emotional connections to your physical discomfort, offering pathways to release deeply held stress or trauma that may be contributing to your pain. This can lead to profound relief and a deeper understanding of your body's messages.
What kind of commitment is required for emotional healing in Holistic Medicine?
Emotional healing within our Holistic Medicine framework is a personal process. We meet you where you are, offering a supportive and non-judgmental space. The commitment involves your willingness to explore your inner landscape and engage with the practices we offer. Many active people find that even a few sessions can create significant shifts in their emotional well-being and physical experience.
