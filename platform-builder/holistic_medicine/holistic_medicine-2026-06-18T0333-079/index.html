Frequently Asked Questions

How can Holistic Medicine specifically help me manage the stress of parenting young children?
We focus on nervous system regulation techniques tailored to the unique stressors parents face, like sleep deprivation, constant demands, and emotional overwhelm. We'll equip you with practical strategies to reduce your body's stress response and build resilience, helping you feel calmer and more in control amidst daily chaos.
I'm already so busy. How will I find time for your Holistic Medicine practices?
We understand your time is precious. Our approach is designed to integrate seamlessly into your life. We'll work together to identify small, impactful practices that fit your schedule, focusing on consistency over intensity. Even a few minutes a day can make a significant difference in regulating your nervous system and reducing stress.
Will Holistic Medicine mean I have to make huge lifestyle changes to reduce my household stress?
Not at all. Our goal is to empower you with sustainable changes. We start by identifying manageable steps that feel right for you and your family. Our Holistic Medicine approach emphasizes gradual, personalized adjustments rather than drastic overhauls, ensuring the practices you learn are practical and long-lasting for stress reduction.
How is your Holistic Medicine approach different from just 'managing' my stress as a parent?
Instead of just coping with stress, we help you address its underlying impact on your nervous system. Our Holistic Medicine methods aim to fundamentally shift your body's response to stressors, reducing chronic tension and improving your capacity for calm. This leads to a deeper, more lasting sense of peace, not just temporary relief.
