Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is 'trauma-informed care' in the context of Holistic Medicine for new mothers?
Trauma-informed care means we recognize that many new mothers may have experienced past difficult events, or find the birth experience itself overwhelming. Our Holistic Medicine approach prioritizes your emotional safety, trust, and choice. We avoid re-traumatizing practices and focus on building your sense of control and empowerment in your recovery.
I'm a new mother and feel completely drained. How can Holistic Medicine help with my emotional exhaustion?
Emotional exhaustion is very common postpartum. Our Holistic Medicine practice will gently explore the roots of your exhaustion, looking beyond just sleep deprivation. We'll work on calming your nervous system, exploring nutritional support, and teaching you simple, accessible practices to replenish your emotional reserves, helping you feel more centered and less overwhelmed.
Do I need prior experience with 'wellness' practices to benefit from your Holistic Medicine for new mothers?
Absolutely not! Our Holistic Medicine approach is designed to be gentle and accessible, especially for new mothers who are already juggling so much. There's no prior experience needed. We meet you exactly where you are, offering simple, practical tools and support that fit into your life, without adding extra pressure.
I'm worried about feeling judged or misunderstood. How is your Holistic Medicine different for new mothers?
We understand that vulnerability can be challenging, especially during such a tender time. Our Holistic Medicine practice is built on a foundation of non-judgment and deep empathy. We create a confidential and supportive space where your feelings are validated, and your experiences are honored. You are safe here to share your true postpartum experience without fear of criticism.
