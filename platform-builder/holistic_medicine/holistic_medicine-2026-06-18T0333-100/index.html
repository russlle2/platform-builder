Frequently Asked Questions

How can Holistic Medicine specifically help my stress during a big life change?
Holistic Medicine addresses stress by looking at your whole system, not just symptoms. During transitions, your nervous system can become dysregulated. We use specific techniques, like acupuncture or herbal remedies, to calm your fight-or-flight response, improve sleep, and enhance your body's natural ability to cope with change, helping you feel more grounded.
Is this approach suitable for any type of major life transition?
Yes, our integrative Holistic Medicine approach is designed to support your nervous system's resilience through various major transitions, whether it's a career change, relocation, relationship shift, or other significant event. The core focus is on regulating your stress response and promoting adaptability, which is beneficial regardless of the specific change you are experiencing.
What kind of results can I expect for my nervous system regulation?
Clients often report feeling a greater sense of calm, improved sleep quality, reduced anxiety, and increased capacity to manage daily stressors. Your nervous system will learn to shift more easily out of heightened states, allowing for clearer thinking and a more balanced emotional state, which is crucial when navigating new circumstances.
How does Holistic Medicine differ from traditional stress management during these times?
While traditional stress management offers valuable tools, our Holistic Medicine approach integrates ancient wisdom with modern science. We focus on physiological regulation of your nervous system using methods like energetic balancing and botanical medicine, alongside mindfulness, offering a deeper, more comprehensive way to build resilience during periods of significant change.
