Frequently Asked Questions

How does Holistic Medicine specifically help with stress during big life changes?
Our Holistic Medicine approach focuses on directly addressing the physiological impact of stress on your nervous system. We use methods designed to downregulate your fight-or-flight response and enhance your body's ability to self-regulate, helping you navigate changes with more composure rather than reactivity.
What does 'nervous system regulation' mean in the context of Holistic Medicine?
Nervous system regulation, in our Holistic Medicine practice, means guiding your autonomic nervous system back to a balanced state. When you're undergoing major transitions, your system can get stuck in stress responses. We provide tools and support to restore flexibility, allowing you to respond to challenges rather than being overwhelmed by them.
Is this Holistic Medicine approach suitable for any type of life transition?
Yes, our Holistic Medicine methods are designed to support your nervous system's resilience through a wide range of significant life changes – from career shifts and relational changes to relocation or personal growth phases. The core principles of stress reduction and regulation are universally beneficial for adapting to new circumstances.
What can I expect in an initial Holistic Medicine consultation?
During your first Holistic Medicine consultation, we'll discuss your current experiences, the specific life transition you're navigating, and the stress it's creating. We'll assess your nervous system's current state and begin to outline a personalized plan focusing on concrete strategies for stress reduction and regulation, tailored to your unique needs.
