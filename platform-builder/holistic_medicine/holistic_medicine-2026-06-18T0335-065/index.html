Frequently Asked Questions

How can this fit into my 6am-9pm schedule?
We use your existing moments—like waiting for coffee or between calls—to build nervous system resilience. These aren't additional hours; they're recalibration tools that work within your current rhythm.
Do I need to meditate for hours?
No. We use somatic techniques you can do while walking to your next meeting or during your 3pm energy slump. Your nervous system regulation happens through movement and breath—not just sitting in silence.
What if I'm skeptical of 'holistic' approaches?
We work with your existing science-based mindset. Every technique is rooted in neurobiology and clinical evidence—not just nature metaphors. You'll understand the 'why' behind each tool before you try it.
How soon will I notice calm?
Most clients feel subtle shifts in stress response within 72 hours—like not getting 'stuck' in frustration during tough meetings. Consistent practice builds visible resilience in about 4 weeks, without adding extra steps to your routine.
