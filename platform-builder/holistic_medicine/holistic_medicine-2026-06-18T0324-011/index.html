Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly will I notice changes in my stress response?
Many clients feel physical relief within 2-3 sessions. We teach tools you can deploy immediately during work hours—like a 60-second grounding technique for back-to-back meetings—to reduce tension as it happens.
What if my schedule is too tight for regular sessions?
Sessions are designed around your reality: 45 minutes max, with options for shorter check-ins. We focus on one actionable skill per session you can implement during commutes or lunch breaks—no extra hours needed.
Why does this work better than standard therapy for work burnout?
Standard therapy often addresses thoughts and behaviors. We target the physiological stress response itself—the root of burnout. You learn to regulate your nervous system directly, so you feel calmer during high-pressure deadlines, not just after the fact.
Do you work with my employer's insurance?
We're in-network with most major insurers for stress-related care. We verify coverage upfront—no surprise bills. If you're self-pay, we offer transparent pricing with no hidden costs.
