Frequently Asked Questions

How does Holistic Medicine specifically address sleep issues for someone recovering from chronic illness?
Our Holistic Medicine approach considers the systemic impact of chronic illness or injury on your sleep. We look at factors like inflammation, pain management, medication side effects, and emotional stress, developing a personalized plan that integrates various therapies to stabilize your sleep architecture and promote genuine restoration.
Is this approach suitable if my injury causes significant discomfort that interferes with sleep?
Yes, it is. We understand that pain is a primary barrier to sleep during recovery. Our Holistic Medicine strategies include non-pharmacological approaches to discomfort management, alongside techniques to calm your nervous system, allowing your body to find positions of greater ease and promoting the physiological conditions necessary for sleep, even with persistent injury-related discomfort.
What kind of commitment is required to see improvements in my rest?
Improving sleep during recovery is a process that requires consistent effort. While some clients experience subtle shifts quickly, significant, lasting improvements typically emerge over several weeks or months. Our Holistic Medicine programs are designed to be sustainable, providing you with practical tools and ongoing support to integrate new habits into your daily routine at your own pace.
Can Holistic Medicine help if I'm reliant on sleep aids due to my recovery?
Our goal in Holistic Medicine is to support your body's natural capacity for sleep. If you are currently using sleep aids, we work carefully to understand their role in your recovery. We develop a gradual, supportive strategy to optimize your inherent sleep mechanisms, potentially reducing your reliance on aids as your natural sleep patterns improve and stabilize under our guidance.
