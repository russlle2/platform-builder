Holistic Medicine: Understanding Your Stress Beyond the Symptoms

College life can feel like a constant sprint. You might be experiencing panic attacks before exams, constant worry about your grades, or a persistent feeling of not being enough. Instead of just managing these symptoms, our Holistic Medicine approach looks at the whole picture – your academic environment, social pressures, past experiences, and physical well-being – to understand the root causes of your anxiety and pressure. We're here to listen without judgment and help you make sense of it all.
