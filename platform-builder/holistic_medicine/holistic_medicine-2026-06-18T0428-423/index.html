Frequently Asked Questions

How does Holistic Medicine specifically support memory and focus for seniors?
Our Holistic Medicine approach for seniors gently addresses cognitive wellness by looking at the whole person. We consider aspects like nutrition, gentle movement, stress reduction, and mind-body practices, all of which contribute to improving circulation to the brain, enhancing neurotransmitter function, and reducing inflammation, thus supporting your memory and ability to focus with greater ease.
Is Holistic Medicine suitable if I'm already taking other medications?
Absolutely. Our Holistic Medicine practices are designed to complement your existing care, not replace it. We always encourage you to keep your primary healthcare provider informed. Our gentle methods are chosen to work synergistically, supporting your overall well-being without interfering with other treatments, always with your safety and comfort as our top priority.
What can I expect during my first consultation for cognitive wellness with Holistic Medicine?
During your first Holistic Medicine consultation, we'll have a warm, open conversation about your current cognitive experiences, your lifestyle, and your personal goals for mental clarity. We'll explore your unique history and discuss gentle, individualized strategies that resonate with your inner wisdom, ensuring you feel heard, understood, and hopeful about your path forward.
How long does it take to see improvements in mental clarity with Holistic Medicine?
Just as each person is unique, so is the pace of improvement. With our gentle Holistic Medicine approach, many individuals report feeling a greater sense of calm and mental ease within a few weeks. Consistent engagement with the personalized practices we co-create will naturally lead to more sustained improvements in your cognitive sharpness and overall mental well-being over time.
