How can Holistic Medicine specifically help my postpartum physical pain?

Holistic Medicine addresses the root causes of your postpartum physical pain, rather than just masking symptoms. We use gentle, yet effective, modalities to alleviate common issues like back pain, pelvic discomfort, and persistent tension, fostering your body's innate ability to heal and regain strength.

Is somatic healing relevant to my postpartum recovery?

Absolutely. Postpartum recovery isn't just physical; it's deeply somatic. Our somatic healing practices help you release the emotional and physical tension your body may be holding from childbirth and new motherhood, promoting deeper relaxation, reducing stress, and enhancing your overall sense of well-being.

What makes your Holistic Medicine approach exclusive for new mothers?

Our Holistic Medicine practice is entirely dedicated to the unique needs of new mothers. We understand the specific physiological and emotional landscape of postpartum recovery, offering specialized techniques and a deeply empathetic environment that caters precisely to your journey towards renewed strength and comfort.

How soon after childbirth can I begin Holistic Medicine sessions?

The ideal time to begin your Holistic Medicine sessions will depend on your individual recovery and your doctor's recommendations. We typically suggest waiting until your six-week postpartum check-up, but we encourage you to schedule a consultation to discuss your specific circumstances and create a safe, effective plan for your earliest possible comfort.
