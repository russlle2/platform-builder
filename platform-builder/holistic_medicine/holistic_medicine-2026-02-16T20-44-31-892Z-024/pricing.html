How we choose tests and treatments

We emphasize minimally invasive, evidence-aligned options. Labs are offered to clarify physiology when results will change management. Expect a clear explanation of benefit, risk, and cost before ordering.

  • Clinical-first: decisions start with your history and symptoms.
  • Stepwise testing: only when results will impact care.
  • Education: we teach you what each measure means for daily choices.

Typical extras

Functional labs: targeted hormone, nutrient, or stool testing (separate fee).
Supplement guidance: discussed only when clinically appropriate.
Referrals: coordinated with local specialists as needed.
