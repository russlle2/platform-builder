Small Habits, Lasting Change

Micro-habits are the building blocks of sustainable health—tiny actions you can repeat daily that compound over time.

1. Morning Hydration

Start with 2 cups of water and a short breath sequence to support digestion and focus.

2. Two-Minute Movement

Simple mobility or a brisk walk to reset energy and reduce stiffness.

3. Evening Wind-Down

30 minutes screen-free ritual with gentle stretching or journaling to improve sleep latency.
