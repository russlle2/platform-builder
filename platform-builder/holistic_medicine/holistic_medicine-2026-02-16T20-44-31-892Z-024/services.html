Clinical diagnostic approach

We combine detailed history-taking, validated screening tools, and focused labs when indicated. Testing is used judiciously to inform care, not to label people.

  • Systems review: sleep, mood, digestion, energy, and immune health.
  • Objective measures: select labs, functional markers, and symptom tracking.
  • Collaborative interpretation: we explain results and options in plain language.

Micro-habits to start today

Small, consistent changes often yield the most reliable improvements. Here are examples we commonly recommend.

  • Sleep anchor: pick a 30‑minute wind-down window before bed.
  • Digestive pause: eat slowly, chew thoroughly for each meal.
  • Breath reset: 3 minutes of paced breathing twice daily to reduce reactivity.
  • Movement bites: short movement breaks every 90 minutes during the day.
