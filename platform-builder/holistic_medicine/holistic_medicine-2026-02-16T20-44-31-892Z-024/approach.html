How we partner with you

Our method is structured yet flexible: listen, investigate (as needed), create a phased plan, and follow-up with measurable steps. Testing is optional and explained clearly as an educational tool.

Intake & discovery

We review comprehensive history, symptoms, sleep, stress, diet, and activity. Validated questionnaires help prioritize targets for care.

Evidence-focused interpretation

If testing is appropriate we discuss benefits, limits, and alternatives. Labs are used to inform practical recommendations, not to oversimplify complex health stories.

Shared planning

We co-create a phased plan with clear goals, small daily actions, and options for nutrition or targeted supplements when indicated.

Follow-up & trajectory

Follow-up visits focus on tracking progress, troubleshooting obstacles, and refining the plan. Frequency is individualized to needs and changeability.

Short-check
15–30 minutes to review immediate changes and adjust micro-habits.
Comprehensive review
30–60 minutes to reassess systems, repeat measures, and re-calibrate the plan.

Optional labs — an educational resource

We discuss targeted labs only when test results will change management. Typical examples include metabolic panels, basic inflammation markers, or targeted nutrient testing. Tests are not mandatory and are explained plainly before ordering.

Daily micro-habits

Consistent wake time
3-minute breathing breaks
Protein at breakfast
Move for 10 minutes
Bedtime wind-down

Small actions compound. We measure progress in meaningful, realistic steps rather than grand promises.
