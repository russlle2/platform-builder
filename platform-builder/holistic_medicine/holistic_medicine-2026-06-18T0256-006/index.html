Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is Holistic Medicine?
Holistic Medicine is an approach to health and wellness that considers the entire person—mind, body, spirit, and emotions—in the quest for optimal health. Instead of just treating symptoms, it seeks to identify and address the root causes of illness, promoting overall balance and well-being.
Is Holistic Medicine suitable for my specific health concerns?
Holistic Medicine can be beneficial for a wide range of health concerns, both chronic and acute, as it focuses on enhancing your body's natural healing capabilities. During your initial consultation, we will thoroughly discuss your unique health history and goals to determine how our approach can best support you.
How long does a typical Holistic Medicine treatment plan last?
The duration of your Holistic Medicine treatment plan varies greatly depending on your individual health needs and goals. Some clients experience significant improvements within a few weeks, while others with more complex conditions may require ongoing support. We work collaboratively with you to set realistic expectations and monitor your progress.
Will Holistic Medicine treatments interfere with my current medications?
Your safety is paramount. We always conduct a thorough review of your current medications, supplements, and health conditions. Our approach to Holistic Medicine is designed to complement conventional care, and we will discuss any potential interactions or contraindications to ensure your well-being. We encourage open communication with your primary care provider.
