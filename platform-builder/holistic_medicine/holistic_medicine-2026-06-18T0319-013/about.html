What exactly is 'nervous system regulation' and why is it important for seniors in Holistic Medicine?

Nervous system regulation refers to your body's ability to maintain a balanced state, especially under stress. For seniors, a well-regulated nervous system can mean better sleep, reduced anxiety, improved digestion, and an overall greater sense of calm and well-being. Our Holistic Medicine approach helps gently guide your system back to this balanced state.

How gentle are the stress reduction techniques in your Holistic Medicine practice?

We understand the importance of gentleness for our senior clients. All our stress reduction techniques are non-invasive, low-impact, and tailored to your individual physical capabilities and comfort levels. We never push beyond what feels right for you, ensuring a safe and calming experience within our Holistic Medicine framework.

Will Holistic Medicine treatments interfere with my current medications or other doctors' care?

Our Holistic Medicine approach is designed to complement your existing healthcare. We always encourage open communication with your primary care physician and other specialists. We will carefully review your health history and current medications to ensure all our recommendations are safe and supportive of your overall health plan.

What can I expect during a first visit to your Holistic Medicine practice?

Your first visit will involve a comprehensive, unhurried conversation where we listen to your concerns, understand your health goals, and discuss your lifestyle. We'll explain our Holistic Medicine approach in detail and together, we'll develop a personalized plan focused on gently supporting your nervous system and reducing stress, ensuring you feel heard and respected.
