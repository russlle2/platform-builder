Frequently Asked Questions

How does Holistic Medicine specifically help with the emotional impact of chronic illness or injury?
Holistic Medicine at views your emotional state as integral to your overall health. We focus on modalities that help regulate your nervous system, process grief, reduce anxiety related to your condition, and build coping strategies tailored to your unique experience of chronic illness or injury, promoting inner peace and resilience.
I've tried other therapies; what makes your trauma-informed Holistic Medicine different for my recovery?
Our trauma-informed Holistic Medicine approach is distinguished by its deep understanding of how chronic illness or injury can create ongoing stress or past trauma. We prioritize creating a sense of safety and agency for you, using gentle techniques that respect your boundaries and pace, rather than pushing you beyond your comfort zone, which is crucial for lasting emotional integration.
Will this approach address both my physical symptoms and emotional well-being simultaneously?
While our primary focus is on the emotional and psychological aspects of recovering from chronic illness or injury, we acknowledge their deep connection to physical well-being. Our Holistic Medicine perspective supports your body's natural healing capabilities by reducing stress and fostering emotional regulation, which can indirectly support your physical recovery and symptom management.
What if I'm feeling overwhelmed by my condition; is Holistic Medicine still suitable for me?
Absolutely. If you're feeling overwhelmed, that's a natural response to chronic illness or injury. Our Holistic Medicine practice is designed to meet you where you are. We offer gentle, supportive strategies to help you manage overwhelming feelings, providing a grounded space to explore your experiences and gradually build your capacity for emotional processing and self-care.
