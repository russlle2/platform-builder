Our story

We started as a small group of clinicians and teachers who wanted practical ways to help people weave steady habits into busy lives. Cohorts let people learn together, iterate quickly, and build peer accountability without the pressure of one-on-one dependence.

We blend evidence-aware guidance with gentle inquiry: asking about context, constraints, and what actually fits into your weeks. That way, growth is about being possible, not perfect.

How we think about change

  • Small experiments over big prescriptions
  • Patterns, not one-off fixes
  • Peer learning to normalize progress and setbacks

Principles we share with participants

  1. Start with one tiny anchor.
  2. Adjust practice to your context.
  3. Measure kindness as much as metrics.
