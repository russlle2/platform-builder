Layered skills, short timelines

We design programs so you can learn, try, and keep the parts that work. Each cohort emphasizes one clear target—sleep, digestion, energy, concentration—and teaches a small toolkit to test over weeks.

Practical framework

Three layers to bring a skill into daily life.

Anchor

One simple habit to use as a reliable scaffold (e.g., a 3-minute breath before bed).

Micro-practice

Short, repeatable practices you can do in pockets of time.

Environment tune

Small context edits—lighting, meal timing, device rules—that reduce friction for the habit.

Evidence and limits

We use practices supported by behavior-change and sleep research. Our work is educational and community-based; it is not individualized medical treatment. We welcome collaboration with clinicians and will refer when specialized care is needed.

Working with a cohort

  1. Pick a target and join the next cycle.
  2. Practice short assignments and bring questions to the group.
  3. At the end of the cycle, choose what to keep and how to adapt it.
