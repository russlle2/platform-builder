Conditions we support

We offer cohort-based education, practical routines and community support for common concerns that benefit from lifestyle and behavioral approaches.

Important: Our cohorts and resources are educational. They do not replace medical diagnosis or individualized treatment. If you have a serious or life-threatening condition, contact emergency services or a licensed health professional. If you are under medical care, consult your clinician before changing medications or treatment plans.

Mild-to-moderate sleep disruption

What we focus on: sleep timing, evening habits, light exposure and short bedtime rituals that support consistent sleep without complex prescriptions.

Educational approach: stepwise evening experiments and group troubleshooting, not medication management.

Stress-related digestive symptoms

What we focus on: gentle dietary patterns, stress-moderation techniques and pacing strategies that can ease common GI complaints influenced by stress.

Educational approach: real-world routines and pacing rather than one-size-fits-all diets. See a clinician if you have severe pain, blood in stools, or unexplained weight loss.

Persistent low energy & overwhelm

What we focus on: small habit cycles—movement, rest windows, and priority-setting—so energy stabilizes across a week.

Educational approach: cohort tools to test micro-changes and track effects. Not a replacement for assessment of medical causes of fatigue.

Anxiety that interrupts routine

What we focus on: grounding exercises, short breath practices and behavioral experiments you can practice between sessions.

Educational approach: supportive skill-building. If anxiety is severe or disabling, we will recommend clinical care and collaboration.

How to choose support

  1. Identify one pattern you want to change (sleep, digestion, energy).
  2. Pick a short cohort that targets that domain.
  3. Use the group for accountability; bring medical questions to your clinician.
Find a cohort
If you are under current medical treatment, discuss cohort participation with your provider. Our materials are educational and community-based.

Referral and safety

When a cohort participant needs more specialized care (e.g. severe psychiatric symptoms, complex gastrointestinal disease, or urgent medical issues), we will recommend appropriate referrals and provide supportive resources to coordinate care. Our facilitators will not alter prescribed medication regimens. If you are in crisis, call local emergency services or a crisis line.
