Why an integrative approach?

We combine evidence-informed medicine with nutrition, movement, sleep hygiene, and stress resiliency to address root contributors. Education is central — you’ll leave each visit with practical steps you can use right away.

  • Patient-centered collaborative planning
  • Practical strategies for daily life
  • Respect for both conventional and supportive therapies

Our care framework

1. Comprehensive Intake

A detailed review of history, lifestyle, and goals. This informs a tailored plan; we listen first, diagnose thoughtfully, and avoid one-size-fits-all fixes.

2. Personalized Plan

Behavioral recommendations, nutrition support, targeted supplements when appropriate, and a timeline for progress. We emphasize measurable habits and education.

3. Follow-ups & Labs

Regular visits to refine plans. Optional lab testing is available as an educational tool — used to guide decisions, not to promise cures.
