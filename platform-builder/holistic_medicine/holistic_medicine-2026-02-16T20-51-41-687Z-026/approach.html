How we work — a collaborative roadmap

A clear, stepwise approach centered on education, shared decisions, safety and measurable progress. We avoid one-size-fits-all promises.

1) Intake & assessment

The initial visit is thorough: health history, symptom mapping, medication and supplements review, and context (sleep, stress, diet, activity). We discuss priorities and identify immediate, safe steps.

2) Personalized care plan

Priorities
We list 2–3 actionable changes to focus on first.
Therapies
Nutrition, targeted supplements, mind-body practices, and lifestyle adjustments chosen with you.
Referrals
When specialties or diagnostics are needed we coordinate referrals.

3) Optional testing (educational)

Targeted labs can be useful to clarify metabolic, hormonal or inflammatory contributors. We review the evidence for each test and how results would change management. Labs are optional and discussed case-by-case; they are not required for all patients.

4) Follow-ups & re-assessment

Follow-up visits focus on measuring response, troubleshooting, and incremental adjustments. Progress is tracked using symptom scales and practical markers rather than promises.

Patient partnership

You remain in control: we provide education, options, and rationale so you can choose what fits your life. Serious or worsening symptoms may require urgent care — we facilitate safe coordination with other providers.
