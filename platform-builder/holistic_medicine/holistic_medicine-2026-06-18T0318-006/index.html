Frequently Asked Questions

How can Holistic Medicine help my nervous system during chronic recovery?
Chronic illness and injury can keep your nervous system in a 'fight or flight' state, making recovery slower and more stressful. Our Holistic Medicine practices specifically aim to shift your body into a 'rest and digest' state, promoting relaxation, reducing inflammation, and supporting your body's natural healing processes. We help you learn to down-regulate your stress response.
Is this approach suitable if I have severe pain or fatigue from my condition?
Absolutely. We understand that your energy and pain levels can fluctuate significantly. Our Holistic Medicine methods are gentle, adaptable, and always tailored to your current capacity. We prioritize your comfort and safety, ensuring that any practice we introduce supports rather than strains your system, even on days when you feel most vulnerable.
What kind of techniques does use for stress reduction?
We use a blend of evidence-informed Holistic Medicine techniques designed to soothe an overactive nervous system. This can include guided relaxation, mindful movement, somatic awareness exercises to release tension, personalized breathwork, and gentle self-regulation strategies. The focus is always on empowering you with practical tools for daily calm.
How long does it take to see results in nervous system regulation?
Every person's journey is unique, especially when recovering from chronic conditions. While some clients report feeling a sense of calm and reduced stress after just a few sessions, consistent practice and patience are key. We'll work together at your pace, celebrating small shifts and building sustainable habits that support your nervous system's long-term well-being.
