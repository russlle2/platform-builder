Frequently Asked Questions

What if my birth was traumatic? Can you help?
Yes. We address birth trauma through somatic therapy, not 'just talk.' We work with the body’s memory of the event, so you don’t have to relive it. Your emotions are valid—no pressure to 'get over it.'
How do you handle my anxiety when I can’t even breathe?
We meet you in the moment. Using grounding techniques that fit your energy level (yes, even when you’re holding a baby), we create calm without demanding 'more effort' from you.
Do you work with postpartum depression?
We focus on emotional healing when depression is part of your postpartum experience. We collaborate with your care team and use Holistic Medicine that complements—never replaces—medical treatment.
What if I need to pause sessions for my baby’s sleep schedule?
Sessions are flexible. We schedule around your reality—no guilt over 'not prioritizing yourself.' Your baby’s needs are part of the care plan, not a barrier.
