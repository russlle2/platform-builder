Small habits, steady change

Sustainable progress usually comes from small, consistent practices rather than dramatic fixes. Below are examples we often introduce.

Evening buffer

30–60 minutes of low-stimulation time before sleep: lights down, screens off, gentle breathwork.

Digestive reset

Simple rituals: sit down for meals, chew well, and note fullness cues to support digestion and energy.

Energy pacing

Short, timed activity breaks and midday micro-rests to manage fatigue and sustain focus.

Micro-habits are educational tools. They are offered as options that may improve symptoms over time for some patients.
