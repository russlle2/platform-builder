Questions people often ask

Do you treat specific conditions?
We provide education and whole-person plans for concerns like sleep disruption, stress, digestion, low energy, and chronic inflammation. This is educational and supportive care, not a promise of cure. For urgent needs, seek immediate medical attention.
What are membership benefits?
Membership bundles include regular short check-ins, priority scheduling, and curated educational content. Membership is optional and is designed for people who prefer ongoing guidance.

Labs are optional and used as educational tools when a deeper look is helpful. We discuss pros, limits, and next steps before ordering anything.
