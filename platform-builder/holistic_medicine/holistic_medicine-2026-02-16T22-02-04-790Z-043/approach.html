How our care flows — a human-centered sequence

We aim to make each step clear and collaborative: from listening and assessment to an individualized plan and gentle follow-ups. Below we describe the typical path and when optional educational labs might be introduced.

01

Intake & Deep Listening

We begin with an extended consult to understand your history, priorities, environment, and patterns. This is an educational conversation where you can share what matters most.

02

Assessment & Targets

Next we identify the most impactful, research-informed targets — for example sleep regularity, digestion support, stress buffering, or activity. The goal is to prioritize simple, measurable changes.

03

Personalized Plan

Your plan includes educational rationale, practical steps, and timelines. Recommendations may involve nutrition patterns, movement prescription, sleep strategies, and mind-body practices — explained so you can use them confidently.

04

Follow-ups & Iteration

Change happens over time. We schedule focused follow-ups to troubleshoot, celebrate progress, and adapt plans as life changes. Memberships provide regular touchpoints for continuity.

05

Optional Educational Labs

Sometimes educational tests (e.g., targeted hormone panels, food response education, or microbiome exploration) help clarify patterns. These are offered to inform care when helpful; they're optional and never required to receive supportive recommendations.
