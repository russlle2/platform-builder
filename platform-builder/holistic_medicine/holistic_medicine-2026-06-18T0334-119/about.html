How is Holistic Medicine different for parental sleep issues?

Our Holistic Medicine approach for parents considers the unique stressors of child-rearing and household management. We don't just look at sleep in isolation; we assess how nutrition, stress, environment, and family dynamics collectively impact your rest. This allows us to create integrated, practical solutions tailored to your specific situation, rather than generic sleep advice.

Will I need to make drastic changes to my family's routine?

Our goal is to implement effective, sustainable changes without causing further disruption. We understand that parents have limited bandwidth. We prioritize targeted interventions that yield the most significant impact on your sleep quality, often starting with small, manageable adjustments to your existing routine. Our guidance is designed to integrate seamlessly into your family life.

How quickly can I expect to see improvements in my sleep?

Improvement timelines vary for each individual, as sleep challenges are complex. However, many clients report initial shifts in sleep quality and reduced daytime fatigue within a few weeks of consistently applying our evidence-based strategies. We focus on building long-term, restorative sleep habits, which often means gradual, steady progress rather than an overnight fix.

Is this approach suitable if my child also has sleep difficulties?

While our primary focus is on parental sleep, we recognize that your child's sleep profoundly impacts yours. We can provide general guidance on creating a sleep-conducive environment for your family and resources for addressing common child sleep challenges, but our direct interventions will be centered on optimizing your own rest and stress management as a parent.
