Frequently Asked Questions

How does Holistic Medicine specifically address the stress of major life transitions?
Our Holistic Medicine approach focuses on the physiological impact of stress during transitions. We utilize methods that directly influence your nervous system's ability to cope, reducing reactivity and promoting a sense of calm. This helps your body and mind adapt more effectively to new circumstances.
What kind of techniques does Holistic Medicine use for nervous system regulation?
We employ a range of evidence-informed techniques within Holistic Medicine, chosen specifically for their impact on nervous system regulation. This can include specific breathwork, targeted nutritional support, adaptogenic herbs, and mindful movement practices, all tailored to your unique physiological needs during transition.
Will Holistic Medicine help me with decision-making during a big life change?
While Holistic Medicine doesn't make decisions for you, by regulating your nervous system and reducing chronic stress, we enhance your clarity and cognitive function. A calmer, more balanced state allows you to approach complex decisions with greater presence and less emotional reactivity, supporting more effective choices.
How long does it take to see results from Holistic Medicine for stress reduction?
The timeline for experiencing results with Holistic Medicine varies based on individual factors and the nature of your life transition. Many individuals report feeling a noticeable difference in their stress levels and nervous system regulation within a few weeks of consistent engagement with their personalized plan. Our goal is sustainable change, not quick fixes.
