Frequently Asked Questions

How does Holistic Medicine specifically help athletes manage physical stress?
For athletes, Holistic Medicine targets stress at its root by balancing your nervous system, optimizing recovery pathways, and reducing systemic inflammation. We help you adapt better to training loads, prevent burnout, and improve your body's natural ability to repair and rebuild, translating to more consistent performance and fewer setbacks.
Can Holistic Medicine improve my recovery time after intense training or competition?
Absolutely. Our focus on nervous system regulation and stress reduction directly impacts recovery. By shifting your body into a restorative state more efficiently, we help reduce muscle soreness, improve sleep quality, and enhance cellular repair, allowing you to return to training feeling refreshed and ready sooner.
Is Holistic Medicine compatible with my existing training regimen and sports doctors?
Yes, our Holistic Medicine approach is designed to be integrative and complementary. We work alongside your coaches, physical therapists, and other healthcare providers to ensure a cohesive strategy. Our goal is to enhance your current regimen by addressing underlying imbalances that might be limiting your progress or increasing your susceptibility to stress and injury.
What kind of techniques does Holistic Medicine use to regulate the nervous system?
We utilize a range of techniques, including targeted nutritional interventions, specific botanical remedies, acupuncture, breathwork, and personalized stress reduction practices. Each plan in our Holistic Medicine practice is tailored to your unique needs, aiming to modulate your stress response and promote a balanced, resilient nervous system.
