Myths & Realities of Holistic Care

Myth: Holistic means unscientific

Truth: We integrate research-informed modalities with personalized assessment. Education and measured steps are core to every plan.

Myth: Fixes are fast and guaranteed

Truth: Intensive programs accelerate insight and change, but outcomes depend on many factors. We focus on tools you can sustain.

Myth: It's all supplements

Truth: Supplements can be useful but are only one piece. Lifestyle, sleep, stress, and relationships are often more impactful.
