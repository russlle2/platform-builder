How we partner with you

A predictable, transparent process: intake, personalized plan, regular follow-ups, and optional labs when they add clarity.

Intensive blocks available
Choose a focused timeframe for concentrated change — includes weekly check-ins and actionable milestones.

1) Deep intake

A thorough conversation about medical history, routines, stressors, and goals. We listen for patterns and prioritize what will move the needle.

2) Evidence-informed plan

A practical roadmap: nutrition shifts, sleep and movement adjustments, tools for stress regulation, and selective supplements or botanicals when indicated.

3) Follow-ups & iterative care

Short weekly or biweekly touchpoints to assess responses and refine protocols. Care is adaptive — we track effects and change course when needed.

4) Optional testing & interpretation

Labs are educational tools. When helpful, we recommend targeted tests (metabolic panels, micronutrients, GI testing, hormone snapshots) and translate results into actionable steps.

Myth vs. Expectation

Myth: One supplement or diet will erase a problem overnight.

Reality: We combine small sustainable changes with clarity from testing and monitoring; progress is tracked and celebrated along the way.

Team-based care

We partner with your existing clinicians when needed and provide detailed notes so you’re supported across providers.

Short case vignette

Client C: Recurrent insomnia with daytime brain fog. Approach: sleep window recalibration, daytime activity tweaks, magnesium-based evening routine, and a short diagnostic blood panel. Result: improved sleep efficiency and cognitive clarity after 6 weeks; continued maintenance plan.
