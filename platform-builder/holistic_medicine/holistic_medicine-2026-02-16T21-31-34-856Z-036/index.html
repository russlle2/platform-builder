The plan: personalized, teachable, and trackable

After assessment we co-create a stepwise plan with measurable goals. It may include nutrition, targeted botanicals, movement, sleep hygiene and optional lab follow-ups.

  1. Phase 1 — Stabilize: Basic sleep and digestion adjustments, anti-inflammatory nutrition.
  2. Phase 2 — Rebuild: Focused nutrients, personalized movement and stress resiliency work.
  3. Phase 3 — Thrive: Long-term maintenance, self-monitoring and periodic reassessments.

We never promise cures; we aim for improved function, clarity and sustainable habits tailored to you.

Small habits, real momentum

Gentle, specific changes are easier to keep. Choose one and build for 21 days.

Evening wind-down

30 minutes of low-light time before bed: no screens, warm tea, breathing practice.

Micro-movement

3 short movement breaks (2–5 minutes each) across the day to reduce tension and support digestion.

Protein-forward breakfasts

Start with balanced protein to stabilize energy and reduce mid-morning crashes.

One-minute checks

Pause midday to note stress level on a 1–5 scale. Small awareness often precedes change.
