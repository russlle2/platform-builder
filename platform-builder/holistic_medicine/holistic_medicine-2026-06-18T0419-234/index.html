Frequently Asked Questions

How does emotional healing in Holistic Medicine specifically help athletes manage physical stress?
Emotional healing helps you process the stress, anxiety, or frustration that often accompanies intense physical training, injuries, or performance pressures. By addressing these underlying emotional factors, your body can often recover more efficiently, reduce muscle tension, and improve mental focus, directly impacting your physical performance and well-being.
Is trauma-informed care relevant if I haven't experienced a major physical trauma?
Absolutely. Trauma-informed care recognizes that 'trauma' can be subtle, including cumulative stress from training, performance setbacks, or even chronic pain. Our approach ensures your emotional responses to these experiences are understood and addressed with sensitivity, creating a safer space for deep healing and preventing further emotional or physical strain.
What unique aspects of Holistic Medicine are beneficial for active individuals?
For active individuals, holistic medicine integrates mental and emotional processing with your physical recovery needs. We don't just look at the injury or stressor; we explore how it impacts your identity, motivation, and overall sense of self, offering comprehensive support that goes beyond traditional physical therapy to foster complete resilience.
How quickly can an athlete expect to see results from this type of emotional support?
The timeline for emotional processing varies for everyone. Some athletes report feeling a sense of relief and clarity after just a few sessions, while deeper patterns may take more consistent work. Our goal is to equip you with tools and strategies that foster ongoing emotional regulation and resilience, positively impacting your physical and mental state over time.
