Frequently Asked Questions

How does Holistic Medicine specifically address stress for high-achievers?
Our Holistic Medicine protocols for high-achievers are designed to analyze the specific physiological markers of stress in your system, not just the symptoms. We focus on neuroendocrine balance, mitochondrial health, and nervous system resilience, offering targeted interventions that go beyond general stress management to support your intense professional demands.
Will Holistic Medicine fit into my demanding schedule?
Absolutely. We understand your time is a critical asset. Our Holistic Medicine programs are structured with efficiency in mind, offering streamlined assessment processes and practical, implementable strategies that integrate into your existing routine. The aim is to optimize your well-being without adding further burden to your schedule.
What is the evidence behind your Holistic Medicine methods for nervous system regulation?
Our Holistic Medicine practice is built upon a foundation of current scientific research in psychoneuroimmunology, neurobiology, and stress physiology. We incorporate modalities with documented efficacy in enhancing vagal tone, modulating cortisol response, and improving autonomic nervous system balance. Your care is informed by data, not just intuition.
How quickly can I expect to see improvements in my stress levels with Holistic Medicine?
While individual responses vary, many high-achievers report initial shifts in perceived stress and improved sleep quality within the first few weeks of consistent engagement with our Holistic Medicine protocols. Our goal is sustained, long-term regulation, which is a process, but noticeable relief often begins relatively quickly as your nervous system starts to rebalance.
