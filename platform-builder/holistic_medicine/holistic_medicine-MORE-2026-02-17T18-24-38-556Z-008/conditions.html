Conditions we commonly support

Educational support and practical strategies for everyday life. Our work is person-centered and evidence-aware.

Important: The information here is educational and supportive. It is not a substitute for medical advice. If you have a serious or urgent health concern, please contact a licensed medical professional or emergency services. Our sessions aim to complement, not replace, ongoing medical care.

How we approach conditions

We listen first, then co-design simple practices that can change the day-to-day. That may include movement, sleep adjustments, guided practices, lifestyle coaching, and coordination with other providers when helpful.

Areas of support

Mild–Moderate Anxiety
Breathing anchors, exposure planning, routine building
Persistent Fatigue
Energy pacing, sleep ecology, nutrition-oriented planning
Digestive Upsets
Diet patterns, somatic awareness, referral pathways
Movement & Pain Management
Gentle movement progressions, load management, coping strategies
Stress-Related Sleep Disruption
Bedtime routines, circadian cues, short practices

When to reach out

If you’re looking for a collaborative plan that fits your daily life, or you want a second perspective to integrate with existing care, a short consult can help clarify next steps.
