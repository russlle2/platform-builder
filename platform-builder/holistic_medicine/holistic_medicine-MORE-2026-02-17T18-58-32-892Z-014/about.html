Who we are

An integrative practice blending evidence, lived experience, and thoughtful rhythm-setting. We listen first, then design practical steps you can try between sessions.

Our commitments

  • No overpromises — education and skill-building first
  • Individualized pacing and measurable checkpoints
  • Collaborative records and referrals when helpful
Our approach
We blend short guided interventions with lifestyle framing to co-create pathways you can maintain. It’s practical, iterative, and tailored.

Programs rethought

We frame care as rhythms: short pulses to clarify, series to re-pattern, and intensives when layered work is needed.

Meet the lead

A clinician with broad training in whole-person care, emphasizing physiology, behavior, and contextual supports. Clinical decisions are collaborative and transparent.

Clinical ethos

  1. Start with clarity: what matters most now?
  2. Design for your life’s rhythm
  3. Measure, adapt, and teach skills you keep
