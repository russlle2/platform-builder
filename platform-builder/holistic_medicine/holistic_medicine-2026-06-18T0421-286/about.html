How can Holistic Medicine help my emotional healing after chronic illness or injury?

Our Holistic Medicine approach focuses on the connection between your physical and emotional states. We offer gentle, trauma-informed techniques to help you process the emotional impact of your illness or injury, reduce stress, and cultivate a sense of inner calm, supporting your overall recovery.

I've never tried Holistic Medicine before; is this right for me during my recovery?

Absolutely. Our practice is designed to be accessible and gentle, especially for those new to Holistic Medicine. We understand that recovery from chronic illness or injury is demanding, so we meet you where you are, explaining everything clearly and working at a pace that feels comfortable for you.

What does 'trauma-informed care' mean in the context of Holistic Medicine for my recovery?

Trauma-informed care means we recognize that your experiences with chronic illness or injury may have been deeply impactful. Our Holistic Medicine approach prioritizes creating a safe, trustworthy environment, empowering you with choices, and avoiding any practices that might re-trigger difficult emotions, ensuring your emotional comfort throughout our work together.

Will Holistic Medicine treatments interfere with my current medical treatments for chronic illness or injury?

Our Holistic Medicine services are designed to complement your existing medical care, not replace it. We encourage open communication with your primary healthcare providers. Our focus is on your emotional well-being and resilience, working in harmony with any physical treatments you are undergoing.
