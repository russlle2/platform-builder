Frequently Asked Questions

How does Holistic Medicine specifically address sleep challenges for athletes?
For athletes, sleep isn't just rest; it's a critical recovery period. Our Holistic Medicine approach goes beyond conventional sleep aids by analyzing your training volume, nutritional intake, stress levels, and hormonal balance. We then craft a personalized strategy that may include targeted nutrient support, adaptogenic herbs, and specific evening routines to optimize your body's repair processes and prepare you for peak performance.
I'm always sore and tired. Can improved sleep through Holistic Medicine really help my physical stress?
Absolutely. When you're constantly pushing your body, inadequate sleep can amplify inflammation, impair muscle repair, and elevate stress hormones. Through Holistic Medicine, we work to enhance the deep, restorative stages of sleep, which are crucial for cellular repair, growth hormone release, and reducing cortisol. This directly translates to better recovery from physical stress, reduced soreness, and increased energy.
What kind of commitment is required to see results with your Holistic Medicine sleep program?
Your commitment is key to unlocking your best sleep. While some changes might offer immediate relief, achieving lasting restorative sleep is an ongoing process. We'll work together, typically over several weeks or months, to implement and adjust strategies. Our Holistic Medicine approach emphasizes sustainable habits and understanding your body's signals, empowering you with lifelong tools for optimal rest.
Will Holistic Medicine interfere with my current training schedule or sports nutrition plan?
Our Holistic Medicine approach is designed to complement, not conflict with, your existing athletic regimen. We take your current training schedule, nutrition plan, and any other health professionals you work with into account. Our goal is to enhance your recovery and performance from within, integrating seamlessly with your active lifestyle to support your overall well-being.
