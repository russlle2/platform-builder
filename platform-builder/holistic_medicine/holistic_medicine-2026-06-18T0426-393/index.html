Frequently Asked Questions

What kind of physical pain can Holistic Medicine help me with?
Our gentle Holistic Medicine approach can help with a variety of physical discomforts often experienced in later years, such as chronic back pain, joint stiffness, neuropathy, muscle aches, and even general fatigue that impacts your mobility. We focus on improving your comfort and restoring ease of movement.
How is your Holistic Medicine approach different for seniors?
Our Holistic Medicine practice is specifically designed with seniors in mind. This means our methods are gentle, respectful of your body's current capabilities, and focused on sustainable improvements rather than aggressive interventions. We prioritize your comfort, safety, and individual pace throughout your sessions.
Will Holistic Medicine treatments interfere with my current medications?
No, our Holistic Medicine approaches are carefully designed to complement your existing medical care. will always take a detailed history of your health and medications to ensure that any recommendations or treatments are safe and beneficial for you. We encourage open communication with your primary care physician.
What does 'somatic healing' mean in the context of my physical pain?
Somatic healing in our Holistic Medicine practice means helping you become more aware of your body's sensations and how they relate to your physical pain and emotional well-being. By gently guiding your attention to your body, we can help release old patterns of tension and discomfort, fostering a deeper sense of ease and integration within yourself.
