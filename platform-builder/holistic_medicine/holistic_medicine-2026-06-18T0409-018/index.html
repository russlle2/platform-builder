Frequently Asked Questions

How can Holistic Medicine help me manage stress in retirement?
Our approach focuses on identifying the specific triggers for your stress and implementing gentle, evidence-based techniques to rebalance your nervous system. This can include individualized nutritional guidance, mindful movement, and targeted botanical support, all designed to promote a sustained sense of calm.
Is nervous system regulation relevant for maintaining mental clarity?
Absolutely. A well-regulated nervous system directly contributes to improved focus, memory, and cognitive resilience. By reducing chronic stress and promoting a state of calm, we help create an optimal environment for your brain to function at its best, supporting your mental sharpness.
What does a typical consultation involve at ?
Your first consultation is a comprehensive conversation where we discuss your health history, current concerns, and personal goals for vitality and clarity. We then develop a personalized plan that may include dietary recommendations, lifestyle adjustments, and specific nervous system support techniques, all tailored to your unique needs.
Are your Holistic Medicine methods safe for seniors with existing conditions?
Yes, our methods are carefully selected to be gentle and supportive. We always take your complete medical history and any current medications into account. Our approach is designed to complement your existing care, and we work collaboratively to ensure your safety and well-being throughout your time with us.
