Frequently Asked Questions

Why do I wake up exhausted after a hard game but not a light training day?
Your body processes high-intensity stress differently. We’ll map your workout intensity to your sleep disruption patterns so we can stabilize your recovery window.
Can holistic medicine help if I’ve tried sleep apps and caffeine cuts?
Yes—most apps ignore the physical stress cycle. We address the root cause: how training affects your sleep neurochemistry, not just your bedtime routine.
Do I need to stop training to improve my sleep?
Absolutely not. We’ll adjust your rest protocol around your schedule so you keep training while your sleep rebuilds itself.
How soon will I notice better sleep?
You’ll feel more refreshed within 3 days as your body shifts out of stress mode. Full restorative cycles take 2-3 weeks of consistent protocol.
