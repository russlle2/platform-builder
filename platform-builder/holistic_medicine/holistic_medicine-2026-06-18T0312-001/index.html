Frequently Asked Questions

How does holistic medicine differ from my primary care provider's approach?
Your primary care focuses on symptom management within the medical system, while holistic medicine addresses your whole life context. We explore how stress, nutrition, and environment impact your health—creating a personalized roadmap for lasting wellness that complements conventional care.
What conditions respond best to holistic medicine?
Chronic issues often benefit most: digestive disorders, autoimmune patterns, stress-related fatigue, and hormonal imbalances. We help you identify root causes like nutrient gaps or emotional patterns, not just manage symptoms, so you feel supported in healing at your own pace.
Do you accept insurance or offer payment plans?
We don't bill insurance directly but provide detailed receipts for out-of-network reimbursement. We also offer flexible payment plans—no one should delay care due to financial barriers. Your wellness matters, so we'll work with you to make services accessible.
What should I prepare for my first holistic consultation?
Bring your health history and any current medications/supplements. We'll discuss your daily rhythms, stress patterns, and wellness goals. No need to 'diagnose' yourself—just share what matters to you, and we'll create your plan together.
