Understanding Your Burnout-Related Physical Pain with Holistic Medicine

When you're consistently pushing hard at work, your body often pays the price. That persistent tension in your shoulders, the dull ache in your lower back, or the recurring headaches aren't just 'part of the job.' Here, we look at how the demands of your professional life impact your physical well-being, offering a supportive space to address these connections. You don't need a background in wellness to understand how stress manifests physically; we're here to guide you through it, gently and effectively.
