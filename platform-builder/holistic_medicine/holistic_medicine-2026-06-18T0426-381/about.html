I'm always busy. How can Holistic Medicine help with my burnout and physical pain if I barely have time?

We understand your schedule is packed. Our sessions are designed to be efficient and impactful, focusing on immediate physical relief and simple techniques you can integrate easily into your day. Even short, focused sessions can make a significant difference in releasing the physical tension that comes with burnout, helping you feel more comfortable and less overwhelmed.

I'm new to Holistic Medicine and somatic work. Is this too 'out there' for me?

Absolutely not. Our approach is grounded, gentle, and accessible. We explain everything clearly, ensuring you feel comfortable and understand the process. There's no need for any prior experience or specific beliefs – just an openness to exploring how your body holds stress and how you can gently release it to reduce physical pain. We meet you exactly where you are.

My physical pain feels constant due to work stress. Can Holistic Medicine really make a difference?

Yes, chronic work-related stress often manifests as persistent physical pain like neck stiffness, backaches, or headaches. Our Holistic Medicine approach focuses on releasing the deep tension patterns your body has adopted, not just masking symptoms. By addressing the root of this physical strain, you can experience noticeable and lasting relief, helping you feel more at ease in your body even amidst a demanding work life.

What kind of commitment is involved? I can't add another huge obligation to my life.

We prioritize making this a supportive, rather than demanding, experience for you. We'll discuss a personalized plan that fits your capacity and goals, whether that's a few targeted sessions to address acute pain or a more spaced-out approach for ongoing relief. Our goal is to empower you with tools and comfort, not to add another source of stress to your already busy life.
