Frequently Asked Questions

How does Holistic Medicine specifically help athletes with sleep and recovery?
Holistic Medicine for athletes focuses on understanding the systemic impact of training stress on your sleep and recovery. We look beyond basic sleep hygiene, addressing factors like nutrient deficiencies, stress hormones, nervous system regulation, and mitochondrial function that directly affect your ability to get truly restorative sleep and recover effectively from physical exertion.
I'm already focused on nutrition; how is your approach different for sleep improvement?
While nutrition is crucial, our Holistic Medicine approach goes deeper into how specific macronutrients, micronutrients, and even meal timing impact your circadian rhythm and sleep-wake cycles, especially under high physical stress. We might identify subtle imbalances or inflammation that are disrupting your sleep, even with a seemingly healthy diet, and provide targeted interventions for deep rest.
Will this interfere with my existing training or medical regimen?
Our Holistic Medicine approach is designed to be complementary. We carefully assess your current training schedule, dietary intake, and any existing medical protocols. Our goal is to enhance your recovery and sleep without disrupting your performance goals or prescribed treatments, always prioritizing your safety and well-being. We work to integrate seamlessly with your existing routine.
What kind of results can I expect from optimizing my sleep with Holistic Medicine?
By optimizing your sleep and restorative processes through our Holistic Medicine strategies, you can expect improved energy levels, faster physical recovery, reduced muscle soreness, enhanced mental clarity and focus, and a greater capacity to handle training stress. Ultimately, this translates to more consistent performance and a reduced risk of burnout or injury, allowing you to sustain your active lifestyle.
