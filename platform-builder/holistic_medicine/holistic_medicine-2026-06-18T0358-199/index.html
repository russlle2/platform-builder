Frequently Asked Questions

How is this different from 'general' stress management?
We don't ask you to 'relax harder.' We identify specific trauma responses from overwork (like hypervigilance during deadlines) and build tools that fit around your actual schedule—not another thing to add.
I'm too tired to even think about this. Where do we start?
Your first session is just noticing where you're holding stress right now—your jaw, shoulders, or that tightness during Monday meetings. We work with what you have available, not what you 'should' be able to do.
Will you tell me to quit my job?
No. We help you navigate your current role with more clarity. You'll learn to recognize when to say 'no' without guilt, and how to request changes that actually work for your energy—like meeting times or inbox limits.
How soon will I feel different?
You'll notice shifts within weeks: less physical tension during work, clearer boundaries with colleagues, and the freedom to say 'I need a break' without shame. This isn't about fixing but unloading what you've carried alone.
