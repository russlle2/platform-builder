Frequently Asked Questions

I'm a college student with a crazy schedule; how can Holistic Medicine realistically help my sleep?
We understand the demands of college life. Our approach focuses on integrating small, sustainable changes into your routine. We'll explore practical strategies, rooted in ancient wisdom, that fit your schedule and help you optimize the sleep you do get, rather than adding more pressure.
Is this just about 'relaxing more,' or is there a concrete method to your Holistic Medicine approach for sleep?
It's much more than just 'relaxing.' Our method involves a deep dive into your unique sleep patterns, academic stressors, and anxiety triggers. We then use evidence-informed holistic practices like specific herbal support, guided relaxation techniques, and lifestyle adjustments to address the root causes of your sleep disruptions.
I've tried everything for my sleep, from apps to medications. What makes your Holistic Medicine different?
Unlike superficial solutions, our Holistic Medicine approach looks at your entire being – mind, body, and spirit. We don't just mask symptoms; we work to re-establish your body's innate ability to rest and restore itself, understanding that true sleep health comes from deep balance, not just external interventions.
Will I have to drastically change my diet or lifestyle to see results with Holistic Medicine?
We believe in gradual, manageable shifts. While nutrition and lifestyle play a role, we'll work with you to identify areas where small adjustments can make a big impact on your sleep without overwhelming your already busy college life. It's about empowering you with sustainable choices.
