Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is 'Holistic Medicine' for emotional healing?
Holistic Medicine, for your emotional well-being, means we look at you as a whole person, not just your feelings in isolation. We consider how your emotions connect with your body, mind, and spirit. It's about finding gentle ways to bring balance and peace to all parts of your experience, helping you feel more complete and at ease.
I've never done anything like this before. Is this Holistic Medicine approach suitable for me?
Absolutely. Our Holistic Medicine practice is specifically designed for individuals like you, who may not have prior wellness experience. We use an approachable, conversational style and gentle techniques, ensuring you feel comfortable and understood every step of the way. There's no expectation for you to know anything beforehand; just come as you are.
How can Holistic Medicine help with feelings from past experiences or difficult times?
Our trauma-informed Holistic Medicine approach helps you gently process lingering feelings from past experiences. We create a safe space to acknowledge these emotions without judgment. Through compassionate listening and supportive techniques, we aim to help you find a sense of release and peace, allowing you to move forward with less emotional weight.
What should I expect during my first visit for Holistic Medicine?
Your first visit with our Holistic Medicine practice will be a relaxed conversation. We'll chat about what brings you in and what you hope to gain. It's a chance for us to get to know each other, for you to ask questions, and for us to understand how we can best support your emotional well-being. There's no pressure, just a warm welcome and an open ear.
