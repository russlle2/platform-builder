Micro-habits: small daily choices that add up

Tiny, repeatable actions reduce friction and build momentum. These are science-friendly habits you can actually keep.

Wake window: Aim for a consistent 20-minute morning routine to set tone for the day.
Bite balance: Add a simple protein + vegetable to one meal to stabilize energy.
Evening wind-down: 30 minutes of low-light activity to cue restful sleep.
Breathe: Two rounds of 4–6 breath cycles mid-day to reset stress reactivity.

These suggestions are educational. They are not medical guarantees. Individual results vary.
