A clear, compassionate process for intensive care

We prioritize curiosity, measurement and practical steps. Below is how a typical intensive unfolds. You can opt into testing and deeper labs, or begin with simple, well-targeted lifestyle shifts.

1) Intake & Story

A deep initial conversation: medical history, symptom timeline, sleep patterns, diet, stressors, movement and priorities. This forms the scaffold for all next steps.

2) Educational Diagnosis

We translate your history into actionable ideas — not labels. Expect handouts, plain-language explanations and clear priorities for the first 2–6 weeks.

3) Tailored Plan

Nutrition templates, micro-habit prescriptions, supportive botanicals where appropriate, and recommended movement or breathwork. Plans are practical and adapt to real life.

4) Follow-ups & Iteration

Regular follow-ups track symptoms, labs and function. We iterate quickly: keep what helps, stop what doesn’t, and fine tune dosing and timing.

Optional testing — an educational view

Testing is offered to illuminate biology, not to replace clinical judgment. Typical options discussed in an intensive:

Basic blood panel: Thyroid, CBC, metabolic markers. Useful for safety and broad context.
Functional labs: Stool microbiome, breath testing, specialized hormone profiles. These are chosen selectively and discussed as tools, not definitive answers.
Biomarker tracking: When appropriate we track objective measures (sleep, activity, labs) to guide decisions and reduce guesswork.

We never require testing; it’s offered when it meaningfully changes care. All options are educational and framed to respect your values and budget.
