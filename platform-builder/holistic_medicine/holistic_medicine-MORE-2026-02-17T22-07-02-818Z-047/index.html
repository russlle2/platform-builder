    A three-phase planner

    A staged roadmap to move from stabilization to longer-term resilience. Educational, customizable, and revisited often.

    Phase 1
    Stabilize

    Focus on immediate regulation: sleep rhythm, hydration, and a 2-week micro-habit set to reduce daily load.

    • Simple sleep window and wind-down cues
    • Daily 5-minute breath and grounding practice
    • One predictable protein-rich meal
    Educational guidance only; not a substitute for clinical advice.
    Phase 2
    Build Capacity

    Introduce structured routines and functional testing as appropriate. Focus on movement habit and digestive rhythm over 6–12 weeks.

    • Short strength or mobility sessions 3x/week
    • Meal timing and simple gut-support strategies
    • Stress-smart scheduling and energy mapping
    This is educational planning; clinical decisions require a consult.
    Phase 3
    Sustain & Thrive

    Layer in longer-term supports and preventive practices, tailored to your goals over months. Emphasize sustainability and community supports.

    • Quarterly planning reviews
    • Community sessions and skill workshops
    • Maintenance micro-habit library
    Not personalized diagnosis. Adjust with your clinician as needed.
    Every plan is individualized. We recommend discussing specifics during your first membership meeting.
