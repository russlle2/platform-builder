Frequently Asked Questions

How does Holistic Medicine specifically help with chronic illness or injury recovery?
Our Holistic Medicine approach focuses on regulating your nervous system, which often becomes dysregulated during chronic illness or injury. By gently addressing stress and promoting internal balance, we help your body and mind conserve energy for recovery and reduce the overwhelming sensations you might be experiencing. We aim to ease the burden on your system, allowing for a more harmonious healing environment.
What kind of techniques will you use to help me reduce stress and regulate my nervous system?
We use a variety of gentle, personalized Holistic Medicine techniques. These might include specific breathing practices, mindful movement, guided relaxation, and other subtle interventions designed to calm your central nervous system. Our focus is always on what feels safe and supportive for your unique recovery journey, never pushing your body beyond its current capacity.
Is this approach suitable if I'm feeling very sensitive or easily overwhelmed?
Absolutely. Our Holistic Medicine practice is built on the understanding that individuals recovering from chronic illness or injury often have heightened sensitivities. We prioritize a very gentle, paced approach, ensuring that every session respects your current capacity and comfort levels. Your well-being and sense of safety are our top priorities, and we'll always move at a pace that feels right for you.
How long does it take to see results with Holistic Medicine for stress reduction?
The timeline for experiencing shifts varies for everyone, as recovery from chronic illness or injury is deeply personal. However, many of our clients report feeling a greater sense of calm and improved ability to manage stress within a few sessions. Our consistent support helps build resilience in your nervous system over time, leading to more sustained feelings of ease and well-being as you continue your recovery.
