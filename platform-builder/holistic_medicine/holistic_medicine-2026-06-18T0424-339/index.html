Frequently Asked Questions

How does Holistic Medicine address the specific pains parents experience?
The physical demands of parenting, from lifting children to hours of feeding or comforting, can create unique strain. Our Holistic Medicine approach focuses on releasing compensatory patterns and strengthening foundational movements, directly addressing the root causes of your back pain, wrist strain, or neck stiffness, rather than just masking symptoms.
I'm always short on time. How can I fit Holistic Medicine into my busy schedule?
We understand your schedule is tight. Our sessions are designed to be efficient yet effective. We provide you with simple, actionable practices you can integrate into your day, empowering you to maintain progress between appointments. Our goal is to give you tools that fit your life, not add another burden.
Is somatic healing suitable for long-term stress and burnout from parenting?
Absolutely. Somatic healing within Holistic Medicine helps you reconnect with your body's signals, often dulled by chronic stress. By gently addressing stored tension and emotional imprints in your tissues, you'll learn to regulate your nervous system, fostering a deeper sense of calm and resilience against burnout.
What if my pain feels more emotional than physical?
It's common for emotional stress to manifest as physical discomfort. Our Holistic Medicine framework acknowledges this mind-body connection. We provide a safe space to explore how your emotional landscape influences your physical sensations, offering techniques that help release both emotional and physical tension, promoting comprehensive somatic healing.
