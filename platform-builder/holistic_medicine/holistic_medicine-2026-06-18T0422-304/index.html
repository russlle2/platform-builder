Frequently Asked Questions

How can Holistic Medicine specifically help with my athletic pain and stress?
Holistic Medicine at focuses on the interconnectedness of your body. Instead of just masking pain, we investigate underlying imbalances, nervous system dysregulation, or movement patterns contributing to your discomfort. We aim to reduce inflammation, improve recovery, and enhance your body's natural healing capacity, helping you manage physical stress more effectively and return to your activities stronger.
What kind of techniques does use for active individuals?
We utilize a personalized blend of techniques tailored to your specific needs as an active person. This might include targeted bodywork to release tension, somatic practices to improve body awareness and nervous system regulation, nutritional guidance for optimal recovery, or mindful movement to support injury prevention. Our approach is always collaborative and designed to integrate into your existing training.
Will Holistic Medicine interfere with my current training or medical treatments?
Our goal is to complement your existing routine, not disrupt it. We work with you to ensure our Holistic Medicine strategies support your training goals and integrate seamlessly with any other medical care you might be receiving. Open communication about your full health picture allows us to create a safe and effective plan that enhances your overall well-being and performance.
How long until I feel relief from physical stress or pain?
The timeline for relief varies depending on the nature and duration of your physical stress or pain. Many clients report feeling a difference after just a few sessions as their body begins to respond to targeted care. Our focus is on sustainable improvement, equipping you with tools for long-term self-management, so you can maintain your active lifestyle with greater ease and less discomfort.
