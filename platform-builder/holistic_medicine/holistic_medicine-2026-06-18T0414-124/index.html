Frequently Asked Questions

How does Holistic Medicine specifically help athletes improve sleep and recover from intense physical stress?
For athletes, Holistic Medicine delves into your unique physiological responses to training. We utilize practices that calm your nervous system, reduce inflammation, and balance energy, all of which are crucial for transitioning into deep, restorative sleep and accelerating your body's repair processes after strenuous activity.
I feel wired after workouts, even hours later. Can Holistic Medicine address this specific challenge for my sleep?
Absolutely. That 'wired' feeling is often your sympathetic nervous system still highly active. Our Holistic Medicine methods specifically target this overstimulation, helping your body gently downshift. We focus on techniques that encourage parasympathetic dominance, allowing your system to naturally prepare for sleep and reduce nighttime waking.
Is the approach suitable for all types of athletes, regardless of their sport or training intensity?
Yes, our Holistic Medicine approach is highly adaptable. While the specific modalities might vary, the core principles of reducing physical stress, balancing your system, and enhancing natural recovery apply to any athlete, from endurance runners to powerlifters. We tailor your plan to your sport's demands and your body's responses.
What kind of commitment is required to see improvements in sleep and recovery with Holistic Medicine?
Commitment to your well-being is key. We typically start with a focused series of sessions to establish foundational changes. Consistency with the personalized Holistic Medicine practices recommends for home use is also vital. Most athletes notice significant shifts in sleep quality and recovery within a few weeks of dedicated engagement.
