Our four-part process

1. Intake

Comprehensive 90–120 minute visit to gather history, priorities, and current meds/supplements.

2. Hypothesis & Plan

We form a working hypothesis and co-create a stepwise plan focused on education and measurable changes.

3. Implementation Support

Coaching, recipes, movement prompts, and gentle botanicals as appropriate — we prioritize safety and interactions.

4. Follow-up & Iterate

Regular check-ins to review progress, adjust, and decide if optional labs or referrals are warranted.

About optional labs (educational)

We may recommend targeted labs to illuminate patterns. Labs are presented as educational tools — we review results with context and avoid over-testing.

Functional metabolic panels (educational)
Comprehensive stool analysis (selective use)
Hormone snapshots (timed, when relevant)
Nutritional biomarkers (iron, B12, vitamin D)

What you can expect in the intake

  • Detailed timeline of symptoms, previous treatments, and lifestyle.
  • Medication and supplement review for safety.
  • Clear shared goals and an initial set of small experiments.

Follow-ups & coaching

Follow-ups are focused and practical. We use data, observations, and your feedback to refine the plan — typically weekly or biweekly during an intensive phase.
