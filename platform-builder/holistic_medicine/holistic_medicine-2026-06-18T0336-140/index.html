Frequently Asked Questions

How does Holistic Medicine specifically address sleep issues during a life transition?
When you're navigating significant changes, your body's stress response can overwhelm your natural sleep mechanisms. Our Holistic Medicine approach looks at how these transitions impact your nervous system, hormones, and daily rhythms. We then tailor interventions to rebalance these systems, helping your body and mind adapt more smoothly to change while prioritizing deep rest.
Is this approach suitable if my sleep problems are primarily stress-related due to my life changes?
Absolutely. Stress is a primary disruptor of sleep, especially during periods of transition. Our methods are specifically designed to calm the nervous system, reduce physiological stress responses, and create conditions conducive to sleep, even when your external circumstances are demanding. We focus on building your resilience to stress through targeted, science-backed strategies.
What kind of commitment is required to see results with this sleep-focused Holistic Medicine?
Improving sleep is a process that requires consistent effort. We'll work together to implement manageable, step-by-step changes into your routine. While individual results vary, clients typically begin to experience noticeable improvements in sleep quality and daytime energy within a few weeks of consistent application of their personalized plan.
Will I need to take medications with this Holistic Medicine approach for sleep?
Our Holistic Medicine approach prioritizes natural, evidence-based methods to support your sleep, aiming to minimize or avoid reliance on medications. We focus on identifying and addressing underlying imbalances through lifestyle adjustments, nutritional support, and stress management techniques. If you are currently on medication, we will work collaboratively with your prescribing physician.
