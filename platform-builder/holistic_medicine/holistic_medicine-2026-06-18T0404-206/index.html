Frequently Asked Questions

Will this interfere with my current medications or health routine?
No—your care is fully coordinated. We collaborate with your doctors to ensure gentle, complementary support. You’ll never need to stop your current treatments.
How quickly will I notice calmer days?
You’ll feel a subtle shift in your first few sessions. With consistent support, many notice improved focus and reduced tension within 4-6 sessions—no intense ‘work,’ just gentle, sustainable progress.
Do you specialize in older adults' trauma?
Yes. We address lifetime patterns, not just recent stress. Whether it’s early life experiences or later challenges like loss, we honor your entire story without retraumatizing you.
How are sessions different from therapy?
We focus on the body’s natural wisdom for emotional release—no deep dives into past trauma. Using safe, grounding techniques, you’ll learn to release tension directly, not through discussion alone.
