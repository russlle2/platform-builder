How a personalized plan unfolds

1. Intake & story

We gather your health history, lifestyle context, and priorities through conversation and targeted questionnaires.

2. Integrative formulation

Combines nutrition, movement, sleep strategies, targeted supplements, mind-body tools, and when helpful, optional lab testing for clarity. Labs are educational options — not required for every plan.

3. Follow-ups & adaptations

Short check-ins and periodic reviews keep the plan practical and evolving. Education is central: we teach skills you can use independently.

Tiny habits, measurable returns

Small, repeatable practices stack into reliable change. Try one this week.

  • Night anchor: 10 minutes of screen-free wind-down and a consistent bedtime.
  • Two-minute breath: 2 slow diaphragmatic breaths three times a day to reset stress physiology.
  • Fiber first: Add one serving of whole fiber to a daily meal to support digestion.
  • Light first: 5 minutes of natural light exposure within an hour of waking to support circadian rhythm.

These are educational suggestions. They complement care, not replace personalized medical advice.
