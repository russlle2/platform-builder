Intake & early plan — what happens next

If you request an intake, we will:

  1. Review the contact note before meeting to focus our time.
  2. Begin a whole-person intake: sleep, digestion, movement, stress, relationships, and medication context.
  3. Co-create a gentle plan that includes education: micro-habits, targeted nutrition or lifestyle suggestions, and follow-up timing.
  4. Offer optional labs or referrals as educational tools; these are discussed case-by-case.

Micro-habits: small shifts

Tiny, repeatable actions often matter more than big changes. Try one for two weeks and notice.

  • 30 seconds of diaphragmatic breathing on waking.
  • Add one vegetable at lunch and dinner.
  • Walk 6 minutes after a meal to support digestion.
  • Set a 30-minute wind-down before sleep (screen-free).

When to reach out sooner

Contact us if symptoms intensify, new worrying symptoms develop, or mood/energy shifts significantly. This page is not a substitute for emergency care.
