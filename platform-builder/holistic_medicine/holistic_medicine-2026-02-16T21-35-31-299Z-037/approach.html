Our philosophy

An integrative path centered on education and partnership

We meet you where you are, review your story, and build a stepwise plan emphasizing safety, measurable small changes, and durable habits. Nothing is forced — options are discussed and consented.

Intake

Deep listening: history, priorities, and context. We use questionnaires as a starting point.

Diagnostic reasoning

Synthesis of symptoms, patterns, and basic labs when relevant; an educational review of findings.

Plan

Specific, time-bound goals plus micro-habits and resources to try in a defined interval.

Follow-ups

Iterative support to refine the plan and address barriers as they arise.

Assessments & testing (educational)

We prioritize non-invasive assessments: thorough history, symptom mapping, and lifestyle review. Labs are optional and discussed transparently: we explain why a test might help, its limits, and costs.

Common optional labs
  • Basic metabolic markers (when clinically indicated)
  • Targeted nutritional markers (B12, vitamin D, iron panels)
  • Selected inflammatory or endocrine studies as education tools

What a typical plan looks like

Plans combine behavior nudges, sleep and stress strategies, and modest dietary shifts. Interventions are chosen for feasibility and monitored over weeks. Supplements or referrals are only added when clearly helpful.

Example timeline

  1. Week 0 — Intake & goal-setting
  2. Weeks 1–4 — Try micro-habit interventions and track
  3. Week 6 — Follow-up to measure progress and adjust

Follow-ups and support

Follow-ups can be brief and focused. We use outcome-focused measures and adapt with an aim toward independence: fewer interventions as resilience improves.

Micro-habit examples
  • Two-minute morning movement + hydration
  • Evening wind-down: low-light and a brief breathing routine
  • Digestive rhythm: small post-meal stroll or gentle belly massage
