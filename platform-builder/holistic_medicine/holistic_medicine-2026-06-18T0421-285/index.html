Frequently Asked Questions

Holistic Medicine: Is this a replacement for therapy or medication?
No, our Holistic Medicine services are complementary. We work alongside any existing care you might be receiving, focusing on natural, gentle ways to support your emotional well-being. We can help you build coping skills and reduce stress, but we always recommend discussing your full care plan with your medical doctor or therapist.
I'm a busy college student. How can I fit Holistic Medicine into my schedule?
We get it – your time is precious. Our Holistic Medicine sessions are designed to be efficient and impactful. We focus on teaching you practical strategies you can integrate into your daily routine. We also offer flexible scheduling options to accommodate your academic commitments, making it easier to prioritize your mental health.
What if I've never tried Holistic Medicine or anything like this before?
That's perfectly fine! Many college students come to us with no prior experience in Holistic Medicine. Our approach is gentle and accessible, and we'll guide you through everything step by step. There's no right or wrong way to feel, and we're here to meet you exactly where you are, making sure you feel comfortable and understood.
How does Holistic Medicine specifically help with academic pressure and anxiety?
Our Holistic Medicine methods address the root causes of academic pressure and anxiety by teaching you to regulate your nervous system, manage overwhelming thoughts, and build emotional resilience. We help you develop strategies to stay grounded during exams, reduce procrastination, and cultivate a calmer mindset so you can perform your best without burning out.
