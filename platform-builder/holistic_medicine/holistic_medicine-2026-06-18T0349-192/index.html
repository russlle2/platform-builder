Frequently Asked Questions

How does sleep timing actually affect my next training session?
Your body repairs muscles during deep sleep (stages 3-4). If you train hard at 7 PM but your sleep cycle doesn’t hit deep sleep until 1 AM, you’ll miss the critical growth hormone window. We time your wind-down so deep sleep aligns with your recovery needs.
Do you work with athletes who travel across time zones?
Absolutely. We adjust your circadian rhythm using light exposure and strategic naps—no more arriving at races feeling like you’re still on a different continent’s clock. Your sleep architecture gets recalibrated for your new schedule.
What if my sleep issues started after I increased my training load?
That’s common. Overtraining disrupts sleep architecture. We analyze your training logs against sleep data to pinpoint the exact stress threshold. It’s not about sleeping more—it’s about sleeping smarter for your current volume.
How quickly can I expect to see better rest?
Most athletes notice improved sleep onset and deeper rest within 2 weeks. We track via your fitness tracker (optional) to show concrete changes in deep sleep duration and next-day recovery scores—no guessing.
