Frequently Asked Questions

What specifically does Holistic Medicine offer for postpartum emotional recovery?
Our Holistic Medicine approach provides a safe, non-judgmental space to process the intense emotions that can arise after childbirth. We focus on natural, gentle modalities to support your nervous system, help you ground your feelings, and develop coping strategies that honor your unique experience as a new mother.
How is your care trauma-informed for new mothers?
Being trauma-informed means we understand that past experiences, including birth itself, can impact your emotional well-being. We prioritize creating a secure environment, offering choices, and ensuring our interactions are always respectful of your boundaries and emotional state. Your comfort and sense of control are paramount in every session.
Will I have to talk about difficult birth experiences if I don't want to?
You are always in control of what you share. Our focus is on supporting your emotional regulation and healing in the present. While processing difficult experiences can be part of healing, it is never forced. We meet you exactly where you are, offering tools and support at your own pace, creating a space where you feel safe to simply be.
What does 'nature-inspired' Holistic Medicine mean for my emotional healing?
Nature-inspired care means we look to the wisdom of the earth for guidance in your healing. This could involve practices that encourage grounding, connection to your natural rhythms, or using elements like breathwork, sensory experiences, and mindful awareness to cultivate a sense of peace and resilience, mirroring the cyclical strength found in nature.
