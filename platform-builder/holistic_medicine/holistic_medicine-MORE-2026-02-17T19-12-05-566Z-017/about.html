How we work

Discover

Listen to history, current goals, and immediate constraints. Identify three testable priorities.

Design

Pick small, measurable changes. Draft a short experiment and specify what to track.

Review

Iterate based on data. Adjust scope and align next steps with life demands.

Coordinate

We connect to other providers and resources as requested.

Values and limits

We emphasize clear boundaries: educational support, shared decision-making, and careful documentation. We do not make guarantees about outcomes; results depend on many factors.

Clinical safeguards
  • Medication safety checks
  • Referral for urgent concerns
  • Transparent documentation for other clinicians
