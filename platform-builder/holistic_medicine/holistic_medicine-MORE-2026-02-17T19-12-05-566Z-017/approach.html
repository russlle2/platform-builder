How we structure care

Our practice applies a layered workflow: concise assessment, targeted interventions, then sustainable rhythms. Each layer is documented and reversible — it's about learning what works for you.

Short, testable tactics: movement, sleep, micro-habits, and targeted tools chosen for low friction and measurable effect.
Monitor & Adapt
Regular check-ins and simple tracking to refine the plan. We favor pragmatic metrics over complex dashboards.
