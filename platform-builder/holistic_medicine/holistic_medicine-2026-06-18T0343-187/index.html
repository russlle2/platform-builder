Frequently Asked Questions

What if my baby/nursery isn't quiet at bedtime?
We work with your actual nighttime reality—not a quiet, empty home. We build sleep strategies around your current schedule, including how to get rest during naps or when your partner is home.
Do I need to give up coffee to sleep better?
No—your coffee habit is part of your life. We help you schedule it so it supports your natural rhythms instead of disrupting them, keeping you feeling energized without relying on caffeine.
How is this different from a sleep coach?
We look at your whole picture: stress levels, nutrition, and daily rhythms that impact sleep. It's not just 'sleep training'—it's holistic medicine for your sleep health, designed for the unique stressors of parenting young kids.
Can this work if I'm up with a newborn?
Absolutely. For new parents, we focus on short, effective practices during your baby's awake times—like 5-minute breathwork while they nap or adjusting your evening meals to support rest when you can finally sit down.
