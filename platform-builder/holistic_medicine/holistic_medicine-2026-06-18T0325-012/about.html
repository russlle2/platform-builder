Why Your Nervous System Needs Quiet Care Right Now

After birth, your body is still processing hormonal shifts and exhaustion. The stress response (fight-or-flight) can stay activated, making rest feel impossible. We help calm this overdrive through gentle, evidence-backed techniques—so you can feel present with your baby without constant tension.

How soon after birth can I start nervous system support?

You can begin as early as 2 weeks postpartum, once your body has stabilized from delivery. We adjust techniques to your current recovery stage—no pressure to 'be ready.'

What if I can't sit still for 20 minutes?

Your sessions are designed around your baby's schedule. We use micro-practices (5-10 minutes) you can integrate during feedings or quiet time—no extra time needed.

Will this make me feel 'calmer' in a way I can't control?

No. We teach you specific tools to recognize and shift your body’s stress response. You’ll notice fewer headaches, less irritability, and more patience with your baby—without losing yourself in the process.

Do you use herbal supplements?

We prioritize non-pharmacological approaches first. When supplements are needed, we partner with a trusted naturopath who works with postpartum safety parameters.
