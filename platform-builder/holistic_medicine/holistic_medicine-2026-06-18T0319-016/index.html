Frequently Asked Questions

How does Holistic Medicine specifically help with chronic illness or injury recovery?
Our Holistic Medicine approach focuses on modulating the physiological stress response common in chronic conditions. By reducing systemic stress and improving nervous system regulation, we aim to enhance your body's intrinsic capacity for healing and improve overall resilience during recovery.
What kind of stress reduction techniques do you use?
We employ a range of evidence-informed techniques tailored to your individual needs. These may include specific breathing practices, mindful body awareness, and gentle movement, all designed to downregulate an overactive nervous system and promote a state of calm conducive to recovery.
Is this approach compatible with my existing medical treatments?
Absolutely. Our Holistic Medicine services are designed to complement your current medical care. We operate with a clear understanding of your overall treatment plan, ensuring our interventions support rather than interfere with your ongoing medical management for chronic illness or injury.
How quickly can I expect to feel a difference in my nervous system regulation?
The timeline for experiencing changes varies for each individual, especially with chronic conditions. Our focus is on sustainable progress. While some individuals report subtle shifts quickly, the most significant benefits typically emerge through consistent practice and dedicated engagement with the strategies we provide, leading to more stable nervous system function over time.
