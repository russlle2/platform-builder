How can Holistic Medicine specifically address my postpartum back pain?

Postpartum back pain often stems from core weakness, pelvic instability, and postural changes. Our Holistic Medicine approach assesses these factors, utilizing gentle somatic techniques to release tension in surrounding muscles, re-educate core engagement, and restore spinal alignment, offering you targeted relief beyond just symptomatic treatment.

Is somatic healing relevant if my pain feels purely physical?

Absolutely. Physical pain, especially after the profound experience of childbirth, often has a somatic component. Somatic healing in Holistic Medicine helps you understand the connection between your body's physical sensations and your nervous system's response, allowing for deeper release of chronic tension and a more complete resolution of your physical discomfort.

What makes your Holistic Medicine approach different for new mothers?

Our Holistic Medicine practice is unique because we specialize in the postpartum period, recognizing the specific physiological and emotional demands on your body. We don't just treat symptoms; we empower you with knowledge and techniques to understand your body's changes, manage pain, and build resilience for the long term, focusing on your unique recovery.

How quickly can I expect to see improvements with Holistic Medicine for my postpartum recovery?

Every mother's recovery timeline is unique. With our Holistic Medicine approach, you can often feel subtle shifts and increased comfort within a few sessions, as we begin to address underlying tensions and imbalances. Sustained improvement comes with consistent engagement in the practices and insights we share, supporting your body's gradual and profound healing process.
