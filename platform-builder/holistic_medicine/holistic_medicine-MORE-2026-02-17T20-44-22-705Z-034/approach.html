Our working method

We combine careful listening with measurable experiments — small changes you can try, observe, and adapt. The goal is clearer decisions, less confusion, and tangible improvements people notice in daily life.

A clear process

1
Mapping

We build a concise map of your sleep, stress, appetite, movement, and routines — not to label, but to find leverage points.

2
Targeted tests & review

When useful, we review labs and prior notes. Tests are suggested selectively and in conversation.

3
Practical experiments

Plans focus on one to three measurable changes and a simple tracking approach so we can tell what’s working.

4
Refine & scale

We iterate based on data and lived experience, adding supports like guided cycles or coaching when helpful.

Useful to know

Visits are conversational. Expect practical steps, not long lectures. We aim for plans you can try for 2–6 weeks before reassessing.

Stories of small shifts

Clients often report that one or two consistent adjustments — such as a lightly earlier bedtime, a modest reduction in evening screen time, or a 10-minute morning sequence — made daily life easier to manage. These changes compound when tracked and refined.
