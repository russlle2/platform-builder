Frequently Asked Questions

How does Holistic Medicine specifically help athletes improve sleep?
For athletes, Holistic Medicine addresses sleep by focusing on nervous system regulation, reducing exercise-induced inflammation, and balancing stress hormones. We use practices like targeted botanical support, acupuncture, and personalized relaxation techniques to ensure your body can truly switch into restorative sleep, accelerating physical and mental recovery.
I'm always sore and fatigued. Can Holistic Medicine improve my restorative rest?
Absolutely. Chronic soreness and fatigue are often indicators that your body isn't fully recovering. Holistic Medicine approaches restorative rest by improving circulation, reducing systemic inflammation, and supporting cellular repair mechanisms during sleep. This helps your body naturally flush out metabolic waste and rebuild tissues more effectively.
What unique approaches does your Holistic Medicine practice use for sleep improvement?
Our Holistic Medicine practice integrates unique approaches such as traditional Eastern diagnostic methods to identify specific imbalances, alongside Western functional medicine insights into neurochemistry and circadian rhythms. This allows us to create a truly comprehensive plan that goes beyond conventional sleep advice, tailored to your active lifestyle and physical demands.
Will these Holistic Medicine strategies fit into my demanding training schedule?
Yes, we understand your schedule is rigorous. Our Holistic Medicine strategies are designed to integrate seamlessly into your routine. Many recommendations involve small, impactful adjustments to your evening rituals, nutrition, or quick, targeted therapies like specific breathwork or acupressure, maximizing recovery without adding significant time burdens.
