Frequently Asked Questions

How does Holistic Medicine specifically help with maintaining mental clarity as I get older?
Holistic Medicine at Sage Garden looks beyond isolated symptoms. We consider your entire lifestyle – from diet and sleep patterns to stress management and social engagement – to develop a personalized plan that supports optimal brain function and helps you sustain your mental sharpness and focus.
I'm concerned about occasional forgetfulness; can your approach with Holistic Medicine address this?
It's common to notice changes as we age, and our approach is designed to support your cognitive function proactively. We’ll explore various factors that contribute to mental acuity and provide you with practical, gentle strategies through Holistic Medicine to help you feel more confident in your memory and recall.
What kind of commitment is involved in working with Sage Garden Holistic Medicine?
Our goal is to integrate cognitive wellness into your life seamlessly. We start with a comprehensive discussion to understand your needs and then create a realistic, personalized plan. The commitment involves incorporating easy-to-follow recommendations into your daily routine, supported by regular check-ins to ensure you feel empowered and on track.
Will I need to take a lot of supplements or follow a strict diet with your Holistic Medicine recommendations?
Our approach is nurturing and tailored to you. While nutritional support can be beneficial, we don't believe in rigid rules. We focus on sustainable, gentle adjustments to your diet and lifestyle, recommending supplements only when truly indicated and always explaining their purpose, ensuring your comfort and understanding.
