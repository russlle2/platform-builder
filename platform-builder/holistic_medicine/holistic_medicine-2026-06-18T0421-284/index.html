Frequently Asked Questions

How can Holistic Medicine help me manage the emotional stress of my athletic pursuits?
Our Holistic Medicine approach focuses on the mind-body connection. We use gentle, trauma-informed techniques to help you process the emotional impact of training, injuries, or performance pressure, supporting your nervous system and fostering emotional resilience so you can feel more balanced.
I'm new to holistic practices. Is this still right for me as an active person?
Absolutely! We specialize in making Holistic Medicine accessible. You don't need any prior experience. Our approach is gentle and conversational, designed to meet you exactly where you are and provide support that feels comfortable and understandable for your unique needs as an active individual.
What does 'trauma-informed care' mean in the context of Holistic Medicine for athletes?
For athletes and active people, trauma-informed care means we understand that intense physical experiences or setbacks can have emotional imprints. We create a safe, supportive space where your comfort and agency are prioritized, helping you address stress and emotional discomfort without re-triggering past experiences, all within a Holistic Medicine framework.
Will Holistic Medicine interfere with my training or recovery protocols?
Not at all. Our Holistic Medicine approach is designed to complement your existing training and recovery. We focus on emotional regulation, stress reduction, and nervous system support, which can enhance your body's natural healing processes and improve your overall well-being, helping you perform and recover more effectively.
