Frequently Asked Questions

I've never tried anything like Holistic Medicine before. Is it for me?
Absolutely! Many college students we work with are new to these approaches. We meet you where you are, explaining everything in an easy-to-understand way. There's no prior experience or special knowledge required, just a willingness to explore new ways to feel better.
How can Holistic Medicine help with my academic anxiety specifically?
We focus on practical strategies that directly impact how your body and mind respond to academic pressure. This includes techniques to reduce 'fight or flight' responses before exams, improve focus during studying, and establish routines that support better sleep and less stress, all of which contribute to clearer thinking and better performance.
I'm on a tight student budget. Are your Holistic Medicine services affordable?
We understand that college life comes with financial considerations. We offer various session lengths and package options to make our support accessible. Let's chat about your needs and find a plan that works for you, because your well-being shouldn't add to your financial stress.
Will Holistic Medicine treatments interfere with my busy college schedule?
We design our sessions and recommended practices to fit into a student's life, not disrupt it. Many techniques can be done in just a few minutes, right at your desk or between classes. Our aim is to provide practical, time-efficient tools that seamlessly integrate into your daily routine, helping you manage stress without adding extra burdens.
