Stepwise framework

1. Map
A structured intake that maps symptoms, habits, risk, and supports.
2. Prioritize
We pick 1–3 practical targets to move first, small enough to try quickly.
3. Test
Short trials of changes or focused labs when useful; we learn from data, not guesswork.
4. Embed
Translate what's helpful into steady rhythms—habits, environment, and brief check-ins.

We track a small set of signals: sleep consistency, one symptom rating, and one behavior metric. Changes are assessed at short intervals so plans stay nimble and manageable.
