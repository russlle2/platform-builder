Frequently Asked Questions

How does Holistic Medicine specifically help with chronic illness or injury recovery?
Our Holistic Medicine approach focuses on the body's entire system, particularly how chronic stress and physical challenges impact your nervous system. We work to rebalance your stress response, reduce inflammation, and support your body's natural restorative processes, which is crucial when you're recovering from long-term health issues or physical trauma.
I'm experiencing a lot of stress and anxiety due to my condition. Can Holistic Medicine address this?
Absolutely. Stress reduction and nervous system regulation are at the core of what we do. Chronic illness and injury often lead to a persistent 'fight or flight' state. We employ specific techniques and practices to gently guide your nervous system back to a calmer, more regulated state, helping to alleviate anxiety, improve sleep, and enhance your overall sense of peace.
Will Holistic Medicine interfere with my current medical treatments for chronic illness or injury?
Our approach to Holistic Medicine is designed to complement your existing medical care, not replace it. We prioritize open communication with your other healthcare providers to ensure our strategies for stress reduction and nervous system regulation work synergistically with your current treatments, supporting your recovery comprehensively.
What should I expect during my first visit to understand if Holistic Medicine is right for my recovery?
During your initial visit, we'll spend time thoroughly discussing your health history, current challenges related to your chronic illness or injury, and your specific goals for nervous system regulation and stress reduction. This comprehensive conversation allows us to understand your unique needs and develop a personalized plan that integrates Eastern and Western perspectives to support your recovery effectively.
