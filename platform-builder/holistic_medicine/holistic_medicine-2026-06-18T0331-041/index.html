Frequently Asked Questions

I'm a busy professional; how can Holistic Medicine realistically fit into my schedule?
We get it – your time is precious. Our Holistic Medicine approach is designed with your demanding schedule in mind. We focus on practical, actionable strategies that you can integrate into your daily life without feeling overwhelmed. Think short, impactful practices and personalized guidance, not lengthy, time-consuming commitments. We'll work together to find what truly fits for you.
How does Holistic Medicine specifically address burnout symptoms like chronic fatigue or irritability?
Burnout often stems from a dysregulated nervous system. Our Holistic Medicine practice targets this directly by implementing techniques to calm your stress response. For fatigue, we explore energy-supporting nutrients and gentle restorative practices. For irritability, we focus on nervous system regulation techniques that help you respond more calmly. We look at the root causes, not just the surface symptoms you're experiencing.
I've tried other things for stress. What makes your Holistic Medicine approach different?
Many approaches address stress, but our Holistic Medicine practice offers a comprehensive, personalized plan that acknowledges your whole self. We don't offer generic solutions. Instead, we dive into your specific stressors, your lifestyle, and your body's unique needs to create a tailored strategy for nervous system regulation and stress reduction that feels genuinely sustainable for you. We focus on building your inherent resilience.
Is Holistic Medicine only for severe burnout, or can it help prevent it too?
Our Holistic Medicine approach is beneficial at any stage. While we excel at supporting those already experiencing burnout, we also empower working professionals to build resilience and prevent burnout from taking hold. Learning nervous system regulation techniques proactively can be a game-changer, helping you manage daily pressures more effectively and maintain your well-being long-term, rather than waiting until you're exhausted.
