A layered, iterative method

We combine small experiments, shared measurement, and practical supports to make care fit your day. No promises of cure — only clear steps to test and learn.

01
Assess patterns
Short, structured intake to map routines, triggers, and priorities.
02
Small experiments
Try modest changes (3–21 days) and track outcomes. We favor reversible steps with measurable signals.
03
Adjust and support
Refine the plan, add micro-practices, or recommend testing when needed.
04
Embed what works
Scale down supports and create simple prompts so beneficial changes are sustainable.

Shared decision-making

You and your clinician decide together. We present options, likely outcomes, and trade-offs so you can choose an approach that fits your values and capacity.

How we scope testing and herbs

Testing is used purposefully — to resolve a question that changes care. Herbal and nutritional supports are introduced with attention to interactions and evidence. We explain benefits, limits, and monitoring.

Tools you learn

  • Micro-practices (1–8 minutes)
  • Symptom tracking templates
  • Simple movement sequences
  • Decision checklists for testing

Our limits
