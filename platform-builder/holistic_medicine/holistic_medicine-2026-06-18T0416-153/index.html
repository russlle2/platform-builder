Frequently Asked Questions

How does Holistic Medicine specifically address sleep concerns for seniors?
Our Holistic Medicine approach for seniors focuses on gentle, non-invasive methods that consider the unique physiological changes that can affect sleep as you age. We explore the root causes of your restless nights, such as subtle imbalances or lifestyle factors, rather than just masking symptoms. Our aim is to re-establish your body's natural capacity for deep rest.
Is your Holistic Medicine suitable for seniors who are already on other medications?
Absolutely. Your safety and well-being are our utmost priority. During your initial consultation, we conduct a thorough review of your current health status and any medications you are taking. Our Holistic Medicine protocols are carefully designed to integrate safely and complement your existing healthcare regimen, ensuring no adverse interactions while supporting your sleep.
What kind of results can I expect from your Holistic Medicine for sleep improvement?
Clients often report experiencing a noticeable improvement in sleep quality, such as falling asleep more easily, waking less frequently, and feeling more refreshed upon waking. Our Holistic Medicine aims for a sustained sense of calm and renewed energy throughout your day, reflecting a more restorative nightly rest. Results are personalized, reflecting your unique response to care.
How often would I need to visit for Holistic Medicine to improve my sleep?
The frequency of visits for Holistic Medicine is tailored to your individual needs and progress. We typically begin with a series of focused sessions to initiate significant changes in your sleep patterns. As you experience improvements, we transition to less frequent maintenance appointments, ensuring your continued restful sleep and overall well-being. We discuss a personalized plan with you at your first consultation.
