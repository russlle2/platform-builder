    How a cycle unfolds

    These phases are flexible. Treatment choices should be discussed with your care team and tailored to medical context.

    1. Phase 1 — Intake & Safety
      Gather history, prioritize safety checks, and define measurable goals.
    2. Phase 2 — Targeted Work
      Implement interventions in short cycles, with tracking and small-step progressions.
    3. Phase 3 — Review & Maintain
      Regular check-ins, maintenance plan, and transition strategy when goals achieved.

    Small shifts you can try this week

    Micro-practices that stack into sustainable change. Try just one for seven days.

    • 2-minute wind-down — an evening signal to prepare for sleep.
    • Single focus block — 25 minutes of undistracted work, then a break.
    • Midday movement — 8 minutes of gentle mobility before lunch.
    • Check-in card — note energy, sleep, mood each morning.
