Roadmap — what a cycle looks like

Phase 1
Assess & simplify. Intake conversation, baseline markers, and two priorities to act on immediately.
Phase 2
Apply & adapt. Practical routines, brief coaching touchpoints, and early habit supports.
Phase 3
Stabilize & transfer. Consolidate tools, plan follow-ups, and options for ongoing care.
These phases are a general educational guide. Individual pacing and needs vary. This program does not promise cure; it offers structured learning and supportive care coordination when appropriate.

Choosing the right start

If you want a quick reset, choose the Grounding Cycle. If there are layers you want to explore with regular review, the Recharge Sequence fits. Prefer lighter, ongoing guidance? The Sustain Track provides monthly touchpoints. You can move between these as you learn — flexibility is part of the design.
