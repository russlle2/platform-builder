How we work — a guided, educational pathway

Our process is designed to gather context, co-create a plan, and revisit progress with humility and curiosity. Labs are optional educational tools.

1

Intake & Listening Session

A thorough intake explores symptom patterns, lifestyle, relationships to food and stress, environmental context, and medication history. This visit is about mapping — not rushing to a single solution.

2

Collaborative Plan

Together we co-design steps: habit shifts, sleep and movement targets, mind-body practices, and when appropriate, botanical or nutraceutical options as retail add-ons. Recommendations are individualized and educational.

3

Follow-up Rhythm

Short follow-ups focus on measured progress, troubleshooting, and incremental adjustments. Frequency is personalized; we value steady practice over episodic interventions.

4

Optional Labs & Education

When useful, we discuss laboratory options to illuminate metabolic, inflammatory, or hormonal contributors. Labs are educational — they inform the plan rather than dictate it. Any testing is chosen selectively and explained clearly.

5

Sustained Support + Retail Add-ons

We may offer curated retail add-ons — botanicals, targeted supplements, or home tools — as supportive elements. Recommendations are specific, evidence-aware, and framed for education. No promises of cures are made.

Questions we often answer

How long until I notice change?
Varies. Many clients notice small shifts in weeks; sustained patterns take months. We set realistic milestones.
Do you coordinate with my other providers?
Yes — with your permission we collaborate and ensure recommendations are safe and cohesive.
Are supplements required?
Not required. They may be suggested as optional tools within a broader plan.
