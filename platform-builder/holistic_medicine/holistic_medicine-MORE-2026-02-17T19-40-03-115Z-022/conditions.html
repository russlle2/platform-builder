Conditions we support with whole-person context

Education-forward, non-prescriptive support that layers lifestyle, behavioral tools, and clinical reasoning. This is not a substitute for urgent or specialist care.

Important: The information here is educational and supportive. We do not offer guaranteed cures. If you experience severe symptoms (chest pain, loss of consciousness, suicidal thoughts, or acute changes), seek immediate medical care. For complex or high-risk conditions, care is coordinated with your existing providers.

Sleep disturbance

Practical assessments: sleep timing, pre-sleep behaviors, and daytime rhythm. Interventions emphasize small, testable adjustments rather than sweeping promises of cure.

What to expect: a baseline sleep pattern, a 2-week experiment, and stepwise refinement with attention to safety and coexisting conditions.

Digestive complaints

We review patterns around meals, symptoms, and medication. Plans might include food-symptom logs, targeted eliminations, and supportive behavior changes. Testing or specialist referral is used when indicated.

Mood & stress-related symptoms

Care focuses on regulation skills, structure, and small behavioral experiments. For moderate-to-severe mood disorders, we collaborate with mental health clinicians and prioritize safety planning when needed.

Chronic low energy

A pattern-based review of sleep, activity, nutrition, and medication. Interventions are staged and monitored to avoid overpromising rapid fixes.

Pain & movement limitations

We outline gentle, progressive movement, pacing strategies, and referral to physical therapy or specialist care when indicated.

Autoimmune or complex chronic illness

Support here is integrative: symptom tracking, stress moderation, and collaboration with your specialist team. Respect for existing treatment plans is central.

How we frame care

We ask: what patterns are most disruptive? What small, measurable experiments can we try? We document changes and err on the side of safety and evidence when suggesting supplements or tests.
