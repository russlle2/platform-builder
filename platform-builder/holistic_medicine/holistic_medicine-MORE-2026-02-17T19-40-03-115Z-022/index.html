Questions that come up

      

        

          
Is this medical care?

          
We offer integrative, educational services—informative support and practical plans. Not emergency care; we work alongside your other providers and make referrals when needed.

        

        

          
Can I expect a cure?

          
There are no guarantees. Our focus is on clarifying options, making small sustainable changes, and tracking what shifts. We design steps that you can test and adapt.

        

        

          
How long until I notice change?

          
People notice different signals at different times. We'll outline near-term practices (days), short-term markers (weeks), and maintenance plans (months).
