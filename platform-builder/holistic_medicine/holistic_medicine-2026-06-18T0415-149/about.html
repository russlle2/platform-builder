How can Holistic Medicine help my sleep when I'm constantly interrupted by my children?

We understand that complete, uninterrupted sleep might not always be possible right now. Our Holistic Medicine approach focuses on optimizing the quality of the sleep you do get, improving your body's ability to relax quickly, and building resilience to sleep fragmentation. We address underlying stress and nutritional factors that impact your overall restorative capacity, even with wake-ups.

Is Holistic Medicine suitable for parents dealing with high stress and anxiety about their child's sleep, too?

Absolutely. Your stress and anxiety about your child's sleep often directly impact your own ability to rest. Our Holistic Medicine practices provide gentle strategies to manage your own nervous system, helping to reduce that parental anxiety. When you feel calmer and more rested, you're better equipped to support your child's sleep patterns, too.

What kind of time commitment is involved in a Holistic Medicine approach to improving my sleep?

We are mindful of your demanding schedule as a parent. Our Holistic Medicine plans are designed to be integrated gradually, focusing on sustainable changes rather than overwhelming demands. Initial consultations provide a comprehensive understanding, and subsequent sessions are tailored to offer practical steps that you can realistically implement, building momentum over time without adding to your stress.

Will Holistic Medicine conflict with any existing routines or healthcare I have for my family?

Our Holistic Medicine approach is designed to complement your existing routines and healthcare. We emphasize communication and integration, working with you to ensure our recommendations align with your family's needs and any current medical advice. Our goal is to support your well-being holistically, never to replace essential care you or your children may be receiving.
