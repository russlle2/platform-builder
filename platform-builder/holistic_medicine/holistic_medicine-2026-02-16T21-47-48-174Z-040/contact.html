A little about how we meet

Appointments begin with listening — your history, symptoms, routines, and what matters most. We weave conventional insight with lifestyle, nutrition, movement, and mind-body practices to form a personalized plan. No promises of cures — only education, partnership, and practical next steps.

How a first visit flows

  1. Intake: detailed history, priorities, and screening.
  2. Plan: collaborative goals, practical steps, non-invasive options.
  3. Optional labs: tests are explained as educational tools when useful.
  4. Follow-up: adjustments, monitoring, and resources.

This is educational care; treatment plans are individualized and shared as guidance rather than guarantees.
