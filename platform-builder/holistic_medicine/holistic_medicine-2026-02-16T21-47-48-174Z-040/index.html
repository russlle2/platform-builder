Our Framework

A simple, repeatable rhythm: intake, synthesis, plan, follow-up, and optional learning labs.

1. Intake & Listening

Comprehensive history, symptom mapping, and lifestyle conversation to see you as a whole context.

2. Synthesis & Education

We translate findings into plain language: what may be contributing and what you can try first.

3. Personalized Plan

Targeted supports that fit your life — sleep habits, digestion strategies, movement, stress tools, and nutritional ideas.

4. Follow-ups & Iteration

Regular check-ins to refine the plan. Health is a process; we adjust with data and lived feedback.

5. Optional Labs & Learning

When useful, we discuss educational lab options and workshops. Labs are used to inform, not to label — always optional and reviewed in context.
