Our process: deliberate, transparent, and collaborative

We prioritize listening, education, and measurable steps. Below is how a typical partnership unfolds; recommendations are educational and individualized.

1

Intake & story-gathering

A thorough conversation about history, current concerns, medications, and goals. We document patterns and identify safety considerations and priorities.

2

Co-created plan

We propose a layered, time-bound plan: small experiments, lifestyle adjustments, possible botanical or nutritional supports, and clear metrics to track.

3

Optional labs (educational)

If indicated, we discuss labs as tools to inform choices—bloodwork, metabolic panels, or specific functional tests—with emphasis on when they change management.

4

Follow-ups & adjustment

Short follow-ups to review outcomes, tweak doses, and refine behaviors. We favor small, sustainable changes and iterative learning over one-time fixes.

5

Coordination & referral

When your needs exceed our scope, we collaborate with your medical team or refer to specialists. Safety and transparency guide decisions.

6

Transition to self-directed care

The goal is to empower you with skills and a plan you can maintain, with periodic check-ins as needed. Ongoing care is available but not obligatory.
