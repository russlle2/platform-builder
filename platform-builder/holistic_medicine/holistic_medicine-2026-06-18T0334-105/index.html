Frequently Asked Questions

I'm a college student constantly anxious about grades and future plans. How can Holistic Medicine help my sleep?
It's completely normal for anxiety about academics and your future to impact your sleep. Our Holistic Medicine approach addresses this by helping you regulate your nervous system, reduce evening rumination, and establish calming routines. We look at natural ways to support your body's stress response, so you can quiet your mind and fall asleep more easily, waking up feeling more capable of tackling your day.
My schedule is packed with classes, studying, and extracurriculars. Will I have time for a Holistic Medicine sleep plan?
We totally understand that your time is precious. Our Holistic Medicine sleep plans are designed to be practical and integrate seamlessly into a busy college schedule. We focus on small, impactful changes and strategies that fit *your* life, rather than demanding a complete overhaul. Our goal is to empower you with tools that are effective and sustainable, not add more items to your already long to-do list.
I've tried everything for my sleep – apps, herbal teas, even over-the-counter aids. How is Holistic Medicine different?
That's a common experience, and it shows you're motivated to find solutions! Holistic Medicine goes beyond surface-level fixes. We delve into the unique factors influencing *your* sleep – from your diet and daily routines to your stress triggers and emotional well-being. We create a personalized plan using natural, evidence-informed approaches, rather than a one-size-fits-all solution, to address the root causes of your sleep disruption and help you achieve truly restorative rest.
Is Holistic Medicine only for people with severe sleep issues, or can it help if I just feel perpetually tired from college life?
Absolutely not! Holistic Medicine is beneficial whether you're experiencing severe insomnia or just that constant, underlying fatigue that so many college students feel. If you're consistently waking up tired, struggling to focus, or feeling overwhelmed by your workload because of poor rest, our approach can provide personalized support to boost your energy, improve your sleep quality, and help you feel more resilient and engaged in your studies and life.
