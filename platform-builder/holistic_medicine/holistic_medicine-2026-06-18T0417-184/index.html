Frequently Asked Questions

How can Holistic Medicine specifically help my sleep as an athlete?
For athletes and active people, Holistic Medicine focuses on optimizing your body's natural recovery processes. We look at factors beyond just bedtime, like stress management, nutrition, and daily routines, to help you achieve deeper, more restorative sleep that directly supports your physical demands and reduces the impact of physical stress.
I'm new to Holistic Medicine. Is this approach too complicated for me?
Absolutely not! is designed for everyone, especially if you're new to wellness practices. We break down concepts into simple, actionable steps. Our focus is on practical, easy-to-understand strategies that you can seamlessly incorporate into your active life to improve your rest without any prior experience.
Will Holistic Medicine interfere with my current training or recovery protocols?
Our Holistic Medicine approach is designed to complement your existing training and recovery. We don't aim to replace what you're already doing but rather to enhance it by optimizing your body's ability to recover. The goal is to make your current efforts more effective by improving the quality of your rest, ensuring you get the most out of your hard work.
What kind of commitment is required to see results with Holistic Medicine for sleep?
Improving sleep and recovery with Holistic Medicine is a collaborative process. We'll work together to identify small, consistent changes that fit your lifestyle. You'll find that even minor adjustments can lead to significant improvements in how you feel and perform, without requiring a massive overhaul of your routine. It's about sustainable, gentle progress.
