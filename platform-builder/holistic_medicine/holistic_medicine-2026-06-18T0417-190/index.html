Frequently Asked Questions

I'm new to Holistic Medicine. Can it really help my sleep when I'm going through so much change?
Absolutely. Our practice is designed for individuals just like you, experiencing life transitions and new to holistic approaches. We focus on gentle, accessible methods that don't require any prior wellness knowledge. We'll start with simple, supportive steps to help you find more calm and improve your sleep, ensuring you feel comfortable and understood every step of the way.
My sleep has been a mess since my recent life change. How quickly can Holistic Medicine make a difference?
While individual results vary, many clients report feeling a greater sense of ease and experiencing improvements in sleep quality within a few sessions. Our focus is on sustainable change, not just quick fixes. We'll work at your pace, implementing gentle strategies that encourage your body's natural ability to rest and recover, especially during your significant life transition.
I'm worried about adding more 'to-dos' to my already busy life. Will Holistic Medicine for sleep require a lot of effort?
We understand that your plate is full, especially during a life transition. Our Holistic Medicine approach for sleep is designed to be integrated gently into your current routine. We prioritize practical, low-effort strategies that yield significant benefits. The goal is to reduce stress, not add to it, by finding simple, effective ways to improve your restorative rest.
What if I've tried other sleep solutions and nothing has worked for me while I'm navigating changes?
It's common to feel frustrated when previous attempts haven't delivered the rest you need, especially during a life transition. Our Holistic Medicine approach is unique because it considers your whole being and the specific impacts of your current changes. We'll gently explore various angles to find what truly resonates with you, offering fresh perspectives and personalized strategies for more restorative sleep.
