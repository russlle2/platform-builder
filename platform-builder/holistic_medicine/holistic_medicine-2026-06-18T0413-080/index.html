Frequently Asked Questions

How does Holistic Medicine specifically help with stress during life transitions?
During major life transitions, your body's stress response can become heightened. Our Holistic Medicine approach focuses on identifying the root causes of this elevated stress and provides personalized strategies—like targeted nutrition, herbal remedies, and specific relaxation techniques—to bring your nervous system back to a state of calm. You'll learn to modulate your stress response proactively.
What kind of nervous system regulation techniques can I expect to learn?
You'll learn practical, accessible techniques tailored to your needs. This might include specific breathing exercises to activate your parasympathetic nervous system, gentle movement practices, or guided mindfulness strategies. Our aim is to equip you with tools you can use daily to manage your internal state, reducing overwhelm and increasing your capacity for calm.
Is this Holistic Medicine approach suitable for any type of life transition?
Yes, our Holistic Medicine methods are designed to support you through a wide range of significant life changes—from career shifts and relocations to relationship changes or personal growth phases. The core principle is helping you manage the associated stress and maintain nervous system balance, regardless of the specific circumstances you are navigating.
How quickly can I expect to feel a difference with Holistic Medicine for stress reduction?
Many clients report feeling an initial shift towards greater calm within a few weeks of consistently applying the Holistic Medicine strategies we develop together. Sustainable nervous system regulation is a process, and your progress will depend on your unique situation and commitment to the practices. We'll work collaboratively to set realistic expectations and monitor your improvements.
