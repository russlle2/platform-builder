Understanding Your Postpartum Body with Holistic Medicine

Your body has been through so much, and now it's working hard in new ways. We understand the unique physical discomforts that can arise after childbirth, from backaches and pelvic pressure to stiff shoulders and general fatigue. Our approach to Holistic Medicine is all about acknowledging these shifts and providing gentle support to help your body feel more comfortable and at ease as you navigate motherhood. We're here to listen and create a plan that fits just you.

What kind of physical pain can Holistic Medicine help me with after childbirth?

Our Holistic Medicine approach can help you address a range of common postpartum physical discomforts, like lower back pain from nursing or carrying your baby, hip and pelvic tenderness, shoulder and neck stiffness, and general body aches. We focus on gentle, supportive methods to bring you relief and comfort.

I'm new to Holistic Medicine. Is this suitable for someone without prior experience?

Absolutely! Our practice is designed to be gentle and accessible for everyone, especially new mothers who might be new to Holistic Medicine. We explain everything clearly, listen to your needs, and ensure you feel comfortable and understood every step of the way. No prior experience is needed at all.

How does somatic healing specifically support postpartum recovery?

Somatic healing in the postpartum period helps you gently tune into your body's sensations and responses after birth. It can reduce feelings of overwhelm, release physical tension that might be held in your body, and help you feel more grounded and present. It’s about creating a deeper connection with your body's natural wisdom for comfort and calm.

Will Holistic Medicine interfere with my other postpartum medical care?

Our Holistic Medicine services are designed to complement your existing medical care. We encourage open communication with your healthcare providers and can work collaboratively to ensure a cohesive and supportive approach to your postpartum recovery. We are here to support your comfort and well-being alongside any other treatments you may be receiving.
