Frequently Asked Questions

How can Holistic Medicine help me with my college anxiety and sleep issues?
Our Holistic Medicine approach looks at your entire well-being – not just isolated symptoms. We help you connect the dots between your academic stress, anxiety, and sleep patterns. By identifying underlying factors and implementing personalized, natural strategies, we empower you to reduce anxiety and cultivate the conditions for truly restorative rest, helping you feel more in control and prepared for your college journey.
I'm a busy college student. Will I have time for Holistic Medicine practices?
We get it – your schedule is packed. Our Holistic Medicine strategies are designed with your college life in mind. We focus on practical, actionable steps that can be integrated into your existing routine, rather than adding more stress. Our goal is to help you create efficient, impactful practices that yield significant improvements in your sleep and overall energy, making your busy life more manageable.
Is Holistic Medicine a substitute for traditional medical care for sleep problems?
Holistic Medicine at is complementary and integrative. We work alongside your existing healthcare providers and encourage you to continue any prescribed treatments. Our role is to offer additional, natural tools and insights to support your body's innate ability to rest and recover, especially when academic pressures and anxiety are impacting your sleep quality.
What kind of results can I expect from improving my sleep with Holistic Medicine?
When you consistently prioritize restorative rest through our Holistic Medicine guidance, you can expect to experience reduced anxiety, improved focus and concentration in your studies, enhanced mood stability, and more consistent energy levels throughout your day. Imagine approaching your exams and projects feeling calm, clear-headed, and well-rested – that's the transformation we coach you towards.
