Conditions we often support

Our work emphasizes understanding the whole context of a person’s life and building stepwise supports. The information below is educational and meant to guide conversations. It is not medical advice.

Common concerns

  • Sleep disruption and circadian irregularities
  • Chronic digestive upset and functional GI symptoms
  • Fatigue and low energy not explained by single labs
  • Chronic pain management support alongside conventional care
  • Stress, overwhelm, and nervous-system regulation
  • Metabolic tuning and weight-support strategies

Supportive framing & disclaimers

Important: Our descriptions are educational. We do not promise cures. If you have an urgent medical problem or are considering stopping medications, please consult your prescribing clinician first. We coordinate care with primary and specialty teams when needed.

Where appropriate we recommend testing, collaborate with other clinicians, and prioritize safety over rapid change. Plans are individualized and evolve over time.

When to reach out

  1. You want a clear, prioritized plan to address persistent symptoms.
  2. You need a coordinator to interpret labs and suggest practical steps.
  3. You seek a sustainable set of daily practices that fit a busy life.
