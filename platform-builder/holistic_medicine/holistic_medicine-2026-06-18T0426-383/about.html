What exactly is Holistic Medicine, and is it right for someone my age?

Holistic Medicine at our practice means we look at your whole self – your physical feelings, your daily routines, and your emotional well-being – rather than just focusing on one symptom. It's especially well-suited for seniors because we prioritize gentle, non-invasive methods that support your body's natural abilities to find balance and comfort, without requiring strenuous effort from your side.

I'm not familiar with 'somatic healing.' What does that involve?

Somatic healing is a gentle way to help your body release long-held tensions that can contribute to pain or stiffness. It involves simple, guided movements and attentive listening to your body's sensations. There's no complex learning required; it’s about becoming more aware of your body in a relaxed way, which can lead to a surprising sense of relief and improved comfort. We guide you through every step, making it very accessible.

Will I have to do anything difficult or uncomfortable during a session?

Absolutely not. Your comfort and safety are our top priorities. All our techniques in Holistic Medicine are gentle and designed to be relaxing. You're always in control, and we encourage you to communicate what feels right for your body. Our aim is to bring you more ease, not more discomfort, so we move at your pace and with your permission every step of the way.

I've never tried anything like this before. Do I need any special knowledge or experience?

Not at all! Many of our clients are new to Holistic Medicine and somatic healing, and that's perfectly fine. We are here to guide you with patience and understanding. You don't need any prior wellness experience or special knowledge. Just bring your willingness to explore new ways of finding comfort, and we'll take care of the rest, explaining everything clearly as we go.
