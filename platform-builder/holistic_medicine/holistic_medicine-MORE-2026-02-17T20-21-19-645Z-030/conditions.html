What we support

We provide educational, integrative support for common concerns. Nothing here is a promise of cure — care is individualized and may include referrals.

Digestive & metabolic patterns

Symptoms: bloating, irregular bowel habits, fluctuating weight, post-meal fatigue.
Approach: dietary experiments, targeted labs, gentle elimination trials, and pacing plans.

Sleep, energy, and circadian alignment

Symptoms: difficulty falling asleep, frequent waking, daytime exhaustion.
Approach: tailored sleep routines, light and activity scheduling, and pragmatic habit shifts.

Mental load & stress resilience

Symptoms: persistent worry, overwhelm, difficulty with focus.
Approach: short behavioral experiments, pacing strategies, and collaboratively chosen coping tools. When needed, we coordinate with mental health specialists.

Chronic pain & movement

Symptoms: recurring joint or back discomfort, movement avoidance, postural fatigue.
Approach: functional movement assessments, gentle plan building, and referrals to PT or specialists as appropriate.
Important: This information is educational. We do not promise specific outcomes. Our work is a process of exploration — tests, thoughtful changes, and frequent adjustments. If you are experiencing an emergency, seek immediate medical care. For conditions that require specialist care, we will coordinate or refer.

How we decide what to try

  • Start with low-risk, high-relevance experiments.
  • Use tests selectively to reduce guesswork.
  • Keep plans simple and trackable so you can see what changes.

When to reach out

If a pattern is interfering with your work, relationships, or daily routines, an initial conversation can help map next steps.

Notes on medication & specialist care

We collaborate with existing prescribers and specialists. We do not stop critical medications without a supervising clinician. If medication management is needed, we coordinate or refer.

Accessibility

We strive to make care usable for people with busy lives. If you need adjustments (timing, remote visits, summaries), tell us on booking.
