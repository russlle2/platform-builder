Frequently Asked Questions

How does Holistic Medicine at specifically help athletes with pain?
At , our Holistic Medicine approach for athletes goes beyond temporary fixes. We use methods like acupuncture, targeted bodywork, and somatic practices to address inflammation, improve circulation, release deep muscle tension, and re-educate your nervous system. This helps reduce chronic pain, accelerate recovery, and build resilience against future injuries, keeping you in the game.
I'm always stressed about my performance. Can Holistic Medicine really help with that physical stress?
Absolutely. The pressure of performance can manifest as significant physical stress, leading to tension, fatigue, and even injury. Our Holistic Medicine practices incorporate techniques that calm your nervous system, reduce cortisol levels, and enhance your body's ability to cope with stress. This means not only feeling more relaxed but also experiencing less physical discomfort and improved mental clarity for your sport.
What kind of somatic healing is involved in your Holistic Medicine practice for active people?
Somatic healing in our Holistic Medicine practice focuses on the mind-body connection to release physical and emotional blockages. For active people, this might involve guided awareness exercises, gentle movement, or hands-on techniques to help you identify and release chronic tension patterns, improve proprioception, and integrate past physical traumas. The goal is to restore your body's natural movement and reduce pain.
Is Holistic Medicine a substitute for my current physical therapy or sports medicine treatments?
Our Holistic Medicine approach at is designed to complement, not replace, your existing physical therapy or sports medicine treatments. We work integratively, providing deeper support for pain management, stress reduction, and overall physical well-being. Think of it as enhancing your current recovery plan, addressing aspects that conventional treatments might not cover, and accelerating your return to optimal performance.
