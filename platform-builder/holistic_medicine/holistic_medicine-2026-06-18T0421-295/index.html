Frequently Asked Questions

How can Holistic Medicine help with my college anxiety and academic pressure?
Holistic Medicine at offers a comprehensive approach. We don't just focus on the symptoms of anxiety or stress. Instead, we look at how your diet, sleep, thought patterns, and past experiences might be contributing to your current struggles, providing integrated strategies to help you feel more balanced and focused.
Is this approach different from traditional therapy for college students?
Yes, our Holistic Medicine practice broadens the scope beyond traditional therapy. While we value psychological insights, we also incorporate body-centered techniques, nutritional considerations, and mindful practices. This blend provides a wider toolkit for addressing the multifaceted nature of college stress and emotional well-being.
I'm worried about time, will Holistic Medicine sessions fit into my demanding college schedule?
We understand that your time is valuable. Our sessions are designed to be efficient and impactful, providing you with practical strategies you can integrate into your daily life. We'll work with your schedule to find suitable appointment times and focus on tools that offer significant benefit without adding extra burden to your studies.
What does 'trauma-informed care' mean for a college student at ?
For you, 'trauma-informed care' means that we recognize that past difficult experiences can subtly influence your current reactions to stress and anxiety. provides a non-judgmental and safe space, ensuring that our conversations and techniques are always respectful of your history and aimed at building a sense of security and resilience.
