Philosophy in a few lines

I combine evidence-informed nutrition, lifestyle coaching, and gentle complementary therapies to help you learn what your body is telling you. The work is collaborative: we set measurable goals, track progress, and adjust. You will leave each visit with practical steps — not vague promises.

Education-first
I teach tools you can use right away — breathwork, sleep hygiene, gut-supporting habits, and stress mapping.
Whole-person
We look at sleep, digestion, movement, work, and relationships as parts of the same picture.
Practical
Plans are realistic, paced, and tailored to your life — not a one-size regimen.
