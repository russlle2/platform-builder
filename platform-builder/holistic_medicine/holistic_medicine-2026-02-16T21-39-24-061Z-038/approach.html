How a typical collaboration unfolds

1. Intake
Comprehensive history, goals, lifestyle, medications, and priorities. 90–120 minutes.
2. Baseline plan
A focused, time-bound plan with 3–5 high-impact experiments and education resources.
3. Follow-ups
Regular check-ins to track progress, refine steps, and troubleshoot barriers.
4. Iteration
Data-driven adjustments — if something isn\'t moving the needle, we change the approach.

Optional educational labs

Labs are tools for learning — they may include basic metabolic panels, targeted nutrient tests, or gut panels when clinically indicated. They are never required and are discussed as part of shared decision-making.

  • When helpful: to clarify a question raised in the intake
  • When optional: to guide or personalize a plan, not to replace behavior change
  • Interpretation: you get an explanation and actionable steps tied to results

What to expect from an intake

  1. Pre-visit intake form to save time
  2. Deep history, current routines, and priorities
  3. Clinical reasoning and an initial plan you can start immediately
  4. Clear follow-up schedule and homework

Approach FAQ

How quickly will I see changes?
Some experiments produce quick feedback (days to weeks); other areas require longer trends. We focus on measurable short-term targets and long-term habits.
Do you prescribe medications?
Medication management is coordinated with your prescribing clinicians. We discuss when medication changes might be appropriate but work collaboratively.
