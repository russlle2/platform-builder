Frequently Asked Questions

How does Holistic Medicine at specifically help with physical stress from activity?
Our Holistic Medicine approach at focuses on identifying the root causes of your physical stress, beyond just the immediate pain. We use natural methods to reduce inflammation, improve circulation, release deep-seated tension, and strengthen your body's natural recovery processes, helping you sustain your activity levels with greater ease and less discomfort.
What kind of somatic healing is involved in your Holistic Medicine practice for athletes?
Somatic healing in our Holistic Medicine practice involves gentle, yet profound techniques that help you become more attuned to your body's sensations and patterns of tension. This can include specific bodywork, breathwork, and mindful movement exercises designed to release chronic holding patterns, improve body awareness, and restore functional movement, directly supporting your athletic performance and recovery.
Will Holistic Medicine treatments interfere with my training schedule or competitive events?
Our Holistic Medicine treatments are designed to complement your training, not disrupt it. We work with you to create a plan that supports your recovery and performance goals, ensuring any interventions are timed appropriately around your schedule. Our aim is to enhance your body's resilience and reduce recovery time, allowing you to maintain consistency.
Is 's Holistic Medicine suitable for all types of athletes and active people?
Yes, our Holistic Medicine services are tailored for a wide range of athletes and active individuals, from weekend warriors to competitive professionals. Whether you're dealing with repetitive strain, acute injury recovery, or general physical fatigue, our methods are adaptable to support your specific sport, activity level, and personal wellness objectives.
