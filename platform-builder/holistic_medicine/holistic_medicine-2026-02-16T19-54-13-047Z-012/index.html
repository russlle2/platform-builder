Small practices that compound

Tiny, consistent actions move the needle. Try one for a week and notice the ripple.

3-Breath Reset

Pause, inhale for 4, hold 2, exhale 6. Repeat 3 times to reduce immediate stress cues.

Sleep Wind-Down

Bright screens off 60 minutes before bed; dim lights and a warm drink to cue rest.

Move the Mood

10 minutes of gentle movement after a meal or midday to support digestion and energy.

Mini Gratitude

One sentence each evening about something that went well—rewires focus slowly.

Micro habits are educational tools. If you’re managing a health condition, discuss habit changes with your practitioner.
