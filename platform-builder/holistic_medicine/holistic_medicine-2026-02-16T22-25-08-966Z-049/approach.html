Our practical care workflow

We emphasize clear steps you can follow: intake, prioritized plan, simple experiments, and focused follow-up. Optional testing is used selectively to inform decisions.

1. Intake & Story

A comprehensive history that centers your goals, context, and previous tests or treatments. We listen first, then build understanding.

2. Assessment & Priorities

We map symptoms to systems and identify 1–3 priorities to address first, with practical measures you can start immediately.

3. Evidence-informed Plan

Combination of lifestyle, nutrition, mind-body skills, and selective supplements or herbs explained with rationale and safety considerations.

4. Measure & Follow-up

Short follow-ups focus on what changed, what to adjust, and when further testing or specialist collaboration is needed.

Our philosophy on testing

Labs and functional testing can be helpful when results will change management. We discuss options, costs, and interpretation so testing supports clear decisions rather than creating unnecessary complexity.

When helpful: clarify nutrient gaps
When not helpful: low-yield routine panels
We order with a plan for how results change care
