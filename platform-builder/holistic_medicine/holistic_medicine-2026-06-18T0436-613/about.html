How does Holistic Medicine specifically support immune health for someone over 60?

Our Holistic Medicine approach focuses on gentle, evidence-based strategies tailored for seniors. This includes targeted nutritional support to address age-related changes, botanical remedies known for immune modulation, and lifestyle adjustments that reduce inflammation and enhance your body's natural defenses, all without harsh interventions.

What kind of vitality building can I expect from Holistic Medicine at my age?

With Holistic Medicine, you can expect a personalized plan aimed at improving your energy, mental clarity, and overall zest for life. We address factors like sleep quality, gentle physical activity, and stress management, helping you to feel more resilient and engaged, rather than just treating symptoms of fatigue.

Is Holistic Medicine safe to combine with my current medications?

Absolutely. Your safety is our top priority. We thoroughly review your medical history and current medications to ensure that any Holistic Medicine recommendations are complementary and do not interact negatively. Our approach is always collaborative, and we encourage open communication with your primary care physician.
