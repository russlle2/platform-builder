Frequently Asked Questions

How does Holistic Medicine specifically address immune challenges for athletes?
For athletes, we focus on identifying specific stressors that compromise your immune system, such as overtraining, nutrient deficiencies, or gut imbalances. We then build a personalized plan using advanced diagnostic testing, targeted supplements, and dietary adjustments to strengthen your immune response, helping you avoid setbacks and maintain training consistency.
Can Holistic Medicine really help with my post-workout recovery and vitality?
Absolutely. We look beyond basic recovery to optimize your cellular energy production and reduce systemic inflammation. By addressing factors like adrenal fatigue, sleep architecture, and micronutrient status, we help your body recover more efficiently, enhance your overall energy levels, and sustain your peak performance over the long term.
Is this approach evidence-based, especially for active individuals?
Yes, our methods are deeply rooted in current scientific research, particularly in sports nutrition, psychoneuroimmunology, and botanical medicine. We integrate clinical evidence with your individual physiological data to create a precise, effective strategy tailored to the demands of your active lifestyle and athletic goals.
What should I expect during my first consultation at ?
Your first consultation is a comprehensive discussion about your athletic history, training regimen, health concerns, and performance goals. We'll explore your diet, sleep patterns, stress levels, and perform an initial assessment. This detailed intake allows us to understand your unique needs and begin crafting a personalized Holistic Medicine plan to support your immune system and vitality.
