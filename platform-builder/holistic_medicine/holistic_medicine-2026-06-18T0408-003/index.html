Frequently Asked Questions

What does 'nervous system regulation' mean for me as a senior in Holistic Medicine?
Nervous system regulation means helping your body learn to move gracefully between states of alertness and relaxation. For you, this translates to feeling less anxious, sleeping more soundly, and handling daily challenges with greater ease, all through gentle Holistic Medicine approaches tailored to your needs.
How can Holistic Medicine help me reduce stress specifically at my age?
Our Holistic Medicine approach considers the unique stressors often faced by seniors, such as changes in routine or managing health. We use gentle, non-invasive techniques to help your body release stored tension, quiet an overactive mind, and build resilience, allowing you to experience more calm and comfort in your daily life.
Will the Holistic Medicine treatments be gentle and safe for someone my age?
Absolutely. Our Holistic Medicine practices are specifically chosen for their gentle nature and safety, keeping your comfort and well-being as our highest priority. We always discuss any concerns you might have and adapt our methods to suit your individual health and mobility, ensuring a supportive and nurturing experience.
What can I expect during my first Holistic Medicine session focused on stress reduction?
Your first Holistic Medicine session will be a warm, conversational time where we listen to your concerns and aspirations. We'll gently explore your current experiences with stress and discuss how our tailored Holistic Medicine approach can help you cultivate more peace and ease, creating a personalized plan together.
