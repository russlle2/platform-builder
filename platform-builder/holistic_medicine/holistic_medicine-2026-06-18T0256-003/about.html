What exactly is Holistic Medicine?

Holistic Medicine is an approach to health that considers your entire being – mind, body, and spirit – rather than just individual symptoms. It aims to restore balance and promote natural healing by addressing the root causes of health concerns and empowering you to take an active role in your wellness.

Is Holistic Medicine suitable for all health conditions?

Holistic Medicine can complement conventional treatments for many health conditions and is often very effective for chronic issues, preventative care, and general well-being. We always recommend discussing your specific health concerns with us during an initial consultation to determine how our approach can best support you.

How long does it take to see results with Holistic Medicine?

The timeline for seeing results can vary greatly depending on your individual health concerns, the duration of your condition, and your commitment to the recommended plan. Some individuals experience shifts relatively quickly, while others may require more time for deeper healing and lasting changes. Our focus is on sustainable progress.

Will my insurance cover Holistic Medicine services?

Coverage for Holistic Medicine services varies widely depending on your specific insurance provider and plan. We recommend contacting your insurance company directly to inquire about their policies regarding integrative and holistic health services. We can provide you with the necessary documentation to submit for potential reimbursement.
