Frequently asked questions

We focus on symptom management and improving resilience through education and lifestyle interventions. No clinic guarantees cures; our work is to support better function and quality of life.

Are labs required?

Labs are optional and discussed when they add value to clinical decisions. We emphasize understanding and minimizing unnecessary testing.

Coverage varies. We provide documentation you can submit to insurers; please ask our team for details based on your plan.
