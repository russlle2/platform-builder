Frequently Asked Questions

How can Holistic Medicine specifically help me recover from chronic illness or injury?
Our Holistic Medicine approach focuses on your entire well-being, not just symptoms. We'll explore nutrition, gentle movement, stress reduction, and targeted natural therapies to support your immune system and help your body rebuild its strength after chronic illness or injury, rather than just masking discomfort.
What kind of immune support can I expect with your Holistic Medicine practice?
We tailor immune support to your specific situation. This might include personalized dietary recommendations to reduce inflammation, specific botanical supplements to strengthen your defenses, or lifestyle adjustments that optimize immune function without overstimulating a recovering system. Our goal is to build your resilience gently and effectively.
Will Holistic Medicine treatments interfere with my current medical care for chronic recovery?
Our Holistic Medicine practice prioritizes your safety and well-being. We always encourage open communication with your primary care team. We carefully select therapies that complement your existing medical treatments, working synergistically to support your recovery without causing adverse interactions. Your integrated care is our priority.
How long does it typically take to see results in terms of vitality and immune strength?
Recovery is personal, and the timeline for seeing results in vitality and immune strength varies for everyone. Our Holistic Medicine approach is designed for sustainable progress, not quick fixes. We work at your body's pace, focusing on gentle, consistent improvements that build lasting resilience. We'll regularly check in on your progress together.
