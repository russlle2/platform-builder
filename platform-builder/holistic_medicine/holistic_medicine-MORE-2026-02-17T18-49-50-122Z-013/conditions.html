Common concerns I support

Below are topics frequently addressed in integrative consultations. Entries are educational: they describe supportive strategies, typical focuses in visits, and ways we may work together. They are not medical guarantees.

Conditions & supportive guidance

  • Sleep difficulties
    Focus: evening routines, circadian cues, gentle sleep skills, and nutrition timing. Approach: map current patterns, introduce micro-practices, review stimulant timing and light exposure.
  • Digestive complaints
    Focus: meal rhythms, mindful eating, simple elimination trials, and supportive botanicals when appropriate. Approach: timeline of symptoms, practical dietary adjustments, and movement practices to support digestion.
  • Stress, worry, and nervous system dysregulation
    Focus: breath-work, small somatic practices, daily pacing, and anchoring routines. Approach: build tolerable exposures to routines, scaffold restoration moments into the day.
  • Chronic aches & movement recovery
    Focus: alignment, graded movement, sleep and nutrition that support repair. Approach: short movement prescriptions, recovery emphasis, and coordination with other providers as needed.

How to read this page

This content is informational and does not replace individualized assessment. During visits we integrate clinical reasoning with your lived priorities. Where medical testing or urgent care are indicated, we will recommend appropriate steps and collaborate with other clinicians.

Disclaimer: No treatment is guaranteed. Recommendations are educational and tailored to each person's context. If you have an emergency or life-threatening condition, seek immediate medical care.

Next steps
