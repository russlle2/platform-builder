How I work

Care is an invitation: we look, listen, and choose small, practical steps together. Plans respect your life, not the other way around.

Phases

1. Map
A compassionate intake to understand patterns and the most useful next moves.
2. Act
Concrete, limited practices and adjustments you can integrate without feeling overwhelmed.
3. Tune
Short check-ins to refine, adjust dosage, and support longer-term resilience.

What this means in a session

  • We prioritize clarity over complexity.
  • Suggestions are trialable and reversible.
  • Work is collaborative: you keep agency over choices.
