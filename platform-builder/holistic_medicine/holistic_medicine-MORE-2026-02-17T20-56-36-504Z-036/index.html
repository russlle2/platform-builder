Common shortcuts, reimagined

Myth: Quick fixes are enough

Truth: Symptoms are signals. We teach how to respond to them with layered steps that stack, not sprint.

Myth: One-size plans work

Truth: Context matters—work rhythms, sleep windows, and relationships all change what will stick. We co-design plans that fit you.

Myth: You must choose between medicine and lifestyle

Truth: Thoughtful combinations of evidence-based care and day-to-day habits often give the best, safest results.

Frequently asked questions

What will I do in a cohort?

Weekly learning labs, small-group problem solving, measurable short-term goals, and clinician check-ins. You leave with an adaptable plan, not a rigid prescription.

Is this therapy or medical care?

We blend education, lifestyle support, and clinical oversight where appropriate. For urgent medical or psychiatric needs, seek direct medical attention. Cohorts are not crisis services.

How is pricing structured?

Programs are cohort-priced with optional private add-ons. See the Invest page for current cycles and enrollment windows.
