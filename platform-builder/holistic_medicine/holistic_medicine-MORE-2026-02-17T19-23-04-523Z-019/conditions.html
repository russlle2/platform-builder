Conditions & concerns we commonly support

The items below describe areas where we provide educational guidance, lifestyle tools, and coordinated referrals. This is not a substitute for medical care.

Sleep difficulties

Short sleep, frequent awakenings, or irregular schedules. We work with routines, behavioural tools, and environmental adjustments. Recommended: Sleep Mapping add-on, Rhythm Reset Series.

Digestive discomfort

Bloating, reflux, irregular stools, or food-response patterns. We use a structured intake to identify patterns, trial supportive changes, and coordinate testing when indicated. Recommended: Foundations Intake + Nutritional Audit.

Chronic stress & burnout

Ongoing overwhelm, difficulty recovering after work, or changes in concentration. We prioritise small, repeatable practices, pacing, and environmental edits. Recommended: Sustained Support subscription with Mindfulness micro-practices.

Pain & mobility concerns

Recurring musculoskeletal pain, stiffness, or reduced function. We focus on movement education, graded exposure, and referral to movement specialists when needed. Recommended: Movement Map add-on and tailored session plan.

Mood variation

Low mood, irritability, or emotional ups and downs. We offer behavioral supports, sleep and nutrition checks, and close collaboration with mental health providers for integrated care. Recommended: Foundations Intake and coordination with your therapist or prescriber.

Complex chronic conditions

For multi-system or long-standing conditions we offer coordination, education, and practical steps to reduce treatment burden. We do not replace specialist medical care; we help translate and integrate strategies into daily life.

Important: We do not promise cures. Our services are educational and supportive. If you have an urgent medical concern or rapidly changing symptoms, contact your primary medical provider or emergency services.

How we frame support
