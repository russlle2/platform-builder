How we work — a practical, integrative method

Work begins with a map of your life and moves toward simple practices that fit your routine. We call our approach "Map, Try, Tune" — a loop that values early wins and real-world adaptation.

Map

Collect the important context: medical history, current priorities, daily rhythms, and constraints. This helps us set realistic goals.

Try

Introduce a small set of experiments with clear timing and measures. These are practical tasks you can do between sessions.

Tune

Review what worked, adjust intensity, and refine steps. The goal is steady, sustainable change rather than dramatic overhaul.

Our session process

1. Intake & priorities — A focused review to create a short list of prioritized changes.
2. Short-term trials — 2–6 week experiments with measurable checkpoints.
3. Integration — Embedding practices into routines and sequencing follow-ups at the right cadence.
