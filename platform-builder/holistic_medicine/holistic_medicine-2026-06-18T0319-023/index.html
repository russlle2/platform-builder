Frequently Asked Questions

How does Holistic Medicine at specifically address stress for those over 60?
Our Holistic Medicine approach for seniors focuses on gentle, non-invasive techniques to calm your nervous system. We prioritize methods that consider your body's natural rhythms and sensitivities, aiming to reduce tension and foster a profound sense of inner peace without aggressive interventions.
Will these spiritual practices be new or overwhelming for me?
Not at all. Our spiritual approach in Holistic Medicine is about connecting with your own inner wisdom and serenity. We introduce practices gently and at your own pace, ensuring they feel comforting and natural to you. There's no pressure, only an invitation to explore practices that resonate with your spirit.
What if I'm not familiar with holistic concepts or ancient healing traditions?
That's perfectly fine. Many of our clients are new to Holistic Medicine. We explain everything clearly and simply, focusing on how these time-honored practices can bring calm and balance to your nervous system. Our goal is to make these traditions accessible and beneficial for your well-being.
How quickly can I expect to feel a difference in my stress levels?
While individual experiences vary, many clients report feeling a sense of calm and relaxation after their very first session. Our gentle Holistic Medicine practices are designed to offer immediate soothing, with deeper and more lasting nervous system regulation developing consistently over time with regular engagement.
