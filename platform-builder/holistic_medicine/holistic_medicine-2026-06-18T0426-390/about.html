What exactly is 'somatic healing' in Holistic Medicine, and how can it help with my physical pain during a life transition?

Somatic healing within Holistic Medicine focuses on the connection between your mind and body. When you're going through big changes, your body can hold onto stress, leading to physical pain like tension, headaches, or digestive issues. We use gentle, body-aware techniques to help you recognize and release these stored tensions, offering a path to reduce physical discomfort and feel more settled in your body as you navigate your transition.

I've never tried Holistic Medicine before. Is this approach suitable for someone new to these kinds of practices?

Absolutely. Our Holistic Medicine practice is designed to be gentle and accessible for everyone, especially if you're new to these approaches. We don't assume any prior knowledge or experience. Our focus is on listening to your needs and introducing methods that feel comfortable and supportive for your body, particularly when you're experiencing physical pain during a life change.

My physical pain seems to get worse during stressful life changes. Can Holistic Medicine truly help with this connection?

Yes, it can. Many people find their physical pain amplifies during stressful life transitions. Holistic Medicine acknowledges this deep connection. We don't just treat the symptom; we look at how your body is responding to the broader changes in your life. By addressing both the physical discomfort and the underlying somatic responses, we can help you find more sustained relief and better coping mechanisms for future stress.

How quickly can I expect to feel relief from physical pain with Holistic Medicine, especially when I'm in the middle of a big life change?

The speed of relief can vary for everyone, as your body's response is unique. However, many of our clients report feeling a sense of calm and reduced physical tension even after their first few sessions. Our goal is to provide gentle, cumulative support, helping your body gradually release discomfort and build resilience as you move through your life transition. We prioritize making you feel heard and comfortable throughout the process.
