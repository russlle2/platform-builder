Frequently Asked Questions

I've never tried Holistic Medicine before. Is it right for a college student like me?
Absolutely! Our approach is designed to be gentle and super accessible, even if you have zero prior wellness experience. We break things down into easy steps and focus on practical strategies you can use right away to help with sleep and academic pressure.
How can Holistic Medicine specifically help with my sleep when I'm so stressed about exams?
We understand that exam stress can really mess with your sleep. Our Holistic Medicine sessions will provide you with specific, calming techniques and lifestyle adjustments tailored to your college schedule, helping you quiet your mind and prepare your body for more restful sleep, even during peak academic times.
My schedule is packed. Can I actually fit Holistic Medicine into my busy college life?
We get that your time is precious. Our strategies are designed to be efficient and integrate smoothly into your existing routine. We focus on small, impactful changes that don't require a huge time commitment but still deliver real benefits for your sleep and overall well-being.
What if I'm skeptical about 'natural' approaches? Will Holistic Medicine still work for my anxiety?
It's totally normal to have questions! We use evidence-informed methods within Holistic Medicine that are practical and effective for reducing anxiety and improving sleep. You'll find our approach grounded in understanding your unique challenges and offering concrete tools you can test for yourself.
