Frequently Asked Questions

How can Holistic Medicine specifically help my physical pain during a life transition?
When you're navigating significant changes, your body often holds onto stress and tension, leading to new or exacerbated pain. Our Holistic Medicine approach focuses on uncovering these connections, using gentle techniques to soothe your nervous system, release muscle tension, and restore your body's natural balance, helping you move through your transition with greater physical ease.
What does 'somatic healing' mean in the context of my pain and life changes?
Somatic healing means we look at how your physical sensations, emotions, and thoughts are interconnected, especially during times of change. It's about tuning into your body's signals and gently releasing stored stress or emotional residue that contributes to your pain. This process helps you cultivate a deeper sense of safety and presence within your own body, even amidst external shifts.
Will Holistic Medicine treatments interfere with other support I'm receiving for my life transition?
Our Holistic Medicine approach is designed to complement any other support you might be receiving. We focus on your physical well-being and somatic experience, which can enhance your overall resilience. We encourage open communication about your current care team so we can work together to provide you with the most integrated and beneficial support possible.
I'm feeling overwhelmed by my transition; will this add more to my plate?
We understand that adding another appointment can feel like a lot. Our Holistic Medicine sessions are designed to be a supportive, calming space for you. The goal is to reduce your burden, not add to it, by addressing the physical pain and discomfort that might be making your transition even harder. We meet you where you are and proceed at a pace that feels right for you.
