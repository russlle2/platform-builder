Frequently Asked Questions

How can Holistic Medicine specifically help with my academic anxiety?
Our Holistic Medicine approach directly targets the physiological mechanisms of anxiety that often impact academic performance. We use methods to regulate your nervous system, which means reducing the physical symptoms of stress like racing thoughts or difficulty concentrating, so you can approach your studies with a clearer, calmer mind.
I'm a college student with a packed schedule. How realistic is it to incorporate Holistic Medicine?
We understand your time is precious. Our Holistic Medicine strategies are designed to be efficient and integrate seamlessly into your life. We focus on impactful, actionable techniques that don't demand hours of your day but still yield significant results in stress reduction and nervous system regulation. We'll work with your schedule.
Is Holistic Medicine backed by science, or is it more 'woo-woo'?
Our practice is firmly rooted in a science-meets-wellness philosophy. Every Holistic Medicine technique we recommend for stress reduction and nervous system regulation is supported by current research in neuroscience, psychophysiology, and integrative health. We provide clear explanations and data-driven insights, not just vague promises.
What kind of results can I expect from Holistic Medicine for my stress and academic pressure?
You can expect to learn concrete skills to manage your stress response, improve your focus, and reduce feelings of overwhelm. Our Holistic Medicine approach aims to help you achieve a more regulated nervous system, leading to better sleep, sustained energy, and a greater sense of calm and control, even when academic demands are high.
