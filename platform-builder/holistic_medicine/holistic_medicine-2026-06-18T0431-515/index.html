Frequently Asked Questions

How can Holistic Medicine specifically help with my college anxiety and academic pressure?
Our Holistic Medicine approach provides targeted strategies to manage the unique stressors of college. We focus on evidence-based techniques for emotional regulation, stress reduction, and cultivating a deeper sense of spiritual calm, which can directly improve your focus, reduce anxiety during exams, and enhance your overall academic experience.
Is spiritual growth a core part of Holistic Medicine here, and what does that mean for me?
Yes, spiritual growth is central to our Holistic Medicine practice. For you, this means exploring practices that connect you to a sense of purpose and inner peace beyond your academic achievements. It's about building resilience, finding meaning, and developing a stable inner core that can withstand the ups and downs of college life and beyond.
I'm skeptical about 'Holistic Medicine.' How is your approach evidence-based?
We understand your skepticism and embrace a clinical, evidence-based approach within Holistic Medicine. Our methods are informed by research in psychology, neuroscience, and traditional practices proven to support mental and spiritual well-being. We’ll discuss the rationale behind each recommendation, ensuring you feel confident in your path.
How can I fit Holistic Medicine sessions into my busy college schedule?
We prioritize making our Holistic Medicine services accessible. We offer flexible scheduling options, including virtual sessions, to accommodate your academic commitments. Our goal is to provide practical support that integrates seamlessly into your life, not add another source of stress.
