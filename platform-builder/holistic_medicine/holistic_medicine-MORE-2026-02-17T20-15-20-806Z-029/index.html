Questions people usually ask

Is this medical treatment?

I offer integrative coaching and supportive practices. I do not promise cures. If you have medical concerns, we coordinate with your clinical providers and follow recommended care.

How long before I notice change?

Micro-practices can yield early shifts in days; deeper pattern change is gradual. The work is about testing and learning, not quick fixes.

How do sessions happen?

Sessions are remote or local depending on availability. We'll choose formats that fit your schedule and energy.
