A gentle structure: intake, plan, follow-up

Our model values clarity and small experiments. Below is how the work typically unfolds — an educational path, not a promise.

1. Guided Intake

A whole-life conversation: timeline of health, priorities, and practical constraints. We identify what matters most and set near-term aims.

2. A pragmatic plan

A focused set of actions — sleep adjustments, targeted nutrition, movement, and stress-support practices. Suggestions are educational and personalized.

3. Optional labs (educational)

When a test will change options, we discuss it plainly: costs, interpretation, and how results would shape the plan. Tests are optional and never mandatory.

4. Follow-up & refinement

Short, focused check-ins to adjust steps, troubleshoot barriers, and build durable routines. Progress is measured in small wins.

5. Community learning

Group series and workshops extend education and offer peer support — ideal for skill-building and shared practice.

6. When to bring other clinicians

We collaborate with specialists when needed and coordinate care. Integration, not replacement, is the goal.

Myth vs. truth

Myth: Holistic means slow and vague.
Truth: Holistic care can be targeted, measurable, and practical — education and partnership accelerate meaningful change.

A short case note

A patient arrives with tiredness and fragmentary sleep. We prioritize sleep timing, one dietary tweak, and two nightly rituals. After three check-ins small gains translate into restored energy and clearer day rhythms.

Questions I often hear

How fast will I feel different?
It varies. Some shifts are immediate (sleep hygiene changes), others take weeks. The plan is paced to fit real life.
Do you prescribe medications?
When appropriate, medications are discussed and coordinated with prescribing clinicians. Our focus is to expand options thoughtfully.
