Selected case notes (anonymized)

Improved sleep + lowered stress response

Context: Mid-40s, shifting work schedule, persistent nighttime rumination. Plan: Sleep hygiene, brief CBT tools, circadian-aligned light exposure and optional saliva cortisol education. Outcome: Patient reported more consistent sleep within 8 weeks and learned self-regulation tools. No guarantees; individual results vary.

Digestive steadiness and energy

Context: Fluctuating energy, bloating after meals. Plan: Food pattern reset, targeted elimination, microbiome-aware nutrition and selective testing when helpful. Outcome: Noticeable reduction in bloating, improved midday energy after 10 weeks of consistent changes.

Common questions

Do you promise cures?
No. We focus on education, measurable change and shared decision-making. We do not guarantee specific outcomes.
Will you order labs?
Sometimes. Labs are offered when they meaningfully inform a plan; they remain optional and are discussed up front.
How long until I see change?
It depends on the concern and consistency of interventions; many people notice early shifts within 4–12 weeks while others need longer.
