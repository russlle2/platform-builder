Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly does 'nervous system regulation' mean in Holistic Medicine for seniors?
Nervous system regulation refers to helping your body and mind maintain a balanced state, even when faced with daily stressors. For seniors, this means learning to shift out of states of high tension or anxiety more easily, promoting better sleep, digestion, and overall emotional well-being. Our Holistic Medicine approach uses gentle techniques to support this natural balance within you.
Are the Holistic Medicine methods suitable for someone my age (60+)?
Absolutely. All our Holistic Medicine practices are specifically chosen and adapted to be gentle, safe, and effective for seniors aged 60 and above. We prioritize your comfort and ensure that any recommended techniques, whether they are breathwork, mindful movement, or nutritional guidance, are appropriate for your current health and mobility levels. Your safety and well-being are paramount.
How quickly can I expect to see improvements in stress levels through Holistic Medicine?
The timeline for experiencing improvements can vary for each individual, as everyone's body responds differently. However, many of our clients report feeling a greater sense of calm and improved sleep within a few weeks of consistently applying our Holistic Medicine strategies. Our goal is to equip you with sustainable practices that lead to lasting positive changes in your stress response and nervous system function over time.
Do I need any prior experience with Holistic Medicine or mindfulness to benefit?
Not at all. No prior experience with Holistic Medicine, mindfulness, or any wellness practices is required. We welcome you exactly as you are. will guide you step-by-step through every technique, explaining the science behind it in an easy-to-understand way. Our focus is on making these beneficial practices accessible and comfortable for you, regardless of your background.
