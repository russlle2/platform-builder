1. Intake & story-gathering

A comprehensive intake explores your medical history, lifestyle rhythms, stressors, and priorities. The aim is clarity — we listen first, then educate.

2. Personalized plan

Plans include practical diet and sleep guidance, stress tools, movement suggestions, and prioritized small experiments. We present options with rationale so you can choose what fits.

3. Optional educational labs

When helpful, we may suggest non-invasive lab tests as educational tools to illuminate patterns. These are optional and always explained in plain language; lab results inform discussion, not unilateral commands.

Examples: nutrient markers, basic inflammation panels, metabolic snapshots

4. Follow-up & iterative care

Follow-ups refine the plan, troubleshoot barriers, and celebrate sustainable wins. Membership offers scheduled check-ins and messaging so small changes accumulate over time.

Collaborative education

We prioritize teaching: why a suggestion might help, what to expect, and how to measure progress. This empowers sustainable habit change.

Coordination of care

When medical care is needed, we communicate and coordinate with your providers so plans are safe, sensible, and complementary.
