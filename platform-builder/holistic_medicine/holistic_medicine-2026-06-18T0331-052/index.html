Frequently Asked Questions

How does Holistic Medicine specifically address postpartum stress and anxiety?
Our Holistic Medicine approach for new mothers focuses on identifying the root causes of your postpartum stress and anxiety, which can range from hormonal imbalances to nutrient depletion and nervous system overload. We then develop a personalized plan using natural therapies to gently rebalance your system, support your adrenal health, and enhance your body's natural ability to cope with stressors, leading to a profound sense of calm.
What kind of results can I expect from nervous system regulation during postpartum recovery?
With consistent engagement in your personalized nervous system regulation plan, you can expect to experience a significant reduction in feelings of overwhelm, improved sleep quality, a more stable mood, and an increased capacity to handle the demands of new motherhood without feeling constantly drained. Our goal is to help you feel more grounded, resilient, and connected to yourself and your baby.
Is Holistic Medicine safe to pursue while breastfeeding or recovering from childbirth?
Absolutely. Safety is our paramount concern. All our Holistic Medicine protocols are carefully chosen to be gentle and supportive, especially during breastfeeding and physical recovery. We ensure that any recommended supplements or therapies are compatible with your current health status and any medications you may be taking, always prioritizing your well-being and that of your baby.
How long does it typically take to see improvements in my nervous system health and stress levels?
The timeline for improvement varies for everyone, as each new mother's experience is unique. However, many of our clients report noticing positive shifts in their stress levels and overall sense of calm within a few weeks of beginning their personalized Holistic Medicine plan. Our approach is designed for sustained results, building your resilience over time rather than offering quick fixes.
