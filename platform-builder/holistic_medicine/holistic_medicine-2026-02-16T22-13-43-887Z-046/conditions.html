Common concerns people bring to integrative care

Below are educational overviews of typical areas we address. Recommendations are personalized; labs or medical referrals may be suggested when appropriate.

Stress & overwhelm

Chronic stress affects sleep, digestion, immunity, and mood. Interventions focus on rhythms, boundaries, breathwork, and practical resilience skills.

Educational only. Severe anxiety or panic should be discussed with a mental health provider.

Poor sleep & restorative rest

We review routines, environment, substances, and circadian cues. Behavioral adjustments are first-line; targeted testing or referrals considered if needed.

Not a substitute for acute medical evaluation for sleep apnea or other serious sleep disorders.

Digestion & gut symptoms

Dietary patterns, food sensitivities, microbiome context, and stress interactions are explored. Nutritional guidance is individualized and evidence-informed.

If you experience severe abdominal pain, bleeding, or unexplained weight loss, seek immediate care.

Low energy & fatigue

We look at sleep, nutrition, movement, mood, and metabolic factors. Selective labs may help clarify causes; interventions emphasize sustainable changes.

Fatigue can be a symptom of medical conditions; appropriate medical workup may be recommended.

Chronic inflammation & pain

Lifestyle, diet, sleep, and stress interplay with inflammation. We prioritize safe strategies to reduce inflammatory drivers and improve function.

This is educational; chronic pain benefits from coordinated care with your medical team.

Support during transitions

Pregnancy planning, menopause, return-to-work, or major life transitions can shift health priorities. Plans are tailored to each phase.

Specific medical or obstetric care should remain coordinated with your clinician.
