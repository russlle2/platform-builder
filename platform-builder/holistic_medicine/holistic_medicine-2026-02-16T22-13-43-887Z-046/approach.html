Our practical framework for lasting change

This page explains the steps most clients move through: intake, collaborative planning, follow-up rhythm, and how optional labs are used for education and clarity.

1) In-depth intake

An extended first visit (often 75–90 minutes) to review history, symptoms, lifestyle, and priorities. The goal is to understand root contributors and set realistic objectives.

We collect a health timeline, current meds/supplements, sleep and stress patterns, and relevant labs you already have.

2) Collaborative plan

We co-create an individualized plan with small, achievable steps across nutrition, movement, sleep, and stress-management that fit your schedule and values.

Plans prioritize safety, simplicity, and measurable milestones rather than overwhelming lists.

3) Follow-up rhythm

Short follow-ups focus on progress, troubleshooting, and refining the plan. Frequency is tailored — more hands-on at first, then shifting to maintenance.

Regular check-ins help build habits and keep momentum; they are where learning and adaptation happen.

4) Optional testing & learning

When useful, targeted labs (e.g., metabolic panels, micronutrient screens, or specialty testing) are proposed to clarify uncertainties and guide decisions.

Tests are educational: discussed in advance, used selectively, and interpreted to inform practical next steps.

How the VIP Day fits

A VIP Day is an intensive, structured session for people who want concentrated planning and rapid momentum. It includes deeper lifestyle mapping, tool-building, and a clear 90-day roadmap.

Education first

We emphasize teaching you the why and how so you can manage health independently over time.

Shared decision-making

Any recommendation, including labs or supplements, is a shared choice with clear rationale and a follow-up plan.
