How does Holistic Medicine specifically help parents with physical pain?

As a parent, your body endures unique stressors – lifting, bending, lack of sleep, and emotional demands. Our Holistic Medicine approach acknowledges these factors, focusing on somatic practices and gentle techniques to address the specific muscle tension, postural issues, and nervous system dysregulation that often lead to chronic pain in parents. We help you find practical, lasting relief.

I'm always short on time. How can I fit somatic healing into my busy schedule?

We understand your time is precious. Our somatic healing sessions are designed to be efficient yet impactful. We also equip you with simple, short practices you can integrate into your daily routine – moments of mindful movement or breathwork that can be done in minutes, helping you release tension and reconnect with your body even amidst the busiest days.

Is Holistic Medicine only for chronic pain, or can it help with everyday aches from parenting?

Holistic Medicine is beneficial for both! While we excel at addressing chronic, deep-seated pain, our methods are also incredibly effective for the everyday aches and stiffness that accumulate from the physical demands of parenting. We focus on building your body's resilience so those minor discomforts don't escalate into bigger issues, allowing you to feel more comfortable in your skin, daily.

Will somatic healing require me to talk extensively about my emotional stress?

While emotional stress often manifests physically, somatic healing focuses primarily on your body's sensations and responses. You are always in control of what you share. The process involves gentle guidance to notice physical feelings, movements, and breath, helping your body release tension without needing to delve deeply into verbal emotional narratives unless you choose to do so.
