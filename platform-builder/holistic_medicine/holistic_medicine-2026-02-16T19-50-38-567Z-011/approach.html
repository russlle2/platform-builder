A small map of how we work

Our practice centers on education, curiosity, and measurable small steps. We combine listening with clear micro-prescriptions to support sustainable change.

1. Thoughtful intake

The first visit is a time to tell your story: symptoms, rhythms, and what you hope to change. We gather context and identify immediate safety needs.

2. Collaborative plan

Plans are co-created and practical: clear experiments, suggested rhythms, simple dietary shifts, movement, and breathwork. We emphasize actions you can try this week.

3. Optional educational labs

When useful, we discuss non-invasive labs to provide additional insight. Labs are offered as educational tools; they are optional and decided collaboratively. Costs vary and are discussed up front.

Examples: targeted nutrient panels, food-sensitivity education, basic metabolic labs.

4. Follow-ups & refinement

Short follow-ups let us review responses, refine practices, and introduce next micro-habits. Progress is measured in small, steady wins.

What to expect from a visit

  • Curious listening without judgment.
  • Actionable, evidence-informed suggestions.
  • Respect for existing medical care and coordination where needed.
  • Practical micro-habits with tracking prompts.
