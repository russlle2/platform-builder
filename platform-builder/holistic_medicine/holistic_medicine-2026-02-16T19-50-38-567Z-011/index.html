Micro habits you can start today

Small, consistent practices accumulate into resilience. Choose one and practice it for two weeks.

  • 2-minute breath break — pause twice daily and breathe slowly for 2 minutes.
  • Evening wind-down — dim lights and a 20-minute screen-free ritual before bed.
  • Plant-forward plate — add a colorful vegetable to one meal each day.

These suggestions are educational. They are not a substitute for medical advice.
