Frequently Asked Questions

What if my child's sleep schedule is all over the place?
We don't require perfect alignment. We'll adjust your rest plan around their actual wake times using sleep science, not rigid schedules. You'll learn to rest during their short naps or quiet playtime, making rest part of your existing flow.
Do I need to learn a new bedtime routine for my child?
Absolutely not. Our approach focuses on your rest, not changing your child's sleep. We teach you how to pause for 10 minutes during their calm moments—using Eastern relaxation techniques—to recharge without adding steps to your routine.
How is your method different from sleep consultants?
Sleep consultants focus on your child. We help you reclaim rest through science-backed sleep hygiene paired with Eastern practices like mindful breathing. You'll get personalized tools that work with your current parenting style, not a new program to implement.
Can you help if I wake up exhausted every morning?
Yes. We identify the root cause—like cortisol spikes from stress—using sleep medicine principles. Then we integrate Eastern stress-relief methods to lower your anxiety. You'll see measurable sleep quality improvements in under 3 weeks, not just tips for better sleep.
