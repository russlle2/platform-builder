Frequently Asked Questions

How does Holistic Medicine specifically address anxiety-related physical pain for college students?
When you're under academic pressure, your body can tense up, leading to headaches, neck pain, or stomach issues. We use techniques like acupuncture, targeted bodywork, and mindful movement to calm your nervous system, release muscle tension, and reduce inflammation, directly addressing the physical symptoms that arise from anxiety.
Will these Holistic Medicine sessions fit into my demanding college schedule?
We understand your time is valuable. Our sessions are designed to be efficient yet effective. We'll work with you to find appointment times that align with your class schedule and commitments, ensuring you get the support you need without adding more stress to your plate.
Is this approach only for severe pain, or can it help with general stress and stiffness?
Our Holistic Medicine approach is beneficial for both acute pain and general discomfort. If you're experiencing persistent stiffness from studying, tension in your jaw from stress, or just a general feeling of being 'wound up,' we can help release that tension and teach you strategies to prevent it from building up in the future.
What can I expect during a first Holistic Medicine consultation?
Your first consultation is a conversation where we listen to your specific concerns about physical pain and academic stress. We'll discuss your health history, daily habits, and academic routine to understand the full picture. Then, we'll outline a personalized plan focusing on somatic healing techniques and practical steps you can take to start feeling better.
