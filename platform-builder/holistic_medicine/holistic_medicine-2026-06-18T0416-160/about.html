How does Holistic Medicine specifically address sleep challenges during significant life changes?

During major life shifts, your body experiences increased stress, which can manifest as sleep disturbances. Our Holistic Medicine approach looks beyond superficial symptoms, assessing your unique physiological and psychological responses to these changes. We then tailor interventions, such as adaptogenic herbs, targeted nutritional support, and advanced relaxation techniques, to directly mitigate the impact of stress on your sleep architecture, fostering deeper, more restorative rest.
