Frequently Asked Questions

I'm always busy. How can I possibly fit stress reduction into my already packed schedule?
We understand your time is precious. Our Holistic Medicine approach focuses on integrating small, impactful practices into your existing routine. You won't need to block out hours; instead, we'll find ways to create moments of calm that genuinely fit into your day, making stress reduction accessible and sustainable for you.
I've tried 'wellness' before and it felt overwhelming. Is this different?
Yes, completely. Our Holistic Medicine practice is designed specifically for working professionals aged 30-45 who are new to wellness or have felt overwhelmed by it. We keep things gentle, practical, and jargon-free, focusing on simple steps that address your burnout without adding more pressure. You don't need any prior experience to feel the benefits.
Will Holistic Medicine just tell me to meditate more? I struggle with that.
While mindfulness can be a tool, our Holistic Medicine approach is much broader than just meditation. We focus on various accessible techniques for nervous system regulation and stress reduction, which might include specific breathing exercises, gentle movement, dietary considerations, or understanding your body's signals. We find what truly resonates with you.
How quickly can I expect to feel a difference in my burnout?
Many clients report feeling a sense of calm and clarity even after their first few sessions as they begin to understand their nervous system better. Our Holistic Medicine guidance is designed to provide you with practical tools to start implementing immediately, leading to a noticeable reduction in stress and an improved sense of well-being over time as you integrate these practices.
