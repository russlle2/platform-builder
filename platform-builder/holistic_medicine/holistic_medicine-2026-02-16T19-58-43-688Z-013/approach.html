A compass for your health — clear, supportive steps

We use a layered approach: listening, mapping patterns, testing selectively, and supporting sustainable change through education and tailored plans. No guarantees—only a collaborative path forward.

1
Intake & listening
A thorough review of your history, current symptoms, lifestyle, and priorities. This is where we learn patterns and set realistic goals together.
2
Personalized plan
We craft an integrative plan that may include nutrition shifts, movement, sleep strategies, stress tools, and safe herbal or supplemental options when appropriate.
3
Optional labs (educational)
When data will clarify the plan, we discuss targeted lab testing. Tests are presented as educational tools—not mandatory steps—and are chosen only when they inform care.
4
Follow-ups & adjustment
Regular check-ins to monitor progress, refine strategies, and support habit formation. Follow-ups focus on realistic, measurable steps.

Practical things to expect

  • Initial visit: 60–90 minutes; follow-ups: 30–45 minutes.
  • Emphasis on education—understanding "why" behind recommendations.
  • Coordination with your medical providers when helpful.

Safety & scope

We do not replace urgent or specialized medical care. If concerning or emergent issues arise, you will be directed to appropriate services.

A note on testing and supplements

Labs can be useful educational tools, but they are not always necessary. When recommended, we explain the purpose, limitations, and how results might change care. Supplements or herbal supports are suggested cautiously, with attention to interactions and safety.

Frequently asked

Will this replace my current doctor?

No — we aim to complement and coordinate, not replace. For medication changes or urgent matters, we work with your primary providers.

How many visits will I need?

It depends on goals and complexity. We offer a hybrid model: short-term plans or ongoing support, decided together.
