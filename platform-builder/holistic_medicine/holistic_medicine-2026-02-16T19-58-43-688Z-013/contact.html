A short ritual to start — breath & orientation

Before a visit, we recommend a 3-minute settling practice: sit quietly, place a hand on your chest, breathe slowly in for 4, out for 6. Note one area you want to discuss. Small rituals help orient attention for a productive consult.

Do you coordinate with my primary doctor? Yes — with your permission we share plans and collaborate on treatments.

Are supplements recommended? Some patients benefit from targeted supplementation; we discuss evidence, dosing, and interactions before recommending anything.
