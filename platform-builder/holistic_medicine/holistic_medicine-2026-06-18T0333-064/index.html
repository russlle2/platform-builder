Frequently Asked Questions

How is this different from other stress reduction services?
Most stress programs assume you have energy to 'do' more. We meet you where you are—using gentle, low-energy practices that don't drain you. Our focus is on regulatory skills your nervous system actually needs, not just 'feeling better' temporarily.
Will this work if I'm too exhausted to try anything new?
Yes. We start with micro-practices that require minimal energy—like noticing your feet on the floor for 30 seconds. The goal isn't adding tasks; it's creating small, achievable moments that help your nervous system reset without fatigue.
How does this actually help with physical recovery?
When your nervous system is regulated, your body stops staying in stress mode. This lowers inflammation, improves sleep quality, and frees up energy for healing. It's not about 'fixing' your illness—it's about creating the internal space your body needs to recover.
Do you work with people who've tried 'holistic' approaches that didn't help?
Absolutely. Many people have been told to 'just eat clean' or 'meditate more' without addressing their nervous system's actual state. We skip the generic advice and focus on what your body needs *right now*—based on your lived experience of chronic illness or injury.
