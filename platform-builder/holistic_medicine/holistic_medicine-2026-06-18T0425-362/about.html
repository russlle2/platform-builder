What specific physical pain does Holistic Medicine address for new mothers?

Our Holistic Medicine approach for new mothers specifically targets common postpartum physical discomforts such as pelvic floor tension, lower back pain, sciatica, neck and shoulder stiffness from feeding, wrist and hand pain, and overall body soreness. We focus on restoring balance and ease.

How does somatic healing help with postpartum recovery?

Somatic healing in your postpartum recovery helps you tune into your body's innate wisdom. It addresses the physical manifestations of stress or trauma from birth, releasing held tension, improving body awareness, and calming your nervous system. This leads to a profound sense of physical and emotional release, easing chronic aches and promoting a feeling of being 'at home' in your body again.

Is this approach suitable if my birth was physically challenging?

Absolutely. Our Holistic Medicine and somatic healing practices are particularly beneficial if your birth experience left you with physical challenges or lingering discomforts. We provide gentle, trauma-informed care to help your body process, release tension, and regain strength at your own pace, honoring your unique experience.

How long will it take to feel relief with Holistic Medicine for postpartum pain?

The timeline for relief varies for every new mother, as each body heals uniquely. However, many clients report feeling a noticeable difference in their physical comfort and sense of calm within a few sessions. Our Holistic Medicine approach focuses on sustainable healing, equipping you with practices for long-term well-being beyond our time together.
