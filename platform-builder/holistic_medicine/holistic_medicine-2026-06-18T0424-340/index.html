Frequently Asked Questions

How can Holistic Medicine help with physical pain during a life transition?
Major life transitions often create stress that manifests as physical pain, tension, or fatigue. Our Holistic Medicine approach helps you understand and address these somatic responses by integrating gentle bodywork, targeted movement, and nervous system regulation techniques. We provide tools to calm your body's stress response and alleviate discomfort, allowing you to adapt more smoothly to your new circumstances.
What kind of somatic healing methods do you use in Holistic Medicine?
In our Holistic Medicine practice, we focus on evidence-informed somatic healing methods. These include techniques designed to release chronic tension, improve body awareness, and re-pattern movement. We utilize practices that help you reconnect with your body's signals, fostering a sense of grounding and stability, which is especially beneficial when you're navigating significant life shifts.
Is Holistic Medicine suitable if my pain isn't directly from an injury?
Absolutely. Many individuals experience physical pain or discomfort that isn't tied to a specific injury but rather to chronic stress, emotional shifts, or long-term postural patterns—all common during life transitions. Our Holistic Medicine approach is specifically designed to address these less obvious somatic connections, helping you find relief and restore balance to your body's systems.
How long does it typically take to see results with Holistic Medicine for physical pain?
The timeline for seeing results with Holistic Medicine varies for everyone, as your body's response is unique. However, many of our clients report experiencing a sense of ease and reduced discomfort within a few sessions. We focus on sustainable, long-term relief by equipping you with practices you can integrate into your daily life, supporting your body through ongoing transitions.
