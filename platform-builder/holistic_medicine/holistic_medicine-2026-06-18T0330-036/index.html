Frequently Asked Questions

How does Holistic Medicine specifically address nervous system dysregulation for someone recovering from chronic illness?
Our Holistic Medicine approach recognizes that chronic illness and injury can put your nervous system into overdrive. We use precise assessments to understand your unique patterns of dysregulation, then implement targeted interventions like specific nutritional protocols, vagal nerve stimulation techniques, and gentle movement therapies designed to calm an overactive stress response and enhance your body's natural healing capacity.
Can Holistic Medicine really help reduce the constant feeling of stress often associated with chronic health conditions?
Absolutely. The persistent stress from chronic conditions can be exhausting. Our Holistic Medicine methods are directly aimed at reducing that physiological burden. We focus on practical, actionable strategies that you can integrate into your daily life, helping to lower cortisol levels, improve sleep quality, and foster a sense of calm, which is crucial for your recovery process.
What kind of techniques are used in Holistic Medicine to regulate the nervous system after an injury or long-term illness?
We employ a science-backed, personalized toolkit. This can include specialized breathing exercises to activate your parasympathetic nervous system, specific dietary adjustments to support neurotransmitter balance, targeted botanical medicine for nerve health, and gentle mind-body practices to improve interoception. Each technique is chosen to specifically support your body's recovery and nervous system regulation.
How long does it take to see results in nervous system regulation with Holistic Medicine?
The timeline for seeing results can vary, as your body's response is unique. However, many clients report feeling a noticeable shift in their stress levels and overall calm within a few weeks of consistent practice with our Holistic Medicine strategies. Our goal is to provide you with sustainable tools that empower you to manage and improve your nervous system health over the long term, supporting your full recovery.
