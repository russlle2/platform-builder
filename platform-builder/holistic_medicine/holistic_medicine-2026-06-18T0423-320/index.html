Frequently Asked Questions

How does Holistic Medicine address physical pain specifically related to life transitions?
Major life changes can manifest as physical pain, tension, or fatigue. Our Holistic Medicine approach acknowledges this mind-body connection, offering therapies that not only target the pain directly but also help your nervous system regulate the stress associated with your transition. We focus on restoring your body's equilibrium and resilience.
Is your approach to somatic healing evidence-based?
Yes, our somatic healing methods are grounded in scientific understanding of the nervous system and body-mind physiology. We draw from modalities proven to release stored tension, improve body awareness, and reduce chronic pain, ensuring your care is both compassionate and clinically sound.
What can I expect in a first session for physical pain during a life transition?
Your initial session will involve a thorough discussion of your current physical symptoms, your life changes, and your health history. We'll conduct a gentle physical assessment and then collaboratively establish a clear, personalized plan for your care, focusing on immediate relief and long-term well-being. It's a space for you to feel heard and understood.
Will Holistic Medicine treatments interfere with other medical care I'm receiving?
We prioritize an integrative approach. It's important to inform us of any other medical treatments you are undergoing. We can then ensure our Holistic Medicine care complements your existing healthcare plan, always putting your safety and well-being first. Our aim is to work alongside your current providers for optimal results.
