How does Holistic Medicine specifically address physical pain experienced by parents?

For parents, physical pain often originates from repetitive motions, poor posture during childcare, and chronic stress. Our Holistic Medicine approach identifies these specific triggers and employs targeted somatic therapies to release accumulated tension, re-educate movement patterns, and reduce inflammation, providing relief that acknowledges your unique lifestyle.

I'm always short on time. How can I fit somatic healing into my busy parenting schedule?

We understand your schedule is incredibly demanding. Our sessions are designed for maximum efficacy within a focused timeframe. We also provide practical, brief somatic practices you can integrate into your day, empowering you to maintain progress between appointments without adding further burden to your already full plate.

Will somatic healing truly help with the stress and exhaustion that comes with managing a household?

Absolutely. Somatic healing directly addresses how stress manifests in your body — as tension, fatigue, and pain. By releasing these physical imprints, you'll experience a profound sense of lightness and calm, improving your capacity to manage household demands with greater resilience and less exhaustion. It's about restoring your inner equilibrium.

What makes your Holistic Medicine approach different for parents compared to a general practice?

Our practice is distinguished by its deep understanding of the unique physical and emotional stressors faced by parents of young children. We don't offer generic treatments; instead, we tailor every aspect of your Holistic Medicine experience, from initial assessment to ongoing care, to directly address the specific impacts of parenting on your body and well-being.
