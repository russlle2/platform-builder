Our working method: layered assessment + simple rhythms

We design care around systems and the demands of daily life — mapping drivers quickly, choosing low-burden tests or trials, and sequencing follow-ups to maintain momentum.

Quick view: what to expect

An intake maps priorities and creates a 60–90 minute agenda. Follow-ups are scheduled to match need — weekly for early stabilization, then spaced as gains consolidate.

Listen & Map
A focused history: symptoms, routines, resources and daily stressors.
Low-risk trials
Short, measurable experiments that carry minimal downside — sleep tweaks, movement anchors, micro-habits.
Coordination
When needed, we coordinate labs and referrals and keep your plan simple across providers.

How we set follow-up cadence

Cadence balances urgency, complexity and your real-life bandwidth. Typical templates we use:

  • Initial stabilization: weekly or biweekly check-ins for the first 4–6 weeks.
  • Active cycle: 6–8 week guided rhythm with short check-ins and tools.
  • Maintenance: monthly or as-needed planning to sustain gains.

During intake we co-design a cadence that you can keep up with — flexibility matters as much as clinical priority.
