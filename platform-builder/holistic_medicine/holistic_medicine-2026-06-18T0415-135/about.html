How does Holistic Medicine specifically help college students with sleep?

We focus on the unique stressors of college life – academic demands, social pressures, and irregular schedules – to create a personalized Holistic Medicine plan. This often involves optimizing your circadian rhythm, integrating targeted stress reduction techniques before bed, and nutritional support to improve sleep quality.

I've tried everything for my sleep. What makes Holistic Medicine different?

Unlike generic sleep advice, our Holistic Medicine approach looks at your entire system. We go beyond surface-level fixes to identify underlying imbalances contributing to your sleep issues, such as chronic stress, nutritional deficiencies, or light exposure patterns, offering a more comprehensive and sustainable solution.

Will Holistic Medicine treatments interfere with my demanding college schedule?

Absolutely not. Our Holistic Medicine strategies are designed to integrate seamlessly into your busy college life. We prioritize practical, time-efficient techniques that you can easily implement, ensuring that improving your sleep doesn't add another layer of stress to your already packed schedule.

Is Holistic Medicine backed by science for sleep improvement?

Yes, our Holistic Medicine practice utilizes evidence-informed modalities. We draw from current research in neuroscience, chronobiology, and psychoneuroimmunology to ensure our recommendations for sleep and anxiety are scientifically sound and effective for college students.
