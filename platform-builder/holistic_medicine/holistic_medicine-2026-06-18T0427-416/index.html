Frequently Asked Questions

How does Holistic Medicine specifically address mental clarity issues after a chronic illness or injury?
Our Holistic Medicine approach involves a comprehensive assessment to identify specific physiological factors impacting your cognitive function, such as inflammation, nutrient deficiencies, or altered gut-brain axis communication. We then develop a personalized plan using targeted nutrition, botanicals, and lifestyle modifications designed to optimize your brain health and restore mental sharpness.
Is the approach at Sage Garden Holistic Medicine suitable if my primary concern is memory and focus after an accident?
Absolutely. If you're experiencing challenges with memory, focus, or processing speed following an accident, our Holistic Medicine framework is specifically designed to help. We evaluate potential neurochemical imbalances and systemic stressors that may be contributing to your symptoms, then implement science-backed strategies to support neural repair and enhance your cognitive performance.
What kind of evidence supports the methods used in Holistic Medicine for cognitive wellness?
Our methods are rooted in current scientific literature, drawing from fields like neuroendocrinology, nutritional science, and psychoneuroimmunology. We integrate practices with demonstrated efficacy in supporting neurological health, reducing inflammation, and optimizing brain function, ensuring your treatment plan is both evidence-informed and tailored to your specific needs.
How long does it typically take to see improvements in mental clarity with Holistic Medicine after a chronic health issue?
The timeline for improvement varies depending on the complexity and duration of your chronic health issue, as well as your individual response to treatment. Our approach is systematic and patient, focusing on sustainable progress. While some individuals report subtle shifts within weeks, significant and lasting improvements in mental clarity often develop over several months as foundational imbalances are addressed.
