Frequently Asked Questions

How does Holistic Medicine specifically address emotional healing for athletes under physical stress?
For athletes, physical stress often correlates with emotional strain. Our Holistic Medicine approach focuses on identifying and processing the emotional roots of your physical symptoms, such as chronic pain or performance plateaus. We help you release stored tension and trauma, allowing your body to recover more efficiently and your mind to achieve optimal focus.
I've always been told to 'tough it out.' How can trauma-informed care benefit my athletic performance?
The 'tough it out' mentality can sometimes lead to suppressing emotions, which can manifest as physical issues or mental blocks. Trauma-informed care helps you understand how past experiences affect your present reactions and performance. By gently addressing these, you can build true resilience, not just suppression, leading to more consistent and powerful performance.
Will addressing emotional healing interfere with my training schedule or competitive goals?
Quite the opposite. Our goal is to enhance your training and competitive edge by removing emotional barriers. We work collaboratively to integrate emotional healing into your existing routine, ensuring it complements your physical regimen and supports your goals, ultimately leading to more sustainable success and fewer setbacks.
What should I expect during my first session focused on emotional healing in Holistic Medicine?
Your first session will be a confidential space to discuss your unique challenges, both physical and emotional. We'll listen empathetically to your experiences and goals, beginning to understand the connection between your emotional well-being and athletic performance. This allows us to craft a personalized plan that supports your journey toward greater resilience and peak health.
