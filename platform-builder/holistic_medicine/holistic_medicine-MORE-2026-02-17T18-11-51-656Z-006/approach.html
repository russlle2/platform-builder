Our method is rooted in small experiments

We favor short, practical tests you can fold into your life and refine. Each suggestion is framed as a trial with clear measures — duration, what to notice, and when to reassess.

Observe

We start by mapping daily patterns, not by labeling. A simple 3-day rhythm log often reveals where tiny shifts matter most.

Try

Short, practical steps are introduced — a bedtime cue, a 5-minute breath practice, or a mealtime tweak. The emphasis is on what you can sustain.

Reflect

At follow-up we review what changed, and whether the step fits your life. If a practice isn't useful, we adapt rather than persist.

Shared language

We use simple phrases so plans are easy to recall: "small daily cue," "evening unwind," or "two-minute reset." Language is practical and descriptive, not clinical.

Collaboration and boundaries

Our role is to listen, provide evidence-informed strategies, and point to other supports when appropriate. We do not replace medical or psychiatric care. When needed, we help coordinate referrals.
