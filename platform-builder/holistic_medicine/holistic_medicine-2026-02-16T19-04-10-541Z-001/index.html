Myths vs. truths

A playful reality-check: understand what integrative care actually offers.

Myth: Natural = harmless and universally curative

Truth: "Natural" options have effects, interactions, and limits. We weigh benefits and risks with evidence and your story.

Myth: One protocol fixes everything

Truth: Sustainable change is individualized. We prioritize education and layered interventions, not one-size-fits-all promises.

Myth: Holistic care avoids science

Truth: Integrative care blends lab insights, conventional treatments, and lifestyle strategies to support long-term wellbeing.

Common questions

Do you offer testing?

Yes — we discuss optional, evidence-informed labs during intake. Testing is educational and used only when it will change management.

Will you replace my current doctor?

We coordinate care. We do not replace necessary specialty or primary care but often work alongside your team to optimize outcomes.

Are results guaranteed?

No. Medicine has uncertainty. We aim to maximize your chance of measurable improvement through education, monitoring, and iterative plans.

Ready for an intensive, human-centered plan?

We spend time to understand context, labs, and life logistics so your plan is realistic and sustainable.
