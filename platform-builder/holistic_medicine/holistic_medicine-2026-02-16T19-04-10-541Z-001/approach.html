How we work: intake, plan, follow-through

An educational, collaborative approach—measuring what matters and changing what you can.

1. Thorough intake

Deep history, medication review and symptom mapping so we know where to start.

2. Prioritized plan

Time-bound goals, simple nutrition & movement frameworks, stress tools and education.

3. Optional testing

If helpful, we may suggest targeted labs—stool, hormone panels or nutrient screens—as educational tools.

4. Close follow-up

Regular check-ins, adjustments and skill-building to make changes stick.
Educational labs

Labs are used selectively to add clarity. We always review results in plain language and balance them with clinical context. Tests are never required to begin working together.

Working with providers & specialists

We collaborate with primary care and specialists when needed. If a referral or urgent medical workup is indicated, we facilitate communication. Our role is education, coordination and whole-person support—never a substitute for emergency care.

Follow-ups & adjustments

Follow-ups are scheduled depending on the plan—commonly 2–6 weeks after the initial visit for progress checks. Intensive programs include weekly or biweekly contact.
