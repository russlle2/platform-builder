Frequently Asked Questions

How does Holistic Medicine at specifically help with athletic stress?
We focus on your body's stress response and nervous system regulation. This means looking beyond just symptoms to understand how training, nutrition, sleep, and mental load impact your overall system. Our approaches are designed to reduce inflammation, improve recovery times, and build resilience against the intense demands of an active lifestyle.
Is this approach suitable if I'm not a professional athlete, but still very active?
Absolutely. Whether you're a weekend warrior, a dedicated cross-fitter, or someone who loves staying active, the principles of stress reduction and nervous system regulation are vital for everyone. We tailor our Holistic Medicine support to your specific activity level and lifestyle, ensuring you can maintain your passion without compromising your health.
What kind of practices or therapies might be involved in nervous system regulation?
Our Holistic Medicine approach can include a range of evidence-informed practices. This might involve breathwork techniques, targeted nutritional recommendations, lifestyle adjustments for better sleep, and gentle movement practices designed to calm your fight-or-flight response. The specific tools we use are always personalized to your unique needs and goals.
How quickly can I expect to see improvements in my stress levels or recovery?
While individual results vary, many of our active clients report feeling a difference in their stress levels, sleep quality, and overall sense of calm within a few weeks of consistent practice. Our Holistic Medicine approach emphasizes building sustainable habits, so the long-term benefits of a regulated nervous system will continue to support your performance and well-being.
