Frequently Asked Questions

I'm going through a big life change. How can Holistic Medicine help my emotions?
When life shifts, your emotions can feel chaotic. Our Holistic Medicine approach offers a gentle way to process these feelings, helping you understand their roots and develop coping strategies. We focus on emotional healing, providing a compassionate space for you to feel heard and supported through your transition.
I don't have any experience with wellness practices. Is this still for me?
Absolutely! Our Holistic Medicine practice is designed to be accessible to everyone, regardless of your past experience with wellness. We use conversational and approachable language, ensuring you feel comfortable and understood from your very first interaction. There's no prior knowledge needed, just a willingness to explore your emotional wellbeing.
What does 'trauma-informed care' mean in the context of Holistic Medicine?
Trauma-informed care means we understand that challenging experiences can impact your current emotional responses, especially during major changes. Our Holistic Medicine approach prioritizes your safety and autonomy, working at your pace to help you process and integrate these experiences gently, without re-traumatization. We create a supportive environment where your past is acknowledged and respected.
I feel really overwhelmed by my current situation. Can Holistic Medicine truly help with this emotional burden?
Feeling overwhelmed during a major transition is a very common experience. Our Holistic Medicine practice specializes in emotional healing, offering gentle strategies to help you manage and lighten that burden. We'll work together to identify the sources of your overwhelm and equip you with tools to navigate these complex emotions, bringing you a sense of calm and clarity.
