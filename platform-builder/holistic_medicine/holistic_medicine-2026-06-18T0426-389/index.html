Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is Holistic Medicine and how can it help my body as a parent?
Holistic Medicine at looks at your whole self – not just your pain. For parents, this means we consider the physical demands of childcare, the stress of household management, and how these factors contribute to aches in your back, shoulders, or neck. We use gentle, non-invasive techniques to ease these specific tensions, helping your body feel more balanced and less strained.
I'm always busy with my kids. How can I possibly fit Holistic Medicine into my schedule?
We completely understand your limited time. Our Holistic Medicine sessions are structured to be efficient and focused on giving you immediate, practical relief. We also provide simple, short practices you can do at home, even for just a few minutes, to maintain your comfort. Our aim is to support you without adding more stress to your already full day.
I’m new to Holistic Medicine and somatic healing. Is this suitable for someone like me?
Absolutely! Our approach to Holistic Medicine and somatic healing is designed to be gentle and accessible for everyone, especially those new to these concepts. There's no prior experience needed. We'll guide you step-by-step, making sure you feel comfortable and understood throughout the process. Our focus is on simple, clear techniques to help your body feel better.
Can Holistic Medicine truly help with the chronic aches I get from lifting and carrying my children?
Yes, many parents experience specific pains from the physical demands of raising young children – from lower back strain to sore wrists and shoulders. Our Holistic Medicine practices are tailored to address these common issues. We work with your body to release chronic tension, improve your posture, and teach you how to move more comfortably, reducing the daily aches that come with parenting.
