Frequently Asked Questions

How does Holistic Medicine at specifically address sleep concerns for seniors?
We focus on gentle, non-invasive methods that respect your body's natural rhythms. Our Holistic Medicine approach for seniors involves evaluating your individual health, medications, and lifestyle to identify root causes of sleep disturbances, rather than just masking symptoms. We then create a personalized plan using evidence-based natural therapies.
Will the Holistic Medicine treatments interfere with my current medications?
Your safety and well-being are our top priority. Before recommending any Holistic Medicine treatments, will conduct a thorough review of your current medications and health conditions. We ensure that all our recommendations are safe and complementary to any existing medical care you are receiving, always advocating for open communication with your primary care physician.
What kind of results can I expect from Holistic Medicine for sleep improvement?
Many seniors report improvements in falling asleep more easily, experiencing fewer awakenings, and feeling more refreshed upon waking. Our Holistic Medicine methods aim for sustainable improvements in your sleep quality. While results vary for each individual, our goal is to help you achieve more consistent and restorative rest through gentle, integrated care.
Is Holistic Medicine only for severe sleep issues, or can it help with general restlessness?
Holistic Medicine is beneficial for a wide range of sleep concerns, from occasional restlessness to more persistent difficulties. Our approach is designed to optimize your body's natural sleep processes, helping you achieve deeper, more satisfying rest, whether your challenges are mild or more pronounced. We focus on enhancing your overall well-being to support better sleep.
