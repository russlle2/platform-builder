Conditions we commonly support

Below are common areas where people choose an integrative, evidence-aware approach. This is educational and not a diagnosis. If you are in crisis, please call your local emergency services.

Clinical disclaimer & scope

Our materials and consultations are informational and supportive. We do not promise cures. Clinical recommendations are personalized after review of your history, medications, and tests. For urgent or emergency needs, seek immediate care.

Sleep disturbances

Practical circadian strategies, behavioral supports, and collaboration with sleep medicine when needed. We aim to reduce fragmentation and improve daytime function.

Chronic fatigue & low energy

A systematic approach: activity pacing, nutrition, targeted testing, and stepwise reconditioning. We prioritize safety and rule-out of medical causes.

Digestive complaints

Food pattern adjustments, symptom tracking, and selective testing to build an achievable nutrition plan without unnecessary restrictions.

Anxiety & mood concerns

Psychological skills, lifestyle supports, and coordination with mental health specialists. Medication discussion is part of shared decision-making when appropriate.

Metabolic issues

Practical changes aimed at glucose resilience, sleep hygiene, and activity structure; we partner with primary care for medical management if needed.

Multisystem, unclear symptoms

When symptoms cross systems, we prioritize clarity: a structured intake, selective testing, and a time-limited plan to reduce noise and find the high-yield next steps.

How we work with concerns

  1. Listen closely to the story and context.
  2. Identify safety issues and necessary medical checks.
  3. Prioritize 1–3 evidence-informed steps you can implement.
  4. Iterate with measurement and adjustments that fit your life.

If your situation involves urgent risk or a medical emergency, please call local emergency services or contact your primary care immediately.
