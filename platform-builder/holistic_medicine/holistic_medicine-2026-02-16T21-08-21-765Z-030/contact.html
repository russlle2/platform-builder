FAQ

Do you guarantee a cure?
No. Our approach is educational and collaborative. We provide personalized recommendations that support health, but there are no guarantees. Outcomes depend on many factors.
Can I do lab testing during the VIP Day?
We can arrange same-day or follow-up labs where appropriate. Some tests require preparation — we’ll advise what’s best for your case.
Is this right for chronic conditions?
The VIP Day is ideal for building a focused plan for chronic issues like sleep disruption, digestive concerns, or persistent low energy. We emphasize realistic, evidence-informed strategies.
How do I prepare?
We’ll send an intake packet and practical pre-visit checklist once you book. Bringing a list of current meds, supplements, and any recent labs is helpful.
