What to expect during your VIP Day

A practical walkthrough of the visit so you arrive ready and confident.

  1. Warm intake: a guided review of your history, daily rhythm, and priorities.
  2. Focused assessment: systems-based evaluation — digestion, sleep, stress response, and inflammation patterns.
  3. Education: explanations of why symptoms occur and how the plan addresses root contributors.
  4. Personalized plan: prioritized steps, nutrition, targeted supplements when indicated, movement and sleep guidance.
  5. Follow-up: a 30-minute check-in within 2 weeks to refine the plan.

Optional: targeted labs can be discussed as educational tools; they are not mandatory and are used selectively to clarify pathways.
