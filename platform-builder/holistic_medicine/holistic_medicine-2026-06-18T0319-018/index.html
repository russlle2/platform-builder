Frequently Asked Questions

How does Holistic Medicine specifically address stress for retirees?
Our Holistic Medicine approach for retirees focuses on the subtle ways stress impacts cognitive function and physical energy as we age. We use targeted, gentle interventions to help your nervous system shift from a state of 'fight or flight' to 'rest and digest,' which is crucial for maintaining mental clarity and reducing fatigue.
Can Holistic Medicine truly help me maintain my mental sharpness?
Absolutely. By regulating your nervous system, our Holistic Medicine practices reduce the physiological burden of stress that can cloud thinking and impact memory. When your system is calmer and more balanced, your brain can function more optimally, helping you sustain your focus and mental acuity.
What should I expect during my first Holistic Medicine consultation?
During your initial Holistic Medicine consultation, we will have a detailed, compassionate conversation about your current well-being, your health history, and your goals for vitality in retirement. We'll explore how stress might be affecting you and discuss a personalized plan grounded in evidence-based strategies to support your nervous system regulation.
Is Holistic Medicine a suitable complement to my existing medical care?
Yes, our Holistic Medicine services are designed to complement and enhance your existing medical care. We focus on areas like stress reduction and nervous system regulation that often aren't fully addressed in conventional settings, providing a supportive layer that helps your body and mind function better alongside any treatments you may already be receiving.
