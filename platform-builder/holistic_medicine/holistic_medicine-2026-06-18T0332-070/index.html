Frequently Asked Questions

How can Holistic Medicine specifically help with stress during a big life change?
During significant transitions, your nervous system can go into overdrive. Holistic Medicine provides a personalized toolkit, using gentle, natural approaches like adaptogenic herbs, targeted nutritional support, and specific breathwork to calm the 'fight or flight' response. This helps your body and mind adapt more smoothly, reducing the emotional and physical impact of stress.
What does nervous system regulation mean in the context of life transitions?
Nervous system regulation means teaching your body how to shift more easily between states of calm and alertness. When you're going through a big life change, your nervous system might get 'stuck' in high alert. Holistic Medicine helps re-pattern these responses, so you can feel more grounded, sleep better, and react to new challenges with greater composure, rather than feeling constantly overwhelmed.
Is Holistic Medicine a good fit if I'm feeling overwhelmed by a major life event?
Absolutely. Overwhelm is a common signal that your system is struggling to cope with change. Our Holistic Medicine approach is designed to meet you where you are, offering strategies to gently reduce that feeling of being swamped. We'll work together to build your resilience, so you can navigate your life transition with more clarity and less emotional strain.
What can I expect in a first consultation for Holistic Medicine focusing on life transitions?
In your first Holistic Medicine consultation, we'll have a conversational discussion about your current life transition, how it's impacting your stress levels, and the specific ways your nervous system is reacting. We'll explore your health history, daily routines, and personal goals to co-create a personalized plan focused on bringing more calm and stability into your life during this important time.
