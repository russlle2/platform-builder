Frequently Asked Questions

How does Holistic Medicine specifically help athletes manage physical stress?
For athletes, physical stress often carries an emotional component, whether it's performance anxiety, injury frustration, or the pressure of competition. Our Holistic Medicine approach addresses these intertwined aspects by providing tools and support to process the emotional impact, which in turn can improve physical recovery, mental clarity, and overall resilience in your active life.
What does 'trauma-informed care' mean for someone who is physically active?
Trauma-informed care means we recognize that past experiences, even seemingly minor ones, can influence your present reactions to physical stress, pain, or performance demands. We create a safe and supportive environment where your experiences are honored, helping you to understand and gently release emotional patterns that might be hindering your physical progress or well-being.
Will Holistic Medicine replace my existing physical training or medical treatments?
No, our Holistic Medicine services are designed to complement your existing physical training, rehabilitation, and medical care. We focus on the emotional and energetic aspects that can significantly impact your physical health and performance. We work collaboratively to support your overall well-being, providing an additional layer of care for a more integrated approach to your health.
How can I tell if emotional healing could benefit my physical recovery or performance?
If you notice persistent fatigue despite adequate rest, recurring injuries without clear physical cause, difficulty sleeping, or feelings of overwhelm or frustration related to your physical activity, emotional healing through Holistic Medicine could be beneficial. These are often signs that your body and mind are holding onto stress that needs gentle, intentional release.
