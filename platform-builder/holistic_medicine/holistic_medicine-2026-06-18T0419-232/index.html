Frequently Asked Questions

How does Holistic Medicine specifically address postpartum emotional health?
Our Holistic Medicine approach looks at your entire system – your sleep, nutrition, hormonal shifts, and emotional processing – to understand the root causes of your postpartum emotional experiences. We don't just focus on symptoms; we aim to support your body's innate ability to recalibrate and heal after childbirth, offering specialized care for new mothers.
I'm feeling overwhelmed and perhaps traumatized by my birth experience. Can you help?
Absolutely. Our practice is trauma-informed, meaning we understand the potential impact of birth on your emotional well-being. We offer a safe and confidential space to process these experiences, using gentle techniques designed to help you integrate and move through difficult emotions without re-traumatization. Your emotional safety is our priority.
What kind of techniques or practices do you use in your emotional support for new mothers?
We employ a range of evidence-informed practices tailored to your specific needs, focusing on nervous system regulation, somatic awareness, and gentle cognitive reframing. This might include breathwork, targeted mindfulness, and specific strategies to help you navigate anxiety or intrusive thoughts common in the postpartum period, always with a focus on your unique experience.
Is this a replacement for traditional therapy or medical care for postpartum depression?
Our Holistic Medicine services are designed to complement, not replace, traditional medical or psychological care for conditions like postpartum depression. We work alongside your existing healthcare providers to offer comprehensive support, focusing on the emotional and energetic aspects of your recovery. We can also help you connect with appropriate referrals if needed.
