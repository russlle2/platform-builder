Frequently Asked Questions

How does Holistic Medicine specifically address sleep issues for new mothers?
We look at the whole picture of your health – hormonal shifts, nutritional deficiencies from pregnancy and breastfeeding, stress levels, and nervous system regulation. By addressing these interconnected factors, our Holistic Medicine approach aims to rebalance your body and mind, creating a foundation for more restful sleep during your postpartum recovery.
Is Holistic Medicine safe while I'm breastfeeding or recovering from childbirth?
Absolutely. Your safety and well-being are our top priority. We carefully select gentle, evidence-informed modalities that are appropriate for breastfeeding mothers and those recovering postpartum. We'll discuss any concerns and ensure your personalized plan is tailored to your specific circumstances.
What kind of commitment is required to see improvements in my sleep with Holistic Medicine?
Improvements can be gradual, but many new mothers notice shifts within a few weeks of implementing their personalized Holistic Medicine plan. We recommend committing to the process, as true restorative change often involves consistent, gentle adjustments rather than quick fixes. We'll be with you every step of the way.
How can Holistic Medicine help me feel more rested even if I can't get a full night's sleep?
We focus on optimizing the quality of the sleep you do get, and supporting your energy throughout the day. This includes strategies to reduce fragmented sleep, enhance deep sleep phases, and practical tools to manage fatigue and promote restorative moments, even when long stretches of sleep aren't possible. Our Holistic Medicine approach emphasizes overall vitality.
