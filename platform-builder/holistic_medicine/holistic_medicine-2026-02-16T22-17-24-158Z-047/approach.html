My approach: scaffolded, science-forward, person-first

I blend research-aligned tools with lifestyle medicine and mindful rituals. The process is educational: we learn what works for you and build simple practices that fit your life.

Quick framework
Intake → Tailored Plan → Follow-ups → Ongoing refinement
Begin intake

Intake & story-gathering

We start with a generous intake: history, symptoms, routines, relationships with food, movement, and sleep. This is a partnership — you bring the context, I bring frameworks and curiosity. Typical intake length: 90–150 minutes depending on needs.

Co-created plan

Plans focus on a few high-leverage changes: 1–3 practical rituals (sleep cues, micro-movements, breath practice), clear nutrition or herbal education when relevant, and measurable, time-bound goals. Plans are intentionally small to encourage adherence.

Follow-ups & iteration

Follow-up sessions refine interventions, troubleshoot barriers, and teach small skills. These check-ins are tools for learning, not judgement. Frequency depends on your needs: weekly, biweekly, or monthly.

Optional labs (educational)

When labs can add clarity (e.g., basic metabolic markers, targeted nutrient panels, or sleep assessments), we discuss what to test, what the results might mean, and how they might shift the plan. Ordering or interpreting labs is always collaborative and framed as educational information, not definitive cures.

When labs help
Unexplained fatigue, metabolic concerns, persistent gut issues.
When labs may not be needed
Mild, recent symptoms that respond to simple habit changes.

A note on safety & coordination

If you are under medical care, we welcome coordination. Integrative work complements ongoing medical treatment; we do not replace urgent or emergency care. Any herbal or supplemental recommendations are discussed with attention to interactions and safety.

Common questions

How many sessions will I need?

It varies. Some people see meaningful shifts in a few visits; others benefit from ongoing coaching. We review progress and adjust timelines together.

Do you prescribe medication?

No — this practice offers education and integrative strategies. For prescription needs, we coordinate with your medical provider as appropriate.
