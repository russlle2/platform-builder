What to expect in an intensive plan

We design an evidence-informed roadmap. Your journey may include in-depth intake, optional labs for extra clarity, a personalized plan, and frequent follow-ups during the intensive period.

1. Intake

A comprehensive conversation about your history, priorities, and context.

2. Data & Options

Optional labs and diagnostics are presented as educational tools — not as a single answer.

3. Personalized Plan

Targeted interventions: nutrition, supplements (if appropriate), mind-body practices, and referrals.

4. Follow-ups

Structured check-ins to refine the plan and teach you practical skills for self-management.

Scheduling & Flow

An intensive typically runs across a concentrated window with regular touchpoints so we can iterate quickly and keep momentum.

  • Initial visit — 75 minutes (intake & baseline)
  • Plan visit — 45 minutes (review labs, set interventions)
  • Weekly check-ins — 20-30 minutes (adjust and teach)
  • Wrap & maintenance — 30-45 minutes (transition plan)
