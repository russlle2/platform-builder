FAQ — quick answers

Do you accept insurance?
We do not bill insurance directly in most cases; we can provide receipts you can submit to your insurer. Check with your plan for out-of-network benefits.
Do you offer telehealth?
Yes. Virtual sessions are available across state lines where permitted. In-person visits are available in {{CITY}}, {{STATE}}.
Are lab tests required?
Labs are optional and used for education and targeted planning. We discuss utility, costs, and interpretation before recommending any test.
What if I need to cancel?
We ask for 24 hours notice for cancellations. Late cancellations or no-shows may incur a fee. Contact us to discuss exceptions.
Is this a cure?
We focus on education, resilience, and measurable steps. While many patients improve their symptoms with consistent work, we do not promise cures.
