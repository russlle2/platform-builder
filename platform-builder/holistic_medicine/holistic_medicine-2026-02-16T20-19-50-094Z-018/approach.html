A thoughtful, teachable approach — step by step

We move at a pace that fits your life. Every recommendation is backed by explanation so you leave each visit with clarity and next steps you can actually do.

Quick philosophy

Whole-person care: sleep, movement, nutrition, and stress all inform our plans — plus targeted testing only when it informs meaningful change.

Process overview

1

Comprehensive Intake

A 75–90 minute visit to understand your history, lifestyle, symptoms, and goals — we listen more than we lecture.

2

Collaborative Plan

Tailored lifestyle steps, prioritized so the most impactful changes come first. Clear rationale for each suggestion so you know why it matters.

3

Follow-ups & Adjustments

Shorter sessions to review progress, troubleshoot barriers, and adapt the plan. Accountability that feels supportive, not pressuring.

4

Long-term Maintenance

Skills and routines to maintain gains: gradual habit stacking and preventive check-ins to keep momentum without burnout.

Optional labs & testing (educational)

When indicated, lab work is used to inform discussions — for example, basic metabolic panels, thyroid screening, nutrient markers, or targeted inflammatory tests. Testing is offered as an educational tool, not as a guaranteed fix; results are interpreted in context of your symptoms and preferences.

Questions people often ask

How soon will I feel better?
Every person is different. Some notice changes in sleep or mood within a few weeks; others need months for steady improvements. We set realistic, measurable steps.
Will I be pushed to take supplements?
Supplement suggestions are optional and explained clearly. The goal is to use the minimum necessary support while prioritizing sustainable lifestyle changes.
Do you work with specialists?
Yes — collaborative care is part of the model. When a condition is beyond the scope of integrative care, we coordinate referrals and co-manage thoughtfully.
