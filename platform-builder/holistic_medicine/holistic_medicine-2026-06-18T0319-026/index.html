Frequently Asked Questions

How can Holistic Medicine specifically help my nervous system when I'm recovering from chronic illness or injury?
Our Holistic Medicine approach focuses on recalibrating your body's stress response, which often goes into overdrive during chronic illness or injury recovery. We employ gentle, ancient techniques to down-regulate your sympathetic nervous system and activate your parasympathetic (rest and digest) system. This fosters a calm internal environment essential for your body to prioritize healing and repair, rather than perpetually reacting to perceived threats.
Will the practices be too demanding for my body while I'm in recovery?
Absolutely not. Every practice in our Holistic Medicine sessions is carefully adapted to your current physical capacity and energy levels. Our primary goal is to provide profound nervous system regulation without adding any strain. We prioritize restorative and gentle methods, ensuring that you feel nurtured and supported, never pushed, as you navigate your recovery from chronic illness or injury.
How long will it take to feel the effects of stress reduction from these Holistic Medicine practices?
Many individuals recovering from chronic illness or injury report a noticeable sense of calm and reduced tension even after their first Holistic Medicine session. Consistent engagement with these practices, however, allows for deeper, more lasting changes in your nervous system's regulation. The timeline is unique to you, but our aim is to equip you with tools for immediate relief and long-term stability.
Can Holistic Medicine integrate with my existing medical treatments for chronic illness or injury?
Yes, our Holistic Medicine approach is designed to complement and enhance your current medical care. We encourage open communication with your healthcare providers. Our focus on stress reduction and nervous system regulation creates an optimal internal environment that supports the efficacy of other treatments, helping your body to respond more effectively during your recovery from chronic illness or injury.
