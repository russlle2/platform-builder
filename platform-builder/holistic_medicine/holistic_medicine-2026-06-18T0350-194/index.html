Frequently Asked Questions

I'm too fatigued to try new sleep routines after my injury—can you help?
Yes. We start where your energy is. Your first step might be just adjusting your bedtime routine by 10 minutes. We build strategies around your current capacity, not idealized 'perfect' sleep habits.
How is your sleep approach different from other integrative clinics?
We don't treat sleep as an isolated issue. We integrate it directly into your chronic pain management. Your sleep plan syncs with your physical therapy schedule and medication timing for seamless recovery support.
Will this replace my current pain management?
No. We collaborate with your existing care team. Our sleep protocols are designed to work *alongside* your pain medication and physical therapy—reducing the need for additional sleep aids.
How soon will I notice better rest?
Most clients report less nighttime pain within 7-10 days and waking up feeling more refreshed by week 2. We measure progress through your energy levels and pain tracking, not just sleep metrics.
