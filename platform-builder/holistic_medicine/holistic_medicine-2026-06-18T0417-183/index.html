Frequently Asked Questions

What does 'gentle Holistic Medicine' mean for my sleep?
Gentle Holistic Medicine means we use natural, non-invasive methods to support your sleep, focusing on your comfort and overall well-being. We won't suggest anything harsh or complicated; instead, we'll explore simple, accessible ways to encourage restful sleep, tailored to your individual needs as a senior.
Do I need any previous experience with wellness or Holistic Medicine to get help with my sleep?
Absolutely not! Our approach is designed to be very accessible, especially for seniors who are new to Holistic Medicine. You don't need any prior experience or knowledge. We're here to gently introduce you to supportive practices and guide you through each step, making it easy to understand and integrate into your life.
How quickly can I expect to see improvements in my sleep with your Holistic Medicine approach?
Everyone's body is different, so the timeframe for improvement can vary. Our gentle Holistic Medicine methods aim for gradual, sustainable changes rather than quick fixes. Many seniors notice small positive shifts in their sleep patterns within a few weeks, and these improvements tend to deepen over time with consistent, gentle practice.
Are your Holistic Medicine recommendations safe for seniors already taking medication?
Yes, safety is our top priority. We always consider your current health and any medications you are taking. Our Holistic Medicine recommendations for sleep improvement are gentle and designed to complement your existing healthcare. We encourage open communication with your primary care doctor about any new approaches you undertake.
