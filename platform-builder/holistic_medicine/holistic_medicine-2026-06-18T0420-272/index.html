Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is 'trauma-informed care' in the context of Holistic Medicine for new mothers?
Trauma-informed care means we recognize that many new mothers may have experienced birth trauma, past traumas, or the emotional overwhelm of postpartum, which can impact their current well-being. Our Holistic Medicine approach is designed to create a safe, non-judgmental space where your experiences are honored. We use gentle techniques to help regulate your nervous system and process emotions without re-traumatizing you, always focusing on your comfort and empowerment.
How can Holistic Medicine specifically help with postpartum emotional struggles like anxiety or sadness?
Our Holistic Medicine approach addresses the root causes of postpartum emotional struggles. We look at the interconnectedness of your physical, mental, and emotional health. This can involve gentle somatic practices to release stored tension, mindfulness techniques to manage anxiety, and supportive dialogue to process feelings of sadness or overwhelm. The goal is to restore balance and equip you with practical strategies to navigate these feelings effectively.
Is Holistic Medicine compatible with traditional medical care I might be receiving?
Absolutely. Our Holistic Medicine services are designed to complement and enhance any traditional medical care you are receiving from your doctor or other healthcare providers. We believe in an integrative approach, working alongside your existing care team to provide comprehensive support for your postpartum emotional well-being. We encourage open communication about all aspects of your health.
What can I expect in a typical Holistic Medicine session for postpartum emotional support?
A typical Holistic Medicine session with will begin with a compassionate conversation about your current emotional landscape and any challenges you're facing. From there, we may explore various gentle techniques such as guided relaxation, breathing exercises, or somatic awareness practices tailored to your comfort and needs. The focus is always on creating a safe space for emotional processing and empowering you with tools to use outside of our sessions.
