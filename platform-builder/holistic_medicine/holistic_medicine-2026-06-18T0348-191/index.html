Frequently Asked Questions

What if I wake up at 3 a.m. every night?
This often signals nervous system overactivity, not just aging. We’ll identify gentle ways to calm that ‘inner alert’—like specific breathing exercises or targeted acupuncture points—to help you settle back without frustration or extra pills.
Can this work with my daily medications?
Absolutely. We review your current medications with your doctor to ensure safe integration. Our focus is on calming your nervous system—not replacing or interfering with your prescriptions.
How soon will I notice better sleep?
Many feel calmer within 2-3 sessions. For consistent restful nights, we typically recommend 4-6 gentle weekly sessions, tailored to your pace. You’ll track small wins with us, like fewer nighttime awakenings.
Do I need to change my current bedtime routine?
No—we work with the routine you have. We simply add one or two easy, calming practices (like a 2-minute breath focus before bed) that fit seamlessly into your existing evening routine.
