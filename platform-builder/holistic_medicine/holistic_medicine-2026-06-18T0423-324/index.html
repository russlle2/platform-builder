Frequently Asked Questions

How does Holistic Medicine at specifically address athletic physical stress?
We understand that athletes experience unique physical demands. Our Holistic Medicine approach goes beyond symptom management, addressing the root causes of your discomfort, whether it's muscle fatigue, joint strain, or nervous system overload, through personalized somatic and energetic healing techniques that support your athletic recovery.
What kind of somatic healing practices can I expect for physical pain relief?
Our somatic healing practices are designed to release deep-seated tension and facilitate recovery. This might include gentle manual therapies, specific breathwork techniques, or energetic balancing to help your body unwind from the intensity of your training, allowing for greater physical ease and resilience.
Is Holistic Medicine a good fit for someone who's constantly pushing their physical limits?
Absolutely. For those who push their physical boundaries, Holistic Medicine at offers vital support. We focus on restoring your body's natural equilibrium, preventing burnout, enhancing your recovery cycles, and mitigating the cumulative effects of physical stress, so you can continue to perform at your best, safely and sustainably.
How long will it take to see results from Holistic Medicine for my physical stress?
The timeline for seeing results can vary, as it depends on the nature and duration of your physical stress and your body's individual response. Many active individuals report feeling a sense of immediate relief and calm after their first session, with continued improvements in physical comfort and somatic awareness over time with consistent engagement in their personalized plan.
