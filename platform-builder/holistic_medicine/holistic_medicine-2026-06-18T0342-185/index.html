Frequently Asked Questions

Will this work if I only sleep 5 hours?
Yes—our focus is on *quality* of rest, not just hours. We help you get the deep, restorative sleep your brain needs, so 5 hours feels more like 7.
Do I need to give up coffee?
No. We optimize your caffeine rhythm based on your natural cortisol peaks, so you get your boost without wrecking your sleep.
What if I tried meditation and it didn't stick?
We skip the apps and focus on what *actually* disrupts your rest: your schedule, your caffeine, and your evening wind-down. No new habits to learn.
How soon will I notice a difference?
Many clients report falling asleep 20 minutes faster within the first week. Your energy and focus will shift as your sleep becomes more consistent.
