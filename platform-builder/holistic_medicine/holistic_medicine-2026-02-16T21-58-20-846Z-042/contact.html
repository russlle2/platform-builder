Intake & diagnostic focus

What to expect

The intake on your VIP Day explores medical history, current symptoms, lifestyle patterns, and priorities. We use validated questionnaires, targeted conversations, and optional testing recommendations to create a full picture. This is educational and collaborative; we do not promise cures but aim for measurable improvements through balanced planning.

Screening & safety

When appropriate, we screen for underlying conditions and coordinate with your primary care. Any suggested labs or supplements are explained with risks, benefits, and alternatives. If you have urgent medical symptoms, please contact your primary provider or emergency services.

VIP Day agenda & plan

Morning — Discovery

Comprehensive intake, lifestyle review, symptom mapping, and goal-setting. We prioritize what matters most to you and identify quick actions.

Afternoon — Plan & tools

Personalized nutrition, sleep, movement, stress strategies, and a stepwise plan you can implement in the first 30 days. Lab guidance is optional and discussed when helpful.

Follow-up

A focused check-in (30–60 min) within 3–6 weeks to review progress, refine the plan, and address barriers. Additional coaching or referrals provided as needed.

Small habits with big returns

We emphasize micro-habits — short, repeatable actions that stack into meaningful change. Here are examples commonly introduced during a VIP Day. Start one at a time; consistency matters more than intensity.

  • Morning hydration: 250–500ml water within 30 minutes of waking to support digestion and alertness.
  • Two-minute breath practice: Slow nasal breathing for 2–5 minutes mid-day to interrupt stress cycles.
  • Simplified dinner plate: Half vegetables, a lean protein, and whole grains — aim for balance, not perfection.
  • Digital sunset: Reduce screens 60 minutes before bed; replace with reading, light stretching, or journaling.
