Micro-habits for immediate improvement

Sustainable change is built from doable, measurable habits. Here are examples often introduced in early visits.

Morning micro-routine: 5 minutes of breath work + 10 minutes of sunlight exposure when possible.
Digestive cue: eat mindfully for first 20 minutes of a meal, chew slowly, and pause between bites.
Sleep anchor: consistent wake time with a 30-minute evening wind-down; reduce screens 60 minutes before bed.
