Stepwise care

Each step is collaborative. We track small wins and adapt the plan as we learn what works for you.

1. Intake & story

A thorough review of medical history, lifestyle, and priorities. This informs a hypothesis-driven plan.

2. Prioritized plan

Clear, time-bound actions with realistic micro-habits and education to support change.

3. Optional testing

When helpful, targeted labs are discussed as tools to refine the plan; not every case needs testing.

4. Follow-up & adjustment

Regular check-ins to measure progress, troubleshoot barriers, and step up or down interventions as needed.

How labs and tests are used

Labs are educational: they help us prioritize and tailor interventions. Tests are only recommended when results are likely to change the care plan.

  • Basic metabolic and screening labs to rule out reversible contributors.
  • Targeted nutrient or inflammation markers when clinically indicated.
  • Functional panels only when interpretation will meaningfully alter next steps.

Micro-habits we introduce early

2-minute breath reset
Consistent wake time
Slow first bite at meals
5-minute movement break

These small, repeatable actions create the foundation for larger change. They are customized to be achievable in your daily routine.

Follow-up cadence

Follow-ups vary by need: many start with 4–8 week intervals, with messaging support between visits. For concentrated change, a 90-day framework after a VIP Day helps sustain momentum.

Next steps

If you want a guided, efficient path: schedule an Initial Visit. If you want to compress several visits into one working session: consider the VIP Day.
