Why a whole-person approach matters

My practice focuses on connecting symptoms to daily patterns—sleep, stress, movement, and nutrition—so you can make changes that stick. I combine behavioral strategies with educational resources and, when appropriate, targeted tests to clarify next steps.

Background & orientation

I trained in both conventional and integrative frameworks and bring a systems-minded, practical stance. My goal is to translate complex information into manageable next steps you can implement this week. Expect clear takeaways, short experiments, and measured progress.

Clinical focus areas

  • Stress resilience and nervous system regulation
  • Sleep optimization and restorative routines
  • Digestive wellness and meal structure
  • Chronic low-level inflammation and energy stabilization

What I do not promise

I do not offer guaranteed cures. My responsibility is to educate, co-create practical plans, and monitor progress. Some conditions may require collaboration with other clinicians or specialist testing; I help coordinate that when needed.

Quick intake snapshot

A concise diagnostic framework I use during intake to guide individualized plans.

  1. Sleep: timing, quality, routines
  2. Stress: triggers, coping strategies, nervous system balance
  3. Nutrition: meal timing, stabilization, digestion
  4. Movement: consistency and restorative practices
  5. Labs (optional): used only to clarify educational next steps

Starter micro-habits

  • 3-minute diaphragmatic breathing twice daily
  • Two meals with balanced protein in the morning and midday
  • 15 minutes of natural light within 60 minutes of waking
