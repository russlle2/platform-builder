Micro-habits that move the needle

Small changes compound. Below are practical habits you can start this week to support sleep, digestion, and energy.

Sleep reset

Dim lights 60–90 minutes before bed, and keep a regular wake time. Track sleep window for two weeks.

Digestive rhythm

Prioritize protein at breakfast, chew slowly, and record reactions to identify triggers with awareness — not restriction.

Stress micro-breaks

Three 90-second diaphragmatic breathing breaks per day to reduce acute stress reactivity.

Energy pacing

Alternate focused work with 10-minute walks to stabilize energy without overstimulation.
