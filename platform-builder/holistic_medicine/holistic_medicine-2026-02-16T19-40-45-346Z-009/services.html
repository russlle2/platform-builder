Why choose integrative care?

We prioritize safety, transparency, and collaborative decision-making. Our goal is to educate and empower—you remain central to every step.

  • Evidence-informed options combined with individualized plans.
  • Continuity across visits with clear measurable goals.
  • Coordination with your other clinicians when desired.

How a typical service sequence unfolds

  1. Intake and health history to identify priorities and safety flags.
  2. Targeted education and an initial plan (therapies, nutrition, labs if appropriate).
  3. Short follow-ups to refine and longer reviews to assess progress.
Note: Some cases benefit from optional lab tests or specialist collaboration. Tests are used as educational tools, not guarantees.

Frequently asked

Do you accept insurance?
We are an out-of-network provider for many insurers; we provide superbills for potential reimbursement. Check with your plan.
Will I be asked to stop medications?
Recommendations are collaborative. We do not abruptly discontinue prescribed medications without coordinated planning with your prescriber.
