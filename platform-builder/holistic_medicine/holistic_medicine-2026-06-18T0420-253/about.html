What does 'trauma-informed' Holistic Medicine mean for me?

For you, 'trauma-informed' means our approach always prioritizes your comfort and safety. We understand that past experiences can influence your present emotional state, and our methods are designed to be gentle, respectful, and never re-traumatizing. Your pace and boundaries are always honored during our Holistic Medicine sessions.

How does emotional healing in Holistic Medicine differ for seniors?

Emotional healing in our Holistic Medicine practice for seniors is tailored to your unique life stage. We focus on methods that respect your physical and emotional capacity, emphasizing gentle integration rather than intense processing. Our aim is to bring you peace and contentment, acknowledging the wisdom and experiences you carry, without causing undue stress.

Will I have to revisit difficult memories in your Holistic Medicine sessions?

Our approach within Holistic Medicine is not about forcing you to relive difficult memories. Instead, we gently address the emotional imprints these memories may have left. We use subtle techniques to help release their lingering effects, allowing you to find greater emotional ease without intense confrontation. Your comfort is paramount.

Is Holistic Medicine suitable if I'm new to this type of care?

Absolutely. Our Holistic Medicine practice is designed to be welcoming and accessible, especially if you're new to this form of care. We begin with a thorough, gentle conversation to understand your needs and concerns. Our methods are non-intrusive and explained clearly, ensuring you feel completely at ease and supported throughout your experience with us.
