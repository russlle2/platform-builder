Frequently Asked Questions

How does Holistic Medicine specifically address sleep for new mothers?
Our Holistic Medicine approach considers the entire picture of your health during postpartum. We look at hormonal balance, nutrient deficiencies, stress responses, and daily rhythms unique to new motherhood. This allows us to create a targeted strategy for improving your sleep quality and duration, beyond just managing symptoms.
Will this approach require me to sleep train my baby?
No, our focus is entirely on optimizing your sleep and restorative rest as the mother. We work with the realities of caring for a newborn and aim to maximize the quality of the sleep you do get, rather than dictating your baby's sleep schedule. Your personalized plan will consider your baby's needs and integrate seamlessly into your current routines.
Is the Holistic Medicine approach evidence-based for postpartum recovery?
Absolutely. Our methods are grounded in current scientific research regarding maternal physiology, neurobiology of sleep, and validated integrative practices. We combine modern scientific understanding with time-tested holistic principles to provide care that is both effective and nurturing for your postpartum body.
How quickly can I expect to see improvements in my sleep?
While individual results vary, many new mothers report noticeable improvements in their sleep quality and overall energy within a few weeks of implementing their personalized Holistic Medicine plan. Consistency with the recommendations is key, and we'll be there to support you every step of the way as you regain your rest.
