Frequently Asked Questions

How can Holistic Medicine help my recovery from chronic illness or injury?
Our Holistic Medicine approach focuses on gently supporting your body's natural ability to regulate stress and calm your nervous system. This can help reduce the physical and mental strain often associated with chronic illness or injury, creating a more conducive environment for your recovery without feeling overwhelming.
I'm new to this. Do I need any prior experience with wellness practices?
Absolutely not! We understand you're navigating a lot already. Our Holistic Medicine services are designed to be completely accessible, even if you've never tried any wellness practices before. We'll introduce concepts and techniques at your pace, ensuring you feel comfortable and supported without any pressure.
Will Holistic Medicine treatments be too much for my sensitive system during recovery?
We prioritize gentleness and your comfort above all else. Our Holistic Medicine methods are carefully selected to be non-invasive and deeply calming, specifically for those with sensitive systems due to chronic illness or injury. We always check in with you and adjust our approach to ensure it feels supportive and never overstimulating.
How quickly can I expect to feel a difference in my stress levels or nervous system?
Everyone's experience is unique, especially during recovery. Some people report feeling a sense of calm and ease after just one session of Holistic Medicine, while for others, the benefits accumulate gradually. Our aim is to provide consistent, gentle support that helps you build resilience and find sustained peace over time, at a pace that honors your body.
