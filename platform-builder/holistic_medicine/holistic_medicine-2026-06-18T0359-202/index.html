Frequently Asked Questions

Can emotional work actually improve my performance?
Absolutely. When stored stress isn't released, it drains your mental energy and increases physical tension. We help you process what your body holds—so you move with more ease and focus during training and competition.
What if I'm not used to talking about my feelings?
You don't need to. We honor your athletic mindset and work at your pace. Many of our clients start by just noticing where tension lives in their body—no long sessions required.
How is this different from regular therapy?
We specialize in athletes. We understand your training schedule, the pressure to be 'strong,' and how injuries create emotional impact. You won't get generic advice—you'll get strategies that fit your lifestyle and sport.
Do you work with people recovering from injury?
Yes. We help athletes process the emotional weight of healing—like frustration over missed games or fear of re-injury—so you can return to training with your full mental and physical presence.
