Frequently Asked Questions

What if I panic during class but can't get to a session right away?
We'll give you one 2-minute technique you can use in the moment—like pressing your palms together to anchor yourself. It's not about fixing everything in that second, but creating a tiny pause so you can choose your next move.
Do you work with students during finals week?
Yes. We offer 'crisis-resilience' sessions during high-pressure times. Instead of adding more to your plate, we focus on one small, actionable step to calm your nervous system during your most intense moments.
How is this different from regular therapy?
We specifically address how academic pressure triggers your nervous system, not just your thoughts. You'll learn to recognize physical symptoms (like shallow breathing during lectures) and respond with tools that work within your campus schedule.
What if I've tried meditation and it just makes me more anxious?
We skip the 'just breathe' advice. Instead, we find tiny, physical practices that match where your body is right now—like shifting your weight in your chair during a lecture—to help you feel grounded without adding more pressure.
