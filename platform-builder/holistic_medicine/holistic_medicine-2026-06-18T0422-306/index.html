Frequently Asked Questions

How can Holistic Medicine help with my specific chronic pain from illness or injury?
When you're recovering from chronic illness or injury, your body often develops compensatory patterns or holds onto tension. Our Holistic Medicine approach identifies these specific patterns contributing to your pain. We use targeted, gentle techniques to release muscle guarding, improve circulation, and calm your nervous system, directly addressing the physical discomfort you're experiencing so you can regain a sense of ease.
Is Holistic Medicine a safe option for someone still recovering and feeling delicate?
Absolutely. Our Holistic Medicine practices are designed with utmost sensitivity for bodies in recovery. We always start gently, respecting your current physical capacity and pain levels. We prioritize techniques that support your body's natural healing process without causing additional strain, ensuring a comfortable and beneficial experience as you rebuild your strength.
What does 'somatic healing' mean in the context of my recovery?
Somatic healing, in your recovery, means we focus on the direct connection between your physical body and your experiences. Often, chronic illness or injury leaves a lasting imprint of tension or protective patterns in your tissues. Somatic healing helps you gently release these stored physical and emotional responses, allowing your body to relax, reduce pain, and move with greater freedom, fostering a deeper sense of physical well-being.
How quickly can I expect to feel results from Holistic Medicine for my chronic pain?
Your recovery is unique, and progress often unfolds at its own pace. While some individuals experience immediate relief from chronic pain after a Holistic Medicine session, for others, results build gradually over time. Our focus is on sustainable improvement, gently guiding your body towards lasting comfort and resilience. We'll work together to set realistic expectations and celebrate every step forward in your journey to feeling better.
