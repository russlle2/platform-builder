Frequently Asked Questions

How does Holistic Medicine at specifically help with age-related pain?
Our Holistic Medicine approach focuses on addressing the root causes of discomfort, not just the symptoms. We use gentle, non-invasive techniques like mindful touch, specific herbal remedies, and movement exercises tailored to improve circulation, reduce inflammation, and restore mobility, helping you move with more ease and less pain as you age.
What is 'somatic healing' and how can it benefit me?
Somatic healing in Holistic Medicine helps you reconnect with your body's innate wisdom. It involves gentle practices that bring awareness to physical sensations and emotional patterns held within your body. For seniors, this can mean releasing long-held tension, improving body awareness, and fostering a deeper sense of peace and comfort in your physical self, which directly contributes to pain reduction.
Will these Holistic Medicine treatments interfere with my current medications?
Your safety is paramount. Before any Holistic Medicine treatment begins, we conduct a thorough review of your health history and current medications. Our approach is designed to complement your existing care, not conflict with it. We will always discuss potential interactions and ensure our recommendations are safe and beneficial for your specific health profile.
What can I expect during my first visit for Holistic Medicine at ?
Your first Holistic Medicine visit is a gentle conversation where we listen intently to your concerns, health history, and what you hope to achieve. We'll then conduct a gentle assessment and discuss a personalized care plan tailored to your needs and comfort level. Our goal is to create a supportive space where you feel heard and understood from the very beginning.
