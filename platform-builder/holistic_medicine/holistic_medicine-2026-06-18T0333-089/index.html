Frequently Asked Questions

What does Holistic Medicine offer specifically for parents dealing with household stress?
Our Holistic Medicine approach provides practical, gentle methods to help you manage the unique stresses of parenting young children. We focus on techniques that calm your nervous system, reduce feelings of overwhelm, and help you respond more calmly to daily challenges, all without requiring any previous wellness experience.
I'm already so busy. How can I possibly add Holistic Medicine practices to my day?
We understand your time is precious. Our Holistic Medicine strategies are designed to be integrated seamlessly into your existing routine. We teach short, effective practices that can be done in minutes, like mindful breathing during a diaper change or a quick body scan while waiting for the kettle to boil. We help you find moments of calm, not create more tasks.
Do I need any prior knowledge or experience with Holistic Medicine to benefit?
Absolutely not! Our Holistic Medicine practice is built on accessibility. We meet you exactly where you are, whether you've never tried any wellness practices before or you're looking for new, gentle ways to cope. Our guidance is clear, simple, and always supportive, ensuring you feel comfortable and empowered from day one.
How quickly can I expect to feel a difference using Holistic Medicine for stress reduction?
While everyone's experience is unique, many parents report feeling a subtle shift towards greater calm and reduced reactivity within a few weeks of consistently applying our Holistic Medicine techniques. The goal isn't instant perfection, but rather building sustainable habits that gently re-regulate your nervous system over time, leading to more lasting peace in your daily life.
