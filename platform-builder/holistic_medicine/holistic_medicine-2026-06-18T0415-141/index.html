Frequently Asked Questions

How does Holistic Medicine specifically address sleep issues caused by burnout?
Our Holistic Medicine approach looks at the whole picture – your stress levels, diet, lifestyle, and environment – to identify and address the underlying factors contributing to your burnout-related sleep problems. We help you calm your nervous system and re-establish healthy sleep cycles, not just mask the symptoms.
What kind of commitment is required to see results with your Holistic Medicine sleep programs?
Committing to your rest is an investment in yourself. Our programs are designed to be integrated into your busy life, offering practical, actionable steps. While consistency is key, we focus on sustainable changes that fit your schedule, typically seeing initial improvements within a few weeks of dedicated practice.
Is Holistic Medicine suitable for someone who has tried everything for their sleep without success?
Absolutely. Many working professionals come to us after exhausting conventional options. Our Holistic Medicine perspective offers a fresh lens, often uncovering overlooked factors. We provide a supportive, inclusive space to explore new strategies and find what truly resonates with your body and mind.
How does your Holistic Medicine practice create an inclusive environment for all backgrounds?
Our practice is built on the foundation of respect and understanding for everyone. We prioritize active listening, cultural sensitivity, and open communication to ensure you feel seen, heard, and valued. Your comfort and trust are paramount as we work together on your sleep and rest goals.
