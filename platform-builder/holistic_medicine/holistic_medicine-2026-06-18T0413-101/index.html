Frequently Asked Questions

I'm a busy professional, aged 30-45, and feel completely burnt out. Can Holistic Medicine truly help with my sleep?
Absolutely. Your burnout often manifests as sleep difficulties. We specifically address the root causes of your fatigue and restless nights, offering personalized Holistic Medicine strategies that fit into your demanding schedule, helping you reclaim deep, restorative sleep.
How is Holistic Medicine different from just trying to 'sleep more' or using over-the-counter sleep aids?
Unlike temporary fixes, Holistic Medicine looks at all aspects of your well-being – stress, nutrition, daily habits, and emotional state – that contribute to your sleep challenges. We create a comprehensive plan that's tailored to your unique burnout experience, fostering sustainable sleep improvement rather than quick, fleeting relief.
I've tried everything for my sleep and nothing sticks. How will your Holistic Medicine approach be different for me?
We understand that frustration. Our Holistic Medicine approach for working professionals aged 30-45 focuses on understanding your specific stressors and energy drains. We don't use a one-size-fits-all method; instead, we partner with you to build practical, gentle habits that integrate seamlessly into your life, leading to lasting rest.
What can I expect in a first consultation for sleep improvement through Holistic Medicine?
In your first consultation, we'll have a warm, in-depth conversation about your current sleep patterns, daily routines, energy levels, and any stress you're experiencing as a professional. This allows us to understand your unique situation and begin mapping out a personalized Holistic Medicine plan focused on bringing you back to restorative rest.
