Frequently Asked Questions

How can Holistic Medicine specifically help my 'mom brain' symptoms?
Holistic Medicine addresses the underlying physiological and emotional factors contributing to cognitive fog, forgetfulness, and difficulty concentrating during postpartum. We assess hormonal balance, nutrient deficiencies, sleep quality, and stress levels, then develop a personalized plan using natural therapies to optimize your brain function and mental energy.
Is Holistic Medicine safe to use while I am breastfeeding?
Absolutely. Your safety and your baby's well-being are our top priorities. All Holistic Medicine recommendations, whether nutritional adjustments, supplements, or lifestyle modifications, are carefully evaluated for compatibility with breastfeeding to ensure they are gentle and effective for you both.
What kind of results can I expect for my mental clarity with Holistic Medicine?
While individual results vary, many mothers report significant improvements in their ability to focus, recall information, and manage daily tasks with greater ease. Our Holistic Medicine approach aims to reduce feelings of overwhelm and enhance your overall sense of cognitive control and emotional balance during postpartum.
How long will it take to see improvements in my cognitive wellness using Holistic Medicine?
The timeline for improvement is unique to each individual and depends on various factors. However, many clients begin to notice positive shifts in their energy and mental sharpness within a few weeks of consistently applying their personalized Holistic Medicine plan. We work collaboratively to track your progress and adjust as needed.
