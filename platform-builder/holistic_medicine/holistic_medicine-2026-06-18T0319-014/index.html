Frequently Asked Questions

How does Holistic Medicine specifically address physical stress for athletes?
For athletes, physical stress isn't just muscle soreness; it impacts your entire system. Our Holistic Medicine approach focuses on optimizing your body's physiological responses to training loads. We employ strategies to reduce inflammation, improve sleep quality, and enhance cellular recovery by carefully modulating your nervous system, ensuring you don't accumulate detrimental stress.
Can Holistic Medicine help regulate my nervous system to improve recovery?
Absolutely. A regulated nervous system is foundational for efficient recovery. We use precise techniques to shift your body from a constant 'fight or flight' state, often exacerbated by intense training, to a 'rest and digest' state. This promotes deeper restorative sleep, optimizes nutrient absorption, and accelerates tissue repair, directly improving your recovery capacity.
Is this Holistic Medicine approach suitable for all types of athletes and active people?
Yes, our Holistic Medicine methods are adaptable to a wide range of athletic pursuits and activity levels. Whether you're a professional athlete, a dedicated amateur, or someone managing an active lifestyle, the principles of stress reduction and nervous system regulation are universally beneficial for enhancing performance and preventing burnout.
What can I expect during an initial Holistic Medicine consultation?
Your first Holistic Medicine consultation will involve a thorough assessment of your training regimen, recovery practices, and any symptoms of physical stress you're experiencing. We'll discuss your specific goals and collaboratively develop an individualized plan focusing on nervous system regulation and stress reduction, always grounded in clear, clinical reasoning.
