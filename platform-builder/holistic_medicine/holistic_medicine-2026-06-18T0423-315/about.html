How can Holistic Medicine help with my anxiety and academic pressure?

The physical tension you feel from anxiety or academic pressure—like a stiff neck or persistent headaches—is your body's way of reacting. Holistic Medicine, specifically somatic healing, helps by gently releasing these physical stress patterns, calming your nervous system, and reducing pain, which in turn can lessen your overall feelings of anxiety and improve focus.

Is Holistic Medicine safe and effective for college students?

Yes, our Holistic Medicine practices are gentle, non-invasive, and rooted in an understanding of the body's natural healing capabilities. They are effective in addressing physical pain and stress responses often experienced by college students. We prioritize your comfort and safety, using clinically informed techniques.

What should I expect during a Holistic Medicine session?

During a Holistic Medicine session, you'll typically lie comfortably on a treatment table, fully clothed. Dr. Morgan Ellis will use a light, sensitive touch to assess and release areas of tension in your body. It's a very calm and often profoundly relaxing experience, designed to help your body unwind and find its natural balance.

How quickly will I feel relief from my physical pain or tension?

While individual experiences vary, many students report feeling a sense of immediate calm and reduced tension after their first Holistic Medicine session. Consistent engagement with the practice often leads to more sustained relief and a greater capacity to manage the physical symptoms associated with academic and social pressures. We'll discuss your progress and tailor your care.
