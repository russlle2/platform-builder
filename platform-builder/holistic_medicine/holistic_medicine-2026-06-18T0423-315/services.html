Holistic Medicine: Addressing Your Body's Response to Stress

The intense environment of college often translates into headaches, neck tension, stomach issues, or persistent fatigue. You're not imagining it; your body is responding. Our approach in Holistic Medicine acknowledges this deep connection, offering gentle, effective somatic practices to release built-up physical stress and alleviate chronic discomfort. We focus on helping you understand these signals and providing tools for lasting relief.
