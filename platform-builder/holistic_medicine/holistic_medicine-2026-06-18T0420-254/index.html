Frequently Asked Questions

How does Holistic Medicine specifically address the emotional stress faced by athletes?
Our Holistic Medicine approach acknowledges that physical performance is intrinsically linked to emotional well-being. We delve into the unique stressors athletes face—injury, competition, pressure—and offer targeted, trauma-informed strategies to process these experiences, preventing them from undermining your capabilities or causing long-term impact.
Can Holistic Medicine help with performance blocks or mental barriers related to past experiences?
Absolutely. Many performance blocks stem from unresolved emotional stress or past traumatic experiences. Our Holistic Medicine framework provides a safe, structured space to gently explore these internal barriers, allowing you to release their hold and regain your mental clarity and physical fluidity, optimizing your peak performance.
Is this approach suitable for someone who hasn't experienced 'major' trauma but deals with chronic stress?
Yes, our trauma-informed Holistic Medicine care is highly beneficial for chronic stress. Even seemingly minor or accumulated stressors can significantly impact your nervous system and performance. We help you build robust emotional regulation skills, transforming how you respond to daily pressures and fostering greater resilience.
What should I expect during my first session in Holistic Medicine?
Your initial Holistic Medicine session is a thoughtful conversation where we listen intently to your experiences, goals, and challenges. It's an opportunity for us to understand the nuances of your emotional landscape and for you to feel heard and respected. We'll outline a personalized approach designed to support your unique path to emotional and physical mastery.
