Will this interfere with my current medications?

We always coordinate with your prescribing doctor. Our gentle methods—like bedtime tea rituals or breathing exercises—work alongside your medications without conflict. We’ll check this with you first.

How soon will I notice better sleep?

Many people feel a difference in 1-2 weeks—like waking up without that heavy fog. We start with just one or two simple steps you can try tonight, so you’ll see progress fast.

Do I need to do anything special before my visit?

Just bring your usual bedtime routine (even if it’s just watching TV). We’ll build from there. No preparation needed—just be ready to relax.

What if I’ve tried other sleep programs before?

Many seniors tell us they tried complicated apps or exercise plans that didn’t fit. We focus on what works for your life: short, calm moments you can do in your living room chair after dinner.
