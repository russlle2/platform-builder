Frequently Asked Questions

How does Holistic Medicine specifically address sleep for athletes?
We focus on optimizing sleep architecture, managing training-induced stress, and enhancing recovery at a cellular level. This means looking at your unique physiological responses, dietary factors, environmental influences, and mental state to create a comprehensive plan for deep, restorative sleep that supports your performance goals.
I'm already active; why do I need a specialized sleep plan?
While activity is vital, the demands on an athlete's body for repair and adaptation are significantly higher. General sleep advice often falls short. We provide targeted strategies to improve sleep quality, reduce inflammation, and accelerate muscle recovery, ensuring your body is fully prepared for consistent high-level output without burnout.
What kind of techniques does use for restorative rest?
integrates modalities such as advanced sleep hygiene protocols, targeted nutritional support for recovery, specific stress reduction techniques, and personalized lifestyle adjustments. The focus is always on scientific evidence and your individual needs to foster truly restorative rest.
How long until I see improvements in my sleep and recovery?
Many clients report noticeable improvements in sleep quality and energy levels within a few weeks of implementing their personalized plan. Sustained and profound changes typically develop over 2-3 months as your body adapts and new habits become established. We work with you to track progress and adjust as needed.
