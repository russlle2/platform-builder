Frequently Asked Questions

I'm a working professional aged 30-45 and feel completely burnt out. Can Holistic Medicine really help me sleep better?
Absolutely. Your burnout is often deeply intertwined with poor sleep. Our Holistic Medicine approach focuses specifically on identifying how your daily stressors impact your rest and provides gentle, practical strategies to improve your sleep quality, helping you feel more recovered and less depleted.
I've never tried anything 'holistic' before. Do I need any special knowledge or experience?
Not at all! Our approach is designed to be gentle and accessible, especially for those new to Holistic Medicine. We'll guide you every step of the way, explaining everything clearly and ensuring you feel comfortable and confident with the practices we suggest for improving your sleep.
My schedule is packed. How can I possibly fit in more 'wellness' to improve my sleep?
We understand your time is precious. Our Holistic Medicine strategies are designed to integrate seamlessly into your busy life. We focus on small, impactful changes that don't add stress, but rather create pockets of calm and preparation for better sleep, even with a full schedule.
I often wake up feeling just as tired as when I went to bed. What can I expect from your Holistic Medicine sleep support?
That feeling of unrefreshed sleep is a common sign of burnout. Our Holistic Medicine support aims to help you achieve truly restorative rest, so you wake up feeling genuinely recharged. We'll explore underlying factors impacting your sleep and create a plan to help you experience deeper, more effective rest.
