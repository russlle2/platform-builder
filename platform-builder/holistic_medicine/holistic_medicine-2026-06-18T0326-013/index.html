Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to move a lot during sessions?
No. All techniques are seated or supine—no standing, stretching, or physical exertion. We work with your current mobility, ensuring safety and ease at every step.
How soon will I notice less tension?
Many find immediate relief in their shoulders or breathing after just one session. Consistent practice helps your nervous system recognize calm more easily over time—like learning a quiet rhythm you can return to anytime.
Is this covered by insurance?
Most plans cover holistic medicine for stress-related symptoms. We’ll help you verify coverage and provide documentation. No surprises—just clear guidance about what your plan offers.
What if I have other health conditions?
We work alongside your care team. Just share your usual medications or health notes during your first call—we’ll integrate techniques that honor your entire health picture without conflict.
