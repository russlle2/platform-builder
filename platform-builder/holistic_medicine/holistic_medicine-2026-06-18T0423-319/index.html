Frequently Asked Questions

How can Holistic Medicine specifically help with the physical pain I experience as a parent?
Many parents experience pain related to repetitive tasks, lack of sleep, and emotional stress. Our Holistic Medicine approach identifies the root causes of your physical discomfort, whether it's chronic back pain from lifting, tension in your shoulders, or stress-induced headaches. We use somatic healing techniques to release physical tension, calm your nervous system, and provide you with practical strategies to alleviate and prevent future pain, helping you move through your day with greater ease.
Is this Holistic Medicine approach suitable if I'm already feeling overwhelmed and have limited time?
Absolutely. We understand that your time is precious and your energy is often stretched thin. Our Holistic Medicine sessions are designed to be efficient and highly effective, focusing on targeted interventions that yield noticeable results. We prioritize practical, manageable strategies that integrate seamlessly into your busy life, so you don't feel like you're adding another burden. Our goal is to reduce your stress, not increase it.
What kind of evidence supports the somatic healing methods used in your Holistic Medicine practice?
We are committed to evidence-informed care. The somatic healing methods we employ in our Holistic Medicine practice, such as specific bodywork techniques, breathwork, and nervous system regulation practices, are supported by research demonstrating their efficacy in reducing chronic pain, alleviating stress, and improving overall well-being. We can discuss the specific evidence base for the modalities most relevant to your individual needs during your consultation, ensuring you feel confident in your care.
Will Holistic Medicine help me manage the emotional stress that often accompanies physical pain when parenting?
Yes, our Holistic Medicine practice recognizes the intricate connection between your physical body and emotional state, especially when managing the stresses of parenting. By addressing the physical manifestations of stress through somatic healing, we often see a profound positive impact on your emotional well-being. Releasing physical tension can create space for emotional calm, helping you feel more resilient and less reactive to daily stressors, ultimately supporting a more balanced emotional landscape.
