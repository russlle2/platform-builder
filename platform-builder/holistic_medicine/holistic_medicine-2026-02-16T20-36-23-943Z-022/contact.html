A brief note on our approach

We emphasize learning and partnership. Initial visits concentrate on understanding history, priorities, and available data. Recommendations may include lifestyle interventions, guided supplements, and optional educational lab testing when helpful. We do not offer guarantees — our focus is on clear information and stepwise progress.

The care framework

1. Intake & listening. Comprehensive review of symptoms, context, and goals.
2. Clarify contributors. Sleep, stress responses, digestion, inflammation, movement, and nutrition.
3. Shared plan. Practical next steps: habits, targeted interventions, optional educational labs.
4. Follow-ups. Narrow-focused visits for progress checks and plan adjustments.

Optional labs are offered for greater diagnostic clarity when clinically useful; they are educational and not required for treatment to begin.
