How we assess and support you

Our focus is practical education and tailored plans. Below are the typical steps in a new therapeutic partnership. Optional educational tests are discussed transparently — we never order anything without explaining purpose, limits, and alternatives.

1 — Intake & Listening

Extended first visit to review medical history, lifestyle patterns, medications, exposures, and goals. The aim is to understand context and identify priorities.

2 — Assessment & Shared Plan

We translate findings into a personalized, realistic plan that may include dietary changes, movement, stress tools, and targeted safe interventions. Education drives decisions.

3 — Optional Educational Labs

If helpful, we discuss non-invasive labs to refine the plan (eg, basic metabolic panels, targeted nutrient testing, or stool/cardiometabolic markers). These are educational tools used selectively.

4 — Implementation Support

We provide pragmatic steps, resources, and follow-up points. Small, measurable changes are emphasized to support sustainable improvement.

5 — Follow-ups & Adjustment

Regular follow-ups to monitor progress, adjust plans, and coordinate with other clinicians. We track outcomes and adapt based on what is working and what is not.

6 — Maintenance & Self-care

Long-term focus on resilience-building skills and prevention strategies so you can manage health with confidence.

Note: Care is integrative and collaborative. We educate about potential benefits and risks for each option. Results vary by individual; this information is not a promise of cure.
