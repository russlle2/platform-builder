Beyond Stress: Understanding Your Nervous System

It's not just 'stress' you're feeling; it's often a dysregulated nervous system. For working professionals aged 30-45, the constant demands of career and life can push your body into a chronic fight-or-flight state. Our Holistic Medicine approach explains the 'why' behind your fatigue, anxiety, and difficulty focusing, offering clear pathways to re-establish physiological equilibrium. We focus on evidence-based methods to help you understand and consciously shift out of patterns that contribute to burnout.
