Conditions we often support

Educational support and integrative approaches tailored to the whole person. Not a substitute for medical care; see full disclaimer below.
Digestive & Metabolic Patterns
Irritable bowel symptoms, functional bloating, postprandial discomfort. We emphasize diet experiments, sleep and stress rhythm work.
Blood sugar variability, fatigue after meals. Coaching focuses on patterns and small actionable tests you can try safely.
Sleep & Energy Dysregulation
Trouble falling asleep, fragmented nights, low daytime energy. We layer behavioral strategies, light and timing adjustments, and pacing.
If you have signs of a sleep disorder (apnea, narcolepsy), we will refer to a sleep clinic for diagnostics.
Chronic Stress, Mood, & Resilience
Persistent worry, low mood, or brain fog linked to long-term stress. We use short experiments and skills to restore baseline functioning.
We do not provide emergency psychiatric services; if you are in crisis, please contact local emergency resources immediately.
Important disclaimer
This site provides general educational information about integrative approaches. It is not medical advice and does not replace evaluation by a licensed healthcare provider. If you have a serious or life-threatening condition, seek immediate medical care.
If you are under medical treatment, we work collaboratively with your care team when appropriate. All program participation is voluntary and informed consent is obtained.
When to reach out
  • New or worsening symptoms (fever, severe pain, unintentional weight loss) — seek medical care.
  • Concerns about medication changes — coordinate with prescribing clinician before altering doses.
  • Questions about program suitability — book a consult.
Evidence & reporting
We collect anonymized outcomes and produce plain-language case notes and summaries. Our materials aim to be transparent about uncertainty and limits.
