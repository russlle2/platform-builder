Case note — an illustrative example
A person with fragmented sleep and midday crashes tried a single timing change: light exposure within 30 minutes of waking and a small midafternoon walk. Over three weeks their average sleep continuity improved and naps decreased. This is an educational example, not a guaranteed outcome.
