Common misunderstandings — and a clearer frame

Myth: 'All holistic care is the same'

Truth: Approaches vary widely. We prioritize interventions with a clear rationale and documented effects, and we integrate them with your medical context.

Myth: 'It takes months before anything helps'

Truth: Small, measurable adjustments can change energy and sleep quickly; deeper shifts often build over cycles and tailored support.

Myth: 'This replaces medical care'

Truth: We complement conventional care. We don't promise cures — we offer education, frameworks, and coordinated referrals when needed.

Questions people ask

Will this replace my doctor?

We work alongside medical care. Our role is educational and supportive; we do not diagnose or replace primary medical treatment.

How long before I see change?

Some metrics (sleep, energy) can shift in weeks with focused adjustments; complex conditions take longer and map to staged plans.

Do you offer sliding scale or scholarship?

Occasionally. Please reach out via the contact form and describe your situation; we aim for equitable access.
