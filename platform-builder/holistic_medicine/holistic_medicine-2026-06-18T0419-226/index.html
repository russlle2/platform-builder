Frequently Asked Questions

How does Holistic Medicine specifically address emotional healing after chronic illness or injury?
Our Holistic Medicine approach recognizes that physical setbacks often bring significant emotional distress. We use methods that gently explore how your illness or injury has impacted your spirit and mind, offering tools to process grief, anxiety, and frustration. The goal is to integrate these experiences, not just suppress them, helping you find a path to emotional equilibrium.
I've experienced medical trauma. How will your trauma-informed Holistic Medicine care support me?
We prioritize creating a profoundly safe and respectful environment. Our trauma-informed Holistic Medicine care means we proceed at your pace, always seeking your consent and understanding your unique triggers. We focus on practices that help regulate your nervous system and re-establish a sense of safety within your body, allowing for gentle emotional release and reconnection.
Will Holistic Medicine clash with my ongoing medical treatments for chronic conditions?
Absolutely not. Our Holistic Medicine practices are designed to complement and support your conventional medical care. We view ourselves as part of your broader recovery team, aiming to enhance your emotional well-being and resilience, which in turn can positively impact your physical healing. We encourage open communication with all your healthcare providers.
What kind of changes can I expect from engaging in this type of Holistic Medicine support?
You can anticipate a deeper sense of self-awareness and emotional regulation. Many clients report feeling more grounded, experiencing reduced anxiety, and developing a more compassionate relationship with their bodies. Our Holistic Medicine focus is on helping you cultivate inner resources to navigate your recovery with greater peace and resilience.
