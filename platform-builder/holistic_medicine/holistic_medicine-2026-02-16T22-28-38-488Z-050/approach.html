A stepwise, educational path to balanced wellbeing

We approach care like a series of experiments: intake, collaborative plan, short cycles of implementation, and reassessment. Optional labs are offered as tools for clarity, not as the sole answer.

Schedule an intake
Philosophy

Whole-person: body, mind, rhythms, and environment. Each plan is tailored — educational, achievable, and measurable.

How a typical engagement flows

Intake

Comprehensive history, goals, and baseline education. We listen first and gather what matters most.

Personalized Plan

A tiered plan with short-term actions and educational resources: sleep practices, movement, nutrition patterns, stress tools.

Follow-ups

Short visits to review data, troubleshoot, and adapt. We use measurable small goals rather than vague prescriptions.

Optional labs & testing — educational context

When indicated, we may suggest targeted labs (e.g., thyroid panel, basic metabolic, micronutrient context, or digestive markers) to inform the plan. Testing is optional and occurs with informed consent. Labs are interpreted in partnership with your primary clinician when needed.

Commonly discussed tests
  • Basic metabolic & endocrine panels
  • Thyroid function tests
  • Micronutrient screening where relevant
  • Stool or breath testing for persistent digestive symptoms

Shared decision-making & education

Every recommendation includes rationale, alternatives, and expected timeframes. You remain the primary decision-maker; we are a guide and an educator throughout.

Payment & packages

We offer single visits and package pricing for ongoing support. Retail items are optional add-ons to support the plan.
