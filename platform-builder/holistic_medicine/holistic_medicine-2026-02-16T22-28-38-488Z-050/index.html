Why whole-person care?

Symptoms are clues. We look beyond isolated complaints to lifestyle, relationships, stress biology, digestion, sleep, and movement. When appropriate, we layer targeted testing and safe, evidence-aware supplements or botanicals.

Our approach is:

  • Collaborative — you are the expert on your life.
  • Practical — small, sustainable changes first.
  • Curious — we prioritize education and measurable progress.

Framework for care

A simple cycle to guide healing and resilience.

  1. Intake & context

    We map health history, priorities, and current stressors. This is the most important step.

  2. Personalized plan

    Targeted nutrition, movement, sleep, stress tools, and optional lab-informed additions.

  3. Action & support

    Coaching, brief check-ins, and resources to keep momentum.

  4. Review & refine

    Regular reviews help measure outcomes and pivot safely.
