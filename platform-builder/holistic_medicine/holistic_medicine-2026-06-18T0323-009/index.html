Frequently Asked Questions

Can I actually use these techniques when my toddler is having a meltdown *right now*?
Yes. We teach one 90-second technique you can do while holding a screaming toddler in the grocery aisle—no extra time needed. It’s designed for the moment when you feel overwhelmed, not an hour after.
Do I need to stop doing everything to practice this?
No. These tools fit into existing moments: sync breath cues with diaper changes, or use grounding while washing dishes. You’re not adding tasks—you’re changing how you interact with the chaos you’re already in.
How is this different from other 'stress relief' for parents?
We focus on *nervous system regulation*—not just 'relaxing.' You’ll learn why you snap at your kid over spilled milk (it’s your body’s stress response), and how to reset it *before* you regret it. This is about immediate, physiological shifts, not abstract 'mindfulness.'
Will this work if I only have 2 minutes?
Absolutely. We’ve designed techniques for the 2-minute window between changing diapers and answering work emails. These aren’t for meditation retreats—they’re for real, messy parenting moments.
