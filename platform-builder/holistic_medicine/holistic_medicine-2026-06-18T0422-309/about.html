Holistic Medicine for the Overwhelmed Parent

It's easy to put yourself last when you're caring for young children. But persistent pain and stress make everything harder. Our Holistic Medicine approach understands that your physical symptoms are often linked to the emotional and mental demands of parenthood. We don't just treat symptoms; we help you address the root causes, so you can feel more present and comfortable in your body.

What kind of physical pain can Holistic Medicine help me with as a parent?

Many parents come to us with common issues like lower back pain from lifting, neck and shoulder tension from feeding or carrying, wrist pain from repetitive tasks, and general body aches from lack of rest. Our Holistic Medicine approach is designed to address these specific physical discomforts linked to the demands of parenting.

How does somatic healing specifically help with the stress of parenting?

Somatic healing recognizes that emotional and mental stress often manifest as physical tension, aches, or even fatigue in your body. We'll explore techniques to help you release this stored stress physically, leading to reduced pain, a calmer nervous system, and a greater sense of resilience when faced with daily parenting challenges. It's about finding physical relief that also calms your mind.

Is Holistic Medicine safe for me if I'm pregnant or breastfeeding?

Absolutely. We prioritize your safety and comfort. Please let Dr. Morgan Ellis know if you are pregnant, breastfeeding, or planning to conceive. We will tailor all Holistic Medicine and somatic healing practices to be completely safe and supportive for your specific stage of parenthood, ensuring both your well-being and that of your child.

How quickly can I expect to feel a difference with your Holistic Medicine approach?

While everyone's experience is unique, many parents report feeling a noticeable difference in their physical discomfort and overall stress levels after just a few sessions. Our Holistic Medicine approach focuses on providing both immediate relief and sustainable strategies, so you can experience progress and build tools for long-term well-being.
