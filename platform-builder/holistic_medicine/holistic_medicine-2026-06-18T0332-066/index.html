Frequently Asked Questions

How does Holistic Medicine specifically help with chronic illness or injury recovery?
For those recovering from chronic conditions or injuries, Holistic Medicine focuses on reducing the chronic stress response that often accompanies these experiences. We work to downregulate your nervous system, which can improve sleep, lessen pain sensitivity, and enhance your body's innate ability to repair and heal, creating a more sustainable path to well-being.
Will these approaches interfere with my current medical treatments?
Our Holistic Medicine practices are designed to complement, not replace, your existing medical care. We prioritize open communication with your healthcare team and ensure our methods support your overall treatment plan. Our aim is to provide additional layers of support for stress reduction and nervous system regulation, enhancing your recovery journey.
How quickly can I expect to feel a difference in my stress levels and nervous system?
Everyone's response to Holistic Medicine is unique, especially when recovering from chronic illness or injury. Some individuals report feeling a gentle shift towards calm and reduced stress after their first session, while for others, the benefits accumulate steadily over time as their nervous system learns to regulate more effectively. Consistency is key for lasting change.
What if I'm feeling overwhelmed by my recovery process and don't know where to start?
It's completely normal to feel overwhelmed when navigating recovery from chronic illness or injury. Our initial consultations are designed to create a safe space for you to share your experiences without judgment. We’ll gently explore your current challenges and collaboratively build a plan to support your nervous system, making the process feel manageable and empowering.
