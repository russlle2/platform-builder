What to bring

Contraindications:

If you are pregnant, have epilepsy, are undergoing acute psychiatric treatment, or have concerns about sound/loud vibration, consult a medical provider before attending. Our practices are supportive, not prescriptive.

Quick questions

How long are intensives?

We offer micro (45m), standard (90m), and deep (120m) options. Choose depending on how much integration time you want.

Do I need experience?

No. Guidance is offered and practices are accessible for newcomers and regulars alike.
