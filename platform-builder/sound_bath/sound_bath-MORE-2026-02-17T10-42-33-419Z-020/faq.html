Frequently asked — practical answers

Short, direct info to help you decide if a session is right for you. For other questions, contact {{EMAIL}} or call {{PHONE}}.

What should I bring?
Bring a cushion or mat, a blanket for warmth, and a water bottle. If you use hearing aids or have specific ear sensitivity, please let us know when you book.
How loud will it be?
Volumes are kept moderate and mindful. We avoid sharp peaks and prioritize a comfortable listening level. If you prefer lower volume, speak with the host upon arrival.
Are these meditations?
Sessions include guided prompts and breath cues, but they are not a formal meditation course. Think of them as structured listening windows with simple guidance to anchor attention.
Is there an age limit?
We recommend attendees be 16+. Children under 16 require parent supervision and prior arrangement.
Refunds and transfers
Full refund or transfer available up to 48 hours before an event. Within 48 hours, credit may be issued at our discretion.
Contraindications
If you have epilepsy, a pacemaker, cochlear implant, recent ear/head surgery, are pregnant, or have a condition sensitive to vibration or sound exposure, consult your healthcare provider before attending. We can recommend alternatives; reach us at {{EMAIL}}.
