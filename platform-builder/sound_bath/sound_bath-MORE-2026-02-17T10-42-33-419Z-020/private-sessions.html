Private Alignment Sessions

A focused, guided session tailored for emotional easing and practical rest — in-studio or at home.
Deep Session 60–90 minutes

What to expect

  • Calibration of breath and attention using tuned sound and guided transitions.
  • Short somatic checks and an invitation to set a practical intention.
  • A take-home journaling prompt and a short practice to continue integration.

Session flow

  1. Arrival & settling (10–12 min)
  2. Brief check-in & intention setting (8–10 min)
  3. Sound-guided session (40–60 min)
  4. Transition & journaling (10 min)

Contraindications & safety

Sound work can be deeply restorative but is not appropriate in every circumstance. If you have epilepsy, recent head injury, are pregnant without clinical clearance, or are under the influence of psychoactive substances, please consult your healthcare practitioner before booking. If you have implanted medical devices that could be affected by vibrations, contact us at {{EMAIL}} for guidance.
