How can a Sound Bath help with my academic anxiety?

A Sound Bath offers a dedicated space for your nervous system to calm down. The gentle, immersive sounds encourage your brainwaves to slow, helping to reduce the frantic mental loop often associated with academic pressure. It's a natural way to hit the reset button and approach your studies with a clearer, more relaxed mind.

I'm always slouching at my desk. Can a Sound Bath improve my posture?

Absolutely! While a Sound Bath isn't a direct chiropractic adjustment, the deep relaxation it induces can help release chronic muscle tension that contributes to poor posture. By fostering greater body awareness, you become more attuned to how you're holding yourself, making it easier to naturally adopt a more aligned and comfortable stance after the session.

What should I expect during my first Sound Bath session?

You'll lie down comfortably, often on a mat with blankets and pillows, and simply listen. Dr. Morgan Ellis will play various instruments like singing bowls and gongs, creating a rich soundscape. There's nothing you need to 'do' except relax and allow the sounds to wash over you. Most people feel deeply relaxed and some even drift into a light sleep.

Is a Sound Bath right for me if I'm not into 'spiritual' practices?

Yes, completely! Our Sound Bath focuses on the tangible benefits of sound on your body and mind, particularly for stress reduction and physical awareness. You don't need to subscribe to any specific beliefs. Just come as you are, ready to experience a unique form of deep relaxation and a natural way to support your well-being during your college years.
