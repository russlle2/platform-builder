What exactly is a Sound Bath and how will it help me as a new mother?

A Sound Bath is a deeply immersive, full-body listening experience where you are bathed in sound waves produced by various instruments like crystal bowls and gongs. For new mothers, it's designed to downshift your nervous system, reduce the physical and mental stress of postpartum, and encourage deep relaxation, which aids in your recovery and emotional well-being.

I'm worried about finding time or being comfortable. What can I expect during a session?

We understand that your time and comfort are precious. Our sessions are structured to be accessible. You'll simply lie down on a comfortable mat, often with blankets and pillows for support, and let the sounds wash over you. There's nothing you need to 'do' except exist. We create a nurturing space where you can fully relax, without judgment or expectation.

Will a Sound Bath help with the 'baby brain' fogginess or feeling overwhelmed?

Absolutely. The gentle frequencies and vibrations encourage your brain to move into a more relaxed state, which can help to clear mental fog and improve focus. By regulating your nervous system, a Sound Bath helps to calm the overwhelm, allowing your mind to quiet down and process thoughts more clearly, giving you a sense of renewed clarity.

Is a Sound Bath safe for me during my postpartum recovery period?

Yes, Sound Baths are generally very safe and highly beneficial during postpartum. The sounds are not jarring or intrusive. If you have any specific health concerns, it's always wise to consult with your healthcare provider. Our intention is always to create a gentle, supportive, and restorative experience tailored to your unique needs as a new mother.
