Frequently Asked Questions

Is a Sound Bath safe for someone in their postpartum recovery period?
Absolutely. Our Sound Bath sessions are designed with gentle frequencies and a tranquil environment, making them very safe and supportive for new mothers. We encourage you to listen to your body and make yourself as comfortable as possible throughout the session.
What should I expect during my first Sound Bath session as a new mother?
You'll lie down comfortably, perhaps with a blanket or pillow, and simply listen as various instruments are played. There's nothing specific you need to 'do.' The goal is for you to relax, allow the sounds to wash over you, and find a moment of peace for your spiritual and emotional well-being.
How can a Sound Bath specifically help with spiritual growth and inner peace during postpartum?
The deep relaxation induced by the sound frequencies helps to calm the overactive mind, creating an opening for introspection and connection to your inner self. This can foster a profound sense of peace and clarity, supporting your spiritual journey as you navigate the new dimensions of motherhood.
Can I bring my baby to a Sound Bath session?
For the deepest experience of inner peace and spiritual reflection, we generally recommend arranging childcare so you can fully immerse yourself. However, we also offer specific 'Mom & Baby' Sound Bath sessions designed to accommodate your little one, so please inquire about those options.
