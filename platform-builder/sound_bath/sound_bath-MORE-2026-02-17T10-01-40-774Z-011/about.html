How we work — simple, mapped, repeatable

An outline you can expect each session to follow.
  1. Brief arrival check: name, body, and aim (5–7min).
  2. Guided tuning and breath cues into sound work (10–15min).
  3. Main sound phase shaped to mood and intensity (20–30min).
  4. Structured close: note-taking and a clear re-entry cue (5–10min).
