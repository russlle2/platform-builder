Questions people ask before they come

Concise answers below. If something's not listed, message us at {{EMAIL}} or call {{PHONE}}.
Will I be asked to participate actively?
Sessions ask you to arrive present and follow simple breath cues. Movement is optional; most of the work is listening and rest.
How do you choose instruments?
We select bowls, chimes, tongue drums, and overtone instruments with a focus on clear harmonics and safe low frequencies.
Can you make accommodations?
Yes. Email us ahead and we will reserve accessible seating, supports, or an adjusted sound plan.
What if I'm pregnant?
Share that in registration. Low-volume, gentle formats are usually recommended; consult your health provider with questions.
Is it safe with medications?
If you take medication that alters alertness, balance, or sensory processing, check with your clinician first.
