Is a Sound Bath suitable for new mothers who are still recovering?

Absolutely. Our Sound Bath sessions are designed to be incredibly gentle and restorative, perfect for your postpartum recovery. You'll simply lie down in a comfortable position, making it accessible even with physical tenderness or fatigue. The focus is on deep relaxation and inner peace, supporting your body's natural healing processes.

What should I expect during my first Sound Bath session?

You can expect a calm and supportive environment. You'll be invited to lie down comfortably, perhaps with a blanket and eye mask. Dr. Morgan Ellis will guide you through the experience as various instruments are played, creating a soothing soundscape. There's nothing you need to 'do' except allow yourself to relax and receive the sounds. Many new mothers find it a profound experience of deep rest and mental clarity.

How can a Sound Bath help with the emotional and spiritual demands of new motherhood?

The resonant frequencies in a Sound Bath help to calm your nervous system, which in turn can alleviate feelings of overwhelm or anxiety often experienced postpartum. By fostering a state of deep meditation, it creates space for introspection and spiritual reconnection, helping you to feel more grounded, centered, and at peace with the shifts occurring in your life. It's a dedicated time for your inner world.

Do I need any prior experience with meditation or sound therapy?

Not at all. Our Sound Bath sessions require no prior experience. Just come as you are, with an open mind. Dr. Morgan Ellis will create a welcoming and inclusive atmosphere, explaining everything you need to know. The beauty of a Sound Bath is its accessibility; the sounds do the work, allowing your mind and body to naturally find their way to a state of calm.
