Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is a Sound Bath and how will it benefit me?
A Sound Bath is a deeply relaxing experience where you lie down and listen as various resonant instruments are played around you. For seniors, it's a gentle way to encourage mental calm, reduce feelings of stress, and foster a beautiful sense of inner peace and spiritual connection without needing any physical movement.
Is the Sound Bath suitable if I have mobility concerns or hearing aids?
Absolutely. Our Sound Baths are designed to be fully accessible and gentle. You'll be comfortably seated or reclined, and no physical movement is required. If you wear hearing aids, you might find it more comfortable to adjust their settings or remove them, as the vibrations are felt as much as heard. We're happy to accommodate your needs.
What should I wear or bring to my Sound Bath session?
We recommend wearing comfortable, loose clothing that allows you to relax fully. You might also want to bring a water bottle for hydration. We provide all necessary mats, blankets, and bolsters for your comfort, ensuring a cozy and supportive environment for your experience.
How can a Sound Bath help with my spiritual growth or inner peace?
The gentle, immersive sounds of a Sound Bath can help quiet the busy mind, allowing you to enter a state of deep meditation and introspection. Many seniors find this a wonderful opportunity to reflect, connect with their inner wisdom, and cultivate a profound sense of calm and acceptance, nurturing their spiritual well-being.
