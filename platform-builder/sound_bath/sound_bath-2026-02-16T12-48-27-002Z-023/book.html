Session Flow — what to expect

  1. Welcome & intake (5–10 min)
  2. Grounding and guided breathwork (8–12 min)
  3. Main soundscape (25–40 min) — instruments may include singing bowls, monochord, light gong and harmonic chimes
  4. Slow tapering, grounding, optional check-out (10–15 min)

We maintain an enclosed, low-stimulus environment and moderate sound levels to be accessible to as many participants as possible.

Private & Specialty Formats

  • 1:1 session — personalized pacing and targeted somatic support (45–75 min).
  • Couples — shared sound container with relational cues and guided co-regulation (60 min).
  • Corporate / team — brief group offerings focused on stress resilience and team grounding (30–45 min).

Safety & Contraindications

Sound work is generally gentle, but if you have photosensitive epilepsy, implanted devices that are vibration-sensitive, uncontrolled psychiatric conditions, or are pregnant and require specific clearance, please consult your clinician and notify us at {{EMAIL}} prior to attending.
