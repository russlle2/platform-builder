1:1 Individual Sessions

Deeply personalized session including intake, 50–70 minute sound immersion, and 15-minute integration. Ideal for nervous-system regulation, rest, and focused transitions.

Typical flow
  1. Intake & intention setting (10–15m)
  2. Sound immersion (50–70m) with responsive playing
  3. Slow return + notes and aftercare (10–15m)

Instruments in rotation

Gong • Singing bowls (metal) • Koshi chimes

Contraindications & safety

Not advised for uncontrolled epilepsy, recent surgeries, or for those with certain implants. If pregnant or on medication that affects sensory processing, consult your physician first.

Couples & shared sessions

Designed to support relational attunement. Sessions emphasize breath-syncing, mirrored pauses, and a shared sonic field. Includes a brief collaborative check-in.

Goals
  • Co-regulation
  • Shared somatic awareness
  • Guided integration practices

Instruments

Crystal bowls • Tuning forks • Soft hand drum

Corporate & team sound experiences

Short-form interventions for teams (30–60 minutes) to support focus, collective rest, and reset between demanding work cycles. We provide a brief evidence-minded intro, the sonic intervention, and practical takeaways.

Suitable for

  • Leadership retreats
  • Deep work days
  • Wellness allowances / team benefits

Instruments

Monochord • Crystal singing bowls • Light chimes
