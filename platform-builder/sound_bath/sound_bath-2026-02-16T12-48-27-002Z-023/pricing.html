Policies & important notes

  • Cancellation: 72-hour notice for full refund. Within 72 hours: credit only.
  • Deposit: Private bookings require a non-refundable deposit to secure date.
  • Health safety: Disclose any implants, pregnancy, or recent surgical procedures before booking.

Contraindications

Not suitable for individuals with uncontrolled epilepsy, active acute psychiatric crisis, or those advised by their physician to avoid sustained acoustic exposure. If you have a pacemaker or implanted neurostimulator, confirm safety with your clinician and inform us prior to session.

What to bring

Comfortable layered clothing, a blanket, pillow or bolster, water bottle, and any assistive items you require. We provide a limited supply of mats and bolsters.
