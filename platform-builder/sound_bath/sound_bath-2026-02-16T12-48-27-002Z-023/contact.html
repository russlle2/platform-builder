Myth vs. truth

A clear-headed look at common assumptions.

Myth

Sound baths are only for yogis or mystics.

Truth

They are evidence-informed practices for relaxation and nervous system regulation — accessible to beginners.

Myth

You must do anything special to prepare.

Truth

Comfortable clothing and a receptive mindset are sufficient; see "what to bring" for details.

Facilitation notes & case highlights

Clinical observations from sessions — anonymized and curated for clarity.

Quiet re-entry

We facilitate gradual sensory downshift to avoid overstimulation during re-entry; most participants report clearer sleep the night of the session.

Adaptive cues

For clients with sensory sensitivities we modulate amplitude and frequency, offer ear-covering options, and emphasize pre-session intake.

Frequently asked

Are sound baths safe during pregnancy?

Many find them supportive; however, we advise a medical check with your provider. Let us know on intake so we can adapt the session.

Contraindications?

If you have a history of epilepsy, severe cardiovascular conditions, or recent head trauma, please consult a clinician before attending. This information helps us tailor modulation and proximity of instruments.

Can I fall asleep?

Yes. Falling asleep is a natural physiological response; we allow a gentle reentry window at the end to support safety and integration.
