Myths, then clarity

Myth: A sound bath is mystical theatrics

Truth: It’s guided sensory modulation using precise tones to calm the nervous system. We design each session with clinical calm to create reliable relaxation responses.

Myth: You need to surrender control

Truth: You remain fully present. Practices are accessible, adaptive, and trauma-informed. Expect gentle instruction and options to opt out anytime.

We build sessions on three pillars

Precision

Curated instruments (crystal singing bowls, tuning forks, chimes) tuned to intention and breath patterns to guide nervous system regulation.

Safety

Facilitator-led briefings, contraindications screening, and clear exit signals to ensure every participant feels in control.

Ritual with reason

We use a predictable flow—arrival, grounding, sound immersion, gentle return—to create dependable transitions into and out of deep rest.

Common questions

Bring a yoga mat or blanket, eye pillow, water, and an optional thin pillow for neck support. Dress in comfortable layers for temperature changes.
If you are pregnant, have a pacemaker, recent head injury, uncontrolled seizures, or severe hearing conditions, consult your medical provider first and inform the facilitator. Sessions are not a replacement for medical care.
Arrival & quieting (10 min), orientation & intention (5–10 min), sound immersion (30–45 min), soft reentry & journaling (10–15 min). Facilitator guides transitions throughout.
We may use crystal bowls, chimes, a monochord, and targeted tuning forks. Instrument selection varies by session focus.
