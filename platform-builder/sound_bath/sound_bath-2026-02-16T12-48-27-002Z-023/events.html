Contraindications: Not recommended during early pregnancy, for uncontrolled epilepsy, or within 24 hours of certain medical procedures. If you have a pacemaker or implanted device please consult your physician before attending.

Practical — What to bring

Small list for a premium sensory experience: soft blanket, pillow for neck support, socks, water bottle, eye mask if desired. A limited number of bolsters are available on site.

Flow of an evening

  1. Check-in & grounding (15–20 min) with orientation and breath guidance.
  2. Gradual tuning + warm-up including pendulum chimes and tuning forks.
  3. Deep immersion (approx. 55 min) — sustained bowls, gentle gong strokes, and harmonic overlays.
  4. Soft return, silent pause, guided integration and optional tea (10–20 min).

Frequently asked in brief

Is this meditation? It shares elements with meditation and body-centered therapy but is non-directive and somatic-first.

We keep groups small and the tone clinical, calm, and attentive to ensure accessibility for first-timers and regulars alike.
