Myths vs. Truths

Myth: It’s just background noise

Truth: Sound baths are an intentional, layered sound-field designed to guide attention, breath, and nervous system regulation. Instruments are tuned with purpose.

Myth: You need prior meditation experience

Truth: No experience required. Sessions are guided so that everyone can find a restful state; the sound scaffolds your experience.

Myth: It’s physically intrusive

Truth: Sound is delivered at therapeutic levels. Setups prioritize comfort, consent, and pacing. Contraindications are screened beforehand.

Three Guiding Pillars

Safety

Pre-session check-ins, low lighting, and clear instructions. We list contraindications openly and offer alternative positions (chair, bolster, or lying).

Immersion

Multi-instrument soundscapes combining crystal bowls, gong swells, chimes, and the monochord to create motion and harmonic clarity.

Intention

Each session follows a clear flow: arrival + intention setting, grounding sounds, deep immersion, gentle return, and integration with tea or journaling prompts.

Frequently Asked — Practicalities

What should I bring?

Bring a yoga mat, a blanket or two, and a pillow for head support. We provide bolsters and eye-pillows, but you’re welcome to bring your own for comfort.

Are there any contraindications?

Not suitable for pregnant people without prior approval, those with recent seizures, uncontrolled heart conditions, or individuals using certain medical implants. Please contact us at {{EMAIL}} if you have concerns.

What is the flow of a session?
  1. Arrival & welcome: settle in, brief intention setting.
  2. Grounding: breathwork and gentle chimes or tuning forks.
  3. Immersion: layered bowls, gong, and melodic drone (30–50 min).
  4. Return: soft tones and integration, followed by tea or quiet journaling time.
