Frequently asked

Do I need to bring anything special?
Bring a mat or blanket, a small pillow, a light cover, and water. We provide a limited number of bolsters; bring your own if you prefer.
What instruments will be used?
This venue's palette often includes a gong for layered undertones, a monochord to create gentle drones, and crystal bowls for shimmering harmonics.
Can I leave early?
Yes. Sessions are non-invasive. If you need to exit, do so quietly — facilitators can suggest a discreet path.
Are sessions therapeutic?
We offer restorative experiences, not psychotherapy. If you seek clinical therapy, please consult a licensed practitioner alongside this work.

Safety & contraindications

If you have implanted medical devices, are pregnant in the first trimester, have epilepsy, or are currently experiencing acute psychiatric symptoms, contact us before booking. For tailored guidance email {{EMAIL}}.

About attending

Arrive 10–15 minutes early to settle. Wear layers. Keep phones off or on airplane mode to preserve the field.
