Myths & clarity — the sound bath explained

Myth

Sound baths are just music you lie to. They’re loud and chaotic.

Truth

These are carefully held, sensory environments using tuned instruments to invite parasympathetic rest. Volume is intentional, not intrusive.

How to prepare

Wear layers, bring an eye cover if you prefer. We provide mats, but personal mats are welcome.

What to bring

Light blanket, water bottle, phone on airplane mode, personal pillow for neck support.

Contraindications

If you are pregnant, have a pacemaker, recent surgeries, epilepsy, or severe psychiatric conditions please consult with your care provider and notify us before booking.
