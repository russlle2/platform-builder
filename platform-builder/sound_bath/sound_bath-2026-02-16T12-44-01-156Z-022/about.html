Common myths and what actually happens

Myth:

Sound baths are hypnotic shows where you lose control.

Truth:

They are guided experiences that support conscious presence. You remain fully embodied and can come and go; the facilitator cues breath and space.

Myth:

Lying down is mandatory.

Truth:

You may sit, recline, or lie—whatever your body needs. Comfort always comes first.

How a session flows

Example structure — a careful scaffolding for nervous system reset:
  1. Arrival & orientation: 10–12 minutes to settle, choose props and positioning.
  2. Opening tone & breath cue: gentle introduction to the sonic space.
  3. Main sound journey: 25–35 minutes of layered instruments (crystal bowls, tuning forks, Himalayan chimes).
  4. Gradual return: soft grounding tones and a short integration period with space to journal or share.
