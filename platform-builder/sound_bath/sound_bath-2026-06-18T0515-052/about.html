Is a Sound Bath suitable for new mothers still in early postpartum recovery?

Absolutely. Our Sound Bath sessions are designed to be gentle and restorative, making them ideal for new mothers at any stage of their postpartum recovery. The experience is entirely passive; you simply lie back and receive the sounds, allowing your body to relax without any physical exertion. We prioritize your comfort and can accommodate specific needs.

How does a Sound Bath specifically help with postpartum nervous system regulation?

The resonant frequencies and vibrations from our instruments encourage your brainwaves to slow down, shifting from a stressed beta state to a more relaxed alpha or theta state. This shift signals your nervous system to move out of 'alert' mode, reducing the physiological indicators of stress like heart rate and muscle tension, which is crucial for a new mother's recuperation.

What if I find it difficult to relax or quiet my mind during a Sound Bath?

It's completely normal for your mind to wander, especially during the postpartum period. There's no pressure to 'achieve' a certain state of relaxation. Simply allow the sounds to wash over you. The beauty of a Sound Bath is that its effects are often felt even if your mind remains active. Over time, with regular sessions, you may find it becomes easier to settle into deeper states of calm.

What should I wear or bring to my Sound Bath session?

For your utmost comfort, we recommend wearing soft, loose-fitting clothing. You'll be lying down for the duration of the session, so dressing in layers can be helpful to adjust to the room temperature. You don't need to bring anything specific, as we provide all necessary comforts like mats, blankets, and bolsters to ensure you feel completely supported and at ease.
