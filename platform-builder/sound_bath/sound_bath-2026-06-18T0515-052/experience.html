Sound Bath

Crafting Calm: The Midnight Bloom Sound Bath Experience

Each Midnight Bloom Sound Bath is a curated experience, designed with your specific postpartum needs in mind. We utilize a refined selection of crystal singing bowls, gongs, and chimes, chosen for their pure tones and harmonizing frequencies. These sounds create an immersive sonic environment that bypasses cognitive resistance, allowing you to effortlessly enter a state of deep relaxation. This precise application of sound helps to quiet the incessant mental chatter, reducing cortisol levels and promoting a sense of grounded tranquility essential for new mothers.
