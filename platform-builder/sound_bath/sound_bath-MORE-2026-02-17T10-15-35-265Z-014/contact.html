How the selector works

The Mood-to-Method selector maps your emotional tone to a short, intentional structure. Paired with the sound preference mixer, it produces an approach name, what to expect, and the CTA phrasing that best fits — e.g., "Book a Short Grounding" vs "Reserve a Deep Resonance". This is a suggested match; we confirm details after you reach out.

Examples
  • Frayed + Gentle → "Cocoon Ease" — 25-min calming sequence with soft bowls & breath cues.
  • Wired + Intense → "Root Release" — 40-min layered gong & drumming with periods of silence to land.
  • Foggy + Medium → "Clarity Loom" — 30-min focused harmonic tones with guided attention anchors.
