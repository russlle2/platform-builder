Questions & health guidance

How long are sessions?
Typical group sessions run 40–60 minutes. Private sessions are usually 50 minutes. Special gatherings are indicated on the events calendar.
What should I bring?
Bring comfortable clothing, a mat or cushion if you like, and a light blanket. We provide bolsters and mats at most venues; check the event notes.
Health & safety — important contraindications
While many people benefit from sound sessions, they are not suitable for everyone. Please consult your healthcare provider before attending if you have any of the following: epilepsy or a history of seizures; recent traumatic brain injury; recent major surgery; untreated bipolar disorder; implanted medical devices that could be affected by vibration (if unsure, check with your clinician). If you are pregnant, please inform the facilitator before booking. For any acute mental health concerns please seek medical advice; sound work is a complementary practice, not a replacement for medical or psychiatric care.
Are sessions loud?
We aim for texture over volume. Instruments are played intentionally; facilitators adjust intensity to the group or individual. If you prefer softer textures, choose the "Gentle" setting in the sound preference mixer below.
