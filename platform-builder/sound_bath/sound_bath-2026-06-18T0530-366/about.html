How can a Sound Bath help with physical pain from chronic illness or injury?

The gentle vibrations from the Sound Bath instruments can encourage your muscles to relax and your nervous system to settle, which often reduces the sensation of chronic physical pain. It helps your body shift from a 'fight or flight' state, which can exacerbate pain, into a state of deep rest and somatic recalibration, supporting your recovery.

Is a Sound Bath safe if I'm recovering from a specific injury?

Yes, Sound Baths are generally very gentle and non-invasive. We encourage you to choose the most comfortable position for your body, using props like bolsters and blankets to support any areas of injury. The focus is always on creating a safe and supportive environment for your body's specific needs as you recover.

What should I bring or wear to a Sound Bath session?

To ensure your comfort during the Sound Bath, we recommend wearing loose, comfortable clothing. You might also consider bringing a water bottle, and if you have a favorite blanket or eye mask that brings you comfort, feel free to bring those along too. Our goal is for you to feel completely at ease.

How often should someone in recovery attend Sound Bath sessions to see benefits?

The frequency that works best for you will depend on your individual recovery process and how your body responds. Many find that regular sessions, perhaps once a week or bi-weekly, help maintain a state of physical ease and support ongoing somatic healing. We can discuss a personalized approach based on your specific needs.
