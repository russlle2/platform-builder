How does a Sound Bath specifically assist individuals recovering from chronic illness or injury?

Our Sound Bath sessions are designed to support your recovery by focusing on nervous system regulation. The specific sound frequencies and vibrations encourage a deep state of relaxation, which helps reduce the chronic stress response often associated with prolonged illness or injury. This, in turn, can create a more optimal internal environment for your body's restorative processes.

Will the sounds be too intense or overwhelming for my sensitive system during recovery?

We prioritize your comfort and well-being. Our Sound Bath sessions are carefully moderated to ensure a gentle and soothing experience. Dr. Morgan Ellis is adept at creating an environment that respects the sensitivities often present during recovery, ensuring the soundscape is deeply calming rather than overwhelming.

What should I expect to feel or experience during a Sound Bath session?

During a Sound Bath, you can anticipate a profound sense of inner quiet and physical ease. Many clients report a significant reduction in muscle tension, a slowing of mental chatter, and a feeling of being deeply supported. Your nervous system will be gently guided towards a state of rest, allowing for stress reduction and a renewed sense of calm.

How frequently should I attend Sound Bath sessions to support my recovery?

The optimal frequency for your Sound Bath sessions depends on your individual recovery needs and current state. We recommend beginning with a consistent schedule to establish nervous system regulation. Dr. Morgan Ellis can discuss a personalized plan with you during your initial consultation, ensuring our approach aligns perfectly with your recovery goals.
