Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly happens during a Sound Bath, and is it suitable for me?
During a Sound Bath, you lie down comfortably while I play various instruments, creating a tapestry of gentle sounds. It's perfectly suited for you, as there's no physical movement required. The intention is to help you relax deeply and find a sense of inner calm, which many of our senior clients find very beneficial for spiritual well-being.
Do I need any special preparation or experience for a Sound Bath?
No special preparation is needed. Just wear comfortable clothing and arrive ready to relax. You don't need any prior experience with meditation or sound practices. Just bring an open mind and a willingness to simply be present with the sounds.
How can a Sound Bath support my spiritual growth and inner peace?
The gentle vibrations and harmonic sounds in a Sound Bath can help quiet the mind and reduce mental chatter. This allows for a deeper state of relaxation and introspection, which can open pathways to spiritual reflection, a greater sense of connection, and a profound feeling of inner peace. It’s a gentle way to nurture your spirit.
I have some hearing aids; will a Sound Bath still be effective for me?
Many individuals with hearing aids enjoy Sound Bath sessions. You can adjust your hearing aids to a comfortable level, or even remove them if you prefer to feel the vibrations more directly. The experience is often about the felt sense of sound as much as what you hear, and we can discuss any adjustments to ensure your comfort.
