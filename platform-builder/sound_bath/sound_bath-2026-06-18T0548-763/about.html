What exactly happens during a Sound Bath session?

During a Sound Bath, you'll lie down or sit comfortably while Dr. Morgan Ellis plays various instruments like crystal singing bowls and gongs. The sounds create gentle vibrations that encourage your body and mind to relax deeply. It's a very passive experience; you simply rest and let the sounds wash over you.

Is a Sound Bath suitable for someone with mobility concerns?

Absolutely. We prioritize your comfort. You can participate in a Sound Bath while sitting in a chair, or if you prefer, lying down on a soft mat with pillows and blankets. Please let us know your needs when you book, and we will ensure you are well-supported and comfortable throughout the session.

How can a Sound Bath help with my relationships or emotional well-being?

Many of our seniors find that the deep relaxation from a Sound Bath helps to quiet the mind and reduce stress. When you're feeling calmer and more centered, you often find it easier to connect with others, listen attentively, and respond with more patience and understanding in your relationships. It fosters a gentle emotional balance.

What should I wear or bring to a Sound Bath?

We recommend wearing comfortable, loose-fitting clothing that allows you to relax fully. You might also want to bring a pair of warm socks or a light sweater, as your body temperature can drop during deep relaxation. We provide mats, blankets, and pillows, so you don't need to bring anything else unless you have a favorite comfort item.
