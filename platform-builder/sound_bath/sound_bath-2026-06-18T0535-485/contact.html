Sound Bath

What's a Sound Bath, and How Does it Help Your College Brain?

A Sound Bath is a chill, seated or reclined experience where you're bathed in soothing sounds from instruments like singing bowls and gongs. Think of it as a mental massage for your stressed-out college brain. It's not about 'doing' anything; you just show up, lie back, and let the sounds help quiet the endless to-do lists and exam worries, paving the way for clearer thinking.
