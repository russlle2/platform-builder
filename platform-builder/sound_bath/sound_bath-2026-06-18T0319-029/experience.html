The Gentle Art of a Sound Bath: Calming Your Overwhelmed Nerves

Imagine your nervous system as a finely tuned instrument that sometimes plays too loudly. During a Sound Bath, the vibrations from various instruments encourage it to settle into a more harmonious rhythm. This isn't about escaping, but rather about providing your system with the subtle cues it needs to release stress and find a state of deep, restorative peace. You simply lie back and receive.
