Questions & practicals

Answers for what to expect, safety notes, and how to prepare.

Do I need experience?
No. Our sessions are designed for first-timers and regulars alike. Guidance is minimal but precise.
How long is a cohort session?
Most gatherings run 60–75 minutes, with time to arrive and settle beforehand.
What should I wear?
Comfortable, layered clothing is ideal. The room is kept at a cool, comfortable temperature.
Contraindications — important
If you are pregnant, have uncontrolled epilepsy, severe cardiovascular disease, recent head injury, or use an implanted medical device (e.g., pacemaker), please consult your clinician and let us know before booking. Some auditory patterns and low-frequency sounds may be intense for certain conditions. If in doubt, contact your healthcare provider or reach out to us at {{EMAIL}}.
Do you offer private bookings?
Yes — see Solo Sessions on the site and reach out for custom scheduling and space options.
