How we work

We structure sessions in three clear acts: arrival settling, a tuned sound arc, and a soft re-emergence. Each act is guided so staying present is simple — not forced.

Why cohort format?

A cohort holds a gentle container: people move together through the rhythm of the event, amplifying calm without pressure to perform.
