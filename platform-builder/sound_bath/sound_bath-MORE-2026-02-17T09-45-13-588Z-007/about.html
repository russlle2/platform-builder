How we design a cohort series

We sequence short iterations — five sessions is a typical WaveSeries — so you and a steady group can notice change without committing to indefinite enrollment.

Programs are named for their intent: Flow, Reset, and Anchor. Each series includes session notes, a short practice to use between meetings, and a recorded recap for the cohort.

A little on our method

We combine tuned bowls, gentle percussive texture, and guided breath cues to shepherd attention. Sessions are intentionally concise — built to recalibrate without extending into ritual pressure. Our cohorts are limited in size so everyone has a guided journey within a held container.

Pricing and schedules shift with each epoch; we favor short-series passes that reward consistency without creating dependency. If you need private support, we guide you toward one-on-one options.

Contraindications & care

Sound immersion is gentle but not suitable for everyone. Please consult with your healthcare provider if you have epilepsy, are pregnant and have concerns, have recent head or inner-ear surgery, or use medical implants. If you have severe anxiety or PTSD, reach out before booking so we can find the right format for you — sometimes a private session or a different practitioner is safer. We will always suggest modifications.

If you take medication that affects heart rate or consciousness, please notify us before arriving. Your safety is our priority.
