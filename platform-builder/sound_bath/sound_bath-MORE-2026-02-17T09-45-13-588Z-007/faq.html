Frequently Asked Questions

What should I expect at a cohort session?
Arrive a few minutes early, settle into a chair or cushion. Sessions begin with a short orientation, move through tuned bowls and guided breath cues, and close with a brief integration period. Come in clothes you can relax in. Hydration and a light meal beforehand are suggested.
How big are the groups?
We limit cohorts to a size that lets everyone settle into the work — typically 8–18 people depending on venue. The aim is a held container where the guide can attend to the field without losing intimacy.
What if I’m on medication or have medical concerns?
If you take medication that impacts heart rate, consciousness, or balance, consult with your clinician. If you have a diagnosis such as epilepsy, vestibular disorders, recent head/ear surgeries, or use medical implants, check with us and your provider first. We will recommend a private session or modified practice when appropriate.
Can I record sessions?
Recordings are not permitted during live, in-person cohorts to protect the shared container. Cohort leaders may provide short recorded recaps or practice snippets for cohort members.
Refunds and transfers
If you need to cancel, please contact us as soon as possible. Cohort passes may be transferable to another named attendee with notice. Refund policies vary by program; see the Investment page for specifics or contact us directly.
Contraindications & safety

Sound immersion can be relaxing and potent. If you live with severe anxiety, PTSD, epilepsy, or have had recent inner-ear or cranial surgery, please reach out before enrolling. If you are pregnant and concerned about sensations, get medical guidance first. We will always suggest safer formats — a private session, an alternative guide, or additional supports.
