During a Sound Bath, the resonant tones encourage your brain to shift from beta (active, alert) to alpha and theta states (relaxed, meditative). This physiological shift helps to reduce the chronic stress response common in postpartum, aiding in better sleep, emotional balance, and overall physical recovery. Frequently Asked Questions

Is a Sound Bath suitable for new mothers who are still recovering physically?
Absolutely. Our Sound Bath sessions are designed to be completely passive. You simply lie down comfortably, or sit if preferred, and allow the sounds to wash over you. There is no physical exertion required, making it ideal for all stages of postpartum recovery, whether you're weeks or months in.
How does Sound Bath specifically help with nervous system regulation during postpartum?
During a Sound Bath, the sustained tones and frequencies gently guide your brainwaves into slower states, like alpha and theta. This shift activates your parasympathetic nervous system, commonly known as your 'rest and digest' system, counteracting the 'fight or flight' response often heightened during postpartum. It's a direct route to calming your body's stress response.
I'm worried about finding time for myself. How long is a typical Sound Bath session?
We understand that your time is precious. Our typical Sound Bath sessions are designed to be effective yet efficient, lasting approximately 60 minutes. This allows for dedicated time to deeply relax and reset your nervous system without requiring a significant time commitment away from your baby.
What should I expect to feel or experience during a Postpartum Sound Bath?
You can expect to experience a deep sense of relaxation and peace. Many participants report feeling a gentle 'reset' for their mind and body. You might notice a decrease in mental chatter, a softening of physical tension, and an overall feeling of being more grounded and calm. Each experience is unique, but the common thread is profound rest.
