How Sound Bath Supports Your Postural Health

Our Sound Bath sessions are crafted to encourage a deeper connection to your physical self. The gentle vibrations and calming tones help quiet external distractions, allowing you to tune into your body's natural alignment. This increased awareness can subtly guide you toward more balanced and comfortable postures, reducing everyday strains and improving your sense of ease.

What should I expect during my first Sound Bath session?

You'll be invited to lie comfortably on a mat, often with a blanket and pillow for support. Dr. Morgan Ellis will guide you through a gentle introduction to the experience. The session involves various instruments creating resonant sounds. Your only task is to relax and allow the sounds to wash over you. Many people report feeling deeply relaxed and centered afterwards.

Is the Sound Bath suitable for someone with limited mobility or physical discomfort?

Absolutely. Our Sound Bath sessions are designed to be entirely passive and gentle. You remain in a comfortable, reclined position throughout. There's no physical exertion required, making it ideal for individuals with limited mobility or those seeking relief from chronic discomfort. We can also provide extra support cushions if needed.

How can Sound Bath specifically help with my posture as a senior?

The calming effect of the Sound Bath helps relax tense muscles, which often contribute to poor posture. By reducing stress and fostering a deeper connection to your body, you can become more attuned to your natural alignment. This increased body awareness empowers you to gently adjust and maintain more balanced postures in your daily life, reducing strain.

What kind of sounds will I hear during a Sound Bath?

You'll experience a tapestry of gentle, resonant sounds produced by instruments such as crystal singing bowls, gongs, chimes, and other calming percussive tools. These are chosen for their soothing qualities and ability to create a deeply relaxing environment, supporting your body's natural inclination towards ease and balance.
