Frequently Asked Questions

How does a Sound Bath specifically address the emotional stress of athletes?
Our Sound Bath sessions are designed to create a safe space where your nervous system can downregulate. This allows you to process the emotional residue of intense training, competition pressures, or injury, rather than suppressing it. It's about acknowledging the full spectrum of your experience as an athlete, not just the physical.
I'm always active; will I be able to relax during a Sound Bath?
Many active individuals find the structured environment of a Sound Bath surprisingly effective for relaxation. The consistent, enveloping sound frequencies provide a focal point, often making it easier to quiet an active mind and body than traditional meditation. We guide you gently, ensuring your comfort and ease throughout the session.
Is Sound Bath safe if I'm dealing with a recent injury or physical discomfort?
Absolutely. A Sound Bath is a non-physical practice. You'll rest comfortably, and the sound works on a vibrational level. Please inform us of any injuries so we can ensure your positioning is optimally supportive and adjust the sound intensity if necessary, focusing on your emotional well-being during recovery.
What should I expect emotionally from a trauma-informed Sound Bath?
Our trauma-informed approach means we prioritize your safety and autonomy. You might experience a range of emotions as your system relaxes. This is a natural part of processing. We create a supportive, non-judgmental environment, allowing you to simply be with whatever arises, without pressure or expectation for a specific outcome.
