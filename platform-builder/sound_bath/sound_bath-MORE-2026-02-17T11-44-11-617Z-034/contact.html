Health & safety

Sound practices can use sustained tones and deep vibrations. Please note the following contraindications: recent concussion, uncontrolled epilepsy, severe psychiatric distress, pregnancy (consult provider), and implanted medical devices that might be sensitive to vibration. If in doubt, consult a clinician before attending. Inform the facilitator of any concerns prior to a session.
