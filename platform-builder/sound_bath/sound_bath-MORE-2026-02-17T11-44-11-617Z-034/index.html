Common questions & safety notes

Is this suitable if I'm anxious or new to sound?

Yes. Sessions are paced simply and include verbal cues. You may remain seated, keep your eyes open, or use ear protection if that helps you ease in.

Contraindications

Sound sessions are generally safe for most people, but they may not be appropriate for anyone with recent head/neck injury, uncontrolled epilepsy, a history of severe psychosis, or pregnancy complications without practitioner clearance. If you have concerns, please consult your healthcare provider before attending and email us at {{EMAIL}} for a pre-session consultation.
