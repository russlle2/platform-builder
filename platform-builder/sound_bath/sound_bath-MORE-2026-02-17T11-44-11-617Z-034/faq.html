Questions & practical notes

Quick answers about attendance, safety, and how to prepare. If you need more, reach out at {{EMAIL}}.

What should I bring?
Bring a mat, blanket, pillow, and water. Wear comfortable layers. We provide a limited number of mats—if you need one, note it when you reserve.
How long is a typical gathering?
Most public gatherings run 60–75 minutes. Private VIP Day immersions are multi-hour and include consultation, multiple sound frames, and follow-up notes.
Are recordings available?
We generally do not record live gatherings. For private clients, curated follow-up sound tracks may be created as part of a VIP Day package.
Can I talk during the session?
Short check-ins at the start and finish are normal. During the sound phase we ask for quiet and inward attention to preserve the field for others.
Is this therapy?
Sessions are a form of facilitation and relaxation practice, not psychotherapy. If you are in active mental health treatment or have severe trauma histories, discuss with your clinician and let us know before booking.

Safety & contraindications

Do not attend if you have any of the following without medical clearance: a pacemaker or implanted electrical device, uncontrolled epilepsy, active psychosis, or are in the first trimester of pregnancy with a high-risk pregnancy. If you have concerns, email {{EMAIL}} or call {{PHONE}}.
