Contraindications & accessibility

Private sessions are adjusted when necessary. However, for safety: please disclose pregnancy (especially first trimester), implanted medical devices, recent traumatic brain injury, or uncontrolled seizures. We will adapt or recommend alternatives if needed. If you rely on sensory assistance, tell us in advance so we can prepare the space.
