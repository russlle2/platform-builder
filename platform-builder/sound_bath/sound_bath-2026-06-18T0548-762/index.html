Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Sound Bath specifically support my postpartum recovery?
A Sound Bath offers a gentle, non-invasive way to calm your nervous system, which is often overstimulated during postpartum. It helps reduce feelings of anxiety and fatigue, promoting deeper rest and emotional regulation. This creates a more peaceful internal space, allowing you to better connect with your baby and support your own healing.
Will a Sound Bath help me reconnect with my partner after having a baby?
Absolutely. The tranquil environment of a Sound Bath can be a powerful tool for relationship healing. By individually finding a state of calm and presence, you and your partner can then come together from a more grounded place, fostering deeper empathy, understanding, and heartfelt communication. It’s a shared space for gentle emotional recalibration.
What should I expect during my first Sound Bath session as a new mother?
You can expect a peaceful, comfortable space where you'll lie down and simply listen. The gentle vibrations and tones from various instruments will wash over you, encouraging deep relaxation. There's nothing you need to 'do' except allow yourself to receive the sounds. Many new mothers find it a profound opportunity for quiet reflection and emotional release.
Is a Sound Bath safe for me if I'm still feeling very sensitive or emotional postpartum?
Yes, our Sound Bath sessions are designed with utmost sensitivity and care, particularly for new mothers. The vibrations are gentle and non-intrusive. We create a supportive, understanding space where any emotions that arise are simply acknowledged without judgment. It's an opportunity to lovingly tend to your emotional landscape during this tender time.
