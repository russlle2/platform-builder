Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Sound Bath and how can it help me during postpartum recovery?
A Sound Bath is a meditative experience where you are bathed in sounds produced by various instruments like singing bowls and gongs. During your postpartum recovery, it can help calm your nervous system, reduce feelings of overwhelm, and provide a dedicated time for spiritual reflection, helping you recenter and find inner peace amidst the demands of new motherhood.
Do I need any prior experience with meditation or Sound Bath to participate?
Absolutely not! Our Sound Bath sessions are designed to be accessible for everyone, especially new mothers who might be seeking a gentle way to connect with their spiritual side. You don't need any prior experience; simply come as you are, and allow the sounds to guide you into a state of deep relaxation and inner calm.
Can I bring my baby to the Sound Bath session?
We understand the challenges of finding childcare during postpartum. While we encourage you to prioritize this time for your individual spiritual care, please contact us directly at or to discuss options for bringing your baby. Our priority is to create a peaceful and undisturbed environment for all participants to fully benefit from the Sound Bath.
What should I expect to feel or experience during a postpartum Sound Bath?
During your Sound Bath, you can expect to lie down comfortably and simply listen. Many new mothers report feeling a profound sense of relaxation, reduced stress, and an increased feeling of inner peace. You might experience gentle shifts in your emotional state, clarity of mind, or a deeper connection to your own spirit. Each experience is unique and exactly what you need at that moment.
