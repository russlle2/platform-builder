Core elements

Three practices that make up our approach — clarity, surface, and container.

Clarity

Simple, repeatable tones that let the mind re-orient. We prefer fewer, deliberate sounds rather than layering for spectacle.

Surface

Soft vibrational textures — metal and skin instruments tuned to a narrow palette to minimize internal conflict.

Container

A clear start and close, with cues for returning attention. Sessions are framed as practices, not products.
