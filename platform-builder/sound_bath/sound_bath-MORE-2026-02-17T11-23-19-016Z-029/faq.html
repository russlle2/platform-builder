Questions & practical notes

Clear answers for people curious about our format, safety, and how to arrive prepared.

What should I expect during a session?

Sessions are 30–60 minutes. You will lie or sit comfortably. We move through a small set of tuned tones and guided breathing cues. There is a clear opening and close so attention can return reliably.

How many people attend?

Group events are capped at 20 people to keep the field intimate. Private bookings are arranged depending on the space and request.

Do I need prior experience?

No. Our sessions are designed for people new to contemplative sound and those who have tried dozens of practices. We meet you where you are.

Contraindications and safety

Responsible practice: sound immersion is generally safe for most people, but if you have a history of epilepsy, severe psychiatric conditions (e.g., psychosis), serious cardiovascular conditions, recent head injury, or active substance withdrawal, please consult a medical provider before attending and contact us to discuss modifications. If you have implanted medical devices (e.g., pacemakers) or are pregnant, mention this when booking so we can adjust any music or positioning. Our facilitators will provide lower-volume and seated options when needed.

I have tinnitus / vestibular sensitivity. Can I attend?

Many clients with tinnitus or sensitivity attend with adjustments. We avoid sudden volume bursts and can offer a room setup that minimizes direct contact vibrations. Contact us ahead of time and we will plan a suitable approach.

Myth

Sound baths are always loud, immersive spectacles.

Truth

We prioritize restraint: fewer tones, clear structure, and volume calibrated to breath. The goal is a steady inner field, not overwhelm.
