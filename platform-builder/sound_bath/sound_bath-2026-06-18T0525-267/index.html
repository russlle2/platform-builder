Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Sound Bath specifically help me as an entrepreneur or high-achiever?
Our Sound Bath sessions are designed to address the unique stressors of your demanding lifestyle. The resonant frequencies help to quiet the overactive mind, reduce anxiety from constant decision-making, and create a space for emotional regulation, allowing you to return to your work with renewed focus and resilience, rather than burnout.
I'm always on the go; will I be able to relax during a Sound Bath?
Many high-achievers find it challenging to 'switch off.' The beauty of a Sound Bath is that you don't need to 'try' to relax. The powerful, immersive sounds naturally guide your brainwaves into deeper, more restful states. Your only task is to lie comfortably and allow the vibrations to work their gentle magic, facilitating a deep sense of calm you might not have accessed in years.
What does 'trauma-informed' mean in the context of a Sound Bath?
Being trauma-informed means we prioritize your emotional safety and autonomy. We create a predictable, respectful, and transparent environment, explaining the process clearly. We understand that intense sensations or certain sounds can be impactful, and we ensure you always feel in control, able to adjust your experience as needed, fostering a space for genuine, gentle emotional processing.
What should I expect during my first Sound Bath session?
You'll lie comfortably on a mat, often with blankets and bolsters, in a serene, natural-inspired setting. I'll play various instruments, creating an immersive soundscape. There's nothing for you to 'do' except listen and allow the sounds to wash over you. Most people experience deep relaxation, mental clarity, and a profound sense of inner peace by the end of the session, ready to tackle their next challenge with renewed vigor.
