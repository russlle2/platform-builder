Frequently Asked Questions

How does a Sound Bath specifically help with chronic illness or injury recovery?
Our Sound Bath sessions are tailored to help regulate your nervous system, often overstimulated during chronic illness or injury. The gentle vibrations encourage your body to shift into a 'rest and digest' state, which can reduce inflammation, ease pain perception, and improve sleep quality—all crucial aspects of recovery.
Will the sounds be too intense for my sensitive system during my recovery?
We prioritize a gentle and supportive environment. carefully curates the soundscape to be soothing, never jarring. We encourage you to communicate any sensitivities, and adjustments can always be made to ensure your comfort and provide a deeply relaxing experience without overstimulation.
Do I need to have prior experience with meditation or sound therapy to benefit from a Sound Bath?
Absolutely not. No prior experience is required. The beauty of a Sound Bath is its passive nature. You simply lie down and allow the sounds to wash over you. It's an accessible practice for anyone, especially beneficial when you're managing health challenges and need a low-effort way to find calm.
What should I bring or wear to a Sound Bath session for optimal comfort during my recovery?
We recommend wearing comfortable, loose-fitting clothing. You'll be lying down for the duration of the session, so anything that allows you to relax fully is ideal. You may also wish to bring a water bottle, and if you have a favorite blanket or eye mask for extra comfort, please feel free to bring those too.
