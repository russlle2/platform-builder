Below are the common queries that come as people choose to experience a sound bath. If yours isn't listed, reach out — we reply personally.

What should I bring?

Comfort items: a mat or blanket, pillow, cozy socks, and a light shawl. We have some mats; request one when you book. Hydration is encouraged before and after sessions.

How long is a typical session?

Group events run roughly 65–75 minutes including settling and integration. Private sessions vary by package — see booking options.

Is it safe?

Generally yes. Contraindications include recent head trauma, pregnancy in early stages without clearance, photosensitive epilepsy, and implanted medical electronics. If unsure, consult your healthcare provider; contact us first for guidance.

Do I need prior experience?

No. The work is accessible: lie back, breathe, and be held by sound. Facilitated cues guide you through the session.

What instruments are used?

We curate a textured sound environment. Typical instruments include crystal bowls, a soft gong, monochord, and bowed instruments. Each event has a unique palette.

Will I fall asleep?

Many do sleep; some have profound restful awareness without sleep. Both responses are welcomed and integrated.

Before you attend

Avoid heavy meals right before the session. If you use hearing aids or have concerns about vibration, contact us so we can adapt the experience.
