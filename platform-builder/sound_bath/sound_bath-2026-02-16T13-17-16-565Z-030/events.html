What to expect — a soft arc

Each gathering is curated for sensory depth. We begin with a gentle arrival, a short intention-setting, a 45–60 minute sound immersion, and a quiet closing to ease back into the room.

The flow

  1. Welcome & grounding (10 minutes) — breath and orientation.
  2. Sound immersion (45–60 minutes) — layered timbres build & release.
  3. Rest & integration (10 minutes) — silence to land softly.

Instruments for this circle

Crystal bowls, gong, Himalayan chimes, hand-tuned forks, rainstick — arranged to create harmonic washes and subtle overtones.

What to bring

  • Yoga mat or blanket, pillow, water, optional eye mask.
  • Wear warm, comfortable layers — the body cools during rest.

Contraindications: Not suitable for uncontrolled seizures, certain cardiovascular implants, or when directed otherwise by a medical professional. Please disclose pregnancy, pacemakers, or any device/sensitivity when booking.
