A brief note on flow

Sessions begin with a soft centering — guided breath, gentle voice, and attunement. Instruments arrive in layers: a monochord or crystal bowls to open space, chimes for shimmer, and a gong or lower tone to settle. We close slowly, returning to silence. Expect a guided entry and a quiet reorientation at the end.
