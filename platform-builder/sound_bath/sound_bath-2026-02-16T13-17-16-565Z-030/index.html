How the ritual unfolds

A slow, held structure: arrival, intention, sound immersion, gentle return.

Arrival & Grounding

Soft lighting, warm tea, an invitation to rest. We begin with breath and a short guided centering to arrive in the body.

Intention

A brief reflection to set a personal focus — this is optional but helps anchor the session.

Sound Immersion

Layered soundscapes led by crystal bowls, a deep gong, and hand-hummed drones create a bath of resonant frequencies. Expect evolving textures and dynamic stillness.

Return & Integration

We close slowly with silence, a grounding prompt, and time for tea. You leave with a short guide for integrating the experience.

What to bring & practical notes

  • Yoga mat or padded mat, blanket, pillow for neck support
  • Water bottle; dress in comfortable layers
  • Eye mask or scarf recommended for deeper rest

Frequently asked

Will I fall asleep?

Many people do drift into sleep; that is fine. The practice invites both rest and felt presence. You will be softly reawakened for the closing.

How loud is the gong?

Volume is guided to support comfort — there are no sudden detonations. We tailor intensity and provide quieter alternatives if needed.

Can I arrive late?

Doors close once the session begins to preserve the field. Please arrive early to settle.
