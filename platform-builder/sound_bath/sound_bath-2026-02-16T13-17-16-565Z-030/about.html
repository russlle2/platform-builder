Our approach

We hold sound as a subtle architecture. In each session the field is shaped with intention — vibration becomes a map, and the body is invited to read it. Sessions are crafted for spaciousness, with attention to breath, rest and safety. Facilitated by {{FACILITATOR_NAME}}, our gatherings blend ritual and gentle technique to support relaxation, nervous-system regulation and creative clarity.

The flow of a typical gathering

  1. Arrival, soft tea & grounding (10–15 min)
  2. Short orientation & intention setting (5–10 min)
  3. Guided breath & progressive rest (8–10 min)
  4. Main sound immersion: layered bowls & resonant textures (30–40 min)
  5. Gentle return and integration (10–12 min)

Every event includes time for quiet after the sound so you can settle before moving on.

Instruments we favor

An evolving palette — each set is chosen for timbral balance and depth.

  • Crystal singing bowls
  • Gong (soft, bowed textures)
  • Tuning forks
  • Monochord
  • Wind chimes

Practical — what to bring

Comfort matters. Bring: a yoga mat or blanket, a pillow, socks, and a light cover. We provide mats and bolsters on request.
