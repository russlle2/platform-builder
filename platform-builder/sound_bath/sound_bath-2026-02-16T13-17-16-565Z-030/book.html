Flow — what to expect

  1. Arrival & gentle orientation (10 min)
  2. Guided breathing & rest (8–12 min)
  3. Main sonic immersion: layered instruments (25–45 min)
  4. Return, time to integrate and hydrate (10 min)

Bring layers and anything that supports comfort; avoid heavy fragrances.

Contraindications & safety

If you have a pacemaker, are pregnant, have a recent concussion, seizures, or are sensitive to sustained vibration, contact us before booking. We prioritize safety and can adapt where possible.

What to bring

  • Yoga mat or blanket
  • Pillow or bolster
  • Warm socks and a shawl/cover
  • Water bottle
