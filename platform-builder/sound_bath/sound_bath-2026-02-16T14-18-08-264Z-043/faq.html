What should I bring?
Comfortable layers, a travel yoga mat or thick blanket, a pillow for the head, and an open curiosity. Optional: eye mask and a small notebook for reflections.
Is this safe with medical devices?
If you use a pacemaker or other implanted device, or are pregnant, please consult your physician first and email us at {{EMAIL}} before booking. Certain devices can interact with low-frequency or sustained vibrational fields.
Do I need prior meditation experience?
No. Sessions are guided and intentionally gentle; everyone is invited regardless of experience.
What is the session flow?
A typical evening: arrival + tea (15m), settling and intention (10m), immersion (45–55m) with layers of sound using singing bowls, soft gongs, and handheld chimes, then gentle reintegration (10–15m).
Will I fall asleep?
Many guests drift into deep rest—this is part of the work. You will be guided back at the close so you leave grounded.

Contraindications

Please refrain from booking without medical advice if you have:

  • Unstable cardiac conditions or pacemakers
  • Recent surgeries or acute injuries
  • Severe photosensitive epilepsy

Hearing considerations

While we favor gentle dynamics, some tones can be intense for those with auditory sensitivities. Earplugs are welcome.
