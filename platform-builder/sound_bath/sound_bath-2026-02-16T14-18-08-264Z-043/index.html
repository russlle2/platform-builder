What to bring & notes

Comfort

  • Yoga mat or blanket for reclining
  • Small pillow for head/neck support
  • Layered clothing — rooms are dim and temperature-regulated

Practical

  • Water bottle
  • Arrive 15 minutes early for settling
  • Phones on silent; take notes after the closing bell

Contraindications: Not recommended if you are pregnant without clearance, have uncontrolled epilepsy, or are sensitive to loud percussion. If you have medical implants or recent surgeries consult your physician first.

Questions people often ask

Will I fall asleep?

Many rest deeply—and sleep is a natural response. You will still benefit even if you drift in and out of consciousness.

Is this therapy?

Our events are experiential and supportive of wellbeing, not a substitute for clinical therapy. For medical concerns, consult a professional.

Can I request quieter volume?

Yes — let the facilitator know at arrival. We calibrate field intensity to the room and attendees.
