How these sessions unfold

Each session begins with a brief intake to set an intention and note any health considerations. The facilitator crafts a sound field attuned to the person(s): focused tuning, sustained bowls, and subtle overtones to encourage nervous-system regulation. After sound, a short integration conversation and resourcing practices are offered.

What to bring & safety

  • Comfortable layers, mat/blanket, eye cover.
  • If mobility-limited, notify us; chairs are available.
  • Contraindications: pacemakers, implants sensitive to vibration, recent surgery, or uncontrolled seizure disorder — consult before booking.

Instruments & palette

Sessions are bespoke. Typical selections include tuning forks for precise frequency, koshi chimes for high overtones, and gentle bowed instruments for sustained warmth.
