The ritual — a short note

We hold space with intention: breath-led settling, a 40–60 minute sonic immersion, and a gentle reintegration. Instruments are mixed to weave texture — here we favor chimes, tuning forks, and a warm monochord for harmonic grounding. The room is dim; pillows and blankets invited.

What to bring • Contraindications • Flow

What to bring
  • Yoga mat or blanket
  • Pillow and light throw
  • Water bottle
  • Eye mask or scarf (optional)
Contraindications

If you are pregnant, have epilepsy, uncontrolled blood pressure, or are sensitive to loud transients, consult your physician and inform the facilitator. Tinnitus or metallic implants are welcome to be discussed ahead of time.

Session flow

We begin with 8–10 minutes of breath and centering, then enter a 40–60 minute sound journey using selected instruments (here: chimes, tuning forks, a gentle mono-string resonance). We close with a soft gong nod and five minutes of shared breath to ground.
