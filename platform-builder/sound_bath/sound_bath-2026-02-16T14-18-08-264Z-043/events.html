Ritual outline

The gathering moves as a tide: welcome + intention setting, guided breath + body scan, layered soundscapes, deep rest, soft return. Instruments are played with attentive dynamics to meet the room.

  • Welcome & intention (10–12 min)
  • Guided breath and tuning (8–10 min)
  • Main sound field (30–40 min)
  • Slow return & tea (10–15 min)

What to bring

Bring a yoga mat, blanket, pillow/bolster, eye covering if you prefer. Dress warm layers; the body cools in deep rest.

Contraindications: If you have a pacemaker, recent surgery, are in late-stage pregnancy, or have seizure disorder, please consult your physician and tell the facilitator before the session.
