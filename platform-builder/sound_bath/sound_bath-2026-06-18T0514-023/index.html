Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly happens during a Sound Bath session?
During a Sound Bath, you’ll recline comfortably while plays a variety of instruments like singing bowls and gongs. The gentle sounds and vibrations create an immersive atmosphere, helping your mind and body to relax deeply, reducing stress, and calming your nervous system without any effort on your part.
Is a Sound Bath suitable for someone with limited mobility or health concerns?
Absolutely. Our Sound Bath practice is designed to be very gentle and accessible. You can participate in whatever position is most comfortable for you – lying down, seated, or even in a wheelchair. The experience is entirely passive, meaning you don't need to do anything but relax and receive the sounds. We prioritize your comfort and well-being.
Do I need any prior experience with meditation or similar practices?
Not at all. There’s no need for any previous experience with meditation or other spiritual practices. The sounds themselves do the work of guiding you into a relaxed state. Your only task is to be present and allow the vibrations to wash over you. It's a wonderful way to experience deep relaxation, even if you're new to these types of practices.
What are the specific benefits for seniors regarding stress and nervous system regulation?
For seniors, our Sound Bath sessions specifically help in reducing the everyday stresses that can impact well-being. The gentle sound frequencies can promote better sleep, ease muscle tension, and help to lower blood pressure. By calming your nervous system, you may experience increased mental clarity, improved mood, and a greater sense of overall peace and resilience.
