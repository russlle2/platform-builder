Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is a Sound Bath and how does it help parents?
A Sound Bath is a deeply immersive, full-body listening experience where you are 'bathed' in sound waves produced by various instruments. For parents, it's a structured approach to stress reduction. The specific frequencies used help to downshift your nervous system from a state of fight-or-flight to rest-and-digest, which is vital for emotional regulation and preventing burnout when managing household demands.
Is a Sound Bath suitable for someone experiencing high levels of stress or past trauma?
Yes, our Sound Baths are trauma-informed. This means we create a safe, predictable environment and use sound in a way that respects your nervous system's capacity. We focus on gentle, consistent frequencies to avoid overwhelming your system, providing a supportive space for you to process stress and find emotional grounding without re-traumatization. We encourage you to communicate any concerns so we can best support you.
What should I expect to feel or experience during a Sound Bath session?
During a Sound Bath, you might experience a sense of deep relaxation, a slowing of your thoughts, or even a feeling of lightness. Some people report a reduction in physical tension or mental chatter. It's common for your body to enter a state where it can begin to process accumulated stress, leading to a profound sense of calm and clarity after the session. Your individual experience is unique and always respected.
How often should I attend a Sound Bath to see results for managing parenting stress?
Consistency is key when aiming for sustained emotional regulation, especially for parents facing ongoing stressors. Many parents find that regular engagement, such as attending a Sound Bath weekly or bi-weekly, helps to maintain a more regulated nervous system and build emotional resilience. We recommend listening to your own needs and observing how your body and mind respond.
