Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Sound Bath help with my chronic pain or injury recovery?
Sound Bath sessions encourage a deep relaxation response, which can help reduce stress, alleviate muscle tension, and shift your perception of pain. The gentle vibrations can also support your body's natural restorative processes, complementing your physical therapy or medical treatments.
What should I expect during a Sound Bath session for physical discomfort?
You'll lie comfortably on a mat, fully clothed, as I play various instruments around you. The experience is deeply relaxing; many people report feeling a sense of calm, reduced physical tension, and a mental spaciousness that can be very beneficial when managing ongoing physical challenges.
Is Sound Bath safe if I'm recovering from a specific injury or surgery?
Yes, Sound Bath is generally very gentle and non-invasive. However, it's always best to consult with your doctor or physical therapist first to ensure it's appropriate for your specific condition. Please also share any concerns or limitations with me before your session so I can tailor the experience to your needs.
Will Sound Bath sessions interfere with my current medical treatments?
Sound Bath is a complementary practice, designed to support your existing medical care, not replace it. It can enhance the effectiveness of your treatments by promoting relaxation and reducing stress, which are crucial for recovery. There are no known contraindications with most medical treatments, but open communication with your healthcare provider is always encouraged.
