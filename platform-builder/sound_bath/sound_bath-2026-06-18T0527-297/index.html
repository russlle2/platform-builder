Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Sound Bath specifically help an entrepreneur manage high-pressure stress?
For entrepreneurs, your mind is constantly active. A Sound Bath acts as a forced pause, shifting your brain from beta (alert, problem-solving) to alpha or theta waves (relaxed, meditative). This allows your nervous system to downregulate, reducing cortisol levels and creating mental space to process emotional residue from daily pressures, rather than just pushing through it.
I'm always busy. How long is a Sound Bath session and what should I expect?
Our typical Sound Bath sessions are designed to be efficient yet deeply effective, usually lasting between 60 to 75 minutes. You'll lie comfortably, fully clothed, as I guide you through a soundscape. Your only task is to relax and allow the sounds to wash over you. Many high-achievers report feeling a significant mental reset and improved clarity afterward, making the time investment worthwhile.
Will a Sound Bath really address the deeper emotional burdens I carry, or is it just temporary relaxation?
While you'll certainly experience relaxation, our trauma-informed Sound Bath approach aims deeper. The vibrational frequencies can help to gently dislodge emotional blocks and unprocessed experiences stored in the body. It’s not a quick fix, but a supportive practice that, over time, helps you develop greater emotional resilience and a more integrated sense of self, beyond just coping with stress.
I'm skeptical about 'alternative' practices. What is the scientific basis for Sound Bath's effects?
That's a fair question for a results-oriented individual. Research suggests that specific sound frequencies can influence brainwave states, heart rate variability, and even immune response. Our Sound Bath utilizes these principles to induce a state of parasympathetic nervous system dominance, reducing stress hormones and promoting cellular repair. It's a physiological response, not just a spiritual one, aiding your mental and emotional recovery.
