Session flow

Arrival & settling → short breathwork and intention → immersive soundset (30–55 minutes) → gentle re-entry and time for questions. Private sessions include a brief intake before sound work.

Before you come

What to bring: mat or thick blanket, pillow, water. Wear loose layers. We provide bolsters and eye masks when available.

Safety & contraindications

If you have an implanted medical device, epilepsy, or recent cranial injury, consult your clinician and let us know. Not all sound forms are recommended for pregnancy without approval.

Instruments likely at your session

Expect warm-frame gong, tuning forks, shruti box, and a selection of hand bowls — textures designed to anchor and to ripple movement in the body.
