Practical details

What should I bring?

Please bring a yoga mat, a comfy blanket, a pillow for your head, and an eye mask if you like deeper darkness. We have a limited supply of mats if needed.

Flow of a typical session

We open with a brief intention-setting and breath lead, move into an approximate 40–50 minute sound journey (crystal bowls, gong, chimes, tuning forks, minimal voice), and close with gentle reorientation and optional journaling or tea.

Contraindications & safety

Sound baths are generally safe but not recommended during pregnancy without medical guidance, for individuals with certain seizure disorders, or if you have implants that are sound-sensitive. Please consult us at {{EMAIL}} if you have concerns.
