Myths gently unwrapped — what a sound bath actually is

There are myths swirling around sound baths. Below we briefly separate story from reality so you arrive prepared and curious.

Myth: It’s just background noise

Truth: Sound bathing is intentional sonic architecture. Instruments are tuned and sequenced to invite shifts in attention, breath, and bodily resonance.

Myth: You’ll be hypnotized

Truth: Sessions are deeply relaxing and meditative, but you remain aware. Comfort and consent guide every moment — there’s no loss of agency.

Myth: Everyone experiences it the same

Truth: Responses vary — some feel warmth, others see images, some simply breathe easier. All responses are welcome.

Our practice pillars

Membership shapes what we offer: a calm monthly rhythm, priority booking for private work, and community exchanges. These pillars guide every session.

1 — Intentional Curation

Each set is composed with a beginning, expansion, and gentle close. Instruments used in rotation include crystal bowls, chimes, and a tuned monochord to create layered harmonics.

2 — Sensory Hospitality

Soft lighting, vetted scent-free space, and comfortable floor seating. We provide blankets and bolsters; membership includes a small welcome kit.

3 — Safety & Access

We screen for contraindications, offer quiet corners, and can adapt sessions (shorter, private, or 1:1) for medical or sensory needs.

Common questions

Practical answers so you know what to bring and how to prepare.
What should I bring? Please bring: a yoga mat or thick blanket, a light blanket for warmth, a small eye pillow or scarf, and any personal comfort items. We provide bolsters and extra blankets on request. If you prefer silence after the session, bring earplugs.
Are there contraindications? If you are pregnant, have epilepsy, recent head trauma, severe cardiovascular conditions, or are under acute psychiatric care, please contact us before booking. Sound can be powerful; we will adapt or recommend alternatives when needed.
What is the session flow? We begin with a short orientation (5–10 minutes), move into a 40–60 minute sound set that progresses from grounding low frequencies to brighter harmonic textures, and finish with a 10–15 minute gentle return and optional sharing circle. Facilitator adjustments are made live to support comfort.
How does membership work? Members receive priority booking, discounted private sessions, and a monthly sound-bath credit. Memberships are intentional — we limit seats to keep sessions intimate and premium.
