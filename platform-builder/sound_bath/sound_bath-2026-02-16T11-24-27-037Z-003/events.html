Myths & Truths about sound baths

Myth: You must be spiritual to benefit.

Truth: Sound affects the nervous system directly — benefits are physiological as well as emotional.

Myth: It's loud and invasive.

Truth: Sessions are designed to cradle the listener; volume and texture are tuned to comfort.

Important — Contraindications

If you are pregnant, have epilepsy, a pacemaker, recent surgery, or severe psychiatric conditions, please consult your clinician before attending. Sound work is gentle but can be intense for some nervous systems.

What to bring

  • Yoga mat or sleep pad (we provide extras)
  • Eye mask or scarf to block light
  • Water bottle and an open heart
