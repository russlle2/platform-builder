Questions people ask

Practical answers for attending and for deciding whether this sound work is right for you. If we missed something, reach out: {{EMAIL}}.

What should I bring?

Bring a mat or thick blanket, a pillow, and a light sweater. A water bottle is recommended. If you prefer a chair for comfort, tell us when you arrive.

How long is the experience and what is the flow?

Typically 60–75 minutes. We start with a gentle orientation and breathing invitation, move into layered soundscapes (20–50 minutes), then return with grounding cues and optional sharing.

Are sound baths the same as meditation?

They overlap. Sound baths are a sonic container for mindful attention, often less didactic than a formal meditation class — more listening, less instruction.

Contraindications — when to check with a clinician

If you have a pacemaker, recent head injury, uncontrolled epilepsy, or are in the first trimester of pregnancy, please consult your healthcare provider and inform us before participating.

What instruments do you use?

In our FAQ room you'll commonly hear a monochord, tuning forks, rainstick, and a warm gong. Each event is different by design.

Is it loud?

Dynamics are intentional. Some low frequencies can be felt as vibrations rather than loudness; we provide ear cushions and chair options if sensitivity is a concern.

Before you arrive

Arrive 10–15 minutes early for check-in, to set your mat, and to receive orientation. We keep the doors dim for a smooth descent into listening.

Membership perks

Members get discounted bookings, early-bird notice for limited-capacity events, and an annual guest pass for a friend.
