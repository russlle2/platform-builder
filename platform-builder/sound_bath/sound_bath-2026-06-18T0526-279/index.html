Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly happens in a Sound Bath and how does it help with parenting stress?
During a Sound Bath, you'll recline comfortably while I play various instruments like singing bowls and gongs. The sounds create vibrations that encourage your brain to shift into a more relaxed state, similar to meditation. For parents, this means a much-needed pause from cognitive overload, allowing your nervous system to regulate and emotional tension to dissipate, ultimately reducing the feeling of being constantly overwhelmed.
Is a Sound Bath suitable if I'm feeling completely depleted and overwhelmed as a parent?
Absolutely. Our Sound Bath practice is specifically designed for parents experiencing depletion and overwhelm. There's nothing for you to 'do' except to be present. The sounds work to quiet your mind and body, providing a space for deep rest and emotional recalibration, which is exactly what's needed when you're running on empty. You don't need any prior experience or mental energy to benefit.
How is your Sound Bath approach trauma-informed for parents dealing with stress?
Our approach prioritizes your sense of safety and agency. We create an environment where you feel in control, with clear communication and options for comfort. The sounds are introduced gently, allowing your nervous system to adjust without being startled. This careful, empathetic framework ensures that the Sound Bath experience is restorative and supportive, especially for those carrying emotional burdens or past challenging experiences.
I struggle to 'turn off' my thoughts as a busy parent. Can a Sound Bath really help me find calm?
Yes, many parents find their minds constantly racing. The beauty of a Sound Bath is that the resonant frequencies provide a focal point, naturally drawing your attention away from intrusive thoughts without requiring effort from you. It's a gentle, passive way to achieve mental quietude, helping you access a state of calm that feels out of reach during your busy days. You might be surprised at how easily your mind can settle.
