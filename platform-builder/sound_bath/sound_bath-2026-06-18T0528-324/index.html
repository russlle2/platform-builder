Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Sound Bath specifically help with my athletic recovery?
Our Sound Bath sessions utilize specific frequencies known to promote deep relaxation and tissue regeneration. This helps to reduce muscle soreness, decrease inflammation, and calm your nervous system, allowing your body to enter a prime state for recovery after intense physical activity.
I often feel stiff and have persistent aches; how does Sound Bath address physical pain?
The resonant vibrations from the instruments in a Sound Bath can penetrate deep into your body, gently encouraging muscle release and improving circulation. This facilitates the removal of metabolic waste and brings fresh oxygen to tense areas, directly contributing to the easing of chronic aches and stiffness.
What is somatic healing, and how does a Sound Bath facilitate it for active individuals?
Somatic healing focuses on releasing stored tension and stress from your body's cellular memory. For active individuals, this can manifest as chronic tightness or limited mobility. A Sound Bath creates a safe space for your nervous system to relax, allowing these deeply held patterns to surface and dissipate, promoting a more fluid and pain-free physical state.
Will I need to do anything during the Sound Bath, or can I just relax?
Your only role during a Sound Bath is to relax. You'll lie comfortably, and the soundscape will do the work. The vibrations will envelop you, creating an environment where your body and mind can naturally release tension and find a state of restorative calm without any effort from your side.
