How a Sound Bath Supports Your Sleep Cycle During Recovery

When your body is working hard to heal, quality sleep is not just a luxury, it's essential. Our Sound Bath sessions create an environment where your nervous system can unwind, moving you away from states of heightened stress. The gentle vibrations encourage your brainwaves to slow, guiding you towards the deeper, more regenerative sleep stages often elusive during recovery from chronic conditions. This isn't just about feeling relaxed; it's about actively supporting your body's innate ability to mend and restore itself overnight.
