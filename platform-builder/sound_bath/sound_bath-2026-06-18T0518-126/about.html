How can a Sound Bath specifically help with sleep challenges related to chronic illness or injury?

Chronic illness and injury often keep your nervous system in a state of alert, making deep sleep difficult. Our Sound Bath uses specific frequencies and vibrations to gently guide your brain into calmer states (alpha and theta brainwaves), which are conducive to falling asleep and staying asleep. This helps quiet the physical discomfort and mental anxiety often associated with recovery, allowing for more profound, restorative rest.

What should I expect during my first Sound Bath session for sleep improvement?

You'll lie down comfortably, typically on a mat with blankets and pillows, in a quiet, dimly lit space. Dr. Morgan Ellis will begin playing a variety of instruments, creating a continuous wave of sound. Your only task is to relax and allow the sounds to wash over you. There's nothing you need to 'do.' Many people describe feeling a deep sense of relaxation, sometimes drifting into a dream-like state, which directly supports your body's natural inclination towards rest.

Are there any physical sensations I might experience during a Sound Bath?

As the sounds resonate, you might feel gentle vibrations throughout your body. Some people experience a tingling sensation, a sense of warmth, or a deep release of tension in their muscles. These are all normal responses as your body relaxes and releases stored stress. We encourage you to simply observe these sensations without judgment, allowing them to facilitate your deeper state of rest.

How often should I attend a Sound Bath to see improvements in my sleep during recovery?

The frequency can vary for each individual. Many clients find that attending weekly or bi-weekly Sound Bath sessions provides consistent support for their sleep and overall well-being during recovery. Like any supportive practice, regularity tends to yield the most significant benefits, helping you establish a new rhythm for restful sleep. We can discuss a personalized recommendation during your initial consultation.
