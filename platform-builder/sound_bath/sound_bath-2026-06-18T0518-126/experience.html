Sound Bath

Ancient Rhythms for Modern Restoration: The Sound Bath Experience

Our Sound Bath isn't merely background music; it's an intentional practice drawing from ancient traditions to promote deep peace. You'll recline comfortably as Dr. Morgan Ellis guides you through a soundscape created with instruments like Himalayan singing bowls, gongs, and chimes. These carefully chosen sounds resonate deeply within your body, helping to release tension and quiet mental chatter. The focus is always on creating a safe, serene space where your body feels secure enough to let go and fully embrace the rest it needs for healing.
