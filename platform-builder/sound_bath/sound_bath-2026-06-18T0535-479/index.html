Frequently Asked Questions

How does a Sound Bath specifically help parents manage household stress?
Our Sound Bath sessions are tailored to address the unique mental load of parenting. The focused sound frequencies help quiet an overactive mind, allowing you to mentally declutter and process daily stressors more effectively. This leads to improved mental clarity and a reduced feeling of being overwhelmed by household demands.
I'm always short on time. How long is a Sound Bath session and how quickly will I feel a difference?
We understand your time is precious. Our typical Sound Bath sessions range from 60 to 75 minutes. Many clients report feeling a noticeable sense of calm and mental refreshment after just one session, with continued benefits accumulating with regular practice. It's a focused investment in your cognitive wellness.
Will a Sound Bath help me improve my focus and decision-making when I'm constantly interrupted by my children?
Absolutely. By providing a dedicated period of deep mental rest, Sound Bath helps to restore your cognitive resources. This enhanced mental capacity can translate into better focus, sharper decision-making, and greater resilience when faced with constant interruptions, allowing you to respond more thoughtfully rather than reactively.
Is a Sound Bath suitable if I've never tried anything like this before?
Yes, completely. No prior experience is needed. You simply lie down, get comfortable, and allow the sounds to wash over you. Our practitioner will guide you through the process, ensuring you feel safe and supported, making it an accessible and empowering experience for any parent seeking mental respite.
