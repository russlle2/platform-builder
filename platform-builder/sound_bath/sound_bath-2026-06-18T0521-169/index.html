Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Sound Bath specifically help parents improve their sleep?
Our Sound Bath sessions are designed to calm the overactive mind that often keeps parents awake. The resonant frequencies help shift your brain into a relaxed state, making it easier to fall asleep and experience deeper, more continuous rest, even if your nights are often interrupted.
What should I expect during my first Sound Bath session?
You'll simply lie comfortably on a mat, often with a blanket and eye mask. will guide you into relaxation as various instruments are played around you. There's no right or wrong way to experience it; just allow yourself to be immersed in the sounds and vibrations. Many parents report feeling profoundly relaxed and even drifting off to sleep during the session.
Is a Sound Bath safe for parents who are breastfeeding or newly postpartum?
Yes, Sound Baths are generally safe and can be incredibly beneficial for new and breastfeeding parents, offering a much-needed opportunity for deep rest and stress reduction. We always recommend consulting with your healthcare provider if you have any specific concerns, but the gentle nature of the sounds is typically very soothing.
How often should I attend a Sound Bath to see benefits for my sleep and stress levels?
The frequency that works best varies for everyone. Many parents find that attending a Sound Bath weekly or bi-weekly helps maintain a consistent state of calm and supports improved sleep patterns. Even occasional sessions can provide a significant reset when you're feeling particularly overwhelmed.
