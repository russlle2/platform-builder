Why join a cohort?

  • Shared container

    Small groups create a predictable pace — people settle together more quickly than in an open format.

  • Layered sound design

    Instruments, voice, and silence are composed to meet different nervous system states through the hour.

  • Take-home anchors

    Simple practices and a short reflection at the end help sustain the effect for days.

What to expect

  1. Arrival: Soft seating, brief welcome, intention invite.
  2. Settle: A short breath-led entry and gentle attunement.
  3. Sound journey: 35–45 minutes of layered sound with pauses for presence.
  4. Integration: Grounding and a chance to journal or share reflections.

Practical notes & safety

What should I bring?

Comfortable layers, a mat or blanket, and anything that helps you feel supported. We provide bolsters and blankets on request.

Contraindications

Sound work is generally low-risk but may not suit people with certain conditions: recent traumatic brain injury, uncontrolled epilepsy, certain cardiac devices, or other medical concerns. If you have doubts, consult your healthcare provider and contact us at {{EMAIL}} before attending.

Accessibility

Our space is single-level and we strive to accommodate sensory and mobility needs. Email {{EMAIL}} with any access requests.
