We lead small cohorts through tuned listening

A seasonal series for intentional rest, steady focus, and gentle shared descent. We blend bowls, subtle textures, and guided attention for a single-session arc.

Why join a cohort?

Cohorts lean into continuity: the same circle, a shared rhythm, and cumulative ease. Expect steady lead-ins, short ritual, and 40–55 minutes of tuned sound work.

Shared arc

You move with a small set of familiar faces; the shape supports deeper listening over weeks.

Guided entry

Short guided practices before sound help skillful attention and settling.

Intention-forward

We hold a brief intention-setting ritual so each session lands with clarity.

How a typical cohort evening flows

Gather & orient

Welcome, grounding instruction, and a one-minute centering exercise.

Short practice

A guided breath or prompt to shape listening and settle bodies.

Sound arc

A curated sequence across tones and textures for 35–45 minutes.

Re-entry

Slow return, optional sharing, and short journaling to land back in the room.

Practical notes

Sessions are held in a calm, carpeted room. Please bring a mat, blanket, and anything to make lying comfortably for the session easier. We keep the experience low-tech and scent-free.

Contraindications: Sound sessions are supportive for many people, but may not be suitable for those with uncontrolled epilepsy, recent head surgery or severe inner-ear conditions, pacemakers sensitive to vibrational devices, or if you are under recent surgical recovery. If you have a serious psychiatric diagnosis, are pregnant, or have concerns about intense auditory exposure, consult your healthcare provider before attending.
