Contraindications: Sound baths are deeply soothing but are not recommended without medical advice for people with epilepsy, recent head injury, implanted medical devices (eg pacemakers), or those in late pregnancy. If you have concerns, please consult a medical professional before attending.

How these gatherings flow

  1. Welcome & settle: We orient, set intentions, and invite the body to land in the room.
  2. Layered sound: Bowls and tuned bells move through textures — not loud, always attentive.
  3. Return & share: Quiet closure with a short check-in or journaling prompt.

Doors open 15 minutes early for arrival and light settling. We begin on time to preserve the group's container.

What to bring
