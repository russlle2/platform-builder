Private Pod — "Quiet Circle"

A small-group experience for friends, teams, or family. Led by a facilitator who shapes the arc and attends to individual needs within the pod container.

Features

  • 60–75 minutes with time for arrival and short check-out
  • Adaptive instruments tuned to the room
  • Pre-session intake to note sensitivities
Request a private pod
Contraindications: If you have epilepsy, recent head injury, implanted devices, or are late in pregnancy, please consult a medical professional before booking. We will ask about relevant medical notes on intake.

One-on-one — "Personal Attunement"

A bespoke session designed to meet you where you are. Emphasis on pacing, targeted support, and an intake conversation to shape the experience.
