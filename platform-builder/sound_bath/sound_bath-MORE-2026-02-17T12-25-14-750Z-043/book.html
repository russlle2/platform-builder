Booking terms — cohort model

Cohort pricing is framed as a series investment rather than per-night tickets. This helps us plan consistent capacity and keeps rates accessible. If you join mid-series, prorated options may be available.

Contraindications: Please do not attend without clinical advice if you have uncontrolled epilepsy, a medical device that might react to vibration, are recovering from recent head or ear surgery, or have other concerns that make prolonged auditory exposure unsafe.
