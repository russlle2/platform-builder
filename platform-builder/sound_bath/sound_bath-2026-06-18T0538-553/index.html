Frequently Asked Questions

What can I expect during my first Sound Bath session?
During your first Sound Bath, you can anticipate a comfortable and serene environment. You'll recline on a supportive mat, and will guide you through gentle relaxation. The session involves listening to a harmonious blend of sounds from various instruments, designed to calm your mind and body. There's nothing for you to do but relax and allow the sounds to wash over you.
Is a Sound Bath suitable for someone with limited mobility?
Absolutely. Our Sound Bath sessions are designed to be accessible and comfortable for everyone, including those with limited mobility. We ensure you have ample support and space to relax in whatever position is most comfortable for you, whether lying down or seated. Your comfort and ease are our highest priorities.
How can a Sound Bath support my spiritual growth?
A Sound Bath offers a unique space for quiet contemplation, which can deeply support your spiritual growth. The gentle sounds help quiet the external world, allowing you to connect more deeply with your inner self, reflect on your life's path, and cultivate a sense of inner wisdom and peace. It's a non-denominational practice focusing on personal introspection.
What should I wear for a Sound Bath session?
For your utmost comfort during a Sound Bath, we recommend wearing soft, loose-fitting clothing. Think of what you would wear for a relaxing evening at home. Layers are also a good idea, as body temperature can shift during deep relaxation. The goal is for you to feel completely at ease throughout your experience.
