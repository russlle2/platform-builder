What makes a Sound Bath session suitable for seniors aged 60+?

Our Sound Bath sessions are specifically designed with your comfort and emotional well-being in mind. We use gentle volumes, provide ample support for easeful reclining or sitting, and focus on creating a deeply calming atmosphere. The approach is non-intrusive and respects your personal space and emotional pace, offering a tender way to find peace.

How can Sound Bath help with emotional healing or past difficult experiences?

The gentle vibrations and calming sounds in a Sound Bath can create a safe internal space for emotional processing. It's not about re-experiencing trauma, but rather allowing your nervous system to relax deeply, which can naturally help soften the grip of difficult memories and create a sense of release and peace without needing to speak about them.

Do I need to do anything special or prepare for a Sound Bath session?

No special preparation is needed. We simply ask that you wear comfortable clothing. You'll be invited to lie down or sit in a relaxed position. Our goal is to make it as easy and comfortable for you as possible to simply receive the sounds and allow yourself to relax deeply.

Is a Sound Bath safe if I have health concerns or hearing aids?

Yes, Sound Bath is generally very safe. We maintain gentle sound levels, and the focus is on vibration rather than loud noise. If you wear hearing aids, you might find it more comfortable to remove them, or simply adjust them as needed. If you have specific health concerns, it's always wise to mention them when you book, so we can ensure your utmost comfort and safety.
