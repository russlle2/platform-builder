How Sound Bath Addresses Professional Burnout

Your professional life demands constant mental and emotional output, often leading to a dysregulated nervous system. Sound Bath provides a specific, passive method to gently guide your body out of 'fight or flight' and into a state conducive to rest and repair. We utilize precise frequencies to encourage parasympathetic activation, helping to reduce the chronic stress response that contributes to burnout.
