Questions that arrive often

How long is a typical session?

Group gatherings run 45–60 minutes. Private sessions usually run 60 minutes. Short series are three weekly sessions.

What should I bring?

Comfortable clothing, a small blanket or yoga mat, and an openness to breathe. We provide bolsters if needed.

Are group sessions suitable for beginners?

Yes. Practices are guided and accessible. If you have medical concerns please read the contraindications above.
