Questions & Notes

A concise guide to attending, preparing, and what to expect.

What should I bring?
Comfortable layers, a mat or cushion, and an open window if you prefer ventilation. We provide bolsters and some blankets, but bring what helps you settle.
How long is a session?
Most group gatherings are 60–75 minutes; short intensives are 30–45. Private sessions vary by agreement.
Are sessions safe with medical conditions?
We include a responsible contraindications note: if you are pregnant, have epilepsy, uncontrolled hypertension, or are under medications/substances that alter perception, consult your doctor and contact us before booking.
Can I record or bring a phone?
We ask phones to be off/silent and kept out of the shared field to protect the group's focus. Recording is by arrangement only.
Refunds and cancellations?
See our pricing page for cancellations. Typically a 48-hour notice is appreciated; exceptions considered case-by-case.
Accessibility
Spaces vary; contact us for step-free options and sensory considerations. We can adapt session length and volume on request.
