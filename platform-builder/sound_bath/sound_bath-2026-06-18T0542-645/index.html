Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Sound Bath specifically help with college anxiety and immune support?
Our Sound Bath sessions provide a deeply restorative experience that calms your nervous system, which is often overtaxed by academic pressure. This deep relaxation helps reduce cortisol levels, a stress hormone that can suppress your immune system. By creating a state of calm, your body can better focus on its natural immune functions and vitality building.
Do I need any prior experience with meditation or Sound Baths to attend?
Absolutely not! Our Sound Bath sessions are designed to be accessible for everyone, regardless of prior experience. All you need to do is show up, get comfortable, and allow the sounds to wash over you. We'll guide you through the process, making it easy to receive the benefits of deep relaxation and immune support.
What should I bring or wear to a Sound Bath session?
We recommend wearing comfortable, loose clothing that allows you to relax fully. You might also consider bringing a water bottle to stay hydrated. We provide mats, blankets, and pillows, but feel free to bring your own if you have a favorite. The goal is for you to be as cozy as possible to maximize your relaxation and vitality boost.
How often should I attend a Sound Bath for the best results in boosting my vitality and managing stress?
Consistency is key for building resilience and robust vitality. Many students find regular sessions, such as once a week or every other week, to be most beneficial for managing ongoing academic pressure and supporting their immune health. Even occasional sessions can offer significant relief when you're feeling overwhelmed or rundown.
