What exactly is a Sound Bath, and is it right for me?

A Sound Bath is a gentle experience where you relax while soothing sounds are played around you. It's especially wonderful for you if you're looking for a natural way to improve your sleep or simply want to feel more rested and calm without any strenuous activity. Many seniors find it incredibly beneficial.

Do I need to do anything specific during the Sound Bath?

Absolutely not! Your only job is to get comfortable, perhaps close your eyes, and just listen. There's no right or wrong way to experience the sounds. Just let them wash over you and allow your body to relax deeply, which often leads to better sleep later.

I'm concerned about getting comfortable for an hour. What if I can't lie still?

We prioritize your comfort. We have soft mats, blankets, and pillows to help you find a truly relaxed position. If lying down for the entire time isn't comfortable for you, sitting in a comfortable chair is perfectly fine too. The most important thing is that you feel at ease.

How can a Sound Bath help with my sleep specifically?

The gentle sounds and vibrations in a Sound Bath encourage a state of deep relaxation, similar to what your body experiences just before falling asleep. This helps quiet your mind, reduce everyday worries, and prepare your nervous system for a more peaceful and uninterrupted night's rest. Many find they fall asleep more easily and wake feeling more refreshed.
