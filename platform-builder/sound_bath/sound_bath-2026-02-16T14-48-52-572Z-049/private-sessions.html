What to expect in a private session

Sessions begin with a brief intake to understand intentions and sensitivities. Then a crafted sound journey follows: layered tones, microtonal adjustments, and space for integration. The session closes with a gentle reorientation and suggestions for aftercare.

What to bring: mat or yoga mat, blanket, pillow, and any items for comfort. Wear clothing you can lie still in.

Contraindications: Please disclose pregnancy, cardiac devices, or seizure history. If you are under psychiatric care or in crisis, inform us and consult your provider. Vibrational work is powerful; medical questions should be directed to your clinician.
