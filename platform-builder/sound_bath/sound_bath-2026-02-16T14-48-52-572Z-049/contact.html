What to bring

Contraindications & gentle notes

Sound bathing is deeply relaxing but may not be appropriate for everyone. Please consult with a physician if you are pregnant, have a pacemaker or implantable device, recent surgery, epilepsy, or are currently under psychiatric care. If you are taking medication that changes consciousness, please reach out before booking.
