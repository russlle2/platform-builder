Questions & clarity

What is a sound bath here?
A guided group immersion where tuned instruments create a moving sound field. Expect a restful posture, breath prompts, and a composed arc from arrival to integration. Instruments vary by series: gongs, tuning forks, and bowed monochord appear in rotation.
How long are sessions?
Most gatherings last 60–75 minutes. Private offerings vary (30–90 minutes) depending on format.
What should I bring?
Bring a mat or blanket, a pillow, a small wrap, and an eye covering if preferred. Hydrate beforehand. We provide bolsters on request but recommend personal items for comfort.
Contraindications — when to check in first?
If you have epilepsy, are undergoing intensive psychiatric care, have an implanted medical device sensitive to vibration, or are pregnant and advised to avoid intense vibrations, please consult your physician and contact us before attending. We can adapt sessions but safety first.
Can I fall asleep?
Yes. Gentle sleep may happen; it is a natural part of deep restorative work and perfectly welcome.
Are children allowed?
Most events are for adults. Private family sessions can be arranged—contact us to discuss age-appropriate adjustments.
Booking essentials
Space is limited to preserve an intimate environment. We recommend arriving 10 minutes early to settle.

More specifics — sound & safety

We keep decibels within a comfortable range and intentionally layer frequencies rather than blast volume. If you use hearing aids or sensitive implants, let us know so we can adapt placement and dynamics. Pregnant attendees: we offer reduced-intensity placements or a respite area if needed.
