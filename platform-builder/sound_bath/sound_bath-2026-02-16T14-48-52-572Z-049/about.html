Who we are — a quiet atelier of sound

{{BUSINESS_NAME}} practices intentional sonic ritual: layered harmonics, breath-led transitions, and held silence. We sit in an earthy warmth—carefully tuned, minimally adorned, richly felt.

Our approach

Each session is curated like a short rite. We begin with gentle arrival, move through resonant waves using crystal bowls and soft chimes, and close with an integrative pause where intention is invited back into the body.

What to expect
  • Light-eyed rest; you may lay or sit.
  • Guided openings to settle breath and posture.
  • Layered soundscapes: crystal bowls, rain-chimes, and a single-frame monochord.
  • Gentle reorientation and optional journaling after.
Experience & safety

Our facilitators maintain a low sensory palette and use sound intentionally. If you have epilepsy, serious heart conditions, or pregnancy concerns, please consult with your doctor and contact us before booking.

Ritual outline — a 75-minute flow
  1. Arrival & gentle grounding (10–12 min)
  2. Intention setting — brief vocal guidance (6–8 min)
  3. Main sound immersion: layered crystal bowls with subtle chimes (40 min)
  4. Slow decrescendo and mindful pause (10 min)
  5. Optional warm tea and quiet integration (rest of time)
What to bring

Comfortable clothing, a rug or yoga mat, a light blanket, an eye mask if you like, and an open curiosity. For privacy, we ask phones be silenced and stowed.
