Finding Your Center: Sound Bath for Postural Alignment After Birth

Your body has undergone incredible changes. Our Sound Bath sessions are designed to gently guide your awareness back to your core, helping you recognize and release tension that contributes to postpartum aches. Through resonant frequencies, you'll find a deeper connection to your abdominal muscles and pelvic floor, crucial for rebuilding stability and confidence in your posture. It's about consciously feeling and improving your body's support system.
