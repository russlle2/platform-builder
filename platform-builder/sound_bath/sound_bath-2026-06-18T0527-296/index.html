Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Sound Bath specifically help with the emotional toll of chronic illness or injury?
Our Sound Bath sessions are designed to gently soothe your nervous system, which is often in overdrive during chronic illness or injury. The calming vibrations can help reduce anxiety, alleviate feelings of overwhelm, and create a space for you to process the emotional challenges of your recovery, fostering a sense of peace and acceptance.
I'm concerned about sensory overload due to my condition. Are your Sound Bath sessions suitable for me?
Absolutely. We conduct our Sound Bath sessions with a trauma-informed approach, meaning we prioritize your comfort and control. The sounds are introduced gently, and we can adjust the volume and intensity to ensure it feels safe and supportive for your unique sensory needs. Your well-being is our utmost priority, and we encourage open communication about any sensitivities you may have.
What if I experience emotional release or discomfort during a Sound Bath?
It's not uncommon for emotions to surface during a Sound Bath, especially when navigating chronic recovery. Our practitioner is trained to hold a safe and compassionate space for whatever arises. We encourage you to simply observe these feelings without judgment, and know that you are in a supportive environment. We'll always check in with you and ensure you feel grounded before and after the session.
How often should I attend Sound Bath sessions to support my emotional recovery from chronic conditions?
The frequency of Sound Bath sessions is highly individual, depending on your personal needs and how you respond. Many people find regular sessions, perhaps weekly or bi-weekly, are most beneficial for consistent emotional support during recovery. We recommend discussing your specific situation with us to determine a rhythm that best suits your path to well-being.
