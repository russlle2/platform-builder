Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Sound Bath specifically help with my college anxiety and academic stress?
A Sound Bath provides a structured environment for your nervous system to downregulate, which is crucial when you're constantly in 'fight or flight' due to deadlines and pressure. The resonant frequencies help quiet an overactive mind, allowing you to process underlying anxieties, reduce mental clutter, and improve your ability to focus when you return to your studies.
Do I need any prior experience with meditation or Sound Baths to benefit?
Absolutely not! Our Sound Bath sessions are designed to be accessible for everyone, especially those new to these practices. There's no right or wrong way to experience it. Your only job is to get comfortable and allow the sounds to wash over you. We provide all the guidance and support you need to feel safe and engaged.
What should I expect during a Sound Bath session?
You'll lie down comfortably on a mat with blankets and pillows. will then play various instruments like crystal singing bowls and gongs, creating a rich soundscape. The experience is deeply relaxing, often leading to a meditative state where you can release tension and gain emotional clarity, all within a trauma-informed and supportive setting.
Is a Sound Bath a substitute for therapy or counseling for my anxiety?
While Sound Baths are profoundly supportive for emotional well-being and stress reduction, they are a complementary practice and not a substitute for professional therapy or counseling. If you are experiencing severe anxiety or trauma, we encourage you to seek professional mental health support. Our Sound Baths can be a powerful addition to your overall care plan.
