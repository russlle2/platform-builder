Sound Bath: Releasing the Grip of College Pressure

College life can feel like a constant exam, and the emotional toll is real. Our Sound Bath sessions are designed to help you gently release the tension and anxiety that build up from academic demands and social pressures. This isn't just about relaxation; it's about creating a safe space for your nervous system to regulate, allowing you to process overwhelming emotions in a supported way, so you can return to your studies with a clearer mind.
