Frequently asked

Practical answers for attending and choosing a session.

What should I bring?
A yoga mat or cushioned pad, a light blanket, and anything you use to stay comfortable (eye pillow, bolster). We provide mats upon request.
How long are sessions?
Group gatherings range from 30 to 75 minutes. Private sessions are scheduled for 45 to 90 minutes depending on intention. Use the mixer below to see how intensity and length pair.
Safety & contraindications
We take safety seriously. If you have epilepsy, certain cardiac devices, are pregnant, or are in treatment for acute psychiatric episodes, check with your medical provider before attending. Some instruments produce low frequencies and vibrations that may not be appropriate in those circumstances.
If you have specific medical concerns, email us at {{EMAIL}} with details so we can advise.
