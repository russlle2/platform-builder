Questions and concerns

Is this safe if I have a medical condition?

Consult your clinician first. High-volume gongs, certain vibrational therapies, and prolonged sessions can be inappropriate for specific conditions. See our contraindications below.

What should I bring?

Comfortable clothes, a mat and small blanket if you like. We provide bolsters and some cushions.

Contraindications: If you are pregnant, have epilepsy, recent head injury, metal implants that resonate, severe cardiovascular conditions, or are on medications that influence balance and blood pressure, consult your provider before attending. For private sessions we can adapt technique — mention medical notes when booking.
