A short note — why it feels premium

Our sessions are designed for gentle sensory recalibration. We combine nuanced frequencies, intentional pacing, and a calm, clinical touch. This lets you sink into sound without excess stimulation — a curated environment for recalibration and rest.

Session flow — what to expect

  • Arrival & grounding — brief intake, choice of soft lighting and positioning.
  • Intention & breath — guided breathing and gentle prompts to orient the nervous system.
  • Sound immersion — a layered set of instruments played with space and restraint.
  • Integration — soft silence and a short check-in to transition back.

Typical session length: 60–90 minutes. We prioritize safety and a predictable arc so guests feel secure throughout.

Instruments & textural palette

Our curated palette prioritizes clarity and harmonic richness. A recent set may include:

  • Monochord: slow, sympathetic vibrations that underpin tone.
  • Crystal singing bowls: clear, high sustain notes for spaciousness.
  • Wind chimes & koshi: subtle high-frequency sparkles.
  • Tuning forks: focused therapeutic tones for precise entrainment.
  • Soft frame drum: a low tactile anchor when gently applied.

Practical essentials

What to bring: a yoga mat or blanket, a small pillow, water, and any props you prefer. We provide bolsters and extra blankets on request.

Contraindications & safety: If you are pregnant, have uncontrolled epilepsy, recent head injury, or are under heavy influence of sedatives, please contact us before booking. Sound practices can be powerful; we screen for safety and adapt to medical needs.

Accessibility: venue accessibility varies by site — call or email with specific needs and we will accommodate where possible.
