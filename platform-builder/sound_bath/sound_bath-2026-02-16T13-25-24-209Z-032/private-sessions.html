Session structure

Private sessions are intentionally modular. Below is a typical order which we adapt to client needs:

  • Intake & intention setting (5–10 min).
  • Grounding (breath + gentle movement, 5–7 min).
  • Main sound work (20–60 min depending on booking): we feature tuning forks, monochord, bronze bowls — instruments selected to support your goals.
  • Integration and aftercare guidance (10 min).

What to bring & safety

Bring a mat, blanket, and anything that supports comfort. Contraindications include pregnancy in the first trimester without clearance, uncontrolled cardiovascular conditions, and active seizure disorders. Please disclose implants or devices and any ongoing psychiatric care prior to session.

Instruments highlighted for private work

For one-on-one work we emphasize tuning forks, a deep wooden monochord, and hand-held chimes to enable precise somatic attunement.
