Why attend a group sound bath?

Our sessions are created with a clinical calm approach: measured pacing, clear safety guidance, and sensory cues that emphasize grounding. Expect a premium, low-stimulus environment with carefully curated instruments.

  • Shared resonance amplifies perceived depth of relaxation.
  • Facilitated integration time after the journey.
  • Hybrid access: attend in the studio or join by live stream.

Session flow — what to expect

  1. Arrival & settle (10–15 min): check-in, temperature, and brief orientation.
  2. Grounding practices (5–10 min): breathwork and micro-body scan.
  3. Main sound journey (40–50 min): layering of instruments with intentional crescendos and rests.
  4. Integration & journaling (10–15 min): quiet reflection and optional sharing.

Instruments in this group series

Each event highlights a curated palette. For the upcoming gatherings we feature crystal singing bowls, bronze gong, and Himalayan chimes — balanced for clarity and warmth.

Practicals — what to bring

  • Yoga mat, blanket, pillow or bolster for support.
  • Water bottle; comfortable layered clothing.
  • Optional eye mask and light journal for notes after the session.

Contraindications: If you are pregnant, have uncontrolled hypertension, recent surgery, implanted electronic medical devices (e.g., pacemakers), or are under seizure risk, please consult with a clinician and inform the facilitator prior to attending.
