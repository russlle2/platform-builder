Common questions — clear expectations

Concise answers to help you prepare. If you don’t see your question, reach out at {{EMAIL}}.

What is a sound bath and how long does it last?
A sound bath is a guided session where live instruments create enveloping resonance to support relaxation. Typical group circles run 60–75 minutes: 10–15 minutes to settle, 35–45 minutes immersive sound, and 10–15 minutes for gentle wake and reflection.
How should I prepare?
Arrive rested, hydrate, and wear layered comfortable clothing. Bring your mat, blanket, and anything to block light if you prefer. Keep jewelry minimal. We recommend minimal caffeine beforehand.
Are sound baths safe? Any contraindications?
Most people find them restorative. However, please do not attend without consent from a medical provider if you have epilepsy, a pacemaker or other implantable medical device, severe psychiatric conditions, or are in your first trimester of pregnancy without approval. If you have tinnitus, inform the facilitator; we can adapt the experience.
What instruments will be used?
We rotate ensembles to keep the palette fresh. Common instruments include: a gong for deep harmonic wash, singing bowls (crystal or metal) for sustaining overtones, and a monochord for subtle drone. Each event’s lineup is posted on the event page.
Can I bring my phone? Will I be recorded?
We request phones be silenced and stored away to preserve the container. Sessions are not recorded unless explicitly stated; any recordings for remote participants are offered as optional, guided audio files.
