Frequently Asked Questions

I'm always busy and feel guilty taking time for myself. How can a Sound Bath help with my burnout?
It's completely understandable to feel that way when you're dealing with burnout. Our Sound Bath sessions are a gentle, low-effort way to give back to yourself. Think of it as a crucial reset button that actually helps you be more present and effective in your work and relationships, not less. Even 60 minutes can make a noticeable difference in your capacity to handle daily demands.
My relationships feel strained due to my stress. Can a Sound Bath really make a difference?
Absolutely. When you're burnt out, your patience wears thin, and it impacts how you interact with others. A Sound Bath helps calm your nervous system, reducing irritability and increasing your emotional resilience. This allows you to approach conversations and interactions with more understanding and less reactivity, naturally improving the quality of your connections.
I've never done anything like a Sound Bath before. Will I know what to do?
You don't need any special preparation or experience! Our Sound Bath sessions are designed to be completely accessible. You simply lie down comfortably, close your eyes if you wish, and let the sounds wash over you. There's no right or wrong way to experience it – just show up as you are, and let the sounds do the work.
I'm skeptical about 'energy work'. Is this for me?
We understand that skepticism. Our Sound Bath practice is rooted in the physiological effects of sound on the body and mind – specifically, its ability to help shift brainwaves and calm the nervous system. While some people find deeper spiritual connections, the core benefit for you will be a reduction in stress and a feeling of deep relaxation, without needing to buy into anything you're uncomfortable with.
