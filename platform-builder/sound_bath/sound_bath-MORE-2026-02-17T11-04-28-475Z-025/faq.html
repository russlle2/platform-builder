Questions & Considerations

Who benefits from these sessions?
People looking to downshift attention, recover from stress, or improve rest. Sessions support steady breathing and gentle somatic awareness.
Do I need experience?
No—most offerings are accessible. We structure time and provide gentle cues to help you return from sound without strain.
Are sessions loud?
We calibrate to the room. You can choose gentle / medium / intense textures when booking. Intense does not mean harmful volume—it's about sustained resonance and density.
Contraindications & safety
Most people attend safely. Please consult your physician before attending if you have any of the following: epilepsy or seizure disorder, recent head injury, recent eye surgery, sensitivity to sustained low frequencies, pacemaker or certain implanted medical devices, or are in the early stages of pregnancy. If you are unsure, contact us at {{EMAIL}}. Staff will offer quieter placement and can adapt the practice.
Privacy & accessibility
We hold space that is fragrance-free by request and wheelchair accessible at select venues. Notify us when reserving for specific accommodations.
Bookings & fees
Can I book privately?
Yes — bespoke sessions and workplace gatherings are offered. See private sessions page for options and sliding-scale framing.
Cancellation policy
Please notify us 24 hours in advance to reschedule or receive a partial credit. Late cancellations may forfeit the fee.
