Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Sound Bath specifically help me as a parent managing household stress?
Our Sound Bath sessions are designed to create a deep state of relaxation that helps calm your nervous system. For parents, this means a chance to release built-up tension, reduce mental chatter, and find a clearer headspace. This reset can improve your capacity for patience, empathy, and present-moment connection with your children and partner, fostering a more peaceful home environment.
I'm always busy; how long does a Sound Bath take, and what should I expect?
Sessions typically last about 60 minutes, including time to settle in and gently reorient afterward. You'll lie comfortably on a mat, often with blankets and pillows, while I play various instruments around you. Most people experience deep relaxation, some drift into a light sleep, and others feel a profound sense of calm. The goal is simply to allow yourself to receive the sounds without expectation.
Will a Sound Bath help me improve my relationships at home?
While a Sound Bath doesn't directly involve talking through relationship issues, it creates an internal space for profound emotional and mental clarity. By reducing your stress and fostering a sense of inner peace, you'll likely find yourself more present, less reactive, and more open-hearted in your interactions with your family, naturally strengthening your bonds and improving communication.
What if I can't relax easily or have never done anything like a Sound Bath before?
That's perfectly normal! Many parents carry a lot of tension and find it hard to 'switch off.' Our Sound Baths are designed to be accessible to everyone, regardless of prior experience. The sounds themselves gently guide your mind and body into a relaxed state without effort on your part. Just come with an open mind, and allow the experience to unfold naturally for you.
