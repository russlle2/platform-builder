How we think about progress

Sound isn’t a magic switch. We combine paced listening, brief breath frameworks, and guided reflection to help you notice small shifts over multiple sessions.

Important contraindications

If you are pregnant, have epilepsy, a pacemaker or other implanted device, are on photosensitizing medication, or have seizure history, please consult your medical provider before attending. Inform us ahead of private sessions to adapt tone and posture.

A short portrait

Sessions last 45–75 minutes. We favor smaller groups and intention-led openings. Memberships provide priority booking and lower per-session cost.
