Frequently asked

Do I need experience?

No. Sessions are accessible and guided—participants of all levels are welcome.

Is this therapy?

These offerings are supportive experiential sessions. They are not a substitute for medical or psychiatric care. Private work may be coordinated with a clinician when appropriate.

Contraindications

People with epilepsy, severe hearing conditions, certain cardiac devices, or uncontrolled psychiatric symptoms should consult a medical professional before attending. If you are pregnant, have recent head trauma, or experience panic in enclosed spaces, please reach out to discuss accommodations or consider a private session.
