Questions people ask before coming

What should I bring?
+
Comfortable clothes, an intention, and anything that helps with lying or sitting (mat, pillow). We provide some mats and blankets but bring favorites for full comfort.
How loud are the sessions?
+
Volume is moderated to remain supportive rather than overwhelming. We also offer a low-volume lane; contact us if you are sound-sensitive.
Can I attend if I have health concerns?
+
If you have epilepsy, a pacemaker, are pregnant, or have other medical concerns, please consult your provider and contact us. Private formats can be adapted.
Do I need to meditate already?
+
No. Sessions are accessible to newcomers and those with experience. Guidance is given throughout, and you can participate however feels right.
