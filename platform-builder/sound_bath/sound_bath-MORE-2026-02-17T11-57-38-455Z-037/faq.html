Questions people bring

Short answers for practical planning; reach out if you need clarification.

When should I arrive and what should I bring?

Arrive 10–15 minutes early to settle. Bring a yoga mat, blanket, and eye cover if you like. We provide bolster options. Wear comfortable layers.

Are there any medical cautions?

Contraindications: If you are pregnant, have a pacemaker, or suffer from epilepsy or certain seizure disorders, or are currently in acute psychiatric crisis, consult your physician before attending. Sound can be intense for some; tell a facilitator about implants or relevant conditions before the session.

Will sessions be recorded?

We generally do not record group sessions. Sometimes short, practice-focused audios are shared with participants; opt-in is requested at sign-up.

Booking, cancellations and refunds

Bookings are reserved online. Cancellations within 48 hours may be partially refundable depending on the event; contact us at {{EMAIL}} or call {{PHONE}} for specifics.

Can I bring my child?

Most public group events are intended for adults. For family or youth options, look for labeled sessions on the Events page or inquire about a private gathering.

The sound feels intense — is that normal?

Responses vary. We present a range of intensities and offer modifications. If you feel overwhelmed, signal a facilitator and we will create space for you to re-anchor.
