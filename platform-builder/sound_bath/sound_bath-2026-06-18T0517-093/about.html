How Sound Bath Supports Your Well-being

Your nervous system can benefit greatly from the gentle vibrations of a Sound Bath. These sessions are crafted to help quiet the 'fight or flight' response, encouraging a state of deep relaxation. You'll find it easier to unwind, allowing your body and mind to release accumulated tension and settle into a more regulated rhythm.

What exactly happens during a Sound Bath session?

During a Sound Bath, you'll recline comfortably, perhaps on a mat or in a chair, while Dr. Morgan Ellis plays various instruments like singing bowls and gongs. The sounds wash over you, creating a deeply relaxing environment. There's no physical interaction required from you, just quiet listening.

Is a Sound Bath suitable for someone with limited mobility?

Absolutely. Sound Bath sessions are designed to be entirely accessible. You can participate comfortably seated in a chair or lying down, whichever is best for your body. The benefits come from listening, not from movement, making it ideal for those with mobility considerations.

How can a Sound Bath help with stress and an overactive nervous system?

The gentle vibrations and harmonic frequencies encourage your brain to shift into more relaxed states, similar to deep meditation. This helps quiet the part of your nervous system responsible for stress, promoting a natural calm response, easing tension, and helping you feel more centered.

What should I bring or wear to a Sound Bath?

We recommend you wear comfortable, loose-fitting clothing that allows you to relax fully. You might also want to bring a small blanket or a cushion for extra comfort, though we provide some amenities. The key is to ensure you feel cozy and at ease.
