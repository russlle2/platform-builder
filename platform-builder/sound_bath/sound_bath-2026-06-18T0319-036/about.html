Sound Bath for Nervous System Support

Recovering from chronic illness or injury often places significant demand on your nervous system. Our Sound Bath sessions are specifically designed to shift your body from a heightened 'fight or flight' state towards a restorative 'rest and digest' response. This intentional downshift helps mitigate the physiological stress that can impede your recovery progress, allowing your system to allocate more resources to healing.

How does a Sound Bath specifically help with chronic illness or injury recovery?

Our Sound Bath sessions are geared towards reducing the chronic stress response often associated with long-term health issues. By promoting deep relaxation, we aim to lower cortisol levels, improve sleep quality, and encourage a parasympathetic nervous system state, all of which are vital for your body's repair and recovery processes.

Is a Sound Bath safe if I have specific sensitivities or pain points from my condition?

Yes, your comfort and safety are paramount. We encourage you to communicate any sensitivities or specific pain areas beforehand. We can adjust the volume and instrument proximity to ensure a gentle and beneficial experience. The goal is to support, not overwhelm, your system.

What should I expect to feel during and after a Sound Bath session?

During the session, you may experience a profound sense of calm, reduced physical tension, and a quieted mind. Afterward, many individuals report feeling more rested, centered, and less agitated. These effects can contribute to better sleep and a more regulated emotional state, supporting your overall recovery.

How frequently should I attend Sound Bath sessions to support my recovery?

The ideal frequency can vary based on your individual needs and recovery journey. We recommend starting with regular sessions, perhaps weekly or bi-weekly, to build a foundation of nervous system regulation. As you progress, we can discuss a frequency that best sustains your sense of calm and supports your ongoing well-being.
