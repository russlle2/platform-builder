What Exactly is a Sound Bath for College Stress?

Think of a Sound Bath as a dedicated pause button for your brain. You simply lie back and listen as a variety of gentle sounds wash over you. There’s no chanting, no complex poses – just pure, calming acoustics. It’s a passive experience designed to help your body and mind shift out of 'fight or flight' mode, which is super common when you're dealing with academic pressure and anxiety.
