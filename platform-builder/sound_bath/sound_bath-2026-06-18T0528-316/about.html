How can a Sound Bath help with my chronic physical pain or injury recovery?

Our Sound Bath sessions utilize specific frequencies and vibrations to encourage deep relaxation, which can help to reduce muscle tension and calm your nervous system. This supportive environment allows your body to shift out of a heightened state of discomfort, potentially alleviating some of the persistent physical pain associated with chronic illness or injury recovery.

Is Sound Bath safe if I am sensitive to certain sounds or have specific physical limitations during my recovery?

Yes, your comfort and safety are our priority. We maintain a gentle soundscape, and you are always in control of your experience. We encourage you to communicate any sensitivities or physical limitations before or during the session so we can adjust the sound intensity or suggest comfortable positions to ensure a supportive experience for your recovery.

What should I expect to feel during a Sound Bath session for physical pain relief?

Many individuals report a sense of deep relaxation and a reduction in the perception of pain during and after a Sound Bath. You might feel gentle vibrations throughout your body, which can be soothing. The goal is to create an environment where your nervous system can settle, allowing your body to release tension and find a more comfortable, stable state.

How frequently should I attend Sound Bath sessions to support my recovery from chronic illness or injury?

The frequency that is most beneficial for you will depend on your individual needs and the nature of your recovery. We recommend starting with a consistent schedule, such as weekly or bi-weekly sessions, to allow your body to adapt and integrate the benefits. We can discuss a personalized plan during your consultation to best support your physical pain relief and somatic healing goals.
