Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Sound Bath specifically help with emotional recovery from chronic illness or injury?
Our Sound Bath sessions are designed with your specific needs in mind. The carefully chosen sounds and vibrations work to calm your nervous system, reducing the physiological stress response often associated with chronic health challenges. This creates an optimal environment for gentle emotional processing and can help mitigate feelings of anxiety or overwhelm that arise during recovery.
Will the Sound Bath be too stimulating or overwhelming for my sensitive system during recovery?
We prioritize your comfort and safety above all else. Our Sound Bath sessions are conducted with a trauma-informed approach, meaning we create a predictable and gentle sonic landscape. The volume and intensity are carefully managed to avoid overstimulation, ensuring you can relax without feeling overwhelmed, which is especially important during your recovery.
What should I expect during a Sound Bath session, considering my chronic condition or injury?
You'll be invited to lie down comfortably, supported by props if needed, to ensure you are at ease. The practitioner, , will guide you through the session, using various instruments to create a cohesive soundscape. Your role is simply to rest and allow the sounds to wash over you. There's no pressure to 'do' anything, only to be present and allow for relaxation and emotional settling.
Is there any scientific basis for using Sound Baths in trauma-informed care for chronic recovery?
Yes, there is a growing body of research supporting the physiological and psychological benefits of sound and vibration for stress reduction and nervous system regulation. Our approach is informed by these findings, utilizing specific sound frequencies to promote parasympathetic nervous system activation, which is crucial for emotional healing and reducing the impact of trauma on the body during your recovery period.
