Frequently Asked Questions

How does a Sound Bath specifically benefit my posture?
Sound Bath sessions use resonant vibrations to encourage deep relaxation in your muscles and fascia. This relaxation can help release chronic tension that often contributes to poor posture, allowing your body to naturally find a more aligned and balanced position. You'll also likely notice an increased awareness of how you hold your body.
Can a Sound Bath help with mental clarity and focus?
Absolutely. The structured yet fluid soundscapes of a Sound Bath provide a unique environment for your mind. By focusing on the intricate sounds, you engage in a form of active listening that can quiet mental chatter, improve your attention span, and leave you feeling more centered and mentally sharp.
What should I expect during my first Sound Bath session?
You'll lie comfortably on a mat, often with blankets and pillows for support. will then play various instruments like singing bowls and gongs. Your only task is to relax and listen. You might feel gentle vibrations, hear a range of tones, and experience a profound sense of calm. It's a very passive yet deeply engaging experience.
Is a Sound Bath suitable for someone with limited mobility?
Yes, it is. A Sound Bath is a non-physical practice, meaning you don't need to perform any movements or strenuous activities. You simply rest in a comfortable position that works for you. If you have specific comfort needs, please let us know when you book, and we'll ensure you're well-supported.
