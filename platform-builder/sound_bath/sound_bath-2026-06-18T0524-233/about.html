Understanding Sound Bath for Emotional Healing

It’s a passive, yet profound, way to connect with inner peace.

What exactly happens during a Sound Bath session?

During a Sound Bath, you’ll lie or sit comfortably while Dr. Morgan Ellis plays a variety of instruments that produce gentle, resonant sounds. You simply relax and allow the sound vibrations to envelop you. There's no interaction required on your part, just a quiet space for your mind and body to rest.

Is a Sound Bath suitable for someone with past difficult experiences?

Absolutely. Our Sound Bath sessions are designed with a trauma-informed approach. We prioritize creating a safe, calm, and predictable environment, using gentle sounds to ensure your comfort and peace of mind. Your well-being is our primary concern, and we strive to make this a supportive experience for everyone.

Do I need any special equipment or experience to participate in a Sound Bath?

No special equipment or prior experience is needed. We provide everything for your comfort, such as mats and blankets. Just come as you are, dressed in comfortable clothing. Your willingness to relax and be open to the experience is all that’s required.

How can Sound Bath sessions specifically help with emotional well-being for seniors?

Sound Bath sessions offer a non-verbal pathway to emotional comfort. The gentle sounds can help reduce feelings of stress or anxiety, promote relaxation, and create a sense of inner quiet. For seniors, this can be particularly beneficial for easing emotional burdens and fostering a greater sense of peace and groundedness without requiring strenuous effort.
