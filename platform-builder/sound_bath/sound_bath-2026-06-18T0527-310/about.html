How can a Sound Bath help with the physical pain I feel during a big life change?

When you're going through a major transition, your body often holds stress as physical tension, leading to discomfort. The gentle vibrations of a Sound Bath can help your muscles relax deeply, calm your nervous system, and encourage your body to release stored pain, giving you a sense of physical relief and ease during this time.

I'm not used to focusing on my body's sensations. Is Sound Bath suitable for somatic healing?

Absolutely. Sound Bath is a wonderful way to gently connect with your body's wisdom. It doesn't require any prior experience. The sounds create a safe space for you to notice and release physical sensations, promoting a natural somatic healing process that can be incredibly supportive when navigating big life changes.

Will a Sound Bath help me feel more stable and less overwhelmed during my transition?

Many clients find that the deep relaxation induced by a Sound Bath helps to settle their nervous system, reducing feelings of overwhelm and promoting a sense of inner stability. It brings your awareness back to your own body, offering a grounding experience that can be very beneficial when life feels uncertain or tumultuous.

What should I expect physically during a Sound Bath session?

You'll lie comfortably, often with blankets and props, as you allow the sounds to wash over you. Physically, you might experience deep relaxation, tingling sensations, a release of muscle tension, or even a sense of lightness. It's an opportunity for your body to deeply rest and reset, supporting your physical well-being through your transition.
