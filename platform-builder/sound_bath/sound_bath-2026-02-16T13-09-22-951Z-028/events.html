What to expect — flow of the evening

Each evening follows a calm arc: arrival, gentle breathwork, a layered sound journey, and soft re-entry. We allow ample pause between pieces so the body can integrate the vibrations.

  1. Welcome & settling (10–15 min)
  2. Guided breath + intention (5–10 min)
  3. Main sound journey (30–45 min)
  4. Soft closure + tea (10–15 min)

Heard in the room

We compose texture from chosen instruments — not a concert, but a shared field of resonance.

  • Crystal singing bowls tuned to harmonic intervals
  • Light tuning-fork work for focused tones
  • Soft wind chimes and subtle electronic drones

Myths & truths

We often meet myths about sound baths — let us invite clarity.

Myth: You must be silent and still.
Truth: Movement, tears, or sleep are all valid responses — the sound invites your body to speak.
Myth: Sound will fix everything.
Truth: It is a companion for restoration, not a replacement for medical care.

Contraindications & safety

Sound is generally safe, but please note:

  • Not recommended during the first trimester of pregnancy — disclose if pregnant
  • Consult your physician if you have pacemakers or severe hearing conditions
  • If you are on medication for psychiatric conditions, contact us before booking

What to bring

  • A yoga mat and a soft blanket for warmth
  • An eye mask or scarf to gently dim light
  • Water bottle and an open heart

Notes from the room
