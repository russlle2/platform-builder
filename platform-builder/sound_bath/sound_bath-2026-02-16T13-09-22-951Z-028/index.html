Practical FAQ

What to bring?

Comfortable layers, a yoga mat or blanket, a pillow for neck support, water, and an eye mask if you prefer darkness. We provide bolsters and extra blankets on request.

Flow of a session

Arrival & settling (10–15 min) — intention & brief guidance — progressive sound arc (45–60 min) — soft re-grounding & sharing (10–15 min). You are welcome to rest quietly during sharing.

Contraindications

If you have epilepsy, are pregnant, have implanted medical devices, or have recent head/ear surgery please consult your physician and notify the facilitator ({{FACILITATOR_NAME}}) before attending. Sessions are not a replacement for medical care.
