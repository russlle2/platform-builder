Questions, softened

Practical answers for curious listeners. We favor clarity and gentle guidance — below is what most guests ask before arriving.

Do I need experience with meditation?
No. Sessions are accessible. We offer brief guidance and the music carries much of the invitation.
What instruments do you use?
A carefully chosen palette: Tibetan bowls, a mellow gong, and harmonic tuning forks often appear. Each event varies to keep texture alive.
How long is a typical group sound bath?
Generally 60–90 minutes. We begin with an orientation and close with a short grounding period.
What to bring?
Yoga mat or cushion
Blanket
Water bottle
Eye pillow (optional)
Contraindications & safety
Please consult your healthcare provider if you are pregnant in the first trimester, have a history of seizures, uncontrolled psychiatric conditions, or recent head trauma. Sound can be intense — disclose implants, pacemakers, or recent surgeries when booking.
Can I attend if I’m sensitive to loud sound?
Yes. Facilitators aim for a mid-range dynamic and will avoid sudden volume spikes. If you are concerned, request a front-row or quieter positioning when booking.
Is the space accessible?
Our venue is on one level with a short ramp. If you need additional accommodations, email {{EMAIL}}.

How a session flows

  1. Arrival & gentle orientation (10 min)
  2. Guided breathing + settling (5–10 min)
  3. Main sound field — layered instruments (40–60 min)
  4. Slow return & sharing space (5–10 min)

Expect long tones, harmonic washes, and moments of near-silence that let the body resonate.
