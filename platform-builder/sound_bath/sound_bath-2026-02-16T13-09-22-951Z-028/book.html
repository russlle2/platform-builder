Reserve your place

Select a session and prepare with intention. We keep groups small to honor quiet and clarity.

Arrival
Check-in, settle your mat, and find your place. Soft lighting lets the body relax before sound begins.
Practice
Guided breathing → layered instruments (crystal bowls, chimes, bowed monocord) → field of sound.
Return
We close with silence and a brief grounding; time to journal or share quietly.

What to bring

  • Yoga mat or thick blanket (cushion if preferred)
  • Extra blanket — layering keeps you warm in stillness
  • Water bottle and optional eye pillow
  • Earplugs if you prefer a very muted field

A note on sound

We layer instruments such as gongs, tuning forks, and soft crystal bowls. Each event varies the palette so returning guests find new textures. The field is intentionally curated — loud moments are measured, and facilitators adapt to group needs.
