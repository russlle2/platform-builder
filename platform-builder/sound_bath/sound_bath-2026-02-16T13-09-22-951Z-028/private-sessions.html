Corporate & team sessions

Session flow

Arrival & intentions — We pause to name a focus or gentle aim.
Sound immersion — Layered textures guide a descent and then gentle lift.
Integration — Quiet space, grounding suggestions, and optional notes.

What to bring

We want you comfortable. For private sessions bring:

  • A mat or soft surface, layered blankets, and a pillow for support
  • Socks, an eye cover, and water
  • Any notes about physical limitations or recent surgeries

Contraindications

Please contact us prior to booking if you have any of the following:

  • Implanted electronic medical devices (e.g., pacemaker)
  • Severe auditory disorders or recent ear surgery
  • Active psychosis or unmanaged epilepsy
