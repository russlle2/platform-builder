What to bring

  • Something soft to lie on — yoga mat + blanket.
  • Pillow for neck/head comfort.
  • Water bottle to hydrate after the sound journey.
  • Optional: eye mask or scarf for gentle darkness.

Flow of a session — what to expect

Each gathering is crafted as a gentle arc. We begin seated for arrival and intention, move into a guided breath and subtle movement, and then enter the sound landscape. Instruments are played with attention to breath and space, inviting deep resonance and rest. We close with soft return, journaling, and time to integrate.

Arrival
Check-in, settle, brief orientation. (10–15 mins)
Immersion
A layered sound journey using curated instruments. (35–55 mins)
Return
Soft landing, hydration, and optional sharing. (10–20 mins)

Contraindications & safety

Sound experiences are generally safe but not suitable for everyone. Please consult us prior to booking if you are pregnant, have epilepsy, severe heart conditions, recent surgeries, pacemakers, or are under intensive psychiatric care. Sound can be potent — do not attend under the influence of substances that significantly impair perception. If in doubt, contact us at {{EMAIL}}.
