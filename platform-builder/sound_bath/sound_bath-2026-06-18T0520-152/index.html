Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Sound Bath specifically help with postpartum sleep?
Our Sound Baths are tailored to address the unique sleep challenges of new mothers. The specific frequencies and vibrations used help to down-regulate your nervous system, reduce cortisol levels, and promote brainwave states conducive to deep, restorative sleep, directly combating the effects of sleep deprivation you might be experiencing.
Is the Sound Bath safe for mothers in any stage of postpartum recovery?
Yes, our Sound Bath sessions are designed to be a gentle and safe experience for mothers at any stage of their postpartum recovery. We recommend a brief consultation prior to your first session to ensure we can fully accommodate your comfort and specific needs, creating the most beneficial environment for you.
What should I expect during my first Sound Bath session?
You'll be invited to lie down comfortably on a soft mat, wrapped in cozy blankets. The practitioner will then begin to play various instruments, creating an immersive soundscape. Your only task is to relax and allow the sounds to wash over you. Many find themselves drifting into a deeply peaceful, dream-like state, often leading to profound rest afterward.
How often should I attend a Sound Bath to see results for my sleep?
While even a single session can provide significant relief, consistency is key for establishing new patterns of restorative rest. We recommend starting with weekly sessions for the first few weeks to help re-establish a healthy sleep rhythm. We can discuss a personalized plan that fits your schedule and recovery needs.
