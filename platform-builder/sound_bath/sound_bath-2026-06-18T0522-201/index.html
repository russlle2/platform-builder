Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Sound Bath specifically help with my burnout?
Burnout often stems from chronic stress, leading to emotional exhaustion and a feeling of being overwhelmed. Our Sound Bath sessions are designed to gently guide your nervous system into a parasympathetic state, which is essential for rest and repair. This deep relaxation helps to reduce cortisol levels, quiet an overactive mind, and create a safe space for emotional release, allowing you to regain a sense of balance and calm you might be missing.
I'm always on the go. Will I really be able to relax during a Sound Bath?
We understand that truly 'unplugging' can be challenging when you're accustomed to a fast-paced environment. The beauty of a Sound Bath is that the therapeutic vibrations do most of the work for you. You don't need to 'do' anything but lie comfortably and listen. The immersive soundscape provides a natural anchor for your attention, helping to quiet the mental chatter and guide you into a state of profound relaxation, even if you typically find it hard to switch off.
What makes your Sound Bath approach trauma-informed?
Being trauma-informed means we prioritize your safety and comfort above all else. This includes offering clear explanations, ensuring you have control over your experience (e.g., blanket, eye mask), and using instruments and tones that are grounding and non-intrusive. We aim to create an environment where you feel secure and supported, allowing for gentle emotional processing without re-triggering or overwhelming your system. Your well-being is our utmost concern.
What should I expect during my first Sound Bath session?
When you arrive, you'll be welcomed into a peaceful space. You'll find a comfortable mat, blankets, and an eye mask – everything you need to relax. The session involves lying down while I play a variety of instruments like crystal bowls and gongs. You don't need to do anything but listen and allow the sounds to wash over you. Many people report feeling deeply relaxed, a sense of lightness, and improved clarity afterwards. We'll have a moment for gentle reorientation before you leave.
