Finding Your Anchor: What is an Earthbound Echoes Sound Bath?

In a Sound Bath, you’ll lie comfortably as a rich tapestry of sound washes over you. We use natural instruments like crystal bowls and gongs to create vibrations that encourage your nervous system to settle. For college students facing constant deadlines and social demands, this practice offers a dedicated space to release accumulated tension and find a moment of stillness, helping to ease the physical and mental symptoms of anxiety.
