Practical details

What to bring
  • Yoga mat or low-profile pad
  • Blanket + pillow for warmth
  • Eye mask or scarf
  • Water bottle, optional journal

Safety & contraindications

Please note
  • Not recommended during first trimester of pregnancy
  • Those with implanted medical devices should consult their physician
  • Notify the facilitator of recent seizures or severe auditory sensitivity

Is this for you? A quick diagnostic

If you seek an elegant, sensory pause and are comfortable in a quiet, low-light group setting — this is designed to help reset nervous-system tone.
Who benefits
People wanting improved sleep, reduced stress, and clearer focus
  • Busy professionals
  • Caregivers seeking downtime
  • Anyone curious about sound medicine
Not right now
If you require active medical monitoring, this group format may not be appropriate
  • Active psychosis
  • Unstable heart conditions without clearance

Day-of plan

Efficient arrival to ease into silence
  1. Check-in & gentle welcome: 10–15 minutes
  2. Guided centering: breath & body scan
  3. Sound journey: 45–55 minutes
  4. Slow reentry: 5–10 minutes, tea available

Small habits to prepare

Micro steps for a deeper session

  • Avoid heavy meals within an hour of the session
  • Turn off notifications beforehand
