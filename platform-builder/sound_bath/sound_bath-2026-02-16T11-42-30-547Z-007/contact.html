How a booking unfolds — a straightforward plan

We offer clear stages so you know what to expect. The VIP Day model compresses planning and production into a single premium experience.

Intro & Intake

30–45 min call to define objectives, contraindications, and logistics. We ask about energy levels, hearing sensitivity, and mobility.
