A practical guide to booking and what happens next

Session flow (what will happen)

Consult — A quick check-in to tailor the session and note any contraindications (15–30 min).
Arrival & attunement — Set intention and get comfortable (10–15 min).
Immersive sound — The main sound field; length varies by session type (45–90 min).
Integration — Gentle close and next-steps, tea, or short reflection (10–20 min).

What to bring

  • Yoga mat / thick blanket
  • Pillow and warm layer
  • Water bottle (plastic or BPA-free)

Contraindications

If you have a pacemaker, were recently hospitalized, are pregnant, or have a history of seizures, inform us before booking. Loud instruments are adjustable and we can offer softer alternatives.
