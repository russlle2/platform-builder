Common questions — practical answers

Fast guidance on how we hold space, what to bring, and when to opt out. If your question isn’t covered, email {{EMAIL}}.

How long is a group session?

Generally 60–90 minutes total. Arrival and integration are part of the timeframe so plan to arrive 10–15 minutes early to settle in.

What should I bring?

Bring a mat or thick blanket, a cushion/pillow, and a light cover. Wear layers so you can adjust body temperature. Avoid heavy fragrances.

Are sound baths safe?

For most people they’re safe. Caution is advised if you have epilepsy, are pregnant, have a pacemaker, or are on medication that alters balance/consciousness. Please notify us in advance so we can adapt the sound. This is not medical advice.

What happens during a session?

We begin with breath and orientation, a short voice-led intention, followed by layered sound. We use dynamic shifts: soft drones, evolving overtone textures, and light percussive touches. The close is gradual and we allow time for gentle transition back to day-to-day.

Can I sleep during the session?

Yes. Sleeping is normal and often restorative. If you need more support on waking gently, tell a facilitator at arrival.

Do you offer private or corporate sessions?
