Meet the practice — calm, curated sound design

We craft sensory, premium sound bath experiences rooted in intention and safety. Our gatherings blend resonance, breathwork, and gentle guidance so you can rest into a recalibrated nervous system. We host group events and private VIP day immersions for deep restoration.

Our approach

We combine sensitive listening with structured sound architecture. A session moves through arrival, attunement, a sound field, and gentle closing. Elements vary so every experience feels bespoke — we emphasize safety, presence, and a tactile sense of relief.

Flow in a typical group event

  • Arrival & grounding (10–15 minutes): breath + orientation
  • Intent setting (5 minutes): quiet, spoken invitation
  • Immersive soundscape (40–60 minutes): layers of tones
  • Soft close & integration (10–15 minutes): tea and reflection

What to expect

Rooms are dim, temperatures cozy, and tactile props available. The session is guided; you may rest, move slightly, or cry — all responses are welcome.

What to bring

  • Yoga mat or blanket for floor comfort
  • Soft pillow and a lightweight throw
  • Water bottle (no glass) and comfortable clothes

Contraindications & safety

For safety, please tell us if you are pregnant, have epilepsy, implanted medical devices, severe hearing conditions, or are currently under a medication that alters consciousness. Loud gongs and metallic tones can be intense — we can provide alternatives. If in doubt, consult your healthcare provider before attending.

Instruments we use

  • Crystal singing bowls — clear, sustained harmonics
  • Tuning forks — precise, directional tones
  • Bamboo chimes — soft percussive color
