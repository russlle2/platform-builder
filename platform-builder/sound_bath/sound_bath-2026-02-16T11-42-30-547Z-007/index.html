Daily micro-habits to carry the sound home

Three small practices to stabilize the nervous system between events.

2-minute bell pause

Ring a small tuning fork or bell. Close your eyes and follow the vibration until it fades. Repeat twice.

Evening body scan

Lying in bed, take 6 slow breaths and scan from toes to crown, noticing tension without fixing it.

Breath rhythm reset

4 seconds inhale, 6 seconds exhale — repeat for five cycles to shift into parasympathetic mode.
