What should I expect during a Sound Bath session?

During your Sound Bath, you'll lie comfortably on a mat, often with blankets and pillows for support. You simply relax and allow the sounds of various instruments, like singing bowls and gongs, to wash over you. There's nothing you need to do, just be present and let the vibrations work their gentle magic on your body and mind.

Is a Sound Bath suitable if I have mobility concerns or physical limitations?

Absolutely. Our Sound Bath sessions are designed to be accessible and gentle. We ensure you are comfortable throughout, whether lying down or seated, and can accommodate various physical needs. The experience is passive; you don't need to move or exert yourself. We prioritize your comfort and well-being above all else.

How can a Sound Bath help with physical aches and stiffness?

The gentle vibrations from the instruments can help to relax tense muscles, improve circulation, and reduce inflammation. This deep relaxation encourages your body to release held tension, which often contributes to aches and stiffness. Many clients report a noticeable easing of physical discomfort and increased flexibility after a session.

What if I fall asleep during the Sound Bath?

It's perfectly normal and even encouraged to drift into a state of deep rest, or even fall asleep, during a Sound Bath. Your body and mind still receive the benefits of the sound vibrations and relaxation, regardless of whether you're consciously awake. Simply allow yourself to rest as deeply as your body needs.
