Frequently Asked Questions

How does a Sound Bath specifically benefit athletes managing physical stress?
A Sound Bath helps athletes by reducing nervous system overactivity, which is often heightened by intense training and competition. It aids in releasing the emotional tension stored in the body from physical exertion, performance pressure, or injury setbacks, promoting deeper rest and mental clarity essential for recovery.
Can a Sound Bath truly address emotional healing and trauma related to my athletic experience?
Absolutely. Our trauma-informed Sound Bath approach creates a safe environment for your nervous system to process and release emotional imprints from challenging athletic experiences, such as injuries, performance failures, or competitive stress. It's about gently guiding your body and mind toward a state where healing can occur without direct confrontation.
What should I expect during my first Sound Bath session at ?
During your first Sound Bath, you'll lie comfortably while plays various instruments like gongs and singing bowls. The sounds create resonant vibrations that encourage your body to relax deeply. There's no physical interaction required from you; simply allow the soundscape to facilitate a release of tension and emotional weight, promoting a profound sense of calm.
How frequently should I incorporate Sound Bath into my recovery routine?
The ideal frequency depends on your training intensity, competitive schedule, and personal stress levels. Many athletes find weekly or bi-weekly Sound Bath sessions to be highly beneficial for consistent emotional regulation and nervous system support, especially during peak training or after significant events. We can discuss a personalized plan during your consultation.
