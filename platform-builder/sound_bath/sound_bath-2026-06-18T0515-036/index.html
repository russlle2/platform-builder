Frequently Asked Questions

How does a Sound Bath specifically help with chronic illness or injury recovery?
Our Sound Bath sessions are tailored to help reduce the chronic stress response often experienced during recovery. The specific sound frequencies assist in shifting your nervous system from a high-alert state to a more relaxed one, which can aid in managing pain, improving sleep, and supporting your body's restorative functions.
Do I need to do anything during a Sound Bath session?
No, your primary role is simply to recline comfortably and allow the sounds to wash over you. There's no need for active participation or prior experience. We encourage you to focus on your breath and observe any sensations, allowing the sounds to guide your nervous system into a more regulated state.
Is Sound Bath safe if I have specific sensitivities or conditions?
Generally, Sound Bath is very safe. However, if you have specific sound sensitivities, epilepsy, or are pregnant, we recommend consulting your healthcare provider before attending. We can also discuss any concerns you have to ensure your comfort and safety during the session.
How often should I attend Sound Bath sessions for optimal benefit during my recovery?
The frequency often depends on your individual needs and the nature of your recovery. Many individuals find regular, consistent sessions (e.g., weekly or bi-weekly) to be most beneficial for sustained stress reduction and nervous system regulation. We can discuss a personalized recommendation during your initial consultation.
