How can a Sound Bath help with the physical aches I feel as a parent?

The deep vibrations from the instruments in a Sound Bath penetrate your tissues, promoting relaxation and increasing circulation. This can significantly reduce muscle tension and soothe chronic aches often associated with carrying children, bending, and the general physical demands of parenting.

I'm always juggling multiple tasks. Will I be able to relax enough during a Sound Bath?

Absolutely. The beauty of a Sound Bath is that the sounds themselves guide you into a state of deep relaxation. You don't need to 'try' to meditate. Just lie back, listen, and allow the resonant frequencies to gently quiet your mind and release your body's stress. It's a passive yet profoundly effective way to find calm.

What does 'somatic healing' mean in the context of a Sound Bath for parents?

Somatic healing means addressing how stress and emotions are held in your body. For parents, this might manifest as persistent neck pain, shoulder tension, or a general feeling of tightness. A Sound Bath gently encourages your body to release these physical imprints of stress, helping you feel lighter and more comfortable in your own skin.

I'm worried about finding childcare. How can I fit a Sound Bath into my busy schedule?

We understand finding time for yourself is a challenge. Many parents arrange for a partner or trusted friend to watch their children for just an hour, viewing this as essential self-care that recharges them for their family. We also offer various session times to accommodate different schedules, making it easier to prioritize your well-being.
