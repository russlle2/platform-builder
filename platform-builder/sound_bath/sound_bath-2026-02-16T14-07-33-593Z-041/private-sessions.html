Personal, tuned, attentive

Private sessions let us sculpt a sound field for your needs — deeper restorative work, couple's attunement, or team-focused mindful breaks. Each session blends instruments intentionally: for 1:1 we favor deep crystal bowls and tuning forks; for couples we arrange harmonic interplay with chimes and bowls; for corporate we offer a concise, grounding sequence with monochord and bowls.

Flow (typical)

  1. Intake & intention (10–15 min)
  2. Gentle centering; breath and posture (5–10 min)
  3. Sound immersion (30–55 min) — dynamic layering, space for embodiment
  4. Soft return & brief integration (10 min)

We adapt length and intensity to comfort and goals. If you have specific sensitivities (e.g., noise sensitivity, devices), mention them when booking.

What to bring

  • Comfortable clothes
  • Mat & blanket for longer sessions
  • List of intentions or questions (optional)

Contraindications

Not recommended for those with epilepsy or acute psychiatric crises; consult your physician for implanted devices. For bespoke questions: {{EMAIL}}

Session types

1:1 Session

A focused session for personal restoration. Instruments: deep crystal bowls, tuning forks, and occasional gong wash. 60–90 min.

Outcome: personalized grounding and integration plan.

Couples & paired

Shared resonance to cultivate presence and listening. Instruments: paired crystal bowls, chimes, and harmonic overlaps. 60–75 min.

Outcome: co-regulation and slowed communication space.

Corporate & team

Short, restorative sessions designed for work settings — 30–45 minutes. Instruments: monochord and selective bowls to lower stress and sharpen focus.

Outcome: reset and clearer attention for collaborative work.

Series & retreats

Multi-session packages for deep integration. We design a progression of instruments and pacing across visits.
