What should I bring and wear?
Comfortable layers, a mat or blanket, a small pillow for the neck/head, and water. Soft earplugs are optional if you prefer to dampen higher frequencies.
Are sound baths safe with medical conditions?
Most people find them safe. Contraindications: recent major surgery, uncontrolled epilepsy, and some implanted devices. Vibrational therapies can impact pacemakers or certain implants; consult your clinician. If pregnant, discuss with your care provider before booking. For specific concerns, email {{EMAIL}} or call {{PHONE}}.
How loud will it be? Will there be a gong?
We favor subtle, enveloping resonance. Instruments for our public groups often include a monochord harp and tuned bowls; occasional gentle gong strokes are brief and carefully placed. Private sessions can vary—ask when booking.
Do I need experience?
No. Everyone—from seasoned meditators to those new to contemplative sound—is welcome. We provide brief orientation at the start.
How should I prepare the day of the event?
Eat lightly, hydrate, avoid heavy caffeine. Plan to arrive 10–15 minutes early to settle. If taking medicines that affect balance, consider bringing a companion.
Cancellation & refund policy
Cancellations made 72+ hours before the event may receive a refund or credit. Within 72 hours there are limited refunds. For private bookings, a different policy applies—see the booking page or contact {{EMAIL}}.
What instruments do you use in your sessions?
We rotate instruments to sculpt different sound textures. Expect combinations such as crystal bowls, small chimes, tuned metal bowls, and occasionally a softly played monochord. Each event is curated with attention to subtlety.
