How a session unfolds

A single gathering is arranged like a slow tide: arrival, gentle orientation, deep listening, and a soft return. The flow below is typical but held lightly.
1 — Arrival & settling
Check in, lay down, and set an intention. We dim lights and invite rest.
2 — Opening voice & tuning
Breath guidance, a brief vocal or bell to center, and slow tuning of instruments.
3 — Sound immersion
An evolving soundscape using crystal bowls, chimes and a gong. Dynamics are gentle with intentional space for silence.
4 — Return
A closing tone, quiet minutes to integrate, then a calm transition to standing; tea offered after some sessions.

Contraindications & safety

Sound baths are generally safe for most people. Please do not book if you are within 24–48 hours of surgery, have severe uncontrolled epilepsy, or if you have a pacemaker or medical implants sensitive to vibration without physician clearance. If pregnant, consult your provider—gentle sessions may be appropriate. When in doubt, email {{EMAIL}} or call {{PHONE}}.

Small practices that extend the field

  • Gentle ear self-massage before sleep.
