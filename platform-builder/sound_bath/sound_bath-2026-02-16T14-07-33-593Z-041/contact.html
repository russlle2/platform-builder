A brief intake — what helps us craft the sound

A few questions we typically ask to match the soundscape to your needs: energy of the group, any sensitivities to volume or vibration, mobility needs, and whether the intent is meditation, ceremony, or relaxation.

Contraindications

Sound baths involve sustained vibration and low-frequency tones. If you have a pacemaker, are pregnant without provider approval, have recent head injury, epilepsy, or are particularly sensitive to strong vibrations, please consult your doctor and inform us before attending.

What to bring

  • Comfortable layers and a blanket
  • A small pillow and eye cover
  • Water and any medication you may need
  • Note: We provide yoga mats on request; let us know in advance

Session flow — an outline for group gatherings

Each gathering balances invitation, sound, and gentle transition. We curate the arc to hold stillness and bring people back with care.

  1. Arrival & settling (10–15 min) — soft tea, brief orientation, space for intention-setting.
  2. Guided breath & alignment (5–10 min) — grounding cues to receive sound.
  3. Sound journey (35–50 min) — layered instruments: crystal bowls, Tibetan gong, chimes, and low tuning forks create a shifting field.
  4. Return & integration (10–15 min) — gentle movement, journaling prompt, and optional sharing.

Timing adapts to the group. Private sessions may extend for 1:1 work with targeted sonic tools.

Micro-habits to extend the practice

Tiny rituals that echo the session: a three-breath pause before emails, a minute of mindful listening on your walk, and a short journaling prompt each evening.

Evening pause

Close your eyes, inhale for 4, hold for 2, exhale 6. Repeat 3 times.

Sound checkpoint

At midday, listen for 60 seconds — notice layers: traffic, wind, voices, birds. Only observe.
