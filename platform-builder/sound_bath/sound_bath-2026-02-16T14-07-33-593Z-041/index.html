Evening flow

A gentle arc that invites someone in and eases them out, held with intention.

  1. Arrive & settle (15m)

    Quiet check-in, choose a spot, soft breathwork to arrive in the body.

  2. Main soundscape (45m)

    Layered instruments — crystal bowls, tuning forks, and a soft monochord — guide resonant frequencies through the room.

  3. Integration & gentle close (15m)

    Return to eyes-open slowly, a short guided reflection and tea to ground.

Tiny practices between gatherings

Short, daily rituals to carry the reset forward.

  • Sound pause — 3 minutes

    Play a single singing bowl tone on exhale, listen until silence.

  • Feet-on-earth

    Stand barefoot for 60 seconds focusing on contact and breath.

  • Breath count

    Inhale 4, exhale 6 for six rounds before sleep.
