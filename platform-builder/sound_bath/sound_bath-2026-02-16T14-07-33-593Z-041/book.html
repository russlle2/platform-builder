A note about acoustics & comfort

Our gatherings are intentionally curated to favor deep relaxation over theatrical volume. Instruments vary by event—recent sets have included tuning forks, small metal bowls, and a gently struck frame drum. For private bookings we may include a monochord or harp tone depending on space and request.
