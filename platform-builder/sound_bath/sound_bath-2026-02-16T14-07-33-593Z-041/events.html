How an evening unfolds

Arrival & ground
We welcome you with a short centering and intention-setting — soft tea available.
Guided breath & gentle movement
Simple breathwork and gentle stretches create receptive space for listening.
The sound immersion
Layered instruments build a sound field: polished crystal bowls, sustained gong tones, delicate chimes and harmonic tuning forks. The journey moves from a cradle of textures toward a broad, immersive wash and a gentle return.
Return & integration
We close with silence and a short sharing, time to rest before leaving.

What to bring

  • Yoga mat or meditation cushion
  • Blanket and sweater (rooms are kept cool)
  • Reusable water bottle
  • Optional: eye mask or scarf

We provide bolsters on request. Please arrive 10–15 minutes early to settle in.

Contraindications & safety

Sound baths are safe for most people, but not recommended during the first trimester of pregnancy, for people with epilepsy, or those with certain acute psychiatric conditions. If you have a pacemaker or other implanted electronic device, consult your healthcare provider before attending. Contact us at {{EMAIL}} for questions.
