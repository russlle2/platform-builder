How can a Sound Bath specifically help my immune system during postpartum recovery?

During your postpartum period, deep relaxation and reduced stress are vital for a strong immune system. Our Sound Bath sessions guide you into a state of profound calm, which helps lower stress hormones that can suppress immunity. This allows your body to dedicate more energy to repair and immune defense, helping you feel more resilient and less susceptible to common ailments.

I'm so tired. Will a Sound Bath truly help me regain my vitality and energy?

Yes, absolutely! Exhaustion is a hallmark of new motherhood. A Sound Bath provides a rare opportunity for your body to enter a deep, restorative rest state. This isn't just napping; it's a profound relaxation that enables your body to recover, replenish energy stores, and combat the physical and mental fatigue that drains your vitality. Many new mothers report feeling significantly more refreshed and energized afterward.

Is a Sound Bath safe and gentle enough for me during my postpartum period?

Our Sound Bath practices are designed with utmost gentleness and safety in mind, especially for postpartum mothers. The sounds are never jarring, and the environment is always nurturing and supportive. We encourage you to listen to your body, and you'll always be comfortable. It's a passive experience, meaning you simply lie back and receive the gentle vibrations, requiring no physical exertion from you.

What should I expect during a Sound Bath session, and how can it support my recovery?

You'll lie comfortably on a mat, often with blankets and pillows for support. Dr. Morgan Ellis will play a variety of instruments, creating a tapestry of gentle sounds and vibrations. Your only job is to relax and allow the sounds to wash over you. This deep relaxation supports your recovery by reducing inflammation, promoting better sleep, and providing a much-needed mental break, all contributing to stronger immunity and renewed vitality.
