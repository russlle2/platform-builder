Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Sound Bath specifically help me when I'm going through a big life transition?
During major life transitions, your emotional and nervous system can feel overwhelmed. A Sound Bath provides a non-invasive way to bring your system back into a state of calm. The gentle vibrations help to soothe the nervous system, reducing anxiety and creating a space where you can process your emotions more clearly and ground yourself amidst the changes you're experiencing.
What does 'trauma-informed' mean in the context of your Sound Bath practice?
Being trauma-informed means we understand that life transitions can inadvertently trigger past difficult experiences. Our approach prioritizes safety, choice, and trust. We create an environment where you feel empowered to listen to your body's needs, ensuring the sound experience is gentle and supportive, never overwhelming, so you can engage with the healing process at your own pace.
I'm new to Sound Baths; what should I expect during a session, especially with my emotional state?
You can expect a comfortable, quiet space where you'll lie down and simply listen. There's nothing you need to 'do' other than allow the sounds to wash over you. If emotions arise, that's a natural part of processing. We encourage you to simply observe them without judgment. Our intention is to hold a supportive space for whatever comes up, allowing you to feel safe and held throughout.
Will a Sound Bath help me cope with the stress and uncertainty of my current life changes?
Absolutely. The resonant frequencies in a Sound Bath are known to induce a deep state of relaxation, which directly counteracts stress and anxiety. By calming your nervous system, you'll likely find increased clarity, reduced emotional reactivity, and a greater sense of inner peace, which are invaluable tools for navigating periods of uncertainty and significant change.
