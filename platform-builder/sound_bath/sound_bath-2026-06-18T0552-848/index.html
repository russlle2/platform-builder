Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly happens during a Sound Bath session?
During a Sound Bath, you'll lie comfortably on a mat or sit in a chair, whichever feels best for you. will then play various instruments like singing bowls and gongs, creating a rich tapestry of sounds. Your role is simply to relax and let the sounds wash over you, allowing the vibrations to gently guide your experience. There’s no right or wrong way to feel, just an opportunity to be present.
How can a Sound Bath help with my posture and body awareness?
The deep relaxation and heightened sensory perception cultivated during a Sound Bath can make you more aware of how you hold your body. As tension releases, you might naturally find yourself adjusting your alignment. This increased body awareness carries over into your daily life, helping you maintain better posture and move with more ease and less discomfort, without needing to actively 'correct' yourself.
Is the Sound Bath suitable for someone new to these types of practices?
Absolutely! Our Sound Bath sessions are designed to be welcoming and accessible to everyone, especially those who are new to sound-based practices. There's no special skill or preparation required, just an open mind. creates a very inclusive and supportive environment, ensuring you feel comfortable and at ease throughout your experience with us.
What should I bring or wear to a Sound Bath at ?
We recommend wearing comfortable clothing that allows for easy movement and relaxation. You might also want to bring a water bottle to stay hydrated. We provide mats and blankets, but feel free to bring your own cushion or a favorite blanket if you wish to enhance your personal comfort. Our goal is for you to feel completely at ease and supported.
