What exactly happens during a Sound Bath session and how does it help with emotional well-being?

During a Sound Bath, you'll lie comfortably while Dr. Morgan Ellis plays various instruments like crystal bowls and gongs. The sounds create gentle vibrations that help your body and mind relax deeply. This deep relaxation can calm your nervous system, making it easier for you to release tension, reduce stress, and process emotions in a gentle, non-confrontational way, supporting your overall emotional health.

I'm concerned about my mobility. Is a Sound Bath accessible and comfortable for seniors?

Absolutely. Your comfort is our priority. We ensure that our Sound Bath sessions are fully accessible. You can choose to lie on a comfortable mat with pillows and blankets, or if you prefer, you can sit in a supportive chair. We are here to accommodate any specific needs you may have, ensuring you feel safe and comfortable throughout your entire experience.

Will I need to talk about my past experiences or trauma during a Sound Bath?

No, you will not. Our Sound Bath sessions are designed to be a non-verbal emotional support. There's no expectation or requirement for you to discuss any personal experiences or trauma. The gentle sounds work to support your emotional processing organically, creating a space for release and calm without the need for verbal expression. You simply relax and allow the sounds to work their magic.

How often should I attend a Sound Bath to experience emotional benefits?

The frequency that works best for you can vary, as everyone's emotional needs are unique. Some seniors find that attending a Sound Bath weekly or bi-weekly provides consistent emotional support and stress reduction. Others prefer monthly sessions as a way to maintain their sense of calm and well-being. We recommend you listen to your own feelings and observe how the sessions benefit you over time to determine your ideal schedule.
