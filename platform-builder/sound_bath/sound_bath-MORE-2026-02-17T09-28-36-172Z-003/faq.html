Questions & Safety Notes

What should I expect at a group session?
A guided arrival, a steady sequence of tones and breath cues, and a short return phase. Sessions emphasize comfort and clear instructions. You can sit or lie down.
How long are sessions?
Most community gatherings are 45–50 minutes. Private sessions run 60–90 minutes depending on intake and goals.
What should I bring?
A mat or cushion, a light blanket, and anything that helps you relax. We provide bolsters on request.
Are the sounds safe for everyone?
Generally yes, but please read the contraindications below and consult our team if you have medical concerns.

Contraindications — important

  • People with uncontrolled epilepsy should not participate in sound-bath style sessions without medical clearance.
  • If you have a pacemaker or other implanted electronic device, inform the facilitator before attending.
  • Recent head injury, severe psychiatric instability, or active psychosis are reasons to seek individualized clinical care instead of group sound sessions.
  • Pregnancy is not a contraindication, but discuss positioning and comfort needs with staff.

This service is not a substitute for emergency medical treatment. If you are in crisis, call your local emergency number or a crisis line immediately.

More about sound, safety, and accessibility

We maintain low-to-moderate overall volume and avoid sudden, high-decibel events. Rooms are dimmed rather than dark, and facilitators share arrival scripts so participants know when phrases will repeat. If you have specific sensory needs, we can arrange quiet seating or an accompanier. Please email {{EMAIL}} to request accommodations.
