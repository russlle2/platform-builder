Drowning in Deadlines? Re-regulate with a Sound Bath

College life is a constant juggle, and the pressure can leave your nervous system buzzing with anxiety. Our Sound Bath practice provides a gentle yet powerful way to shift your internal state. Imagine waves of sound washing over you, signaling your body to release tension and allowing your mind to quiet. It's a dedicated pause button for your overstimulated brain, helping you move from fight-or-flight to a state of calm focus.
