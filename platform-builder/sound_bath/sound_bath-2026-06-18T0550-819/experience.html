Sound Bath: Supporting Your Postural Health as a Parent

The demands of parenting often lead to suboptimal postures: feeding, lifting, carrying, and bending. Our Sound Bath methodology is designed to help you identify and release chronic tension patterns that contribute to discomfort. By carefully applied sound frequencies, we encourage a deep state of relaxation, allowing your muscles to soften and your body's natural alignment to be gently re-established, reducing the strain you feel daily.
