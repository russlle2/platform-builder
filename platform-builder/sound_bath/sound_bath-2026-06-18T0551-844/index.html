Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Sound Bath specifically help my athletic performance and posture?
A Sound Bath helps by reducing chronic muscle tension and calming your nervous system, which are key to maintaining good posture and preventing injury during strenuous activity. It also enhances your body awareness, allowing you to identify and correct postural imbalances more effectively, supporting better form and efficiency in your movements.
Do I need to do anything special or have prior experience for a Sound Bath?
No prior experience is necessary. Simply arrive as you are, ready to relax. We provide everything you need for comfort. Your only task is to lie down, listen, and allow the sounds to guide your body into a state of deep rest, fostering an environment for physical release and mental clarity.
Will a Sound Bath help with existing physical discomfort or tightness from training?
Many active individuals find that the deep relaxation induced by a Sound Bath can significantly help alleviate physical discomfort and tightness. The resonant frequencies can encourage muscle relaxation and improve circulation, aiding in the release of tension that contributes to poor posture and aches from training.
What should I expect immediately after a Sound Bath session?
After a Sound Bath, you can expect to feel a profound sense of calm and physical lightness. Many report improved mental clarity and a greater connection to their body, feeling more aligned and less stressed. We recommend allowing yourself some quiet time afterward to integrate these feelings.
