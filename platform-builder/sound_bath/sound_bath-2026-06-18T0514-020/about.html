How can a Sound Bath specifically help with managing a life transition?

During significant life changes, your nervous system often goes into overdrive. A Sound Bath provides a dedicated, structured period of deep relaxation, helping to downregulate your stress response. This allows you to approach your transition with greater mental clarity and emotional resilience.

What should I expect during my first Sound Bath session?

You'll be invited to lie down comfortably on a mat, often with blankets and pillows for support. Dr. Morgan Ellis will then play various instruments, creating a layered soundscape. Your role is simply to relax and allow the sounds to wash over you. Most individuals report a profound sense of calm and a reduction in mental chatter.

Is the Sound Bath suitable if I'm feeling particularly fragile or overwhelmed right now?

Yes, our Sound Bath sessions are designed to be gentle and supportive, especially for those experiencing heightened emotional states. We prioritize creating a safe, non-judgmental space where you can simply be. It's a passive therapy, requiring no effort from you other than presence.

How does Sound Bath contribute to nervous system regulation?

The resonant frequencies produced during a Sound Bath stimulate the vagus nerve, which plays a crucial role in your body's relaxation response. This activation helps shift your autonomic nervous system from sympathetic (fight or flight) dominance to parasympathetic (rest and digest) dominance, promoting balance and reducing chronic stress.
