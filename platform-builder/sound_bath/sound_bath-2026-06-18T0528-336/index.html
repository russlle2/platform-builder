Frequently Asked Questions

How does a Sound Bath specifically help with chronic pain or injury recovery?
Our Sound Bath sessions leverage targeted frequencies and sustained vibrations to encourage deep relaxation and downregulate your nervous system. This helps reduce muscle tension, decrease inflammation, and can alter your perception of pain, creating an optimal environment for your body's innate healing mechanisms during recovery from chronic conditions or injuries.
Do I need to do anything specific during a Sound Bath session?
No, your primary role is simply to relax. You'll lie comfortably, often on a mat, and allow the sounds and vibrations to wash over you. There's no movement or active participation required. We encourage you to focus on your breath and observe any sensations, allowing your body to respond naturally to the therapeutic soundscape.
Is Sound Bath safe if I have specific physical limitations or sensitivities?
Yes, our Sound Bath practice is generally very gentle and adaptable. Please communicate any specific physical limitations, chronic conditions, or sensitivities you have before your session. can adjust the intensity and proximity of instruments to ensure your comfort and safety, providing a supportive experience tailored to your needs.
How many Sound Bath sessions are recommended for noticeable improvement?
The response to Sound Bath is unique to each individual and their specific recovery journey. Many clients report feeling a sense of calm and relief after just one session. For more sustained results and deeper somatic integration, a series of regular sessions is often beneficial. We can discuss a personalized plan during your initial consultation to best support your long-term well-being.
