Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly happens during a Sound Bath session?
During a Sound Bath, you’ll relax comfortably, often lying down, while various instruments like singing bowls and gongs are played. The sounds are not music in the traditional sense, but rather a sequence of gentle, resonant tones. You simply listen and allow the sounds to wash over you, encouraging a state of deep relaxation and emotional calm. There's nothing you need to do or understand; just be present.
Do I need any special preparation or experience for a Sound Bath?
Absolutely not. Our Sound Bath sessions are designed to be accessible for everyone, regardless of prior experience with wellness practices. Just wear comfortable clothing, and you might bring a water bottle. We provide everything else you need to relax. Your comfort and ease are our top priorities, and we'll guide you gently through the entire experience.
How can a Sound Bath specifically help with emotional burdens from the past?
The deep relaxation induced by a Sound Bath can create space for your mind to process emotions that may have been stored away. It's not about reliving difficult memories, but rather allowing your nervous system to gently release tension associated with them. This can lead to a feeling of emotional lightness and greater peace, helping you move forward with more vitality and clarity in your retirement.
Is a Sound Bath suitable for someone who feels overwhelmed or anxious?
Yes, many people find Sound Bath to be incredibly soothing for feelings of overwhelm or anxiety. The consistent, calming vibrations can help to regulate the nervous system, reducing mental chatter and promoting a sense of inner quiet. We create a very gentle and supportive atmosphere, ensuring you feel safe and respected throughout the session, which is crucial for managing anxious feelings.
