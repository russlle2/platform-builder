A Trauma-Informed Approach to Your Sound Bath Experience

We understand that everyone carries their own unique life story. Our Sound Bath practice is built on a trauma-informed foundation, meaning we prioritize your comfort and sense of security above all else. We create a predictable and supportive environment where you are always in control.
