Clinical flow & safety notes

Personalized intake

We begin with a short intake to understand intention, medical considerations, and any sensory preferences. Sessions are trauma-informed and paced for your nervous system.

Contraindications: If pregnant, have epilepsy, serious cardiac conditions, recent head/ear surgery, or are acutely intoxicated, please consult us first.

Session structure

Arrival & orientation → focused immersion → slow reorientation and integration. Duration varies by format (30–90 minutes).

Service types

1:1 sessions

One-on-one, bespoke sessions tuned to your needs. Instruments used may include monochord, tuning forks, and soft crystal bowls for precise modulation.

  • 30-minute focused reset
  • 60-minute deep session

Couples

Paired sessions that support co-regulation, shared breathwork, and attuned soundscapes. Ideal for relational recalibration and mutual relaxation.

Corporate & groups

Short restorative sessions and lunchtime offerings for teams, focused on stress reduction and improved focus. We bring a portable setup and a clinical approach.

Practical clarifications

Myth: Private means intensive loud sounds.

Truth: Private work allows fine-tuned dynamics. Instruments like tuning forks and monochord offer subtle modulation rather than volume-driven intensity.

We provide guidance on positioning, and you may request ear protection at any time.

Questions we often hear

Can we customize instruments?
Yes. Your facilitator will design a palette to suit therapeutic goals — from deeply resonant strings to precise tuning forks.
Are couples sessions private?
Yes. Couples sessions are conducted in a private, clinical setting with time for debriefing when needed.
What about corporate scheduling?
We offer flexible booking windows and can provide short written guidance for aftercare and quiet re-entry.
