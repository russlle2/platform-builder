Myths vs. Clinical Truths

Myth

Sound baths are just ambient music — passive background for sleep.

Clinical Truth

They are guided, intention-led sessions that combine timbre, frequency, and breathwork to modulate the autonomic nervous system and support measurable relaxation.

Myth

Anyone can improvise a sound bath with a few bowls and call it therapeutic.

Clinical Truth

Our facilitation follows a structured flow — assessment, intention-setting, layered soundscape, integration — delivered with clinical sensitivity and safety guidelines.

Three Pillars of Our Sessions

Somatic Anchoring

Breath-led centering, gentle movement, and body literacy to anchor the nervous system.

Precise Instrumentation

Layered textures (gongs, crystal bowls, tuning forks) arranged to support coherent entrainment and felt safety.

Integration & Aftercare

Guided return, journaling prompts, and a practical aftercare plan you can use at home.
