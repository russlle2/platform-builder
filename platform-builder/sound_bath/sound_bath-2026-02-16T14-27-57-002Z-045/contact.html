Myths vs. Clinical Truths

Myth: Sound baths are just background music.
Truth: Sessions are intentionally structured resonance practices with therapeutic intent—rooted in pacing, safety, and somatic awareness.
Myth: They are one-size-fits-all.
Truth: We adapt tone, volume, and duration based on sensitivity, medical considerations, and personal goals.
Myth: You must meditate perfectly.
Truth: There is no required outcome—rest, reflection, or a light nap are all expected and valid experiences.

Frequently asked — practical details

What should I bring?
Bring a yoga mat or blanket, eye-cover, and a small pillow. We provide extra blankets and ear-safe buffering pads. Dress in comfortable layers.
Contraindications / safety
If you are pregnant, have epilepsy, recent head injury, metal implants in the skull, or are undergoing certain acute medical treatments, please consult your healthcare provider and inform us. Loud, repetitive gongs can be adjusted or omitted. We require disclosure so we can adapt instruments and proximity.
Flow of a session
Sessions typically begin with a brief intake and orientation (5–10 min), preparing the space (5 min), followed by a guided sonic journey (30–60 min) that layers instruments such as crystal bowls, tuning forks, chimes, and monochord. We close with an integration period and optional post-session notes (10–15 min).
Instruments we may use
We draw from a curated palette: crystal bowls, soft chimes, tuning forks, monochord tones, and gentle gongs when appropriate. Each set is chosen for timbral compatibility and therapeutic intent.
Cancellation / rescheduling
For private sessions, please notify us 48 hours in advance to reschedule. Group event refunds and transfers follow the policy listed on the Pricing page.
