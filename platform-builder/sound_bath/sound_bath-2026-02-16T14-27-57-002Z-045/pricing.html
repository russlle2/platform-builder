Session composition

Group sessions typically layer instruments such as crystal bowls, gong, and light chimes to create broad-spectrum resonance. Private offerings may prioritize monochord and tuning fork work for precise somatic effect.

Flow: arrival & intake → 40–55 minute immersion → slow reorientation. "What to bring": mat, blanket, pillow, water, and an eye covering. Ear protection available upon request.

Contraindications note: If you have epilepsy, serious heart conditions, recent ear/head surgery, or are pregnant, please consult before booking.
