Frequently asked — clear answers

Below are concise, practical answers to common questions about our sound baths and private sessions.

What instruments are used and how are they chosen?
We select instruments to support a spectrum of vibrations: Himalayan crystal bowls, gong for low-frequency swells, and Koshi chimes for delicate high overtones. Instrument choices are intentional and vary by session theme.
How should I prepare? What to bring?
Bring a mat, a warm blanket, additional bolsters or pillows for comfort, and an eye covering if you prefer. Avoid heavy meals immediately beforehand. Hydrate lightly.
Who should avoid sound baths?
Contraindications include recent traumatic episodes, uncontrolled seizures, and some implants sensitive to vibration. If pregnant (first trimester especially), or if you have cardiovascular concerns, please consult your healthcare provider and contact us before booking: {{EMAIL}}.
What happens during a group session?
Flow: brief intake → guided settling with breathwork → layered sound journey using tuning forks, monophonic drones, and bowls → gentle reorientation and integration. Expect to be guided throughout; you are never left without cues.
Are sessions therapeutic or medical treatment?
Our work is somatic and wellness-oriented; it is not a substitute for medical or psychiatric treatment. We offer supportive practices that may complement care but do not diagnose or treat medical conditions.
Can I book a private or corporate offering?
Yes. We provide 1:1 therapeutic-style sessions, couples sound baths, and tailored corporate resilience workshops. See private-sessions.html for formats and corporate packages.
How do you ensure safety for neurodiverse participants?
We include pre-session intake, adjustable volume levels, and offer quiet corners. Facilitators are trained to provide predictable structure and clear cues for transitions.
