Logistics — what to bring

  • Blanket and 1–2 pillows for support
  • Eye-covering (sleep mask) if desired
  • Bottle of water and light socks

Session flow (typical)

  1. Arrival & intake (15 min): orientation and safety check.
  2. Grounding (10 min): breathwork and somatic scanning.
  3. Sound journey (35–45 min): layers of crystal bowls, chimes, and tuning forks that map to breath and body cues.
  4. Integration (10–15 min): gentle return, water, and journaling prompts.
