What to expect — the structure

Arrival & grounding

We open doors 20 minutes early. A brief orientation provides safety notes and an invitation to settle. Please arrive early to claim a spot and orient your body.

Immersive sound journey

45–55 minutes of layered sound using crystal singing bowls, gong, and wind chimes. The flow moves from present-moment breath work to deeper resonance and gentle resurfacing.

Integration

We close with soft spoken guidance and a 5–10 minute period for post-session integration. Tea and quiet conversation may follow when appropriate.

What to bring
  • Yoga mat, blanket, and a small pillow
  • Eye mask or light scarf
  • Water bottle and layers for comfort

Common myths — clarified

Myth: You must meditate perfectly to benefit.

Truth: Sound is non-verbal. Presence — not perfection — is the only requirement. The session is designed for anyone, including first-timers.

Myth: Sound baths are unpredictable and intense.

Truth: Our facilitation follows a clinical rhythm: intention, slow immersion, and gentle closure. You are always in control of your experience.

Contraindications: If you are pregnant, have epilepsy, serious cardiac conditions, recent head/ear surgery, or are under influence of substances, please consult your clinician or contact us before booking.

Clinical notes — flow and safety

Sessions follow a predictable, trauma-informed structure. The facilitator, {{FACILITATOR_NAME}}, will offer verbal cues before major shifts in sound intensity. If at any time you feel dysregulated you may sit up, open your eyes, or step out — no explanation required.

Instruments used tonight: crystal singing bowls, gong, and tuned wind chimes. Volume and duration are calibrated to maintain a supportive sensory field. Earplugs are available on request.

Frequently asked questions

How long is a typical group session?
About 75–90 minutes including arrival and integration. The sound immersion itself runs ~45–55 minutes.
Is this suitable for first-time participants?
Yes. Our invitations are gentle and accessible to newcomers. Quiet rest or soft movement are both fine.
Can I bring my child?
Most events are adult-focused. Check the specific event listing for family-friendly sessions.
