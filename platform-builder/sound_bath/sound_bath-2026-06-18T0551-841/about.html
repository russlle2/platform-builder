How can a Sound Bath specifically help with my posture if I'm dealing with burnout?

Burnout often manifests as physical tension, leading to poor posture. A Sound Bath helps by deeply relaxing your muscles and nervous system, making you more aware of where you hold tension. This increased body awareness allows you to consciously release chronic contractions, subtly guiding your body towards better alignment and reducing the strain that contributes to burnout.

I'm always on the go. How will a Sound Bath fit into my busy schedule and actually make a difference?

We understand your time is valuable. Our Sound Bath sessions offer a profound reset in a focused timeframe. Even one session can significantly reduce mental chatter and physical stiffness, providing a much-needed break from your daily pressures. Regular practice helps you build resilience against the physical and mental demands of your professional life, making your workdays feel more manageable.

Is a Sound Bath suitable for someone new to body awareness practices, especially if I feel really disconnected from my body due to stress?

Absolutely. A Sound Bath is incredibly accessible. You simply lie down and listen. The sounds gently guide your focus inward, without requiring any specific movements or prior experience. Many people find it's an ideal entry point for reconnecting with their body's sensations, helping to bridge that gap of disconnection caused by prolonged stress and burnout.

What should I expect during a Sound Bath session, and how does it relate to improving my body awareness?

During a Sound Bath, you'll lie comfortably while Dr. Morgan Ellis plays various resonant instruments. The goal is deep relaxation. As your mind settles, your attention naturally shifts to your physical body. You might notice subtle sensations, areas of tension, or moments of release. This heightened perception is key to improving your overall body awareness, allowing you to make more conscious choices about your posture and movement outside of the session.
