Frequently Asked Questions

How can Sound Bath help with my academic anxiety?
Sound Bath sessions provide a dedicated space for your mind to disengage from anxious thoughts. The structured soundscape helps to regulate your nervous system, promoting a state of calm that can reduce the intensity of academic anxiety and improve your ability to cope with pressure.
Will Sound Bath improve my posture, especially with all the studying I do?
While Sound Bath isn't a direct postural treatment, it significantly enhances your body awareness. By fostering a deeper connection to your physical sensations, you become more attuned to habitual tension and misalignments, which can empower you to consciously adjust and improve your sitting and standing posture during long study hours.
Is Sound Bath evidence-based, or is it just a 'feel-good' session?
Our approach to Sound Bath is grounded in evidence. Research suggests sound and vibration can influence brainwave states and reduce physiological markers of stress. We focus on the measurable benefits of deep relaxation and enhanced body awareness, supporting your mental and physical health with clinical compassion.
I'm always pressed for time. How long is a Sound Bath session?
We understand your academic schedule is demanding. Our sessions are designed to be efficient yet effective, typically lasting between 45-60 minutes. This allows you to integrate a beneficial relaxation practice into your routine without sacrificing valuable study or rest time.
