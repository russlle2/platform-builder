Our Story

Founded as a small circle of neighbors and artists, {{BUSINESS_NAME}} grew from a simple impulse: to make a room that sings people back into themselves. We combine a premium, sensory approach with a slow, ritual cadence — crafted for modern life that craves soft, intentional pauses.

Facilitated by {{FACILITATOR_NAME}} — an alchemist of tone and atmosphere — sessions are limited to preserve spaciousness and depth.

Sensory Details

  • Instruments: crystal bowls, Tibetan gong, a map of tuning forks and gentle chimes.
  • Ambience: low amber lighting, natural fibers, curated aroma in non-allergenic forms.
  • Capacity: boutique — we keep the circle intimate to ensure resonance.

The Flow

  1. Arrive & Settle: soft tea, lay your mat, gentle orientation (10–15 min).
  2. Opening Intention: guided breathwork to arrive (5–10 min).
  3. Resonant Journey: layered instruments — crystal bowls, tuned monochord, chimes and light gong — this is the main immersion (40–50 min).
  4. Transit: a soft return through silence and anointing with warm tea (10 min).

What to Bring

  • a yoga mat or blanket;
  • an eye pillow or scarf;
  • comfortable layers; we recommend socks and a light sweater;
  • an open heart and headphones-free phone (silenced).

Contraindications

Sound baths are deeply relaxing but not suitable for everyone. If you are pregnant, have epilepsy, recent head injury, implanted medical devices, severe PTSD, or are currently under seizure risk, please consult your healthcare provider before attending. Email {{EMAIL}} with questions.
