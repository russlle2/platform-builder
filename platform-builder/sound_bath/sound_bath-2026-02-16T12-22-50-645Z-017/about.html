Our origin story

What began as a weekend experiment with a single singing bowl evolved into a practice built for the senses. {{FACILITATOR_NAME}} trained in breath-based facilitation and sound design, stitching ritual, live tone, and gentle guidance into an accessible evening for newcomers and seasoned listeners alike.

Instruments & texture

We sculpt a field of sound using a thoughtful palette: crystal singing bowls, Himalayan bowls, hand chimes and soft tuned percussion. Each session is arranged deliberately to emphasize warmth and clarity rather than volume.

How a session flows

Think of a session like entering a harbor: slowing the approach, settling into a berth, and then being cradled while the tide of sound moves through you. The typical flow:

  • Arrival & grounding (10–15 min): gentle centering and intention setting
  • Guided breath and focused listening (5–10 min)
  • Main sound journey (35–45 min): layered tones, harmonics, and soft swells
  • Soft return & integration (10–15 min): silence, tea, and sharing as desired
