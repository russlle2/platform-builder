Why reach out?

We blend curated instruments with hospitality — think warm textiles, gentle lighting, and layers of harmonic sound. Whether you’re curious about membership or need a tailored private session, we can craft an offering that fits your life.

The flow — what to expect

1
Arrival & settling. You’ll be welcomed into the space, offered a blanket and bolster. A brief check-in ensures we honor any sensitivities.
2
Guided transition. Breath and gentle guidance lead you into the sound bath — soft chime, tuning fork harmonics, and a foundation of crystal bowls or monochord.
3
Sound immersion. A 35–50 minute unfurling of textures (gongs, crystal bowls, chimes, kalimba) followed by a quiet integration and tea.
4
Closing & takeaways. We re-enter movement slowly, share aftercare suggestions, and open a gentle space for reflection.

What to bring

  • Comfortable layers, a yoga mat or cushion if you prefer your own, an eye pillow or small scarf.
  • Water bottle and curiosity — headphones not needed.

Contraindications & safety

Sound baths are gentle, but please disclose if you are pregnant, have epilepsy, severe tinnitus, or any recent surgeries/traumas. We cannot, in good conscience, recommend intense gong work for those with certain cardiovascular or vestibular conditions — reach out and we will advise alternatives.
