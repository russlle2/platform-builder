Questions about the experience

Practical answers to help you arrive prepared for the sound field. If something feels missing, reach out at {{EMAIL}}.
What should I bring?
Items that help comfort and intention.
  • Comfortable layers and a light blanket — sessions can feel cool while you rest.
  • A yoga mat or a thick foldable blanket and a pillow for head support.
  • Water bottle and an optional eye mask for deeper rest.
  • Your membership card or booking confirmation.
Are there contraindications?
Safety and suitability information.

Sound baths are gentle, but certain situations require caution. Please consult with your physician before attending if you:

  • Are pregnant (we offer modified positions and separate guidance — tell us during booking)
  • Have an implantable medical device such as a pacemaker
  • Suffer from epilepsy or severe vestibular disorders
  • Are currently under heavy stimulant medication that affects sensory processing

If in doubt, get in touch — we can recommend a private session or modified placement.

What is the session flow?
A brief map of the evening.
Arrival (10–15m)
Welcome, orientation, and optional tea.
Grounding (10m)
Breathwork and intention setting.
Sound Journey (30–45m)
Layered tones — gong swells, monochord-like drones, chimes — designed to cradle the nervous system.
Return (10–15m)
Gradual reintroduction to ambient sound and soft sharing.
What instruments do you use?
We vary textures to fit the theme of each evening.

Examples of tools you might hear: large gong swells, tubular hand chimes, tuning forks for focused harmonic points, and a bowed monochord for a steady low drone.

Booking, cancellations & accessibility
Policies at a glance.

Memberships enjoy flexible cancellation windows; for drop-in seats please cancel at least 24 hours in advance for a refund. The venue is wheelchair-accessible; email {{EMAIL}} with any access needs and we'll assist.

Can I lie down or sit?
Comfort is the priority.

Both are fine — many participants lie on mats for deeper rest, while others prefer upright seats. We provide mats, blankets, and bolsters upon request.

Why a membership?
The practice of return.

Membership invites rhythm: monthly seats, lower per-visit cost, and periodic member-only meditations. It’s ideal if you want to make sound bathing part of your self-care cadence.
