What to Expect — the Flow

Each gathering moves through an intentional arc:

  1. Arrival & grounding (10–15 min): settle in, set an intention.
  2. Guided breath & light movement (5–10 min) to soften tension.
  3. Sound immersion (35–45 min): sculpted layers of sound to support deep relaxation.
  4. Return & integration (10 min): gentle reorientation and tea.

Instruments featured tonight: crystal singing bowls, bowed gong, Himalayan chimes, and breath-led toning.

What to Bring & Practicals

  • Yoga mat or blanket, an extra blanket to stay warm.
  • Small pillow for neck support; eye mask if desired.
  • Water bottle; arrive 10–15 min early to settle in.

Contraindications: If you are pregnant, have a pacemaker or major hearing implants, recent head injury, or severe epilepsy, please consult us before attending. Sound experiences can be intense — email {{EMAIL}} or call {{PHONE}} for a pre-screen conversation.
