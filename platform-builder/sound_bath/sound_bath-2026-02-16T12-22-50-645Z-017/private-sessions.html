Couples & Shared Space

Partnered sessions to deepen presence. Instruments may include a monochord drone, koshi chimes, and shared breathwork. Great for anniversaries, transitions, or recalibration together.

Corporate & Small Teams

Design a 30–45 minute sound pause for teams to ease cognitive load and restore focus. Portable setup includes handpan tones, chimes, and soft guided grounding.

What to Bring

  • Comfortable clothing; a blanket if you like.
  • For couples: any shared objects or an intention note.
  • For corporate events: a quiet room and enough floor seating or chairs.

Contraindications: people with certain neurological conditions, metal implants close to the ear, or recent cranial surgery should consult a clinician prior to rhythmic or loud resonances. Contact {{EMAIL}} for a screening call.
