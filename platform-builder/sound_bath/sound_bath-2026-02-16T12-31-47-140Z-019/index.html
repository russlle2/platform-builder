A slow arrival

We craft evenings that feel like a soft exhale — warm light, layered tones, slow breath. Each session is a carefully framed arc: arrival, attunement, immersion, and gentle return.

Why we gather

Sound reshapes attention. In the room we lay down an intentional field — for rest, for recalibration, for community held in quiet.

Our promise

Premium sensory design: natural textiles, cushioned lounges, warm herbal teas and thoughtfully tuned instruments that meet you with clarity.

The flow of an evening

  1. Arrival & grounding — 20 minutes: settle in, tea, brief orientation.
  2. Intention & breath — 10 minutes: soft prompts to anchor presence.
  3. Immersion — 45 minutes: layered instruments guide resonance from subtle to full-bodied.
  4. Integration — 15 minutes: gentle return with whispering tones, rest, and sharing if desired.

What to bring

  • Comfortable clothing and a light blanket
  • Yoga mat or meditation cushion (limited mats on-site)
  • Water bottle — we provide herbal tea

Health & contraindications

Sound bathing is generally gentle. Please consult before attending if you have severe epilepsy, recent head injury, are in the first trimester of pregnancy, or have medical implants that may be affected by vibration. Contact us at {{EMAIL}} for specifics.
