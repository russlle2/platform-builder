What to bring

  • A yoga mat or blanket for comfortable reclining
  • A pillow, eye-covering, and a bottle of water
  • Layered clothing — the room settles into deep calm

Session flow — what to expect

Each sound bath is a gentle arc: arrival, settling, immersion, and gentle return.

  1. Arrival & grounding: soft welcome, intention setting (5–10 min)
  2. Sonic immersion: live playing with tuned instruments (40–55 min)
  3. Transition & journaling: quiet sound tapering, space to rest (10–15 min)
  4. Closing: brief sharing or reflection, practical guidance for re-entry

Contraindications & safety

Sound baths are restorative for many, but please note:

  • If you are pregnant, have cardiovascular implants, epilepsy, or recent head/ear surgery, consult your physician and inform the facilitator before booking.
  • Live amplification and low-frequency gongs can be intense — reach out and we will advise on suitable formats.
  • Not a substitute for medical care; this is a complementary practice.
