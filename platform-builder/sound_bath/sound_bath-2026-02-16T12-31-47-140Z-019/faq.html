What should I bring to a group sound bath?

Bring a yoga mat or thick cushion, a blanket to stay warm, and an eye pillow for darkness. A small bolster or folded towel can support your knees. Keep your phone on silent — this is a device-free invitation.

Is it safe for everyone?

Most people can participate. Contraindications: epilepsy, recent traumatic brain injury, uncontrolled bipolar disorder, or implanted medical devices such as pacemakers — please consult your clinician. Pregnant attendees should obtain medical clearance. If you have specific concerns, contact us at {{EMAIL}} before booking.

What instruments might I hear?

We vary the palette: crystal singing bowls, a warm gong wash, tuning forks for precise harmonic notes, and handheld chimes for delicate punctuations. Instruments are selected to support the arc of the session — expect gentle variety, never noise.

How long is a session and what is the flow?

Typical group sessions last 75–90 minutes. Flow: arrival & brief guidance, a slow opening with tuning tones, 40–60 minutes of sustained immersion in layered instruments, gentle re-emergence with grounding tones, and time to rest with tea. Private sessions vary.

Can I fall asleep?

Yes — and that can be a beautiful part of the process. We create a safe container and will use slow re-entry cues to help you return to wakefulness at the session close.

What is your cancellation policy?

We appreciate 48 hours notice for cancellations. Late cancellations may forfeit the ticket unless we can offer your place to someone on the waitlist.

Short guide

Clothing: Layers, comfortable and non-restrictive.
