Why we gather

We build a warm room — a place where sound moves the body and softens the mind. Each gathering is crafted to be sensory and precise: slow pacing, attentive space, and instruments tuned to the room. The evening is ceremonial without formality; think of it as a quiet, layered tide of tone.

What to expect — the flow

Arrival & settling (15 min)
Mats, cushions, and blankets arranged. Low light. Gentle orientation.
Opening breath & intention (10 min)
A guided breath and brief invitation to rest the attention.
Resonant soundscape (45–50 min)
Layered bowls and gong gradually shift the tonal field; soft chimes appear like light.
Return & integration (10–15 min)
Slow reorientation, a cup of tea, and space for notes.

Instruments: crystal singing bowls, gong, alpine chimes, tingsha bells — balanced to linger, not overwhelm.

Contraindications

If you are pregnant, have epilepsy, an implanted medical device, or a history of severe mental health episodes, please consult the facilitator or your physician before attending. Sound is supportive but not a substitute for medical care.
