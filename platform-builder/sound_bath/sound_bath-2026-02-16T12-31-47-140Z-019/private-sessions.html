Tools & tone

For private work the palette is selected to mirror the intention. Instruments might include the monochord for warm harmonic sustain, calibrated tuning forks for precise energetic alignment, koshi chimes for delicate articulation, and a handpan for round, grounding overtones.

What to bring

  • A cushion and a blanket — private sessions are often more restorative when warm.
  • Comfortable clothing and any support props you prefer.
  • Arrive hydrated; we’ll provide tea afterward when possible.

Contraindications & safety

If you use a pacemaker or other implanted device, are pregnant, or have epilepsy, please contact us before booking. For mental health crises, please consult a licensed clinician — sound sessions complement but do not replace medical care.

How we proceed — the private flow

A brief intake, a short grounding breathwork, the core sound period (tailored length), then a quiet re-entry. Time is kept generous to allow integration and questions.
