Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Sound Bath and how can it help with sleep for seniors?
A Sound Bath involves listening to gentle, sustained sounds from instruments like singing bowls and gongs. For seniors, these specific sound frequencies can help quiet an overactive mind, reduce tension, and promote a relaxed state, making it easier for you to fall asleep and experience more continuous, restful sleep.
Is a Sound Bath safe and appropriate for someone aged 60+?
Yes, our Sound Bath sessions are designed to be very gentle and are particularly suitable for seniors. We prioritize a calm, non-invasive environment, ensuring your comfort and safety. There's no physical exertion required, just quiet listening, making it a safe option for promoting relaxation and better sleep at any age.
What should I expect to feel during and after a Sound Bath session?
During a session, you might feel a deep sense of calm, warmth, or a pleasant drifting sensation. Some individuals even doze off lightly. Afterward, many clients report feeling refreshed, less stressed, and noticeably more relaxed, which often translates to improved sleep quality and a greater sense of overall well-being in the days that follow.
Do I need any special equipment or preparation for a Sound Bath?
No special equipment is needed. We recommend wearing comfortable clothing so you can fully relax. You'll lie down on a comfortable mat, and we provide blankets and pillows to ensure you are cozy. Your only preparation is to arrive with an open mind, ready to experience gentle sound.
