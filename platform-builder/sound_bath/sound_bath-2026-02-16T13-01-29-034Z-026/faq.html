Questions answered

Practical notes for a calm arrival. Below: what to bring, how a session flows, and safety guidance.

What should I bring?
Come in comfortable clothing. We recommend: a yoga mat or blanket, a small cushion or neck pillow, and a cozy blanket to stay warm during stillness. Optional: eye mask, a water bottle, and a journal for notes afterwards.
How long is a session and what is the flow?
Sessions typically run 75–90 minutes. The flow:
  1. Arrival, settling, and brief intention-setting.
  2. Short breath or grounding exercise to anchor the nervous system.
  3. Main sound journey: layered textures, gradual build and release (instruments tuned for comfort).
  4. Soft reorientation and a moment for integration with tea or quiet reflection.
Are sound baths safe? Contraindications?
For most people this is gentle, non-invasive work. However, please take special care if any of the following apply: epilepsy, implanted medical devices (e.g., pacemaker), recent surgery, pregnancy (first trimester caution), severe psychiatric diagnoses, or current substance intoxication. If you have inner ear conditions or are on medications that affect blood pressure or sensory processing, consult your healthcare provider. When in doubt, email {{EMAIL}} before booking.
Do I need experience?
No. We welcome first-timers and regulars alike. There is no right way to receive sound. You may sit, lie down, or rest with your eyes open or closed.
What instruments are used?
Our textures are curated for each gathering. Instruments commonly woven together include: tuning forks for precise tones, hand chimes for bright punctuation, and a monochord for long harmonic drones — each chosen to create a layered, warm field of sound.
How do you accommodate sensory needs or mobility differences?
We keep accessibility in mind: chairs are available, floor cushioning can be adjusted, and volume is moderated. Please note any needs when you book or email {{EMAIL}} ahead of time.
Refunds, cancellations, and late arrival policy
Reservations can be rescheduled up to 72 hours prior for a full transfer. Within 72 hours we cannot guarantee transfers. Late arrivals are accommodated at the facilitator's discretion to preserve the field for others.
Short disclaimer

This practice is supportive and complementary. It is not a substitute for medical or psychiatric care. If you have serious health concerns, please consult a licensed professional before attending.
