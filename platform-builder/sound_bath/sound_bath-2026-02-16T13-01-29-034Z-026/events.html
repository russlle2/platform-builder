Myths and the quiet truths

Myth

A sound bath is background noise for meditation.

Truth

It is a guided sensory architecture: intentional sound design that supports embodied rest and nervous system regulation.

Myth

You must be quiet; any movement is wrong.

Truth

Movement, tears, and vocal release are welcome; the space holds them without judgement.

The pillars of our practice

Attunement

We start with breath and alignment — a gentle settling into the body.

Resonance

Curated tones and overtones from crystal bowls, gong, and chimes to move through layers of attention.

Return

A guided re-entry with tea and integrative prompts to carry the session beyond the room.

Practicals — what to bring & cautions

What to bring?

A yoga mat or blanket, an extra pillow, eye cover (optional), water, and warm layers. We provide bolsters and blankets, but feel free to bring your favorites.

Session flow — how it unfolds

Welcome & intention (10m) → Guided breath + slow soundscape (40–50m) → Gentle return + tea and reflection (15m). Total time ~75 minutes.

Contraindications

Not recommended during the first trimester of pregnancy, for those with severe cardiovascular conditions, recent major surgery, or uncontrolled epilepsy. If you have implants or are sensitive to strong vibrations, consult your physician and email us at {{EMAIL}}.

Reserve your space in the next circle

Small circles — spacious resonance. We keep gatherings intimate to preserve depth.
