Three Pillars of the Series

Sense

Intentional acoustics: crystal bowls, monochord and chimes unfold a soft architecture of sound.

Rest

Slow transitions, guided breath and silence to integrate the waves between tones.

Return

Light reintegration: journaling prompts, gentle tea and time to ground after the session.

Practical Questions

What to bring?

Yoga mat or blanket, a pillow for your head, water, and an open layer. We provide bolsters and blankets if needed.

Contraindications

If you are pregnant, have uncontrolled epilepsy, recent head injury, or serious cardiac conditions, please consult your physician and inform the facilitator before attending.

Session Flow

Arrival & settling (10m) — Guided breath & intention (10m) — Sound journey (40–50m) — Silent integration & return (10–15m) — Tea and soft close.

Instruments you might hear

Crystal singing bowls, koshi chimes, tuning forks and a suspended gong — textures that shape different edges of silence.
