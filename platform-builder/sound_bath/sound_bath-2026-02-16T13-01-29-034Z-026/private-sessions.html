Private sessions — intimate and curated

Work directly with {{FACILITATOR_NAME}} to craft a session aimed at rest, nervous system regulation, or focused team well‑being.

Instruments and textures vary to suit the frame: tuning forks, monochord, and selected crystal bowls for clarity and depth.

Core intentions

Presence

A quiet, one-on-one sound mapping to settle chronic tension, support sleep, or reduce anxiety.

Relationship

Couples sessions use shared resonance to deepen attunement and emotional regulation.

Collective care

Corporate wellness: a concise group ritual for teams to reset before or after intensive cycles.

Session types

1:1 — Deep Listening

60–75 minutes. Pre-session intake; tailored tone palette using tuning forks and selected crystal bowls; integration chat and after‑care guide.

Couples — Shared Resonance

75 minutes. Guided relational prompts, mirrored sound work, and slow partner exercises. Designed to cultivate mutual regulation.

Corporate — Team Reset

30–45 minutes for groups up to 20. Short, focused sound protocol using monochord and light percussion to leave the team quiet and present.

Follow-up

A recommended 30-minute check-in within 2–4 weeks for longer-term support and refinement of home practices.

Practical notes & safety

What to bring

Whatever makes you comfortable: mat, cozy layers, eye-cover. We provide blankets, water, and bolsters when possible.

Contraindications

Please disclose pregnancy status, implanted medical devices, recent surgeries, or seizure disorders before booking. If in doubt, clear with your provider and email {{EMAIL}} for an adapted session.

Flow overview

Greeting & intake (10m) → Instrumental resonance (35–50m) → Re-orientation + integration (10–15m). Times are adaptable.

FAQ

Do I need experience?

No. Sessions are crafted for where you are, whether curious or familiar with sound work.

Can I bring a partner who is nervous about sound?

Yes. Conversations and pacing are adapted; we can keep dynamics gentle with minimal vibration if preferred.

How long until I notice change?

Many report immediate relaxation; deeper nervous system shifts can appear across days—consistency helps.
