Session flow • what to expect

A typical group sound bath moves through a considered rhythm: gentle arrival and check-in (10–15 min), guided settling and body awareness (10 min), immersive sound journey with layered instruments (35–45 min), and a soft return with grounding and optional sharing (10–15 min).

What to bring

Mat or yoga mat, warm blanket, pillow for neck support, eye mask if you like, a water bottle, and any small comforts to make resting easy.

Contraindications & safety

High-sensitivity to sound, recent surgeries, pacemakers, or seizure history may need a conversation first. Pregnant participants: please consult your care provider. This practice is complementary and not a substitute for medical care.

How long are sessions?

Public gatherings are typically 75–90 minutes. Private 1:1 sessions can range 60–90 minutes depending on intention.

Are instruments loud?

Volume is moderated for comfort. Instruments include crystal bowls, a gong for texture, chimes, and tuning forks; each is introduced with intention and care.
