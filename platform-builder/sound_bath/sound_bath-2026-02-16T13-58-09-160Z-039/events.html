How a typical session unfolds

Arrival & grounding

We begin with a short check-in and gentle breathing to orient the group. This primes the body and nervous system for the sound field.

Sound immersion (the flow)

A 40–60 minute sound journey using a curated mix of instruments: crystal bowls, Tibetan bowls, a soothing gong, and handheld chimes. Layers build and recede — held by steady pacing to allow deep rest.

Safety notes & contraindications

Sound baths are restorative, but not medical treatment. Please avoid if you have a pacemaker or implanted medical device affected by vibration, active ear infection, recent surgery, or are pregnant without clearance from your provider. If you have epilepsy or other seizure disorders, consult your clinician first. When in doubt, email {{EMAIL}}.
