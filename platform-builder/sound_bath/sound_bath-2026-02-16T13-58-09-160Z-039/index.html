Is a sound bath right for you?

We blend gongs, chimes, and focused harmonic bowls to create a layered spectrum. Our sessions are sensory-forward and intentionally slow — ideal if you want to decompress from stress, improve sleep architecture, or deepen meditation practice.

Who benefits

  • Stressed professionals seeking grounding
  • People curious about somatic reset
  • Those craving a premium, guided rest experience

Contraindications

Please consult your physician if you have: a history of seizures, implanted medical devices (e.g., pacemaker), uncontrolled psychiatric symptoms, or are pregnant and have concerns. Sounds are powerful — reach out at {{EMAIL}} with any medical questions before booking.

Session flow — what to expect

  1. Arrival & grounding: Soft lighting, tea, and a brief orientation to posture and props.
  2. Set intention: Short guided breathwork and optional journaling.
  3. Main sound journey: A 45–60 minute tapestry of instruments — crystal bowls, monochord, and tuned chimes create evolving overtones.
  4. Integration: Slow rewarming, light movement, and space to share or reflect.

Each event is tailored; instrument selection and pacing vary to support different energetic needs.

Small practices to extend the effects

Evening ritual

Dim lights for 30 minutes before bed; listen to low-volume humming or an app with binaural hums to prolong parasympathetic activation.

Morning check-in

Spend 3 minutes on belly breathing to anchor your nervous system each morning after the event.

Weekly embodiment

Wear comfortable clothes and practice 10 minutes of mindful movement to integrate somatic changes.
