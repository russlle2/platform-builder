Session flow — how we structure a premium sound bath

  1. Welcoming & orientation — 10–15 minutes to set intention and arrange comfort supports (bolsters, blankets).
  2. Grounding sequence — breathwork and gentle movement to settle the nervous system.
  3. Main sound journey — 40–60 minutes of layered instruments; dynamic arc from gentle textures to richer harmonics and back down.
  4. Integration — soft silence and a gentle return; optional tea and short reflection.

What to bring

  • Layered clothing and a light blanket
  • A yoga mat or thick carpet is helpful (we provide bolsters)
  • Water bottle; arrive hydrated

Contraindications & safety

Sound baths are generally low-risk, but please avoid attending if you are currently pregnant in your first trimester, have an active seizure disorder, recent head trauma, or rely on medical devices sensitive to strong vibrations. Contact us before booking for any pacemaker, implanted device, or psychiatric conditions. This information is not medical advice; consult your provider.

Micro practices to extend session benefits

Small habits amplify the relaxation you find in a session. Try these short practices between visits:

  • 2 minutes of slow, nasal breathing on waking.
  • Evening ritual: dim lights + 5 minutes of quiet listening.
  • Weekly intention writing — 3 lines about what you want to feel.
