What to bring

  • Comfortable layers and a cosy blanket
  • Pillow or neck support
  • Reusable water bottle and any personal props

Contraindications

If you are pregnant, have severe cardiovascular conditions, uncontrolled seizures, or are under acute psychiatric care, please discuss with your provider and notify us before booking. Sound baths are deeply relaxing but not a replacement for medical treatment.

Typical session flow

  • Arrival & grounding circle (10–15 min)
  • Intentions & gentle breathwork (10 min)
  • Sound journey with instruments (45–60 min) — tuned crystal bowls, gong, chimes and a monochord in rotation
  • Soft re-entry, tea, and integration (15–20 min)
