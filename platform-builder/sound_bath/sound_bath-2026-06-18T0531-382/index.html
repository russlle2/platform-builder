Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Sound Bath and how will it help my postpartum body?
A Sound Bath is a deeply relaxing experience where you lie down and are immersed in gentle, resonant sounds from various instruments. For new mothers, it helps by encouraging muscle relaxation, reducing physical tension often found in the neck, shoulders, and back, and calming your nervous system to support your body's natural recovery process.
Do I need any special equipment or prior experience for a Sound Bath?
Absolutely not! You don't need any prior wellness experience. Just wear comfortable clothes, and we'll provide everything else you need to be cozy, like mats, blankets, and pillows. Your only job is to lie back and let the sounds wash over you.
Is it safe to attend a Sound Bath while I'm still recovering postpartum?
Yes, our Sound Baths are designed to be very gentle and safe for postpartum bodies. We create a supportive environment where you can adjust your position as needed for comfort. If you have any specific concerns, we recommend checking with your healthcare provider, but generally, it's a wonderful way to support your physical and mental recovery.
How long are the Sound Bath sessions, and what should I expect during one?
Our sessions typically last about 60 minutes. You'll enter a calm space, find a comfortable spot to lie down, and then simply relax as gentle, live sounds fill the room. Many people feel a sense of deep relaxation, some drift into a light sleep, and others notice a release of physical tension. You can expect to leave feeling refreshed and more centered.
