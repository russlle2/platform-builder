Frequently Asked Questions

How does a Sound Bath specifically help my immune system?
A Sound Bath promotes deep relaxation, which is known to reduce stress hormones. When your body is less stressed, your immune system is better able to function optimally, helping you maintain your health and ward off common ailments. It’s about creating an internal environment where your body can thrive.
Will a Sound Bath help me with memory or focus?
While not a medical treatment, many of our clients find that the focused attention required during a Sound Bath helps to quiet mental chatter, leading to improved concentration and mental clarity afterward. It's like giving your mind a gentle workout, enhancing your ability to focus and think clearly.
Do I need to do anything special during a Sound Bath session?
Not at all! Your only task is to relax comfortably. You can lie down or sit, whatever feels best. Simply allow the sounds to wash over you. There's no right or wrong way to experience it; just be open to the gentle vibrations and their calming effects on your body and mind.
Is a Sound Bath suitable for someone who has never tried it before?
Absolutely! Our Sound Bath sessions are designed to be accessible and beneficial for everyone, regardless of prior experience. creates a warm, nurturing environment, guiding you gently through the experience. You'll find it to be a very peaceful and restorative practice.
