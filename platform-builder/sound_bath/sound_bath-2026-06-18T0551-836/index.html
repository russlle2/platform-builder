Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Sound Bath specifically help with my postural health during recovery?
Sound Bath sessions create a deep state of relaxation, which can reduce muscle guarding and tension often associated with chronic illness or injury. This allows your body to gently release holding patterns, promoting a more natural alignment and enhancing your awareness of your posture without requiring strenuous physical effort.
Is a Sound Bath suitable if I have limited mobility or chronic pain?
Absolutely. Sound Bath is a passive experience where you simply lie down or sit comfortably. You don't need to move or exert yourself. The gentle vibrations and frequencies work on a subtle level, making it a highly accessible and beneficial practice for those with limited mobility or managing chronic pain, without causing additional stress.
What should I expect to feel during and after a Sound Bath session?
During the session, you might feel deep relaxation, a sense of calm, or subtle vibrations throughout your body. Afterward, many individuals report feeling refreshed, grounded, and more connected to their physical self. You may notice an increased awareness of your body's alignment and a reduction in lingering tension, contributing to improved postural control.
How often should I attend Sound Bath sessions to support my recovery?
The frequency depends on your individual recovery goals and current condition. Many people find regular sessions, perhaps weekly or bi-weekly, to be most effective in reinforcing positive postural habits and maintaining a heightened sense of body awareness. We can discuss a personalized recommendation during your initial consultation.
