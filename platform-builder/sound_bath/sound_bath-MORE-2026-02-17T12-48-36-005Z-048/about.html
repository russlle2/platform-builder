Health & safety

We welcome most people but some conditions need extra care. Please consult with your provider or our team before reserving if any of the following apply:

  • Epilepsy or seizure disorders.
  • Pregnancy in the first trimester or any pregnancy with complications — check with your clinician.
  • Implanted medical devices like pacemakers.
  • Active severe psychiatric episodes or unmanaged psychosis.
