Home Gatherings Reserve Connect

How long is a typical session?

Most group gatherings are around 60 minutes. Private sessions vary — usually 50 to 75 minutes depending on the format.

What should I bring?

A yoga mat or blanket, a pillow for support, water, and anything you need to feel comfortable. We provide mats on request where noted.

Can anyone attend?

Most people can join. If you have seizures, pacemakers, or are in an unstable psychiatric state please consult with us or your provider first. Pregnant participants should check with their clinician for comfort and timing.

How do I cancel or change a reservation?

Cancellations up to 48 hours before an event receive a full credit. After that, credits are discretionary and we may offer a transfer to another date when possible.
