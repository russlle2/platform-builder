How a session unfolds

Arrival & attuning

Settle into a supported posture. We orient you to the sound field and safety markers for comfort.

Layers of resonance progress from sustaining tones to textured envelopes, tailored to the chosen intensity.

Slow re-entry

Contraindications:

People with certain medical implants (e.g., pacemakers), recent head/ear surgery, or uncontrolled seizures should consult their provider before attending. If you are pregnant, have acute psychiatric conditions, or are managing severe cardiovascular issues, please contact us before booking.

Common concerns

Do I need prior experience with meditation?

No. Sessions are led with clear directions; you can rest, listen, or nap. We create a safety-first environment for all experience levels.

What should I bring?

Comfortable clothing, a small blanket, and a pillow are recommended. We provide mats and supports on request.

How long is a typical gathering?

Most group sessions last 60–75 minutes. Private sessions vary by format; see pricing for details.

Ready to experiment with sound?

Reserve a spot for a group session or request a private intake. We’ll help you pick the right intensity.
