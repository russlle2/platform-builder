Health & contraindications — please read

Sound sessions are generally gentle but are not suitable for everyone. If you have any of the following, please consult your healthcare provider and reach out before booking:

  • Active pregnancy (consultation required for positioning)
  • Recent surgery or severe musculoskeletal injuries
  • Uncontrolled epilepsy or photosensitive conditions
  • Severe psychiatric conditions that might be triggered by altered states

If in doubt, use the contact form or call to clarify — we will help make a safe plan or suggest alternatives.
