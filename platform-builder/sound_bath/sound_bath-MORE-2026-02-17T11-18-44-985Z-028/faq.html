Frequently asked questions

What should I expect at a group meeting?
Arrive 10–15 minutes early for a brief orientation, settle into a supported posture, then follow the facilitator's clear cues. Sessions end with a guided return so you leave oriented and steady.
How does membership work?
Membership grants priority booking, a modest discount on private sessions, and access to a recorded library. Billing is monthly; members may pause enrollment with notice.
Can I attend if I'm pregnant or have medical concerns?
If you are pregnant, have a pacemaker, are undergoing active psychiatric care, or have a cardio condition, please consult with your clinician before attending. Share health details confidentially on booking so facilitators can adapt the experience when possible.
Are recordings provided?
Members have access to short, practical recordings focused on settling and breath awareness. They are intended for daily practice, not as medical treatment.
What is your refund or transfer policy?
Tickets can be transferred to another date up to 48 hours before the session. Memberships can be paused or canceled according to the terms displayed on the Memberships page.
Contraindications (brief)
Sound work is not a substitute for medical care. Avoid attendance during active psychiatric crisis, untreated severe cardiovascular issues, or when medical devices may be affected. When in doubt, seek medical approval and notify us ahead of arrival.
