Common questions

What should I bring?

Comfortable layers, a mat or thick blanket, and anything you use for personal comfort. We provide bolsters and blankets as needed.

Are sessions safe for everyone?

Most people find sound gatherings supportive. However, people with a history of epilepsy, recent head injury, severe psychiatric conditions, or those who are pregnant should consult a physician before attending. If you have implants or sensitivity to sustained vibrations, check with a clinician and reach out to us so we can advise.

Do members get discounts?

Yes — membership includes reduced pricing for private sessions and early access to limited events.
