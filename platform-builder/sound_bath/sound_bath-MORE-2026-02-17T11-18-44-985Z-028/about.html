Our approach

Sessions are arranged in predictable segments: arrival and settling, tone layers with clear cues, and a guided re-orientation. Each meeting is led by trained facilitators and designed to support daily recovery rather than deep therapy.

Safety and contraindications
Not suitable for people with uncontrolled epilepsy, certain types of implanted medical devices without prior medical approval, or unstable cardiovascular conditions. If you are pregnant, have a history of dissociation, or are under psychiatric crisis, consult a medical professional before attending. Our facilitators can adapt sessions; please disclose health concerns when booking.

Membership highlights

Members enjoy discounted booking, early notice of limited gatherings, and curated recordings for home practice. Membership is billed monthly and can be paused.
