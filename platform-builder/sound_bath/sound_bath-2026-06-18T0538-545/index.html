Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is a Sound Bath and how can it help with college anxiety?
A Sound Bath is a deeply immersive, full-body listening experience where you are 'bathed' in sound waves produced by various instruments. For college anxiety, these sessions help to quiet the overactive mind, reduce stress hormones, and encourage a state of deep relaxation, allowing you to find a calm center amidst academic demands.
Do I need any special experience or beliefs to participate in a Sound Bath?
Absolutely not! Our Sound Bath sessions are open to everyone, regardless of your background or spiritual beliefs. You don't need any prior experience. Just come as you are, with an open mind, and be ready to relax and receive the sounds.
What should I bring or wear to a Sound Bath session?
We recommend wearing comfortable clothing that allows you to fully relax. You might want to bring a water bottle. We provide mats and blankets, but if you have a favorite cushion or blanket that brings you extra comfort, feel free to bring it along to enhance your experience.
How can a Sound Bath support my spiritual growth while I'm focused on academics?
Even with academic focus, nurturing your inner self is vital. A Sound Bath offers a unique opportunity for introspection and mindfulness. By quieting external distractions, you can connect with your inner wisdom, gain perspective on your challenges, and foster a deeper sense of self, which contributes significantly to your overall spiritual well-being.
