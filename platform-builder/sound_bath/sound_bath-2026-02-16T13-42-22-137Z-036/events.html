Myths vs. truths

Myth:

Sound baths are just ambient music or background noise.

Truth:

They are crafted therapeutic experiences — tone, timing, and intention combine to guide the nervous system toward rest.

What to bring & safety

  • Yoga mat or rug, a cozy blanket, eye-covering (sleep mask), pillow.
  • Water bottle — stay hydrated before and after the session.
  • Wear comfortable, layered clothing; we recommend socks.

Contraindications: If you have epilepsy, are pregnant, have recent head/ear surgery, take photosensitive medication, or are concerned about extreme sound sensitivity, consult your healthcare provider before attending. Contact us at {{EMAIL}} for guidance.

Notes from sessions
