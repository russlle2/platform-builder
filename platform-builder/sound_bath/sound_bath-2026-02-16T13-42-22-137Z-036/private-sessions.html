Private sessions — crafted for you

A calm, luxuriously quiet container — designed for one, two, or groups. Sound is calibrated to intention: relaxation, team reset, or ceremonial accompaniment.

Formats we offer

1:1 Therapeutic Session
A focused session using monochord, tuning forks, crystal bowls to address sleep, tension, or nervous-system regulation. 60–90 minutes.
Couples & Duo Experience
Shared space for connection work — soft harmonic overtones, gentle gongs, and chime threads. 75 minutes.
Corporate & Retreat Mini-Sound
Short resets for teams (20–40 minutes) or full sessions for retreat groups — focused on cohesion and mindful presence. We bring chimes, gongs, and bowed instruments.

How it unfolds

  1. Brief intake and intention-setting.
  2. Soft breath or movement primer.
  3. Immersive sound phase tailored for the format.
  4. Integration — tea and reflection or practical next steps.

What to bring

  • Comfortable layers, a mat if you prefer, and any props you like (eye pillow, bolster).
  • For corporate: a quiet room and seating arrangement; we adapt to chairs or floor.

Safety notes

If you or participants have pacemakers, are pregnant, have seizures or recent ear surgery, please notify us in advance. Sound intensity can be adapted; we prioritize consent and comfort.

Case notes — tailored outcomes

After private work, clients commonly report improved sleep architecture, emotional relief, and enhanced focus. Corporate groups often describe increased clarity and calmer team dynamics.
