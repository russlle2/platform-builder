Preparation & flow

Arrive 15 minutes early to settle in. Sessions begin with a short orientation and intention setting. Our sound journey often includes a monochord, planetary bowls, and tuning forks — instruments selected for sustained resonance and clarity designed to allow the body to entrain and soften.

What to bring

  • Thick mat or yoga blanket and a small cushion.
  • Warm layers and an eye mask or dark scarf.
  • Water bottle. Leave jewelry for comfort if it bothers you.

Contraindications (important)

Do not attend without medical approval if you have: implanted medical devices (pacemaker), pregnancy within first trimester (please check with your care provider), recent surgeries, a history of severe seizures/epilepsy, or active psychosis. If you take medications that affect consciousness, consult your clinician. When uncertain, email us at {{EMAIL}}.
