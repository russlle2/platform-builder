Questions in tune

A short playbook — what to expect, how to prepare, and safety notes so your experience is smooth and restorative.

What happens during a sound bath?

We begin with gentle grounding: breath and posture. The facilitator layers instruments — in our gatherings you might hear a deep gong, singing bowls, and tuning forks — moving from warm, single tones into a harmonized wash and then a soft release. Sessions end with silent integration and optional tea.

How long is a session?

Group Sundays are typically 75–90 minutes (arrival + intro + 45–60 minute soundscape + integration). Private sessions vary; check the booking page for options.

What should I bring?

Bring a thick mat or blanket, a pillow, an eye covering, and a water bottle. Dress in layers — you'll want to be cozy. Avoid heavy meals right before the session.

Are there any contraindications?

If you are pregnant, have recent surgery, use a pacemaker, have epilepsy, severe PTSD, or are on certain psychiatric medications, consult with your care team before attending. Sound can be intense; safety and consent are primary. When in doubt, contact us at {{EMAIL}}.

Can I fall asleep?

Yes — and that's okay. Many guests shift into a deep rest state. The experience remains beneficial whether awake or drifting in and out.

Do you accommodate sensory needs?

We keep groups small and volumes gentle; please email us ahead of time about mobility, sensory sensitivities, or any requests and we will do our best to create a comfortable environment.

Quick tips

Arrive 10–15 minutes early. Silence phones. Let the facilitator know if you need extra padding or a chair.
