Myths & Reality: What a Sound Bath Actually Does

Myth

It’s just pretty music you hear from a distance.

Truth

It’s a sensory journey. Intentionally designed vibrations, delivered with attention, guide nervous system recalibration and altered states of restful awareness.

Myth

Everyone has the same experience.

Truth

Responses vary: some move, some sleep, some weep. All are supported. Sessions are curated to be deeply accessible and supportive.

Our Pillars of Care

Sensory Craft

Layered instruments — crystal bowls, a warm gong, and delicate chimes — sculpt a landscape of frequency.

Intentional Facilitation

Pre-session framing, breath cues, and mid-bath check-ins create safety and depth.

Premium Setting

Soft lighting, plush bolsters, and curated scent (optional) provide a ritual-grade environment.

How a Session Flows

  1. Arrival & Settle: You arrive 15 minutes early. We offer blankets and bolsters; set an intention.
  2. Intentions & Breath: Short welcome, safety notes, gentle breathing to attune to the space.
  3. Main Sound Journey: 40–60 min of layered sound — tuning forks, crystal bowls, and a soft monochord or gong — with facilitatory cues.
  4. Come Back Slowly: A gradual re-entry with grounding tones and a short check-in.

What to bring

  • A yoga mat or large blanket.
  • Layers: socks and a sweater for changing body temperature.
  • An eye-mask or scarf if you prefer darkness.
  • A water bottle for after the session.

Contraindications: Not recommended if you have uncontrolled seizures, are in the first trimester of pregnancy without clearance, or have severe cardiovascular instability. Please consult with your physician and reach out to us via {{EMAIL}} to discuss accommodations.
