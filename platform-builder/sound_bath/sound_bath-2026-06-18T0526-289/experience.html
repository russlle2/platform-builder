Releasing the Daily Strain with Sound Bath

Parenting is a constant give-and-take, often leaving you feeling emotionally drained and stretched thin. Our Sound Bath sessions are specifically designed to help you gently release the emotional burdens that accumulate from managing a household and raising young children. You don't need any prior experience; just come as you are, and let the calming vibrations create a space for your nervous system to find a moment of peace and quiet amidst the noise.
