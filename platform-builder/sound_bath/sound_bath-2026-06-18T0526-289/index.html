Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is a Sound Bath, and how will it help my stress as a parent?
A Sound Bath is a deeply relaxing experience where you lie comfortably while various instruments, like singing bowls and gongs, are played. For parents, it's a dedicated time for your nervous system to unwind from constant vigilance. The sounds help quiet your busy mind, offering a respite from the mental load of parenting and allowing you to feel more emotionally steady.
I'm always juggling so much; do I need to prepare anything or have a quiet mind for a Sound Bath?
Absolutely not! You don't need any special preparation or a perfectly quiet mind. Just come as you are. We understand that your mind is often busy, and that's perfectly normal. The sounds themselves are designed to gently guide you towards relaxation. Our goal is to create a nurturing space where you can simply receive, without any expectations on your part.
I'm worried about being emotional during a Sound Bath. Is that okay?
It is absolutely okay to experience emotions during a Sound Bath. We cultivate a trauma-informed environment where all feelings are welcome without judgment. The gentle vibrations can sometimes bring emotions to the surface, which is a natural part of releasing stress. You'll be in a supportive space, and there's no pressure to feel a certain way or to suppress anything that arises.
How often should I attend a Sound Bath to feel the benefits for my parenting stress?
Many parents feel a sense of calm and relief after just one session. For ongoing support in managing the demands of household stress and parenting, regular sessions can be very beneficial. We recommend listening to your own needs. Whether it's weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly, consistent moments of emotional grounding can make a significant difference in your overall well-being.
