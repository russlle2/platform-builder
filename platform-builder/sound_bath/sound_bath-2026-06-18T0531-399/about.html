I'm always so tired. Will I just fall asleep during a Sound Bath?

While it's common to feel deeply relaxed, even drowsy, the goal of a Sound Bath isn't just sleep. The vibrations are designed to work on a deeper level, encouraging your body to release tension and recalibrate. Many parents find themselves in a 'twilight' state, experiencing profound physical and mental rest without fully losing consciousness, leading to a feeling of rejuvenation, not just sleepiness.

My back and shoulders always ache. Can a Sound Bath really help with physical pain from carrying kids?

Absolutely. The resonant frequencies from the instruments used in a Sound Bath can travel deep into your tissues, promoting circulation and encouraging muscle relaxation. Many parents report a noticeable decrease in chronic pain in areas like the neck, shoulders, and lower back, often attributed to the repetitive movements and physical demands of parenting. It's a gentle yet effective way to address those persistent aches.

I feel so overwhelmed and 'touched out' by the end of the day. How does a Sound Bath address that emotional fatigue?

The constant demands of parenting can lead to significant emotional and sensory overwhelm. A Sound Bath provides a dedicated time and space for your nervous system to downregulate. The soothing sounds help to quiet the mental chatter and reduce cortisol levels, allowing your emotional state to settle. This dedicated 'you time' helps you process and release the emotional burden, leaving you feeling less 'touched out' and more centered.

I'm new to this. Do I need to 'do' anything specific during the Sound Bath?

Not at all. Your only job is to lie down, get comfortable, and allow the sounds to wash over you. There's no right or wrong way to experience it. You don't need to meditate or focus on anything in particular. Simply being present and open to the experience is enough. We provide everything you need for comfort, so you can truly let go and receive the benefits.
