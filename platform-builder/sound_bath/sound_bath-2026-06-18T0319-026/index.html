Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Sound Bath specifically help with my chronic illness or injury recovery?
Our Sound Baths are tailored to gently down-regulate your nervous system, moving you from a state of stress to deep relaxation. This reduced stress response is crucial for your body to prioritize healing, manage pain perception, and conserve energy that might otherwise be consumed by anxiety or tension related to your condition.
What should I expect during a Sound Bath session?
You'll lie comfortably, often on a mat with blankets and pillows, while our practitioner uses various instruments like singing bowls and gongs. There's no action required from you other than to relax and receive the sounds. Many people find themselves entering a deeply meditative state, leading to feelings of profound peace and rest.
Is a Sound Bath suitable if I have limited mobility or sensitivity to sound?
Yes, our Sound Bath sessions are designed to be accessible. We can adjust the volume and instrument selection to accommodate your sensitivities. We encourage you to communicate any concerns about mobility or sound levels beforehand so we can ensure your comfort and provide a truly restorative experience.
How often should I attend Sound Bath sessions to support my recovery?
The frequency often depends on your individual needs and how your body responds. Many clients find that regular sessions, such as weekly or bi-weekly, help maintain the calming effects and support consistent nervous system regulation, which is beneficial for ongoing recovery and stress management.
