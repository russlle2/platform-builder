Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Sound Bath specifically help with 'brain fog' from chronic illness?
Our Sound Bath sessions utilize specific frequencies and sustained tones to guide your brain into deeper states of relaxation, like alpha and theta brainwaves. These states are known to help quiet an overactive mind, reduce mental fatigue, and improve your ability to focus and process information, directly addressing the mental sluggishness often associated with chronic illness recovery.
Is the Sound Bath suitable if my energy levels are low due to my condition?
Absolutely. The Sound Bath requires no physical exertion from you. You simply lie down comfortably and allow the sounds to wash over you. It's a completely passive yet deeply restorative experience designed to be gentle and supportive, even when your energy reserves are limited. Many clients find it profoundly restful and energizing for the mind.
Will the sounds be too overwhelming if I'm sensitive after an injury or illness?
We are very mindful of sensitivities. The sounds in our Sound Bath are carefully curated to be gentle and immersive, not jarring or overwhelming. We use instruments that create sustained, harmonious tones, and adjusts the intensity to ensure a comfortable and soothing experience for everyone, especially those with heightened sensitivities during recovery.
How frequently should I attend Sound Bath sessions to see cognitive benefits?
Consistency is key for sustained cognitive improvement. While many clients report feeling clearer after just one session, we recommend regular engagement, such as weekly or bi-weekly sessions, to build and maintain mental resilience. This allows your brain to consistently experience the restorative effects, reinforcing pathways for improved focus and reduced mental strain.
