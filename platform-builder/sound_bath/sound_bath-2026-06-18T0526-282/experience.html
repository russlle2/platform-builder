Sound Bath

Creating Emotional Safety: Your First Sound Bath Experience

You might be wondering what to expect from a Sound Bath, especially when you're feeling vulnerable. Our approach is always trauma-informed, meaning we prioritize your comfort and sense of control. You'll simply lie back in a cozy space, and I'll play various instruments like singing bowls and chimes, allowing the sounds to wash over you. There’s no right or wrong way to experience it, just an invitation to rest and receive.
