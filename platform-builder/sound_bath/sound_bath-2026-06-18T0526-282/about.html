What if I’ve never experienced a Sound Bath before and feel overwhelmed?

That's perfectly fine! Our Sound Baths are designed to be accessible for everyone, especially new mothers who might be feeling extra sensitive. There's no preparation needed, and you don't need any prior wellness experience. You just lie down, get comfortable, and let the sounds unfold around you. We focus on creating a very gentle and supportive atmosphere.

How can a Sound Bath specifically help with postpartum emotional recovery?

During postpartum, your nervous system can be in overdrive. A Sound Bath helps by creating a deep state of relaxation that can calm your fight-or-flight response. This allows your body and mind to gently release accumulated stress, process emotions without feeling overwhelmed, and find a quiet space for emotional replenishment. It's about tenderly supporting your internal well-being.

Is it okay if I fall asleep during the Sound Bath?

Absolutely! Many people find themselves drifting into a light sleep or a deeply relaxed state during a Sound Bath, especially new mothers who are often sleep-deprived. This is a sign that your body and mind are truly letting go and receiving the benefits of deep rest. There's no need to try and stay awake; just allow yourself to be present in whatever way feels most natural.

What should I bring or wear to my first Sound Bath session?

For your comfort, we recommend wearing soft, loose-fitting clothing. You'll be lying down for the session, so dress in layers to ensure you stay warm and cozy. We provide mats, blankets, and pillows, but if you have a favorite blanket or cushion that brings you extra comfort, please feel free to bring it. Just bring yourself, ready to relax.
