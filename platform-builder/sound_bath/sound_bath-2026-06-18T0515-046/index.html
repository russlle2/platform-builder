Your Gentle Immersion: What to Expect in a Sound Bath

During a Sound Bath, you'll lie comfortably, fully clothed, while I play a variety of instruments like crystal singing bowls and gongs. Frequently Asked Questions

Is a Sound Bath suitable if I have limited mobility or specific sensitivities?
Absolutely. Our Sound Bath sessions are designed to be fully accessible. You'll be able to find a comfortable position that works best for your body, whether lying down or seated. We encourage you to share any concerns or sensitivities beforehand so we can ensure your experience is as supportive and comfortable as possible, without any physical demands on your part.
How can a Sound Bath specifically help with chronic pain or fatigue?
While a Sound Bath isn't a cure, it's a powerful tool for managing the stress response often associated with chronic pain and fatigue. By helping your nervous system to downregulate, it can reduce overall tension, which might contribute to pain reduction. Many clients report feeling more rested and experiencing an improved ability to cope with their symptoms, even if the underlying condition remains.
What if I find it hard to relax or my mind races during a session?
It's completely normal for your mind to be active, especially when you're navigating recovery. There's no pressure to achieve a specific state of relaxation. Simply allow yourself to listen to the sounds, without judgment. The gentle vibrations and tones often naturally guide your mind towards a calmer state over time. Think of it as practice for your nervous system, and every session offers an opportunity for a little more ease.
Do I need any special equipment or preparation for a Sound Bath?
Not at all. You just need to arrive in comfortable clothing, ready to relax. We provide everything you'll need for comfort, including mats, blankets, and pillows. We encourage you to hydrate before and after the session, just as you would with any gentle self-care practice. Your only preparation is to come with an open mind, ready to receive.
