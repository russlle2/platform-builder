Your Gentle Retreat: What to Expect During a Sound Bath

Imagine stepping into a calm space where gentle gongs, singing bowls, and other resonant instruments create a symphony of peace. All you need to do is get comfortable and allow the sounds to guide you into a deep state of relaxation. You might find your thoughts slowing, your body feeling lighter, and a sense of quiet joy emerging. Many parents share how this practice helps them return home feeling refreshed and more present for their family.
