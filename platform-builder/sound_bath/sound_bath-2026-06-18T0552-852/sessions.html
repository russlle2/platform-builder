Gentle Support for Your Postpartum Posture in Sound Bath

Your body has been a vessel of life, and now it deserves dedicated care to regain its strength and alignment. The resonant frequencies work subtly to release tension in areas like your hips, back, and shoulders, commonly affected by carrying your little one and nursing.
