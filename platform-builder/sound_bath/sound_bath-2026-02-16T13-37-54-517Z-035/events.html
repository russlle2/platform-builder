Why you might come

People come when they crave a deep nervous-system reset, an evening without screens, or a gentle container for grief and creativity. Typical outcomes: clearer sleep, softened tension, and expanded breath.

Signs you’ll benefit
  • Restless nights or fragmented sleep
  • Sustained tension in neck/shoulders
  • Difficulty switching off from work
Not right for
  • Severe claustrophobia in shared rooms
  • Unmanaged psychiatric emergencies

Instruments on this series

Crystal bowls, koshi chimes, bowed gong, and subtle tuning forks woven with breath cues — a tonal palette designed for full-body resonance.

Session Flow — what happens

  1. Arrival & gentle seating — 10 minutes (soft grounding prompts)
  2. Short breath practice — 8 minutes to anchor
  3. Main sound offering — 45 minutes of layered bowls, gong wash, and chimes
  4. Return & integration — sustained quiet, then soft talkback — 12 minutes

We pace the sound intentionally: sustained tones give tissues time to attune, and silence between layers allows the nervous system to reorganize.

Accessibility & format

Mats and blocks available on request. Rooms kept dim and temperature-moderate. If you need a seat instead of floor, notify us when you book.

Micro practices to take home

2-minute breath reset

4-count inhale, 6-count exhale, repeat four times. Anchor with hand on belly.

Sound-scan

Hum once, listen for internal echoes, then breathe into the area that springs to attention.
