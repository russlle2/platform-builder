Questions people ask before their first intensive

How long is a session and what happens?
Typical group intensives are 75–90 minutes. We begin with arrival, gentle breathwork and an intention-setting moment, move through the sound immersion featuring tuned instruments, and finish with a slow reintegration and optional tea. Expect low lights, comfortable repose and spacious timing.
What should I bring and wear?
Bring a yoga mat or thick blanket, a small pillow for neck support, and an extra blanket for warmth. Dress in comfortable layers. Avoid heavy perfumes.
Are there contraindications?
If you have epilepsy, are in the first trimester of pregnancy, have a pacemaker, recent surgery, or are under treatment for severe cardiac conditions, please consult your doctor and let us know before attending. We can adapt many things but safety-first is our policy.
Are recordings allowed?
To preserve the group field and intimacy of the work we ask that you do not record during immersive segments. Phone-free experiences are best.
Do you accept walk-ins?
