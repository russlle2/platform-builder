Session flow

Our intensive sound baths follow a gentle arc: soft arrival + intention setting, a sonic immersion with layered instruments (for events typically featuring crystal bowls, gong and chimes), and a slow reintegration with tea and optional sharing. Expect 75–90 minutes from arrival to close.

Contraindications & safety

Sound work is deeply restorative but not suited for everyone. Please consult with a provider before attending if you: have seizures, are in the first trimester of pregnancy, have been advised to avoid strong vibrational stimuli, have implanted auditory devices, or manage severe psychiatric conditions. Not medical advice — contact your clinician with concerns.
