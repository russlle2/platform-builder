Which private is right for you?

We meet you where you are: somatic recalibration, targeted pain relief, or hosted sound for group cohesion.

1:1 Healing Session — 60–90m

Individual intake, resonant mapping with a monochord and crystal bowl duet, followed by bespoke tone sequences. You’ll get tailored micro-practices and a short integration plan to continue at home.

Couples & Partner Work — 75m

Shared field-building to cultivate presence and collective breathwork. Instruments like chimes and tuned handpans make relational listening practical and delicious.

Corporate & Team — 90m

A facilitated session that blends sound with micro-break practices and a short debrief focused on focus & resilience. Great for leadership retreats and reset days.

What to bring

Loose clothing, any personal props that aid rest, and openness to lie or sit. We provide bolsters, blankets, and mats.

Contraindications

Private sessions are adaptable. If you have implants or a history of seizures, please consult your physician and inform us before booking.

Typical Private Session Flow

Each offering is unique, but here’s a common rhythm:

  1. Check-in & breath mapping — 10–15m
  2. Focused tone offering — 30–50m using a curated orchestration
  3. Quiet integration & journaling — 5–10m
  4. Aftercare suggestions and micro-habits — 5m

Short practices you’ll keep

Evening settling

Two minutes of slow hums to dissolve jaw and neck tension. Great before sleep.

Desk reset

Three shoulder rolls and a 30-second neck scan with a single tuning fork hum to re-center mid-day.
