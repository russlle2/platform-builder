Session flow — the ritual roadmap

A clear sequence so you know what to expect in our intensive sound sessions.

  1. Arrival & grounding (10–15 min) — soft tea, settle in, intention setting.
  2. Breath & gentle movement (5–10 min) — guided to move energy and tune the nervous system.
  3. Main soundscape (40–50 min) — layered instruments: crystal, monochord, specific gong work — tailored dynamics.
  4. Transition & silent reflection (5–8 min) — allow integration; slow return to room.
  5. Closing circle (5–10 min) — share, grounding tips, micro-habits to extend the benefit.

Instruments we might use

Small rituals to extend the session

Playful, premium micro-habits — easy to do and high-return.

  • Lie down, play a single singing bowl or hum softly. Rest. Repeat for three nights post-session.
  • 5 rounds of longer exhales — (inhale 4, hold 2, exhale 6) — helps anchor the nervous system after intense sound exposure.
  • Warm water with lemon within 30 minutes; gentle movement and grounding foods that evening.
