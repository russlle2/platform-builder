Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is a Sound Bath and how does it help parents?
A Sound Bath is a deeply relaxing experience where you lie comfortably while various instruments like singing bowls and gongs are played. For parents, it's a chance to quiet your mind, reduce mental chatter, and improve your ability to focus, making it easier to handle daily stressors with more clarity.
Do I need any special equipment or experience for a Sound Bath?
Absolutely not! You just need to show up as you are. We provide everything you need for comfort, like mats and blankets. There's no prior experience with meditation or wellness practices required – just an open mind and a desire for some peace.
How can a Sound Bath help me manage the stress of parenting?
The gentle sounds help to calm your nervous system, which can significantly reduce feelings of overwhelm and stress. Many parents find that regular sessions help them feel more patient, present, and better equipped to respond to their children's needs without feeling constantly drained.
Will I be able to think more clearly after a Sound Bath session?
Yes, that's a key benefit! By allowing your mind to deeply relax, a Sound Bath helps to clear mental fog and improve cognitive function. You'll likely find yourself making decisions with more ease, remembering things better, and feeling a greater sense of mental order.
