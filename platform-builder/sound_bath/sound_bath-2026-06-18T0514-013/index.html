Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is a Sound Bath and how can it help me?
A Sound Bath is a deeply relaxing experience where you lie down comfortably while a practitioner plays various instruments. The gentle vibrations and sounds encourage your brainwaves to slow down, helping to reduce stress, calm an overactive nervous system, and promote a sense of overall well-being and peacefulness.
Is a Sound Bath suitable for someone my age, especially if I have mobility concerns?
Absolutely. Our Sound Baths are designed with your comfort in mind. You can relax in any position that feels best for you, whether lying on a mat, sitting in a comfortable chair, or even remaining seated in a wheelchair. The experience is entirely passive, requiring no physical exertion from you.
What should I expect to feel during and after a Sound Bath session?
During a Sound Bath, many people describe feeling a profound sense of relaxation, warmth, or even a floating sensation. Afterward, it's common to feel refreshed, clear-headed, and more at peace. You might notice improved sleep patterns and a greater ability to manage daily stressors in the days following your session.
How often should I attend a Sound Bath to see benefits for stress reduction?
The frequency that works best for you can vary. Some individuals find significant benefits from attending once a month, while others prefer more regular sessions, perhaps every two weeks. We encourage you to listen to your body and observe how the sessions contribute to your sustained calm and nervous system regulation.
