Frequently Asked Questions

Is a Sound Bath safe for me during postpartum recovery?
Yes, Sound Bath is generally very safe and beneficial during postpartum recovery. Our sessions are designed with gentle frequencies and a calming environment to support your sensitive nervous system. If you have any specific health concerns, we recommend discussing them with your healthcare provider first.
How does Sound Bath specifically help with postpartum stress and anxiety?
The resonant frequencies in a Sound Bath encourage your brainwaves to shift from active states (beta) to more relaxed states (alpha and theta). This physiological response helps to reduce cortisol levels, ease muscle tension, and activate your parasympathetic nervous system, which is responsible for rest and digestion. This process directly counters the 'fight or flight' response often heightened during postpartum.
What should I expect during my first Sound Bath session?
You will be invited to lie down comfortably on a mat, often with blankets and pillows for support. We will then begin playing various instruments, creating an immersive soundscape. Your role is simply to relax and allow the sounds to wash over you. You might experience deep relaxation, a sense of calm, or even fall into a light sleep. We encourage you to listen to your body and rest as needed.
How often should I attend a Sound Bath for optimal postpartum nervous system regulation?
The frequency can vary based on your individual needs and how you feel. Many mothers find weekly or bi-weekly sessions to be very supportive in establishing a consistent state of calm and reducing stress accumulation during their postpartum period. We can discuss a personalized recommendation during your consultation.
