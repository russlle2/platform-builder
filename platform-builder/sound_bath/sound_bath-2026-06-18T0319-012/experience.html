Your Gentle Return to Self: The Sound Bath Experience

In your Sound Bath session, you'll lie comfortably, enveloped in gentle sounds. There’s nothing for you to do but allow the vibrations to wash over you. This dedicated time is for your recovery, offering a respite from the demands of new motherhood. Many mothers report a significant reduction in feelings of overwhelm and an improved sense of calm and clarity after a session. We aim to support your body's innate ability to find balance and ease.
