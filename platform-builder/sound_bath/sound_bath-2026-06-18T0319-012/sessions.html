Sound Bath

Our Method: Structured Sound Bath for Stress Regulation

Each Sound Bath session is carefully structured to guide you into a state of profound relaxation. Using specific frequencies and resonant vibrations from instruments like gongs and singing bowls, we create an auditory landscape that encourages brainwave entrainment. This process helps to quiet mental chatter, lower cortisol levels, and promote restorative states. Our approach is rooted in understanding the physiological responses to sound, ensuring a beneficial experience for your postpartum well-being.
