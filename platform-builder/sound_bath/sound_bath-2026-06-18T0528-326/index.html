Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Sound Bath specifically help with my chronic physical pain?
Our Sound Bath sessions create deep relaxation, which can help to reduce muscle tension and quiet overactive nerve signals often associated with chronic pain. The subtle vibrations work to soothe your somatic system, promoting a sense of calm and allowing your body to release stored discomfort naturally.
Is a Sound Bath safe if I have a specific injury or sensitive areas in my body?
Yes, Sound Baths are a non-contact, gentle practice. You simply lie or sit comfortably, and the sounds wash over you. We prioritize your comfort, and you're always in control of your position. If you have concerns, please discuss them with beforehand.
What should I expect to feel during and after a Sound Bath focused on somatic healing?
During the Sound Bath, you might experience deep relaxation, a sense of lightness, or subtle vibrations within your body. Afterward, many report feeling a profound sense of peace, reduced physical tension, and a clearer connection to their body's needs, often with improved comfort and ease of movement.
How often should I attend Sound Bath sessions to support my recovery from chronic illness?
The frequency that best supports your recovery can vary. Many individuals find regular sessions, such as weekly or bi-weekly, most beneficial for consistent pain relief and somatic recalibration. We can discuss a personalized approach tailored to your specific needs and recovery goals.
