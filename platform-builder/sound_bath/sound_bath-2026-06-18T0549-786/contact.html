Rebuilding Trust: Your Body and Sound Bath

When your body has been through chronic illness or injury, it can feel like a stranger, or even an adversary. Our Sound Bath sessions are crafted to help you slowly and gently rebuild a kind, trusting relationship with your physical self. You don't need to 'do' anything; simply rest, listen, and allow the gentle vibrations to support a sense of safety and ease within your own skin. It's about finding a soft, internal anchor when the world feels uncertain.
