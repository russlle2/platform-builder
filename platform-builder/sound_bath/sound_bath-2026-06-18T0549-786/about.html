Rebuilding Trust: Your Body and Sound Bath

Rebuilding Trust: Your Body and Sound Bath

I'm new to this. Do I need any prior experience with Sound Bath?

Absolutely not. Our Sound Bath sessions are designed to be accessible for everyone, especially if you're new to this kind of experience. There's no right or wrong way to participate; you simply rest comfortably and allow the sounds to wash over you. We'll guide you gently through everything, so you can feel completely at ease.

I'm recovering from a physical injury/illness. Will I be comfortable during a Sound Bath?

Your comfort is our top priority. We provide ample support, including soft mats, bolsters, and blankets, to help you find a comfortable position that works for your body. We encourage you to communicate any specific needs or concerns beforehand or upon arrival, so we can ensure you have the most supportive and gentle experience possible.

How can a Sound Bath help me specifically with chronic illness or injury recovery?

Our Sound Bath sessions offer a unique space for deep rest and nervous system regulation. When you're recovering, your body and mind can be under immense stress. The gentle vibrations can help calm your system, reduce tension, and foster a sense of internal peace. This supports your overall well-being and helps you cultivate a kinder, more patient relationship with your body as it heals.

What if I feel emotional during a Sound Bath session?

It's completely normal for emotions to surface during a Sound Bath, especially when you're in a vulnerable state of recovery. This space is safe and non-judgmental. We encourage you to simply acknowledge any feelings that arise without expectation or pressure. You are held in a supportive environment, and it’s okay to simply be with whatever comes up.
