I'm an athlete and always on the go. Will a Sound Bath actually help with my physical stress?

Absolutely! Many athletes find Sound Bath sessions incredibly beneficial. The deep relaxation induced by the sounds can help calm your nervous system, reduce muscle tension, and promote a sense of overall physical ease. It's a unique way to offer your hard-working body a different kind of recovery, helping you release built-up stress from training and competition.

I've never done anything like a Sound Bath before. Is it complicated or will I feel out of place?

Not at all! Our Sound Bath sessions are designed to be completely accessible, even if you have zero prior experience. There's nothing to 'do' except lie back, listen, and allow the sounds to wash over you. There's no right or wrong way to experience it, and we'll guide you gently through the process. It's a space created for comfort and ease, no wellness jargon required.

How does a Sound Bath relate to 'relationship healing' for an active person?

For many active people, the relationship with their body can become purely about performance and pushing limits. 'Relationship healing' here means shifting from seeing your body as just a tool to embracing it with kindness and understanding. Sound Baths encourage you to listen to your body's signals with compassion, fostering a deeper, more nurturing connection beyond just what it can achieve.

Will I need to do anything specific or wear special clothes for a Sound Bath?

No special clothes or preparation needed! Just wear something comfortable that you can relax in – think gym clothes, but for relaxing. You'll typically lie down, so comfort is key. We provide everything else you need, like mats and blankets. Just come as you are, ready to unwind.
