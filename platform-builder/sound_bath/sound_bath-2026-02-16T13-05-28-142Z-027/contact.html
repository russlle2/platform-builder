What to expect at a session

Our gatherings are intentionally slow, sensory, and held with a premium touch. Below is the common flow for shared sound baths — exact timings may vary.

  • Arrival & settling (10–15 min) — gentle welcome, silence signal, guidance on supported posture (mats, bolsters available).
  • Opening ritual (5–10 min) — breathwork and intention setting led by {{FACILITATOR_NAME}}.
  • Sound immersion (30–45 min) — layered tones from a curated selection of instruments create a spacious field.
  • Close & grounding (10 min) — soft chimes, time to emerge, optional tea and gentle circulation.

Instruments you may hear

Each sound experience is unique; we may include crystal singing bowls, tuning forks, chimes, and a monochord to hold deep resonant tones.

What to bring

  • Comfortable layered clothing to keep warm.
  • A yoga mat, blanket, and a small pillow or bolster (we provide extras).
  • Water and an open heart — we encourage arriving 15 minutes early to settle.

Contraindications & safety

If you are pregnant, have a history of seizures, unstable cardiovascular conditions, recent surgeries, or are sensitive to loud vibrations, please contact us before booking. Sound experiences can be intense for some; we will discuss accommodations and alternatives.
