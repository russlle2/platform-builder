The ritual we hold

Each gathering is a gentle arc designed for arrival, descent, and return. We guide you through breathwork, layered sound journeys, and an intentional re-entry so you leave settled and soothed.

Instruments on a typical evening

  • Crystal bowls for long, harmonically rich tones
  • Large gong for bloom and release
  • Monochord to hold drone textures
  • Tuning forks for focused resonance and body-points

What to bring

  • A yoga mat or blanket for comfort
  • One or two pillows for head/legs
  • An eye mask or scarf to block light
  • Water to hydrate after the session

We provide bolsters and a few shared blankets, but space is limited — bring what makes your body settle.

Contraindications & care

Sound baths are deeply relaxing but not suitable for everyone. If you are pregnant, have implanted medical devices (e.g., pacemaker), epilepsy, severe migraines triggered by sound, or recent head/neck injuries, please consult your physician and tell the facilitator before booking.

Arrival + flow

Arrive 20–30 minutes early to settle. We move from gentle breathwork into extended sound layers (30–50 minutes), followed by a soft return and offered tea. The room is kept calm and low-lit to preserve the internal journey.

FAQ — quick notes

Do I need experience?
No prior meditation or sound work required — just a willingness to rest.
Can I fall asleep?
Yes — it's common. We encourage gentle re-entry and offer time to integrate before you leave.
How long is each session?
Most gatherings are 75–90 minutes from arrival to close.
