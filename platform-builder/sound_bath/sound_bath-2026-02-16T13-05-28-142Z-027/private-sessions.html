The session flow

  1. Intake & intention setting (10–15m)
  2. Supported arrival: breath + gentle touch if requested (10m)
  3. Deep listening span with layered instruments (30–60m)
  4. Slow re-entry, grounding, and aftercare discussion (10–15m)

Instruments chosen for private sessions are curated — crystal bowls, chimes, monochord, small gong, and tuning forks are used selectively to support your stated goal.

Safety & contraindications

Please notify the facilitator if you have a pacemaker, are pregnant, have epilepsy, recent surgeries, or severe sound sensitivity. We adapt where possible and will recommend alternatives when needed.

What to bring

A mat or blanket, water, and any comfort items. Wear comfortable clothing and arrive rested enough to lie still for extended periods.
