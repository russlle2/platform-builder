How we ritualize the sound bath

Every gathering follows a gentle architecture: arrival, intention setting, immersive sound, and a soft return. Each element is designed to support nervous system regulation and embodied rest.

Arrival & Grounding

We begin with warm tea and a short centering practice to arrive in the body and clarify intention.

Sound Immersion

Crystalline bowls, a warm gong wash, chimes, and subtle tuning fork work blend into a layered sound field that supports deep listening.

Integration

We finish with a soft bell and time for journaling or quiet conversation—space to land gently.

What to bring & who it's for

Our sessions are sensory, premium, and held in a quiet, low-light room. Whether you're new to sound or a seasoned attendee, you'll find a steady, enveloping presence here.

  • Yoga mat or blanket (we provide a few, but bring yours for comfort)
  • Pillow or bolster for head/neck support
  • Water bottle and an open heart
  • Lightweight eye covering if you wish

Contraindications & safety

Sound baths are deeply restorative, but certain conditions warrant caution. Please consult us if you are pregnant, have had a recent major surgery, have a pacemaker, or have severe auditory sensitivities. Sound can be powerful—notify the facilitator before the session if you have concerns.

Flow of a typical session

  1. Welcoming & settling (10–15 minutes)
  2. Guided breath and intention (5–10 minutes)
  3. Immersive sound journey (30–50 minutes)
  4. Soft closure & tea (10–15 minutes)
