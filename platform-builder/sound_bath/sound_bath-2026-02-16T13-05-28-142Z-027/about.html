The Ritual — how a ceremony unfolds

Our gatherings are composed like a short pilgrimage: arrival, attuning, resonance, and soft return. Each part is intentional and paced for deep listening.

Arrival & settling (10–15 min)
Guests arrive, remove shoes, choose blankets and bolsters. We share a brief orientation and intention setting.
Grounding & breath (8–12 min)
A guided breath and body scan to invite presence.
Sound journey (30–45 min)
We layer instruments — a warm monochord, crystal singing bowls, and reverent chimes — to shape an evolving landscape of tone.
Rest & return (10–15 min)
Soft silence, a gentle bell, and an invitation back to sitting. Light tea afterwards for lingering connection.

Instruments you might hear

  • Monochord — long, humming harmonic bed
  • Crystal bowls — crystalline sustain and shimmer
  • Tuning forks — precise, focused frequencies

Note: Instrument selection varies by session to keep each gathering unique.

What to bring

  • Soft blanket or yoga mat
  • Pillow or bolster for knees/support
  • An eye mask if you prefer darkness
  • Water bottle
