Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Sound Bath specifically help with emotional healing after chronic illness or injury?
Our Sound Bath sessions are carefully designed to create a safe container where your nervous system can downshift from chronic stress. The resonant frequencies help to release tension, alleviate anxiety, and gently bring awareness to emotional blockages often associated with prolonged illness or injury, supporting your innate capacity for emotional processing and healing.
Is a Sound Bath safe if I have physical limitations or sensitivities from my recovery?
Absolutely. Your comfort and safety are paramount. We create an accommodating environment where you can relax in any position that feels best for your body. Our approach is trauma-informed, meaning we prioritize your autonomy and ensure the sound levels and experience are always within your comfortable range, making it highly adaptable for various physical needs.
What should I expect during my first trauma-informed Sound Bath session?
You can expect a supportive and calm environment. After a brief, gentle check-in about your needs and comfort, you'll lie down or sit comfortably. I'll then play a variety of instruments like singing bowls and gongs, creating a rich soundscape. Your role is simply to relax and allow the sounds to wash over you. There's no expectation or 'right way' to experience it; simply be present with what arises for you.
How does Sound Bath support my ongoing recovery and emotional resilience?
Sound Bath is a powerful tool for building emotional resilience by fostering deep relaxation and self-regulation. Regular sessions can help you develop a greater capacity to cope with stress, manage pain-related anxieties, and cultivate a stronger sense of inner peace. It's about empowering you to proactively support your emotional health as you navigate the complexities of long-term recovery.
