Is a Sound Bath safe for new mothers, especially if I'm still recovering?

Absolutely. Our Sound Baths are designed with profound gentleness in mind, making them very safe for new mothers at any stage of postpartum recovery. The sounds are soothing and non-invasive, focused on relaxation and nervous system regulation rather than intense stimulation. Your comfort and well-being are our top priority, and we encourage you to listen to your body.

What should I expect during a Sound Bath session?

You'll be invited to lie down comfortably, often on a mat with blankets and pillows, in a peaceful space. Dr. Morgan Ellis will then play various instruments like crystal singing bowls, gongs, and chimes, creating an immersive soundscape. There's nothing for you to 'do' except relax and allow the sounds to wash over you. Many find it deeply meditative and restorative, often drifting into a state of deep rest.

How can a Sound Bath specifically help with postpartum stress?

The gentle vibrations and frequencies of a Sound Bath encourage your brainwaves to slow down, shifting you from a state of active thinking to one of deep relaxation. This helps to reduce the physiological symptoms of stress – like a racing heart or tense muscles – which are common during postpartum. It supports your nervous system in rebalancing, making it easier to cope with daily demands and promoting a sense of calm amidst the beautiful changes.

Do I need any experience with meditation or sound therapy to attend?

Not at all! Our Sound Baths are accessible for everyone, regardless of prior experience. There's no right or wrong way to experience the sounds. Just come as you are, with an open heart, and allow the experience to unfold naturally. Dr. Morgan Ellis creates a welcoming and supportive atmosphere where you can feel completely at ease.
