How can Sound Bath help my sleep when I'm dealing with chronic pain or fatigue?

Sound Bath sessions are designed to gently soothe your nervous system, which can be overactive when managing chronic conditions. The calming vibrations encourage your body to shift into a 'rest and digest' state, helping to quiet discomfort and mental chatter, making it easier for you to fall into a deeper, more restorative sleep cycle.

Is Sound Bath suitable for someone with mobility limitations or who needs to stay in one position?

Absolutely. Our Sound Bath sessions are highly accommodating. You can participate in whatever position is most comfortable for you – lying down, seated, or even in a recliner. Our focus is on your comfort and ability to relax, ensuring you can fully experience the benefits without strain during your recovery.

I often feel overstimulated easily; will the sounds be too much for me?

We prioritize a gentle and inclusive experience. Dr. Morgan Ellis plays instruments with a focus on creating a soothing, non-overwhelming soundscape. We use softer tones and can adjust the volume and intensity to ensure you feel comfortable and secure throughout the session, allowing you to relax without feeling overloaded.

What should I expect from my first Sound Bath experience with Sound Bath Studio?

For your first Sound Bath, you can expect a warm welcome and a comfortable space to relax. You'll lie down or sit, covered with a blanket if you wish, and simply listen as Dr. Morgan Ellis plays. There’s nothing specific you need to do, just allow the sounds to wash over you. Many people report feeling deeply relaxed and sometimes even drift into a light sleep, leading to improved rest later.
