What is a Sound Bath and how does it help seniors with emotional healing?

A Sound Bath involves lying down and being immersed in live therapeutic sounds. For seniors, it's a gentle way to encourage deep relaxation, which can naturally help soothe emotional distress, reduce anxiety, and promote a sense of inner peace. It's a non-invasive method to support your emotional well-being without requiring any physical effort on your part.

Is the Sound Bath suitable if I have experienced difficult life events or past trauma?

Absolutely. Our Sound Bath sessions are designed with a trauma-informed approach, meaning we prioritize your safety and comfort above all else. The sounds are gentle, and you are always in control of your experience. Dr. Morgan Ellis creates a supportive environment where you can feel secure and gently process emotions at your own pace, without any pressure.

What should I expect during my first Sound Bath session?

For your first Sound Bath, you can expect to arrive and find a comfortable spot to recline or sit. Dr. Morgan Ellis will begin with a brief introduction, explaining what to expect. Then, you'll simply close your eyes and listen as gentle sounds fill the space. Many people experience a deep sense of calm, mental clarity, or even fall into a light meditative state. You don't need to 'do' anything, just relax and receive.

Will the sounds be too loud or overwhelming for me?

No, we ensure the Sound Bath experience is gentle and never overwhelming. The volume and intensity are carefully managed to create a soothing, comforting atmosphere. Our goal is to provide a serene soundscape that helps you relax, not to startle or discomfort you. We use specific instruments known for their calming effects, tailored to be gentle on your senses.
