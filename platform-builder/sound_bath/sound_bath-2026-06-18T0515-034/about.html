How does Sound Bath specifically help with physical stress from training?

Sound Bath helps by shifting your nervous system into a parasympathetic state, which is crucial for recovery. This reduces muscle tension, lowers stress hormones like cortisol, and improves blood flow, allowing your body to repair and adapt more efficiently after strenuous activity.

Will a Sound Bath make me too relaxed or groggy for my next training session?

Quite the opposite. While deeply relaxing, a Sound Bath is designed to optimize your nervous system, leading to clearer mental focus and reduced fatigue rather than grogginess. You'll likely feel refreshed and more attuned, not sluggish, making it an excellent pre- or post-activity practice.

What should I expect during a Sound Bath session?

You'll lie comfortably on a mat while Dr. Morgan Ellis plays various instruments. Your role is simply to relax and allow the sound frequencies to wash over you. Many active individuals report a significant reduction in mental chatter and physical tension, often leading to a sense of deep calm and rejuvenation.

Is Sound Bath a replacement for other recovery methods like stretching or massage?

Sound Bath complements your existing recovery strategies. It addresses the neurological and mental aspects of recovery that traditional methods might not fully cover. Think of it as an essential tool for nervous system regulation, enhancing the effectiveness of your physical recovery practices, not replacing them.
