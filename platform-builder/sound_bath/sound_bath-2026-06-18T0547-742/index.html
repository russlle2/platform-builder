Frequently Asked Questions

Is a Sound Bath suitable for new mothers in all stages of postpartum recovery?
Yes, our Sound Bath sessions are designed to be gentle and supportive for new mothers at any point in their postpartum recovery. We encourage you to listen to your body and engage in a way that feels comfortable and nourishing for you.
Do I need any prior experience with Sound Bath or meditation?
Absolutely not! No prior experience is necessary. Our Sound Bath sessions are accessible to everyone. Just come as you are, find a comfortable position, and allow the sounds to wash over you. There's no right or wrong way to experience it.
Can I bring my baby to the Sound Bath session?
We understand the unique needs of new mothers. Please contact us directly to discuss options for bringing your baby. Our priority is to create a peaceful and restorative environment for all participants, and we're happy to explore how best to accommodate you.
What should I wear or bring to a Sound Bath for postpartum care?
We recommend wearing comfortable clothing that allows you to relax fully. You might also want to bring a water bottle, and anything else that helps you feel cozy and supported, like a small pillow or blanket. We'll provide mats and bolsters to ensure your comfort.
