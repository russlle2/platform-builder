Myth vs. truth: What a sound bath actually is

There are many myths about sound bathing. We prefer clarity and care.

Myth

Sound baths are just background noise — anyone can run one.

Truth

Each session is intentional: instrument selection, pacing, and energetic containment are guided by an experienced facilitator.

Myth

You must be spiritual to attend.

Truth

Sound baths are sensory journeys suitable for many backgrounds—it's about safety, consent, and comfort.

Session flow & what to bring

Typical private session (60–90 min)

  1. Brief intake (5–10 min) — intention, comfort, medical notes.
  2. Arrival & settling with guided breathwork (10 min).
  3. Main sound immersion: layers of crystal bowls, chimes, and tuning forks interwoven with stillness (30–60 min).
  4. Gentle re-entry: grounding tones and time to journal (5–10 min).

What to bring

  • Light blanket and a yoga mat (we also provide premium mats on request).
  • Water, comfortable clothing, and an open intention.
  • Optional: a small journal or anything for grounding afterward.

Retail add-ons: eye pillows, aromatic mists, and curated crystal sets are available for purchase — mention this when booking.

Contraindications & safety

If you are pregnant, have a history of seizures, recent surgery, or are under the care of a mental health professional for acute conditions, please consult with us before booking. Sound work can be intense; for some people with certain neurological or cardiac conditions, it may not be recommended.

Quick questions

How long until I feel the effects?

Yes — we design retreats and workplace offerings. There’s an intake to align objectives and logistics.
