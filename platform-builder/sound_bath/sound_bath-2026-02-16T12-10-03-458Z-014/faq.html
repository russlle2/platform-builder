Frequently asked questions

Below are practical details about attending, safety and what to expect. If your question isn’t answered, email {{EMAIL}}.

General

What is a sound bath?

A meditative session where tuned instruments create sustained tones and overtones. We use sound to guide attention, soften muscular tension and promote rest. Our approach is sensory-forward and premium: soft textiles, careful pacing and careful volume control.

How long is a session?

Group sessions are typically 60–75 minutes (arrival + immersion + gentle exit). Private sessions vary from 45 to 90 minutes depending on the format.

Booking & arrival

How do I book?

Reserve on the Book page or contact us at {{EMAIL}}. For next event dates see the Events page. A small deposit may be requested for private bookings.

When should I arrive?

Arrive 10–15 minutes early to settle in. Late arrivals may be seated quietly but we hold the session container and may close doors once sound begins.

What to bring

Essentials: a cozy blanket, a mat or bolster if you prefer, an eye pillow optional. Wear comfortable layers; the room is climate-controlled but layering helps with the shifting states of relaxation.

Session flow

A typical arc: greeting & breath practice → sustained sound field (bowls, gong swells or chime washes) → a slow descent into longer tones → subtle tuning-fork grounding and gentle reorientation. Facilitators cue breath and offer brief verbal anchors at start and finish.

Instruments we use

Depending on repertoire: crystal singing bowls, gongs for low-pressure blooms, chimes for gentle sparks, monochord drones and tuning forks for specific resonance work.

Contraindications & safety

If you are pregnant, have uncontrolled epilepsy, recent head/ear surgery, severe heart conditions or a pace-maker, consult a medical professional before attending. Notify us of implants or devices that could be affected by prolonged vibrations. Sound experiences can be intense — if you have any concerns, email {{EMAIL}} before booking.

Sensory considerations

We keep scents minimal. If you are sensitive to fragrance, let us know and we will adapt. Lights are dimmed; if complete darkness is an issue we provide soft ambient cues and will ensure a gentle re-entry.
