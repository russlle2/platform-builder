Quick Questions

What should I bring?

Bring a yoga mat, a thick blanket, pillow or bolster, and a water bottle. We provide extras on request.

Are there contraindications?

Pregnancy, epilepsy, and recent major surgery may require clearance. If you have a pacemaker or serious medical condition, please consult your physician and inform us ahead of time.

What is the flow?

We begin with a gentle settling, guided breathwork, a 45–60 minute sound sequence with intentional crescendos and rests, and finish with grounding integration and tea.
