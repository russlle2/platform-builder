Flow & what to expect

Our sessions are intentionally paced. A typical private or group booking includes:

  1. Welcome & intention setting (5–15 min)
  2. Immersive soundscape (30–60 min) — instruments vary; we often combine gongs, crystal bowls, chimes or a monochord drone
  3. Grounding & gentle return (5–15 min) — tuning forks, breath prompts

What to bring

  • Blanket, layers and an eye pillow if you use one
  • A mat or cushion for comfort
  • Water — hydrate before and after

Contraindications — please read

If you are pregnant, have uncontrolled epilepsy, recent inner-ear or neurologic surgery, a pacemaker, or severe cardiovascular conditions, consult your clinician before participating. Certain instruments (e.g. large gongs) produce low-frequency energy — disclose devices or implants when booking. If in doubt, email {{EMAIL}}.
