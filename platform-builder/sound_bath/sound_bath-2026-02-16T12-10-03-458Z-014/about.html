Philosophy & craft

We view sound as a restorative technology. Instruments are chosen for overtone clarity and sympathetic resonance — that means invitations to drop into breath and tissue, not to fill silence with noise. Each session is structured to move from arrival to immersion to gentle re-emergence.

A short session map

  1. Arrival & settling (10 mins) — welcome, intention, breath cues
  2. Immersive sound field (35-45 mins) — layered bowls, chimes, a single gong bloom
  3. Transition & grounding (10 mins) — gentle tuning forks, breath return

What to bring

  • Comfortable layers and a small blanket
  • A mat or yoga-style cushion if you prefer your own
  • An open mind — silence and sensory attention are the work
