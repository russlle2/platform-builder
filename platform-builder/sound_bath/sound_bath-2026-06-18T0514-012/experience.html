Sound Bath

A Supportive Sound Bath Experience Tailored for Postpartum Recovery

We understand the demands of new motherhood. Our Sound Bath sessions are designed with your comfort and recovery in mind. You'll simply lie back, supported by bolsters and blankets, and allow the sounds to wash over you. There's no expectation or effort required from you, only an invitation to receive. This passive yet profound practice offers a dedicated period of uninterrupted rest, helping you to conserve precious energy and replenish your inner resources during this intensive phase.
