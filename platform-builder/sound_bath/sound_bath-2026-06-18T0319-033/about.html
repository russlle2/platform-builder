Sound Bath: A Gentle Approach to Stress Reduction for Seniors

As we age, managing daily stress becomes increasingly important for overall well-being. Our Sound Bath sessions offer a restorative experience, using carefully selected frequencies to encourage deep relaxation and help your nervous system find a more balanced state. It's a comfortable, passive way to unwind and address the subtle tensions that accumulate, promoting a sense of peace without physical exertion.

What exactly happens during a Sound Bath session?

During a Sound Bath, you’ll lie down comfortably while a practitioner plays various instruments like singing bowls and gongs. These sounds create vibrations and frequencies designed to gently calm your mind and body. Your only role is to relax and allow the sounds to wash over you.

Is a Sound Bath safe and appropriate for someone my age?

Absolutely. Our Sound Bath sessions are specifically designed with the comfort and well-being of seniors in mind. It's a non-invasive, gentle practice that requires no physical exertion, making it very safe and beneficial for individuals seeking a peaceful way to reduce stress.

How can Sound Bath help with stress and my nervous system?

The resonant frequencies in a Sound Bath help to shift your brainwave state from active to more relaxed, encouraging your nervous system to move from 'fight or flight' to 'rest and digest.' This promotes a profound sense of calm, helping to reduce chronic stress and improve overall nervous system regulation.

What should I wear or bring to a Sound Bath session?

We recommend wearing comfortable, loose-fitting clothing that allows you to relax fully. You might also consider bringing a personal blanket or cushion for extra comfort, although we provide mats and props to ensure you are cozy throughout the session.
