Understanding Sound Bath: A Gentle Path to Calm for Seniors

A Sound Bath is a deeply relaxing experience where you are bathed in resonant sound waves from various instruments. For our senior community, this means a quiet, comfortable space where you can simply rest and let the gentle vibrations wash over you. It's a wonderful opportunity to unwind, release tension, and connect with a sense of inner quiet, all without any physical exertion.
