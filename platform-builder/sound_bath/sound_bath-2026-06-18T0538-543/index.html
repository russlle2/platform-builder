Frequently Asked Questions

What should I expect during my first Sound Bath session?
You can expect a very relaxed atmosphere. You'll lie comfortably on a mat, often with blankets and pillows, while our practitioner plays various instruments. Your only task is to listen and allow the sounds to soothe you. There's no right or wrong way to experience it; simply relax and be open to the gentle vibrations.
Is the Sound Bath suitable for someone with hearing aids or limited mobility?
Absolutely. The sounds are gentle and resonant, not overly loud. We can adjust volume if needed. For limited mobility, we ensure comfortable seating or lying arrangements are available, and you're welcome to bring any aids that help you feel most at ease. Your comfort is our priority.
How can a Sound Bath help with spiritual growth and inner peace for someone my age?
Many seniors find that the calming nature of a Sound Bath provides a unique opportunity for introspection and quiet contemplation. It can help reduce daily stresses, allowing a clearer space for you to connect with your inner wisdom, reflect on life's journey, and cultivate a deeper sense of contentment and spiritual grounding.
Do I need to bring anything specific to a Sound Bath session?
We recommend wearing comfortable clothing. You might also consider bringing a water bottle. We provide mats, blankets, and pillows, but you're welcome to bring your own if you prefer. Just bring an open heart and a willingness to relax.
