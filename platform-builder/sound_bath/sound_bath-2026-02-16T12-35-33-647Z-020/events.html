Session flow — evening outline

  1. Arrival & settling: tea and soft instructions (10–15 min)
  2. Short breath practice to orient the body (5 min)
  3. Main sound container: layered bowls, gong, chimes (40–50 min)
  4. Re-entry: gentle grounding, especially for standing up (10 min)

Please move at your own pace when leaving; there will be soft lighting and quiet time for integration.

Frequently asked

Can I lie down?
Yes. Most participants lie on mats. Chairs are available on request.
Is it loud?
The session includes resonant low frequencies; it will be felt as vibration more than harsh volume.
Contraindications?
If you have a pacemaker, recent surgery, or are pregnant, please consult with a medical provider and contact us before attending.
