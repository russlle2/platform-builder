What this session supports

  • Deep nervous-system downshift — rhythmic overtone layers encourage parasympathetic activation.
  • Improved sleep & focus — sound patterns help reset habitual arousal.
  • Subtle body realignment — vibrational tones meet fascia and circulation.
  • Community container — held, small-group experience for safety and connection.

How a session unfolds

  1. Arrival & grounding (15 min) — gentle check-in, optional breathwork, settle into your mat.
  2. Opening tone & intention (10 min) — facilitator introduces instruments and safety notes.
  3. Deep immersion (45–60 min) — layered soundscape: crystal bowls, gong, chimes, and occasional tuning forks; moments of silence between waves.
  4. Soft reorientation (10–15 min) — slow return with harp-like monochord tones and tea for grounding.

What to bring

  • Yoga mat or blanket, small pillow, eye mask or scarf
  • Layers for warmth — the body cools during rest
  • Reusable water bottle

Contraindications & safety

Sound baths are deeply relaxing but not appropriate for everyone. Please consult with us before booking if you are pregnant, have a pacemaker, recent head injury, seizure history, or are currently under psychiatric care for severe anxiety or psychosis.
