What to bring

Comfortable clothes, a cozy blanket, and any props that support your nervous system (pillows, eye-mask). For virtual sessions, ensure a quiet, reclining space and quality headphones if preferred.

Contraindications

If you have a cardiac implant, are pregnant, have a history of seizures, or any condition where sustained vibration may affect health, please consult with your clinician and inform the facilitator prior to booking.

Why choose private work?

Private sessions allow intention-led sound design: we can focus tone, pacing, and the energetic field to match clinical needs or desired outcomes. This is suitable for deeper therapeutic integration beyond group offerings.
