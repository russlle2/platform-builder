Why people choose our baths

Sensorial clarity

Sound textures are arranged to reduce overstimulation and invite restful attention — slow crescendos, sustained tones, and subtle overtones.

Clinical calm

Guided breathing and body-awareness cues are embedded; language is simple, precise, and trauma-informed.

Hybrid delivery

Attend in person or join remotely — the same structure, adapted to the medium to preserve safety and depth.

Typical session flow

  1. Arrival & grounding — brief intake, settling on blankets. You may bring a pillow or use ours.
  2. Intent setting — minimal prompts, optional silence to set an intention.
  3. Sound journey — a curated blend of crystal bowls, chimes, and soft tuning forks; subtle harmonic layering to support rest.
  4. Integration — a gentle return with silence and a few reflective minutes to ground the experience.
What to bring

A yoga mat or blanket, a small cushion, and an eye-covering are all you need. Dress in comfortable layers.

Contraindications & safety

If you have uncontrolled epilepsy, are in the first trimester of pregnancy, have a pacemaker, or severe auditory hypersensitivity, please consult a clinician before attending and contact us at {{EMAIL}} for support. Sound can be intense; we adapt volume and instruments when needed.
