Quick note on contraindications

Sound baths are deeply sensory. If you are pregnant, have epilepsy, recent concussion, uncontrolled high blood pressure, or severe psychiatric conditions, please consult your healthcare provider and notify us before booking. We may tailor or decline modalities to prioritize safety.

Session flow — what to expect
  1. Arrival & brief intake: 10–15 minutes for goals and any contraindications
  2. Set-up: settle into comfortable space; facilitator calibrates instruments
  3. Immersion: 30–50 minutes of guided sound, breath prompts and gentle transitions using tuned instruments
  4. Closure: slow reorientation, journaling prompts, and optional check-in

FAQ
How long are sessions?

Group immersions are typically 60–70 minutes including arrival and closure. Private sessions range 45–90 minutes depending on format.

Are instruments loud?

Instruments like crystal bowls and chimes are delivered with careful dynamics; facilitator adjusts volume for comfort.
