Questions — clear, concise answers

Below you’ll find operational details, safety notes, and guidance for first-time attendees. If you need more, contact {{EMAIL}}.

What should I bring?
Comfort is primary: a blanket or yoga mat, a cushion for your head, and an eye-covering if you prefer complete darkness. We provide extra blankets and blocks on request.
Are sound baths safe?
In general, yes. However, some situations require caution: uncontrolled epilepsy, certain cardiac devices (e.g., pacemakers), and acute auditory hyperacusis. Pregnant people in the first trimester should consult their care provider. If you have concerns, email {{EMAIL}} in advance and we will adapt the experience.
What instruments do you use?
We rotate instruments to create a balanced texture: gong for broad wash, crystal bowls for overtone richness, a monochord for grounding drone, and soft chimes for transitions. Volume and proximity are adjusted to comfort.
How long is a group event?
Group sound baths typically last 75 minutes: 10–15 min arrival, 50–55 min sound journey, and 10 min integration.
What if I’m pregnant, have implants, or epilepsy?
Please reach out prior to booking. Some conditions require clinician approval or a modified format. We will discuss alternatives like quieter instrument sets or private sessions.
Do you offer remote sessions?
Yes. Our hybrid model provides remote-safe cues and specially mixed audio to preserve depth. Remote attendees receive a short prep file and guidance about speaker/headphone setup.
Cancellation policy?
Cancellations made at least 48 hours in advance receive a full refund or credit. Within 48 hours, refunds are by discretion; transfers to another date are encouraged.
Quick tips

Arrive 10–15 minutes early, silence your phone, and avoid caffeine immediately before the session. Hydrate gently after the event.
