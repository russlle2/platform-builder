Notes on bookings & policy

All bookings require a deposit to hold a spot. Cancellations with 72+ hours notice receive a full refund; within 72 hours, deposits are held as credit. For corporate and multi-session packages, contracts are provided to detail responsibilities and travel fees if applicable.

Contraindications: if you have a pacemaker, serious cardiovascular condition, or are pregnant, please consult your clinician before participating. Our events use musical instruments including gong, crystal bowls, and occasional percussion. If you have special accommodation needs, please get in touch.
