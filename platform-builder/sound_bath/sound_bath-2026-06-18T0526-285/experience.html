Sound Bath

Your First Sound Bath: No Wellness Experience Needed

Never tried a Sound Bath? No problem at all! This isn't about mastering a technique or having prior meditation experience. Just come as you are, perhaps after a tough lecture or a long study session. We'll explain everything simply and clearly. All you need to do is lie down, get comfortable, and let the sounds wash over you. It's a completely accessible way to give your mind and body a restorative break from the demands of your student life.
