Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly happens during a Sound Bath session?
During a Sound Bath, you'll lie comfortably on a mat while plays various instruments like gongs and singing bowls. The sounds create vibrations that help you relax deeply, reduce stress, and regulate your nervous system. You just need to listen and allow yourself to be present.
I'm a busy parent; how will a Sound Bath help with my daily stress?
A Sound Bath offers a dedicated time for your nervous system to downshift. This helps to interrupt the constant stress response often experienced by parents. Many find it leads to feeling more grounded, less reactive, and more capable of managing the day's demands after a session.
Do I need any special equipment or experience to attend a Sound Bath?
Absolutely not! You don't need any prior experience. Just come as you are, dressed comfortably. We provide mats and blankets, though you're welcome to bring your own pillow or eye mask for extra comfort. The main requirement is an openness to relax and receive the sounds.
How often should I attend a Sound Bath to see benefits for my stress levels?
The frequency that works best for you will depend on your personal needs. Many parents find that attending a Sound Bath regularly, perhaps once a month, helps maintain a calmer nervous system and consistently reduces their overall stress. Even a single session can offer significant relief.
