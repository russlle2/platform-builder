How can a Sound Bath help my posture when I'm constantly lifting and bending for my children?

A Sound Bath helps by deeply relaxing your muscles and nervous system, which can release chronic tension that contributes to poor posture. This allows your body to naturally find a more aligned state. It also enhances your body awareness, so you can more consciously adjust your posture during daily activities.

I'm always stressed. Will I be able to relax enough to benefit from a Sound Bath?

Yes, many parents find the immersive nature of a Sound Bath particularly effective for stress reduction. The specific frequencies and vibrations gently guide your brain into a meditative state, even if you're new to relaxation practices. You don't need to 'try' to relax; the sound does the work for you.

What should I expect during my first Sound Bath session?

You'll lie comfortably on a mat, often with blankets and bolsters, while Dr. Morgan Ellis plays various instruments like singing bowls and gongs around you. The experience is passive; you simply listen and allow the sounds to wash over you. Most people experience deep relaxation, and some even fall into a light sleep.

Is a Sound Bath a substitute for physical therapy or chiropractic care for postural issues?

A Sound Bath is a complementary practice, not a replacement for medical or therapeutic care. It excels at reducing stress and muscle tension, which can significantly support and enhance the benefits of physical therapy or chiropractic adjustments by preparing your body for optimal alignment.
