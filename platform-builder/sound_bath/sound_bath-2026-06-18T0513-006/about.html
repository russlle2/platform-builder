How can a Sound Bath specifically help with chronic illness or injury recovery?

Our Sound Baths are tailored to support your nervous system during recovery. By promoting deep relaxation and reducing stress hormones, they can help create a more optimal environment for your body to heal and manage discomfort, rather than adding to your system's burden.

Will the sounds be too intense or overwhelming for my sensitive system?

No, we use gentle, carefully chosen instruments and play at volumes designed to be comforting and nurturing. Our priority is to create a soothing experience that helps regulate your nervous system, not overstimulate it. Your comfort and ease are paramount.

What should I expect to feel during a Sound Bath session?

Many people report feeling a profound sense of calm, a reduction in mental chatter, and physical relaxation. Some may experience gentle tingling or a sense of weightlessness. The most common feedback is a deep sense of peace and a refreshed nervous system, which is incredibly valuable during recovery.

Is there anything specific I need to do to prepare for my first Sound Bath?

Just come as you are! We recommend wearing comfortable clothing and perhaps bringing a water bottle. Our space is designed for your comfort, with soft mats and blankets available. Your only task is to allow yourself to relax and receive the calming sounds.
