Common questions — concise answers

What should I expect during a session?

We begin with a brief orientation, introduce the tone field, guide a few minutes of breath, and then let the layers of sound support rest. Return cues are clear, and we allow time at the end for gentle reorientation.

How long is a typical group event?

Most public gatherings run 45–55 minutes. We offer shorter 'micro' sessions and extended practices at select times.

Do I need experience to attend?

No. Sessions are designed for newcomers and seasoned listeners. We give simple instructions and options for anyone who wants quieter adaptations.

What should I bring?

Comfortable clothing, a mat or blanket if you like, and optionally pillows. We provide limited bolsters; reserves recommended for private sessions.

How do you accommodate different needs?

Reach out before your visit and we'll suggest seating, reduced volume, or a private slot. Email {{EMAIL}}.

Safety & contraindications

Please review before booking. If you have serious medical implants (e.g., pacemakers), recent head trauma, ongoing seizures, or serious psychiatric episodes, consult a clinician prior to attending. For pregnancy or other concerns, check with your provider and tell us before arrival.
