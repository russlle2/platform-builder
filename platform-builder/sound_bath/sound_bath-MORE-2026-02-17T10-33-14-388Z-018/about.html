Care notes & safety

Sound baths are gentle, non-invasive experiences. Please note the following contraindications and reach out before booking if you have any of these conditions.

- Recent skull fracture, post-surgical cranial implants, or any implanted electronic device (e.g., pacemaker) must consult a medical professional first.
- Pregnancy in the first trimester: check with your practitioner if unsure.
- Epilepsy, vertigo disorders, or acute psychiatric conditions should consult a clinician before attending.
