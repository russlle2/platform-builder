What exactly happens during a Sound Bath, and how will it help my burnout?

During a Sound Bath, you'll lie down comfortably while Dr. Morgan Ellis plays various instruments like crystal singing bowls and gongs. The gentle, immersive sounds guide your mind into a deeply relaxed state, helping to soothe your overstimulated nervous system. This deep relaxation is key for releasing the chronic stress associated with burnout, allowing your mind to rest and your spirit to find quiet.

I'm always on the go. Will I really be able to relax enough for a Sound Bath to make a difference?

Absolutely. It's completely normal to feel restless initially, especially when you're accustomed to constant activity. The beauty of a Sound Bath is that the sounds do the work for you. There's no need to 'try' to relax; simply allow the vibrations to wash over you. Most people find themselves surprisingly able to surrender and experience profound peace, even if they arrive feeling highly stressed.

How often should I attend a Sound Bath to genuinely combat my burnout?

While even a single session can offer immediate relief, consistent practice tends to yield the most lasting benefits for managing and recovering from burnout. We often recommend starting with a few sessions spaced a week or two apart to build a foundation of calm, then adjusting based on your personal needs and how you feel your inner peace developing.

Is a Sound Bath suitable for someone new to spiritual practices or meditation?

Yes, it's an excellent entry point! A Sound Bath requires no prior experience with meditation or spiritual practices. There are no techniques to learn, just an invitation to listen and receive. The sounds themselves provide a natural focal point, making it easier for your mind to settle than traditional silent meditation might, offering a gentle path to inner quiet.
