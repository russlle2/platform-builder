Finding Your Anchor: Sound Bath for Spiritual Grounding After Illness or Injury

We’ll use resonant tones to quiet external noise, allowing you to find a stable center and cultivate a deep sense of spiritual peace, even amidst ongoing recovery. It's about building resilience from within.
