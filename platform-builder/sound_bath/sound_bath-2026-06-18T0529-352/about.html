How can a Sound Bath specifically help with my postpartum physical pain?

Our Sound Bath sessions utilize precise sound frequencies and vibrations that are known to gently penetrate muscle tissue, reducing inflammation, easing tension, and improving circulation. This can provide significant relief for common postpartum discomforts like back pain, pelvic soreness, and overall body aches, helping your body to recover more comfortably.

Is the Sound Bath safe for my postpartum body, especially if I'm still recovering?

Absolutely. Our Sound Bath sessions are designed with the utmost care for new mothers in their postpartum recovery. You will be comfortably reclined, and the sound experience is non-invasive and deeply gentle. We always recommend consulting with your healthcare provider about any new practices during your recovery, but sound baths are generally considered very safe and beneficial for soothing the body.

Will a Sound Bath help with the emotional and physical exhaustion of new motherhood?

Yes, profoundly. While focusing on physical pain relief, the deep relaxation induced by a Sound Bath also significantly calms your nervous system. This can alleviate the overwhelming feelings of exhaustion and stress common in new mothers, allowing for mental clarity and emotional restoration alongside physical rejuvenation. It's a holistic approach to your well-being.

What should I expect during my first postpartum Sound Bath session?

You'll be welcomed into a tranquil space, guided to a comfortable position, typically lying down, and invited to simply relax. Dr. Morgan Ellis will use various instruments like crystal bowls and gongs to create an immersive soundscape. There's nothing for you to 'do' except to allow the sounds to wash over you, facilitating deep physical and mental release. Many new mothers report feeling profoundly rested and eased of physical tension afterwards.
