Frequently Asked Questions

How does a Sound Bath specifically support recovery from chronic illness or injury?
A Sound Bath directly addresses the heightened stress and sympathetic nervous system activation often associated with chronic conditions. The specific frequencies and sustained tones encourage your brain to shift into more relaxed states, reducing cortisol levels and promoting parasympathetic nervous system dominance, which is essential for your body's repair processes and overall regulation. It's about giving your body the space to calm down and focus on healing, rather than being constantly on alert.
Do I need to do anything special or have prior experience to benefit from a Sound Bath?
Absolutely not! Your only requirement is to arrive and allow yourself to be comfortable. There's no meditation experience necessary, no specific postures to hold, and nothing to 'get right.' You simply lie down, get cozy with blankets and pillows, and let the sounds guide you into a state of deep relaxation. We create a supportive environment where you can simply be, without effort or expectation, making it accessible for anyone, regardless of their physical limitations or energy levels.
Will a Sound Bath interfere with my current medical treatments or physical therapy?
A Sound Bath is designed to complement, not replace, your existing medical treatments and therapies. It acts as a powerful support for stress reduction and nervous system regulation, which can enhance your body's ability to respond to other forms of care. We encourage you to discuss any new complementary practices with your healthcare provider, but generally, Sound Bath is a non-invasive, gentle practice that can be safely integrated into your overall recovery plan.
What can I expect to feel during and after a Sound Bath session?
During a session, you might experience a range of sensations, from deep relaxation and a quiet mind to subtle vibrations in your body. Many clients describe feeling incredibly peaceful, calm, and even a sense of lightness. Afterward, most people report feeling refreshed, grounded, and noticeably less stressed. You may find improved sleep, reduced anxiety, and a greater sense of well-being, all of which contribute positively to your recovery journey.
