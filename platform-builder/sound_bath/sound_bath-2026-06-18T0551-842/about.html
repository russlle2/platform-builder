How can a Sound Bath specifically help my postpartum posture?

Sound Bath sessions encourage deep relaxation, which helps release chronic tension in muscles often strained during pregnancy and early motherhood. By calming your nervous system, your body can naturally soften and realign, supporting better postural habits and reducing discomfort in areas like your upper back and hips.

Is Sound Bath suitable for all stages of postpartum recovery?

Yes, our Sound Bath sessions are designed to be gentle and restorative for all stages of postpartum recovery. Whether you're weeks or months into your journey, the non-invasive nature of sound therapy provides a safe way to support your physical and emotional well-being without requiring any strenuous movement.

What should I expect to feel during a Sound Bath focused on body awareness?

During a Sound Bath, you can expect to lie comfortably and simply listen. The resonant vibrations from various instruments can help quiet your mind, allowing you to become more attuned to sensations within your body. Many new mothers report feeling a heightened sense of their core, a release of physical tension, and a renewed feeling of being grounded in their body.

Can I bring my baby to a Sound Bath session?

Our Sound Bath sessions are designed for deep rest and internal focus for the mother. We encourage you to arrange childcare so you can fully immerse yourself in the experience. This dedicated time for yourself is vital for your recovery and allows for uninterrupted focus on your postural health and body awareness.
