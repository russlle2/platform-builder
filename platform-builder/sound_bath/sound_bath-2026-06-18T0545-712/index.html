Frequently Asked Questions

Is a Sound Bath safe for me during my postpartum recovery period?
Yes, our Sound Bath sessions are designed with your postpartum needs in mind. We create a gentle, supportive environment, and is attentive to your comfort throughout the session. Please inform us of any specific concerns, and we can discuss accommodations to ensure your safety and relaxation.
How can Sound Bath specifically help me with feelings of overwhelm as a new mother?
The resonant tones in a Sound Bath help to quiet the mind and settle the nervous system, which can be incredibly beneficial when you're feeling overwhelmed. This deep state of relaxation allows you to create emotional space, promoting clarity and a renewed sense of inner calm, helping you better manage the demands of new motherhood.
Do I need any prior experience with meditation or Sound Baths?
Absolutely not. Our Sound Bath sessions are accessible to everyone, regardless of prior experience. Your only requirement is to arrive, get comfortable, and allow yourself to receive the gentle sounds. will guide you through the process, ensuring you feel supported and at ease.
What should I expect during a Sound Bath session at ?
You'll lie comfortably, often on a mat, while plays various instruments like singing bowls and gongs. The sounds create an immersive experience, encouraging deep relaxation and a meditative state. Many new mothers report feeling profoundly rested, peaceful, and more connected to themselves and their babies afterward.
