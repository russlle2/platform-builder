What to expect

Sessions begin with a short orientation, a guided breath phase, then a sequence of tuned instruments. You lie or sit comfortably while a gentle sound field shifts in texture and volume to encourage ease and mental clarity.

  • Doors open 15 minutes before start; doors close 10 minutes after start.
  • Wear comfortable layers; we provide bolsters on request.
  • Accessible entry and quiet rest area available.
Contraindications

Not recommended during pregnancy without medical sign-off, for people with uncontrolled epilepsy, or if you use a pacemaker without clearance. If you have recent surgery or any condition where sound or lowered awareness could interfere with care, consult your clinician first.
