Private resonance sessions

One-to-one time designed to adapt sound, pacing, and touchless modulation to your needs. We use same calibrated instruments and a trauma-aware approach, but the structure is tailored: choice of guided integration, silent rest, or active breathwork.

How it runs

  1. Pre-session conversation (5–10 min) to set context and contraindications.
  2. Sound field spanning 30–75 minutes depending on length chosen.
  3. Short debrief and next steps.
Health & safety

If you have active implants (e.g., pacemaker), significant cardiovascular conditions, are pregnant, or recently had surgery, request a clinician clearance before booking. If in doubt, contact us at {{EMAIL}}.
