Common questions and clear answers

Short, direct answers so you can decide with confidence. For anything else, reach us at {{EMAIL}}.

What should I bring?
Comfortable clothing, a blanket or mat, and a small pillow if you like. We provide mats on request.
Are sessions safe for people with hearing sensitivity?
Yes — we work to keep sound levels moderate and predictable. If you have hyperacusis or other sensitivity, contact us in advance to arrange a partial-masked spot or a seat at the edge of the room.
Do I need to be a skilled listener or meditator?
No. Sessions are structured to be accessible whether you practice daily or are entirely new to sound work.
Can I speak to staff about medical concerns?
Yes. We include a contraindication note: people with recent head trauma, uncontrolled seizure disorders, or acute psychiatric conditions should consult a clinician before attending. If unsure, email {{EMAIL}}.
