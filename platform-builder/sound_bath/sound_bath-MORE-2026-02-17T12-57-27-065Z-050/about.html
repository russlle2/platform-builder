Our origin story

A small collective began convening sessions with a single idea: sound can provide reference points for attention. Over time the gatherings became streamlined — shorter frames, fewer cues, clearer intention — so that the practice is approachable and repeatable. We run both communal sessions and individualized work that borrows the same structure.

We operate from a membership model for people who want regular practice and offer drop-in seats for neighbors exploring the format.

Structure
Three-phase session
  1. Orientation & intention (5–10 min)
  2. Primary sound field (30–40 min)
  3. Reintegration & notes (5–10 min)
Focus
We tune toward clarity — not sedation — so you leave with a small practical change you can take into your day.
