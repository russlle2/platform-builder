Contraindications & safety

Sound-based sessions may not be appropriate for everyone. If you have epilepsy, recent traumatic brain injury, certain cardiovascular conditions, or are pregnant, consult your healthcare provider before attending. Notify staff of any sensory or medical concerns in advance. Our gatherings are supportive but not a substitute for medical care.
