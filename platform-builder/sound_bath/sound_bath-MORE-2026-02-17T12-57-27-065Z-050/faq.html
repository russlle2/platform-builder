Questions & practical details

Below are answers to frequent questions and guidance so you can plan a visit with confidence.

What should I expect in a session?

Sessions are guided with a short orientation, a continuous sound field, and a brief return to sitting. You will be seated or reclined; the room is dim and quiet before we begin.

Is the experience religious or meditative?

No. The format is secular and practical: we use sound and timing to create a structure for attention and rest. You are welcome to bring your own intentions.

Contraindications & safety notes

If you have a history of epilepsy, uncontrolled psychiatric conditions, implantable medical devices, are pregnant with complications, or have any condition where altered states are a concern, consult a medical professional before attending. If you take medications that affect consciousness, please check with your provider. These gatherings are not a substitute for medical care.

My seating needs

We provide mats, bolsters, and chairs. If you need particular accommodations, email {{EMAIL}} before your visit.

How early should I arrive?

Plan to arrive 10–15 minutes before start to settle in. Doors close a few minutes after start time to preserve the field.
