How we make sound that meets you

We shape gatherings so sound acts like a careful passage — not a spectacle. Each offering is assembled from simple materials: tuned bowls, breath, and attentive pacing. Expect warm layers, measured rests, and clear cues so you can arrive without needing to perform.

Our facilitators train across somatic, breath, and acoustic practices. We keep groups small and outline clear safety practices before each session.

A short lineage

This work began as a study in resonance — how tones meet the body and the edge where attention shifts. We borrow from older traditions and keep what works: slow entrance, predictable pacing, and sound choices that invite rather than demand.
