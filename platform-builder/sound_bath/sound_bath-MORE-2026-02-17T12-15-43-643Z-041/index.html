Contraindications:

Sound sessions are low-impact but not appropriate for everyone. If you are pregnant, have a history of seizures, uncontrolled psychiatric conditions, or sensitivity to extended loud vibrations, please consult your healthcare provider before attending and contact us for accommodations.

Questions and hesitations

Will I be forced to talk?

No. Sharing is always optional. The core of the evening is listening and resting with sound.

How loud is it?

We favor sustained tones at comfortable listening levels. If you are sensitive, tell us ahead of time and we can adapt placement or seating.

What if I fall asleep?

That’s fine. Many people rest deeply; participants leave feeling settled. Consider sitting at the back if you prefer easy exit.
