Questions people bring

Practical answers so you can arrive with less guesswork. If you still need help, reach out at {{EMAIL}}.

What should I wear?
Comfortable layers that let you relax. Bare feet or socks are fine. You will usually lie or sit on floor props; bring clothing that stays warm when still.
How loud is it?
Sound is mixed for warmth, not startling peaks. We avoid abrupt high-volume strikes. If you’re sensitive to sound, let us know and we will place you where it’s gentlest.
Can I fall asleep?
Yes. Falling asleep is a normal response and welcomed. We include clear cues at the end to help reorient.
Contraindications
If you have recent head injury, epilepsy, uncontrolled mental health conditions, or are pregnant without medical clearance, please consult your provider before attending. Inform us of implants or devices sensitive to vibration. This information is not a replacement for medical advice.
If in doubt, contact your clinician. For urgent medical concerns do not attend.
