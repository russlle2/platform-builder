I'm always busy. How long is a Sound Bath session and what should I expect?

Our typical Sound Bath sessions are 60 minutes long, including a few minutes at the beginning to settle in and a moment at the end to gently reorient. You simply lie comfortably on a mat, covered with a blanket if you wish, and close your eyes. Your only task is to listen and allow the sounds to wash over you. It's an efficient way to find deep rest and release in your demanding schedule.

I have no experience with meditation or wellness. Is a Sound Bath still for me?

Absolutely. A Sound Bath is perfect for you because it requires no prior experience or effort. You don't need to 'do' anything, meditate, or clear your mind. The sounds do the work for you, gently guiding your brain into a relaxed state. It's an accessible way to experience deep calm and physical relief, especially when traditional relaxation methods feel like too much work.

Can a Sound Bath really help with my physical aches and pains from burnout?

Many clients find significant relief from stress-related physical discomforts like tense shoulders, neck pain, and headaches after a Sound Bath. The deep relaxation induced by the sounds helps to calm the nervous system, which in turn can reduce muscle tension, improve circulation, and alleviate the physical manifestations of chronic stress and burnout in your body.

What should I wear or bring to a Sound Bath session?

We recommend wearing comfortable, loose-fitting clothing that allows you to fully relax. You'll be lying down for the duration of the session, so dressing warmly in layers is also a good idea as body temperature can drop during deep relaxation. We provide mats, blankets, and pillows, but you are welcome to bring your own eye mask or small comfort items if you prefer.
