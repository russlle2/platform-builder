Frequently Asked Questions

How does a Sound Bath specifically benefit my posture?
A Sound Bath encourages deep relaxation, which can release muscle tension that often contributes to poor posture. The resonant vibrations help you become more attuned to your body's subtle cues, allowing you to gently re-align and hold yourself with greater ease and awareness.
Is the Sound Bath experience suitable for someone with limited mobility?
Absolutely. Our Sound Bath sessions are designed to be accessible and gentle. You simply recline comfortably, and the therapeutic sounds do the work. There's no physical exertion required, making it ideal for supporting your postural health without strain.
What should I expect during my first Sound Bath at ?
You will be welcomed into a tranquil space. After settling comfortably, you will experience a sequence of rich, resonant sounds from various instruments. Your role is simply to listen and allow the sounds to wash over you, fostering a deep sense of calm and heightened body awareness. We are here to guide you every step of the way.
How often should I attend a Sound Bath to see improvements in my body awareness?
Many of our clients report a noticeable shift in body awareness after just one session. For sustained benefits and to truly integrate these insights into your daily posture, regular sessions are recommended. We can discuss a personalized schedule that fits your preferences and goals during your initial consultation.
