How can Sound Bath specifically help with chronic illness recovery?

Sound Bath sessions offer deep relaxation, which is vital for your body's repair processes. The gentle vibrations help calm your nervous system, reducing stress that can hinder recovery and immune function. This allows your body to focus its energy on healing and rebuilding your core vitality.

Is Sound Bath suitable if I have limited mobility or energy due to my condition?

Absolutely. Sound Bath requires no physical exertion. You simply lie down or sit comfortably, fully supported. The experience is designed to meet you exactly where you are, providing profound rest and subtle support without draining your precious energy reserves.

What should I expect during a Sound Bath session for vitality building?

You'll be invited to lie down comfortably, often with blankets and pillows. Dr. Morgan Ellis will then play a variety of instruments, creating soothing soundscapes. Your role is simply to relax and allow the sounds to wash over you. Many people report feeling deeply rested, a sense of inner calm, and a subtle revitalization afterward.

How often should I attend Sound Bath sessions to support my immune system?

Consistency is key for sustained support. Many of our clients in recovery find that regular weekly or bi-weekly Sound Bath sessions help maintain a state of calm, reduce stress, and continually support their immune system and vitality. We can discuss a schedule that best fits your recovery needs during your initial consultation.
