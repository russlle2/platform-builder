Your Calming Sound Bath Session Experience

During a Sound Bath at Sound Bath Studio, you'll comfortably recline while I, Dr. Morgan Ellis, guide you through a soundscape of bowls and chimes. There's nothing for you to 'do' except listen and allow the sounds to wash over you. Many clients describe feeling a sense of calm and mental refreshment, like a soft mental dusting that leaves your thoughts feeling clearer and more accessible.
