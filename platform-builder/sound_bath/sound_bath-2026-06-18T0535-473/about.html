What is a Sound Bath and how does it benefit my cognitive function?

A Sound Bath is a gentle, immersive experience where you relax and listen to various harmonic instruments. For your cognitive function, these calming sounds can reduce stress, which in turn helps improve focus, memory, and overall mental clarity, making your thoughts feel more accessible and organized.

Is a Sound Bath safe and suitable for seniors aged 60 and above?

Absolutely. Sound Baths are a non-invasive, gentle practice, making them exceptionally safe and suitable for seniors. We prioritize your comfort and well-being, ensuring every aspect of the session supports your physical and mental ease without any strenuous activity.

What should I expect during my first Sound Bath session for mental clarity?

You can expect a peaceful, comfortable experience. You'll lie down or sit comfortably, fully clothed, and simply listen to the sounds. Many people report feeling deeply relaxed, followed by a sense of mental lightness and improved focus in the hours and days after their session, like a mental fog has lifted.

How often should I attend a Sound Bath to see improvements in mental sharpness?

While individual experiences vary, many clients find that regular, consistent Sound Bath sessions — perhaps once or twice a month — help maintain and even enhance their mental sharpness over time. It's about creating a consistent rhythm of mental calm and rejuvenation for your mind.
