Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly happens during a Sound Bath, and how will it help my parenting stress?
During a Sound Bath, you'll lie comfortably while plays various instruments like singing bowls and gongs. The sounds create vibrations that encourage your brain to slow down, shifting from active beta waves to calmer alpha and theta states. This directly helps downregulate your nervous system, reducing the physiological indicators of stress and helping you feel more centered and less reactive to everyday parenting triggers.
I'm always multitasking. Can I really relax enough in a Sound Bath to feel a difference?
Yes, absolutely. The design of our Sound Bath sessions is specifically to make it easy for your nervous system to disengage from constant 'doing.' You don't need to 'try' to relax; the resonant frequencies do the work for you. Many parents find that even with a busy mind, the sounds gently guide them into a deep state of rest, providing a mental and emotional break that's hard to achieve otherwise.
Is a Sound Bath safe for everyone, especially if I'm feeling particularly overwhelmed?
Generally, Sound Baths are very safe and beneficial. However, if you have certain medical conditions like epilepsy, a pacemaker, or are in the first trimester of pregnancy, it's always best to consult your doctor before attending. For general overwhelm, it's often incredibly helpful, offering a much-needed reset for your stress response system. We prioritize your comfort and can discuss any concerns you have.
How often should I attend a Sound Bath to see lasting effects on my stress levels?
The frequency that works best for you can vary. Many parents find that regular sessions, perhaps once a week or bi-weekly, help to maintain a more regulated nervous system and build resilience against daily stressors. Think of it as consistent self-care for your brain. Even occasional sessions can provide a significant reset when you're feeling particularly overwhelmed.
