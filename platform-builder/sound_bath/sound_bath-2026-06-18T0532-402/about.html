How can a Sound Bath specifically help my postpartum mental clarity?

A Sound Bath provides a dedicated space for your mind to rest and recalibrate. The specific frequencies and vibrations encourage your brain to move into a state of deep relaxation, which can reduce the mental fog and overwhelm often experienced postpartum, helping you regain focus and a sense of calm.

What should I expect during my first Sound Bath session?

You'll lie down comfortably on a mat, often with blankets and pillows for support. Dr. Morgan Ellis will then play various instruments, creating an immersive soundscape. Your only task is to relax and let the sounds wash over you. There's no right or wrong way to experience it; simply be open to the gentle mental quiet it can bring.

Will I be able to fully relax in a Sound Bath if my mind is constantly racing?

Yes, absolutely. The beauty of a Sound Bath is that the sounds themselves do much of the work. Even if your mind starts out racing, the consistent and soothing vibrations gently guide your brain towards a more peaceful state. Many new mothers find it surprisingly effective for quieting the internal dialogue and achieving a sense of mental stillness.

How often should I attend a Sound Bath to see benefits for my cognitive wellness?

The frequency is truly up to you and what feels right for your schedule and needs. Many new mothers find that regular sessions, even weekly or bi-weekly, help maintain a consistent level of mental clarity and reduce stress. Even a single session can offer a much-needed mental break and a fresh perspective.
