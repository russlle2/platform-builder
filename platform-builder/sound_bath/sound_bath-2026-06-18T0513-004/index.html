Frequently Asked Questions

How does a Sound Bath specifically help with physical stress from training?
A Sound Bath uses specific frequencies and vibrations that encourage your parasympathetic nervous system to activate. For athletes, this means your body can switch from a high-alert state, common after intense training, into a mode of deep relaxation. This helps reduce muscle tension, lower cortisol levels, and promotes the restorative processes vital for your recovery and performance.
Will a Sound Bath interfere with my current training schedule or recovery methods?
Not at all! A Sound Bath is a complementary practice designed to enhance your existing recovery strategies. It provides a unique form of rest that supports your body’s natural ability to recuperate, without physical exertion. Many athletes find it integrates seamlessly, actually making their other recovery methods, like stretching or massage, even more effective by priming the body for relaxation.
I'm new to Sound Baths. Do I need any special experience or preparation?
Absolutely not! You don't need any prior experience with meditation or sound practices. Just come as you are, dressed comfortably, and be open to simply lying down and listening. We provide everything you need to be comfortable. The sounds will do the work, guiding your nervous system to a state of calm. Your only job is to relax.
How often should I attend a Sound Bath to see benefits for my athletic performance and stress levels?
The frequency often depends on your training intensity and personal stress levels. Many athletes find that attending a Sound Bath once every week or two significantly helps manage physical and mental stress, aiding consistent recovery. Others prefer to come before or after particularly demanding training blocks or competitions. We can discuss what rhythm might work best for your unique routine.
