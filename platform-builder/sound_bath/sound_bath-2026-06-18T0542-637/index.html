Frequently Asked Questions

How does a Sound Bath specifically aid immune support for high-achievers?
Chronic stress, common for high-achievers, can suppress immune function. Our Sound Bath sessions activate the parasympathetic nervous system, lowering cortisol and supporting immune cell efficiency. This helps your body allocate resources more effectively to defense and recovery, rather than constant stress response.
Is there a scientific basis for Sound Bath improving vitality?
Yes, research indicates that specific sound frequencies can influence brainwave states, heart rate variability, and stress hormone levels. By facilitating deep relaxation, Sound Bath practice helps conserve and regenerate energy, improving your overall vitality and mental sharpness for sustained performance.
What should I expect during my first Sound Bath session?
You'll lie comfortably, fully clothed, as I guide you through a sonic landscape using various instruments. The experience is passive; your only task is to relax and allow the sounds to wash over you. Most clients report feeling deeply rested and mentally clear afterward, with a noticeable reduction in tension.
How frequently should I engage in Sound Bath for optimal benefits?
For entrepreneurs and high-achievers managing significant stress, consistent engagement is key. Many clients find weekly or bi-weekly sessions provide the most effective support for maintaining immune resilience and sustaining high vitality. We can discuss a personalized frequency during your initial consultation.
