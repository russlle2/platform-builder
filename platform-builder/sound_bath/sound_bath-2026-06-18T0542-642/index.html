Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Sound Bath specifically help my postpartum immune system?
During your postpartum recovery, your body is working hard. A Sound Bath helps by promoting deep relaxation, which reduces stress and anxiety. When your stress levels decrease, your body can better direct its energy towards immune function and overall restoration, helping you feel stronger and more resilient.
I'm a new mother and feel constantly tired. Will a Sound Bath help me regain my vitality?
Absolutely. The gentle, immersive sounds in a Sound Bath guide your body into a state of profound rest, often deeper than regular sleep. This restorative state allows your body to conserve and rebuild energy, helping to combat fatigue and enhancing your overall sense of vitality, which is so crucial in your postpartum period.
Is a Sound Bath safe for me if I'm still recovering from childbirth?
Yes, our Sound Bath sessions are designed to be very gentle and are safe for new mothers in their postpartum recovery. You'll simply lie down comfortably, or sit if that's better for you. There's no physical exertion required, just relaxation. If you have any specific concerns, it's always best to consult with your healthcare provider first.
What should I expect during my first Sound Bath session at ?
You'll be welcomed into a warm, comfortable space. We'll invite you to lie down on a mat with blankets and pillows, ensuring you're completely at ease. Then, will play various instruments, creating soothing soundscapes. Your only task is to relax and allow the sounds to wash over you, supporting your rest and rejuvenation.
