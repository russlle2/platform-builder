Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Sound Bath specifically help my immune system during postpartum recovery?
During a Sound Bath, the deep relaxation you experience can significantly lower stress hormones. Reduced stress is directly linked to a stronger immune response, helping your body focus its energy on repair and protecting you from illness as you navigate the demands of new motherhood.
Will a Sound Bath help me feel less tired and more vital after childbirth?
Absolutely. The profound rest achieved in a Sound Bath allows your body to enter a restorative state, similar to deep sleep. This deep rest helps to replenish your energy reserves, calm your nervous system, and contribute to an overall feeling of renewed vitality, helping you manage the fatigue often associated with postpartum.
Is a Sound Bath safe for new mothers, especially if I'm still recovering physically?
Yes, our Sound Bath sessions are designed to be very gentle and safe. You'll simply lie down comfortably, wrapped in a blanket, and allow the sounds to wash over you. There's no physical exertion required, making it an ideal, non-strenuous way to support your body's healing during postpartum recovery.
What should I expect during my first postpartum Sound Bath session?
You'll be invited to lie down in a comfortable, quiet space. will use various instruments like singing bowls and gongs to create soothing vibrations. Your only job is to relax and let the sounds guide you into a state of deep calm. Many new mothers find it helps them release tension and promotes a sense of inner peace, which is vital for your immune system and overall well-being.
