What exactly happens during a Sound Bath session?

During a Sound Bath, you'll lie down or sit comfortably, fully clothed. I’ll play various instruments like singing bowls and gongs, creating gentle, resonant sounds. You simply relax and allow the sounds to wash over you, which can help quiet your mind and soothe your body, often leading to a sense of deep calm and physical relaxation.

Is a Sound Bath suitable if I have specific mobility concerns or pain?

Yes, absolutely. Our Sound Bath sessions are designed with your comfort in mind. You can choose to recline on a soft mat or sit in a comfortable chair, whatever feels best for your body. The experience is entirely passive, so there's no need for strenuous movement, making it very gentle and accessible even with mobility limitations or chronic pain.

How can Sound Bath help with my physical aches and stiffness?

The gentle vibrations from the instruments in a Sound Bath can encourage your muscles to relax and your nervous system to calm down. Many people find this helps to reduce feelings of stiffness and ease everyday aches. It’s a subtle way to encourage your body to release tension and find a greater sense of comfort and flexibility.

What should I bring or do to prepare for my first Sound Bath?

For your first Sound Bath, simply wear comfortable clothing. You might also bring a small blanket or an extra pillow if you like, to ensure you're as cozy as possible. The most important thing is to arrive with an open mind, ready to relax and allow the sounds to support your well-being. We provide everything else you'll need for comfort.
