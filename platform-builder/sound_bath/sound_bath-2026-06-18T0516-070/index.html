Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Sound Bath specifically help with major life transitions?
During significant life changes, your nervous system can go into overdrive. A Sound Bath provides a dedicated time and space for deep relaxation, helping to downshift your sympathetic nervous system (fight or flight) and activate your parasympathetic system (rest and digest). This promotes stress reduction and helps your body adapt more calmly to new circumstances.
What should I expect during my first Sound Bath session?
You'll be invited to lie down comfortably, perhaps with a blanket or pillow, and simply listen. We'll guide you into a relaxed state, and then the sounds of crystal bowls, gongs, and chimes will begin. There's nothing you need to do but allow the vibrations to wash over you. Many people feel deeply relaxed, some even fall into a light sleep, and often report feeling refreshed afterwards.
Is the Sound Bath suitable if I'm feeling particularly overwhelmed or anxious?
Absolutely. Our Sound Bath is specifically designed to support nervous system regulation, which is crucial when experiencing overwhelm or anxiety, especially during transitional periods. The gentle, consistent sound vibrations can help to quiet racing thoughts and bring a sense of grounding, offering a natural pathway to stress reduction.
What do I need to bring or wear to a Sound Bath?
We recommend wearing comfortable, loose clothing that allows you to fully relax. You might also consider bringing a water bottle to stay hydrated. We provide mats, blankets, and bolsters, but you're welcome to bring your own if you prefer. The most important thing is to create a comfortable environment for your body and mind.
