What to bring & what we suggest

For a premium sensory experience, arrive 10–15 minutes early. We offer mats and blankets, but bringing your own favorite blanket or small pillow makes a difference.

  • Comfortable layers — temperatures can shift.
  • A water bottle; we refresh with herbal water afterward.
  • Optional eye covering for deep rest (eye pillow recommended).
  • Notes for the facilitator about recent injuries, pregnancy, or implants.

Contraindications & care (please read):

  • Not recommended as a substitute for medical care. If you have seizures, severe psychiatric conditions, or are in the first trimester of pregnancy, consult your healthcare provider first.
  • Metal implants can resonate differently; let us know ahead of time.
  • We offer quieter setups for those sensitive to intense vibration.

Flow of a typical group session: gentle centering → guided breath → immersive soundscape (instruments: crystal bowls, monochord, chimes) → soft return with tea & grounding. Private sessions follow a similar arc but are personalized.

Brief questions, simple answers

How long is a session?

Group classes typically run 75 minutes. Private sessions are commonly 60 to 90 minutes.

Can I record or photograph?

We ask that sessions remain screen-free to preserve the communal and introspective space. Facilitator may record portions for promotion only with consent.

Which instruments might I hear?

We rotate instruments to craft a layered sound experience: crystal bowls, a gentle gong, tuned chimes, and monochord textures. Private requests like tuning forks or Himalayan bowls are welcome.
