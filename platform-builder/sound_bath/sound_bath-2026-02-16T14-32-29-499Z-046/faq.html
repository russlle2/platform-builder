Common questions about our sound offerings

Short, clear answers for a calm booking experience. If you don't see your question, reach out at {{EMAIL}}.

What does a typical session feel like?

Expect a spacious, slow-moving sound field. This session leans into metallic resonance: tuned gongs, Koshi chimes, and tuning forks create layering of tones and microharmonics. You'll lie comfortably as sound pulses and long sustains encourage breath lengthening and softening of the nervous system.

How should I prepare and what should I bring?

Bring a yoga mat, a few blankets, a neck pillow, and an eye covering. We recommend layers since temperature preferences vary. Avoid heavy meals right before. Hydrate beforehand and plan for a gentle return to activity after the session.

Are sound baths safe for everyone?

Contraindications: If you use a pacemaker, have a history of photosensitive epilepsy, are in early pregnancy, or recently had major surgery, please consult your doctor and contact us before booking. Some participants with severe tinnitus or Meniere's disease should approach carefully — we can modify exposure.

What if I fall asleep?

Sleeping is a sign your system relaxed — it's welcome. We design sequences to be non-invasive so those who sleep still benefit from auditory entrainment and integration afterward.

Do you accept walk-ins?

We maintain intimate capacities. Walk-ins are rare; we recommend reserving online. If space allows we may offer last-minute openings 15 minutes before start.

Can I request a private or corporate session?

Yes. We offer 1:1 sessions, couple's baths, and workplace sound rituals. Pricing varies with travel and customization. Use the contact page for a tailored quote.

What about accessibility?

Our venues aim to be accessible. Please note mobility needs in your booking so we can reserve a suitable space. If stairs are involved we will arrange assistance where possible.

How far in advance should I arrive?

Arrive 15–20 minutes early to settle, enjoy tea, and set your intention. Late arrivals may not be admitted once the sound begins to minimize disruption to others.

How do refunds and cancellations work?

Full refunds are available up to 72 hours before the event. Cancellations inside 72 hours may be offered a credit. Please see our pricing page for full policy details or contact us directly.

What instruments do you use?

Our instrument palette rotates. Typical selections include tuning forks, a deep gong, Koshi chimes, and Himalayan singing bowls. Each facilitator curates texture to support the intention of the gathering.
