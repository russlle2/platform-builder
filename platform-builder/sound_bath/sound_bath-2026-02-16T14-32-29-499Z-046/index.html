The Ritual

Our gatherings are ceremonial and sensory: a brief settling, a guided breathwork vignette, and a twenty-five to forty-minute soundscape. The session closes with quiet integration and an optional tea. You are invited to lie, rest, and let the tones move you.

Instruments & Atmosphere

  • Crystal singing bowls (harmonic tunings)
  • Wind chimes & koshi tones
  • Tuning forks for precise resonance

Lighting is dim, textures are soft — a premium sensory field designed to feel secure and expansive.

What to Bring & How to Prepare

What to Bring

  • A yoga mat or blanket for lounging
  • A pillow or bolster for head support
  • An eye cushion or soft scarf

Contraindications

If you are pregnant, have an active seizure disorder, pacemaker, or are currently in treatment for acute psychosis, please consult with a medical professional before attending. Contact us anytime at {{EMAIL}} for specifics.

Session Flow

  1. Welcome & gentle orientation (10 minutes)
  2. Guided breath + arrival practice (5–7 minutes)
  3. Main sound journey (25–40 minutes)
  4. Gradual re-emergence & sharing (5–10 minutes)
