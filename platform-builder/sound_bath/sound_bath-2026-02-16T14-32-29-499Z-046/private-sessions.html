What to expect

Private sessions begin with intake — physical sensitivities, intentions, and any medical considerations are reviewed. The facilitator curates the tone palette to support your needs.

Typical flow

  1. Consultation & settling (15–20 min)
  2. Sound immersion (35–60 min, depending on package)
  3. Integration & next steps (10–15 min)

What to bring

  • Comfortable layering (temperature varies)
  • Mat, blanket, pillow; we can provide for an additional fee
  • Quiet intention and openness to the experience

Instrument palette for private work

The facilitator chooses from a curated inventory — for private work we often favor lower-register instruments to create a container of safety.

Featured instruments: gong, tuning forks, frame harp, ocean drum.

Contraindications & safety

Private work can be deep. Please disclose recent surgeries, pregnancy, implanted devices, recent head trauma, or serious psychiatric conditions. We may request a referral or modified protocol for safety.
