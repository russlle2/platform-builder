The ritual: arrival to integration

We treat arrival and departure as part of the medicine. The experience is structured so neutral nervous tone can be restored.

Arrival & grounding

Welcome, gentle orientation, optional tea. 20 minutes for settling into your mat and intention.

The sound journey

75 minutes of layered instruments — a moving landscape of crystal bowls, koshi chimes, tuning forks and a warm monochord to guide a deep state of resonance.

What to expect & what to bring

Expect a fulsome sensory field: low frequencies, shimmering overtones, a slow arc of intensity. We recommend the following.

Bring

  • Yoga mat, cozy blanket, and a pillow for neck support
  • Water bottle (no glass recommended)
  • Eye mask or scarf for deeper rest

Contraindications

If you have photosensitive epilepsy, are in your first trimester of pregnancy, have a recent head injury, or wear medical implants (pacemakers), please consult with the facilitator before attending.
