How the ritual unfolds

We begin with a brief grounding — a guided breath and intention-setting. The sound journey moves slowly: sustained crystal bowls, a gentle monochord drone, and chiming textures weave into long spaces of silence. The facilitator fades and returns, shaping dynamics to invite nervous system downshift. After the soundscape we allow a soft return with quiet tea and optional sharing.

Session flow

  1. Arrival & welcome (grounding tea)
  2. Brief intention & breath practice
  3. Sound immersion (45–60 minutes)
  4. Silent integration (5–10 minutes)
  5. Gentle closing & optional sharing

What to bring

  • Yoga mat, blanket, pillow for comfort
  • Eye mask or scarf to block light
  • Water bottle; we provide herbal tea
  • Wear layered, comfortable clothing

Contraindications: If you have a pacemaker, recent surgery, are pregnant in your first trimester, or have epilepsy triggered by rhythmic sound, please consult your physician and contact us before booking.
