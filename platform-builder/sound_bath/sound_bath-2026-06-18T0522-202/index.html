Frequently Asked Questions

How does a Sound Bath specifically help new mothers in their postpartum recovery?
Our Sound Baths are tailored to support new mothers by providing a non-invasive way to reduce stress, improve sleep, and gently process the emotional shifts that come with childbirth and new parenthood. The specific frequencies encourage deep relaxation, which is vital for both physical and emotional healing during this sensitive time.
Is a trauma-informed Sound Bath safe if I'm experiencing postpartum anxiety or birth trauma?
Absolutely. Our approach is trauma-informed, meaning we create a highly supportive and safe environment, understanding the unique sensitivities you might have during postpartum. You are always in control, and we encourage you to listen to your body's needs, offering a gentle space for emotional release without re-traumatization.
What should I expect during my first Sound Bath session as a new mother?
You'll lie down comfortably, often with blankets and pillows, while I play various instruments like singing bowls and gongs. There's nothing for you to do but relax and receive the sounds. It's a peaceful experience designed to calm your nervous system, allowing you to find a deep state of rest and emotional regulation, which is profoundly beneficial for postpartum recovery.
How can regular Sound Bath sessions support my long-term emotional well-being postpartum?
Regular Sound Bath sessions can help establish a consistent rhythm of emotional self-care. They can reduce the accumulation of stress, improve your capacity for emotional resilience, and foster a deeper connection to your inner calm. This ongoing support is invaluable as you navigate the continued demands and joys of motherhood.
