Releasing Physical Strain with Sound Bath Vibrations

Parenting often means carrying a lot, both literally and figuratively. Your shoulders, back, and neck can bear the brunt of everyday tasks. Our Sound Bath sessions use specific frequencies to help your muscles soften and release that built-up tension, offering a gentle, non-invasive way to find comfort and ease in your body.

How can a Sound Bath specifically help with my parenting-related physical pain?

Many parents experience tension in their neck, shoulders, and lower back from lifting children, carrying bags, and daily chores. A Sound Bath uses resonant vibrations that can help relax muscle tissues, reduce inflammation, and improve circulation, offering a gentle way to alleviate these common discomforts and promote physical ease.

I'm always on the go; how can I truly relax during a Sound Bath?

It’s completely normal to feel restless at first, especially with a busy mind. During a Sound Bath, your only job is to lie back comfortably. The sounds and vibrations are designed to guide your nervous system into a relaxed state, even if you just drift in and out. Many parents find that simply allowing themselves this time makes a profound difference.

Will a Sound Bath help me manage the constant mental load of being a parent?

Absolutely. The deep relaxation induced by a Sound Bath helps to quiet the incessant mental chatter and overwhelm that often accompanies parenting. By calming your nervous system, you'll likely find improved mental clarity, reduced irritability, and a greater sense of peace, making it easier to navigate daily challenges with more patience.

What should I expect during my first Sound Bath session with Sound Bath Studio?

You'll lie comfortably on a mat, often with blankets and pillows for support. Dr. Morgan Ellis will play a variety of instruments like singing bowls and gongs around you, creating an enveloping soundscape. There's nothing you need to do but listen and allow the sounds to wash over you. Most parents leave feeling deeply rested and rejuvenated.
