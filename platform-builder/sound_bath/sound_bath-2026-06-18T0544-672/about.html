How does a Sound Bath specifically support my immune system during postpartum recovery?

During a Sound Bath, the calming vibrations help reduce cortisol, your stress hormone. High cortisol can suppress your immune system. By lowering stress, your body can better direct its energy toward immune function and recovery, helping you feel stronger and more resilient as a new mom.

I'm often exhausted as a new mother; will I be able to fully participate in a Sound Bath?

Absolutely. Sound Baths are designed for you to simply lie down, get comfortable, and receive the sounds. There's no effort required on your part. In fact, this deep, effortless relaxation is precisely what your postpartum body needs to rebuild its energy and vitality.

What should I expect during my first Sound Bath session for vitality building?

You'll be invited to lie comfortably, often with blankets and bolsters. As the session begins, I'll play various instruments, creating a rich soundscape. Your role is simply to relax and allow the sounds to wash over you. Many new mothers report feeling a profound sense of peace, deep rest, and a gentle energizing effect afterward, directly contributing to your vitality.

Is a Sound Bath safe if I'm still feeling delicate or experiencing discomfort postpartum?

Yes, Sound Baths are very gentle and non-invasive. The vibrations are subtle and work on an energetic level. We always prioritize your comfort, and you can communicate any specific needs or concerns beforehand. This practice is specifically tailored to support your sensitive postpartum body in rebuilding strength safely and effectively.
