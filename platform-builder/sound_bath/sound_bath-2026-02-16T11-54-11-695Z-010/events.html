Venue notes

Soft floor seating, blankets provided. Arrive relaxed; private cubbies for belongings available.

Instruments

  • Crystal singing bowls
  • Deep gongs
  • Wind chimes and tuned bowls

Accessibility

Limited stairs; message us at {{EMAIL}} if you need support. Spaces are intentionally small to maintain intimacy.

Ritual flow — what we do

A structured drift from arriving to integration — we hold a slow arc so you can move through rest safely and fully.

Arrival & grounding
Check-in, settle on the floor, brief orientation and breathing practice (10–15 min).
Resonance work
Layered bowls & tuning forks shape the field (25–35 min).
Gong wave + field toning
A wideband wash to shift deeper states (10–15 min).
Emergence
Soft chimes and voice guide gentle return, then a short tea & reflection (10–20 min).

What to bring & safety

Bring a yoga mat or low-profile cushion, blanket, eye cover if you like, and a water bottle. Dress in layers — your body temperature will change.

Contraindications & notes

Sound can be intense. If you have epilepsy, recent concussion, implanted medical devices (e.g., pacemaker), or are pregnant, consult with our facilitator at {{EMAIL}} before booking. This event is not a substitute for medical care.

Short FAQ

Can I move during the bath? Yes — slow, intentional movement is welcome. If you need to leave early, exit quietly.

Are children allowed? Events are 16+ unless otherwise noted for specific family sessions.
