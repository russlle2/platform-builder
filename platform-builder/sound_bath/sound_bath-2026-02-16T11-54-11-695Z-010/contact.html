On attending a sound bath

Our approach is sensory-led and intentionally premium: gentle lighting, warm floor surfaces, and carefully curated instruments tuned for restorative resonance.

What to bring
  • Blanket and an extra layer for warmth
  • A small pillow or yoga bolster for neck support
  • An eye mask or scarf to block light
  • Water bottle; avoid heavy meals immediately beforehand
Typical flow

We begin with gentle grounding and breathwork, followed by a 40–60 minute sound immersion featuring a rotating selection of instruments (for public events we may use crystal bowls, chimes, or tuning forks; private sessions sometimes include gong or monochord). The session closes with soft reorientation and optional sharing.

Contraindications

Sound bathing is generally safe but not recommended for people with epilepsy, certain seizure disorders, or those with recent head/neck surgery without medical clearance. If you are pregnant, have a pacemaker, or have significant cardiovascular concerns, please consult your physician and inform us prior to booking.

Accessibility

Our venue is wheelchair friendly; please let us know of mobility needs so we can prepare floor space or a chair for you.
