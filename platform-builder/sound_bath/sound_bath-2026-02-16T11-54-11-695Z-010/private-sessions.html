How a private session flows

Intake (5–10m) — short conversation about goals, medical flags, and preferred boundaries.
Toning & attunement (10–15m) — gentle microtonal work with tuning forks or monochord to find the nervous system rhythm.
Immersion (30–60m) — layered instruments chosen for depth: crystal bowls, voice, harmonic overtones, and field extensions.
Integration (10–15m) — gentle return, notes for continuing regulation, and a take-home practice.

What to bring & contraindications

Bring a favorite blanket and anything that helps you rest. Please disclose implants (pacemakers), pregnancy, epilepsy, recent head trauma, or severe sensitivity to sound. If taking medications that affect consciousness, check with your provider before booking.
