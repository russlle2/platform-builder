What should I bring to a sound bath?
Comfortable clothing, a mat, extra blanket, and an eye pillow/scarf are ideal. We provide limited mats and pillows; reserve ahead if you need ours. Hydration before and after is recommended.
Is it loud? Can I sleep?
Sessions are intentionally calibrated and rarely painful. Some instruments (gongs or large metal bowls) can produce high decibel peaks; the facilitator modulates volume. Napping is common and welcomed — we guide a peaceful return to wakefulness.
Who should not attend?
Contraindications include uncontrolled seizures, recent cranial surgery, or unstable psychiatric conditions. If you have implanted medical devices (e.g., pacemaker), discuss with us before booking. Pregnant guests should notify the facilitator for adaptations.
What instruments are used?
We rotate instruments to shape distinct soundscapes. Recent offerings include crystal singing bowls, tuned chimes, a monochord drone, and a gentle gong bloom. Private sessions often include tuning forks for precise somatic work.
How long is a typical session?
Community baths are 75–90 minutes (arrival + ritual + 45–60 minute sound field + integration). Private sessions vary with VIP offerings extending to half- or full-day formats.
Can I bring a partner or book a private session?
Yes. We offer 1:1, couples, and corporate/private group sessions customized for intention and size. Visit the Private page or email {{EMAIL}} to coordinate logistics and pricing.
Are refunds available?
Refund and transfer policies are outlined on the booking page. Typically, transfers are accommodated up to 72 hours before an event; exceptions are handled case-by-case for medical reasons.
Quick notes
If you are sensitive to sound or vibration, tell the facilitator at arrival — we can reposition you, lower volume, or adjust instrument selection. Our space is wheelchair accessible; notify us ahead of time for particular needs.

How do you structure trauma-informed soundwork?
We use predictable cues, give control to participants (ability to leave or signal pause), and avoid surprise ascents in volume. Our facilitators are trained to support physiological regulation and to offer grounding practices pre- and post-sound field. If you wish a deeper screening call before attending, we are available.
