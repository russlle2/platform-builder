Ritual & structure

Sessions follow a steady architecture that balances guidance and spaciousness.

  1. Arrival & Settling — Gentle orientation, breathing cues, and alignment with the room.
  2. Grounding — Low-frequency tones to anchor the nervous system.
  3. Sonic Wave — Layered instruments (this event: crystal bowls + tuning forks + chime textures).
  4. Stillness — Intentional silence and personal integration.
  5. Return — Soft re-entry, tea, and optional check-in.

Safety, contraindications & accessibility

Sound baths are safe for most, but we make clear, clinical recommendations to ensure inclusivity and wellbeing.

  • Contraindications: If you have epilepsy, uncontrolled psychiatric conditions, or recent head trauma, consult your healthcare provider before attending.
  • Pregnancy: Gentle sound is usually okay; inform the facilitator so we can adapt volume & proximity.
  • Medical Devices: If you have pacemakers or sensitive implants, let us know; some instruments emit frequencies that may interfere.
