Ritual outline

How we arrive and move through sound

Our evening unfolds as an intentional arc: settling, sonic immersion, gentle reorientation. Each segment is guided so you can rest without wondering what comes next.

1. Arrival & settling

We begin with light grounding, choices for seating or lying down, and a brief orientation to the instruments and pacing.

2. Breath + tone

Soft breathwork and vocal cues lead into layered soundscapes created with tuned instruments to ease the nervous system.

3. Resonant immersion

A curated selection of instruments—crystal bowls, chimes, and a warm-handled monochord—creates evolving textures for deep resonance.

4. Gentle return

We slow the sound, guide a short reawakening, and offer tea or gentle conversation to integrate the experience.

What to expect

Comfort, clarity, and a sensory-rich environment

Expect a carefully tuned room temperature, soft mats and blankets, and an invitation to surrender to sound. Our sessions are small by design to keep the experience intimate and premium.

What to bring

  • Comfortable clothing and layers for warmth
  • A yoga mat, blanket, pillow (we provide extras if needed)
  • An eye mask or scarf to block light
  • Water bottle; avoid heavy meals just before the session

Contraindications & safety

Sound baths are generally safe, but please consult your physician if you have severe hearing issues, epilepsy, recent head injury, or are pregnant—especially if you are within the first trimester. Inform the facilitator of any implants or medical devices.
