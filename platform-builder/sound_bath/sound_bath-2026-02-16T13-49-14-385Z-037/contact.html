Myths + Clarifications

Myth: You must be spiritual or meditate for years.
Many expect a formal practice; actually, sound baths are accessible — the experience meets you where you are.
Truth: Sounds guide nervous system regulation.
Gently layered instruments like crystal bowls and chimes create a safe sonic landscape for rest and recalibration.

Frequently asked — quick

What to bring
  • Yoga mat or mattress topper
  • Blanket and pillow to stay cozy
  • Eye mask or scarf for darkness
  • Water bottle — hydration is lovely after a bath
Contraindications
If you are pregnant, have a pacemaker, recent concussion, or epilepsy, please consult your physician and let us know before booking. Sound can be intense; we adapt sessions where needed.

Typical session flow

  1. Arrival & settling (10–15 min): tune into breath and intentions.
  2. Intro & gentle movement (5–10 min): optional grounding cues.
  3. Sound bath (30–45 min): layered instruments—crystal bowls, mono-chord, chimes, and soft gong—guide a slow arousal of sensation.
  4. Integration (10–15 min): return to seated, share reflections, and sip water.
