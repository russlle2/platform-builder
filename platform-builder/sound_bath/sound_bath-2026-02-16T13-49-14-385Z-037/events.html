Myths & Realities — Quick Notes

Myth:

Sound baths are just background noise or "sleepy music."

Truth:

They are intentional sonic environments designed to guide nervous system regulation and foster deep inward attention.

Instruments & Textures

For public gatherings we blend crystalline tones with metallic resonance to create both clarity and body.

  • Crystal singing bowls (harmonic overtones)
  • Tibetan singing bowls (warm sustain)
  • Soft chimes & ocean drum for transitional textures
"I arrived scattered and left wrapped in something like clarity." — Community attendee

Flow — How an evening moves

  1. Welcome & orientation (10–15 mins)
  2. Breathwork + intention (10 mins)
  3. Layered soundscape (45–60 mins)
  4. Soft landing & sharing (10–15 mins)

What to bring: yoga mat or cushion, blanket for coverage, eye mask or scarf, water bottle. Optional: small bolster.

FAQs

Do I need experience?
No. Sessions are accessible; we guide you through settling practices and how to use props for comfort.
Are sessions loud?
Volume is curated. Expect rich resonance but not the kind of sustained high volume; we prioritize comfort and safety.
How early should I arrive?
Arrive 15–20 minutes early to settle, sign in, and choose a comfortable spot.

Spaces are intentionally limited to preserve the immersive quality.
