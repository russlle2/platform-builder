How sessions flow

  1. Welcome + intake (5–10m) — a quiet check-in to note anything we should adapt.
  2. Settling phase with soft chimes and breath cues (10m).
  3. Main sound tapestry — crystal bowls, gong swells, and harmonic drones (30–40m).
  4. Return & integration — grounding tones, gentle movement, & tea (5–10m).
