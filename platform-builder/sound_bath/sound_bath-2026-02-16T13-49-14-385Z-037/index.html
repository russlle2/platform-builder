Myth vs. Truth: What a sound bath actually is

Myth

Sound baths are loud, chaotic, or purely spiritual performance art.

Truth

They are guided sonic environments — intentionally composed tones that invite nervous system regulation, rest, and somatic awareness. Our sessions are curated, therapeutic in intention, and held with care.

Contraindications: If you have a history of seizures, uncontrolled hypertension, implanted medical devices sensitive to vibration, or are pregnant, please consult with your physician and contact us before booking. We adapt for many needs — reach out: {{EMAIL}}.

Frequently asked (short & practical)

Do I need experience?
None. Come as you are. We provide mats, bolsters and blankets, though you are welcome to bring your own for comfort.
What should I bring?
A yoga mat or thick blanket, pillow for head support, water bottle, an eye mask if desired, and a notebook for post-session reflection. We also provide essential items on request.
How loud will it be?
Volume is shaped to comfort: enveloping but never harmful. If you are sensitive to sound, let us know ahead and we will adapt the instruments and positioning.
