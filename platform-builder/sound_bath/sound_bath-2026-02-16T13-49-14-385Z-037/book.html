How a booking unfolds

  1. We confirm availability & intake via email.
  2. If adaptations are requested, we refine the sonic palette (e.g., more tuning forks, fewer gong swells).
  3. Arrive 10 minutes before start to settle. We provide mats, bolsters, and optional eye pillows.

Important health guidance

Contraindications: If you are pregnant (first trimester), have a pacemaker, uncontrolled epilepsy, or recent surgery, please consult your provider and contact us before booking. If you are in acute psychiatric crisis, please prioritize clinical support.

What to bring

  • Personal props (eye pillow, scarf, or small cushion)
  • Water bottle

Instruments you might hear

Each session differs. Expect a curated blend such as crystal bowls, a soft gong, and tuned singing bowls — chosen to support the intention of the evening.
