What is a sound bath & what will I feel?

It’s a guided, sonic session where we layer instruments such as tuning forks, chimes, and the monochord to support nervous-system downshift. People report relaxation, tingling, altered time perception, tears, and relief. Experiences vary each time.

How long does a session run and what is the flow?

Typical small-group sessions run ~60 minutes: a brief intake (5-10m), gentle settling with breath and chime cues (10m), a main sound journey (35-40m), then soft grounding and optional tea (5-10m).

What to bring?

Please bring: warm layers, socks, a favorite small blanket or eye pillow (if you have one). We provide mats, bolsters, and props, but personal items can make the experience cozier.

Are there contraindications?

Yes. If you are pregnant in the first trimester, have uncontrolled epilepsy, recent surgery, a pacemaker, or are currently in acute psychiatric crisis, please consult your provider and our team before booking. Sound can be intense; we adapt practice but prioritize safety.

Can I attend with injuries or mobility needs?

Absolutely. Tell us during intake so we can provide chairs, adjusted mat setups, or a shorter listening position. Accessibility is part of our care model.

What instruments do you use?

We rotate instruments: crystal bowls, soft gongs, percussion chimes, and tuned singing bowls. Each session has its own palette curated for intention and resonance.

Do you accept private or corporate bookings?

Yes. We offer private 1:1 sessions, couples, and workplace sound workshops designed to be restorative and brief. See Private Sessions for details.

Before you arrive

Plan to turn phones off or to silent. Hydrate lightly; avoid heavy meals. Wear comfortable, layered clothing. If you’re traveling, arrive 10-15 minutes early.

A note on sensory intensity

We intentionally craft crescendos and still points. If loud textures or low-frequency vibrations bother you, reach out before booking so we can tailor a gentler palette (more chimes, fewer gong swells).
