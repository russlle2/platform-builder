What to bring & safety

Bring a mat, blanket, pillow/head cushion, water and an eye mask if desired. Disclose medical implants or conditions when booking.

Contraindications: pacemakers or implanted electrical devices, recent head/ear surgery, seizure history, first-trimester pregnancy — consult your provider before reserving a session.
