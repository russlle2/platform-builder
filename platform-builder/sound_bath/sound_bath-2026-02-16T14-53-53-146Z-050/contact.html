How we plan a session

A simple, consultative flow — designed for clarity and calm.

  1. Inquiry: Tell us your goals, preferred dates, and the number of participants.
  2. Screening: We review contraindications and any accommodations (see screening below).
  3. Proposal: Receive a tailored outline and investment breakdown — from single sessions to a full VIP Day.
  4. Arrival & Integration: Confirm logistics and arrival details; we handle set up and take-down.

Pre-session guide & screening

To keep everyone safe and comfortable, please review the short screening and guidance below before booking.

What to bring
  • Comfortable layers and a small blanket or yoga mat
  • An eye pillow or soft scarf, water bottle, and any personal props
  • Earplugs if you are sensitive to low frequencies
Contraindications — please read

Inform facilitator if you are pregnant, have a pacemaker, recent surgery, epilepsy, severe mental health instability, or are currently on heavy-duty pain medication. This work can involve sustained low frequencies; if you have medical implants or conditions affected by vibrations, consult your provider first.

Session flow
  1. Arrival & grounding: brief intake, positioning, and intention setting.
  2. Sound journey: progressive layers of instruments (crystal bowls, gong overtones, chimes, monochord) lead you into deeper states for 45–70 minutes.
  3. Integration: soft return, tea, and a short sharing or notes for aftercare.

Micro-habits between sessions

A few small, daily practices to extend the effects of sound work.

  • 5 minutes of deep belly breathing each morning.
  • Evening 3-note humming practice (10 breaths) to anchor calm.
  • Hydration ritual: a glass of water with intention after each session.
