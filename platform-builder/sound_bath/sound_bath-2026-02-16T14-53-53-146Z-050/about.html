Session flow — what to expect

  1. Arrival & grounding: soft tea, gentle seating cues, and a short breath practice.
  2. Settling: guided scan to shift out of doing mode into receptive listening.
  3. Sound journey: a 30–45 minute weaving of tones — bowls, gong swells, chimes — that move through timbre and density.
  4. Integration: a slow return, shared silence, and optional journaling prompts.

What to bring: yoga mat or blanket, pillow for head support, water bottle, eye-cover or scarf.

Contraindications: pregnancy (first trimester consult recommended), recent head/ear surgery, pacemakers or implanted devices — consult your provider and inform the facilitator before booking.

Micro practices you can try between sessions

2-minute ear reset

Close eyes, slow inhale for 4s, exhale 6s, focus only on the internal soundscape.

Evening tone ritual

Play one bowl or hum for 30s before bed to cue parasympathetic downshift.

Portable grounding

Carry a small crystal or smooth stone; pause and breathe while placing it on your lap for 60s.
