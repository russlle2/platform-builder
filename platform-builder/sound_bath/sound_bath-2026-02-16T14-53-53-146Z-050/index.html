Is a sound bath right for you?

A practical five-minute check to match intention and safety.

I want to downshift quickly

Gentle, guided sound lowers arousal and supports sleep onset. Expect 20–40 minutes of passive listening in most group events.

I need trauma-informed cues

We provide grounding prompts, optional eye masks, and opportunities to step out. You remain in control of your experience.

I have medical questions

Contraindications: not recommended for people with severe hearing conditions, uncontrolled epilepsy, or during the first trimester of pregnancy without medical approval. Consult your practitioner if in doubt.

Session flow & sensory journey

Every gathering is arranged to support embodiment — from arrival to integration.

  1. Arrival & grounding: Soft tea, moment of intention, and space setup with cushions and blankets.
  2. Settling: Breathwork and gentle guided attention to anchor into the body.
  3. Sound immersion (30–60 min): Curated resonance using instruments such as crystal singing bowls, gong, chimes and a monochord to create layered harmonics and sustained timbre.
  4. Return: Slow reorientation, sip of water, and a short sharing circle if desired.

Featured sound palette

We blend a rotating set of instruments for texture — crystal bowls, Himalayan bowls, Koshi chimes, tuned gongs, and pitch-perfect tuning forks create both ambient wash and focal tones.

Small aftercare practices

Micro-habits to extend the session's benefit into your day.

  • Hydrate: Slowly sip water for 20 minutes afterward.
  • Ground: Barefoot walk for 5 minutes or press palms to a tree.
  • Journal: One sentence about how you feel; no editing.
  • Sleep ritual: Dim lights and avoid screens for 30 minutes if planning to rest.
