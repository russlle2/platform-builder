Private sound experiences — curated for you

One-to-one attention, couples ceremonies, and corporate sessions designed to elevate rest, creativity, and team cohesion. Each session is a crafted arc of intention, vibration, and gentle re-entry.

Session flow (typical)

  1. Consultation & intention-setting (10–20 min)
  2. Arrangements & settling (5–10 min)
  3. Immersive soundscape (40–60 min) — tailored dynamics and instrument mix
  4. Integration & aftercare (10–15 min) — suggestions and resources

Instruments we might use

Tuning forks, koshi chimes, monochord and subtle planetary bowls — chosen to suit the intention and space.

What to bring & prep

Loose clothing, a personal blanket if you prefer, and any props that help you feel supported. We provide bolsters and mats on request. For in-home or corporate bookings we can bring all necessary setup.

Contraindications

Please notify us if participants have implants, heart conditions, are pregnant, have severe psychiatric conditions, or are sensitive to strong sound; sessions can be adapted.
