Reserve a session

Choose a group session or inquire about private facilitation. Please read the guidelines below before booking.

Flow & what to expect

  1. Arrival & intake (10–20 min): brief health check & comfort setup.
  2. Grounding (10–15 min): gentle breathing cues & orientation.
  3. Sound immersion (40–60 min): layered instruments for sustained resonance.
  4. Guided reorientation (10 min): slow return, hydration suggestion, post-session notes.

What to bring

  • Yoga mat or padded surface
  • Blanket, pillow, eye mask
  • Water bottle — no heavy meals immediately beforehand

Contraindications & safety

If you have epilepsy, recent head injury, severe psychiatric conditions, are pregnant with complications, or use devices that are sensitive to vibration (e.g., certain implants), please consult your clinician and contact us prior to booking. We can often adapt volume and instrument selection.

Myth vs. Practice

Myth: louder is better. Practice: calibrated amplitude and spectral balance produce deeper relaxation with fewer startle responses.
