Frequently asked

How long is a typical session?
Arrival and grounding 15 min • Sound immersion 45–55 min • Gentle reorientation 10–15 min. We recommend allowing 20 extra minutes for arrival and check-in.
What should I bring?
Bring a yoga mat, a blanket, a pillow or bolster for support, and an eye mask if you prefer darkness. We provide extra blankets and cushions on request.
Are there contraindications?
If you have a history of epilepsy, recent head trauma, severe psychiatric conditions, or are pregnant with specific concerns, please consult your clinician first and inform the facilitator. High-volume gongs are adjusted as needed.
Do I need experience?
No. Sessions are guided and accessible to first-timers. The environment is paced to support restful engagement regardless of prior practice.
Can I go to sleep?
Yes. Many participants nap. Facilitators monitor the group and reorient slowly to ensure calm transitions.

Clarifying misconception

Myth: "All sound baths are the same." Truth: Intention, instrument palette, pacing and environment create distinct outcomes. We blend clinical pacing with premium materials for reproducibility.
