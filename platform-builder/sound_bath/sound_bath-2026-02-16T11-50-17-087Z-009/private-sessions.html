Safety notes & practical guidance

Please disclose medical implants, epilepsy history, or pregnancy during booking. We will adapt or recommend alternatives when needed.

What to bring
Comfy clothing, a water bottle, yoga mat, and any personal props that support comfort.
Contraindications
Unstable cardiovascular conditions, recent head injuries, or conditions where loud transients are contraindicated require medical clearance.
Session flow
Assessment (10–15m) → sound immersion (40–60m) → gentle return & written notes (10–15m).
