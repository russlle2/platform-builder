Myths vs. Measured Truths

Myth: "Sound baths are mystical only"

Truth: We frame sessions with intention, sensory cues, and research-informed pacing — not mysticism.

Myth: "You must "empty" your mind"

Truth: We guide attention and breathing patterns; the practice is gentle redirection rather than forced clearing.

Myth: "Any sound is fine"

Truth: Instrument timbre, frequency blends and silence are curated to support nervous system stabilization.

Core Pillars of a Session

Sensation

Deliberate textures — crystal bowls, a Himalayan chime, and the deep wash of a gong — create layered experience for the body to register and release.

Structure

Begin with orientation, then a guided breath and gentle sound introduction; a 30–45 minute immersion follows, closing with integration and journaling prompts.

Safety

We screen for contraindications, offer ear protection, and maintain a calming, supervised environment for all participants.

Frequently Asked — Sensory & Safety

What should I bring?

Bring a comfortable mat or yoga pad, a cozy blanket, a small pillow for neck support, and an eye cover. We provide mats on request.

Are there contraindications?

People with recent head injuries, uncontrolled seizures, implanted medical devices sensitive to vibration, pregnancy in the first trimester, or active psychosis should consult their clinician first. If you use medications that affect consciousness, inform the facilitator. This is not a substitute for medical care.

Session flow — what to expect

Arrival & check-in (10–15 min), orientation & breathwork (5–10 min), sound immersion (30–45 min), slow reorientation and optional sharing (10–15 min). Total time usually 70–90 minutes.

Can I nap during the sound bath?

Yes. Many participants rest or sleep; the experience is self‑paced and safe, with staff present to assist.
