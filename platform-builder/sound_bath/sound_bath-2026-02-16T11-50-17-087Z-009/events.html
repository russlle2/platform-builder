Session snapshot

Arrive 15 minutes early. 75 minute sound journey: 10 min settling, 50 min immersive resonance, 15 min gentle re-orientation and integration.
  • Sensory-first — layers of texture and silence to guide nervous system down.
  • Curated instrumentation — curated selection of bowls, gong and chimes tuned to intention.
  • Gentle structure — soft openings and clear closing to keep transitions safe.

Instruments on this evening

A tapestry of crystal singing bowls, a deep gong for body resonance, and delicate chimes for micro-attunement.

Common myths & measured truths

Myth: Sound baths are just about pleasant noise.
Truth: They are intentional somatic practices using frequency, silence, and pacing to shift nervous system states.
Myth: You must meditate perfectly to benefit.
Truth: Benefits arise from relaxed attention and physiological resonance, not from performance.

What to bring

  • Yoga mat or futon
  • Blanket & small cushion
  • Light eye cover (optional)

Contraindications & safety

If you are pregnant, have uncontrolled seizures, a cardiac device, or are undergoing intense psychiatric treatment, consult your clinician before attending. Not a substitute for medical care.

The flow

  2. Breathwork and intention setting (soft, guided).
  3. Immersive soundscape (primary) — progressive layers and rests.
  4. Slow reorientation and sharing (optional).

About 75 minutes total including brief arrival and integration.
