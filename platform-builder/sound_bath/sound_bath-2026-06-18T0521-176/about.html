How does a Sound Bath address my specific challenges with sleep due to chronic illness?

Our Sound Bath sessions are carefully designed to help regulate your nervous system, which is often overstimulated during chronic illness or injury. The calming frequencies encourage your brain to shift into relaxed states (alpha and theta waves), reducing the physiological stress that interferes with deep, restorative sleep. This helps your body find the peace it needs to truly rest and recover.

Will I be able to sleep during a Sound Bath session?

It's common for participants to drift into a deeply relaxed state, sometimes even falling asleep, during a Sound Bath. The goal isn't necessarily to sleep through the session, but to achieve a state of profound rest where your body and mind can release tension and stress, creating a foundation for better sleep later that night and in the days to come.

Do I need to do anything special to prepare for a Sound Bath, especially with my recovery needs?

We recommend wearing comfortable clothing and ensuring you can lie down or sit comfortably for the duration of the session. If you have specific mobility concerns or comfort needs due to your chronic illness or injury, please let us know when booking. We'll ensure you have the necessary support, like extra bolsters or blankets, to make your experience as restful as possible.

How frequently should I attend Sound Bath sessions to see an improvement in my sleep and recovery?

The frequency depends on your individual needs and the severity of your sleep challenges. Many clients recovering from chronic illness or injury find significant benefits from weekly or bi-weekly sessions initially, to establish a consistent rhythm of deep rest. As your sleep patterns improve, you might choose to attend less frequently for maintenance. We can discuss a personalized plan during your initial consultation.
