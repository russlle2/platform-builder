A careful note on safety

Sound sessions are gentle but not suitable for everyone. Please consult with a medical practitioner before participating if you have: recent cranial surgery, pre-existing seizure disorder, uncontrolled hypertension, severe psychiatric conditions, or pregnancy complications. If you use medical implants or are under medication affecting consciousness, check with your clinician first. Our facilitators will always offer quieter options and seating on request.
