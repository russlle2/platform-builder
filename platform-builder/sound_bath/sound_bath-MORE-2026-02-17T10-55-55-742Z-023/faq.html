Short answers that help you decide quickly. Prefer to speak? Call {{PHONE}}.

What should I expect at a group session?
A guided sequence of layered sounds and quiet intervals. Sessions are structured to keep participants safe and grounded; facilitators offer seating options and volume adjustments. Average length depends on format; see events for specific times.
Can I attend if I have medical concerns?
Short answer: consult your provider. Sound can interact with certain conditions. If you have epilepsy, recent head surgery, unstable cardiovascular conditions, or are pregnant with complications, please get medical sign-off. We list contraindications clearly on booking and will offer quieter or private options for people who need them.
How does membership work?
Monthly plans provide recurring credits, priority booking, and a lower per-session rate for both group and private work. No long-term lock-ins beyond a monthly cancellation window. See the Plans page for tiered pricing and what each credit covers.
