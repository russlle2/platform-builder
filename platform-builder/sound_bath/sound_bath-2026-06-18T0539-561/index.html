Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly happens during a Sound Bath session at ?
During your Sound Bath, you'll lie comfortably while plays various instruments like crystal bowls and gongs. The sounds create gentle vibrations that encourage deep relaxation and help to quiet your mind, allowing you to settle into a state of inner calm. It's a passive experience, simply requiring you to be present.
How can a Sound Bath specifically help with professional burnout?
Burnout often stems from mental overload and a disconnect from your inner self. Sound Baths offer a unique way to reset. The deep relaxation fostered by the sounds reduces stress hormones, improves mental clarity, and helps you reconnect with your spiritual core, providing a much-needed mental and emotional break from constant professional demands.
Do I need any prior experience with meditation or spiritual practices for a Sound Bath?
Absolutely not. Our Sound Bath sessions are designed to be accessible to everyone, regardless of previous experience. There's no need to 'do' anything specific; simply allow the sounds to wash over you. will guide you gently, making it easy to ease into the experience and find your inner peace.
What should I bring or wear to a Sound Bath session in ?
For your comfort during the Sound Bath, we recommend wearing loose, comfortable clothing. You might also want to bring a water bottle, and if you prefer, your own eye mask or a small pillow to enhance your relaxation. We provide mats and blankets, but feel free to bring anything else that makes you feel cozy and supported.
