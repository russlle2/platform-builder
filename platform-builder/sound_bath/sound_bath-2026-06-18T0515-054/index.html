Frequently Asked Questions

How does a Sound Bath specifically benefit athletes managing physical stress?
A Sound Bath helps athletes by intentionally shifting your nervous system into a parasympathetic state. This promotes deeper rest, reduces muscle tension, and supports cellular repair, all critical for managing the high physical demands of your active lifestyle and accelerating recovery.
Will a Sound Bath interfere with my training schedule or energy levels?
Quite the opposite. A Sound Bath is designed to enhance your recovery, not hinder it. Sessions are deeply relaxing and typically leave you feeling refreshed and revitalized, contributing positively to your overall energy management and readiness for subsequent training.
What should I expect during my first Sound Bath session at ?
You will lie comfortably, fully clothed, as I guide you through an immersive experience with various instruments. The focus is on deep relaxation and allowing the sound frequencies to naturally calm your nervous system and reduce any accumulated physical stress. Simply relax and receive.
How often should an active individual or athlete attend a Sound Bath for optimal results?
The frequency for optimal results can vary, but many active individuals find significant benefit from regular sessions, perhaps once a week or bi-weekly, especially during intense training cycles. This consistency supports ongoing nervous system regulation and stress reduction for sustained performance.
