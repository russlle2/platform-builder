Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is a Sound Bath, and how will it help me postpartum?
A Sound Bath involves lying down and receiving a gentle, immersive experience of sound vibrations from instruments like singing bowls and gongs. For new mothers, it helps calm your overstimulated nervous system, reduce stress, and promote deep relaxation, which is vital for your postpartum recovery.
Do I need any prior experience with meditation or sound healing for a Sound Bath?
Absolutely not! No prior experience is needed. Your only job is to get comfortable, relax, and allow the sounds to wash over you. We provide a welcoming space where you can simply receive the benefits without any effort or 'doing'.
Is a Sound Bath safe if I am still healing from childbirth or breastfeeding?
Yes, our Sound Bath sessions are gentle and non-invasive, making them very safe during postpartum recovery and while breastfeeding. In fact, many mothers find the deep relaxation beneficial for milk production and overall healing. If you have any specific health concerns, please consult with your doctor.
What should I wear or bring to my Sound Bath session?
Please wear comfortable, loose clothing that allows you to fully relax. You might also bring a water bottle, and if you have a favorite blanket or eye mask that brings you extra comfort, feel free to bring those along too. We provide mats and props for your comfort.
