Frequently Asked Questions

What specifically is a Sound Bath, and how does it help with mental clarity during recovery?
A Sound Bath is an immersive experience where you relax while various instruments (like singing bowls and gongs) are played, creating resonant vibrations. For recovery, these gentle sounds help quiet an overactive mind, reduce mental fatigue, and improve your ability to focus and process thoughts more clearly, without demanding active participation.
Do I need any prior experience or special preparation for a Sound Bath, especially during my recovery?
Absolutely not. Our Sound Bath sessions require no prior experience. We simply ask you to arrive ready to relax. You don't need to do anything specific; just lie back, get comfortable, and allow the sounds to wash over you. We provide everything you need for comfort, ensuring it's an easy and accessible experience for your recovery.
How often should I attend a Sound Bath to see benefits for my cognitive wellness?
The frequency that works best for your cognitive wellness often varies, but many find regular sessions beneficial. Some prefer weekly, while others benefit from bi-weekly attendance. We encourage you to listen to your body and mind, and we're happy to discuss a schedule that aligns with your recovery pace and goals for mental clarity.
Is the Sound Bath suitable if I have specific sensitivities or physical limitations due to my chronic illness or injury?
Yes, our Sound Bath environment is designed to be inclusive and adaptable. We prioritize your comfort and safety. Please let us know about any specific sensitivities or physical limitations you may have when booking, so we can ensure you have the most comfortable and beneficial experience possible. Our goal is to support your recovery without adding any strain.
