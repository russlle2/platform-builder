What if I've never tried a Sound Bath or any wellness practice before?

That's perfectly fine! Our Sound Bath sessions are designed to be completely accessible, even if you have no prior experience with wellness practices. There's nothing you need to 'do' or 'know' beforehand. Simply arrive, get comfortable, and let the sounds guide you into a state of relaxation. We'll be there to support you every step of the way, creating a welcoming and gentle atmosphere.

How can a Sound Bath help me specifically with life transitions?

Life transitions often bring emotional turbulence, stress, and uncertainty. A Sound Bath provides a dedicated space for your nervous system to calm down. The gentle vibrations can help reduce anxiety, quiet overthinking, and create mental clarity, allowing you to process your emotions more effectively and approach your new circumstances with a greater sense of peace and resilience.

Will I have to share or talk about my experiences during the Sound Bath?

Absolutely not. Our Sound Bath sessions are a non-verbal experience. There's no expectation or requirement for you to share or discuss anything about your life transitions or emotional state. The focus is entirely on your internal experience and allowing the sounds to facilitate deep relaxation and emotional release in a private, personal way.

Is a Sound Bath suitable if I'm feeling particularly vulnerable or emotional right now?

Yes, our Sound Bath practice is held with a trauma-informed approach, making it especially suitable during times of emotional vulnerability. We create a very gentle and safe space, respecting your boundaries and emotional capacity. The goal is to offer comfort and support, allowing you to simply receive the soothing sounds without any pressure. If you have specific concerns, please feel free to reach out to us directly.
