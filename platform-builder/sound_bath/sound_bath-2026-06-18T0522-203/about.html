How Gentle Sound Bath Supports Your Emotional Well-being

Our Sound Bath sessions offer a comforting embrace, using vibrations to soothe your nervous system. This gentle approach helps quiet persistent worries and brings a sense of calm. We understand that emotional healing at this stage of life benefits from a tender touch, allowing you to process feelings without pressure and find a renewed sense of emotional balance.

What can I expect during my first Sound Bath session?

During your first Sound Bath, you'll recline comfortably, fully clothed, while I play various instruments like singing bowls and gongs. The sounds are gentle and enveloping. Your only task is to relax and allow the sounds to wash over you. Many people describe it as a deeply peaceful and calming experience, helping to quiet the mind.

Is a Sound Bath suitable if I have experienced emotional difficulties in the past?

Absolutely. Our Sound Bath practice is designed with a trauma-informed approach, meaning we prioritize creating a safe and supportive environment. The sounds are never jarring, and you are always in control of your experience. We aim to help you feel held and soothed, offering a gentle pathway to emotional comfort.

How can Sound Bath help with feelings of loneliness or anxiety in older age?

Sound Bath offers a unique form of comfort. The gentle vibrations can help calm the nervous system, which often reduces feelings of anxiety. For loneliness, the shared, peaceful experience in a group setting can foster a sense of connection, even without direct interaction. It's a space where you can simply be, and feel supported.

Do I need to do anything special to prepare for a Sound Bath?

No special preparation is needed. We recommend wearing comfortable clothing so you can fully relax. You might also bring a water bottle to stay hydrated. Our focus is on making this experience as easy and accessible as possible for you, so just come as you are.
