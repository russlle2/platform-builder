Daily signals you can use

Micro-habits—two minutes of breath, a one-line journal prompt, or a soft bell to mark a switch—build a steady foundation.

  • Two slow breaths — inhale 4s, exhale 6s. Repeat twice.
  • One-line check-in — jot a sentence: Where is attention now?
  • Shift bell — a small waypoint to end a task and start another.
