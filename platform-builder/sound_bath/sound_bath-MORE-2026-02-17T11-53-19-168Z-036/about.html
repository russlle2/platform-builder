A practical framework

We think of sessions as composed layers: orientation (what you bring), guided support (breathing and prompts), and tonal field (sound that holds attention). Each element is simple — the design is in how they're sequenced.

You’ll leave with one clear practice you can use at home: a short intention + a breath pattern + a two-note sonic anchor.

Program names: Resonance Sessions (weekly), Focus Arc (multi-week), and One-to-One Harmonies (private). Pricing and details are on the booking page.

Who benefits

People who want a reliable container for slowing down without rituals that feel loud. These gatherings work whether you come with a recent stressor, a curiosity, or just a need to re-center.

  • Shift from doing to sensing in 15–25 minutes
  • Practice a brief anchoring routine to use anywhere
  • Find clarity on a small decision through intention setting

Small steps to integrate

  1. Three-breath reset: inhale fully, hold two counts, exhale three.
  2. Evening 2-minute note: place a hand, listen to your skin, write one word.
  3. Weekly check-in: what did sound show you about attention?

How we frame value

Instead of "packages," we offer participation frames: drop-in, commitment cycle, and private deep-dive. Each is priced to reflect access, preparation, and the time we hold together.

  • Drop-in — single session, flexible dates.
  • Commitment cycle — 4 sessions, recommended cadence once weekly.
  • Private deep-dive — 75-minute one-on-one format.

Contraindications & safety

If you have a history of seizure disorder, uncontrolled hypertension, recent head injury, pregnancy concerns, or are under psychiatric care with active medication changes, please consult your physician before attending. Vibrational work and resonant sound can feel intense for some people; if you use a pacemaker or are sensitive to sound, contact us for guidance.
