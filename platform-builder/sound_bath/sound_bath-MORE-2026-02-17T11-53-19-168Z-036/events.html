What to expect

Sessions move through three phases: settling, sound field, and gentle return. Expect guided cues, varied instruments (bowls, gongs, voice), and short guidance before and after the sound work.

Arrival
Doors open 15 min early for check-in and quiet settling.
Sound Field
A curated progression of tone and silence lasting 35–50 minutes.
Reintegration
A short closing exchange to ground and capture intentions.

Contraindications & care notes

Sound gatherings are generally gentle, but please avoid attendance or consult a clinician if you have: uncontrolled epilepsy, recent major surgery, severe hypertension, implanted medical devices sensitive to vibrations, or are pregnant and have specific medical concerns. If you have severe anxiety around loud noises, consider a private 1:1 session instead. Our facilitators are available to speak with you beforehand at {{PHONE}} or {{EMAIL}}.
