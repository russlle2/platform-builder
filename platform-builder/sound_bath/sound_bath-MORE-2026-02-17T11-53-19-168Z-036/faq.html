Practical notes for attending, preparing, and understanding safety. If something is missing, reach out — we’ll reply within two business days.

What should I bring?
Bring a mat, blanket, and something to cover your eyes if you like. We provide bolsters.
▸

Comfort is the priority. Layer clothing for temperature changes, and avoid heavy perfumes.

Will the sounds be loud?
No — the session uses tonal instruments at comfortable listening levels.
▸

The design favors subtle resonance and silence. If you have hearing sensitivity, tell us before the session and we will adapt placement and levels.

How early should I arrive?
Arrive 10 minutes early for settling and orientation.
▸

We close doors once the session begins to preserve the field. Late arrivals may be admitted quietly at a suitable break.

Contraindications?
See the full safety guidance and consult your clinician when in doubt.
▸

If you have a cardiac device, seizure history, are pregnant with concerns, or under active psychiatric medication adjustments, consult your provider first. Contact us if unsure.

Can I record or photograph?
We ask that sessions remain device-free for presence.
▸

No recordings, please, unless part of a pre-arranged private session. Respect others' privacy.
