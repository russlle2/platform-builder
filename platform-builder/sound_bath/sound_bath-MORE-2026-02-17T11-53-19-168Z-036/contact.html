Contraindications & guidance

Sound sessions are gentle but not without contraindications. If any of the following apply to you, please contact us before booking so we can make an informed plan:

  • Pregnancy beyond the first trimester — please discuss specifics.
  • Epilepsy or photosensitive conditions (some visual elements can be adapted).
  • Recent surgery, implanted medical devices, or severe vestibular disorders.
  • Severe psychiatric conditions that require clinical support — we encourage coordination with your care team.

If in doubt, choose a private pre-session call so we can tailor the experience safely for you.
