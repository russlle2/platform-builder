Private tuning and tailored sessions

Book a focused, one-to-one encounter for therapeutic support, deeper rest, or group sessions for small teams. Sessions are customizable: sound-led, talk-and-tone, or a mixed approach oriented to your needs.

One-to-One
60–90 minute sessions designed for deeper exploration. Facilitator adapts instruments and pacing to your sensitivities and goals.

How a private session flows

  1. Brief intake about health, goals, and comfort with volume.
  2. Guided settling and choice of anchors (breath, body, point of focus).
  3. Sound sequence tailored to your nervous system for 30–60 min.
  4. Slow reintegration and suggestions for follow-up practices.
A private option is a safer alternative if you have strong sensitivity to communal sound or health contraindications.

Contraindications & safety

Please avoid or consult a clinician about attending if you have implanted electronic medical devices, recent surgery, or uncontrolled cardiovascular conditions. If you have concern about seizures, inner-ear conditions, pregnancy-related issues, or strong sound sensitivity, contact us first at {{PHONE}} or {{EMAIL}}.
