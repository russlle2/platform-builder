What is a Sound Bath and how does it help with relationships in my senior years?

A Sound Bath involves being immersed in the gentle vibrations of various instruments, like singing bowls and gongs. For seniors, it offers a peaceful respite, allowing your mind to quiet and your heart to open. This can lead to a calmer perspective on your relationships, helping you feel more connected and compassionate with family and friends.

Do I need to do anything special or have prior experience for a Sound Bath?

Not at all. Your only requirement is to arrive comfortably and be open to the experience. You simply lie down or sit comfortably, allowing the sounds to wash over you. There's no need for any special techniques or prior knowledge; just bring your willingness to relax and receive.

Is the Sound Bath suitable if I have hearing aids or specific health concerns?

The Sound Bath is designed to be very gentle. If you use hearing aids, you might find it more comfortable to remove them, or simply adjust them as needed. We always recommend discussing any specific health concerns with Dr. Morgan Ellis beforehand so we can ensure your comfort and tailor the experience to your needs.

How often should I experience a Sound Bath to support my heart-centered connections?

The ideal frequency is truly personal to you. Many find that regular sessions, perhaps once a month, help to maintain a sense of inner peace and foster loving connections. Others choose to come as they feel called, whenever they need a gentle re-centering. We can discuss what feels right for your unique rhythm.
