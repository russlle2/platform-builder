Health & safety — important
Contraindications: If you are pregnant, have epilepsy, a history of seizures, a recent concussion, or are under the influence of mind-altering substances, please consult your healthcare provider before attending. Sound experiences can be intense for some bodies; let us know about significant medical or psychiatric history when you register so we can recommend appropriately tailored sessions or a private appointment.
Cohort pricing
We use a cohort-cycle model rather than per-class tickets. This supports a shared practice and fair pricing across the month. Ask for current cycle rates when you contact us.
Accessibility
Most venues are wheelchair accessible; if you need specific accommodations, note it in your message and we’ll do our best to support you.
Common questions
What should I bring?
A mat, a blanket, and an eye pillow if you like; we have extras for first-timers.
Is there a refund policy?
Cohort cycles are prepaid; partial refunds depend on timing. We will discuss specifics on contact.
Are sessions suitable for beginners?
Yes. Cohort formats include orientation moments and gentle options for first-time attendees.
Can I schedule a private session?
Absolutely — select "Private session" in the form and we’ll propose openings and bespoke pricing.
