Questions—answered with care

What should I bring?

Bring comfortable layers, a mat or blanket if you'd like, and an open curiosity. We provide bolsters and eye pillows on request.

How long is a typical session?

Most public gatherings run 60–80 minutes. Private sessions vary by agreement; see the booking page for options.

Can I fall asleep?

Yes. Sleep is a natural response. Facilitators give gentle cues to help anyone who wants grounding afterward.

Are these therapeutic sessions?

They are restorative and somatic, but not a replacement for therapy. We partner with clinicians and can provide referrals when needed.

Contraindications & safety

If you have implanted medical devices, are pregnant, prone to seizures, or currently in an uncontrolled psychiatric episode, please consult your clinician before attending. For any concerns, email us at {{EMAIL}}.
