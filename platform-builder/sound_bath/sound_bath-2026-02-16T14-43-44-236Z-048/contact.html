The sound journey - what to expect

We craft a gradual descent into embodied listening. Guests arrive and settle, we open with intention and gentle guided breathing, then move through layered instruments that build a textured soundfield. We weave crystal bowls, tuning forks, and chimes with occasional gong swells — each wave meeting you like a tide. The session closes with soft returning tones and a short sharing circle.

Flow

  1. Arrival & grounding (15 min)
  2. Intention + breath practice (5-10 min)
  3. Sound immersion (40–60 min)
  4. Slow re-entry + tea (10–15 min)

Instruments you might hear

  • Crystal singing bowls
  • Tuning forks
  • Chimes and small bell instruments

Frequently asked

How long is a session?

Most group events run 75–90 minutes including arrival and closing circles. Private sessions vary by appointment length.

Can I nap?

Yes — many guests drift into a restful state. We recommend a blanket and pillow for comfort.

Is this a performance?

No. It's a guided container for inner listening and somatic rest.
