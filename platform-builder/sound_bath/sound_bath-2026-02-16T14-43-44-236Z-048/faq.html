Questions and gentle answers

Below we collect the most common curiosities about attending a sound bath with us, plus membership and safety notes for new listeners.

What should I bring?
Bring a yoga mat or comfortable sleeping pad, a soft blanket, an eye pillow or scarf, and a bottle of water. If you prefer a cushion for your neck, bring that too. We provide extra blankets and mats on request.
Who should avoid sound baths or check with a clinician?
Contraindications: untreated seizure disorder, recent cranial surgery, or implanted cardiac devices that could be affected by strong vibrations. If you are pregnant, have severe tinnitus, or are on medical advice regarding sensory therapies, please consult your provider first and tell the facilitator before the session.
What is the typical flow of a group session?
Our circles usually begin with a brief grounding and intention-setting, followed by 35–55 minutes of layered sound. We use instruments like gongs, soft chimes, and oceanic bowls to create sustained textures. Periods of silence are interspersed to let resonance settle. The closing includes gentle reorientation and tea.
How loud are the instruments?
We prioritize therapeutic volume levels. Expect immersive sound that can be felt more than it is painfully loud. If at any point you need less intensity, raise your hand or move toward the edge of the room.
Do I need any experience?
No. Beginners and seasoned practitioners alike are welcome. Sessions are guided to meet a range of needs; you are invited simply to listen and rest.
How does membership work?
Membership provides recurring credits for sessions, priority booking, and monthly member-only mini-circles. Members also receive a welcome audio and discounted private sessions.
Do you offer private or corporate sessions?
Yes. We offer 1:1 private sessions, couple sessions for shared restoration, and corporate or team workshops tailored to workplace wellbeing. See the private sessions page for details.

Practical notes

A typical session is 75 minutes. Doors close 10 minutes after start to honor the interior decline. We recommend arriving 15 minutes early for check-in and tea.
