Why a private session?

A private environment allows deeper exploration — we tailor instruments, timing and intention to your needs.

  • Targeted nervous system regulation
  • Personalized sound prescriptions for sleep, focus, or emotional release
  • Follow-up guidance and simple home practices

Session flow

  1. Intake conversation (10–15 min): goals, sensitivities, recent health.
  2. Co-created sound field (30–50 min): a carefully layered sequence.
  3. Integration (10 min): silence, tea, and recommended next steps.

We alter amplitude and timbre to suit 1:1 or shared sessions — loudness is always consensual.

What to bring

Mat, cushion/pillow, blanket, and any comfort item. If you prefer to lie on a massage table, let us know when booking.

Contraindications & safety

Not suitable for people with severe sound hypersensitivity, recent head injury, or certain cardiac implants. Please disclose pacemakers, pregnancy, or epilepsy when booking.
