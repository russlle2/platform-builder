How a session unfolds

  1. Arrival & settle (10–15 min) — light journaling, positioning, and intention setting.
  2. Slow breath guide — gentle cues to relax and invite presence.
  3. Sound immersion (35–45 min) — shifting timbres: tuning forks and chimes, then crystal bowls, building into gong waves, finishing on a monochord hum.
  4. Silent rest — 8–12 minutes of quiet integration.
  5. Reintegration — soft tea and shared reflections if desired.

What to bring

  • Yoga mat or bolsters and a cozy blanket
  • Eye mask and a small pillow
  • Water bottle; arrive hydrated
Contraindications: If you are pregnant, have epilepsy, recent head/neck surgery, a history of seizures, or have implanted medical devices (e.g., pacemakers), please consult your physician and contact us before attending. Sound can shift blood pressure and arousal states.

Common questions

How long is a typical event?

Most gatherings last 75–90 minutes including arrival and post-session integration.

Can I fall asleep?

Yes — falling asleep is a natural part of deep relaxation. You will still receive the benefit of the sound field.

What if I need to leave early?

Quietly exit if necessary; we ask you to step out gently to preserve the space for others.
