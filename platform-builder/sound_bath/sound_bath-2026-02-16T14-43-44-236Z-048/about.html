How a session feels — the flow

Sessions are intentionally paced to hold safety and depth. Below is a distilled flow that we use as a frame.

  1. Arrival & settling — gentle tea, set an intention, ground with breathwork.
  2. Sound immersion — layered bowls and tuning forks create evolving textures to invite nervous system downshift.
  3. Silent resonance — periods of silence help integrate sensations.
  4. Closing — guided return, soft journaling prompts, tea and time to reorient.

Instruments used here include crystal singing bowls and harmonic tuning forks, chosen for their clarity and overtones.

Quick facts

What to bring: a yoga mat or pad, a blanket, an eye pillow or light scarf, and water.
Contraindications: please avoid if you have untreated seizures, recent head injuries, or are under medical instruction to avoid vibrational therapies. Always check with your healthcare provider and notify the facilitator if you are pregnant or have implants sensitive to sound.
