What to bring
A mat or pad, a warm blanket, and an eye pillow. Loose, comfortable clothing is best.
Contraindications
If you have a history of seizures, recent head trauma, or hardware that may be sensitive to vibrations, contact your physician first and tell us before booking. Pregnant people should consult their care provider.
Session flow
Arrival — 10–15 minutes for check-in and settling.
Main immersion — 40–60 minutes layered sound using a field of instruments like a monochord, planet bowls, and soft percussion.
Integration — 10–15 minutes of gentle return and tea.
Private sessions
1:1 sessions are tailored to navigate trauma-sensitive approaches and therapeutic aims. Couple and corporate offerings can be customized on request.
