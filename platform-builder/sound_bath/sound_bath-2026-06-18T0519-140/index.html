Frequently Asked Questions

How does a Sound Bath specifically help with sleep during a major life transition?
Major life transitions often cause stress, which elevates cortisol and disrupts sleep. Our Sound Bath sessions use specific frequencies to activate your parasympathetic nervous system, lowering stress hormones and guiding your brain into alpha and theta states. This naturally prepares your body for deeper, more restorative sleep, helping you cope better with the demands of change.
What should I expect during my first Sound Bath session?
You'll lie down comfortably, typically on a mat with blankets and pillows. The practitioner will play various instruments, creating an immersive soundscape. Your only task is to relax and allow the sounds to wash over you. Many people feel a sense of deep calm, sometimes drifting into a dream-like state, which is indicative of the brain entering sleep-supportive frequencies.
Do I need any prior experience with meditation or sound therapy?
Absolutely not. Our Sound Bath sessions require no prior experience. The sounds themselves do the work of guiding your brain into a relaxed state. Just come as you are, with an open mind, and allow the vibrations to facilitate your natural ability to unwind and prepare for rest, especially beneficial during times of significant personal change.
How often should I attend Sound Bath sessions to improve my sleep?
While even one session can offer immediate benefits, consistent engagement often yields more lasting results, particularly when navigating a life transition. Many clients find that weekly or bi-weekly sessions help to solidify healthy sleep patterns and build resilience against stress, making the transition smoother and more restful.
