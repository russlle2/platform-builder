Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Sound Bath and how does it help parents with stress?
A Sound Bath is a deeply immersive, full-body listening experience where you are 'bathed' in sound waves produced by various instruments. For parents, this practice is designed to gently shift your nervous system out of its 'fight or flight' response, promoting a state of deep relaxation and helping to reduce the cumulative stress of daily parenting demands. It's a structured approach to foster calm.
Do I need any prior experience with meditation or Sound Baths?
Absolutely not. No prior experience is necessary. Our Sound Bath sessions are designed to be accessible to everyone, regardless of your background with meditation or sound practices. Your only requirement is to lie down, get comfortable, and allow the sounds to guide you into a relaxed state. We provide all the guidance you need.
How often should a parent attend a Sound Bath for optimal benefits?
The frequency of sessions can vary based on your individual needs and stress levels. Many parents find that attending a Sound Bath bi-weekly or monthly helps maintain a consistent state of nervous system regulation and stress reduction. Some prefer to come more often during periods of high demand. We can discuss a personalized plan that works best for your schedule and well-being goals.
Is there any evidence to support Sound Baths for stress reduction?
Yes, there is growing research indicating the positive effects of sound on the nervous system. Studies suggest that sound and vibration can influence brainwave states, reduce cortisol levels, and promote relaxation by activating the parasympathetic nervous system. Our approach is informed by these findings, offering you a evidence-based method to manage your stress effectively.
