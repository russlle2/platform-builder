How a Sound Bath Supports Nervous System Regulation for Parents

Your nervous system is constantly working to keep you alert and responsive, especially when caring for young children. Our Sound Bath sessions utilize specific frequencies and resonant tones to encourage your brainwaves to shift from an active, beta state to more relaxed alpha and theta states. This physiological shift promotes deep relaxation, helping to downregulate your stress response and restore a sense of inner quiet.
