Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Sound Bath specifically help with my athletic recovery?
A Sound Bath supports your athletic recovery by promoting a deep state of relaxation, which helps to downregulate your nervous system. This can reduce muscle tension, improve circulation, and enhance your body's natural restorative processes, aiding in faster physical recovery after training or competition.
Is the Sound Bath suitable if I'm currently managing a physical injury?
Yes, a Sound Bath can be beneficial when managing a physical injury. The gentle, non-invasive nature of sound therapy can help reduce pain perception, decrease stress associated with injury, and create a calm environment conducive to healing. We always recommend consulting with your medical professional regarding your injury.
What does 'heart-centered care' mean within a Sound Bath context for active people?
'Heart-centered care' in our Sound Bath practice means we focus on fostering a compassionate connection between your mind and body. For active individuals, this translates to learning to listen to your body's subtle cues, acknowledging its efforts, and treating yourself with kindness, which can prevent burnout and foster a more sustainable relationship with your athletic pursuits.
Will a Sound Bath interfere with my training schedule or energy levels?
On the contrary, a Sound Bath is designed to enhance your energy levels and complement your training schedule. It provides a dedicated time for deep rest and nervous system regulation, which can improve your sleep, reduce fatigue, and help you approach your training with renewed focus and vitality, rather than interfering with it.
