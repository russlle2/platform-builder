Understanding Sound Bath for Your Well-being

A Sound Bath is a gentle experience where you relax comfortably while soft, resonant sounds wash over you. Think of it as a calming soundscape designed to help your mind quiet down and your body ease tension. It's a peaceful way to give your nervous system a much-needed break from daily stresses, supporting a feeling of overall calm without any effort on your part.
