Finding Your Center: How Sound Bath Supports Postural Awareness in Recovery

When you're recovering from chronic illness or injury, simply 'feeling' your body can be challenging. Our Sound Bath sessions create a serene space where the gentle vibrations act as a compassionate guide, helping you tune into your physical sensations without strain. This can help you rediscover where your body holds tension, where it feels supported, and how to gently encourage a more comfortable, aligned posture. It's about listening deeply to what your body needs right now, fostering a sense of grounded stability as you heal.
