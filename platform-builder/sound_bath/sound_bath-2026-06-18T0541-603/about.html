Think of it as a calming reset, helping your body conserve energy and focus on what it does best: keeping you healthy and vibrant, especially as you age.

How does a Sound Bath specifically support my immune system as a senior?

Our Sound Bath sessions promote deep relaxation, which is known to reduce stress. Lowering stress levels can positively impact your immune system, helping it function more effectively. When your body is calm, it can better allocate its resources to maintaining your health and resilience.

I'm new to Sound Baths. Is it suitable for someone with my mobility concerns?

Absolutely! Our Sound Bath practice is designed to be gentle and accessible for everyone, regardless of mobility. You'll be comfortably seated or lying down, whatever feels best for you. There's no physical exertion required, just a peaceful space for you to relax and receive the soothing sounds.

How often should I experience a Sound Bath to feel its benefits for vitality?

Many of our clients find that regular sessions, perhaps once a week or bi-weekly, help maintain a consistent feeling of calm and renewed energy. However, even occasional sessions can provide a wonderful sense of relaxation and support your overall vitality. We encourage you to listen to your body and what feels right for you.

Will the sounds be too loud or overwhelming for me during a Sound Bath?

Not at all. Dr. Morgan Ellis carefully curates the soundscape to be gentle and non-intrusive. The sounds are designed to be soothing and harmonious, creating a comforting atmosphere, never overwhelming. Your comfort is our priority, and we ensure the volume is always appropriate for a deeply relaxing experience.
