Frequently Asked Questions

Is a Sound Bath suitable for someone my age, especially if I have mobility concerns?
Absolutely. Our Sound Bath sessions are designed to be very gentle and accessible. You can lie down comfortably on a mat, or if you prefer, sit in a comfortable chair. We prioritize your comfort and can make any necessary adjustments to ensure you feel secure and relaxed throughout the session. There's no strenuous movement involved, just peaceful listening.
How can a Sound Bath specifically help with improving my sleep?
A Sound Bath works by guiding your brainwaves into a slower, more relaxed state, similar to what happens just before you fall asleep. The gentle, consistent sounds help quiet your busy mind and release physical tension. This deep relaxation can make it easier for you to fall asleep more readily at night and experience a more sustained, quality rest.
What if I've never experienced a Sound Bath before or am sensitive to sounds?
Many of our cherished clients are new to Sound Baths, and we welcome you warmly. The sounds are never harsh or jarring; they are carefully chosen to be soothing and harmonious. If you're sensitive to sounds, please let us know beforehand. We can ensure you are positioned in a comfortable spot and even adjust the volume to your preference, ensuring a pleasant experience.
How often should I attend a Sound Bath to notice improvements in my rest?
The benefits of a Sound Bath can be felt even after a single session, with many clients reporting immediate feelings of calm and improved sleep that same night. For more sustained improvement in your sleep patterns and overall restorative rest, we often suggest attending regularly, perhaps once every week or two, to allow the gentle effects to accumulate and deepen over time.
