Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly happens during a Sound Bath session?
During a Sound Bath, you'll relax in a comfortable position, often lying down. Our practitioner, , will play various instruments, creating soothing sounds and vibrations that wash over you. There's no participation required from you, just simply listening and allowing yourself to be present with the sounds.
Is a Sound Bath suitable for someone with mobility concerns?
Absolutely. Our Sound Bath sessions are designed to be fully accessible and comfortable for everyone. We can accommodate various seating or lying arrangements, ensuring you feel completely at ease throughout the session, regardless of your mobility.
How can a Sound Bath help with stress and an overactive mind?
The gentle, continuous sounds in a Sound Bath help to quiet your mental chatter and shift your brainwaves to a more relaxed state. This encourages your nervous system to calm down, reducing feelings of stress and promoting a sense of tranquility and mental clarity.
What should I bring or wear to a Sound Bath?
We recommend wearing comfortable clothing that allows you to relax fully. You might also want to bring a small pillow or a favorite blanket for extra comfort, though we also provide soft coverings. The most important thing is that you feel completely at ease.
