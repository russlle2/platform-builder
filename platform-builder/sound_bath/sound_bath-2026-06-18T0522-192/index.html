Frequently Asked Questions

Is a Sound Bath safe for me during postpartum recovery?
Absolutely. Our Sound Bath sessions are designed with the delicate needs of postpartum mothers in mind. The vibrations are gentle and non-invasive, focused on promoting relaxation and calm. We create a nurturing environment specifically to support your recovery and well-being. If you have specific concerns, we encourage you to discuss them with your healthcare provider.
How can a Sound Bath specifically help with my sleep as a new mother?
During postpartum, your nervous system can be highly activated. A Sound Bath helps to down-regulate this activation, guiding your brain into theta and delta wave states, which are associated with deep relaxation and restorative sleep. By reducing stress and quieting your mind, you'll find it easier to fall asleep and experience more profound, uninterrupted rest, even if it's in short bursts.
What should I expect during my first Sound Bath session?
You'll be invited to lie down comfortably on a mat, often with blankets and pillows for support. We'll begin with a brief introduction, and then you'll simply relax as the practitioner plays various instruments. Most people experience a profound sense of calm, and it's common to drift into a deeply relaxed or meditative state. You might even fall asleep, which is perfectly fine and often indicates your body is receiving the rest it needs.
Do I need to do anything special to prepare for a Sound Bath?
No special preparation is needed. We recommend wearing comfortable clothing, and perhaps bringing an eye mask if you prefer complete darkness. The most important thing is to arrive with an open mind, ready to relax and receive the sounds. We provide everything else you'll need for your comfort during the session.
