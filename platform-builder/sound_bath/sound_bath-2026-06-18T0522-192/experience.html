Sound Bath

Your Path to Deeper Rest with a Postpartum Sound Bath

In a private Sound Bath session, you'll lie comfortably, allowing the sounds to wash over you. There's no effort required on your part, just an invitation to receive. Many new mothers find that this dedicated time for stillness helps them release the day's anxieties, making it easier to wind down and experience a quality of rest that often feels elusive during postpartum. We aim to help you feel more refreshed and present for yourself and your baby.
