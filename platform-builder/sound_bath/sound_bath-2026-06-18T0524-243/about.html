What is a Sound Bath and how is it helpful for seniors?

A Sound Bath is a gentle experience where you relax comfortably while various instruments, like singing bowls and gongs, create resonant sounds. For seniors, it's particularly helpful for easing stress, supporting emotional processing, and finding deep relaxation without any physical exertion. It's a peaceful way to find inner quiet.

Will I need to do anything during the Sound Bath session?

Absolutely not. Your only role is to relax and receive the sounds. You can lie down or sit comfortably, whatever feels best for your body. There's no right or wrong way to experience it; simply allow the gentle vibrations to wash over you and support your emotional comfort.

Is the Sound Bath suitable if I have experienced difficult life events?

Yes, our Sound Bath practice is trauma-informed. This means we prioritize creating a safe and gentle environment, offering choices and respecting your boundaries. The experience is designed to be invitational and supportive, allowing you to engage only as much as feels comfortable, helping you feel secure and at ease.

What if I fall asleep during the Sound Bath?

That's perfectly fine! Many people find the sounds so relaxing that they drift off to sleep, or into a deeply relaxed state. It's a sign that your body and mind are feeling safe enough to rest and release tension. You'll still receive the benefits of the gentle vibrations, whether you're awake or asleep.
