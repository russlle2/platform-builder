How a session flows

  1. Welcome & short intake
  2. Grounding breath + guided relaxation
  3. Layered sound journey (~45-60 min)

What to bring

  • Yoga mat, blanket, pillow
  • Light eye covering (bandana okay)
  • Water bottle and an intention

Contraindications

Not recommended if you are pregnant without provider sign-off, have severe vertigo, or recently had major surgery. Please reach out before booking for any implants, epilepsy, or cardiovascular concerns.
