Myths vs reality of a sound bath

A playful, premium clearing of misconceptions so you arrive relaxed and informed.

Myth: It’s passive background noise

Some think sound baths are just pleasant ambient music. That’s not the case: this is an intentionally crafted sound journey designed to move attention and body rhythms.

Truth: It’s a guided, sensory process

Facilitated sound uses tuned instruments and pacing to guide breath, reduce nervous-system arousal, and invite interoception. Expect structure: arrival, immersion, integration.

Our pillars

Three commitments that shape every session.

1. Sensory Design

Soundscapes built for attunement — layers of crystal bowls, tuning forks and a gentle monochord create harmonic space.

2. Safety & Care

Clear guidance, contraindications, and soft transitions ensure everyone’s nervous system is supported.

3. Integration

Time and tools to anchor insights: journaling prompts, breath work, and gentle movement at the close.

Session flow & what to expect

A brief walk-through so you can arrive prepared.

  1. Arrival & Settle: We begin with grounding prompts and choose a comfortable position on your mat.
  2. Intention Setting: A short guided breath and optional journaling to anchor your goals for the session.
  3. Immersion: 35–60 minutes of layered sound — crystal bowls, chimes, gong and tuning forks. Waves of intensity, then soft returns.
  4. Return & Integration: Gentle re-entry with breathwork, tea, and space for notes or sharing.

What to bring

  • Yoga mat or thick blanket
  • Pillow and layers to stay warm
  • Eye mask (optional) and open curiosity

Contraindications & safety

Not recommended if you have untreated epilepsy, recent head injury, or are currently under a heavy psychotropic regimen without facilitator clearance. If you have significant hearing sensitivity, pregnancy or implants that react to vibration, please consult with us before booking.
