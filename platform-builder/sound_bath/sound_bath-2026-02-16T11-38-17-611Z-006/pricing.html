What's included & sound profile

Every session includes intentional holding, a curated instrument palette, and time for integration. For groups we commonly blend:

  • Bronze singing bowls and light harmonic layers
  • Soft tuning fork interventions for targeted release
  • Long-form drones and textural shimmers

Note: instrument combinations vary by offering to provide distinct sonic experiences.

Payment & policy

We accept card and bank transfers. Cancellations: group ticket refunds up to 48 hours before start. Private session cancellations require 72 hours notice for full refund.

For corporate bookings, a deposit is required to secure date. Custom proposals available on request.

Contraindications & care

If you have a pacemaker, are in a fragile medical state, or are pregnant, please consult a physician and tell us before booking. We design lower-intensity alternatives or distance-based practices when necessary.
