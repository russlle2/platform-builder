What to bring

A small checklist to arrive prepared and comfortable.

  • Yoga mat or meditation cushion (we provide limited mats)
  • Blanket & light pillow for comfort and warmth
  • Water bottle (stay hydrated before and after)
  • Optional eye mask and ear plugs for deeper focus

How a session flows

A gentle roadmap of a typical offering.

  1. Welcome & intention setting (5–10 min) — arrivals, grounding, and breathwork.
  2. Sound immersion (30–60 min) — layered instruments create waves of tone and silence.
  3. Transition & integration (10–15 min) — soft tones guide you back; optional journaling.
  4. Sharing & scheduling (5 min) — time for questions and next steps.

Contraindications & note

Sound baths are restorative but not suitable for everyone. Please consult if you have recent head injury, epilepsy, pacemaker, or are in early pregnancy. This is not a substitute for medical care. Contact us if you’re unsure.

Myths vs. truths about sound baths

Myth

Sound baths will force sleep.

Truth

While many drift into a rested state, the experience is about embodied listening and nervous system regulation. Wakeful integration is common.

Myth

Loud instruments are always used.

Truth

We curate dynamics intentionally. Soft crystal tones, tuning forks, and monochord drones are layered with space to breathe.

Myth

One session fits all.

Truth

Each offering is designed for its container—community gatherings differ from 1:1 immersions and corporate sound meditations.

Frequently asked

How long are group sound baths?
Typical public offerings are 60–90 minutes including an arrival buffer. Private sessions can be tailored from 45 to 120 minutes.
Which instruments do you use?
Our palette may include crystal bowls, chimes, tuning forks, gongs, and the monochord. For private bookings we curate an instrument mix that supports your goals.
Can I bring a friend?
Yes—for group events bring friends and for private sessions you can book couples or small groups; let us know headcount when contacting.
