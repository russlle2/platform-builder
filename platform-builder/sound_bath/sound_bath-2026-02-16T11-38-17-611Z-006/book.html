Session types

Drop-in Circle
45–60 min • gentle, communal container • perfect for quick resets.
Intensive
90–120 min • 1:1 or small group • deep somatic protocols, sound layering, and post-session integration.
Private offerings
1:1 focused sessions, couples intensives, and corporate wellness packages are available. Each intensive includes tailored pre-session intake and a follow-up integration plan.

Session flow (intensive)

  1. Pre-session intake & intention setting
  2. Preparatory breath and embodied anchoring
  3. Layered sound journey (90–120 min)
  4. Slow re-entry, journaling & integration plan

What to bring

  • Mat, blanket, pillow, and eye covering
  • Water and any herbal aids you use habitually
  • Open curiosity and a journal for intensives
Safety note: If you are pregnant, have a pacemaker, epilepsy, or recent major surgery, please consult with us before booking. Certain low-frequency instruments may be adapted or omitted.

Instruments & intention

For intensives we favor a contemplative palette. Expect combinations like the monochord, crystal bowls, shruti box, and soft tuning forks chosen to sculpt long-form listening. Each modality is applied deliberately, not all at once.
