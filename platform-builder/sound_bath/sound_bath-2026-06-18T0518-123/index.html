Frequently Asked Questions

How does Sound Bath specifically help seniors with sleep?
Our Sound Bath sessions use gentle, resonant frequencies that encourage your body and mind to enter a deeply relaxed state. This helps quiet an overactive mind and soothe physical discomforts, making it easier for you to fall asleep naturally and enjoy more restful, uninterrupted nights.
Do I need any special preparation for a Sound Bath session?
Not at all. We simply ask that you come dressed in comfortable clothing. You'll be resting comfortably on a mat or in a recliner, so no physical exertion is required. Just bring an open heart and a willingness to receive the gentle sounds.
Is Sound Bath suitable if I have hearing aids or specific health conditions?
The sounds are gentle and non-intrusive. If you wear hearing aids, you can keep them in or remove them, depending on your comfort. We always recommend discussing any specific health concerns with your doctor and mentioning them to so we can ensure your utmost comfort and safety during the session.
How often should I attend a Sound Bath to see improvements in my sleep?
Many of our cherished seniors report feeling more relaxed and sleeping better after just one session. For sustained improvement in sleep quality and overall calm, we suggest attending regularly, perhaps once a week or bi-weekly. We can discuss a rhythm that feels right for you.
