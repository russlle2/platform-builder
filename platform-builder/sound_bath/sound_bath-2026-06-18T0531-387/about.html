What exactly happens during a Sound Bath session?

During a Sound Bath, you’ll lie down comfortably on a mat, often with a blanket and pillow, while Dr. Morgan Ellis plays various instruments like gongs, crystal singing bowls, and chimes. The sounds create a gentle, immersive experience that helps your body and mind relax without you needing to do anything specific. Your only task is to be present and allow the sounds to wash over you.

Do I need any prior experience with meditation or wellness practices?

Absolutely not! Our Sound Bath sessions are designed for everyone, especially those new to wellness practices. There’s no right or wrong way to experience it. You don't need to meditate or have any special skills. Just come as you are, relax, and let the sounds guide you into a state of calm.

How can a Sound Bath help with physical pain and stress from my demanding schedule?

The gentle vibrations from the instruments can help to release muscle tension and calm your nervous system, which are often contributors to physical pain like headaches, neck stiffness, or jaw clenching common in high-stress roles. By promoting deep relaxation, your body gets a chance to unwind and reset, reducing the physical manifestations of stress. Many clients report feeling a significant easing of discomfort after a session.

What should I bring or wear to a Sound Bath?

We recommend wearing comfortable clothing that allows you to fully relax. You might also want to bring a water bottle to stay hydrated. We provide mats, blankets, and pillows, but if you have a favorite personal blanket or eye mask that enhances your comfort, you’re welcome to bring it along. The goal is for you to feel completely at ease.
