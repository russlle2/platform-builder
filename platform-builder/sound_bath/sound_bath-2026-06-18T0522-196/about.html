Will a Sound Bath really help my sleep when I'm dealing with chronic illness?

Yes, many individuals recovering from chronic conditions find Sound Bath to be profoundly helpful. The deep relaxation induced by the specific frequencies can calm an overactive nervous system, which is often a barrier to restorative sleep during recovery. It's designed to help your body remember how to access deeper states of rest.

What if I can't lie still for a long time due to my injury or illness?

Your comfort is our top priority. We provide options for various positions, including lying down with bolsters and blankets, or even seated if that's more comfortable for you. The benefits of the sound vibrations are still received regardless of your physical posture. We'll ensure you're fully supported throughout the session.

How often should I attend a Sound Bath to see improvements in my sleep?

While even one session can offer significant relaxation, consistent engagement often yields the best results for long-term sleep improvement during recovery. Many clients find that weekly or bi-weekly sessions help to retrain their body and mind for deeper rest, creating a cumulative effect that supports their overall healing.

Is Sound Bath safe if I'm taking medications for my chronic condition?

Sound Bath is generally safe and non-invasive. However, if you have specific concerns due to medications or medical devices, we always recommend consulting with your primary healthcare provider before attending. We can discuss any adaptations to ensure your complete comfort and safety during the session.
