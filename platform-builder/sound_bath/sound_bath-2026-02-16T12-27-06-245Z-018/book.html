Session flow — what to expect

  1. Arrival & soft welcome — settle, drink water, share any needs.
  2. Brief centering — breath and intention (10–15 mins).
  3. Main sound journey — layered instruments guide a slow arc (40–60 mins).
  4. Slow closing & integration — space for quiet reflection, tea, and gentle reorientation.

Prepare — what to bring

  • Thick mat or yoga mat + a blanket
  • Pillow or folded blanket for head/neck support
  • Extra layer (sweater or shawl)
  • Water bottle

We provide bolsters and extra blankets when possible; bringing your own ensures comfort.

Contraindications & safety

If any of the following apply, please contact your healthcare provider and inform the facilitator before the session: pregnancy, epilepsy, implanted electronic devices (e.g. pacemaker), recent head surgery, or severe cardiac conditions. Sound practice should not replace medical advice.

Instruments for this series

  • Tibetan & brass singing bowls — warm harmonic sustain
  • Koshi & wind chimes — bright, airy overtones
  • Ocean drum — soft, textural wash

For private or corporate bookings we can curate a different instrumental palette upon request.
