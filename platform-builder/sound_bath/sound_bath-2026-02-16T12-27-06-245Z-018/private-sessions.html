Flow of a private session

  1. Intake conversation & intention setting
  2. Light somatic centering (5–10 min)
  3. Tailored sound immersion (40–70 min, depending on package)
  4. Slow return, reflections, and aftercare suggestions

What to bring: comfortable cushions, water, any supportive blankets. Wear clothing that allows restful stillness.

Contraindications: If you have severe cardiovascular conditions, recent head trauma, or implanted electronic medical devices, please consult your physician before booking.
