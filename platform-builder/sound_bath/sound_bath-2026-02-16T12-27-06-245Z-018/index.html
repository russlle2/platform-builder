Before you arrive — a quick guide

What to bring

  • Yoga mat or cushion, blanket, eye pillow
  • Water bottle, light layer for warmth
  • Open curiosity — phones off

Contraindications

Sound baths are gentle but powerful. Please consult us before booking if you have: pregnancy, active epilepsy, pacemaker/metal implants, recent surgery, severe psychiatric conditions. This session is not a substitute for medical care.

Flow of the evening

  1. Arrival & grounding — intention setting

  4. Soft reintegration — silence and tea

How a sound experience is designed

A careful architecture of frequency and silence: we layer crystal bowls, a warm gong swell, metallic chimes and light monophonic drones to open the nervous system, then steady the descent with tuning forks and voice.
