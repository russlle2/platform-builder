Evenings of resonance and gentle recalibration

Join a small circle for a guided sound journey — a blend of crystal bowls, chimes, and subtle tuning forks to open the senses and settle the nervous system.

What to expect
Arrival & grounding — soft tea, settling breathwork.
Main immersion — 45–55 minutes of layered soundscapes; instruments shift and sustain.
Return — gentle silence, optional journaling, and time to reorient.

Contraindications

Not recommended during acute psychiatric crises, unstable epilepsy, or if you are pregnant and advised to avoid sustained loud vibration. If you have implanted medical devices (pacemaker), consult a clinician first.

What to bring

  • Yoga mat or blanket, pillow for head support
  • Warm layers and an eye mask
  • Water bottle; leave earbuds and active phones off

Is a sound bath right for you?

Who benefits

  • People seeking rest from overwhelm
  • Those curious about somatic relaxation
  • Anyone wanting a gentle meditative container

Sensory notes

  • Low harmonics wash over the body
  • Soft silence between tones invites introspection
  • Not loud; vibration is felt more than heard

Session plan

A concise map of the evening — we begin with anchoring, move into resonance, then offer a gentle landing.

  1. Welcome & intention setting (10 min)
  2. Breathwork & vocalized tone (5–10 min)
  3. Main sound immersion — layers of crystal bowls, chimes, and tuning forks (45–55 min)
  4. Rest & integration (10–15 min)
