What to bring — and what to tell us

A sensory, premium offering: we cultivate a slow, safe atmosphere. Tell us about anything that affects your comfort.

  • Bring: a yoga mat or blanket, pillow, cozy layers, and a bottle of water.
  • Optional: eye-cover, small bolster, and earplugs if you prefer quieter frequency transitions.
  • Contraindications: Please let us know if you are pregnant, have epilepsy, pacemaker, recent surgery, severe anxiety or PTSD; some instruments and frequencies may be avoided or adapted. This session is not a substitute for medical care.

Flow: arrival & settling → short intention setting → a 50–70 minute immersion of instruments (crystal bowls, chimes, tuning forks, occasional gong) → gentle re-entry and brief sharing.
