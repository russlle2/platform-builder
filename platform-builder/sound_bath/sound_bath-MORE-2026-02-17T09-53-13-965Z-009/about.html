We curate shared sound spaces for steady attention and rest.

How we approach sound

We build simple structures: invitation, sustained tone, and a return. The intention is to support clearer breath, a quieter mind, and practical calm you can carry home.

Our story

We started as a handful of people looking for practices that fit between therapy and life tasks. Over time we found a steady rhythm that blends curated tones, guided breath, and moments to write or set an intention—tools you can use beyond the room.

What to expect

  • Sessions last 45–70 minutes with clear arrival and closing guidance.
  • Gentle movement and seated options are supported.
  • Phones on silent; quiet conversation before and after is welcome.
Contraindications & safety:
If you are pregnant, have uncontrolled epilepsy, recent head injury, are highly sensitive to sound, or have any implantable medical device that could be affected by sustained vibration, please consult a physician before attending. If you are in active crisis or need urgent mental health care, this is not a replacement for clinical treatment.
