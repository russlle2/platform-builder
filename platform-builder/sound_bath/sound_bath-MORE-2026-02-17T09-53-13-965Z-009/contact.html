Visiting & COVID / health notes

Arrive 10 minutes early for check-in. We maintain sanitized spaces and gentle ventilation between sessions.

Contraindications — please read

Sound work is a gentle adjunct practice, but it is not appropriate for everyone. Please consult a medical professional before attending if you have any of the following: epilepsy, uncontrolled heart conditions, recent major surgery, pregnancy (first-trimester consult recommended), or severe psychiatric conditions such as active psychosis. If you’re unsure, contact us and we’ll speak about how to make sessions safe for you.
