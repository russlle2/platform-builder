Common questions

What should I bring?

Comfortable clothing, something to lie on (yoga mat), a light blanket, and an open curiosity. We have a small selection of borrowed mats and blankets if you need them.

How loud are sessions?

We use tuned metal and crystal bowls and layered textures. Volume is moderated for the room and there are quiet seating options in the back. If you are sound-sensitive, get in touch before your visit.

Is this therapy?

These gatherings are supportive practices but not a substitute for mental health care. If you are receiving clinical treatment or are in crisis, please contact a licensed provider.

Are children allowed?

Our public sessions are intended for adults. Family-friendly or intergenerational offerings will be noted where appropriate.

Safety & contraindications

Please note:
If you have uncontrolled seizures, a history of traumatic brain injury, are in the later stages of pregnancy, use certain implantable medical devices, or are highly sensitive to vibration, consult a medical professional before attending. If you are currently in acute psychological crisis, these events are not a substitute for clinical care.
