How membership works

Members are billed monthly and may pause with 30 days notice. Memberships include complimentary check-ins and reduced rates on private sessions. We keep numbers small to preserve the intimate container.

Before you arrive

  • Bring a mat, cushion, and blanket or request a kit on booking.
  • Avoid heavy meals and alcohol within a few hours of your session.
  • Hydrate and arrive with time to settle.

Contraindications & notes

Please inform us of pregnancy, implanted devices, inner ear sensitivity, recent surgeries, or seizure history. We adapt instrumentation (for instance: softer chimes instead of loud gongs) to ensure safety and comfort.

Session flow (short)

Arrival → intake → grounding → sound immersion (crystal bowls, chimes, gentle gong or tuning forks) → silent rest → soft reorientation.
