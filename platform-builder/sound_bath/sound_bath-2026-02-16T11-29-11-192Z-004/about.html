How a session is designed

Think of it like being led through a gentle tide: arrival, grounding, the sound journey, soft re-entry. We balance tone and silence to shape a meditative arc.

Arrival

Warm tea, ambient light, a short orientation so your body can soften.

Sound Journey

An hour of layered resonance: crystal bowls, hand chimes and a one-string monochord weave slow frequencies to guide breath.

Close

A period of silence, gentle reawakening, and optional journaling prompts for integration.

What to bring & safety notes

You don’t need much. Our intention is comfort and attentiveness.

  • Yoga mat or cushion (we have a limited supply)
  • A light blanket and an eye mask for longer stays
  • Water bottle and contact info for pick-up

Contraindications: If you have epilepsy, recent concussion, severe psychiatric conditions, or are pregnant and not cleared by a provider, please contact us before booking. Sound is deeply felt; we will advise alternatives as needed.

Small practices between gatherings

  1. 5-minute breath at sunrise — sit, listen to one bowl recording.
  2. Weekly audio check-in — notice how your body responds to sound.
  3. Member circle — one short reflection after a session to keep the rhythm alive.
