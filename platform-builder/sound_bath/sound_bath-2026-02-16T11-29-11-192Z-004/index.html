Which session fits your rhythm?

Whether you arrive curious or carried by grief, there is a soundscape designed for you. Consider these guidelines to choose the right circle.

What we play

Our group rituals weave crystal bowls, a gentle gong, chimes, and a tuned monochord. Each instrument is selected for tonal clarity and depth to hold your attention without demanding it.

What to bring

  • A yoga mat or padded blanket
  • A soft blanket and eye mask for warmth and dark
  • Optional pillow for neck support
  • Water to stay grounded after the session

Contraindications & safety

Sound can be intense; please consult a healthcare provider if pregnant, have recent surgery, epilepsy, implanted medical devices (e.g., pacemaker), or are under medication that affects sensory processing. If in doubt, reach out at {{EMAIL}}.

Session flow

We begin with a gentle settling breath and intention, move through a live instrument set focused on breath-synced tones, and close with time to journal and assimilate. Expect 60–75 minutes in total.

Micro-habits for daily calm

Tiny, repeatable practices to extend the resonance of the sound bath into everyday life.

  2. Wind-down scan: lie for five minutes and visualize each rib cage softening with the out-breath.
  3. Sonic bookmark: pick a bell sound as a cue to take three deep, mindful breaths.
