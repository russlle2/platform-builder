What to expect

A gentle arc from arrival to integration. We use layered instruments like crystal bowls, a single gong pulse and hand chimes to transition you through an embodied listening journey.

Duration 75 min

What to bring

  • Yoga mat, blanket and pillow/bolster
  • Water bottle and comfortable layers
  • Earplugs if you prefer softer dynamics

Health note

Contraindications: pregnancy (consult facilitator), recent surgery, uncontrolled seizures, or inner ear conditions. Please email {{EMAIL}} for any questions.

Who this is for

A sensory offering for anyone seeking a ritual pause — people navigating stress, creatives needing clarity, and those curious about somatic sound work.

Session flow

  1. Arrival + gentle arrival breath (10–15 min)
  2. Guided body scan and intention setting (10 min)
  3. Sound journey with layered instruments (45–50 min): bowed monochord, crystal bowls, chimes
  4. Silent rest and gradual re-entry (8–12 min)
  5. Optional tea + silent sharing after

Pre-event micro-habits

Hydrate

Drink water in the hours before; avoid heavy meals.

Simplify

Switch your phone to airplane mode and wear layers you can remove.

Rest

If possible, avoid caffeine for several hours — arrive from a quiet place.
