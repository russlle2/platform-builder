Tiny listening practices

  • 1-minute bell before bed for three nights.
  • Midday pause: notice sounds without labeling them.
  • Weekly member check-in recording — five minutes.

Practical details

Arrive 15–20 minutes early to settle. Typical flow: greeting, centering, 50 minutes of sound, five minutes of silence and reorientation. We ask guests to be quiet in the lobby to preserve the field.

What to bring
  • Cushion or mat (we provide for members)
  • Warm blanket and socks
  • Earplugs if you are sensitive to low frequencies
Contraindications & safety

If you have epilepsy, pacemakers, severe psychiatric conditions or are pregnant without clearance, contact us before booking. For louder instruments we can arrange a less intense placement.

Sound palette for your booking

For private bookings we often include crystal singing bowls, koshi chimes, and a shruti box to create layered, intimate resonance.
