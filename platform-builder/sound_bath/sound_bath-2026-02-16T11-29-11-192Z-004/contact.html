What to bring

Comfort deepens the experience. Bring:

  • Comfortable mat, blanket, and a bolster or pillow
  • Eye covering for gentle darkness
  • Reusable water bottle and an openness to rest

Contraindications & safety

While sound baths are gentle, please consult with a medical professional and inform the facilitator before attending if you have epilepsy, severe cardiovascular issues, recent head injury, or are in early pregnancy.

  3. Soft reorientation & tea (10–15 min)
