Offerings

1:1 Therapeutic Session

A private, uninterrupted container for deep nervous system work. Instruments used may include tuning forks, koshi chimes, and a bowed monochord for sustained harmonics.

Couples & Shared Sessions

Designed to support shared relaxation and co-regulation — slightly longer settling time with paired intention-setting.

Corporate & Team Wellness

Mini sound baths for off-sites, retreats or focused reset sessions. We adapt to space and group size; sessions emphasize coherence and stress relief.

Typical flow (flexible)

  1. Intake & intentions (5–10 min)
  2. Guided breathwork & grounding (10 min)
  3. Sound immersion (30–50 min) — orchestration with tuning forks, singing bowls, small gong
  4. Integration & notes (10 min)

Who benefits

People recovering from burnout, chronic tension, performers prepping for events, and teams seeking collective stillness.
