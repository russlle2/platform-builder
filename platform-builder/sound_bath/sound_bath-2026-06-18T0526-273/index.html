Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Sound Bath and how can it help me, as a senior?
A Sound Bath is a deeply relaxing experience where you are bathed in therapeutic sound vibrations. For seniors, it offers a gentle, non-physical way to reduce stress, improve sleep, and subtly address emotional burdens or past experiences. It’s designed to soothe your nervous system and promote a sense of inner calm without any strenuous effort on your part.
Is the Sound Bath safe for someone with my health considerations?
Yes, our Sound Bath sessions are intentionally gentle and are considered very safe. We maintain a calm and supportive environment. If you have specific health concerns, we encourage you to discuss them with beforehand. Your comfort and well-being are paramount, and we will ensure the experience is tailored to your needs.
I've carried some difficult memories. Can Sound Bath really help with emotional healing?
Our approach is trauma-informed, meaning we understand and respect the sensitivities around difficult memories. Sound Bath provides a gentle, non-confrontational way to help calm your nervous system, which can create a safe space for emotions to surface and release naturally, without pressure. It’s about fostering resilience and finding peace within, at your own pace.
What should I wear or bring to a Sound Bath session?
We recommend wearing comfortable, loose-fitting clothing that allows you to relax fully. You might also consider bringing a warm blanket or a small pillow to enhance your comfort, though we provide all necessary props. The goal is for you to feel completely at ease and supported throughout the session.
