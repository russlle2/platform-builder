Questions answered

Short, practical answers for attending and booking. If you need help, reach out by email or phone.

What should I bring?

Wear comfortable layers. We provide mats, blankets, and eye coverings if you prefer. If you bring your own cushion, all the better.

How long are sessions?

Group gatherings typically run 60–75 minutes. Private sessions can be 45–90 minutes, depending on the chosen format.

Are sessions suitable for first-timers?

Yes. We guide beginners through the room’s flow and offer adjustments for sensory needs. Tell the host if you'd like a quieter spot.

Can I bring a phone or recording device?

Phones must be silent and put away. Recordings are discouraged to protect others' privacy and the integrity of the experience.

How do private sessions differ?

Private sessions are tailored: we orient the sound and timing to your needs. They blend assessment, targeted resonance, and time to integrate.

Contraindications and safety

Sound practices can affect blood pressure, balance, and emotional intensity. Please consult a healthcare provider if you are pregnant, have a recent concussion, uncontrolled epilepsy, severe psychiatric conditions, or implanted medical devices. Share relevant health information before booking; we can often adapt sessions to be safer and more comfortable.
