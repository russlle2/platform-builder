Anything to know before you come?

Arrive 10 minutes early. Wear layers. We provide bolsters and a limited number of mats — bringing your own mat or blanket is recommended.

Is it suitable if I have health concerns?

While many benefit from a sound session, there are important cautions. If you have a history of severe psychiatric episodes, photosensitivity-driven seizures, are medically unstable, or have recent concussions, consult your clinician before attending.

Contraindications guide: pregnancy with complications, implanted pacemakers or similar devices—contact us first at {{EMAIL}}.
