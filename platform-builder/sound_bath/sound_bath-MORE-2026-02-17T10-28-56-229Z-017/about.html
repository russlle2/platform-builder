Our approach

We blend tuned bowls, breath-led cues, and short reflective prompts so that the room is both refuge and toolkit. Each session returns you to intention with specific practices you can repeat at home.

What we do not do

This is not a diagnostic therapy session. If you are seeking medical treatment, psychoanalytic therapy, or emergency care, please pursue licensed professionals. Our offerings are complementary practices for rest and focus.

Contraindications: If you have epilepsy triggered by flashing lights, recent head injury, severe psychiatric instability, or pregnancy with complications, consult a healthcare provider before attending. If you use oxygen or have implanted medical devices, contact us first: {{EMAIL}}.
