Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is a Sound Bath, and how can it support me during postpartum?
A Sound Bath is a deeply relaxing experience where you lie comfortably while various instruments like crystal bowls and chimes are played. For new mothers, it offers a dedicated time to rest, reduce stress, and quiet the mind, helping you reconnect with your spiritual core and find inner peace amidst the demands of caring for a newborn.
Is a Sound Bath safe for me if I'm still recovering physically from childbirth?
Absolutely. Our Sound Baths are designed to be incredibly gentle and passive. You simply lie down in a comfortable position, often with blankets and pillows for support. There's no physical exertion required, making it perfectly safe and beneficial for your physical recovery, allowing your body to rest deeply while your spirit is soothed.
I feel so overwhelmed and tired. Will I be able to relax enough to benefit from a Sound Bath?
It's completely normal to feel overwhelmed and tired as a new mother. Many of our clients express similar feelings. The beauty of a Sound Bath is that the sounds themselves do the work of guiding you into a relaxed state. You don't need to 'try' to relax; just allow the gentle vibrations to wash over you, and you might be surprised by the profound sense of calm you find.
How can a Sound Bath help me with the emotional and spiritual shifts of motherhood?
The resonant frequencies in a Sound Bath can help clear mental fog and emotional tension, creating space for clarity and acceptance. By quieting the external noise, you can tune into your intuition, process new feelings, and foster a deeper connection to your spiritual self, supporting your emotional well-being and inner peace during this transformative period.
