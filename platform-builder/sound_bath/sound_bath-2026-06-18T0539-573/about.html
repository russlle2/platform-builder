Is a Sound Bath suitable for seniors with mobility concerns?

Absolutely. Our Sound Bath sessions are designed for comfort and accessibility. You can recline on a mat, sit in a comfortable chair, or even remain in your wheelchair if preferred. The experience is entirely passive; your only requirement is to relax and receive the sounds.

What should I expect to feel during a Sound Bath?

Many participants report feeling deeply relaxed, a sense of lightness, or even a gentle tingling sensation. Some experience mental clarity, emotional release, or a profound sense of peace. Your experience will be unique, and there's no right or wrong way to feel. The intention is always to support your inner tranquility.

Do I need any prior experience with meditation or spiritual practices?

Not at all. A Sound Bath requires no prior experience or special skills. Just come with an open mind and a willingness to relax. Dr. Morgan Ellis will guide you gently through the session, making it accessible for everyone, regardless of your background in spiritual practices.

How can a Sound Bath support my spiritual growth and inner peace?

The resonant sounds help to quiet the mental chatter, allowing you to access a deeper state of calm and introspection. This can create space for clarity, self-reflection, and a stronger connection to your inner wisdom, fostering a renewed sense of purpose and spiritual well-being in your life.
