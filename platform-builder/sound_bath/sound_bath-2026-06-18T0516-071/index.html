Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Sound Bath specifically help with my professional burnout?
Our Sound Bath sessions are designed to target the physiological effects of chronic stress. The resonant frequencies help to down-regulate your overactive nervous system, reducing cortisol and promoting deep relaxation. This directly combats mental fatigue, difficulty concentrating, and the persistent 'on-edge' feeling often associated with professional burnout, helping you reclaim your mental sharpness and emotional equilibrium.
Do I need to do anything during a Sound Bath session?
Absolutely not. Your only task is to lie back comfortably and allow the sounds to wash over you. There’s no meditation experience required, no specific breathing techniques to master. The instruments do the work, guiding your brainwaves into a state of deep rest, allowing your body to naturally de-stress and reset without any effort from your side.
What if my mind keeps racing during a Sound Bath?
It's completely normal for your mind to be active, especially when you're accustomed to a demanding schedule. The beauty of a Sound Bath is that the continuous, immersive soundscape provides a gentle anchor for your attention. Even if your thoughts wander, the vibrations are still working to soothe your nervous system. Many clients find that after a few minutes, the racing thoughts naturally begin to dissipate as the sounds take over.
How often should I attend Sound Bath sessions to see results for burnout?
For sustained relief from burnout and to build resilience, consistency is key. We recommend starting with a weekly session for 4-6 weeks to allow your nervous system to deeply re-regulate. From there, many working professionals find that bi-weekly or monthly sessions are excellent for maintaining balance and preventing the recurrence of burnout symptoms, keeping you performing at your best.
