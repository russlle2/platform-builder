Sound Bath

Your Gentle Journey: What to Expect During a Postpartum Sound Bath

In a Sound Bath, you'll lie comfortably, wrapped in warmth, while therapeutic sounds wash over you. There's no need to 'do' anything; simply receive. This passive experience encourages your mind to quiet, allowing for deep relaxation and the gentle processing of stored emotions. Many new mothers find this helps integrate their birth story and ease the transition into their new role.
