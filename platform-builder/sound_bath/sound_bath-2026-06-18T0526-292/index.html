Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Sound Bath help with the emotional intensity of postpartum?
Our Sound Bath sessions provide a non-verbal, gentle way to calm your often-overwhelmed nervous system during postpartum. The specific frequencies used can help reduce anxiety, promote emotional release, and create a sense of safety, allowing you to process the intense feelings that often accompany this period.
Is a Sound Bath safe if I've experienced birth trauma?
Yes, our Sound Bath practice is designed with trauma-informed principles. We prioritize creating a safe, supportive space where you feel in control. The sounds are never overwhelming, and you're encouraged to communicate any needs. It can be a powerful, gentle tool for integrating difficult experiences and promoting emotional repair.
What if I can't stay still or my baby needs me during a Sound Bath?
We understand the realities of new motherhood. Our sessions are designed to be flexible and understanding. You are welcome to adjust your position, tend to your baby if present, or step out if needed. The focus is always on your comfort and ability to receive the benefits at your own pace.
Will a Sound Bath replace therapy or medical care for postpartum mood challenges?
No, our Sound Bath practice is a complementary modality. It offers deep emotional support and stress reduction, which can significantly aid your well-being during postpartum. However, it is not a substitute for professional medical or mental health care, especially for conditions like postpartum depression or anxiety. We encourage you to seek appropriate professional support when needed.
