Finding Your Anchor: Sound Bath for the Postpartum Body

Your body has done incredible work, and now it deserves deep rest and regulation. Our Sound Bath sessions are crafted to support your physical and emotional recovery. We use resonant tones to gently guide your nervous system away from the 'fight or flight' response, fostering a state of profound relaxation that new mothers so rarely experience.

How can a Sound Bath specifically help my postpartum stress?

During a Sound Bath, the calming vibrations help to slow your brainwaves and downregulate your sympathetic nervous system, which is often overactive in the postpartum period due to sleep deprivation and new responsibilities. This gentle shift encourages deep relaxation, reducing cortisol levels and helping you feel more grounded and less overwhelmed.

Do I need to do anything during a Sound Bath session?

No, your only task is to simply lie back, get comfortable, and allow the sounds to wash over you. There's no need to meditate or follow any instructions; the sound waves do the work of gently encouraging your nervous system into a state of calm. You can close your eyes and just be.

Is a Sound Bath suitable if I'm still feeling very sensitive or emotional postpartum?

Absolutely. Our Sound Bath sessions are designed with gentleness in mind, creating a safe and supportive space for all emotional states. The intention is to offer a soft landing, helping to soothe heightened emotions and bring a sense of stability. We encourage you to listen to your body and communicate any concerns you may have.

What should I wear or bring to a Sound Bath session?

For maximum comfort and relaxation, please wear loose, comfortable clothing. We recommend bringing a water bottle to stay hydrated. We provide mats, blankets, and bolsters, but if you have a favorite pillow or eye mask that enhances your comfort, feel free to bring it along. The goal is for you to feel completely at ease.
