Frequently Asked Questions

What should I expect during my first Sound Bath session?
You'll lie down comfortably on a mat, often with a blanket and eye mask. will then play various instruments, creating an enveloping soundscape. Your only job is to relax and let the sounds wash over you. Many students find it easier to clear their minds and enter a state of deep relaxation, which can be very beneficial for reducing academic stress.
How can a Sound Bath specifically help with college anxiety and pressure?
Sound Baths help by activating your parasympathetic nervous system, which is your body's 'rest and digest' mode. This counteracts the 'fight or flight' response often triggered by academic pressure. Regular sessions can lead to a significant reduction in anxiety levels, improved emotional regulation, and enhanced mental clarity, helping you tackle your studies with a calmer, more focused mind.
Do I need any prior experience with meditation or sound therapy to attend?
Absolutely not! Our Sound Bath sessions are designed for everyone, regardless of prior experience. There's no right or wrong way to experience a Sound Bath. Just come with an open mind, and will guide you through the process, ensuring you feel comfortable and supported throughout the session. It's an accessible way to support your mental well-being.
What should I bring or wear to a Sound Bath session?
We recommend wearing comfortable, loose-fitting clothing that allows you to relax fully. You might want to bring a water bottle to stay hydrated. We provide mats, blankets, and eye masks, but feel free to bring your own if you prefer. The most important thing is to ensure you're cozy and ready to unwind for an hour of mental rejuvenation.
