Frequently Asked Questions

Is a Sound Bath safe for someone with mobility limitations or specific health conditions?
Absolutely. Our Sound Bath sessions are designed to be completely gentle and non-strenuous. You'll recline comfortably on a mat or even a chair if preferred, with no physical exertion required. If you have specific health concerns, it's always wise to mention them beforehand, and we can discuss any accommodations to ensure your utmost comfort and safety.
What should I expect to feel during a Sound Bath, especially for pain relief?
During a Sound Bath, you might experience deep relaxation, a sense of calm, and a gentle 'buzzing' or vibration through your body. Many seniors report a softening of muscle tension, reduced stiffness, and a noticeable decrease in the intensity of chronic aches. The goal is to encourage your body to release stress and find a more comfortable, balanced state.
How often should I attend a Sound Bath to see benefits for my physical pain?
The frequency that works best can vary for each individual. Some seniors find weekly sessions provide consistent relief and build cumulative benefits, while others prefer bi-weekly or monthly. We recommend trying a few sessions to observe how your body responds and then we can discuss a schedule that aligns with your specific needs and goals for pain management.
Do I need to do anything specific or have prior experience with meditation for a Sound Bath to work?
Not at all. No prior experience with meditation, yoga, or any specific practice is necessary. Your only 'task' during a Sound Bath is to relax and receive. The sounds do the work of guiding your mind and body into a state of deep rest, allowing your natural healing processes to activate. Just come as you are, and we'll take care of the rest.
