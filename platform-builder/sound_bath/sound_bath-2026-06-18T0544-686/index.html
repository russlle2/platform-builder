Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is a Sound Bath and how does it help with recovery?
A Sound Bath is a deeply relaxing experience where you are bathed in resonant sound waves produced by instruments like gongs and singing bowls. For those recovering from chronic illness or injury, this gentle immersion helps calm your nervous system, which in turn supports your body's natural ability to rest and restore. It's about creating a peaceful internal environment that can aid in immune support and rebuilding your energy.
Do I need any prior experience with meditation or wellness to participate in a Sound Bath?
Absolutely not! Our Sound Bath sessions are designed to be completely accessible, even if you have no prior experience with meditation or any other wellness practices. There's no right or wrong way to experience it. You just need to find a comfortable position, close your eyes if you wish, and allow the sounds to wash over you. We're here to guide you gently every step of the way.
What should I expect to feel or experience during a Sound Bath?
Most people report feeling deeply relaxed and peaceful during and after a Sound Bath. You might notice your mind quiet down, your body feel more at ease, and a sense of gentle calm. Some individuals experience subtle sensations from the vibrations, while others simply drift into a serene state. Whatever your experience, know that it's contributing to your body's ability to rest and recharge.
Is a Sound Bath suitable if I have specific sensitivities or physical limitations due to my recovery?
Yes, we prioritize your comfort and well-being. Our Sound Bath sessions are conducted with gentle care, and we can make adjustments to ensure you are as comfortable as possible. If you have specific sensitivities to sound or any physical limitations, please let us know beforehand. We can discuss your needs and ensure the experience is supportive and beneficial for your unique recovery journey.
