Why this format helps

We design each session to guide you through somatic arrival, deep listening, and gentle integration. Scientific research supports low-frequency sound and sustained tones for relaxation and improved sleep.

  • Instruments today: crystal bowls, gong, chimes — layered for full-spectrum resonance.
  • How it feels: a steady release of tension, subtle shifts in breathing, and a resting clarity afterwards.
  • Contraindications: If you have a pacemaker, recent surgery, epilepsy, pregnancy without medical clearance, or acute psychiatric crisis please consult your clinician before attending.

What to bring

  • Yoga mat or travel mattress
  • Blanket and extra layer — space can be cool
  • Eye pillow or small towel
  • Water bottle — hydrating before and after helps integration

If you need to borrow a blanket or bolster, arrive 15 minutes early and ask the facilitator.

Session flow — what to expect

  1. Arrival & settling (10–15m): gentle orientation, optional intake and setting intention.
  2. Guided breath & body (10m): slow breathwork and posture for safety and receptivity.
  3. The sound field (50–60m): progressive layers using crystal bowls, gong swells and chimes to encourage deeper brainwave states.
  4. Soft return & integration (10–15m): grounding prompts, tea, and time for questions.

Small habits to deepen your practice

Night before

Avoid heavy meals and alcohol. Light stretching and a short walk help nervous system regulation.

Arrive early

Give yourself ten minutes in silence to release schedules and transition.

Reflect

Spend 5 minutes journaling after the session to anchor insights.
