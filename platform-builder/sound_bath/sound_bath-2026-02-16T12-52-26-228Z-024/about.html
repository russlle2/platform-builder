Quick details

Length: 75–90 minutes • Small groups • Sensory-first setup

Next open event: {{NEXT_EVENT_DATE}}

Who this is for

People seeking a premium, sensory experience to reset. Folks who want guided rest—not meditation homework. It’s suitable for most adults, with restrictions listed below.

Instruments you may hear

  • Crystal singing bowls tuned to harmonic intervals
  • Subtle chimes and singing tube textures
  • Tuning forks for close, focused resonance

What the session flow looks like

  1. Arrival & gentle orientation (10–12 min)
  2. Guided settling and breathwork (8–12 min)
  3. Main soundscape — layered bowls & chimes (45–55 min)
  4. Slow reintegration, tea & space for reflection (10–15 min)

What to bring

  • A yoga mat or thick blanket, and a cushion for support
  • An eye-cover or light scarf for comfort
  • Water bottle; layers—rooms are gently cool

Medical & safety note

Contraindications: If you are pregnant, have a pacemaker, epilepsy, recent surgery, severe psychiatric conditions, or are under heavy medication that affects consciousness, please consult your physician before attending. Loud gongs may be used occasionally; we provide ear protection on request. This is not a replacement for medical care.
