Who benefits most

Individuals experiencing chronic tension, sleep disruption, or transition-related anxiety often report marked improvement after a private session. Our approach centers safety and pacing.

  • Contraindications: metal implants that interact with vibrations, seizure disorders, recent head trauma, or pregnancy without clearance. If in doubt, message us before booking.
  • Intake: we begin with a short interview to determine intensity and accommodations.

What to bring

  • Comfortable clothes
  • Favorite blanket or pillow
  • Any mobility aids you use

A typical private session

  1. Arrival & settling — intake questions and intention setting (10–15m)
  2. Slow somatic lead-in — breath and simple movements (5–10m)
  3. Sound immersion — calibrated to your nervous system (30–60m)
  4. Integration & aftercare — steps for sleep and gentle reintegration (10m)

Prep and follow-up micro-habits

Small consistent practices amplify session effects.

  • Hydrate well the day of the session.
  • Limit caffeine for 4–6 hours beforehand.
  • In the 24 hours after, prioritize rest and avoid intense conversations to allow integration.
