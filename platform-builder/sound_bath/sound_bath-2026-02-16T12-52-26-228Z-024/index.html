Micro-habits — sustainable sensory practice

Small daily gestures that compound. Built for people with busy lives who want deep change.

  • 1-minute bell: Ring a chime once on waking to orient attention.
  • Sound pause: 2 minutes of silence + breath before screens.
  • Evening tone: A 7-minute guided listening sequence before sleep.

Rotate between binaural listening, gentle humming, and a five-minute abdominal breathing practice. Keep a simple log for two weeks.

What to bring & safety

What to bring

  • Yoga mat or blanket
  • Pillow and cozy layers
  • Water bottle

Contraindications

To keep experiences safe: consult before booking if you have a pacemaker, epilepsy, recent concussion, are pregnant without medical clearance, or have severe auditory sensitivity. We may adapt or recommend alternatives.
