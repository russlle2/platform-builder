Micro Habits — Prepare & Recover
Small practices that heighten benefit
Practical • evidence-informed
  • Hydrate well — Drink water the day of and avoid heavy meals right before.
  • Wear soft layers — Comfortable clothing, socks, and a blanket for market-level temperature sensitivity.
  • Bring a pillow or eye-cover if you prefer. We provide mats but feel free to bring your favorites.
  • Journal — Bring a small notebook for reflections post-session.
  • Post-session — Light movement and water help integrate vibrational shifts.
What to bring: mat or blanket (if you have one), small pillow, water bottle, eye mask, and an open curiosity. For VIP Days we recommend a change of clothes and layers for comfort.
