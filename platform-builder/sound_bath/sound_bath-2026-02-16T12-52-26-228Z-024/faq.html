Short, practical answers to common logistics and safety points. If you don’t see your question, please reach out by phone or email.

Do I need experience to attend?

No. Sessions are guided and accessible. Expect a sensory-led environment; you’ll be invited to lie down and receive the sounds. We offer simple orientation upon arrival.

How loud are the instruments?

Volume is always moderated for comfort. Typical instruments used for FAQs include a gong for low-frequency depth and a monochord for sustained harmonic layers. We provide ear protection if you prefer reduced decibel exposure.

What should I bring?

Bring a yoga mat or thick blanket, a bolster (if you have one), an eye mask or scarf, and water. We supply blankets and some props, but space is limited—bringing your own ensures maximum comfort.

Contraindications — is this safe for everyone?

Contraindications include pregnancy (please consult your doctor), cardiac pacemakers, severe psychiatric disorders, photosensitive epilepsy, or recent major surgery. If you have concerns, email {{EMAIL}} or call {{PHONE}} before booking.

Cancellation & late-arrival policy

Cancellations with full refund up to 48 hours prior. Late arrivals may miss portions of the experience; we do not pro-rate sessions. For private and VIP bookings, please check our policies on the private page or contact us directly.
