Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Sound Bath specifically help my immune system as a new mother?
Our Sound Bath sessions promote deep relaxation, which can significantly reduce stress. High stress levels can suppress your immune system, so by creating a calm environment, we help your body shift into a restorative state. This supports your natural ability to fight off illness and recover more efficiently during this crucial postpartum period.
I'm so tired. Will I be able to stay awake during a Sound Bath, or is it okay if I fall asleep?
It's absolutely okay if you fall asleep! Many new mothers find themselves drifting off during a Sound Bath, and that's a sign your body is getting the deep rest it desperately needs. Whether you're awake or asleep, your body and mind will still benefit from the calming vibrations and supportive atmosphere, helping to rebuild your energy reserves.
I've never done anything like this before. Do I need any special preparation for a Sound Bath?
Not at all! You don't need any prior experience or special preparation. Just come as you are, dressed in comfortable clothes. We provide everything you need for a cozy experience, including mats and blankets. Our focus is on making this a gentle and accessible way for you to find peace and support your postpartum vitality.
Will a Sound Bath help with the 'baby brain' or feeling overwhelmed that I sometimes experience?
Many new mothers report feeling a sense of clarity and calm after a Sound Bath. The focused listening to the soothing sounds can help quiet a busy mind, offering a mental break from the constant demands of new motherhood. While it's not a cure, it can provide a valuable reset, helping you feel more centered and less overwhelmed.
