Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is a Sound Bath and how can it help my memory?
A Sound Bath involves reclining comfortably while gentle sounds from instruments like singing bowls wash over you. For seniors, these soothing vibrations help quiet the mind, which can improve focus and make it easier to recall information. It's about creating a calm mental space where your cognitive functions can operate more smoothly.
Do I need any special equipment or experience to participate in a Sound Bath?
Not at all! You simply need to arrive ready to relax. We provide comfortable mats and blankets. No prior experience with meditation or sound practices is needed. Our sessions are designed to be accessible and beneficial for everyone, especially those new to this gentle approach to mental well-being.
Is the Sound Bath suitable if I have hearing aids or specific health concerns?
Our Sound Bath sessions are very gentle, and the volume is always kept at a comfortable level. If you wear hearing aids, you might find it best to adjust them or remove them based on your comfort. Please do share any specific health concerns with before the session so we can ensure you have the most comfortable and beneficial experience possible.
How often should I attend a Sound Bath for better mental clarity?
Many seniors find that regular attendance, perhaps once or twice a month, helps maintain a consistent level of mental calm and clarity. However, even occasional sessions can offer noticeable benefits. Listen to your own needs and observe how your mind feels after each session to determine what rhythm works best for you.
