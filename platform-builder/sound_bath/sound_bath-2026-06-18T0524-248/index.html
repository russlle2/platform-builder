Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly happens during a Sound Bath session and how does it support my emotional health?
During a Sound Bath, you’ll relax comfortably, often lying down, as plays various instruments like singing bowls and gongs. The gentle, immersive sounds create vibrations that encourage deep relaxation, which can help calm your nervous system. This calm state supports emotional processing, helping you gently release built-up stress and tension, fostering a greater sense of emotional peace and well-being.
I'm concerned about my mental clarity; how can a Sound Bath specifically help with that in retirement?
Many of our clients find that the deep relaxation induced by a Sound Bath can significantly reduce mental chatter and improve focus. By quieting the mind, you create space for clearer thought and enhanced cognitive function. This practice helps to reduce 'brain fog' and promote a sense of mental sharpness, contributing to your overall vitality and engagement in daily activities.
Is a Sound Bath safe if I’ve experienced past emotional difficulties or trauma?
Absolutely. Our Sound Bath practice at is trauma-informed, meaning we prioritize creating a safe, gentle, and inclusive environment. You are always in control of your experience, and is trained to support you with sensitivity and respect, ensuring you feel comfortable and secure throughout the session. We focus on gentle emotional release and fostering resilience.
I'm new to this; what should I expect as a first-time participant in a Sound Bath?
As a first-time participant, you can expect a very gentle and welcoming experience. We'll provide comfortable mats and blankets. You simply lie back, close your eyes, and allow the sounds to wash over you. There's nothing you need to 'do' other than relax. Many people describe feeling deeply rested and refreshed afterwards, with a clearer mind and a lighter emotional state. We're here to answer any questions you have before or after your session.
