What exactly is a Sound Bath and how will it feel for me?

A Sound Bath is a gentle, immersive experience where you relax in a comfortable position while soft, resonant sounds are played. Most people describe feeling deeply relaxed, peaceful, and sometimes even a little sleepy. There's no physical activity involved, just pure relaxation for your mind and body.

Do I need to do anything special to prepare for a Sound Bath session?

No special preparation is needed. We simply recommend wearing comfortable clothing and perhaps bringing a pair of warm socks. You don't need any prior experience with meditation or sound practices. Just arrive ready to relax and be open to the experience.

Is a Sound Bath safe and suitable for seniors with health considerations?

Yes, Sound Baths are generally very gentle and safe. We prioritize your comfort and well-being. If you have specific health concerns, it's always wise to consult your doctor, but the experience is designed to be very calming and non-strenuous, making it suitable for most seniors.

How often should I consider attending a Sound Bath for the best results?

The frequency that works best for you can vary. Some individuals enjoy weekly sessions to maintain a consistent sense of calm, while others find monthly sessions perfectly suit their needs for stress reduction and nervous system regulation. We encourage you to listen to your body and see what feels right.
