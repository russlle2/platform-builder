Session flow

Arrival & intake — a brief check-in to set comfort and volume preferences.
Grounding — guided breath and gentle movement to prepare the body.
Sound journey — a 40–55 minute curated arc using layered instruments tuned to the session intention.
Return & integration — slow reorientation, shared notes, and practical suggestions to carry the experience home.

What to bring

  • Mat or soft pad
  • Blanket and a small pillow
  • Water bottle
  • Optional: eye covering, earplugs if you are sensitive

Contraindications & safety

Please notify us in advance if you are pregnant, have a seizure disorder, recent head/neck injury, or implanted medical devices. For certain conditions we offer modified placements, reduced volume, or alternative practices. If in doubt, consult your healthcare provider.
Not medical advice. Attendance is at your discretion; by booking you confirm awareness of the above. For private/VIP bookings, specific waivers may apply.

Instruments and approach

We compose each session with care. Typical instruments for private bookings might include: chimes, bowed monochord, Himalayan bowls, and low-register gongs — placed to modulate near-field and far-field perception.
