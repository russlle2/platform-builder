Contraindications & safety
Sound baths are gentle but powerful. If you are pregnant, have a pacemaker, severe tinnitus, epilepsy, or are currently under heavy psychiatric medication, consult your physician before attending and notify our facilitator upon booking. By booking you confirm awareness of these guidelines.
