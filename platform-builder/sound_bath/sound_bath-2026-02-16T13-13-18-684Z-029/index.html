Myths & Truths about sound baths

Myth: It’s just background music

Many imagine soft sounds in the background. Truth: sessions are architected with intentional harmonic progressions that scaffold attention and physiology.

Truth: It’s a sensory practice

Expect palpable vibrations, guided breath, and curated silence. Our approach is multisensory—lighting, touch-friendly mats, and slow movement cues sharpen presence.

Core pillars of our sound journeys

Intention

Each gathering begins with a short intention-setting and breath anchor to orient the nervous system.

Timbre & Texture

We weave instruments like crystal bowls, chimes, and a gentle monochord to create layered harmonics that invite deep listening.

Transition

Slow dynamic arcs guide movement between states—from arriving to release and a grounded close.

How a session unfolds

1 — Arrival & Grounding

Guests settle on mats. We offer short breathwork and light-oriented cues to arrive safely into the body.

2 — Sound Architecture

Layered instruments (gong, tuned bowls, Himalayan chimes, and subtle tuning forks) create evolving textures that support shifting depth.

3 — Integration & Close

We finish with a period of silence and soft voice guidance to anchor what emerged, followed by quiet integration time.

What to bring

  • a yoga mat or thick blanket
  • pillow or eye cushion
  • water bottle
  • light layers for temperature changes

Contraindications & safety

Sound work is generally safe, but please avoid participation or consult your physician if you have:

  • epilepsy or photosensitive conditions
  • recently implanted medical devices or pacemakers
  • pregnancy (consult first)
  • active severe psychiatric episodes

Common questions

How long is a typical group sound bath?

Most gatherings run 75–90 minutes total: 10 minutes arrival, 50–60 minutes soundscape, and 10–15 minutes integration.

Can I fall asleep?

Yes—and that’s fine. Falling asleep can be part of the nervous system resetting. We hold space for rest and return slowly before closing.

Do I need prior experience?

No. We welcome first-timers. Sessions include gentle guidance and options for quieter placement if vibrations feel intense.
