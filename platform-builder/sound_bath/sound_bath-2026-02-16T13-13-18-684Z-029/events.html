The evening flow

Arrival (15 min) — settle in, choose your blanket and bolster. We offer a short breathing cue to land in the body.
Sound immersion (45–55 min) — layered bowls and tuning forks weave slow textures. Volume and dynamics are intentionally gentle, intentionally expansive.
Integration (10–15 min) — we close with soft chimes, a few grounding tones, and time for tea and reflection.
Departure — facilitators will offer brief aftercare and optional one-on-one notes.

What to bring

Comfort is central: bring a yoga mat or blanket, a pillow or bolster, a light blanket for covering, and a water bottle. We provide mats/props on request for a premium experience.

Contraindications & safety

Sound baths are gentle but can be intense for people with seizure disorders, certain heart devices, or extreme sensitivity to sound. If you are pregnant, have a recent surgery, epilepsy, or use a pacemaker/ICD, consult your healthcare provider first. Please notify the facilitator upon arrival.

Quick FAQs

How early should I arrive? Arrive 15–20 minutes early to settle.

Is it meditation? It's a somatic sound experience — not guided meditation, though it often supports meditative states.
