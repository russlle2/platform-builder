Private sound sessions — tailored resonance

One-on-one attention or intimate shared sessions designed to address nervous system tone, relaxation, and focused restoration. Sessions are held in our quiet studio or can be offered at your chosen location for corporate or special events.

1:1 deep session

60–90 minutes. A personalized sequence to meet your current needs — restorative, restorative-with-tonework, or integrative coaching add-on.
Typical instruments: gong, monochord, subtle voice layers.

Couples & small groups

Shared sessions for partners or friends. Adjusted sequencing to hold relational field and safety.
Typical instruments: harp-like monochord, soft bowls, hand chimes.

Corporate & events

Larger room sessions or sound lounges for retreats and offsite experiences. We build a sensory brief to match tone and timing.
Typical instruments: standing gongs, bowed bowls, warm drone elements.

Session flow

A private session usually follows: arrival & intention (5–10 min), short intake, tuned soundwork (40–70 min) using specific instruments to meet your nervous system, closing tones and practical integration, and a brief debrief.

What to bring & accessibility

Bring a blanket, pillow, water, and anything that makes you comfortable. If you have mobility needs or require floor to chair adjustments, tell us when booking and we will prepare the space.

Contraindications

Private sessions can be more intense. Please disclose pregnancies, seizures, recent surgeries, or implanted devices (pacemakers/ICDs). We may adapt or decline certain modalities for safety.
Why these instruments?

We select tools to sculpt vibration: the gong for broad-spectrum waves, monochord for long, harmonic sustains, and gentle vocal harmonics to anchor presence.

Customization

Each session is curated: louder or softer, short-form or long-form according to your current nervous system cues.
