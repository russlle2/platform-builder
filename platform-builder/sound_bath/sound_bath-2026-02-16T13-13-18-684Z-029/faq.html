Questions we commonly get

What should I bring?
Bring a yoga mat or soft padding, a blanket, a small pillow for head support, water, and any personal eye covering (sleep mask). Layered clothing is best — rooms can cool during the latter part of the session.
Are there contraindications?
Yes. Please notify us if you are pregnant, have a history of seizures, severe cardiovascular conditions, or recent head/neck injuries. If you have implanted medical devices (e.g., pacemaker), consult your healthcare provider before attending. We maintain low-to-moderate volumes and provide alternatives when necessary.
How long is a typical session and what is the flow?
Most group sessions run 75–90 minutes. Typical flow: arrival & intake (10–15 min), guided grounding (10 min), the sound journey with layered instruments (40–55 min), gentle emergence and journaling (10 min). We close with optional sharing.
Can I fall asleep? Will that affect the benefit?
Falling asleep is common and part of the restorative process. Even if you drift in and out, the body benefits from the regulated sound and pacing.
What instruments do you use?
We rotate our palette intentionally: crystal singing bowls, silk gongs, koshi chimes, monochord, and selected tuning forks for close-field work. Each session uses a combination tailored to the intention.
How do private bookings differ?
Private options include 1:1 sessions, couples, small groups, and corporate offerings. VIP day engagements involve bespoke sonic design, space consultation, and an extended session length with follow-up guidance.
What is your cancellation policy?
Please review the policy on the booking page or contact us. For private and VIP bookings, a deposit is typically required and nonrefundable within a specific window.
