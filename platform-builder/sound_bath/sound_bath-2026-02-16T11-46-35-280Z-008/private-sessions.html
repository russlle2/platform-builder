Offerings

  • 1:1 Therapeutic Session — Deeply personalized sound journey focused on relaxation, sleep reset, or stress regulation. Typical 60–90 minutes.
  • Couples Session — Shared sound field for connection and co-regulation; gentle guidance for shared breathing and listening.
  • Corporate & Team — Onsite or in-studio sessions focused on workplace rest, alignment, and creative reset (half-day and full-day options).

Instruments & textures

We choose instruments to meet session goals; examples include:

  • Monochord for sustained harmonic foundation
  • Crystal singing bowls for bright overtone clarity
  • Tuning forks for somatic tuning and point work
  • Handpan and soft percussion for rhythmic touch

Session flow (typical)

  1. Intake & intention setting (10–20 min)
  2. Gentle preparation — breath & posture (5–10 min)
  3. Main sound immersion (40–70 min) — dynamic layers tailored to your body
  4. Slow return, journaling, and practical aftercare (10–15 min)

What to bring

  • Comfortable clothing
  • Personal blanket or pillow (we provide extras)
  • Medical notes if you have specific concerns

Contraindications: please notify us if you are pregnant, have active seizures, implantable devices, or acute injuries. Sessions can be adapted but safety is primary.
