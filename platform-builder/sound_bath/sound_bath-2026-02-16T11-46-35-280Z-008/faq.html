Frequently asked — sound bath logistics & safety

Concise, practical guidance about what happens during a session, how to prepare, and medical considerations. If you need personalized guidance, reach out before booking.

What should I bring?
Comfortable clothes, a yoga mat or blanket if you prefer your own, an eye mask, and a water bottle. For extended private sessions bring a small journal. We provide bolsters and blankets but space is limited for personal props.
Are there contraindications?
Yes — please do not attend without consulting us if you have: a pacemaker or implanted electronic device, uncontrolled epilepsy, recent surgery, pregnancy in the first trimester without physician sign-off, or any condition where loud vibrational exposure is a risk. We ask all attendees to fill a brief intake form prior to arrival.
What instruments do you use?
Our palette varies by event; common instruments include tuned gongs, precision tuning forks, and bronze/metal chimes, combined with breath-led silence. Each session is engineered for clarity and safety — instruments are positioned to avoid direct contact with implanted devices.
What is the typical flow of a session?
We begin with a short welcome and grounding (10 minutes), an intake/adjustment period, a 40–55 minute immersion using layered live instruments, and a slow return with tea and integration pointers. For VIP/private days the immersion is longer and includes a pre-session consultation.
Can I fall asleep?
Yes. Falling into a restorative sleep is common and part of the restorative benefit. You’ll be guided to reorient gently after the sound concludes; allow time to transition before driving.
Are recordings available?
We do not typically record live baths due to acoustics and privacy, but we offer curated audio for post-session integration for VIP clients and repeat attendees.
What about accessibility?
Our venue is accessible; please let us know any mobility needs when booking so we can reserve an optimal spot. Chairs are available for those who cannot recline on the floor.
