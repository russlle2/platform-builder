What to expect — an evening flow

We design each gathering like a curated sound tapestry. The flow is simple and intentional:

  • Arrival & grounding: gentle breathwork and settling (10–15 min)
  • Guided sound journey: layered instruments move from subtle to immersive (45–60 min)
  • Reintegration: soft chimes, silence, tea & integration time (10–20 min)

Instruments you may hear

A shifting, tactile palette — tuned to body and room acoustics:

  • Crystal singing bowls (harmonic overtones)
  • Planetary gong (deep resonance)
  • Wind chimes & gentle percussion
  • Tuning forks for focused tones

Sensory guidance

Expect gentle invitations to surrender: we prioritize low lighting, breathable ventilation, and soft textures. This is curated for sensory safety and premium comfort.
