How does a Sound Bath specifically assist with postural health during chronic recovery?

A Sound Bath helps by inducing a deep state of relaxation, which allows your muscles and fascia to soften and release chronic tension. This release can gently encourage your body to return to a more natural, balanced alignment. The subtle vibrations also heighten your internal awareness, helping you to consciously recognize and adjust your posture more effectively as you recover.

What should I expect to feel during a Sound Bath regarding my body awareness?

During a Sound Bath, you might experience a gentle tingling or warmth as the sound vibrations move through your body. Many people report a heightened sense of their physical boundaries and inner structure. You may become more aware of areas holding tension and then notice them subtly softening, leading to a feeling of greater spaciousness and fluidity in your posture.

Is the Sound Bath suitable if I have specific physical limitations from my chronic illness or injury?

Yes, our Sound Bath sessions are designed to be deeply supportive and non-strenuous. You simply lie down in a comfortable position that works for your body. We encourage you to communicate any specific limitations before your session so we can ensure you are fully supported and comfortable throughout, allowing the sound to work its gentle magic without any physical exertion from your part.

How often should I engage in Sound Bath sessions to support my postural recovery?

The frequency that best supports your postural recovery is unique to you. Many individuals find that consistent, regular Sound Bath sessions — perhaps weekly or bi-weekly — provide the most sustained benefits for maintaining body awareness and supporting gentle postural realignment. We can discuss a personalized approach based on your specific needs and recovery goals.
