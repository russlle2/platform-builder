How a session is structured

  1. Short arrival ritual & intention setting (6–8 min)
  2. Layered sound landscape with breath guidance (35–45 min)
  3. Transition & grounding with take-home practice (8–10 min)

Who benefits most

  • People seeking better sleep or a reset
  • Those recovering from burnout or high stress
  • Anyone curious about gentle body-focused practices

Meet the facilitators

We blend clinical breathing techniques and soundcraft. Sessions are led by practitioners trained in somatic methods and percussion-based sound work.

Contraindications & safety

If you have uncontrolled seizures, some implanted medical devices, acute psychiatric conditions, are pregnant without clearance from a clinician, or have been told to avoid vibrational therapies, please consult your healthcare provider before attending.
