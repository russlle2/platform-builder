Questions people often ask

Practical answers about what to expect, safety, and how to make a session work for your day.

Will I have to speak during the session?+
No. Most of the session is received silently. We begin with an optional brief check-in and end with a short grounding. If you prefer full silence, indicate when you arrive and the facilitator will honor that.
Can I fall asleep during the sound bath?+
Yes — falling into rest is common and welcomed. We advise not to drive immediately if you feel very drowsy; ask staff about post-session options if you need time to reorient.
Are there cases where I should not attend?+
If you have uncontrolled epilepsy, certain implanted medical devices, acute psychiatric symptoms, or are pregnant without clinical clearance, check with a clinician first. For individualized guidance, contact us at {{EMAIL}}.
What should I bring?+
Comfortable layers, any props for body support (blanket, pillow), and an openness to rest. We provide mats and basic bolsters; bring your own if you prefer.
How often should I come?+
There’s no single prescription. Many people begin with a weekly or biweekly rhythm for a month and then adjust. We offer short series for consistent practice and single sessions for resets.
