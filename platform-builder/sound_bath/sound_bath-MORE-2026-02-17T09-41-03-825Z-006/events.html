What to expect

Arrive seated or lying. We move through gentle breath cues, curated sound textures, and moments of silence. Sessions last ~75 minutes; you leave with a short practice to anchor the shift.

  • Soft entry: low-volume bowls and calming voice cues
  • Main soundscape: layered singing bowls, chimes, and subtle drone
  • Return: grounding guidance and a brief space to journal or share
Contraindications: If you are pregnant, have a pacemaker or other implanted medical devices, have uncontrolled seizures, serious psychiatric conditions, or are under substances that impair responsiveness, consult your clinician before attending. If in doubt, reach out at {{EMAIL}}.

Common hesitations

Will it be loud?

No — volume is intentionally gentle. The emphasis is on vibration and resonance, not high decibel noise.

Do I have to share?

Nope. Sharing is optional; many people attend quietly and leave afterwards without discussion.
