Questions people often have

Will I fall asleep?

Some people do — we see that as a sign the nervous system is down-regulating. You’ll still leave with improved clarity.

Is this safe for those with epilepsy or pregnancy?

Certain sound experiences can be contraindicated. Please consult your clinician and contact us before booking. We screen for conditions such as epilepsy, pregnancy, and recent head injury.

Do I need prior experience?

No. Sessions are guided and designed to be accessible for first-timers and regulars alike.

Contraindications: If you are pregnant, have a pacemaker, are on seizure-prone medication, or have an acute psychiatric crisis, consult your healthcare provider before attending. Contact us at {{EMAIL}} with any concerns.
