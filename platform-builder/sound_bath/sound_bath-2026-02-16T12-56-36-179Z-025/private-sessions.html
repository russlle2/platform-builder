What to bring
  • Comfortable clothing and extra blanket
  • List of medications or devices to note
  • Open intention; water to hydrate after the session
Contraindications

If you have epilepsy, certain cardiac devices, recent surgeries, or are actively psychotic, contact your care provider before booking. We will adapt or recommend alternatives where needed.

Client flow & preparation

Arrive 10–15 minutes early for check-in. Wear layers. Sessions are trauma-informed; you may remain seated if reclined posture feels unsafe. Facilitator cues slow breathing and provides options for sensory regulation throughout.
