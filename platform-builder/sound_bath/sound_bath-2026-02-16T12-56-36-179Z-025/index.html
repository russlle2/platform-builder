The flow — how a session unfolds

We steward each moment with intention. Typical flow for group events and private VIP days:

  1. Arrival & grounding (10–15 min)Welcome, settling breathwork, orientation to the instruments.
  2. Opening tones (10 min)Warm-up with chimes and tuning forks to attune the room.
  3. Immersive soundscape (35–50 min)Layered bowls, gong, and voice create an enveloping field; slow transitions allow depth.
  4. Integration (10–20 min)Soft re-entry with monochord waves and gentle silence, followed by tea and optional sharing.

What to bring

  • Yoga mat or thick blanket, bolster or pillow.
  • Warm socks, eye mask, water bottle.
  • Optional: journal for after-session notes.

Contraindications: Not recommended during pregnancy without clearance, for those with recent head/inner-ear surgery, or uncontrolled epilepsy. If you use a pacemaker or have other electronic implants, check with your physician before booking.

Questions we often hear

How loud is a sound bath?
It varies: most of the experience is gentle vibrations and sustained tones. The gong can be dynamic but is never uncomfortably loud; we tailor intensity to the group.
Is there a dress code?
Comfort-first: layered clothing that keeps you warm when lying still.
Can I bring a friend?
Yes — check events for group bookings or choose a private session for tailored experiences (1:1, couples, corporate).
What if I have medical concerns?
Please read contraindications above; contact us for a brief screening if in doubt.
