Questions & Practical Notes

Clear, concise answers about attending and safety — practical guidance for your visit.

How long is a typical session?
Most group sound baths last 75–90 minutes including arrival and integration. Private VIP sessions are longer and tailored to the client.
What instruments do you use?
Our palette varies by event to shape the experience. Typical selections include steel tongue drum, large chimes, ocean drum, and subtle voice work. We intentionally layer tones rather than creating loud crescendos.
What should I bring?
Bring a mat or thick blanket, warm layers, and an eye cover if you prefer. A bottle of water is recommended for after the session. If you need props, let us know when booking.
Can I attend if I'm pregnant or have a medical condition?
If you are pregnant, have epilepsy, recent head trauma, or rely on a pacemaker, consult your healthcare provider first and notify the facilitator. We may modify instruments or advise alternate experiences. Safety and consent are prioritized.
What is the flow of a typical group session?
We begin with gentle breathing and orientation, move into 40–60 minutes of curated sound with intentional pauses for integration, and end with a guided emergence and time to journal or sip tea. The facilitator cues transitions and adapts volume and instrumentation in real time.
Are masks or vaccinations required?
Local health guidance is followed. If you have acute respiratory symptoms, please reschedule. For private bookings, we accommodate customer preferences regarding mask use.

Quick logistics

Arrival: 15 minutes before start for intake. Doors close a few minutes after beginning to preserve the field. Late arrivals may be guided to a quiet re-entry or offered credit for a future session.

Private & VIP options

1:1 deep sessions, couples journeys, and curated corporate pauses are available. Private bookings often include additional intake and post-session integration time.

Additional safety notes

If you wear hearing aids or other assistive devices, consult with us before arrival so we can configure the room and minimize discomfort. Intense vibrational experiences can be disorienting for some; our facilitator monitors the group continuously and will lower intensity when needed.
