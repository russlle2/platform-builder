How we work — an overview

Our sessions are intentional, multi-sensory gatherings combining sound, breath, and light touch guidance. We design each meeting with a premium, slow-paced aesthetic — soft lighting, plush blankets, aromatic notes, and curated instruments.

What to bring

  • Comfortable clothing, layers for temperature shifts
  • Your own mat or yoga blanket (we provide extras on request)
  • Eye pillow or small scarf for gentle darkness
  • Reusable water bottle

Contraindications & safety

Sound therapy is gentle, but consult us if you have:

  • Recent or unstable cardiac conditions
  • Epilepsy or photosensitive conditions
  • Severe psychiatric conditions without therapist approval
  • Pregnancy — reach out to discuss modifications
This page is informational and not medical advice. Please contact your healthcare provider for personalized guidance.

Typical session flow

  1. Arrival & settling: herbal tea, short intake (10–15 min).
  2. Intention setting & gentle breathwork (5–10 min).
  3. Sound journey (30–60 min) using a tailored mix of instruments.
  4. Slow re-entry with silence, grounding prompts, and reflection (10–15 min).
  5. Post-session notes & booking follow-up.

Instruments used here vary by offering — crystal bowls, chimes, gong swells, tuning forks, and a tuned monochord are commonly woven into private and group experiences.
