Contraindications & safety

Sound baths are generally safe, but not suitable for everyone. If you have epilepsy, are pregnant, have recent head trauma, are prone to seizures, or have a pacemaker, consult a medical professional before attending. If you experience disorientation or distress during a session, notify the facilitator immediately. For private sessions, we adapt intensity and instrument choice.
