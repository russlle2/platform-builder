The instruments we bring
  • Crystal singing bowls for sustained harmonic fields

What to bring
  • Yoga mat, blanket, and a small pillow for support
  • Eye-covering (sleep mask or scarf) for softer sensory focus
  • Water bottle; stay hydrated before and after
Contraindications & safety

If you are pregnant, have epilepsy, severe cardiovascular conditions, or are sensitive to intense vibration, please consult your physician before attending. If you have implanted devices (pacemakers, cochlear implants), contact us prior to booking.

How a session flows
  1. Welcome + brief intention-setting circle (10 minutes)
  2. Guided recline and breath coaching (5–10 minutes)
  3. Sound immersion with layered instruments (40–60 minutes)
  4. Gradual tonal re-entry + sharing time (10–15 minutes)
