Is a Sound Bath suitable if I'm recovering from a specific injury or chronic illness?

Yes, our Sound Bath sessions are designed to be gentle and accessible. The focus is on creating a supportive environment where you can rest comfortably. The gentle sounds and vibrations can help ease physical tension and discomfort, making it a soothing experience as your body recovers. We encourage you to listen to your body and make any adjustments needed for your comfort during the session.

What should I expect during my first Sound Bath session for physical relief?

You can expect to lie down or sit comfortably. Dr. Morgan Ellis will use various instruments to create soft, resonant sounds. There's no expectation for you to do anything other than relax. Many people report feeling a sense of calm and gentle physical release. The experience is designed to be non-intrusive and supportive for your body, especially during recovery.

Will a Sound Bath help with my chronic pain or physical limitations?

While a Sound Bath is not a medical treatment, many individuals find the deep relaxation it offers can help reduce the perception of chronic pain and ease physical limitations by calming the nervous system and promoting muscle relaxation. It's a complementary approach that supports your overall well-being as you manage your physical health. We aim to create a space where your body can find some ease.

Do I need any prior experience with sound or relaxation practices to attend a Sound Bath?

Absolutely not. Our Sound Bath sessions are designed for everyone, especially those who might be new to wellness practices or are focusing on physical recovery. There's nothing you need to 'do' or 'understand.' Simply come as you are, and allow the sounds to support your comfort and relaxation. Dr. Morgan Ellis will guide you through the process, making it approachable and gentle.
