Contraindications & safety

Sound immersion is supportive for many, but please consult a healthcare provider before attending if any of the following apply to you:

  • Pregnancy beyond 12 weeks or with complications — check with your provider
  • Severe cardiac conditions or pacemaker implants
  • Active seizures or epilepsy without medical clearance
  • Any condition where loud vibrations might interfere with a medical device

If in doubt, include a note in your message and we'll advise on suitable options or accommodations.
