Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is a Sound Bath, and how will it help my body?
A Sound Bath involves lying down and being 'bathed' in the gentle sounds and vibrations from instruments like singing bowls. These sounds encourage your nervous system to relax, which can help release muscle tension, reduce chronic aches, and ease the physical discomfort often felt during stressful life changes. It's a passive experience, meaning your body does the work of releasing without effort from you.
I'm new to this. Do I need any special experience for a Sound Bath?
Absolutely not! Our Sound Bath sessions are designed for everyone, especially those new to somatic practices or relaxation techniques. There's no right or wrong way to experience it. Just come as you are, lie down comfortably, and let the sounds guide your body towards a state of ease. We'll make sure you feel completely comfortable and supported.
How can a Sound Bath specifically support me through a major life transition?
Major life transitions often bring physical stress, anxiety, and a feeling of being unmoored. A Sound Bath helps ground your body by reducing the physical symptoms of stress. It offers a dedicated space for deep rest, allowing your body to release the emotional and physical burdens of change. This can help you feel more stable and less overwhelmed as you navigate your new path.
What should I wear or bring to my Sound Bath session?
We recommend wearing comfortable, loose-fitting clothing that allows you to relax fully. You don't need to bring anything special, as we provide blankets and props to ensure your comfort. Just bring yourself, ready to rest. You might even find yourself drifting into a light sleep, which is perfectly normal and a sign your body is deeply relaxing.
