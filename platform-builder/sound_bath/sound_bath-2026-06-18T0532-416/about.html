How does a Sound Bath specifically help with mental clarity during chronic illness recovery?

Our Sound Bath sessions utilize specific auditory frequencies to encourage brainwave states conducive to mental focus and calm. For individuals recovering from chronic illness or injury, this can help reduce the mental fog often associated with these conditions, supporting improved concentration and cognitive function.

What should I expect during a Sound Bath session at Sound Bath Studio?

You will relax comfortably while Dr. Morgan Ellis plays various instruments like singing bowls and gongs. The sounds create a gentle, immersive environment designed to support your nervous system and encourage mental stillness, aiding in cognitive restoration without requiring active effort on your part.

Is the Sound Bath suitable if I have sensitivities due to my chronic condition?

We prioritize your comfort and well-being. Please inform us of any sensitivities or concerns you may have before your session. Our Sound Bath sessions are designed to be gentle, and we can adjust the volume and intensity to ensure a supportive experience for your specific needs during recovery.

How often should I attend Sound Bath sessions to see cognitive benefits?

The frequency of Sound Bath sessions can vary based on your individual recovery goals and current cognitive state. Many clients find regular, consistent sessions beneficial for maintaining mental clarity and supporting sustained cognitive wellness. We can discuss a personalized plan that best suits your needs.
