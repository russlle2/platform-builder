Questions & clarifications

Short answers for common concerns about attending cohorts and sessions.

What should I bring?
A mat or blanket, a small pillow for your head, and a water bottle. We provide extra blankets on request.
How loud is a session?
Volumes are intentionally balanced to support a relaxing field. You choose a preferred intensity (gentle / medium / intense) when booking; facilitators will tune levels in the room accordingly.
Are cohorts for beginners?
Yes. Cohorts are structured to welcome beginners while offering deeper practice for returning participants.
Contraindications and safety
Sound can be powerful. If you have epilepsy, certain heart conditions, are pregnant, have photosensitivity, or recent head/neck injuries, consult your healthcare provider before attending. Also notify facilitators if you are on medication that makes you drowsy or if you use medical devices. We offer reduced-intensity options and can advise which cohorts are appropriate.
Can I arrive late or leave early?
We recommend arriving 10–15 minutes early to settle. Late arrivals can be accommodated quietly; if you must leave early, please inform a facilitator beforehand.
Are there refund or transfer policies?
Because cohorts are small and capacity-controlled, transfers are permitted up to 72 hours before the first meeting. Refunds follow the cohort policy listed on the booking page.
