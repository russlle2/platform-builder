Important Guidance & Contraindications

Please read these notes before booking. Sound sessions are supportive but not a substitute for medical care.

  • If you are pregnant, have epilepsy, a recent head injury, or active psychiatric crisis, consult a clinician before attending.
  • People with recent surgery, a pacemaker, or certain medical implants should seek medical advice first.
  • Inform the facilitators of any conditions or concerns before the session begins; adjustments can often be made.
