Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Sound Bath specifically help my postpartum posture?
During postpartum, your core and pelvic floor undergo significant changes. A Sound Bath encourages deep relaxation, which can help release compensatory tension in your shoulders, neck, and lower back. This softening allows your body to gently realign, improving your overall postural awareness and comfort as you carry, feed, and interact with your baby.
Is a Sound Bath safe for new mothers, especially if I'm still recovering?
Yes, Sound Bath is a very gentle and passive practice, making it ideal for postpartum recovery. You'll simply lie comfortably, allowing the sounds to wash over you. We recommend consulting with your healthcare provider before starting any new wellness practice, but generally, it's a safe way to support your physical and emotional well-being during this sensitive time.
What should I expect during a Sound Bath session focusing on body awareness?
You'll be invited to lie down in a comfortable position, often on your back with support for your knees or head, whatever feels best for your body. will then play various instruments, creating a rich soundscape. Your role is simply to listen and allow the sounds to guide your attention to different parts of your body, noticing sensations without judgment, and allowing for deep relaxation and subtle postural release.
How often should I attend a Sound Bath to support my postpartum body?
The frequency that works best for you will depend on your individual needs and how you feel. Many new mothers find that attending weekly or bi-weekly sessions provides consistent support for their physical comfort and mental calm. Even occasional sessions can offer significant benefits in re-centering your body and mind during this busy period.
