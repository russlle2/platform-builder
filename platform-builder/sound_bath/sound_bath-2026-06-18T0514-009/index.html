Frequently Asked Questions

I'm so busy, how will I find time for a Sound Bath?
We understand that finding time for yourself is a challenge. Our Sound Bath sessions are intentionally designed to be an efficient and powerful way to reset your nervous system. Even an hour can make a significant difference in how you handle the rest of your week. Consider it a vital investment in your capacity to parent from a place of calm, rather than constant stress.
Will a Sound Bath really help with my daily parenting stress?
Absolutely. The consistent demands of parenting can keep your nervous system in a heightened state. A Sound Bath specifically works to downshift this response, helping to reduce cortisol levels and encourage a more relaxed physiological state. This means you may find yourself reacting to minor upsets with more patience and feeling less overwhelmed by the daily juggle.
I've never done a Sound Bath before. What if I can't relax?
It's completely normal to feel unsure! Many first-timers worry about relaxing, but the beauty of a Sound Bath is that the sounds do most of the work. You don't need to try to relax; simply allow the vibrations to envelop you. Our comfortable setting and gentle guidance from create a welcoming space where even the most stressed parent can find moments of peace. Just come as you are, and let the sounds support you.
What should I bring or wear to a Sound Bath session?
To ensure your comfort during the Sound Bath, we recommend wearing loose, comfortable clothing. You'll be lying down for the session, so think cozy! We provide mats, blankets, and eye masks, but if you have a favorite blanket or pillow that brings you extra comfort, you are welcome to bring it along. The most important thing is to feel completely at ease.
