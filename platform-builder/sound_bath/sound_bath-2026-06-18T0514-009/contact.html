How Sound Bath Helps Your Nervous System Unwind

As a parent, your nervous system is often in overdrive. Our Sound Bath sessions provide a focused opportunity to shift from that 'on-guard' state to one of deep relaxation. The specific frequencies and vibrations encourage your brainwaves to slow down, mimicking the state you experience just before sleep. This gentle re-calibration helps reduce the physiological symptoms of stress, leaving you feeling less frazzled and more grounded.
