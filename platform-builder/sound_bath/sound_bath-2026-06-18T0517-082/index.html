Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is a Sound Bath and how will it help me postpartum?
A Sound Bath is a deeply relaxing experience where you rest comfortably while gentle, resonant sounds are played around you. For new mothers, it's a wonderful way to reduce stress, calm an overstimulated nervous system, and find moments of quiet that are often hard to come by during postpartum recovery.
Do I need any special experience or preparation to attend a Sound Bath?
Absolutely not! Our Sound Bath sessions are designed to be completely accessible. You just need to arrive, find a comfortable spot, and allow yourself to relax. There’s no prior wellness experience needed, making it perfect for your postpartum journey.
Is a Sound Bath safe if I’m still recovering from childbirth?
Yes, Sound Baths are generally very gentle and safe. You'll be lying down comfortably, and the sounds themselves are soothing, not jarring. If you have any specific health concerns, it's always good to check with your doctor first, but for most new mothers, it’s a restorative experience.
How long are the Sound Bath sessions, and what should I bring?
Our sessions typically last around 60 minutes. We recommend wearing comfortable clothing. You might also want to bring a water bottle, and if you prefer extra comfort, a small pillow or blanket. We provide mats, but personal items can enhance your relaxation.
