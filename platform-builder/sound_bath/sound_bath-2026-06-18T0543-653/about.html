Gentle Sound Bath: A Sanctuary for Your Immunity

How does a Sound Bath specifically aid immune health for seniors?

Our Sound Bath sessions induce a profound state of relaxation, which helps reduce stress hormones. Chronic stress can suppress immune function. By promoting deep calm, our practice supports a balanced nervous system, thereby creating an optimal environment for your immune system to thrive and operate more efficiently.

What should I expect during my first Sound Bath session?

You will be invited to recline comfortably on a cushioned mat, often with a blanket for warmth. Dr. Morgan Ellis will then play a variety of instruments, creating a tapestry of gentle sounds and vibrations that wash over you. There is no participation required on your part, simply an invitation to relax and allow the sounds to envelop you. Many describe it as a deeply peaceful and restorative experience.

Is a Sound Bath suitable if I have hearing aids or specific health conditions?

Absolutely. Our Sound Bath is designed to be gentle and accessible. We can adjust the intensity and proximity of the instruments to ensure your comfort, especially if you wear hearing aids. If you have any specific health concerns, we encourage you to discuss them with Dr. Morgan Ellis beforehand so we can tailor your experience to your needs and ensure your utmost safety and enjoyment.

How often should I attend a Sound Bath to see benefits in vitality?

The frequency for optimal vitality enhancement can vary for each individual. Many of our clients find that regular, consistent sessions—perhaps once or twice a month—provide the most sustained benefits for maintaining energy levels and a sense of well-being. We can discuss a personalized schedule that aligns with your lifestyle and vitality goals during your initial consultation.
