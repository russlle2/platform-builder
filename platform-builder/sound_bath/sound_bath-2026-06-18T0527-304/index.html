Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Sound Bath specifically help with my athletic recovery?
A Sound Bath helps by creating vibrations that can penetrate deep into your tissues, encouraging muscle relaxation and reducing inflammation. This gentle stimulation supports your body’s natural ability to repair and reduces the physical stress accumulated from training, helping you recover more effectively.
Will a Sound Bath replace my current physical therapy or recovery routine?
No, a Sound Bath is designed to complement your existing recovery protocols, not replace them. It's a powerful tool for deep relaxation and somatic release that can enhance the benefits of physical therapy, massage, and other recovery methods, helping your body respond better to treatment.
What should I expect to feel physically during a Sound Bath session?
During a Sound Bath, you might feel subtle vibrations throughout your body, a deep sense of calm, and a release of tension in specific muscle groups. Many people report feeling lighter, more grounded, and experience a reduction in aches or stiffness afterward. It's common to feel very relaxed, sometimes even falling into a light sleep.
Is a Sound Bath suitable if I have specific injuries or chronic pain?
Yes, a Sound Bath can be very beneficial for managing discomfort associated with injuries or chronic pain, as it promotes deep relaxation and can help reduce the body's stress response. However, if you have specific concerns, it’s always best to consult with your healthcare provider first and let us know about any conditions before your session.
