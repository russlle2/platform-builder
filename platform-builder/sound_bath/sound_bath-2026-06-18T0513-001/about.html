I'm always busy, how can a Sound Bath help my burnout specifically?

When you're experiencing burnout, your sympathetic nervous system (fight-or-flight) is overactive. A Sound Bath helps to activate your parasympathetic nervous system (rest-and-digest), promoting deep relaxation and giving your body a chance to recover from chronic stress. It's a dedicated pause for your overwhelmed system.

Will I have to do anything or learn a technique during the Sound Bath?

Not at all. Your only requirement is to find a comfortable position, typically lying down, and allow the sounds to wash over you. There's no technique to master, no specific way you 'should' feel. The sounds do the work of gently guiding your nervous system towards a more regulated state.

I find it hard to switch off. Will a Sound Bath actually work for me?

Many working professionals who struggle with quieting their minds find Sound Baths incredibly effective. The immersive nature of the soundscape provides a focal point that can gently pull your attention away from racing thoughts, making it easier to enter a state of deep relaxation than traditional meditation might.

How often should I attend a Sound Bath to see benefits for stress reduction?

While even a single session can offer immediate relief, consistent engagement is key for lasting nervous system regulation and significant stress reduction. Many clients find that attending bi-weekly or monthly helps them maintain a more balanced state and prevent burnout from creeping back in.
