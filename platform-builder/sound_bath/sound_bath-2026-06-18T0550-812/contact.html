Sound Bath: Supporting Your Postural Recovery

Postpartum changes can shift your body's alignment. Our Sound Bath sessions are crafted to help you gently reconnect with your physical self, encouraging a sense of grounding and stability. The carefully chosen sound frequencies promote deep relaxation, allowing your muscles to release tension that often contributes to discomfort and poor posture after childbirth. This mindful approach helps you cultivate a subtle awareness of your body's position and needs.
