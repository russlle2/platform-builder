How can a Sound Bath help with my postpartum body pain?

Our Sound Bath sessions use specific sound frequencies to encourage deep relaxation in your muscles and nervous system. This deep relaxation helps to release stored tension in areas like your back, hips, and shoulders, which are often stressed during postpartum recovery, leading to a noticeable reduction in physical aches and discomfort for you.

Is a Sound Bath safe for new mothers, especially if I'm still recovering from childbirth?

Yes, our Sound Bath is a gentle and non-invasive practice. You will be comfortably reclined or seated, and the sounds are carefully modulated to be soothing and supportive. We always recommend consulting your healthcare provider about any new practices during your postpartum period, but typically, it's a very safe and beneficial experience for your recovery.

What should I expect to feel during and after a Sound Bath session?

During a Sound Bath, you might experience deep relaxation, a sense of calm, and a gentle humming or tingling sensation as the vibrations move through your body. Afterward, many new mothers report feeling lighter, more rested, and experiencing reduced physical tension and discomfort. Your body often feels more aligned and at ease.

How often should I consider a Sound Bath for postpartum physical relief?

The frequency that works best for you can vary. Some new mothers find significant relief and benefit from weekly sessions, especially in the early postpartum period, while others prefer bi-weekly or monthly to maintain their physical and emotional balance. We can discuss a personalized plan that supports your specific needs and recovery journey.
