Who we are and how we shape time

We design short ceremonial sound experiences that fold attention inward and invite new perspectives. Our gatherings combine tuned instruments, guided intention, and soft transitions so your nervous system has room to change.

Duration & Flow
Sessions range 45–90 minutes. We build a slow arc: grounding, layered tones, gentle closure.
Tools
We use singing bowls, gongs, chimes and voice; no bright lights, minimal talking.
Group Size
Comfortable cohort sizes to preserve spaciousness—see Booking for live availability.
Intent
We invite curiosity, not therapy. Participants set private intentions; facilitators hold field and safety.

Health & safety
Contraindications: If you have epilepsy, severe cardiovascular conditions, pregnancy complications, or are sensitive to prolonged vibration, consult your healthcare provider before attending. Not a substitute for medical or mental health care.

A note on language

We speak in terms of practice and containment rather than cure. Our role is to accompany attention: to hold a sonic field where you can notice what needed noticing.
