Questions people actually ask

Short answers, clear boundaries. If you need personal guidance, contact our team.

What should I expect the first time?

A calm entry with a brief orientation, a guided settling, then layered sound. You can rest, breathe, or lightly open your eyes. You won't be asked to perform.

Is it therapeutic?

Our program supports regulation and presence but is not psychotherapy. If you are in active mental health treatment, consult with your provider about complementary practices.

Are recordings used?

We sometimes record for training; informed consent is requested for any captures. No cameras during sessions unless explicitly permitted.

Accessibility

Our space is single-floor, with comfortable cushions and chairs. Let us know any needs when booking so we can prepare seating and entry.

What about contraindications?

Contraindications: seizure disorders, uncontrolled blood pressure issues, pregnancy complications, or extreme sensitivity to vibration. If any apply, consult medical care before attending.

Still unsure?

Try a short practice to sense whether the approach fits you.

Health note
Attending is not a medical intervention. If you have any serious health concerns, please consult your clinician prior to booking.
