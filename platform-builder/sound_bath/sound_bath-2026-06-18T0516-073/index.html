Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is a Sound Bath and how will it help my stress?
A Sound Bath is a deeply relaxing experience where you lie down comfortably while gentle, resonant sounds are played around you. These sounds create vibrations that help to quiet your mind and soothe your nervous system, naturally reducing stress and promoting a sense of calm within you. It's a gentle way to find your inner peace.
Do I need to do anything special during a Sound Bath session?
Not at all! Your only 'job' is to get comfortable, whether that's lying down or sitting, and simply listen. You don't need to meditate or have any prior experience. Just allow yourself to relax and let the sounds wash over you. We provide everything you need for comfort, so you can simply be present.
Is a Sound Bath suitable for seniors with mobility concerns?
Absolutely. Our Sound Bath sessions are designed to be accessible and gentle. You can choose to lie on a yoga mat with cushions and blankets, or sit comfortably in a chair if that's preferred. Your comfort is our priority, and we ensure the environment supports your ease and relaxation throughout the session.
How often should I attend a Sound Bath to feel the benefits?
The frequency that works best for you can vary. Many individuals find that attending regularly, perhaps once a month or every few weeks, helps to maintain a consistent sense of calm and nervous system balance. Even a single session can offer significant relaxation, but consistent participation can deepen your experience and benefits.
