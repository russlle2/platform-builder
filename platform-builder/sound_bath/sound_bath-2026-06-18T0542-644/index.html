Frequently Asked Questions

How does a Sound Bath specifically benefit an athlete's immune system?
For athletes, a Sound Bath helps lower stress hormones like cortisol, which can suppress immunity. By inducing deep relaxation, your body can prioritize immune response and repair, making you less susceptible to illness and promoting consistent training. It's about giving your body the best chance to fight off bugs.
Can a Sound Bath truly help with my physical recovery after strenuous activity?
Absolutely. The deep relaxation achieved in a Sound Bath promotes increased blood flow and cellular regeneration, crucial for muscle repair and reducing inflammation. It helps your body shift into a parasympathetic state, the 'rest and digest' mode, which is essential for efficient physical recovery and reducing soreness.
Is Sound Bath suitable for all types of athletes and active people?
Yes, our Sound Bath sessions are beneficial for anyone managing physical stress, from professional athletes to weekend warriors. The focus is on supporting your body's natural recovery processes and enhancing vitality, regardless of your sport or activity level. It's an inclusive practice for all who push their physical limits.
What should I expect during my first Sound Bath session for immune support?
You'll lie comfortably, often on a mat, while I play various instruments like singing bowls and gongs. The sounds create vibrations that encourage deep relaxation and a meditative state. Many active individuals report feeling profoundly rested and refreshed, noticing improved sleep and a sense of calm after their sessions, which directly aids immune health and recovery.
