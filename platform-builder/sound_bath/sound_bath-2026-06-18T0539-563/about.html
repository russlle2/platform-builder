What exactly is a Sound Bath, and how will it benefit me as a senior?

A Sound Bath is a gentle, meditative experience where you relax while being immersed in soothing sounds from various instruments. For seniors, it offers a peaceful way to reduce stress, calm the mind, and support spiritual reflection without any physical exertion. It's about finding inner quiet and a sense of well-being.

Do I need to do anything during the Sound Bath, or can I just relax?

Your only task is to relax! You'll typically lie down comfortably or sit in a supportive chair. There's nothing specific you need to 'do' during the session. Just allow the gentle sounds to wash over you, and notice any feelings of calm or peace that arise.

Is the Sound Bath suitable for someone with hearing aids or sensitive hearing?

We keep the volume at a gentle and comfortable level. If you have hearing aids, you might find it more comfortable to remove them, but it's entirely up to you. Please let Dr. Morgan Ellis know if you have sensitive hearing, and we can ensure your experience is as pleasant as possible.

What should I bring or wear to my Sound Bath session?

We recommend wearing comfortable, loose-fitting clothing that allows you to fully relax. You might also consider bringing a personal blanket or small pillow for extra comfort, though we provide all necessary props. The goal is for you to feel completely at ease.
