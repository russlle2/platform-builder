Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly happens during a Sound Bath session?
During a Sound Bath, you'll recline comfortably while I play various instruments like crystal bowls and gongs. The sounds create an immersive auditory experience designed to promote deep relaxation and nervous system regulation. Your only task is to listen and allow the sounds to influence your state of mind and body.
How can a Sound Bath specifically help with mental clarity?
The calming effect of a Sound Bath helps reduce mental clutter and stress, which are common inhibitors of clear thinking. By promoting a relaxed nervous system state, your brain can function more efficiently, supporting improved focus, memory, and overall cognitive function vital for maintaining mental sharpness.
Do I need any prior experience with meditation or similar practices?
Absolutely not. A Sound Bath requires no prior experience. There's no right or wrong way to experience it. Simply come as you are, with an open mind, and allow the sounds to do their work. It's a passive experience designed for ease and accessibility for everyone.
What should I wear or bring to a Sound Bath session?
For your comfort, we recommend wearing loose, comfortable clothing. You'll be lying down for the duration of the session, so choose attire that won't restrict you. We provide mats and blankets, but feel free to bring your own pillow or eye mask if that enhances your personal comfort.
