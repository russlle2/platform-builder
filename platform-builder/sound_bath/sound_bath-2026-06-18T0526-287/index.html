Frequently Asked Questions

I'm always on the go. How can a Sound Bath help me specifically with my intense schedule?
Your demanding schedule often means you're operating in a constant state of alert. A Sound Bath offers a crucial counterpoint, guiding your nervous system into a deep state of rest. This helps to reduce chronic stress, clear mental clutter from your packed days, and even improve your sleep quality, leaving you more refreshed and focused for your next challenge.
I don't have any 'wellness experience'. Do I need to prepare anything or know what to do?
Absolutely not. Our Sound Bath sessions are designed to be completely accessible, even if you've never tried anything like this before. There's no prior experience or special knowledge required. You simply arrive, get comfortable, and allow the sounds to do their work. We'll guide you gently through the entire process, ensuring you feel supported every step of the way.
I'm looking for emotional processing, not just relaxation. How does Sound Bath address this?
The resonant frequencies in a Sound Bath can gently encourage the release of stored emotional tension in your body. It creates a non-verbal space where your system can process feelings without the need for conscious analysis or confrontation. This subtle emotional release often leads to a profound sense of lightness and clarity, supporting your emotional healing journey.
Is this a form of therapy? What should I expect during a session?
While Sound Bath can be deeply therapeutic and support emotional healing, it is not a substitute for traditional therapy. During a session, you'll lie down comfortably, often covered with a blanket. will play various instruments like crystal bowls and gongs, creating an immersive soundscape. Your role is simply to receive the sounds, allowing your body and mind to respond naturally. Many people experience deep relaxation, vivid imagery, or a sense of emotional release.
