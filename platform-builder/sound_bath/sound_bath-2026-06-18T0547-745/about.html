What exactly happens during a Sound Bath?

During a Sound Bath, you'll lie down comfortably while Dr. Morgan Ellis plays a variety of instruments like singing bowls and gongs. The sounds create vibrations that help quiet your mind and body, providing a deep sense of relaxation. It's a chance to release tension and find a moment of peace, especially helpful when you're feeling overwhelmed by academic demands.

Do I need any special experience or equipment for a Sound Bath?

Absolutely not! You don't need any prior experience with meditation or sound practices. Just come as you are. We provide everything you need for comfort, like mats and blankets. Simply wear comfortable clothing, and be ready to relax and be open to the experience. Our goal is to make it easy and accessible for every college student.

How can a Sound Bath help with anxiety and academic pressure?

The deep relaxation induced by a Sound Bath can help calm your nervous system, reducing feelings of anxiety and the physical symptoms of stress. By creating a space for mental clarity and emotional processing, it can help you approach your studies and relationships with a more centered and focused perspective, making it easier to manage academic pressures.

Is a Sound Bath a good way to connect with others?

While the Sound Bath itself is a personal experience, participating in a group session creates a shared sense of calm and community. It's a gentle way to feel connected to others in a supportive environment, without any pressure to interact. Many students find that by nurturing their own inner peace, they naturally feel more open and connected in their broader college relationships.
