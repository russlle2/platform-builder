How Sound Bath Supports Sleep During Major Transitions

Major life changes, like career shifts, new parenthood, or relocation, can disrupt sleep patterns significantly. Our Sound Bath methodology is designed to gently guide your nervous system into a state conducive to deep rest. By using specific frequencies and sustained tones, we aim to reduce the physiological markers of stress that often prevent sound sleep, offering a non-pharmacological approach to improving your nightly rest.
