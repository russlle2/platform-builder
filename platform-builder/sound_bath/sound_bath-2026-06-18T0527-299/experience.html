Sound Bath

From Overwhelm to Ease: A Trauma-Informed Sound Bath Experience

Your well-being as a parent is paramount. Our Sound Bath experience is rooted in trauma-informed principles, meaning we prioritize your comfort and agency above all else. There's no pressure, just an invitation to rest and receive. The carefully chosen instruments and soothing soundscapes are crafted to help your mind and body shift from a state of overwhelm to one of ease, allowing you to process emotions without feeling re-traumatized or overwhelmed.
