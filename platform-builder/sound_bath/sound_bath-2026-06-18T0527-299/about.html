How can a Sound Bath specifically help me manage the stress of parenting young children?

Our Sound Bath sessions are tailored to address the unique stresses parents face. The gentle vibrations encourage your nervous system to downregulate, helping to reduce feelings of overwhelm and emotional reactivity often associated with constant demands. It's a dedicated time for your mind and body to reset, allowing you to approach parenting with renewed calm.

I'm always on the go. Will I truly be able to relax during a Sound Bath?

We understand that finding stillness can be a challenge when your life revolves around little ones. Our Sound Bath environment is designed for deep relaxation. You simply lie down and allow the sounds to wash over you. Many parents find that even if their mind is initially busy, the immersive soundscape gently guides them into a state of profound rest and emotional release.

Is this a 'quick fix' for parental burnout, or does it offer deeper support?

While you'll likely feel immediate relief, our Sound Bath practice offers deeper, trauma-informed support for emotional healing. It's not about a quick fix, but about creating space for your nervous system to integrate experiences and build resilience over time. Regular sessions can help you cultivate a more sustained sense of emotional balance, empowering you in your parenting journey.

I'm new to this. What should I expect during my first Sound Bath session?

You can expect a welcoming, calm environment where your comfort is paramount. You'll lie comfortably on a mat, often with blankets and props, and simply close your eyes. Dr. Morgan Ellis will play a variety of instruments, creating an immersive soundscape. There's nothing for you to 'do' other than to allow the sounds to wash over you and observe any sensations or emotions that arise, knowing you are in a safe, supported space.
