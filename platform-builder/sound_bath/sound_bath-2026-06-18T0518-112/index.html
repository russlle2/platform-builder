Frequently Asked Questions

Is Sound Bath safe for me during my specific postpartum recovery stage?
Our Sound Bath sessions are generally very gentle and safe. However, we always recommend consulting with your healthcare provider before starting any new wellness practice, especially during the early stages of postpartum recovery. Please let us know if you have any specific concerns, and we can discuss them.
How can Sound Bath specifically help with my sleep challenges as a new mother?
Our Sound Bath focuses on downregulating your nervous system, which is crucial for initiating and maintaining sleep. By creating an environment of deep relaxation through specific sound frequencies, we help quiet the mental chatter and physical tension that often prevent new mothers from achieving restorative rest, even during short opportunities.
What if I can't stay still or fully relax during a Sound Bath session?
It's completely normal to have a busy mind, especially as a new mother. There's no expectation to 'achieve' a certain state of relaxation. Simply allowing yourself to be present with the sounds is beneficial. Many find that even with some initial restlessness, the sounds naturally encourage a deeper state of calm over the session. We provide comfortable support to help you relax as much as possible.
How often should I attend Sound Bath sessions to see an improvement in my rest?
The frequency that works best for you can be personal. Many new mothers find that attending weekly or bi-weekly sessions helps to establish a consistent pattern of deep relaxation and supports ongoing sleep improvement. We can discuss a personalized plan during your consultation to best fit your recovery needs.
