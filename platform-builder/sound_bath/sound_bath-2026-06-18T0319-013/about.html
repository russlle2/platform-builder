What should I expect during my first Sound Bath session?

During your first Sound Bath, you'll be invited to lie down or sit comfortably. I will then play various instruments, creating gentle, immersive sounds. Your only role is to relax and allow the sounds to wash over you. Many seniors report feeling deeply relaxed, some even drifting into a light sleep, and emerging with a sense of calm and mental clarity.

Is a Sound Bath suitable for seniors with mobility concerns?

Absolutely. Our Sound Bath sessions are designed to be fully accessible and gentle. You can choose to lie on a comfortable mat or remain seated in a chair, whatever suits your comfort and mobility needs best. There are no strenuous movements required, making it ideal for those with limited mobility. Your comfort is our priority.

How can a Sound Bath help with stress and nervous system regulation for me?

The specific frequencies used in a Sound Bath encourage your brainwaves to shift into more relaxed states, which helps to calm your nervous system. This can lead to a reduction in feelings of stress, anxiety, and even improve sleep quality. For seniors, a regulated nervous system supports overall well-being and a greater sense of inner peace, helping you navigate daily life with more ease.

What if I fall asleep during the Sound Bath?

It's quite common for participants, especially seniors, to fall asleep or enter a very relaxed, dream-like state during a Sound Bath. This is a sign that your body and mind are deeply relaxing and receiving the benefits of the session. There's no need to worry; simply allow yourself to rest. You'll awaken feeling refreshed and rejuvenated.
