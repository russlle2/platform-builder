The Science Behind Your Sound Bath Experience

We leverage specific frequencies and resonant tones within our Sound Bath practice to facilitate a shift from beta brainwaves (associated with alertness) to alpha and theta states (linked to deep relaxation and sleep readiness). This physiologically supported approach helps mitigate the chronic stress that often impedes restorative rest for high-achievers. You'll experience a palpable reduction in mental chatter, setting the stage for more efficient and effective sleep.
