Contraindications (please read)
Sound baths are restorative, but not suitable for everyone. Please consult us before attending if you are pregnant, have epilepsy, recent head/neck injury, pacemaker or other implanted device, or significant cardiovascular conditions. This is not medical advice; contact your provider if unsure.
The flow — what to expect
Our sessions follow a gentle arc that supports grounding, release, and integration.
Typical length: 75–90 minutes
Welcome & grounding (10–15 min) — soft seating, brief intention-setting, and breath awareness to settle the body and senses.
Layered sound immersion (40–55 min) — we move through resonant textures using crystal bowls, chimes, tuning forks, and a Himalayan gong for slow, evolving soundscapes.
Rest & resonance (10–15 min) — a deep, quiet period for somatic listening and absorption.
Integration & close (10 min) — gentle reorientation, tea or water when available, and brief sharing if desired.
