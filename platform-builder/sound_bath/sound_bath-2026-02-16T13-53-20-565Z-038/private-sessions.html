How a private session unfolds

  1. Intake & alignment — 10–15 minutes to set intention and note preferences/contraindications.
  2. Breath and grounding — short somatic practice to meet your present state.
  3. Main immersion — 50–60 minutes of tailored sound including tuning forks and bowed strings to sculpt the field.
  4. Integration & aftercare — 10–15 minutes with hydration and gentle movement prompts.

Contraindications: Please inform us if you have photosensitive epilepsy, recent surgery, pregnancy, heart conditions, or implanted medical devices. If in doubt, consult your doctor.

What to bring

A mat or blanket, pillow, eye-cover, and comfortable layers. We provide bolsters on request for 1:1 sessions.
