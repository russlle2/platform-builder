Quick session guide

Flow: brief check-in → sound immersion → soft re-entry. Instruments chosen for private bookings often include crystal bowls, chimes, and monochord textures. Group sessions favor a warm blend of crystal bowls and singing bowls.

What to bring

  • Pillow/bolster
  • Eye covering
  • Water bottle

Contraindications

If pregnant, have a pacemaker, epilepsy, or recent surgeries, please contact us before booking so we can adapt the session.
