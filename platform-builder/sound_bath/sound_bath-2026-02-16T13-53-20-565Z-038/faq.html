What happens during a sound bath?

You arrive and lie down in a comfortable setting. After a brief check-in, the facilitator layers instruments — often a selection like gong, monochord, or tuning forks — to guide sensations. We alternate sustained tones with moments of silence so the body can integrate.

How long are sessions and how should I arrive?

Most group sessions run 75–90 minutes. Arrive 10–15 minutes early to set up your space and settle in. Light clothing is best; bring a mat, blanket, and eye covering if you like.

What to bring?

Essentials: a mat or thick blanket, pillow or bolster for head/neck support, an eye mask, and a water bottle. Layers are recommended since temperature can change during rest.

Contraindications — when to check in first

If you are pregnant, have epilepsy, a pacemaker, recent surgeries, or significant hearing issues, email us before booking. Certain medical conditions require modifications to ensure comfort and safety.

I get anxious in quiet — will I be overwhelmed?

We structure sessions with soft entrances and gentle crescendos rather than abrupt silences. If you need a gentler experience, request a quieter placement when you book or speak to the facilitator on arrival.

Instruments we lean on

We rotate instruments to keep the field fresh and intention-driven. Recent mixes have included: Tibetan singing bowls, crystal bowls, chimes, and selected tuning forks.
