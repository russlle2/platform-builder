The flow

A soft, intentional sequence to arrive and integrate.

  1. Arrival & Grounding — Gentle breathwork and a short check-in to set an intention.
  2. Resonance Build — Tones layer in: crystal singing bowls and warm monochord create a foundation.
  3. Deep Field — Gong and chimes weave through tuning forks and harmonic overtones for embodied surrender.
  4. Return — Soft vocal tones, silence, and time to journal or share if you wish.

Instruments we often use: crystal bowls, monocord, vintage gong, handheld chimes, and a selection of Tibetan bells and tuning forks — arranged to support a spacious, tactile sonic palette.

Tiny rituals that amplify the experience

Small practices to try before and after sessions.

Pre-session

  • Hydrate steadily during the day.
  • Softly limit caffeine for a few hours prior.
  • Set a single-word intention — write it down if you like.

Aftercare

  • Move slowly for 20–30 minutes; stretching helps integrate sound into the body.
  • Rest with warm tea and low light; journal any impressions.
  • Space your next session 2–4 weeks to notice cumulative shifts.
