Frequently Asked Questions

How does a Sound Bath specifically help seniors with immune support?
A Sound Bath helps seniors by promoting deep relaxation, which can reduce stress hormones and support a healthy immune response. The gentle frequencies encourage a calm state, allowing your body to focus its energy on maintaining well-being and resilience.
Is the Sound Bath suitable for someone with mobility concerns?
Absolutely. Our Sound Bath sessions are designed to be fully accessible. You can choose to lie down on a comfortable mat or sit in a chair, whatever feels best for you. Your comfort and ease are our top priorities throughout the experience.
What kind of sounds will I hear during a Sound Bath?
You will experience a blend of harmonious sounds from various instruments like Himalayan singing bowls, gongs, chimes, and gentle percussion. These sounds are chosen specifically for their soothing qualities, designed to create a peaceful and supportive environment.
How often should I attend a Sound Bath to experience its benefits for vitality?
Many seniors find regular attendance, perhaps once or twice a month, helps maintain a sustained sense of calm, improved sleep, and enhanced vitality. We recommend starting with a session and seeing how it resonates with your personal well-being goals.
