Frequently Asked Questions

What makes Sound Bath different for new mothers?
Our Sound Bath sessions are specifically designed with your postpartum recovery in mind. We prioritize emotional healing, a trauma-informed approach, and create a highly supportive, non-judgmental environment to address the unique sensitivities and needs of new mothers.
Is a Sound Bath suitable if I'm feeling particularly fragile or overwhelmed postpartum?
Absolutely. Our sessions are crafted to be gentle and restorative. We understand that postpartum can be a tender time, and the sounds are chosen to be soothing, offering a space for quiet emotional processing without any pressure to engage or perform. Your comfort and emotional safety are our highest priorities.
Do I need to do anything special or have any experience for a postpartum Sound Bath?
No prior experience is necessary. You simply need to arrive as you are. We encourage you to wear comfortable clothing and bring anything that will help you feel cozy, like a favorite blanket. Our role is to create a welcoming and safe space for your emotional restoration, requiring nothing more from you than your presence.
Can I bring my baby to a Sound Bath session?
We offer specific Sound Bath sessions designed for mothers with infants, as well as sessions focused solely on the mother's recovery. Please check our schedule or contact us directly to find the best option that supports both your needs and your baby's comfort. We aim to be as inclusive and supportive as possible for your postpartum experience.
