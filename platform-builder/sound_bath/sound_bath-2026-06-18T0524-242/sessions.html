Sound Bath

Gentle Vibrations for Postpartum Hearts

Your postpartum period is a time of immense change, and it's okay if it feels overwhelming. Our Sound Bath sessions are specifically designed to meet you where you are, offering a soft landing for your emotions. We use intentional soundscapes to help quiet the mental chatter, allowing you to process experiences and find moments of peace without pressure or expectation. This space is for your emotional well-being, acknowledging the unique challenges and triumphs of new motherhood.
