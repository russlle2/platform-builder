Sound Bath

From Anxiety to Awareness: Your Sound Bath Experience

Feeling overwhelmed by test anxiety or the pressure to perform? A Sound Bath session invites you to simply be. You'll lie comfortably as a rich tapestry of sounds from various instruments washes over you. There’s nothing to 'do' or 'achieve' – just an opportunity to allow the resonant vibrations to soothe your nervous system. This mindful practice fosters a state of deep relaxation, enabling you to reduce stress and cultivate a more present, aware mindset that benefits all aspects of your college experience.
