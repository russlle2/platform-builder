Sound Bath

Your Comforting Sound Bath Experience in Your City

When you join us for a Sound Bath, you'll simply lie back on a comfortable mat, perhaps with a blanket and pillow, and allow the gentle sounds of bowls, gongs, and chimes to wash over you. There's nothing you need to do but relax. Dr. Morgan Ellis creates a safe and peaceful environment specifically designed for seniors to find moments of deep calm and physical respite. It's a time for your body to simply be, and to receive gentle support.
