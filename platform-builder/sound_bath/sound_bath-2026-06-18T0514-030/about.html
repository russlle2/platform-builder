How does a Sound Bath specifically help with life transitions and stress?

A Sound Bath offers targeted support by using specific frequencies and vibrations that are known to calm the sympathetic nervous system. When you're navigating big changes, your stress response can be elevated. The immersive sound helps to gently guide your body out of 'fight or flight' and into a 'rest and digest' state, which is crucial for reducing anxiety, promoting emotional regulation, and helping you feel more grounded during uncertain times.

What should I expect during my first Sound Bath session?

You'll lie comfortably on a mat, often with blankets and bolsters for extra support. Dr. Morgan Ellis will then play various instruments like crystal singing bowls, gongs, and chimes, creating an immersive soundscape. There's nothing for you to do but listen and allow the sounds to wash over you. Many people experience deep relaxation, mental clarity, and a profound sense of peace, even falling into a meditative state.

Do I need any prior experience with meditation or sound healing for a Sound Bath?

Absolutely not. The beauty of a Sound Bath is that it requires no prior experience or effort from your side. The sounds themselves do the work, guiding your brainwaves into a meditative state effortlessly. It's a perfect practice for anyone, especially those feeling overwhelmed by life changes, who may find traditional meditation challenging.

How often should I attend a Sound Bath to feel the benefits during a major life transition?

While even one Sound Bath session can provide immediate relief and relaxation, consistent engagement tends to yield more profound and lasting benefits for nervous system regulation during transitions. Many clients find that attending weekly or bi-weekly sessions helps them maintain a sense of calm and resilience, providing a regular reset that supports their ability to navigate change with greater ease and clarity.
