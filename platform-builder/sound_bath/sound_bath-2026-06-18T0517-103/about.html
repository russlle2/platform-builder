What exactly happens during a Sound Bath, especially for seniors?

During a Sound Bath, you'll simply relax in a comfortable position, either lying down or sitting, while gentle sounds are created using various instruments like Himalayan singing bowls and gongs. There's no physical activity required from you, just an invitation to listen and let the soothing vibrations help your body and mind relax deeply. It's a very passive and peaceful experience, designed for your comfort and ease.

How can a Sound Bath specifically help with my sleep as I get older?

Our Sound Baths create a tranquil environment that helps quiet an overactive mind and soothe your nervous system. For seniors, this can be particularly beneficial in reducing the common anxieties or physical discomforts that often disrupt sleep. By promoting a deep state of relaxation, your body becomes better prepared for sleep, potentially leading to falling asleep more easily and experiencing more uninterrupted, restorative rest throughout the night.

Do I need to do anything special to prepare for my first Sound Bath session?

Not at all! We recommend wearing comfortable clothing, and you might bring a water bottle to stay hydrated. We provide blankets and cushions, but feel free to bring anything else that would make you feel more cozy and at ease, like a favorite pillow or eye mask. The most important thing is to arrive with an open mind, ready to relax and receive the calming sounds.

Is a Sound Bath safe if I have certain health conditions or hearing aids?

Sound Baths are generally very gentle and safe. If you use hearing aids, you can keep them in or remove them, depending on what feels most comfortable for you and allows you to experience the sounds best. If you have any specific health concerns, it's always wise to mention them to us beforehand, or consult with your doctor, just to ensure your complete comfort and peace of mind during the session. Your well-being is our top priority.
