What exactly is a Sound Bath and how can it help my body?

A Sound Bath involves relaxing in a comfortable position while Dr. Morgan Ellis plays various instruments like singing bowls and gongs. The gentle sounds and vibrations encourage your body to relax deeply, which can help reduce muscle tension, ease joint discomfort, and calm your nervous system, offering a gentle way to address physical pain.

Do I need to do anything specific during the Sound Bath session?

No, your only task is to get comfortable and allow yourself to relax. You can lie down or sit, whichever is most supportive for your body. There's no movement required, just an invitation to listen and let the sounds wash over you. We provide blankets and cushions to ensure your complete ease.

Is the Sound Bath suitable if I have specific mobility concerns or health conditions?

Absolutely. Our Sound Bath sessions are designed with accessibility in mind. Please let Dr. Morgan Ellis know about any concerns beforehand, and we will ensure you are comfortable and supported throughout. The experience is gentle and non-invasive, making it suitable for a wide range of physical needs.

How often should I attend a Sound Bath to feel the benefits for my physical discomfort?

The frequency often depends on your individual needs and how your body responds. Many seniors find that regular sessions, perhaps once a week or bi-weekly, help maintain a consistent level of comfort and ease. We encourage you to listen to your body and attend as often as feels right for you.
