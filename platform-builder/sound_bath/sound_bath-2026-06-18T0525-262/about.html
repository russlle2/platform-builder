Is a Sound Bath safe for me during postpartum recovery?

Yes, our Sound Bath sessions are specifically designed with your postpartum recovery in mind. We prioritize gentle frequencies and a calm environment to support your physical and emotional healing without any undue stress. It's a wonderful way to offer yourself much-needed rest and recalibration.

What should I expect during my first Postpartum Sound Bath session?

You'll be invited to lie down comfortably on a mat, perhaps with a blanket and pillow, just as you are. Our practitioner will then play various instruments like crystal bowls and gongs. You don't need to do anything but relax and receive the sounds. Many new mothers find it deeply restful and restorative.

How can a Sound Bath help with the emotional challenges of new motherhood?

The gentle vibrations from a Sound Bath can help quiet an overactive mind and soothe your nervous system, which is often stretched during postpartum. This can create space for emotional processing, reduce feelings of anxiety or overwhelm, and help you feel more centered and connected to your inner self amidst the demands of caring for a new baby.

Do I need to have any prior experience with sound healing to attend?

Absolutely not. Our Postpartum Sound Bath is accessible to everyone, regardless of prior experience. All you need is an openness to rest and receive. We guide you through the process and ensure you feel comfortable and supported every step of the way.
