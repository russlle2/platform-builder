Targeted Stress Reduction with Sound Bath

When you are recovering from a chronic illness or injury, managing stress is crucial. Our Sound Bath sessions are designed to provide a specific, non-invasive method for reducing the physiological markers of stress, helping to calm an overactive nervous system. We focus on creating an environment where your body can shift from 'fight or flight' into a state conducive to rest and repair.

How does a Sound Bath specifically help with chronic illness or injury recovery?

Our Sound Bath sessions are structured to induce a deep relaxation response, which is vital for recovery. By calming your nervous system, we aim to reduce chronic stress hormones, improve sleep quality, and support your body's natural restorative processes. This can help manage symptoms like pain and fatigue associated with chronic conditions.

Is a Sound Bath safe if I have specific sensitivities or physical limitations due to my condition?

Absolutely. Your comfort and safety are paramount. Please communicate any sensitivities or physical limitations to Dr. Morgan Ellis beforehand. We can adjust sound levels and suggest positions that ensure you have a supportive and beneficial experience tailored to your specific needs.

What should I expect during my first Sound Bath session?

You will lie comfortably, typically on a mat, with blankets and pillows for support. Dr. Morgan Ellis will play various instruments, creating a rich soundscape. Your role is simply to relax and allow the sounds to wash over you. Most individuals experience a deep state of calm, and some may even fall into a light sleep, aiding nervous system regulation.

How often should I attend Sound Bath sessions to see benefits for my stress and nervous system?

The frequency of sessions can vary based on your individual needs and recovery goals. Many individuals find weekly or bi-weekly sessions most beneficial for consistent stress reduction and sustained nervous system regulation. We can discuss a personalized schedule during your initial consultation.
