How Sound Bath Assists with Physical Pain and Somatic Healing

Our Sound Bath sessions are carefully structured to create an environment where your body can find deep relaxation. The gentle vibrations can help calm the nervous system, potentially easing muscle tension and reducing the perception of chronic pain. We focus on somatic healing, inviting your body to release stored stress and discomfort at its own pace, supporting your recovery journey.
