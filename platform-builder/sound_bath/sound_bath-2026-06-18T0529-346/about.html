How does a Sound Bath specifically help with chronic physical pain?

A Sound Bath uses resonant frequencies to encourage deep relaxation, which can help calm your nervous system. This calming effect can reduce muscle tension, decrease inflammation, and shift your focus from pain, potentially lowering your overall perception of chronic physical discomfort and aiding your body's natural healing responses.

Is a Sound Bath safe if I have specific injuries or mobility limitations?

Absolutely. Our Sound Bath sessions are designed to be fully accessible. You're encouraged to find the most comfortable position for your body, whether lying down, seated, or supported with props. We can discuss any specific needs you have before the session to ensure your experience is safe, gentle, and supportive for your unique recovery.

What can I expect to feel during a Sound Bath if I'm recovering from an illness?

During a Sound Bath, many people report feeling a deep sense of relaxation and a release of tension. You might notice a reduction in mental chatter, a calming of your heart rate, and a general sense of peace. For those recovering from illness, it can offer a much-needed period of rest and somatic restoration, helping your body conserve energy for healing.

How often should I attend a Sound Bath to support my physical recovery?

The frequency that best supports your physical recovery can vary. Many individuals find regular attendance, perhaps weekly or bi-weekly, helps maintain a consistent state of relaxation and pain reduction. We encourage you to listen to your body and discuss your specific needs with us to find a rhythm that feels right for your ongoing healing.
