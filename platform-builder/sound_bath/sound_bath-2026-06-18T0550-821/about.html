How can a Sound Bath specifically help with my posture if I'm not actively moving?

A Sound Bath works by deeply relaxing your nervous system and releasing tension held in your body. When your muscles and fascia are no longer gripped by stress, they can naturally realign. This allows your body to remember and settle into a more optimal, supported posture, even without active movement. You'll find a subtle yet profound shift in your bodily awareness.

I'm experiencing burnout; will a Sound Bath be too overwhelming or stimulating?

Not at all. A Sound Bath is specifically designed to be deeply restorative and calming. The sounds are carefully chosen to induce a state of relaxation, helping to quiet your overactive mind and soothe your stressed nervous system. It's a passive experience where you simply lie down and receive, making it ideal for those needing profound rest and mental decompression.

Do I need any prior experience with meditation or sound practices to benefit from a Sound Bath?

Absolutely no prior experience is needed. The beauty of a Sound Bath is its accessibility. You don't need to 'do' anything; simply arrive, lie comfortably, and allow the vibrations to wash over you. Your body and mind will naturally respond to the frequencies, guiding you into a state of deep relaxation and body awareness without any effort on your part.

What should I wear or bring to a Sound Bath session to maximize its benefits for my body?

Wear comfortable, loose-fitting clothing that allows you to fully relax without any constriction. You'll be lying down for the duration, so consider layers to stay warm. We provide mats, blankets, and bolsters, but you're welcome to bring your own eye mask or a small pillow for extra comfort to support your head and neck if that helps your preferred lying posture.
