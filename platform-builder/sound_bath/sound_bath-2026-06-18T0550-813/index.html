Frequently Asked Questions

How does a Sound Bath specifically benefit my postural health as a senior?
Our Sound Bath sessions create a deep state of relaxation, which can alleviate chronic muscle tension that often contributes to poor posture. By reducing this tension, your body can more easily find and maintain its natural, aligned position, supporting a more upright and comfortable stance without conscious effort.
Will I need to perform any physical movements during a Sound Bath?
No, you don't. During a Sound Bath, your primary role is to relax comfortably. You'll typically lie down or sit in a supported position. The sound vibrations do the 'work,' encouraging your body and mind to unwind, which in turn supports your natural body awareness and postural alignment without any physical exertion from your side.
Is a Sound Bath suitable if I have limited mobility or specific physical concerns?
Absolutely. Our Sound Bath practice is designed to be gentle and inclusive. We prioritize your comfort, offering various supported lying or seating options. Please share any specific concerns you have before your session, and will ensure your experience is tailored to your needs, focusing on profound relaxation for your body.
What can I expect to feel during and after a Sound Bath focused on body awareness?
During the session, you may feel deeply relaxed, noticing a gentle vibration throughout your body. Afterward, many clients report feeling a greater sense of calm, mental clarity, and a heightened awareness of their physical form. You might notice your posture feels more naturally aligned, and movements feel a little smoother and more integrated.
