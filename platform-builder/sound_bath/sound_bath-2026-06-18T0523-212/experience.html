The Science Behind Serenity: Understanding Your Sound Bath Experience

Utilizing carefully selected instruments like crystal singing bowls and gongs, our Sound Bath sessions are designed to guide your nervous system into a state of deep relaxation. This state, often associated with delta and theta brainwave frequencies, can help reduce anxiety, improve sleep quality, and support emotional processing. We approach each session with a clinical understanding of how sound affects the body and mind, ensuring a therapeutic experience tailored to the sensitivities of postpartum recovery.
