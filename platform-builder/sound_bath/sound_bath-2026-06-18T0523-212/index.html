Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is a Sound Bath and how does it help new mothers in their postpartum recovery?
A Sound Bath is a deeply relaxing experience where you are bathed in the soothing sounds and vibrations of various instruments. For new mothers, it offers a dedicated time to rest, reduce stress, and gently process emotions that arise during postpartum. The sounds help quiet your mind, allowing your nervous system to calm, which can aid in emotional integration and overall well-being during this unique period.
Is a Sound Bath safe and appropriate if I'm feeling particularly vulnerable or overwhelmed postpartum?
Yes, absolutely. Our Sound Bath sessions are specifically designed with a trauma-informed approach, meaning your emotional safety and comfort are our highest priorities. We create a gentle, supportive environment where you can feel secure and in control. If you're feeling vulnerable, we encourage you to communicate your needs, and we will ensure your experience is as nurturing and calm as possible.
Do I need to do anything during the Sound Bath, or can I just rest?
Your only requirement during a Sound Bath is to simply rest and receive the sounds. There's no need to 'do' anything specific. You can lie down comfortably, close your eyes, and allow the vibrations to wash over you. Our intention is to provide a space where you can fully relax and allow your body and mind to find their own rhythm of calm and healing.
How often should I attend a Sound Bath to experience benefits during my postpartum recovery?
The frequency of Sound Bath sessions depends on your individual needs and how you feel. Many new mothers find regular sessions, perhaps weekly or bi-weekly, to be most beneficial for consistent emotional support and stress reduction. Others may choose to attend whenever they feel particularly overwhelmed or in need of deep relaxation. We can discuss a personalized approach that aligns with your recovery journey.
