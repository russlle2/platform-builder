Sound Bath

What to Expect at Your Ocean Breeze Sound Bath

When you join us for an Ocean Breeze Sound Bath, you'll find a welcoming, comfortable space designed for relaxation. You'll simply lie back, get cozy, and allow the gentle, resonant sounds from various instruments – like singing bowls and gongs – to wash over you. There's nothing for you to 'do' except to be present and receive. Many of our participants describe feeling a deep sense of peace and tranquility, often reporting improved sleep quality and a renewed sense of well-being after their sessions.
