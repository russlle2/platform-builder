Frequently Asked Questions

How does a Sound Bath specifically assist with emotional healing during major life transitions?
Our Sound Bath sessions are designed to gently guide your nervous system into a state of deep relaxation. This allows for a non-verbal processing of emotions and experiences associated with your life changes. The curated sound frequencies can help to release stored tension and foster a sense of clarity, supporting your emotional integration without requiring you to verbally articulate every detail.
Is the trauma-informed aspect of your Sound Bath different from a typical session?
Absolutely. Our trauma-informed approach means that every aspect of your Sound Bath is delivered with an acute awareness of potential triggers and sensitivities. We prioritize establishing a sense of safety and control for you, offering gentle guidance and ensuring the environment supports your individual needs for processing, rather than overwhelming you. Your comfort and agency are at the forefront of every session.
What should I expect to feel during and after a private Sound Bath session?
During a Sound Bath, you might experience deep relaxation, a sense of calm, or even emotional release. Afterwards, clients often report feeling lighter, more centered, and emotionally regulated. The effects can be subtle yet profound, contributing to a greater sense of peace and an improved capacity to navigate your ongoing life transitions with grace.
How many Sound Bath sessions are typically recommended for significant emotional recalibration?
The number of Sound Bath sessions required varies based on your individual needs and the nature of your life transitions. While a single session can offer immediate benefits, consistent engagement over several sessions often yields the most profound and lasting emotional recalibration. We can discuss a personalized plan during your initial consultation to best support your unique circumstances.
