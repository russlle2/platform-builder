How can a Sound Bath specifically help my postpartum stress and nervous system?

During your postpartum period, your nervous system is often in a state of heightened alert. Sound Bath frequencies can help shift your brainwaves from an active beta state to a more relaxed alpha or theta state, promoting deep rest, reducing cortisol levels, and helping you feel more grounded and less overwhelmed. It’s a gentle way to encourage your system to find calm.

Is it safe to attend a Sound Bath while I’m still recovering physically from childbirth?

Absolutely. Our Sound Bath sessions are non-invasive and deeply gentle. You'll be invited to lie down in a comfortable position that suits your body's current needs, whether that's on your back with support or on your side. The sounds are soothing, not jarring, making it a perfectly safe and restorative practice for your physical recovery and emotional well-being.

I'm sleep-deprived and find it hard to relax. Will a Sound Bath truly help me?

Yes, many new mothers who struggle with relaxation and sleep find significant relief. The consistent, resonating sounds act as an anchor, helping your mind to quiet down from the constant demands of new motherhood. Even if you don't 'fall asleep,' the deep relaxation achieved in a Sound Bath can be as restorative as several hours of sleep, helping to reset your circadian rhythms and improve sleep quality over time.

What should I bring or do to prepare for my first postpartum Sound Bath session?

Just bring yourself, dressed in comfortable clothing. You might also bring a water bottle. We provide everything you need for comfort, including mats, blankets, and bolsters. The most important preparation is simply setting the intention to allow yourself to receive and relax. We'll guide you through the rest, ensuring you feel supported from the moment you arrive.
