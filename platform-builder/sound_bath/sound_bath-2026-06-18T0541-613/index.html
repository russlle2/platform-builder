Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is a Sound Bath and how is it suitable for seniors aged 60+?
A Sound Bath involves relaxing as gentle, resonant sounds from instruments like singing bowls and gongs wash over you. For seniors, it's particularly suitable because it requires no physical activity, offering a deeply restful experience that can reduce stress and promote a sense of calm, which is beneficial for immune health and overall vitality.
Will a Sound Bath session help me feel more energetic and vital?
Many of our clients report feeling a renewed sense of calm energy and mental clarity after a Sound Bath. By reducing stress and fostering deep relaxation, these sessions can improve sleep quality and ease tension, contributing to a more vibrant and engaged feeling in your daily life. It’s a gentle way to support your inner resources.
Is the Sound Bath experience safe if I have specific health concerns?
Our Sound Bath sessions are designed to be very gentle and non-invasive. While generally safe, if you have specific health concerns or use medical devices, we recommend consulting with your doctor before attending. You are also welcome to speak with directly to discuss any questions you might have about your comfort and safety during the session.
What should I bring or wear to a Sound Bath session at ?
To ensure your utmost comfort, we suggest wearing loose, comfortable clothing. You might also consider bringing a small pillow or a light blanket to make your resting position even cozier, though we provide mats and some blankets. The goal is for you to feel completely at ease and able to relax deeply during the session.
