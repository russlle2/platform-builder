What should I wear to a Sound Bath?

Please wear comfortable, loose-fitting clothing that allows you to relax fully. You’ll be lying down for the session, so think about what makes you feel cozy and at ease.

Will I have to do anything during the Sound Bath?

Absolutely not. Your only job is to lie back, close your eyes if you wish, and simply listen. There's no movement or participation required from you at all. Just let the sounds wash over you.

Is a Sound Bath too loud for sensitive ears?

Dr. Morgan Ellis plays the instruments very gently to create a soothing atmosphere, not a jarring one. The focus is on gentle resonance and relaxation. If you have concerns, please feel free to discuss them with us beforehand.

How might a Sound Bath help with physical discomfort?

Many people find that the gentle vibrations and calming sounds of a Sound Bath encourage deep relaxation, which can help to reduce muscle tension, ease stiffness, and lessen general aches. It offers your body a chance to unwind and reset.
