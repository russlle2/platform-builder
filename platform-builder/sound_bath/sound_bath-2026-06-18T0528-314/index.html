Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Sound Bath specifically help with my athletic recovery or physical stress?
Our Sound Bath sessions are designed to lower your body's stress response, which can directly impact muscle recovery. The specific frequencies can help to reduce muscle tension, decrease inflammation, and improve circulation, all vital for an athlete's physical restoration and management of persistent physical stress.
Is a Sound Bath safe if I have a specific physical injury I'm managing?
Generally, yes. Sound baths are non-invasive and can be very beneficial for easing discomfort and promoting relaxation around injuries. However, it's always advisable to consult with your medical professional about any new therapy, and please inform of any specific concerns or conditions you have before your session.
What should I expect to feel during a Sound Bath session for physical relief?
Many active individuals report a profound sense of physical relaxation, often feeling tension release in their muscles and joints. You might experience subtle vibrations throughout your body, a slowing of your breath, and a deep mental quietness that allows your physical form to truly rest and recalibrate.
How often should I attend Sound Bath sessions to support my active lifestyle?
The frequency depends on your individual training intensity and recovery needs. Many athletes find weekly or bi-weekly sessions beneficial for consistent physical stress management and proactive recovery. We can discuss a personalized plan during your initial consultation based on your activity level and goals.
