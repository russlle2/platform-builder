Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is a Sound Bath and how will it help my 'mom brain'?
A Sound Bath involves lying down and being immersed in sounds produced by various instruments like singing bowls and gongs. For new mothers, these vibrations help calm an overactive mind, encouraging your brain to enter a more relaxed state. This can significantly reduce mental fog, improve your focus, and help you feel more mentally clear and organized during your postpartum period.
Do I need any special experience or preparation for a Sound Bath?
Absolutely not! You don't need any prior wellness experience or special preparation. Just wear comfortable clothing, and we'll provide everything else you need, like mats and blankets. The goal is simply for you to relax and allow the sounds to wash over you, offering a gentle way to support your mental well-being as a new mother.
Is a Sound Bath safe for me if I'm still recovering postpartum?
Yes, Sound Baths are a very gentle and passive practice, making them ideal for postpartum recovery. There's no physical exertion required, and you can adjust your position as needed for comfort. It's a safe space to nurture your mental and emotional health during this delicate time, helping you to feel more grounded and mentally refreshed.
How often should I attend a Sound Bath to feel the benefits for my mental clarity?
Even a single Sound Bath can offer a noticeable shift in your mental state, providing immediate relief from mental clutter. For more sustained improvement in your cognitive wellness and to truly combat 'mom brain,' many new mothers find that attending regularly, perhaps weekly or bi-weekly, helps maintain a consistent sense of mental clarity and calm.
