Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is a Sound Bath and how does it help my posture?
A Sound Bath is a deeply relaxing experience where you lie down and are immersed in gentle, resonant sounds from various instruments. These vibrations encourage deep relaxation, which can ease muscle tension and improve your body's natural alignment. By calming your nervous system, it helps you become more aware of how you hold your body, supporting better posture without physical exertion.
Do I need to do anything during a Sound Bath session?
No, your only task is to relax and allow the sounds to wash over you. We provide comfortable mats, blankets, and pillows. You simply lie back, close your eyes if you wish, and listen. There's no movement required, making it an ideal practice for gentle self-care focused on body awareness and comfort.
Is a Sound Bath suitable for seniors with limited mobility?
Absolutely. Sound Baths are a non-invasive, gentle practice perfect for those with limited mobility. You can remain seated if lying down is uncomfortable, and the sounds will still provide their beneficial effects. Our focus is on creating a peaceful environment where everyone can feel comfortable and supported in nurturing their well-being.
How often should I attend a Sound Bath to see benefits for my body awareness?
The frequency is a personal choice, but many seniors find that regular sessions, perhaps once a week or bi-weekly, help to maintain the sense of relaxation and body awareness they gain. Consistent exposure to these soothing vibrations can reinforce your body's ability to relax and hold itself with greater ease and poise.
