Questions & safety

What should I bring?
A mat or thick blanket, warm layers, and your curiosity. We provide bolsters and eye covers on request.
Are there medical precautions?
Contraindications include pregnancy, seizure disorder, and recent brain injury. If you have implants (e.g., cochlear implants), consult with us before attending. Inform the facilitator of any concerns.
How flexible is the schedule?
Membership credits roll monthly. We offer make-up options and a pause feature for life events — contact {{EMAIL}} for account support.
