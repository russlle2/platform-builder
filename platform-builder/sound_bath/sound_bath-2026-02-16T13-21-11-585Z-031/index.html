The Ritual

Our gatherings are designed as an elegant, slow unfolding — a shared ritual that guides you from arrival to rest. Membership surfaces a seasonal arc of practice and priority booking for limited seats.

Arrival

We begin with a warm welcome, gentle intention-setting, and a chance to settle into the space. Soft lighting and cushions are provided.

Sound Field

In the sound field we weave crystal bowls, chimes, and a deep-grooving monochord. The arrangement is curated to move you through layers — resonance, edge, and dissolve.

Return

We close with guided reorientation: a slow bell, a cup of herbal tea for grounding, and space for quiet reflection.

Care

Membership includes a seasonal grounding practice, priority booking for private sessions, and access to a members-only sound library.

What to expect & what to bring

A sensory, premium experience: our sessions emphasize subtlety, safety, and spaciousness. Seats are arranged so sound washes over you; we recommend arriving early to choose your intention.

  • Bring: a yoga mat, soft blanket, pillow for head support, and an eye cover if you prefer darkness.
  • Wear: layers you can add or remove — comfort is essential.
  • Optional: a notebook for after-session reflections.

Contraindications & safety

Please consult with your healthcare provider if you are pregnant, have recently had surgery, manage epilepsy, severe psychiatric conditions, or are using medical implants. Loud gongs or deep low-frequency tones may not be suitable for everyone; we offer quieter bowls and tuning forks upon request.
