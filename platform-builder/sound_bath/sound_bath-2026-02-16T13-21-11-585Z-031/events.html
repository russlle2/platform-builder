The ritual we hold

Each gathering is designed as a supportive container: breathwork, a guided settling, a long soundscape and a slow reintegration. The space is intentionally minimal — lighting soft, blankets and bolsters available.

Gong — low, sustaining washes
Chimes — bright micro-cuts of clarity
Monochord — harmonic drone
Tuning forks — focused frequency points

What to bring & session flow

What to bring: a yoga mat or thick blanket, warm layers, eye cover if you prefer, and an openness to a quiet hour.

  1. Arrival & gentle check-in (10 mins)
  2. Grounding with breath and intention (10 mins)
  3. Extended sound journey (40–50 mins)
  4. Soft re-entry and tea (10–15 mins)

Contraindications: If you are pregnant, have epilepsy, recent head injury, or are undergoing psychiatric crisis, please consult your clinician before attending. Inform the facilitator of implants, prosthetics, or sound sensitivity.
