Ritual & Flow

Each gathering is a carefully arranged arc of arrival, immersion, and gentle return. We begin with a guided settling — soft breath and intention setting — followed by a layered soundscape using crystal bowls, a deep gong, and chimes to support shifting attention. You are invited to lie down, soften, and let the frequencies wash through. Sessions close with space for reflection and light movement.

What to bring

  • Yoga mat, blanket, and a pillow for comfort
  • Water bottle and any small comforts (eye pillow encouraged)
  • Wear layers for warmth; socks are helpful

Contraindications & safety

Sound baths are deeply relaxing but not suitable for everyone. If you are pregnant, have seizures, are fitted with a pacemaker, are in acute psychiatric crisis, or have serious cardiovascular conditions, please consult your medical provider before attending. Contact us directly for modifications — safety and care are central to our practice.
