Common Questions, answered with care

Sensitive, premium sound events — our answers help you arrive prepared and comfortable. If something's not here, contact us at {{EMAIL}}.

Are these sessions loud?
No. Our work emphasizes texture, harmonic layering, and low-frequency presence without startling volume. Instruments include gongs, tuning forks, and a bowed monochord for deep, resonant tones tuned to support the body’s natural rhythms.
How early should I arrive?
Please arrive 10–15 minutes before the published start time to settle into a cushion and complete any brief intake. Doors close a few minutes into the session to preserve the group field.
What should I bring?
Bring a blanket, pillow, and an eye cover if you prefer darkness. We supply a limited number of mats and blankets on request; please note availability when booking.
Do you accommodate pregnancy or medical devices?
We include a contraindications consult for anyone pregnant, with implanted medical devices, severe tinnitus, or a history of seizures. Please email us ahead of time; we will suggest adaptations or private alternatives.
Refunds and cancellations?
Cancellations more than 72 hours before event receive a full refund. Within 72 hours, we offer credit toward another session. Membership credits and policies vary — see booking or contact us.
What if I'm sensitive to sound?
We offer a seat toward the back and can lower certain sources. Let the facilitator know upon arrival. Earplugs are welcome and often useful for people who prefer softened textures.
