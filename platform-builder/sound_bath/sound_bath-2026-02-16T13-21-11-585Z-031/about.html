Our practice — a modern ritual

At the heart of our gatherings is a slow arc: settling, immersion, and gentle return. We combine harmonic instruments and breath cues so the sound supports internal attention without overwhelm. Sessions are led with attention to safety, scent-free atmosphere, and dim, comforting light.

How a session flows

  • Arrival & soft seating (10–15 minutes) — a guided centering.
  • Main soundscape (30–45 minutes) — layered textures with crystal bowls and chimes, a bowed monochord, and subtle tuning forks for resonance.
  • Rest & integration (10–15 minutes) — quiet, with optional journaling prompts.
  • Closing circle — gentle cues to re-orient and share if desired.

What to bring

  • A blanket and pillow for comfort (we provide a limited supply on request).
  • An eye-cover or sleep mask to deepen the experience.
  • Water and any personal props that help you settle.

If you have implanted medical devices, are pregnant, or have a history of seizure, please consult with your healthcare provider and contact us before attending. Our sessions are low-volume but include low-frequency energy; we will always adapt for safety and comfort.

Membership & Access

Members enjoy priority booking, sliding scale events, and an ongoing series of monthly rituals. Memberships are tailored to encourage regular practice and community — ideal for anyone seeking a steady, sensory practice.
