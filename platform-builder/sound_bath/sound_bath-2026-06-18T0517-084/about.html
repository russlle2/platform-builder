Sound Bath for Athletic Recovery and Stress Reduction

Your training and active lifestyle can lead to accumulated tension. Our Sound Bath sessions are designed to help you release that stress. By immersing you in soothing frequencies, we guide your nervous system into a relaxed state, promoting a deeper sense of calm that supports physical recovery without needing intense effort from your side. Think of it as a guided cooldown for your entire being.

What exactly happens during a Sound Bath, and how does it help my active body?

During a Sound Bath, you'll lie comfortably while I play various instruments like singing bowls and gongs. The gentle sound frequencies encourage your brainwaves to slow down, shifting your nervous system from a 'ready for action' state to a 'rest and repair' state. This helps reduce muscle tension, calms your mind, and aids in physical recovery after intense activity.

Do I need to do anything specific or have prior experience to benefit from a Sound Bath?

Absolutely not! There's no experience necessary. You simply come as you are, find a comfortable position, and allow the sounds to wash over you. There’s nothing for you to 'do' other than relax and be open to the experience. It’s designed to be completely accessible and effortless.

How can a Sound Bath specifically assist me with managing physical stress from training or sports?

Consistent physical activity, even beneficial, can accumulate stress in your body and mind. A Sound Bath helps by activating your body's natural relaxation response, which in turn can lower stress hormones, improve circulation, and reduce inflammation. This gentle regulation supports faster recovery, better sleep, and helps prevent burnout, keeping you performing at your best.

What should I bring or wear to a Sound Bath session?

We recommend wearing comfortable, loose clothing that allows you to fully relax. You might also want to bring a water bottle to stay hydrated. We provide mats, blankets, and bolsters, but feel free to bring any personal items that enhance your comfort, like a favorite eye mask or small pillow.
