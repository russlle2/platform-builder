Ritual & tone

We craft a slow arc: intention, soft vocal invocation, a woven landscape of tones, and a gradual return. Each session is held with intention and safety at the center — a premium experience for the senses.

The arc of the ceremony

  1. Arrival & gentle settling (30 min) — soft tea and check-in.
  2. Opening invocation — breath and intention setting.
  3. Sound immersion (40–60 min) — layered bowls, gong, chimes.
  4. Soft transition — silence, grounding, light sharing time.

What to bring

  • Yoga mat, blanket, eye pillow, anything you need to stay warm.
  • Water bottle and light snack for after the session.
  • Wear comfortable layers; shoes off during the soundfield.
Contraindications: If you are pregnant, have severe epilepsy, recent surgery with metallic implants, or have concerns about loud vibrations, please consult with the facilitator before attending. Not a substitute for medical care.

What to expect

Sessions are deeply sensory and non-verbal. You may experience shifts in heartbeat, breath patterns, and emotions — all natural and supported. We ask for quiet arrival and a cell-free environment.
