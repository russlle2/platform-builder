Common questions, gently answered

Short, clear guidance to prepare you for the experience.

What should I bring?

Comfortable clothing, a water bottle, and optionally a light blanket or eye pillow. We provide mats, bolsters, and ear-friendly blankets.

Is it safe if I'm pregnant or have implants?

We recommend consulting your healthcare provider. If you have an implanted device (pacemaker, neurostimulator), please contact us before attending. Pregnant attendees may prefer private sessions tailored to safety and comfort.

Who benefits from a sound bath?

Beginners to long-time practitioners. People seeking stress relief, deep rest, or an embodied listening practice. If you have a history of trauma or seizure, please read contraindications and reach out.

What instruments do you use?

We rotate instruments. Typical textures include gong, tuning forks, and crystal bowls blended with soft chime accents for high shimmer.

How loud are sessions?

Sessions are curated to be enveloping but not overpowering. We control dynamics and monitor the room — let the facilitator know if you need adjustments.

Where should I park and arrive?

Arrive 10–20 minutes early to sign in. Parking details are included in your confirmation. Late arrival may be allowed but you may miss the opening breath and attunement.

Cancellation and refunds

Individual event tickets are refundable up to 72 hours before the event. Private sessions require a 48-hour notice for full refund. See full policy on the booking page.
