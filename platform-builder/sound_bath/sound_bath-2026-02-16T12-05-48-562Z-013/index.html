Ritual frame

We begin with arrival — a few quiet breaths, a guided grounding, and intention-setting. The central sound journey is layered: bowls and chimes open space; a gong and tuned strings deepen resonance; tuning forks offer subtle alignment. The close is slow: gentle reorientation and tea to return softly.

Arrival

Check-in, settle, and a short breath practice to arrive in your body.

Sound Journey

A curated flow of tones — crystal bowls, gong, chimes, and a monochord weave through three phases: release, expand, integrate.

Return

Soft awakening, tea, and space to journal or share quietly.

What to bring & important notes

  • Bring a yoga mat or cushion, a blanket, and an eye mask for warmth and comfort.
  • Wear layers. We provide bolsters and extra blankets upon request.
  • Arrive 15 minutes early to settle in and set an intention.

Contraindications & safety

Sound baths are deeply restorative for most people. Please consult or avoid participation if you are pregnant, have a history of seizures, severe cardiac conditions, active ear pathology, or recent head injury. If you have hearing implants or tinnitus, notify the facilitator in advance.

The flow

Sessions typically run 90 minutes: 10–15 minutes arrival and intention, 60 minutes of sound journey, 10–15 minutes gentle return. Water and herbal tea available afterwards.
