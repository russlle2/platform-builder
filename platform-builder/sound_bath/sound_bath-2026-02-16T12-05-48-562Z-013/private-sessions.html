What to bring
  • Mat or cushion, blanket, eye covering.
  • Medical notes if relevant, openness to communicate limitations.
Contraindications

Private sessions are adapted for many conditions. If you have implants, pacemakers, or recent surgeries, inform the facilitator. Sessions are not a replacement for medical treatment.

Session flow

Each private session weaves assessment with embodied practice. Typical landmarks:

  1. Intake & intention (10–20 min)
  2. Grounding and breathwork (5–15 min)
  3. Sound field (30–60 min depending on length)
  4. Integration & aftercare notes (10 min)
