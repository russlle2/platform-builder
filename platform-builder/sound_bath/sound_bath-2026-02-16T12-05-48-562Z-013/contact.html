Contraindications & safety

Sound immersion is deeply restorative for many, but not suitable for everyone. Please consult a physician before attending if you have any of the following:

  • Recent head injury, aneurysm, or severe cardiovascular conditions.
  • Severe uncontrolled epilepsy or light/sound-triggered conditions.
  • Pregnancy concerns — reach out and we will recommend adjustments.

If you use a pacemaker or other implanted medical device, please tell us prior to booking so we can adapt the session safely.
