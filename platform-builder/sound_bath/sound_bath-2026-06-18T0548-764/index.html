Frequently Asked Questions

How does a Sound Bath specifically help athletes manage physical stress?
A Sound Bath uses resonant vibrations to encourage deep relaxation and release tension held in the body. For athletes, this means helping your muscles and fascia unwind from strenuous activity, promoting recovery and reducing the physical and mental fatigue that often accompanies intense training.
Can a Sound Bath improve my relationship with my athletic performance?
Absolutely. By fostering a state of deep calm and self-awareness, a Sound Bath allows you to connect more profoundly with your body's signals and needs. This heart-centered approach can help you approach your training and competition with greater compassion and mindfulness, strengthening your intuitive understanding of your own athletic capacity.
What should I expect during a Sound Bath session at ?
During your Sound Bath, you'll lie comfortably on a mat while I play various instruments like singing bowls, gongs, and chimes. The sounds create a deeply immersive experience, guiding you into a state of profound relaxation where your mind can quiet, and your body can release accumulated stress and tension.
Is a Sound Bath suitable for athletes recovering from injury?
Yes, many athletes find Sound Baths incredibly beneficial during injury recovery. The gentle vibrations and calming environment can support your body's natural healing processes, reduce stress related to setbacks, and help you maintain a positive, connected relationship with your body as it mends.
