Frequently Asked Questions

How does a Sound Bath address physical pain from burnout?
A Sound Bath uses resonant frequencies that interact with your body's cellular structure, promoting deep relaxation and reducing muscle tension. This helps to alleviate the physical aches, stiffness, and chronic pain often experienced by professionals dealing with burnout, by shifting your nervous system out of a stressed state.
Will a Sound Bath help with chronic headaches or neck pain from computer work?
Many clients report significant relief from tension headaches, neck stiffness, and shoulder pain after Sound Bath sessions. The vibrational sounds help to release deep-seated muscle contractions and improve circulation, which can directly reduce discomfort associated with prolonged desk work and high-stress environments.
What should I expect during my first Sound Bath session?
You'll lie comfortably on a mat, often with blankets and bolsters, while plays various instruments around you. The experience is deeply relaxing, and you might feel vibrations within your body. The goal is to allow your nervous system to settle, providing a profound sense of physical and mental relief without active participation.
Is this approach scientifically supported for stress and pain relief?
Yes, research in psychoneuroimmunology supports the physiological benefits of sound and vibration on the human body. Sound therapy has been shown to reduce cortisol levels, lower heart rate, and decrease blood pressure, all contributing to a reduction in stress-related pain and an overall improvement in somatic well-being.
