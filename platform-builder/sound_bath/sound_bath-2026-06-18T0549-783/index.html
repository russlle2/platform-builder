Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Sound Bath, and is it suitable for someone with no wellness experience?
A Sound Bath is a gentle experience where you relax comfortably while various instruments create soothing sounds. It's absolutely perfect for you if you've never tried any wellness practices before. There's nothing to 'do'—just lie back and let the sounds wash over you, providing a peaceful moment for your heart and mind.
How can a Sound Bath help with relationship healing and heart-centered care for me?
The calming vibrations of a Sound Bath can help quiet your mind and ease any tension, creating a gentle space for reflection. This inner peace often translates to a more open heart, making it easier to connect with others, foster understanding, and nurture the relationships that matter most to you, all without any effort on your part.
What should I wear or bring to my first Sound Bath session?
For your comfort, we suggest wearing loose, comfortable clothing that you can easily relax in. You don't need to bring anything specific, as we provide everything you'll need for a cozy experience. Just bring yourself, ready to enjoy a moment of peace and gentle sounds.
Is the Sound Bath experience physically demanding, or is it gentle enough for seniors?
Our Sound Bath sessions are specifically designed to be incredibly gentle and non-demanding, making them ideal for seniors. You’ll be resting comfortably, typically lying down or seated, for the entire session. There are no physical movements required, allowing you to fully relax and receive the benefits at your own pace and comfort level.
