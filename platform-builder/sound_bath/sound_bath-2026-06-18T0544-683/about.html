How can a Sound Bath specifically help my immune system as I get older?

As we age, managing stress becomes even more crucial for immune health. Our gentle Sound Bath sessions are designed to lower your stress levels, which in turn allows your body to dedicate its resources more effectively to maintaining a strong immune response. It’s a very calm and natural way to offer support.

I'm not very flexible; can I still comfortably participate in a Sound Bath?

Absolutely! Your comfort is our priority. You can choose to sit in a comfortable chair, recline on a mat, or find any position that feels best for your body. There’s no need for specific movements or postures, just an open mind to experience the soothing sounds.

What does 'vitality building' mean in the context of a Sound Bath for seniors?

For you, vitality building through a Sound Bath means gently encouraging a renewed sense of energy and well-being. It’s about fostering deeper, more restful sleep, reducing everyday fatigue, and helping you feel more mentally and physically refreshed to enjoy your life fully, without any strenuous activity required.

Is a Sound Bath safe if I have certain health conditions or hearing aids?

Our Sound Bath sessions are designed to be gentle and safe. If you have specific health concerns, it's always wise to consult your doctor first. For those with hearing aids, you can usually leave them in; the sounds are not excessively loud but are designed to be felt. We can also adjust the sound intensity to ensure your comfort.
