Unwind with Sound: A Heart-Centered Sound Bath for College Students

College life can be a whirlwind of deadlines and social pressures. This Sound Bath offers you a dedicated time to simply be. Through gentle vibrations, we create a space where you can release the tension of academic anxiety and reconnect with your inner calm. It's not about fixing anything, but about offering your heart a moment of quiet care and support, fostering a kinder relationship with your own well-being.
