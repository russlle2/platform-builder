How Sound Bath Supports Your Sleep When You're Burned Out

Your demanding schedule often means your brain struggles to switch off, even when your body is exhausted. Sound Bath sessions create an environment where your nervous system can finally downshift. The subtle, sustained vibrations encourage your brainwaves to slow, moving you from a state of alert activity to profound relaxation, setting the stage for truly restorative sleep later that night. It's not about forcing sleep, but gently inviting your body to remember how to rest deeply.
