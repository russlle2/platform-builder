Sound Bath

Experience Restorative Rest: What Happens in a Sound Bath?

Picture this: you lie comfortably, perhaps with a soft blanket, as I, Dr. Morgan Ellis, play a curated selection of instruments like crystal bowls, gongs, and chimes. These aren't performances; they're carefully chosen tools designed to produce specific frequencies that resonate with your body. You don't need to 'do' anything but listen. Many participants describe feeling a deep sense of calm, a release of tension, and a mental spaciousness that's hard to achieve in daily life. This isn't therapy; it's an accessible way to help your system find peace.
