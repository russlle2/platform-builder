Common myths, calmly corrected

Myth: It’s mystical fluff

Truth: Our sessions are anchored in measurable breath, pacing and acoustic design — instruments tuned to sympathetic frequencies, careful amplitude control, and guided transitions for nervous-system regulation.

Myth: You must meditate perfectly

Truth: No prior practice needed. We provide clear prompts and a structured flow so participants settle into embodied rest without performance pressure.

Four design pillars

Precision

Careful tuning, measured dynamics, and intentional transitions.

Sensory integrity

Lighting, texture, scent and acoustic spacing that support restorative states.

Accessibility

Sessions designed to be welcoming for first-timers and recurring practitioners.

Aftercare

Integrative notes, optional journaling prompts and post-session grounding.

A typical session — annotated

Arrival • Soft check-in, choice of cushioning and eye-covering. Gentle scent choices available.

Settle (10–15 min) • Guided breathwork and progressive relaxation.

Sound arc (30–40 min) • Staged layering: crystal bowls and overtone bowls open, tuning forks and chimes for micro-reset, a bowed monochord for continuous harmonic bed. Peaks are intentional and limited; we favor low-frequency envelopes for somatic release.

Transition • Return to ambient sound, light guided movement and stillness.

Integration (10–15 min) • Quiet reflection, optional notes, and gentle reorientation before leaving.

What to bring & safety

Bring: a yoga mat or blanket, an extra layer, and an eye-cover if you prefer. We provide bolsters and some blankets — if you have a personal cushion or aromatherapy preference, bring it.

Contraindications: If you are pregnant, have a pacemaker, epilepsy, recent surgery, acute psychiatric conditions, or are on heavy medication that causes dizziness, please consult your physician before attending and notify the facilitator. Our offerings are supportive but not a substitute for medical care.

Quick Q&A
