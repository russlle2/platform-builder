Myths & Truths — setting expectations

Myth

Sound baths are only for deep spiritual seekers and require prior practice.

Truth

These sessions are sensory, inclusive, and designed for anyone seeking relaxation, nervous-system regulation, or restorative rest — no prior experience required.

The Four Pillars of Our Offering

A hybrid model: group events with community resonance, and private sessions tailored to clinical and personal needs.

  • Somatic Safety

    Gentle guidance to enter states of calm; we prioritize alignment with your health profile.

  • Curated Sound

    Instruments selected for clarity and depth — crystal bowls, chimes, and a healing monochord for focal bass.

  • Clinical Integration

    Breath prompts and simple grounding tools to help integrate the experience afterwards.

  • Accessible Design

    Comfort-forward spaces, premium mats, and options for chairs or bolsters.

Session Flow & Practical Notes

  1. Arrival & Intake — 10–15 minutes: settle in; brief intake review to honor any contraindications.
  2. Orientation — 5 minutes: posture, breath anchors, and what to expect.
  3. Immersion — 45–60 minutes: layered soundscapes using tuned bowls, gong, tuning forks, and subtle chimes.
  4. Integration — 10 minutes: soft silence, journaling prompts, and reorientation to upright activity.

What to Bring

  • Yoga mat or blanket, pillow or bolster for neck support.
  • Light layers; rooms can be cool during waves of resonance.
  • An open mind and any medications you need during the session.

Contraindications & Safety Notice

If you are pregnant, have severe cardiovascular conditions, have a history of seizures, or are sensitive to loud vibrations, consult your healthcare provider before attending. Sound is powerful — we adapt, but medical clearance may be required for some conditions.

Frequently Asked — Clinical, calm answers

How loud is a sound bath? Will it be overwhelming?

We prioritize controlled dynamics. Sessions feature both low-frequency tones and gentle overtones. You'll be advised on ear safety and positioned for comfort; please notify the facilitator if you prefer lower volume.

Can I fall asleep?

Yes — falling into rest is common and considered restorative. We provide a soft reorientation period at the end to transition you safely back to activity.

Do I need prior meditation experience?

No. Our guidance is non-judgmental and stepwise, suitable for beginners and seasoned practitioners alike.
