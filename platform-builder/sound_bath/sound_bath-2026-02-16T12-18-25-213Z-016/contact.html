Before you arrive

We aim to make each experience calm, accessible, and restorative. Below are quick notes to help you prepare.

What to bring
  • Comfortable layers, a yoga mat or blanket, and a pillow.
  • Eye mask and water bottle to remain hydrated.
  • Optional: small journal to note sensations post-session.
Contraindications (brief)

If you are pregnant, have epilepsy, recent surgery, or are under intensive psychiatric care, please consult your clinician before attending. Sound can be intense; disclose implants, pacemakers, or severe sensitivity to vibrations.

Typical session flow
  1. Welcome & intake: 5–10 minutes to share intention and safety notes.
  2. Guided settling: gentle breath and body awareness for 5–10 minutes.
  3. Main soundscape: 30–45 minutes — instruments such as chimes, tuning forks, and monochord support deep resonance.
  4. Re-entry: soft grounding, water, and optional notes for integration.
