Questions we hear most

Short, calm answers — read before you arrive for the best experience.

How long is a session?
Typical group sessions last 60–80 minutes including guided grounding. Private sessions are scheduled for 60 or 90 minutes, depending on scope.
What should I bring?
Bring a mat or blanket, an extra layer for warmth, and an eye-cover if that helps. We supply bolsters, a limited number of blankets, and eye-covers upon request.
Is it safe if I'm pregnant or have medical conditions?
Please consult your healthcare provider first. Inform the facilitator before the session — certain instruments (e.g., close-proximity gongs) or loud peaks can be adapted or omitted. Contraindications include implanted electronic devices like pacemakers, epilepsy with frequent seizures, and recent surgery.
How loud are the instruments?
We design a controlled dynamic range. Instruments such as crystal bowls, tuning forks, a bowed monochord and gentle gongs are used with careful amplitude envelopes so that even low-frequency content is present without being abrasive.
Can I fall asleep?
Yes — many people do. You will never be judged for sleeping. Our integration period after sound helps you transition if you've drifted off.
Do you offer private 1:1 or corporate sessions?
Yes. We provide private therapeutic 1:1, couples sessions, and corporate sessions focused on wellbeing and reset. Visit the Reservations page to request details.
Cancellation & refunds?
Group bookings can be cancelled up to 48 hours before the event for a full refund. Private sessions have a 72-hour cancellation window. See our booking terms on the reservation page.
Before you arrive
Arrive 10–12 minutes early for check-in. Silence phones, hydrate lightly, and avoid heavy meals within an hour of the session.
If you use hearing aids or ear plugs, bring them. We can adapt sound placement for comfort.
