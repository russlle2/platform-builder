Session flow & what to expect

Arrival — quiet check-in and settling, choose pillows/blankets. Settle — guided breathing and progressive relaxation. Sound phase — curated layering: tuning forks and chimes for micro-resets, crystal and overtone bowls to establish harmonic field, a bowed monochord for continuous bed; occasional soft gong texturing for release. Integration — 10–15 minutes to reorient with gentle prompts and optional journaling.

Bring: mat/blanket, eye-cover, and any personal cushion. Avoid heavy meals immediately before a session.

Safety & contraindications

If you have a pacemaker, epilepsy with frequent seizures, are in the first trimester of pregnancy (please consult), or have recent surgery, please consult medical advice and notify us prior to booking. Loud peaks can be modified on request.

For private corporate bookings please include estimated attendees and room size in the notes. We can provide onsite setup and short educational framing.
