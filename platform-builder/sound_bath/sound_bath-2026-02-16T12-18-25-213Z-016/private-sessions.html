What private work actually does

Clinical clarity

Sessions are paced with physiological markers in mind — breath, heart-rate cues, and somatic anchoring to avoid overstimulation.

Curated sensory field

Sound is mixed intentionally; intensity and frequency content are matched to the goal: clarity, grounding, or expanded attention.

Core pillars

Confidentiality
Sessions are private and held with professional presence.
Customization
Tools and tempo are adapted to client needs.
Integration
Homework and somatic practices as desired.

Typical private session flow

  1. Intake & intention setting (5–10 min)
  2. Settling breath and attunement (5–10 min)
  3. Designed sound sequence (30–60 min)
  4. Slow reorientation, integration notes (10 min)

Bring / beforehand

Comfortable clothing, any supportive cushions, and an openness to guided breathwork. Notify us of implants, pregnancy, or anxiety related to loud sound.

Contraindications

Private work may be contraindicated for untreated severe psychiatric conditions, certain implants, or recent head trauma. Contact {{EMAIL}} to discuss suitability.
