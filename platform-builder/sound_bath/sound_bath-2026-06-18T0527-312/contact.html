How Sound Bath Supports Your Postpartum Body's Healing

Your body has undergone a profound transformation. Our Sound Bath sessions offer a dedicated space to address the physical aches, tension, and fatigue common in postpartum recovery. This isn't just about feeling better mentally; it's about providing your physical self with the conditions it needs to recover.
