Sound Bath

Your Calm Space for Somatic Release and Recovery in a Sound Bath

We understand the demands of new motherhood. Our Sound Bath environment is meticulously crafted to be a haven for your recovery. Lying comfortably, you'll be enveloped in carefully curated sounds from instruments like crystal bowls and gongs. This immersive experience helps shift your focus from physical discomfort and mental overwhelm, allowing your body to initiate its own healing responses. It's a non-invasive, gentle method to re-establish a sense of physical ease and mental tranquility as you navigate postpartum.
