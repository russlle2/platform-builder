Frequently Asked Questions

How does a Sound Bath specifically help with postpartum physical pain?
Sound Bath vibrations can penetrate deep into your tissues, promoting muscle relaxation and reducing inflammation, which is beneficial for postpartum aches. The deep relaxation achieved also lowers stress hormones, aiding your body's natural pain management and recovery processes.
Is a Sound Bath safe for my body during early postpartum recovery?
Yes, our Sound Bath sessions are designed with postpartum sensitivity in mind. The sounds are never overwhelming, and you'll be in a comfortable, supported position. We recommend consulting your healthcare provider, especially if you have specific medical concerns, but generally, it's a very gentle and safe practice.
Will I need to do anything during the Sound Bath session?
No, your only task is to relax and allow the sounds to wash over you. You'll lie comfortably, often covered with a blanket, and simply receive the experience. There’s no physical exertion required, making it ideal for your recovery period.
How often should I attend a Sound Bath for best results in my postpartum recovery?
The frequency can vary based on your individual needs and how your body responds. Many new mothers find weekly or bi-weekly sessions most beneficial for consistent pain relief and somatic support. We can discuss a personalized recommendation during your initial consultation.
