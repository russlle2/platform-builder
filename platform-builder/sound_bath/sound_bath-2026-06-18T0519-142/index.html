Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Sound Bath and how can it help with my postpartum sleep?
A Sound Bath is a deeply immersive, full-body listening experience using sound to invite gentle yet powerful therapeutic and restorative processes to nurture your mind and body. For new mothers, it helps to calm the nervous system, reduce stress hormones, and create a state of deep relaxation conducive to improved sleep quality and duration during your recovery.
Is a Sound Bath safe and comfortable for someone in postpartum recovery?
Absolutely. Our Sound Bath sessions are designed with your comfort and safety in mind. You'll be invited to lie down in a comfortable position, and the sounds are always played gently. We encourage you to communicate any specific needs or concerns you might have, ensuring a supportive and nurturing environment for your recovery.
What should I expect during my first Sound Bath session for rest?
You can expect to lie or sit comfortably, fully clothed, while our practitioner plays various instruments around you. The sounds are soothing and continuous, guiding you into a state of deep relaxation. Many new mothers report feeling profoundly rested and calm afterwards, often experiencing better sleep in the days following a session.
How often should a new mother attend Sound Bath sessions to improve sleep?
The frequency that works best for you will depend on your individual needs and recovery pace. Many new mothers find that attending weekly or bi-weekly sessions provides consistent support for their sleep and overall well-being. We recommend starting with a session and seeing how your body responds to determine a rhythm that feels right for you.
