Finding Your Anchor: Sound Bath for Postpartum Trauma

The early days of motherhood can unearth unexpected emotions and past experiences. Our Sound Bath practice is specifically designed to create a safe harbor for your heart. We use carefully selected frequencies to help your nervous system gently recalibrate, inviting a deep sense of peace that supports your emotional integration after birth.

Is a Sound Bath safe for new mothers, especially if I'm feeling emotionally fragile?

Absolutely. Our Sound Bath practice is trauma-informed and incredibly gentle. We prioritize creating a safe, supportive space where you feel completely in control. The sounds are never jarring, and you're encouraged to communicate any needs or discomforts at any time. It's designed to be deeply nurturing for your sensitive postpartum state.

How can Sound Bath specifically help with the emotional challenges of postpartum recovery?

Sound Bath works on a deep energetic level to help release stored emotional tension and promote nervous system regulation. For new mothers, this means it can help alleviate feelings of anxiety, overwhelm, and even sadness, supporting your emotional integration and helping you reconnect with your inner strength during this profound life transition.

Do I need to do anything special or have prior experience with Sound Bath?

Not at all. Your only requirement is to arrive and allow yourself to receive. You don't need any prior experience or special preparation. Just wear comfortable clothing, and we'll provide everything you need for a comfortable and restorative experience. It's a time for you to simply be.

Can I bring my baby to a Sound Bath session?

Our group Sound Bath sessions are designed for you to have uninterrupted time for yourself, which is crucial for your emotional recovery. We recommend arranging childcare if possible. However, we also offer private sessions where we can discuss accommodations if bringing your baby is essential. Please contact us to explore options.
