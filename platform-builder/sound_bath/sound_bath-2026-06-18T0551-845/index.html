Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is a Sound Bath and how will it help my posture?
A Sound Bath involves lying down comfortably while a practitioner plays various instruments like singing bowls and gongs. The deep vibrations and resonant tones help your body enter a state of deep relaxation, which can release muscle tension that contributes to poor posture, like rounded shoulders from studying. It helps you become more aware of how you hold your body.
I'm always stressed about exams. Can a Sound Bath really help with my anxiety and focus?
Absolutely. The calming effect of a Sound Bath can significantly reduce feelings of anxiety by slowing your brainwaves and nervous system. When your mind is calmer, it's easier to focus and retain information. Many students find the sessions help quiet the mental chatter, making it easier to approach academic tasks with a clear head.
I've never done anything like this before. Do I need any special experience or equipment for a Sound Bath?
Not at all! Our Sound Bath sessions are designed for everyone, especially those new to body awareness practices. You don't need any prior experience. Just come as you are, dressed in comfortable clothes. We provide everything you need for a comfortable and supportive experience.
How often should I attend a Sound Bath to see benefits for my posture and stress levels?
The frequency depends on your individual needs and how much tension you're carrying. Many students find regular sessions, perhaps once every week or two, provide consistent relief from academic stress and help maintain better postural habits. Even a single session can offer immediate relaxation and a renewed sense of body awareness.
