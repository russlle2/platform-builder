Finding Your Center with Postpartum Sound Bath

The early days of motherhood are a period of immense change, both outwardly and within. Our Sound Bath practice is designed to gently guide you back to your own spiritual core, helping you quiet the external demands and listen to your inner wisdom. This practice is a deliberate act of self-care, allowing you to honor your body and spirit as you navigate this powerful transition.
