Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is a Sound Bath and how can it help me as a new mother?
A Sound Bath is a deeply immersive, full-body listening experience where you are 'bathed' in sound waves produced by various instruments. For new mothers, it offers a dedicated time for deep rest, helping to soothe postpartum fatigue, reduce stress, and create a calm mental space, supporting your spiritual and emotional well-being during recovery.
Is the Sound Bath safe for my postpartum body and recovery?
Absolutely. Our Sound Bath sessions are gentle and designed to be deeply relaxing. You will lie comfortably, fully clothed, and simply receive the sounds. There is no physical exertion required. We encourage you to listen to your body and make any adjustments to your comfort as needed, ensuring a serene experience that supports your recovery.
How can a Sound Bath support my spiritual growth and inner peace postpartum?
The resonant vibrations in a Sound Bath can quiet the busy mind, allowing you to connect with a deeper sense of self. This practice fosters introspection and mindfulness, which are crucial for spiritual growth. By creating a space for stillness, you can find greater clarity, emotional balance, and a profound sense of inner peace during this transformative period of motherhood.
What should I bring or wear to a Sound Bath session?
For your utmost comfort during a Sound Bath, we recommend wearing loose, comfortable clothing. You might also want to bring a water bottle, an eye mask, or your favorite blanket to enhance your relaxation. Our goal is for you to feel completely at ease and supported as you immerse yourself in the sounds.
