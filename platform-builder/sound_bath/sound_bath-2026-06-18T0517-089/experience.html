Sound Bath

No Experience Needed: Just Show Up and Relax

We understand that adding another 'thing to learn' can feel overwhelming. That's why our Sound Bath sessions are designed to be completely accessible. You don't need to meditate, chant, or even know what a singing bowl is. Just arrive as you are, find a comfortable spot, and allow the sounds to do the work. It's a zero-pressure way to give back to yourself, making stress reduction easy for even the busiest parents.
