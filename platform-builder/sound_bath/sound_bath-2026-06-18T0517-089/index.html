Frequently Asked Questions

What should I expect during my first Sound Bath session?
You can expect a very relaxed and gentle experience. You'll lie down comfortably, often on a mat with blankets and pillows, while plays various instruments like singing bowls and gongs. Your only job is to listen and let the sounds guide you into a state of deep relaxation. Many parents describe it as a much-needed 'mini-vacation' for their mind and body.
I'm always on the go with my kids; will I be able to relax during a Sound Bath?
Absolutely. We specifically design our Sound Bath sessions to help active minds unwind. The sounds naturally encourage your brain to slow down, even if you arrive feeling very scattered. There's no pressure to achieve a specific state; simply being present in the soundscape is enough to help your nervous system find a calmer rhythm. It’s a wonderful way to switch off autopilot for a bit.
Do I need to bring anything specific to a Sound Bath?
Not at all! We provide everything you'll need for comfort, including mats, blankets, and pillows. Just wear comfortable clothing, as if you were getting ready to relax at home. If you have a favorite eye mask or a special comfort item, you're welcome to bring it, but it's certainly not required. We want to make it as easy as possible for you to show up and relax.
How can Sound Bath help me manage the daily stress of parenting?
Sound Bath sessions offer a dedicated time for your nervous system to downregulate. By gently shifting your brainwaves and encouraging deep rest, these sessions can reduce the chronic 'fight or flight' response often experienced by busy parents. This leads to feeling less overwhelmed, more centered, and better equipped to handle the everyday demands, helping you respond to situations rather than react.
