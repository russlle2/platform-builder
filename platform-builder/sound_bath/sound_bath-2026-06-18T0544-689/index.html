Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is a Sound Bath and how can it help my immune system as a parent?
A Sound Bath is a deeply relaxing experience where you lie comfortably while I play various instruments like singing bowls and gongs. The gentle sounds create vibrations that help quiet your mind and relax your body. For parents, this deep relaxation helps lower stress, which in turn supports your body's natural immune functions, helping you feel more resilient against illness and everyday fatigue.
I'm a busy parent with no time for complicated wellness routines. Is a Sound Bath right for me?
Absolutely! A Sound Bath is perfect for busy parents because it requires nothing from you except to show up and lie down. There's no effort, no learning, just pure relaxation. It's a dedicated time for you to recharge, offering a simple yet powerful way to support your energy and vitality without adding another task to your already full plate.
Will I be able to fully relax during a Sound Bath if my mind is constantly thinking about my kids or to-do lists?
It's completely normal for a parent's mind to be busy! The beauty of a Sound Bath is that the sounds themselves provide a gentle focal point, naturally drawing your attention away from your thoughts. Many parents find that even if their mind wanders at first, the consistent, soothing vibrations eventually help them settle into a profound state of calm, even with a busy mind.
What should I bring or wear to a Sound Bath session?
For your comfort, we recommend wearing something soft and cozy that you can relax in, like comfortable casual clothes. You don't need to bring anything special, as mats, blankets, and pillows are provided. Just come as you are, ready to simply rest and receive the soothing sounds.
