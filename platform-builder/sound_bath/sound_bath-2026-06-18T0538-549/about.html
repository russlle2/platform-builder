What exactly happens during a Sound Bath session for parents?

During a Sound Bath, you'll lie down comfortably while I play various instruments that produce soothing, resonant sounds. There's nothing for you to do but relax and allow the vibrations to wash over you, helping to calm your mind and body. It's a time for quiet introspection, designed to help parents find a moment of peace.

How can a Sound Bath help me specifically with the stresses of parenting?

The consistent demands of parenting can leave you feeling depleted. A Sound Bath offers a unique opportunity to deeply relax and quiet the mental noise often associated with household stress. This intentional downtime helps restore your emotional and spiritual reserves, allowing you to approach your family responsibilities with renewed patience and a clearer mind.

Do I need any prior experience with meditation or sound healing to attend a Sound Bath?

Absolutely not! Our Sound Bath sessions are welcoming to everyone, regardless of your experience level. There's no right or wrong way to experience the sounds. Just come as you are, find a comfortable spot, and allow yourself to receive the benefits. We are an inclusive community, ready to support you.

Can I bring my young children to a Sound Bath session?

Our Sound Bath sessions are specifically designed as an uninterrupted time for parents to focus on their own spiritual growth and inner peace. To best serve this purpose, we encourage you to arrange childcare so you can fully immerse yourself without distraction. We believe in dedicated self-care for parents.
