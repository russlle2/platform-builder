What can I expect during my first Sound Bath session for sleep improvement?

You can expect a calm, private environment where your comfort is prioritized. After a brief chat about your sleep goals, you'll recline comfortably as Dr. Morgan Ellis plays a curated selection of instruments. The experience is deeply relaxing, designed to gently guide your body and mind towards a state of profound rest, which often translates into better sleep at home.

How does a Sound Bath specifically help with improving sleep for seniors?

Our Sound Bath sessions employ specific frequencies and vibrations that are known to calm the nervous system and slow brainwave activity. For seniors, this can be particularly beneficial in reducing nighttime awakenings and making it easier to fall asleep, fostering a more consistent and restorative sleep cycle without the need for medication.

Is the Sound Bath experience physically demanding or uncomfortable?

Absolutely not. The Sound Bath is an incredibly gentle experience. You simply lie comfortably, either on a padded mat or in a recliner, fully clothed. There's no physical exertion required, just passive receptivity to the soothing sounds. Your comfort and relaxation are our primary focus throughout the entire session.

How many Sound Bath sessions are recommended to see improvements in sleep?

Many clients report feeling more relaxed and sleeping better after just one Sound Bath session. However, for sustained improvements in sleep quality and to establish new patterns of restorative rest, we often recommend a series of sessions. Dr. Morgan Ellis can discuss a personalized plan with you during your initial consultation.
