How can a Sound Bath specifically help with chronic pain or injury recovery?

A Sound Bath works by encouraging your nervous system to shift into a more relaxed state. When your body is deeply relaxed, it can reduce the perception of pain and support your body's natural restorative processes, which is crucial when you're recovering from chronic illness or injury. The vibrations can also help release muscle tension and promote a sense of overall physical ease.

Do I need to do anything during a Sound Bath session?

Absolutely not. Your only job is to get comfortable, typically lying down, and allow the sounds to envelop you. There's no movement or active participation required. We encourage you to simply relax and let the therapeutic vibrations do their work, supporting your body's process of somatic healing and pain relief.

Is a Sound Bath safe if I have a specific injury or sensitivity?

Yes, Sound Baths are generally very gentle and safe. We always recommend you share any specific injuries, conditions, or sensitivities with Dr. Morgan Ellis before your session. This allows us to ensure you're positioned comfortably and to adjust the intensity or proximity of instruments if needed, ensuring a supportive and beneficial experience tailored to your unique needs.

What should I expect to feel after a Sound Bath for my recovery?

Many people report feeling a profound sense of calm and relaxation after a Sound Bath. You might notice a reduction in muscle tension, a softening of persistent aches, or simply a clearer, more peaceful mind. These sessions are designed to gently reset your system, supporting your body's return to a state of ease and contributing to your ongoing recovery from chronic pain or injury.
