Your Moment of Quiet: What to Expect in a Sound Bath Session

Just show up as you are – no prior experience or special equipment needed. You'll lie comfortably, wrapped in a blanket, as the sounds from gongs, singing bowls, and other instruments wash over you. There's nothing for you to do but listen and allow. Many students report feeling a sense of release, like a heavy weight has been lifted, and a noticeable reduction in physical discomfort. It's your time to just be, away from the constant demands of college life.
