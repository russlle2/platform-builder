Questions we hear often

Practical answers and a few suggestions to prepare for a sound gathering with us.

Reserve a spot
What should I bring?

Comfortable clothing, a cushion or yoga mat, a light blanket, and water. If you have a preferred pillow or neck support bring it along.

How long are the sessions?

Typical gatherings are 45–60 minutes. We also offer 30-minute options for those who prefer a shorter arrival.

Are sessions suitable for beginners?

Yes. We welcome anyone. The environment is non-judgmental and the invitation is to notice how sound interacts with your attention.

Contraindications and safety

If you are pregnant, have a diagnosis of epilepsy, are recovering from a recent head injury, or have inner-ear conditions, please consult your physician before attending. Our sessions are not a substitute for medical care.
