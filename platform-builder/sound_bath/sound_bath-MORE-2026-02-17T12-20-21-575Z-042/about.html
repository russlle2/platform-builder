Who we are and how we work with sound

We host slow, warm gatherings that use tuned instruments and gentle rhythm to create a soft container: attentive, relaxed, and open to small changes. Our practice is about shaping space for rest and clarity, not performance.

Our approach — a brief process

  1. Arrival & settling: Quiet check-in, choice of cushion or chair, 5–10 minutes of guided grounding.
  2. Sound arc: Instruments layered in a slow build and gentle resolution.
  3. Soft close: A brief recalibration and space for water and questions.
