Sound Bath

Understanding Your Body Anew Through Sound

Chronic illness or injury can disconnect you from your body's subtle cues. Our Sound Bath sessions create a supportive environment to re-establish that vital connection. We focus on gentle auditory experiences that encourage your nervous system to relax, allowing you to notice and release tension patterns that impact your posture. This mindful awareness is the first step toward rebuilding strength and balance, helping you feel more grounded and aligned in your body once again.
