Frequently Asked Questions

Is a Sound Bath suitable for someone my age, especially if I have mobility concerns?
Absolutely. Our Sound Baths are designed to be entirely gentle and accessible. You'll be invited to lie down or sit in whatever position is most comfortable for you, often on a yoga mat with cushions and blankets provided. There’s no movement required from you; simply relax and receive the sounds. Your comfort is our highest priority.
What should I wear or bring to my first Sound Bath session?
We recommend wearing comfortable, loose-fitting clothing that allows you to fully relax. You might also want to bring a pair of warm socks or a light sweater, as your body temperature can drop during deep relaxation. We provide mats, blankets, and bolsters, but feel free to bring anything else that enhances your personal comfort, like a favorite eye mask.
I'm not familiar with meditation. Do I need to 'do' anything specific during the Sound Bath?
Not at all. There’s no specific meditation technique you need to practice. The beauty of a Sound Bath is that the sounds themselves do the work of guiding you into a relaxed state. Your only task is to listen and allow the vibrations to wash over you. If your mind wanders, gently bring your attention back to the sounds without judgment.
How can a Sound Bath specifically help with stress or anxiety that I experience?
The deep, resonant tones and vibrations from the instruments are known to help shift your brainwaves into a more relaxed state, similar to what happens during deep meditation or sleep. This can gently calm your sympathetic nervous system, which is responsible for the 'fight or flight' response, and activate your parasympathetic nervous system, promoting rest and digestion. Many find this deeply soothing for chronic stress and anxiety.
