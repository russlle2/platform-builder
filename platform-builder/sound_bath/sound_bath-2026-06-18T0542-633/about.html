How can Sound Bath specifically benefit my immune system as a senior?

Our Sound Bath sessions are designed to gently quiet your nervous system, moving you from a state of 'fight or flight' to 'rest and digest.' This shift is crucial because a calm nervous system is better equipped to support your immune function and maintain your body's natural defenses, helping you stay healthier.

Is Sound Bath a safe and appropriate practice for someone my age?

Absolutely. Sound Bath is a remarkably gentle practice. You simply lie comfortably or sit, and allow the sounds to wash over you. There's no physical exertion required, making it ideal for seniors who prefer a low-impact yet highly effective approach to boosting their well-being and vitality.

What should I expect during my first Sound Bath session for vitality building?

During your first session, you can expect to be in a comfortable, peaceful environment. You'll relax as Dr. Morgan Ellis guides you through a soundscape created with various instruments. Many seniors report feeling deeply relaxed, experiencing reduced mental chatter, and a renewed sense of peaceful energy, contributing to overall vitality.

How often should I attend Sound Bath sessions to notice benefits for my immune health?

Like any supportive practice, consistency is key. Many seniors find that attending regular Sound Bath sessions, perhaps once or twice a month, helps maintain the benefits for immune support and sustained vitality. We encourage you to listen to your body and see what rhythm works best for you.
