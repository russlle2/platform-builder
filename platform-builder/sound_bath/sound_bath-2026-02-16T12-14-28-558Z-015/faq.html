Frequently asked

Short answers for a thoughtful arrival. If your question is personal, email us at {{EMAIL}}.

What should I bring?

Bring a yoga mat or a thick floor cushion, a blanket, and an eye pillow if you have one. Dress in comfortable layers—the environment is kept warm but you may feel cool during stillness.

What is the session flow?

We begin with a brief orientation and centering breathwork, then move into a 30–45 minute sound immersion, concluding with a guided re-entry and optional reflection. Arrival 10–15 minutes early is appreciated.

Are there contraindications?

Sound baths are generally safe, but please avoid attending if you have severe uncontrolled epilepsy, recent head injury, or if you are currently pregnant without medical clearance. If you have implants or medical devices sensitive to vibration or metal (e.g., cochlear implants), contact us before booking. This information is not a substitute for medical advice—consult your provider.

Will I fall asleep?

Many guests drift into deep rest—that’s normal. The experience is non-religious and you are always free to rest or participate as you wish. After the session we allow time for gentle reintegration.

What instruments are used?

Instruments vary by facilitator and event. Common tools include gongs, tuning forks, and hand chimes. Each event lists the specific instrumentation.

Arrival tips
Arrive hydrated, limit caffeine before a session, and silence phones. We provide extra blankets and bolsters when available.
Safety & comfort
If loud low frequencies bother you, mention this when booking. We can tailor intensity for sensitivity.
