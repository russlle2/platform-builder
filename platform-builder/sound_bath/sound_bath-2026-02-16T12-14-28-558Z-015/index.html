Where are you right now?

A quick sensory check to help you choose the right experience. This intensive model is for those ready to slow, rest, and drop into a concentrated container.

  • Physical: Stiff or tired? Our sessions support deep tissue relaxation.
  • Mental: Overstimulated or scattered? Intention and binaural-friendly frequencies invite focus.
  • Emotional: Want gentle release? The ritual provides somatic safety and containment.

Session flow — what to expect

Our intensive sessions follow a clear, slow arc: arrival and settling, guided breath + sound induction, deep immersive soundscape, and a gentle re-entry. Each moment is timed to support safe nervous system downshifting.

  1. Arrival & grounding: Welcome, orientation, and a short check-in.
  2. Breathwork & intention: Simple breath to attune to the space.
  3. Immersion: 30–45 minutes of layered sound (you rest, we hold the field).
  4. Integration: Slow return, journaling prompts, tea optional.

Instruments vary by session. Expect a curated blend such as crystal bowls, gong, chimes, and tuning forks — each gathering chooses a different palette for texture and depth.

What to bring

  • Yoga mat, blanket, and a pillow for extra comfort.
  • Water bottle and an eye mask if you use one.
  • Wear layers — we keep the room cozy but you may cool down.

Contraindications

If you are pregnant, have a pacemaker, epilepsy, or uncontrolled hypertension, please consult your medical provider before attending and notify the facilitator. Sound can be deep and intensive; safety first.
