Sensory details
Our instruments change tone and texture depending on the intention: crystal singing bowls for clarity, bowed monochord for sustained warmth, chimes and light bells to create silver threads of attention. We calibrate volume and frequency to body comfort — soft to the bones, not the ears.
What to bring

  • Blanket, pillow, and eye covering
  • Gentle earplugs available on request
