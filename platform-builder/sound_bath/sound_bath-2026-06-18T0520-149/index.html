Frequently Asked Questions

I'm a busy parent; how will a Sound Bath help me specifically with sleep and stress?
Our Sound Bath sessions are designed to calm your overactive mind and nervous system, which are often taxed by parenting responsibilities. This deep relaxation makes it easier for you to fall asleep, stay asleep, and experience more restorative rest, helping you wake up feeling less stressed and more refreshed to tackle your day.
What if I can't 'turn off my brain' during a Sound Bath? I'm always thinking about my kids and household tasks.
That's completely normal for parents! The beauty of a Sound Bath is that the resonant frequencies provide a focal point, gently guiding your mind away from daily worries. You don't need to 'try' to relax; just allow the sounds to wash over you. Many parents find that even if thoughts come and go, the overall experience leads to a profound sense of calm and mental clarity.
What should I expect during my first Sound Bath session?
You'll lie down comfortably, often with blankets and pillows, in a quiet, peaceful space. will play various instruments like singing bowls and gongs, creating a rich soundscape. Your only job is to relax and let the sounds envelop you. It’s a passive experience aimed at deep relaxation, helping you unwind from the demands of parenting and find internal peace.
Will a Sound Bath really make a difference in my energy levels and patience with my children?
Many parents report significant improvements in their overall well-being. By fostering deeper rest and reducing chronic stress, Sound Bath sessions can lead to increased energy, better emotional regulation, and a greater sense of patience. When you're well-rested, you're better equipped to handle the ups and downs of family life with more grace and presence.
