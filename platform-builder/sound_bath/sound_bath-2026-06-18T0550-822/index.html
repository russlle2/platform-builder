Frequently Asked Questions

How does a Sound Bath specifically help with postpartum postural alignment?
During a Sound Bath, the deep vibrations encourage a profound state of relaxation. This allows your muscles to release tension, particularly in areas often affected by pregnancy and infant care like your shoulders, back, and hips. This release creates space for your body to naturally re-align and for you to become more aware of how you're holding yourself, facilitating a more conscious and supportive posture.
Is a Sound Bath suitable for my body during postpartum recovery?
Yes, our Sound Bath sessions are designed to be gentle and supportive, making them ideal for postpartum recovery. You'll lie comfortably, and the experience is non-invasive. The focus is on relaxation and internal awareness, which can greatly assist in easing physical discomfort and promoting a sense of calm as your body heals and adapts to new demands.
Will I need to do specific movements or exercises during the Sound Bath for my posture?
No, there are no specific movements or exercises required during our Sound Bath. Your only task is to relax and allow the sounds to wash over you. The postural benefits come from the deep relaxation and increased body awareness that the sound frequencies induce, helping your body to naturally find a more balanced state without active effort on your part.
How can a Sound Bath improve my body awareness as a new mother?
The resonant frequencies in a Sound Bath create an internal landscape for introspection. As you relax deeply, you become more attuned to subtle sensations within your body – where you might be holding tension, or where your posture could be better supported. This heightened sensitivity helps you develop a stronger connection to your physical self, empowering you to make conscious adjustments throughout your day.
