Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly happens during a Sound Bath, and how does it help my mind?
During a Sound Bath, you'll comfortably rest while I play various instruments like singing bowls and gongs. The gentle sounds and vibrations are designed to guide your mind into a relaxed, meditative state, which can help reduce mental clutter and improve your ability to focus and think clearly.
Do I need any special experience or preparation for a Sound Bath to benefit my cognitive health?
Absolutely not. You don't need any prior experience with meditation or sound practices. Just arrive comfortably dressed, and we’ll guide you through the rest. The benefits for your cognitive well-being are accessible to everyone, regardless of their background.
How often should I attend a Sound Bath to see improvements in my mental clarity?
Many individuals find that regular sessions, perhaps once every week or two, provide the most consistent support for mental clarity and focus. However, even a single session can offer a noticeable sense of calm and mental refreshment. We can discuss a schedule that suits your needs best.
Is a Sound Bath suitable for seniors with specific cognitive concerns or health conditions?
Our Sound Baths are gentle and non-invasive. While generally very safe, if you have specific cognitive concerns or health conditions, it’s always wise to consult with your healthcare provider before trying new practices. We are happy to discuss any concerns you might have.
