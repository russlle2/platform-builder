Finding Your Core in the Current: Sound Bath for Postural Reconnection

Daily demands often pull your attention outwards, leaving your body feeling stiff or misaligned. Our Sound Bath offers a dedicated space to gently bring that focus inward. The sustained vibrations encourage a release of tension held deep within muscles, particularly those supporting your spine and shoulders. This isn't about forced correction; it's about inviting your body to remember its natural, upright state, reducing the common aches associated with prolonged sitting and mental strain.
