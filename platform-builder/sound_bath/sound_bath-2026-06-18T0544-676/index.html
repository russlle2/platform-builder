Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Sound Bath specifically assist my recovery from chronic illness or injury?
A Sound Bath helps by promoting a deep state of relaxation, which directly reduces stress hormones that can suppress your immune system. This allows your body to reallocate energy towards repair and rebuilding, fostering a stronger foundation for your recovery and helping to restore your natural vitality.
Will the Sound Bath be too overwhelming if I'm feeling sensitive or low on energy?
Our Sound Bath sessions are designed with sensitivity in mind, especially for those recovering. The sounds are introduced gently and are never jarring. You are always in control of your experience, and we encourage you to listen to your body, making it a truly restorative and comfortable environment for you to regain strength.
What should I expect during my first Sound Bath session for immune support?
You'll lie comfortably, often on a mat with blankets and pillows, while I play various instruments. Your only task is to relax and allow the sounds to wash over you. Many clients report feeling a sense of deep calm, reduced physical tension, and a clearer mind, all contributing to an improved sense of well-being and immune resilience.
How frequently should I attend Sound Bath sessions to support my vitality building?
The ideal frequency often depends on your individual recovery phase and how your body responds. Many clients find regular weekly or bi-weekly sessions most beneficial for consistent immune support and vitality building. We can discuss a personalized plan during your consultation to best suit your needs.
