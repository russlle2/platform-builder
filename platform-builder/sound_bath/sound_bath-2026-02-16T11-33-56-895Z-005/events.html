Why gather here

A ritual of sound that meets the body where it is — sensory, premium, softened by intention.

  • Immersive resonance — layered bowls and chimes wash the room in harmonic overtones.
  • Grounded facilitation — guided breath and gentle cues from {{FACILITATOR_NAME}}.
  • Intentional space — dim lights, bolsters, and a brief integration to leave refreshed.

What to bring

A yoga mat or bolster, cozy blanket, water, and an open belly. Small pillow recommended for neck support.

Instruments tonight

Crystal singing bowls, Himalayan tingsha, handpan textures.

Contraindications

If you are pregnant, have epilepsy, recent surgeries, or severe cardiovascular conditions, consult a clinician before attending. Sound therapies are gentle but vibrational — reach out at {{EMAIL}} for a pre-session check.
