The flow — a gentle arc

  1. Arrive & root

    We open doors 20 minutes early. Lay down your mat, receive tea, and set a private intention.

  2. Ground & breathe

    Short guided breathwork to ease the body into receptivity.

  3. Sound journey

    A curated sound narrative using crystal bowls, chimes, and tuning forks — moving from soft to immersive, then back to quiet.

  4. Return & share

    We close with a moment of silence, gentle tea, and optional sharing.
