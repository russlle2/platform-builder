Frequently asked

How long is a session?
Group gatherings run ~75–90 minutes including check-in and post-sound integration.
Is it noisy?
It is immersive rather than loud. Instruments are tuned to create a field you can rest into. If you have sound sensitivity, tell us on the form and we will adapt.
Can I record?
We ask that sessions remain device-free to protect the container. We sometimes record short excerpts for promotional use with explicit consent.
Do you offer private sessions?
Yes — 1:1, couples, and corporate bookings are available. See our private sessions page or mention it in the form.
