Questions that often arrive

How long is a group sound bath?
Most sessions run 60–75 minutes. We begin with arrival and gentle grounding, proceed through a layered sound set and end with a soft re-entry.
What should I bring?
Bring a yoga mat or thick blanket, a pillow/bolster, warm layers and water. We provide extra blankets and mats if needed.
Are there any contraindications?
Yes. Please avoid attending without medical advice if you are pregnant, have epilepsy, severe cardiac conditions, recent concussion, or active psychosis. For implanted devices (e.g., pacemakers), contact us first. For guidance call {{PHONE}}.
Can I move or fall asleep?
Yes. Many people rest or drift between states. Movement is fine; the facilitation is non-invasive and supportive.
How do I book?
Reserve via the booking page or call. For group passes and series pricing see pricing page. Our next event: {{NEXT_EVENT_DATE}}.
Cancellation & refunds?
Cancellations more than 72 hours before the session receive a refund. Within 72 hours we may offer credit toward another event.
Are sessions accessible?
Yes. We welcome accessibility needs—step-free entry, supportive seating on request. Mention needs when booking.
