Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Sound Bath specifically help with chronic illness or injury recovery?
A Sound Bath is particularly beneficial because it directly addresses nervous system dysregulation, a common challenge in chronic conditions. The gentle frequencies encourage your body to move into a deep state of relaxation, reducing stress hormones and supporting your body's natural capacity for repair and restoration, without requiring physical effort from you.
Will I need to do anything during the Sound Bath?
Absolutely not. Your only 'job' during a Sound Bath is to get comfortable and allow yourself to be present. You can lie down, close your eyes, and simply receive the sounds. There's no movement or active participation required, making it ideal for those with limited mobility or energy due to chronic illness or injury.
Is a Sound Bath suitable if I'm sensitive to sounds?
We understand that sensitivity can be heightened during recovery. Our Sound Baths are designed to be gentle and non-overwhelming. We use instruments that create sustained, harmonious tones rather than sharp or jarring sounds. If you have specific concerns, please let us know beforehand so we can ensure your comfort and tailor the experience.
What should I expect to feel after a Sound Bath session?
Many people report feeling a profound sense of calm, mental clarity, and physical ease after a Sound Bath. You might notice reduced muscle tension, a quieter mind, and a more regulated emotional state. These effects can contribute to better sleep and an overall improved sense of well-being, which are vital components of recovery.
