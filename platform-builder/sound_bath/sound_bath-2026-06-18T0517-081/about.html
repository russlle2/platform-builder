I've never done anything like a Sound Bath before. Will I know what to do?

Absolutely. You don't need any prior experience or special knowledge for a Sound Bath. Your only task is to lie down, get comfortable, and simply listen. We'll guide you through what to expect, and there's no right or wrong way to experience the sounds. It's designed to be completely accessible and gentle, especially if you're new to these practices.

How can a Sound Bath specifically help with my work-related burnout?

Burnout often stems from chronic stress and an overactive nervous system. A Sound Bath helps by creating an environment where your body can naturally shift into a deeply relaxed state. This process actively calms your nervous system, reduces the physical and mental tension associated with work stress, and helps you detach from the constant demands that contribute to burnout, giving you a much-needed mental break.

I'm worried about finding time for another commitment. How long are the Sound Bath sessions?

We've designed our Sound Bath sessions to be effective yet respectful of your busy schedule. Each session typically runs for 60-75 minutes, including a brief introduction and time to gently reorient afterward. We know your time is valuable, and our aim is to provide maximum stress relief in a manageable timeframe that fits into your professional life.

What should I wear or bring to a Sound Bath?

Comfort is key! Please wear comfortable, loose-fitting clothing that you can easily relax in. We provide mats, blankets, and pillows to ensure you're cozy. You might also consider bringing a water bottle to stay hydrated. The most important thing to bring is your openness to simply relax and let the sounds wash over you.
