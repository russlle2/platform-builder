Frequently Asked Questions

How does a Sound Bath specifically help with the unique stresses of entrepreneurship?
Entrepreneurs face constant pressure and high cognitive load. Our Sound Bath sessions are designed to actively downregulate your sympathetic nervous system, which is often overstimulated by demanding schedules. This helps reduce decision fatigue, improve focus, and prevent burnout, allowing you to maintain peak performance without the emotional cost.
I have a packed schedule. How long is a typical Sound Bath session?
We understand your time is valuable. Most of our individual Sound Bath sessions are 60-75 minutes, including a brief check-in and integration period. We also offer focused 45-minute sessions for those who need a powerful reset within a tighter timeframe, ensuring you can fit profound stress relief into your busy day.
Will I need to 'do' anything during the Sound Bath, or can I just relax?
Your only task is to lie back comfortably and allow the sound vibrations to wash over you. There's no specific technique or effort required. The instruments do the work of guiding your brainwaves and nervous system into a state of deep rest, enabling you to effortlessly release tension and recalibrate.
What should I expect to feel during and after a Sound Bath, specifically for stress relief?
During the Sound Bath, many report a sense of profound calm, a slowing of racing thoughts, and a gentle release of physical tension. Afterward, clients often experience enhanced mental clarity, a sustained reduction in anxiety, and a feeling of being 'recharged' and grounded, ready to approach tasks with renewed composure and efficiency.
