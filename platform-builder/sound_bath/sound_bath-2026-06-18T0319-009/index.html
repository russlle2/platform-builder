Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is a Sound Bath and how can it help me as a parent?
A Sound Bath is a deeply immersive experience where you lie comfortably while various instruments like singing bowls and gongs are played. For parents, it's a powerful tool to reduce the constant buzz of daily stress, helping to calm an overactive mind and soothe your nervous system. It creates a space for genuine rest that often feels out of reach.
I'm always multitasking. Can I really relax during a Sound Bath?
Absolutely. It's completely normal for your mind to wander at first, especially when you're used to being 'on' all the time. The beauty of a Sound Bath is that you don't need to force relaxation. The resonant frequencies gently guide your brainwaves into a more peaceful state, making it easier for you to let go and simply be present, even if just for a little while.
Will a Sound Bath help me feel less irritable or overwhelmed with my kids?
Many parents find that after a Sound Bath, they feel a noticeable shift in their emotional regulation. By resetting your nervous system, you're better able to respond to daily stressors rather than react. This can translate into more patience, less overwhelm, and a greater capacity to navigate the beautiful chaos of family life with a calmer presence.
What should I bring or do to prepare for my first Sound Bath session?
Just bring yourself, dressed in comfortable clothing. You might want to bring a water bottle to stay hydrated. We provide everything else you need, like mats, blankets, and eye masks. Simply arrive a few minutes early to settle in, and be ready to give yourself permission to completely relax and receive the benefits.
