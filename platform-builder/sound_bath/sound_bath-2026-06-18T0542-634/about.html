How does a Sound Bath specifically benefit my athletic recovery?

A Sound Bath helps downregulate your sympathetic nervous system, shifting your body into a 'rest and digest' state. This promotes cellular repair, reduces inflammation, and can accelerate muscle recovery, getting you back to training stronger and faster.

Can Sound Bath truly boost my immune system if I'm physically stressed?

Absolutely. Chronic physical stress can suppress immune function. By inducing deep relaxation and reducing stress hormones, Sound Bath sessions create an optimal environment for your immune system to strengthen and function effectively, keeping you resilient against illness.

What should I expect during my first Sound Bath session at Sound Bath Studio?

You'll lie comfortably, fully clothed, as Dr. Morgan Ellis plays various instruments creating immersive soundscapes. Your only task is to relax and allow the sounds and vibrations to guide you into a state of deep rest, aiding your body's natural recovery processes.

How often should I attend Sound Bath sessions to see benefits for my vitality?

Many active individuals find weekly or bi-weekly sessions to be highly effective for consistent stress reduction, improved recovery, and sustained vitality. We recommend starting with a few sessions to experience the cumulative benefits for your specific needs.
