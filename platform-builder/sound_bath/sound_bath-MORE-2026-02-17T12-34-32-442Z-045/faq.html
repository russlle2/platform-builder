Frequently asked questions

What should I expect in a group session?
Expect 10–12 people, reclining on mats, with a facilitator guiding breath and instruments. Sessions are careful and low-stimulation; we progress from grounding to a sound field, then gentle reorientation.
How early should I arrive?
Arrive 10–15 minutes early to settle, choose a comfortable spot, and adjust your padding. Doors typically close a few minutes after the start time to respect the silence.
Are sessions suitable for people with anxiety or sleep difficulties?
Many people with anxiety or sleep issues find sessions calming, but if you have severe panic disorder, PTSD, or recent trauma, please check with your clinician. Use the contact link if you want to discuss accommodations.
Can I bring my own props?
Yes. A thin mat, blanket, and small pillow are welcome. We also provide limited extras — but space is better with your familiar props.
What are contraindications?
Contraindications include uncontrolled epilepsy, implanted devices sensitive to vibration, unstable cardiovascular conditions, and any medical instruction to avoid intense vibration. If pregnant or postpartum, please clear attendance with a provider. For specifics, email {{EMAIL}}.
