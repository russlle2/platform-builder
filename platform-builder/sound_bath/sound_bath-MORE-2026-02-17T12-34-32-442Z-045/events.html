Contraindications & safety

Sound work is gentle but not suitable for everyone. Please avoid participation if you have an implanted medical device (such as a pacemaker), are in the first trimester of pregnancy without medical sign-off, have a severe seizure disorder, or recent head injury. If you take medication that affects cognition or balance, consult your clinician. Contact us at {{EMAIL}} for guidance or to arrange an adjusted session.
