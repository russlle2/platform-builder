How can Sound Bath specifically help my immune system if I'm recovering from chronic illness?

Our Sound Bath sessions are designed to induce a profound state of relaxation, which directly supports your immune system by reducing stress hormones. When your body is relaxed, it can better focus its energy on repair and strengthening your natural defenses, crucial during chronic illness recovery.

Will the Sound Bath be too intense if I'm feeling sensitive or fragile due to my injury or illness?

Absolutely not. We prioritize gentleness and comfort. The volume and intensity of our Sound Baths are always moderated to ensure a deeply soothing experience, especially for those who are sensitive or in a delicate state of recovery. Your comfort and well-being are our primary concern, and you are welcome to communicate any needs during your session.

What should I expect to feel during a Sound Bath, particularly regarding energy and vitality?

Many people report feeling deeply rested and a sense of calm during and after a Sound Bath. Over time, consistent sessions can help you feel a gentle restoration of your energy and a clearer sense of your inner vitality returning, as your nervous system finds more balance and your body experiences less stress.

Is Sound Bath a suitable complement to my existing medical treatments for recovery?

Yes, Sound Bath can be a wonderful complementary practice. It offers a non-invasive way to support your overall well-being, aiding relaxation and stress reduction, which can be beneficial alongside medical care for chronic illness or injury recovery. Always discuss any new practices with your healthcare provider to ensure it aligns with your treatment plan.
