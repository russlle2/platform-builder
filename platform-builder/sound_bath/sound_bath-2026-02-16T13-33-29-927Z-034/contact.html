How a session flows — a sensory outline

Our sessions are crafted with a sensory arc: arrival, attunement, a layered sound journey, and integration. Below is a concise guide so you know what to expect.

  • Arrival & grounding: Gentle welcome, settle into your mat or cushion; a brief intention-setting.
  • Attunement: Breathwork and low vibrations to align the nervous system.
  • Sound journey: A curated progression of instruments (crystal bowls, gongs, chimes, tuning forks, and monochord textures) designed to shift energy and support deep rest.
  • Integration: Soft tones, silence, and a gentle return with tea or water offered post-session.
Duration: 60–90 minutes (group). Private sessions vary.

  • Mat or yoga mat, blanket, and a small cushion for head support.

Contraindications & safety

Please consult with a medical professional before attending if you are pregnant, have uncontrolled epilepsy, aneurysms, recent head injury, or use medical devices (e.g., pacemakers). Loud gongs and low frequencies can be intense — notify us in advance so we can tailor the session.
