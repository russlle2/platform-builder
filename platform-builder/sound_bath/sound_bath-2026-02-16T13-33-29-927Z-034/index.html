Why sound baths? A sensory reset

We craft slow, layered soundscapes that invite the body to release tension and the mind to rest. Each session is held in intentional silence between pieces so you have space to integrate the sound.

What to expect (flow)

  1. Arrival & grounding (10 minutes) — settle into cushions and blankets.
  2. Brief check-in & intention-setting (5 minutes).
  3. Main immersion (45–60 minutes) — textural layers of instruments and breath-led pacing.
  4. Soft re-entry (10 minutes) — gentle chimes, a slow bell, and guided breath to close.

What we play

Instruments change to suit each set: crystal singing bowls for harmonic clarity, a deep gong for embodiment, and tuning forks to refine pitch and subtle vibration.

What to bring

  • Yoga mat, blanket, pillow or bolster for comfort.
  • Eye mask or scarf to block light.
  • Water bottle for after the session.

Contraindications

Not recommended for those with uncontrolled epilepsy, implanted medical devices sensitive to vibration, or in the first trimester of pregnancy unless cleared by a provider. If you have concerns, contact us at {{EMAIL}} before booking.

Our approach — listen with structure

We combine tonal layering, amplitude pacing and silence as a third instrumental element. Each session follows a three-part architecture: arrival + attunement, immersive resonance, and gentle integration. This creates a predictable container that supports deep relaxation.

Intentional pacing

Sound moves slowly to allow the autonomic nervous system to down-regulate safely.

Textural variety

Different timbres (monochord, chimes, bowls) create layers that the body can track without taxing attention.

Integration support

We close sessions with grounding cues and time to reorient, reducing post-session disorientation.
