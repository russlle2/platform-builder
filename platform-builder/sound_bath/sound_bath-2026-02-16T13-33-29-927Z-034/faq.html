Questions people ask most

What should I bring?
Bring a yoga mat or a soft blanket, an extra cozy blanket for warmth, a pillow or bolster for neck support, and water. Wear comfortable layers. We provide eye masks on request, though many guests bring their own. For VIP Day clients, we suggest a journal and headphones for optional grounding practices.
How long is a session and what is the flow?
Typical group sessions run 75–90 minutes. The practical arc: arrival & settling (10–15 min), intention & light breathwork (5–10 min), progressive sound layering moving into a deep rest core (35–50 min), gentle re-entry and integration (10–15 min). Facilitators offer verbal cues and optional prompts for embodiment.
Are there contraindications or safety notes?
If you have a pacemaker, recent surgery, uncontrolled epilepsy, severe psychiatric conditions, or are pregnant and your practitioner has advised limited exposure, consult your medical provider before attending. Loud low-frequency sounds can affect blood pressure and balance — if you have concerns, please contact us in advance. This is not a substitute for medical care.
Contraindications: individuals with implanted electronic devices (e.g., pacemakers) or certain cardiovascular conditions should get medical clearance prior to attending.
What instruments will be played?
We curate instruments to the container of each session. Common choices include: tuning forks, monochord, tibetan bowls, and small chime accents. Sound selection is adapted to the group and space; intimate events may favor subtle, higher-register textures.
Can I fall asleep?
Yes. Falling asleep is a natural, restorative response for many. We encourage you to enter whichever state is best for integration. Facilitators will guide a gentle re-entry so guests can transition back slowly.

Private & small-group notes

For 1:1, couples, and corporate offerings we adapt the sound palette and consult on goals: deep rest, team reset, or creative immersion. Sessions include a pre-session call for intentions and setup.

More safety details

If you use hearing aids or implants, or experience vertigo, email us with relevant details. We keep volume within a therapeutic range and offer quieter seating options near the entrance.
