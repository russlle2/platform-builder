Flow of a private session

We begin with a brief intake, then a tailored sonic arc (25–60 min) weaving sustained instruments, subtle percussion, and tuned resonance; closing includes gentle grounding and takeaways.

What to bring

  • Comfortable clothing
  • Blanket or mat for lying down
  • Any mobility supports (bolster, chair)

Contraindications

If you have a pacemaker, recent surgery, or severe psychiatric conditions, please contact us before booking. High-volume instruments are adjustable on request.
