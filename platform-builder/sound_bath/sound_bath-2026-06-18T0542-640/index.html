Frequently Asked Questions

How does Sound Bath specifically support immunity during life transitions?
During major life transitions, stress can elevate cortisol, impacting your immune response. Our Sound Bath sessions are designed to activate your parasympathetic nervous system, reducing stress and creating an optimal internal environment where your body can better allocate resources to immune function and repair.
Will a Sound Bath help me manage the stress associated with significant changes?
Absolutely. The carefully selected frequencies and vibrations in our Sound Bath sessions encourage a deep state of relaxation, which directly helps to lower stress hormones and calm your nervous system. This state of calm is critical for maintaining mental clarity and emotional equilibrium as you navigate new circumstances.
I'm new to Sound Bath. What can I expect from a session focused on vitality?
You can expect to lie comfortably and be immersed in a landscape of resonant sound. The experience is designed for passive reception, allowing the sound frequencies to gently guide your body and mind into a restorative state. Many clients report feeling deeply rested and rejuvenated, with a noticeable lift in their overall energy and sense of well-being post-session.
How frequently should I engage in Sound Bath for sustained immune support during a transition?
For sustained benefit and robust immune support during a major life transition, we recommend regular sessions. The optimal frequency can vary per individual, but consistent engagement, perhaps weekly or bi-weekly, allows your body to build a more resilient foundation and better adapt to ongoing changes. We can discuss a personalized plan during your initial consultation.
