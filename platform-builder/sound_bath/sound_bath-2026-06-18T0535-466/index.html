Frequently Asked Questions

How does a Sound Bath specifically help with mental clarity during recovery?
During recovery, your brain can experience fatigue and 'fog.' A Sound Bath uses specific frequencies to gently guide your brain into a relaxed state, similar to meditation. This allows your mind to rest, process thoughts more efficiently, and can significantly improve your ability to focus and think clearly without the usual mental strain.
Will I need to do anything during the Sound Bath session?
Absolutely not. Your only task is to get comfortable. You simply lie down, close your eyes if you wish, and allow the sounds to wash over you. There's no pressure to 'do' anything or achieve a certain state. Just be present and let the sounds support your mental calm as you recover.
Is Sound Bath suitable if I have limited mobility or specific sensitivities due to my condition?
Yes, our Sound Bath practice is designed to be highly accessible and gentle. We ensure a comfortable and supportive environment. Please inform us of any specific needs or sensitivities beforehand so we can make appropriate accommodations. The experience is passive and can be enjoyed from any comfortable position.
How often should I attend a Sound Bath to see benefits for my cognitive wellness?
The frequency that works best for you can vary. Many individuals recovering from chronic illness or injury find that regular, consistent sessions—perhaps once a week or bi-weekly—help maintain mental clarity and reduce cognitive fatigue over time. We encourage you to listen to your body and mind and choose what feels most supportive for your unique recovery path.
