What should I bring?

Bring anything that helps you rest: a mat, pillow, blanket, and an open attention. We provide chairs on request.

Are sessions suitable for beginners?

Yes. Guidance is short and precise. We welcome those new to sound gatherings as well as people who attend regularly.

How long are gatherings?

Typical group sessions run 50 minutes. Shortforms are 30 minutes. The listing on events.html shows the length for each entry.

Contraindications & safety

In most cases these gatherings are gentle. However, if you have any of the following, please consult a clinician before attending: pregnancy, seizure disorders, recent head trauma, implanted electronic devices, or any condition where sustained vibration or guided breathwork could be harmful. If in doubt, email us at {{EMAIL}} before booking.

Do you offer private or corporate sessions?

Yes. We design focused sound gatherings for small groups and workplace pauses. See private-sessions.html or contact us to discuss scope and pricing.

Cancellation policy

You can cancel up to 24 hours before a public session for a full refund. Private bookings follow a bespoke agreement defined at the time of booking.
Important: This site and our sessions are not a substitute for medical care. If you are under treatment for mental health, cardiovascular conditions, or neurological issues, please check with your provider first.
