What to bring

Soft items that enhance sensation and comfort: a yoga mat or thick blanket, a pillow, a cozy throw, and a water bottle. We provide blankets, bolsters, and ear-friendly seating where possible. Please label anything personal.

Contraindications & safety

Please consult with a healthcare professional if you have: active pregnancy (first trimester caution and note required), a pacemaker or other implanted medical device, epilepsy, uncontrolled psychiatric conditions, or recent head trauma. Sound work is supportive, not a substitute for medical care. If you use medication that affects sensory processing, let us know in advance so we can adapt the session.
