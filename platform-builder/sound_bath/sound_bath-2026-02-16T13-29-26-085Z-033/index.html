Who benefits — and when to check first

This intensive sound bath is for people seeking a deep sensory pivot: nervous system downshift, clearer sleep, and embodied presence.

Immediate effects

  • Slow, restorative breath work paired with immersive tones.
  • Reduced heart rate variability spikes and mental chatter.
  • Heightened interoception — you feel rather than think.

Who should be cautious

Contraindications: pregnancy (first trimester consult), epilepsy, active psychosis, recent head injury, or pacemakers — please check with a physician first.

What to bring

  • Comfortable layers, a pillow, and a warm blanket.
  • Yoga mat or soft pad. Eye mask optional.
  • Water bottle and an open heart.

How the intensive unfolds

A carefully crafted arc of invitation, vibration, and integration — anchored in sensory richness.

  1. Arrival & Grounding (15 min)

    We set intention, tune in with gentle breathwork, and settle into supportive props.

  2. Layered Sound Journey (45–60 min)

    Crystal bowls meet the deep wash of a gong, with chimes and tuning forks threading clarity. Expect big, immersive waves and quiet harmonic micro-moments.

  3. Slow Emergence (10–15 min)

    Soft monochord or bell strokes bring you gently back. We close with journaling prompts and a brief sharing circle for integration.

Tiny rituals that deepen the work

Before: Hydrate & Slow Down

Skip heavy meals and alcohol for at least 3 hours. Drink water and arrive 10 minutes early to breathe.

During: Notice Sensations

Label sensations gently: warm, tingling, sinking. No need to narrate — naming helps the nervous system integrate.

After: Move Softly

Give yourself a half hour post-session for slow walking, tea, and journaling to anchor insights.
