Contraindications & safety

Sound baths are contemplative and low-risk; however, if you are pregnant, have epilepsy, a pacemaker or severe cardiovascular issues, or are currently under psychiatric crisis, please consult your physician and notify the facilitator before attending.

Diagnostic — Are these gatherings for you?

If you feel overstimulated, anxious, or sleep-deprived, the deep harmonic layers are designed to calm and re-orient. Not suitable if you require constant medical monitoring.

Plan — How we hold the space

Intimate group sizes, candle-lights, curated transitions between frequencies. The facilitator guides breath cues and subtle movement to enhance integration.

Micro-habits — before & after

Arrive 10 minutes early; hydrate; avoid heavy meals. After: rest for 20 minutes, sip water, journal one sentence about how you feel.
