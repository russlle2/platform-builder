Is this right for you?

Quick diagnostic — sound baths are ideal when you want to:

  • Release tension and slow the nervous system
  • Support restful sleep or deepen meditation
  • Reconnect with embodied stillness in a guided container

Not recommended if you have severe sensory sensitivities, recent concussion, or are currently experiencing psychosis. See contraindications below.

What a typical session looks like

Arrival & settling

Warm welcome, orientation, optional intention-setting (10 minutes)

Intro & breath work

Gentle guided breath exercises to orient the nervous system (8–12 minutes)

Main sound journey

Layered tones from bowls, monochord, and chimes; sustained spaces for internal exploration (40–50 minutes)

Integration

Soft re-entry, tea, and optional sharing (10–15 minutes)
