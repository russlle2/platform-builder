Questions we get a lot

Clear, calm answers so you arrive relaxed. If it isn't here, drop us a line at {{EMAIL}}.

What should I bring?
Please bring a yoga mat or cushioned pad, a cozy blanket, and an eye mask if you like darkness. Water is essential — carry a bottle. Pillows and bolsters are welcome for additional support.
Are there any contraindications?
Sound baths are gentle but not suitable for everyone. Avoid group sessions if you have: severe or uncontrolled epilepsy, recent head injury or concussion, active psychosis, or if you are currently undergoing certain intensive psychiatric treatments. If you are pregnant, have a pacemaker, or other implanted device, please consult your physician and inform the facilitator prior to booking.
How loud are they — will it be overwhelming?
Our approach is intentionally enveloping, not intrusive. We favor harmonic layering and resonance over percussion. If you prefer lower volume, the facilitator will accommodate seating and positioning to reduce intensity.
What is the structure of a session?
Typical flow: arrival & orientation (10m), breath & grounding (10m), the main sound journey (35–55m), and a gentle re-entry with tea (10–15m). For private sessions this can be adjusted.
Which instruments do you use?
We rotate instruments for tonal variety and intention. Commonly used tools include: gongs, tuning forks, koshi chimes, and select crystal bowls tuned to specific frequencies.

Before you book

Short checklist and micro-habits to amplify the experience.

  • Hydrate well in the 24 hours prior
  • Avoid heavy meals right before a session
  • Wear breathable layers — bring socks and a blanket
