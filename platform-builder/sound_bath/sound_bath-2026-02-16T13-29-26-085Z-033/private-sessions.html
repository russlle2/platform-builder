Session types & intents

Each private session is calibrated to your needs. Below are common formats; we also design custom packages for offsite retreats and immersive days.

1:1 Deep Reset — 60–90m

Personalized frequency mapping and guided integration. Instruments may include a monochord, tuning forks, and crystal bowls tuned to your energetic profile.

Couples & Pairs — 75m

Shared resonance to facilitate listening and attunement. Often uses paired bowls, a small harp or monochord, and subtle breath cues.

Corporate & Team — 45–90m

Focus, reset, and group cohesion. A short talk on nervous-system regulation followed by a tailored sound-journey using gongs, chimes, and voice toning exercises.

Contraindications

If anyone in the private group is pregnant, has a pacemaker, epilepsy, or recent surgery, please declare this at booking. We can adapt close-contact protocols or create a modified acoustic approach.

Diagnostic — prep & intake

Before the session we send a short intake form to tailor frequencies and volume levels, and to accommodate mobility or sensory preferences.

Flow — a private session

  1. Arrival & consult (10–15m)
  2. Gradual immersion (10m)
  3. Core soundwork (30–60m)

Micro-habits to extend benefit

After a private session, keep your phone away for an hour, hydrate, and avoid stimulants. Try 5 minutes of mindful breathing each evening for better sleep consolidation.
