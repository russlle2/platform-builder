Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Sound Bath and how can it benefit me at this stage of life?
A Sound Bath is a meditative experience where you are bathed in ambient sound produced by various instruments. For seniors over 60, it offers a gentle way to reduce everyday worries, encourage restful sleep, and foster a deeper sense of inner peace, supporting your spiritual well-being without any physical exertion.
Do I need to do anything special during a Sound Bath session?
Not at all. Your only 'task' is to get comfortable. You can lie down, sit in a chair, or find any position that feels most relaxing. Simply close your eyes if you wish, breathe gently, and allow the sounds to wash over you. There's no right or wrong way to experience it; just let yourself be.
Is a Sound Bath too loud or overwhelming for someone my age?
We specifically tailor our Sound Bath sessions to be gentle and soothing, particularly for seniors. The volume is always kept at a comfortable level, designed to relax rather than stimulate. Our aim is to provide a comforting and serene environment where you feel completely at ease.
What if I have trouble sitting or lying down for an extended period during a Sound Bath?
Your comfort is our priority. We encourage you to adjust your position at any time during the Sound Bath. Whether you prefer to lie on your back, on your side, or sit in a comfortable chair, you are welcome to choose what works best for your body. We also provide supportive props to ensure your utmost comfort.
