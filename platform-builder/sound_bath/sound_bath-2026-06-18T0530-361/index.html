Frequently Asked Questions

How does a Sound Bath specifically help with burnout-related physical pain?
A Sound Bath uses specific frequencies and vibrations that encourage deep relaxation in your muscles and nervous system. For burnout, this means addressing chronic tension in areas like your neck, shoulders, and back by prompting your body to release stored stress and return to a more neutral, pain-free state, rather than just masking the symptoms.
I'm always busy. How can I justify taking time for a Sound Bath?
We understand your schedule is packed. Think of a Sound Bath as a strategic pause, an investment in your physical and mental energy for the week ahead. Giving yourself this dedicated time to decompress can actually improve your focus and resilience, making your busy hours more effective and less draining in the long run. It’s about sustainable productivity.
What should I expect during a Sound Bath session for somatic healing?
During a Sound Bath, you'll lie down comfortably while various instruments like gongs and singing bowls are played. The sounds create a deeply immersive experience. For somatic healing, we focus on guiding your awareness to sensations in your body, allowing any held tension or discomfort to surface and gently release. It's a very personal and often profound experience of reconnecting with your physical self.
Will a Sound Bath really help me feel more grounded and less overwhelmed by work?
Yes, many working professionals find that the deep relaxation and vibrational nature of a Sound Bath help quiet the overactive mind and soothe the overstimulated body. By shifting your focus from external stressors to your internal state, you can cultivate a stronger sense of inner calm and stability, making it easier to manage the demands of your professional life without feeling constantly overwhelmed.
