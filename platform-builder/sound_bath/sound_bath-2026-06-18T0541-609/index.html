Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Sound Bath specifically help my immune system as a parent?
As a parent, chronic stress directly impacts your immune function, making you more susceptible to illness. A Sound Bath deeply relaxes your nervous system, reducing stress hormones and creating an optimal environment for your immune system to strengthen and repair itself. This can mean fewer sick days for you, and ultimately, for your family.
I'm always tired from parenting; will a Sound Bath truly help my energy levels?
Absolutely. Persistent fatigue in parents often stems from a combination of physical exertion and mental overload. A Sound Bath provides a unique opportunity for profound rest and cellular repair, which is far more restorative than typical sleep. By allowing your body to enter this deep state, you're not just resting; you’re actively rebuilding your vital energy reserves, helping you feel more sustained throughout your busy days.
What if I can't 'turn off my brain' during a Sound Bath, especially with so much on my mind as a parent?
It’s completely normal for your mind to be busy, especially when you're a parent. The beauty of a Sound Bath is that the resonant frequencies do the work for you. You don't need to 'try' to relax. Simply lie back and allow the sounds to wash over you. Most parents find that even if their mind wanders initially, the consistent, soothing vibrations naturally guide them into a more peaceful state, releasing the mental chatter.
Is a Sound Bath safe if I'm pregnant or breastfeeding, as I'm focused on my child's health too?
Generally, Sound Baths are considered very safe and beneficial during pregnancy and breastfeeding, offering deep relaxation and stress reduction for both you and your baby. However, it's always wise to consult with your healthcare provider first, especially if you have any specific concerns or medical conditions. We're happy to discuss any adaptations or considerations to ensure your comfort and peace of mind during your session.
