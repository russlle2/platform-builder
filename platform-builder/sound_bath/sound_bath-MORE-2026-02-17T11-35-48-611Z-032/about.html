What to expect

Sessions open with orientation, move through layered sound phrases, and close with a short integration period. We provide mats and low lighting; you may bring a small blanket or eye cover.

  • Arrival & grounding: 7–10 minutes
  • Main sound work: curated 20–70 minutes
  • Closure & practical takeaways: 5–10 minutes

Contraindications & safety notes

Sound practices are gentle but not suitable for everyone. Please tell us if you have a pacemaker, recent concussion, epilepsy, pregnancy beyond the first trimester without prior clearance, or severe hearing conditions. For any medical uncertainty, consult your care provider prior to booking.

Still unsure?

If you want a lighter introduction, choose a shorter session or schedule a consult. Our aim is usable calm—tools you can take with you.
