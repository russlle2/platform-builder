What to expect

Each intensive follows a clear arc: arrival (orientation), focused sound work (guided), and reintegration (practical takeaway). You leave with a short practice to use immediately.

Orientation

We set an intention, check contraindications, and arrange a minimal environment for sensory ease.

Focused sound

Layered sound tools are used with targeted timing — not a long lull, but a structured sequence with clear phases.

Reintegration

A short talk-back and a compact practice to take with you — 3-5 steps for daily access to the effect.

Will it be loud?

Is this meditation?

          
It borrows practices but is a procedural sound intensive: clear instructions and outcomes, not freeform silence.

          
Are there contraindications?

          
Please see the health note above and contact us if you have specific medical concerns.
