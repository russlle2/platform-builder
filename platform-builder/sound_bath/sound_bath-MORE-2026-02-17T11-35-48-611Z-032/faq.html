Frequently asked

What should I bring?

Comfortable clothing, a small blanket or light pillow, and any personal hearing aids if you use them. We provide mats, bolsters, and headphones when needed.

Will the sound be loud?

No. Sessions are intentionally calibrated. We introduce anchors at gentle levels, and there are options for milder textures if you're sensitive.

Is this therapy?

These are experiential sessions aimed at regulation and restoration, not a replacement for psychotherapy or medical care.

Can I leave early?

Yes. You may step out quietly at any time. Let the facilitator know if you are concerned about sensory overload.

Booking, cancellations, and refunds

Full policy is available on the booking page. We offer a small-window refund with notice; later cancellations may receive a credit toward future classes.

Safety & contraindications

If you have a pacemaker, recent head trauma, epilepsy, or uncertain pregnancy status, please check with your clinician before attending and tell us before booking. We can adapt many sessions but will not proceed when there is a clear medical risk.
