From Frazzled to Fresher: Your Sound Bath Experience

Your experience with a Sound Bath is wonderfully simple. You arrive, find a comfortable spot to lie down, and we begin. There's no right or wrong way to experience it; just allow yourself to listen and relax. Many parents find this dedicated time helps them to 'switch off' from the constant demands, leading to a clearer mind and a more relaxed body. It's a gentle way to hit the reset button, helping you feel more patient and rested for whatever your day, or night, brings.
