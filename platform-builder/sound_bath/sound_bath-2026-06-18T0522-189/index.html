Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is a Sound Bath and how will it help my sleep?
A Sound Bath is a deeply relaxing experience where you are bathed in the vibrations of various instruments. For parents, it's a gentle way to quiet your busy mind and nervous system, making it easier to fall asleep and experience more restorative rest. It helps to reduce the mental chatter that often keeps you awake.
I'm always so busy. Do I need to 'do' anything special during a Sound Bath?
Absolutely not! That's the beauty of it. You simply lie down comfortably, close your eyes if you wish, and let the sounds do the work. There's no meditation experience required, no special poses – just relax and allow yourself to receive the gentle vibrations. It's truly a time for you to simply be.
I'm worried I won't be able to relax. What if my mind keeps wandering during the Sound Bath?
It's perfectly normal for your mind to wander, especially when you're managing so much. The sounds themselves act as a gentle anchor, helping to draw your attention back to the present moment. Even if your mind wanders, you'll still be receiving the benefits of deep relaxation. Just be kind to yourself and let the sounds guide you gently.
Is a Sound Bath safe for everyone, especially for tired parents?
Sound Baths are generally very safe and beneficial for most people. If you have any specific health concerns, especially related to sound sensitivity or certain medical conditions, it's always a good idea to chat with your doctor first. For exhausted parents, it's often a much-needed opportunity to unwind and recharge safely.
