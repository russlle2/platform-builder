Frequently Asked Questions

Is this Sound Bath specifically for new mothers?
Yes, our Sound Bath sessions are thoughtfully designed with the unique needs of new mothers in their postpartum recovery period in mind. We understand the emotional and physical demands you face and create a supportive environment tailored to your well-being.
How does a trauma-informed Sound Bath differ for postpartum recovery?
Our trauma-informed approach means we focus on creating a safe, gentle, and empowering experience. We prioritize your comfort, offering guidance on how to engage with the sounds in a way that feels best for your body and emotional state, without pushing or overwhelming you. This is crucial for supporting your nervous system during a sensitive time.
What should I expect during a Sound Bath session?
You'll lie down comfortably, typically on a mat with blankets and pillows. We'll play various instruments, creating soothing vibrations that wash over you. There's nothing you need to do but relax and allow the sounds to support your body's natural ability to unwind and find balance. You are welcome to feed or comfort your baby as needed during the session.
Can I bring my baby to the Sound Bath?
We understand the challenges of finding childcare postpartum. Please contact us directly to discuss options for bringing your baby. Our priority is to ensure a calm and restorative environment for all participants, and we can explore how best to accommodate you and your little one.
