How does a Sound Bath specifically help with postpartum recovery and immunity?

During postpartum, your body is undergoing immense changes and requires deep rest to heal. Our Sound Bath sessions create an environment for profound relaxation, which lowers stress hormones. This allows your immune system to function more effectively and helps your body conserve energy for repair and vitality building, supporting your physical and emotional recovery.

Is a Sound Bath safe and gentle enough for new mothers, even if I'm feeling delicate?

Absolutely. Sound Baths are a completely passive and gentle practice. You simply lie down comfortably while the sounds wash over you. We prioritize a serene and supportive atmosphere, making it ideal for new mothers who need tender care and a non-strenuous way to unwind and replenish their energy.

What should I expect during my first Sound Bath session to support my postpartum vitality?

You'll be invited to lie down comfortably on a mat with blankets and pillows. Dr. Morgan Ellis will then play various instruments like singing bowls and gongs, creating a symphony of therapeutic sounds. Your only task is to relax and allow the vibrations to soothe you. Many mothers report feeling deeply rested and rejuvenated, with a sense of calm and renewed vigor after their session.

How often should I attend a Sound Bath to experience its benefits for my immune system and energy levels?

The frequency often depends on your individual needs and recovery pace. Some mothers find weekly sessions incredibly supportive for maintaining their energy and immune health, while others prefer bi-weekly or monthly for consistent replenishment. We can discuss a personalized approach that best supports your postpartum well-being during your initial consultation.
