How Mood-to-Method works

Pick a feeling — the recommendation highlights the best format and nudges the sound palette. The intensity control tilts the program: gentle adds space and breath, medium adds melodic bowls, intense uses layered tones and longer resonances. Mixing these lets you find something that fits this moment.

  • Ruffled + Gentle → Short arrival practice, soft bowls, grounding cohort seat.
  • Heavy + Gentle → Longer settling, extra integration, recommended private or small cohort.
  • Curious + Intense → Exploratory soundscapes, slight dynamic contrast, cohort format works well.
