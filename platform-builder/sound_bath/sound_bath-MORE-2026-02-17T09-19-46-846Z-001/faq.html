Frequently asked questions

Short answers here; if something feels unclear, email {{EMAIL}} or call {{PHONE}}.

What should I bring?

Practical
A yoga mat or blanket, a small pillow for comfort, and water. Wear layers — we dim the room and some people feel cool during deep rest.

Are the sounds loud?

Safety
We work with tuned bowls and gentle resonances designed to be felt more than heard. If you are sensitive to vibration, tell the facilitator before we begin — we can adapt placement or offer ear covers.

What's the cancellation policy?

Booking
For single gatherings, cancellations up to 48 hours before the session receive a full refund. Cohort commitments have different terms listed at booking; contact us at {{EMAIL}} for details.

Are there contraindications?

Health
If you have an implanted medical device, active uncontrolled epilepsy, or significant acute psychiatric symptoms, please consult with us before booking. Pregnant people with complications should check with their provider. We can offer private or modified sessions to make participation safer.

Can children attend?

Logistics
Our gatherings are sized for adults. For family-friendly offerings, check the Events calendar for special sessions or contact us about private bookings.
