Why sound, why together?

Sound works on the edges of cognition: it helps soften the tightness around thoughts and makes breathing less effortful. In cohorts, people notice new perspectives appear between sessions — small shifts that compound.

Attunement

We curate tonal journeys that guide attention away from pressure and toward ease: not about doing it right, but about coming along.

Structure

Each session has an arrival check-in, a central sound arc, and a short integration to help transfer the felt shift to the week ahead.

Contraindications and safety

Sound baths are deeply restorative for many people, but they are not right for everyone. If you have an implanted medical device (e.g., pacemaker), are pregnant and experiencing complications, have uncontrolled epilepsy, or severe psychiatric conditions that include recent psychosis or active suicidal ideation, please contact us before booking. We will help you choose a safe format — private, adjusted, or recommend clinical care when needed.
