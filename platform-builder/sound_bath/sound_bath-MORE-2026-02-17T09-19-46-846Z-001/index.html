What happens here?

  1. Arrive & Settle: Soft lighting, cushions, and a gentle orientation.
  2. Intention & Breath: Short guided centering to arrive in the present.
  3. The Sound Field: 30–60 minutes of curated tones. You may rest, move, or do gentle stretches.
  4. Grounding: We land together with a quiet prompt and time to reorient.

Sessions work best with loose, comfortable clothing and an openness to non-goal experience.

Not sure? Quick answers

Will I fall asleep?

Maybe — and that can be restorative. If you need wakeful focus, pick a medium or intense mix.

Is this religious?

No. These gatherings use sound and breath as tools; they're accessible to people of any background.

I'm sensitive to noise — will this be loud?

We tailor volume and vibration to the room and to participant needs. Use the "gentle" setting to prioritize soft, long tones.

Ready to join a session?

We run small, attentive gatherings and private harmonics. Book a single session or a three-part wave-series to notice gradual change.
