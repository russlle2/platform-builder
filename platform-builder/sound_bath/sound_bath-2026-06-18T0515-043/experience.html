Sound Bath

Your Comforting Sound Bath Experience with Us

When you join us for a Sound Bath, you’ll be invited to lie down or sit comfortably, fully clothed, in a supportive space. There’s nothing for you to do but relax and listen. Dr. Morgan Ellis will guide you through the experience, ensuring you feel safe and at ease. We focus on creating an inclusive environment where everyone, regardless of background, can feel welcome and experience the gentle benefits of sound for stress reduction.
