Your Safe Haven: The Sound Bath Experience Designed for Trauma-Informed Care

We recognize that recovery can be a vulnerable time, and our Sound Bath sessions are rooted in trauma-informed principles. This means we prioritize your comfort and sense of safety above all else. You are invited to simply receive, without expectation or pressure, allowing the sounds to meet you where you are, supporting your emotional reintegration and helping to ease the lingering effects of illness or injury on your spirit.
