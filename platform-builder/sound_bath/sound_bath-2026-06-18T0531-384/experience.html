Understanding the Instruments: What You'll Experience in a Sound Bath

During your Sound Bath, you'll hear a variety of instruments like crystal singing bowls and gongs. These aren't just for ambiance; they produce specific vibrations that gently interact with your body. Many active individuals find these sounds help to quiet their overactive minds, allowing their physical body to settle and respond more effectively to the soothing frequencies, promoting a sense of calm and well-being.
