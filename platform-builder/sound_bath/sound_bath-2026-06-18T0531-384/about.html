How can a Sound Bath specifically help with my athletic recovery?

A Sound Bath promotes deep relaxation, which is key for muscle repair and reducing inflammation. The gentle vibrations can help release fascial tension and improve circulation, supporting your body's natural recovery processes after strenuous activity or a long day on your feet.

I'm new to this. Do I need any special experience to benefit from a Sound Bath?

Absolutely not! Our Sound Bath sessions are designed for everyone, especially those with no prior wellness experience. You just need to show up, lie down comfortably, and allow the sounds to do their work. There’s no right or wrong way to experience it, and we're here to guide you.

Will a Sound Bath help with chronic pain from old injuries?

While a Sound Bath isn't a medical treatment, many active individuals find that the deep relaxation and nervous system regulation it provides can significantly reduce the perception of chronic discomfort. By easing overall tension, your body can find a more relaxed state, which often lessens the impact of lingering aches and pains.

What should I wear or bring to my Sound Bath session?

We recommend wearing comfortable, loose-fitting clothing that allows you to relax fully. You don't need to bring anything specific, as we provide all necessary comforts like mats and blankets. Just come as you are, ready to unwind and let go.
