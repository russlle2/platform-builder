Frequently asked questions

What should I bring?
Comfortable clothing, a yoga mat or blanket, water. We provide pillows and bolsters on request.
Is this a meditation class?
No formal meditation instructions are given. Sessions use sound as a modality to redirect attention; verbal cues are minimal and gentle.
How early should I arrive?
Plan for 10 minutes before the scheduled start. This allows for quiet transition and brief orientation.
Are recordings allowed?
To protect the container and privacy of attendees, recording is not permitted in group sessions.
Can I bring a friend?
Yes. We welcome guests; capacity is limited so check the booking page for group size and reserve early.
What if I have a hearing device or cochlear implant?
Please consult with the device manufacturer and your clinician. The experience is generally safe, but because sound levels can vary, exercise caution.
Contraindications & safety
Sessions are low risk for most people. However, please do not attend if you have uncontrolled seizures, recent head trauma, or are experiencing acute psychiatric symptoms. If pregnant or on medications that affect balance or consciousness, consult your clinician before booking. For any concerns call {{PHONE}} or email {{EMAIL}}.
