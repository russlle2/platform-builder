Frequently Asked Questions

I'm always busy, how long is a typical Sound Bath session and will it fit my schedule?
Our Sound Bath sessions are typically 60-75 minutes long, including a brief settling-in and integration period. We understand your time is valuable, which is why we offer various session times throughout the week, including evenings, specifically designed to help working professionals like you carve out this essential time for rest and recovery without disrupting your workday.
I have trouble 'switching off' my mind. Can a Sound Bath really help me quiet my thoughts for better sleep?
Absolutely. That's precisely what a Sound Bath excels at. The rich, resonant tones provide a focal point, naturally drawing your attention away from your busy thoughts. The frequencies emitted by the instruments help to downshift your brainwave activity, guiding your mind into a more peaceful, meditative state where it's much easier to disengage from mental chatter and prepare for restorative sleep.
Is a Sound Bath like meditation? I find it hard to meditate.
While both can induce relaxation, a Sound Bath is often much more accessible if you struggle with traditional meditation. You don't need to 'do' anything; the sounds do the work for you. You simply lie back and receive. The vibrational experience gently coaxes your mind and body into a deeply relaxed state, making it an effortless way to achieve mental quiet and prepare for sleep without needing to focus or control your thoughts.
I'm dealing with burnout. How quickly can I expect to feel the benefits for my sleep and energy?
Many clients report feeling a significant sense of calm and mental clarity after their very first Sound Bath session, often leading to improved sleep quality that same night. For deeper, more lasting benefits in combating burnout and sustaining restorative rest, regular sessions are recommended. We can discuss a personalized plan that fits your needs and helps you integrate this powerful tool into your self-care routine.
