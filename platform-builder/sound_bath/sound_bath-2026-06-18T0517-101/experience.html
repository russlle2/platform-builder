Sound Bath

What to Expect: Your First Sound Bath for Better Sleep

When you step into our Sound Bath space, you'll be welcomed into a calming environment designed for your comfort. You'll lie down comfortably, close your eyes, and simply listen. Dr. Morgan Ellis will guide you through an immersive experience using a curated selection of instruments, carefully chosen for their ability to promote deep relaxation and mental quiet. There's nothing to 'do' but allow the sounds to wash over you, gently guiding your system towards a state of profound peace and preparation for a night of restorative sleep. Many clients report feeling a significant sense of calm and mental clarity after just one session.
