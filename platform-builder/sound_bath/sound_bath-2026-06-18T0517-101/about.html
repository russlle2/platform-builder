I'm always busy, how long is a typical Sound Bath session and will it fit my schedule?

Our Sound Bath sessions are typically 60-75 minutes long, including a brief settling-in and integration period. I have trouble 'switching off' my mind. Can a Sound Bath really help me quiet my thoughts for better sleep?

Absolutely. That's precisely what a Sound Bath excels at. The rich, resonant tones provide a focal point, naturally drawing your attention away from your busy thoughts. The frequencies emitted by the instruments help to downshift your brainwave activity, guiding your mind into a more peaceful, meditative state where it's much easier to disengage from mental chatter and prepare for restorative sleep.

Is a Sound Bath like meditation? I find it hard to meditate.

While both can induce relaxation, a Sound Bath is often much more accessible if you struggle with traditional meditation. You don't need to 'do' anything; the sounds do the work for you. You simply lie back and receive. The vibrational experience gently coaxes your mind and body into a deeply relaxed state, making it an effortless way to achieve mental quiet and prepare for sleep without needing to focus or control your thoughts.

For deeper, more lasting benefits in combating burnout and sustaining restorative rest, regular sessions are recommended.
