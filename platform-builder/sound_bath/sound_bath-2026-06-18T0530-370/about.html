How can a Sound Bath specifically help with physical pain during a life transition?

A Sound Bath helps by creating a deeply relaxing state that encourages your nervous system to calm down. This can reduce muscle tension, ease chronic aches, and alleviate the physical manifestations of stress often heightened during life changes, allowing your body to find a more comfortable equilibrium.

I'm feeling very overwhelmed by changes. Will a Sound Bath be too stimulating for me?

Our Sound Bath sessions are designed to be profoundly soothing, not stimulating. The gentle, sustained vibrations and natural tones are intended to guide you into a state of deep rest, helping to quiet an overactive mind and nervous system. It's a space for passive reception, allowing you to simply be, which can be very beneficial when feeling overwhelmed.

What does 'somatic healing' mean in the context of a Sound Bath?

Somatic healing through a Sound Bath means addressing the physical sensations and stored tension within your body that often accompany stress and change. The vibrations help to gently release these bodily imprints, allowing for a more harmonious connection between your mind and body, and reducing physical discomfort without needing to intellectualize it.

Is a Sound Bath an active or passive experience for someone navigating big changes?

A Sound Bath is a wonderfully passive experience. You simply lie back, get comfortable, and allow the sounds to wash over you. There's no need to 'do' anything, which is often a relief when you're already actively navigating many challenges. This passive reception supports deep relaxation and somatic release, allowing your system to reset.
