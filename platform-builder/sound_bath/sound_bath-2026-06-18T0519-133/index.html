Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly happens during a Sound Bath session?
During a Sound Bath, you'll lie comfortably while I play various instruments like singing bowls and chimes. The sounds create a gentle, immersive environment that encourages your body and mind to relax deeply, helping you prepare for better sleep.
Is a Sound Bath suitable for someone with mobility concerns?
Absolutely. Our sessions are designed with your comfort in mind. You can choose to lie down or sit in a comfortable chair. We ensure the environment is accessible and that you feel completely at ease throughout the experience.
How quickly can I expect to see improvements in my sleep from Sound Bath?
Many of our clients report feeling more relaxed and sleeping better after just one or two sessions. Like any gentle practice, consistent engagement often yields the most noticeable and lasting improvements for your sleep patterns.
Will the sounds be too loud or overwhelming for me?
Not at all. We carefully control the volume and tone to ensure a gentle and soothing experience. The goal is deep relaxation, not stimulation. The sounds are designed to be calming and never jarring, creating a peaceful atmosphere for you.
