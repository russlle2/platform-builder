Sound Bath

The Gentle Approach of Sound Bath for Seniors

You’ll settle into a comfortable position, often reclined, as I, Dr. Morgan Ellis, introduce a sequence of soothing tones. There's nothing for you to do but listen and allow the sounds to wash over you. This passive experience bypasses mental effort, offering a direct path to tranquility. It’s a gentle, non-invasive method that supports your body's natural ability to find calm and prepare for rest.
