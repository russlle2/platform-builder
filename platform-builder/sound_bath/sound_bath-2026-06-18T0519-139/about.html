How does a Sound Bath specifically help parents improve sleep?

Our Sound Bath sessions provide a dedicated space for deep relaxation, which is often elusive for parents. The specific sound frequencies help to down-regulate your nervous system, reducing the cortisol (stress hormone) levels that often interfere with falling asleep and staying asleep. It’s a targeted reset for your overactive parent brain.

Will I be able to quiet my mind during the Sound Bath if I'm constantly thinking about my kids?

It's completely normal for your mind to wander, especially as a busy parent. The beauty of a Sound Bath is that the continuous, immersive soundscape provides a gentle anchor for your attention. Even if your thoughts drift, the vibrations naturally guide your focus back to the present moment, creating a mental quietude that's distinct from forced concentration.

What should I wear or bring to my Sound Bath session?

We recommend wearing comfortable, loose-fitting clothing that allows you to relax fully. You don't need to bring anything specific; we provide mats, blankets, and eye masks for your comfort. Just bring yourself, ready to lie down and receive the restorative benefits of sound.

Is this just another wellness trend, or is there a real benefit for sleep?

While Sound Baths are gaining popularity, their principles are rooted in the physiological effects of sound on the body and brain. By encouraging alpha and theta brainwave states, Sound Baths can significantly reduce stress and promote relaxation, which are critical precursors to healthy sleep. We focus on these measurable, science-backed benefits for parents.
