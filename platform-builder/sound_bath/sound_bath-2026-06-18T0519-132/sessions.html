Your Postpartum Sound Bath: A Scientific Approach to Sleep

Our Sound Bath sessions are carefully structured to guide your brain into a state of deep relaxation, similar to meditation or light sleep. Using specific frequencies and resonant tones, we encourage your nervous system to down-regulate, reducing cortisol and promoting the release of sleep-inducing hormones. It's not just about feeling calm; it's about giving your body the physiological reset it craves after childbirth and sleepless nights.
