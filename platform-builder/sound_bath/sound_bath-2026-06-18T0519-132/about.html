How can a Sound Bath specifically help with postpartum sleep?

Sound Baths help regulate your nervous system, encouraging your brainwaves to slow down to alpha and theta states, which are associated with deep relaxation and sleep. For new mothers, this means an opportunity to quiet the mind, reduce stress hormones, and create a physiological environment more conducive to falling asleep and experiencing more restorative rest, even if it's not a full night's sleep.

Is it safe for me to attend a Sound Bath while I'm still recovering postpartum?

Absolutely. Sound Baths are a gentle, non-invasive practice that involves simply lying down and listening. We ensure a comfortable and supportive environment. If you have any specific medical concerns related to your postpartum recovery, we always recommend consulting with your healthcare provider before attending, but generally, it's a very safe and beneficial practice.

What should I expect during my first Sound Bath session?

You'll lie comfortably on a mat, often with blankets and pillows for support. Dr. Morgan Ellis will guide you through the experience, playing a variety of instruments. Your only task is to relax and allow the sounds to wash over you. Many experience a deep sense of calm, some may drift into a light sleep, and others might feel a gentle tingling or release of tension. The goal is deep relaxation and mental quiet.

How often should I attend a Sound Bath to see improvements in my rest?

The frequency can vary for everyone, but consistent engagement often yields the best results for sleep improvement. Many new mothers find that attending weekly or bi-weekly sessions helps to maintain a state of calm and supports ongoing sleep regulation. We can discuss personalized recommendations based on your unique needs and recovery journey.
