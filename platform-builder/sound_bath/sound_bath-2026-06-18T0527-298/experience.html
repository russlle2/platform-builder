Sound Bath

A Gentle Approach to Past Experiences with Sound

Our trauma-informed Sound Bath practice acknowledges that everyone carries their own history. We never ask you to revisit difficult memories directly. Instead, the carefully curated soundscapes provide a supportive space for your body to process and integrate past experiences at its own pace. This gentle approach helps reduce the physical and emotional burdens that can weigh on us, promoting a sense of lightness and renewed mental energy without re-traumatization.
