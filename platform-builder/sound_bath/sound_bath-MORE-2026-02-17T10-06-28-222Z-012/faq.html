Frequently Asked

What should I expect at a group session?
A guided sound sequence with marker phrases. Minimal movement required. You may sit or lie depending on the event listing. Facilitators offer cues for breath and re-orientation.
Can I attend if I have hearing sensitivity?
Yes — notify us before the session and we’ll accommodate lower volumes or seat placement. See contraindications below.
What's your cancellation policy?
Refunds available up to 48 hours before the event. For private sessions please contact us directly at {{EMAIL}}.
Do you accept walk-ins?
Most events require pre-booking. We occasionally open a limited number of same-day spots; check the event page for updates.

Contraindications & safety

Our sessions include repetitive tones and low-frequency components. Please avoid booking if you have any of the following without prior medical clearance: seizure disorders, recent head trauma, certain cardiovascular conditions, or implanted electronic medical devices. Pregnant participants should consult their clinician. Alert staff to any concerns before the session.
