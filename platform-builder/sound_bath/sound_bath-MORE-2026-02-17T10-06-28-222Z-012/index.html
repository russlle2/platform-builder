Quick questions

      

        What should I bring?
        
A mat or blanket, water, and anything for comfort. We provide cushions and blankets when needed.

      

      

        What if I startle during sound?
        
It’s common. Facilitators provide gentle signposts and space to re-orient. You can sit up or step out at any time.

      

      

        Are sessions appropriate for first-timers?
        
Yes. We keep first sessions shorter and framed to help people land in safety and ease.
