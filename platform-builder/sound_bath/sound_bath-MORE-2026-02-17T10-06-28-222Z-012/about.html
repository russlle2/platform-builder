Responsible care

Contraindications: Sound practices involve low-frequency vibration and prolonged listening. Please consult a medical professional if you have seizures, recent head injury, severe tinnitus, pregnancy concerns, or an implanted medical device. Inform facilitators of any cardiovascular or neurological conditions before booking.
