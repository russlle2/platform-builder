Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Sound Bath specifically help my posture during postpartum recovery?
Our Sound Bath sessions provide a unique opportunity for deep relaxation, which is crucial for releasing muscular tension that often contributes to poor posture postpartum. The resonant sounds encourage your body to settle into a more natural alignment, helping you to become more aware of how you hold yourself, especially when feeding or carrying your baby.
Is the Sound Bath safe for my recovering postpartum body?
Absolutely. Our Sound Bath sessions are completely passive and gentle. You'll lie comfortably, supported by props if needed, and simply receive the sounds. There's no physical exertion required, making it an ideal practice for your postpartum recovery period. We focus on creating a nurturing space that honors your body's current state.
I'm always tired as a new mom. Will I be able to stay awake during a Sound Bath?
It’s perfectly normal to feel tired, and many new mothers actually fall into a restorative sleep during a Sound Bath. Whether you remain awake or drift off, your body and mind will still receive the benefits of deep relaxation and stress reduction. Consider it a much-needed moment of peaceful rest and renewal, however it unfolds for you.
What should I wear or bring to my Sound Bath session?
Please wear comfortable, loose-fitting clothing that allows you to relax fully. We recommend bringing a water bottle to stay hydrated. You might also want to bring a favorite blanket or an eye mask for added comfort, though we provide mats and cushions. Our goal is for you to feel as supported and at ease as possible.
