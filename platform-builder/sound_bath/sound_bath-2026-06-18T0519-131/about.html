How can a Sound Bath specifically help with my burnout and sleep issues?

Our Sound Bath sessions are engineered to downregulate your nervous system, shifting your brainwaves from an active, stressed state to one of deep relaxation. This directly combats the physiological effects of burnout and prepares your body and mind for more restorative sleep, helping you wake up feeling genuinely refreshed.

Do I need any prior experience with meditation or sound therapy to benefit from a Sound Bath?

Absolutely not. Our Sound Bath experiences are designed to be accessible for everyone, regardless of prior experience. You simply need to arrive, lie down, and allow the sounds to work their subtle but profound effect. There’s no technique to learn, just a state of receptive relaxation to embrace.

What should I expect during a typical Sound Bath session?

You'll be invited to lie comfortably on a mat, often with blankets and pillows provided. Dr. Morgan Ellis will then guide you through a soundscape created with various instruments like gongs, singing bowls, and chimes. The sounds are not musical in a traditional sense, but rather a rich tapestry of frequencies intended to induce deep relaxation and help quiet your mind for better rest.

How often should I attend a Sound Bath to see improvements in my sleep and energy?

While even a single session can provide immediate relief from stress, consistent engagement often yields the most significant and lasting results for sleep improvement and burnout recovery. Many clients find that attending weekly or bi-weekly sessions helps maintain a sustained state of calm and significantly enhances their sleep quality over time.
