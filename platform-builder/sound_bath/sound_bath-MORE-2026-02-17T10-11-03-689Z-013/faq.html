Helpful questions

Short, direct answers to what most people ask before coming. If you need a tailored response, email {{EMAIL}}.

What should I expect at my first gathering?
A short intake, a guided settling, and sound layers. Most sessions last between 45 and 75 minutes. Expect low lights, soft mats, and moments of silence between sound layers.
Do I need experience with meditation or breathwork?
No. Sessions are accessible. Instructions invite simple breath patterns and gentle listening — you are always free to rest or open your eyes.
Can I move or leave early?
Yes. You are encouraged to listen to your body. If you need assistance during a session, discreetly signal a facilitator.
What about sound sensitivity or headphones?
We can adapt the experience. Many sounds are resonant, not loud. If you have heightened sensitivity, tell us during booking and we will reduce intensity or offer a seat further from instruments.
Is this a cure or medical treatment?
No. Sound and breath are supportive practices. If you have medical questions or conditions, consult your healthcare provider before attending.

Contraindications

Please note: If you have epilepsy, are pregnant, have had a recent head injury, use a pacemaker, or are currently under intensive psychiatric care, contact us before booking. Some forms of vibration, pulsed sound, and sustained low frequencies are not advised for these conditions. This note is informational and is not a substitute for medical advice.
