Why people come

Sound that creates space without asking for a life overhaul. You can come once, or come often — the format supports both.

  • Short and anchored — sessions run 45–70 minutes and end with simple integration guidance.
  • Accessible — easy postures, chair-friendly options, and clear contraindication guidance below.
  • Practical takeaways — breathing cues and a gentle journaling prompt to use after the session.

A brief view of how it feels

A short entrance, the main sound arc, then a quiet close. Our sessions are structured so you know when to arrive, how to settle, and how to re-enter your day.

  1. Gathering & gentle tuning (10–12 min)
  2. Sound arc with anchored breathing (30–40 min)
  3. Return, journaling prompt, and practical talk (5–10 min)
