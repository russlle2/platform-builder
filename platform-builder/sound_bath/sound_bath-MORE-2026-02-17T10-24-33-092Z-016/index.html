Our Approach

We combine tuned instruments, breath orientation, and gentle guidance so each session becomes a reliable container for rest and attention.

  • AnchorBreath-led settling and posture alignment
  • ToneLayered instruments tuned to support resonance
  • IntegrationShort reflection and movement to return to everyday life

Contraindications: Sound immersion can be intense. Please consult your healthcare provider if you have epilepsy, recent head injury, active psychosis, or are pregnant. Tell us about implants or devices that may be sensitive to vibration.
