Frequently asked questions

Practical answers for arriving with ease. If you still have questions, reach out at {{EMAIL}}.
What should I bring?

Bring a small blanket, a cushion if you prefer, and an open intention. We provide mats, bolsters, and eye covers on request.

How long is a session?

Formats vary. Short intensives run 30–40 minutes; community gatherings 60–75 minutes. Check the event listing for exact duration.

Is this a meditation class?

It's not a meditation class in the strict sense. Sound and breath guide attention and nervous system recalibration; there is no dogma or chanting required.

Are there health considerations?

Yes. Please see the contraindications note below and consult your clinician if you have concerns. If pregnant, have a pacemaker, or are epilepsy-prone, contact us before booking.

Contraindications: If you are pregnant, have an implanted electronic device (e.g., pacemaker), a history of epilepsy or seizure disorder, or are under heavy psychiatric medication, please consult your healthcare provider and contact our team prior to attendance. Some instruments produce strong vibrations which may not be suitable for all conditions.
