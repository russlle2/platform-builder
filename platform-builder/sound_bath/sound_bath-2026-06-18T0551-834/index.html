Frequently Asked Questions

How does a Sound Bath specifically benefit an athlete's posture?
A Sound Bath creates a deeply relaxed state where your nervous system can downregulate. This allows you to become more attuned to subtle sensations within your body, helping you identify areas of tension or misalignment you might otherwise overlook. It's about enhancing your proprioception to consciously refine your postural habits during and after activity.
Will a Sound Bath replace my existing physical therapy or recovery routine?
No, a Sound Bath is designed to complement your existing physical therapy, training, and recovery protocols. It's a powerful tool for stress reduction, nervous system regulation, and enhanced body awareness, all of which contribute to better recovery and performance. Always follow the advice of your medical and coaching professionals.
What should I expect to feel during a Sound Bath related to body awareness?
Many athletes report a heightened sense of their body's position in space and a clearer perception of areas holding tension. You might experience a release of muscular tightness, a feeling of lengthening in your spine, or simply a clearer internal 'map' of your musculoskeletal system, leading to improved body awareness in your daily movements and sport.
How frequently should I incorporate Sound Bath for optimal postural benefits?
Consistency is key for sustained benefits. We recommend starting with a weekly or bi-weekly session to establish a baseline of improved body awareness and stress reduction. As you become more attuned to your body's needs, you can adjust the frequency to best support your training cycles and recovery demands.
