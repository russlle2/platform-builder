How does a Sound Bath specifically help with chronic illness or injury recovery?

For those recovering from chronic illness or injury, the body often remains in a state of stress. Sound Bath sessions are designed to gently guide your nervous system into a parasympathetic (rest and digest) state, which is essential for cellular repair, inflammation reduction, and overall physical and mental restoration. It's a supportive, non-invasive method for managing the physiological impacts of chronic conditions.

Will the sounds be too intense if I have sensitivities due to my condition?

Our Sound Bath sessions are carefully calibrated to be gentle and therapeutic. We prioritize a calm and controlled sound environment, especially for individuals with sensitivities. Please communicate any concerns you have prior to your session, and we can discuss adjustments to ensure your comfort and safety during the experience.

What should I expect to feel during and after a Sound Bath?

During a Sound Bath, many people report feeling deeply relaxed, sometimes even drifting into a light sleep state. You might notice a decrease in physical tension or a quietening of your mind. Afterward, clients often describe feeling a sense of calm, clarity, and reduced stress, which can persist for several hours or even days, supporting your ongoing recovery process.

Is a Sound Bath a replacement for medical treatment for chronic conditions?

No, a Sound Bath is a complementary practice designed to support your existing medical treatment. It is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Our aim is to provide a supportive environment for stress reduction and nervous system regulation that can enhance your recovery journey in conjunction with your healthcare providers.
