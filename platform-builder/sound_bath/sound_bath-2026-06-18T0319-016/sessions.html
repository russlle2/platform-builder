Sound Bath: A Structured Approach to Stress Reduction

For individuals managing chronic illness or injury, persistent stress can hinder recovery. Our Sound Bath sessions provide a controlled environment where specific sound frequencies are used to encourage a state of deep relaxation. This precise application of sound aims to downregulate an overactive nervous system, moving you from a state of heightened alert to one of rest and repair, which is crucial for physical and mental restoration.
