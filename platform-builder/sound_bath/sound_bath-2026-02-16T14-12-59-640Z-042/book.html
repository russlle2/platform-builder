What to expect after booking

We will email confirmation and a short health checklist. For private sessions we will propose times and a deposit link.

Flow of a private session

Private sessions begin with a 10–15 minute intake, 50-minute sound journey, and 10–15 minutes for aftercare and integration. Options include 1:1, couples, and small corporate groups.

Instruments commonly used: gong (softly voiced), tuning forks for focal entrainment, and a monochord for sustained harmonic texture.

Before you arrive

  • Bring a mat/blanket and pillow; we have extras on request.
  • Wear layered, comfortable clothing to allow temperature shifts.
  • Arrive 10 minutes early for orientation and settling.

Contraindications & safety

Not advised without medical sign-off: uncontrolled epilepsy, recent head or ear surgery, or if you have a medical implant that could be affected by strong vibrations. If pregnant, tell us—some tonal choices are adapted.
