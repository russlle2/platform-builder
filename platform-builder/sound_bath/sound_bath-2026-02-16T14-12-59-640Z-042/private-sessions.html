Private sound sessions

Personalized tuning of breath, tone, and intention. Choose a format below: 1:1, couple, or corporate. Each experience begins with a short intake and an attuned sound design.

1:1 — Deep Listening
60 or 90 min. A single practitioner field with monochord, small crystal bowls, and focused tuning forks to meet your nervous system where it is.
Couples — Shared Resonance
90 min. Designed for shared intention and attunement with handpan undertones and paired bowls to explore relational presence.
Corporate — Team Reset
30–60 min sessions for teams, scalable. Subtle chimes, guided breath, and short sound immersion to lower stress and increase focus.

Session flow

  1. Intake & intention (5–10 min)
  2. Guided settling (breath + body scan)
  3. Main immersion (instrument sets tailored to needs)
  4. Slow return and integration, time for notes or tea

What to bring

A yoga mat or blanket, pillow, water, and any scent-free comforts. Wear layered clothing. For on-site bookings, we can provide mats on request.

Contraindications & safety

If you have implanted medical devices, are pregnant, have seizures, or severe cardiovascular conditions, please consult a care provider and inform us before booking. Private sessions can be modified for sensitivity.
