Flow of a typical session
We hold a gentle arc: arrival & settling; intention & short breathwork; the immersion (instruments unfold); guided return & tea. Expect 60–90 minutes, soft lighting, and comfortable reclining.
What to bring
A yoga mat, a cozy blanket, a small pillow, water, and anything that helps you relax (eye cover, socked feet). We provide bolsters and extra blankets on request.
Contraindications & safety
Sound can shift heart rate and sensation. Please disclose pregnancy, epilepsy, recent head trauma, pacemakers, or severe unmanaged anxiety. For clinical contraindications, consult your healthcare provider before attending. Contact us for alternatives and accommodations.
