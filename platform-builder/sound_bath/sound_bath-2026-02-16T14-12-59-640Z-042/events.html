What the evening feels like — gentle orchestration

We begin seated, settle with guided breath, and then recline as layers of tone move through the room. Expect long-held crystal bowl tones, soft chimes, and a culminating gong bloom for integration.

Before you arrive — practical notes

  • What to bring: mat, blanket, pillow, eye mask, water.
  • Dress: warm, layered, socks recommended.
  • Space: we provide bolsters and some mats but please bring what you prefer.
Contraindications

If you are pregnant, have uncontrolled epilepsy, severe cardiovascular conditions, or are concerned about intense vibrational experiences, consult your clinician. Inform the facilitator ahead of time.

Myth vs. truth

Myth: It’s hypnotic performance
Truth: It’s a guided, consensual field. You’re always in choice and present agency.
Myth: You must have a ‘spiritual’ background
Truth: All backgrounds welcome. The work is sensory and bodily, not dogmatic.

Instruments and color

Expect a curated mix: crystal singing bowls, a gentle gong, tuned chimes, and travel-sized tuning forks. Each instrument shapes a different register for relaxation.
