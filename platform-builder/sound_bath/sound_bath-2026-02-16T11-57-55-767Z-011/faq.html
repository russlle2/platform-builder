General questions

What should I expect in a sound bath?
A guided listening experience: arrival, short orientation, a 45–60 minute sound field with gradual build and release, and a gentle re-entry. Expect lowered breath rate and an expanded felt sense of resonance rather than loud music.
What instruments do you use?
For group work we often combine crystal singing bowls, gong undertones, and rainstick textures to create layered, enveloping soundscapes — chosen to support resonance and sustained listening.
I’m new — can I attend?
Yes. Sessions are accessible to first-timers. We provide clear orientation, options for seated or reclined positioning, and staff support for sensory sensitivities.

Practical details

What to bring?
A yoga mat or soft blanket, two pillows (one for under your knees), a light wrap, and optional earplugs if you prefer a gentler experience. We provide bolsters and extra blankets when possible.
Contraindications — what should I tell you?
If you are pregnant, have a pacemaker or other implanted medical device, recent head trauma, severe psychiatric conditions, or active seizures, please consult your physician and disclose during intake. Sound can be intense; we adapt when needed.

More health notes

People sensitive to loud, low-frequency sound may experience dizziness or nausea. We recommend arriving rested and hydrated. Notify staff of any hearing devices or implants.

Technical questions

Sessions are delivered live in a curated acoustic space. Remote participants receive a modified mix optimized for headphones: clear harmonics, careful EQ, and less tactile low-end to avoid discomfort.
