Who benefits

A hybrid model — restorative resonance that pairs measurable relaxation techniques with creative sonic textures.

  • Stress modulation

    Guided breathwork plus low-frequency bowls to support the autonomic nervous system.

  • Improved focus

    Targeted overtones and sustained harmonics to aid attentional reset.

  • Deep rest

    Extended landscape sections designed to facilitate nervous system downshift.

Session flow — what to expect

  1. Arrival & settling

    Quiet entry, orientation, and an intentional 10–15 minute grounding routine with breath cues.

  2. Sonic immersion

    Instruments used vary by event: crystal bowls, gong, chimes, and a monochord for layered harmonics. Facilitator-led transitions scaffold the experience.

  3. Integration

    Soft re-orientation and optional journaling time; we close with short spoken reflection and practical aftercare recommendations.

What to bring

Yoga mat or blanket, an eye-cover, a pillow, and water. Wear layers for temperature comfort.

Clinical FAQs

Are there contraindications?

How long is a typical group sound bath?
Group sessions usually run 60–75 minutes including arrival and integration time.
Can I fall asleep?
Yes — falling asleep can be a natural part of deep relaxation. We create a safe, low-light environment suitable for restorative rest.
