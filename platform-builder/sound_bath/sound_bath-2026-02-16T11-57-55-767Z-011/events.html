Practicals — what to bring & safety
Please bring a yoga mat or blanket, pillow, water bottle, and an eye mask if you wish. We provide mats on request but recommend your own for comfort.
Contraindications: Sound baths can be intense for those with epilepsy, severe PTSD, or those who are pregnant without prior consultation. If you have medical implants (e.g., pacemakers) or recent surgery, please consult your clinician and notify the facilitator before booking.
