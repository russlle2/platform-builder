Our sonic vocabulary

We select instruments to sculpt space: crystal singing bowls, koshi chimes, bowed monochord and hand-held tuning forks. Choices are intentional to create long, harmonically-rich textures that support breath and attention.

How a group session feels

1
Arrival & settling
Soft lighting, cushioned mats, brief orientation and an invitation to set intention.
2
Listening arc
Layered timbres build and release — a 45–60 minute sound landscape that supports physiological downshift.
3
Reintegration
Gentle reawakening with space for journaling or sharing; practical suggestions to extend benefits at home.

Curated for clarity

Sessions are crafted to regulate the nervous system. Expect intentional tempo, harmonic overlap, and pockets of silence as part of the therapeutic field.
