Practical info & safety

What should I bring?
Bring a mat or yoga pad if you prefer (we provide cushions and blankets). Comfortable clothing, an eye-cover or small towel, and water. If you use medical devices, bring documentation and notify the facilitator.
Are there contraindications?
Yes — please notify us if you are pregnant, have epilepsy, are taking medication affecting consciousness, have recent fractures, or have implanted electronic medical devices. Our facilitators perform a brief screening and can recommend a suitable modality or referral.
How long is a typical session?
Public sound baths usually run 60–75 minutes including arrival and integration. Private sessions vary — ask in your booking message.
