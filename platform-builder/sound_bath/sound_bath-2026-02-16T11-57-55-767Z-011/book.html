What to bring

  • Mat or blanket and an extra throw
  • Pillow(s) for head and knees
  • Layered clothing (rooms are meditative-warm)
  • Optional: journal and pen

Contraindications & safety

Please inform us if you have a pacemaker or other implanted device, are pregnant, have recent head injury, epilepsy, or severe psychiatric conditions. Sound can produce physiological effects; we accommodate and adapt sessions for safety.

Session flow (what will happen)

Check-in — brief intake to set intention and note accommodations.
Settling — guided breath and body awareness to orient to the field.
Sound arc — instruments such as tuning forks, bowed monochord, and chimes are layered over 45–75 minutes depending on format.
Closure — soft returns to baseline and suggestions for integration.
