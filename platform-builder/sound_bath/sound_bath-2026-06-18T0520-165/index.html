Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly happens during a Sound Bath?
During a Sound Bath, you'll lie down comfortably, often on a mat with blankets and pillows. will play various instruments around you, creating a landscape of resonant sounds. There's no water involved; it's an immersive experience where you simply listen and allow the sounds to facilitate deep relaxation, helping your body and mind unwind.
Will a Sound Bath really help with my sleep and college stress?
Absolutely. Many college students find that the continuous, soothing sounds in a Sound Bath help quiet the 'monkey mind' often caused by academic pressure and anxiety. This deep state of relaxation can recalibrate your nervous system, making it easier to fall asleep and experience more restorative rest, which in turn helps manage stress during your waking hours.
Do I need to do anything or have prior experience for a Sound Bath?
Not at all. A Sound Bath is a passive experience, meaning you don't need any special skills or prior knowledge. Just arrive, find a comfortable spot, and allow yourself to relax and receive the sounds. It's a perfect way to unplug and destress without any effort, ideal for busy college schedules.
What should I bring or wear to a Sound Bath session?
We recommend wearing comfortable, loose-fitting clothing that allows you to fully relax. You might also want to bring a water bottle, and if you have a favorite blanket or eye mask for extra comfort, feel free to bring those too. Our goal is for you to feel as cozy and at ease as possible, so you can fully immerse yourself in the experience.
