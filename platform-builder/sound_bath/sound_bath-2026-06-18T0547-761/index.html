Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Sound Bath specifically help me address burnout and relationship strain?
Our Sound Bath sessions are designed to gently guide your nervous system into a deep state of rest. This allows you to release accumulated stress and emotional fatigue, which often contribute to feeling disconnected in your relationships. By fostering inner calm, you'll find more capacity to engage with empathy and presence in your personal life.
I'm always busy; how long are the Sound Bath sessions, and will I truly be able to switch off?
Sessions are typically 60-75 minutes long, designed to fit into your demanding schedule while offering profound respite. Many working professionals like yourself find that the immersive nature of the Sound Bath makes it surprisingly easy to 'switch off' and experience deep relaxation, even on your first visit.
What should I expect during my first heart-centered Sound Bath experience?
You'll lie comfortably on a mat or cushion while a variety of instruments, like crystal bowls and gongs, are played around you. The experience is passive; simply relax and allow the sounds to wash over you. There's no right or wrong way to feel; just an opportunity to rest and reset your emotional and mental state.
Will a Sound Bath help me feel more connected and less irritable in my personal life?
Yes, many clients report feeling a greater sense of peace and emotional openness after a Sound Bath. By reducing your overall stress and irritability, you'll likely find yourself more present, patient, and available to the people and relationships that are important to you, fostering a more heart-centered approach to your daily interactions.
