What to expect

Each cohort session is curated with intention: gentle setup, a guided arrival, layered sound textures, and a soft landing. Sessions are social in structure but private in experience — you keep your own pace while following a shared arc.

  • Arrival & orientation (10–15 min)
  • Sound layering and focused practice (30–50 min)
  • Return, integration, and sharing (10–15 min)

Questions & concerns

Is this safe for everyone?

There are important contraindications. If you have epilepsy, severe cardiovascular conditions, recent concussions, or are pregnant, consult your healthcare provider before attending. If you take medication that affects balance or consciousness, please contact us to discuss accommodations. This is not a medical treatment; our facilitators are not clinicians.

I’m new — will I be given instructions?

Yes. Each cohort begins with brief orientation and options for sitting or lying. You remain in control of your posture and participation.

Pricing and group size

We frame pricing per cohort rather than per attendee for many events — this lets you treat sessions as a shared investment. Ask us for a tailored proposal.
