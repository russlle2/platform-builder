Our method

We blend sustained tones, textural chimes, and breath-friendly pacing to create a steady frame for rest. Sessions are taught with clear cues, so people of all ranges settle without pressure.

We call our program sessions "Cohorts"—small, scheduled groups with a facilitator and guided re-entry time.

Contraindications & safety

Sound sessions are gentle, but may not suit everyone. Please avoid participation or consult a clinician if you have:

  • recent head/ear surgery or perforated eardrum
  • epilepsy triggered by sensory input
  • severe psychiatric conditions without clinician approval
