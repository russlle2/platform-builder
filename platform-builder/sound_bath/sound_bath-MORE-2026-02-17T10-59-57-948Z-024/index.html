Common questions

        

          
Do I need to bring anything?

          
Wear comfortable clothing. We provide mats, blankets, eye covers, and cushions. You may bring a water bottle.

          
What if I fall asleep?

          
That can be a natural outcome. Gentle reorientation at the end makes space for integration — it’s fine.

          
Are sessions loud?

          
Sound is layered and attentive: shifts in volume happen but we prioritize comfort and will adjust for the group.
