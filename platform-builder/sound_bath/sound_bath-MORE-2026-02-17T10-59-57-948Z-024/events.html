What to expect

Rooms are calm and dimly lit. Sessions begin with simple orientation, followed by guided resonant sound. Expect periods of silence, and a careful close. Come as you are — you can sit, lie, or use the provided bolsters.

Accessibility

Contraindications: if you have uncontrolled epilepsy, recent major surgery, or are pregnant and have concerns, consult your medical provider before attending. For anyone with a history of severe PTSD or dissociation, consider checking in with a clinician first.

How we choose the next dates

We seed dates based on community demand and local availability. Spots are intentionally limited so cohorts stay intimate. If a date fills, add your name to the waitlist and we’ll notify you when a new cohort opens.
