Helpful notes & common questions

Short answers for quick planning. If your question isn't listed, reach out at {{EMAIL}} or call {{PHONE}}.

What should I bring?
A mat or blanket, a light pillow if you like, and water. We provide bolsters and ear-friendly cushions on request. Please avoid strong perfumes for the comfort of others.
Are sessions suitable for beginners?
Yes — our cohorts are paced for newcomers. Facilitators give clear verbal cues, and you are welcome to enter and leave the session quietly as needed.
Is there an age limit?
Most sessions are 16+. Private sessions can be arranged for younger participants with guardian presence. Contact us to arrange youth-appropriate pacing.
Payment & refunds
Cohort credits are issued for cancellations at least 24 hours before the session. For private bookings, different terms apply — see the booking form on the Reserve page.
Noise & sensitivity concerns
If you have sound sensitivity, let us know before the session — we can offer seating further from instruments and lower overall volume. For medical questions, consult your clinician.

Contraindications — read before booking

While sessions are gentle, please do not attend without prior clinician approval if you have:

  • recent ear or cranial surgery
  • a history of seizures provoked by sensory input
  • unstable cardiac or psychiatric conditions
