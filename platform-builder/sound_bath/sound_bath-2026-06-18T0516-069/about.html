How does a Sound Bath specifically help parents reduce daily household stress?

A Sound Bath offers a dedicated time for your nervous system to rest and reset. For parents juggling household demands, the immersive sounds help to quiet the constant mental to-do lists, lower cortisol levels, and encourage a shift from 'fight or flight' to a more relaxed state, making you feel less frazzled and more grounded in your daily life.

Do I need any special experience or preparation for a Sound Bath?

Not at all! You don't need any prior experience with meditation or sound practices. Just come as you are, dressed comfortably. We provide everything you need to lie back and simply receive the sounds. It's a gentle, passive experience designed for ease and relaxation, perfect for busy parents who need a break.

Will I be able to fully relax in a Sound Bath if my mind is constantly thinking about my children?

It's completely normal for your mind to be busy, especially as a parent. The beauty of a Sound Bath is that the continuous, gentle soundscape gives your mind something soft to focus on, naturally drawing you away from thoughts about your children or daily tasks. Many parents find that even if their mind wanders initially, the sounds gradually lead them to a deeper state of calm and presence.

How often should I attend a Sound Bath to see benefits for my stress levels?

While even a single Sound Bath can offer immediate relief, regular attendance can build resilience and strengthen your nervous system's ability to regulate stress over time. Many parents find that attending once or twice a month provides consistent support, helping them maintain a more balanced and calm demeanor amidst the daily rhythms of family life.
