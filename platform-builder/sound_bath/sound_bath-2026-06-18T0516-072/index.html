Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is a Sound Bath and how does it help new mothers?
A Sound Bath is an immersive experience where you are bathed in therapeutic sound vibrations from instruments like singing bowls and gongs. For new mothers, it's designed to down-regulate your nervous system, reducing the 'fight or flight' response often triggered by postpartum stress and sleep deprivation. This deep relaxation supports your physical recovery and emotional well-being.
Is a Sound Bath safe for me during my postpartum recovery period?
Absolutely. Our Sound Bath sessions are gentle and non-invasive, making them perfectly safe and beneficial for mothers in all stages of postpartum recovery. We prioritize your comfort and can accommodate any specific needs you may have, ensuring a nurturing and supportive environment for your rest.
I'm always exhausted. Will I be able to fully relax during a Sound Bath?
Yes, even if you arrive feeling utterly exhausted, the soothing frequencies are designed to effortlessly guide you into a state of deep rest. Many new mothers find themselves drifting into a restorative sleep-like state during a Sound Bath, which is incredibly beneficial for recovery and stress reduction. You don't need to 'do' anything, just allow the sounds to wash over you.
How often should I attend a Sound Bath to see results for my stress and nervous system?
Even a single Sound Bath can offer profound relaxation. For sustained benefits in stress reduction and nervous system regulation, we recommend regular sessions, perhaps weekly or bi-weekly. Consistency helps to re-pattern your nervous system, building a stronger foundation of calm and resilience as you navigate new motherhood.
