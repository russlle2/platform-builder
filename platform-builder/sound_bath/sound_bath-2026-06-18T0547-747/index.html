Frequently Asked Questions

How does a Sound Bath specifically help with the stress entrepreneurs and high-achievers face?
Our Sound Bath sessions are designed to interrupt the constant mental chatter and 'on' state that high-achievers experience. The resonant frequencies guide your brain into deeper, more restorative states, reducing cortisol levels and fostering mental clarity. This allows you to step away from the pressure and return with a refreshed perspective, crucial for decision-making and managing demanding schedules.
Can a Sound Bath really help me with relationship healing, even if I feel disconnected?
Yes, absolutely. Relationship healing often begins with your own inner state. When you're stressed and overwhelmed, your capacity for empathy and connection diminishes. A Sound Bath helps you release tension and cultivate a more open, compassionate heart space. This internal shift empowers you to approach your relationships with greater patience, understanding, and authentic presence, fostering deeper bonds.
I'm new to this. Will I feel out of place in your Sound Bath community?
Not at all. Our Sound Bath community is built on inclusivity and a welcoming spirit. We understand that exploring new paths to well-being can feel unfamiliar, especially for those accustomed to traditional methods of stress management. We provide a gentle introduction, explain everything clearly, and create a safe space where you can simply relax and receive the benefits without any expectation or judgment.
What should I expect during a Sound Bath session, and how do I prepare?
During a Sound Bath, you'll lie comfortably on a mat while plays various instruments like singing bowls and gongs. Your only job is to relax and let the sounds wash over you. There's no right or wrong way to experience it. We recommend wearing comfortable clothing, arriving with an open mind, and avoiding heavy meals right before the session. Simply show up as you are, and we’ll guide you through the rest.
