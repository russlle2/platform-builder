Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Sound Bath specifically help me reduce stress during a big life change?
A Sound Bath uses specific sound frequencies to entrain your brainwaves, shifting them from a high-stress beta state to more relaxed alpha and theta states. This physiological change directly reduces cortisol levels, quiets an overactive mind, and allows your nervous system to reset, helping you manage the emotional and physical demands of transition more effectively.
What should I expect during my first Sound Bath session for nervous system regulation?
You'll lie comfortably on a mat while plays a variety of instruments. The sounds are designed to be immersive, not jarring. Your only task is to relax and allow the vibrations to wash over you. Many clients report feeling deeply calm, centered, and experiencing a significant reduction in mental chatter, which is crucial for nervous system recovery.
Is Sound Bath a suitable practice if I feel overwhelmed by my current life transition?
Absolutely. Feeling overwhelmed is a common response to significant change. Our Sound Bath sessions are specifically designed to provide a gentle, non-verbal method for you to release that overwhelm. The consistent, rhythmic sounds offer an anchor, helping your nervous system find its way back to a state of balance and peace, giving you renewed clarity.
How frequently should I attend Sound Bath sessions to support my nervous system during a transition?
The frequency depends on your individual needs and the intensity of your transition. Many clients find weekly or bi-weekly sessions to be most effective in establishing and maintaining nervous system regulation. Consistency helps reinforce the calming effects, building your resilience and empowering you to navigate your changes with greater ease.
