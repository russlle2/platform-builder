Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly happens during a Sound Bath?
During a Sound Bath, you'll lie down comfortably, often on a mat with blankets and pillows. I'll then play various instruments like crystal singing bowls and gongs. You just relax and let the sounds wash over you. There's no active participation needed; your only role is to rest and receive.
I'm always busy; how can a Sound Bath help my productivity?
A Sound Bath offers a crucial reset for your mind. By reducing stress and quieting mental noise, you often return to your work with heightened focus, improved clarity, and a fresh perspective. Think of it as a strategic pause that ultimately enhances your overall efficiency and creativity.
Do I need any special experience or preparation for a Sound Bath?
Absolutely not! This practice is designed to be accessible for everyone, regardless of your background in wellness or meditation. Just come as you are, dressed in comfortable clothing, and be open to a new experience. That's all you need.
What if I can't 'turn off my brain' during the session?
It's completely normal for your mind to wander, especially when you're accustomed to constant activity. The beauty of a Sound Bath is that the sounds themselves do the work of gently guiding your brainwaves into a more relaxed state. Even if your thoughts are active, you'll still experience the benefits of deep relaxation and a nervous system reset.
