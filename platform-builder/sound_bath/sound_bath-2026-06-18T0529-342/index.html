Frequently Asked Questions

Is a Sound Bath safe and beneficial for someone in postpartum recovery?
Absolutely! Our Sound Bath sessions are designed with the specific needs of postpartum individuals in mind. The gentle vibrations and calming sounds can help reduce muscle tension, ease discomfort, and support your nervous system as it rebalances after childbirth. It's a non-invasive way to encourage physical and emotional rest.
How can a Sound Bath specifically address my physical pain from childbirth or caring for my baby?
The low-frequency vibrations from the instruments can penetrate soft tissues, promoting relaxation in muscles that might be tight from labor, breastfeeding positions, or carrying your baby. Many new mothers report relief from back pain, shoulder stiffness, and general body aches as the sound encourages deep muscular release and improved circulation.
I feel disconnected from my body after having a baby. How does somatic healing through Sound Bath help with this?
Somatic healing focuses on the mind-body connection. During a Sound Bath, the vibrations help you become more aware of your body's sensations without judgment. This increased awareness can help you re-integrate your physical experiences, acknowledging changes and fostering a sense of acceptance and comfort within your postpartum body, promoting a stronger sense of self.
What if I need to feed or tend to my baby during a Sound Bath session?
We prioritize your comfort and your baby's needs. We encourage you to communicate any concerns beforehand. While most group sessions are designed for individual focus, private sessions can often accommodate your specific situation. Our space is inclusive, and we understand the realities of postpartum life. Please reach out to to discuss your unique needs.
