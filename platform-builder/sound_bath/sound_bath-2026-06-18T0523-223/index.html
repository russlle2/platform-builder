Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly happens during a Sound Bath session?
During a Sound Bath, you'll recline comfortably, perhaps on a mat with blankets and pillows. will then play various instruments like singing bowls and chimes, creating an immersive soundscape. There's nothing for you to 'do' except to listen and allow the sounds to wash over you, encouraging deep relaxation and a sense of calm within your being.
Is a Sound Bath suitable for someone with mobility limitations?
Absolutely. Our Sound Bath sessions are designed to be accessible and gentle. You can choose to lie down on a comfortable mat, sit in a chair, or whatever position brings you the most ease. We prioritize your comfort and can make accommodations to ensure your experience is as relaxing and supportive as possible. Please communicate any specific needs you may have.
I've carried emotional burdens for a long time. Can a Sound Bath truly help?
Many individuals find a Sound Bath to be profoundly helpful for emotional release and finding peace with past experiences. While it doesn't replace therapy, the deep relaxation induced by the sounds can create space for gentle emotional processing. It's about offering a safe and nurturing environment for your spirit to find comfort and release without feeling overwhelmed or pressured.
What should I bring or wear to a Sound Bath?
We recommend wearing comfortable, loose-fitting clothing that allows you to relax fully. You might also consider bringing a water bottle to stay hydrated. We provide mats, blankets, and pillows, but if you have a favorite personal blanket or cushion that brings you extra comfort, you are welcome to bring it along.
