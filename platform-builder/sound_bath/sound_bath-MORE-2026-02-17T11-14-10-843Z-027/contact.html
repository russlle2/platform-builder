Contraindications & safety

Sound baths are deeply restorative for many, but not everyone. If you have epilepsy, recent head injury, are pregnant (first trimester), have severe psychiatric conditions, pacemaker or similar implanted devices, or any concern about intense vibration/pressure, please consult a clinician before attending and let us know. Contact us to discuss reasonable accommodations.
