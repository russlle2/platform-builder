Common questions

Are there contraindications?
People with epilepsy, recent cochlear surgery, or strong sound sensitivity should consult a clinician. If you have medical concerns please reach out before attending.
Can I pause my membership?
Yes — you can pause or cancel through your account settings or contact us directly.
