About the method

Our sessions are an intentional container: warm tones, measured pacing, and simple invitations to restore breath and attention. We combine long-form harmonic textures with guided focal moments so you return to daily life steadier.

Contraindications & safety

Sound immersion is gentle but not always suitable. If you have recent head injury, epilepsy, pregnancy concerns, severe anxiety triggered by sound, or active substance effects, please consult your healthcare provider and contact us before booking.
