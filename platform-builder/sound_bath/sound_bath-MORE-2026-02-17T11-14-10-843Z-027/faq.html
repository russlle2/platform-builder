Questions people ask

  • What should I bring?
    A mat, blanket, a small cushion for comfort, water, and anything you use to stay warm. Avoid heavy fragrances; you may want a small notebook or pen for integration notes.
  • How long are gatherings?
    Most group sessions run 60–75 minutes: a brief arrival, a sustained sound field, and a short closing to assist reorientation.
  • Are sessions suitable for nervousness or insomnia?
    Many people find the practice calming; however, if you have severe panic disorder, trauma that may be triggered by sustained tones, or are undergoing certain kinds of therapy, discuss with us before booking.
  • Do you accept walk-ins?
    We prioritize members and reservations. If space allows we may accept walk-ins; check the Gatherings page for the next event and capacity.
  • What is a membership ('Circle')?
    Our Circle plans offer regular access at a reduced rate, priority bookings, and member-only sessions. Visit Circle Plans to see tiers.

On safety

Please consult a medical professional if you have epilepsy, recent concussion, are in early pregnancy, or have sound-sensitive conditions. If in doubt, contact us before registering.
