Sound Bath

Trauma-Informed Soundscapes: Respecting Your Postpartum Experience

Every Sound Bath is designed with a deep understanding of the unique sensitivities new mothers may experience. We utilize specially selected instruments and compositions to create soundscapes that are deeply calming and non-intrusive, honoring your personal boundaries and emotional state. This trauma-informed approach ensures a deeply restorative experience, supporting your nervous system in finding a profound sense of peace and stability.
