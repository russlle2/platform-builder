Frequently Asked Questions

Is a Sound Bath safe for my postpartum body, especially after delivery?
Absolutely. Our Sound Bath sessions are incredibly gentle and non-invasive, making them ideal for postpartum recovery. You'll be resting comfortably in a supported position, and the vibrations are subtle and soothing, never jarring. Many new mothers find it deeply relaxing and beneficial for easing physical and emotional strain after childbirth.
How can a Sound Bath help with the specific physical pains I'm feeling as a new mother?
The resonant frequencies from the instruments can help to relax tight muscles and connective tissues, which often bear the brunt of pregnancy and birth. This can reduce discomfort in areas like your lower back, hips, shoulders, and neck. It encourages your body to release tension and promotes a sense of ease, supporting your physical recovery.
What should I expect during a Sound Bath session at ?
You'll lie down comfortably on a mat, often with blankets and pillows for support. I'll then play various instruments like singing bowls and gongs, creating a rich soundscape. Your only job is to relax and allow the sounds and vibrations to wash over you. Most mothers experience a deep sense of calm, and some even drift into a light sleep, which is perfectly normal and restorative.
I'm always exhausted. Will a Sound Bath truly help me feel more rested?
Yes, many new mothers report feeling significantly more rested and rejuvenated after a Sound Bath. The deep relaxation induced by the sounds helps to calm your nervous system, which is crucial for genuine rest. Even if you don't sleep, the meditative state can be as restorative as several hours of sleep, helping to combat the profound fatigue of postpartum life.
