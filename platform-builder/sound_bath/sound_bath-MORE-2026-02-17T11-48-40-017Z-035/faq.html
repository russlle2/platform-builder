Common questions

If you don’t find what you need here, email {{EMAIL}} or call {{PHONE}}.

What should I bring?
Most people bring a mat or cushion, a light blanket, and anything making them comfortable (eye mask, small bolster). We recommend layers — spaces can be cool.
How loud is it?
Sound is intentionally gentle but can include low frequencies. We avoid sudden loud bangs. If you are sensitive to vibration, choose a seat near the door or request a softer mix by email.
Are sessions therapeutic?
Sessions are not therapy. Facilitators are not therapists unless explicitly stated. If you are in active mental health treatment, check with your provider to confirm soundwork is appropriate for you.
Cancellation & refunds
Refunds are available up to 48 hours before the event. Within 48 hours we may offer credit for a future session depending on circumstance.
Contraindications and safety
Please do not attend if you have medical devices that can be affected by sustained vibrations without medical clearance. If you have epilepsy, recent cranial surgery, or are pregnant, consult your care provider first. If you experience intense dissociation, consider attending with a companion or choosing a private session so we can adapt the environment.
