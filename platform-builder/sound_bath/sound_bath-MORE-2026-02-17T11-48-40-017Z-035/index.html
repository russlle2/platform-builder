Stories beneath the sound

We think of a session as a short story: arriving, a move inward, and a gentle return. Each meeting combines tuned bowls, guided intention, and space for private reflection. The frame is simple: small group, soft lighting, and a steady arc of sound that invites noticing.

Contraindications: If you are pregnant, have uncontrolled epilepsy, pacemaker or other implanted medical device, or are currently under strong psychiatric medication changes, consult your clinician before attending. If you have specific concerns, contact us at {{EMAIL}}.

How we gather

  • Brief welcome + intention setting
  • Layered resonant instruments (bowls, chimes, gentle drums)
  • Silent return with journaling prompts

Our session structure

Ground (10–15 min)

Settling into breath and noticing the body as a home for sound.

Field (30–40 min)

Longer resonant passages create a surface for attention to rest.

Return (10–15 min)

A short journaling prompt and shared close to bring insights into daily life.
