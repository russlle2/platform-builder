We gather sound around simple beginnings

A compact explanation of how we shape a session: tuned layers, a small script of intention, and a few held rules that keep everyone safe and clear.

What to expect

We open with a brief welcome, provide a short intention prompt, and offer 45–70 minutes of guided sound — bowls, chimes, and soft drones. Our focus is on supporting rest, reflection, and gentle re-patterning. If you arrive with expectations, we invite curiosity; if you arrive in need, we offer presence.

Our approach

We treat each event like a short ceremony: clear instructions, a small compass for intention, and sound arranged so each person can rest or attend. Facilitators are trained in listening, not in therapy; if you are working with medical or psychological needs, please consult your care provider before joining.

Why these rooms matter

We think of sound as a companion to attention: a way to hold stillness while feelings uncoil. The environment we architect is intentionally small — low lighting, guided pacing, and minimal talk. Our aim is to make it easy to arrive and to leave changed, if only by a degree.

Accessibility & care notes

We welcome neurodiverse attendees and offer adaptations. However, some situations are contraindicated: if you have epilepsy (seizures triggered by sound or light), recent head/ear surgery, are in the first trimester of pregnancy without medical sign-off, or have implanted medical devices that might be affected by sustained vibrations, please consult your healthcare provider before attending. If you are managing severe anxiety, PTSD, or dissociation, consider joining with a support person or booking a private session to adapt pacing and proximity.
