What exactly happens during a Sound Bath, especially for seniors focused on sleep?

During a Sound Bath at Sound Bath Studio, you will recline comfortably, fully clothed, as Dr. Morgan Ellis plays various instruments like singing bowls and gongs. The gentle sounds and vibrations create an environment where your mind can quiet and your body can deeply relax, preparing your system for more restful sleep. Many find it's like a deep, calming meditation without needing to 'do' anything but listen.

Is a Sound Bath safe and appropriate for someone my age, particularly if I have health considerations?

Absolutely. Our Sound Bath sessions are designed to be gentle and non-invasive, making them very suitable for seniors. We prioritize your comfort and well-being. If you have specific health concerns, it's always wise to mention them to Dr. Morgan Ellis beforehand, but typically, the gentle nature of sound relaxation is very beneficial and safe.

How often should I attend a Sound Bath to see improvements in my sleep and rest?

Many of our clients experience immediate relaxation from their very first Sound Bath. For noticeable and sustained improvements in sleep and overall restorative rest, we often recommend regular sessions, perhaps once a week or bi-weekly. Consistency helps your body learn to relax more deeply and efficiently, building on the benefits over time.

What should I bring or wear to my Sound Bath session to maximize my comfort for better sleep?

We recommend wearing comfortable, loose-fitting clothing that allows you to fully relax. You don't need to bring anything specific, as we provide mats, blankets, and pillows. However, if you have a favorite small pillow or eye mask that enhances your comfort, please feel free to bring it along to help you settle in for a truly restorative experience.
