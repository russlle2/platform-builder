Frequently Asked Questions

How does a Sound Bath specifically support a new mother's immune system?
Our Sound Bath sessions induce a profound state of relaxation, which directly lowers stress hormones like cortisol. Chronic stress suppresses the immune system. By regularly engaging in deep relaxation through sound, you help your body shift into a 'rest and digest' state, allowing your immune system to function more optimally and protect you during this demanding postpartum period.
I'm experiencing significant fatigue; how can Sound Bath help me rebuild my vitality?
Fatigue often stems from both physical exertion and mental overload. Our Sound Bath addresses both by providing a space for deep rest and mental quietude. The soothing vibrations help to calm your nervous system, allowing your body to conserve and redirect energy towards cellular repair and replenishment. Many new mothers report feeling more rested and energetically balanced after their sessions.
Is Sound Bath safe for me if I'm still physically recovering from childbirth?
Absolutely. Sound Bath is a non-invasive, gentle practice. You'll lie comfortably, fully clothed, and simply receive the sounds. There's no physical exertion required. We prioritize your comfort and can adjust your positioning as needed to ensure a safe and restorative experience, regardless of your stage of physical postpartum recovery.
How often should I attend Sound Bath sessions to see a benefit in my postpartum recovery?
Consistency is key for building sustained benefits. Many new mothers find that weekly or bi-weekly sessions are most effective in providing ongoing immune support, vitality building, and emotional recalibration. We can discuss a personalized plan that fits your schedule and recovery needs during your consultation.
