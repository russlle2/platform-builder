Sound Bath

Experience Deeper Body Awareness with Sound

Imagine lying comfortably, surrounded by soft, resonant tones. During a Sound Bath, the vibrations from instruments like singing bowls and gongs create a gentle internal massage for your body. This sensory experience helps quiet the mind, allowing you to focus inward and become more attuned to your physical sensations. This increased body awareness can help you recognize subtle cues your body gives you throughout the day, supporting better posture and reducing discomfort. It’s a peaceful way to check in with yourself.
