How it helps

Multi-sensory resonance supports nervous system regulation and encourages embodied rest.

  • Improved sleep and slower heart rate
  • Reduced stress markers and softened tension
  • Heightened body awareness and mental clarity
  • Community-held space for reflection and letting go

Session flow

  1. Welcome & gentle intention setting (5–10 min)
  2. Short guided breath to ground the body (5 min)
  3. Sound immersion — layered instruments and guided cues (45–60 min)
  4. Soft re-entry: light movement, tea, shared space (10–15 min)
Contraindications: If you are pregnant, have a history of seizures, uncontrolled epilepsy, or a pacemaker/implant, consult your physician before attending. Sound can affect vestibular sensitivity—sit near the room edge if concerned.

Quick FAQs
