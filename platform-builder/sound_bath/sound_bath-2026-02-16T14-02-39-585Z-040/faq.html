What should I bring and wear?

Comfortable, layered clothing; a mat or blanket if you prefer; an eye mask is optional. We have bolsters and blankets if you need them.

What instruments do you use?

We rotate instruments to keep each immersion distinct. Expect crystal singing bowls, a soft gong, Himalayan chimes, and occasional tuning forks tuned for somatic resonance.

Is it safe with medical conditions?

Sound work is low-impact but not without considerations. Contraindications include pregnancy in late stages without clearance, epilepsy, recent head trauma, or implanted electronic medical devices. Always consult your physician when unsure.

How long are sessions?

Group sessions are typically 60–75 minutes total (arrival + immersion + reorientation). Private sessions vary—see pricing.

Will I fall asleep?

Many guests drift into sleep; that's a natural, restorative response. You will be gently re-oriented at the end, and given time to come back.

A note on sound intensity

Our approach favors depth over volume. We sculpt harmonic layers rather than rely on high decibel impact. If you are sensitive to sound, let us know in advance—adaptive positioning and ear protection can be arranged.

Flow of a typical gathering

  • Arrival & brief orientation — settle in.
  • Guided breath + opening tones.
  • Main immersion with evolving instrument textures.
  • Slow return with grounding tones and reflection.
