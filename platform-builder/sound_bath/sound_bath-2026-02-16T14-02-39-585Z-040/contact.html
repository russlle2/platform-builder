Questions + considerations

Are sound baths safe?
For most people, yes. We design sessions to be gentle and non-invasive. If you have any concerns related to epilepsy, pregnancy (first trimester in particular), implanted medical devices (e.g., pacemakers), or severe psychiatric conditions, please consult your physician and notify us in advance. This is not medical advice; it's a safety-minded recommendation.
What if I have trouble lying down or need accommodation?
We can adapt to seated positions or shorter sessions. Tell us in the form or call and we will plan a comfortable experience.
How long is a typical session?
Most group baths run 60–75 minutes; private sessions vary (45–90 minutes) depending on goals.
