Formats & flow

Each private session is curated to the client’s threshold and intention. Below are common frames — we adapt on the day.

1:1 Session

  1. Intake & intention (5–10 min)
  2. Guided centering and conscious breath (5 min)
  3. Sound immersion with targeted instruments (40–60 min)
  4. Gentle re-entry and reflections (5–10 min)

Couples & Pairs

Shared soundbeds and alternating focus points — a connective listening container that supports communication and presence.

Corporate & Team

We design short rituals (20–40 min) for onboarding into a creative flow or debriefing after intensive work. Includes brief facilitation and a grounding audio for the team.

Contraindications: If you have a pacemaker, a history of epilepsy, or are pregnant, please consult with a physician before booking. Some instruments may produce low-frequency vibrations—inform the facilitator of any sensitivity.

Instruments & sonic palette

  • Hz-tuned singing bowls (quartz & ceramic)
  • Tuning forks for targeted somatic points
  • Monochord strings for continuous harmonic drone
  • Soft chimes for breath and punctuation

What to bring

A yoga mat or soft surface, a blanket, a water bottle, and if you like, a scarf or eye cushion. Wear comfortable layers.
