A sensory note and contraindications

Sound is a somatic medium. While many find sessions restorative, people with the following should consult a medical professional before attending: epilepsy, recent head injury, certain implanted devices, or if you are currently under treatment where sensory overstimulation is a concern. If pregnant, please inform us when booking so we can advise modifications.
