Why a sound bath helps

  • Deep relaxation

    Slow, enveloping tones encourage parasympathetic activation and release of tension.

  • Focused clarity

    Layered harmonics—crystal bowls, tuning forks, and chimes—help dissolve looped thinking.

  • Somatic integration

    Guided breathwork and reclining posture create a safe container to land into the body.

What to expect — our flow

  1. Arrival + Settle

    Soft lighting, mats spaced gently. We invite quiet; you can arrive early to ground.

  2. Intention + Breath

    A short guided breathing sequence to orient the nervous system and set a personal intention.

  3. Sound Immersion

    30–45 minutes of uninterrupted tones. Instruments vary by session: crystal bowls, gong, monochord, and chimes are used thoughtfully.

  4. Slow Return

    We conclude with gentle silence, tea, and space to integrate before departing.

What to bring

A yoga mat, blanket, pillow for neck support, eye shade if you prefer, and water.

Frequently asked

How long is a session?

Group sound baths are typically 60–90 minutes including arrival and closing. Private sessions vary by format.

Are sessions loud?

Not necessarily. Dynamics are curated; some instruments like the gong may produce louder overtones but are played mindfully to support ease.

Can I bring a partner?

Yes — we offer couples and 1:1 private formats. See Private sessions for options.
