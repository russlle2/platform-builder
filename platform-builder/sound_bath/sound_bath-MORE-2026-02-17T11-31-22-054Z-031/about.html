Why this works

Our practice uses predictable structure to lower cognitive load: clear openings, tonal scaffolding during the session, and a brief closing ritual. The result is a short, reliable container for changing how your nervous system settles.

Contraindications

If you have active ear infections, recent cranial injury, severe uncontrolled epilepsy, or are pregnant and have been advised to avoid sound therapy, please consult a clinician before attending.
