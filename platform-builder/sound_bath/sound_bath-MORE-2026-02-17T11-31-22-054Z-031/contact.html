Quick questions & contraindications

Sound experiences are deeply restorative for many people. Please consult with your medical provider if you have any of the following: epilepsy or seizure disorders, uncontrolled cardiac conditions, new pregnancy without medical clearance, implanted devices sensitive to vibrational exposure, or severe psychiatric conditions that may be affected by immersive auditory stimulation.
