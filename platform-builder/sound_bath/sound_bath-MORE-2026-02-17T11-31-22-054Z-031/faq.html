Frequently Asked

Answers to common questions and a small interactive packing helper so you don’t forget essentials on the day.

What should I bring?
We provide mats and bolsters on request, but many people bring their own mat, blanket, eye cover and a bottle of water. Use the packing helper on the right to create a checklist.
Is sound safe for everyone?
Most people find sessions calming. Please do not attend if you have a current ear infection, recent head trauma, or severe uncontrolled epilepsy without clinician clearance. If you are pregnant, consult your healthcare provider. If you have concerns, contact us at {{EMAIL}} or call {{PHONE}}.
Arrival & late policy
Arrive 10–15 minutes early for check-in. Late entry is limited to preserve the experience for others; in some cases you may be asked to wait for the next opening.
Payment & cancellation
Payments are taken at booking. To cancel or reschedule, please notify us at least 24 hours in advance to receive a credit. For VIP Day and private bookings, different terms apply; see our Invest page for details.
Do you accept group or corporate bookings?
Yes — we offer tailored private immersions and workshops. Reach out via the contact form or email {{EMAIL}} for a consult.
