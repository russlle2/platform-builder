Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is a Sound Bath and how does it help with physical stress?
A Sound Bath is a deeply relaxing experience where you lie down and are immersed in the sounds and vibrations produced by various instruments like gongs and singing bowls. For active individuals, it helps by calming your nervous system, which often goes into overdrive with training and competition. This deep relaxation aids in releasing tension, both physical and emotional, that accumulates from demanding physical activity.
Do I need any special experience or preparation to attend a Sound Bath?
Absolutely not! Our Sound Bath sessions are designed to be accessible for everyone, regardless of prior experience with wellness practices. You just need to show up in comfortable clothes, and we provide everything else. There's no right or wrong way to experience it; simply allow yourself to relax and receive the sounds.
How does Sound Bath address the emotional impact of injuries or setbacks?
Injuries and setbacks can be incredibly frustrating and emotionally challenging for athletes. Sound Bath offers a gentle, non-verbal way to process these feelings. The calming vibrations can help reduce anxiety and a sense of helplessness, creating a space for emotional release and acceptance. It supports your body’s natural ability to restore equilibrium, aiding in mental recovery alongside physical healing.
Will a Sound Bath make me feel sleepy or too relaxed to continue my day?
While deep relaxation is a primary benefit, most people report feeling refreshed and clear-headed after a Sound Bath, rather than overly sleepy. The experience helps to reset your nervous system, often leading to improved focus and a sense of calm energy. You'll likely feel more centered and better equipped to handle the demands of your day or prepare for your next activity.
