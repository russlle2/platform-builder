Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Sound Bath specifically support my postpartum recovery?
A Sound Bath offers new mothers a unique opportunity to deeply relax, which is vital for physical and emotional healing after childbirth. The specific sound frequencies help to calm the nervous system, reduce stress, and promote a sense of spiritual grounding, aiding your return to inner balance amidst the changes of motherhood.
Is a Sound Bath safe for new mothers, especially if I'm still feeling sensitive?
Yes, our Sound Baths are gentle and safe. We create a nurturing environment with soft sounds and comfortable positioning, mindful of your postpartum body and any sensitivities. It's a passive experience where you simply receive the vibrations, designed to support rather than overwhelm your system.
What if I can't fully relax during the Sound Bath because my mind is always thinking about my baby?
It's completely normal for your thoughts to drift, especially as a new mother. The beauty of a Sound Bath is that you don't need to 'try' to relax. The sounds work on a subconscious level, gently inviting your mind and body into a state of calm. Even if your thoughts wander, you'll still experience the benefits of deep vibrational healing and spiritual comfort.
How often should I attend a Sound Bath to see spiritual benefits in my postpartum period?
Consistency is key for deeper spiritual integration. Many new mothers find that regular Sound Bath sessions, perhaps once a week or every two weeks, help sustain their sense of inner peace and spiritual connection during their postpartum recovery. However, even a single session can offer significant respite and re-centering.
