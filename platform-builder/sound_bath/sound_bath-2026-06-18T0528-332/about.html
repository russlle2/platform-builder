How can a Sound Bath specifically help my postpartum physical pain?

Our Sound Bath sessions utilize specific resonant frequencies known to reduce muscle tension, alleviate joint stiffness, and decrease inflammatory responses often associated with postpartum recovery. The gentle vibrations penetrate deep tissues, promoting physical release and improved comfort.

Is a Sound Bath safe if I'm still recovering from birth, including C-section?

Yes, Sound Bath is a non-invasive and gentle practice. We ensure your comfort with supportive props and adjust the sound intensity to suit your needs. For C-section recovery, the subtle vibrations can actually encourage circulation and support healing around the incision site, always within your comfort level.

What should I expect during my first postpartum Sound Bath session?

You'll lie comfortably, fully clothed, while a variety of instruments create a rich sonic environment. Your only task is to relax and allow the sounds to wash over you. Many new mothers report feeling a significant reduction in physical strain and a profound sense of calm, often leading to restorative sleep post-session.

How often should I attend Sound Bath sessions for optimal postpartum relief?

The frequency depends on your individual needs and recovery pace. Many new mothers find weekly or bi-weekly sessions most beneficial in the early postpartum period for consistent pain management and nervous system regulation. We'll discuss a personalized plan during your initial consultation.
