Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Sound Bath specifically help with physical pain from parenting?
Our Sound Bath sessions utilize specific sound frequencies that encourage deep relaxation in your muscles and nervous system. This helps to release chronic tension often held in your back, shoulders, and neck from lifting children, carrying gear, and constant movement, providing tangible physical relief.
I'm always multitasking. Can I really relax in a Sound Bath?
Absolutely. The beauty of a Sound Bath is its immersive nature. The sounds naturally guide your mind away from daily stressors and into a state of deep rest, even if you typically struggle to quiet your thoughts. You don't need to 'do' anything; just lie back and let the sounds work their gentle magic on your nervous system.
What if I can't lie still for an entire session due to discomfort?
We prioritize your comfort. You'll be supported with bolsters and blankets to find a position that works best for your body. If you need to shift or adjust during the session, that's perfectly fine. The goal is for you to be as relaxed as possible, allowing the Sound Bath to effectively address your somatic tension.
Is this just another relaxation technique, or does it offer deeper benefits for parents?
While deeply relaxing, a Sound Bath goes beyond simple stress reduction. It engages your parasympathetic nervous system, which is crucial for somatic healing and physical recovery. For parents, this means not just feeling calmer, but also experiencing a reduction in chronic physical aches and a greater capacity to handle daily demands with less physical strain.
