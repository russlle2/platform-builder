Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Sound Bath specifically help with postpartum emotional recovery?
Our Sound Baths are designed to help regulate your nervous system, which is often overstimulated during postpartum. The specific sound frequencies can encourage your brain to enter states associated with deep rest and emotional processing, helping you gently release tension, reduce anxiety, and foster a sense of inner calm as you navigate this tender time.
I'm feeling overwhelmed and exhausted. Is a Sound Bath suitable for me right now?
Absolutely. Our Sound Baths are intentionally gentle and require no effort from you. It's a space where you can simply lie down, be held by sound, and allow your body and mind to rest deeply. This passive relaxation can be incredibly restorative, especially when you're feeling overwhelmed or sleep-deprived, offering a crucial pause in your demanding schedule.
What if I have experienced a difficult birth or postpartum trauma? Is this space safe for me?
Yes, our practice is rooted in trauma-informed principles. This means we prioritize creating a safe, predictable, and empowering environment. We understand that personal experiences can be sensitive, and our approach ensures you have control over your experience, with no pressure to engage in any way that feels uncomfortable. Your comfort and safety are our highest priority.
Do I need any prior experience with meditation or sound therapy to attend a Sound Bath?
No prior experience is necessary. Our Sound Baths are accessible to everyone, regardless of their background with meditation or sound therapy. You simply need to come as you are, find a comfortable position, and allow the sounds to wash over you. We provide all the guidance and support you need to have a beneficial experience.
