Contraindications

Sound baths are restorative for many, but may not suit everyone. Please consult a physician before attending if you have epilepsy, are in early pregnancy, have a history of seizures, have a pacemaker, or are recovering from recent surgery. If you have serious psychiatric conditions or are actively experiencing severe dissociation, contact a healthcare professional before booking. This practice is not a substitute for medical care.

Micro-practices to carry

  • Two slow exhales between tasks — a mini reset.
  • Pair a short sound loop (30s) with a walk to anchor attention.
  • If anxious, count three gentle breaths and soften your jaw.
