Common Questions

What should I bring?
A mat or blanket, a pillow, and anything that helps you feel settled. We provide bolsters on request.
How long is a session?
Most gatherings run 60–75 minutes. Our short format sits at 45 minutes when listed as "Pulse" events.
Is this therapy?
No — these are guided sound practices and are not a replacement for mental health treatment. If you are in active treatment, please consult your provider before joining.
Can I attend if I'm pregnant or have medical devices?
