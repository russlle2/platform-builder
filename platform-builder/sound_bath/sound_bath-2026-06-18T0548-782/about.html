What exactly is a Sound Bath and how will it help me as a new mother?

A Sound Bath is a deeply relaxing experience where you are bathed in soothing sounds produced by various instruments. For new mothers, it offers a dedicated time to rest, reduce stress, and re-center. The gentle vibrations can calm your nervous system, helping you feel more grounded and connected during your postpartum period.

Will I need to do anything during the Sound Bath session?

No, you don't need to do anything specific. Your role is simply to relax and receive the sounds. You'll typically lie comfortably on a mat, covered with a blanket, and allow the sounds to wash over you. There's no right or wrong way to experience it; just let yourself be.

Is it safe to bring my baby to a Sound Bath session?

Our group Sound Bath sessions are designed for mothers to have dedicated time for themselves. For private sessions, we can discuss adaptations to ensure both you and your baby are comfortable and safe. Please contact us to chat about options that best suit your needs.

How can a Sound Bath help me with my relationship with myself and my baby?

By offering a peaceful space for deep relaxation, a Sound Bath can help quiet the mind and reduce mental chatter. This allows you to reconnect with your inner self, fostering self-compassion and clarity. This renewed sense of peace can then extend to your interactions with your baby, promoting a more present and heart-centered bond.
