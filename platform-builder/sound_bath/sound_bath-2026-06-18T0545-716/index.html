Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Sound Bath specifically support my recovery from chronic illness or injury?
A Sound Bath helps by creating a deep state of relaxation, which can reduce stress and quiet the nervous system. This calm environment supports your body's natural restorative processes, helping you gently reconnect with yourself and foster a more positive internal relationship during your recovery.
What kind of sounds will I experience in a Sound Bath, and are they safe for my sensitive state?
You will experience gentle, sustained tones from instruments like singing bowls and gongs. These sounds are carefully chosen for their soothing qualities and are played at a comfortable volume. Our sessions are designed to be non-invasive and deeply calming, ensuring a safe and supportive experience for your sensitive state during recovery.
Will I need to do anything specific during the Sound Bath if I'm managing pain or discomfort?
Your only role is to rest comfortably. We encourage you to find the most comfortable position for your body, whether lying down or seated, and we provide props for support. There's no expectation to 'do' anything; simply allow the sounds to wash over you. Your comfort and well-being are our primary focus.
How does Sound Bath foster 'relationship healing' within the context of chronic illness?
Relationship healing in this context refers to mending the connection you have with your own body and internal experience. Chronic illness can strain this bond. A Sound Bath offers a quiet, supportive space to listen inward, acknowledge your body's state with compassion, and begin to rebuild a relationship of trust and kindness with yourself, which is crucial for long-term well-being.
