Frequently Asked Questions

How does Sound Bath specifically benefit entrepreneurs and high-achievers?
Our Sound Bath sessions are tailored to address the unique stressors of your high-pressure environment. By promoting deep nervous system regulation, they help you mitigate burnout, improve decision-making clarity, and cultivate the emotional resilience essential for sustained peak performance.
Is the Sound Bath designed for emotional healing or just relaxation?
It's designed for both. While deep relaxation is a significant benefit, the core of our Sound Bath practice is trauma-informed emotional healing. The sound frequencies create a safe space for your system to process and release subtle emotional patterns that may be contributing to stress or mental blocks, without the need for verbal processing.
What should I expect during a Sound Bath session?
You will lie comfortably, fully clothed, as I play a range of instruments like crystal bowls, gongs, and chimes. The session is typically 60-75 minutes, and your only task is to relax and allow the sound vibrations to wash over you. Most clients report a profound sense of calm, mental clarity, and often deeper sleep following a session.
How frequently should I engage in Sound Bath for optimal results?
For initial stress reduction and emotional pattern processing, we recommend a series of 3-4 sessions, spaced weekly or bi-weekly. After that, many high-achievers integrate Sound Bath into their routine monthly or bi-monthly as a proactive measure for maintaining emotional equilibrium and mental sharpness.
