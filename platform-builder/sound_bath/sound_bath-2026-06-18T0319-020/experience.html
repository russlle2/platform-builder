Sound Bath

How Sound Bath Supports Nervous System Regulation

During a Sound Bath, you are immersed in a curated soundscape created by therapeutic instruments. These gentle, sustained vibrations encourage a shift in brainwave patterns, often from a beta (alert) to alpha (relaxed) or theta (meditative) state. This physiological shift is key to reducing cortisol levels and activating your parasympathetic nervous system, fostering a deep sense of calm and allowing your system to process and integrate changes more effectively.
