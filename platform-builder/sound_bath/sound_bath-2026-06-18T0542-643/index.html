Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Sound Bath and how can it help my immunity?
A Sound Bath at involves resting comfortably while plays various instruments that create soothing vibrations. This deep relaxation helps reduce stress, which in turn supports your body's natural immune functions. It's a gentle way to encourage your immune system to work its best.
Will a Sound Bath be too overwhelming or loud for me?
Not at all. Our Sound Bath sessions are specifically designed to be gentle and comforting, with careful attention to volume and rhythm. We ensure the sound experience is always pleasant and never jarring, prioritizing your comfort and well-being. You'll find it a very peaceful and approachable experience.
Do I need any special equipment or prior experience to join a Sound Bath?
No, you don't need anything special! Just bring yourself, dressed in comfortable clothing. We provide all the necessary props like mats, blankets, and pillows to ensure you are completely at ease. No prior experience with sound sessions is needed – just an open mind.
How can regular Sound Bath sessions contribute to my vitality?
Regular Sound Bath sessions can help you cultivate a sense of calm and inner balance. By consistently reducing stress and promoting deep rest, you may find your overall energy levels improve, your sleep becomes more restorative, and you feel a greater sense of well-being, all contributing to your vitality.
