Helpful questions

What should I bring?

Comfortable clothing, a mat or blanket if you have one, and curiosity. We provide cushions and bolsters for communal sessions.

Contraindications?

Not suitable as a replacement for medical or psychiatric care. If you have epilepsy, recent head injuries, severe psychiatric conditions, are pregnant, or use pacemakers, consult a clinician before attending. If in doubt, reach out via {{EMAIL}} or call {{PHONE}}.

Will I fall asleep?

Some do, and that's fine. Others experience quiet alertness. Both are normal responses to practice.
