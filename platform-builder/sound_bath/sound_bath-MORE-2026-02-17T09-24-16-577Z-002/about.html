Meet the team and why our sessions feel like a small ritual

We design gentle group sound experiences that help you slow down, notice where energy is stuck, and take a tiny, clear next step. Sessions are crafted for small cohorts in comfortable city spaces.

Our approach

We work in small groups, with instruments tuned to support deep listening. Expect breathing cues, guided transitions, and time to settle after the sound. Sessions are playful, clear, and intentionally brief enough to fit into a weeknight.

Cohort rhythm

Most participants join a short series of 3 sessions, designed to be cumulative. We also offer single events and private sessions upon request.

Why cohorts?

Cohorts let the field of the group build safely: people arrive with similar expectations and the steady cadence helps practice settling more quickly. We cap groups to keep the space intimate.
