Questions people ask before a session

Below are practical answers, plus a small interactive seat selector that helps you see what to bring when you reserve a spot.

What should I bring?

A yoga mat or blanket, a pillow for your head, water, and a light sweater. You can tailor this from the packing generator to suit an evening or morning session.

How crowded are sessions?

We cap groups to keep the field intimate — usually 12–18 people depending on the space. Private sessions are available.

Contraindications

If you have metal implants that react to low-frequency vibration, uncontrolled seizures, recent surgery, or are pregnant, please consult with your physician before attending and notify us when booking.

Cancellation

Cancellations with full refund are available up to 48 hours prior. For cohort bookings, credits may apply for later sessions.
