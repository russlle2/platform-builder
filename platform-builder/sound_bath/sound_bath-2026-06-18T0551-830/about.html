How can a Sound Bath specifically help me with major life transitions?

A Sound Bath provides a dedicated space for profound rest, allowing your nervous system to reset. During major transitions, your body often holds stress and uncertainty. The resonant frequencies help to release this physical tension, promoting a more relaxed posture and a clearer mental state, which are crucial for navigating change with grace and resilience.

Will I need to do anything during the Sound Bath to improve my body awareness?

Your only task is to relax and listen. We encourage you to simply observe any sensations in your body without judgment. The sounds themselves gently guide your attention inward, naturally enhancing your awareness of your physical form and how it responds to the vibrations. You'll find yourself more attuned to your own postural habits.

Is a Sound Bath suitable if I'm feeling physically out of alignment due to stress?

Absolutely. Stress often manifests as physical tension, leading to poor posture and discomfort. The gentle vibrations of a Sound Bath can help to soothe your muscles and fascia, encouraging a natural release and return to a more balanced alignment. It's a supportive environment to find physical ease when your spirit feels burdened by change.

What should I bring to a Sound Bath to maximize my experience of postural relief?

We recommend wearing comfortable clothing that allows for full relaxation. You might also bring a small pillow for head support or a blanket if you tend to get cool, ensuring your body can fully surrender and release any physical tension. The more comfortable you are, the more readily your body can respond to the sounds and find its natural alignment.
