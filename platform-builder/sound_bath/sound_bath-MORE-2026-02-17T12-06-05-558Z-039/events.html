What to expect

Sessions begin with a short settling practice, then move through layered instruments and vocal tones. We emphasize safe, embodied release and clear post-session integration.

  • Small groups — attentive facilitation
  • 75–90 minutes including a brief check-in and grounding
  • Tools: bowls, gongs, chimes, and voice

Quick logistics

Arrive 10 minutes early. Bring a mat, blanket, and pillow. Water is recommended after sessions. Late entry is minimized to maintain the field.

Try a quick focus practice

A three-part micro-experience: breathing, intention, short journaling. Takes ~4 minutes.

Breathe

Slow inhalation for 4s, hold 2s, exhale 6s. Repeat.

Set an intention

Choose a single short phrase to carry with you after the practice.

Quick reflection

Write one line about how you feel now. This is private to you — copy to your notes if you want to keep it.

This exercise is a brief guided practice — not a substitute for medical or mental health care.
