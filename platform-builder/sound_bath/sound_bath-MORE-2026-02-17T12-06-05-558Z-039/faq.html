Common questions

What should I bring?Show me
Bring a yoga mat or blanket, a small pillow if you like, water, and comfortable layers. We provide bolsters and shared blankets upon request.
How long does a gathering last?Time
Most gatherings run 60–75 minutes: a short welcome, a guided sound arc, then gentle reintegration. Private sessions vary from 45 to 90 minutes.
Are sessions suitable for beginners?Beginner-friendly
Yes. Sessions are accessible and invite curiosity. We offer gentle orientation for first-time guests and options to rest at any point.
Contraindications & safety

If you are pregnant, have a diagnosis of epilepsy, severe cardiovascular conditions, or are currently undergoing certain psychiatric treatments, please consult your healthcare provider before attending. If you use implanted medical devices, check with your clinician about exposure to sustained vibrations. This practice is not a substitute for medical care.

Session-specific questions

Can I fall asleep?Yes
Sleeping is fine. The intention is not to stay awake but to come away more rested. If necessary, we’ll gently wake guests after the closing tone.
Are there refunds or transfers?Policy
We offer transfers up to 48 hours before an event. For private bookings, see the reservation page for details or contact us directly.
