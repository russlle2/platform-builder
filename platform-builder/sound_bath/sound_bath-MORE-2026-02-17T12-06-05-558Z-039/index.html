How a session unfolds

  1. Arrival & Settling

    Comfortable mats and a quiet transition—5–10 minutes to adjust and set your posture.

  2. Guided Intention

    Brief soft spoken prompts to orient attention and create a simple intention for the time.

  3. Sound Field

    30–50 minutes of layered instruments and tones arranged to encourage measured attention and release.

  4. Return

    Gentle closure with notes and a short invitation to carry the intention forward.

Practical notes

How should I prepare?

Dress in comfortable layers. Bring a mat or use ours. Hydrate lightly. Arrival 10 minutes early recommended.

Are recordings available?

We occasionally share short takeaways after sessions; full recordings are not provided to preserve the live experience.

Contraindications

Not suitable for individuals with unmanaged epilepsy, certain inner ear conditions, or those who have medical instructions against exposure to prolonged tones. If you are pregnant, have implants, or medical devices, consult your physician before attending. If unsure, contact us at {{EMAIL}}.
