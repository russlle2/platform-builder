Questions, myths & clarifications

Common misreadings

This is not a medical treatment nor a replacement for clinical care. Sound supports settling and breath awareness; it is not designed to diagnose or cure.

Practical notes

Wear layers. Avoid heavy perfumes. Silence phones. We recommend avoiding stimulants before sessions for clearer reception.

Foundations

Intent: short rituals tuned to breath and presence. Tools: singing bowls, chimes, voice, subtle percussion. Design: steady layers, slow shifts, quiet returns.

Outcomes we notice: calmer pulse, clearer attention, muscular easing. Results vary; gentle practice increases access over time.
