Frequently asked

Who benefits from a session?
People looking for a contained rest practice—no prior experience required. Not a substitute for therapy or medical care.
How long should I arrive before start?
Arrive 10–15 minutes early to settle, choose a seat, and check any mobility needs with the host.
What if I'm sensitive to sound?
We keep levels moderate and invite you to choose seats at the periphery. You can also request a low-volume format when booking.
