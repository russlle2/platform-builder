Flow of the session

We begin with gentle grounding, invite intention sharing, move into a 40–60 minute sound journey led by crystal bowls and tuning instruments, and close with soft spoken reflection and space to rehydrate. You may return to movement slowly.

Contraindications & safety

If you are pregnant, have severe tinnitus, uncontrolled epilepsy, or recent head/neck injuries, please consult your physician and let us know before booking. Sound can be powerful; we adapt sessions for safety.
