How an evening unfolds

  1. Arrival & intention (15 min)

    Settle. We offer tea and a quiet check-in to hold your intention for the practice.

  2. Grounding sequence (10 min)

    Gentle breath and stillness to orient the body towards listening.

  3. Main sound journey (40–60 min)

    Layered resonance from crystal bowls, chimes, and a large gong — tuned to guided waves that ebb and return.

  4. Rest & reintegration (10–15 min)

    Soft silence, optional journaling, and tea to integrate the experience.

What to bring: a yoga mat or pad, a blanket, an eye pillow or scarf, water, and any small comforts you prefer.

Contraindications: If you are pregnant, have epilepsy, an active heart condition, or recent head injury, please consult your physician and contact us before attending. Some instruments produce intense vibration; we will accommodate where needed.

Questions we often hear

How long is each session?

Group sessions typically last 75—90 minutes including arrival and closing. Private sessions vary by booking.

Is the sound loud?

Volume is curated for comfort. The room resonates; you may feel vibrations. We adapt for sensitivity.

Can I fall asleep?

Yes — and it is fine. The work continues beneath waking; we support gentle re-entry.
