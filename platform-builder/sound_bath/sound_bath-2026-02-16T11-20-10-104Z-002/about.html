Short answers

Is this meditation?
It is an auditory, embodied practice — not a mantra-based meditation. You may rest or remain lightly aware.
How should I dress?
Layers are best — the body cools during deep rest. Bring socks and a spare blanket.
Contraindications?
People with recent concussion, certain cardiac devices, first-trimester pregnancy, or who have active psychiatric crises should consult us or a clinician first. This is not a substitute for medical care.
