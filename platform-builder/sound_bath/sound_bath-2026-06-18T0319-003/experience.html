Sound Bath

Your Relaxing Experience in a Sound Bath

There's nothing for you to 'do' during a Sound Bath, except to relax. You'll be invited to lie down comfortably, perhaps with a blanket and pillow, and simply listen. Many seniors tell me they feel a profound sense of calm, a reduction in anxious thoughts, and sometimes even improved sleep after a session. It's a gentle practice that meets you exactly where you are.
