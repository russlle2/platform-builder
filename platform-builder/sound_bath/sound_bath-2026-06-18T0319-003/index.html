Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is a Sound Bath and how does it help with stress?
A Sound Bath is a session where you rest comfortably while gentle, resonant sounds are played. The calming vibrations encourage your mind and body to relax, which naturally helps to quiet your nervous system and reduce feelings of stress and anxiety. It's a very passive and peaceful experience.
Is a Sound Bath suitable for someone with limited mobility or hearing challenges?
Absolutely. Our Sound Baths are designed to be accessible and gentle. You can relax in any comfortable position, whether lying down or seated. The vibrations are felt as well as heard, so even if your hearing isn't perfect, you can still benefit from the soothing sensations.
What should I wear or bring to a Sound Bath session?
We recommend wearing comfortable, loose-fitting clothing that you can relax in. You might also want to bring a small pillow or a light blanket to ensure you're cozy, though we often have some available. The main goal is for you to feel completely at ease.
Will I have to do anything during the Sound Bath, or just listen?
You don't need to do anything at all! The beauty of a Sound Bath is that you simply lie back, close your eyes if you wish, and allow the sounds to wash over you. There's no participation required, just pure, gentle relaxation.
