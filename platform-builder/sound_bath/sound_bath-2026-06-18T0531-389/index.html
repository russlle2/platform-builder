Frequently Asked Questions

I've never done anything like a Sound Bath before. Is it for me?
Absolutely! Our Sound Bath sessions are designed for everyone, especially if you're new to wellness practices. There's no special skill or prior experience required. You simply lie down comfortably, listen to the sounds, and let yourself relax. We keep it gentle and accessible, so you can ease into it without any pressure.
How can a Sound Bath help with the physical exhaustion I feel as a parent?
The gentle vibrations from the instruments in a Sound Bath help quiet your nervous system, which can reduce muscle tension and physical fatigue. When your body is in a relaxed state, it has a better opportunity to recover from the constant demands of parenting, leaving you feeling more rested and less achy.
I worry about finding time away from my kids. How long is a Sound Bath session?
We understand how precious your time is. Our Sound Bath sessions are typically 60-75 minutes long, including a little time before and after to get settled and gently transition back. We aim to make it a manageable break that provides significant relief without taking up your entire day.
Will a Sound Bath really help with my stress and the constant mental load of parenting?
Many parents find that the immersive sounds of a Sound Bath create a unique environment for mental quiet. It offers a much-needed break from your thoughts, helping to reduce the feeling of being overwhelmed and constantly 'on.' This mental reset can help you feel more centered and clear-headed afterward, even amidst your daily responsibilities.
