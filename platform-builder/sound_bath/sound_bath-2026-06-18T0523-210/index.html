Frequently Asked Questions

What should I expect emotionally from a Sound Bath when I'm going through a big change?
You might experience a range of emotions during a Sound Bath, from deep relaxation to a gentle surfacing of feelings related to your life transition. The sounds create a supportive container for these emotions, allowing them to be acknowledged without judgment. Your experience is unique, and we encourage you to simply observe what comes up for you.
Is a Sound Bath suitable for someone feeling overwhelmed by a recent life event?
Absolutely. Sound Baths are particularly beneficial for those feeling overwhelmed. The subtle vibrations and sustained tones help to down-regulate the nervous system, which can significantly reduce feelings of stress and anxiety. It offers a gentle respite, giving your mind and body a chance to rest and re-center amidst the chaos of change.
How does a Sound Bath provide trauma-informed care during difficult transitions?
Our trauma-informed approach means we prioritize your sense of control and safety. We offer clear guidance, maintain a respectful space, and never push you into states of discomfort. The non-directive nature of a Sound Bath allows you to engage with the experience at your own pace, creating an environment where gentle emotional processing can occur without feeling re-traumatized.
Will a Sound Bath help me process grief or loss associated with a life change?
While a Sound Bath doesn't 'fix' grief, it can offer immense comfort and support during periods of loss. The resonant frequencies can create a space for emotional release, allowing you to feel and acknowledge your grief in a gentle, held environment. Many find it provides a sense of peace and helps to soften the sharp edges of sorrow, aiding in emotional integration.
