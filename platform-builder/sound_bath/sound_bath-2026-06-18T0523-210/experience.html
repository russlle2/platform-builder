How a Sound Bath Supports Emotional Processing During Transitions

Navigating significant life changes can stir up a wide range of emotions. Our Sound Bath sessions provide a safe, non-verbal space where you can simply be with what arises. The gentle, layered tones are carefully chosen to help calm your nervous system, offering a moment of quiet reflection that can be deeply supportive as you integrate new experiences and manage feelings of uncertainty or grief.
