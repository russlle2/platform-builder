How can a Sound Bath specifically help with postpartum physical pain?

The low-frequency vibrations from the instruments in a Sound Bath can penetrate deep into your tissues, encouraging muscle relaxation and increasing blood flow to areas like your lower back, hips, and pelvic region. This can significantly alleviate the aches and stiffness often experienced after childbirth and during recovery.

Is a Sound Bath safe for someone still recovering postpartum?

Yes, absolutely. Our Sound Bath sessions are incredibly gentle and non-invasive. You'll be resting comfortably, and the experience is designed to be deeply relaxing and restorative, making it very safe and beneficial for your postpartum recovery. We recommend consulting your healthcare provider if you have specific concerns.

What should I expect to feel during a Sound Bath focused on somatic healing?

Many new mothers report a sense of deep relaxation, warmth, and a feeling of 'release' in their bodies. You might notice muscles softening, a reduction in tension, and a profound sense of calm. Some also experience emotional release as the body lets go of stored stress, contributing to overall somatic healing.

Will I be able to fully relax if I'm worried about my baby or my recovery?

We understand that your mind may be active. Our Sound Bath environment is designed to be a sanctuary, a dedicated time for you to turn your focus inward. The immersive sounds naturally guide your attention away from external worries, allowing your body and mind to gently settle into a state of deep relaxation and receptivity.
