What to bring & how it feels

A sound bath is a sensory journey. To make it comfortable and restorative, bring items that help you settle into stillness.

Suggested kit

  • Yoga mat or meditation pad
  • Comfortable blanket and a pillow for head support
  • Water bottle (non-glass advised)
  • Eye mask or scarf to soften light
  • Optional: journal for post-session reflections

Flow of a typical session

  1. Arrival & settling: Gentle check-in and orientation (10–15 min).
  2. Intent & breath: Guided breathing and intention setting.
  3. Sound journey: A layered soundscape (30–50 min) featuring gong, crystal bowls, chimes, and a monochord — designed to move you through sensation to rest.
  4. Return & sharing: Transition with grounding tones, space for tea and quiet reflection.

Contraindications & safety

Sound baths are deeply relaxing but not suitable for everyone. Please consult your healthcare provider before attending if you have any of the following: pregnancy (especially first trimester), uncontrolled epilepsy, recent surgeries, implanted medical devices (e.g., pacemakers), severe PTSD triggers, or an active psychiatric episode. If you use medication that affects balance or consciousness, consider attending with a companion. This list is informational and not exhaustive.
