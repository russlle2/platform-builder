Common myths & honest truths

A quick guide
Myth:

Sound baths are only for meditation veterans.

Truth: They are designed for anyone who needs sound to come back to rest. We pace the experience for all levels.

Myth:

You must lie down perfectly still.

Truth: Comfort is the priority—sit, recline, or rest. Movement is welcome when needed.

Myth:

Sound baths are mystical therapy.

Truth: They are an accessible sensory practice that supports nervous system regulation—not a medical treatment.

What to bring & contraindications

Bring:
  • Yoga mat or thick blanket
  • Small cushion or bolster
  • Eye covering or scarf
  • Water bottle
Contraindications

If you are pregnant, have a pacemaker, recent head/ear surgery, or severe heart conditions, consult your healthcare provider before attending. Sound baths are supportive but not a substitute for medical advice.
