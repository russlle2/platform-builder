Three pillars of our practice

A membership model built for regular restoration and depth.

Ritual Flow

We move deliberately: arrival, breathwork, layered soundscape, gentle reorientation. Sessions last ~75 minutes to allow full nervous system response.

Curated Instruments

Our palette varies by night — crystal bowls with alloyed harmonics, tuning forks, and a hand-played monochord — each arrangement tuned to support deep relaxation.

Membership Access

Members get priority booking, monthly community circles, and a discount on private sessions. Choose presence over one-off consumption.

Myth vs. Truth

Gentle clarifications for newcomers and careful participants.

Myth
Sound baths are hypnotic shows where you lose control.
Truth
They are guided experiences that invite rest; you remain aware and can step out anytime.
What to bring
Yoga mat, blanket, pillow for neck support, eye mask, water, and an intention.
Contraindications
Not recommended if you have uncontrolled epilepsy, recent head injury, or are in active psychiatric crisis. If pregnant or have implants, consult us before booking.
Flow of a session
Arrival & settling (10m) → Breath & intention (10m) → Gradual sound layering (45m) → Soft return & sharing (10m). We close with water and optional journaling prompts.
