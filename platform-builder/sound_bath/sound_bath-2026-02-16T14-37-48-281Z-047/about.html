What people assume — and what actually happens

Myth: It’s just relaxing music

Sound baths are sometimes mistaken for background music. In reality, they are a guided sensory practice with deliberate frequency work and atmospheric intention.

Truth: It’s an embodied session with clear flow

Our sessions follow a steady structure: arrival and grounding, layered tonal work, a deep resonant wash, and a gentle closing that helps you reorient. We describe the flow below and provide guidance for what to bring.

A note about instruments

We rotate instruments for texture and intention. Our roster includes crystal singing bowls, hand-hammered gong, and alpine chimes for shimmering overtones. The selection varies to keep each gathering distinct.

Practical membership details

Membership grants early booking, modest discounts on private sessions, and a monthly check-in email with recommended preparatory practices. We encourage commitment to a practice of at least three sessions for deeper change.
