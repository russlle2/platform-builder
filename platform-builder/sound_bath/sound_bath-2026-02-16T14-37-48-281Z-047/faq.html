Questions & practical notes

Below are common questions about the sensory format, what to bring, and medical disclaimers. If something you need isn’t here, email {{EMAIL}} or call {{PHONE}}.

What should I bring?
A soft mat or yoga mat, two blankets (one to lie on, one for warmth), a small pillow, and an eye mask if you prefer darkness. We provide bolsters and folding mats on request for members. Hydration is encouraged before and after the session.
What is the session flow?
Sessions are structured in four parts: arrival & grounding (10–15 min), layered tone work with instruments (30–40 min), a resonant sustain or gong wash (8–10 min), and a gentle reorientation + closing (5–10 min). Facilitator prompts are soft and infrequent to preserve listening.
Are there contraindications?
Yes. Sound baths are generally safe but not appropriate for everyone. Please consult your physician before attending if you are pregnant, have a history of seizures, uncontrolled epilepsy, have a cardiac condition with implanted devices, or are sensitive to intense vibration. Inform the facilitator of any implants or recent surgery. Attendance is at your own risk; we provide a brief intake form for clarification.
What instruments might be used?
Instrument rotation varies. Expect crystal bowls, tuned chimes, and tuning forks on most evenings. We sometimes include the monochord for deep harmonic overtone work. Instrument choice is described on the event page for each gathering.
How does membership work?
Membership gives priority registration, a modest discount on private bookings, and access to members-only gatherings. It’s a subscription model oriented toward regular practice. Contact us to learn about billing and pause options.
What about accessibility and sensory needs?
Our venue is wheelchair accessible; please let us know in advance so we can reserve accessible floor space. We keep lighting low and scent minimal; if you have sensory sensitivities, tell us and we will adapt placement and distance to reduce intensity.
Cancellation & refund policy
Booking changes are accepted up to 48 hours before group events. Memberships follow their own billing rules; see the pricing page for details. Private sessions have a 72-hour cancellation window for full refunds.

Quick guidance

Arrive 15 minutes early to settle in. For intense sound sensitivity, ask to sit further from the instruments or request lower volumes. We avoid amplification unless necessary and favor acoustic tone.
