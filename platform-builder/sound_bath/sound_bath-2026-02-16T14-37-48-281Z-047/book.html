What to expect

Arrival & check-in: 10–15 minutes. We orient you to the space and seating. Please arrive early to settle into a suggested spot.

What to bring

  • Mat and two blankets (or request on-site cushions)
  • Eye-cover or light scarf
  • Water and comfortable clothing

Contraindications

If pregnant, have seizure history, are recovering from surgery, or have implanted medical devices, please consult your physician before attending and note it when booking. If in doubt, reach out to {{FACILITATOR_NAME}} at {{EMAIL}}.

Session flow at a glance

  1. Settling & breath (10–15 min)
  2. Layered tones using instruments like the gong, tuning forks, and handpan (30–45 min)
  3. Slow release and closing (8–10 min)
