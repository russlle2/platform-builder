Offerings

1:1 restorative

A focused session for nervous system renewal. We blend a monochord, crystal singing bowl, and subtle chimes to create a gentle scaffold for personal integration.

Couples & shared rituals

Designed to foster connection and mutual calm. Elements include shared breathwork, bowl harmonics, and guided stillness in a held space.

Corporate & team resets

Shorter formats (20–40 mins) to downregulate teams before or after intense work periods. We use percussive chimes, gongs, and low-frequency bowls to create clarity and rest.

Health & safety

If you use a pacemaker, have serious cardiovascular conditions, are pregnant, or have recent surgeries, please consult your clinician before booking. We will adapt sound proximity and volume as needed.
