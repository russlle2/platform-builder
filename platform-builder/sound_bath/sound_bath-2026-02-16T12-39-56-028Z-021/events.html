The ritual

We open with guided centering, move through sustained soundscapes, and close with a gentle re-entry. The facilitator holds time with subtle cues and quiet voice; most of the evening is an ocean of layered tone.

Instruments for this circle: crystal bowls, hammer-weighted gong, glockenspiel chimes. Each series leans into different textures — expect evolving timbres.

What to bring

  • Comfortable clothing and an extra blanket
  • Yoga mat, cushion, or bolster
  • Water bottle and small towel

Contraindications: If you are pregnant, have epilepsy, a recent concussion, pacemaker, or other implanted device, or are currently under heavy psychiatric medication, please consult your clinician before attending. If uncertain, contact us at {{EMAIL}}.

What to expect

The flow is simple and sensory: arrival & grounding → intention setting → immersive sound bath → gentle wake & integration. Sessions favor sustained listening over story. You may experience warmth, body vibration, imagery, or deep rest.
