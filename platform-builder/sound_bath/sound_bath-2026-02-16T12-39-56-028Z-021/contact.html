FAQ highlight

What should I bring?
soft blanket, water, pillow
Bring a yoga mat or blanket, a small pillow, two layers to stay warm, and a bottle of water. We provide props on request. If you need assistance with mobility or seating, include that in your note.
Contraindications
safety first
Sound baths are generally gentle but not suitable for everyone. Please consult us if you are pregnant, have a pacemaker or tinnitus, epilepsy, or recent head injury. We can adapt or recommend alternatives.
