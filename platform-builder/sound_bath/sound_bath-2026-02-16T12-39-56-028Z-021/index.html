The Ritual

We guide you through a quiet architecture of sound — breath, tone, and stillness. The evening is curated to carry you through gentle awakenings and deep rest.

Guided Opening

A soft arrival: centering breath, brief intention, gentle rolling chimes to settle the nerves.

Immersive Soundscape

Layered instruments: crystal singing bowls, a deep gong, and handheld chimes weave continuous tones to soothe the nervous system.

Sustained Return

A slow re-emergence with grounding resonances and a short sharing circle for optional reflection.

What to Bring & Notes

What to bring

  • A yoga mat or thick blanket
  • One or two cushions for support
  • Water bottle and warm socks
  • Eye mask or scarf for deeper rest

Contraindications & care

Please note: sound baths are generally gentle but are not recommended in the immediate aftermath of surgery, within three months of a major cardiovascular event, or during pregnancy without medical approval. If you have severe hearing disorders, epilepsy, or are taking heavy sedatives, consult your practitioner before attending. This information is informational and not a substitute for medical advice.

The flow, in brief

  1. Arrival and soft settling (10–15 min)
  2. Opening breath and intention (5 min)
  3. Immersive sound journey (40–60 min)
  4. Gradual reintegration and closing (10–15 min)

Questions

How long should I arrive before the session?

We recommend arriving 20–30 minutes early to get settled, choose a comfortable position, and experience a quiet transition before the sound begins.

Will I fall asleep?

Many guests enter a deeply restorative state and may drift into sleep. The journey is safe and guided; we provide gentle cues to bring you back when the session concludes.

Do you accept walk-ins?

Some sessions accept limited walk-ins. Booking ahead is advised to guarantee a spot.
