Session flow (what to expect)

Arrival

Warm greeting, sign-in, and space to lie down. 5–10 minutes to settle.

Opening

A brief centering and breath guidance to orient attention.

Sound field

35–60 minutes of woven tones (harp-like crystal bowls, rattles, metallic chimes), gradually tapering.

Return

Soft reintroduction to the room and optional sharing.

Contraindications & guidance

If you are pregnant, have metal implants, heart pacemakers, epilepsy, or recent cranial surgery, consult your medical provider before booking. Inform us so we can adapt the soundscape. We also provide ear protection on request.

What to bring

Mat or blanket, pillow, sweater, water bottle. We supply extras on-site.

Instruments typically used

  • Crystal bowls
  • Monochord (long string instrument)
  • Soft mallet gong
