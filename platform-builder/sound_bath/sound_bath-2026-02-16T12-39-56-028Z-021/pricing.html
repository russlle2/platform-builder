What to bring & session flow

Arrive 10–15 minutes early. Bring a mat, blanket, bottle of water, and any supports you need. A typical public session: ambient arrival → guided breath → layered soundscape featuring singing bowls and chime duo → soft silence → grounding and gentle re-entry.

Contraindications & safety

If you have an implanted medical device (e.g., pacemaker), epilepsy, recent concussion, are pregnant, or under heavy psychiatric medication, consult your clinician before attending and notify us at {{EMAIL}}. Sessions involve sustained tones and low-frequency sounds which may not be suitable for all conditions.

Instruments & textures

For public series we rotate instrumentation to keep a fresh palette: crystal singing bowls, tuned handpans, soft chime banks, and small gongs. Private sessions may include more focused devices like tuning forks and monochord for targeted resonance.
