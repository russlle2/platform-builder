Frequently asked questions

What should I bring to a session?
Bring a yoga mat or thick blanket to lie on, a small cushion for neck support, an extra blanket to stay warm, and water. Eye masks and earplugs are optional. Wear comfortable, layered clothing.
Are sound baths safe? Any contraindications?
Generally yes, but not always. If you are pregnant, have epilepsy, recent head injury, serious psychiatric diagnoses, or implanted medical devices (e.g., pacemaker), consult your doctor and contact us beforehand. This is not a replacement for medical care.
How long is a typical session?
Public group sessions typically run 60–90 minutes including arrival and closing. The immersive sound portion is usually 30–50 minutes depending on the event. Private sessions vary by format.
I notice different instruments used — what will I hear?
Our instrument palette changes by setting. Expect a careful mix of timbres such as a warm gong swell, a harmonic monochord layer, crystal bowls, and small metallic resonators for clarity. We avoid harsh, sudden sounds.
Do you offer memberships or packages?
Yes — monthly memberships offer priority booking, guest passes, and discounted private sessions. See pricing for current tiers.
