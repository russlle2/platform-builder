How a sound bath unfolds

We guide you through a gentle arc so the senses can settle: a short greeting, grounding breath work, a reclining journey of layered tones, and a peaceful close with time to reorient.

Typical flow

  1. Arrive 15 minutes early — settle, hydrate, and choose your place.
  2. Brief intention-setting and gentle breath practice.
  3. Sound journey — 30–50 minutes of continuous immersion.
  4. Soft return — tea, silence, and optional check-ins.

What to bring

  • A yoga mat or thick blanket to lie on
  • A small cushion for neck support and an extra blanket for warmth
  • Water bottle and any small comforts — eye mask, socks

Safety & contraindications

Sound baths are deeply relaxing but not suitable in all cases. If you are pregnant, have uncontrolled epilepsy, severe psychiatric conditions, implanted medical devices, or recent surgery, please consult your healthcare provider and contact us before attending.
