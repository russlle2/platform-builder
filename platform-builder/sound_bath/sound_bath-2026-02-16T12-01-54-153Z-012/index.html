What to bring

  • Yoga mat, blanket, and small pillow
  • Eye covering (sleep mask) for deeper rest
  • Water bottle — we provide limited extras

Session flow

Arrive 15 minutes early for settling. We begin with a short intention-setting circle, then a 45–60 minute soundscape (instruments vary: crystal bowls, chimes, monochord, tuning forks). We close with gentle reorientation and optional tea.

Contraindications

Please consult with medical providers if you are pregnant, have epilepsy, or are prone to seizures. High-sensitivity vestibular issues or certain implanted devices may require physician approval. Contact us for more details.
