What to expect

Sessions are intentionally slow. We begin with gentle arrival, short breathwork, and then a layered sound journey. The instruments and textures vary; our current palette focuses on warm low-frequency resonance and crystalline harmonics.

Example instruments for bookings

  • Gong for deep, long-form washes
  • Monochord for sustained harmonic bed
  • Small metal tuning forks for bright accents

Contraindications

If you have implanted electrical medical devices, epilepsy, are pregnant, or have recent surgery, please consult your provider and contact us before attending. Sound affects the nervous system; we prioritize safety and will suggest alternatives where needed.
