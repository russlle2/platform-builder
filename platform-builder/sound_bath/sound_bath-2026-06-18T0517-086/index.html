Frequently Asked Questions

I'm recovering from a chronic illness and have limited mobility. Can I still participate in a Sound Bath?
Absolutely. Our Sound Bath sessions are designed to be fully accessible. You're welcome to sit in a chair, lie down, or position yourself in any way that feels comfortable for your body. The only requirement is to be present and open to the sounds, which you can do from any position.
I've never done anything like a Sound Bath before. Will I know what to do?
There's nothing special you need to 'do' during a Sound Bath. Your only task is to get comfortable and listen. We'll guide you through the process, and you can simply relax and let the sounds wash over you. There's no right or wrong way to experience it, and no prior experience is necessary.
How can a Sound Bath specifically help with my chronic pain or fatigue?
While a Sound Bath isn't a cure for chronic pain or fatigue, it can be a powerful tool for nervous system regulation. By encouraging a deep state of relaxation, it helps to reduce the stress response in your body, which can often exacerbate pain and fatigue. Many participants find it provides a gentle, temporary reprieve and improves their overall sense of well-being.
I'm sensitive to sounds. Will a Sound Bath be too overwhelming for me?
We use gentle, resonant instruments to create a soothing soundscape, not loud or jarring noises. We also encourage you to communicate any sensitivities you have. You're welcome to sit further away from the instruments or bring earplugs if that makes you feel more comfortable. Our goal is for you to feel safe and relaxed throughout the session.
