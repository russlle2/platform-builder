Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Sound Bath and how does it help with mental clarity?
A Sound Bath involves lying comfortably while listening to a variety of gentle, resonant instruments. The soothing vibrations help calm your nervous system, which can reduce mental clutter and promote a state of relaxed alertness, supporting your focus and cognitive functions.
Do I need any special preparation for a Sound Bath session?
Not at all! You simply need to arrive ready to relax. We recommend wearing comfortable clothing. There's no physical exertion required, just an openness to the sounds. We provide everything else you'll need for your comfort during the session.
Is a Sound Bath suitable for everyone, particularly for seniors?
Yes, our Sound Bath sessions are designed to be very gentle and are particularly well-suited for seniors. The experience is passive and non-invasive, making it a safe and accessible way to support mental relaxation and cognitive well-being for almost everyone.
How often should I attend a Sound Bath to experience benefits for my memory and focus?
Many of our clients find that regular participation, perhaps once or twice a month, helps maintain a sustained sense of mental clarity and calm. The benefits tend to build over time, much like any gentle practice that supports your overall well-being.
