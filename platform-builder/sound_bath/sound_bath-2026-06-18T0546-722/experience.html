Embracing Your New Heartbeat: Postpartum Sound Bath Sessions

Your postpartum period is a time of immense change, and your emotional landscape shifts with it. Our Sound Bath sessions are crafted with your unique needs in mind, providing an oasis of calm. We use specific sound vibrations to help soothe your nervous system, allowing you to release tension and create a gentle space for self-compassion and connection with your baby and partner. It's about tenderly supporting your heart through this beautiful, demanding phase.
