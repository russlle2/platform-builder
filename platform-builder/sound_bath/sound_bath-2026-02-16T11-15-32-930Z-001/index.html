Is a sound bath right for you?

We prioritize safety and clarity. Use this short guide to determine suitability and to prepare.

What to bring

  • Yoga mat, blanket, and a pillow for support
  • Water bottle and layers for temperature changes
  • Eye mask or scarf for deep inward focus

Contraindications (please read)

  • If you are pregnant in the first trimester, consult your caregiver
  • Those with recent head/neck surgery, severe epilepsy, or certain cardiac implants should seek medical advice
  • Not a substitute for medical care — notify facilitator of any implants or conditions

Session flow — what to expect

Each gathering is intentionally sequenced: arrival, attunement, immersion, and gentle return. Facilitator: {{FACILITATOR_NAME}}.

  1. Arrival & Check-in

    Settling in quietly; orientation to the space and minimal logistics (restrooms, emergency exit).

  2. Grounding & Intention

    Breathwork and a short guided centering to orient your nervous system.

  3. Sound Immersion

    Layered resonance with instruments such as crystal bowls, gong, tuning forks, and a monochord for sustained harmonic textures.

  4. Integration

    Soft re-entry with talk-back, water, and optional journaling prompts.

Flow duration: typically 75–90 minutes for group events; private sessions vary.

Micro practices to extend the session

Clinical, calm suggestions to weave resonance into daily life.

Two-minute tuning

Hum on an open vowel for 2 minutes to steady breath and lower heart rate.

Bedtime buffer

Play a single singing bowl tone for 3–4 minutes before sleep to cue relaxation.

Pause for three

Three slow diaphragmatic breaths before meetings to reset nervous system response.

Listening moment

Notice ambient sounds for 60 seconds — a practice in non-reactive attention.
