  • Optional eye mask, water bottle, and layers for temperature shifts.
  • Ear protection on request for sensitive listeners.

Contraindications & safety

Sound baths are generally gentle but not suitable for everyone. If you are pregnant, have a recent concussion, epilepsy, or unstable cardiovascular conditions, please consult your physician before booking. We will work with you to adapt or recommend alternatives.

Session flow (what to expect)

Sessions begin with a brief orientation, gentle breathwork, then a 25–60 minute sound immersion using tuned instruments. We close with grounding and an optional integrative chat. For private work, timings are adapted to your goals.
