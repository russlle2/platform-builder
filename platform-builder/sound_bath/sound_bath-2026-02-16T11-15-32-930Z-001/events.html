Practical notes

What to bring

  • Yoga mat, blanket, and a small pillow for head support
  • Water bottle — hydration helps integrate the session
  • Mask or eye-cover if you prefer deeper internal focus

Contraindications

If you are pregnant, have epilepsy, a history of severe psychiatric episodes, recent surgery, or a pacemaker, please consult your physician and notify the facilitator before attending.

How the gathering unfolds

A clear, steady structure to support embodiment and safety.

  1. Arrival & orientation: Gentle breathing and alignment (10 minutes).
  2. Set intention: Short prompt to center attention (5 minutes).
  3. Sound immersion: Layered tones from crystal singing bowls, koshi chimes, and tuning forks — a curated arc of building then releasing (35–40 minutes).
  4. Transition & grounding: Gradual return with silence and light movement (5–10 minutes).

Micro-habits for preparation & integration

  • Hydrate well the morning of the session.
  • Avoid heavy meals within 90 minutes prior.
  • After: sit quietly for five minutes and journal a single line about what shifted.
