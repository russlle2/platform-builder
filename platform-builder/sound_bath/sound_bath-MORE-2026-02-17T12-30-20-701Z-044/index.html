Myths vs. Responsible Practice

Myth

Sound baths are one-size-fits-all and risk-free for everyone.

Truth

We adapt intensity and provide alternatives. Certain conditions (e.g., pregnancy, recent head injury, uncontrolled seizures, photosensitive disorders) require consultation or avoidance. If you have medical concerns, check with your provider before attending.

Quick Questions

What should I bring?

Comfortable layers, a mat, and an open willingness. We provide bolsters and blankets if needed.

Is this suitable for beginners?

Yes. We guide from arrival and offer quieter alternatives when requested.

Contraindications?

We cannot recommend attendance for people with uncontrolled epilepsy, recent skull/brain injury, or any acute psychiatric crisis. If pregnant or on medical devices, please consult us before booking.
