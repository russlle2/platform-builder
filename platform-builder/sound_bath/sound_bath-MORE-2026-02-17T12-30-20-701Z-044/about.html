Contraindications & safety

Sound immersions are generally gentle but may not be suitable for everyone. If you have epilepsy, are pregnant and have specific medical guidance, have implanted medical devices, are in acute psychiatric crisis, or experience sensitivity to strong vibrations, consult your healthcare provider before attending. If you are taking medication that affects alertness, or have a history of dissociation, please contact us before booking. We prioritize clear communication and can adapt elements where possible.
