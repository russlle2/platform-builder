Contraindications

Sound bathing is gentle but may not be appropriate for everyone. If you have epilepsy, recent head injury, are within the first trimester of pregnancy, wear an implanted medical device (pacemaker), or are under a physician’s active care for cardiovascular or neurological conditions, please consult your healthcare provider before attending. For sensory processing concerns or severe anxiety, contact us — we can tailor participation options or suggest alternatives. This page is informational and not medical advice.
