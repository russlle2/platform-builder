Questions & clarifications

Below you’ll find pragmatic answers for attending, preparing, and deciding if an immersive sound meeting is right for you.

What should I wear?

Comfortable layers and something you can lie or sit in for an extended period. Mats and blankets are available; bring what helps you rest.

Can I move or fall asleep?

Movement is allowed; long periods of sleep can reduce the integration benefit. Facilitators will guide a gentle return if you drift.

How long is a session?

Our group cycles are typically 45–60 minutes, with a short integration window afterwards.

Is this therapy?

These offerings are experiential and supportive but are not a substitute for clinical therapy. If you are in active therapy, consider coordinating with your clinician.

Are sessions safe for everyone?

Most people find them gentle. Contraindications include epilepsy, recent head injury, certain cardiac devices, and acute psychiatric states. Contact us for adaptations.

Payment & cancellation

Details are listed on the Rates page. We offer sliding options for some intensive evenings — please contact us if finances are a constraint.

More technical questions

Why instruments?

Acoustic overtones and low-frequency vibrations engage attention differently than spoken guidance. Our instruments are chosen for clarity and safety.

Can I request a private evening?

Yes — we run private intensives tailored to small cohorts. See the Private sessions page for details.
