Safety & contraindications

If you have a pacemaker, pregnancy concerns, seizure disorder, or recent head injury, please consult your physician before booking. For any tinnitus or sensitivity to low-frequency sound, state your preference and we'll adapt instrumentation (we often use a monochord or tuning forks for gentler profiles).

A note on instrumentation

Sessions may feature a monochord, Tibetan bowls, and soft ocean drum textures — chosen to support relaxation without overwhelming the system.
