Myths, and the quieter truths

Myth

Sound baths are just music for sleeping.

Truth

They are guided, intention-rich practices — sensory journeys that balance nervous system activation and facilitate somatic processing.

Myth

Anyone can sit quietly and get the same effect.

Truth

Our offerings combine space design, breath cues, and specific instruments (crystal bowls, tuning forks, monochord) to shape the experience for deeper integration.

Three pillars of our work

Sensory Craft

We design soundscapes with intention: timbre, frequency, and silence woven to support relaxation and nervous system regulation.

Clinical Comfort

Rooms are curated for accessibility, warmth, and low sensory overwhelm; we include contraindications and clinical considerations with every booking.

Intent-Led Flow

Arrival, grounding, the sound journey, and integration — four clear phases that hold space for individual processing.

What to bring

  • A yoga mat or blanket and extra layers for warmth
  • Eye covering or scarf, water bottle, any personal comfort item
  • Optional: journal for integration notes

Contraindications

Please inform us if you are pregnant, have epilepsy, pacemaker/implanted devices, acute injury, or are currently under psychiatric crisis. Sound can be intense; we provide modifications and alternative placements.

Typical flow

  1. Arrival & grounding: gentle breathing and intention setting
  2. Settling: supported lying/seat, props provided
  3. Sound journey: layered instruments — crystal bowls, chimes, monospheric tones, tuning forks — held with pacing and silence
  4. Integration: gentle return, short reflection, and tea before departure

Questions we often get

How long is a typical group sound bath?
Most gatherings run 75–90 minutes including arrival and integration. Plan to arrive 10–15 minutes early.
What should I bring?
See above, but in short: layers, a blanket, an eye covering, water, and anything that helps you feel held.
Are there medical contraindications?
Yes. Let us know about pregnancy, epilepsy, implanted stimulators, recent head injury, or psychiatric crisis. We will offer modifications or advise alternatives.
Can I book a private session?
Yes — 1:1, couples, and corporate sound offerings are available on the Sessions page.
