Questions we often hear

Practical answers for newcomers and returning guests — sensory-forward and safety-conscious.

What should I bring?
A yoga mat or cushion, warm layers, an eye pillow if you like, and an open mind. We provide extra blankets and bolsters upon request.
How long are sessions?
Typical public sessions are 60–75 minutes. Private sessions vary from 45 minutes (intro) to 90 minutes for bespoke formats.
Are there contraindications?
If you have a pacemaker, are pregnant, have epilepsy, or are recovering from surgery, please check with your healthcare provider before attending. Loud gong exposure may not be appropriate for some conditions.
Will I fall asleep?
Some guests nap; others experience deep mental clarity. Both are normal. We structure sessions to include intentional periods of silence so integration can occur.
What instruments do you use?
We rotate instruments seasonally — crystal bowls and Japanese rin bowls for high clarity, suspended gongs for long spectral washes, and chimes for micro-transitions.

Myth vs. truth — a short primer

Myth: sound baths are a one-size ritual. Truth: each session is composed with intention — breathwork, graded dynamics, tonal arcs, and silent windows for embodiment.
