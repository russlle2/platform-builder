Sound Bath: Supporting Your Inner Relationships During Recovery

When navigating recovery from chronic illness or injury, your relationship with yourself can shift. Our Sound Bath sessions provide a dedicated space to gently recalibrate and foster kindness within. The carefully selected vibrations encourage a release of tension, helping you reconnect with your body's innate wisdom and cultivate a deeper sense of self-compassion, vital for your healing process.

How can a Sound Bath specifically assist my recovery from chronic illness or injury?

Our Sound Bath sessions are designed to create a deeply restful state, which can be profoundly beneficial for your body's restorative processes. The gentle vibrations help calm the nervous system, reduce muscle tension, and alleviate mental fatigue, all of which are crucial aspects of managing chronic conditions and supporting physical repair.

Will the Sound Bath help me feel more connected to my loved ones during my recovery?

Yes, many individuals find that the profound relaxation experienced during a Sound Bath helps them feel more emotionally regulated and present. This internal calm can translate into improved capacity for empathy, patience, and clear communication, strengthening your heart-centered connections with family and friends as you navigate recovery together.

What should I expect during a Sound Bath session tailored for recovery?

You will lie comfortably, often on a mat with blankets and pillows, while Dr. Morgan Ellis plays various instruments like singing bowls and gongs. The focus is on creating a supportive, non-demanding environment where your body and mind can simply rest and receive the sound vibrations. There's no expectation for you to 'do' anything, just to allow yourself to be present.

Is a Sound Bath suitable if I have specific sensitivities due to my chronic condition?

We prioritize your comfort and safety. Please communicate any specific sensitivities or concerns regarding sound, light, or positioning when you book your session. Dr. Morgan Ellis is experienced in adapting the environment and intensity to ensure your Sound Bath experience is gentle, supportive, and beneficial for your unique needs during recovery.
