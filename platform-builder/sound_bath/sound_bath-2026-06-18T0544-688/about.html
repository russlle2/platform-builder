What exactly happens during a Sound Bath session?

During a Sound Bath, you'll comfortably recline while Dr. Morgan Ellis plays a variety of instruments like singing bowls and gongs. The gentle sounds and vibrations create a deeply relaxing environment, helping your mind quiet down and your body unwind. You simply relax and receive the benefits.

How can a Sound Bath help with my vitality and immune support?

The deep relaxation experienced during a Sound Bath can reduce stress, which is a key factor in immune health. When you're relaxed, your body can dedicate more energy to its natural healing and restorative processes, helping to bolster your vitality and support your immune system's function.

I've never tried anything like this. Is a Sound Bath suitable for beginners?

Absolutely! Our Sound Bath sessions are specifically designed to be gentle and accessible for everyone, especially if you're new to wellness practices. There's nothing you need to 'do' except find a comfortable position and allow yourself to relax into the sounds. Dr. Morgan Ellis creates a welcoming atmosphere for all.

What should I bring or wear to a Sound Bath session?

For your comfort, we recommend wearing loose, comfortable clothing. You might also want to bring a small blanket or an extra layer, as your body temperature can drop slightly during deep relaxation. We provide mats and pillows, but feel free to bring anything else that helps you feel cozy and at ease.
