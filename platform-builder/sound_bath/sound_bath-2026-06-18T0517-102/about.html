Finding Deep Sleep with Sound Bath After Baby Arrives

How can a Sound Bath specifically help my sleep during postpartum?

Our Sound Bath sessions use specific frequencies and instruments to gently slow your brainwaves, moving you from an active beta state into more relaxed alpha and theta states. This deeply calming effect helps prepare your body for more restorative sleep, even if you only have short windows of opportunity to rest. It's about improving the quality of your rest, not just the quantity.

Is a Sound Bath safe for my postpartum body, especially if I'm still recovering?

Absolutely. Our Sound Bath is a non-invasive, gentle practice. You'll simply lie comfortably, supported by props if needed, and receive the sounds. There's no physical exertion required. We create a nurturing and safe environment, understanding the unique needs of a postpartum body, ensuring your comfort and ease throughout the session.

What should I expect during my first Sound Bath session for postpartum rest?

You'll be invited to lie down comfortably, often on a mat with blankets and pillows. Dr. Morgan Ellis will then play various instruments like crystal singing bowls and gongs, creating a rich tapestry of sound. Your only job is to relax and let the sounds wash over you. Many new mothers find themselves drifting into a deep, meditative state or even a light sleep, waking up feeling refreshed and peaceful.

How often should I attend a Sound Bath to see improvements in my rest?

The benefits of Sound Bath are cumulative, meaning regular sessions can deepen their impact on your rest and recovery. Many new mothers find weekly or bi-weekly sessions to be incredibly supportive for maintaining consistent sleep improvements and managing the demands of postpartum life. We recommend listening to your body and finding a rhythm that works best for you.
