What people misunderstand

Myth: Sound baths are passive spectacles.
Reality: We design structure: intention, measured pacing, and takeaways for day to day renewal.
Myth: You need prior practice.
Reality: These sessions meet any starting point and prioritize safety and clarity.
Myth: They are only for rest.
Reality: Many leave with clearer decision focus and improved breath regulation.

Practice pillars

Intention first

Every session begins with a short guided settling and an explicit intention. This frames the listening and helps integrate findings after the sound work.

Measured layering

We use a modest palette of sustaining tones, light percussive textures, and silence so participants can track changes and learn to self-regulate.

Tools to take home

Simple breath cues and short journaling prompts offered at the end for ongoing practice at home.
