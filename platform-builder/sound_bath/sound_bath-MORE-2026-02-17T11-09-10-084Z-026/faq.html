Frequently asked questions

Practical answers for attending, accessibility, and safety.
Prefer to try a short focus first?
Do I need experience?
No. Sessions are designed to support beginners and experienced listeners. Each gathering begins with a brief orientation and clear cues so you can participate at your comfort level.
What should I bring?
Comfortable clothing, a cushion or yoga mat, and a light blanket if you tend to get cold. We provide mats on request but recommend bringing your own for hygiene.
How loud are sessions?
Sound is intentionally balanced and not overpowering. We emphasize sustain and low frequencies with clear guidance on when to open and close quiet windows.
Are there contraindications?
Yes. If you are pregnant, have epilepsy, uncontrolled hypertension, recent surgery, or severe vestibular disorders, please consult your medical provider before attending. Inform us when booking so we can advise alternatives such as a private consultation.
Can I bring a friend?
Yes. We encourage friends to join. Group sizes are limited for quality and attention to safety.

Cancellation and refunds

Cancellations made more than 48 hours before a session receive a full refund. Within 48 hours, transfers to another date may be considered depending on availability.
