Every aspect of our Sound Bath practice is rooted in trauma-informed care. This means we prioritize your comfort, autonomy, and emotional safety above all else. We understand that past experiences can impact your present well-being, especially during recovery. Frequently Asked Questions

How is a Sound Bath different for someone recovering from chronic illness or injury?
Our Sound Bath sessions are specifically tailored to be trauma-informed and gentle, recognizing the unique emotional and physical sensitivities you might have. We focus on creating a deeply supportive and adaptable environment, allowing you to participate in a way that feels safe and comfortable for your specific recovery needs. It's about meeting you where you are.
Do I need to do anything specific during the Sound Bath session?
No, your only 'task' is to find a comfortable position – lying down or seated – and simply allow yourself to receive the sounds. There’s no right or wrong way to experience it. If any emotions arise, you are encouraged to acknowledge them without judgment. Our space is designed to be non-demanding, letting you rest and relax at your own pace.
What if I experience discomfort or strong emotions during a Sound Bath?
It's completely normal for various emotions or sensations to surface. We create a container of safety and non-judgment. You are always in control and encouraged to listen to your body. If you feel overwhelmed, you can shift your position, open your eyes, or step out for a moment. Our practitioner, , is present to offer a grounding presence and support if needed, without intrusion.
Will a Sound Bath interfere with my current medical treatments or recovery plan?
Our Sound Bath is a complementary practice focused on emotional well-being and stress reduction. It is not a substitute for medical treatment or professional therapy. We always recommend you consult with your healthcare provider about incorporating new practices into your recovery plan. Our aim is to support your emotional resilience alongside your existing care.
