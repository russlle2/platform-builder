Frequently Asked Questions

How does Wellness Coaching specifically address stress after chronic illness or injury?
Our Wellness Coach approach recognizes that your body has undergone significant strain. We focus on techniques that directly down-regulate an overactive nervous system, helping to mitigate the chronic stress response often associated with long-term recovery, allowing your system to rest and repair.
Is this Wellness Coaching suitable if my mobility is still limited due to injury?
Absolutely. Our Wellness Coach programs are designed with adaptability in mind. We meet you where you are, incorporating gentle, accessible practices for nervous system regulation that do not require strenuous physical activity, ensuring your comfort and safety at every step.
What makes your Wellness Coach service an exclusive experience?
Our exclusivity stems from the highly individualized attention and advanced methodologies we employ. As your Wellness Coach, I dedicate myself to understanding the nuances of your recovery, providing a level of bespoke support and sophisticated strategies for stress reduction that transcend standard offerings.
How quickly can I expect to feel the benefits of nervous system regulation with a Wellness Coach?
While individual experiences vary, many clients report a noticeable shift in their sense of calm and well-being within the first few sessions. Consistent engagement with your Wellness Coach and the practices we establish will deepen these benefits, leading to more profound and enduring nervous system regulation over time.
