Frequently Asked Questions

How can I practice when I'm completely overwhelmed?
We start with 30 seconds—while putting on your shoes or waiting for the kettle. Your nervous system doesn't need hours; it needs small, consistent anchors. We'll find the 30 seconds you already have and build from there.
Do these tools require special equipment?
Nope. Your breath, a kitchen counter, or even a tree outside your window are all you need. These are nature-inspired techniques that work anywhere—no apps, no mats, no extra time.
How is this different from other stress reduction methods?
Most teach you to 'calm down' during crisis. We teach you to calm your nervous system *before* the crisis peaks, using the body's natural rhythms. It's not about adding to your to-do list—it's about using what's already there.
Will this help me sleep better?
Yes. When you regulate your nervous system during the day (not just at night), your body naturally prepares for rest. You'll notice yourself unwinding faster after dinner instead of lying awake replaying the chaos.
