Frequently Asked Questions

I'm a new mom and feel completely exhausted. Can a Wellness Coach really help with my sleep?
Yes, absolutely! As your Wellness Coach, we focus on practical, gentle strategies tailored to your postpartum experience. We'll explore small, achievable adjustments to your routine and environment that can make a big difference in how rested you feel, without adding more pressure.
I don't have much time between feedings and baby care. How can I possibly fit Wellness Coaching into my day?
We understand that your time is incredibly precious right now. Our Wellness Coach sessions are designed to be flexible and efficient, focusing on easily implementable steps. We'll work within your existing schedule to find moments for rest and self-care that feel natural and supportive, not like another task on your list.
I've heard about sleep training for babies, but what does a Wellness Coach do for *my* sleep?
While baby sleep is important, our Wellness Coaching focuses entirely on *your* restorative rest as a new mother. We'll look at your sleep environment, daily habits, and stress levels to help you create conditions that support deeper, more consistent sleep for yourself, even when your baby's sleep patterns are still developing.
I feel like I'm not good at wellness or self-care. Do I need any prior experience for this Wellness Coaching?
Not at all! Our Wellness Coaching is designed to be gentle and accessible, especially for new mothers who might feel overwhelmed. There's no prior wellness experience needed. We meet you exactly where you are, offering supportive guidance and simple, practical steps that are easy to understand and incorporate into your daily life.
