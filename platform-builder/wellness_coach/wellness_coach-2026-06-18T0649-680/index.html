Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Wellness Coach specifically help my immune system after childbirth?
After childbirth, your immune system needs extra support. As your Wellness Coach, I'll guide you through simple, evidence-based nutrition and lifestyle adjustments tailored for new mothers to help bolster your natural defenses and recover more effectively, without adding stress to your plate.
I'm so tired. Can a Wellness Coach really help me build vitality postpartum?
Absolutely. Postpartum fatigue is real, and my focus as your Wellness Coach is on gentle, sustainable ways to increase your energy. We'll look at nourishing foods, restorative practices, and small steps you can take to build your vitality without feeling overwhelmed, even with a newborn.
I have no wellness experience. Is this Wellness Coaching for me?
Yes! My approach is designed to be gentle and accessible, especially for new mothers who are completely new to wellness practices. We start exactly where you are, making sure every step is understandable and easy to incorporate into your life, focusing on immune support and feeling stronger.
What kind of time commitment does Wellness Coaching for postpartum recovery require?
We understand your time is precious as a new mom. Our Wellness Coaching sessions are designed to be efficient and effective. We'll work together to create strategies that fit into your life, focusing on small, impactful changes that don't demand a lot of extra time, prioritizing your immune health and energy.
