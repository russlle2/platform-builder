Why do new mothers need specific immune support during postpartum recovery?

Childbirth and the demands of caring for a newborn can significantly deplete your body's resources, making your immune system more vulnerable. Specialized support helps you recover faster, ward off common illnesses, and maintain the energy you need for yourself and your baby.

What kind of vitality building does a Wellness Coach offer for postpartum moms?

As your Wellness Coach, I focus on practical strategies to restore your energy levels. This includes optimizing your nutrition for recovery, suggesting gentle ways to reintroduce movement, and creating routines that support restful sleep, all tailored to the realities of new motherhood.

Is this Wellness Coach service only for moms struggling, or can it benefit any new mother?

This Wellness Coach support is beneficial for any new mother! Whether you're feeling completely depleted or just want to proactively enhance your recovery and ensure you're at your best, I can help you build a strong foundation for long-term health and vitality.

How long does a typical program with a Wellness Coach last for postpartum recovery?

The duration of our work together as Wellness Coach and client is flexible and tailored to your needs. Many new mothers find great benefit from 3-6 months of consistent support, allowing enough time to implement new habits and see sustained improvements in their immune health and energy.
