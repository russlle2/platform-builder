Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Wellness Coach specifically help my immune system during a life transition?
During significant life changes, stress can suppress your immune function. As your Wellness Coach, I help you implement evidence-based practices – like targeted nutrition, stress reduction techniques, and personalized lifestyle adjustments – to strengthen your immune resilience, ensuring your body can adapt effectively.
What does 'vitality building' mean in the context of your Wellness Coach practice?
Vitality building, in my practice as a Wellness Coach, means optimizing your sustained energy, mental clarity, and overall physical stamina. We focus on strategies that improve cellular health, enhance sleep quality, and balance energy levels, so you have the resources to navigate your life changes with ease and focus.
Is this Wellness Coach program suitable if I'm managing a demanding career change?
Absolutely. A demanding career change is a prime example of a major life transition that requires robust immune support and sustained vitality. As your Wellness Coach, I'll help you create routines and protocols that minimize the physiological impact of stress, keeping your energy high and your immune system strong.
What kind of scientific approach does this Wellness Coach use?
My approach as your Wellness Coach is grounded in current scientific understanding of human physiology, immunology, and bioenergetics. We utilize data-driven insights to inform your personalized plan, focusing on measurable outcomes and practical strategies that are supported by research, not just anecdotal evidence.
