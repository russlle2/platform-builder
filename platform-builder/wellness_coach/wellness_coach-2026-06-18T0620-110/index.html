Frequently Asked Questions

How does a Wellness Coach specifically help with postpartum sleep?
As a Wellness Coach specializing in postpartum recovery, we understand the hormonal shifts, physical demands, and emotional landscape that impact your sleep. We provide targeted strategies that address these unique factors, helping you navigate nighttime feedings, manage sleep fragmentation, and establish routines that support your healing and rest.
Is this program suitable if my baby is still waking frequently?
Absolutely. Our Wellness Coach approach isn't about ignoring your baby's needs, but about optimizing your sleep within the realities of frequent awakenings. We focus on improving the quality of the sleep you do get, teaching you techniques to fall asleep faster, maximize short rest periods, and reduce anxiety around sleep disruption.
What kind of commitment is required for the sleep improvement programs?
Our Wellness Coach programs are designed to be practical and integrate into your life as a new mother. We recommend dedicating time to implement the strategies we discuss, but we also understand your limitations. Our goal is to create sustainable changes, not add more pressure. Consistency, even in small steps, yields significant results.
Will I have to make my baby sleep through the night for this to work?
No, our Wellness Coach philosophy centers on improving *your* rest and recuperation, not solely on your baby's sleep patterns. While we can discuss strategies that may indirectly support your baby's sleep development, the primary focus is equipping *you* with tools to achieve more restorative rest, regardless of your baby's current sleep stage.
