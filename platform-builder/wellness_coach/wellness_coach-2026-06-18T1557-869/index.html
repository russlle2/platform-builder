Frequently Asked Questions

How does a Wellness Coach specifically help seniors with their posture?
As your Wellness Coach, I focus on gentle, age-appropriate exercises and awareness techniques. We work on understanding your current postural habits, then introduce practices that gently realign your body, strengthen supportive muscles, and enhance your overall stability, all while respecting your body's current capabilities.
Will these postural health sessions be too strenuous for someone my age?
Absolutely not. My approach as your Wellness Coach is always gentle and tailored. We begin where you are, ensuring that all movements and exercises are comfortable, safe, and progressive. The goal is to build strength and awareness without strain, empowering you to move with greater ease, not discomfort.
What kind of results can I expect from working with a Wellness Coach on my body awareness?
You can expect to gain a deeper understanding of your body's mechanics, leading to improved balance, reduced stiffness, and a more upright, confident posture. Many clients report less everyday pain, increased energy, and a greater sense of control over their physical well-being. Your Wellness Coach supports these positive shifts.
How long does it take to see improvements in my posture and body awareness with a Wellness Coach?
While individual progress varies, many seniors notice subtle improvements in their comfort and body awareness within a few weeks of consistent practice. Significant, lasting changes develop over time as you integrate new habits. Your Wellness Coach is here to guide and encourage you through every step of this empowering process.
