Frequently Asked Questions

I'm a busy professional; how can I fit Wellness Coach sessions into my schedule?
Your time is valuable, and we respect that. Our sessions are designed to be efficient and focused, and we'll work together to find times that integrate smoothly with your demanding work schedule. The strategies you learn are also designed to be quick and easy to weave into your existing daily routine, not add more to your plate.
What does a Wellness Coach mean by 'nervous system regulation' and why is it important for burnout?
Nervous system regulation simply means helping your body move out of that 'fight or flight' stress response and into a more calm and rested state. For burnout, it's crucial because chronic stress keeps your nervous system on high alert, leading to exhaustion. We'll explore gentle techniques to help your body feel safer and more relaxed, directly addressing the root of your burnout.
I've never done anything like this before. Do I need prior experience with wellness practices?
Absolutely not! This Wellness Coach practice is built for individuals just like you – those who are new to focusing on their well-being. We start with the basics, explaining everything clearly and gently. There's no expectation for you to have any previous wellness knowledge or experience. We'll guide you every step of the way.
How quickly can I expect to feel a difference working with a Wellness Coach?
While individual experiences vary, many clients report feeling a subtle shift towards more calm and clarity within the first few weeks as they begin implementing the simple, practical tools we discuss. Consistent application of these strategies is key, and we'll celebrate every step of your progress together.
