Questions people often ask

Will this work if I have an unpredictable schedule?
Yes. Programs emphasize micro-habits that adapt to variable days; the cohort structure also shares creative adaptations from others with similar constraints.
How much time per week?
Most cohorts ask for 30–60 minutes of focused engagement weekly, plus small daily micro-practices (often 2-10 minutes).
Are sessions recorded?
Yes, all live content is recorded. Group work and live exchanges are best experienced in real time but recordings cover lessons and prompts.
