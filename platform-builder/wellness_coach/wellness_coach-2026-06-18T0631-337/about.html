What exactly is Somatic Wellness Coaching for parents?

Somatic Wellness Coaching focuses on the connection between your mind and body, specifically addressing physical tension and pain that often arise from the demands of parenting. We use gentle, movement-based techniques and focused awareness to help you release chronic muscle contraction and improve your body's functional comfort.

How is this different from physical therapy or massage for pain relief?

While physical therapy and massage are excellent for acute issues, Somatic Wellness Coaching empowers you with self-care tools for long-term change. We teach you to identify and release patterns of tension yourself, giving you agency over your physical comfort, rather than relying solely on external treatments. It's about learning sustainable body intelligence.

I'm a busy parent; how much time will this commitment require?

We understand your time is precious. Our sessions are structured to be efficient and impactful, providing you with practical strategies you can integrate into your daily life. Even short, consistent practices can yield significant results. We work with you to find what's realistic and sustainable for your schedule.

Will this help with the emotional stress of parenting too?

Absolutely. Physical tension and emotional stress are deeply linked. By releasing chronic physical contraction through somatic practices, you often experience a corresponding decrease in emotional overwhelm and an increase in mental clarity and calm. It's a fundamental way to support your overall well-being as a parent.
