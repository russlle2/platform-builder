Easing Postpartum Physical Discomfort with Wellness Coaching

Your body has undergone incredible changes. It's common to experience back pain, hip discomfort, pelvic floor issues, or generalized soreness during postpartum recovery. We'll explore personalized techniques that respect your body's current state and support its natural healing process, guiding you towards greater ease day by day.

What kind of physical pain can a Wellness Coach help me with during postpartum recovery?

Our Wellness Coaching focuses on common postpartum discomforts like lower back pain, shoulder and neck tension from feeding or holding your baby, hip soreness, and overall body fatigue. We use gentle, non-invasive techniques to help you find relief and improve your physical comfort as you heal.

Is Wellness Coaching a substitute for medical care for my postpartum pain?

No, our Wellness Coaching complements medical care. We encourage you to always consult with your doctor or physical therapist for any specific medical conditions or severe pain. Our role is to provide supportive strategies and somatic practices that work alongside your medical treatment, enhancing your overall recovery.

What does 'somatic healing' mean in the context of postpartum Wellness Coaching?

Somatic healing in our Wellness Coaching means tuning into your body's physical sensations and emotions to release tension and promote healing. It's about gently listening to what your body needs after birth, helping you process the physical and emotional imprints of this intense period, and fostering a sense of calm and integration.

How can Wellness Coaching help me feel more like myself again after having a baby?

Our Wellness Coaching provides a safe space for you to acknowledge and address the physical and emotional shifts of postpartum. By focusing on gentle pain relief and somatic practices, we help you reconnect with your body, build resilience, and establish new routines that support your well-being, helping you feel more grounded and comfortable in your new identity.
