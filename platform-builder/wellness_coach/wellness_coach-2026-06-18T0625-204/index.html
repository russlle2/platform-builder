Frequently Asked Questions

How can Wellness Coaching specifically help me after a chronic illness or injury?
Wellness Coaching provides a dedicated space for you to process the often-overlooked emotional and mental impacts of your health journey. We focus on gentle strategies to manage stress, reduce anxiety, and rebuild your sense of self, which can be significantly altered by prolonged health challenges. It's about supporting your emotional healing alongside your physical recovery.
What does 'trauma-informed care' mean in the context of Wellness Coaching?
Trauma-informed care means I understand that your experiences with chronic illness or injury might have been deeply impactful, potentially leading to emotional distress or even trauma. Our sessions are designed to be respectful, safe, and empowering, avoiding re-traumatization. We prioritize your comfort and control, working at your pace to help you feel secure and supported throughout your emotional healing process.
Will Wellness Coaching interfere with my medical treatment or physical therapy?
Absolutely not. Wellness Coaching is a complementary support system. My role is to help you navigate the emotional landscape of your recovery, which can enhance your ability to engage with and benefit from your medical treatments. We'll work on building emotional resilience, managing pain-related stress, and fostering a positive mindset, all of which can indirectly support your physical progress. I encourage open communication with your healthcare providers.
I feel emotionally drained; how can I even begin Wellness Coaching?
It's completely normal to feel drained. That's precisely why Wellness Coaching can be so beneficial. We start exactly where you are, with gentle conversations and small, manageable steps. There's no pressure to perform or push through. Our initial focus will be on creating a supportive and calming environment where you can simply be, without judgment, and slowly begin to explore what emotional healing means for you.
