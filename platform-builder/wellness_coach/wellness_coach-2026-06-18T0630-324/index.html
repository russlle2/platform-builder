Frequently Asked Questions

How does Wellness Coaching specifically address physical pain from chronic illness or injury?
My Wellness Coaching approach for chronic pain centers on somatic practices that help your body release stored tension and trauma. We explore gentle movements, breathwork, and mindful awareness techniques to re-educate your nervous system, promoting a sense of safety and reducing the perception of pain, rather than just masking symptoms.
Is this Wellness Coaching suitable if my chronic condition is still active or I'm still recovering from an injury?
Absolutely. My Wellness Coaching is designed to meet you exactly where you are in your recovery. We adapt all practices to your current physical capabilities and energy levels. The focus is always on supporting your body's innate capacity for healing and comfort, without pushing or creating further strain.
What makes your Wellness Coaching different from physical therapy or medical treatment?
While medical treatments address the pathology and physical therapy rebuilds function, my Wellness Coaching focuses on the deeper, soulful connection to your body's experience of illness or injury. We work on releasing the emotional and energetic imprints of pain, helping you cultivate resilience, inner peace, and a more comfortable relationship with your body beyond clinical rehabilitation.
How quickly can I expect to feel a difference through your Wellness Coaching for somatic relief?
The experience of somatic relief is unique for everyone, especially when recovering from chronic conditions. While some clients report subtle shifts in comfort and peace after just a few sessions, deeper, more sustained changes typically unfold over several weeks or months. Our work is about gentle, consistent progress, honoring your body's own rhythm for healing and integration.
