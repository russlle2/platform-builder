Frequently Asked Questions

How does a Wellness Coach support me specifically after chronic illness or injury?
As your Wellness Coach, I specialize in the unique needs of individuals rebuilding health after significant challenges. My focus is on boosting your immune system and increasing your vitality through personalized plans, tailored to your specific recovery phase and health goals. You'll receive guidance on nutrition, gentle activity, stress resilience, and sleep to strategically strengthen your body.
Will this program interfere with my doctor's recommendations for my recovery?
Absolutely not. My role as your Wellness Coach is to complement and support the medical advice you receive from your healthcare providers. We'll work within your doctor's guidelines, creating a wellness plan that enhances your body's natural abilities to recover and thrive, ensuring all strategies align with your ongoing medical care. Your safety and progress are paramount.
What kind of results can I expect from working with a Wellness Coach focused on immune support?
Clients typically report increased energy levels, improved resilience against common illnesses, better sleep quality, and a greater sense of control over their health. You can expect to develop sustainable habits that bolster your immune system and elevate your overall vitality, helping you move beyond the limitations of your past illness or injury with confidence.
Is this Wellness Coach program suitable if I'm still feeling very fatigued or limited?
Yes, absolutely. This program is designed with your current capacity in mind. We start exactly where you are, gradually introducing practices that build your strength and energy without overwhelming you. As your Wellness Coach, my priority is to create a supportive, step-by-step path that honors your body's current needs while gently guiding you towards greater vitality and immune resilience.
