Frequently Asked Questions

What does a Wellness Coach do for sleep improvement?
As your Wellness Coach, I provide personalized support and gentle guidance to help you understand and improve your sleep. We'll work together to identify simple habits and routines that can lead to more consistent and restorative rest, focusing on practical steps that fit your lifestyle without needing any prior wellness experience.
I'm not tech-savvy; will your Wellness Coach programs be too complicated?
Absolutely not! My approach as a Wellness Coach is designed to be gentle and accessible. We'll focus on practical, easy-to-understand strategies. If we use any tools or resources, I'll walk you through them step-by-step, ensuring you feel comfortable and confident. Your comfort and ease are my priority.
How quickly can I expect to see improvements in my sleep with a Wellness Coach?
Every individual is unique, so the timeline for improvement can vary. However, many clients working with a Wellness Coach notice small, positive shifts in their sleep patterns and overall well-being within a few weeks of consistently applying the gentle strategies we discuss. The key is consistent, manageable steps tailored to you.
Is this a replacement for medical advice from my doctor?
No, as your Wellness Coach, I do not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. My role is to offer gentle guidance and support for lifestyle habits that promote better sleep and overall vitality. It's always important to consult with your doctor regarding any health concerns or before making significant changes to your health routine.
