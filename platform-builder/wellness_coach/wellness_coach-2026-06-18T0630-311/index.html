Frequently Asked Questions

What kind of physical pain does Wellness Coaching for seniors address?
Our Wellness Coaching for seniors aged 60+ focuses on common age-related discomforts such as joint stiffness, muscle aches, lower back pain, and general bodily tension. We work to provide gentle strategies for reducing these types of physical discomfort.
Is Wellness Coaching suitable for seniors with limited mobility?
Absolutely. Our Wellness Coaching programs are specifically designed to be gentle and adaptable for seniors with varying levels of mobility. We prioritize your safety and comfort, tailoring every approach to your current physical capabilities and needs.
How does somatic healing fit into Wellness Coaching for seniors?
Somatic healing in our Wellness Coaching involves gentle techniques that help you become more aware of your body's sensations. For seniors, this can be particularly effective in releasing long-held tension, improving body awareness, and reducing chronic physical pain by addressing its roots in the body.
What can I expect in a typical Wellness Coaching session for pain relief?
In a Wellness Coaching session, you can expect a calm, conversational approach. We'll discuss your specific discomforts and goals, then gently guide you through simple, evidence-based movements or awareness exercises. The focus is always on your comfort, understanding, and gradual improvement.
