Frequently Asked Questions

What does a Wellness Coach focused on postural health for seniors involve?
As an Elder Grace Wellness Coach, I guide you through gentle practices and mindful exercises specifically designed to improve your posture, balance, and overall body awareness. We focus on movements that enhance comfort and stability, respecting your body’s unique needs as you age, helping you feel more aligned and at ease.
How can a Wellness Coach help me improve my balance and prevent falls?
We'll work together on subtle movements and awareness techniques that strengthen your core stability and improve your proprioception – your body’s sense of its position in space. This gentle approach helps you feel more grounded and confident in your footing, reducing the likelihood of imbalances and falls, and fostering a greater sense of security in your daily activities.
Is this Wellness Coaching suitable if I have existing physical limitations or discomfort?
Absolutely. My approach as an Elder Grace Wellness Coach is highly individualized and gentle. We start exactly where you are, adapting all practices to your current comfort level and any physical considerations you might have. Our aim is to alleviate discomfort and enhance your body's capabilities without pushing past your natural limits, always with deep respect for your body's wisdom.
What kind of commitment is required for this Wellness Coaching?
Our Wellness Coaching sessions are typically weekly or bi-weekly, lasting about an hour. The most impactful commitment is your willingness to engage gently with the practices we explore and to bring a sense of mindful awareness into your daily life. Consistency, even in small doses, yields the most beautiful results for your postural health and overall well-being.
