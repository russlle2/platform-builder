How is this Wellness Coaching different for someone recovering from chronic illness or injury?

Our Wellness Coaching is uniquely tailored to your specific needs, focusing on gentle, non-overwhelming methods to regulate your nervous system. We avoid intense approaches that might exacerbate your condition, instead prioritizing practices that support your body's delicate healing process and reduce undue stress.

Will Wellness Coaching help with my physical pain related to my chronic condition?

While Wellness Coaching doesn't directly treat physical pain, by regulating your nervous system and reducing stress, many clients report a significant reduction in their perception of pain and an increased ability to cope with discomfort. A calmer nervous system often leads to less tension and a greater sense of ease in the body.

I'm often fatigued. Will I have the energy for Wellness Coaching sessions?

Absolutely. Our Wellness Coaching sessions are designed to be restorative, not draining. We work at your pace, incorporating gentle techniques that can be adapted to your energy levels. The goal is to conserve and build your energy, not deplete it further, by teaching you how to manage your stress response effectively.

What kind of results can I expect from Wellness Coaching for nervous system regulation?

You can expect to learn practical tools for daily stress reduction, experience improved sleep, feel more grounded, and develop a greater sense of inner calm. Our Wellness Coaching aims to empower you with strategies to gently guide your body out of 'fight or flight' and into a more restful 'rest and digest' state, which is crucial for long-term recovery.
