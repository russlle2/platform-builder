Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly does a Wellness Coach do for new mothers during postpartum recovery?
As your Wellness Coach, I provide dedicated support for the unique emotional and relational shifts that come with new motherhood. We’ll focus on gentle ways to strengthen your bonds with your partner and family, and help you reconnect with your own sense of self, all while honoring your postpartum recovery needs.
I'm feeling overwhelmed and don't have much energy. Is this Wellness Coach program right for me?
Absolutely. Our approach is designed with your limited time and energy in mind. We meet you where you are, offering accessible tools and conversations that don't add more to your plate, but rather help lighten your load by improving communication and emotional connection in your relationships.
My partner and I are struggling to communicate since the baby arrived. Can a Wellness Coach help with relationship healing?
Yes, many new parents experience communication challenges. As your Wellness Coach, I offer a supportive space to explore these dynamics. We'll work on practical, heart-centered ways for you and your partner to understand each other better, express needs, and rebuild closeness during this new chapter.
I've never worked with a Wellness Coach before. What should I expect?
You can expect a warm, non-judgmental space where your experiences are valued. We'll chat about what's on your mind regarding your relationships and well-being as a new mother. There’s no prior wellness experience needed; we simply explore gentle, actionable steps together to bring more peace and connection into your life.
