How Your Nervous System Actually Gets Stuck

When back-to-back meetings and 2 a.m. work emails become your normal, your nervous system stays locked in 'alert' mode. This isn't 'just stress'—it's your biology screaming for relief. We map your specific stress triggers (like Slack notifications or weekend work guilt) and build regulation practices that interrupt the cycle with zero extra time.

Can you help me when I’m overwhelmed *during* a work call?

Yes. We practice 'invisible regulation' techniques you use while nodding along to a meeting—like subtle breath anchors that reset your nervous system without anyone noticing. Your focus stays on the call, not your panic.

Do I need to stop working after hours to heal?

No. We build regulation into your existing workflow. For example: using your commute to practice a 60-second reset routine, or scheduling 'calm blocks' instead of 'me time.' Your healing happens *while* you work, not after.

What if I’ve tried meditation and it didn’t work?

Medication isn’t for nervous system regulation. We use evidence-based methods you can apply *in the moment*—like using the sensation of your feet on the floor to ground yourself during a stressful email. It’s practical, not philosophical.

How long until I feel less drained at 4 p.m.?

Most clients notice reduced 4 p.m. crash within 2 weeks. We track your nervous system shifts with simple daily check-ins (not journaling overload), so you see tangible progress as your body learns to disengage from 'always on' mode.
