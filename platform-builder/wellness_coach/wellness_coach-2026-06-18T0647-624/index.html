Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Wellness Coach specifically help me rebuild my immune system after a long illness?
As your Wellness Coach, we'll design a personalized plan focusing on nutrient-dense foods, specific herbs, stress reduction techniques, and gentle movement that are known to support immune function. This is especially tailored to your post-illness state, ensuring practices are restorative, not depleting.
I'm recovering from an injury; how does a Wellness Coach assist with vitality building?
For recovery from injury, your Wellness Coach will guide you in practices that enhance circulation, reduce inflammation, and support tissue regeneration, all of which are vital for rebuilding overall vitality. We focus on nourishing your body deeply to accelerate recovery and restore your energetic reserves.
What ancient traditions does your Wellness Coach practice draw upon for immune support?
Our Wellness Coach approach integrates principles from Ayurvedic medicine, Traditional Chinese Medicine, and indigenous wisdom traditions. These include practices like herbal adaptogens, specific breathing exercises, gentle energy work, and dietary modifications to bring your system back into balance and strengthen your natural defenses.
Will a Wellness Coach program be too strenuous for someone with low energy from chronic illness?
Absolutely not. Your Wellness Coach program is designed with your current energy levels in mind. We start with very gentle, foundational practices and gradually build as your strength returns. The emphasis is always on nurturing and replenishing, ensuring that every step contributes positively to your recovery without overwhelming your system.
