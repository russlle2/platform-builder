Frequently Asked Questions

Why is immune support so crucial when I'm going through a big life change?
Major life transitions often bring increased stress and altered routines, which can directly impact your immune system's effectiveness. As your Wellness Coach, I help you proactively strengthen your body's defenses during these times, so you can navigate changes with greater resilience and less susceptibility to illness, keeping your energy steady.
How does your Wellness Coach approach to immune support differ from just taking supplements?
While supplements can play a role, your Wellness Coach focuses on a foundational approach. We look at optimizing your diet, sleep patterns, stress management, and movement—the core pillars of immune health. This integrative strategy creates sustainable strength, rather than relying solely on quick fixes, ensuring your vitality is built from within.
What kind of commitment is required to work with a Wellness Coach on vitality building?
Commitment varies per individual, but generally, it involves dedicated weekly or bi-weekly sessions with your Wellness Coach and consistent application of the strategies we develop together. Your progress is cumulative, meaning the more consistently you engage, the more robust your immune system and overall vitality will become over time.
Can a Wellness Coach help me understand the connection between my energy levels and immune health?
Absolutely. As your Wellness Coach, I specialize in connecting these dots for you. We'll explore how factors like sleep quality, nutritional choices, and even emotional well-being directly impact both your daily energy and your immune system's ability to protect you. You'll gain clarity and practical tools to optimize both.
