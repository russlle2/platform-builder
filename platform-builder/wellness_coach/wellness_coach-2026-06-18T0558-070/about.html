How is your approach different from general stress management for students?

We focus exclusively on nervous system regulation, not just relaxation. You'll learn to interrupt stress responses in real-time—like when your heart races before a quiz. This targets the acute pressure of college life, not vague 'self-care' advice.

Do you work with students on medication for anxiety?

Yes! Our coaching complements medication by giving you tools to manage physical symptoms immediately. You'll learn techniques to reduce anxiety intensity so medication works better. We never replace your doctor, but provide practical support.

How quickly can I expect to see results?

Many notice calm during a stressful lecture in the first session. Consistent practice leads to measurable shifts in handling academic pressure within 2-4 weeks. We track progress together so you see tangible change.

What if I'm not ready for a full coaching program?

Start with one session to get a personalized nervous system tool. You'll walk away with one practical technique to use immediately, and we'll discuss next steps. No pressure to commit to a long program.
