Frequently Asked Questions

I'm a busy college student. How can a Wellness Coach fit into my demanding schedule?
We understand your time is precious. Our sessions are designed to be efficient and effective, giving you actionable techniques you can integrate into your daily routine. Even short, consistent practices can make a huge difference in managing physical tension and anxiety, helping you feel better without adding more stress to your plate.
My back always hurts from studying. Can a Wellness Coach really help with that specific pain?
Absolutely! Many college students report back and neck pain directly linked to long hours at desks and high-stress levels. We'll work together to identify the physical patterns contributing to your discomfort and teach you somatic exercises and self-awareness tools to reduce and prevent those specific pains, bringing more comfort to your study sessions.
Is Wellness Coaching just for when things feel really bad, or can it help prevent problems too?
Wellness Coaching is incredibly effective for both! While we can certainly help when you're experiencing acute pain or high anxiety, our approach also focuses on proactive strategies. Learning to recognize early signs of tension and having tools to address them can prevent minor discomforts from escalating, helping you maintain a more stable and comfortable state throughout your college career.
What's the difference between a Wellness Coach and just seeing a therapist for my anxiety?
While both can be beneficial, a Wellness Coach, particularly one focused on somatic healing, centers on the physical manifestations of anxiety and stress. We'll explore how your emotions show up in your body—like a tight jaw or churning stomach—and use body-based techniques to release that tension, complementing any mental health support you might be receiving. We're here to help your body feel better, which in turn supports your overall well-being.
