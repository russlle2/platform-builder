Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Wellness Coach specifically help my sleep when I'm recovering from illness or injury?
As your Wellness Coach, I focus specifically on the unique challenges chronic illness or injury presents to your sleep. We'll explore gentle, tailored strategies that acknowledge your body's current state, helping you to soothe discomfort, reduce restlessness, and gradually re-establish a natural sleep cycle that supports your ongoing recovery and overall well-being.
Will this involve medication or drastic changes to my routine?
My Wellness Coach approach emphasizes natural, gentle methods. We won't be focusing on medication. Instead, we'll explore mindful practices, subtle shifts in your environment, and personalized routines that honor your body's current capacity. The goal is sustainable, restorative sleep that feels deeply aligned with your healing journey, not forced or overwhelming.
I've tried many things for sleep already; how is this different?
Your experience is understood and valued. As your Wellness Coach, my difference lies in tailoring ancient wisdom and deep listening to your specific recovery needs. This isn't a one-size-fits-all program. We delve into the spiritual and soulful aspects of rest, addressing not just symptoms, but the deeper energetic and emotional patterns that might be disrupting your sleep, especially while navigating chronic illness or injury.
What kind of commitment is required to see improvements in my sleep?
Committing to improving your sleep with a Wellness Coach during recovery is a gentle, yet consistent process. We'll start with small, manageable steps that build momentum. While individual progress varies, clients typically begin to notice subtle shifts within a few weeks, with more profound and sustained improvements emerging over a few months of dedicated, personalized guidance. Your pace is always respected.
