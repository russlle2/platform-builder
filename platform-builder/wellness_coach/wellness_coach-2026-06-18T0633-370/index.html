Frequently Asked Questions

How does Wellness Coaching specifically address postpartum physical pain?
Our Wellness Coach methodology focuses on understanding the root causes of your specific postpartum discomforts. We develop personalized strategies that may include gentle movement, breathwork, targeted stretches, and somatic practices to alleviate back pain, pelvic discomfort, shoulder tension, and more, helping your body recover effectively.
Is somatic healing suitable for all stages of postpartum recovery?
Yes, somatic healing techniques, guided by your Wellness Coach, are adaptable and beneficial at various stages of postpartum recovery. Whether you're weeks or months postpartum, these practices help you process physical sensations, release tension, and foster a deeper connection with your body, promoting sustained comfort and well-being.
What can I expect in a typical session with a Wellness Coach?
In a session with your Wellness Coach, you can expect a supportive and confidential space. We'll discuss your current physical concerns and recovery goals. Sessions typically involve guided somatic exercises, gentle physical practices, and personalized strategies for pain relief and body awareness that you can integrate into your daily life.
How quickly can I expect to feel relief from my postpartum physical discomfort?
While individual experiences vary, many new mothers report feeling a noticeable difference in their physical comfort and body awareness within a few sessions with their Wellness Coach. Consistency with the practices we co-create will accelerate your progress toward lasting relief and a more comfortable postpartum experience.
