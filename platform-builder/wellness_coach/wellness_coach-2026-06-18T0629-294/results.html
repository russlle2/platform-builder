Wellness Coach

Structured Support for Your Emotional Landscape After Illness or Injury

Your experience with chronic illness or injury can leave deep imprints, not just physically, but emotionally. My Wellness Coaching programs are specifically designed to address these emotional impacts, offering structured guidance to help you process grief, fear, frustration, and the sense of loss that often accompanies such challenges. We focus on rebuilding your internal sense of safety and capability, moving at your pace to cultivate lasting emotional well-being.
