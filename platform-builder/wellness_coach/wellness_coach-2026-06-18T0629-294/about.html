Structured Support for Your Emotional Landscape After Illness or Injury

How does Wellness Coaching specifically help with the emotional aftermath of chronic illness or injury?

My Wellness Coaching provides a safe, non-judgmental space to process the unique emotional stressors that arise from chronic health conditions or injuries. We focus on trauma-informed techniques to help you understand and manage feelings like anxiety, fear of recurrence, grief for lost abilities, and frustration, fostering emotional stability and resilience.

Is this Wellness Coaching suitable if I'm still actively dealing with my physical recovery?

Absolutely. Emotional recovery often runs parallel to physical recovery. My Wellness Coaching supports you during all phases, helping you manage the emotional toll that physical challenges can bring. We work to integrate emotional well-being into your broader recovery plan, ensuring you feel supported mentally as your body heals.

What kind of techniques does your Wellness Coaching use to address emotional trauma from illness or injury?

My Wellness Coaching uses an integrative approach, drawing on tools from mindfulness, somatic awareness, cognitive reframing, and stress reduction. The goal is to help you gently re-engage with your body and emotions, build self-compassion, and develop practical strategies for navigating triggers and fostering internal safety, all tailored to your specific experiences.

Will Wellness Coaching replace therapy or medical care for my chronic condition or injury?

No, Wellness Coaching is a distinct and complementary form of support. It focuses on present and future-oriented strategies for emotional well-being and personal growth, working alongside your medical treatments or therapy. I emphasize collaboration with your healthcare team to ensure a comprehensive approach to your overall recovery.
