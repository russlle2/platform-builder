Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly does a Wellness Coach for postpartum posture do?
As your Wellness Coach, I'll guide you through understanding the changes in your body after childbirth and help you gently re-establish comfortable posture and body awareness. We focus on practical movements you do every day, like holding your baby or bending down, to help reduce discomfort and build confidence in your body.
I'm not athletic at all. Is this Wellness Coaching still for me?
Absolutely! Our Wellness Coaching is designed for new mothers of all activity levels, especially those without prior wellness experience. We start exactly where you are, focusing on gentle, accessible movements and awareness that don't require any special skills or equipment. It's about feeling better in your daily life, not about intense workouts.
How is this different from physical therapy?
While physical therapy often addresses specific injuries or dysfunctions, my Wellness Coaching focuses on empowering you with a deeper understanding of your body's posture and movement patterns in your everyday life. We work on building sustainable habits for comfort and awareness, complementing any clinical care you might be receiving by integrating practical well-being strategies into your routine.
How long will it take to feel a difference in my posture?
Many mothers report feeling a noticeable difference in their comfort and awareness within a few sessions. Consistency with the gentle practices we discuss is key. Our goal is to equip you with tools and understanding that you can use daily, leading to lasting improvements in how your body feels and functions as you navigate motherhood.
