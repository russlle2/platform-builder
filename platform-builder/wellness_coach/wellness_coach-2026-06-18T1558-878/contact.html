Postpartum Posture Wellness Coach: Finding Your Center Again

After welcoming your little one, you might notice new aches, different ways your body feels, or simply feel 'off'. Our programs are designed to gently guide you back to comfortable posture and body awareness. We'll focus on how you carry your baby, sit to feed, and move through your day, helping you feel more aligned and less strained without needing any prior wellness experience.
