Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is Relationship Wellness Coaching for seniors?
Relationship Wellness Coaching for seniors focuses on supporting you in building and maintaining healthy, joyful connections with others. It's about enhancing communication, resolving misunderstandings gently, and strengthening the bonds with your family and friends, all within a kind and understanding space.
How does this type of Wellness Coaching help with family dynamics?
As your Wellness Coach, I can help you explore current family dynamics, offering gentle strategies to improve communication and understanding. We work together to bridge generational gaps, express your needs clearly, and foster a more harmonious and loving environment with your children, grandchildren, and other relatives.
Is Wellness Coaching suitable if I’m feeling isolated or lonely?
Absolutely. If you're experiencing feelings of isolation or loneliness, Wellness Coaching can provide a supportive space to explore these emotions. We can work on ways to reconnect with your community, deepen existing friendships, and even help you feel more comfortable initiating new, meaningful social interactions, all at your own pace.
What should I expect in a typical Wellness Coaching session?
In a typical session, you can expect a warm, confidential conversation. We'll chat about your concerns and hopes for your relationships. I'll listen without judgment and offer gentle insights and practical approaches tailored to your unique situation. It's a comfortable, collaborative process, always focused on your well-being and goals.
