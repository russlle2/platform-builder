How is Wellness Coaching different from therapy for college students?

While therapy often addresses past traumas or deep-seated mental health conditions, Wellness Coaching focuses on your present and future. We work on developing practical strategies, skills, and habits to improve your cognitive wellness and manage academic stress and anxiety effectively. It’s about building your strengths and moving forward with clear goals.

What specific issues can Wellness Coaching help me with as a college student?

Wellness Coaching is particularly helpful for managing academic pressure, reducing test anxiety, improving focus and concentration, developing effective study habits, combating procrastination, and navigating social anxieties common in a university setting. We'll help you gain mental clarity and build resilience against college stressors.

Is Wellness Coaching a good fit if I'm already seeing a therapist?

Absolutely! Wellness Coaching can complement therapy by providing practical, action-oriented support for your day-to-day college life. If your therapist is helping you with deeper emotional work, your Wellness Coach can help you implement strategies for better academic performance and stress management, acting as a bridge between insight and action.

How quickly can I expect to see results from Wellness Coaching for my anxiety and academic pressure?

The timeline for seeing results varies for everyone, but many students report feeling more in control and clearer-headed after just a few sessions. We focus on implementing actionable strategies right away, so you'll start building momentum and seeing improvements in your focus and stress levels as you consistently apply the techniques we develop together.
