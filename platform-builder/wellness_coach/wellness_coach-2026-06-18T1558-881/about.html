I've never done anything like this before. Is this Wellness Coach for me?

Absolutely! Our services are designed for students with no prior wellness experience. We start with the basics, focusing on gentle awareness and practical steps you can take right where you are. There's no judgment, just support.

How does improving my posture help with academic pressure and anxiety?

When you carry tension in your body, it can amplify feelings of stress and anxiety. By improving your posture and body awareness, you can learn to release physical tension, breathe more fully, and create a more open, calm physical state. This often translates to a clearer mind and a greater capacity to handle academic demands.

I spend so much time at my desk. How can I make changes without a huge time commitment?

We understand how packed your schedule is. Our Wellness Coach sessions focus on small, impactful changes you can integrate into your existing routines – whether it's how you sit while studying or how you stand while waiting in line. These aren't extra tasks, but gentle adjustments to your everyday movements.

What exactly is 'body awareness' and why is it important for college students?

Body awareness is simply noticing how your body feels and holds itself. For college students, who often push through discomfort, it's crucial. Becoming aware of your physical habits – like slumping or tensing up – helps you recognize when you're stressed and gives you tools to gently shift towards a more comfortable and supportive posture, which can calm your nervous system.
