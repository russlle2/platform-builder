What kind of physical pain does a Postpartum Wellness Coach address?

As your Wellness Coach, I focus on common postpartum physical discomforts such as lower back pain, hip soreness, neck and shoulder tension from feeding, pelvic discomfort, and general body stiffness. We'll work on gentle, somatic practices to alleviate these specific concerns.

Is this approach safe if I'm still recovering from childbirth?

Absolutely. Our methods are gentle and prioritize your body's healing process. As your Wellness Coach, I'll ensure all practices are tailored to your specific stage of postpartum recovery, taking into account C-section recovery, diastasis recti, and pelvic floor considerations, always encouraging you to listen to your body.

How is a Wellness Coach different from a physical therapist for postpartum recovery?

While a physical therapist focuses on clinical diagnosis and rehabilitation, as your Wellness Coach, I complement that by providing ongoing support for somatic healing, pain management techniques you can integrate daily, and guidance on rebuilding body awareness and comfort in your new role. We work together on practical, gentle strategies for your everyday life.

What if I feel too tired or overwhelmed to start a new program?

That's completely understandable, new motherhood is demanding! As your Wellness Coach, I meet you exactly where you are. Our sessions are designed to be supportive and restorative, not draining. We start small, with simple, accessible practices that fit into your life, gradually building your capacity for comfort and calm without adding to your overwhelm.
