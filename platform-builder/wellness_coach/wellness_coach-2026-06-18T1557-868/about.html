What exactly does 'postural health and body awareness' mean for me as a new mom?

It means we focus on how you hold your body, move, and stand throughout your day, especially with the new demands of caring for a baby. We'll address common issues like upper back tension from nursing, pelvic floor weakness, or a shifted center of gravity, helping you move more efficiently and comfortably.

Is Wellness Coaching like physical therapy for postpartum recovery?

While we complement the work of physical therapists, Wellness Coaching focuses more on education, personalized movement strategies for daily life, and building conscious body awareness. We empower you to integrate healthy movement patterns and self-care into your routine, beyond just rehabilitative exercises.

I'm feeling overwhelmed and tired. How can I possibly add another 'thing' to my schedule?

We completely understand! Our approach is designed to fit into your life, not add more stress. We focus on small, impactful shifts and practical strategies that you can integrate into your existing routines, rather than demanding extensive new time commitments. Our goal is to simplify, not complicate.

How soon after childbirth can I start Wellness Coaching for my posture?

It's always best to consult with your healthcare provider first. Generally, once you've received clearance at your postpartum check-up (typically around 6 weeks), we can begin with gentle, foundational work. We tailor everything to your individual recovery pace and specific needs.
