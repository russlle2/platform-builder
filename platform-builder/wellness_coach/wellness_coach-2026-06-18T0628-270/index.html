Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is trauma-informed care in postpartum Wellness Coaching?
Trauma-informed care means we understand that past or present difficult experiences can impact your postpartum recovery. Your Wellness Coach will approach your situation with sensitivity, respect, and an awareness of potential triggers, ensuring a safe and supportive environment for your emotional healing.
Is this Wellness Coaching only for mothers who experienced birth trauma?
No, our Wellness Coaching supports all new mothers in their postpartum recovery. While we are equipped to assist with birth trauma, our care extends to anxiety, depression, identity shifts, relationship challenges, and any emotional distress unique to this intense period of your life.
How quickly can I expect to feel a difference with postpartum Wellness Coaching?
Emotional healing is a personal process, and results vary. Many clients report feeling a sense of relief and greater clarity after just a few sessions. Your Wellness Coach will work with you at your pace, focusing on sustainable strategies that build long-term emotional resilience.
What if I feel overwhelmed and don't know where to start with Wellness Coaching?
Feeling overwhelmed is completely understandable. Your Wellness Coach will meet you exactly where you are. We'll start by creating a calm space for you to voice your concerns, then gently guide you through practical steps tailored to your immediate needs and long-term goals for emotional well-being.
