Finding Ground When Your World Shifts: A Wellness Coach Approach

How does a Wellness Coach specifically help with physical pain during life transitions?

As your Wellness Coach, we focus on the direct link between significant life changes and your body's physical responses. We guide you through somatic practices and mindful movements to release tension, alleviate chronic aches, and build resilience, rather than just discussing the emotional aspects of the transition.

What kind of techniques does your Wellness Coach practice involve for somatic healing?

Our Wellness Coach approach incorporates elements like guided body awareness, gentle stretching, focused breathwork, and practical self-regulation exercises. These techniques are designed to help you tune into your body's signals, release stored stress, and cultivate a deeper sense of physical comfort and presence.

Is Wellness Coaching suitable if my physical pain is long-standing, not just related to a recent change?

Absolutely. While we specialize in transitions, our Wellness Coach methods for somatic healing are effective for long-standing physical discomfort. We explore how past experiences might be held in your body, providing gentle ways to release chronic tension and find new patterns of ease, regardless of their origin.

What can I expect in a typical session with your Wellness Coach?

In a session with your Wellness Coach, you can expect a conversation about your current physical experience and life circumstances. We'll then move into guided somatic practices, which might include gentle movements, breathing exercises, or body scans, all designed to help you connect with and soothe your physical self. You'll leave with practical tools to use between sessions.
