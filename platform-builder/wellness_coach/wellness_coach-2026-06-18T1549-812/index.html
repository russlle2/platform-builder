Frequently Asked Questions

How is this Wellness Coaching different for someone recovering from chronic illness or injury?
Our Wellness Coaching is specifically adapted to respect the unique considerations of recovery. We prioritize gentle, non-aggressive methods, focusing on building foundational strength and awareness without exacerbating existing conditions. Your program is carefully calibrated to your specific health status and recovery phase, ensuring safety and efficacy.
I'm concerned about pain during postural adjustments. How do you address this in Wellness Coaching?
Your comfort and safety are paramount. We work within your pain-free range, using subtle cues and modifications to avoid discomfort. Our focus is on identifying and alleviating sources of strain through improved body mechanics, not on pushing through pain. We encourage open communication about any sensations you experience, adjusting our approach immediately.
Will Wellness Coaching interfere with my current medical treatments or physical therapy?
Our Wellness Coaching is designed to complement, not replace, medical treatments or physical therapy. We can coordinate with your healthcare providers if you wish, ensuring our postural and body awareness work aligns with your overall recovery plan. Our aim is to enhance your body's natural healing capabilities and support the work you're already doing.
How quickly can I expect to see improvements in my postural health and body awareness through Wellness Coaching?
Progress varies for everyone, especially during recovery. However, many clients report noticing subtle shifts in comfort and awareness within a few sessions. Our consistent, gentle approach is designed for steady, sustainable progress. We focus on building a resilient foundation rather than quick fixes, supporting your body's natural pace of adaptation.
