What exactly does a Wellness Coach do for postpartum mothers?

As your Wellness Coach, I specialize in helping new mothers regain body awareness and improve postural health during postpartum recovery. We focus on gentle core strengthening, pelvic floor connection, and practical alignment tips for daily activities like feeding and lifting, ensuring you move with greater comfort and confidence.

Is it safe to start a Wellness Coach program soon after giving birth?

It's always best to get clearance from your healthcare provider before starting any new exercise or wellness program after childbirth. Once you have their approval, our Wellness Coach programs are designed to be very gentle and progressive, tailored to your individual recovery stage and needs. We prioritize safety and gradual progress.

How is this different from traditional physical therapy for postpartum recovery?

While physical therapy addresses specific injuries or dysfunctions, our Wellness Coach approach focuses on education, preventative movement, and establishing long-term healthy postural habits. We complement physical therapy by providing ongoing support and practical strategies for integrating body awareness into your daily life as a new mother.

Will a Wellness Coach help with my back pain from holding my baby?

Absolutely. Many new mothers experience back pain due to changes in posture and the demands of caring for a baby. As your Wellness Coach, I'll guide you through exercises and practical adjustments to improve your body mechanics, strengthen supporting muscles, and develop habits that reduce strain, helping you feel more comfortable and supported.
