Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly does a Wellness Coach do for college students facing anxiety?
As your Wellness Coach, I provide a safe, confidential space to discuss the specific pressures you face in college. We'll work together to identify triggers for your anxiety and academic stress, then develop practical, actionable strategies to manage them, improve your emotional well-being, and help you build a more supportive routine.
I've never done anything like this before. Is Wellness Coaching for me?
Absolutely! Many college students haven't. Our approach is designed to be gentle and accessible, with no prior wellness experience needed. We'll start exactly where you are, making sure you feel comfortable and understood every step of the way. It's about finding what works for you.
How is this different from therapy or academic advising?
While therapy often delves into diagnosing and treating mental health conditions, and academic advising focuses on course selection and career paths, Wellness Coaching is about empowering you with tools to manage your day-to-day emotional well-being, reduce stress, and navigate college life more effectively. We complement, rather than replace, other forms of support.
I'm worried about the time commitment with my busy academic schedule. How does coaching fit in?
We get it – your time is precious. Our sessions are designed to be efficient and focused, typically 45-60 minutes, and we can schedule them around your classes and study times. The goal is to give you strategies that actually save you time and mental energy in the long run, not add more stress to your plate.
