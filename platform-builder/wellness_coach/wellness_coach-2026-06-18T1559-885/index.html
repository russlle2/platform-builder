Frequently Asked Questions

I'm so busy with my kids. How can I fit Wellness Coaching into my schedule?
We understand! Our sessions are designed to be flexible and practical. We focus on small, impactful changes you can integrate into your existing daily routines, like how you hold your child or stand while doing dishes. It's about making your everyday movements work better for you, not adding extra tasks.
I'm not an 'athletic' person. Do I need any special fitness experience for this Wellness Coaching?
Absolutely not! Our Wellness Coaching is for everyone, especially those new to thinking about body awareness. There's no prior fitness experience required. We'll start right where you are and explore gentle ways to understand and support your body, making it accessible and comfortable for you.
What exactly will I learn about my posture in Wellness Coaching?
You'll learn to recognize common postural habits that might be contributing to your discomfort, especially those related to parenting tasks like feeding, carrying, or playing on the floor. We'll explore simple, gentle ways to align your body more comfortably, helping you feel more supported and less strained throughout your day.
Will Wellness Coaching really help with the stress of parenting?
Many parents find that physical discomfort adds to their overall stress. By addressing your body's aches and improving how you move, you free up energy and reduce a common source of irritation. When your body feels more comfortable, it's easier to respond calmly to the demands of parenting, and generally feel a bit more at ease.
