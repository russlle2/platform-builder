Frequently Asked Questions

How does Wellness Coaching specifically help with major life transitions?
As your Wellness Coach, I provide a structured, supportive space to process the emotional complexities that come with big life shifts. We focus on identifying underlying emotional patterns, integrating past experiences, and building practical coping mechanisms so you can navigate change with greater ease and self-awareness.
What does 'trauma-informed' mean in the context of your Wellness Coaching?
Being trauma-informed means I understand that past experiences can significantly impact how you respond to current life changes. Our Wellness Coaching sessions prioritize safety, choice, and collaboration, ensuring we address emotional healing with sensitivity and respect for your unique history, without retraumatizing you.
Is Wellness Coaching suitable if I'm feeling overwhelmed or stuck during a transition?
Absolutely. Feeling overwhelmed or stuck is a common response to significant change. My Wellness Coaching is specifically designed to help you untangle those feelings, develop emotional resilience, and create a clear path forward. We'll work together to establish grounding practices and clarify your next steps.
How do you integrate Eastern and Western approaches in your Wellness Coaching practice?
I blend the introspective and mindful practices often found in Eastern traditions, like breathwork and somatic awareness, with evidence-based psychological tools from Western approaches. This integrative Wellness Coaching method helps you understand both the 'why' behind your emotions and the 'how' to effectively manage them during periods of transition.
