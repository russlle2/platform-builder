Our approach in three short steps

Set a single outcome, apply a compact daily sequence, reflect weekly.
  1. Choose one measurable outcome (sleep, steady energy, focused work).
  2. Follow a targeted micro-sequence each day for a week and track a single metric.
  3. Adjust the sequence; keep what works, discard what doesn’t.
