Frequently Asked Questions

I’m a college student with a crazy schedule. How can Wellness Coaching help me prioritize sleep?
As your Wellness Coach, I understand your demanding schedule. We'll explore small, realistic adjustments you can make that fit into your college routine, not disrupt it. The goal is to build sustainable habits that improve your sleep without adding more pressure.
I’ve never done anything like Wellness Coaching before. Do I need any special knowledge?
Absolutely not! This Wellness Coaching is designed for everyone, especially those new to prioritizing their well-being. We'll start with the basics, and I'll guide you gently through every step, ensuring you feel comfortable and supported.
My anxiety makes it hard to fall asleep. Can Wellness Coaching really help with that?
Yes, many college students find that anxiety significantly impacts their sleep. As your Wellness Coach, we'll focus on practical techniques to quiet your mind before bed and create a calming environment that encourages restorative rest. It's about finding what soothes your specific worries.
What kind of commitment does Wellness Coaching require from a busy college student?
Our Wellness Coaching sessions are designed to be flexible and impactful. We'll work together to set achievable goals and develop strategies that fit your life. The commitment is more about consistent, small actions you take for yourself, not adding another huge task to your already full plate.
