Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is nervous system regulation and how does Wellness Coaching help?
Nervous system regulation refers to bringing your body's stress response system into a more balanced state. Our Wellness Coaching provides you with guided practices and insights to calm an overactive nervous system, which can be very common during chronic illness or injury recovery. We focus on gentle techniques that support your body's natural ability to rest and repair.
Is Wellness Coaching suitable if I have limited energy due to my condition?
Absolutely. Our Wellness Coaching sessions are designed to be flexible and considerate of your energy levels. We prioritize gentle, accessible strategies that don't require significant exertion. The goal is to reduce your burden, not add to it. We'll work together to find approaches that fit comfortably within your current capacity.
How is this different from traditional therapy or medical treatment?
Wellness Coaching at complements, rather than replaces, medical treatment or therapy. We focus on practical, day-to-day strategies for stress reduction and nervous system regulation, empowering you with tools you can use immediately. We don't diagnose or treat medical conditions, but rather support your well-being alongside your healthcare team.
What can I expect in a typical Wellness Coaching session?
In a typical Wellness Coaching session, we'll start by checking in on your current experience and any specific stressors you're facing. We'll then explore and practice simple techniques for calming your nervous system, such as mindful breathing or gentle movement. We'll also discuss strategies for integrating these practices into your daily life, always with your unique recovery needs in mind. It's a collaborative and supportive space.
