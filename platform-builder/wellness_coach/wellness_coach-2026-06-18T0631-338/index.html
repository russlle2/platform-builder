Frequently Asked Questions

How does wellness coaching specifically help with physical pain during a life transition?
Major life changes often manifest as physical symptoms like chronic tension or discomfort. Your wellness coach at helps you identify these somatic responses, providing targeted techniques and a scientific framework to reduce pain and increase your body's capacity to adapt to stress, rather than being overwhelmed by it.
Is this different from traditional physical therapy or mental health support?
Yes, our approach complements these services by focusing on the mind-body connection during transitions. We don't diagnose or treat medical conditions, but rather, we coach you on self-regulation techniques and somatic awareness practices that empower you to manage your physical responses to stress and change, enhancing your overall well-being.
What kind of results can I realistically expect from this type of wellness coaching?
You can expect to develop a deeper understanding of your body's stress patterns, learn practical strategies for releasing physical tension, and experience a greater sense of groundedness and calm. Many clients report reduced pain, improved posture, and enhanced emotional resilience as they navigate their life shifts.
I'm not sure if my pain is related to my life transition. How can I tell?
It's very common for the body to hold stress from significant life events. Your initial consultation with your wellness coach will involve a detailed discussion to explore the connections between your current physical symptoms and your life circumstances, helping you determine if our somatic-focused coaching is the right fit for your needs.
