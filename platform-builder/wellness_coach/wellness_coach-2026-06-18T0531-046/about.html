How does this differ from typical stress management coaching?

We focus solely on nervous system regulation—no wellness buzzwords, no 'find your passion' exercises. You get science-based tools to physically reset your stress response, so you handle workload without feeling depleted.

I'm busy. How do you fit this into a demanding schedule?

Your program is built around your existing routine. We’ll find 15 minutes that actually work for you—not an hour you’ll cancel. You’ll reset during your lunch break or before your next meeting, no extra time required.

What if I've tried other stress methods that didn't work?

That’s why we skip generic advice. Our neurobiology-based approach gives you precise techniques to calm your nervous system in the moment—no 10-day retreat needed to feel better.

How quickly can I expect to see results?

Most notice a shift in stress response within the first week—like not getting triggered by a tough email. By week 4, you’ve built a new physiological baseline, so high-pressure moments no longer drain you.
