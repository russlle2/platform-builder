Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Wellness Coach specifically help with college anxiety?
As your Wellness Coach, I provide tailored strategies for academic stress, social anxieties, and future uncertainties common in college. We'll work on practical coping mechanisms, time management without overwhelm, and emotional regulation techniques that directly apply to your student experience.
What does 'trauma-informed care' mean in a Wellness Coach setting for students?
It means we recognize that past difficult experiences can affect your present emotional responses. Your Wellness Coach will create a safe, non-judgmental space, offering tools to gently process emotional triggers related to stress or pressure, without re-traumatizing you. Your comfort and emotional safety are paramount.
Is working with a Wellness Coach confidential?
Absolutely. All your sessions with your Wellness Coach are completely confidential. This is your private space to discuss academic challenges, personal anxieties, and emotional well-being without fear of judgment or disclosure to your university or family, unless there's an immediate safety concern.
How quickly can I expect to see results from working with a Wellness Coach?
While individual progress varies, many students report feeling a sense of relief and gaining practical coping skills within the first few sessions with their Wellness Coach. Sustainable change takes consistent effort, but you'll start building momentum and a stronger sense of control over your emotional landscape relatively quickly.
