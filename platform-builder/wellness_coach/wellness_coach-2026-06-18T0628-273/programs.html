Unpacking Academic Pressure with Your Wellness Coach

College life throws a lot at you. From demanding coursework to social pressures, it's easy to feel constantly on edge. As your Wellness Coach, I offer a safe space to unpack the anxieties specific to your academic journey. We'll identify the roots of your stress and develop personalized tools to manage overwhelm, turning academic pressure into a manageable challenge, not a crushing burden. You're not alone in this.
