Frequently Asked Questions

How does Wellness Coaching specifically help athletes with their immune system?
As your Wellness Coach, I help you identify lifestyle factors, like nutrition gaps or recovery deficits, that might be weakening your immune response. We then create a personalized plan to optimize these areas, focusing on nutrient-dense foods, specific supplements if needed, and tailored rest strategies to keep your defenses strong and ready for your next challenge.
I'm always feeling tired despite being active. Can a Wellness Coach help me build more vitality?
Absolutely. Chronic fatigue is common among active individuals. Your Wellness Coach will work with you to uncover the underlying reasons for your low energy, whether it's related to sleep quality, hydration, stress management, or nutrient timing. We'll develop actionable steps to boost your natural vitality, helping you feel more energetic and engaged throughout your day.
What kind of support can I expect from a Wellness Coach to manage physical stress?
Your Wellness Coach provides guidance on integrating effective recovery practices into your routine, such as targeted nutrition for muscle repair, mindful movement, and strategies to improve sleep quality. We focus on practical, gentle methods to help your body adapt to the demands of your active lifestyle, preventing injury and burnout.
Is Wellness Coaching suitable for all levels of athletes and active people?
Yes, Wellness Coaching is beneficial whether you're a seasoned athlete or just starting an active routine. My approach as a Wellness Coach is designed to be accessible and tailored to your individual needs, helping you build a stronger foundation for health and performance regardless of your current fitness level.
