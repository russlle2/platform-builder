The 5-Phase Map

I translate your diagnostic into a five-phase map: Clear, Align, Build, Anchor, and Integrate. Each phase has a weekly focus and an intentional habit set.

  1. Clear: clarify values and commitments
  2. Align: match actions to values
  3. Build: install micro-habits
  4. Anchor: ritualize and scale
  5. Integrate: sustain with community
Daily Seeds

Micro-habits are small, non-negotiable practices planted into your day. They compound. Examples we teach in cohorts:

  • Morning 5: two breaths + one intentional action
  • Pause Check: 60-second alignment before a meeting
  • Evening Note: one line of gratitude or learning
