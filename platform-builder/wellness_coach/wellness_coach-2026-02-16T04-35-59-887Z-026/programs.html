Questions People Ask
How does a cohort differ from 1:1?
Cohorts emphasize peer practice and shared accountability; 1:1 is tailored deep work. Both are complementary.
What if I miss a session?
Recordings and practice notes are available; live participation is encouraged for best results.
Is this spiritual coaching?
Yes — the work is spiritually informed and focused on embodied habits and ethical practice without medical claims.
