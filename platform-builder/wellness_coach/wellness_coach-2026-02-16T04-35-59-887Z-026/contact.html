Cohort Layout — Seasons & Sessions

Our cohorts move in communal seasons: a 10-week rhythm, weekly gatherings, and focused implementation sprints. Each season centers one framework — Breath, Boundaries, or Bio-Rhythm — and includes peer accountability circles.

  • Week 1–2: Awareness and gentle assessment
  • Week 3–6: Habit stacking and rituals (micro-practices)
  • Week 7–10: Integration, reflection, and next-step mapping
