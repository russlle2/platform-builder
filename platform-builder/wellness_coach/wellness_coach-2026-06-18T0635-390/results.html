Targeting Postpartum Discomfort: A Wellness Coach Approach

Your body's landscape has shifted, often bringing new points of tension in the back, neck, hips, or pelvic floor. Our Wellness Coach programs are specifically designed to address these physical challenges, guiding you through gentle practices that honor your recovery phase. We focus on restoring comfort and functional movement, helping you feel more like yourself again without pushing too hard, too fast.
