Questions people ask about pricing

How quickly will I see progress?
We focus on 2–3 high-leverage habits each month. Clients often notice energy and focus shifts within 3–6 weeks when they consistently do the micro-practices.
Can I change plans mid-way?
Yes — plans are adjustable. We'll review your outcomes quarterly and recommend changes to match your momentum.
Are there refunds?
Payments cover coaching time and resources. We handle exceptions case-by-case; the best path is an initial discovery call to confirm fit.
Do you work with health conditions?
I focus on habits, routines, and resilience strategies. I do not provide medical advice; if you have a health condition, please consult your provider and we can adapt coaching accordingly.
