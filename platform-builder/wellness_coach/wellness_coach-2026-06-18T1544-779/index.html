Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly does a Wellness Coach do for seniors focused on relationships?
As your Wellness Coach, I provide gentle guidance and a listening ear to help you navigate relationship challenges. We'll explore ways to improve communication with family, friends, or even new acquaintances, always focusing on your comfort and peace of mind. It's about finding practical, heartfelt solutions together.
I'm not very tech-savvy. Is Wellness Coaching still accessible for me?
Absolutely! We understand that technology can be a hurdle. Your Wellness Coach, , is happy to offer sessions in a way that's most comfortable for you, whether that's over the phone or in-person in . We make it easy to connect and receive support without any extra stress.
What if I feel shy about discussing personal relationship matters?
It's completely normal to feel a bit hesitant, and we honor that. Your Wellness Coach provides a confidential and non-judgmental space where you can share at your own pace. There's no pressure, just a supportive environment for you to explore your feelings and goals around your relationships.
How long does a typical Wellness Coaching program last?
The length of your Wellness Coaching program is entirely up to you and your needs. Some seniors find a few sessions are enough to get started, while others prefer ongoing support. We’ll discuss what feels right for your situation during our initial chat, ensuring it fits your comfort and schedule.
