Frequently Asked Questions

How does a Wellness Coach specifically help with major life transitions?
As your Wellness Coach, we provide tailored support to help you process the emotional impact of significant life changes. We focus on techniques for emotional regulation, stress reduction, and building resilience, ensuring you feel supported and equipped to adapt to your new circumstances without feeling lost or overwhelmed.
What does 'trauma-informed care' mean in Wellness Coaching?
Trauma-informed care means we understand that past experiences can deeply influence how you respond to current stressors. Your Wellness Coach creates a safe, respectful environment, offering tools that prioritize your emotional safety and empower you to process feelings gently, never pushing you beyond your comfort zone.
Will I feel grounded and calm even with big changes happening around me?
Our goal is to help you cultivate an inner sense of stability, regardless of external circumstances. Your Wellness Coach will guide you through practices that connect you to your core strength, helping you manage anxiety and find moments of peace, even when your outer world feels chaotic.
How long does Wellness Coaching for life transitions typically take?
The duration of Wellness Coaching varies for each individual, as everyone's pace for processing and integrating change is unique. Your Wellness Coach will work with you to create a personalized plan, focusing on your specific needs and ensuring you feel adequately supported through your transition.
