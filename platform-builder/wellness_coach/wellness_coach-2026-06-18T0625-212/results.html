Wellness Coach

Beyond the Physical: Addressing Emotional Strain in Active Lifestyles

Your body pushes limits, and so does your mind. As an athlete or active individual, you face unique emotional demands: performance pressure, injury setbacks, and the constant drive for improvement. These can accumulate, creating silent stress that impacts not just your mental state, but your physical recovery and overall well-being. This Wellness Coach practice offers a space to process these experiences, acknowledging their profound effect on your capacity and resilience.
