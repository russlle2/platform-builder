Why this approach?

You keep what fits. The work here is to make habit simple, social, and easy to keep.

  • Outcome-first planning: we set a single measurable target each week.
  • Recipe fragments: tiny, repeatable actions that stack across days.
  • Group labs: short weekly sessions to test adjustments and keep momentum.

Tangible habits you build

Anchor rituals

Two simple, fixed moments you do each day—morning intention and evening reset. They act as anchors for other habits.

Micro-practices

1–3 minute actions (breathing, one-minute planning) that slot into your schedule and add up.

Weekly check labs

A 60-minute group session to adjust targets, swap tactics, and track progress.

Journal pulses

Short prompts that surface what worked and what’s next—keeps learning clear.
