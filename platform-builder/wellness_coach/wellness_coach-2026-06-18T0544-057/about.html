Why Your Nervous System Needs Special Care Right Now

After birth, your body isn't just recovering—it's been in high-alert mode for weeks. Cortisol stays elevated, sleep gets fractured, and your nervous system is flooded. This isn't 'just tired'; it's your biology trying to recalibrate. My postpartum wellness coaching focuses *exclusively* on lowering that constant 'danger' signal so you can feel steady, even in chaos.

Can I do this while my baby is awake?

Absolutely. All practices are designed for your actual reality: holding your baby, waiting for a feeding, or during a quiet moment while they nap. They work in 3-5 minute bursts—no extra time needed.

How is this different from therapy?

I focus *only* on nervous system regulation—not processing trauma or emotions. We work with your biology, not your past. Think of it as training your body's 'rest and digest' system to override the birth stress response.

Do I need to know yoga or meditation?

No. I break down breathwork into 'what to notice' (like the weight of your feet on the floor) instead of 'how to breathe.' It's physical, not spiritual—no chanting or special clothing required.

What if I'm exhausted all the time?

That's why the practices are micro. We start with what *already* happens in your day (e.g., when you put your baby down for a nap) and anchor you there. You're not adding to your load—you're using moments you already have.
