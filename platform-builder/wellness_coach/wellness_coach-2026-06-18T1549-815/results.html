Understanding Parental Postural Strain with Your Wellness Coach

The repetitive movements of childcare – lifting, carrying, bending – can lead to significant postural challenges. As your Wellness Coach, I help you identify these patterns and develop practical strategies to mitigate their impact. We'll examine how your everyday interactions with your children and home environment contribute to physical tension, offering clear, actionable steps to foster healthier body mechanics without adding to your already full plate.
