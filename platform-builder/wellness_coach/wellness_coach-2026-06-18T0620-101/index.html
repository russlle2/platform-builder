Frequently Asked Questions

How does Senior Sleep Wellness Coaching specifically help someone over 60?
Our coaching is tailored to address the unique sleep changes common in seniors, such as altered sleep architecture, sensitivity to light, and the impact of certain medications or comfort issues. We focus on gentle, non-invasive strategies that support your body's natural rhythms, helping you achieve more consistent, restorative sleep without harsh interventions. It's about understanding and respecting your body's current needs.
What kind of changes can I expect from working with a Wellness Coach on my sleep?
You can expect to learn practical techniques for calming your mind before bed, establishing a more comfortable sleep environment, and developing a consistent routine that signals to your body it's time to rest. Many clients experience a reduction in the time it takes to fall asleep, fewer nighttime awakenings, and a greater sense of being refreshed upon waking. We aim for sustainable improvements that fit naturally into your life.
Is this Senior Sleep Wellness Coaching suitable if I have existing health conditions?
Absolutely. Our approach is gentle and complementary. We always recommend you continue to consult with your medical doctor for any health conditions. Our coaching works alongside your healthcare plan, focusing on lifestyle and behavioral adjustments that support better sleep. We prioritize your safety and comfort, ensuring our recommendations are appropriate for your individual circumstances and never interfere with medical advice.
How long does it typically take to see improvements in sleep with a Wellness Coach?
The timeline for improvement varies for everyone, as sleep patterns are deeply individual. Some clients notice subtle positive shifts within a few weeks of consistently applying new strategies, while for others, it may take a bit longer to establish new habits. Our focus is on sustainable progress and equipping you with tools for lasting better sleep, rather than quick fixes. We're here to support you every step of the way.
