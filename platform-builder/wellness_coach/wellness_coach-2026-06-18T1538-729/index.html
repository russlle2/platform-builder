Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly does a Wellness Coach do for seniors focusing on relationships?
As your Wellness Coach, helps you thoughtfully examine your current relationships – with family, friends, and yourself. We work together to identify areas where you might feel disconnected or wish for more understanding, then create gentle, actionable steps to improve communication, set healthy boundaries, and nurture warmer, more satisfying connections in your life.
Is Wellness Coaching appropriate if I’m feeling a bit overwhelmed by family situations?
Absolutely. It's entirely normal to feel overwhelmed sometimes, especially with evolving family dynamics. Our Wellness Coaching offers a calm, confidential space to discuss these feelings without judgment. We’ll explore gentle strategies to manage stress, express your perspective clearly, and foster greater harmony, helping you feel more in control and at peace.
How is this different from talking to a friend or family member about my relationship concerns?
While friends and family offer valuable support, a Wellness Coach provides an objective, professional perspective. offers structured guidance and proven techniques, free from personal biases or history, to help you gain new insights and develop specific skills for relationship improvement. It's a dedicated space focused entirely on your well-being and relational goals.
What if I'm not very comfortable with technology? Can I still benefit from Wellness Coaching?
Of course! We understand that comfort levels with technology vary. Our primary goal is to make Wellness Coaching accessible and comfortable for you. We can discuss options for in-person sessions if you're in , virtual calls with simple instructions, or even phone consultations. Your comfort is our priority, and we'll find a way that works best for you to connect.
