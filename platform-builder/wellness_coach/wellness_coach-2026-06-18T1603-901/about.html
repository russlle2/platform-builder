What exactly does a Wellness Coach do for college students?

As your Wellness Coach, I provide personalized support and practical strategies specifically designed for the challenges of college life. We'll work on managing academic stress, anxiety, improving sleep, creating healthy routines, and finding balance so you can excel without feeling constantly overwhelmed.

How can seasonal wellness coaching help with my academic pressure?

Seasonal wellness coaching helps you anticipate and prepare for the specific demands of different times in the academic year. For example, before finals, we can develop stress-reduction techniques and study schedules to prevent burnout, ensuring you approach high-pressure periods feeling calm and capable.

Is this just another therapy session, or is it different?

Wellness coaching is distinct from therapy. While therapy often delves into past experiences and mental health conditions, coaching is forward-looking and action-oriented. We focus on setting and achieving present and future goals, building specific skills, and implementing practical strategies to improve your daily well-being as a student.

I'm already swamped; how can I fit wellness coaching into my schedule?

I understand your time is precious. Our sessions are designed to be efficient and focused on actionable steps that integrate into your existing schedule. In fact, many students find that by investing a little time in coaching, they actually gain more time and energy by becoming more organized and less stressed overall.
