How can a Wellness Coach specifically help me with postpartum sleep when my baby wakes up frequently?

As your Wellness Coach, we'll focus on optimizing the sleep you do get. This includes strategies for maximizing rest during naps, improving your sleep environment, and developing a resilient mindset around interrupted sleep. We'll work on practical routines and techniques that fit into your baby's schedule, ensuring you feel more rested even with awakenings.

Is Wellness Coaching just about sleep schedules, or does it cover broader aspects of postpartum recovery?

While sleep improvement is a primary focus, as your Wellness Coach, we understand it's deeply connected to your overall postpartum well-being. We'll address factors impacting your rest, such as stress management, establishing boundaries, and nurturing your own needs, all contributing to more restorative sleep and a stronger recovery.

I'm already overwhelmed. Will adding a Wellness Coach to my routine make things more complicated?

Absolutely not. As your Wellness Coach, my role is to simplify, not complicate. We'll create a plan that feels manageable and empowering, integrating seamlessly into your existing life. Our sessions are designed to reduce your mental load and provide clear, actionable steps, giving you more energy rather than taking it away.

What makes your Wellness Coach approach different for new mothers compared to general sleep advice?

Our Wellness Coach approach is deeply tailored to the unique physiological and emotional demands of postpartum recovery. We recognize that traditional sleep advice often doesn't apply to new mothers. We focus on realistic, compassionate strategies that honor your body's recovery, your baby's needs, and your evolving role, empowering you to prioritize your rest effectively.
