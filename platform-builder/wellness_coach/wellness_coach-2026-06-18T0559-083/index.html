Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is 'nervous system regulation' in Wellness Coaching for seniors?
Nervous system regulation in Wellness Coaching means learning gentle ways to help your body and mind feel more balanced and at ease. It’s about reducing feelings of being overwhelmed or anxious, and instead encouraging a sense of calm and comfort. We do this through simple, practical techniques that you can easily use in your daily life.
Do I need any special equipment or prior experience for Wellness Coaching?
Absolutely not! Our Wellness Coaching is designed to be gentle and accessible for everyone, especially seniors. You won't need any special equipment, and you certainly don't need any prior wellness experience. We start exactly where you are and guide you every step of the way with straightforward, easy-to-understand methods.
How can Wellness Coaching help with everyday stress I experience?
Wellness Coaching helps you recognize the subtle signs of stress in your body and mind, and then provides you with very simple, gentle tools to respond to it. We focus on practical ways to bring a sense of peace into your day, helping you feel more in control and less burdened by daily worries. It's about finding small moments of calm that add up to big changes.
What if I'm not sure if Wellness Coaching is right for me?
It's perfectly normal to have questions! Our Wellness Coaching is for any senior aged 60 and over who is looking for a gentle, supportive way to reduce stress and feel more at ease. If you're curious about finding more comfort and calm in your life, we encourage you to schedule a friendly chat. There's no pressure, just an opportunity to see if it feels like a good fit for you.
