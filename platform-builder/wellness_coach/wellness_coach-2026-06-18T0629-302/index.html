Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly does a Wellness Coach do for physical pain?
As your Wellness Coach, I help you identify the root causes of your physical pain and stress, beyond just symptoms. We'll use somatic techniques to release tension, improve body awareness, and develop personalized strategies to manage and reduce discomfort, helping you feel more in control of your body.
How is this different from seeing a physical therapist or chiropractor?
While I don't provide medical diagnoses or treatments like a physical therapist or chiropractor, as your Wellness Coach, I complement their work by focusing on the mind-body connection and developing sustainable self-care practices. I empower you with tools for ongoing physical and emotional regulation, addressing the stress component that often exacerbates physical issues.
Can a Wellness Coach really help with long-standing physical stress?
Absolutely. Long-standing physical stress often has a somatic component – your body holds onto tension. As your Wellness Coach, I guide you through practices that help release these ingrained patterns, promoting deeper relaxation, improved movement, and a significant reduction in chronic discomfort. It's about retraining your nervous system for greater ease.
What kind of commitment is required to work with a Wellness Coach?
The commitment involves actively engaging in our sessions and integrating the practices we develop into your daily routine. As your Wellness Coach, I'm here to support you in building consistency. The more you practice, the faster you'll notice improvements in your physical comfort, stress management, and overall athletic well-being.
