Program FAQs

How big are cohorts?

We keep groups intentionally small (8–18 people) to preserve safety and meaningful feedback.

Do you provide templates and trackers?

Yes. Every cohort gets a compact habit tracker and a one-page brief tailored to the cohort curriculum.

How do I know which path fits me?

If you want faster iteration choose Seed; for deep scaffolding choose Foundations; for life changes choose Embodied. Book a discovery call for a recommendation.
