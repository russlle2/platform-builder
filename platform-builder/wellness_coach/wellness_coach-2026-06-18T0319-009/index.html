Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Wellness Coach specifically help me as a parent of young children?
As a Wellness Coach, I specialize in the unique stressors parents face: sleep deprivation, constant demands, and the emotional rollercoaster of raising little ones. We'll focus on practical strategies to calm your nervous system, manage daily overwhelm, and help you feel more in control and less reactive, leading to a more harmonious home environment.
Is Wellness Coaching just another thing I have to add to my already packed schedule?
Absolutely not. My approach as your Wellness Coach is about streamlining, not adding. We identify small, impactful shifts that integrate seamlessly into your existing routine. The goal is to reduce your stress and free up your mental energy, not create more demands. We find what works for your unique family rhythm.
I feel so overwhelmed I don't even know where to start. Can a Wellness Coach really help with that?
Yes, that's exactly where I come in as your Wellness Coach. Feeling overwhelmed is a common starting point. We'll begin by gently uncovering the biggest stressors and then, step-by-step, build simple, effective strategies for nervous system regulation. My role is to provide clarity, support, and a clear path forward, so you don't have to figure it all out alone.
What's the difference between a Wellness Coach and therapy for stress management?
While both can be helpful, as a Wellness Coach, I focus on forward-looking, actionable strategies to manage present stress and regulate your nervous system. We identify specific challenges and build new habits and coping mechanisms. Therapy often delves deeper into past experiences or underlying mental health conditions. I can help you determine if my coaching or another resource is the best fit for your needs.
