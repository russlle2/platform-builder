Frequently Asked Questions

What kind of emotional challenges does postpartum Wellness Coaching address?
Our Wellness Coaching specifically supports new mothers through emotional fluctuations, feelings of overwhelm, birth processing, and the general emotional intensity that can accompany the postpartum period. We help you navigate these feelings with grace and effective coping strategies.
How is this Wellness Coaching different from therapy for new mothers?
While both offer support, our Wellness Coaching is focused on forward movement and building your emotional toolkit for daily life. We offer practical strategies and a supportive framework to help you cultivate resilience and emotional well-being, rather than diagnosing or treating clinical conditions. We focus on your strengths and capabilities.
Is your approach truly trauma-informed for postpartum recovery?
Absolutely. Our Wellness Coaching is grounded in a trauma-informed lens, meaning we recognize the potential impact of birth experiences and early motherhood on your emotional state. We create a safe, non-judgmental space, prioritize your sense of control, and work at your pace to ensure you feel secure and respected throughout our sessions.
How quickly can I expect to feel a difference with postpartum Wellness Coaching?
The pace of emotional repair is unique to everyone. Many mothers report feeling a sense of relief and greater clarity after just a few sessions, as they begin to process their experiences and implement new coping mechanisms. Consistent engagement with the strategies we explore will help you build lasting emotional stability.
