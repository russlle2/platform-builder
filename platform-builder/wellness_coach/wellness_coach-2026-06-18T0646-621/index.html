Frequently Asked Questions

What kind of immune support does your Wellness Coaching offer for seniors?
Our Wellness Coaching provides gentle, holistic strategies for immune support, focusing on ancient practices like specific breathing exercises, mindful movement, and nutritional guidance that are appropriate for seniors. We aim to help you build your body's natural defenses in a way that feels nurturing and sustainable.
How does your Wellness Coach approach help me build vitality in my golden years?
We focus on building vitality by addressing your physical, mental, and spiritual well-being. This includes exploring gentle energy-boosting techniques, practices for mental clarity, and ways to connect with your inner peace, all tailored to help you feel more energetic and engaged with life.
Is Wellness Coaching suitable for seniors with existing health conditions?
Our Wellness Coaching is designed to complement your existing healthcare. We provide gentle, non-medical support for overall well-being. It's always important to consult with your primary healthcare provider about any new wellness practices, and we encourage open communication with your doctors.
What can I expect from my first session with a Senior Wellness Coach?
In your first session, we'll have a warm, conversational chat about your current well-being, your aspirations for immune support and vitality, and what gentle practices might resonate with you. It's an opportunity for us to get to know each other and begin to map out a personalized path for your unique needs.
