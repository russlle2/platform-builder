Frequently Asked Questions

What makes this Wellness Coaching approach suitable for seniors 60+ specifically for sleep?
Our approach is gentle and holistic, understanding that your body's needs change with age. We prioritize non-invasive methods, respecting your comfort and focusing on sustainable, natural ways to improve your sleep, rather than aggressive interventions. We tailor every recommendation to your specific health profile and preferences.
Will this Wellness Coach work with my existing health conditions or medications?
Absolutely. Our methods are designed to complement your current health regimen. We will always encourage you to consult with your primary healthcare provider regarding any new practices, and we carefully consider your existing conditions and medications when suggesting gentle sleep-supportive techniques. Your safety and well-being are paramount.
How long does it typically take to see improvements in my sleep with a Wellness Coach?
Improvements can vary for everyone, as sleep patterns are deeply personal. Many clients report feeling a difference in their ability to relax and fall asleep more easily within a few weeks of consistent practice. Sustained, deeper improvements in sleep quality often develop over several months as you integrate new habits into your daily routine.
Is Wellness Coaching for sleep improvement done in person or can it be virtual?
We offer flexible options to suit your comfort and convenience. You can engage in Wellness Coaching sessions in person here in CITY, or opt for virtual sessions from the comfort of your own home. Both formats provide the same personalized attention and effective guidance to help you achieve better sleep.
