Frequently Asked Questions

Can you really help if I'm training 5+ days a week?
Absolutely. Your nervous system thrives on consistency—not more hours. We build micro-practices that fit into your existing schedule, so you recover better without cutting training time.
What if I don't have time for 'self-care'?
This isn't 'self-care' as a separate thing. These are 3-90 second tools you use while waiting for your next set, during your cool-down, or even while drinking coffee. They’re designed for active people who already have full calendars.
How is this different from regular stress management?
We target the nervous system specifically—not just 'calming down.' Athletes need precise regulation: helping your body switch from 'fight' to 'recover' after effort. It’s science-backed, not just feel-good advice.
Do you work with runners, CrossFit, or team sports?
Yes. Whether you’re chasing PRs, hitting the gym, or playing soccer, your nervous system responds the same way to stress. We tailor techniques to your movement patterns and training load, so it works for your sport.
