Frequently Asked Questions

How does a Senior Wellness Coach specifically help with immune support for individuals over 60?
As your Senior Wellness Coach, I develop customized plans that consider the unique needs of an aging immune system. This includes strategies for nutrient optimization, incorporating specific micronutrients known to support immunity, and guiding you on gentle activities that enhance circulation and cellular health, all without putting undue stress on your body.
What kind of activities will I be doing to build vitality with a Wellness Coach?
Our vitality-building activities are gentle and accessible. We might explore light strength exercises that support bone density, balance routines to prevent falls, or mindful breathing practices that reduce fatigue. The goal is to incrementally increase your energy levels and physical capability in a safe, enjoyable way, guided by your Senior Wellness Coach.
Is the approach of this Senior Wellness Coach suitable for someone with existing health conditions?
Absolutely. My approach as a Senior Wellness Coach is always gentle and highly personalized. We'll discuss your current health status and any existing conditions in detail. While I don't diagnose or treat, I can help you implement lifestyle practices that complement your medical care and support your overall well-being, always encouraging consultation with your primary healthcare provider.
How often would I meet with my Senior Wellness Coach, and what does a typical session involve?
Typically, clients meet with their Senior Wellness Coach weekly or bi-weekly, depending on their program and needs. A session usually involves reviewing your progress, discussing any challenges, learning new strategies for immune support or vitality, and setting achievable goals for the coming period. It's a collaborative and supportive conversation focused entirely on your health objectives.
