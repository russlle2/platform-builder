Frequently Asked Questions

How does Wellness Coaching specifically help with athletic stress?
Your Wellness Coach provides specialized techniques and protocols designed to downregulate your nervous system after intense physical exertion. This helps reduce inflammation, improve sleep quality, and accelerate muscle repair, directly addressing the unique physiological demands of athletes and active people.
Is this Wellness Coaching for all athletes, or only those experiencing high stress?
This Wellness Coaching benefits any active individual or athlete looking to optimize recovery and prevent stress-related performance plateaus or injuries. Whether you're feeling overwhelmed or proactively seeking an edge, managing your nervous system is crucial for sustained performance and well-being.
What kind of techniques will your Wellness Coach program involve?
Your program will integrate evidence-based practices such as targeted breathing exercises, guided somatic awareness, and specific recovery protocols. We focus on practical, measurable strategies that you can integrate into your daily routine to effectively regulate your stress response and enhance your body's repair mechanisms.
How quickly can I expect to see results from this Wellness Coach approach?
While individual responses vary, clients often report improved sleep quality and a greater sense of calm within a few weeks of consistently applying the techniques. Significant improvements in recovery, resilience, and sustained performance typically become evident over two to three months as your nervous system adapts and rebalances.
