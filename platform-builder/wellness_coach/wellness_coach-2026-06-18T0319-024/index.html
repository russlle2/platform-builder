Frequently Asked Questions

How does Wellness Coaching specifically address the physical stress athletes face?
Our Wellness Coaching focuses on nervous system regulation, which directly impacts your body's ability to recover from intense physical exertion. We teach you techniques to switch from 'fight or flight' (sympathetic dominance) to 'rest and digest' (parasympathetic dominance), reducing inflammation and promoting cellular repair, crucial for an athlete's recovery.
Will this type of Wellness Coaching interfere with my existing training regimen?
Not at all. Our Wellness Coaching complements your training by enhancing your body's capacity to absorb and adapt to your physical demands. It's about optimizing your internal environment for better recovery and stress resilience, rather than adding another physical burden. It integrates seamlessly to support your athletic goals.
What kind of techniques will I learn for stress reduction and nervous system regulation?
You'll learn practical, accessible techniques such as targeted breathwork, guided mindfulness practices, and specific movement patterns designed to soothe your vagus nerve. These methods are rooted in ancient wisdom and proven to calm the nervous system, reduce perceived exertion, and improve your body's physiological response to stress.
How quickly can I expect to see improvements in my physical stress and recovery?
Many athletes report feeling a noticeable difference in their sense of calm and recovery within a few sessions, especially concerning sleep quality and reduced muscle tension. Consistent application of the learned techniques will lead to more profound and lasting improvements in your overall stress resilience and physical well-being.
