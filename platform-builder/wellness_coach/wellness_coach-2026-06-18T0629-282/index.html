Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is trauma-informed care in Wellness Coaching for athletes?
Trauma-informed care means we understand that stress, injury, and intense competition can sometimes feel overwhelming, and past experiences can influence your current emotional state. Your Wellness Coach creates a safe, understanding environment where you feel respected and in control, helping you gently process these feelings without judgment.
I've never done wellness coaching before. Is this right for me as an athlete?
Absolutely! Our Wellness Coaching is designed to be accessible for everyone, especially those new to wellness practices. You don't need any prior experience. We meet you where you are, offering clear, actionable support to help you manage the emotional aspects of your active life.
How can Wellness Coaching help me if my main issue is physical pain or injury?
While we don't treat physical injuries, your Wellness Coach helps you address the emotional impact of pain, setbacks, and recovery. This includes managing frustration, fear of re-injury, identity shifts, and the mental fortitude needed for rehabilitation. By supporting your emotional well-being, we help you approach your physical recovery with greater resilience.
What should I expect in a typical Wellness Coaching session?
In a session with your Wellness Coach, you can expect a confidential and non-judgmental conversation. We'll explore what's currently challenging you emotionally regarding your physical activity. We'll listen, offer insights, and collaboratively develop practical strategies for managing stress, building emotional coping skills, and moving towards your personal well-being goals.
