Frequently Asked Questions

How does a Wellness Coach specifically help with postpartum stress?
As your Wellness Coach, I provide targeted, evidence-based strategies to help you identify triggers, implement stress-reducing practices, and understand the physiological aspects of your nervous system during postpartum. My guidance focuses on practical, sustainable methods to bring you more calm and regulation.
Is this coaching suitable if I'm severely sleep-deprived as a new mother?
Absolutely. Our strategies are designed to be accessible even when you're sleep-deprived. We focus on small, impactful actions that don't require significant time or energy, ensuring you can still make progress towards stress reduction and nervous system regulation without feeling overwhelmed by the process itself.
What kind of results can I expect from nervous system regulation coaching?
Clients typically report feeling more grounded, experiencing less frequent or intense moments of overwhelm, and developing a greater sense of control over their emotional responses. My aim as your Wellness Coach is to equip you with tools to navigate the challenges of postpartum with increased resilience and calm.
How does your Wellness Coaching differ from therapy for postpartum anxiety?
While both can be beneficial, my Wellness Coaching focuses on proactive stress reduction, nervous system regulation techniques, and developing coping strategies for the normal stressors of postpartum. If you are experiencing clinical anxiety or depression, I would recommend consulting with a mental health professional, and I can support you alongside that process.
