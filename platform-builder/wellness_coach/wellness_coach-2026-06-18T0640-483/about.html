What exactly does a Wellness Coach do for college students?

As your Wellness Coach, I partner with you to develop personalized strategies for managing academic stress, improving focus, and reducing anxiety so you can perform better in your studies and feel more balanced in your daily college life.

I'm super busy with classes and assignments. Will I have time for Wellness Coaching?

Absolutely! Sessions with your Wellness Coach are designed to fit into your busy schedule. We focus on actionable, time-efficient strategies that integrate seamlessly into your routine, helping you gain back time by being more effective.

I've never done anything like this before. Do I need prior wellness experience?

Not at all! Your Wellness Coach approach is gentle and accessible. We start exactly where you are, building foundational skills at your pace. No prior experience with wellness or coaching is needed – just a willingness to try something new.

How can a Wellness Coach help me specifically with academic pressure?

Your Wellness Coach will help you identify the root causes of your academic pressure. We'll work on techniques for effective time management, breaking down large assignments, handling procrastination, and developing a resilient mindset to tackle challenging coursework without feeling constantly overwhelmed.
