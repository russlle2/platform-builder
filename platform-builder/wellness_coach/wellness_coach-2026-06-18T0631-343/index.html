Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Wellness Coach help with my study-related physical pain?
Many college students experience physical discomfort like neck stiffness or headaches due to prolonged sitting and stress. Your Wellness Coach will guide you through simple somatic exercises and awareness techniques to release tension, improve posture, and alleviate these aches, helping you study more comfortably.
Is Wellness Coaching really for me if I'm just feeling 'stressed out'?
Absolutely. Stress often manifests physically, leading to muscle tension, shallow breathing, and even digestive issues. Your Wellness Coach will help you connect with your body's signals, offering practical strategies to calm your nervous system and reduce the physical impact of academic and social pressures.
What does somatic healing mean in the context of college anxiety?
Somatic healing focuses on the body's sensations to process emotions and release tension. For college anxiety, this means we'll explore how anxiety 'feels' in your body—perhaps as a tight chest or restless legs—and then use gentle movement and breathwork to create a sense of ease and calm, helping you feel more grounded.
Will Wellness Coaching fit into my busy college schedule?
Yes, our sessions are designed to be efficient and practical. We'll focus on tools and techniques you can easily integrate into your daily routine, even between classes or during study breaks. The goal is to provide accessible ways for you to manage physical discomfort and stress without adding more pressure to your schedule.
