Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly does a Wellness Coach do for senior cognitive wellness?
As your Wellness Coach, I provide personalized support and gentle strategies specifically designed to enhance your mental clarity, memory, and focus. We'll work together on practical techniques and lifestyle adjustments that support your brain health, tailored to your unique needs and pace.
Is Wellness Coaching suitable if I'm already feeling a bit forgetful?
Absolutely. Our gentle Wellness Coaching is perfect for those who want to proactively support their cognitive function or address mild, age-related changes in memory or focus. It's about building resilience and confidence in your mental abilities, not addressing medical conditions.
What kind of commitment is involved with your Wellness Coach program?
Our programs are designed to be flexible and respectful of your time. We typically start with an initial conversation to understand your goals, then schedule regular, gentle coaching sessions. The commitment is about consistent, manageable steps that fit comfortably into your life.
How can a Wellness Coach help me stay engaged and mentally active?
We'll explore enjoyable activities and mental exercises that keep your brain stimulated in ways that resonate with you. This might include memory-boosting games, new learning opportunities, or strategies to improve your daily organization and routine, all guided by your preferences and comfort.
