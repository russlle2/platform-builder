I'm a college student with a crazy schedule. How can Wellness Coaching help me find time for better sleep?

That's a super common concern! We'll work together to pinpoint realistic, small changes that fit into your existing routine. It's not about adding more to your plate, but optimizing what you're already doing and making sleep a priority in a way that feels manageable and sustainable for *your* unique schedule. We'll find those pockets of opportunity.

I feel like my anxiety about school keeps me awake. Can Wellness Coaching really address that?

Absolutely. Anxiety and sleep are deeply connected, especially for college students. My Wellness Coaching approach directly tackles the anxious thoughts and academic pressures that often interfere with restful sleep. We'll develop strategies to calm your mind before bed, manage stress during the day, and create a sense of peace that allows you to fall asleep more easily and stay asleep.

What makes your Wellness Coaching different from just getting more sleep advice online?

While there's a lot of great info out there, online advice is rarely personalized. As your Wellness Coach, I offer one-on-one support tailored specifically to your challenges, your academic environment, and your personal goals. We'll go beyond generic tips to create a practical plan that truly resonates with you and provides the accountability you need to make lasting changes.

How quickly can I expect to see improvements in my sleep and anxiety with Wellness Coaching?

Every student's journey is unique, but many clients report feeling a difference in their stress levels and sleep quality within a few sessions. Consistency is key! We'll set realistic expectations and celebrate your progress along the way. My goal is to equip you with tools and habits that lead to sustainable, long-term improvements in your well-being, not just quick fixes.
