Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Wellness Coach help me with posture when I'm always lifting children?
As your Wellness Coach, I'll teach you practical body mechanics for everyday parenting tasks, like lifting and carrying, to minimize strain. We'll focus on small, consistent adjustments that make a big difference in how your body feels throughout the day, helping you move more efficiently and comfortably.
I'm a busy parent; will Wellness Coaching add more to my already full plate?
Absolutely not. Our Wellness Coach approach is about integrating simple, effective strategies into your existing routine, not creating new burdens. We'll find quick, impactful ways to boost your body awareness and comfort that fit seamlessly into your busy schedule, giving you more energy rather than taking it away.
My body often feels stiff and tired from stress. Can a Wellness Coach really help with that?
Yes! As your Wellness Coach, I'll help you recognize how stress manifests physically and provide gentle techniques to release tension and improve your body's flexibility. By enhancing your postural health, you'll find greater ease and reduced fatigue, even during stressful periods.
Is Wellness Coaching only for people with specific body issues?
Not at all. Our Wellness Coach services are for any parent who wants to feel more connected to their body, improve their comfort, and move through daily life with greater ease. Whether you have specific aches or just want to feel more present and grounded, we can help you build better body awareness.
