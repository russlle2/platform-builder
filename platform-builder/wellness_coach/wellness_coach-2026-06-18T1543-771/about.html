What exactly does a Wellness Coach do for college students?

As your Wellness Coach, we provide personalized support to help you navigate the unique challenges of college life, specifically focusing on anxiety, academic pressure, and relationship dynamics. We guide you in developing self-awareness, coping strategies, and effective communication skills to foster a more balanced and fulfilling experience.

How can heart-centered care help with my college anxiety?

Heart-centered care means we approach your well-being by acknowledging your emotions and personal values as central to your growth. For anxiety, this involves learning to listen to your body, understand your triggers, and develop compassionate responses to your inner critic, helping you build resilience from within rather than just managing symptoms.

My relationships with friends and family are strained by college stress. Can Wellness Coaching help?

Absolutely. Our Wellness Coaching emphasizes relationship healing. We'll work on improving your communication, setting healthy boundaries, and understanding how your stress impacts your interactions. The goal is to help you cultivate more supportive and less draining relationships, both with others and with yourself.

Is Wellness Coaching just another way to manage my time better for academics?

While time management can be a component, Wellness Coaching goes much deeper. We address the underlying anxiety and pressure that often make academic tasks feel insurmountable. Our focus is on empowering you with mental and emotional tools, allowing you to approach your studies and college life with greater clarity, confidence, and a sense of calm, rather than just ticking off tasks.
