Frequently Asked Questions

How can Wellness Coaching specifically help me after a chronic illness or injury?
Our Wellness Coaching is tailored to address the unique emotional and relational challenges that arise after chronic illness or injury. We help you navigate feelings of loss, frustration, and disconnection, guiding you to re-establish a nurturing relationship with your body and your sense of self, fostering inner peace and resilience.
What does 'relationship healing' mean in the context of my recovery?
Relationship healing here refers to mending the connection you have with yourself, your body, and your life after it's been impacted by illness or injury. It's about developing self-compassion, understanding your new limits, and building supportive routines that honor your current needs, rather than fighting against them.
Is Wellness Coaching suitable if I'm still actively managing my condition?
Absolutely. Our Wellness Coaching complements your medical care by focusing on your emotional well-being and inner resilience. We help you develop coping strategies, manage stress, and maintain a positive outlook, which can be incredibly supportive while you're actively managing a chronic condition or recovering from an injury.
What kind of support can I expect from as my Wellness Coach?
As your Wellness Coach, provides a judgment-free space where you'll receive empathetic listening, gentle guidance, and practical tools. You can expect a trusted friend who offers expert knowledge, helping you clarify your feelings, set achievable goals, and rebuild your confidence with heart-centered care.
