Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Student Wellness Coach specifically help with college anxiety?
As your Student Wellness Coach, I'll provide you with tailored strategies to directly address the unique stressors of college life. We'll focus on practical techniques for reducing anxiety symptoms, such as nervous system regulation exercises, time management for academic load, and developing coping mechanisms for social pressures, helping you feel more grounded and in control.
I'm already swamped with school; how will I fit coaching into my schedule?
I completely understand that your time is precious. Our coaching sessions are designed to be efficient and impactful, fitting into your busy college schedule. We'll prioritize actionable steps you can integrate seamlessly into your daily routine, rather than adding more to your plate. The goal is to create more calm and capacity, not less.
Is this just another way to tell me to 'relax' when I'm under intense academic pressure?
Absolutely not. As your Student Wellness Coach, I recognize that 'just relaxing' isn't helpful when you're facing real academic demands. Instead, we'll equip you with concrete tools and understanding to manage the physiological response to stress, regulate your nervous system, and build resilience so you can perform your best even under pressure, without feeling overwhelmed.
What kind of techniques will I learn to reduce stress and anxiety?
You'll learn a variety of practical, evidence-based techniques specifically for stress reduction and nervous system regulation. This includes breathwork for immediate calm, mindfulness practices tailored for focus, strategies for improving sleep quality, and methods to reframe anxious thoughts. These are not one-size-fits-all solutions, but rather tools we'll adapt to best support your unique needs as a college student.
