Frequently Asked Questions

How is this Wellness Coaching different for new mothers in postpartum recovery?
Our Wellness Coaching specifically acknowledges the profound physical and emotional changes you're experiencing postpartum. We focus on gentle, sustainable strategies tailored to your unique recovery needs, addressing sleep fragmentation, hormonal shifts, and the demands of caring for a newborn, rather than generic sleep advice.
Will Wellness Coaching help me sleep more, even with frequent night wakings?
While we can't control your baby's sleep patterns, our Wellness Coaching helps you maximize the quality of the sleep you do get. We'll focus on optimizing your sleep environment, calming your nervous system, and developing strategies to fall back asleep more easily, so your rest is more restorative, even in shorter bursts.
What if I feel too overwhelmed and tired to start a new Wellness Coaching program?
We completely understand! Our Wellness Coaching starts exactly where you are, with small, manageable steps that feel nurturing, not overwhelming. We prioritize simplicity and ease, helping you integrate gentle practices that support your rest without adding more to your plate. Your comfort and capacity are always our guide.
What kind of natural approaches does your Wellness Coach use for sleep improvement?
As a Wellness Coach, I emphasize nature-inspired techniques like mindful breathwork to soothe your nervous system, creating evening rituals with natural scents, gentle stretching or movement, and practical guidance on optimizing your sleep space for deep rest. We focus on holistic, non-pharmacological methods to support your body's innate ability to find calm and sleep.
