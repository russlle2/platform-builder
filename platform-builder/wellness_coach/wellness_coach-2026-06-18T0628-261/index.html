Frequently Asked Questions

What does a Wellness Coach do for seniors specifically?
As your Wellness Coach, I offer gentle, tailored emotional support for seniors, focusing on processing past experiences, managing current anxieties, and fostering a sense of peace. Our work respects your life experience and current needs, providing a calm, non-judgmental space for your emotional well-being.
How is your trauma-informed care different for older adults?
Our trauma-informed approach for older adults is especially gentle and respectful of your lifetime of experiences. We prioritize creating a safe, predictable environment, ensuring you feel heard and understood without any pressure to relive difficult memories. The focus is on building coping skills and finding inner calm, always at your comfortable pace.
I'm not sure if I need a Wellness Coach. What are some signs it might help me?
If you're feeling persistent sadness, struggling with changes in your life, finding it hard to enjoy activities you once loved, or experiencing lingering unease from past events, connecting with a Wellness Coach could be very beneficial. We offer a supportive hand to help you navigate these feelings and find a path toward greater emotional comfort.
How often would I meet with my Wellness Coach and what is the typical commitment?
Our sessions are typically scheduled weekly or bi-weekly, depending on your preferences and needs, to ensure consistent, gentle support. The total commitment varies for everyone, as healing is a personal journey. We aim for sustainable progress, not quick fixes, and will discuss a plan that feels right and supportive for you.
