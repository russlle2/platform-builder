Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Wellness Coach specifically help with postpartum stress?
As your Wellness Coach, I understand the unique stressors of postpartum. We'll focus on practical, non-judgmental strategies to reduce overwhelm, identify your specific triggers, and build daily habits that calm your nervous system. This isn't therapy; it's about actionable support to help you feel more stable and centered.
What does 'nervous system regulation' mean for a new mom?
For new moms, nervous system regulation means moving out of constant 'on-alert' mode. We'll work on techniques like gentle breathing, mindful moments, and subtle movement to help your body shift from stress to calm. It's about feeling less agitated and more present, even amidst the chaos of new motherhood.
Will coaching add more to my already busy schedule?
Absolutely not! As your Wellness Coach, my priority is to simplify, not complicate. Our sessions are designed to fit into your life, and the strategies we develop are about integrating small, impactful changes, not adding overwhelming tasks. We'll find what works for you, right where you are.
Is this a replacement for medical care or therapy?
No, as a Wellness Coach, I provide complementary support. I focus on stress reduction, nervous system regulation, and well-being strategies. If you have medical concerns or are experiencing severe mental health challenges, please consult with your healthcare provider or a licensed therapist. My role is to support your daily calm and resilience.
