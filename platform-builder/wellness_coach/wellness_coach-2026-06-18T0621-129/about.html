How does a Wellness Coach specifically address burnout related to poor sleep?

As your Wellness Coach, I focus directly on the sleep-burnout connection. We'll identify the root causes of your sleep disruption – be it stress, screen time, or an inconsistent schedule – and implement evidence-based techniques to improve sleep onset, duration, and quality. This targeted approach helps your body and mind recover more effectively from daily demands.

Is this Wellness Coach program suitable for someone with a very demanding work schedule?

Absolutely. My Wellness Coach programs are designed with the busy professional aged 30-45 in mind. We create practical, sustainable strategies that fit into your existing routine, rather than adding more to your plate. The goal is to optimize your current time for maximum restorative benefit, even if your hours are long.

What kind of commitment is required for your Wellness Coach sleep improvement programs?

Commitment is key, but it's about consistency, not excessive time. As your Wellness Coach, I typically recommend a minimum of 8-12 weeks to establish new, lasting sleep habits. This involves regular check-ins and dedicated practice of the strategies we develop, ensuring you build a solid foundation for long-term restorative rest.

How is your Wellness Coach approach different from just reading about sleep tips online?

While online tips can be helpful, as your Wellness Coach, I provide personalized guidance, accountability, and a tailored plan that considers your unique physiology, lifestyle, and specific burnout triggers. We move beyond generic advice to implement a precise, science-backed methodology designed for your specific needs, ensuring real, measurable improvements.
