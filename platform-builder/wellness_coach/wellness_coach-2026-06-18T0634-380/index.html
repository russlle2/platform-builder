Frequently Asked Questions

What kind of physical pain can your Wellness Coaching help with postpartum?
Our Wellness Coaching is designed to help new mothers address common postpartum physical discomforts such as lower back pain, hip soreness, shoulder tension from feeding, pelvic floor discomfort, and general body aches. We focus on gentle, accessible practices to bring you relief.
Do I need any prior experience with wellness practices to benefit from your coaching?
Absolutely not! Our Wellness Coaching is created for new mothers with no prior wellness experience. We use simple, easy-to-follow techniques and guide you every step of the way, ensuring you feel comfortable and supported as you learn.
How does somatic healing specifically support new mothers in postpartum recovery through your Wellness Coaching?
Somatic healing in our Wellness Coaching helps new mothers reconnect with their bodies after the profound experience of childbirth. It involves gently noticing and releasing physical tension, addressing feelings of disconnect, and fostering a sense of safety and comfort within your body, supporting your overall emotional and physical well-being.
Is your Wellness Coaching a substitute for medical care for postpartum issues?
No, our Wellness Coaching is a complementary support service. We focus on gentle practices for comfort and body awareness. It's important to continue seeing your medical doctor or physical therapist for any specific medical conditions or severe pain you may be experiencing postpartum. We're here to work alongside your medical team.
