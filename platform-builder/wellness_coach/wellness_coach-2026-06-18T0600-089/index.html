Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is Wellness Coaching for parents and how can it help me?
Wellness Coaching for parents helps you identify and address the unique stressors of raising young children. We focus on practical strategies to calm your nervous system and reduce the feeling of being overwhelmed, so you can navigate daily life with more ease and less reactivity. It's about finding simple, sustainable ways to feel better.
I'm already so busy. Will Wellness Coaching just add more to my to-do list?
Absolutely not! My Wellness Coaching is designed with your busy schedule in mind. We prioritize small, manageable steps that integrate seamlessly into your existing routines. The goal is to simplify, not complicate, helping you create more space for calm without adding extra burdens.
I don't have any 'wellness experience.' Is this still for me?
Yes, completely! You don't need any prior wellness experience. My Wellness Coaching approach is gentle and accessible, designed for anyone who wants to feel less stressed and more in control. We start exactly where you are and build from there, making it easy to understand and apply the techniques.
How quickly can I expect to see changes from Wellness Coaching?
Many parents report feeling a sense of relief and clarity even after our first few sessions. While significant shifts take consistent practice, you'll start gaining practical tools immediately that can help you respond to stressful situations differently. The pace is always tailored to what feels right for you and your family.
