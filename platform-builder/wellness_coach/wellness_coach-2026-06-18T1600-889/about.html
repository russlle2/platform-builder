What does a Postural Wellness Coach session involve for someone over 60?

Your session as a Wellness Coach client will be gentle and personalized. We'll start by discussing your current comfort levels and movement goals. Then, we might explore light stretches, mindful breathing exercises, or simple positional adjustments, all designed to enhance your body awareness and improve your posture without strain. Everything is adapted to your individual capabilities.

How can improving my posture impact my daily life as a senior?

Improving your posture as a senior can significantly enhance your daily life. With a Wellness Coach's guidance, you can experience less back and neck discomfort, improved balance for walking and daily tasks, and even increased energy. Better posture supports your organs and makes breathing easier, contributing to a greater sense of overall well-being and independence.

Is this Wellness Coaching suitable if I have specific joint concerns or limited mobility?

Absolutely. As your Wellness Coach, my priority is your safety and comfort. We will always work within your current capabilities and consider any specific joint concerns or mobility limitations you may have. The focus is on gentle, adaptive movements and awareness practices that support your body where it is, without pushing into pain.

What's the difference between this Wellness Coaching and physical therapy?

While both aim for improved physical function, a Wellness Coach focuses on education, preventative strategies, and enhancing general body awareness for long-term comfort and well-being. Physical therapy typically addresses recovery from injury or specific medical conditions. Our approach complements therapeutic care by building sustainable habits for postural health and gentle movement.
