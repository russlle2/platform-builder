Navigating Stress and Cultivating Spirit with Your Wellness Coach

College is a time of immense growth, but it often comes with a hefty dose of stress. You might feel a constant hum of anxiety about grades, future plans, or just fitting in. My programs are designed to help you quiet that noise, guiding you to build a stronger inner foundation. We’ll explore practices that bring a sense of grounding and purpose, so you can face deadlines and social pressures with a more settled spirit, not just survive, but truly thrive.
