Unpacking Academic Pressure with Your Wellness Coach

College isn't just about grades; it's about navigating new expectations and often intense academic pressure. We'll work together to identify the sources of your stress, build coping strategies, and reframe your approach to studies so you can thrive without burning out. This isn't about pushing harder, but about finding a healthier rhythm that supports your well-being alongside your academic goals.
