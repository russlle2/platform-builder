How can a Wellness Coach specifically help me with college anxiety?

As your Wellness Coach, I'll help you pinpoint the specific triggers for your anxiety in college, whether it's academic demands, social pressures, or future uncertainties. We'll then develop personalized, practical strategies to manage those feelings, build resilience, and improve your overall emotional well-being so you can navigate campus life with more ease.

What does 'trauma-informed' mean in the context of Wellness Coaching for students?

Trauma-informed care means I understand that past stressful or overwhelming experiences can impact how you respond to current college pressures. As your Wellness Coach, I approach our sessions with sensitivity, safety, and respect, helping you understand and gently process these impacts without judgment, fostering healing and empowerment.

Will Wellness Coaching interfere with my academic schedule or add more pressure?

Absolutely not! Wellness Coaching is designed to alleviate pressure, not add to it. We'll work together to create a schedule that fits seamlessly into your academic life. The goal is to provide you with tools and support that make your college experience feel more manageable and less stressful, ultimately enhancing your academic performance by improving your mental well-being.

Is Wellness Coaching just for students with severe anxiety, or can it help with everyday college stress?

Wellness Coaching is beneficial for any college student experiencing stress, anxiety, or overwhelm, regardless of the intensity. If you're feeling the typical pressures of deadlines, exams, or social adjustments, a Wellness Coach can offer proactive strategies and a supportive space to prevent these feelings from escalating and help you build healthier coping mechanisms.
