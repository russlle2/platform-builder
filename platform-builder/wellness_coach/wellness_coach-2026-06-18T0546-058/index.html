Frequently Asked Questions

How soon will I notice changes in my body?
Many notice lighter shoulders or clearer sleep within 3-5 days of practicing just 5 minutes daily. We focus on what your body can feel, not abstract 'results.'
Can I do this if I have arthritis or limited mobility?
Absolutely. All practices happen seated or standing with support. We adjust for your body—no new movements, just what feels safe in your chair or while holding a kitchen counter.
Do I need special equipment?
No. Just a comfortable chair, a warm mug, and your own hands. We use what's already in your home: the weight of a blanket, the sound of birds outside, your steady breath.
Is this different from regular 'relaxation' advice?
Yes. We work with the nervous system's physical responses—like how tension lives in your jaw or shoulders—using simple, sensory-based cues. Not just 'think calm.' Feeling calm.
