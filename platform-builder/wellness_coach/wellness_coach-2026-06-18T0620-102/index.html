Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Wellness Coach specifically help my sleep as an athlete?
As your Wellness Coach, I'll work with you to analyze your current sleep patterns, training schedule, and recovery needs. We'll identify specific stressors impacting your rest and create actionable, personalized strategies to improve sleep quality, reduce physical stress, and enhance your overall recovery for better performance.
I'm already very active; will adding sleep coaching just add more to my plate?
My goal as your Wellness Coach is to simplify, not complicate. We'll integrate sleep improvements organically into your existing routines, focusing on efficient, high-impact changes. The aim is to create sustainable habits that feel supportive, not burdensome, ultimately freeing up energy rather than consuming it.
What if my sleep issues are due to stress from training or competition?
That's a common challenge for athletes, and it's exactly where I can help as your Wellness Coach. We'll explore targeted techniques to manage pre-competition jitters, post-workout overstimulation, and mental fatigue, ensuring your nervous system can downshift effectively for restorative sleep, even under pressure.
What kind of results can I expect from working with a Wellness Coach on my sleep?
You can expect to feel more consistently energized, experience faster physical recovery, notice improved mental clarity and focus during your activities, and potentially see a reduction in minor aches or susceptibility to injury. The goal is a more resilient body and mind, ready for whatever your active life demands.
