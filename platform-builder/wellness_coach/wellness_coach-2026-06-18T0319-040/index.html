Frequently Asked Questions

How does a Wellness Coach specifically help with nervous system regulation during a life transition?
As your Wellness Coach, I provide you with personalized strategies and tools to monitor and modulate your physiological stress response. We focus on practices that directly impact your vagal tone and sympathetic nervous system activity, helping you maintain internal balance when external circumstances are shifting dramatically.
Is this approach suitable if my life transition feels overwhelming and I'm highly stressed?
Absolutely. My approach is specifically designed for individuals experiencing heightened stress due to major life changes. We start with foundational techniques to bring immediate relief and gradually build your capacity for self-regulation, ensuring you feel supported and empowered, not further overwhelmed.
What kind of practical tools will I learn to manage stress from my Wellness Coach?
You'll learn a range of evidence-based techniques, including specific breathing patterns to activate your parasympathetic nervous system, practices to enhance interoception (your body's internal awareness), and strategies for creating a supportive daily routine that reduces nervous system overload during your transition.
How is this Wellness Coach service different from general therapy or counseling?
While therapy often focuses on processing past experiences and emotional patterns, my Wellness Coach practice centers on forward-looking, actionable strategies for physiological regulation and stress reduction during current life transitions. We work together to build your practical skillset for immediate and ongoing nervous system support.
