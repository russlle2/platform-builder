Questions people ask

      

        How quickly will I see results?
        
We focus on habits you can test in 7–21 days. Meaningful shifts show in consistent weeks, not instant days.

      

      

        Do you provide meal plans or medical advice?
        
We design habits and structures. We do not give medical diagnoses or personalized medical treatment.

      

      

        How are sessions structured?
        
We use an outcomes-first approach: set a measurable outcome, pick micro-actions, review progress, and adapt.
