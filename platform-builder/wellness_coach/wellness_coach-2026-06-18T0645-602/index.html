Frequently Asked Questions

Why is immune support especially important for athletes and active people?
When you're constantly pushing your body, whether through intense training, long hours, or competitive events, you're placing extra demands on your immune system. This can make you more susceptible to illness and slow down your recovery. Prioritizing immune health helps you stay consistent, perform at your best, and avoid interruptions to your routine.
How is your Wellness Coach approach different for someone with an active lifestyle?
Our approach is specifically tailored to the unique stressors and recovery needs of athletes and active individuals. We don't offer generic advice; instead, we focus on strategies that directly impact performance, injury prevention, and rapid recovery, all while boosting your body's natural resilience. Your program will align with your training schedule and performance goals.
What kind of results can I expect from focusing on vitality building?
By focusing on vitality building, you can expect more consistent energy levels throughout your day and during your activities, improved sleep quality, enhanced mental clarity, and a stronger ability to bounce back from physical exertion. This leads to better performance, greater enjoyment of your active life, and a feeling of being truly 'on fire'.
Will this involve drastic changes to my diet or training regimen?
Our goal isn't to overhaul your entire life but to integrate sustainable, impactful strategies that complement your current diet and training. We'll work collaboratively to find practical adjustments and additions that fit your lifestyle and enhance your immune function and vitality without disrupting what's already working for you.
