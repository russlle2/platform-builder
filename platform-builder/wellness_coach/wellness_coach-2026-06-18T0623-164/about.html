How can a Wellness Coach specifically help my sleep when I'm recovering from chronic illness or injury?

As your Wellness Coach, I focus on your unique challenges. We'll explore how your recovery impacts your sleep and develop personalized strategies that support deep, restorative rest, respecting your body's current limitations and promoting natural healing. This isn't generic advice; it's tailored to your specific needs during this sensitive time.

My energy is already low; will these sleep improvements require a lot of effort from me?

Absolutely not. As your Wellness Coach, my goal is to simplify, not complicate. We'll implement gentle, sustainable changes that fit into your existing energy levels. The focus is on small, consistent steps that build momentum, making restorative sleep accessible without adding to your fatigue or overwhelming your recovery process.

How long does it typically take to see improvements in sleep with a Wellness Coach?

Every recovery journey is unique, and so is the timeline for sleep improvements. As your Wellness Coach, I work with you at your pace. Some clients experience initial shifts within a few weeks as we implement foundational practices, while deeper, more consistent rest often unfolds over a few months. Our focus is on sustainable progress, not just quick fixes.

Is working with a Wellness Coach a substitute for medical treatment for my chronic illness or injury?

No, as your Wellness Coach, I provide complementary support. My role is to help you optimize your sleep and well-being within the context of your medical care. I encourage you to maintain open communication with your healthcare providers, and our work together will always be in alignment with their recommendations for your recovery.
