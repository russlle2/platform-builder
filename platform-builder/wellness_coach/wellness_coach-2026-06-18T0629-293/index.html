Frequently Asked Questions

How can Wellness Coaching specifically help with my college anxiety?
Your Wellness Coach provides tailored strategies to manage the unique stressors of college life, from exam panic to social pressures. We'll work together to identify triggers, develop grounding techniques, and build a personalized toolkit for emotional resilience.
Is this approach different from traditional therapy, and how does it benefit students?
While both offer support, Wellness Coaching is often more present-focused and action-oriented, providing practical skills for immediate application in your academic and personal life. Our trauma-informed lens means we understand and gently address how past experiences might be impacting your current stress responses, without necessarily delving into extensive clinical therapy.
What if my anxiety feels too overwhelming to even start coaching?
It's completely normal to feel that way. Your Wellness Coach is here to meet you where you are, creating a gentle and supportive environment. We start with small, manageable steps to build your comfort and confidence, ensuring the process never feels like another source of pressure.
How long does Wellness Coaching usually take to see results for academic stress?
The timeline is unique to each person, but many students report feeling more equipped to handle stress and anxiety within a few sessions. We focus on empowering you with practical skills and insights that you can apply immediately, leading to noticeable shifts in your emotional landscape and academic performance.
