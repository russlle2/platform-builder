Frequently Asked Questions

How does Wellness Coaching specifically help with recovery from chronic illness or injury?
Our Wellness Coaching focuses on strategies to rebuild your physical resilience and immune function after chronic illness or injury. We work together to identify and implement practical steps in nutrition, rest, and gentle movement that directly support your body's healing processes and help you regain a consistent energy level.
Will your immune support programs be too strenuous for someone in recovery?
Absolutely not. Our programs are designed to be entirely adaptable to your current energy levels and physical limitations. We prioritize gentle, sustainable practices that support your body without causing additional stress, ensuring that every step contributes positively to your recovery and vitality building.
What kind of immune support does your Wellness Coach practice provide?
We provide guidance on nutritional choices that fortify your immune system, stress reduction techniques to prevent immune suppression, and strategies for optimizing sleep—a cornerstone of recovery. Our focus is on creating habits that naturally enhance your body's protective mechanisms and overall resilience as you heal.
How is your Wellness Coaching different from medical treatment for my condition?
Our Wellness Coaching complements medical treatment by focusing on lifestyle and daily habits that support your body's natural capacity to recover and build vitality. We don't diagnose or treat medical conditions; instead, we empower you with actionable strategies to enhance your overall well-being, immune function, and energy levels in conjunction with your medical care.
