Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Wellness Coach specifically help me with sleep when my children are waking me up?
As your Wellness Coach, I understand that children's sleep patterns are unpredictable. We'll focus on what you *can* control: your evening wind-down rituals, stress management during the day, and creating a more sleep-conducive environment for yourself, even amidst interruptions. This helps you fall back asleep faster and achieve deeper rest when you do get the chance.
I'm already so busy. How will adding Wellness Coaching to my schedule help, not hinder, my rest?
That's a very valid concern for a busy parent! My Wellness Coaching is designed to integrate seamlessly into your life. We'll focus on small, impactful changes that free up mental and physical energy in the long run. The goal isn't to add more tasks, but to streamline your routine and give you tools that ultimately create more time and peace for yourself.
Is this just about getting more hours of sleep, or something deeper?
As your Wellness Coach, I look beyond just the quantity of sleep. We focus on the *quality* and *restorative power* of your sleep. It's about helping your body and mind truly rejuvenate, so you feel genuinely refreshed, not just less tired. This involves calming your nervous system and addressing underlying stressors that impact your ability to achieve deep rest.
What if I've tried everything for sleep and nothing works?
It's understandable to feel discouraged. Many parents come to Lavender Mist Wellness Coach feeling the same way. We'll explore personalized strategies that go beyond generic advice, diving into your unique daily life, energy patterns, and challenges. My aim is to offer a fresh perspective and tailored practices that resonate with you, drawing on ancient wisdom for enduring calm.
