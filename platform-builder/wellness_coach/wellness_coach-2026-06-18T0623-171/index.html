Frequently Asked Questions

What makes this Wellness Coaching different for seniors?
My Wellness Coaching is specifically designed for individuals over 60, focusing on gentle, practical, and age-appropriate strategies for sleep improvement. We address the unique factors that can impact sleep later in life, ensuring solutions are supportive and sustainable for you.
I've tried many things for sleep; how can this Wellness Coach help?
Instead of quick fixes, your Wellness Coach provides personalized guidance. We'll look beyond surface symptoms to understand the root causes of your sleep challenges and co-create a plan that fits your lifestyle, ensuring lasting, positive changes to your rest.
Will this Wellness Coaching interfere with my existing health routines?
Absolutely not. As your Wellness Coach, my recommendations are always complementary and designed to work alongside your current health practices. We'll focus on gentle, natural approaches that enhance your overall wellbeing without conflict.
How quickly can I expect to see improvements in my sleep with a Wellness Coach?
While everyone's progress is unique, clients often report feeling more settled and seeing initial improvements in their sleep quality within a few weeks. Your Wellness Coach will support you every step of the way, celebrating your progress and adjusting strategies as needed for consistent gains.
