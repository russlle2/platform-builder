Frequently Asked Questions

What specifically does 'relationship healing' mean in your Wellness Coaching for seniors?
For seniors, relationship healing in Wellness Coaching means gently addressing past misunderstandings, improving current communication with family and friends, and building stronger, more supportive connections. It's about finding peace and contentment within your important relationships, without pressure or judgment.
How is this Wellness Coaching different from traditional therapy for someone over 60?
My Wellness Coaching is distinct from therapy as it focuses on present and future relational growth, rather than delving into deep psychological analysis. It's a proactive, supportive partnership designed to help you cultivate more fulfilling interactions and a greater sense of connection in your daily life, offering practical strategies in a gentle, conversational setting.
Is the Wellness Coaching process truly gentle and accessible for all seniors?
Absolutely. My Wellness Coaching emphasizes a gentle, compassionate approach, tailored to your pace and comfort. Sessions are designed to be approachable and understanding, respecting your life experiences and ensuring you feel heard and supported without ever feeling overwhelmed. Your comfort and well-being are paramount.
What kind of results can I expect from engaging in your relationship Wellness Coaching?
Clients typically experience improved communication with loved ones, a greater sense of peace within their family dynamics, and a renewed ability to express their needs and feelings openly. You can expect to feel more connected, understood, and confident in nurturing your most important relationships, leading to a richer and more harmonious life.
