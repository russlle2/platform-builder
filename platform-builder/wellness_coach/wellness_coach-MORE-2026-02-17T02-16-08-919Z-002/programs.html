Membership options

Memberships are framed around rhythm, not rigid schedules. Choose support sized to your goals—each level provides a scaffold for steady habit change and measurable outcomes.

Root — Community Starter

Weekly short practices, access to templates, and group check-ins.
Contribute monthly

Canopy — Guided Membership

Everything in Root + monthly 1:1 check-ins and priority message coaching.

Monthly contribution

How membership works

  1. We set two concrete outcomes to track.
  2. You try a set of micro-habits for 2–4 weeks.
  3. We review, refine, and build the next set of cues.

On pricing phrasing

Members choose a monthly contribution aligned to the level of access they want. Our focus is on value through consistency and practical rituals rather than fixed one-off sessions.

FAQ

Can I switch tiers?

Yes. We review needs quarterly and adjust level as goals shift.

What if I miss sessions?

We build for flexibility—small, repeatable practices reduce the pressure of perfect attendance.
