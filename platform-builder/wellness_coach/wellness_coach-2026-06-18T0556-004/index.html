Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Wellness Coach specifically help me as an athlete with stress?
As your Wellness Coach, I don't just offer generic advice. We'll pinpoint the unique stressors that come with your training regimen and competition schedule. I'll teach you targeted techniques to calm your body's stress response, improve mental focus under pressure, and accelerate physical recovery, helping you move past feeling constantly 'on edge' or drained.
What does 'nervous system regulation' actually mean for an active person?
For an active person, nervous system regulation means training your body to efficiently switch between high-intensity states (like training or competition) and deep rest states (for recovery and repair). As your Wellness Coach, I'll provide you with practical tools to intentionally activate your body's relaxation response, leading to better sleep, reduced muscle tension, improved digestion, and a greater sense of overall well-being and readiness.
Is this only for elite athletes, or can any active person benefit from a Wellness Coach?
This Wellness Coach practice is for any active individual who recognizes the impact of stress on their physical and mental state. Whether you're a weekend warrior, a competitive amateur, or a professional, if you're pushing your body and want to optimize recovery, reduce physical stress, and enhance your overall well-being, you'll find immense value here.
How quickly can I expect to see results from Wellness Coaching focused on stress reduction?
While individual experiences vary, many clients report feeling a noticeable difference in their stress levels and ability to relax within a few weeks of consistent practice. As your Wellness Coach, I'll equip you with tools you can use immediately, and with ongoing commitment, you'll build robust strategies for long-term nervous system resilience and sustained performance.
