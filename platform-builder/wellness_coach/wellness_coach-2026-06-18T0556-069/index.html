Frequently Asked Questions

How does nervous system regulation actually help my athletic performance?
When your nervous system is regulated, you recover faster between workouts, handle high-pressure situations better, and avoid the physical stress that leads to overtraining. This means you can train more consistently and reach your goals without crashing.
I already do yoga and meditation. Why do I need nervous system coaching?
While yoga and meditation are great, they don't always address the specific nervous system patterns triggered by intense athletic training. I provide targeted techniques that work with your schedule and sport to regulate your stress response in the moment you need it.
How quickly can I expect to see results?
Many athletes notice a difference within the first 2-3 weeks of using the techniques I teach, especially in how they handle post-workout fatigue. But deep regulation takes time—we aim for sustainable changes over 3 months for the best results.
Do you work with athletes who have injuries or chronic pain?
Yes, nervous system regulation can be a powerful tool for managing pain and injury recovery. It helps reduce the stress response that can worsen pain perception and slow healing. We'll tailor the approach to your specific situation.
