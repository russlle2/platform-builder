Your Body Isn't Broken—It's Trying to Survive

When chronic illness or injury keeps your nervous system on high alert, standard 'stress reduction' advice fails. We don't ask you to meditate for 20 minutes when you're exhausted. Instead, I'll help you find micro-moments of safety—like the pause between breaths or grounding your feet on the floor while resting. These tiny actions rebuild your body's capacity to relax, without adding pressure.

What if I can't sit still long enough for 'relaxation'?

That's exactly why we don't use seated meditation. We work with your current capacity—like noticing the coolness of a pillow under your head while resting. Regulation happens in tiny moments, not hours.

Will this replace my medical treatment?

No. I support your nervous system regulation alongside your healthcare team. It’s about managing daily stress that affects your physical recovery, not 'healing' your illness.

How do you know what to suggest when I’m in pain?

We start with what your body can actually do right now. If you’re in pain, we’ll find one small, achievable adjustment—like shifting your weight slightly—to create space for safety without adding effort.

Is this just 'mindfulness' for chronic illness?

Mindfulness can feel overwhelming when you're exhausted. I focus on 'nervous system regulation'—practical, body-based techniques to lower stress without requiring calmness.
