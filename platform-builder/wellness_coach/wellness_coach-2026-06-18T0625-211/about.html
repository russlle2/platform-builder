What exactly is 'emotional healing' for seniors with a Wellness Coach?

Emotional healing, in our context, means gently addressing feelings, memories, or past events that may be causing discomfort or stress now. With your Wellness Coach, it's about finding peace with your experiences and building strategies for emotional well-being in your daily life, rather than reliving trauma.

How does your 'trauma-informed care' approach benefit me as a senior?

Our trauma-informed approach means your Wellness Coach understands that past difficult experiences can impact your present feelings. We create a safe, predictable, and respectful environment, ensuring you feel in control and never pressured. We proceed at your pace, focusing on gentle methods to foster a sense of security and trust.

Is Wellness Coaching suitable for someone my age (60+)?

Absolutely. Our Wellness Coaching is specifically tailored for individuals aged 60 and over. We recognize the wisdom and unique life experiences you bring, and our methods are adapted to be gentle, respectful, and effective for your specific needs and pace. Your Wellness Coach is here to support you.

What should I expect in a session with my Wellness Coach?

In a session with your Wellness Coach, you can expect a calm and confidential conversation. We'll listen attentively to your concerns and experiences. Together, we'll explore gentle, practical strategies to support your emotional well-being, always respecting your comfort levels and proceeding at a comfortable pace. It's a space for reflection and guidance.
