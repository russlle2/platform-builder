Common misunderstandings about coaching

Myth: Quick fixes stick

Reality: Short-term hacks disappear without habit scaffolding. We design repeatable micro-routines you can actually sustain.

Myth: You need perfect willpower

Reality: Systems beat willpower. Change environments, cues, and tiny daily rituals to change outcomes reliably.

Myth: Coaching is one-size-fits-all

Reality: I build frameworks tuned to your schedule, constraints, and measurable feedback — then iterate with data.
