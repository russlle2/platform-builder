Offerings built around habit scaffolds

Each service is structured as a set of repeatable practices, short feedback cycles, and simple metrics so you always know what to adjust.

Core service modules

Foundations Audit

A 60–minute diagnostic to map routines, energy windows, and friction points. You leave with 3 immediate changes to try.

4-Week Habit Lab

An experimental sprint: pick 1 priority, design micro-rules, and measure outcomes over four weekly checkpoints.

Monthly Momentum

Ongoing coaching with monthly check-ins, habit audits, and a rolling experiment plan to keep progress compounding.

How I structure a typical engagement

  1. Baseline mapping (1 session + simple metrics)
  2. Design micro-rituals and environmental adjustments
  3. Two-week tracking windows with short reflection prompts
  4. Iterate: refine routines based on data

Real examples

Case — Evening transition

We moved one long bedtime routine into three micro-rituals tied to device shutdown, low light, and a two-minute unwind. Sleep consistency improved without additional effort.

Case — Focus windows

A knowledge-worker shifted to 50-minute work blocks with 8-minute resets and reported clearer focus and fewer late-hour catch-ups.

Sessions & Logistics

Sessions are 45–60 minutes. Work is delivered via video calls and concise follow-up notes. Simple tracking tools provided.
