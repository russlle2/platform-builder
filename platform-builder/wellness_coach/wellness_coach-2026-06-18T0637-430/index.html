Frequently Asked Questions

What specific cognitive benefits can I expect from Wellness Coaching?
You can expect to experience reduced 'mom brain' symptoms, improved concentration, enhanced memory, and better executive functioning – that's your ability to plan and prioritize. We work on tangible strategies to bring back your mental sharpness.
Is this Wellness Coaching program suitable if I'm feeling completely overwhelmed?
Absolutely. Our programs are designed to meet you where you are. We start with foundational techniques to alleviate overwhelm, creating space for clearer thinking and more effective problem-solving, all at a pace that feels comfortable for you.
How does this Wellness Coaching differ from general therapy or mental health support?
While we complement mental health support, our focus is specifically on optimizing your cognitive function and mental clarity during postpartum. We provide practical tools and strategies rooted in neuroscience to help you regain mental sharpness, rather than solely addressing emotional or mood disorders.
How long does it typically take to see results with your Wellness Coaching?
Many new mothers report feeling a noticeable difference in their mental clarity and focus within a few weeks of consistent practice. The full extent of your results will depend on your dedication to the personalized strategies we develop together, but progress is often steady and encouraging.
