Frequently Asked Questions

How does a Wellness Coach specifically help with life transitions?
As your Wellness Coach, I specialize in the unique stress that accompanies major life changes. We'll work on practical strategies to calm your nervous system, manage emotional fluctuations, and build daily routines that support your well-being, ensuring you feel more stable as you adapt to your new normal.
What kind of techniques does your Wellness Coaching use for stress reduction?
Our Wellness Coaching integrates nature-inspired practices and evidence-based techniques to reduce stress and regulate your nervous system. This includes mindful movement, breathing exercises, sensory grounding, and developing a deeper connection to your body's natural rhythms to foster inner peace during turbulent times.
Is this Wellness Coaching suitable for any type of life transition?
Yes, our Wellness Coaching is designed to support individuals through a wide range of major life transitions, including career changes, relocations, relationship shifts, and more. The common thread is the need to reduce stress and regulate your nervous system to navigate these changes with greater ease and resilience.
How quickly can I expect to feel a difference with Wellness Coaching?
While everyone's experience is unique, many clients report feeling a greater sense of calm and control within a few sessions of Wellness Coaching. Our focus is on providing you with actionable tools you can use immediately to begin reducing stress and bringing your nervous system into better balance.
