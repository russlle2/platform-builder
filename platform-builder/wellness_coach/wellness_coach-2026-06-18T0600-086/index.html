Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Wellness Coach help me specifically with chronic illness or injury recovery?
As your Wellness Coach, I focus on the unique stressors that come with chronic illness or injury. We address how these conditions impact your nervous system, leading to heightened stress. I'll guide you through gentle techniques to reduce that stress and help your nervous system find more balance, creating a calmer internal environment to support your body's healing process.
Do I need any prior experience with stress reduction techniques to work with a Wellness Coach?
Absolutely not! That's precisely what I'm here for. As your Wellness Coach, I specialize in making these practices accessible. We start with the basics, understanding that your energy and focus might be limited. My approach is gentle and step-by-step, ensuring you feel comfortable and capable with every new technique we introduce.
What if I'm too tired or in too much pain for Wellness Coach sessions?
Your comfort and current capacity are always the priority. As your Wellness Coach, I tailor each session to your energy levels on that particular day. We can adjust the pace, duration, and even the type of activity to ensure it's supportive, not draining. Sometimes, simply having a calm, understanding conversation can be incredibly regulating for your nervous system.
What kind of results can I expect from working with a Wellness Coach on stress reduction?
While every individual's experience is unique, clients often report a greater sense of calm, improved sleep quality, and a reduced feeling of overwhelm. As your Wellness Coach, my aim is to help you build a personal toolkit of gentle practices that empower you to soothe your nervous system, manage stress more effectively, and feel more grounded in your day-to-day life during recovery.
