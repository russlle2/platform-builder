Frequently Asked Questions

How does Wellness Coaching specifically help athletes and active people manage physical stress?
As your Wellness Coach, I focus on the often-overlooked emotional and mental aspects of physical stress. We'll explore how performance pressure, injury setbacks, and intense training can impact your emotional regulation and sense of self. Our work helps you build internal resilience, manage expectations, and develop strategies to prevent burnout, all while supporting your physical recovery and mental fortitude.
What does a 'trauma-informed' approach mean for someone active and dealing with physical demands?
A trauma-informed approach means we recognize that past experiences, even seemingly unrelated ones, can influence how you respond to current physical challenges or setbacks. As your Wellness Coach, I create a safe and predictable environment, empowering you to process the emotional load of injuries, intense training, or performance anxiety without re-traumatization. We focus on building your agency and internal resources, ensuring your path forward feels deeply supportive and respectful of your unique history.
I feel disconnected from my body after an injury. Can Wellness Coaching help me regain that connection?
Absolutely. It's common to feel a sense of betrayal or disconnection from your body after an injury. As your Wellness Coach, I guide you through practices that foster gentle reconnection, helping you listen to your body's current needs rather than pushing through old expectations. We work on processing the emotional impact of the injury, rebuilding trust, and cultivating a relationship with your body that supports sustainable recovery and renewed engagement in your active life.
How can I integrate these emotional healing practices into my already busy training schedule?
I understand your schedule is demanding. As your Wellness Coach, I work with you to develop practical, nature-inspired tools and emotional regulation strategies that fit seamlessly into your daily routine. This isn't about adding another burden, but about creating efficient, impactful practices that enhance your recovery, focus, and overall well-being, ultimately supporting your performance and longevity in your chosen activity. We prioritize what feels sustainable and genuinely supportive for you.
