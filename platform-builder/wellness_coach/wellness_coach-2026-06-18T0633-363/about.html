How does Wellness Coaching specifically address physical pain caused by college anxiety?

Your Wellness Coach helps you recognize the subtle ways anxiety tenses your muscles and restricts your breath. We use somatic techniques to release these patterns, teaching you to consciously relax areas like your neck, shoulders, and jaw, which are often tight from studying and stress. You'll learn direct methods to ease physical discomfort.

Will somatic healing help me if my pain feels constant and overwhelming?

Yes, many college students experience persistent physical discomfort. Your Wellness Coach will provide gentle, guided practices that acknowledge and gradually soothe these sensations. We start by building awareness of where the pain lives in your body, then introduce techniques to soften and release it, helping you feel more in control and less overwhelmed.

I'm a busy college student. How can I fit Wellness Coaching into my schedule?

Your Wellness Coach understands the demands of college life. Our sessions are designed to be practical and efficient, providing you with tools you can integrate into your daily routine. We focus on simple, yet powerful, somatic practices that take just a few minutes, allowing you to find relief without adding more stress to your schedule.

What kind of results can I expect from this type of Wellness Coaching?

You can expect to feel more grounded and less physically burdened by anxiety and academic pressure. Many students report reduced headaches, less neck and back stiffness, improved sleep, and a greater sense of calm and focus. Your Wellness Coach will help you build a stronger connection to your body's signals, leading to lasting comfort and resilience.
