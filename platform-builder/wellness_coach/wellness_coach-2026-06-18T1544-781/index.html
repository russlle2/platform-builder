Frequently Asked Questions

I'm a college student with zero wellness experience. Is Wellness Coaching for me?
Absolutely! Our Wellness Coaching is designed with you in mind. We meet you exactly where you are, without any expectation of prior knowledge. We focus on gentle, accessible strategies that make sense for your busy student life, helping you navigate anxiety and academic pressure with ease.
How does Wellness Coaching help with academic pressure and anxiety specifically?
We'll work together to identify the specific sources of your academic pressure and anxiety. Instead of generic advice, we’ll build personalized coping mechanisms, help you develop healthier study habits, and strengthen your relationship with yourself, so you can approach exams and assignments with greater calm and focus.
My main concern is relationship issues in college. Can Wellness Coaching help with that?
Yes, deeply. Our heart-centered approach to Wellness Coaching is perfect for navigating the complexities of college relationships. We'll help you communicate more effectively, set healthy boundaries, and foster genuine connections, reducing the anxiety that often comes with social dynamics.
What can I expect in a typical Wellness Coaching session?
Expect a warm, confidential, and judgment-free conversation. We'll chat about what's on your mind regarding academic stress, anxiety, or your relationships. Together, we'll explore gentle strategies and practical steps you can take to feel more centered and supported in your college life.
