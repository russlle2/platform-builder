Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Wellness Coach help me specifically with chronic illness or injury recovery?
A Wellness Coach specializing in chronic illness or injury recovery helps you navigate the emotional and relational challenges that often arise. We focus on rebuilding your sense of self and strengthening your connections with loved ones, providing tools to communicate your experiences and needs more effectively, fostering mutual understanding and support.
My relationships feel strained due to my health. Can you help me mend them?
Absolutely. It's common for relationships to undergo significant shifts during chronic illness or injury. We work with you to identify areas of strain, develop clear communication strategies, and encourage empathy from both sides. Our goal is to help you rebuild trust and intimacy, transforming challenges into opportunities for deeper connection.
What does 'heart-centered care' mean in your Wellness Coach practice?
Heart-centered care means we approach your situation with profound empathy, compassion, and an understanding that your emotional well-being is central to your recovery. We prioritize listening to your experiences without judgment and guide you toward solutions that resonate with your authentic self and values, fostering inner peace and relational harmony.
Is Wellness Coaching suitable if I'm still actively dealing with symptoms?
Yes, it is. Our Wellness Coaching supports you regardless of your current symptom severity. We adapt our approach to your energy levels and capacity, focusing on manageable steps that empower you to nurture your relationships and emotional health even amidst ongoing challenges. We're here to provide consistent, gentle support.
