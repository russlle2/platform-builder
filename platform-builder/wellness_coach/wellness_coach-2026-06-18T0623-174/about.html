How is this Wellness Coach service different for someone recovering from chronic illness or injury?

Unlike general sleep advice, my coaching focuses on the specific physiological and psychological hurdles you encounter during recovery. We address pain management, medication side effects, emotional stress, and energy limitations directly, tailoring strategies to your unique situation to improve your restorative rest.

I've tried everything for sleep; can a Wellness Coach really help me?

Yes, many clients feel this way. My role is to provide a fresh perspective, an expert eye to identify overlooked factors, and consistent support. We'll develop a personalized plan that honors your body's current state and gradually builds effective habits for deeper, more consistent sleep, even when traditional methods haven't worked.

What kind of commitment is required to see results in my sleep with your coaching?

Improving your sleep and rest is a collaborative effort. While the time commitment varies, you can expect to engage in regular coaching sessions and implement recommended strategies daily. Consistency is key, but we'll start with small, manageable steps to build momentum and avoid overwhelming you during your recovery.

Will working with a Wellness Coach interfere with my current medical treatment or rehabilitation?

Absolutely not. My coaching is designed to complement your medical care. I encourage open communication with your healthcare providers and can help you integrate sleep strategies that support your overall recovery plan, never replacing professional medical advice. My focus is on lifestyle and behavioral adjustments that enhance your well-being.
