Frequently Asked Questions

How is this different from regular wellness coaching?
We don’t talk about 'mindset.' We engineer nervous system regulation using your actual work patterns. You’ll get specific protocols for your calendar, not generic breathing exercises.
Can you help me if I’m already on a productivity app?
Yes—your app’s data is useless if your body’s in chaos. We’ll connect the dots between your app’s metrics and your physiological stress markers. You’ll use your existing tools smarter.
What if I can’t take time for a session?
We design sessions around your actual availability. Most clients fit 30 minutes into their day. And you’ll get protocols that work while you’re driving or in a meeting.
How quickly will I notice a difference?
Most clients feel calmer in their next high-pressure meeting. By week 3, you’ll stop reacting to stressors and start responding strategically—no extra time needed.
