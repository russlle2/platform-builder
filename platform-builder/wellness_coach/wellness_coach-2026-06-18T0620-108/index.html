Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Wellness Coach specifically help my sleep during a significant life transition?
As your Wellness Coach, I'll help you identify how your specific life transition (like a new job, moving, or relationship changes) impacts your sleep patterns. We'll create personalized strategies to manage stress, establish new sleep routines, and optimize your environment, all tailored to your evolving circumstances, so you can achieve consistent, restorative rest.
I'm already overwhelmed by my life changes; will adding a Wellness Coach feel like another task?
Absolutely not. My role as your Wellness Coach is to simplify, not complicate. We'll integrate practical, gentle adjustments into your existing life, making sleep improvement feel like a supportive step, not another burden. The goal is to reduce your overwhelm by giving you the gift of better rest, which in turn helps you manage everything else with more clarity and energy.
What kind of sleep issues does a Wellness Coach address when someone is going through big changes?
As your Wellness Coach, I can help with a range of issues common during transitions: difficulty falling asleep due to an overactive mind, waking frequently at night, feeling unrested despite sleeping, and struggling to establish a consistent sleep schedule in a new routine. We focus on techniques that acknowledge and address the unique stressors of your current life phase.
Is working with a Wellness Coach only about fixing sleep, or is it broader?
While our primary focus is sleep improvement and restorative rest, as your Wellness Coach, we understand that sleep is deeply interconnected with your overall well-being, especially during big changes. We'll touch on stress management, energy regulation, and establishing healthy routines that support both your sleep and your ability to navigate your transition with greater ease and resilience.
