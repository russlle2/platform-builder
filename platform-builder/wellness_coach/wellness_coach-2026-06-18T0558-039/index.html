Frequently Asked Questions

What does a Wellness Coach do specifically for parents under stress?
As your Wellness Coach, we specialize in helping you identify the specific stressors contributing to overwhelm in your parenting and home life. We then create tailored, science-backed strategies to down-regulate your nervous system, improve emotional regulation, and build resilience so you can navigate daily challenges with more ease and less reactivity.
How is this different from therapy or parenting classes?
While therapy often delves into past experiences and parenting classes focus on child behavior, Wellness Coaching with us centers on your internal experience as a parent. We provide actionable techniques to manage your stress response in real-time, regulate your nervous system, and develop habits that support your well-being, directly impacting your capacity to parent effectively in the present.
I barely have time to shower; how can I fit coaching into my schedule?
We understand your time is incredibly limited. Our coaching is designed with busy parents in mind, focusing on efficient, high-impact strategies. Sessions are flexible, and the techniques we teach are integrated into your existing daily routines, requiring minimal extra time. We prioritize practical application that yields significant results without adding to your overwhelm.
What kind of results can I expect from nervous system regulation coaching?
You can expect to experience a tangible reduction in your baseline stress levels, greater emotional stability, and improved capacity to respond calmly to challenging situations. Clients often report feeling more present with their children, experiencing less irritability, and enjoying a deeper sense of peace and control in their household environment.
