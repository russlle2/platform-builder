How is this different from generic stress management coaching?

We focus exclusively on nervous system regulation—the physiological process that keeps you stuck in stress. You'll learn specific neurobiology tools, not vague 'relaxation' advice. Our methods are designed for people who can't pause their work to 'de-stress.'

Can you help me if I'm already burned out?

Absolutely. We start where you are. Your first session identifies your unique stress signature (are you overstimulated or exhausted?), then we build a personalized regulation plan that works with your current energy level—no pressure to 'feel better' faster.

What if I'm not a 'meditation person'?

You don't need to sit silently. We use movement, breath, and sensory input—like holding a cold glass during calls or doing micro-movements at your desk—to shift your nervous system. These are practical actions, not mindfulness practices.

How quickly will I notice changes?

Most clients notice a shift within 72 hours of applying the first tool. You'll catch yourself noticing tension before it escalates, and gain tools to prevent the 3 PM crash. We measure progress through your ability to regulate during high-pressure moments—your actual workdays.
