A threefold pathway: Notice, Commit, Tend

Our framework is simple and cyclical — observe your patterns, commit to one micro-change, and tend it with community accountability.

Notice

Short practices to increase awareness: single-breath check-ins, 2-minute journaling, and sensory grounding. These are invitations, never demands.

Commit

Pick one micro-habit for two weeks. Keep it specific, tiny, and trackable. We practice in pairs inside the cohort so you don't do it alone.

Tend

Ritualize small wins, reflect weekly, and adjust. The cohort model brings ritual, peer feedback, and expert guidance.
