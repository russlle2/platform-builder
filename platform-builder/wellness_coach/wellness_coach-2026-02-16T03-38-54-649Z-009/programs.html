Cohort rhythm — what to expect

  1. Weekly 75-minute live class (teaching + breakout)
  2. Short daily micro-practices (2–5 minutes)
  3. Weekly reflection prompts and peer circle

Cohorts are intentionally small to deepen witness and feedback.

Is a cohort right for you?

Use this mirror test before applying:

  • Do you want steady change more than quick fixes?
  • Can you commit to weekly calls and short daily practices?
  • Are you willing to share learning within a small group?
