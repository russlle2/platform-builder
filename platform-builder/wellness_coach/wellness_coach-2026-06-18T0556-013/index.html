Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is Wellness Coaching for seniors, and how can it help me?
Wellness Coaching for seniors focuses on gentle, evidence-based strategies to reduce stress and improve nervous system regulation. It's a supportive partnership where we explore practical ways to bring more calm and clarity into your daily life, tailored specifically for your needs as you age.
I'm not sure if I'm 'stressed' but often feel tired or uneasy. Is Wellness Coaching still relevant for me?
Absolutely. Subtle unease or persistent fatigue can often be signs of an overactive nervous system. Our Wellness Coaching can help you identify these underlying patterns and learn gentle techniques to restore a sense of balance and well-being, even if you don't label it as 'stress'.
Will Wellness Coaching involve intense exercises or complicated routines?
Not at all. Our Wellness Coaching emphasizes gentle, manageable techniques. We prioritize practices that are easy to integrate into your routine and respect your energy levels, focusing on sustainable changes that feel comfortable and supportive for you.
How does Wellness Coaching differ from traditional therapy or medical advice?
Wellness Coaching focuses on present-day strategies and future-oriented goals for enhancing your well-being. We complement, rather than replace, medical advice or therapy. Our role is to empower you with practical tools for stress reduction and nervous system regulation, supporting your overall health journey.
