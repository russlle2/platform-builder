Frequently Asked Questions

What kind of mental clarity challenges does Sage Garden Wellness Coaching address for people recovering from chronic illness or injury?
We specifically address cognitive issues like 'brain fog,' difficulty concentrating, memory lapses, mental fatigue, and challenges with executive functions such as planning and decision-making, all common after chronic illness or injury. Your Wellness Coach will help you target these areas effectively.
How is this Wellness Coaching different from therapy or medical treatment for cognitive issues?
As your Wellness Coach, we complement, rather than replace, medical and therapeutic care. We focus on practical, lifestyle-based strategies to support your existing recovery, empowering you with tools to manage daily cognitive function and enhance mental well-being in a non-clinical, supportive environment.
Will your Wellness Coaching program be too demanding given my ongoing recovery from chronic illness or injury?
Absolutely not. Your Wellness Coach customizes every aspect of your program to your current energy levels and specific recovery needs. Our focus is on gentle, sustainable progress, ensuring that our strategies support your healing process without adding unnecessary strain.
How does Sage Garden Wellness Coaching incorporate nature-inspired and sustainable practices for mental clarity?
We draw inspiration from natural rhythms and sustainable living to cultivate mental well-being. This might include guided practices for mindful attention, strategies for optimizing your natural environment, and integrating gentle movement, all designed to foster a clear and calm mind, guided by your Wellness Coach.
