Frequently Asked Questions

How does Wellness Coaching specifically address stress during major life transitions?
Our Wellness Coaching zeroes in on the physiological impact of significant life changes. We equip you with evidence-based techniques to re-regulate your nervous system, reducing the felt sense of overwhelm and enhancing your ability to adapt to new circumstances with composure. It's about building resilience, not just coping.
Is this Wellness Coaching focused on specific techniques for nervous system regulation?
Absolutely. Your Wellness Coach will guide you through concrete practices derived from neuroscientific understanding, such as targeted breathwork, interoceptive awareness exercises, and specific self-regulation protocols. These are not generic wellness tips; they are precise tools for modulating your stress response.
What if my life transition feels too overwhelming for Wellness Coaching?
That's precisely when Wellness Coaching can be most beneficial. Our approach is designed to provide structure and support when you feel most adrift. We begin by establishing foundational practices to create a sense of stability, then progressively introduce strategies to manage complexity. You don't need to 'be ready' to feel better; we'll help you get there.
How quickly can I expect to see improvements in my stress levels with Wellness Coaching?
While individual experiences vary, many clients report noticing shifts in their sense of calm and capacity to manage stress within the first few sessions of Wellness Coaching. Consistent application of the techniques you learn is key to experiencing sustained and deepening benefits for your nervous system regulation.
