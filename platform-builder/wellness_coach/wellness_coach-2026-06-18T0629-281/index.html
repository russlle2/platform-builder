Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly does a Wellness Coach do for someone my age?
As your Wellness Coach, I provide gentle, understanding support specifically for seniors. We focus on emotional comfort, helping you navigate feelings, past memories, and daily stresses with kindness and without judgment. It's about finding more peace in your everyday life.
I've never done anything like this before. Is it for me?
Absolutely. Our approach is designed for everyone, especially if you're new to wellness support. There's no special knowledge or experience needed. We start where you are, making sure you feel comfortable and understood every step of the way. It's gentle, approachable, and completely for you.
Will we talk about difficult past experiences?
Only if you feel ready and comfortable. Our care is trauma-informed, meaning we understand that past events can impact how you feel now. We approach these topics with extreme gentleness, respect, and at your pace. Your comfort and safety are always our top priority.
What kind of results can I expect from working with a Wellness Coach?
Clients often share that they feel a greater sense of calm, less emotional burden, and a renewed appreciation for their daily life. You might find yourself feeling more peaceful, more comfortable with your emotions, and generally lighter in spirit. We aim for noticeable, gentle improvements in your emotional well-being.
