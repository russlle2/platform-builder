Wellness Coach

From Playtime to Bedtime: Moving with Less Strain as a Parent

Daily life with young children often means lifting, bending, and carrying in ways that can leave you feeling stiff or sore. As your Body Awareness Wellness Coach, we'll explore how simple shifts in your posture can alleviate common aches from diaper changes to storytelling. Learn practical, gentle methods to move through your day with greater physical comfort and less tension.
