Frequently Asked Questions

What does a Wellness Coach do that's different for retirees?
As your Wellness Coach, I specialize in understanding the unique needs of retirees. We focus on gentle, sustainable strategies for immune support, boosting vitality, and maintaining mental clarity, always respecting your pace and current abilities. It's about simple, impactful changes rather than intense regimens.
I've never focused on wellness before. Is this right for me?
Absolutely! My Wellness Coach programs are designed to be very accessible, especially for those new to wellness. There's no prior experience needed. We'll start with fundamental steps and build from there, ensuring you feel comfortable and confident every step of the way as you learn to support your well-being.
How can a Wellness Coach help with my immune system specifically?
As your Wellness Coach, we'll explore practical ways to nourish your body and mind, which directly supports a stronger immune response. This includes discussing easy dietary adjustments, light physical activities, and stress management techniques that help your body function optimally, making you more resilient.
Will this Wellness Coaching help me with my memory and focus?
Yes, maintaining mental clarity is a key focus. As your Wellness Coach, we'll look at lifestyle factors that support brain health, such as nutrition, gentle movement, and engaging mental activities. These practices are designed to help you feel sharper, more focused, and mentally agile in your daily life.
