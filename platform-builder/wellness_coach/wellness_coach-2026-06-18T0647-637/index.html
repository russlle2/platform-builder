Frequently Asked Questions

How is a Wellness Coach different from a doctor for immune support?
As your Wellness Coach, I complement medical care by focusing on lifestyle factors that profoundly influence your immune health. I provide personalized, actionable strategies for nutrition, stress management, and sleep – areas doctors often don't have time to deep-dive into. We work together to build consistent habits for long-term resilience.
I'm a busy parent; how can I possibly add Wellness Coaching to my schedule?
I understand your time is precious. My Wellness Coach programs are designed with busy parents in mind, focusing on efficient, high-impact strategies. We create realistic plans that integrate seamlessly into your existing routine, rather than adding more tasks. Our goal is to simplify, not complicate, your path to better health.
Will Wellness Coaching really help me feel less stressed and more energetic?
Yes, absolutely. As your Wellness Coach, I provide evidence-based techniques to help you understand and mitigate the physiological impacts of stress, which often manifest as fatigue. We'll work on practical methods to optimize your energy at a cellular level, allowing you to not just cope, but truly thrive amidst your parenting responsibilities.
What specific science-backed methods does your Wellness Coach practice use for immune and vitality building?
My Wellness Coach methodology integrates current scientific understanding of immunology, stress physiology, and nutritional science. We utilize strategies like targeted micronutrient optimization, circadian rhythm alignment for sleep, adaptive stress response techniques, and gut microbiome support – all tailored to your unique needs as a parent.
