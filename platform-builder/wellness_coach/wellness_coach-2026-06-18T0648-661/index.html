Frequently Asked Questions

How does a Wellness Coach specifically help seniors with immune support?
As your Wellness Coach, I work with you to create personalized plans that focus on immune-boosting nutrition, gentle exercise, stress-reducing techniques, and quality sleep. These elements are carefully tailored to your unique needs as a senior, supporting your body's natural defenses without relying on harsh interventions.
What kind of vitality building can I expect from a Wellness Coach?
Working with a Wellness Coach means exploring ways to increase your daily energy and enthusiasm. We'll look at natural rhythms, gentle movement, and dietary choices that fuel your body and mind, helping you feel more alert, engaged, and ready to enjoy your life without feeling depleted.
Is this Wellness Coach approach suitable for someone with existing health conditions?
My Wellness Coach approach is designed to be gentle and complementary. While I don't diagnose or treat medical conditions, we can certainly work within the parameters of your current health status and in consultation with your medical doctor, to support your well-being and vitality.
How is this Wellness Coach service different from general health advice?
Unlike generic health advice, my Wellness Coach services are highly personalized and specifically designed for seniors. We focus on gentle, sustainable practices that align with your lifestyle, ensuring that the recommendations for immune support and vitality building are practical, enjoyable, and truly benefit you.
