Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Wellness Coach specifically help with stress during a big life change?
As your Wellness Coach, I focus on practical, actionable steps to reduce the physical and emotional impact of stress. We identify how your body reacts to change and build a personalized toolkit of strategies for nervous system regulation, helping you feel more in control and less overwhelmed during pivotal moments.
What does nervous system regulation actually mean, and how will it benefit me?
Nervous system regulation means teaching your body and mind to shift out of 'fight or flight' mode and into a state of calm. Your Wellness Coach will guide you through techniques that soothe your stress response, leading to better sleep, improved focus, and a greater sense of peace as you navigate new situations.
I feel constantly anxious about my life transition. Can a Wellness Coach truly make a difference?
Absolutely. As your Wellness Coach, I provide a safe, supportive space to explore your anxieties without judgment. We'll work on understanding their roots and developing concrete practices to interrupt anxious patterns, helping you cultivate a stronger sense of inner stability, even when external circumstances feel uncertain.
Is Wellness Coaching a long-term commitment, or can I get help quickly?
The duration of our work as your Wellness Coach is flexible and tailored to your needs. Some clients benefit from a focused short-term series to address immediate stress, while others prefer ongoing support as they integrate new habits. We'll discuss what feels right for your unique situation.
