How does a Wellness Coach specifically help seniors with posture?

A wellness coach provides personalized, gentle guidance tailored to your body's current capabilities. We focus on exercises and awareness techniques that improve balance, reduce stiffness, and encourage a more aligned posture, specifically for seniors aged 60+.

Will the exercises be too strenuous for me?

Absolutely not. Our approach is intentionally gentle and progressive. All exercises are adapted to your individual comfort level and physical capacity, ensuring a safe and beneficial experience without any strain. We prioritize listening to your body.

What kind of results can I expect from working with a Wellness Coach on my posture?

Clients often report feeling more stable, experiencing less discomfort, and moving with greater ease in their daily activities. You can expect to develop a better understanding of your body's mechanics and gain confidence in maintaining an upright, comfortable stance.

How is this different from physical therapy?

While physical therapy often addresses specific injuries or medical conditions, wellness coaching focuses on proactive health, body awareness, and gentle postural improvements for overall well-being. We complement, rather than replace, any medical care you receive, focusing on lifestyle and movement habits.
