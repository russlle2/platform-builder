How is this Wellness Coaching different from general parenting advice?

Unlike generic advice, our Wellness Coaching is rooted in sleep science and behavioral psychology, tailored specifically to the unique stressors and sleep disruptions experienced by parents of young children. We focus on developing sustainable habits and addressing the root causes of your sleep challenges, not just quick fixes.

I barely have time for myself. How can I fit sleep improvement into my already packed schedule?

We understand your time is precious. Our approach is designed to integrate seamlessly into your existing life, identifying small, high-impact changes that don't add to your burden. We'll prioritize strategies that yield the biggest return on your time investment, making better sleep an efficient part of your routine.

Will I need to make drastic changes to my child's sleep schedule for my own rest to improve?

Not necessarily. While a child's sleep impacts yours, our primary focus is on optimizing your personal sleep hygiene and stress management. We explore strategies that can improve your rest within your current family dynamics, and if appropriate, discuss gentle adjustments that benefit everyone, without drastic overhauls.

What if my sleep issues are more complex, like chronic insomnia or anxiety?

Our Wellness Coaching provides evidence-based strategies for improving sleep and managing stress. If your challenges are clinical, we'll help you recognize that and can guide you on when to seek evaluation from a medical professional, working in conjunction with their recommendations to support your overall well-being.
