Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly does 'trauma-informed' mean in the context of Wellness Coaching for new mothers?
Trauma-informed means we understand that your past experiences, including birth, can deeply affect your current emotional state. Our Wellness Coaching sessions are designed to be sensitive to these potential impacts, ensuring you feel safe, respected, and in control throughout our work together. We avoid re-traumatization and focus on building resilience and emotional safety.
I'm feeling overwhelmed and exhausted. How can Wellness Coaching help with that specifically?
Feeling overwhelmed and exhausted is very common postpartum. Our Wellness Coaching helps you identify the specific stressors contributing to these feelings. We'll work on practical strategies for managing your energy, setting realistic boundaries, improving sleep hygiene, and developing self-compassion, all tailored to your unique situation as a new mother.
Is Wellness Coaching a replacement for therapy or medical care for postpartum depression?
No, Wellness Coaching is not a replacement for therapy or medical care. Our Wellness Coaching focuses on emotional processing, coping skills, and building a supportive framework for your recovery. If you are experiencing symptoms of postpartum depression or anxiety, we strongly encourage you to seek professional medical or psychological support, and we can help you find resources if needed.
How long does a typical Wellness Coaching program last, and what's the commitment?
The duration of your Wellness Coaching program is flexible and tailored to your needs. Many new mothers find benefit from an initial 3-month commitment, with weekly or bi-weekly sessions. This allows ample time to establish new patterns and build foundational skills. We'll discuss what feels right for your recovery during our initial consultation.
