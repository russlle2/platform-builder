Frequently Asked Questions

Why is a Wellness Coach focused on sleep so important for college students?
College life often involves late-night study sessions, early classes, and social pressures, all of which disrupt natural sleep patterns. As your Wellness Coach, I help you understand the scientific impact of poor sleep on your grades, mental health, and overall well-being, and then equip you with specific tools to prioritize and protect your rest amidst these demands.
How is this different from just trying to 'sleep more' or using sleep apps?
While sleep apps can offer data, they rarely address the root causes of sleep disruption in a personalized way. As your Wellness Coach, I provide a tailored, evidence-based strategy that considers your unique schedule, stress triggers, and sleep habits. We'll build sustainable routines and address underlying anxieties, going far beyond generic advice or tracking.
Can I really improve my sleep even with a demanding college schedule?
Absolutely. It's not about finding more hours in the day, but about optimizing the hours you do have for quality rest. We'll work together to identify realistic adjustments, implement effective wind-down routines, and develop strategies to manage academic pressure so it doesn't hijack your sleep. Even small, consistent changes can lead to significant improvements.
What specific strategies will my Wellness Coach teach me to combat anxiety-induced sleeplessness?
We'll explore techniques grounded in cognitive behavioral therapy for insomnia (CBT-I), focusing on identifying and reframing anxious thoughts that keep you awake. You'll learn relaxation protocols, optimal sleep hygiene practices, and strategies to create a calming pre-sleep routine. The goal is to build your resilience against anxiety's impact on your rest.
