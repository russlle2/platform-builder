What exactly is somatic healing in Wellness Coaching, and is it for me?

Somatic healing in Wellness Coaching simply means learning to notice and respond to what your body is telling you. If you experience physical tension, headaches, or general discomfort from stress, this gentle approach is absolutely for you. No flexibility or special skills are required; we meet you exactly where you are.

I'm an entrepreneur with a packed schedule. How can Wellness Coaching fit into my day?

We prioritize efficiency and ease. Our Wellness Coaching sessions are designed to provide powerful, actionable techniques that you can implement in short bursts throughout your day. We focus on practical strategies that integrate seamlessly into your existing routine, helping you get relief without adding more stress to your schedule.

Will Wellness Coaching help with my chronic back pain or neck stiffness?

Many entrepreneurs find significant relief from common pains like chronic back pain, neck stiffness, and tension headaches through our Wellness Coaching. We'll explore gentle movements and awareness practices to release accumulated tension and improve your physical comfort, helping you address the stress patterns contributing to your discomfort.

Do I need any previous experience with wellness practices to benefit from Wellness Coaching?

Absolutely not! Our Wellness Coaching is specifically designed to be accessible for everyone, especially those new to wellness. We use clear, simple language and gentle techniques, ensuring you feel comfortable and supported from your very first session. There's no expectation of prior knowledge or experience.
