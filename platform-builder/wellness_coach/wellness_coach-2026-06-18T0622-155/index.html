Frequently Asked Questions

How is this Wellness Coaching different from general sleep advice?
My Wellness Coaching is specifically tailored for entrepreneurs and high-achievers. It goes beyond generic recommendations, delving into your unique work demands, stress triggers, and lifestyle to create a bespoke sleep strategy that integrates seamlessly with your ambitious schedule, focusing on tangible improvements in your daily performance.
What kind of results can I expect from your Wellness Coaching?
You can expect a significant improvement in your ability to fall asleep, stay asleep, and wake feeling genuinely rested. This translates directly to increased mental clarity, sustained energy throughout your demanding day, sharper decision-making, and a notable reduction in stress, all contributing to your professional and personal success.
Is this Wellness Coaching a long-term commitment?
While results often manifest quickly, my Wellness Coaching emphasizes building sustainable habits. We'll work together for a period that ensures these new patterns are firmly established, typically several weeks to a few months, allowing you to confidently maintain your optimized sleep long after our sessions conclude.
How does your Wellness Coaching address the specific stress of a high-achiever?
My Wellness Coaching directly tackles the unique stressors faced by high-achievers, such as constant demands, mental overload, and the pressure to perform. We develop strategies to compartmentalize work, implement effective wind-down routines, and manage your cognitive load, ensuring your mind can disengage sufficiently for truly restorative rest.
