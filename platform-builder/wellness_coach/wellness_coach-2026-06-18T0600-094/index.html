Frequently Asked Questions

How does Wellness Coaching specifically help athletes with stress?
As an athlete, your stress isn't just mental; it's deeply physical. Our Wellness Coaching focuses on nervous system regulation to directly counter the physiological impacts of intense training and competition. We teach you techniques to lower cortisol, improve heart rate variability, and enhance your body's ability to switch into rest-and-repair mode, leading to better recovery and sustained performance.
Is this Wellness Coaching just about relaxation, or does it impact my physical performance?
It's much more than just relaxation; it directly impacts your physical performance. By regulating your nervous system, you improve your body's capacity for recovery, reduce muscular tension, and enhance mental focus under pressure. This translates to more effective training, better injury prevention, and a stronger ability to perform consistently at your peak.
What kind of techniques does Wellness Coaching use to regulate the nervous system?
We utilize a range of evidence-informed techniques tailored to your needs. This often includes specific breathwork protocols, targeted mindfulness practices, practical strategies for optimizing sleep hygiene, and methods to release physical tension. The focus is always on empowering you with actionable tools to manage your internal state and enhance your overall resilience.
How quickly can I expect to see results from Wellness Coaching focused on stress reduction?
Many active individuals experience noticeable shifts within a few weeks, such as improved sleep quality, reduced perceived stress, and a greater sense of calm. Sustainable changes, however, unfold over a few months as you integrate these new practices consistently. Your commitment to applying the tools we provide is key to accelerating your progress and solidifying your gains.
