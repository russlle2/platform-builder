Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Wellness Coach help with my posture after chronic illness?
After chronic illness or injury, your body might have adapted in ways that affect your natural posture. A Wellness Coach specializing in this area helps you gently re-educate your body, identifying compensatory patterns and guiding you toward more balanced and supportive alignment, reducing strain and increasing comfort.
Is this Wellness Coach approach suitable if I have limited mobility?
Yes, absolutely. The Wellness Coach approach is highly individualized and gentle. We start exactly where your body is, respecting your current capabilities and limitations. The focus is on cultivating internal awareness and small, mindful adjustments that support your unique recovery, rather than strenuous exercises.
How long does it take to see improvements with a Wellness Coach?
Regaining postural awareness and strength after chronic illness is a gradual process, honoring your body's pace. While some clients report subtle improvements in comfort and body sensing within a few sessions, sustained and deeper changes often unfold over several weeks or months of consistent Wellness Coach guidance and personal practice.
What does 'body awareness' mean in the context of Wellness Coaching?
Body awareness, in this Wellness Coach practice, means developing a heightened sense of how your body is positioned, how it moves, and how different parts relate to each other. For someone recovering from illness, this often involves re-establishing connection with areas that may have been overlooked or felt 'disconnected,' fostering a more integrated sense of self.
