How can a Wellness Coach specifically help with college anxiety and academic pressure?

As your Wellness Coach, I provide tailored strategies to manage the unique stressors of college life. We'll work on practical techniques to reduce anxiety, improve focus for studying, and create sustainable routines that support your cognitive health, helping you feel more in control of your academic journey.

What kind of techniques does Sage Garden Wellness Coaching use to improve mental clarity?

Our approach includes nature-inspired mindfulness practices, structured planning methods to reduce overwhelm, and techniques to enhance sustained attention. We focus on building daily habits that declutter your mind and sharpen your ability to concentrate on complex academic tasks.

Is Wellness Coaching a good fit if I'm already seeing a therapist for mental health?

Wellness Coaching with Lora is complementary to therapy. While therapy often addresses underlying mental health conditions, coaching focuses on present-day strategies and future goals for mental clarity and academic performance. We can discuss how our work together can support your overall well-being alongside any other professional support you receive.

How quickly can I expect to see improvements in my academic focus and stress levels?

Many students report feeling a greater sense of calm and improved focus within a few sessions as they begin implementing the strategies we discuss. Consistent application of these tools will lead to more sustained improvements in your cognitive wellness and ability to manage academic pressure effectively.
