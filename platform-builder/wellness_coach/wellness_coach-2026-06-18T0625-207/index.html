Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly does a Wellness Coach do for parents dealing with household stress?
As your Wellness Coach, I provide tailored support to help you identify the emotional and practical stressors unique to parenting young children. We'll develop strategies to manage overwhelming feelings, establish helpful routines, and cultivate resilience, so you can navigate daily challenges with more ease and less reactivity.
How is your Wellness Coaching different for parents seeking emotional healing?
My Wellness Coaching is distinct because it integrates trauma-informed principles, recognizing that your past experiences can significantly impact your current parenting responses. We focus on creating a safe, non-judgmental space to process emotions, build self-compassion, and develop new, healthier ways of responding to stress, rather than just coping with symptoms.
Will Wellness Coaching really help me feel less overwhelmed as a parent?
Absolutely. Many parents feel overwhelmed, and my Wellness Coaching provides practical tools and emotional support to address this. We'll work on setting realistic boundaries, implementing self-care that actually works for your busy schedule, and reframing challenging situations, leading to a significant reduction in your daily overwhelm and increased emotional capacity.
Is Wellness Coaching suitable if I'm feeling constantly drained by parenting young children?
Yes, if you're consistently feeling drained, Wellness Coaching can be incredibly beneficial. We'll explore the root causes of your emotional and physical depletion, helping you create sustainable practices that replenish your energy. Our work together will help you move from feeling constantly exhausted to feeling more present and engaged with your children and your life.
