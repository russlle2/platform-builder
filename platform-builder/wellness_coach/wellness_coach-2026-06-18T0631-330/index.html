Frequently Asked Questions

What kind of physical pain can a Wellness Coach help me with postpartum?
As your Wellness Coach, I specialize in addressing common postpartum physical discomforts such as lower back pain, pelvic girdle pain, shoulder and neck tension from feeding or holding your baby, wrist pain, and overall body stiffness. We focus on gentle, effective strategies to alleviate these specific issues.
Is Wellness Coaching a substitute for physical therapy or medical advice?
No, Wellness Coaching is complementary to, not a replacement for, medical care. While I help you with somatic practices for pain relief and body awareness, it's essential to consult your doctor or a physical therapist for any medical diagnoses or acute injuries. My role is to support your well-being alongside professional medical guidance.
How does somatic healing work for a new mother's body?
Somatic healing for new mothers involves gentle, mindful movements and awareness exercises designed to release ingrained tension patterns from pregnancy, birth, and the demands of newborn care. It helps you tune into your body's signals, re-educate your muscles, and calm your nervous system, promoting natural pain reduction and improved body function.
How quickly can I expect to feel results from Wellness Coaching?
While individual experiences vary, many new mothers report feeling a noticeable difference in their physical comfort and body awareness within a few sessions. Consistent practice of the techniques we co-create will lead to more sustained relief and a deeper connection to your body's strength and resilience over time.
