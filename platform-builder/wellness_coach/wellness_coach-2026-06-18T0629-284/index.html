Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is Wellness Coaching for someone recovering from chronic illness or injury?
Wellness Coaching, in this context, is about providing a gentle, supportive space for your emotional and mental well-being as you recover. We'll work on understanding and processing the feelings that come with chronic health issues, developing coping strategies, and building emotional resilience, all tailored to your unique situation.
Do I need any previous experience with wellness practices to start Wellness Coaching?
Absolutely not. This Wellness Coaching is designed to be accessible for everyone, regardless of prior experience. We'll start with the basics, moving at a pace that feels comfortable and safe for you. Your comfort and understanding are my top priorities.
How does trauma-informed care apply to Wellness Coaching for chronic recovery?
Trauma-informed care means we recognize that chronic illness or injury can be a deeply impactful, even traumatic, experience. As your Wellness Coach, I ensure our sessions are safe, predictable, and empowering, respecting your boundaries and experiences without re-traumatizing. It's about building trust and helping you regain a sense of agency.
What kind of emotional healing can I expect through Wellness Coaching?
Through Wellness Coaching, you can expect to develop a deeper understanding of your emotions, learn practical tools for managing stress and overwhelm, and gently work through feelings of frustration, sadness, or anxiety related to your recovery. The goal is to cultivate inner peace and a sense of strength as you navigate your path forward.
