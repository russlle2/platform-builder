Understanding Your Path: Wellness Coaching After Illness or Injury

Recovering from chronic illness or injury often involves more than just physical mending. It can bring feelings of loss, frustration, or even grief. As your Wellness Coach, I provide a safe, understanding space to acknowledge these emotions and develop practical ways to cope, without judgment or pressure. We'll focus on your emotional well-being as a cornerstone of your overall return to feeling more like yourself.
