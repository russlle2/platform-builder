Frequently Asked Questions

As a parent of young children, how can a Wellness Coach truly help me sleep more when my kids wake up frequently?
It's true that you can't control your children's sleep patterns entirely, but a Wellness Coach can help you optimize the sleep you do get and manage the impact of interruptions. We'll focus on strategies like improving your sleep hygiene, creating a wind-down routine for yourself, and developing coping mechanisms for fragmented sleep, so you can maximize your rest and feel more resilient during the day.
I feel too overwhelmed to even think about adding more 'to-dos.' How will Wellness Coaching fit into my already busy schedule?
We understand that your time is incredibly valuable. Wellness Coaching isn't about adding more to your plate; it's about strategically removing stressors and integrating new, sustainable habits that actually simplify your life. We'll work together to find small, impactful shifts that align with your existing routine, rather than creating an entirely new one. Our goal is to make rest and stress management feel effortless, not like another chore.
What kind of results can I expect from working with a Wellness Coach on sleep and stress?
You can expect to feel a significant increase in your energy levels, improved mood, and a greater sense of calm in your daily life. Clients often report falling asleep faster, experiencing more restorative sleep, and feeling better equipped to handle the demands of parenting. Beyond just sleep, you'll gain practical tools to manage stress, reduce feelings of overwhelm, and foster a more peaceful home environment for your entire family.
Is Wellness Coaching just about my sleep, or does it cover other aspects of managing household stress?
While sleep improvement is a primary focus, your Wellness Coach will also address the broader aspects of household stress that impact your rest and overall well-being. This includes strategies for time management, setting boundaries, establishing routines that benefit the whole family, and developing effective stress-reduction techniques. We look at your life comprehensively to create lasting improvements beyond just the hours you spend in bed.
