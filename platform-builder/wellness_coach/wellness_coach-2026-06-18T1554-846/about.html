How does a Wellness Coach specifically help with posture during life transitions?

As your Wellness Coach, we focus on how stress or new routines from major life changes can manifest in your physical posture and body awareness. We help you identify tension patterns, develop mindful movement strategies, and integrate practices that support your skeletal alignment and comfort through these shifts, rather than just offering general advice.

Is Wellness Coaching for postural health suitable for any major life change?

Absolutely. Whether you're starting a new job, moving to a new city, adjusting to parenthood, or experiencing other significant shifts, our Wellness Coach approach helps you build body awareness to maintain physical comfort and stability. The principles of postural health are universally beneficial for adapting to new physical and emotional demands.

What can I expect in a Wellness Coach session focused on body awareness?

In a Wellness Coach session, we'll discuss your specific life transition and any physical sensations or postural habits you've noticed. We'll then explore gentle movements and awareness exercises tailored to your needs, helping you feel where your body is holding tension and how to release it. You'll leave with practical tools to integrate into your daily routine.

How is this different from physical therapy or a fitness trainer?

While we complement those services, as a Wellness Coach, our primary focus is on empowering your self-awareness and integrating postural health into your broader life context during transitions. We're not diagnosing injuries or prescribing rehabilitation exercises, but rather guiding you to understand your body's signals and proactively support its alignment and comfort through mindful practices and habit shifts.
