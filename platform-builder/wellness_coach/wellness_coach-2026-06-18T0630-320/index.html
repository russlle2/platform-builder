Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Wellness Coach specifically help with my postpartum physical pain?
As your Wellness Coach, I specialize in understanding the unique physical demands of postpartum recovery. We’ll identify the specific areas of discomfort – be it back pain from holding your baby, pelvic floor issues, or general body fatigue – and apply gentle, body-centered techniques to bring you relief and restore your physical resilience.
Is somatic healing suitable for new mothers who feel overwhelmed?
Absolutely. Somatic healing is particularly beneficial for new mothers as it focuses on the body's innate capacity for self-regulation and healing. It’s a gentle, non-verbal approach that doesn't require you to process feelings intellectually. Instead, it invites you to simply be present with your body, releasing stored tension and fostering a sense of calm, which is incredibly supportive during an overwhelming time.
What kind of practices can I expect in a session with a Wellness Coach?
Our sessions focus on personalized, gentle practices designed for your postpartum body. This might include guided awareness exercises to connect with your body, specific breathing techniques to calm your nervous system, or simple, restorative movements that support healing and release physical tension. Everything is tailored to your comfort and current stage of recovery.
How soon after giving birth can I begin Wellness Coaching for pain relief?
It's generally recommended to wait until after your 6-week postpartum check-up, or as advised by your healthcare provider, before beginning new physical practices. However, many gentle somatic awareness and breathing techniques can be beneficial even earlier. We'll discuss your specific situation and ensure any practices are safe and supportive for your body's unique healing timeline.
