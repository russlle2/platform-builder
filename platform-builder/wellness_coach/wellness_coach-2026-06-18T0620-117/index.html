Frequently Asked Questions

How does a Wellness Coach specifically help parents with sleep?
As a Wellness Coach, we focus on identifying the unique sleep disruptors faced by parents of young children, such as fragmented nights, varying schedules, and mental load. We then develop personalized, evidence-based strategies to optimize your sleep environment, implement effective wind-down routines, and address underlying stress that impacts rest. Our guidance is tailored to your family's specific needs, not generic advice.
Is this approach based on scientific evidence?
Absolutely. Our methods are rooted in current sleep science, cognitive behavioral techniques for insomnia (CBT-I adapted for parents), and stress reduction principles. We rely on established research to inform our recommendations, ensuring that the strategies you implement are not just theoretical but have proven efficacy in improving sleep quality and duration for busy parents.
How quickly can I expect to see improvements in my sleep?
While individual results vary, many parents report initial improvements in sleep quality and reduced stress within a few weeks of consistently applying our tailored strategies. Significant, sustainable changes often develop over a few months as new habits become ingrained and you gain confidence in managing your sleep. Our focus is on long-term, restorative rest, not just temporary fixes.
My household is chaotic; can I really find time for this?
We understand the demands of a busy household. Our programs are designed to integrate seamlessly into your existing routines, with practical, manageable steps. We prioritize efficiency and effectiveness, focusing on high-impact strategies that yield the most benefit without requiring extensive time commitments. Our goal is to reduce your stress, not add to it, by creating practical pathways to better rest.
