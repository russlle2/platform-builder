Case study — building a sustainable practice

Client profile: early-career professional with inconsistent routines, limited time for change. Intervention: 10-week habit stacking plus environmental redesign.

  1. Week 1–2: baseline tracking and micro-goal selection.
  2. Week 3–6: anchor routine formation, two tiny wins per day.
  3. Week 7–10: review, scale-up of successful micro-habits, relapse planning.

Measured outcome: consistent execution of daily anchors increased from 12% to 78% across 10 weeks; client reported improved focus and less decision fatigue.

Questions often asked by new clients

How quickly will I see progress?

Yes — we emphasize measurable behavior indicators rather than subjective promises.
Can I move between plans?
Absolutely. Many clients start in a lighter plan and scale up when they want deeper support.
