How does a Wellness Coach specifically help parents with emotional stress?

As your Wellness Coach, I focus on the unique stressors parents face, from sleep deprivation to managing multiple demands. We'll explore how these impact your emotional state and use trauma-informed approaches to help you process feelings of overwhelm, guilt, or anxiety. The goal is to build your emotional resilience so you can parent from a place of calm, rather than reactivity.

What does 'trauma-informed' mean in the context of Wellness Coaching?

Being trauma-informed means we understand that past difficult experiences, even small ones, can impact your current emotional responses. Your Wellness Coach will create a safe, non-judgmental space, helping you recognize how these past patterns might be affecting your parenting and household stress levels, without needing to re-live them. We focus on healing and building new, healthier coping mechanisms.

Is this like therapy, or is a Wellness Coach different?

While both offer support, Wellness Coaching, especially with a trauma-informed lens, is distinct from traditional therapy. As your Wellness Coach, I partner with you to identify specific goals related to emotional well-being and stress management as a parent. We focus on present challenges and future-oriented strategies, building skills for emotional regulation and fostering resilience in your daily life, rather than diagnosing or treating mental health conditions.

How quickly can I expect to see changes with a Wellness Coach?

The pace of emotional healing is unique to everyone. Many parents report feeling a sense of relief and greater self-awareness after just a few sessions with their Wellness Coach. Sustainable shifts in how you manage stress and respond to family dynamics tend to unfold over weeks and months as you consistently apply the strategies and insights gained. Our focus is on lasting, gentle change.
