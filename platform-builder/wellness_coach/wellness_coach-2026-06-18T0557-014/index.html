Frequently Asked Questions

What specific benefits can an athlete expect from Wellness Coaching?
You can expect a measurable reduction in physical and mental stress markers, improved sleep quality, faster recovery between training sessions, and enhanced ability to stay calm and focused under competitive pressure. Our goal is to stabilize your nervous system for optimal output and resilience.
How does Wellness Coaching differ from traditional sports psychology?
While sports psychology often focuses on mental game strategies, our Wellness Coaching emphasizes the physiological regulation of your nervous system. We provide direct tools and techniques to calm your body's stress response, which in turn positively impacts your mental state, recovery, and physical performance.
Is Wellness Coaching only for professional athletes?
Not at all. Our Wellness Coaching is designed for any active individual experiencing physical stress from their activities, whether you're a competitive athlete, a dedicated weekend warrior, or someone with a highly demanding physical job. If you're pushing your body, your nervous system can benefit from regulation.
What commitment is required for Wellness Coaching sessions?
The commitment varies based on your specific needs and goals. Typically, we recommend an initial series of sessions to establish foundational practices, followed by periodic check-ins. Our aim is to empower you with lasting skills, not create dependency.
