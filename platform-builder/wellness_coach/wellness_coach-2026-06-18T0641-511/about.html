What exactly does a Wellness Coach do for spiritual growth in seniors?

As your Wellness Coach, I provide structured, gentle support to help you explore and deepen your spiritual well-being. This can involve guided discussions, personalized reflective exercises, and practical tools to cultivate inner peace and a stronger sense of purpose, all tailored to your individual needs and comfort.

Is Wellness Coaching suitable for someone with no prior spiritual practice?

Absolutely. Your Wellness Coach will meet you exactly where you are. There's no requirement for previous spiritual experience. Our approach is inclusive and gentle, focusing on what resonates with you personally to foster a sense of calm, connection, and spiritual growth at your own pace.

How is this different from therapy or counseling?

While both offer support, as your Wellness Coach, I focus on forward-looking growth, helping you identify and achieve your spiritual and inner peace goals. Therapy often addresses past issues and mental health conditions. Our work together is about cultivating positive spiritual practices and enhancing your present and future well-being.

What kind of commitment is required for Wellness Coaching?

The commitment is flexible and designed to fit your life. We typically work together through a series of sessions, which can be weekly or bi-weekly. Your Wellness Coach will discuss a plan that feels comfortable and effective for you, ensuring it supports your goals for spiritual growth and inner peace without adding stress.
