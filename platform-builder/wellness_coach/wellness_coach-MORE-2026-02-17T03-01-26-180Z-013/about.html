About the practice

A practical partnership for people aiming to steady daily energy, unlock sustainable routines, and define meaningful priorities. We blend outcome-focused planning with habit scaffolds tailored to your rhythm.

Who I work with

Professionals and caretakers who want an approachable coaching loop: clear objectives, action steps for the week, and measurable micro-habits to practice reliably.

Approach

We form short, practical experiments — not rigid regimens. Sessions produce an actionable plan you can try for 1–4 weeks and refine based on what fits your life.

How a first month typically flows

  1. Initial mapping & one focused session to define a practical target.
  2. A short experiment — 7–21 days with a micro-habit and small checkpoints.
  3. Review & adjust; pick next micro-experiment or scale an effective habit.
