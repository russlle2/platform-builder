Frequently Asked Questions

Why is postural health so important during my postpartum recovery?
Postpartum, your body's alignment shifts due to pregnancy and childbirth, often leading to muscle imbalances and discomfort. Focusing on postural health with a Wellness Coach helps you gently realign, strengthen weakened areas, and prevent long-term issues, ensuring you can comfortably support your baby and navigate daily activities.
What does 'body awareness' mean in the context of postpartum Wellness Coaching?
Body awareness means developing a deeper connection to your physical sensations and how your body moves. As a new mother, this helps you recognize early signs of fatigue or tension, adjust your posture instinctively, and make conscious choices that support your physical recovery and overall well-being.
Is this Wellness Coaching suitable if I'm still feeling very tired postpartum?
Absolutely. Our Wellness Coaching is designed to meet you where you are. We start with gentle, restorative practices that respect your energy levels and recovery stage. The focus is on gradual rebuilding and mindful movement, never pushing you beyond what feels comfortable and supportive for your postpartum body.
How is your Wellness Coaching different from physical therapy for new mothers?
While physical therapy addresses specific injuries or dysfunctions, our Wellness Coaching provides a broader, proactive approach to postural health and body awareness. We focus on education, mindful movement, and personalized strategies for daily living, complementing medical care by empowering you to integrate healthy movement habits into your new routine.
