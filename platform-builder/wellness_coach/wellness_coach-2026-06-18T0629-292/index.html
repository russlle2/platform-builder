Frequently Asked Questions

How does emotional healing specifically benefit me as an athlete or active person?
Emotional healing directly impacts your physical recovery and performance. Unprocessed stress or trauma can manifest as chronic pain, fatigue, or performance plateaus. By addressing these emotional roots, you can experience faster physical recovery, reduced injury risk, enhanced focus, and a more sustainable, enjoyable relationship with your activity.
Is Wellness Coaching different from sports psychology or therapy for active individuals?
While there are overlaps, our Wellness Coaching focuses on a holistic, integrative approach to your well-being, specifically through a trauma-informed lens. We address the emotional and psychological aspects that influence your physical state and performance, offering practical strategies and support for deeper healing that complements, rather than replaces, traditional therapy or sports psychology.
I'm not sure if I've experienced 'trauma.' Can this Wellness Coaching still help me?
Absolutely. Our trauma-informed approach recognizes that 'trauma' encompasses a wide range of experiences, from significant events to cumulative stress and micro-aggressions. If you're experiencing chronic physical stress, burnout, anxiety, or difficulty recovering, this coaching can help you understand and address the underlying emotional factors, regardless of how you label your past experiences.
What can I expect in a typical Wellness Coaching session?
Each session is tailored to your unique needs. We'll start by discussing your current physical and emotional state, challenges, and goals. We might explore the connections between your physical symptoms and emotional experiences, using conversational and reflective techniques. You'll gain practical tools and insights to manage stress, process emotions, and build resilience, always in a supportive and confidential environment.
