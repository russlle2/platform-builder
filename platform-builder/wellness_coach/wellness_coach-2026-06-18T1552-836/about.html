How can a Wellness Coach specializing in posture help me during a major life change?

A Wellness Coach focusing on posture helps you recognize and adapt to the physical stress and shifts that accompany life transitions. We’ll work on specific body awareness techniques and movement patterns that provide a sense of groundedness and stability, helping you navigate changes with greater physical and mental ease.

Is this approach to postural health scientific, or more about general 'wellness' concepts?

My approach as a Wellness Coach integrates principles of biomechanics and motor learning, grounded in scientific understanding of the human body. While it certainly promotes overall well-being, the methods used are precise and evidence-informed, focusing on practical application for measurable physical improvement and awareness.

I'm not experiencing pain, but I feel 'off' since my big life change. Can a Wellness Coach still help?

Absolutely. It's common to feel 'off' or disconnected from your body during significant transitions, even without acute pain. As your Wellness Coach, we'll explore how these feelings manifest physically, helping you re-establish a sense of coherence and comfort in your body, promoting proactive postural health rather than reactive pain management.

What kind of commitment is required for working with a Postural Wellness Coach?

The commitment involves regular coaching sessions, typically weekly or bi-weekly, along with daily practices we design together. These practices are brief and integrated into your routine, building consistent body awareness and postural refinement. Your Wellness Coach journey is tailored to your schedule and personal goals.
