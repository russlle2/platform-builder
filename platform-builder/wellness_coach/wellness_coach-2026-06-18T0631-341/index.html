Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Wellness Coach specifically help with my age-related discomfort?
Our Wellness Coach services are specifically tailored for individuals over 60. We focus on gentle, low-impact strategies that respect your body's current abilities, aiming to reduce common aches and improve your mobility without strain. We understand the unique considerations that come with aging.
What kinds of practices do you use to help with physical pain as a Wellness Coach?
As your Wellness Coach, we utilize a range of gentle, somatic-informed practices. This might include guided mindful movement, breathing techniques, and sensory awareness exercises designed to release tension and improve your body's natural alignment. It's all about gentle relief, not intense workouts.
Is Wellness Coaching suitable if I have limited mobility or chronic conditions?
Absolutely. Our Wellness Coach approach is highly adaptable. We work with you to understand your specific limitations and conditions, ensuring all practices are safe, comfortable, and beneficial. The goal is to enhance your comfort and movement within your current capabilities, always gently.
How is a Wellness Coach different from a physical therapist or doctor?
While we don't diagnose or treat medical conditions like a doctor or physical therapist, as your Wellness Coach, we complement their care by focusing on lifestyle, gentle movement, and mindful body practices to support your overall comfort and well-being. We help you integrate healthy habits into daily life for lasting relief.
