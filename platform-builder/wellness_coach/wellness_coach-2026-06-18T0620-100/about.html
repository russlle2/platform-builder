How is a Postpartum Sleep Wellness Coach different from a sleep consultant for babies?

As a Postpartum Sleep Wellness Coach, my focus is entirely on your rest and recovery as the new mother. While we consider your baby's needs, my guidance is about supporting you in finding restorative sleep and managing the demands of postpartum life, rather than solely training your baby to sleep through the night.

I feel so overwhelmed and tired; can a Wellness Coach really help my sleep?

Absolutely. It's incredibly common to feel overwhelmed in this season. As your Wellness Coach, I provide compassionate, non-judgmental support, breaking down overwhelming goals into manageable steps. We'll identify small, impactful changes you can make to improve your rest, even amidst the chaos of new motherhood.

What if I can't commit to a strict sleep schedule with a newborn?

We understand that strict schedules are often unrealistic with a newborn. As your Postpartum Sleep Wellness Coach, our approach is flexible and adaptable. We focus on maximizing your opportunities for rest, optimizing your sleep environment, and teaching you techniques to fall asleep more easily, even when sleep is fragmented. It's about finding realistic solutions that work for your family.

Will working with a Postpartum Sleep Wellness Coach impact my breastfeeding or bonding with my baby?

Our priority is always to support your well-being without compromising your unique parenting choices. As your Wellness Coach, we'll integrate your feeding preferences and bonding time into your personalized rest plan, ensuring our strategies complement your journey as a new mother. Your connection with your baby is paramount.
