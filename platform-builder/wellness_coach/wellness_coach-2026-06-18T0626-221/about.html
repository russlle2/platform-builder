What exactly is 'gentle emotional care' through a Wellness Coach?

Gentle emotional care, as offered by your Wellness Coach, means providing support that respects your life experiences and pace. It’s about creating a calm, safe space for you to explore feelings without pressure, using techniques that are nurturing and non-intrusive to help you find inner peace.

How can Wellness Coaching help me if I feel overwhelmed by past memories?

Your Wellness Coach is here to listen with an open heart. We’ll gently guide you through processes that help acknowledge and understand those memories, without reliving distress. The aim is to help you release any lingering weight, allowing you to view your past with greater acceptance and move forward with lightness.

Is Wellness Coaching suitable for someone my age (60+)?

Absolutely! Our Wellness Coaching is specifically tailored for seniors aged 60 and above. We understand the unique emotional landscape that comes with this stage of life and offer compassionate support that honors your wisdom and experience, focusing on your well-being with utmost respect.

What should I expect during a session with my Wellness Coach?

You can expect a confidential, warm, and supportive conversation. Your Wellness Coach will listen attentively, ask thoughtful questions, and offer gentle guidance. We’ll explore your feelings and aspirations together, creating a personalized approach to help you nurture your emotional health and reconnect with your inner serenity.
