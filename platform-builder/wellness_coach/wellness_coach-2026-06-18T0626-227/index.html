Frequently Asked Questions

How can Wellness Coaching specifically help me manage the everyday stress of parenting young children?
Your Wellness Coaching will focus on teaching you practical, soulful techniques to recenter yourself when demands feel overwhelming. We'll explore ancient breathwork, mindful presence, and gentle energy practices that you can easily weave into your day, helping you respond to stressors with more intention and less reactivity.
What does 'trauma-informed' mean in the context of Wellness Coaching for parents?
Being trauma-informed means your Wellness Coach understands that past experiences, even subtle ones, can impact how you cope with current stress. We create a safe, non-judgmental space, offering gentle guidance to help you understand your emotional responses and cultivate inner safety, allowing you to heal and build resilience at your own pace without re-triggering old wounds.
I feel constantly depleted. Will Wellness Coaching add another thing to my already full plate?
No, your Wellness Coaching is designed to lighten your load, not add to it. We focus on sustainable, gentle practices that integrate seamlessly into your life. Our aim is to help you create more space and energy, rather than taking it away, by addressing the root causes of depletion and empowering you with self-care rituals that truly nourish you.
My household feels chaotic. Can Wellness Coaching really help me find peace amidst it all?
Yes, absolutely. Your Wellness Coach will guide you in cultivating inner stillness, regardless of external circumstances. We teach you how to become an anchor for your family by first anchoring yourself, using time-honored practices to help you respond to chaos with calm, transforming your home environment through your own inner shift.
