Frequently Asked Questions

I'm an entrepreneur; my schedule is unpredictable. Can a Wellness Coach really help my sleep?
Absolutely. Your entrepreneurial spirit is exactly why a tailored approach is crucial. As your Wellness Coach, I specialize in creating flexible, yet effective, sleep strategies that integrate seamlessly into your unique demands, helping you optimize rest even with a dynamic schedule.
I've tried sleep apps and general advice, but nothing sticks. How is working with a Wellness Coach different?
General advice often misses the mark because it's not designed for your specific challenges as a high-achiever. As your Wellness Coach, I provide personalized accountability, expert insights into your unique sleep disruptors, and adapt strategies in real-time to ensure they work for you, creating lasting habits.
Will a Wellness Coach just tell me to go to bed earlier? That's not always possible for me.
Not at all. While consistent timing is beneficial, my approach as a Wellness Coach is far more nuanced. We'll focus on optimizing the quality of the sleep you do get, improving your pre-sleep wind-down, and addressing underlying stressors, rather than just dictating a bedtime.
How quickly can I expect to see improvements in my sleep and stress levels with a Wellness Coach?
Many clients report noticeable improvements in sleep quality and reduced stress within a few weeks of consistent implementation. As your Wellness Coach, I work with you to establish sustainable changes, meaning the benefits will continue to build and deepen over time, providing lasting relief.
