Frequently Asked Questions

How does Wellness Coaching specifically help with chronic illness or injury recovery?
Our Wellness Coaching focuses on the often-overlooked aspect of nervous system regulation. Chronic conditions and injuries can put your body in a persistent state of stress. We provide you with targeted strategies to down-regulate your stress response, which can improve sleep, reduce pain sensitivity, and support your body's natural repair mechanisms, aiding your overall recovery.
Is this approach suitable if I'm already seeing other medical professionals?
Absolutely. Our Wellness Coaching is designed to complement, not replace, your existing medical care. We work collaboratively to ensure our strategies align with your treatment plan, providing an additional layer of support focused on stress reduction and nervous system balance. Our goal is to enhance your overall well-being alongside your clinical treatments.
What kind of techniques will I learn to reduce stress and regulate my nervous system?
You will learn a range of practical, evidence-based techniques. These may include specific breathing exercises to calm your vagus nerve, gentle somatic practices to release tension, and cognitive tools to reframe stress perceptions. Each technique is introduced with clear explanations of its physiological benefits and how it directly supports your recovery from chronic illness or injury.
How long does it take to see benefits from Wellness Coaching?
While individual experiences vary, many clients report feeling a greater sense of calm and control within a few weeks of consistently applying the techniques. The cumulative benefits of nervous system regulation develop over time, building sustained resilience. Our programs are structured to help you integrate these practices into your daily life for long-term improvement.
