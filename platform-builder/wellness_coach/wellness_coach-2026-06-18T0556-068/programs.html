Stress Doesn't Have to Take Over Your Day

You know that tightness in your chest after a phone call? The way your mind races when you're planning meals? That's your nervous system stuck in 'alert' mode. As a wellness coach for seniors, I help you notice these signals early. We'll practice simple breathing and body-awareness techniques that fit into your morning routine—no new gadgets or hour-long sessions. You'll learn to turn down the volume on daily stressors without changing your life.
