Frequently Asked Questions

Will this feel like another big life change?
No. We start exactly where you are—no new routines or supplements required. I'll show you how to use what's already in your day: your morning coffee, your favorite chair, even a quiet moment between checking mail.
How soon will I notice changes?
Most clients feel calmer during routine moments within 3-4 sessions. You'll notice less physical tension, like your hands unclenching after a call, before you realize it's not a habit anymore.
Does this replace my doctor visits?
Absolutely not. This is companion care for daily stress—not medical advice. We work alongside your health team to support your whole wellbeing.
What if I can't do 'mindfulness'?
Many seniors say they tried meditation and got frustrated. We skip that. Your 'mindfulness' is noticing when your toes feel warm or your breath slows—things you already experience naturally, just without the pressure.
