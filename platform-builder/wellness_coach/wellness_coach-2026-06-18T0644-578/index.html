Frequently Asked Questions

How does a Wellness Coach specifically help with spiritual growth during a life transition?
As your Wellness Coach, I help you identify and nurture your personal spiritual practices and beliefs. During big changes, it’s easy to lose touch with what grounds you. We’ll work together to reconnect you with your inner wisdom, helping you find meaning and stability even when your external world is shifting.
What kind of changes can a Wellness Coach help me navigate?
Whether you’re experiencing a career change, an empty nest, a relocation, relationship shifts, or any other significant life event, a Wellness Coach can provide guidance. My focus is on helping you maintain or establish inner peace and spiritual clarity through these often challenging, yet transformative, periods.
Is this like therapy, or is it different from working with a Wellness Coach?
While both can be supportive, Wellness Coaching focuses on forward movement and cultivating inner resources for your present and future. We won't delve into past trauma, but rather equip you with tools and perspectives to navigate current transitions and foster spiritual growth and peace from where you are now.
How quickly can I expect to feel more inner peace and spiritual connection?
The pace of spiritual growth and finding inner peace is unique to everyone. However, clients often report a greater sense of calm and clarity within a few sessions as they begin to integrate the practices and insights we explore. Consistency in applying these tools is key to sustained results.
