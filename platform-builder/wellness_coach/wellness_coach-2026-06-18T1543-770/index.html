Frequently Asked Questions

How does Wellness Coaching specifically help athletes dealing with physical stress?
For athletes, physical stress often creates mental and emotional strain. Our Wellness Coaching provides strategies to re-establish a positive internal relationship with your body and your sport, addressing the emotional impact of injuries, plateaus, or intense training periods. We help you build mental fortitude and emotional resilience that complements your physical training.
What does 'heart-centered care' mean for an active individual like me?
'Heart-centered care' means we focus on your emotional well-being and inner connection as much as your physical performance. It involves listening to your body's signals, understanding your motivations, and processing the emotional weight of your athletic journey. This approach helps you maintain passion, prevent burnout, and cultivate a deeply supportive relationship with yourself.
Will this coaching interfere with my current training regimen or medical treatments?
Our Wellness Coaching is designed to complement, not interfere with, your existing training or medical care. We focus on mental and emotional strategies for stress management, self-awareness, and building internal resilience. We encourage open communication with your coaches and healthcare providers to ensure a holistic and integrated approach to your well-being.
How quickly can I expect to see improvements in my relationship with my physical self?
Improvements are highly individual, but many athletes report a shift in perspective and a stronger sense of self-compassion within a few sessions. Consistent engagement with the coaching principles will progressively deepen your internal harmony, leading to more sustainable and fulfilling engagement with your athletic pursuits. Your commitment directly impacts your progress.
