Frequently Asked Questions

How does Wellness Coaching address physical pain specifically during life transitions?
During significant life changes, your body can hold stress and tension, leading to new or intensified physical pain. Our Wellness Coaching focuses on somatic practices and body awareness to help you understand and alleviate these physical symptoms, providing concrete strategies to reduce discomfort and restore ease as you adapt.
Is this Wellness Coaching suitable if my physical pain is medically diagnosed?
Absolutely. Our Wellness Coaching complements medical care by focusing on how your body processes stress and change, which can significantly impact pain perception and intensity. We work alongside your medical treatments to support your overall physical well-being and teach you self-regulation techniques for managing discomfort.
What kind of somatic practices are involved in your Wellness Coaching?
Our somatic practices are gentle and accessible, designed to help you reconnect with your body's innate wisdom. We might explore guided body scans, mindful movement, targeted breathwork, and techniques to release trapped tension, all aimed at improving your body's capacity to cope with stress and reduce physical pain.
How long does it typically take to see results from Wellness Coaching for physical pain during a transition?
The timeline for seeing results can vary for each person, as every transition and body is unique. Many clients report feeling a greater sense of calm and reduced pain within a few sessions, as they begin to integrate the practices. Sustained relief and improved bodily resilience develop with consistent engagement over time.
