Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly does a Wellness Coach do for emotional healing in seniors?
As your Wellness Coach, I provide structured support and understanding for navigating your emotional landscape. We work together to gently explore past experiences that might be causing distress, developing coping strategies, and fostering a renewed sense of emotional well-being. It’s about building inner strength and calm, tailored to your unique life stage.
Is this type of emotional support appropriate for someone over 60?
Absolutely. Our methods are specifically designed with the experiences and needs of seniors in mind. We prioritize gentle, trauma-informed approaches that respect your life history and pace. The goal is to provide a safe and comforting space for you to process emotions, without pressure, and at a rhythm that feels right for you.
How is this different from traditional therapy?
While there can be overlap, as a Wellness Coach, I focus on present-day emotional well-being and building future-oriented strategies for peace and resilience. We don't diagnose or treat mental health conditions. Instead, we collaborate on practical tools and perspectives to help you live more fully and calmly, addressing emotional burdens gently within a supportive coaching framework.
What if I'm hesitant to discuss difficult past events with a Wellness Coach?
It's completely understandable to feel that way. Your comfort is our highest priority. We begin by building trust and rapport at your own pace. You only share what you feel ready to, and we focus on gentle techniques that don't require you to relive traumatic details. Our approach is always trauma-informed, ensuring a safe and respectful environment for your emotional exploration.
