Frequently Asked Questions

How can Wellness Coaching specifically help with chronic physical pain from illness or injury?
Our Wellness Coaching focuses on non-medical strategies to complement your existing care. We help you identify triggers, develop gentle movement practices, and use somatic techniques to reduce physical discomfort and improve your body's overall sense of well-being, empowering you in your recovery.
What does 'somatic healing' mean in the context of my recovery with Wellness Coaching?
Somatic healing, as part of our Wellness Coaching, means paying close attention to your body's sensations and how they relate to your emotional and physical state. It's about learning to release stored tension, process pain in a gentle way, and reconnect with your body's natural capacity for healing, which is vital during chronic recovery.
Is Wellness Coaching suitable if I'm still actively recovering from an injury or illness?
Absolutely. Our Wellness Coaching is designed to be supportive at any stage of recovery. We work with you to ensure our approaches are gentle, safe, and tailored to your current physical capabilities and medical recommendations, always prioritizing your comfort and progress.
How will Wellness Coaching help me manage the emotional impact of chronic pain or illness?
Beyond the physical, our Wellness Coaching acknowledges the emotional toll of chronic conditions. We provide a space for you to process feelings, build coping strategies, and cultivate resilience, helping you navigate the emotional landscape of your recovery with greater ease and support.
