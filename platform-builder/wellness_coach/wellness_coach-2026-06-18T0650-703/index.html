Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Wellness Coach help me with anxiety related to academics?
As your Wellness Coach, I provide a safe space to explore the roots of your academic anxiety. We'll develop personalized coping strategies, practice mindfulness techniques, and build routines that support your mental well-being, helping you approach studies with greater ease and focus.
My relationships are struggling because of college pressure. Can a Wellness Coach assist with that?
Absolutely. A key focus for your Wellness Coach will be understanding how academic stress impacts your interactions. We'll work on communication skills, setting healthy boundaries, and strategies to nurture important connections, ensuring your relationships remain a source of strength, not stress.
What does 'heart-centered care' mean for a college student working with a Wellness Coach?
'Heart-centered care' with your Wellness Coach means approaching your challenges with empathy and understanding. It's about prioritizing your emotional well-being, encouraging self-compassion, and helping you make decisions that align with your deepest values, even amidst academic demands.
Is working with a Wellness Coach only for students who are really struggling?
Not at all! Many college students work with a Wellness Coach proactively to develop stronger coping mechanisms, improve relationship skills, and gain clarity on their academic path before stress becomes overwhelming. It's a supportive partnership for growth at any stage of your college experience.
