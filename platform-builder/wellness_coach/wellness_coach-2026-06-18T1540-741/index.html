Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is 'heart-centered relationship healing' for a college student?
For you, it means learning to communicate more openly and kindly with your friends, family, and even yourself. It’s about understanding how anxiety and academic stress might make you pull away, and then finding gentle ways to reconnect, build trust, and feel supported in your relationships without adding more pressure.
How can a Wellness Coach help with my academic anxiety specifically?
We don't just focus on study habits. We look at how anxiety impacts your ability to connect with your professors, collaborate with classmates, or even ask for help. By strengthening your emotional resilience and communication skills, you'll feel more confident in academic settings and reduce the isolating effects of pressure.
Is this a good fit for me if I'm struggling with making new friends or feeling isolated on campus?
Absolutely. Our community-focused approach helps you explore what might be holding you back from forming genuine connections. We'll work on building your self-compassion and effective communication, so you can confidently reach out, express yourself, and foster the kind of supportive friendships you deserve.
What if I'm not sure if wellness coaching is right for my specific situation?
That's completely understandable! We encourage you to reach out for a no-pressure chat. We can talk through what you're experiencing, and you can get a feel for how our heart-centered approach might support you in navigating your college challenges. Your comfort and understanding are our top priority.
