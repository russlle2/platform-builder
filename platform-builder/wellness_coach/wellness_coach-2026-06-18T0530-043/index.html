Frequently Asked Questions

I'm used to managing my own stress—can I really benefit from this?
Absolutely. Your nervous system is a muscle—it can be retrained. We meet you where you are, using methods you can actually do without adding more to your plate. No 'more work,' just gentle tools tailored to your life.
How is this different from just 'relaxing'?
Relaxing is passive. We focus on active nervous system regulation: teaching you how to notice tension early, use specific breathing, and create moments of safety. It's science-backed, not just 'take a deep breath' advice.
Can I do this if I'm busy with my grandkids or garden?
Yes! We build practices into *your* existing life. A 90-second grounding pause after putting the kids to bed, or noticing your breath while planting tulips—these small moments add up without disrupting your day.
What if I've tried other stress management and it didn't stick?
Most methods miss the nervous system. We address the root—how your body responds to stress—so the tools actually work for *you*. You'll learn why certain techniques failed before and how to find what truly calms *your* system.
