What exactly does 'Wellness Coaching' mean for seniors?

For seniors, Wellness Coaching means gentle, personalized support to help you feel more comfortable in your body. It's about easing physical discomforts and learning simple ways to move and feel better, without needing any prior experience in wellness. We focus on your unique needs and pace.

Do I need to be flexible or in good shape to start Wellness Coaching?

Absolutely not. Our Wellness Coaching is designed for everyone, especially if you're new to these kinds of practices or have some physical limitations. We start exactly where you are, focusing on very gentle movements and awareness. There's no expectation to be 'in shape' — just to be open to feeling more at ease.

How does Wellness Coaching help with aches and stiffness?

We use gentle, guided practices that help you notice and release areas of tension in your body. It's not about strenuous exercise, but about mindful movement and learning to listen to what your body needs to soften and relax. This can naturally lead to less stiffness and more comfort over time.

What if I'm not sure if this is right for me?

That's perfectly understandable! Many people feel that way. We offer a friendly chat where you can ask any questions you have and we can discuss how Wellness Coaching might help your specific concerns. There's no pressure, just an opportunity to see if it feels like a good fit for you.
