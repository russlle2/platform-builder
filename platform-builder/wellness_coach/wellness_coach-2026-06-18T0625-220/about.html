What exactly is 'trauma-informed' Wellness Coaching for new mothers?

Trauma-informed Wellness Coaching means we understand that past or recent experiences, including birth, can impact your current emotional state. We approach your story with deep respect and sensitivity, creating a safe environment where you feel seen and heard without judgment. Our methods are designed to avoid re-traumatization and instead support your nervous system in healing gently.

How is this Wellness Coaching different from general postpartum support?

While general postpartum support focuses on common challenges, our Wellness Coaching specifically delves into the emotional and spiritual aspects of your recovery, particularly in relation to any difficult or overwhelming experiences. We're not just offering advice; we're guiding you through a process of deep internal healing and integration, honoring the profound shift you've experienced as a mother.

I feel overwhelmed and depleted. Can Wellness Coaching truly help with that?

Absolutely. Feeling overwhelmed and depleted is a common sign that your emotional reserves are low and your nervous system may be overtaxed. Our Wellness Coaching provides specific techniques to help you regulate your emotions, build coping mechanisms, and restore your energy from a soulful perspective. We focus on nurturing you so you can nurture your family from a place of fullness, not emptiness.

Do I need to talk about every detail of my birth experience in Wellness Coaching?

No, you only share what you feel comfortable sharing. Our focus is on how your experiences have impacted you emotionally and how you can move forward with greater peace and resilience. We can explore the emotional imprints without needing to recount every detail, always respecting your boundaries and pace. The most important thing is your feeling of safety and agency in the process.
