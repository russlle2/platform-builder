Frequently Asked Questions

How does Wellness Coaching specifically help seniors with sleep?
Our Wellness Coaching is tailored to the unique physiological and lifestyle factors that can affect sleep in seniors. We focus on gentle, non-pharmacological methods, such as optimizing your sleep environment, reviewing evening routines, and introducing relaxation techniques, all designed to support your body's natural ability to rest deeply.
Is this a medical treatment, or does Wellness Coaching complement my doctor's advice?
Wellness Coaching is not a medical treatment, nor does it replace the care of your physician. We work alongside your existing healthcare plan, providing supportive strategies for sleep improvement. Our approach is complementary, focusing on lifestyle and behavioral adjustments that can enhance your overall rest and well-being, always with your health professional's guidance in mind.
What can I expect during a Wellness Coaching session for sleep?
During your Wellness Coaching session, you can expect a calm, confidential conversation. We'll discuss your current sleep patterns, identify potential areas for gentle adjustment, and collaboratively develop practical, achievable steps. Our goal is to empower you with strategies and understanding, ensuring you feel comfortable and confident in improving your rest.
How long does it typically take to see improvements in sleep with Wellness Coaching?
The timeline for sleep improvement varies for everyone, as each individual's needs are unique. However, many of our senior clients begin to notice positive shifts in their sleep quality and daytime energy within a few weeks of consistently applying the gentle strategies learned in Wellness Coaching. Our focus is on sustainable progress at a pace that feels comfortable for you.
