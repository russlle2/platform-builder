Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly does postural health mean for someone recovering from chronic illness or injury?
For you, postural health means learning to hold your body in ways that support your recovery, minimize pain, and conserve energy. It's about finding comfortable positions, understanding how your body moves, and building gentle strength without causing additional stress or setbacks. We focus on practical, everyday adjustments.
I have very limited mobility right now. Can a Wellness Coach still help me with body awareness?
Absolutely. Our approach is gentle and adaptable. We meet you exactly where you are, no matter your current mobility level. Even small, mindful shifts in how you sit, stand, or rest can make a significant difference in your comfort and body awareness. We start with what feels safe and comfortable for you.
Do I need any special equipment or prior experience for this type of Wellness Coaching?
Not at all. You don't need any special equipment or prior wellness experience. Our coaching uses simple movements and observations you can do anywhere, often just using your own body and a comfortable space. We're here to guide you every step of the way, making it easy to begin.
How is this different from physical therapy or other rehabilitation programs?
While we complement the work of physical therapy, our Wellness Coaching focuses specifically on integrating postural awareness and gentle movement into your daily life, beyond the clinic setting. We empower you with tools and understanding to maintain comfort and good body mechanics independently, supporting your long-term well-being after formal therapy concludes.
