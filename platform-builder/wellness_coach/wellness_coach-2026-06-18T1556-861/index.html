Frequently Asked Questions

How does a Wellness Coach specifically help college students with anxiety and academic pressure?
As your Wellness Coach, we'll work on practical, daily postural adjustments and body awareness exercises. These aren't just about 'standing up straight,' but about cultivating a sense of physical stability that translates to mental calm, helping you feel more in control when academic deadlines loom or anxiety creeps in.
My posture feels like a small thing compared to my college stress. How can Wellness Coaching make a real difference?
It’s true, posture might seem minor, but it’s a foundational piece of your well-being. Poor posture can contribute to fatigue and heightened stress responses. By gently guiding you to improve your body’s alignment and awareness, your Wellness Coach helps you create a physical foundation that supports mental resilience, giving you a more grounded way to approach challenges.
Will Wellness Coaching require a lot of extra time in my already busy college schedule?
Absolutely not. Our Wellness Coaching approach focuses on integrating small, impactful practices into your existing routine. We're not adding another burden, but rather showing you how to infuse mindful body awareness into activities you already do, like studying or walking between classes, making your daily life more sustainable.
What kind of exercises or practices will I learn with a Wellness Coach to improve my body awareness?
Your Wellness Coach will introduce you to simple, natural movements and sensory explorations. We might explore how you sit in class, how you carry your backpack, or gentle stretches inspired by nature. The goal is to reconnect you with your body's signals, allowing you to proactively ease tension and find comfort throughout your day.
