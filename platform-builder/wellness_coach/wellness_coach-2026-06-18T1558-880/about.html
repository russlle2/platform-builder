What exactly does a Wellness Coach do for an athlete's posture?

As your Wellness Coach, I work with you to observe and understand your current postural habits. We'll identify areas of imbalance or strain and then, through gentle guidance and practical exercises, help you develop better body awareness and more efficient movement patterns to reduce physical stress.

I'm not injured, just often stiff. Can a Wellness Coach still help me?

Absolutely! Many athletes experience stiffness or general discomfort before it escalates to an injury. A Wellness Coach focuses on prevention by improving your body awareness and posture, helping you address those early signs of physical stress and move with greater freedom before problems arise.

How is working with a Wellness Coach different from seeing a physical therapist?

While both aim for better physical function, a Wellness Coach focuses on education, awareness, and sustainable habit changes for ongoing postural health and managing physical stress. A physical therapist typically addresses specific injuries or acute pain with therapeutic interventions. We complement each other well!

I'm busy with training. How much time will I need to dedicate to this Wellness Coach program?

We understand your schedule is packed. Our Wellness Coach sessions are designed to be efficient and practical. We'll focus on integrating small, impactful adjustments into your existing routines, so you don't need to add hours of extra work. It's about smart, gentle integration, not overload, to help you manage physical stress effectively.
