Finding Your Center After Chronic Pain: A Wellness Coach Approach

When your body has been through chronic illness or injury, pain often leaves its mark. As your Wellness Coach, I help you gently reconnect with your physical self, understanding how past discomfort influences your present. We focus on somatic practices that encourage your body to release tension and find a new baseline of ease, specific to your needs during recovery.

How does Wellness Coaching specifically help with chronic pain from illness or injury?

Wellness Coaching focuses on understanding your unique experiences with pain from chronic illness or injury. We use somatic techniques to help you listen to your body's signals, release held tension, and create new patterns of movement and sensation that reduce discomfort and foster a sense of physical safety.

Is Wellness Coaching suitable if I'm still actively recovering from an injury?

Yes, absolutely. Wellness Coaching complements medical care by focusing on your body's innate capacity for healing and comfort. We work with your current physical state, always respecting your limits, to gently support your recovery and address any lingering physical discomfort or limitations from your injury.

What kind of somatic practices will I learn with a Wellness Coach?

As your Wellness Coach, I guide you through gentle somatic practices like mindful breathing, body scanning, and subtle movement explorations. These are designed to increase your awareness of internal sensations, helping you identify and release areas of chronic tension or pain that developed from illness or injury.

How can a Wellness Coach help me maintain physical comfort long-term?

Your Wellness Coach will equip you with practical tools and strategies to integrate into your daily life. We'll build your self-awareness around your body's needs and teach you self-regulation techniques, so you can continue to support your physical comfort and resilience long after our sessions conclude, especially after chronic illness or injury.
