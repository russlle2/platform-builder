Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Wellness Coach help me manage the stress of parenting young children?
As your Wellness Coach, I provide structured support to identify specific stressors impacting your mental clarity. We then implement evidence-based strategies to improve your cognitive function, reduce overwhelm, and develop practical systems for household management, allowing you to operate with more focus and less reactivity.
Is Wellness Coaching primarily about time management for parents?
While time management is a component, our Wellness Coach approach goes deeper. It's about optimizing your cognitive wellness to effectively manage the demands on your time and attention. We focus on enhancing your executive function, decision-making, and emotional regulation, which naturally leads to more effective time management and a reduction in mental clutter.
What kind of results can I expect from working with a Wellness Coach?
Clients typically report increased mental clarity, reduced feelings of overwhelm, improved ability to focus on tasks, and more intentional responses to daily stressors. As your Wellness Coach, our goal is to equip you with sustainable practices that lead to a more peaceful and productive family life, allowing you to feel more in control.
How is this Wellness Coach service different from therapy for parents?
As a Wellness Coach, I focus on present-day strategies and future-oriented goal setting to optimize your cognitive function and mental clarity amidst household stress. While therapy often addresses past experiences and underlying emotional patterns, coaching is about building skills and systems for immediate and ongoing improvement in your daily functioning as a parent.
