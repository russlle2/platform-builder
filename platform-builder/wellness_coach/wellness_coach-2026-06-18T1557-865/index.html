Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Wellness Coach help with the constant bending and lifting required as a parent?
As your Wellness Coach, I'll help you develop mindful movement patterns and body awareness techniques. We'll explore how to use your body's natural mechanics more efficiently when lifting children, bending for toys, or carrying bags, reducing strain and building sustainable postural habits.
I feel so stiff and tense from holding my baby. How can a Wellness Coach address this?
Many parents experience this. Your Wellness Coach approach will focus on gentle practices to release tension in common areas like your neck, shoulders, and lower back. We'll explore grounding exercises and simple stretches that can be integrated into your day, helping you feel more fluid and comfortable.
My posture has really suffered since having kids. Can a Wellness Coach truly make a difference?
Absolutely. Your Wellness Coach will provide personalized guidance to help you re-establish a more balanced and supportive posture. We'll identify daily habits that contribute to misalignment and build new, practical strategies to improve your body's natural alignment, leading to greater comfort and less fatigue.
What kind of 'body awareness' will I gain from working with a Wellness Coach?
You'll learn to tune into your body's signals, recognizing early signs of tension or imbalance before they become significant discomfort. Your Wellness Coach will guide you in understanding how your daily movements impact your overall well-being, empowering you to make conscious choices that support your physical health and energy levels.
