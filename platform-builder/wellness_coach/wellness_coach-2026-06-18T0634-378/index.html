Frequently Asked Questions

How does Wellness Coaching address physical pain during a life transition?
When you're navigating significant changes, your body can store stress and tension, often manifesting as physical pain. Our Wellness Coaching helps you recognize these somatic patterns and provides tools to release them. We work with you to build resilience, so your body can better adapt to new demands without creating persistent discomfort.
Is somatic healing suitable for all types of physical pain?
Somatic healing is incredibly effective for pain that has a stress component, or where muscular tension and postural habits contribute to your discomfort. While it complements medical care, it specifically focuses on your body's innate ability to self-regulate and release stored tension. We'll assess if our approach aligns with your specific needs during our initial conversation.
What can I expect in a typical Wellness Coaching session?
A typical session involves a conversational check-in to discuss your present physical and emotional state, followed by guided somatic practices tailored to your needs. These might include gentle movements, breathwork, or body awareness exercises. The goal is to empower you with practical skills you can use outside of sessions to manage discomfort and cultivate physical ease.
How long does it take to see results from this Wellness Coaching?
The timeline for results varies based on individual factors and the nature of your pain. However, many clients report feeling a greater sense of bodily awareness and a reduction in discomfort within a few sessions. Our focus is on sustainable change, equipping you with long-term strategies for physical well-being, rather than just immediate relief.
