Your Brain on Stress: How I Help You Rebalance

College life means deadlines, exams, and a constant stream of new challenges. This can keep your nervous system in overdrive, making it hard to concentrate, sleep, or even relax. My Wellness Coach programs are designed to help you understand your stress responses and equip you with actionable techniques to dial down the fight-or-flight, fostering a more resilient and focused you.

What exactly is 'nervous system regulation' for a college student?

It's about teaching your body and brain to move out of chronic stress (fight, flight, freeze) and into a more balanced state. For you, this means fewer panic attacks before exams, better sleep despite a busy schedule, and improved concentration during lectures, all by understanding and influencing your physiological responses to stress.

How is working with a Wellness Coach different from academic tutoring or therapy?

While tutoring focuses on specific subject matter and therapy addresses mental health conditions, as your Wellness Coach, I bridge the gap by providing proactive strategies for managing the physiological and emotional impacts of academic pressure. I equip you with tools to build resilience and optimize your well-being so you can thrive, not just survive, your college years.

Will this take a lot of time out of my already busy schedule?

My approach as a Wellness Coach is designed to integrate seamlessly into your life. We focus on efficient, impactful strategies that you can implement in short bursts. The goal isn't to add another stressor, but to give you tools that save you time and energy in the long run by improving your focus and reducing recovery time from stress.

Is this only for students with severe anxiety?

Not at all. While I can support those experiencing significant anxiety, my Wellness Coach services are beneficial for any college student feeling the pressure of academic demands, difficulty sleeping, concentration issues, or general overwhelm. Preventing burnout and building resilience are key for all students.
