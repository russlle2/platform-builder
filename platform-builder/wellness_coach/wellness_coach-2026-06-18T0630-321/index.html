Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is gentle somatic wellness coaching for seniors?
Gentle somatic wellness coaching helps you become more aware of your body's subtle sensations and movements. For seniors, this means learning easy, mindful techniques to release chronic muscle tension, reduce stiffness, and improve overall physical comfort without strenuous activity. It’s about listening to your body's wisdom.
How can this type of coaching help with my ongoing aches and stiffness?
We focus on identifying and unwinding the unconscious movement patterns and muscular contractions that often contribute to persistent aches. Through gentle guidance, you'll learn to release these patterns, which can lead to significant reductions in stiffness and discomfort, helping your body move with greater ease and fluidity.
Do I need any special equipment or prior experience for somatic wellness coaching?
Not at all. All you need is a quiet space and comfortable clothing. Our sessions are designed to be accessible for everyone, regardless of your current physical condition or previous experience with mindful practices. We meet you exactly where you are with compassion and understanding.
What makes this approach different from traditional physical therapy or exercise classes?
While valuable, physical therapy often focuses on strengthening and specific exercises. Our somatic wellness coaching emphasizes internal awareness and gentle release. We help you learn to self-regulate tension and cultivate a deeper, more spiritual connection with your body's own capacity for comfort, offering a gentle, self-empowering path to lasting relief.
