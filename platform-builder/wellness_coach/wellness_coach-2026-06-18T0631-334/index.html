Frequently Asked Questions

How does Wellness Coaching specifically address chronic pain from illness or injury?
Our Wellness Coaching focuses on somatic practices that help you understand and re-regulate your body's pain response. We utilize gentle techniques to reduce physical tension, improve nervous system regulation, and foster a more comfortable relationship with your body, targeting the unique challenges of chronic recovery.
Is Wellness Coaching a substitute for medical treatment for my condition?
No, Wellness Coaching is a complementary support system. We work alongside your medical care team, providing strategies for pain management and somatic healing that enhance your overall recovery. Always consult your doctor for medical advice and treatment plans, and we'll help you integrate our methods safely.
What kind of results can I expect from working with a Wellness Coach for physical pain?
Clients often experience a noticeable reduction in their daily pain levels, improved physical comfort, and enhanced body awareness. You can expect to learn practical techniques to self-regulate pain, move with greater ease, and cultivate a sense of agency over your physical well-being, even with a chronic condition.
How long does it take to see improvements in my physical pain with Wellness Coaching?
The timeline for improvement varies for everyone, as chronic recovery is highly individual. However, many clients report feeling subtle shifts and increased comfort within a few sessions. Consistent engagement with the practices we provide typically leads to more significant and lasting changes over time.
