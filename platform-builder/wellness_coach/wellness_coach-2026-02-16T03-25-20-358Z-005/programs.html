Program misconceptions

Are cohorts one-size-fits-all?

No. Cohorts use modular experiments so each participant tailors protocols to their context while sharing learnings.

Do longer programs mean better results?

Longer programs offer more iteration cycles, but progress depends on fidelity to experiments, not duration alone.

Program FAQs

Can I upgrade between paths?

Yes. Upgrades are staggered with prorated credit for unused sessions or cohort materials when applicable.

What if I miss sessions?

Missed sessions are rescheduled when possible. The program focus remains on the experiment cadence rather than perfect attendance.

Is there a refund policy?

Refund policy varies by program. We provide clear terms on booking pages and in the intake form.
