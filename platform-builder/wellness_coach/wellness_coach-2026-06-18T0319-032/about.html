How does a Wellness Coach specifically help new mothers with stress?

As your Wellness Coach, I focus on the unique stressors of postpartum, like sleep deprivation, hormonal fluctuations, and the constant demands of infant care. We implement targeted techniques to reduce cortisol levels, promote parasympathetic nervous system activity, and build your capacity to handle stress, not just cope with it.

Is Wellness Coaching practical for someone with limited time and energy postpartum?

Absolutely. My approach as your Wellness Coach is designed to integrate seamlessly into your life, even in short bursts. We prioritize efficiency and effectiveness, focusing on high-impact strategies that yield significant nervous system regulation without requiring extensive time commitments. Small, consistent actions can lead to profound shifts.

What kind of results can I expect from working with a Wellness Coach on nervous system regulation?

With consistent engagement, you can expect to experience a greater sense of calm, reduced anxiety, improved emotional stability, and enhanced ability to be present with your baby. Your Wellness Coach will help you cultivate a more resilient nervous system, allowing you to navigate postpartum challenges with greater ease and less reactivity.

How is this different from therapy or traditional postpartum support groups?

As a Wellness Coach, my role is to provide proactive, forward-looking strategies and practical tools for stress reduction and nervous system regulation. While therapy often addresses past trauma or mental health diagnoses, and support groups offer peer connection, I provide individualized, science-backed methods to actively build your resilience and self-regulation skills in the present moment.
