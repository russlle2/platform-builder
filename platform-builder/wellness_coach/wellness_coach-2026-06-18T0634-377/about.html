How can Wellness Coaching specifically help with the physical pain parents experience?

Our Wellness Coaching approach targets common parental pains like lower back stiffness from carrying children, neck and shoulder tension from stress and feeding, and general fatigue. We use somatic practices to help you release chronic tension, improve posture, and build resilience in your body, so you can manage daily demands with less discomfort.

I'm already so busy. How will I find time for Wellness Coaching?

We understand your time is precious. Our Wellness Coaching sessions are designed to be efficient and impactful. We focus on practical techniques you can integrate into your existing routines, even in short bursts. The goal is to give you tools that save you time and energy in the long run by reducing pain and improving your overall well-being.

What exactly is 'somatic healing' and how does it relate to my physical pain?

Somatic healing, within our Wellness Coaching, is about understanding how your thoughts, emotions, and experiences manifest as physical sensations and pain in your body. We guide you to tune into these bodily signals, release stored tension, and create new, healthier patterns of movement and sensation. This approach helps you move beyond just treating symptoms to addressing the deeper causes of your discomfort.

Will Wellness Coaching replace my need for a doctor or physical therapist?

Wellness Coaching at Sage Garden is complementary to medical care, not a replacement. We focus on empowering you with self-care tools and somatic practices to manage chronic pain and stress, and improve your overall physical and emotional well-being. We always recommend consulting with your doctor for any acute medical conditions or concerns.
