Your Nervous System is Not Broken

When your toddler screams during breakfast and your phone buzzes with work emails, your body goes into survival mode. We don't ask you to 'fix' yourself—we teach you to gently interrupt that stress cycle. You'll learn to recognize your own stress signals and use tiny, practical tools (like a 90-second breath during diaper changes) that actually work when your hands are full.

I'm too overwhelmed to even try new tools—can this help me *now*?

Absolutely. We start where you are, not where you think you should be. Your first session focuses on recognizing one tiny stress moment you experience daily (like waiting in the school pickup line) and finding an immediate, manageable way to reset your nervous system.

Will this make me feel like I'm failing my kids if I take time for myself?

No. When you regulate your nervous system, you're not failing—you're modeling calm for your kids. Think of it this way: you wouldn't expect a pilot to fly a plane while having a panic attack. We help you stay steady so you can stay present for them.

How does this work with my toddler's constant meltdowns?

We explore how meltdowns actually signal *your* nervous system reaching capacity, not just your child's behavior. You'll learn to gently pause your own reaction (instead of 'fixing' the meltdown) and use a 30-second tool to reset yourself—so you can respond instead of react.

Do I need to stop doing everything to practice this?

No. All tools are designed to work *while* you're in the middle of parenting. We'll find your 'stress anchor' (like the 2 minutes you spend making coffee) and transform that routine into a nervous system reset opportunity.
