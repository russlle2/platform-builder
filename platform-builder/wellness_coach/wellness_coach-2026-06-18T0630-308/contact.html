Wellness Coach

Gentle Somatic Support for Your Changing Life

Major life transitions, like career changes, relocation, or relationship shifts, can bring unexpected physical tension and chronic aches. At Renewed Resilience Wellness Coaching, we understand how these big moments impact your physical well-being. We offer a nurturing approach, using somatic practices to listen to your body, release accumulated stress, and restore a sense of calm and comfort. You don't have to carry the weight of change in your shoulders or back. Let's work together to release physical discomfort so you can embrace your new chapter with greater ease.
