How does a Wellness Coach help with physical pain related to college stress?

As your Wellness Coach, I focus on somatic techniques that help you recognize how academic anxiety and pressure show up in your body. We'll work on releasing chronic tension, improving posture, and calming your nervous system to directly alleviate physical symptoms like headaches, neck pain, or digestive discomfort. It's about teaching your body to relax and reset.

What kind of 'somatic healing' will I experience as a college student?

Somatic healing for college students means learning to listen to your body's signals about stress and overwhelm. Your Wellness Coach will guide you through gentle movements, breathing exercises, and mindfulness practices that help you discharge accumulated tension. This process helps you feel more present and less hijacked by academic pressures, reducing the physical toll they take on you.

Is Wellness Coaching only for students with severe anxiety?

Not at all! While I can certainly support you if you're dealing with significant anxiety, Wellness Coaching is also incredibly beneficial for everyday academic pressure and the general stresses of college life. If you're experiencing any physical discomfort, brain fog, or difficulty focusing due to stress, a Wellness Coach can help you develop proactive strategies for better well-being.

How quickly can I expect to feel better with a Wellness Coach?

Many college students report feeling a sense of relief and greater awareness after just a few sessions with their Wellness Coach. The pace of progress depends on your individual needs and commitment. Our goal is to equip you with lasting tools and practices, so you can continue to manage stress and maintain physical ease long after our coaching concludes.
