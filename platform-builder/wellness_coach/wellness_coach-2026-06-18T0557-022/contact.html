Understanding Your Postpartum Nervous System with a Wellness Coach

The postpartum period asks so much of your body and mind, often leading to an overstimulated nervous system. As your Wellness Coach, I help you recognize the subtle signals of overwhelm, from persistent fatigue to heightened anxiety. We'll explore how these modern demands clash with your ancient, intuitive need for rest and gentle recovery, laying the groundwork for real, sustainable peace.
