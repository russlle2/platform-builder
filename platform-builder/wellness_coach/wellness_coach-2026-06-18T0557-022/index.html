Frequently Asked Questions

Why is nervous system regulation so important for new mothers, from a Wellness Coach perspective?
As a new mother, your body is undergoing immense changes, and sleep deprivation is often a constant. This combination can keep your nervous system in a heightened state of alert. Regulating it helps you manage stress, improve sleep quality, and simply feel more present and grounded with your baby, preventing burnout and supporting your overall well-being.
What specific ancient healing traditions does your Wellness Coach practice incorporate?
My Wellness Coach approach draws from various ancient wisdom traditions that emphasize the mind-body connection. This includes practices like gentle Ayurvedic principles for self-care, specific breathwork techniques from yogic traditions to calm the vagus nerve, and mindfulness practices rooted in Eastern philosophies, all adapted for the unique needs of postpartum recovery.
I feel completely overwhelmed and have very little time. How can a Wellness Coach help me?
I understand that time is a precious commodity right now. As your Wellness Coach, our focus is on simple, effective strategies that fit into your existing routine, not adding more pressure. We'll identify small, powerful shifts you can make, sometimes just a few minutes long, that yield significant impact on your stress levels and sense of control. It's about quality over quantity.
Will working with a Wellness Coach replace therapy or medical care for postpartum anxiety or depression?
No, my services as a Wellness Coach are complementary to, not a replacement for, professional medical or mental health care. If you are experiencing symptoms of postpartum anxiety or depression, please consult with your doctor or a licensed therapist. My role is to support your well-being through stress reduction and nervous system regulation, working alongside any other support you may be receiving.
