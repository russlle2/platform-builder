What exactly is 'nervous system regulation' in the postpartum period?

Nervous system regulation means teaching your body and mind to return to a state of calm after stress. For new mothers, this often involves gentle techniques to reduce feelings of overwhelm, anxiety, and constant 'fight or flight' mode, helping you feel more balanced and rested amidst the demands of caring for a newborn.

I've never done wellness coaching before. Is this for me?

Absolutely! My Wellness Coaching is designed to be gentle and accessible, especially for new mothers who might feel overwhelmed by complicated 'wellness' practices. We start exactly where you are, with simple, practical strategies that fit into your busy life, no prior experience needed. Your comfort and ease are my priority.

How can Wellness Coaching help with sleep when I'm up all night with the baby?

While I can't promise more hours of sleep, Wellness Coaching can help improve the quality of the sleep you do get and reduce the stress around lack of sleep. We'll focus on winding down your nervous system before rest periods, even short ones, and developing calming routines that can make those fragmented sleep moments feel more restorative for your body and mind.

What kind of time commitment does Wellness Coaching require from a new mom?

I understand your time is precious and often unpredictable. Our Wellness Coaching sessions are designed to be efficient and impactful. We'll set realistic goals and implement strategies that can be woven into your existing routine, even if that means just a few minutes a day. It's about gentle integration, not adding more to your plate.
