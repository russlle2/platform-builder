Frequently Asked Questions

How does a Wellness Coach specifically address postural health during major life changes?
As your Wellness Coach, I help you recognize how significant life events—like a new job requiring more desk time, pregnancy, or recovering from an injury—can subtly alter your physical alignment. We'll identify specific stressors on your posture and develop practical, gentle exercises and mindful movement practices to counteract them, supporting your body's stability.
What does 'body awareness' mean in the context of your Wellness Coaching?
Body awareness, in my Wellness Coaching practice, refers to your ability to accurately perceive your body's position, movement, and internal state. During transitions, this awareness can often diminish. We'll cultivate this sense through focused attention and gentle exercises, enabling you to identify early signs of tension or misalignment and make timely adjustments for greater comfort.
Is your Wellness Coaching suitable if I have existing physical discomfort?
My Wellness Coaching is designed to complement, not replace, medical care. If you have existing discomfort, we can work within the recommendations of your healthcare providers to enhance your postural support and body mechanics. The goal is to create a more supportive physical environment for your body, often leading to reduced strain and improved well-being.
How long does a typical Wellness Coaching program for postural health last?
The duration of your Wellness Coaching program is tailored to your individual needs and the nature of your life transition. Some clients benefit from a focused 8-week series to establish new habits, while others prefer ongoing support for more complex or prolonged changes. We'll discuss a suitable timeframe during your initial consultation.
