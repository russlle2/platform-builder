Frequently Asked Questions

How does Wellness Coaching specifically benefit an athlete's immune system?
As an athlete or active person, your body experiences significant physical stress that can temporarily suppress your immune system. Wellness Coaching with me focuses on optimizing your nutritional intake, managing recovery, and identifying lifestyle factors to build a more resilient immune response, helping you avoid illness and maintain training consistency.
What kind of vitality building strategies will we focus on as part of your Wellness Coaching?
Our vitality building strategies go beyond just 'more energy.' We'll dive into optimizing sleep, balancing stress hormones, enhancing mitochondrial function through targeted nutrition, and ensuring your body has the micronutrients it needs to perform and recover, all aimed at a sustained, robust energy profile for your active life.
Is this Wellness Coaching suitable for all levels of active individuals, from weekend warriors to competitive athletes?
Absolutely. Whether you're training for a marathon, a recreational sports enthusiast, or simply maintain a highly active lifestyle, my Wellness Coaching programs are tailored to your specific demands, recovery needs, and immune support requirements. The principles apply universally to anyone managing physical stress.
What makes your Wellness Coach approach different for athletes managing physical stress?
My approach is unique because it combines a deep understanding of exercise physiology and immunology with practical, personalized coaching. We don't just talk about general wellness; we focus on the specific biological stressors of intense activity and implement targeted, evidence-based interventions to fortify your immune system and elevate your vitality, keeping you in the game.
