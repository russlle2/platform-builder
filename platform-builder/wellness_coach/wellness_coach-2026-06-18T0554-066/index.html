Frequently Asked Questions

Can I actually practice nervous system regulation during my 9-5?
Yes—your breaks aren't optional. We teach you to use your 3-minute bathroom stall to reset your nervous system before your next call. No extra time needed, just retraining your body's response to existing pressure.
What if I'm too busy for another 'wellness thing'?
We integrate regulation into what you already do. While you check emails, you'll use micro-pauses to reset. During your commute, you'll practice a 60-second breathing technique. It’s not another task—it’s how you handle existing work without breaking.
How is this different from therapy or meditation?
We focus exclusively on nervous system regulation, not processing past trauma or achieving 'mindfulness.' You'll learn concrete tools to stop stress in its tracks during your actual workday—like recognizing when your shoulders are tense before a meeting and taking a 20-second reset.
Do I need to change my job to feel less overwhelmed?
No. We work with your current reality. Whether you're in corporate or healthcare, we help you regulate your response to your job's demands—not remove the demand. Your nervous system learns to handle the pressure you're already under.
