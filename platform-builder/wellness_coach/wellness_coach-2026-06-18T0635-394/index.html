Frequently Asked Questions

What kind of physical pain can Sage Garden Wellness Coach help me with?
Our Wellness Coaching focuses on persistent physical discomfort often associated with chronic illness or recovery from injury. This includes general body aches, stiffness, limited mobility, and the somatic effects of long-term stress on your nervous system. We work with you to understand your specific pain patterns and develop strategies for relief and greater ease.
How is Wellness Coaching different from physical therapy or medical treatment?
Wellness Coaching complements, rather than replaces, medical treatment and physical therapy. We focus on education, self-care practices, and nervous system regulation to support your body's innate capacity for healing. While medical professionals diagnose and treat conditions, your Wellness Coach guides you in developing daily practices and a deeper somatic awareness to manage discomfort and improve overall physical well-being.
Do I need to be able to do strenuous exercise for Wellness Coaching to be effective?
Absolutely not. Our approach is gentle and highly personalized. We meet you exactly where you are, whether you're largely sedentary or have some mobility. The focus is on subtle body awareness, gentle movement, and regulating your nervous system, not strenuous activity. We adapt all practices to your current physical capabilities and comfort levels.
How long does it take to see results with your Wellness Coaching?
The timeline for experiencing relief and improvement varies for everyone, as recovery from chronic illness or injury is a unique process. Most clients begin to notice subtle shifts in discomfort and a greater sense of calm within a few sessions. Consistent engagement with the practices we co-create will lead to more sustained progress over weeks and months, building resilience in your body and mind.
