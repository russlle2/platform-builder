Frequently Asked Questions

How does Wellness Coaching specifically help with postural health after an illness or injury?
Our Wellness Coaching zeroes in on helping you gently re-educate your body. We guide you through simple, targeted movements and awareness practices that can help realign your posture, reduce strain, and improve your balance, all tailored to your current physical capabilities as you recover.
I feel very disconnected from my body after my experience. Can Wellness Coaching help me with body awareness?
Absolutely. It's very common to feel a disconnect. As your Wellness Coach, we create a safe space for you to gently explore and reconnect with your body. Through guided attention and movement, you'll learn to listen to your body's subtle cues, fostering a stronger sense of presence and understanding.
Is Wellness Coaching suitable if I'm still in the midst of my recovery from a chronic condition?
Yes, our Wellness Coaching is designed to complement your ongoing recovery. We work with you to understand your current limitations and build upon your strengths. Our focus is always on supportive, gentle progress in postural health and body awareness, ensuring it aligns with your overall care plan.
What should I expect in a typical Wellness Coaching session focused on posture?
In a session, your Wellness Coach will start by listening to your experiences and current challenges. We'll then move into gentle, guided movements and observations, helping you feel and understand your body's alignment. It's a collaborative process where you learn practical tools and insights to carry into your daily life for improved comfort and posture.
