Our Framework

A soulful method: Clarify, Habit, Community, Reflect — repeated in a seasonal cadence.

Clarify

Set a one-page intention and 3 priority habits. We use gentle inquiry, values sorting, and short rituals.

Habit

Micro-habits anchored to daily cues. Science-forward but spiritually grounded to build momentum.

Community

Weekly cohort sessions, small practice circles, and accountability pairings for lasting support.

Reflect

Monthly reflection rituals, adaptive plans, and integration practices so change becomes identity.

Cohort model: New cohorts begin quarterly. Each cohort includes 8–12 people and 10 weeks of guided practice.

Micro Habits to Start Today

Tiny shifts, reliable returns. Pick 1, adapt in weeks, then layer another.

  • Breathe 3/3/3: three deep breaths, three slow exhales, three gentle stretches.
  • Mood check: 60-second journal once a day — name one win.
  • Anchor movement: a single five-minute mobility ritual at wake or sleep.
  • Evening gratitude: list one thing that grounded you today.

These are woven into our weekly practices and reinforced with habit trackers in the cohort portal.
