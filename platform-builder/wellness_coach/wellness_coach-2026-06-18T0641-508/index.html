Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Wellness Coach specifically help me during a major life transition?
During significant life changes, a Wellness Coach provides a dedicated space to process emotions, clarify your values, and build new spiritual practices that support your inner peace. We focus on helping you re-establish your sense of self and purpose when your external circumstances are shifting, ensuring you don't just get through the transition, but thrive within it.
What kind of spiritual growth can I expect with a Wellness Coach?
Spiritual growth with your Wellness Coach might involve deepening your connection to your intuition, understanding your core beliefs, cultivating a more compassionate self-dialogue, or finding new ways to experience meaning and purpose. It's about nurturing your inner life so you feel more aligned and peaceful, regardless of external challenges.
I feel overwhelmed by my current life change. Can a Wellness Coach help me find inner peace?
Absolutely. Feeling overwhelmed is a common response to big changes. As your Wellness Coach, I offer gentle, structured support to help you quiet the mental chatter, identify sources of stress, and develop tailored practices like mindful presence or reflective journaling. The aim is to help you reclaim your inner peace and feel more in control of your emotional landscape.
Is Wellness Coaching right for me if I'm not sure what my 'spiritual' path is?
Yes, completely. You don't need a pre-defined spiritual path to benefit from a Wellness Coach. My approach is inclusive and respects your unique perspective. We'll explore what spiritual growth means for you personally – whether that's through nature, creativity, quiet contemplation, or community – to help you find your own authentic way to inner peace during your life transition.
