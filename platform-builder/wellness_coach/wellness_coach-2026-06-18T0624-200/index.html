Frequently Asked Questions

How is postpartum emotional Wellness Coaching different from therapy?
While both offer support, therapy often delves into past traumas or mental health diagnoses. As your Wellness Coach, I focus on present challenges and future goals, providing practical tools and strategies to help you navigate the emotional landscape of postpartum recovery. It's about empowering you to build resilience and foster emotional well-being right now.
Is Wellness Coaching specifically for mothers who've experienced birth trauma?
Our Wellness Coaching is trauma-informed, meaning we approach every mother's experience with sensitivity and understanding, regardless of whether you identify with birth trauma. The postpartum period can be emotionally intense for many reasons, and our support is here for any new mother seeking emotional healing and a gentler recovery from any challenging experience.
What kind of emotional healing can I expect from Wellness Coaching?
You can expect to develop a deeper understanding of your own emotional responses, learn coping mechanisms for overwhelming feelings, and cultivate self-compassion. We work on processing difficult emotions, fostering a sense of peace, and building emotional resilience so you feel more grounded and capable as a new mother.
How does Wellness Coaching fit into my already busy life as a new mother?
We understand that your time is precious. Our Wellness Coaching sessions are designed to be flexible and supportive, integrating seamlessly into your schedule. We focus on actionable strategies that don't add to your overwhelm but instead create space for your emotional well-being, helping you prioritize your healing in a sustainable way.
