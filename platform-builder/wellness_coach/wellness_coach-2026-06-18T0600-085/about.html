I've never done anything like this. Do I need prior 'wellness' experience to work with a Wellness Coach?

Absolutely not! This is designed for students exactly like you. You don't need any special knowledge or experience. We start from scratch, focusing on easy-to-understand techniques and making sure you feel comfortable every step of the way. Your Wellness Coach will guide you gently.

My schedule is crazy busy. How much time will wellness coaching take out of my week?

We get it – college schedules are packed! Our approach is all about integrating simple, quick practices into your existing routine, not adding more to your plate. Sessions are flexible, and the strategies you learn are designed to be short bursts of calm you can use throughout your day, without disrupting your studies.

What kind of results can I expect from working with a Wellness Coach on my anxiety and academic pressure?

You can expect to learn practical ways to calm your nervous system, reduce feelings of overwhelm, and improve your focus. Many students report feeling more grounded, sleeping better, and having a clearer headspace for their academic work. It's about building your inner resilience to handle college challenges more effectively.

Is this like therapy? What's the difference with a Wellness Coach?

While both can be supportive, Wellness Coaching with us is different from therapy. We focus on teaching you forward-looking skills and strategies to manage stress and regulate your nervous system in the present and future. We don't delve into past trauma or clinical mental health diagnoses; instead, we equip you with practical tools to navigate your current college experience with more ease.
