Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly does a Wellness Coach do for seniors focused on seasonal health?
As your Wellness Coach, I help you adapt your daily routines, diet, and activities to naturally complement the changing seasons. This means suggesting gentle ways to boost your immunity in colder months, or finding refreshing practices for warmer weather, always with your well-being and preventive care at the forefront.
Is Wellness Coaching suitable for someone over 60 who prefers gentle approaches?
Absolutely! My approach as a Wellness Coach is specifically designed to be gentle, supportive, and respectful of your body's needs at this stage of life. We focus on sustainable, enjoyable practices that enhance your comfort and vitality, never pushing beyond what feels right for you.
How can a Wellness Coach help me prevent common health concerns as I age?
We'll work together to identify simple, proactive steps you can take. This might involve optimizing your nutrition for bone health, incorporating gentle movement to maintain flexibility, or practicing stress-reducing techniques. Your Wellness Coach will equip you with practical tools to strengthen your body's natural defenses.
What can I expect in a typical Wellness Coaching session with you?
Each session is a friendly conversation focused entirely on you. We'll discuss your current well-being, any seasonal challenges you're facing, and together, we'll brainstorm practical, personalized strategies. Your Wellness Coach will offer guidance, encouragement, and actionable steps to help you feel your best.
