Frequently Asked Questions

How does Wellness Coaching specifically help with immune support during major life changes?
As your Wellness Coach, we focus on identifying the unique stressors associated with your life transition that can impact your immune system. We then co-create a personalized plan integrating nutrition, natural movement, stress reduction, and sleep strategies to fortify your body's defenses and maintain your vitality through demanding periods.
What kind of vitality-building practices will I learn through Wellness Coaching?
As your Wellness Coach, you'll explore earth-inspired practices that build sustained energy, not just temporary boosts. This includes understanding the power of whole foods, gentle movement adapted to your new routines, mindfulness for mental clarity, and techniques to ensure restorative sleep, all contributing to a deep, natural sense of vigor.
Is this Wellness Coaching suitable if my life transition feels overwhelming right now?
Absolutely. As your Wellness Coach, my role is to provide a calm, supportive space where we can break down overwhelming feelings into manageable steps. We'll start with fundamental practices that are easy to implement, gradually building your capacity and confidence to navigate your transition with a stronger, more resilient self.
How long does a typical Wellness Coaching program for immune support last?
The duration of your Wellness Coaching program will be tailored to your specific needs and the nature of your life transition. Generally, clients find significant benefit from programs ranging from 3 to 6 months, allowing ample time to integrate new habits, observe their positive effects on immune function and vitality, and solidify lasting change.
