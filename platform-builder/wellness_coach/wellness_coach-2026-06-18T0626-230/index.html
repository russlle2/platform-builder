Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is 'trauma-informed' care in postpartum Wellness Coaching?
Trauma-informed care means I understand how past or birth-related trauma can impact your current emotional state. We focus on creating a safe space, promoting choice, and building coping skills, rather than re-traumatizing. Your comfort and agency are always prioritized.
How does Wellness Coaching differ from therapy for new mothers?
While both offer support, Wellness Coaching focuses on your present and future, helping you identify goals and build strategies for emotional resilience and self-care during postpartum. Therapy often delves deeper into past experiences to heal psychological wounds. We can discuss which approach aligns best with your needs.
I feel completely overwhelmed. Is Wellness Coaching right for me if I'm struggling intensely?
If you're experiencing severe distress, it's important to consult with a mental health professional first. Wellness Coaching is most effective when you're ready to engage in structured support to process emotions, develop coping mechanisms, and actively work towards emotional healing and reintegration of your identity after birth.
What specific emotional challenges does this Wellness Coaching address for new mothers?
We address feelings of isolation, identity shift, anxiety about mothering, resentment, guilt, difficulty bonding, and processing difficult birth experiences. Our focus is on helping you understand and navigate these complex emotions to foster a stronger, more resilient self.
