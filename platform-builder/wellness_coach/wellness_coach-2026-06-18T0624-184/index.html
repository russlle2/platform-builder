Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Wellness Coach specifically help my sleep when I'm recovering from chronic illness or injury?
As your Wellness Coach, I understand that your recovery brings unique sleep challenges. We'll work together to identify factors specific to your condition that disrupt your rest, and then implement gentle, tailored strategies for improving your sleep environment, evening routines, and daily energy management. The focus is always on supporting your recovery without adding stress.
I'm often fatigued. Will working with a Wellness Coach require a lot of energy?
Absolutely not. Our Wellness Coach approach is designed with your energy levels in mind. We prioritize small, manageable steps and never push you beyond your comfort zone. Our sessions are a supportive space for you to receive guidance and encouragement, not another demanding task. We focus on making improvements accessible, even on low-energy days.
What if I haven't tried 'wellness' things before? Is this Wellness Coach program for me?
Yes, completely! Our Wellness Coach practice is built for individuals just like you. No prior wellness experience is needed. We use clear, simple language and a gentle approach, ensuring everything is easy to understand and implement. You'll never feel like you're behind or that you should already know something. We meet you exactly where you are.
Will a Wellness Coach replace my medical care for my chronic illness or injury?
No, our Wellness Coach services are a complementary support, not a replacement for medical care. We work alongside your existing healthcare team to enhance your well-being, specifically focusing on improving your sleep and restorative rest. We always encourage you to discuss any changes or concerns with your doctor, and we can collaborate with them if needed.
