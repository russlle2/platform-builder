Frequently Asked Questions

How does Wellness Coaching specifically help with college student anxiety?
This Wellness Coaching focuses on nervous system regulation, which is key to managing anxiety. You'll learn techniques to downregulate your fight-or-flight response, build resilience against academic pressures, and cultivate a sense of inner calm, directly addressing the unique stressors you face.
What kind of 'ancient healing traditions' are incorporated into the coaching?
You'll explore practices rooted in traditions like yogic philosophy, mindfulness, and specific breathwork techniques that have been used for centuries to cultivate mental clarity and emotional balance. These are adapted for your modern college life to be accessible and impactful.
Will this Wellness Coaching take up too much of my already busy schedule?
The goal is to integrate simple, potent practices into your existing routine, not add more burden. You'll learn efficient techniques that can be done in minutes, offering powerful relief and better focus, ultimately saving you time and energy in the long run.
Is Wellness Coaching a substitute for therapy or medical treatment?
No, this Wellness Coaching is a complementary support system focused on stress reduction and nervous system regulation. It's not a replacement for medical or psychological treatment. If you have significant mental health concerns, you should consult with a qualified healthcare professional.
