What exactly does a Wellness Coach do for burnout?

As your Wellness Coach, I provide structured guidance and evidence-based techniques specifically designed to address the physiological and mental symptoms of burnout. We work on regulating your nervous system, reducing chronic stress, and building sustainable habits that support your long-term well-being and professional effectiveness.

Is this just another relaxation technique, or is it deeper?

It's much deeper. While relaxation is a component, we focus on understanding and actively shifting your nervous system's response to stress. This involves practical tools based on neuroscience, not just temporary fixes, to re-pattern your stress response and build resilience from the inside out.

How quickly can I expect to see results in managing my stress?

Many clients report feeling a difference within a few weeks as they implement the initial strategies for nervous system regulation. Sustainable change, however, typically unfolds over a few months as you integrate new habits and re-train your body's stress response. Consistency is key, and I'll support you every step of the way.

My schedule is packed; how does this fit into a busy professional life?

I understand your demands. Our programs are designed with the busy professional in mind, focusing on efficient, high-impact strategies that integrate seamlessly into your existing routine. We prioritize practical, time-sensitive techniques that yield significant results without adding more to your plate.
