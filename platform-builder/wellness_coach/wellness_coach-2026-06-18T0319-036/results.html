Understanding Stress in Chronic Recovery: Your Wellness Coach Approach

When your body is working hard to recover from chronic illness or injury, stress isn't just a mental state – it's a physiological burden. We'll identify specific stressors impacting your recovery and develop targeted strategies to minimize their effects on your nervous system. This isn't about ignoring symptoms; it's about actively managing the internal environment for better healing and function.
