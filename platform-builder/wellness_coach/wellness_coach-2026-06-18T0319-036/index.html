Frequently Asked Questions

How does a Wellness Coach specifically help with nervous system regulation during chronic recovery?
As your Wellness Coach, we focus on identifying the unique stressors inherent in chronic recovery that dysregulate your nervous system. We then co-create a personalized plan using science-backed techniques like paced activity, specific breathing practices, and environmental adjustments to help you shift towards a more regulated state, supporting your body's healing process.
Is this approach suitable if my chronic illness or injury is already well-managed medically?
Absolutely. Even with medical management, the persistent demands of chronic conditions can keep your nervous system in a heightened state. Our coaching complements medical care by addressing the stress response itself, helping you optimize your physiological environment for better overall function and quality of life, regardless of your medical status.
What kind of results can I expect from working with a Wellness Coach on stress reduction for recovery?
Clients typically report feeling a greater sense of calm and control, experiencing improved sleep quality, and noticing a reduction in symptom intensity related to stress. You'll gain practical strategies to manage daily demands, which often leads to more consistent energy levels and a more positive outlook on your recovery journey.
How is this different from traditional therapy or physical rehabilitation?
While therapy addresses mental health and rehabilitation focuses on physical function, Wellness Coaching for nervous system regulation bridges the gap by focusing on the physiological impact of stress on your recovery. We provide practical, present-focused strategies to re-pattern your body's stress response, complementing and enhancing the benefits you receive from other care providers.
