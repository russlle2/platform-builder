Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Wellness Coach help me when my children wake up frequently?
As your Wellness Coach, we'll explore your current routines and identify areas where small adjustments can make a big difference. We'll focus on strategies to improve your overall sleep quality, even with interruptions, and help you develop resilience for those inevitable wake-ups. This includes practical techniques for falling back asleep faster and managing daytime fatigue.
I'm always stressed about my to-do list; how can I actually relax enough to sleep?
Your Wellness Coach will work with you on specific techniques to quiet your mind before bed. We'll explore personalized strategies for managing the mental load, such as structured wind-down routines, gentle mindfulness practices, or effective planning methods that help you feel more prepared and less anxious when it's time to rest.
Will I have to make huge, disruptive changes to my family's schedule?
Absolutely not. As your Wellness Coach, we understand that your family life is already full. Our approach involves making gradual, sustainable changes that integrate smoothly into your existing routines. We prioritize strategies that are realistic for parents of young children, ensuring improvements to your sleep without causing unnecessary upheaval.
What if I've tried everything for sleep and nothing works?
It's understandable to feel discouraged. Your Wellness Coach provides a fresh perspective and personalized guidance. We'll delve deeper than generic advice, tailoring strategies specifically to your unique situation as a parent and the dynamics of your household. You'll receive consistent support and accountability to help you finally find the restorative rest you deserve.
