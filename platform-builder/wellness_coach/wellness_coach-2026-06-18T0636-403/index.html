Frequently Asked Questions

How can Wellness Coaching specifically help with my college anxiety?
Wellness Coaching provides you with personalized strategies to identify the root causes of your anxiety, develop effective coping mechanisms, and build resilience. We'll focus on practical techniques to manage stress before it overwhelms you, helping you maintain a clear head even during peak academic times.
Is Wellness Coaching only for students struggling academically?
Not at all! Wellness Coaching is for any college student who wants to enhance their mental clarity, improve focus, and proactively manage the pressures of university life. Whether you're aiming for better grades or just want to feel calmer and more in control, these tools are for you.
What's the difference between Wellness Coaching and therapy for college students?
While both can be beneficial, Wellness Coaching focuses on actionable strategies and skill-building to move you forward, addressing specific challenges like academic pressure and focus. Therapy often delves deeper into past experiences and mental health conditions. I can help you determine if coaching or therapy is the best fit for your current needs.
How long does a typical Wellness Coaching program last for college students?
The duration of your Wellness Coaching program is tailored to your individual goals and needs. Many students find significant progress within 8-12 weeks, as we work to implement new habits and strategies. We'll discuss a personalized plan during your initial consultation to ensure it fits your schedule and objectives.
