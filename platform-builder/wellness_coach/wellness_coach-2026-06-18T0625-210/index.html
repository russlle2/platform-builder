Frequently Asked Questions

What specifically does a Postpartum Wellness Coach address for new mothers?
As your Postpartum Wellness Coach, I specifically address the emotional and psychological aspects of your recovery. This includes navigating identity shifts, processing birth experiences, managing overwhelm, establishing healthy boundaries, and fostering emotional regulation during this unique life stage. My focus is on your internal well-being.
How is this Wellness Coaching different from therapy or traditional mental health support?
My Wellness Coaching provides a distinct, forward-looking partnership focused on developing coping strategies and building emotional resilience. While I offer trauma-informed care, it’s not a substitute for clinical therapy if you're experiencing a diagnosable mental health condition. My role as your Wellness Coach is to empower you with practical tools and compassionate support for your emotional recovery.
Will this Wellness Coaching help me if I'm feeling overwhelmed or anxious about motherhood?
Absolutely. Feeling overwhelmed or anxious is a common experience for new mothers. As your Wellness Coach, we'll explore the roots of these feelings, develop personalized strategies to manage them, and create a supportive framework for your emotional well-being. My approach is designed to provide you with clarity and calm.
What kind of commitment is required for Postpartum Wellness Coaching?
The commitment varies based on your individual needs and goals. We typically start with a series of weekly sessions to build momentum and then adjust frequency as you progress. Consistency is key to establishing new emotional patterns and integrating supportive practices into your daily life. We'll discuss a personalized plan during our initial consultation.
