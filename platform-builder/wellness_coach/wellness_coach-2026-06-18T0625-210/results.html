Understanding Postpartum Emotional Healing with Your Wellness Coach

The postpartum period brings profound changes, and your emotional landscape shifts significantly. As your Wellness Coach, I provide a safe space to process these experiences. We’ll gently explore feelings of overwhelm, anxiety, or sadness without judgment, establishing practices that support your psychological recovery and build resilience during this tender time.
