Frequently Asked Questions

I'm new to wellness. Is Wellness Coaching right for me?
Absolutely! Our Wellness Coaching is designed specifically for parents who might not have any prior wellness experience. We use simple, accessible language and practical steps that are easy to understand and integrate into your daily life. You don't need to be an expert to start feeling better and building your immune strength.
How can Wellness Coaching help my immune system when I'm always around sick kids?
It's true, kids bring home a lot! Our Wellness Coaching focuses on strengthening your body's natural defenses through consistent, gentle support. We'll explore small dietary adjustments, stress-reducing techniques, and sleep strategies that can significantly bolster your immune resilience, helping you ward off those common bugs or recover more quickly when you do get sick.
I barely have time to shower. How will I fit Wellness Coaching into my busy schedule?
We completely understand. Our Wellness Coaching is all about making things manageable. We focus on identifying small, impactful habits that take minimal time but yield significant results for your vitality. We won't add more to your plate; instead, we'll help you find efficiencies and gentle ways to nourish yourself within your existing routine.
What kind of results can I expect from Wellness Coaching for my vitality?
With consistent engagement in Wellness Coaching, you can expect to feel more consistently energized, less prone to the usual dips in energy, and generally more resilient to the daily demands of parenting. You might notice fewer sick days, better quality sleep, and a greater sense of calm and presence in your household.
