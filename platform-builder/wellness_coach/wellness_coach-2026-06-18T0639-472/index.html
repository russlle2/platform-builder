Frequently Asked Questions

How does Wellness Coaching specifically benefit an athlete's mental game?
My Wellness Coaching helps you develop a stronger mental game by focusing on cognitive strategies. We address areas like maintaining focus during high-stress moments, improving decision-making speed, and building resilience to overcome performance plateaus or physical discomfort.
What kind of mental clarity techniques will I learn as an athlete?
You'll learn practical techniques such as targeted visualization, mindfulness for improved presence, and strategies for filtering out distractions. These are tailored to your sport and personal challenges, designed to enhance your mental sharpness and maintain composure under pressure.
How can Wellness Coaching help me manage the physical stress of training and competition?
Beyond physical recovery, mental stress management is key. We'll work on techniques to reduce performance anxiety, improve sleep quality to aid physical recovery, and develop mental toughness to push through discomfort during intense training or competition, ensuring your mind supports your body's demands.
Is this Wellness Coaching only for professional athletes, or can active individuals benefit too?
My Wellness Coaching is designed for anyone leading an active lifestyle who wants to optimize their mental performance, from competitive athletes to dedicated fitness enthusiasts. If you're pushing your physical limits and want to sharpen your cognitive edge, this coaching is for you.
