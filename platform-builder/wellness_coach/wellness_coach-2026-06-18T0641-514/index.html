Frequently Asked Questions

How does Wellness Coaching specifically address recovery from chronic illness or injury?
Our Wellness Coaching is tailored to acknowledge the unique physical and emotional demands of your recovery. We focus on practices that build spiritual resilience and inner calm, helping you navigate discomfort, uncertainty, and changes in routine with greater equanimity. This allows for a gentle yet profound internal strengthening that complements your physical rehabilitation.
What kind of 'spiritual growth' does this Wellness Coaching support?
Spiritual growth, in this context, refers to cultivating a deeper sense of meaning, connection, and peace, regardless of your specific beliefs. It involves exploring your values, fostering self-compassion, and developing practices that help you feel grounded and purposeful, even when facing physical limitations or chronic conditions. It's about nurturing your inner world.
Is Wellness Coaching a substitute for medical treatment or therapy?
No, Wellness Coaching is a distinct supportive service and does not replace medical treatment from your healthcare providers or psychotherapy from a licensed mental health professional. Our role is to complement your existing care by focusing specifically on your spiritual well-being and inner peace during recovery. We encourage you to maintain open communication with your medical team.
How quickly can I expect to see changes in my inner peace or spiritual well-being?
The cultivation of inner peace and spiritual growth is a gradual and personal process. While some clients report feeling a greater sense of calm and clarity early on, lasting shifts typically develop over time with consistent engagement. Our goal is to equip you with sustainable practices that integrate into your daily life, fostering enduring strength and serenity.
