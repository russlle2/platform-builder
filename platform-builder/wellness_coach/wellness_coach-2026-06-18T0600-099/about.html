What exactly does a Wellness Coach do for parents dealing with stress?

As your Wellness Coach, I provide personalized strategies specifically designed for parents of young children. We focus on practical techniques to reduce your daily stress load and regulate your nervous system, so you can respond to parenting challenges with more calm and less overwhelm.

How is this different from traditional therapy for parental stress?

While therapy often delves into past experiences, my role as a Wellness Coach is forward-focused and action-oriented. We'll identify current stressors, build new habits, and implement immediate techniques to help you feel more grounded and capable in your day-to-day parenting life.

Will these strategies actually help me feel less stressed with my busy young children?

Absolutely. The techniques we explore are designed to be integrated into your existing routine, even with young children. We focus on small, consistent shifts that accumulate into significant changes in your nervous system's capacity to handle stress, leading to more peace for you and your family.

What if I don't have much free time for coaching sessions?

I understand that parents of young children have limited time. Our sessions are structured to be efficient and impactful, providing you with actionable steps you can implement right away. We'll find a schedule that genuinely supports your needs, not adds another burden.
