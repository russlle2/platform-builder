Frequently Asked Questions

What if I'm too exhausted to do even simple exercises?
We work with your current energy levels. Your first practice might be just noticing your feet on the floor while your baby nurses. No extra tasks—just retraining your nervous system in moments you're already in.
How is this different from 'mommy yoga' or meditation apps?
These focus on symptom management. We target the neurological pathways causing the stress. It's not 'sitting quietly'—it's training your body to stop reacting to your baby's cry as a danger signal. You'll see measurable shifts in your stress biomarkers.
Do I need to wait until my baby sleeps through the night?
No. The nervous system regulation happens *during* sleep disruptions. We build practices around your actual reality, not an idealized timeline. Your baby's 2 a.m. wake-up becomes part of your reset practice, not a stress trigger.
How long until I notice changes?
Most clients report calmer interactions within 7 days of starting. We track specific markers: reduced tension headaches, fewer panic moments during feedings, and the ability to take a breath before reacting to your baby's cry.
