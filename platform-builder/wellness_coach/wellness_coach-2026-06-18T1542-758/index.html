Frequently Asked Questions

What kind of postpartum challenges does your Wellness Coaching address?
My Wellness Coaching specifically supports new mothers navigating shifts in their relationships—with partners, family, friends, and themselves—during postpartum recovery. We focus on emotional well-being, communication, and reconnecting to your core self amidst new responsibilities.
Is Wellness Coaching suitable if I'm feeling overwhelmed by my new role?
Absolutely. Feeling overwhelmed is a common experience for new mothers. My Wellness Coaching offers a calm, supportive space to explore these feelings, develop coping strategies, and re-establish a sense of balance and purpose in your daily life, always with an eye on your relational health.
How is your Wellness Coaching different from therapy?
My Wellness Coaching focuses on your present and future, helping you identify goals, build resources, and create actionable steps for improved relational well-being and emotional grounding during postpartum. While it offers deep support, it is not a substitute for clinical therapy for mental health conditions.
What if my partner isn't interested in participating in the Wellness Coaching?
That’s perfectly fine. While your relationships are a primary focus, our Wellness Coaching is centered on supporting you. By strengthening your own emotional resilience, communication skills, and self-connection, you naturally create a positive ripple effect in all your relationships.
