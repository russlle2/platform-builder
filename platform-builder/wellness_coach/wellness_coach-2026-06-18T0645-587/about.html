What exactly does a Wellness Coach do for parents like me?

As your Wellness Coach, I provide gentle support and practical tools specifically designed to help parents of young children find more inner peace and spiritual grounding amidst household stress. We'll work together to integrate simple practices into your daily life that foster calm and resilience, without adding more to your already full plate.

Do I need any prior spiritual or wellness experience to work with a Wellness Coach?

Absolutely not! My Wellness Coach approach is designed for everyone, especially those new to focusing on their inner well-being. We’ll start with simple, accessible concepts and build from there, ensuring you feel comfortable and empowered every step of the way. No prior experience is needed, just a willingness to explore.

How can a Wellness Coach help me with the daily stresses of parenting?

Working with a Wellness Coach means we’ll identify the specific stressors you face and create personalized, gentle strategies to help you respond with more patience and less overwhelm. This isn't about eliminating stress, but about cultivating an inner calm that allows you to navigate the ups and downs of family life with greater ease and spiritual fortitude.

What kind of time commitment does Wellness Coach support require?

I understand your time is precious. Our Wellness Coach sessions are structured to be efficient and impactful, focusing on small, consistent actions that fit into your busy schedule. We’ll find practical ways to integrate moments of peace and spiritual connection without feeling like another chore, making lasting change sustainable for you.
