Frequently Asked Questions

Will this work if my symptoms are severe?
Yes. Protocols start exactly where your nervous system is today. We measure your baseline stress markers before introducing techniques, ensuring every step aligns with your current physiology. You’ll never be asked to 'push through' pain.
How is this different from therapy or meditation?
Therapy explores the past; meditation asks you to manage present feelings. We regulate your nervous system in real-time using physiological data. You’ll learn concrete tools to intercept stress before it becomes pain, without needing to 'feel' better first.
Do you work with autoimmune conditions?
Absolutely. We’ve helped over 200 clients with autoimmune conditions by targeting the nervous system's role in inflammation. Your coach tailors protocols to your specific condition’s stress triggers, using data to avoid flare-up risks.
What if I can’t commit to daily practice?
We build regulation into your existing routine. Your protocol includes 2-3 minute 'nervous system resets' you can do while waiting for coffee or before bed. No extra time needed — just integrated into your day without adding pressure.
