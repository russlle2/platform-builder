Frequently Asked Questions

How does Wellness Coaching specifically help athletes manage physical stress?
Physical stress in athletes often has emotional components. Our Wellness Coaching helps you identify and release the underlying emotional tension that can manifest as chronic pain, burnout, or performance plateaus. By addressing these emotional roots, you can improve your body's ability to recover and perform optimally.
What does trauma-informed care mean for active individuals?
Trauma-informed care means we understand that past challenging experiences, even non-athletic ones, can impact your present physical and emotional responses. We create a safe space for you to explore these influences without judgment, helping you build resilience and develop healthier ways of responding to stress, both on and off the field.
Will Wellness Coaching interfere with my physical training regimen?
Absolutely not. Our Wellness Coaching complements your physical training by enhancing your mental and emotional fortitude. When your mind is clearer and less burdened by stress, your body can respond more effectively to training, leading to better focus, faster recovery, and a reduced risk of injury due to mental fatigue.
How quickly can I expect to see improvements in my performance and well-being through Wellness Coaching?
The timeline for improvement varies for everyone. However, many athletes and active individuals report experiencing greater mental clarity, reduced stress, and enhanced emotional resilience within a few sessions. Consistent engagement with the coaching process will lead to sustainable changes in your performance and overall well-being.
