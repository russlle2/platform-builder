Frequently Asked Questions

How is this Wellness Coach program different from general stress management?
Our program is specifically designed for working professionals aged 30-45 experiencing burnout. We go beyond general advice, focusing on the physiological mechanisms of your nervous system and providing targeted, science-backed strategies for regulation that fit your high-demand lifestyle.
I'm already short on time. How will I fit Wellness Coach sessions into my schedule?
We understand your time is valuable. Our sessions are structured to be efficient and impactful. We focus on integrating practical, brief techniques into your existing routine, rather than adding overwhelming new commitments. The goal is to create sustainable change without further burdening your schedule.
What kind of results can I realistically expect from working with a Wellness Coach?
Clients typically report a significant reduction in chronic fatigue, improved sleep quality, enhanced emotional resilience, and a greater ability to manage workplace stress without feeling constantly overwhelmed. Our focus is on building robust nervous system health for lasting change.
Is Wellness Coaching a substitute for therapy or medical advice?
No, our Wellness Coaching services are not a substitute for medical or psychological treatment. We complement professional care by providing strategic guidance and practical tools for stress reduction and nervous system regulation, working within the scope of wellness and performance.
