Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is Wellness Coaching for seniors?
Wellness Coaching for seniors, especially at Sage Garden, is personalized guidance focused on gentle methods to reduce stress and regulate your nervous system. It's about providing you with practical tools and support to cultivate a greater sense of calm and well-being in your everyday life, tailored specifically for those aged 60 and above.
How does this Wellness Coaching help with stress reduction?
Our Wellness Coaching helps with stress reduction by introducing you to gentle techniques like mindful breathing, guided relaxation, and personalized daily routines. These practices are designed to calm your 'fight or flight' response, promoting a more balanced nervous system and helping you feel more at ease and less overwhelmed by daily pressures.
Is the approach gentle enough for someone with limited mobility?
Absolutely. The Wellness Coaching we offer is designed to be very gentle and adaptable to your physical capabilities. We prioritize your comfort and safety, and all practices, whether they involve movement or stillness, can be modified to suit your specific needs and ensure a comfortable, beneficial experience.
What kind of results can I expect from Sage Garden Wellness Coaching?
With consistent engagement in our Wellness Coaching, you can expect to experience a greater sense of inner calm, improved sleep quality, and increased resilience to daily stressors. Many clients report feeling more centered, having a clearer mind, and enjoying a more peaceful and fulfilling daily life.
