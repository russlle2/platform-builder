Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Wellness Coach specifically help with mental clarity after chronic illness or injury?
As your Wellness Coach, I'll help you pinpoint specific cognitive challenges like brain fog, memory issues, or difficulty concentrating. We'll then create tailored strategies using evidence-informed techniques to improve focus, reduce mental fatigue, and enhance your overall cognitive function. It's about providing structured support for your mental recovery.
Is Wellness Coaching suitable if I'm still actively recovering from an injury or illness?
Absolutely. My Wellness Coach approach is designed to complement your existing medical care. We'll focus on the mental and cognitive aspects of your recovery, working within your current limitations to build mental resilience, manage energy levels, and sharpen your thinking processes without overexerting you. It's a supportive, not strenuous, process.
What kind of commitment is required for Wellness Coaching focused on cognitive wellness?
Commitment primarily involves engaging actively in our sessions and consistently applying the strategies we develop together. As your Wellness Coach, I'll provide guidance and accountability, but the real progress comes from your dedication to integrating new habits into your daily routine. We'll start at a pace that feels comfortable for you.
Will Wellness Coaching interfere with my current medical treatments or therapies?
No, Wellness Coaching is a complementary support system. As your Wellness Coach, I always encourage open communication with your healthcare providers. We won't prescribe treatments or medications; instead, we'll focus on lifestyle adjustments, cognitive exercises, and mindset shifts that enhance your overall well-being and mental sharpness alongside your medical care.
