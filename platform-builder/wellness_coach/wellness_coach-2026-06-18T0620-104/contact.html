Understanding Your Need for Rest: A Wellness Coach's Approach to Chronic Illness Recovery

Living with chronic illness or recovering from injury can profoundly disrupt your sleep, making each day feel like an uphill battle. As your Wellness Coach, I understand these unique challenges. We won't just talk about sleep; we'll gently explore how your body's specific needs, daily routines, and even your thoughts impact your ability to truly rest. My aim is to help you feel heard, understood, and supported as we build strategies for more peaceful nights.
