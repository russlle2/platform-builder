Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly does a Wellness Coach do for parents feeling overwhelmed?
As your Wellness Coach, I help you untangle the mental chaos that often comes with parenting. We work on practical strategies to improve your mental clarity, focus, and ability to manage stress effectively, so you can feel more in control of your thoughts and your day.
I'm already short on time; how can Wellness Coaching fit into my busy schedule?
I completely understand! My Wellness Coaching sessions are designed to be efficient and effective. We focus on small, impactful changes that integrate seamlessly into your existing routine, rather than adding more pressure. Our goal is to simplify, not complicate, your life.
Do I need any special knowledge or experience to benefit from your Wellness Coaching?
Absolutely not! My Wellness Coaching is for everyone, especially parents who are new to focusing on their own mental well-being. There's no jargon or complex theories, just practical, gentle guidance to help you develop a clearer, calmer mind.
How will Wellness Coaching specifically help with my mental focus and memory as a parent?
We'll explore techniques to reduce mental overwhelm, improve your ability to concentrate on one task at a time, and create systems that support your memory. This means less 'mom brain' moments and more confidence in your daily planning and interactions.
