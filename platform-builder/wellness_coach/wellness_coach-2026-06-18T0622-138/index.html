Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Wellness Coach help my sleep when I'm going through a big life change?
Major transitions often disrupt circadian rhythms and introduce new stressors that interfere with sleep. As your Wellness Coach, I'll work with you to identify these specific challenges, develop personalized strategies based on sleep science, and implement routines to stabilize your sleep patterns. My focus is on making sleep a reliable anchor during times of change.
Is this just about 'getting more sleep,' or is there more to your Wellness Coach approach?
It's about far more than just hours. Our focus is on restorative rest – the quality of your sleep, not just the quantity. We'll explore factors like sleep efficiency, sleep environment, and daytime habits, all tailored to your unique circumstances. As your Wellness Coach, I help you optimize every aspect of your sleep to maximize its rejuvenating power, especially when your life is in flux.
I've tried sleep apps and advice before. How is working with a Wellness Coach different?
Unlike generic advice, my Wellness Coach approach is highly individualized. We don't just apply a one-size-fits-all solution. We delve into the specifics of your life transition, your personal biology, and your daily routine to craft a strategy that truly fits. This personalized, evidence-based guidance offers a level of support and accountability that self-help resources often cannot.
What kind of commitment is involved in working with a Wellness Coach for sleep improvement?
The commitment varies based on your needs, but typically involves a structured initial period of regular sessions, followed by check-ins. As your Wellness Coach, I encourage consistent effort in implementing new habits. My goal is to equip you with sustainable practices so you can maintain restorative sleep independently long after our coaching concludes, especially as you navigate future transitions.
