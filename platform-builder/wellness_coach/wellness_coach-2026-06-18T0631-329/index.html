Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is 'somatic healing' and how does it help with burnout?
Somatic healing focuses on the mind-body connection, specifically how stress and burnout manifest as physical pain and tension. Through guided techniques, you learn to identify and release these stored patterns in your body, leading to tangible relief from chronic aches, stiffness, and the overall physical exhaustion associated with burnout. It's about addressing the root physical impact, not just the mental.
I'm a busy professional. How do I fit Wellness Coaching into my already packed schedule?
We understand your time is valuable. Our Wellness Coaching sessions are designed to be efficient and highly impactful. We focus on practical, actionable strategies you can integrate into your existing routine, often requiring only short, targeted practices. The goal is to provide tools that reduce your overall physical burden, ultimately freeing up your energy and time, not adding more to your plate.
Is this just another form of therapy or massage?
No, Wellness Coaching is distinct. While it complements therapy and can enhance the benefits of massage, we don't diagnose or treat clinical conditions. Our focus is on education and empowerment: teaching you how to understand and regulate your body's responses to stress and burnout, specifically through somatic practices. You'll gain self-management skills for physical pain relief, rather than relying solely on external treatments.
How quickly can I expect to feel physical relief from my burnout symptoms?
While individual results vary, many clients report a noticeable reduction in tension and an increased sense of physical ease within the first few sessions. Our programs are structured to provide immediate relief while also building sustainable practices for long-term well-being. The more consistently you engage with the techniques, the faster and more profound your physical improvements will be.
