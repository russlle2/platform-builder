Life with a newborn means lots of bending, lifting, and holding. Learning to move with thoughtful body awareness can make a huge difference in your physical comfort and energy levels. Understanding Your New Body: A Wellness Coach's Approach for New Moms

Pregnancy and birth bring significant shifts to your core and pelvic floor. It's completely normal for your posture to feel different, and for some movements to be less comfortable than before. My Wellness Coaching methodology centers on simple, practical strategies that fit into your busy life as a new mother. We'll explore gentle techniques to support your core, ease back tension, and help you feel more grounded and capable in your daily activities.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does a Wellness Coach for postpartum posture actually do?
As your Wellness Coach, I provide personalized guidance to help you understand and support your body during postpartum recovery. We'll explore comfortable posture, gentle movements, and practical ways to reduce strain from everyday activities like feeding and carrying your baby, helping you feel more balanced and at ease.
Is Wellness Coaching suitable for me if I'm still feeling very tired as a new mother?
Absolutely. My Wellness Coaching approach is designed to be gentle and adaptable to your energy levels as a new mother. We focus on small, manageable steps that won't add extra stress, but instead offer support and comfort. We prioritize rest and integrate changes into your current routine, honoring your need for recovery.
How is this different from physical therapy for postpartum recovery?
While physical therapy often addresses specific injuries or dysfunctions, as a Wellness Coach, I focus on general body awareness, comfortable posture, and functional movement for overall well-being during your postpartum period. We work on building sustainable habits that support your body's natural healing and strength in your daily life as a new mother.
Will Wellness Coaching help me with back pain or discomfort from breastfeeding?
Yes, many new mothers find relief by improving their body awareness and posture, especially during breastfeeding. As your Wellness Coach, we'll identify common positions that might contribute to discomfort and explore simple adjustments to help you find more supportive and comfortable ways to feed your baby, easing tension in your back and shoulders.
