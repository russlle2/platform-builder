Wellness Coach

Gentle Guidance for Your Postpartum Posture as a New Mother

Your body has done something incredible, and now it deserves nurturing support. As a Wellness Coach, I'm here to help you gently realign with your post-baby body. We'll focus on understanding how everyday movements – from feeding your baby to lifting them – impact your comfort and stability. It's about building awareness without pressure, honoring where your body is right now, and finding ways to move with greater ease and less strain.
