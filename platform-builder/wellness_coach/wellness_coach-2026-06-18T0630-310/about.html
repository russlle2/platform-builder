Targeted Wellness Coaching for Postpartum Physical Recovery

Your body has undergone a profound experience, and it's natural to feel new aches, strains, or persistent discomfort. Our Wellness Coaching focuses specifically on addressing the physical aftermath of childbirth, from pelvic floor tension to back pain and overall muscular fatigue. We develop personalized strategies rooted in somatic healing principles to help you regain comfort and strength, respecting your body's current state.

What kind of physical pain can a Wellness Coach help with during postpartum recovery?

As your Wellness Coach, I specialize in addressing common postpartum physical discomforts such as pelvic floor tension, lower back pain, hip discomfort, neck and shoulder strain from feeding or carrying your baby, and wrist pain (often called 'mommy thumb'). We focus on understanding the root causes and developing gentle, effective strategies for relief.

Is somatic healing suitable for all new mothers, even if I feel overwhelmed?

Yes, somatic healing is particularly gentle and supportive, making it ideal for new mothers who may feel overwhelmed. It doesn't require intense physical exertion. Instead, it guides you to tune into your body's sensations at your own pace, helping to release tension and find calm without adding more stress. Your comfort and safety are always the priority.

How does Wellness Coaching for postpartum pain differ from physical therapy?

While physical therapy often focuses on diagnosis and rehabilitation of specific injuries, Wellness Coaching for postpartum pain complements this by providing broader support for your overall physical comfort and somatic healing. We focus on daily practices, body awareness, and stress reduction techniques that empower you to manage and prevent discomfort in your everyday life as a new mother.

How quickly can I expect to feel a difference with this type of Wellness Coaching?

The timeline for feeling a difference varies for everyone, as each postpartum recovery is unique. However, many new mothers report experiencing initial relief from acute discomfort and increased body awareness within a few sessions. Consistent practice of the strategies we develop together will lead to more sustained improvements in your physical comfort and well-being over time.
