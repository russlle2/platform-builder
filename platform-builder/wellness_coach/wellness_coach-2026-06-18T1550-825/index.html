Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly does a Wellness Coach focusing on postural health offer for parents?
Your Wellness Coach provides personalized guidance to help you understand how your body moves and holds tension throughout your busy parenting day. We explore gentle adjustments, mindful breathing, and ancient-inspired techniques to alleviate common aches and promote a sense of grounded strength, making daily tasks like lifting children or managing chores feel less strenuous.
How can this Wellness Coaching fit into my already packed schedule as a parent?
We understand your time is precious. Your Wellness Coach sessions are designed to be flexible and integrate easily into your life. We focus on practical, short practices you can weave into your daily routines – a few minutes while you're waiting for water to boil, or a quick re-alignment during naptime. It's about sustainable habits, not adding another chore.
Is this Wellness Coach approach suitable if I'm not very flexible or haven't exercised in a while?
Absolutely. This Wellness Coach practice is for every body, regardless of current fitness level or flexibility. We start exactly where you are, gently exploring your body's current patterns. The focus isn't on pushing limits, but on cultivating awareness and ease, helping you build strength and fluidity at your own pace, with compassion and support.
Beyond physical relief, what other benefits can I expect from working with a Wellness Coach?
Many parents find that by addressing physical tension and improving body awareness, they also experience a deeper sense of calm and mental clarity. Your Wellness Coach helps you connect with your inner resources, fostering resilience and a more soulful presence in your daily life, which can positively impact how you manage stress and interact with your family.
