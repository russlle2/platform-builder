Frequently Asked Questions

What if I'm not ready for big changes yet?
You don't need to be 'ready.' Your nervous system is already working to keep you safe—our job is to help it relax. We start where you are, with tools for *today*, not some future 'perfect' version of you.
How is this different from therapy?
We focus specifically on *physical sensation* and *immediate* regulation—not exploring past trauma. Think of us as your nervous system's coach: 'Hey, your shoulders are tight. Let's do this 1-minute move to reset before your next call.'
Do I need to be 'well' to start?
Absolutely not. If you're feeling overwhelmed, numb, or constantly 'on edge' during a major shift—whether you're 22 or 62—this is for you. We honor all your current experiences, not idealized versions.
What if I can't commit to weekly sessions?
We build flexibility into the work. Many clients start with one 20-minute check-in per month to learn one new tool. Your nervous system doesn't need complex plans—it needs simple, repeatable moments of safety you can own.
