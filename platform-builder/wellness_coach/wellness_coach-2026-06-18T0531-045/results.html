Why Life Transitions Hijack Your Body

When you're navigating divorce, a career pivot, or grief, your nervous system gets stuck in 'alert mode.' You might feel shaky, exhausted, or unable to sleep—not because you're broken, but because your body remembers stress as a threat. We focus on the *actual* mechanics: how to gently shift from 'fight-or-flight' to 'rest-and-digest' with techniques that fit into your already-full life.
