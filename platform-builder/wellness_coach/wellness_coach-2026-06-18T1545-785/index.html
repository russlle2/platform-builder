Frequently Asked Questions

I'm always so busy with my kids. How can Wellness Coaching fit into my packed schedule?
I understand that your time is precious. Our sessions are designed to be flexible and efficient, focusing on practical tools you can integrate into your existing routine. We'll prioritize what feels most supportive for your family right now, ensuring the coaching adds ease, not more pressure, to your life.
My household stress often feels overwhelming. Can Wellness Coaching really help with that?
Yes, absolutely. We'll gently explore the roots of your household stress and develop heart-centered strategies to manage daily pressures. The aim isn't to eliminate all stress, but to equip you with tools to respond more calmly and thoughtfully, creating more moments of peace for you and your family.
What does 'relationship healing' mean for a busy parent like me?
For parents, relationship healing means finding kinder, more effective ways to connect with your children and partner, even when things are tough. It's about mending small rifts, fostering understanding, and strengthening the bonds that make your family feel like a safe and loving haven. We'll focus on simple shifts that make a big difference in how you interact every day.
I've never tried wellness coaching before. Is this right for someone without experience?
You don't need any prior wellness experience at all! My approach is gentle and accessible, designed specifically for parents who are new to this kind of support. We'll go at your pace, focusing on what feels comfortable and helpful for you, making sure you feel understood and supported every step of the way.
