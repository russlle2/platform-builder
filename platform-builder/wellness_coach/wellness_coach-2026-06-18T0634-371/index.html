Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly does a Wellness Coach do for seniors experiencing physical pain?
As your Wellness Coach, I work with you one-on-one to understand your specific physical discomforts and develop a gentle, personalized plan. This includes guided somatic practices and movements designed to alleviate pain, improve mobility, and increase body awareness, all tailored to your unique needs and comfort level as a senior.
Is somatic healing safe and effective for someone my age with existing conditions?
Yes, absolutely. Somatic healing is a very gentle and respectful approach. We always work within your comfort zone, focusing on mindful movements and self-awareness rather than strenuous exercise. It's designed to be safe and highly effective for seniors, even with various physical conditions, helping to reduce pain and improve quality of life.
How quickly can I expect to feel results from working with a Wellness Coach for pain relief?
While individual experiences vary, many clients report feeling a greater sense of ease and reduced discomfort within a few sessions. Our focus is on sustainable, long-term improvement, so consistent practice of the gentle techniques you learn will progressively build your comfort and mobility over time.
Do I need any special equipment or prior experience to begin your Wellness Coach programs?
No special equipment is needed to start. All our practices are designed to be accessible and can often be done from the comfort of your own home with minimal space. No prior experience with somatic work or specific exercises is required; we'll guide you every step of the way.
