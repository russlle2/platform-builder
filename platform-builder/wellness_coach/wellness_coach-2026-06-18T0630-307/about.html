How can a Wellness Coach specifically help with the physical pain I feel as a parent?

As your Wellness Coach, I focus on the unique physical demands of parenting – from carrying toddlers to sleepless nights. We'll identify the root causes of your specific aches and pains, then create practical, gentle strategies for relief that fit into your busy life. This isn't about adding another chore; it's about integrating self-care for lasting comfort.

I'm so busy; how would I even fit Wellness Coach sessions into my schedule?

I completely understand the time crunch parents face! My Wellness Coach programs are designed with flexibility in mind. We'll work together to find session times that suit you, and the practices we develop are often quick and easy to incorporate into your day, even during naptime or while supervising playtime. Small, consistent efforts lead to significant improvements.

What exactly is 'somatic healing' and how does it relate to my parenting stress?

Somatic healing, in the context of my Wellness Coach practice, means tuning into your body's sensations to release stored stress and tension. Parenting is demanding, and your body often holds onto that stress as shoulder pain, tight hips, or general fatigue. We'll use gentle body awareness techniques to help you release these physical manifestations of stress, leading to a calmer mind and a more comfortable body.

Will this really help with my constant back pain from lifting my children?

Absolutely. Many parents experience back pain from the constant lifting, bending, and carrying involved in childcare. As your Wellness Coach, I'll help you understand your body mechanics, introduce ergonomic adjustments for daily tasks, and teach you specific movements and stretches to alleviate and prevent further strain. The goal is to reduce your pain and increase your physical capacity for parenting.
