Releasing the Physical Load of Parenting: A Wellness Coach Approach

Daily life with young children often means lifting, bending, and not enough time for yourself. This physical strain can build into chronic discomfort. My Wellness Coach programs focus on gentle, effective techniques to ease your back pain, shoulder tension, and overall body fatigue, so you can move freely and feel more present with your little ones. We'll explore strategies to integrate relief into your busy schedule.
