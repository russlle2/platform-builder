Frequently Asked Questions

How does Wellness Coaching specifically help with college anxiety and academic pressure?
Your Wellness Coach provides bespoke strategies that directly address the specific stressors of college life. We work on precise emotional regulation techniques, cognitive restructuring for academic challenges, and building resilient internal frameworks so you can approach your studies with clarity and confidence, not just manage symptoms.
Is your approach truly trauma-informed, and what does that mean for me as a college student?
Yes, our approach is deeply trauma-informed. This means your Wellness Coach understands that past experiences, even subtle ones, can impact your present reactions to stress and pressure. We prioritize safety, choice, collaboration, trustworthiness, and empowerment, creating an environment where you can explore and reprocess emotional responses without judgment, fostering genuine healing and resilience.
How is this different from traditional therapy or academic advising?
Your Wellness Coach offers a distinct, forward-moving partnership focused on emotional re-education and strategic skill-building for your current and future success. While therapy often delves into deep psychological analysis and academic advising focuses on coursework, we blend emotional processing with practical application, empowering you to actively recalibrate your responses to academic and personal challenges, ensuring you're thriving, not just surviving.
What kind of commitment is required for Wellness Coaching to be effective?
Effective Wellness Coaching requires your genuine engagement and a willingness to explore new perspectives. Your commitment to consistent sessions and applying the tailored strategies we develop together is key. While individual timelines vary, clients typically experience significant shifts within a few weeks of dedicated partnership with their Wellness Coach, laying the groundwork for sustained emotional well-being and academic excellence.
