Your Body's Anchor: Wellness Coaching Through Transition

When life shifts dramatically – a new career, a move, a family change – your body can feel disconnected or stressed. This Wellness Coaching focuses on guiding you to reconnect with your physical self, understanding how your posture and movement habits impact your emotional state. We'll work together to build a sense of groundedness, helping you carry yourself with ease and confidence through every new experience.
