Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Wellness Coach specializing in posture help me after chronic illness or injury?
After chronic illness or injury, your body might have developed compensatory patterns. A specialized Wellness Coach helps you gently re-establish healthy postural habits and body awareness, supporting your recovery and reducing the likelihood of future discomfort or strain. We focus on practical, sustainable changes for your daily life.
Is this Wellness Coaching suitable if my mobility is currently limited?
Absolutely. Our approach is highly individualized. We start exactly where you are, respecting your current physical capacity. The focus is on small, controlled movements and increasing your body awareness within your safe and comfortable range, gradually building towards greater stability and ease.
What specific aspects of body awareness will I develop with a Wellness Coach?
You'll learn to identify subtle cues your body sends, recognize patterns of tension, and understand how your posture impacts your overall comfort. We'll guide you in sensing your alignment, engaging appropriate muscles, and moving with greater intention and control, rather than relying on old habits that might be hindering your recovery.
How long does it typically take to see improvements in postural health through Wellness Coaching?
Progress varies for everyone, as recovery from chronic illness or injury is unique. However, most clients report feeling more connected to their bodies and noticing improved comfort and stability within a few weeks of consistent practice. Sustained results come from ongoing engagement and integration of these principles into your daily routine.
