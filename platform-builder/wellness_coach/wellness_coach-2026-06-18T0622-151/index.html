Frequently Asked Questions

What makes your Wellness Coaching unique for seniors seeking sleep improvement?
Our approach is entirely dedicated to the specific needs of individuals aged 60 and above. We understand that sleep patterns change, and our methods are gentle, non-invasive, and focus on creating a supportive environment for your body's natural rest cycles, ensuring your comfort and safety are always paramount.
How quickly can I expect to see improvements in my sleep quality?
While individual experiences vary, many of our clients begin to notice subtle, positive shifts in their sleep patterns within a few weeks. Our focus is on sustainable, lasting improvement, not just immediate changes, so we work with you to build habits that contribute to consistently better rest over time.
Do I need to make drastic lifestyle changes to improve my sleep with your coaching?
Absolutely not. Our philosophy emphasizes gentle, manageable adjustments rather than radical overhauls. We collaborate with you to identify small, impactful changes that fit seamlessly into your existing routine, ensuring the process is enjoyable and never overwhelming, respecting your pace and preferences.
Is your Wellness Coaching suitable if I have specific health concerns?
Our coaching complements your medical care. We always encourage you to discuss any new wellness initiatives with your primary care physician. Our role as a Wellness Coach is to support your natural ability to rest and thrive, working gently within the framework of your health needs.
