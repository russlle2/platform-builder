Frequently Asked Questions

How does Wellness Coaching specifically address recovery from chronic illness or injury?
Our Wellness Coaching centers on the unique emotional and psychological challenges that arise during recovery from chronic illness or injury. We help you navigate grief, frustration, identity shifts, and the impact on your relationships, providing tools to build emotional resilience and a supportive community around you. It's about acknowledging the full scope of your experience, not just the physical aspects.
Is your Wellness Coaching suitable if I'm still actively receiving medical treatment?
Absolutely. Our Wellness Coaching complements your medical treatment by focusing on your emotional well-being and mental strength. We work alongside your healthcare team, never replacing medical advice, to help you cope with treatment demands, manage stress, and maintain a positive outlook. Our role is to provide emotional support and strategies that empower you during your ongoing medical care.
What does 'trauma-informed' mean in the context of your Wellness Coaching?
Trauma-informed in our Wellness Coaching means we recognize that chronic illness or injury can lead to traumatic experiences. We create a safe, predictable, and empowering environment, understanding that past experiences can influence your present reactions. Our approach avoids re-traumatization and instead fosters choice, collaboration, and trust, allowing you to process emotions at your own pace and feel truly heard.
How can Wellness Coaching help me reconnect with my community after a long recovery period?
Our Wellness Coaching helps you gently rebuild social connections by addressing anxieties around re-engagement and developing strategies for managing energy and social interactions. We focus on building confidence, setting healthy boundaries, and finding supportive groups or activities that align with your current abilities and interests, helping you feel less isolated and more connected to others.
