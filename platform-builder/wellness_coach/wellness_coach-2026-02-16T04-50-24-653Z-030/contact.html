How coaching works

I combine three complementary practices to help you form consistent habits and see results within weeks.

  • Baseline mapping: identify current rhythms and energy windows.
  • Micro-habits: design 1–3 actions that take 2–10 minutes each day.
  • Weekly calibration: adapt strategies based on what actually worked.

Common questions and concerns

"I don't have time — will this demand hours per week?"
No. The approach favors micro-practices that sit inside your existing routine. Expect 10–30 minutes per day depending on the path you choose.
"How is this different from therapy or medical care?"
Coaching focuses on behavior, systems, and habit change. It is not a substitute for mental health or medical treatment; where needed, I collaborate with healthcare professionals.
"What if I miss sessions?"
We build resilient plans that tolerate missed days. Check-ins ensure momentum even with imperfect attendance.
