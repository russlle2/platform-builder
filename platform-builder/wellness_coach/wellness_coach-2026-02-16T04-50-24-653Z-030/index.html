How we work

A repeatable, transparent process you can see from day one.

  1. Assess: Intake that maps routines, friction points and priorities.
  2. Design: A compact roadmap of 3–6 habits with measurement and checkpoints.
  3. Practice: Short weekly sessions, micro-assignments, and in-between accountability.
  4. Refine: Data-informed adjustments until the habit fits your life.

Common concerns answered

Will this work if I’m busy?

Yes. Programs prioritize micro-habits and time-efficient checks so you make progress without disrupting obligations.

Is this medical advice?

No. Coaching focuses on habits, routines and accountability—not diagnosis or medical treatment.

What if I’ve tried coaching before?

We start from lessons learned and build a different structure: measurement, tiny experiments, and pacing that avoids burnout.
