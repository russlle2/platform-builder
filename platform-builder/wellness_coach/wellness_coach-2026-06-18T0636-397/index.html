Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Wellness Coach help with physical pain when I'm already seeing doctors?
While doctors address specific diagnoses, as your Wellness Coach, I focus on the holistic picture of how your lifestyle, stress levels, and emotional state contribute to your physical pain. We explore somatic practices and body awareness techniques that complement medical treatments, helping you understand and alleviate the underlying tension that often exacerbates or causes discomfort in parents of young children.
I'm a busy parent; how can I fit Wellness Coaching into my schedule?
I understand your time is precious. My Wellness Coach approach emphasizes practical, short, and effective practices that can be integrated seamlessly into your daily routine. We'll work together to find moments for self-care and pain relief that fit your family's schedule, ensuring you get the support you need without adding more stress to your plate.
What kind of 'somatic healing' does a Wellness Coach offer for parents?
Somatic healing with your Wellness Coach means learning to tune into your body's sensations and release stored tension. For parents, this might involve gentle movement, breathwork, or guided relaxation to address common issues like neck stiffness from holding babies, back pain from lifting, or overall fatigue. It's about empowering you to self-regulate and find relief through your body's innate wisdom.
Is this just about relaxation, or will I see real changes in my pain levels with a Wellness Coach?
While relaxation is a beneficial outcome, our work as your Wellness Coach goes deeper. We aim for tangible, sustainable reductions in your physical pain and discomfort. By addressing the root causes of tension and providing you with practical tools, you'll learn to manage and alleviate pain effectively, leading to a noticeable improvement in your physical well-being and ability to navigate daily parental demands.
