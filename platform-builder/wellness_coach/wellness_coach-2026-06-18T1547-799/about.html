How can a Wellness Coach help with posture for seniors?

As your Wellness Coach, I focus on gentle, personalized guidance. We'll work together on specific, accessible movements and awareness exercises tailored to your comfort level, helping to improve your posture, balance, and reduce stiffness, all without strenuous activity.

What does 'body awareness' mean in the context of Wellness Coaching for seniors?

Body awareness for seniors means gently learning to notice how your body feels and moves. It's about understanding your natural alignment and making small, conscious adjustments to improve comfort, stability, and reduce the risk of falls, all in a supportive, nurturing way.

Is this type of Wellness Coaching suitable if I have existing joint discomfort?

Absolutely. My approach as a Wellness Coach is always gentle and adapted to your individual needs and any existing discomfort. We prioritize safety and comfort, focusing on movements that support and soothe your joints, rather than strain them. Always consult your doctor for any new exercise program.

How long does it take to see improvements in posture and comfort with a Wellness Coach?

Improvements can vary, but many of my clients begin to notice small positive shifts in their posture and overall comfort within a few sessions. Consistency with our gentle practices is key, and we celebrate every step forward, no matter how small, as your Wellness Coach.
