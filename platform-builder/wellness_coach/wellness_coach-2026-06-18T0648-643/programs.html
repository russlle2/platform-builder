Fortify Your Defenses: Wellness Coaching for Immune Resilience

College is a melting pot of new experiences and sometimes, new bugs! When anxiety and academic pressure are high, your immune system can take a hit. Our Wellness Coaching focuses on practical strategies to bolster your body's natural defenses, so you can spend less time feeling sick and more time thriving. We'll explore nutrition, sleep patterns, and stress-reduction techniques specifically designed for your busy student schedule, helping you stay well year-round.
