Frequently Asked Questions

How can Wellness Coaching help me specifically with a major life transition?
Wellness Coaching at is tailored to support your emotional needs during significant life changes. We help you process the feelings that arise, identify healthy coping strategies, and build resilience to navigate uncertainty. Our focus is on providing trauma-informed guidance to help you integrate your experiences and move forward with clarity and strength, rather than feeling overwhelmed.
What does 'trauma-informed care' mean in the context of your Wellness Coaching?
Trauma-informed care means that we understand and acknowledge the widespread impact of trauma and actively resist re-traumatization. In our Wellness Coaching, this translates to prioritizing your safety, building trust, offering choices, and promoting collaboration. We respect your history and experiences, ensuring our support is always delivered with sensitivity, empathy, and an awareness of how past events might influence your current emotional state during transitions.
Is this Wellness Coaching suitable for all backgrounds and experiences?
Absolutely. is committed to being an inclusive and community-focused space. We welcome individuals from all backgrounds, cultures, and identities who are navigating major life transitions. Our Wellness Coaching approach emphasizes cultural humility and respect, ensuring that our support is relevant and sensitive to your unique cultural context and personal experiences.
What can I expect in a typical Wellness Coaching session with ?
In a Wellness Coaching session, you can expect a conversational and approachable environment where your voice is paramount. We'll explore the emotional impact of your life transition, identify areas where you feel stuck, and collaboratively develop practical strategies for emotional healing and resilience. will listen attentively, offer insights grounded in trauma-informed principles, and empower you to build the tools you need to navigate your path forward.
