Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Wellness Coach specifically help me with the stress of parenting young children?
As your Wellness Coach, I focus on the unique pressures parents face. We'll explore how daily routines, sleep disruptions, and constant demands impact your nervous system. You'll learn specific, gentle practices to reduce your stress response, cultivate inner peace, and respond more calmly to your children's needs, rather than reacting from a place of overwhelm.
What kind of 'ancient healing traditions' does a Sage Garden Wellness Coach use?
Your Wellness Coach draws upon practices like mindful breathing, gentle movement, and presence techniques, often rooted in traditions that honor the body's natural capacity for self-regulation. These aren't religious practices, but rather universal tools that help quiet the mind and soothe the nervous system, adapted to fit seamlessly into your busy family life.
I barely have time to shower; how will I fit Wellness Coach sessions into my schedule?
Your Wellness Coach understands the time constraints of parenting. Our sessions are designed to be efficient and impactful. More importantly, the practices you learn are meant to be woven into your existing day, often taking just a few moments. We focus on integrating small, powerful shifts that make a big difference without adding more to your already full plate.
Will working with a Wellness Coach truly make me feel less overwhelmed and more soulful?
Yes, it absolutely can. By intentionally working to regulate your nervous system and reduce stress, you create space for your authentic, soulful self to emerge. Your Wellness Coach provides the guidance and support to help you move beyond constant overwhelm, allowing you to experience more joy, connection, and inner peace in your parenting journey.
