Frequently Asked Questions

How does Wellness Coaching specifically address emotional recovery from chronic illness or injury?
Our Wellness Coaching is designed to acknowledge the unique emotional toll of chronic health challenges. We focus on processing the grief, frustration, and anxiety that often accompany these experiences. We'll work on building coping mechanisms tailored to your situation, helping you regain emotional equilibrium and a sense of control over your life, rather than being defined by your illness or injury.
What does trauma-informed care mean in the context of your Wellness Coaching?
Trauma-informed care in our Wellness Coaching means we recognize that chronic illness or injury can be a profoundly traumatic experience. We prioritize creating a safe, supportive, and empowering environment where you feel respected and understood. Our approach avoids re-traumatization and instead focuses on building resilience, fostering self-regulation, and restoring your sense of agency and internal security.
Will Wellness Coaching interfere with my medical treatment or physical therapy?
No, our Wellness Coaching complements your existing medical treatment and physical therapy. We focus on the emotional and mental aspects of your recovery, which are crucial but often overlooked. We encourage open communication with your healthcare providers and ensure our coaching supports your overall well-being without conflicting with your medical care. We're part of your comprehensive recovery team.
How quickly can I expect to see changes through Wellness Coaching?
Emotional healing is a personal process, and the timeline for seeing changes through Wellness Coaching varies for everyone. Some clients experience shifts in perspective and mood relatively quickly, while others benefit from a more extended period of support. Our goal is sustainable change, focusing on building lasting emotional tools and resilience at a pace that feels right and safe for you.
