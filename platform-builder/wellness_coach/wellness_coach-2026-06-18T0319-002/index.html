Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is Wellness Coaching for postpartum mothers?
It's dedicated support to help you manage the unique stresses of postpartum recovery. We focus on practical strategies to regulate your nervous system, reduce feelings of overwhelm, and build resilience so you can feel more calm and capable during this intense period.
How can Wellness Coaching help with my stress as a new mom?
We'll work together to identify your specific stressors and implement gentle, evidence-based techniques to calm your nervous system. This might include breathwork, setting realistic boundaries, creating nourishing routines, and processing emotions in a supportive space. The goal is to lower your overall stress load.
Is this different from therapy, and will it fit into my busy schedule?
Yes, Wellness Coaching is distinct from therapy; it's forward-looking and action-oriented, focusing on practical tools for stress reduction and nervous system regulation. Sessions are designed to be flexible and efficient, recognizing your limited time. We prioritize strategies that integrate easily into your daily life, even with a newborn.
What if I feel completely exhausted and don't know where to start?
That's completely normal, and precisely why this support is for you. We start exactly where you are. There's no pressure to 'do' anything perfectly. Our initial conversations will be gentle, helping us understand your current state and slowly, together, we'll build a personalized plan to bring more ease and calm into your days.
