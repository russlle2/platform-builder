Frequently Asked Questions

How does Wellness Coaching specifically help with life transitions?
As your Wellness Coach, I focus on helping you understand how major life changes impact your nervous system. We'll develop specific, actionable strategies to reduce the stress response, improve your ability to adapt, and maintain a sense of calm and clarity as you move through new phases of your life.
What kind of stress reduction techniques will I learn?
We'll explore a variety of evidence-based techniques tailored to your unique needs and the specifics of your transition. This might include gentle somatic practices, intentional breathing exercises, boundary-setting strategies, and routines that support nervous system regulation, all designed to help you feel more centered and less overwhelmed.
Is Wellness Coaching right for me if I'm feeling completely overwhelmed by change?
Absolutely. If you're feeling overwhelmed, that's often a sign your nervous system is working overtime. My coaching is specifically designed for individuals navigating these challenging moments. I'll provide a supportive and nurturing space for you to process what you're experiencing and learn practical ways to bring your body and mind back into a state of balance.
How quickly can I expect to see improvements in my stress levels?
While individual experiences vary, many clients report feeling a greater sense of calm and control within a few sessions. My goal is to equip you with tools you can use immediately, and with consistent practice, you'll build lasting resilience and a more regulated nervous system, making future transitions feel more manageable.
