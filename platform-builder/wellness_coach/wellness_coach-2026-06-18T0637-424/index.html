Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Wellness Coach help my 'brain fog' after a chronic illness?
As your Wellness Coach, I help you identify gentle practices and routines that support your cognitive function. We work on strategies to reduce mental fatigue, improve focus, and bring greater clarity to your thoughts, acknowledging the specific challenges you face due to your chronic illness or injury.
Is this Wellness Coach service suitable if my injury has left me feeling mentally overwhelmed?
Absolutely. This Wellness Coach program is specifically designed to support individuals who feel overwhelmed or mentally fatigued due to injury or chronic illness. We provide a calm, structured space for you to gently rebuild your mental resilience and regain a sense of control over your cognitive landscape.
What kind of 'ancient healing traditions' does this Wellness Coach incorporate for mental clarity?
As your Wellness Coach, I draw upon practices like mindful breathing, specific visualization techniques, and gentle energy work, all adapted from ancient traditions, to calm your nervous system and enhance mental focus. These methods are carefully integrated to support your cognitive wellness in a way that respects your body's current state of recovery.
Can a Wellness Coach help me improve my memory after a period of poor health?
Yes, as your Wellness Coach, we'll explore techniques to gently stimulate memory recall and improve cognitive retention. This involves developing supportive daily habits and mindful practices that strengthen your neural pathways, all while considering the unique needs that arise from recovering from chronic illness or injury.
