How does Wellness Coaching specifically address postpartum physical pain?

Our Wellness Coaching focuses on gentle, targeted techniques to alleviate the unique physical discomforts new mothers often experience. This includes exercises to release tension in the back and shoulders from carrying and nursing, improve core stability, and reduce overall body aches, helping you feel more comfortable in your daily movements.

What is somatic healing and how can it help me after childbirth?

Somatic healing, in the context of postpartum recovery, helps you reconnect with your body's sensations and release physical stress or trauma that might be held within your tissues from childbirth. It's a gentle way to process physical experiences, reduce lingering discomfort, and foster a deeper sense of ease and integration in your postpartum body.

Will Wellness Coaching fit into my schedule as a new mother?

Absolutely. Our Wellness Coach sessions are designed to be flexible and practical. We understand the demands of new motherhood, so we focus on creating simple, effective practices that you can easily incorporate into your day, even if you only have a few minutes. Our goal is to provide accessible support that doesn't add more stress to your life.

Is your approach to postpartum recovery natural and sustainable?

Yes, our Wellness Coaching emphasizes a natural, earth-centered approach to recovery. We guide you in developing sustainable practices that align with your body's natural healing capabilities. This means focusing on gentle movements, mindful breathing, and practical strategies that empower you to maintain your physical well-being long after our sessions conclude.
