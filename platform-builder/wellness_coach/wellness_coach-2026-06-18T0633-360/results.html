Finding Your Core Again: Wellness Coaching for Postpartum Physical Ease

Your body has done something incredible, and now it deserves gentle attention. As your Wellness Coach, we focus on releasing the common aches and tensions that come with new motherhood – the lower back strain from carrying, the shoulder stiffness from nursing, and the overall physical fatigue. We work with your body's natural rhythms to bring back a sense of comfort and stability, allowing you to be present and grounded with your little one.
