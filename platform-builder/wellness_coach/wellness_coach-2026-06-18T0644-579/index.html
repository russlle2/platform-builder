Frequently Asked Questions

I've never done anything 'spiritual' before. Can a Wellness Coach still help me?
Absolutely! My approach as a Wellness Coach is designed for beginners. We'll start with what feels comfortable and gradually introduce simple practices that resonate with you, without any pressure or expectations. Your comfort and accessibility are key.
How does spiritual growth actually help with professional burnout?
Spiritual growth, in this context, means connecting with your core values and finding a sense of purpose beyond your daily tasks. This helps you gain perspective, reduce feelings of being overwhelmed, and build resilience against burnout by fostering a deeper sense of inner peace and self-awareness.
I'm already so busy. How will I fit Wellness Coach sessions into my schedule?
I understand your time is valuable. Our sessions as your Wellness Coach are structured to be efficient and impactful. We'll also focus on integrating small, manageable practices into your existing routine, rather than adding significant time commitments. It's about quality, not quantity, for your inner calm.
What kind of commitment is required to work with a Wellness Coach?
The commitment is primarily to yourself and your well-being. As your Wellness Coach, I'll guide you, but the real work happens when you consistently apply the insights and practices we discuss. We'll define a pace that feels sustainable and supportive for your current life circumstances.
