The 4-Phase Cohort Framework

1. Notice — Gentle daily diagnostics so you learn what supports you.
2. Choose — A weekly plan that homes in on 1–2 habits for momentum.
3. Anchor — Micro-habits practiced in community to build durable rhythms.
4. Grow — Tools to adapt habits into seasonal life changes, not quick fixes.
