Questions people ask before they start

How long before I notice a difference?

Most clients report clearer focus and at least one small routine that sticks within 3–6 weeks. Deeper change builds with steady practice.

Do you provide medical advice?

No. Coaching focuses on behaviors, habits, and practical frameworks. I do not provide medical or psychiatric treatment.

What if my schedule is unpredictable?

We design flexible micro-habits that adapt to variability. The goal is reliable progress, not perfection.

How do sessions work?

We run guided experiments: set a clear, testable habit, measure a simple metric, and iterate weekly.
