Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is 'gentle emotional support' for seniors?
Gentle emotional support means we approach your feelings with extra care and respect, especially for seniors. Your Wellness Coach focuses on creating a safe, unhurried space where you can share and explore your emotions without pressure. We use compassionate methods to help you understand and process feelings, ensuring you always feel heard, valued, and comfortable, moving at a pace that suits you best.
How does trauma-informed Wellness Coaching help someone my age?
Trauma-informed coaching for seniors acknowledges that past difficult experiences can still impact how you feel today. Your Wellness Coach understands this and creates an environment where you feel secure discussing any memories or feelings that arise. We don't push you to relive events, but rather help you gently process their lasting effects, empowering you to find greater peace and resilience in your present life.
Will this type of Wellness Coaching be too intense for me?
Absolutely not. Our approach is specifically designed to be nurturing and gentle, especially for seniors. Your Wellness Coach will always respect your comfort level and pace. There's no pressure to delve into anything you're not ready for. The goal is to provide a supportive space where you can feel more at ease and find comfort, never overwhelmed.
What can I expect from my first session with a Wellness Coach?
In your first session, you can expect a warm and friendly conversation. Your Wellness Coach will listen carefully as you share what brings you to coaching and what you hope to gain. It's a chance for us to get to know each other, understand your unique needs, and discuss how we can best support you in creating more emotional well-being and peace in your life. There's no expectation, just an open, supportive chat.
