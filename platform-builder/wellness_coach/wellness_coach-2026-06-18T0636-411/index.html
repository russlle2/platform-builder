Frequently Asked Questions

What does a Cognitive Wellness Coach do for someone my age?
As your Cognitive Wellness Coach, I work with you to understand your current cognitive strengths and areas where you’d like more support. We then develop gentle, practical strategies to enhance your mental clarity, focus, and memory, tailored specifically for seniors aged 60 and over. It's about maintaining your sharp mind.
Are the coaching methods gentle and suitable for seniors?
Absolutely. All our methods are designed with gentleness and efficacy in mind, specifically for seniors. We focus on evidence-based techniques that are comfortable, respectful of your pace, and integrate smoothly into your lifestyle. Your comfort and well-being are always our top priority.
How can this coaching help with everyday mental tasks?
Our coaching provides you with straightforward tools and practices to improve your daily mental tasks. This could mean better recall for names, easier concentration during conversations, or more efficient planning for your activities. The goal is to make your everyday mental experiences feel clearer and more manageable.
Is this a replacement for medical advice or treatment?
No, our Cognitive Wellness Coaching is a supportive service and is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. We encourage you to always consult with your physician for any health concerns. Our role is to complement your existing care by focusing on lifestyle and cognitive practices.
