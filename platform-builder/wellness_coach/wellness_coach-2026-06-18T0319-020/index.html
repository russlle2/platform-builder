Frequently Asked Questions

How does Wellness Coaching specifically help with major life transitions?
Our Wellness Coaching focuses on providing you with concrete tools to manage the physiological and emotional impacts of major life changes. We'll work together to stabilize your nervous system, reducing overwhelm and increasing your capacity to adapt to new situations without feeling constantly on edge.
What kind of strategies will I learn to reduce stress and regulate my nervous system?
You will learn evidence-based strategies tailored to your unique needs, which may include specific breathing exercises, gentle movement patterns, environmental adjustments, and cognitive reframing techniques. These are all designed to shift your body out of 'fight or flight' and into a more regulated, calm state.
Is this Wellness Coaching suitable if I'm feeling overwhelmed by my current situation?
Yes, our programs are specifically designed for individuals feeling overwhelmed by major life transitions. We provide a supportive, structured environment to help you systematically address stress and build resilience, moving you from feeling overwhelmed to experiencing greater stability and control.
How is this Wellness Coaching different from therapy or counseling?
While both can be beneficial, Wellness Coaching focuses on present-moment strategies and future-oriented action to build skills for stress reduction and nervous system regulation. We don't delve into past traumas, but rather equip you with practical tools to navigate your current and future challenges effectively.
