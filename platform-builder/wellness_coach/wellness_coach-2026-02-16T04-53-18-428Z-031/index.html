The Core Rhythm Framework

A four-part loop to help you form habits that stick.

  1. 1

    Clarify

    Define the small, measurable change you want and why it matters.

  2. 2

    Design

    Match habit to your context: micro-prompts, simplified choices, and guardrails.

  3. 3

    Practice

    Short cycles of practice with clear measurement and quick adjustments.

  4. 4

    Anchor

    Integrate the new habit into your routine so it becomes automatic over time.

This framework is a compass — we'll tailor the steps to your priorities and schedule.
