How I guide change (simple scaffolds)

I translate broad goals into habit-sized experiments, tracking signals rather than outcomes. Each program uses the same three-step scaffolding below.

1. Map & Measure

Map your current routine and the small wins you already have. We design micro-trials you can measure weekly.

2. Build & Anchor

Anchor new behaviours to existing cues and protect them with low-friction scaffolds so they survive real life.

3. Iterate with Data

Collect simple signals, refine the environment and scale up what consistently works for you.

Principle

Small, consistent inputs compound. My work is to make those inputs inevitable.

Questions I get often

How do you measure progress?
I track weekly signal metrics (habit adherence, perceived effort, contextual friction). They predict long term outcomes better than raw goals.
Is this medical advice?
No. I focus on behavior change and lifestyle systems. For medical questions consult a licensed clinician.
What commitment is typical?
Most clients commit to a 12-week experimental period to establish routines and collect enough data to iterate effectively.
