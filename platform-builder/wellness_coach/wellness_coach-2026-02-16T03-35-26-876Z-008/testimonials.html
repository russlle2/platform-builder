Common questions from clients

How do you quantify progress?

We use simple, repeatable measures (minutes of consistent behavior, number of successful repetitions, and qualitative check-ins) to track trends, not absolutes.

Can I combine coaching with therapy?

Yes. Coaching here focuses on habits, structure, and behavior. Where deeper therapeutic work is needed, we coordinate with licensed professionals.

What kind of clients see the biggest gains?

People committed to small experiments and consistent measurement—professionals, caregivers, and creatives—report steady, compounding results.
