Frequently Asked Questions

How can Wellness Coaching specifically help me as a parent of young children?
As your Wellness Coach, I focus on the unique stressors parents face, such as sleep deprivation, constant demands, and emotional exhaustion. We'll explore strategies for processing overwhelming emotions, setting realistic boundaries, and cultivating self-compassion, all tailored to fit the realities of your family life. It's about empowering you to find your footing and feel more in control amidst the chaos.
What does 'trauma-informed' mean in the context of Wellness Coaching for parents?
Trauma-informed means we acknowledge that your past experiences and current high-stress environment can influence your emotional responses. As your Wellness Coach, I create a safe space where we can gently explore how these factors impact your parenting and well-being, without judgment. We'll focus on building resilience and developing coping mechanisms that honor your emotional history, helping you heal and move forward.
Will Wellness Coaching add more to my already busy schedule?
My goal as your Wellness Coach is to simplify, not complicate. Our sessions are designed to be efficient and impactful, providing you with practical tools you can integrate immediately. We'll work together to create strategies that fit into your existing routine, helping you carve out space for your own emotional well-being without adding another burden to your plate. It's about working smarter, not harder, on your emotional health.
I feel emotionally drained. Can Wellness Coaching truly help me feel better?
Absolutely. Feeling emotionally drained is a common experience for parents, and it's a sign that your emotional resources are depleted. As your Wellness Coach, I will guide you through practices that help you replenish those resources, process accumulated stress, and build emotional stamina. You'll learn to recognize your triggers, regulate your emotions, and cultivate inner peace, leading to a noticeable shift in how you feel and function day-to-day.
