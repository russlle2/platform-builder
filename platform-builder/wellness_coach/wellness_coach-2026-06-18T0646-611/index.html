Frequently Asked Questions

How does a Wellness Coach specifically help seniors with immune support?
As your Wellness Coach, I focus on evidence-based, gentle strategies tailored for the senior body. This includes reviewing your dietary patterns for immune-boosting nutrients, discussing lifestyle factors like sleep and stress that impact immunity, and suggesting safe, effective ways to build your body's natural defenses. It's all about providing you with personalized, practical steps.
Is Wellness Coaching suitable if I have existing health conditions?
Absolutely. Your safety and well-being are my priority. As your Wellness Coach, I always encourage you to keep your primary healthcare providers informed of our work together. My recommendations are designed to complement, not replace, medical care. We'll work within the parameters of your current health status to support your immune system and vitality gently and effectively.
What kind of results can I expect from working with a Senior Wellness Coach?
Clients often report feeling more consistently energetic, experiencing fewer seasonal sniffles, and generally feeling more resilient. As your Wellness Coach, my aim is to help you establish sustainable habits that contribute to a stronger immune system and a greater sense of well-being, allowing you to enjoy your daily life more fully and with greater comfort.
What does a typical session with a Wellness Coach look like for seniors?
Our sessions are conversational and focus entirely on you. As your Wellness Coach, we'll discuss your current health habits, any concerns you have about your immune health or energy levels, and your personal goals. We'll then collaboratively create small, manageable steps you can take, ensuring they fit comfortably into your routine. It's a supportive, judgment-free space designed for your progress.
