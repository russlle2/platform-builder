Frequently Asked Questions

How can Wellness Coaching specifically help with my burnout?
Burnout often comes with physical symptoms like neck pain, back stiffness, and fatigue. We address these by improving your postural alignment and body mechanics, which can significantly reduce physical discomfort and, in turn, alleviate some of the mental load you're experiencing.
I have a demanding job; how can I fit postural health into my busy schedule?
That's exactly what we specialize in! We focus on small, impactful changes that integrate into your existing routine. You'll learn quick, effective techniques you can do at your desk or during short breaks, without adding more stress to your day.
Is this just about sitting up straight, or is there more to it?
It's much more than just 'sitting up straight.' We delve into how your entire body moves and holds itself throughout your day. We'll explore how your habits contribute to tension and show you how to create more supportive, comfortable patterns that reduce strain and boost your overall energy.
What kind of results can I expect from working with ?
Clients typically report reduced physical pain, improved energy levels, and a greater sense of calm. You can expect to feel more comfortable in your own skin, more focused at work, and better equipped to handle the demands of your professional life without feeling completely drained.
