Micro-habits that compound

Small practices you can start today — each takes less than five minutes.

  • One-breath reset — Pause 3 slow breaths before checking your phone.
  • Morning anchor — A single movement and gratitude naming each morning.
  • Evening note — Write one sentence about what went well.
  • Micro-walk — 5 minutes outside after lunch to reset digestion and mood.

These habits are woven into our cohort rituals; they’re designed to be simple enough to keep and meaningful enough to change your days.
