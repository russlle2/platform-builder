Frequently Asked Questions

What does 'nervous system regulation' mean for someone my age?
For seniors, nervous system regulation means teaching your body and mind to shift out of states of tension and into states of calm more easily. It's about reducing the 'fight or flight' response and encouraging the 'rest and digest' response, which can improve your sleep, digestion, and overall peace of mind.
Is Wellness Coaching suitable for someone with existing health conditions?
Our Wellness Coaching is designed to be gentle and complementary to your existing health care. We focus on lifestyle practices that support well-being. We always recommend consulting with your primary care physician about any new health routines, and we can work in conjunction with their advice.
How is this different from traditional therapy or medical treatment?
Wellness Coaching with is distinct from therapy or medical treatment. We focus on proactive strategies for stress reduction and nervous system support through lifestyle adjustments and gentle practices. We don't diagnose or treat medical conditions, but rather empower you with tools to enhance your daily comfort and calm.
What kind of commitment is involved in your Wellness Coaching programs?
Our programs are designed to be accessible and supportive. The commitment typically involves regular, personalized sessions where we work together to integrate gentle practices into your routine. The pace is always tailored to your comfort and availability, ensuring it fits seamlessly into your life without adding extra stress.
