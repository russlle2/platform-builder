Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is Wellness Coaching for seniors?
Wellness Coaching for seniors is a friendly, supportive partnership focused on helping you gently improve your immune health and build more vitality. We work on practical, everyday habits that fit easily into your life, ensuring you feel stronger and more energetic without any overwhelming changes.
Do I need to be in good health to start Wellness Coaching?
Not at all! Our Wellness Coaching is designed to be gentle and adaptable for seniors with varying health needs. We focus on gradual, supportive steps to enhance your well-being, no matter where you're starting from. We'll always encourage you to consult with your doctor for any specific medical concerns.
How does Wellness Coaching help with immune support specifically?
We'll explore gentle strategies that naturally support your body's defenses. This might include simple nutrition tips, easy movement practices, or stress-reducing techniques. The goal is to help your immune system feel more robust, so you can better navigate daily life with confidence.
I've never done anything like this before. Is Wellness Coaching right for me?
Absolutely! Our Wellness Coaching is perfect for seniors who are new to wellness. We pride ourselves on being approachable and ensuring you feel comfortable every step of the way. There's no prior experience needed; just a desire to feel a bit better and more vibrant.
