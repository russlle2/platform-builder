Frequently Asked Questions

Can you really help me when I only have 3 minutes?
Yes. Our protocols are engineered for your reality: a 3-minute reset between wiping spills, a 60-second breathing pattern during your child's meltdown, and a 90-second ritual that works while packing lunches. No extra time needed—just integrated into your chaos.
How is this different from other parenting coaches?
We focus solely on nervous system regulation with measurable outcomes. While others offer general advice, we give you specific techniques proven to lower cortisol levels during household stress—backed by neurobiology, not anecdotal 'wellness'.
Do I need to change my schedule to use this?
No. Our protocols work within your existing rhythm: during naptime, while waiting for your child at school, or even while cooking dinner. We don't add to your to-do list—we optimize what's already there.
What if my child's behavior is the real issue?
We address your stress response to their behavior—not their behavior itself. You'll learn to recognize your nervous system's reactions (racing heart, irritability) and use immediate tools to recenter before reacting. This creates space for more effective parenting.
