Gentle Grounding: Postural Wellness for Your Postpartum Body

Your body has undergone an incredible change. Our Wellness Coaching focuses on gentle, effective ways to reconnect with your core and pelvic floor, helping you address common postpartum challenges like back discomfort or a feeling of disconnect. We'll work with your body's current state, honoring its wisdom and promoting natural recovery, so you can move with ease and confidence again.

How can a Wellness Coach specifically help my postpartum posture?

As your Wellness Coach, I'll provide personalized guidance to help you understand the changes in your body after childbirth. We'll focus on rebuilding core and pelvic floor strength gently, improving your alignment, and teaching you mindful movement patterns for daily activities to alleviate discomfort and prevent future issues.

I feel so disconnected from my body after birth. Can postural awareness Wellness Coaching help?

Absolutely. Many new mothers experience a feeling of disconnect. Our Wellness Coaching emphasizes exercises and techniques to help you gently re-establish a strong mind-body connection, fostering a deeper sense of presence and ownership over your body's new form and capabilities.

Will this Wellness Coaching interfere with my busy schedule with a new baby?

Not at all. We design our Wellness Coaching sessions and practices to be realistic and adaptable to your life. The focus is on integrating small, impactful movements and awareness practices into your daily routine, rather than adding another demanding task to your plate. Your comfort and capacity are always prioritized.

Is this Wellness Coaching suitable for diastasis recti or pelvic floor issues?

While I am a Wellness Coach and not a medical professional, our postural and body awareness practices are foundational for supporting recovery from issues like diastasis recti or pelvic floor dysfunction. We'll work in conjunction with your medical team, providing complementary support focused on gentle strengthening and mindful movement. Always consult your doctor or physical therapist first.
