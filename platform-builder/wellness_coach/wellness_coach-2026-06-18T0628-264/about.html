Gentle Rebuilding: Emotional Wellness Coaching Programs

Our programs are designed to meet you where you are, acknowledging the unique emotional and physical challenges that come with recovering from chronic illness or injury. We focus on establishing steady, sustainable practices that support your emotional repair, helping you feel more grounded and connected to your inner resources.

How can a Wellness Coach specifically help with emotional repair after chronic illness?

A Wellness Coach, particularly one focused on trauma-informed care, provides dedicated support in navigating the emotional aftermath of chronic illness or injury. We help you identify and process feelings like grief, frustration, or fear, offering tools to re-regulate your nervous system and rebuild a sense of emotional stability.

What does 'trauma-informed care' mean in the context of Wellness Coaching for recovery?

Trauma-informed care means we understand that your experiences with chronic illness or injury may have been traumatic, impacting your sense of safety and trust in your body. Our approach is gentle, patient, and respectful of your pace, ensuring you feel empowered and heard throughout your emotional healing process.

Will Wellness Coaching interfere with my existing medical treatment?

Our Wellness Coaching is complementary to your medical treatment, never a replacement. We work alongside your healthcare team by focusing on the emotional and mental aspects of recovery. We encourage open communication with your doctors and can help you advocate for your needs within the medical system.

What if I feel overwhelmed or exhausted by the idea of emotional work?

It's completely understandable to feel overwhelmed, especially during recovery. Our approach is designed to be gentle and incremental. We start small, focusing on building sustainable practices that don't add to your burden but rather create space for ease and calm. Your comfort and capacity are always our priority.
