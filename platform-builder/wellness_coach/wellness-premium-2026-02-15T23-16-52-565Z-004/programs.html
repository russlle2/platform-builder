Frequently asked questions

Clear answers before you begin

Do I need recent lab work? ▾
No. We begin with lifestyle and symptom mapping. If you have recent labs, we incorporate them; optional interpretation is available for select programs.
Is this medical care? ▾
This is coaching, not medical diagnosis or treatment. We happily collaborate with your clinician and work within their guidance.
How much time should I budget weekly? ▾
Most clients spend 60–90 minutes total: one call plus micro-habits embedded into existing routines.
Can my company cover the program? ▾
Yes. Many clients use L&D or wellness stipends. We provide invoices and outcome summaries on request.
