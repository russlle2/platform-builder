Frequently Asked Questions

How does Wellness Coaching specifically help athletes with physical stress?
Wellness Coaching for athletes zeroes in on understanding your body's unique response to intense training and activity. We identify patterns of physical stress, develop targeted somatic techniques to alleviate discomfort, and build strategies for quicker recovery and injury prevention, keeping you performing at your best.
What kind of somatic healing techniques are used to address physical pain?
Our Wellness Coaching incorporates various somatic approaches tailored to your needs. This can include breathwork for tension release, guided body awareness exercises to pinpoint discomfort, and movement practices designed to re-educate your nervous system, all aimed at reducing and resolving physical pain from within.
Is Wellness Coaching a substitute for physical therapy or medical treatment?
No, Wellness Coaching complements and enhances physical therapy or medical treatment. We focus on the mental and emotional aspects of physical stress and pain, helping you build resilience, improve body awareness, and develop coping mechanisms that support your physical recovery and overall well-being, but we do not diagnose or treat medical conditions.
How quickly can I expect to see improvements in my physical stress and pain?
The timeline for improvement varies for everyone, as it depends on the nature and duration of your physical stress and your commitment to the practices. However, many active individuals report feeling a greater sense of body awareness and a reduction in discomfort within the first few sessions of Wellness Coaching, with sustained progress over time.
