Frequently Asked Questions

How does Wellness Coaching specifically help with physical pain from college stress?
Our Wellness Coaching focuses on somatic practices that teach you to identify and release the deep-seated tension often caused by academic pressure. We work on techniques to alleviate specific discomforts like tech neck, shoulder tension, headaches, and jaw pain, giving you practical strategies to manage and reduce these physical symptoms effectively.
Is this Wellness Coaching just for students with severe anxiety?
Not at all. While we effectively support students with higher anxiety, our Wellness Coaching is designed for any college student experiencing physical discomfort or tightness due to academic demands, exam stress, or general pressure. It’s about prevention and proactive relief, not just crisis management.
What makes this Wellness Coaching approach different from general stress relief programs?
Unlike generic stress relief, our Wellness Coaching is highly specialized for the college student demographic and their unique pressures. We specifically address the physical manifestation of academic anxiety and pressure through precise somatic techniques, rather than broad mindfulness. It's a targeted, results-driven approach for your specific challenges.
How quickly can I expect to feel results from Wellness Coaching?
Many clients report feeling a noticeable difference in physical tension and overall ease within just a few sessions of Wellness Coaching. The speed of results can vary, but our tailored approach is designed to provide tangible relief and equip you with self-regulation tools quickly, so you can integrate them into your busy schedule.
