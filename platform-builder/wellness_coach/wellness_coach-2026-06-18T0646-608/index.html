Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Wellness Coach specifically help my immune system during a big life change?
During significant transitions, stress often compromises your immune function. As your Wellness Coach, I work with you to identify specific nutritional gaps, sleep patterns, and stress management techniques that directly impact your immune response, creating a personalized plan to keep your defenses strong and steady.
I'm already overwhelmed by my life transition; will adding Wellness Coaching be too much?
Absolutely not. My role as your Wellness Coach is to simplify and integrate, not to add more burden. We'll start with small, manageable steps that yield significant results for your immune health and vitality, making your transition smoother and more supported, not more stressful.
What kind of vitality building can I expect from Wellness Coaching when everything feels uncertain?
When life feels uncertain, building consistent vitality is key. As your Wellness Coach, we'll focus on practical strategies like balanced meal planning, targeted movement, and restorative practices that directly combat the fatigue often associated with transitions, helping you feel more centered and energetic despite external shifts.
Is Wellness Coaching only for major health issues, or can it help with general well-being during a life change?
Wellness Coaching is ideally suited for supporting your general well-being, especially during times of change. We don't focus on treating medical conditions, but rather on proactively building your immune resilience and overall vitality so you can navigate your life transition with greater strength and fewer health interruptions.
