Frequently Asked Questions

How does Wellness Coaching specifically help with major life transitions?
As your Wellness Coach, I provide a structured framework to help you understand the emotional and practical aspects of your specific transition. We identify key stressors, process any grief or uncertainty, and develop concrete steps to move forward with greater emotional security and purpose.
What does 'trauma-informed care' mean in the context of your Wellness Coach practice?
Trauma-informed care, in my Wellness Coach practice, means creating an environment where your past experiences are acknowledged and respected. We avoid re-traumatization and instead focus on building your safety, trust, and control, allowing for gentle emotional processing and growth at your own pace.
Is Wellness Coaching suitable if I'm feeling overwhelmed or uncertain during a life change?
Absolutely. Feeling overwhelmed or uncertain is a common response to significant life shifts. As your Wellness Coach, my role is to provide a calm, objective perspective and practical tools to help you clarify your thoughts, manage intense emotions, and make informed decisions from a place of inner strength.
What kind of results can I expect from engaging with a Wellness Coach for emotional healing?
Through Wellness Coaching, you can expect to gain a clearer understanding of your emotional landscape, develop effective coping mechanisms, and build greater emotional resilience. Clients often report feeling more grounded, confident, and capable of navigating their evolving circumstances with a renewed sense of purpose and calm.
