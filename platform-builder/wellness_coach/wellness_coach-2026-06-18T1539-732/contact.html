Understanding Relationship Shifts Post-Illness with Your Wellness Coach

Chronic illness or injury fundamentally alters life, including how you connect with loved ones. Our Wellness Coach approach acknowledges these shifts, providing strategies to communicate needs, manage expectations, and rebuild intimacy without added stress. We focus on practical, science-informed methods to help you navigate these sensitive relationship dynamics.
