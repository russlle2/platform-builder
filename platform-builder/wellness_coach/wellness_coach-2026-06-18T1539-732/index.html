Frequently Asked Questions

How does a Wellness Coach specifically help with relationships after chronic illness?
A Wellness Coach provides tailored strategies to address the unique challenges chronic illness or injury places on relationships. We help you articulate your needs, manage emotional fluctuations, and re-establish intimacy and support in ways that honor your recovery process.
My energy levels are low due to recovery. Can I still benefit from Wellness Coach sessions?
Absolutely. Our Wellness Coach sessions are designed to accommodate your energy levels and physical limitations. We prioritize your well-being, offering flexible scheduling and gentle approaches to ensure the support you receive is restorative, not draining.
Is this only for romantic relationships, or can a Wellness Coach help with family and friends too?
Our Wellness Coach services extend to all significant relationships in your life—romantic partners, family members, and close friends. We understand that your entire support system is affected, and we provide tools to strengthen all these vital connections.
What makes your Wellness Coach approach 'heart-centered' for someone in recovery?
Being 'heart-centered' means our Wellness Coach prioritizes empathy, genuine understanding, and emotional intelligence in every interaction. We recognize the profound emotional impact of chronic illness or injury on your relationships and guide you with compassion and respect to rebuild those bonds from a place of authenticity.
