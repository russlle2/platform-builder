Frequently Asked Questions

How does a Wellness Coach specifically help with postpartum stress and nervous system regulation?
As your Wellness Coach, I provide tailored strategies that acknowledge the unique physiological and emotional shifts of postpartum. We focus on gentle practices like diaphragmatic breathing, restorative movement, and personalized routines to down-regulate your stress response and promote a sense of inner safety, which is crucial during this intense period.
What kind of techniques does your Wellness Coaching use to support new mothers?
My Wellness Coaching integrates techniques from various disciplines. This includes somatic exercises to release tension, mindfulness practices to foster presence, nutritional guidance for energy and mood stability, and understanding circadian rhythms for better sleep. We adapt these to your specific needs, ensuring they are sustainable and supportive for your new role.
Is Wellness Coaching suitable if I’m feeling overwhelmed and don’t have much time?
Absolutely. My Wellness Coaching is designed with the realities of new motherhood in mind. We prioritize small, impactful practices that can be integrated into your day, even if you only have a few minutes. The goal is to build resilience and reduce overwhelm efficiently, not to add more to your plate. Your nervous system benefits from consistent, gentle support.
How quickly can I expect to feel a difference in my stress levels with a Wellness Coach?
Many clients report feeling a sense of immediate relief and greater awareness after our first few sessions. Sustained changes in nervous system regulation and stress reduction typically develop over weeks as you consistently apply the personalized strategies we create. It's a gradual process of re-patterning, leading to lasting calm and well-being.
