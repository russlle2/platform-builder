Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is 'nervous system regulation' in the context of Wellness Coaching for seniors?
Nervous system regulation for seniors means learning gentle, practical ways to help your body and mind shift from feeling stressed or overwhelmed to a more calm and settled state. It's about noticing when you feel a bit frazzled and having simple techniques to bring you back to a place of ease and comfort.
I'm not very tech-savvy; how do your Wellness Coaching sessions work?
That's perfectly fine! Our Wellness Coaching sessions are designed to be as straightforward and comfortable as possible. We can conduct them over the phone, or if you prefer, we can guide you through a simple video call. We're here to help you every step of the way, making sure you feel at ease with the process. Your comfort is our priority.
Will Wellness Coaching interfere with my current medical treatments?
Our Wellness Coaching is a complementary approach focused on stress reduction and nervous system comfort, not a medical treatment. We always encourage you to discuss any new wellness practices with your doctor, and we are happy to work alongside your existing healthcare plan to support your overall well-being. We focus on enhancing your daily comfort and inner calm.
What kind of commitment is required for your Wellness Coaching programs?
Our programs are designed with flexibility in mind, understanding that life in your golden years can have its own rhythm. We offer various options, from focused short-term support to more ongoing gentle guidance. We'll discuss what feels right for you during our initial conversation, ensuring the commitment fits comfortably into your schedule and lifestyle.
