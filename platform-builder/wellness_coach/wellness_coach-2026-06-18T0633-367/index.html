Frequently Asked Questions

How can Wellness Coaching specifically help with the physical pain I experience as a parent?
As a parent, your body is often under unique strains from lifting, carrying, and bending. Our Wellness Coaching focuses on somatic practices that help you identify where tension is held in your body due to these demands and daily stress. We'll explore gentle movements and awareness techniques to release these physical knots, improving your posture, flexibility, and overall comfort. It's about listening to your body and giving it what it needs to find ease.
I'm always busy with my kids. Will I have time for your Wellness Coaching practices?
We completely understand the time constraints of parents! Our Wellness Coaching emphasizes integrating simple, effective somatic practices into your existing routine. Many techniques take just a few minutes and can be done anywhere, anytime – while waiting for water to boil, during a quiet moment before bed, or even while playing with your children. Our goal is to provide you with tools that fit seamlessly into your life, not add another item to your to-do list.
How does somatic healing work, and is it suitable for someone dealing with chronic back pain from parenting?
Somatic healing focuses on the connection between your mind and body, helping you become aware of physical sensations and the emotions or stress patterns linked to them. For chronic back pain often experienced by parents, this means we'll gently explore how your daily movements, posture, and stress levels contribute to the pain. You'll learn techniques to release chronic muscle tension, improve your body's natural alignment, and develop a more fluid way of moving, which can significantly reduce discomfort and improve your overall physical well-being.
My household stress feels overwhelming. Can Wellness Coaching really help my physical symptoms?
Absolutely. The connection between mental stress and physical symptoms is profound, especially for parents. High household stress often manifests as tense muscles, headaches, digestive issues, and overall fatigue. Our Wellness Coaching provides you with nature-inspired somatic tools to calm your nervous system, allowing your body to release stored tension. By addressing the root of your stress, you'll often find a significant reduction in your physical symptoms, helping you feel more grounded and resilient.
