Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is somatic healing and how can it help with my pain?
Somatic healing, as a Wellness Coach approach, involves gentle movements and focused awareness to re-educate your nervous system. Many chronic pains stem from unconscious muscle contractions. We help you consciously release these deep-seated tensions, allowing your body to find a new, more comfortable equilibrium. It's about learning to move and feel better from within your own body.
Is Wellness Coaching at suitable for all seniors, regardless of their current physical condition?
Yes, our Wellness Coaching is specifically designed to be gentle and adaptable for seniors of all mobility levels. We prioritize your comfort and safety, tailoring every practice to your individual capabilities. Our focus is on gradual, mindful improvement, ensuring that you can participate effectively and safely, even with existing conditions or limitations.
How long does it typically take to feel results from your pain relief coaching?
While individual experiences vary, many clients report feeling some initial relief and increased body awareness within a few sessions. Consistent practice of the techniques you learn with your Wellness Coach typically leads to more significant and lasting reductions in pain over a few weeks or months. Our goal is sustainable comfort, not just quick fixes.
Do I need any special equipment or prior experience for your Wellness Coaching sessions?
No, you don't need any special equipment or prior experience. Our Wellness Coaching sessions are primarily movement-based and can often be done with just comfortable clothing and a quiet space. We will guide you through everything you need to know, making it accessible and straightforward for you to integrate these gentle practices into your daily life.
