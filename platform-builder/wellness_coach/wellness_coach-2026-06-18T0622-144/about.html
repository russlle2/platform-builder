How can a Wellness Coach specifically help my sleep when I have a chronic illness?

As your Wellness Coach, I understand that chronic illness presents unique sleep challenges. We'll identify specific barriers like pain, medication side effects, or anxiety related to your condition, then develop tailored strategies to mitigate these, focusing on practical adjustments to your routine and environment that promote deeper rest.

Is this Wellness Coach program suitable if my energy levels are very low due to my illness?

Absolutely. Our Wellness Coach programs are designed with flexibility and compassion. We start where you are, focusing on small, manageable changes that don't overwhelm your limited energy. The goal is to gently build momentum towards better sleep without adding stress or exhaustion to your recovery.

What kind of strategies will my Wellness Coach recommend for better sleep?

Your Wellness Coach will guide you through evidence-based strategies, including optimizing your sleep schedule, creating a calming bedtime routine, improving your sleep environment, and gentle techniques to manage nighttime worries. We focus on methods that are achievable and sustainable for someone in recovery, avoiding anything overly demanding.

Will working with a Wellness Coach replace my medical care for my chronic illness?

No, working with a Wellness Coach is complementary to your medical care. We focus on lifestyle and behavioral adjustments to improve your sleep and overall well-being, always respecting and working alongside your primary medical treatments. We encourage open communication with your healthcare providers about any new approaches.
