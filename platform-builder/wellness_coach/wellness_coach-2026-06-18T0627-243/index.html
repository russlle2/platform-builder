Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Wellness Coach specifically help me with college anxiety?
As a college student, you face unique stressors. We'll work on understanding your specific anxiety triggers related to academics, social pressures, or future uncertainties. Then, we'll build tailored strategies for managing those feelings, improving focus, and developing healthier coping mechanisms so you can navigate your studies and social life more effectively.
Is Wellness Coaching different from therapy, especially for emotional healing?
Yes, while both can be supportive, Wellness Coaching focuses on actionable strategies and skill-building to move forward with your goals, especially around managing emotions and academic stress. We provide a supportive, forward-looking space to process feelings and develop resilience, complementing other forms of care you might be receiving. It's about empowering you to take charge of your well-being.
What does 'trauma-informed care' mean for me as a college student?
For you, it means that I recognize how past difficult experiences, even subtle ones, can influence how you react to stress and pressure in college. Your coaching environment will always be safe, respectful, and understanding of your personal history. We prioritize your comfort and collaborate on strategies that feel right for you, helping you build strength without re-traumatizing.
I'm worried about fitting coaching into my busy college schedule. How does that work?
I understand how packed your schedule can be. We offer flexible scheduling options, including virtual sessions, to make it as convenient as possible for you. The goal is to provide support that fits seamlessly into your life, not add another layer of stress. We can discuss your availability and find a rhythm that works best for your academic and personal commitments.
