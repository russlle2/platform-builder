How I structure change

Frameworks not fads — three guiding pillars that shape each plan.

Micro-actions

A habit is a system of tiny moments that add up. We pick the right smallest action first.

Evidence loops

Trackable signals instead of willpower. Decide what to measure and why it moves the needle.

Context design

Change the environment around the behavior so the choice becomes obvious.

Common myths I clear up

Myth: ‘‘Motivation is the answer.’"
Truth: Reliable systems matter more — motivation fluctuates, systems don’t.
Myth: ‘‘I should change everything at once.’"
Truth: Focused, incremental wins scale sustainably.
Myth: ‘‘Willpower wins.’"
Truth: Remove friction and design triggers instead of relying on willpower alone.
