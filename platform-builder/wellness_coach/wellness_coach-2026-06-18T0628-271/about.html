What does 'trauma-informed' mean in your Wellness Coaching?

Being trauma-informed means that as your Wellness Coach, I understand that past difficult experiences can affect how you feel and react today. Our work together will always prioritize your sense of safety and control, moving at a pace that feels right for you, and never pushing you to share anything you're not ready for. It's about respecting your history and empowering your present.

Is Wellness Coaching suitable for someone my age (60+)?

Absolutely! Wellness Coaching is incredibly beneficial at any age, and especially for seniors. Our sessions are designed to be gentle, conversational, and tailored to your life experiences. Many older adults find this an ideal time to reflect, heal, and cultivate deeper emotional well-being, creating a richer and more fulfilling later life.

What kind of emotional challenges can Wellness Coaching help me with?

As your Wellness Coach, I can support you with a range of emotional challenges, including feelings of loneliness, grief, persistent worry, or unresolved feelings from past events. We work together to identify these feelings, understand their roots, and develop practical, gentle ways for you to navigate them, leading to a greater sense of peace and emotional stability.

How is this different from therapy?

While both can be supportive, Wellness Coaching, especially my approach, is focused on empowering you to build new strengths and strategies for your emotional present and future. As your Wellness Coach, I guide you in understanding your inner resources and creating actionable steps toward a more peaceful life. We focus on moving forward and building resilience, rather than deep clinical diagnosis or extended processing of past events like traditional therapy.
