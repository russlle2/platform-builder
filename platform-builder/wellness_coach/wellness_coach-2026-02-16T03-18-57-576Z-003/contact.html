How I work

My coaching uses a three-step framework: Observe, Micro-Design, Repeat. We track habits objectively, build tiny consistent actions, and refine until momentum sticks. No medical advice — just behavior design and clear accountability.

Framework snapshot
  • Observe — Collect one week of normal routine data
  • Micro-Design — Create 1–3 tiny habit triggers
  • Repeat — Schedule check-ins and tweak cadence
