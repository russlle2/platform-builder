Why I started this work

I used to fix everything at once—and then nothing lasted. Over years of coaching, I refined a three-part editorial approach: observe, reframe, and practice. It’s less about perfect habits and more about dependable steps that survive real life.

Observe

Map daily friction and energy leaks so change targets what matters.

Reframe

Replace guilt with experiments that feel doable and motivating.

Practice

Small repetitions power habit architecture and measurable outcomes.
