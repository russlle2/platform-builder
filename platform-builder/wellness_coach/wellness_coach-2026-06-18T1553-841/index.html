Frequently Asked Questions

How can Wellness Coaching specifically help with my college stress and anxiety?
Our Wellness Coaching directly addresses the physical manifestations of student stress. We'll work on understanding how your body holds tension from academic pressure, and then equip you with simple, effective body awareness techniques and postural adjustments that can help reduce physical discomfort and calm your nervous system, allowing you to focus better.
I spend so much time studying. Will postural health really make a difference?
Absolutely! Prolonged sitting, especially with poor posture, contributes to fatigue, headaches, and a lack of mental clarity. By improving your postural habits, you can reduce these physical strains, increase your energy levels, and enhance your ability to concentrate during study sessions, making your time more efficient and less draining.
Is this Wellness Coaching a quick fix, or will I learn lasting skills?
Our goal is to equip you with lasting skills. We focus on teaching you practical body awareness and self-correction techniques that you can integrate into your daily life. This isn't about temporary adjustments; it's about building a foundational understanding of your body that empowers you to maintain better posture and manage stress independently, long after our sessions.
I'm new to body awareness. Is this Wellness Coaching suitable for beginners?
Yes, completely! Our Wellness Coaching is designed to be accessible and welcoming to everyone, regardless of your previous experience with body awareness or movement practices. We start with the basics, guiding you gently through simple exercises and observations to help you build a stronger connection with your body at your own pace.
