Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Wellness Coach specifically help my sleep during a major life change?
During significant life shifts, your internal clock and stress response can be heavily impacted. As your Wellness Coach, I work with you to identify specific disruptors, from new routines to emotional processing, and then implement targeted strategies. These might include evening wind-down rituals, mindful breathing techniques, or environmental adjustments, all customized to your current situation.
What kind of 'Eastern and Western approaches' does your Wellness Coach practice involve for sleep?
My Wellness Coach approach combines evidence-based Western sleep hygiene principles, like optimizing your bedroom and consistent scheduling, with Eastern practices such as gentle stretching, meditation, and specific breathing exercises to calm the nervous system. This blend helps you address both the physical and mental aspects of achieving restorative sleep.
I'm already overwhelmed with my life transition; will adding a Wellness Coach program be too much?
As your Wellness Coach, my goal is to simplify, not complicate. We start with small, manageable steps that fit into your existing routine. The aim is to reduce your overwhelm by improving your sleep, which in turn enhances your capacity to handle other changes. Think of it as investing in your foundational well-being during a critical time.
How quickly can I expect to see improvements in my sleep with a Wellness Coach?
While individual results vary, many clients report noticing positive shifts within a few weeks of consistently applying the strategies we develop together. As your Wellness Coach, I'll guide you through a process of gradual change, focusing on sustainable habits that build over time for lasting, deeper rest.
