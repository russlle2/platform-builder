Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is 'postural wellness' for a parent, and how can a Wellness Coach help?
Postural wellness for parents is about understanding how your body holds itself and moves through daily tasks like picking up toys, pushing a stroller, or even just holding your child. As your Wellness Coach, I help you identify habits that might be causing strain and teach you gentle, effective ways to improve your alignment and reduce physical stress, making those everyday actions feel much easier.
I'm so busy with my kids; how can I possibly fit in another commitment like wellness coaching?
I completely understand! Our sessions are designed to be practical and efficient, focusing on small, impactful changes you can integrate into your existing routine. We’ll work on movements and awareness techniques that directly apply to your daily parenting activities, so you're not adding extra chores, but rather optimizing how you already move. It's about feeling better as you parent, not adding another item to your to-do list.
Will this help with the specific back and neck pain I get from carrying my baby and all their gear?
Absolutely! Many parents experience specific pain points from the unique demands of childcare. We'll focus on your individual challenges, whether it's shoulder tension from a baby carrier, lower back strain from bending over the crib, or neck discomfort from feeding. My coaching provides targeted strategies to release that tension and strengthen the right muscles, so you can carry, lift, and nurture your little ones with greater comfort and confidence.
Is this like physical therapy, or is it different?
While both aim to improve body function, postural wellness coaching with a Wellness Coach is distinct from physical therapy. Physical therapy typically addresses acute injuries or rehabilitation. My coaching focuses on education and awareness, helping you prevent strain, improve your overall body mechanics, and cultivate a deeper understanding of how your body works in your daily life as a parent. It's about proactive care and sustained comfort, rather than injury recovery.
