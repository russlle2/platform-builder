Frequently Asked Questions

How is this Wellness Coach service different from a sleep consultant?
As a Wellness Coach, we focus on your holistic well-being during postpartum recovery, not just your baby's sleep. We address your specific physical and emotional needs as a new mother, integrating self-care, stress reduction, and gentle routines to improve your sleep, ensuring you feel truly restored, not just less tired.
When is the right time to work with a Postpartum Sleep Wellness Coach?
You can start at any point in your postpartum period! Whether you're in the early weeks feeling overwhelmed or several months in and still struggling with fatigue, our Wellness Coach support can provide valuable strategies to help you find more consistent and deeper rest. It's never too late to prioritize your well-being.
Will working with a Wellness Coach mean strict sleep schedules for my baby?
Not at all. Our Wellness Coach approach is flexible and understanding of the realities of new parenthood. We focus on optimizing your rest within your unique family dynamics, without imposing rigid schedules. We aim to help you find pockets of restorative rest that align with your baby's natural rhythms, not fight against them.
What kind of results can I expect from Postpartum Sleep Wellness Coaching?
While every new mother's experience is unique, clients typically report feeling more energetic, having improved mood, experiencing fewer feelings of overwhelm, and finding greater consistency in their sleep patterns. As your Wellness Coach, our goal is to empower you with tools to feel more rested and capable throughout your postpartum recovery.
