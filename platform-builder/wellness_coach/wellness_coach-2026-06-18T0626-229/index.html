Frequently Asked Questions

What makes this Wellness Coach approach different for someone dealing with professional burnout?
Our focus isn't just on stress reduction; it's on understanding the deeper emotional patterns and potential trauma responses that contribute to chronic burnout in working professionals. We provide a structured, science-informed framework to systematically address these root causes, not just the symptoms, ensuring more lasting relief for you.
I'm a busy professional; how much time will Wellness Coaching require from my schedule?
We understand your demanding schedule. Sessions are typically 60 minutes, held weekly or bi-weekly, and designed to provide actionable insights you can integrate into your life immediately. The goal is efficiency and effectiveness, not adding more to your plate. Your consistency in applying strategies between sessions is key to your progress.
Is 'trauma-informed' care suitable if I don't identify with having significant trauma?
Absolutely. 'Trauma-informed' means we understand that chronic stress and burnout can create similar physiological responses to trauma. Our approach ensures we handle your emotional experiences with sensitivity, respect, and an understanding of how past events, big or small, might influence your current stress responses, without labeling your experiences as 'trauma' unless that resonates with you.
What specific tools or techniques will I learn from my Wellness Coach to manage burnout?
You'll learn practical, evidence-based techniques such as targeted emotional regulation exercises, cognitive reframing for stress patterns, boundary-setting strategies, and nervous system calming protocols. We'll equip you with a personalized toolkit to manage acute stressors and build long-term emotional resilience in your professional and personal life.
