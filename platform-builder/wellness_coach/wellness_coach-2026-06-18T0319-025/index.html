Frequently Asked Questions

How does Wellness Coaching specifically help with college anxiety?
Our Wellness Coaching directly addresses the unique stressors of college life by providing you with concrete techniques to calm your nervous system. We focus on practical strategies you can use immediately to reduce the physical and mental symptoms of anxiety, improving your focus and overall well-being amidst academic demands.
Is Wellness Coaching a long-term commitment, or can I get quick relief?
You'll start learning valuable techniques from your very first session that can offer immediate relief. While deeper regulation of your nervous system and lasting habit changes develop over time, our programs are designed to give you both quick wins and sustainable practices that build resilience throughout your academic career.
I'm already swamped with school; how will I fit Wellness Coaching in?
We understand your schedule is packed. Our Wellness Coaching sessions are designed to be efficient and impactful. We focus on integrating simple, powerful practices into your existing routine, rather than adding more tasks. The goal is to free up mental space and energy, making your studies more manageable, not adding another burden.
What kind of 'ancient healing traditions' are incorporated into the Wellness Coaching?
We draw upon wisdom from practices like mindful breathing, specific movement patterns, and guided visualization, all rooted in traditions that have understood the mind-body connection for centuries. These are adapted for the modern student experience, offering you powerful, non-pharmaceutical tools to regulate stress and promote inner calm.
