Frequently Asked Questions

How does a Wellness Coach specifically help athletes dealing with physical stress?
As your Wellness Coach, I focus on the often-overlooked emotional and energetic components contributing to physical stress in athletes. We work on strengthening your relationship with your body's inherent wisdom, helping you interpret its signals more clearly, and fostering inner peace that supports sustained physical recovery and performance.
My physical stress often feels like burnout. Can a Wellness Coach address this?
Absolutely. Burnout often stems from a fundamental disconnect – pushing your physical limits without adequately nurturing your inner reserves. As your Wellness Coach, we'll explore the patterns of self-relationship that lead to exhaustion, guiding you toward practices that replenish your spirit and allow for more balanced, enduring activity.
What does 'heart-centered relationship healing' mean for an active person?
For an active person, 'heart-centered relationship healing' means cultivating deep empathy and understanding for your own body, mind, and spirit. It's about moving beyond simply training hard, to truly listening to your internal rhythms, honoring your limits, and making choices from a place of genuine self-care, which ultimately enhances your physical capacity and joy in movement.
How quickly can I expect to see improvements in managing physical stress with a Wellness Coach?
The pace of change is unique to everyone. As your Wellness Coach, our initial sessions will focus on building awareness and foundational practices. Many clients report feeling a greater sense of inner calm and improved connection with their bodies within a few weeks, with deeper shifts in physical stress management unfolding over subsequent months as new patterns become ingrained.
