Frequently Asked Questions

How does Wellness Coaching specifically benefit athletes and active people?
My Wellness Coaching directly addresses the unique stresses athletes place on their bodies. We focus on optimizing your posture and movement mechanics to prevent injury, improve efficiency, and enhance recovery, ensuring your body can keep up with your demanding training schedule.
What can I expect in a typical postural assessment during Wellness Coaching?
In a postural assessment, we'll carefully observe your static and dynamic movements. This includes analyzing your stance, gait, and specific sport-related actions to pinpoint areas of tension, imbalance, or inefficient movement that could be impacting your performance or causing physical stress.
Is this Wellness Coaching for injury recovery, or performance enhancement?
While Wellness Coaching can complement injury rehabilitation by improving underlying mechanics, its primary focus is on proactive postural health and body awareness for performance enhancement and physical stress management. We aim to build resilience and prevent future issues.
How long does it take to see results from this type of Wellness Coaching?
The timeline for seeing results varies, as every individual's body and needs are unique. However, most clients report increased body awareness and subtle improvements in comfort and movement efficiency within a few sessions, with more significant performance benefits emerging consistently over weeks as new patterns are established.
