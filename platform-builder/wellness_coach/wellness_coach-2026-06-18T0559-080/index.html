Frequently Asked Questions

How does a Wellness Coach specifically help with major life transitions?
As your Wellness Coach, I focus on the unique stressors that come with big life changes. We pinpoint how these transitions impact your nervous system, then develop concrete strategies to manage that stress, helping you maintain composure and make sound decisions even when circumstances are shifting rapidly around you.
What kind of strategies does a Wellness Coach offer for stress reduction?
My approach as your Wellness Coach isn't about generic relaxation. We implement targeted techniques for nervous system regulation, including specific breathing patterns, mindful body awareness, and practical routines that lower cortisol levels and activate your parasympathetic nervous system, helping you feel more grounded day-to-day.
Is Wellness Coaching suitable if I feel completely overwhelmed by my current situation?
Absolutely. Feeling overwhelmed is often a sign your nervous system is in overdrive. As your Wellness Coach, I provide a calm, structured space to process these feelings without judgment. We'll start with foundational techniques to stabilize your immediate stress response, then build your capacity to navigate complexities with greater ease.
How quickly can I expect to see improvements in my stress levels with a Wellness Coach?
While individual experiences vary, many clients report feeling a noticeable shift in their ability to manage stress and regulate their emotions within the first few weeks of working with me as their Wellness Coach. Consistency in applying the strategies we develop together is key to experiencing sustained, positive changes in your nervous system's responsiveness.
