Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is 'relationship healing' in the context of Wellness Coaching for seniors?
Relationship healing for you means gently addressing misunderstandings, unresolved feelings, or distance within your important connections. It's about finding ways to communicate with more kindness and clarity, fostering deeper empathy, and rebuilding trust, so your relationships feel more supportive and fulfilling.
How is this Wellness Coaching different for someone over 60?
Our Wellness Coaching is tailored specifically for you, acknowledging your rich life experiences and the unique dynamics of senior relationships. We focus on gentle, conversational methods, respecting your pace and wisdom, to help you navigate connections with family, friends, and your community in a way that feels supportive and meaningful to you.
Will this Wellness Coaching help me connect better with my adult children or grandchildren?
Absolutely. Many seniors find our Wellness Coaching helpful in bridging generational gaps. We'll explore gentle strategies for open communication, active listening, and understanding different perspectives, helping you build stronger, more harmonious bonds with your adult children and grandchildren, enriching your family life.
What if I feel hesitant or unsure about starting Wellness Coaching for my relationships?
It's completely normal to feel a bit hesitant, and we honor that. Our approach is always gentle and supportive. We start with a comfortable, no-pressure conversation to understand your hopes and concerns. You are in control of the process, and we'll move at a pace that feels right for you, ensuring you feel heard and respected every step of the way.
