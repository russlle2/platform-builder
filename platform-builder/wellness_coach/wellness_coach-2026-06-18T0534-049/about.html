How is nervous system regulation different from regular stress management?

Regular stress management gives you general advice. We focus exclusively on nervous system data — your heart rate variability, cortisol patterns, and recovery capacity — to build protocols that sync with your actual athletic stress triggers, not just life stress.

Can this fit into my existing training schedule?

Absolutely. We design protocols that take 5-7 minutes daily, placed at natural breaks in your routine (post-workout cooldown, pre-competition mental prep). You don’t add time — you optimize what you already do.

What if I’m already using a recovery tool like foam rolling?

We integrate with your existing tools but replace vague advice with precision. For example, instead of 'foam roll more,' we’ll specify exactly when to do it based on your nervous system state, so it actually works for your stress patterns.

Do you work with non-competitive athletes?

Yes. Whether you’re a weekend warrior or a pro, we focus on physical stress — from weekend hiking to competitive events. The protocols are tailored to your specific activity demands, not a one-size-fits-all approach.
