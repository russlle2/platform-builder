Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Wellness Coach specifically help my nervous system after a chronic illness or injury?
After chronic illness or injury, your nervous system can become overactive or dysregulated. A Wellness Coach helps you identify specific stressors and develop personalized, gentle practices. We focus on techniques to down-regulate your nervous system, promoting states of rest and repair, which are crucial for your recovery.
Is this Wellness Coach approach suitable if I have limited energy due to my condition?
Absolutely. Our Wellness Coach methods are designed with your energy levels in mind. We prioritize gentle, low-impact strategies that don't add to your fatigue. We start where you are, focusing on small, sustainable changes that build resilience without demanding excessive effort, always respecting your body's current capacity.
What kind of practices does a Wellness Coach use to help reduce stress for someone in recovery?
As your Wellness Coach, we utilize practices such as mindful breathing, gentle sensory grounding exercises, establishing predictable routines, and incorporating elements of nature to soothe your system. These are all tailored to your unique situation, helping to calm an overstimulated nervous system and foster a sense of security.
How long does it take to see results from Wellness Coach support for nervous system regulation?
The timeline for nervous system regulation is unique to everyone. As your Wellness Coach, we focus on consistent, gentle steps rather than quick fixes. You may notice subtle shifts in your sense of calm and well-being relatively quickly, with more significant and lasting changes building over weeks and months as you consistently apply the practices.
