Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is Wellness Coaching for college students dealing with anxiety and academic pressure?
It's a dedicated space where your Wellness Coach helps you explore the specific stressors you face in college – from exams and social dynamics to living away from home. We use ancient wisdom and trauma-informed care to build your emotional resilience, helping you manage anxiety and academic pressure with more calm and clarity.
How is this different from traditional therapy for student stress?
While therapy often focuses on diagnosis and past events, Wellness Coaching with us is forward-looking and action-oriented. Your Wellness Coach helps you identify current challenges, develop personalized strategies rooted in ancient healing, and build practical skills to navigate anxiety and academic pressure effectively in your daily college life.
I'm skeptical of 'spiritual' approaches. How does that fit into handling my college anxiety?
Our spiritual and soulful approach isn't about religion; it's about connecting with your inner wisdom and strength, drawing from timeless practices that foster peace and emotional balance. Your Wellness Coach will introduce you to accessible techniques that complement modern psychological understanding, helping you cultivate inner calm and reduce anxiety specifically for your college demands.
What if my anxiety feels too big to handle, even with a Wellness Coach?
It's completely normal to feel that way. Our trauma-informed Wellness Coaching acknowledges the depth of your feelings. We start where you are, offering gentle yet powerful tools to regulate your emotions and process overwhelming experiences. Your Wellness Coach provides consistent, compassionate support as you gradually build your capacity to face academic pressures and anxiety with greater ease.
