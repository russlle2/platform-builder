Frequently Asked Questions

As an athlete, why would I need a Wellness Coach specifically for relationships?
You push your body to its limits, and that can create unique pressures. These pressures can sometimes ripple into your personal relationships, leading to misunderstandings or feelings of being unheard. A Wellness Coach helps you navigate these specific challenges, ensuring your support system remains strong and understanding, rather than becoming another source of stress.
How does 'heart-centered care' from a Wellness Coach apply to my active lifestyle?
Heart-centered care means we focus on genuine connection, empathy, and understanding in your relationships. For an active person, this translates to better communication with loved ones about your commitments, managing expectations, and ensuring your emotional well-being is as robust as your physical conditioning. It's about finding peace and support off the track or field.
I'm experiencing physical stress; how will a Wellness Coach help with my relationships?
When you're physically stressed, your emotional bandwidth can shrink, making relational conflicts feel amplified. Your Wellness Coach helps you develop tools to communicate effectively even when you're feeling depleted, setting boundaries, and fostering understanding with those around you. We aim to reduce the emotional burden so you can focus on your recovery and performance.
What kind of commitment is required for relationship healing with a Wellness Coach?
The commitment involves showing up authentically, being open to new perspectives, and practicing the communication strategies we discuss. As your Wellness Coach, I'll guide you through a personalized process, and together we’ll establish a rhythm that fits your demanding schedule, focusing on consistent, manageable steps towards stronger, more fulfilling connections.
