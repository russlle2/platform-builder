Frequently Asked Questions

What specific emotional challenges does this Wellness Coaching address in postpartum?
Our Wellness Coaching specifically supports you through feelings of overwhelm, anxiety, sadness, intrusive thoughts, and the often-unspoken grief or disappointment related to your birth or postpartum experience. We provide tools and a safe space to process these complex emotions, moving towards inner peace and clarity.
How is your Wellness Coaching trauma-informed for new mothers?
Our trauma-informed approach means we recognize that childbirth and postpartum can be traumatic. We use gentle, non-triggering language, empower you with choice and control in our sessions, and help you build internal resources to regulate your nervous system. Your safety and emotional comfort are always paramount in our Wellness Coaching.
Is this Wellness Coaching a substitute for therapy or medical care?
No, our Wellness Coaching provides complementary emotional support and skill-building for your postpartum recovery. It is not a substitute for medical treatment or psychotherapy, especially if you are experiencing severe depression or anxiety. We can help you identify when professional medical or therapeutic intervention might be beneficial and offer referrals if needed.
What kind of commitment is required for your postpartum Wellness Coaching programs?
Our Wellness Coaching programs are designed for focused, results-driven support. We offer various packages with different session frequencies and durations, typically spanning several weeks or months. This allows for consistent progress and deep emotional processing, ensuring you receive the dedicated attention your postpartum healing deserves.
