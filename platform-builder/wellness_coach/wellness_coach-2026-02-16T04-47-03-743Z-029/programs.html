Program FAQs

Can I upgrade between paths?

Yes — members often start with a Launch then transition to Momentum. We map credits and previous work so nothing is lost.

What’s the expected time commitment?

Launch: ~2–4 hours/week during the first month. Momentum: ~30–60 minutes/week in total for reflections and micro-assignments.

Is there a trial?

I offer a short trial protocol (7-day Habit Starter) to evaluate fit before committing.
