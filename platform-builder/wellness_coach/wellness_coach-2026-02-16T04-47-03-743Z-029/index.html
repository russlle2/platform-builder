Questions I hear most

How quickly should I expect results?

Expect small wins in 2–6 weeks when you commit to one micro-habit. Deeper pattern shifts typically take 12+ weeks.

Is this medical advice?

No. Coaching here focuses on behaviors and systems. I do not diagnose or treat medical conditions. If you have clinical concerns, consult a licensed practitioner.

Can I join the membership after a program?

Yes. Many clients start with a focused pathway and then transition into membership for ongoing optimization and community labs.
