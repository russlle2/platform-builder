Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly does a Wellness Coach do for college students?
As your Wellness Coach, I provide a supportive space for you to explore what's causing you stress and anxiety in college. We'll work together to develop personalized practices and strategies that help you cultivate inner peace and a stronger sense of self, making your academic journey feel less overwhelming.
I'm not 'spiritual' – is this still for me?
Absolutely! When we talk about spiritual growth, it's not about any specific religion. It's about connecting with your own deepest values, finding meaning in your experiences, and nurturing your inner well-being. Your Wellness Coach will guide you in ways that resonate personally with you, no dogma required.
How can working with a Wellness Coach fit into my packed college schedule?
I understand how busy college life is. Our sessions are designed to be efficient and impactful, focusing on practical tools you can integrate into your daily routine. We'll find small, manageable ways to help you build inner calm without adding more stress to your plate.
Will a Wellness Coach help me with my grades or academic performance directly?
While I don't tutor or offer academic coaching, fostering inner peace and reducing anxiety often has a positive ripple effect on your academic focus and performance. When your mind is calmer, you can think more clearly, concentrate better, and approach challenges with greater confidence.
