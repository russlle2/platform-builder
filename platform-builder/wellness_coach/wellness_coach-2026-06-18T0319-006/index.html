Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Wellness Coach specifically help with my recovery from chronic illness?
As your Wellness Coach, I focus on supporting your nervous system and reducing stress, which are crucial for recovery from chronic illness or injury. We'll explore gentle, personalized strategies to calm your body's stress response, improve energy management, and build resilience without adding to your fatigue. It's about creating a supportive environment for your healing.
I'm already overwhelmed. Will Wellness Coaching add more to my plate?
Absolutely not. My role as your Wellness Coach is to simplify, not complicate. We start exactly where you are, with gentle, manageable steps. The goal is to reduce your overwhelm, not increase it. We'll focus on small, impactful changes that fit into your current capacity, ensuring you feel supported and not pressured.
What does 'nervous system regulation' mean for someone recovering from injury?
For someone recovering from injury, nervous system regulation means helping your body move out of a constant 'fight or flight' state, which often accompanies pain and physical stress. As your Wellness Coach, we'll practice techniques that encourage your body to rest and repair more effectively, reducing muscle tension, improving sleep, and fostering a greater sense of calm and safety, all of which aid in physical recovery.
Is Wellness Coaching suitable if my illness or injury is unpredictable?
Yes, completely. As your Wellness Coach, I understand that recovery isn't linear, especially with unpredictable conditions. Our work together is flexible and adaptable. We'll build strategies that allow you to adjust to your body's changing needs, providing tools for self-compassion and effective stress management on both good days and challenging ones. Consistency in self-care looks different for everyone, and we'll find what works for you.
