Frequently Asked Questions

Why is Wellness Coaching specifically for entrepreneurs and high-achievers?
Your professional demands are unique, often involving intense pressure, significant responsibility, and constant decision-making. My Wellness Coaching focuses on the specific stressors and emotional challenges prevalent in your world, providing targeted strategies for mental resilience, emotional processing, and sustained high performance that generic wellness approaches often miss.
How does trauma-informed care apply to my entrepreneurial life?
Trauma-informed care recognizes that past experiences, even seemingly minor ones, can influence your stress responses, decision-making, and ability to manage pressure today. In Wellness Coaching, we explore these underlying patterns not to dwell on the past, but to understand and reframe them, enabling you to build stronger emotional foundations and react to challenges with greater control and clarity.
What kind of results can I expect from your Wellness Coaching?
You can expect to develop a profound understanding of your stress triggers, acquire practical tools for emotional regulation, and cultivate a sense of inner calm amidst chaos. Clients often experience increased focus, improved decision-making, reduced burnout, and a renewed sense of purpose and drive, all contributing to both professional and personal fulfillment.
Is this Wellness Coaching a quick fix for stress?
While you will gain immediate, actionable strategies, my Wellness Coaching is not a quick fix. It's a strategic partnership designed for lasting change. We work together to build sustainable emotional resilience and processing skills that will serve you throughout your career and life, ensuring your well-being is as robust as your business strategy.
