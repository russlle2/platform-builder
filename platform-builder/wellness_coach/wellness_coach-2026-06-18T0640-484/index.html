Frequently Asked Questions

What does a Wellness Coach do specifically for someone recovering from chronic illness or injury?
As your Wellness Coach, I focus on helping you re-establish mental clarity and cognitive wellness. This means working on areas like improving focus, reducing brain fog, managing mental fatigue, and developing strategies to support your memory, all within the context of your recovery. We move at your pace, ensuring our work is supportive and empowering.
I'm new to wellness practices. Is this Wellness Coaching right for me?
Absolutely! Our Wellness Coaching is designed to be gentle and accessible, especially if you have no prior wellness experience. We start with the basics, breaking down techniques into manageable steps. My goal is to make these tools easy to understand and integrate into your daily life, without any pressure or complicated jargon.
How long does it take to see improvements in mental clarity with a Wellness Coach?
The timeline for seeing improvements with a Wellness Coach varies for everyone, as recovery is a unique process. However, many clients report noticing subtle shifts in their focus and mental energy within a few weeks of consistent practice. We celebrate every small win, building a foundation for lasting cognitive wellness at a pace that feels comfortable for you.
Will Wellness Coaching interfere with my medical treatment or physical therapy?
No, Wellness Coaching is designed to complement your existing medical treatment and physical therapy. We focus on the mental and cognitive aspects of your recovery, working in harmony with your healthcare team. I encourage you to discuss your Wellness Coaching with your doctors, as many find our approach helpful in supporting your overall wellbeing during recovery.
