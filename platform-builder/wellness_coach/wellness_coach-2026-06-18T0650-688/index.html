Frequently Asked Questions

I'm new to wellness practices. Is this Wellness Coach right for me?
Absolutely! Our approach is designed for everyone, especially those without prior wellness experience. We start with the basics, explaining everything clearly and making sure you feel comfortable and understood as we work to support your immune health and energy.
How can a Wellness Coach help my immune system during a big life change?
Major life changes can put extra stress on your body. As your Wellness Coach, we'll focus on practical strategies like gentle nutrition adjustments, stress-reducing techniques, and consistent energy-boosting habits that are known to support your body's natural defenses, helping you stay well.
What kind of commitment is required to work with a Wellness Coach?
We understand that you're navigating new circumstances, so our programs are flexible. We'll work together to create a plan that fits your current life, focusing on achievable steps. The commitment is about showing up for yourself and being open to gentle guidance as we build your vitality.
Will this Wellness Coach help me manage the stress that comes with transitions?
Yes, absolutely. Stress directly impacts your immune system and energy levels. While our primary focus is immune support and vitality, we naturally incorporate simple, accessible tools and strategies to help you navigate and reduce the emotional and physical impact of stress during your life changes.
