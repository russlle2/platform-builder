The Cohort Framework

A seasonal rhythm: grounding, activation, integration. Each cohort blends teaching, small-group practice, and personal micro-habit plans.

Ground (Weeks 1–2)

Reset your baseline with gentle assessments, morning rituals, and short practices to stabilize energy.

Activate (Weeks 3–6)

Layer habit increments and group accountability — build toward a consistent 10–20 minute daily routine.

Integrate (Weeks 7–10)

Embed practices into life rhythm; learn to adapt and sustain without burnout.

Program model: cohort learning with weekly live sessions, small pods, and guided reflections.
