Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Wellness Coach specifically help me, a parent with young children, improve my sleep?
As your Wellness Coach, we'll delve into the unique challenges you face as a parent, from interrupted nights to managing household stress. We'll craft personalized, nature-aligned strategies to help you establish calmer evening routines, optimize your sleep environment, and find moments of restorative rest that fit your family's rhythm, even if it's not always a full eight hours.
I feel too busy for another 'program'. How much time will this Wellness Coach commitment take?
We understand your time is precious! Our Wellness Coach approach is designed to integrate seamlessly into your already busy life. We focus on small, impactful changes that yield significant results without adding more to your plate. Our sessions are efficient, and the strategies we implement are practical, focusing on making your existing routines more sleep-supportive.
Will this Wellness Coach service help me manage the stress that keeps me from sleeping?
Absolutely. A core part of our Wellness Coach practice is addressing the underlying stress that often impacts parental sleep. We'll explore grounding techniques and natural ways to calm your nervous system, helping you transition from a state of 'on-the-go' to a more relaxed state, making it easier for your body and mind to truly rest.
Is this Wellness Coach service only about getting more sleep, or is it about deeper rest?
It's about both, with a strong emphasis on deeper, more restorative rest. As your Wellness Coach, we recognize that simply adding hours isn isn't always possible or enough. We'll work on improving the *quality* of your sleep and finding ways to integrate small, rejuvenating breaks throughout your day, helping you feel more sustained and present, even amidst the demands of parenting.
