How does Wellness Coaching specifically help with college anxiety?

Wellness Coaching provides you with structured, evidence-based techniques to understand and manage your body's stress response. We focus on nervous system regulation, helping you develop practical coping mechanisms for academic pressures, social anxieties, and future uncertainties inherent in college life. You'll learn tools to reduce physical tension and mental overwhelm.

Is this like therapy? What's the difference for a college student?

While both can be supportive, Wellness Coaching is distinct from therapy. As a Wellness Coach, I focus on present challenges and future-oriented strategies to help you achieve specific goals related to stress reduction and nervous system regulation. We don't delve into past traumas, but rather equip you with practical tools to navigate your current college experience more calmly and effectively. It's about skill-building and proactive well-being.

How much time will this coaching take out of my busy college schedule?

We understand your schedule is packed. Our sessions are designed to be efficient and impactful, typically 45-60 minutes, and we work together to integrate strategies into your existing routine. The aim is to provide you with tools that save you time and energy in the long run by reducing chronic stress and improving your focus, rather than adding another burden to your day.

What kind of techniques will I learn to manage academic pressure and anxiety?

You'll learn various evidence-based techniques including targeted breathing exercises, practical mindfulness for focus, cognitive restructuring for anxious thoughts, and specific nervous system regulation practices. These tools are designed to be actionable and directly applicable to common college stressors like exam anxiety, presentation nerves, and overwhelming workloads, helping you build a more stable internal state.
