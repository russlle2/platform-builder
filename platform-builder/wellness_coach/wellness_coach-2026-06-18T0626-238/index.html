Frequently Asked Questions

What kind of 'major life transitions' does a Wellness Coach support?
A Wellness Coach supports you through significant life changes like career shifts, relationship endings, relocating, parenthood adjustments, or other experiences that fundamentally alter your daily life and sense of self. Our focus is on the emotional processing and adaptation required during these pivotal times.
How does trauma-informed care differ from general emotional support from a Wellness Coach?
Trauma-informed care, as provided by your Wellness Coach, acknowledges that past experiences can shape your present reactions to change. We prioritize safety, trustworthiness, and empowerment, ensuring that our methods respect your emotional boundaries and promote healing without re-traumatization, focusing on integration rather than avoidance.
Is Wellness Coaching a substitute for therapy?
No, Wellness Coaching is not a substitute for therapy. Your Wellness Coach provides structured support for navigating specific life transitions and enhancing emotional resilience. If you are experiencing severe mental health conditions or have complex trauma, we recommend consulting with a licensed therapist or mental health professional.
What can I expect in a typical session with my Wellness Coach?
In a typical session, your Wellness Coach will guide you through structured conversations, exploring your current challenges, emotional responses, and desired outcomes. We'll introduce evidence-based techniques for emotional regulation and provide actionable strategies tailored to your specific life transition, fostering a sense of control and clarity.
