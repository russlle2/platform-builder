Understanding Your Postpartum Posture: Wellness Coach Insights

The demands of caring for a newborn—feeding, lifting, carrying—can significantly impact your spinal alignment and overall posture. Our Wellness Coach approach provides clear, evidence-based understanding of these changes. We'll identify specific areas of strain and develop strategies to support your body's recovery, promoting more comfortable movement and reduced pain as you navigate new motherhood.
