Frequently Asked Questions

What specific postural issues does a Wellness Coach address for new mothers?
As a Wellness Coach, we focus on concerns common to postpartum, such as upper back tension from feeding, pelvic instability, core weakness, and neck discomfort from looking down. We also address how everyday activities, like carrying your baby, impact your alignment, and provide gentle, corrective strategies.
How does Wellness Coaching help with body awareness during postpartum recovery?
Our Wellness Coach approach helps you reconnect with your body through guided attention and gentle movement exercises. We'll assist you in recognizing subtle cues of tension or misalignment, empowering you to adjust your posture proactively and feel more grounded and present in your physical self after childbirth.
Is Wellness Coaching suitable for mothers who had a C-section?
Absolutely. Our Wellness Coach services are designed to be safe and beneficial for all new mothers, including those recovering from a C-section. We adapt all recommendations to your specific recovery needs, focusing on gentle movements that support scar tissue healing and abdominal muscle reintegration without strain.
What can I expect from my first Wellness Coach session regarding postural health?
In your initial Wellness Coach session, we'll have a compassionate discussion about your current physical experiences, daily routines, and any areas of discomfort. We'll then gently assess your posture and movement patterns to create a personalized plan focusing on small, actionable steps to improve your comfort and body awareness, always respecting your postpartum recovery pace.
