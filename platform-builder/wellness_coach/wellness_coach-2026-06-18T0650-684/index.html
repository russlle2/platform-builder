Frequently Asked Questions

What kind of wellness support does a Wellness Coach offer for someone recovering from illness?
As your Wellness Coach, I focus on gentle, practical steps to support your immune system and overall vitality. This includes guidance on restorative sleep, nourishing food choices, appropriate movement, and stress reduction techniques, all tailored to your specific recovery needs and energy levels, ensuring it feels accessible and supportive.
Do I need any previous wellness experience to work with a Wellness Coach?
Absolutely not! My approach is designed for everyone, especially those who are new to wellness practices or feel overwhelmed by their recovery. As your Wellness Coach, I meet you exactly where you are, offering clear, simple guidance without any jargon or expectations of prior knowledge. We'll build your understanding together.
How is this Wellness Coach support different from medical treatment?
My Wellness Coach services complement your medical care by focusing on lifestyle factors that support your recovery and build vitality. I don't diagnose, treat, or prescribe. Instead, I help you implement daily habits that reinforce your health, strengthen your immune system, and improve your quality of life, all in collaboration with your healthcare team.
What if my energy levels fluctuate greatly during recovery while working with a Wellness Coach?
Fluctuating energy is completely normal during recovery, and my Wellness Coach approach is built to be flexible. We'll create plans that can be easily adjusted to accommodate your good days and your more challenging ones, ensuring you never feel pressured to do more than your body can comfortably manage. The goal is consistent, gentle progress, not perfection.
