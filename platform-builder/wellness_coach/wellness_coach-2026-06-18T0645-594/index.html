Frequently Asked Questions

How does Wellness Coaching support my spiritual growth while I'm recovering from a chronic illness or injury?
Our Wellness Coaching is specifically tailored to meet you where you are. We focus on gentle practices like guided meditation, mindful awareness, and contemplative exercises that don't place additional strain on your physical body but significantly nourish your inner landscape, helping you find profound peace and a renewed sense of connection.
Will this Wellness Coach approach conflict with my medical treatment for my chronic condition?
Absolutely not. Our Wellness Coaching is designed to complement your medical care. We encourage open communication with your healthcare providers. Our aim is to support your emotional and spiritual well-being, which can positively impact your overall healing, without interfering with any prescribed treatments or therapies you are undergoing.
I feel emotionally drained from my illness/injury. Can Wellness Coaching truly help me find inner peace?
It’s completely understandable to feel drained. Our Wellness Coach sessions provide a safe and supportive space for you to explore these feelings without judgment. We introduce gentle techniques to help you process emotional fatigue, cultivate self-compassion, and gradually build inner resources that foster a sense of calm and resilience, even when things are tough.
What kind of time commitment does Wellness Coaching for spiritual growth require?
The time commitment for Wellness Coaching is flexible and designed to fit your energy levels. Sessions are typically once a week or bi-weekly, and we'll work together to integrate short, manageable practices into your daily routine. The goal is sustainable, gentle integration, not added stress, ensuring you can consistently nurture your spiritual well-being.
