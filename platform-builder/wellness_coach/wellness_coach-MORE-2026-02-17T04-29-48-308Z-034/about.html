Why a cohort?

A cohort is a small, scheduled container where you test a practice for a few weeks and share what changes. The point is momentum: you get guided structure plus peer feedback so adjustments happen faster than going it alone.

What to expect in a session

  • Actionable habit to try for the coming week
  • A short reflection framework you can repeat on your own
  • A focused group review to refine the habit

Common questions

Is this therapy?
No — this is coaching and systems for habits and routines, not clinical care.
How long is a cohort?
Most run for 6 weeks: a rhythm that lets you test practices and iterate.
Can I join mid-cycle?
Sometimes — reach out and we'll see which step-in options fit the current group.
What kind of time commitment?
Weekly sessions plus short daily micro-practices. Designed to fit busy lives.
