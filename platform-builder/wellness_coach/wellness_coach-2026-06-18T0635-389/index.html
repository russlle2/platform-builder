Frequently Asked Questions

How does Wellness Coaching address my chronic pain if it's stress-related?
Many physical discomforts, like chronic back pain or migraines, are deeply intertwined with stress and burnout. Your Wellness Coach will help you identify these connections, teach you somatic techniques to release stored tension, and develop practical strategies to mitigate stress impact on your body.
I'm a busy professional; how can I fit Wellness Coaching into my demanding schedule?
We understand your time is valuable. Our Wellness Coaching sessions are designed to be efficient and effective. We focus on practical, actionable strategies you can integrate into your existing routine, building sustainable habits that support your physical and mental well-being without adding more stress.
Is Wellness Coaching a substitute for seeing a doctor or physical therapist for my pain?
No, Wellness Coaching is a complementary approach. We do not diagnose or treat medical conditions. Your Wellness Coach will work alongside any existing medical care you're receiving, focusing on lifestyle, stress management, and somatic practices to support your overall physical comfort and resilience.
What specific physical pain issues can Wellness Coaching help me with?
Your Wellness Coach can help you address a range of stress-induced physical discomforts often experienced by professionals, including chronic neck and shoulder tension, lower back pain, tension headaches, jaw clenching, and even digestive discomfort, by addressing the underlying burnout and somatic patterns.
