Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Wellness Coach specifically help with my athletic pain?
Your Wellness Coach focuses on somatic practices that target the root causes of athletic stress and pain. We don't just treat symptoms; we work with your body's inherent intelligence to release deep tension, improve movement patterns, and build resilience, helping you move past nagging aches and prevent recurrence.
Is this approach different from physical therapy or chiropractic care?
Yes, while complementary, a Wellness Coach offers a distinct perspective. We emphasize somatic awareness and mind-body integration, teaching you self-regulation tools to understand and respond to your body's signals. This empowers you to actively participate in your own physical well-being, going beyond passive treatments to cultivate lasting comfort and performance.
How long does it take to see results with Wellness Coaching for physical stress?
Results vary depending on the nature and duration of your physical stress. Many clients report initial shifts in comfort and awareness within a few sessions. Your Wellness Coach works with you to establish realistic goals, fostering a gradual yet profound process of releasing old patterns and integrating new ways of moving and being, leading to sustainable relief.
Will Wellness Coaching interfere with my current training schedule?
Absolutely not. Your Wellness Coach tailors sessions to integrate seamlessly with your existing training. In fact, by reducing physical stress and improving body mechanics, our work often enhances your training capacity and recovery, allowing you to perform better and enjoy your activities more fully, without compromise.
