How does Wellness Coaching specifically help with nervous system regulation during chronic recovery?

Our Wellness Coaching provides structured support and time-honored practices specifically adapted to gently calm an activated nervous system, which is common during chronic recovery. We help you identify your unique triggers and cultivate daily rituals that foster deep relaxation and restore internal balance, without taxing your already compromised system.

I'm already feeling fatigued from my illness/injury; will these practices add to my overwhelm?

Absolutely not. Our approach is rooted in profound gentleness. We understand the delicate nature of your energy levels during recovery. The practices we introduce are designed to be restorative, requiring minimal effort and maximum benefit, aiming to reduce fatigue by calming the underlying stress response, rather than adding to it.

Can Wellness Coaching integrate with my current medical treatments?

Yes, our Wellness Coaching is designed to be a complementary support, not a replacement for medical care. We encourage open communication with your healthcare providers. Our role is to enhance your well-being by addressing the emotional and energetic aspects of recovery, working in harmony with your medical team's directives.

What kind of ancient healing traditions do you draw from in your Wellness Coach practice?

We draw from various traditions that emphasize the mind-body connection and the power of subtle energies, such as mindful breathwork, gentle movement principles, and guided contemplative practices. These methods are adapted to be accessible and beneficial for individuals navigating the complexities of chronic illness and injury, focusing on internal harmony.
