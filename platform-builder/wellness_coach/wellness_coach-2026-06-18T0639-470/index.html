Frequently Asked Questions

Why do new mothers experience 'brain fog' and how can a Wellness Coach help?
Postpartum brain fog is a real experience, often linked to hormonal shifts, sleep deprivation, and the immense mental load of new motherhood. As your Wellness Coach, I help you understand these factors and provide structured strategies to manage them. We’ll work on specific cognitive exercises, organizational techniques, and lifestyle adjustments to sharpen your focus and reduce mental fatigue.
Is this just about managing stress, or does your Wellness Coaching specifically address cognitive function?
While stress management is a component of overall wellness, my Wellness Coaching specifically targets mental clarity and cognitive wellness for new mothers. We go beyond general stress reduction to address areas like memory recall, sustained attention, decision-making, and reducing mental overwhelm, all tailored to your postpartum experience.
How quickly can I expect to see improvements in my mental clarity with your Wellness Coach support?
Improvement timelines vary for everyone, as each postpartum journey is unique. However, clients often report feeling more organized and experiencing small shifts in focus within a few weeks of consistent practice. My Wellness Coaching emphasizes sustainable progress, building habits that lead to lasting cognitive benefits over time.
What if my schedule is unpredictable with a new baby? Can I still benefit from your Wellness Coaching?
Absolutely. I understand that a new mother's schedule is rarely predictable. My Wellness Coaching approach is flexible and adaptable. We’ll design strategies that fit into your unique routine, even if it's fragmented. Our sessions and exercises are structured to be manageable, ensuring you can integrate them effectively without adding more pressure.
