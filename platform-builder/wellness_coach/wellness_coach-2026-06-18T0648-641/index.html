Frequently Asked Questions

What does a Wellness Coach do specifically for seniors like me?
A Wellness Coach for seniors focuses on gentle, age-appropriate strategies to enhance your immune system and overall vitality. We help you identify simple dietary changes, movement routines, and lifestyle habits that support your health, always respecting your comfort and individual needs. It's about empowering you to feel strong and vibrant.
How can a Wellness Coach help me build my immune system at this stage of life?
We'll explore easy ways to incorporate immune-supporting nutrients into your meals, discuss gentle activities that boost circulation and mood, and review sleep and stress reduction techniques. The goal is to fortify your body's natural defenses in ways that are enjoyable and sustainable for you, without any strenuous demands.
Is Wellness Coaching suitable if I have existing health concerns?
Absolutely. Our approach is always gentle and complementary. We never replace the advice of your medical doctors but work alongside it. As your Wellness Coach, we can help you integrate your healthcare recommendations into daily practices that promote overall well-being, always with your safety and comfort as the priority. Please discuss any new wellness approaches with your doctor.
What can I expect from my first session with a Wellness Coach?
In your first session, we'll have a warm, unhurried conversation to understand your current health goals, what's important to you, and any concerns you might have. It's a chance for us to get to know each other, and for you to share what kind of support would best serve your vitality and immune health. We'll discuss a personalized, gentle path forward together.
