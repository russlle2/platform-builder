Frequently Asked Questions

I'm so tired, I don't even know where to start with sleep improvement. Can a Wellness Coach really help me?
Absolutely! That's exactly why I'm here. As your Wellness Coach, I meet you right where you are. We'll break down the overwhelming feeling into small, manageable steps. You don't need any prior wellness experience; I'll guide you gently through understanding your current patterns and introducing simple, effective ways to improve your rest.
My household schedule is chaotic with young children. How can I possibly fit in sleep coaching?
I specialize in working with parents of young children and understand busy schedules. My approach as a Wellness Coach is designed to be flexible and integrated into your existing life, not an added burden. We focus on practical strategies that fit your family's specific needs, helping you find moments for restorative rest without disrupting your daily flow.
Is this just about getting more hours of sleep, or something else?
While increasing sleep duration can be a part of it, as your Wellness Coach, I also focus heavily on the *quality* of your rest. It's about helping you achieve restorative sleep, so you wake up feeling genuinely refreshed and more resilient to daily stresses. We'll explore relaxation techniques and evening routines that promote deeper, more effective rest.
I've tried other sleep advice before and it didn't work. What makes working with a Wellness Coach different?
Unlike generic advice, working with me as your Wellness Coach provides personalized, one-on-one support tailored to your unique family situation and challenges. We address your specific stressors and create solutions that are sustainable for *your* life. There's no one-size-fits-all here; it's about building a gentle, effective path to better sleep that truly works for you.
