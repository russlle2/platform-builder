Frequently Asked Questions

How is your approach to sleep different for new mothers in the postpartum period?
Our approach acknowledges the unique hormonal shifts, physical recovery, and emotional demands of postpartum motherhood. We move beyond generic sleep hygiene, tailoring strategies that consider your infant's needs, your body's healing, and your evolving identity as a mother, integrating both ancient wisdom and modern understanding of restorative rest.
I feel too tired to even think about therapy; how can a Private Practice Therapist help me find more rest?
We understand this profound exhaustion. Our sessions are designed to be gentle and supportive, requiring minimal effort from you initially. We start by identifying small, actionable steps that can bring immediate, albeit subtle, shifts towards greater calm and rest, building momentum slowly as your capacity returns. Your comfort and ease are paramount.
Will I have to make drastic changes to my routine to improve my sleep?
Not at all. We believe in gradual, sustainable shifts rather than overwhelming overhauls. We'll work collaboratively to identify subtle adjustments within your existing routine that can create significant impacts on your ability to rest, honoring your current capacities and responsibilities as a new mother. We focus on integration, not disruption.
What if my baby isn't sleeping through the night? Can a Private Practice Therapist still help me find rest?
Absolutely. Our focus isn't solely on 'sleeping through the night,' but on cultivating deeper, more restorative rest whenever you can find it. We help you navigate fragmented sleep with greater resilience, reduce anxiety around wake-ups, and maximize the quality of your rest, even in short bursts. We help you find peace amidst the interrupted nights.
