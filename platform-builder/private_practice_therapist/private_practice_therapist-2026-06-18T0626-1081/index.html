Frequently Asked Questions

How does Private Practice Therapy specifically address chronic illness or injury recovery?
Our Private Practice Therapy focuses on the often-overlooked emotional and nervous system impact of chronic illness or injury. We help you develop tools to manage the persistent stress, pain, and uncertainty that can accompany these conditions, fostering a calmer internal environment supportive of your overall recovery.
Will this Private Practice Therapy interfere with my medical treatments?
Absolutely not. Our Private Practice Therapy is designed to complement your medical care, not replace it. We collaborate with your healthcare team when appropriate, ensuring our work supports your existing treatment plan by enhancing your emotional resilience and nervous system well-being.
What kind of techniques will we use in Private Practice Therapy for nervous system regulation?
In our Private Practice Therapy, we integrate techniques such as breathwork, gentle somatic practices, mindfulness, and cognitive reframing. These methods are carefully chosen to help you gently recalibrate your nervous system, reducing hyper-vigilance and promoting a sense of safety and calm within your body.
How long does it take to see results in Private Practice Therapy for stress reduction?
The path to stress reduction and nervous system regulation after chronic illness or injury is unique for everyone in Private Practice Therapy. While some individuals experience shifts in a few sessions, sustained change often unfolds over time. Our focus is on building lasting skills and internal resources, honoring your individual pace and progress.
