Fees & Packages

My approach is individual first — here are the common ways I structure time and support. If you’d like a different rhythm (e.g., more frequent check-ins or brief consultations), let’s discuss it when you book.

Clarity Session A single focused 50-minute appointment to explore a specific concern.
Starting $160
Ongoing Therapy Weekly or biweekly clinical sessions, 45–60 minutes. Recommended when deeper relational or mood patterns are in focus.
Starting $180 / session
Short-Term Intensive A 4-session package intended to create momentum during transitional periods.
Package $660
Maintenance Add-On A reduced-rate check-in option for clients who prefer less frequent support between blocks of therapy.
Ask for details

Insurance: I do not bill insurance directly in most cases. I can provide a receipt (superbill) you may submit to your insurer; coverage varies by plan. Fees are subject to periodic review. There are no guarantees of outcomes; therapy aims to support exploration and change over time.

Sessions are 45–60 minutes unless otherwise arranged. I maintain clinical records in a secure system. Communications by email or text are used for scheduling and brief touchpoints; they are not a substitute for clinical work. Confidentiality is protected within legal limits (e.g., risk of harm, child or elder safety, court orders). If you have questions about privacy, please ask — I will explain limits clearly.

Cancellation: Please provide at least 48 hours' notice when possible. Late cancellations or missed sessions may incur a fee.
