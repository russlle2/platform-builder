How I generally work with people

Think of therapy as a series of small experiments rather than a straight ladder to a destination. We name a question, try something modest, observe results, and decide together how to proceed.

1. Clarify

A focused first meeting to surface the core concern and practical goals. We’ll agree on what would count as helpful change over a few weeks.

2. Try

Small, testable changes between sessions—communication experiments, brief routines, or new boundaries. We track what lands and what doesn’t.

3. Reflect

We look at outcomes, adjust the plan, and settle on what next steps feel realistic and meaningful to you.

Boundaries, confidentiality & crisis guidance

Clinical conversations are confidential. Exceptions include imminent risk to life, suspected abuse of a child or vulnerable adult, and situations ordered by a court. If these arise we will communicate clearly about what must be disclosed and why.

Cancellations and session policies are discussed at intake so expectations are mutual and clear.

If you are in immediate danger or a medical emergency, call local emergency services. For urgent mental health support, please contact your local crisis line or national helpline in your area.
