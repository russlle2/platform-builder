Reach out when you need steady company

If you're exploring therapy or returning after a pause, tell me briefly what's on your mind. I respond to messages in 48 business hours and will suggest next steps tailored to your situation.

Session length & scheduling: Typical sessions are 50 minutes. We agree on frequency together. I ask for 24 hours notice to reschedule when possible.

Confidentiality: Your information is kept private within legal and ethical limits. Exceptions include imminent risk of harm, suspected abuse of a minor or vulnerable adult, or court order.

I offer evidence-informed psychotherapy and short-term focused work. I do not provide emergency services, formal legal testimony, or medication management. If you need those, I will help you locate appropriate resources.

If you are in immediate danger, call your local emergency number right away. For suicide or crisis support in the U.S., call or text 988. If you're outside the U.S., contact local emergency resources.
