Frequently asked questions

Short answers from a clinician—practical details and what to expect.

How do I know if we’re a good fit?
A fit is often about feeling heard and understood in the first few sessions. I view the first 1–3 sessions as exploratory: we test ways of working together and set a shared plan. If you’re unsure, consider a Clarity Session to start.
Do you take insurance?
I generally do not bill insurance directly. I can provide a receipt (superbill) you can submit to your carrier. Coverage varies; contact your insurer for details. If cost is a barrier, mention it when you reach out and we can talk about options.
What is telehealth like?
Virtual sessions use a secure platform. We will check connection and privacy at the start of each session. If both parties agree, in-person options may be available depending on location and scheduling.
How long will therapy take?
There is no fixed timeline. Some concerns can be addressed in short-term work, others benefit from longer-term exploration. We will periodically review goals and progress together.
What if I need support outside session?
I am available by brief message for scheduling and urgent administrative matters. I do not provide crisis monitoring through email or text. For immediate danger, call emergency services.

Confidentiality is central. What you share is protected except in specific legal or safety-related circumstances (e.g., imminent danger to self/others, suspected abuse). Records are stored securely. We will discuss telehealth privacy and limits during intake.

Cancellation policy: When possible, give 48 hours' notice. Fees for late cancellations are described in the intake paperwork.

When to choose a single session

A focused Clarity Session is often right when you need one careful conversation to orient next steps. It’s also a reasonable first step if you’re testing the fit.
