What to expect in a first few visits

  • Focused listening: A chance to describe the pattern or problem without pressure to be fixed on the spot.
  • Shared formulation: A simple clinical view of what maintains the difficulty and what small experiments might help.
  • Flexible pacing: Sessions that match the intensity you need—brief check-ins, standard therapy, or a short series to tackle a specific challenge.

I work within established clinical and ethical boundaries. Sessions are confidential within legal limits: if there is imminent risk to life or safety, or as required by law (e.g., court orders, certain reports), I must act to reduce harm. We will talk about what that means for your care before we begin.

Typical session length is 50 minutes for standard therapy; there are also 30-minute and extended options. Cancellations are requested 48 hours in advance when possible; we will clarify fees and policies during scheduling.

If you are in immediate danger: contact emergency services. If you are in the U.S. and experiencing a mental health crisis, you can call or text 988 for support. If you are elsewhere, contact local emergency responders or crisis lines in your region.

Is therapy right for me right now?

  • "I don’t know what to talk about." — That’s common. We’ll start where you are and focus on what matters most this week.

  • "I’m not sure I can commit long-term." — Short series and single-session formats are available to test whether working together is useful.
