A little about the person behind the room

I work with adults who are navigating crossroads—whether that's a shift in relationships, work, identity, or rhythm of life. My approach is rooted in practical reflection, steady curiosity, and a collaborative pace that respects your time and capacity.

A clinician's note

I'm a licensed clinician who draws from relational, narrative, and skills-based methods. In sessions we name what matters, test small shifts, and notice the effect. Therapy is a careful, unfolding conversation—not a rush to fix. Questions we often begin with: What needs holding? Where is a small experiment possible? What would indicate progress for you?

Conversations in sessions are confidential with certain legal exceptions: imminent risk of harm to yourself or others, suspected abuse of a vulnerable person, or court-ordered disclosures. We will talk clearly about how these boundaries apply if they arise.
