Clinical stance & how we work together

My approach blends close listening with practical interventions aimed at changing what isn’t serving you. I draw from cognitive-behavioral skills, relational perspectives, and mindful practices—selecting tools that fit the work you want to do.

Scope & limits
I provide psychotherapy and structured clinical support. I do not provide emergency care or community crisis response. If you are in danger or need urgent help, call emergency services or a local crisis hotline.
Confidentiality
Communications are confidential except where required by law (e.g., safety concerns). We will discuss limits to confidentiality as part of intake so you have clear expectations.
