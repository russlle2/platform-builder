Frequently asked questions

Below are common questions about how I work, what to expect, and practical policies. If you don’t see what you need, please reach out directly.

How does the membership work?
The membership provides regular, scheduled sessions plus modest between-session messaging, and scheduling priority. It is intended for ongoing support rather than urgent matters. Membership details (session length, frequency, and messaging limits) are discussed when you enroll.
Can I use insurance?
I do not bill insurance directly. You can request a receipt to submit to your insurer for potential out-of-network reimbursement. Insurance companies have different rules about coverage and clinical notes—if you plan to seek reimbursement, I can provide a superbill upon request.
What if I need to cancel?
Please notify as early as possible; a 48-hour notice is requested when feasible. Late cancellations or missed appointments may be charged a fee. Specifics are reviewed during intake and included in the intake packet.
Is telehealth available?
Yes. Sessions are offered via secure video. Telephone may be used when appropriate and agreed upon in advance. Location-based rules apply; services are provided in compliance with licensure and jurisdictional requirements.
How will my privacy be protected?
Sessions are confidential and stored according to clinical record-keeping standards. Exceptions include risk of harm to self or others, suspected abuse, or legal processes. Records are not shared without consent except in these limited circumstances or as required by law.
What if I’m in crisis?
If you are in immediate danger, please call emergency services or your local crisis line. This practice does not provide emergency services. For non-immediate safety planning, we can address concerns during scheduled sessions or via suggested community resources.
What happens at the first session?
We review confidential intake information, discuss goals and preferences, and outline a plan for next steps. The first meeting is an opportunity to see whether the therapeutic approach feels like a good fit. You are welcome to bring the self-screening summary created by the online wizard.
Is therapy guaranteed to help?
Therapy is a collaborative process with no guarantees. I offer clinical guidance and practical skills to support your goals. Progress varies by person and circumstance; we will evaluate progress together and adjust the plan as needed.
How do I prepare for my first appointment?
You may begin the self-screening intake wizard (it’s brief and creates a one-page summary you can bring to the session). Also consider your primary goals for therapy and any logistical details (scheduling preferences, immediate concerns).
