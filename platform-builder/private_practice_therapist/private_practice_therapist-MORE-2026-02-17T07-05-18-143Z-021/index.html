How membership works

  1. Orientation session: 30–45 minutes to clarify goals and suitability for membership.
  2. Monthly sessions: Scheduled at a rhythm we agree on; flexible rescheduling within policy.
  3. Between-session tools: Short structured practices and a private message channel for administrative check-ins (not for emergencies).

Membership is a collaborative agreement. It closes the gap between single-session support and ongoing care without making clinical promises about outcomes.

Who this is for

  • People navigating job-related shifts, relocations, or role changes.
  • Those seeking a consistent clinical relationship to manage anxiety, avoidant cycles, or relationship patterns.
  • Individuals who want structured reflection tools between sessions.

Sessions are clinical and confidential. They are not a replacement for emergency services or crisis care.
