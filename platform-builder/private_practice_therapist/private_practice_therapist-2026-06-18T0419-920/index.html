Frequently Asked Questions

How is seasonal wellness different from regular therapy?
Seasonal wellness focuses on anticipating and preventing stress before it escalates. Instead of waiting for burnout, we adjust your plan each semester based on academic demands — like building coping tools in August so you're ready for October midterms.
Can I get help without missing class?
Absolutely. We offer flexible scheduling, including short virtual sessions during your free periods. Many students fit therapy into their routine, sometimes even between back-to-back classes.
Do you work with students dealing with academic anxiety?
Yes, specifically. We help students manage performance anxiety, perfectionism, and the pressure to succeed in school. Our approach is practical: we'll work on strategies to calm your mind during exams and stay focused during long study sessions.
What makes your preventive care unique?
Preventive care here means we start before you hit crisis mode. We assess your academic schedule and stress points at the beginning of the semester, then check in regularly to adjust. It's like a health checkup for your mental well-being before the semester gets overwhelming.
