Why Seasonal Wellness Works for Your Semester

Academic stress follows a rhythm: midterms peak in October, finals in December, and spring brings its own challenges. Seasons Therapy helps you build resilience before the pressure mounts. We focus on the specific stressors of each season — from exam crunch times to spring semester slumps — with practical tools you can use immediately.
