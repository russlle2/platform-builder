About the clinician

I offer dedicated, confidential support for people navigating life shifts, persistent worry, or transitions. My work is grounded in well-established clinical approaches and shaped around the practical realities you bring.

Start a quick intake

Clinical orientation

I combine cognitive, relational, and behavioral strategies to support clearer choices and steadier emotional balance. Treatment is collaborative: we set small, measurable steps and re-evaluate what helps across sessions.

Confidentiality & limits

Clinical conversations are treated as private. Mandatory reporting and safety concerns (imminent harm to self or others) are exceptions where I must act to ensure safety. This service is not crisis management; if you are in immediate danger call emergency services or a local crisis line.

Pre-consultation reflection (self-screening)

A short set of guided prompts to help you notice patterns and prepare questions for a first conversation. This is not a diagnosis—it's a tool for clarity.

Practical support between sessions

When helpful, I offer short, structured check-ins and simple practice tasks you can use between sessions. These are intended to support skill building, not to replace therapy time.
