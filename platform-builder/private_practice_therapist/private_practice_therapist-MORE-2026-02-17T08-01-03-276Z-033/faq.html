Questions people ask before they book

Below are practical answers to common questions. If you don’t see what you need, reach out via {{EMAIL}}.

How do I know if this is the right fit?
A brief initial consult is a good way to see whether my style and schedule match your needs. Use the self-check to prepare focused questions for that consult.
Do you take insurance?
I do not bill insurance directly. You will receive a receipt that you can submit to your insurance for out-of-network reimbursement; policies vary.
What is your cancellation policy?
I request at least 24 hours' notice when possible. Late cancellations or missed sessions may be billed at the session rate; we’ll clarify when we book.
How do you handle confidentiality?
Notes are kept securely and communications are treated as confidential except where disclosure is required by law (e.g., imminent risk to someone). We’ll review limits to confidentiality during an intake.
What happens if I’m in crisis?
This practice is not an emergency service. If you are at immediate risk, call local emergency services or your local crisis line. If you are unsure where to find crisis resources, include that in the self-check and we’ll address it upfront.
