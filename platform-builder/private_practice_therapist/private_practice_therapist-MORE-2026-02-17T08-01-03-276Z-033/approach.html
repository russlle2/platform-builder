How sessions are structured

My practice focuses on steady progress through collaborative planning, skill practice, and attention to real-world constraints. Each meeting is an opportunity to reflect, try something tangible, and plan the next step.

1. Clarify
We name what matters this week and what you'd like to test.
2. Try
We review practice, try a short in-session strategy, and notice what shifts.
3. Plan
Collaboratively set small, feasible steps to use between sessions.

What to expect from membership

Membership is a planning frame: regular sessions plus brief between-session touchpoints. It offers predictability while keeping clinical decisions flexible. Pricing and program details are shared on the Investment page.

  • Scheduled therapy sessions at a consistent cadence.
  • Short, pre-arranged check-ins when needed.
  • Clear boundaries about scope and crisis response.

Process and timing

Early sessions focus on understanding patterns and making a realistic plan. We revisit goals every few sessions and adjust priorities. Progress is individualized and paced to what is sustainable for you.

Boundaries & crisis note

While I work to be responsive, this practice is not a crisis service. If you are at immediate risk, please contact emergency services or your local crisis line. Confidentiality is upheld within legal and ethical limits.

Before your first appointment

If helpful, complete the short self-check to bring to the consult. It highlights priorities and helps us use the first session efficiently.

Micro-practices
