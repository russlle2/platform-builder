How can Private Practice Therapy help me specifically during postpartum recovery?

As a Private Practice Therapist, I focus on helping new mothers navigate the unique stresses of this time. We'll explore gentle techniques to soothe your nervous system, manage overwhelming emotions, and reduce feelings of anxiety or exhaustion, all tailored to your postpartum experience.

I'm new to therapy. Do I need any prior wellness experience to start Private Practice Therapy?

Absolutely not! My approach as a Private Practice Therapist is gentle and accessible. You don't need any prior experience with therapy or wellness practices. We'll start exactly where you are, making sure you feel comfortable and understood every step of the way as we work on stress reduction.

How quickly can I expect to feel a difference in my stress levels?

Every person's experience is unique, but many new mothers report feeling a sense of relief and increased calm after just a few Private Practice Therapy sessions. Our goal is to build sustainable practices for nervous system regulation, helping you feel more grounded over time, at your own pace.

What if I feel too overwhelmed to even schedule an appointment for Private Practice Therapy?

I understand that feeling completely. Taking that first step can be daunting. Please know that reaching out is the hardest part. You can send a brief email or leave a short message, and I'll respond gently to help you get started. There's no pressure, just understanding support.
