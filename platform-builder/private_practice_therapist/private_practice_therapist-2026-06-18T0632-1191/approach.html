Reclaiming Your Narrative: A Trauma-Informed Private Practice Therapy Approach

Your experience with chronic illness or injury can feel like an intrusion, a disruption to your sense of self. Our Private Practice Therapy embraces a trauma-informed approach, recognizing how these events impact your nervous system and perceptions. We collaborate with you to gently re-establish a sense of safety and agency, honoring your unique pace as you re-author your story with strength and compassion.
