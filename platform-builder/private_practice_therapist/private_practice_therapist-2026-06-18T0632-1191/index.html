Frequently Asked Questions

How does Private Practice Therapy specifically support me if I'm recovering from chronic illness or injury?
Our Private Practice Therapy acknowledges that your recovery is multifaceted. We focus on the emotional weight that often accompanies physical challenges, helping you process feelings of anger, sadness, fear, or a sense of altered identity. Your therapy will be attuned to your physical limitations and emotional needs, providing gentle strategies for coping and finding inner peace.
Is your Private Practice Therapy equipped to handle the emotional distress related to medical trauma?
Yes, our Private Practice Therapy is deeply rooted in trauma-informed care. We understand that medical experiences can be traumatic, leaving lasting emotional imprints. We create a secure environment for you to gently explore these experiences, developing tools to manage distress and reclaim a sense of control and safety within yourself.
How can Private Practice Therapy help me cope with the ongoing uncertainty of a chronic condition?
Living with chronic illness or injury often involves a landscape of uncertainty. Our Private Practice Therapy helps you cultivate resilience and acceptance, not as resignation, but as a way to find peace within changing circumstances. We explore strategies for managing anxiety, embracing self-compassion, and finding moments of calm amidst the unknown.
Will Private Practice Therapy sessions accommodate my fluctuating energy levels due to my condition?
Absolutely. Your well-being is our priority. Our Private Practice Therapy sessions are designed to be flexible and accommodating to your energy levels. We can adjust session length, frequency, or even explore different modalities if you're experiencing a particularly challenging day, ensuring your therapy remains a supportive, not strenuous, experience.
