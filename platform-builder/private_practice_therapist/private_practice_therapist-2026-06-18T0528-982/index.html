Frequently Asked Questions

How does seasonal wellness specifically help entrepreneurs?
Entrepreneurs face pressure during business peak seasons like Q4 or product launches. Our plans adjust to these cycles — so you’re not adding stress to your schedule. We integrate prevention into your existing rhythm, turning seasonal shifts into opportunities for calm focus.
I'm busy. How much time does preventive care actually take?
We design micro-practices that fit your routine: 5 minutes of breathwork before a big meeting, or a 15-minute afternoon reset. These small, consistent actions prevent the need for longer recovery later, so you maintain energy without adding hours to your day.
Why blend Eastern and Western approaches?
Western science tells us what works; Eastern practices show us how to embody it. Acupressure targets stress physiology (Western), while mindfulness teaches you to notice rising tension (Eastern). Together, this creates a system that acts before you're overwhelmed — not after.
How soon will I notice a difference?
With preventive care, you'll feel subtle shifts within 2-3 weeks: less reaction to stressful meetings, more energy for your priorities. It’s not a quick fix — it’s building a sustainable system that keeps you sharp all season.
