Frequently asked questions

Below are brief answers to common questions. If you don’t see what you need, reach out via contact or book a brief consult.

What approaches do you use?
I draw from evidence-informed therapies chosen to fit your goals—practical strategies, reflective inquiry, and skills training. We collaborate on a plan tailored to your life and values.
How long will I work with you?
That depends on your goals. Some people find a 4-session plan clarifying; others join a 6-week cohort or continue with weekly sessions. We set expectations together and revisit them periodically.
Do you accept insurance?
This practice uses private pay. I can provide a receipt you may submit to your insurer for possible reimbursement. Please check your specific plan before booking.
What if I am in crisis?
I do not provide emergency care. If you are at immediate risk, please call local emergency services or a crisis line in {{CITY}}, {{STATE}}.
