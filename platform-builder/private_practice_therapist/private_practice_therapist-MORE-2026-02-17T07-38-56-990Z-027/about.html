About the practice

I provide thoughtful, clinician-led therapy for adults, and I run small learning cohorts when people want shared structure. Sessions are private, time-limited, and guided by your goals.

A little about me

I combine evidence-based approaches with practical, human-centered work: helping people notice the patterns that matter, pick experiments that fit daily life, and tend to relationships and roles that often feel tangled. My style is steady, curious, and direct — alongside warmth.

My training & scope

  • Licensed clinician with experience in adult therapy
  • Common focuses: life transitions, anxiety, relationship stress, new-parent adjustment, work-related stress
  • Therapeutic tools include CBT, ACT, and relational approaches
Confidentiality: what you share in sessions is protected by clinical confidentiality, with exceptions required by law (e.g., imminent risk of harm). I do not provide emergency/crisis services. If you are in crisis, please contact local emergency services or a crisis line.

Community & cohorts

When aligned with goals, I lead short cohorts — small, structured groups that blend psychoeducation and practice. They are optional, professionally-facilitated, and run on a calendar so members move together. Participation is always confidential within the group boundaries.
