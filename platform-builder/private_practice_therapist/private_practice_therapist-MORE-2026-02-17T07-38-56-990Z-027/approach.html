How I structure therapy

A pragmatic, collaborative process — I help clients translate concern into steps, and then test those steps in day-to-day life.

1. Map

We map the problem together: what's keeping it alive and what small changes might shift the pattern.

2. Try

We pick one or two experiments: concrete actions you can try between sessions and report back on.

3. Reflect

We review what worked, adjust, and build momentum. When helpful, cohorts add a practice space with peer feedback.

Session rhythm

Most people start weekly, then move to biweekly or occasional check-ins as goals shift. Cohorts run on a set schedule so members can practice together.

Boundaries, confidentiality, and crisis

Therapy is confidential with legal exceptions (e.g., imminent harm). I do not provide emergency services. If you are in crisis, call local emergency services or a crisis hotline. We discuss limits, fees, and scheduling during intake.
