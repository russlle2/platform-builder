Regulating Your Inner Rhythm: Private Practice Therapy for Academic Overwhelm

The pressures of college can feel like a constant hum, making it hard to find your own rhythm. Here, we focus on practical ways to bring your nervous system back into balance. Through gentle guidance, you'll learn techniques to quiet the internal noise, allowing you to approach your studies and social life from a place of greater calm, rather than constant reactivity. This isn't about changing who you are, but about equipping you with tools to feel more grounded.
