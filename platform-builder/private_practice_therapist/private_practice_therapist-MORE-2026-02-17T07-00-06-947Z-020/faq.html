Short, clear responses to help you decide whether to reach out. If you don’t see your question here, you can email {{EMAIL}}.

We look at what you want to get out of therapy, how urgent the concern is, and how much time you can realistically commit. Short series work when the goal is contained; weekly sessions fit work that needs steady adjustment. We can decide together at intake.
Sessions are confidential. Records are stored securely. There are legal limits to confidentiality that I will review at the first appointment (e.g., imminent risk, mandated reporting).
I am currently out-of-network with most insurers. I provide superbills you may submit for reimbursement depending on your plan. We will discuss payment arrangements at first contact.
The first session is an intake: we clarify concerns, goals, history, and practicalities (scheduling, fees, and policies). It’s collaborative: you can ask questions about my approach and how we might work together.
Policy details are shared at booking. Generally, I request 48 hours notice for cancellations where possible. I will share exceptions and work to reschedule when you need to change an appointment.
