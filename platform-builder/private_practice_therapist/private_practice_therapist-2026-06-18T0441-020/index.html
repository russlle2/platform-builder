Frequently Asked Questions

How does Private Practice Therapy specifically help with major life transitions?
During major transitions, your body's stress response can become overactive, leading to anxiety, difficulty sleeping, or feeling overwhelmed. Our therapy focuses on understanding these physiological reactions and providing you with concrete, evidence-based methods to regulate your nervous system. This helps you reduce stress, process changes more effectively, and build resilience.
What does 'nervous system regulation' mean, and how will it benefit me?
Nervous system regulation refers to learning to shift your body and mind out of states of high alert (fight, flight, freeze) and into a more calm, balanced state. For you, this means fewer moments of intense anxiety or overwhelm during your transition, improved sleep, better emotional control, and an enhanced ability to think clearly and make decisions, even amidst uncertainty.
Is this approach suitable if my transition feels very overwhelming or traumatic?
Yes, our clinical and compassionate approach is designed to support individuals experiencing significant distress. We prioritize creating a safe and stable environment where you can gradually process your experiences at your own pace. Our focus on nervous system regulation can be particularly helpful in managing the intense physical and emotional responses often associated with overwhelming or traumatic transitions.
How quickly can I expect to feel a difference with this type of Private Practice Therapy?
The timeline for feeling a difference varies for everyone, as each transition and individual is unique. However, many clients report experiencing a greater sense of calm and better ability to manage their stress responses within a few sessions, as they begin to integrate the regulation techniques into their daily lives. Our goal is to equip you with lasting tools, not just temporary relief.
