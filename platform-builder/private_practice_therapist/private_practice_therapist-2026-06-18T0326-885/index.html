Frequently Asked Questions

How does Private Practice Therapy focusing on posture help with college anxiety?
When you're anxious or stressed, your body often tenses up – shoulders hunch, breathing becomes shallow. By bringing awareness to your posture and learning to release physical tension, you can signal to your nervous system that you're safe, which can directly reduce feelings of anxiety and improve your ability to concentrate on your studies.
I'm not very flexible or 'athletic'. Is this type of Private Practice Therapy for me?
Absolutely! This isn't about being flexible or athletic. It's about cultivating a gentle, internal awareness of your body. The practices are designed to be accessible for everyone, focusing on subtle shifts and mindful movements that fit your comfort level, helping you develop a more supportive relationship with your own body.
Will this Private Practice Therapy involve physical exercises, or is it mostly talk-based?
Your sessions will integrate both. We'll have meaningful conversations about your experiences, and we'll also explore simple, desk-friendly movements, breathing techniques, and sensory awareness practices. The goal is to provide you with practical tools you can use discreetly throughout your day to manage stress and improve focus.
How quickly can I expect to see results from this Private Practice Therapy approach?
Results vary for everyone, but many college students report feeling a greater sense of calm and body awareness after just a few sessions. Consistency with the practices we explore will help you integrate these changes more deeply, leading to sustained improvements in managing anxiety and academic pressure over time.
