Three principles that shape sessions

1. Clear measurement

We set observable indicators of progress and review them regularly to decide next steps.

2. Client-led pacing

You determine the pace; interventions are offered with consent and adjusted when needed.

3. Skills that transfer

Skills are practiced in-session and assigned for real-world testing between sessions.

Misconceptions about therapy—reframed

Misconception: Therapy will immediately remove distress. Reframe: Therapy helps build skills and strategies; discomfort can reduce as skills are applied to daily life.

Questions about process

Do you work with crisis care?

I am not a crisis service. If you are in immediate danger, call emergency services. I will help connect you to crisis resources when needed.

What about privacy and notes?

Clinical notes are kept securely. Limits to confidentiality (such as risk of harm) are discussed at intake and summarized in forms.

Scope & boundaries: I offer adult outpatient psychotherapy focused on anxiety, trauma, relationships, burnout, and life transitions. Confidentiality is maintained within legal limits. If you are experiencing a life-threatening emergency, call local emergency services immediately.
