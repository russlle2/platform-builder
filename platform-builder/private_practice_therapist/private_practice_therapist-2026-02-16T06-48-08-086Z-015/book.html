Start a conversation

I aim for the booking flow to be simple and respectful of your time. Below is how we usually proceed so you can know what to expect.

1. Quick inquiry

Use the booking link to share availability and a brief note about your primary concern. If cost is a concern, say so — we can discuss options before scheduling.

2. Brief intake call (10–15 min)

A short phone or video check-in to ensure fit and answer logistics (session length, telehealth vs in-person, insurance questions).

3. First session

We set collaborative goals and clarify boundaries, privacy, and scheduling. If higher levels of care are indicated, I will discuss referrals.

4. Ongoing planning

We periodically review progress and adjust frequency or structure as needed.

What to prepare

  • Short list of concerns or goals.
  • Notes about previous therapy or medications (if any).
  • Payment method and insurance questions ready for the intake call.

Intake & privacy

Intake forms collect clinical and contact information to coordinate care. Records are stored securely. We will review limits to confidentiality at your first session.

Common booking questions

How soon can I start? Typical wait time varies; availability is shown on the booking calendar. If you are in urgent need, please indicate that on your inquiry.

What if I need to cancel? A 48-hour notice is requested to avoid a late cancellation fee. Emergencies are considered case-by-case.
