Questions I often hear

Below are concise responses rooted in practice norms and ethics. If you want a more specific answer, reach out — I welcome brief clarifying questions by email.

Practical answers

How long is each session and how often should I come?

Sessions are typically 45–55 minutes. Frequency depends on goals and capacity; weekly sessions are common to build momentum, with biweekly as an alternative for maintenance.

Will you diagnose me?

If helpful for treatment planning, I may discuss diagnostic impressions. My focus is on functional goals and coping strategies rather than labels alone.

What if I need more specialized care?

I do not provide medication management. If pharmacological evaluation or intensive services are indicated, I will collaborate with or refer to appropriate professionals.

Are our conversations confidential?

Yes, within the limits of the law. Confidentiality is central; exceptions include imminent harm, abuse reporting, or court-ordered disclosures. We can review privacy in the first session.

How do payments and insurance work?

I am out-of-network but provide superbills for reimbursement. I accept card payment and bank transfer. Sliding scale is limited — ask during intake.

Short clarifications

Emergency protocol

If you are in immediate danger, call emergency services or the nearest crisis line. Therapy appointments are not monitored 24/7.

Records

Clinical notes are kept confidential; clients can request copies per professional guidelines.

Misconceptions I address

Some expect quick fixes or guarantees. Effective therapy is collaborative and takes consistent effort. We track progress and adjust the plan together.
