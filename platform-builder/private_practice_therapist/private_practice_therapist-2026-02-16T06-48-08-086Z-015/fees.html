Common myths about therapy costs

Myth: Therapy is unaffordable unless you use insurance

Truth: Many clinicians offer staggered options: single sessions, short-term packages, or sliding scale slots. I aim to make work sustainable, not burdensome.

Myth: Cheaper means better or worse

Truth: Price reflects framing and structure rather than therapeutic value alone. We can talk about goals and match a plan that fits both time and budget.

How I structure care (principles)

Collaborative planning
