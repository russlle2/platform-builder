Foundations that guide my work

Skill building & integration

Common myths I hear — and what usually helps

Myth: "Therapy is only for severe problems."
Reality: Therapy can help with stress, relationship patterns, and life transitions before they become crises.
Myth: "If I start, I'll be stuck forever."
Reality: Many clients set time-limited goals; progress is often measurable in months rather than years.
