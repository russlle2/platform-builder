Frequently Asked Questions

How does Private Practice Therapy specifically address the unique anxieties of college students?
Our Private Practice Therapy is tailored to the distinct pressures of university life, including academic performance, social integration, future uncertainties, and independence. We focus on teaching you specific techniques to manage exam stress, presentation anxiety, and the constant feeling of needing to achieve, all within the context of your developing identity.
What kind of techniques does Private Practice Therapy use to help regulate my nervous system?
In Private Practice Therapy, we utilize science-backed techniques such as mindful awareness practices, specific breathing exercises to shift physiological states, psychoeducation on the stress response cycle, and somatic (body-based) approaches to help you actively down-regulate an overactive nervous system and cultivate a sense of internal calm amidst external demands.
Will Private Practice Therapy fit into my busy college schedule?
We understand the demands on college students' time. Our Private Practice Therapy offers flexible scheduling options to accommodate your academic commitments. Our goal is to provide accessible support that integrates seamlessly into your life, helping you manage stress rather than adding to it.
What should I expect during my first Private Practice Therapy session?
Your initial Private Practice Therapy session is a space for gentle exploration. We'll discuss your current challenges, your experiences with anxiety and academic pressure, and your hopes for our work together. It's an opportunity for us to get to know each other, for you to ask questions, and to begin charting a path toward greater ease and resilience.
