Navigating Anxiety and Academic Pressure with a Private Practice Therapist

College life brings unique stressors – the pressure to perform, social adjustments, and future uncertainties. If you're grappling with anxiety that feels unmanageable or finding academic demands overwhelming, you're not alone. Our trauma-informed approach helps you understand the roots of these feelings and develop practical strategies for managing them, ensuring you can engage fully with your education and personal growth.
