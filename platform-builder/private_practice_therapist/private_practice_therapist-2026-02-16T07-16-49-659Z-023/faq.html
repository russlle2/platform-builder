Questions people often ask

Below are direct answers to common practical and clinical questions. If you don't see your question, please reach out by phone or email.

Do you take insurance or bill directly?
I do not bill insurance directly. I can provide a superbill after each session which you may submit to your insurer for out-of-network reimbursement. If insurance reimbursement is essential for you, I can help you explore options or a referral.
What is your cancellation and late policy?
I request at least 48 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule. Late cancellations or missed sessions may be charged the full fee unless otherwise negotiated. Exceptions are made for emergencies on a case-by-case basis.
Are telehealth sessions available?
Yes. I offer secure, HIPAA-compliant telehealth for clients across {{STATE}}. In-person sessions are available for those local to {{CITY}}. We'll discuss what suits your needs during the initial consult.
What happens if I'm in crisis between sessions?
If you are in immediate danger, please call emergency services. For acute distress, contact local crisis lines or go to the nearest emergency room. My practice is not an emergency service; I will collaboratively plan for crisis protocols during intake and provide resources for urgent care.
How long will therapy take?
Therapy length depends on your goals. Some people find meaningful change in 8–12 sessions; others pursue longer-term work. Together we set short-term goals and periodically review progress so you can make informed decisions about next steps.

Ethics & boundaries

I adhere to professional ethical standards. Confidentiality is maintained except where disclosure is required to prevent harm, by court order, or when otherwise required by law. Records are stored securely. Scope limits: I work with adult individuals; I do not provide legal or forensic testimony as part of routine therapy.

Privacy & recordkeeping

Your notes and communications are protected. I will review the informed consent and privacy policy with you at intake; you will receive written documentation.
