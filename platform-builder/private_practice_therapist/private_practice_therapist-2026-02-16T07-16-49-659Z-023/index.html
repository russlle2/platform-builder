How I work — three commitments

Grounded collaboration

We set clear goals together and review progress each session. Therapy is a partnership guided by your priorities.

Practical learning

Sessions emphasize skills you can use between appointments: regulation, communication, and decision-making practices that fit your life.

Clinical integrity & privacy

Notes are private and stored securely. I follow professional ethical standards. If you have questions about confidentiality, ask me at any time.

Approaches I blend

I draw from empirically supported approaches tailored to each person. Below are common methods and when we use them.

  • Cognitive behavioral techniques — to identify patterns and develop concrete alternatives for anxiety and mood.
  • EMDR-informed strategies & trauma-aware pacing — slowly building stability before deeper processing.
  • Interpersonal and attachment-focused work — for relationship cycles, communication, and family of origin themes.
  • Mindfulness & somatic regulation — simple practices to re-establish calm and presence during stress.

If a different specialty is needed, I will support referrals to a clinician with the right expertise.

Common hesitations

Not sure therapy will help me

That’s a reasonable concern. Many people start feeling better after a few sessions because we focus on specific, manageable changes—always at your pace.

I don’t have time

We can discuss shorter session options, a focus plan, and steps that fit your schedule. Small consistent steps matter.

Cost worries

I offer a transparent fee schedule and limited sliding-scale availability. Insurance coverage varies; I can provide superbills on request.
