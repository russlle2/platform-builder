How I typically begin

We start with a 1–2 session assessment to map concerns and set a 6–8 session plan. Methods often include Cognitive Behavioral approaches for symptoms, trauma-informed care, and skills coaching for stress and boundaries.

Common hesitations — honest response

"I don't know if therapy will help me."

We begin small and make clear aims; you don't need to commit to an open-ended process before you see early results.

"I'm worried about privacy."

Notes are private and stored securely. We will review confidentiality limits during intake and I will answer any questions.

"I can't afford a long course."

We can design short-term work, prioritize skills, and discuss limited sliding-scale openings if available.
