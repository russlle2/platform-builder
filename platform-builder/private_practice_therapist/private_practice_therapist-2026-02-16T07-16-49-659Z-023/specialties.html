Areas I commonly work with

Focused, pragmatic therapy for adults dealing with the strains of modern life. I specialize in clinically grounded support for anxiety, trauma recovery, relationship concerns, and career-related stress.

Settings: Outpatient individual therapy; short-term and open-ended options. Modalities: {{MODALITIES}}.

Generalized & Panic Anxiety

Targeted skills for managing worry and panic, breathing and exposure work, and restructuring unhelpful cycles that reinforce avoidance.

Trauma & PTSD-informed Care

Stabilization, grounding, and slow-paced processing aligned with your readiness. We prioritize safety and titration over fast exposure.

Relationship Patterns

Attachment-informed work that clarifies relational triggers and helps you negotiate needs with clarity and boundaries.

Life Transitions & Burnout

Practical planning and meaning-making for job changes, caregiving shifts, and mid-life recalibration.

How I tailor care

I start with a focused intake to map symptoms and strengths, then propose a working plan. Interventions often include brief behavioral experiments, narrative reframing, attachment work, and somatic regulation. We review progress every 6–8 sessions.
