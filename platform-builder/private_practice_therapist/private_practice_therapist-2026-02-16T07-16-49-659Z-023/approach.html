Frameworks I use to make therapy concrete

I organize therapy around short, testable frameworks that help you measure change. Below are common pathways clients choose depending on goals and time.

Foundations Plan

A 6–8 session focused package for symptom relief and a clear coping plan — ideal for acute anxiety or situational stress.

Structure: intake, 4 focused skill sessions, consolidation. Pricing: single-package rate discussed at intake.

Focused Renewal

A 12-week pathway that blends skill-building with narrative work for relationship change, burnout recovery, or transition planning.

Includes session summaries, brief between-session exercises, and a progress review at midpoint.

Leadership Resilience

For professionals seeking coaching-adjacent therapy: boundary-setting, decision clarity, and stress modulation tailored to career demands.

Offered as a monthly subscription or hourly engagement. Options discussed during consultation.

Session format & what to expect

Sessions are 45–55 minutes. During the first session we gather history and set 2–3 measurable goals. I use a blend of in-session skill rehearsal and brief between-session tasks. We review safety and boundaries at intake and provide referrals when outside my scope.

How clients describe working together

"I appreciated the clear plan — we knew what we were testing each week and I could see progress in small steps." — client, leader

Common concerns before beginning

Will this take a long time? Some work is short-term; some matters take more time. We map a plan together and revisit it regularly.

What if I need medication? Medication needs are managed by prescribing clinicians. I will coordinate care and offer referrals when needed.
