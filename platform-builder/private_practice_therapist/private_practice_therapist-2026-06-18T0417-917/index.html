Frequently Asked Questions

Do you work with babies in the room?
Yes. We have a calm space for feeding or naps. No need to arrange childcare for your first check-in.
How is this different from general therapy?
We don’t treat postpartum as a symptom to fix. We prevent it from becoming a pattern using seasonal data—like how humidity affects your recovery or why February feels harder than June.
Can we adjust if my baby’s schedule changes?
Absolutely. Your plan updates automatically with your baby’s growth phases. If nap times shift or colds hit, your care adapts without new appointments.
Do you use medication or supplements?
We focus on behavioral and environmental adjustments. For example, we might recommend specific humidity levels for your nursery based on seasonal allergy data—not pills.
