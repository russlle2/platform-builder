What to Expect at Your First Private Practice Therapy Session

Your first visit is a chance for us to connect and understand your unique concerns. We’ll discuss the specific physical discomforts you’re experiencing, how parenting and daily stress impact your body, and what you hope to gain from our sessions. This intake is a conversation, not an interrogation. It’s an opportunity for you to share your story in a supportive, non-judgmental environment. Together, we'll create a personalized plan to address your physical pain and help you feel more grounded and present for your family.

Our Private Practice Therapy focuses on identifying how these daily strains accumulate and gently guiding your body to release stored tension, helping you reclaim physical ease and mental calm. Frequently Asked Questions

How does Private Practice Therapy specifically help with the physical pain from parenting?
We focus on somatic techniques that help you understand and release the physical tension and chronic pain often linked to the demands of caring for young children. This isn't just about managing symptoms; it's about addressing the root causes of discomfort that build up from lifting, carrying, and the constant mental load. You'll learn practical ways to ease your body's burden.
I'm so busy with my children. How can I possibly fit Private Practice Therapy into my schedule?
We understand your time is precious. Our sessions are designed to be efficient and impactful, providing you with tangible relief and tools you can integrate into your daily life. We offer flexible scheduling to accommodate the realities of parenting, and even short, focused sessions can make a significant difference in your physical well-being. Your self-care is an investment in your family's well-being.
Is somatic healing only for emotional stress, or can it genuinely help with physical aches and pains?
Somatic healing directly addresses the connection between your mind and body. Many physical aches, such as neck stiffness, lower back pain, or general fatigue, are often exacerbated or even caused by accumulated stress, worry, and the physical demands of parenting. We work with your body to release these patterns, leading to genuine and lasting physical relief, not just emotional processing.
