Private Practice Therapist

A Gentle, Collaborative Approach to Somatic Healing for Busy Parents

Your experience as a parent is unique, and so is our approach. We don't just talk about stress; we help your body process it. Through somatic techniques, we'll explore how household pressures and emotional weight contribute to physical discomfort. This isn't about quick fixes; it's about building a deeper connection with your body's signals and developing practical tools to manage physical pain and stress responses, making you more resilient for the demands of family life. We work together, at your pace, to bring lasting comfort.
