Holding Your Emotional Landscape: Trauma-Informed Private Practice Therapy

Postpartum can bring forth unexpected emotional currents. As your Private Practice Therapist, we offer a safe harbor to explore the shifts, the griefs, and the joys that arrive with motherhood. Our approach is deeply rooted in understanding how past experiences, even those long forgotten, can surface during this tender time, providing you with tools to process and integrate them without judgment.
