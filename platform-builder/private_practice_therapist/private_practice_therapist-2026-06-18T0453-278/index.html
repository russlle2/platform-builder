Frequently Asked Questions

How does private practice therapy specifically help retirees maintain mental clarity?
Our private practice therapy focuses on identifying and processing unresolved emotional experiences that can often contribute to mental fog or decreased focus. By addressing these underlying factors, we help you clear your mind, improving your ability to concentrate, make decisions, and engage fully with your retirement years.
I've had a long life; why would emotional healing be important now?
Emotional healing at any stage, especially in retirement, allows you to live more fully in the present. Past experiences, even those you've carried for a long time, can unconsciously drain your energy. By gently working through them, you free up emotional and mental resources, leading to greater vitality and a more fulfilling, unburdened retirement.
What does 'trauma-informed care' mean in the context of a private practice therapist?
Trauma-informed care means we understand how past difficult experiences can impact your current well-being, even if you don't consider them 'trauma.' Our private practice therapist approach prioritizes your safety, choice, and control, creating a supportive environment where you can process these experiences at your own pace, without feeling re-traumatized or overwhelmed.
Will private practice therapy feel like re-living old, difficult memories?
Our goal in private practice therapy is not to force you to re-live painful memories. Instead, we help you understand their impact and develop strategies to manage their lingering effects. We focus on building your resilience and emotional regulation, so you can address past experiences from a place of strength, without being overwhelmed by them, fostering greater peace and clarity.
