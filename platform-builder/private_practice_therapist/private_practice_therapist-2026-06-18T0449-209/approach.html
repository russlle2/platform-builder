Understanding Your Triggers: A Trauma-Informed Private Practice Therapist Approach

Often, the chaos of family life can unknowingly activate old stress responses, making small moments feel huge. As your Private Practice Therapist, I'll work with you to identify these triggers and understand their roots. We'll explore gentle, effective ways to regulate your nervous system, allowing you to react less from a place of past hurt and more from intentional choice. This isn't about blaming; it's about empowering you with deeper self-awareness and practical tools.
