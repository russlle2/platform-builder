Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Private Practice Therapist help me, an athlete, with sleep when I'm constantly pushing my body?
As an athlete or active person, your physical output directly impacts your need for quality recovery. We understand the unique stressors you face. Your sessions will focus on understanding how your intense physical demands influence your sleep architecture and developing strategies to optimize rest, allowing your body to repair and rebuild effectively.
I don't have prior experience with therapy; will I feel out of place at Private Practice Therapy?
Absolutely not. Our practice is built on the principle of accessibility and gentleness. We meet you exactly where you are, regardless of your past wellness experience. Your comfort is paramount, and we'll guide you through each step, ensuring you feel safe, heard, and respected as you explore new ways to find rest.
What kind of 'restorative rest' does Private Practice Therapy focus on beyond just sleeping?
Restorative rest, for active individuals, extends beyond mere hours of sleep. It encompasses the quality of your sleep, but also how you wind down, mentally disengage from physical stress, and allow your nervous system to regulate. We'll explore techniques that help you achieve a deeper state of relaxation, both awake and asleep, to truly replenish your energy reserves.
My schedule as an active person is very demanding. How flexible is Private Practice Therapy with appointments?
We recognize the demanding nature of an active lifestyle. We strive to offer appointment times that can accommodate your training, work, and recovery schedules. Our aim is to make your path to better rest as convenient as possible, ensuring therapy becomes a supportive part of your routine, not another source of stress.
