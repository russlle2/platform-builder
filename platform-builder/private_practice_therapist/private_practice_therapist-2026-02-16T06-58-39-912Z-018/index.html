Separating myths from practical truths about therapy

Myth: Therapy fixes everything quickly

Truth: Change is gradual. We set realistic, measurable steps and adjust pacing to your needs.

Myth: Talking is all you do

Truth: We blend evidence-informed methods and actionable tools so you have strategies between sessions.

Myth: Your past determines your future

Truth: Understanding history helps inform new choices; therapy supports building skills for current challenges.

Core principles guiding our work

Trauma-informed

Practical skills
