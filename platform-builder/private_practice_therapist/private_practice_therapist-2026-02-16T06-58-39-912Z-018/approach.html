A typical course of work

1. Initial assessment & goals — We map current difficulties and define 2–4 measurable goals.
2. Skill-building & strategies — Practical tools and in-session practice with homework to test changes.
3. Review & adapt — Regular measurement of outcomes; we shift techniques if needed.
4. Maintenance planning — Consolidation of gains and relapse strategies when appropriate.
