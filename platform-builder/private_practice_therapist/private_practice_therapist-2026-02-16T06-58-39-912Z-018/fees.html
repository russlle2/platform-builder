Common billing myths — and what’s real

Myth: Therapy is only for crisis.
Many people assume therapy is only for extreme situations.

Truth: Therapy is useful for navigation, skills-building, and steady support as much as for acute crises. Investing earlier often reduces needed intensity later.

Myth: Sliding scale means lower quality.
Sliding scale simply adjusts access, not care standards.

Truth: Adjusted fees reflect an effort to broaden access. Clinical standards and confidentiality remain the same for all clients.
