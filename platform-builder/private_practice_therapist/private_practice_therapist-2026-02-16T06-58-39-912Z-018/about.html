Clinical orientation and what I offer

I integrate evidence-informed approaches to support people facing anxiety, trauma reactions, relationship strain, burnout, and life transitions. My work is collaborative: we clarify goals, try methods in-session, and test what changes in daily life. I do not provide medical care; I coordinate with prescribers when needed.

Foundational values

Therapy is a quick fix.

Myth

Meaningful change often takes time and structured practice between sessions.

Care is a guided process.

Reality

I use measurable steps, feedback, and compassionate accountability so progress is visible and respectful of your pace.

Core pillars of my practice

  • Assessment & shared formulation: clear goals and expected steps
  • Skills-based work: emotion regulation, exposure, communication practice
  • Contextual focus: home, work, relationships considered together
  • Privacy & clarity: explicit confidentiality and limits are discussed at intake

Practical details & limits

Sessions are typically 50 minutes. I maintain strict confidentiality except where there is imminent risk of harm, abuse, or by legal mandate. If you are in crisis or immediate danger, call local emergency services or your crisis line. Telehealth is available when clinically appropriate.
