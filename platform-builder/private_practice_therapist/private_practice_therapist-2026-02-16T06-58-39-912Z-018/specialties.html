How I frame care

I prioritize measurable steps and shared decisions. Every plan includes skill-practice and periodic reviews so we know what is helping.

Program options

Rather than long open-ended therapy by default, I offer two common paths to clarify expectations:

  • Short Path — 8 structured sessions focused on symptom-targeted work and skills practice.
  • Exploratory Path — Flexible weekly sessions for ongoing life and relational work.

Quick FAQ

Are services remote?

Telehealth is standard; in-person availability depends on location and scheduling.

Do you do couples work?

I focus primarily on individual therapy but collaborate with partners in goal-focused sessions when clinically appropriate.

How do you measure progress?

We use brief self-report check-ins and goal reviews every 4–6 sessions to ensure alignment.

Accessibility & privacy

I aim to provide a respectful and accessible environment. Records are stored securely and discussed in consent. If you require accommodations, please mention them when contacting the practice.

Crisis note: If you are in immediate danger or experiencing a medical emergency, call emergency services or your local crisis line. This practice does not provide emergency services.
