How can a Private Practice Therapist help with sleep when I'm recovering from chronic illness or injury?

As your Private Practice Therapist, we focus on the unique intersection of your recovery and sleep. We help you identify specific barriers to rest, such as pain management anxiety or medication effects, and then work together to develop gentle, personalized strategies that support your body's need for restorative sleep without adding extra stress.

I've never done therapy before; will I feel comfortable discussing my sleep issues with a Private Practice Therapist?

Absolutely. Your comfort is our priority. As your Private Practice Therapist, we create a very gentle and approachable environment. There's no expectation for you to have any prior therapy experience. We'll guide our conversations, making sure you feel heard and understood as we explore ways to improve your sleep and rest during your recovery.

My energy levels are very low due to my illness/injury. Will I be able to keep up with therapy for sleep improvement?

We completely understand that your energy is precious during recovery. Your Private Practice Therapist sessions are designed to be flexible and paced according to your needs. We focus on small, manageable steps rather than overwhelming tasks, ensuring that any suggestions for improving your sleep fit within your current capacity and support your overall well-being.

What kind of 'restorative rest' will my Private Practice Therapist help me achieve?

Restorative rest, as supported by your Private Practice Therapist, means finding ways for your body and mind to truly recuperate, beyond just sleep. This includes improving the quality of your sleep, but also exploring gentle relaxation techniques, mindful moments, and ways to reduce mental fatigue, all tailored to help your body heal and recharge effectively amidst your recovery.
