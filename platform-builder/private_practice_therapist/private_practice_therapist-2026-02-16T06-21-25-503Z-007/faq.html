Frequently asked practical questions

Clear answers about sessions, confidentiality, and what to expect when you reach out.

How do I know if therapy with you is a good fit?

I usually offer a 20-minute brief consult to understand your needs and explain my approach. Together we can decide if continuing feels like the right match. Sometimes referrals or a short-term plan is a better fit, and I will tell you that candidly.

What can I expect during the first session?

The first meeting is an intake: we gather background, current concerns, and goals. It often includes a clinical history and collaboratively setting priorities for therapy. Sessions are typically 50–60 minutes unless otherwise agreed.

Do you work with insurance?

I am a private-pay practice and provide a receipt (superbill) for out-of-network claims. Please check with your insurer for details about reimbursement policies.

How do cancellations and rescheduling work?

I request at least 48 hours' notice for changes when possible. Late cancellations or no-shows may incur a fee equivalent to the session rate unless we agree otherwise due to extenuating circumstances.

What if I need crisis support?

I do not provide crisis services. If you are in immediate danger, call 911 or your local emergency number. For urgent mental health crises in the U.S., you can call or text 988. If you are outside the U.S., use your local emergency resources.

Do you offer telehealth?

Yes — I offer both in-person ({{CITY}}, {{STATE}}) and secure video sessions. We can decide during intake which format will best support your work.

How long does therapy usually last?

The length varies by goals and life circumstances. Some people work together for a few months to address a specific issue; others prefer ongoing monthly sessions for maintenance. We'll review progress regularly and set collaborative goals.

What are your specialties?

I regularly work with anxiety, relationship strain, life transitions, burnout, and trauma-informed care. My work is integrative and tailored to your needs — I do not promise specific outcomes but focus on practical, sustainable change.
