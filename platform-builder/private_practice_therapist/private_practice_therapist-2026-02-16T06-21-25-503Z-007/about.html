How I structure sessions

I combine collaborative problem-solving with skills practice and reflective inquiry. Sessions are paced to your goals: some people prefer time-limited work focused on a specific problem; others benefit from ongoing support. We’ll check in regularly about progress and next steps.

  1. Connect — build a trusting, confidential space.
  2. Clarify — define what you want to change or understand.
  3. Co-create — try interventions and skills together.
  4. Consolidate — track gains and plan for sustainable change.
