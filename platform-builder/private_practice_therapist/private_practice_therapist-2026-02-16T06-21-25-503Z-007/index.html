A calm, pragmatic partnership

I started private practice to create a space where curiosity and steady support come together. My work is informed by years of clinical training and real-world experience helping people navigate anxiety, trauma responses, shaky relationships, and major life transitions. I value clarity, compassion, and practical tools that feel manageable in daily life.

Confidentiality: sessions are private and records are securely stored. If you are in crisis or feel unsafe, please call local emergency services or your crisis hotline — therapy is not an emergency service.

How we work together

A simple framework to guide our time: Safety, Clarity, and Adaptation.

1. Safety

We begin by building enough safety in the relationship and in your nervous system so that honest work feels possible.

2. Clarity

We map patterns and identify what keeps problems active — therapy includes reflection and concrete skills to test in life.

3. Adaptation

We practice new responses that fit your goals: managing overwhelm, improving connection, or finding direction after change.

Paths I offer

Options shaped to different needs — each path includes individual sessions, progress check-ins, and a plan that fits your life.

Stabilize & Regulate (Short-term)

Focused 8–12 week work for acute anxiety, stress, or when you need immediate tools to feel steadier.

Reconnect (Relationship-focused)

Work on communication patterns, boundaries, and repair strategies for partners or individuals navigating relational pain.

Deep Integration (Long-term)

Open-ended therapy for complex trauma, long-standing patterns, or meaning-making through life transitions.
