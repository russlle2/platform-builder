How I work with people

My style is collaborative, grounded, and intentional. I aim to help you move from feeling stuck to having practical skills and clearer choices.

Four-stage framework

A clear map for therapy so each session has purpose and progress is visible.

  • 1. Grounding & Safety — Establish predictability and immediate coping tools.
  • 2. Assessment & Goals — Collaboratively identify what matters and set short-term targets.
  • 3. Skills + Insight — Integrate practical strategies with reflective work on patterns.
  • 4. Consolidation — Plan for sustainability and next steps outside therapy.

Shared tasks in therapy

Therapy is a team effort. We’ll partner on experiments, reflections, and measurable changes that matter to you.

  • Clear, collaborative goal-setting
  • Practical skills to use between sessions
  • Ongoing review of what helps
