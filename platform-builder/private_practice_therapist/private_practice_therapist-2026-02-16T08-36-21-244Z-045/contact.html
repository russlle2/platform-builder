How I work — a short note

I combine trauma-informed care with practical skill-building so you can feel steadier in day-to-day life. Sessions are collaborative: we clarify what’s most distressing, track what helps, and move at a pace that feels safe and useful.

Who I typically work with

  • Adults experiencing persistent anxiety or panic
  • People processing past hurts or complex loss
  • Couples and partners wanting clearer communication
  • Professionals facing burnout or major career shifts

Quick framework

Assess → Build → Practice

We start by understanding safety and priorities, learn tools (emotion regulation, communication), and create small experiments to test what works.
