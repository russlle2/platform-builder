Why I do this work

I trained in trauma-informed care and brief, evidence-informed therapies. Over years in community and private settings, I learned that people most benefit from a steady, compassionate guide who clarifies what matters and offers tools that fit their life. That balance — warmth plus structure — is central here.

How I structure therapy

Collaborative Goals

We set clear, measurable goals together and revisit them so work stays relevant to your life.

Practical Skills

Tools for regulation, communication, and pacing between sessions so the work lands where it matters.

Reflective Processing

Space to make sense of patterns, values, and relationships in a way that supports sustainable change.

Paths I offer

I tailor a path based on your goals. Examples below are common frameworks I use; we'll design what fits you.

Reflect & Restore

8–12 sessions focusing on anxiety regulation, distress tolerance, and sleep stabilization.

Growth Partnership

18-week series for relationship repair, communication skills, and boundary setting.

Transition Support

Short-term, solution-oriented plan to navigate career, family, or life-stage changes.
