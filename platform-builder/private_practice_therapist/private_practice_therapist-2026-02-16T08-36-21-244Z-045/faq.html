Questions I often hear

Practical answers about how I work, what to expect, and how to begin. If your question isn’t here, please reach out.

How do we start — what is the first session like?

The first session is an intake-style conversation: we clarify your goals, history relevant to current concerns, and any safety needs. We’ll also discuss confidentiality and administrative details.

What kinds of issues do you work with?

I support adults with anxiety, relationship difficulties, trauma recovery, life transitions, and workplace burnout. I draw on evidence-informed approaches and tailor them to your needs.

How long will therapy take?

It varies. Some people pursue time-limited goals (6–12 sessions); others benefit from longer-term weekly work. We set collaboration timelines together and review progress regularly.

Do you offer online sessions?

Yes. I offer telehealth for clients in {{STATE}} and in-person sessions in {{CITY}} when available. We can discuss what format will best meet your needs.

What if I need a tighter budget?

Will you share my notes or communicate with others?

I will not share therapy notes without your written consent except where required by law. If coordination with other providers is helpful, we’ll talk through a release of information.

A brief note on safety

I am not an emergency service. If you are at imminent risk of harming yourself or others, please call local emergency services or your country’s crisis line immediately.
