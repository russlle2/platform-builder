Areas of focus

Starts with stabilization sessions; timeline varies

What to expect in our first meetings

Your first two sessions are an opportunity to share history, identify goals, and co-create a plan. We'll discuss confidentiality, limits to confidentiality, and the practicalities of scheduling and fees.

I maintain clear boundaries about emergency care: I am not able to provide crisis response. If you are in immediate danger, call local emergency services or your region's crisis number.
