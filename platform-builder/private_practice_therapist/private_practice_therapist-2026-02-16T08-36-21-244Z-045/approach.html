Your therapeutic compass

I combine trauma-informed practices, brief evidence-informed techniques, and relational attunement. That means practical tools for regulation plus a steady, reflective relationship that helps you make sense of what you're experiencing.

Core commitments

Safety first — We begin with grounding, predictable structure, and clear consent about what we will explore.
Collaborative pacing — You set the pace; together we decide when to focus on skills or deeper processing.
Practical outcomes — Therapy includes tools you can use between sessions alongside reflective sense-making.

Session types & pacing

Short check-in
25-minute focused session for planning and skill tuning.
Full session
50-minute psychotherapy session for therapeutic work and processing.
Series work
Planned 8–18 session series for deeper patterns and transitions.

A typical plan — example

  1. Intake & assessment: Clarify goals, history, and immediate needs (1–2 sessions).
  2. Stabilization & skills: Build regulation and coping strategies (2–6 sessions).
  3. Targeted processing: Focused exploration of patterns and change work (varies).
  4. Maintenance & transfer: Consolidate gains and plan next steps.
