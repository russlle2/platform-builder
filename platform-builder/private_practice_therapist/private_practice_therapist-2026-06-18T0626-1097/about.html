How can a Private Practice Therapist specifically help my postpartum sleep?

As a Private Practice Therapist, I focus on the unique psychological and emotional components that influence postpartum sleep. We'll identify specific stressors, anxieties, or perfectionistic tendencies that might be hindering your rest, and develop targeted strategies to address them with compassionate, evidence-based approaches.

Is this Private Practice Therapy just about sleep training my baby?

No, this Private Practice Therapy is centered on your restorative rest as the new mother. While we will discuss baby sleep in the context of your overall well-being, the primary focus is on your emotional regulation, stress reduction, and developing practices that allow you to find deeper rest and recovery, regardless of your baby's current sleep patterns.

What if my sleep challenges feel overwhelming and impossible to change?

It is completely understandable to feel overwhelmed during this period. As your Private Practice Therapist, I meet you precisely where you are, offering a gentle, non-judgmental space. We will break down seemingly insurmountable challenges into manageable steps, celebrating small victories as we progressively build towards more consistent and restorative sleep for you.

How soon can I expect to see improvements in my rest through Private Practice Therapy?

The path to restorative rest is unique for every new mother. While some clients report subtle shifts in their ability to relax and cope after a few Private Practice Therapy sessions, significant, lasting changes often unfold over several weeks or months as we implement and refine your personalized strategies. Consistency and gentle persistence are key.
