Therapy boundaries & confidentiality

Session structure & length
What to expect each meeting
+

Standard sessions are 45–50 minutes. We agree on frequency together; many clients begin weekly and adjust as goals shift. If you need to pause or reschedule, we’ll plan that collaboratively.

Confidentiality & limits
Privacy, records, and legal exceptions
+

Conversations are confidential and stored securely. Exceptions include imminent risk of harm to self or others, court orders, or when required by law. We will discuss any limits to confidentiality before services begin.
