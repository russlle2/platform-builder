Session boundaries & logistics
What to expect for scheduling, cancellations, and timing
▾

Typical individual sessions run 45–60 minutes. For the cohort program, there is a clearly described start and finish with scheduled live calls and optional small-group exercises. I ask for 48 hours notice for cancellations when possible; late cancellations may be billed. Details are provided before you enroll or book.

Confidentiality & limits
How I protect what you share and the exceptions
▾

Conversations are treated as confidential. Notes are kept securely and shared only with your consent, except where law or safety concerns require disclosure (for example: imminent risk of harm to self or others, suspected abuse of a minor or vulnerable adult, or as required by court order). We will discuss these boundaries clearly before we begin.

Scope of practice
What I can and cannot offer in therapy
▾

My practice focuses on short-to-mid term psychotherapy and skills-based cohorts. I do not provide medical diagnoses, prescribe medication, or offer forensic evaluations. When appropriate, I will collaborate with or refer to medical providers, psychiatrists, or other specialists.

If you are feeling unsafe

I am not available for crisis response. If you are in immediate danger, please call emergency services. For emotional support in the U.S., text or call 988 to reach the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. For other locations, please use your local emergency numbers or crisis resources.
