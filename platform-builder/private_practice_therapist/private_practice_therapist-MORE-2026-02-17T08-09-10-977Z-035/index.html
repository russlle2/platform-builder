Session boundaries, confidentiality, and scope

Conversations are held in confidence consistent with applicable law. Records are kept securely and shared only with consent, except where law requires disclosure (e.g., imminent risk, court order). I will discuss limits to confidentiality at intake and answer any questions you have.

Payment is due at the time of service. Cancellation policies are shared at intake and in the intake packet. I strive for clarity around scheduling and billing.

I provide psychotherapy and coaching within my scope of training. I do not provide emergency services or clinical supervision here — if you need crisis support I will help connect you with appropriate resources.

If you are in immediate danger or are having thoughts of harming yourself or others:

Please call your local emergency number, or contact crisis services. In the U.S., you can call or text 988 to reach the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. If you are outside the U.S., please contact local emergency services or your local crisis resources.
