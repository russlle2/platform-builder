Common questions

Short answers from a clinical perspective. If your question isn't here, the booking page includes a short intake form to describe your concerns.

What should I expect at my first session?

We will orient to your reason for coming, outline confidentiality and boundaries, and collaborate on initial goals. I usually finish with a clear next step. If you have paperwork from insurance or another clinician, bring it.

How long is a session and how often do people come?

Standard individual sessions are 50 minutes. Frequency is collaborative — weekly is common to build momentum, but some people prefer biweekly or targeted series.

Do you accept insurance?

I am out-of-network for most carriers and provide superbills you can submit for reimbursement. I accept card and bank transfer. Sliding scale is available in limited cases; please ask if cost is a barrier.

What if I need to cancel?

Session cancellations are requested with at least 48 hours' notice when possible. Short-notice cancellations may incur a fee—this preserves the therapeutic container for everyone.

Is what I say confidential?

Yes, within standard clinical limits. I keep clinical notes and will explain limits to confidentiality (e.g., imminent risk of harm, mandatory reporting) before we begin. We can also discuss how you prefer notes to be handled.
