How I work with clients: a practical framework

My approach centers on clarity, testing small changes, and building actionable habits. I use assessed goals to guide session work and revisit progress regularly.

1. Intake & mapping

We build a practical map of current challenges, patterns, and immediate priorities. This is time for listening and identifying concrete targets for the work.

2. Short-cycle experiments

Small, testable actions between sessions—tools to try and reflect on. Each experiment is tailored and measurable so we know what helps.

3. Integration & maintenance

We build routines and decision-checks for lasting change. Maintenance is negotiated—some clients prefer ongoing rhythm; others step down after a short period.

4. Group learning & skill practice

For those in cohorts, shared sessions allow practice and peer feedback while maintaining private individual work as needed.
