Frequently Asked Questions

How can Private Practice Therapy help with my postpartum posture?
Our Private Practice Therapy specifically addresses the unique postural challenges new mothers face, such as changes in spinal alignment, pelvic floor function, and muscle imbalances. We'll work with you to identify and gently correct these shifts, reducing discomfort and supporting your body's natural recovery process.
Is this service suitable if I'm still feeling very tired or overwhelmed?
Absolutely. Our Private Practice Therapy is designed to be gentle and accommodating. We understand the demands of new motherhood, and our sessions are paced to suit your energy levels. Our goal is to provide supportive care that doesn't add to your overwhelm but rather helps you feel more grounded and comfortable in your body.
What does 'body awareness' mean in the context of postpartum recovery?
Body awareness refers to your ability to sense and understand what's happening in your body. In postpartum recovery, this means noticing how you hold your baby, how you sit or stand, and where you might be holding tension. Our Private Practice Therapy helps you tune into these signals, allowing you to make conscious adjustments for better comfort and alignment.
Will this Private Practice Therapy interfere with other postpartum care I'm receiving?
Our Private Practice Therapy is designed to complement other forms of postpartum care, such as physiotherapy or medical check-ups. We encourage open communication about all aspects of your recovery. Our focus on postural health and body awareness can enhance the benefits of other treatments and provide a holistic approach to your well-being.
