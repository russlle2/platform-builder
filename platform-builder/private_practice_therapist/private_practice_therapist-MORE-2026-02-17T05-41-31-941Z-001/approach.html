Therapist perspective

I aim to be pragmatic and humane. Therapy is an experiment: we try things, see what shifts, and adjust together. There are no promises—only careful work and attention to what helps.

Session format

Typical meetings are 45–55 minutes. We focus on skills you can use between sessions and review what you tried, with edits based on your experience.

Boundaries & crisis

I do not provide emergency services. If you are in immediate danger, call local emergency services. For urgent mental health support, please contact your local crisis resources.
