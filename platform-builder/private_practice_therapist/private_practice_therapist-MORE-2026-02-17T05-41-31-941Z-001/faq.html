Frequently asked questions

What should I expect at my first session?

The first session is a collaborative conversation: we clarify your concerns, background, and what you hope to change. I’ll share information about confidentiality and clinical boundaries, and we’ll agree on next steps together.

Do you accept insurance?

I do not bill insurance directly at this time. I can provide a superbill/receipt you may submit to your insurer. Coverage decisions are made by your insurance company; I can’t guarantee reimbursement.

How long are sessions and what is your cancellation policy?

Standard sessions are 50 minutes. If you need to cancel, please provide as much notice as possible—usually 48 hours—so the time can be offered to someone else. We’ll cover specifics at intake.

Can I message you between sessions?

Brief check-ins and scheduling messages are fine; extended therapeutic work happens in sessions. We’ll set boundaries for between-session contact and review limits to confidentiality.
