What to expect in sessions

Therapy here is collaborative and tailored. We begin with a clear assessment, set short-term goals, and practice skills you can reuse. Typical rhythm: initial assessment, a few focused sessions, then flexible follow-ups. Your goals set the pace.

  • First visit: Understanding concerns, background, safety, and confidentiality.
  • Plan: A two- to six-session focus plan or a longer care plan as needed.
  • Skills: Practical tools—emotion regulation, pacing, boundary setting, and coordination with supports.
  • Review: Periodic check-ins to adapt the plan; you can pause or change direction anytime.

Confidentiality & scope: Sessions are confidential within legal and ethical limits. This practice does not provide emergency services. If you are in crisis or danger, call your local emergency number or crisis line immediately.

Common concerns

What if therapy feels hard?

I work alongside you—adjusting pace and method so work feels doable. We track small wins, not perfection.

Can I stop anytime?

Yes. You control the pace. I’ll offer recommendations and explain likely next steps, but you decide whether to continue.

Is this confidential?

Yes, with standard limits for safety and legal requirements. We discuss limits to confidentiality at the first visit.
