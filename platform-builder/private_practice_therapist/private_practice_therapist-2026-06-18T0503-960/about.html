How do I fit therapy into my class schedule?

We schedule check-ins during natural breaks—like between your 10 a.m. and 2 p.m. classes, or right after your library study session. Your calendar is the roadmap, not the obstacle.

What if I'm not 'sick' yet but feel overwhelmed?

That's exactly when we help. Preventive care means adding one small strategy before stress becomes overwhelming—like a 10-minute breathing technique for before exams, not after panic sets in.

Do you help with things like imposter syndrome?

Yes—specifically how it shows up during class presentations or group work. We'll create a 'confidence script' for your next assignment, not just talk about 'feeling better.'

Can I use this during finals week without missing class?

Absolutely. Your sessions are designed around your actual class times. We'll schedule them during your natural breaks, like right after lab or during your lunch hour—no extra time needed.
