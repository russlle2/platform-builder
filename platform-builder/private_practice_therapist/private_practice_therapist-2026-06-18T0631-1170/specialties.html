Untangling the Threads of Sleeplessness: A Private Practice Therapist's Focus

The weight of essays and exams often follows you to bed, turning quiet hours into a restless landscape. We'll explore how anxiety and the relentless pace of college life disrupt your natural sleep patterns, leaving you feeling drained. My role as your Private Practice Therapist is to help you gently unbraid these tangled thoughts, paving the way for deeper, more restorative rest without the need for complex wellness jargon.
