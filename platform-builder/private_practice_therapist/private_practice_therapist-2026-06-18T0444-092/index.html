Frequently Asked Questions

How can Private Practice Therapy specifically help with postpartum stress and nervous system regulation?
As a new mother, your nervous system can be in a heightened state. Private practice therapy provides a dedicated space to understand these shifts and learn specific techniques to reduce stress, improve sleep, and feel more grounded. We focus on practical, actionable strategies for your unique postpartum experience.
Will your Private Practice Therapy approach feel overwhelming when I'm already so tired as a new mom?
Absolutely not. Our private practice therapy is designed to be gentle and supportive, meeting you exactly where you are in your postpartum recovery. We prioritize practices that are easily integrated into your daily life, even with limited time and energy, focusing on small, impactful shifts for nervous system regulation.
What kind of techniques will we use in Private Practice Therapy to help with my postpartum nervous system?
In our private practice therapy, we'll explore a variety of techniques including breathwork, mindful awareness practices, gentle body-based exercises, and cognitive reframing. The focus is always on what feels most effective and accessible for you to reduce stress and support your nervous system during your postpartum period.
Is Private Practice Therapy effective for new mothers even if I don't have a diagnosed condition?
Yes, absolutely. Many new mothers experience increased stress, anxiety, and an overactive nervous system without a formal diagnosis. Our private practice therapy is designed to provide proactive support, helping you build resilience and develop coping mechanisms specifically for the demands of postpartum life, fostering overall well-being.
