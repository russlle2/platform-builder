Frequently Asked Questions

How is seasonal care different from regular therapy?
We focus specifically on how weather, light changes, and seasonal routines impact your comfort – like how shorter days might affect your energy or autumn humidity might influence joint stiffness. It's not about treating illness, but maintaining what works for you now.
What if I'm not ready for big changes?
We begin with tiny, low-effort adjustments you can test right away, like a 5-minute morning movement routine or adjusting your tea time. You decide what feels manageable. We never suggest major overhauls – just small steps that fit seamlessly into your existing life.
Do you work with chronic conditions?
Yes, but gently. We collaborate with your care team to offer practical adjustments that align with your health plan – like how to stay comfortable during colder months without disrupting existing treatments or routines.
How often would I need to visit?
Most clients find weekly or biweekly 30-minute sessions work best for seasonal adjustments. We'll discuss what fits your schedule and needs after your first visit, not before. It's about consistency, not frequency.
