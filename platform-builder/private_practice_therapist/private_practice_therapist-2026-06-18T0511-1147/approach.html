Embracing the Earth's Lullaby: A Private Practice Therapist's Approach to Postpartum Recovery

Your postpartum period is a season of profound change, and true recovery requires more than just surviving the day. As your Private Practice Therapist, I offer a nurturing, earth-centered approach to help you re-establish a sense of groundedness and peace. We will work together to gently re-pattern your sleep, honoring your body's innate need for rest, allowing you to move through motherhood with greater ease and a renewed sense of vitality, much like a plant turning towards the sun.
