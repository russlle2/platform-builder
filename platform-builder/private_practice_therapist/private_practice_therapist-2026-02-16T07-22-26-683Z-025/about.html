Intake snapshot

I begin with a focused intake that clarifies your priorities, safety needs, and practical goals. Typical areas I assess:

  • Presenting concerns and how they impact daily life
  • Relational patterns and attachment-related stress
  • History of trauma or overwhelm and current coping
  • Values, supports, and readiness for change

Your first path

Most people begin with the Foundations Cohort — a 10-week small-group learning container that pairs individual sessions with peer work. For those seeking private work, we create a tailored plan: weekly or biweekly sessions, milestone reviews, and practical exercises between meetings.

Program frame: Foundations Cohort • Private Therapy Tracks • Transitional Intensives

Daily micro-practices

Sustainable change often comes through small, consistent practices. Examples I teach and adapt:

  • 2–5 minute grounding breath at the start/end of day
  • Boundary check: name one need before responding
  • Curiosity pause: notice a bodily sensation for 30s

Ethics, confidentiality & scope

Sessions are confidential within the limits of law. I do not offer emergency services. If you are in crisis or at immediate risk, call local emergency services or the crisis line in your area. I provide supportive psychotherapy and will refer for specialized care when needed.
