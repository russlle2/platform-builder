What to expect in the intake

A thorough but compassionate conversation about what brought you to therapy, history, current supports, and safety. We also clarify confidentiality and administrative items.

Before the session
You will receive an intake packet to complete online. If you prefer paper or have accessibility needs, let me know.
During intake
We explore priorities, immediate stressors, coping resources, and agree on a tentative plan and frequency.
After the first meeting
You’ll receive a summary of agreed aims and suggested next steps. If we decide it’s not a fit, I will offer referrals when possible.
Preparing emotionally
It’s common to feel nervous. A small micro-habit: practice two deep breaths before the session and bring any notes about what matters most to you.
