Privacy & limits of care

Confidentiality is central to our work. Communications are treated as confidential except where disclosure is required by law (e.g., imminent risk of harm, suspected abuse) or when you provide written authorization for release. Electronic contact (email/forms) is not fully secure; use phone if you need greater privacy. See the full privacy notes on the FAQ page.

Crisis disclaimer
