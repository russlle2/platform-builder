How I accompany you

My approach is integrative: relational, somatic, and skill-based. I attend to safety first, then to learning new ways of being that reduce reactivity and expand choice.

A practical diagnostic

In early sessions we map patterns across thoughts, body, and relationships. That map becomes a living plan with measurable short-term steps.

  1. Safety & stabilization
  2. Pattern recognition and experiment design
  3. Skill building integrated into daily life

Pathways & programs

Depending on need we choose one of several paths: the Reflective Cohort (8 weeks), an individual weekly track, or a short-term intensive for concentrated change. Cohorts emphasize shared learning and peer practice.

This season: Reflective Cohort (spring enrollment)

Practices between sessions

Therapy expands when what you learn is practiced. I assign short, personalized micro-steps — rarely more than 10 minutes a day.

  • Boundary rehearsal (30s scripting)
  • Regulation anchor (two slow exhales)
  • Weekly curiosity note (one observation recorded)

Enrollment & rates

I offer tiered enrollment: Core (individual therapy), Cohort (group plus 2 check-ins), and Intensive (3-session package). I will discuss fit and financial options during our first call.
