Areas I support

I work with adults navigating anxiety, relationship shifts, burnout, and the lingering effects of trauma. My stance is collaborative, non-pathologizing, and oriented toward practical change and inner steadiness.

Where we begin

A short assessment helps us identify patterns that maintain distress and the tangible first steps that reduce suffering. Below are the common presenting concerns I see.

Anxiety & chronic worry

Practical tools for nervous-system regulation, alongside therapy for underlying beliefs and anticipatory patterns that keep anxiety active.

Trauma recovery

Phase-oriented, paced work focused on safety, stabilization, and incremental integration of difficult memories and sensations.

Relationship repair

Individual and paired work aimed at communication, boundary clarity, and re-establishing secure connection when trust has frayed.

Burnout & exhaustion

Assessment of demands and values, actionable boundary strategies, and restoration practices that fit your life context.

Life transitions

Support for grief, career shifts, parenting changes, and any season demanding new meaning and practical adaptation.

Identity & belonging

Exploration of identity, cultural context, and how to live with greater authenticity in relationships and work.

Program options

I offer 1:1 therapy, short focused intensives, and cohort learning circles that emphasize peer support and skill-building. The "Sustained Presence" cohort runs quarterly and includes weekly group sessions plus two individual check-ins.

Small practices that matter

I teach brief somatic and relational practices you can use each day. These are meant to be feasible, specific, and adapted to your rhythm.

  • Signal-and-stop: one boundary phrase to practice
  • Micro-breaths for transitions
  • Daily calibration: note one thing that grounded you

Investments & packages

Options include single sessions, 6-session packages, and cohort tuition. I maintain a small sliding-scale pool for those with financial need. Specific rates and package details are discussed during intake.
