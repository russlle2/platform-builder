Frequently Asked Questions

How is your approach different from general therapy?
We focus on the *specific seasons* that stress you (like back-to-school, holiday chaos, or allergy season), not just 'stress' in general. You'll get tools for *when* your stress peaks—not a generic 'feel better' plan.
Do you work with toddlers and young kids?
Absolutely—but we coach *you*. You'll learn how to gently redirect meltdowns during naptime or turn grocery shopping into a 5-minute stress reset. Your kids' behavior is part of the equation, but your strategy is the focus.
How do I fit this into my chaotic schedule?
We integrate wellness into *your existing rhythms*. No extra 30-minute blocks—just 3-minute techniques during your coffee break or while waiting for pickup. Your time is precious; we make it work with your reality.
What if I'm not ready to 'fix' everything?
You don't need to fix everything. We start small: one predictable stress moment per week (e.g., 'Mondays after daycare'). We build your confidence with tiny wins, not overwhelming 'transformations.'
