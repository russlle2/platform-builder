How I work: a gentle, practical framework

My approach is relational and trauma-informed. I aim to help you feel safer in your body, clearer about priorities, and more able to take manageable steps toward what matters.

1. Clarify & stabilize
We begin with a focused assessment, identify immediate concerns, and build skills for regulation so you can engage with difficult material more safely.
2. Explore patterns
We gently map relational and emotional patterns. Insight is paired with experiments you can practice between sessions.
3. Practice change
I offer concrete tools (behavioral experiments, communication skills, grounding practices) and help tailor them to your life context.
4. Consolidate & plan
As goals shift, we create a plan for maintenance and next steps, including referrals when other supports are appropriate.

Therapeutic methods I integrate

  • Trauma-informed care and nervous system stabilization
  • Cognitive-behavioral techniques and behavioral activation
  • Attachment- and relationally-focused interventions
  • Mindfulness, self-compassion, and values-oriented strategies

Who finds this approach helpful?

People seeking practical change with compassionate support—those recovering from burnout, navigating transitions, healing from relational wounds, or wanting to manage anxiety in daily life.

Common questions about how I work

Q: What if I’m not ready to talk about trauma?
I respect pacing. We prioritize stabilization and safety first; deeper exploration only proceeds when you feel ready and have tools for regulation.
Q: Do you assign homework?
Occasionally: small experiments or reflections tailored to what you want and can reasonably try between sessions.
Q: Will you diagnose me?
Assessment may include diagnostic impressions when clinically relevant, but my focus is on functional changes and meaningful goals rather than labels alone.
