Questions I often hear

Below are answers to practical questions about therapy with me. If you don’t see what you need, reach out — I’m glad to clarify.

How do I know if therapy is a good fit right now?
We’ll explore your current concerns during an initial consultation. Fit depends on your goals, timing, and therapeutic approach. It is okay to try a few sessions and reassess together.
What can I expect from the first session?
A first meeting usually includes a brief history, clarifying immediate concerns, discussing confidentiality, and co-creating a plan. It’s common to leave with a small, manageable step to practice between sessions.
Do you accept insurance?
I provide superbills for private pay clients to submit to insurance. Direct billing depends on contract status; please contact me for up-to-date information.
What if I’m in crisis or need emergency help?
I do not provide emergency services. If you are in immediate danger, call emergency services or your local crisis line. If you are in the U.S., you may call 988 for the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline.
How do you protect privacy?
Client records are stored securely and shared only with consent or when disclosure is ethically or legally required. We will review confidentiality limits in intake paperwork.
How long do sessions last and what is your cancellation policy?
Standard sessions are 50 minutes. Please provide 48 hours’ notice for cancellations to avoid being charged the full session fee, except in emergency situations.
