Common questions

How long are sessions, and how often should I come?

Standard sessions are 50 minutes. Frequency depends on need—weekly is common to start; we can adapt over time.

Is this confidential? What about emergencies?

Yes—your privacy is protected by clinical confidentiality. I’ll explain limits (e.g., safety concerns) in the first session. If you are in crisis or an emergency, call local emergency services or a crisis line; therapy is not an emergency service.

Do you accept insurance?

I provide receipts for insurance reimbursement and can discuss options during intake. Sliding scale is available for limited slots.
