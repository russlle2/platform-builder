What makes a Private Practice Therapist different from other mental health professionals?

A Private Practice Therapist offers a personalized and often more flexible approach to mental health care. You typically work directly with one practitioner, fostering a consistent and deeply trusting therapeutic relationship tailored specifically to your needs without the administrative layers sometimes found in larger institutions.

How long does therapy with a Private Practice Therapist typically last?

The duration of therapy is highly individual and depends on your unique goals and circumstances. Some individuals benefit from short-term, focused support, while others find long-term therapy more beneficial for deeper exploration and sustained growth. We'll discuss this collaboratively as we progress, ensuring your therapy aligns with your evolving needs.

What should I do to prepare for my first session with a Private Practice Therapist?

To prepare for your first session, simply find a quiet, private space where you feel comfortable speaking openly. There's no need to bring anything specific or prepare a script. Just come as you are, with an open mind, ready to share what's on your heart. We'll guide you through the process.

Is what I share with my Private Practice Therapist confidential?

Absolutely. Confidentiality is a cornerstone of our therapeutic relationship. What you share in sessions is held in strict confidence, with very limited exceptions as mandated by law (such as imminent danger to yourself or others, or child/elder abuse). We will review the full confidentiality policy during your initial session.
