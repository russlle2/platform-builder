Ways I usually help

I work with adults navigating transitions, anxiety, low mood, stress with caregiving/work, and relationship patterns. Below are the common pathways clients choose—think of these as frames we can adjust together.

Sessions by appointment
50-minute sessions; available via telehealth and select in-person slots.

Stabilize Plan

Intro package: 4 sessions
A short series to map stressors, try 2-3 core skills, and create a safety/check-in plan. Good when life feels unpredictable and you want clear next steps.

Momentum Work

Ongoing weekly or biweekly work
Regular meetings to address patterns, try behavioral experiments, and practice skills between sessions. Better for steady change over time.

Focused Collaborative Session

Single 90-minute deep session (limited)
A concentrated session for complex decisions, planning, or resetting a relationship dynamic. Not a crisis service; best used when you have time to reflect afterward.

Care & Transitions

Short series or ongoing support
Support through life changes: caregiving shifts, job transition, loss, or returning to work. We focus on practical planning and self-care that fits your responsibilities.

Relationship Patterns

Individual therapy focused on interpersonal patterns
Work on communication, boundaries, and navigation of attachment-style concerns. Sessions prioritize safety and clarity about choices.

Skill-Building Sessions

Block of skills sessions
Short, targeted modules focused on sleep, emotion regulation, or worry management, tailored to what you want to practice between sessions.

Privacy & boundaries
Conversations are generally confidential. Exceptions include immediate risk of harm, suspected abuse, or legal requirements. For crises, call emergency services or your local crisis line (U.S.: 988).
