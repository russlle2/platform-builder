Frequently asked questions

Below you'll find practical answers about sessions, privacy, and how I approach care. If your question isn’t listed, reach out — I welcome short intro calls to see if we’re a good fit.

Most sessions are 50 minutes. Longer sessions (e.g., 90 minutes) are available for a focused reset. We’ll talk about timing on our first visit and adjust as needed.
I do not bill insurance directly. I provide a receipt (superbill) you may submit to your insurer. If you’d like help navigating that process, I’ll explain what the receipt includes.
My work blends thoughtful listening with practical tools. I draw from several evidence-informed approaches and tailor them to your situation, always attending to safety, values, and the realities of your life.
Generally I appreciate at least 48 hours’ notice. Late cancellations or missed sessions may be billed; if life intervenes, please contact me and we’ll discuss options.

Confidentiality, limits, and safety

What we discuss is private within the limits required by law and professional ethics. Those limits include situations of imminent danger to self or others, suspected abuse, or court orders. If you have specific privacy concerns, we can address them directly before beginning care.
I provide psychotherapy and will help coordinate referrals when appropriate. I do not provide emergency services; if you are in immediate danger, call emergency services or your local crisis line. For U.S. callers, 988 connects you to the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline.
