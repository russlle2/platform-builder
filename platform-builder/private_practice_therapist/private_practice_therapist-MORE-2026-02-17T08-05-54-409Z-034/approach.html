Session boundaries & confidentiality
My practice keeps predictable boundaries: scheduled sessions, brief administrative messages between appointments, and a clear approach to records. Below are a few specifics.
I offer psychotherapy and brief consultative sessions; I do not provide emergency crisis response. If you are in crisis, please call emergency services or your local crisis line. We will review any referral or stepped-care options as needed.
I maintain legible clinical notes for continuity of care. You have rights about your records; please ask if you want a copy or a conversation about them.
If you are thinking of harming yourself or others, or are in immediate danger, call emergency services right away. In the U.S., you can call 988 to reach the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline.
