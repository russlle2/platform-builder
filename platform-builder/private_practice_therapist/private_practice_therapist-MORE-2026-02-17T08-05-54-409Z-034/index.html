Session boundaries, confidentiality, and safety

Sessions are confidential within the limits of the law: I will keep what you share private except in cases of imminent harm, suspected abuse, or when required by a court. We will discuss confidentiality at the start of work so you know what to expect.

We’ll agree on a session cadence together. I request at least 48 hours notice for cancellations when possible; late cancellations may be billed. Telehealth is available; we’ll confirm secure technology and emergency contact information before our first remote session.

My focus is psychotherapy for adults. I do not prescribe medication. If your needs fall outside my scope, I will discuss referrals or coordinated care options with you.

If you are in crisis: If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call local emergency services right away. If you are in the United States and need urgent help, you can call or text 988 for the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. If you are elsewhere, please contact local emergency resources. This clinic does not provide emergency services.
