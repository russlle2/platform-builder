Session limits, confidentiality, and scope

Sessions are typically 50 minutes, focused on reflection and practical steps. I work collaboratively; we clarify goals and use evidence-informed approaches suited to your priorities. Therapy is not a guarantee of specific outcomes — progress varies by person and circumstances.

Conversations are confidential within the bounds of law and ethical practice. Exceptions may include imminent risk of harm to self or others, suspicion of abuse of a dependent adult or child, or court order. If you have concerns about privacy or recording, please bring them up before sessions begin.

My practice focuses on talk therapy: exploring thoughts, emotions, and behavior patterns and developing practical strategies. I do not provide emergency services, pharmacologic management, or forensic evaluations. If you need immediate medical advice or a medication review, I will help you connect with the appropriate provider.

If you are in crisis
