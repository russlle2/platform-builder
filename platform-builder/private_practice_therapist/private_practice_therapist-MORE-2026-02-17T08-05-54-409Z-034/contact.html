Session boundaries & confidentiality

Scope of work
What therapy covers and what it does not
+

Therapy here focuses on psychological, emotional, and relational concerns. I offer assessment, short-term interventions, and longer-term psychotherapy. Therapy is not a substitute for medical care when that is needed; if concerns would benefit from medical evaluation I will suggest coordination with appropriate providers.

Confidentiality
How your information is safeguarded
+

Your sessions are treated with respect and discretion. I keep clinical notes and basic scheduling/contact information. I will not release your records without written permission except where required by law, such as if there is an immediate risk of serious harm to you or others, reasonable suspicion of abuse of a child, elder, or dependent adult, or if ordered by a court.

Limits & coordination
When I may need to share information
+

If we agree to involve other professionals (e.g., medical providers, legal counsel), we will discuss what information is shared and obtain appropriate written consent. If safety concerns arise, I will work to keep you safe while following legal obligations.

If you would like to review a full copy of privacy practices before your first appointment, I can provide that upon request.
A note about immediate crisis
If you are in immediate danger or feel you may harm yourself or someone else, please contact local emergency services right away (for example, 911 in the United States) or go to the nearest emergency room. If you are within the U.S. and need urgent support, calling or texting 988 connects you with the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. If you are outside the U.S., please contact local emergency resources or crisis teams in your area.
