How sessions usually unfold

I blend evidence-informed methods with pragmatic tasks so therapy feels connected to your day-to-day life. Below is a typical rhythm I propose; we'll customize it together.

1. Orientation

We review the immediate concern, practical constraints, and shared goals. This helps shape short-term work and what to try between sessions.

2. Pattern work

We map recurring thoughts, feelings, or behaviors and run small experiments to test alternatives.

3. Integration

We focus on real-world application and maintenance strategies so shifts last beyond therapy.
