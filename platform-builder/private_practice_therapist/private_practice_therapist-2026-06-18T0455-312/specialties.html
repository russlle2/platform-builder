Understanding Postpartum Physical Sensations in Private Practice Therapy

Your body has undergone significant changes, and it's common to experience new or lingering physical sensations during postpartum recovery. As a Private Practice Therapist specializing in somatic healing, we offer a safe space to explore these experiences. We focus on identifying the roots of discomfort, whether it's muscle tension from feeding positions, pelvic floor shifts, or general body aches, and gently guide you toward relief.
