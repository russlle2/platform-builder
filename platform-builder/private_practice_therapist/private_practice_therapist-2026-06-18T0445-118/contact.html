Understanding Sleep Challenges in Retirement: A Private Practice Therapist's Perspective

The transition into retirement often brings shifts in routine and purpose, which can subtly disrupt sleep patterns. You might find yourself waking more frequently, struggling to fall asleep, or feeling less refreshed. These changes are common, yet they don't have to define your experience. We examine the specific factors influencing your sleep, from daily habits to underlying concerns, to build a precise plan for improvement.
