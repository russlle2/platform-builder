Ancient Rhythms for Modern Recovery: Private Practice Therapy Approaches to Sleep

Drawing from time-honored healing traditions, our Private Practice Therapy incorporates practices that gently guide your nervous system towards a state of calm. We explore techniques like mindful breathing, guided imagery, and gentle movement that are specifically adapted for those with chronic conditions. The aim is to reconnect you with your natural sleep-wake cycles, allowing your body's inherent capacity for repair and restoration to flourish, even amidst your recovery journey.
