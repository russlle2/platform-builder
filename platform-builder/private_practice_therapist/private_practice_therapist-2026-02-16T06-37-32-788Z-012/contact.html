Confidentiality, scope, and safety

Communications are treated with confidentiality consistent with professional and legal standards. Please note that electronic messages are not fully secure; if you have privacy concerns, indicate your preference for phone contact in your message.

I provide psychotherapy for adults and will discuss referrals when needs fall outside my scope (for example, urgent medical care, immediate risk of harm, or specialized psychiatric management). I do not provide emergency services.

Crisis disclaimer: If you are experiencing an emergency or are at immediate risk of harming yourself or others, please call your local emergency number or a crisis line right away (in the U.S. dial 988). If you are outside the U.S., contact local emergency services.
