How I work

I bring a warm, practical approach grounded in evidence-based methods. I meet you where you are — whether you are navigating anxiety, healing from past hurt, repairing relationships, or reorienting your life after a major change. Therapy with me is collaborative: we set clear goals, practice skills between sessions, and review progress together.

Confidentiality is central: sessions are private and protected. I will explain limits to confidentiality, my scope of practice, and provide referrals if needs fall outside therapy. If you are in crisis or danger, please call local emergency services or a crisis line immediately.

A simple framework I use

A practical, four-part rhythm to guide work in therapy.

  1. Listen & map: Clarify patterns and priorities with empathy and curiosity.
  2. Skill-build: Learn tools for managing distress and building new habits.
  3. Practice: Apply strategies between sessions in real-life situations.
  4. Reflect & adjust: Review progress and adapt plans as needed.
