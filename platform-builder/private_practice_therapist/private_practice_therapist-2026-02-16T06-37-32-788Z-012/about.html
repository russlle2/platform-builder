A note on safety and scope

I do not provide emergency services or medication management. If you are in crisis or feeling unsafe, contact local emergency services or a crisis line. All therapy conversations are confidential except where disclosure is required by law.

My gentle framework for change

  1. Connect: Create a trusting space and clarify what matters to you.
  2. Map: Understand patterns — thoughts, body, and relationships — that maintain difficulty.
  3. Try: Practice small experiments to shift unhelpful patterns.
  4. Embed: Build reliable strategies that fit your life and values.
