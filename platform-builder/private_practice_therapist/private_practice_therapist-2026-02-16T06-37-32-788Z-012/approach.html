Therapeutic philosophy and method

I aim to make therapy clear, humane, and useful. My role is to help you notice patterns, test new ways of being, and choose what aligns with your values.

The Five-Step Compass

A concise map I use to guide work together.

1 — Listen
I hold a careful listening presence to understand your story.
2 — Clarify
We identify patterns and what you want to change.
3 — Experiment
Small, safe practices to test new responses.
4 — Reflect
We review what worked and adjust steps.
5 — Sustain
Strategies to maintain gains within daily life.

Tailored pathways

I match structure to need: short-term goal work, medium-term stabilization, or longer relational/trauma-focused paths. Below are examples of how I frame packages — we adapt each plan to your life.

  • One-off Tune-up: 1–3 sessions to address a focused concern.
  • Weekly Support: Ongoing weekly sessions to build skills and reduce distress.
  • Expanded Work: Longer-term weekly work for deeper pattern change.

Common questions people ask

How quickly will I notice change?

Many people feel relief from learning tools within a few sessions; sustainable change often takes weeks to months. We'll set realistic milestones together.

Do you accept insurance?

I can provide a superbill for out-of-network reimbursement. Insurance coverage varies; we'll review options at intake.

What if I'm not sure therapy will help?

A short consultation can help you decide. Therapy is collaborative and you always retain the choice to pause or change approach.

Ethical notes

Confidentiality is standard; exceptions include harm to self or others, abuse of minors, or legal requirements. I do not provide emergency services. If you are in crisis, contact emergency services or a local crisis line immediately. Therapy complements medical care but is not a substitute for medical advice or medication management.
