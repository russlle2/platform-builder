Frequently Asked Questions

How can Private Practice Therapy help me manage physical stress as an athlete?
As an athlete, your body experiences significant stress. Private Practice Therapy helps you understand how this physical stress impacts your nervous system and mental state. We'll work on techniques to regulate your nervous system, reducing the chronic 'fight or flight' response, which in turn can improve your recovery, sleep quality, and overall physical resilience. It's about building inner calm to support your outer strength.
I'm not injured, but I feel constantly drained. Can Private Practice Therapy still help?
Absolutely. Feeling constantly drained, even without a specific injury, is often a sign of an overactive nervous system struggling to keep up with physical demands. Private Practice Therapy focuses on stress reduction and nervous system regulation to address this. We'll explore strategies to promote deeper rest and recovery, helping you restore your energy levels and feel more balanced and present in your training and daily life.
What kind of techniques will we use in Private Practice Therapy to reduce stress?
In Private Practice Therapy, we'll use evidence-based techniques tailored to your needs. This might include mindfulness practices to improve focus and reduce anxiety, somatic awareness to connect with and regulate your body's responses, or cognitive strategies to reframe performance pressures. The goal is to give you practical, actionable tools you can use both in and out of your sport to manage stress effectively.
How long does it take to see results from Private Practice Therapy for nervous system regulation?
The timeline for seeing results can vary for everyone, as your journey is unique. However, many clients report feeling a greater sense of calm and better equipped to manage stress within a few sessions. Consistency is key, and as we continue our Private Practice Therapy work, you'll develop a stronger understanding of your nervous system and more robust skills for long-term regulation and well-being.
