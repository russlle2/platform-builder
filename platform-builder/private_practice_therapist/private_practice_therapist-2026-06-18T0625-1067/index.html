Frequently Asked Questions

How can Private Practice Therapy specifically help with postpartum stress?
Private Practice Therapy offers a dedicated space to process the unique pressures of postpartum life. We focus on gentle techniques to calm your overstimulated nervous system, helping you reduce feelings of overwhelm and cultivate moments of peace amidst the demands of new motherhood. It's about finding your inner anchor.
I've never done anything like this before. Is this Private Practice Therapy right for me?
Absolutely. This Private Practice Therapy is crafted for new mothers, especially those without prior wellness experience. We meet you exactly where you are, offering accessible and gentle tools to help you navigate stress and regulate your nervous system. There's no expectation for you to know anything beforehand, only a willingness to explore a softer way of being.
How quickly can I expect to feel a difference in my nervous system regulation with Private Practice Therapy?
The path to nervous system regulation is a gentle unfolding, not a race. Many mothers report feeling a subtle shift towards more calm and groundedness after just a few Private Practice Therapy sessions. The consistent practice of these gentle techniques can lead to more profound and lasting peace over time, allowing you to integrate these moments into your daily rhythm.
What if I can't find time for Private Practice Therapy with a new baby?
We understand the precious and often unpredictable nature of your time. Our Private Practice Therapy offers flexible scheduling options and can be conducted virtually to fit seamlessly into your postpartum rhythm. Your well-being is paramount, and we're here to make support accessible, even if it means connecting during nap times or quiet moments.
