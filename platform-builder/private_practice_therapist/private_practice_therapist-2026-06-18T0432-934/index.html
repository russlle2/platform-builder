Frequently Asked Questions

How do you help with the chaos of the start of school?
We'll spend your first session mapping out your family's morning routine and building in buffers for unexpected moments. You'll get a simple checklist to keep things moving smoothly while still having quality time with your kids.
Do you focus on preventing burnout, not just after it happens?
Exactly. We meet before you feel overwhelmed. Our preventive approach includes weekly check-ins to adjust strategies and build your personal resilience toolkit, so you're equipped long before stress peaks.
What if my child is having a hard time adjusting to a new season?
We partner with you to handle the transition together. Whether it's a new school year or the quiet of winter, we'll create strategies that support your child's needs while keeping your own energy in check. It's about helping your whole family adjust.
How does this fit into my busy schedule?
We offer flexible scheduling, including virtual sessions that fit between nap times or after bedtime. And because we focus on prevention, you'll spend less time putting out fires and more time connecting with your family.
