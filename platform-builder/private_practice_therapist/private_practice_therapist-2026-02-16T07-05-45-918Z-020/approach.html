My therapeutic orientation

I integrate structured skill-building with reflective work — a pragmatic, human-centered approach aimed at immediate relief and longer-term insight. Treatment is tailored to your goals and life context.

3

Frequently raised concerns about starting therapy

How long will it take? Duration varies by goals. Some people find meaningful change in 6–12 sessions; others continue for longer as new goals emerge.

What if we don’t click? We’ll discuss fit openly; if a referral will better serve you, I’ll help make that transition smooth.
