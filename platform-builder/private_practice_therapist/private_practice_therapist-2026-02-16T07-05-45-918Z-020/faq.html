Questions I hear often

Brief, direct answers to practical questions about starting therapy with {{THERAPIST_NAME}}.

How do I know if this is the right therapist for me?
You can ask about orientation, training, typical session structure, and what to expect during the first three sessions. A 20-minute consult call helps us determine fit without obligation.
What are the areas you focus on clinically?
I work with anxiety, trauma-informed care, relationship difficulties, life transitions, and burnout. I integrate CBT, relational work and somatic-awareness practices. If your concern falls outside my scope (e.g., complex medication management), I will help refer you.
Do you take insurance?
I am a private-pay clinician and provide superbills upon request for out-of-network reimbursement. I will gladly discuss documentation and what paperwork you’ll receive.
How do cancellations work?
Please provide 48 hours' notice when possible. Cancelling within 24 hours may result in full charge, except in documented emergencies. We’ll review policies in the intake paperwork.
Is telehealth an option?
Yes. I offer secure video sessions. We’ll confirm technical requirements and privacy considerations before our first remote meeting.
What if I’m in crisis?
I do not provide emergency crisis response. If you are in immediate danger, please call your local emergency number or a crisis hotline. We can identify local crisis resources during intake.
How long will therapy take?
Duration varies. Some clients work through focused concerns over 6–12 sessions; others continue with less frequent sessions. We set goals together and review progress regularly.
Are sessions confidential?
Yes. I maintain confidentiality except where disclosure is required by law — such as imminent risk of harm to self or others, child or elder abuse, or court order. You’ll receive a privacy notice describing these limits.
