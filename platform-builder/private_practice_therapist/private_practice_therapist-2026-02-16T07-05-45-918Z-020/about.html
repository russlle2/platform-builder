How I typically work

Intake & focus

First sessions emphasize listening and a focused plan: presenting concern, history, safety, and immediate supports.

Tools + reflection

Sessions blend skill-building (breathwork, cognitive reframes, behavioral experiments) with reflective processing for meaning and patterns.

Between-session work

Brief, doable exercises to translate learning into your routines — adapted to your bandwidth.

Review & adapt

Regular check-ins to evaluate what’s helping and to refine the plan together.

Common hesitations I hear

Will therapy make things worse? It’s common to worry about surfacing uncomfortable material. I pace the work and prioritize safety; we pause or shift if something feels destabilizing.

Is online the same? Many people find virtual sessions highly effective. I’ll help create a predictable structure so the work feels contained.
