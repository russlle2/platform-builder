Common concerns, answered

"I’m not sure therapy will help me."

It’s reasonable to be cautious. We begin with practical, time-limited steps so you can evaluate whether the fit and progress feel right. We track goals together.

"I’m worried about privacy."

I protect client privacy and will explain limits (e.g., imminent risk, mandated reporting). Sessions are secure; telehealth uses encrypted platforms discussed at intake.

"Therapy is too expensive / time-consuming."

We can discuss shorter-term focused work, session frequency, or referrals. I provide transparent fee information up-front and discuss insurance/alternative options.

"I’ve tried therapy before and it didn’t stick."

That experience is important to explore. We look at what did and didn’t work and design a different path that fits your current needs.
