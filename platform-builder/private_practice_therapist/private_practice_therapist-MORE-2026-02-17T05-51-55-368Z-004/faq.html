Questions people often bring up

Below are practical answers about sessions, confidentiality, and what to expect when you work with me.

How are sessions scheduled and billed?
Sessions can be scheduled weekly, biweekly, or as part of a pre-paid block. Billing is processed via secure methods. You'll receive a receipt for each payment. Insurance billing may be discussed, but many clients use private-pay for flexibility.
What is your approach in therapy?
I blend reflective inquiry with practical skills and planning. Work is collaborative: we set aims together and track small changes that matter to you. We talk about how progress shows up in daily routines and relationships.
What if I need support between sessions?
Brief check-ins are available within negotiated boundaries, usually for clarifying a skill or updating a plan. For safety or crisis needs, please contact local crisis resources immediately.
Confidentiality and limits
Confidentiality is foundational. Exceptions include imminent risk of harm to self or others and certain legal requirements. We review these at intake and can discuss any concerns you have about privacy.
