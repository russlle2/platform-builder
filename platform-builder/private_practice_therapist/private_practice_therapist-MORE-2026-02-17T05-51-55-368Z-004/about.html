How progress gets built

I orient work around three practical moves: clarify what matters, experiment with small shifts, and adjust as you learn. It’s an iterative, values-aligned process—not a checklist.

  1. Map the pattern—what keeps the issue active now.
  2. Choose a focused experiment you can try over 1–3 weeks.
  3. Review outcomes and refine the next steps.

Confidential, respectful care

I work within clear ethical boundaries. Conversations are confidential except in specific legal or safety situations (risk to self/others, abuse). If you are in crisis, please call local emergency services or crisis lines; this site does not provide emergency support.
