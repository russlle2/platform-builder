Unraveling the Knots of College Anxiety and Academic Pressure

The relentless hum of college life can amplify anxieties, making each assignment feel like a mountain. Here, as your Private Practice Therapist, we gently explore the roots of this pressure, understanding how academic demands and social shifts intertwine to create emotional strain. We’ll carefully disentangle these threads, fostering a deeper understanding of your internal landscape and building resilience against the external tide.
