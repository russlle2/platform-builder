Frequently Asked Questions

Why is nervous system regulation so important specifically during postpartum recovery?
During postpartum, your body and mind are undergoing significant hormonal, sleep, and identity changes. An unregulated nervous system can amplify feelings of anxiety, overwhelm, and exhaustion. Focusing on regulation helps you cope more effectively with sleep deprivation, hormonal fluctuations, and the demands of newborn care, fostering a greater sense of calm and capacity as a new mother.
How is this Private Practice Therapist approach different from general talk therapy for new mothers?
While general talk therapy can be helpful, our Private Practice Therapist approach specifically targets the physiological and psychological aspects of stress reduction and nervous system regulation unique to the postpartum period. We use specific techniques to calm the body's stress response, rather than solely focusing on cognitive processing, giving you practical, embodied tools for immediate relief and long-term stability.
I'm so tired, how can I find the energy to engage in Private Practice Therapy?
We completely understand that your energy is limited right now. Our Private Practice Therapist sessions are designed to be supportive and restorative, not draining. We focus on gentle, accessible strategies that can be integrated into your demanding schedule, often starting with small, manageable steps. Our priority is to help you conserve and build your energy, not deplete it further.
What specific techniques does your Private Practice Therapist practice use to help with stress and nervous system regulation?
Our Private Practice Therapist practice employs evidence-based techniques such as gentle somatic exercises, mindful breathing practices, psychoeducation on the stress response, and strategies for creating micro-moments of calm throughout your day. We focus on practical, actionable tools that empower you to gently shift your nervous system out of fight-or-flight and into a more rested, regulated state.
