Frequently Asked Questions

How can Private Practice Therapy specifically help me with postpartum stress and nervous system regulation?
Our Private Practice Therapy is designed to offer a dedicated space for you to process the unique demands of postpartum life. We use gentle, evidence-informed techniques to help you recognize and respond to stress signals, guiding you toward practices that bring your nervous system back into balance. Think of it as learning to softly anchor yourself amidst the waves of new motherhood.
I'm overwhelmed and don't know where to start. Is prior wellness experience needed for Private Practice Therapy?
Absolutely not. Our Private Practice Therapy is built on the understanding that you are doing incredible work as a new mother, and no prior 'wellness experience' is required. We meet you exactly where you are, offering accessible tools and understanding guidance. Your only requirement is to show up, and we'll walk alongside you.
What if I feel too tired or emotional to engage in Private Practice Therapy?
We understand that energy levels fluctuate dramatically in postpartum. Our Private Practice Therapy sessions are designed to be a soft landing, not another demand. There's no pressure to perform or be 'on.' Sometimes, simply being present in a supportive space is enough to begin the process of nervous system regulation. We move at your pace, always.
Will Private Practice Therapy teach me practical tools I can use immediately?
Yes, our Private Practice Therapy focuses on providing you with gentle, actionable strategies that you can weave into your daily life. These aren't complicated techniques, but rather simple shifts and practices that can offer immediate moments of calm and regulation. We aim for gentle integration, making self-support feel natural and accessible, even on the busiest days.
