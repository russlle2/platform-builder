Frequently Asked Questions

How is this different from my regular therapy?
Regular therapy often treats symptoms after they arise. We build proactive tools *before* burnout hits—using your actual business schedule, not a generalized 'wellness' framework.
Can this really fit into my packed calendar?
Absolutely. Each strategy is designed for 5-15 minutes, embedded into your existing workflow. We don't add time—we optimize it. Your next meeting? We'll attach a reset technique to that calendar slot.
What if I'm not 'stressed' right now?
That's why this works. We focus on prevention when you're operating at capacity—before you hit the wall. Think of it as tuning your engine before the long road trip.
How quickly will I see results?
You'll notice sharper focus at your next high-pressure meeting. After 3 sessions, you'll have specific, scheduled protocols for your peak stress windows—no more last-minute panic.
