How can Private Practice Therapy help with my chronic pain?

Our Private Practice Therapy focuses on understanding the complex interplay between your physical symptoms, emotions, and past experiences. We use somatic techniques to help you gently process physical sensations, reduce muscle tension, and develop coping strategies that can alleviate discomfort and improve your functional abilities in daily life.

Is somatic healing in Private Practice Therapy suitable for all types of injuries?

Yes, our Private Practice Therapy offers somatic healing approaches that can be beneficial for many types of injuries, especially once acute medical care is complete. We focus on addressing the lingering physical and emotional impacts, helping you regain a sense of bodily integrity and reduce pain perception, regardless of the original injury type.

What does a typical Private Practice Therapy session look like for someone recovering from illness?

In a Private Practice Therapy session, we'll start by checking in on your current physical and emotional state. We might explore gentle movements, guided body scans, or discuss how your illness impacts your daily life. The pace is always set by you, ensuring comfort and safety as we work to improve your relationship with your body and foster resilience.

Will I have to talk extensively about my past in Private Practice Therapy?

While understanding your history can be helpful, our Private Practice Therapy emphasizes present-moment body awareness and sensation. You're in control of what you share. We prioritize helping you connect with your body's wisdom to find relief and healing, rather than solely focusing on in-depth narrative exploration, especially concerning physical discomfort.
