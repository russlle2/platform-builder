Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to take time off work for sessions?
No. Sessions fit around your existing schedule like a quick coffee break—usually 25-30 minutes, scheduled during your natural transition times (e.g., after your morning call). We never ask you to cancel meetings.
How is this different from ‘just relaxing’?
It’s not about ‘relaxing’—it’s about building predictable momentum. We focus on concrete, seasonal shifts in your work rhythm (like managing holiday deadline pressure) with tools you can use immediately, not abstract concepts.
What if I’ve never done wellness before?
That’s why we start small. Your first session is just checking in and finding one habit that fits your world—like adjusting your morning routine to avoid the 2 p.m. energy dip. No prior experience needed, just your calendar.
Can this really prevent burnout?
Yes—by targeting stress points before they compound. For example, we’ll work on managing the November client rush *before* it hits, so you’re not scrambling. It’s like keeping your car’s tires checked before the snowstorm.
