Questions people often ask

What should I expect in the first 2–3 sessions?
The first sessions focus on building rapport, clarifying concerns, and collaborating on immediate steps you can try. We also cover practical matters like scheduling, payment, and confidentiality.
Do you accept insurance?
I am a private-pay clinician and provide superbills for insurance reimbursement when possible. I can help you understand options during the intake.
How long are sessions and how often should I come?
Standard sessions are 50 minutes. Frequency is individualized—many people begin weekly and later reduce to biweekly as skills take hold.
