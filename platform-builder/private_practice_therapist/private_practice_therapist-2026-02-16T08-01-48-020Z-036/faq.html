Questions I often hear

Clear answers about process, privacy, and what to expect. If your question isn't below, reach out — I’m happy to clarify.

How do I know therapy is the right step?

You might consider therapy if longstanding patterns cause distress, relationships are strained, or life transitions feel overwhelming. An initial session is a chance to explore fit and goals; there is no obligation to continue.

What is a first session like?

The first visit (50–60 minutes) is an assessment and collaborative planning conversation. We discuss your history, current concerns, and practical goals. If we proceed, we agree on frequency and a tentative plan.

Do you take insurance?

I do not bill insurance directly. I can provide a superbill you may submit to your carrier for possible out-of-network reimbursement. Payment methods are listed on the Fees page.

Are sessions confidential?

Yes. Notes and communications are kept private except where disclosure is required by law (imminent risk of harm to self/others, child or elder abuse) or a valid court order. We can discuss limits to confidentiality during intake.

Can I do telehealth?

Yes. I offer secure telehealth visits and in-person sessions by appointment. Some intensive or assessment work may be recommended in person depending on needs.

What if I'm in crisis?

I do not provide emergency services. If you are in immediate danger, call 911 or your local emergency number. For non-immediate crises, local crisis lines and emergency mental health services can provide support. If you disclose serious intent or plan, I will follow applicable safety protocols.
