Areas I commonly support

Practical, compassionate therapy tailored to everyday life challenges and transitions.

What I work with

Below are common areas where people find our sessions helpful. Each description includes practical focus areas — not promises — just typical ways I support clients.

Anxiety & Worry

Developing steadier routines, grounding skills, and stepwise exposure to reduce avoidance and regain functioning.

Relationship Strain

Communication practices, boundary-setting, and repair strategies for couples and partners.

Trauma Reactions

Paced work on safety, resources, and skills to reduce intrusive memories and hypervigilance without re-traumatization.

Life Transitions

Decision support, values clarification, and practical planning during job changes, moves, or relationship shifts.

Work Stress & Burnout

Strategies to restore energy, renegotiate boundaries, and reframe workplace expectations.

Evidence & methods

What to expect

Initial sessions focus on assessment and priorities. Ongoing work typically combines skill-building with reflection and behavioral experiments tailored to your goals.
