Frequently Asked Questions

How does somatic healing help with the physical stress of parenting?
Somatic healing focuses on the mind-body connection. For parents, this means we address how the constant demands and anxieties of raising children can create physical tension, aches, and even chronic pain. We use gentle techniques to help you release trapped stress from your nervous system, leading to tangible physical relief and a greater sense of calm in your body.
I'm so busy; how can I fit this into my life as a parent?
We completely understand that your time is precious. Our approach is designed to be efficient and practical. We'll teach you simple, short somatic practices that you can easily integrate into your day, even in small pockets of time. The goal is to give you effective tools without adding more pressure to your already full schedule.
Is this just talk therapy, or will we actually address my physical pain?
While we will certainly talk about your experiences, this is not solely talk therapy. As a Private Practice Therapist specializing in somatic healing, we actively engage with your body's sensations, movements, and postures. We focus specifically on identifying and releasing the physical manifestations of stress and pain, helping you to feel more comfortable and at ease in your body.
What if I don't feel comfortable with 'body-focused' work?
It's perfectly normal to feel unsure about something new. Our work is always client-led and gentle. We move at your pace, respecting your comfort level every step of the way. You are always in control, and we'll ensure you feel safe and supported as you explore how your body holds stress and how it can find relief.
