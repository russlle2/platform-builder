FAQ
How long is a typical session?
Sessions are typically 50–60 minutes. We can discuss different cadences (weekly, biweekly) depending on your needs and the program you choose.
Do you accept insurance?
I do not bill insurance directly. I provide superbills that you may submit to your insurer for possible reimbursement if you have out-of-network benefits.
Is telehealth available?
Yes. Most clients meet via secure telehealth. In-person sessions may be available depending on location and public health considerations.
What if I need support between sessions?
I provide brief email check-ins as agreed in our treatment plan. I do not provide crisis services between sessions; please refer to emergency resources if you are in imminent danger.
How do you protect my privacy?
Clinical records are stored securely and shared only with your explicit written consent except where disclosure is required by law (e.g., imminent risk of harm). See the privacy section for details.
Can I end therapy if I don’t feel it’s right?
Absolutely. Therapy is voluntary. If you feel stuck or uncertain, we’ll explore that together and create an exit plan or referral as needed. My aim is to respect your autonomy and safety.
Do you work with couples/families?
I primarily work with individuals. For couples or family work, we will discuss whether that format is appropriate and, if so, set clear goals and boundaries for sessions.
A note on safety and scope
This site and conversations are not emergency contact points. If you are in immediate danger, please contact emergency services or a crisis line. Therapy supports many life challenges but is not a substitute for urgent medical or psychiatric care.
Still have a question?
Reach out briefly and we’ll respond within a few business days.
