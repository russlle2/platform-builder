Reflective check-in

Start with a simple, non-judgmental inventory. These prompts help you (and me) understand where support feels most alive.

  • Which patterns bring you here now? (e.g., tension, avoidance, repeating relationship cycles)
  • When do symptoms feel manageable? What helps, even briefly?
  • What would feel different if this problem eased? Who would you be then?

This is not a diagnostic medical tool but a reflective guide to clarify goals before we meet. For crisis support, call local emergency services or your crisis line.

Pathway: The Inner Compass (cohort model)

A gentle cohort-style course + individual sessions for people wanting steady progress with peer reflection. Designed as a 10-week container with live group sessions and two private check-ins.

  1. Root & Ground (Weeks 1–3) — Build safety, values mapping, small stabilizing practices.
  2. Explore Patterns (Weeks 4–6) — Track habitual responses, experiment with new choices in relationships and work.
  3. Integration & Forwarding (Weeks 7–10) — Strengthen new rhythms, plan supports for transitions beyond the cohort.

Each pathway includes a confidential intake, written agreements about scope and boundaries, and referrals if needs fall outside my scope of practice.

Daily micro-practices

Small practices that compound. Choose one to try for two weeks and notice what shifts.

2-minute ground

Sit with your feet on the floor, breathe into the belly for two minutes before starting work.

Check-in pause

Three times a day, note one feeling and one need. No solution, just noticing.

Compassion letter

Once a week, write one paragraph to yourself acknowledging effort, not results.
