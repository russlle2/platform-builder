How I work: an ethical, paced, and relational model

My practice blends evidence-based techniques, somatic awareness, and compassionate inquiry. Below is an outline of the steps you can expect.

Assessment & shared planning

Early sessions focus on building safety and mapping resources. We create a short care plan with explicit goals and measurable next steps, revisited regularly.

A staged process

1
Stabilize
Skills for regulation, sleep, and managing intense moments.
2
Explore
Kind, focused exploration of patterns, beliefs, and relationships.
3
Integrate
Practice new ways of relating and living, with support for relapse prevention.

Therapeutic stance

I hold a nonjudgmental, curious stance: clear boundaries, attunement, and an invitation to agency. I work to make the therapeutic frame predictable and safe.

Micro-habits I share early

Three-minute morning anchor: breath, intention, and one small doable task.
Boundary rehearsal: a short script you can try in real situations.
Evening consolidation: one insight and one next step recorded in a notebook.
