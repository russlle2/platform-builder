Where people often start

Clients typically seek support for persistent worry, sleep disruption, relationship friction, burnout, or for integration after a difficult life event. Below is a brief orientation to how I see presenting concerns.

  • Anxiety that feels constant or increasing
  • Unresolved relational pain or communication breakdowns
  • After-effects of acute or complex trauma
  • Chronic stress, compassion fatigue, or workplace burnout
  • Major life transitions: parenting, separation, grief, career shifts

How I begin: diagnostic mapping

Our first sessions are gently investigative. We map symptoms, strengths, patterns, and life context. This is collaborative — I offer frameworks, you offer the lived experience. Together we identify small, practical shifts that reduce suffering and increase capacity.

The mosaic plan

Clinical work is rarely linear. I propose layered plans that can include short-term skill work, weekly psychotherapy, or monthly reflective coaching. A typical pathway includes:

Foundational Phase — 4–8 sessions: stabilization, skill-building, sleep and anxiety tools.
Depth Phase — ongoing weekly work for processing relational patterns and trauma.
Integration Phase — tapering support, relapse prevention, and meaning-making.

Quick daily practices

2-minute anchor: notice breath, feel feet, name one safe thing.
Evening review: jot one small win before bed.
Boundary cue: a 30-second grounding pause before saying yes or no.
