Frequently Asked Questions

As a new mother, why is sleep so hard to come by, even when baby sleeps?
It's completely normal for new mothers to struggle with sleep, even during postpartum. Your body is recovering, hormones are shifting, and your mind is often busy with new responsibilities. As your Private Practice Therapist, we can explore gentle techniques to help quiet your mind, manage anxiety, and make the most of the sleep opportunities you do get.
I'm worried about sleep training or strict schedules. Does your Private Practice Therapist approach involve that?
No, my Private Practice Therapist approach focuses on what feels gentle and sustainable for you. We won't be implementing rigid sleep training or strict schedules. Instead, we'll explore flexible strategies to improve your sleep environment, manage expectations, and build routines that support your rest while honoring your baby's needs and your recovery.
How quickly can I expect to see improvements in my sleep with a Private Practice Therapist?
Every new mother's experience is unique. While there isn't a magic timeline, even small, consistent changes can lead to noticeable improvements in your rest and overall well-being. As your Private Practice Therapist, we'll celebrate every step forward and adjust our strategies as needed to ensure you're feeling more rested and supported.
I feel too tired to even think about therapy. How can a Private Practice Therapist help if I'm already overwhelmed?
That feeling is completely valid, and it's precisely why a Private Practice Therapist specializing in postpartum rest can be so helpful. Our sessions are designed to be a supportive, low-stress space. We start small, focusing on one or two manageable changes at a time, ensuring you feel empowered rather than more overwhelmed. Your comfort and capacity are always our priority.
