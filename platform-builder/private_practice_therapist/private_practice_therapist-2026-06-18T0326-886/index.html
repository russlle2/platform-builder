Frequently Asked Questions

How can Private Practice Therapy specifically help my recovery from chronic illness or injury, especially with posture?
Private Practice Therapy helps you gently re-engage with your body's innate intelligence. We focus on identifying subtle postural shifts that might be contributing to discomfort or limiting your movement after chronic illness or injury. By enhancing your body awareness, you learn to move in ways that support your healing, reduce strain, and build lasting resilience, rather than just coping with symptoms.
Is this approach suitable if my physical capabilities are still limited due to my condition?
Absolutely. Our Private Practice Therapy approach is always tailored to your current capabilities and comfort level. We start where you are, using gentle, mindful techniques to explore movement and posture. The goal is to build strength and awareness gradually, respecting your body's pace and ensuring you feel safe and supported throughout the process, regardless of your current physical limitations.
How does 'body awareness' from Private Practice Therapy translate into practical benefits for daily life?
Enhanced body awareness means you become more attuned to how your body feels and moves throughout your day. For someone recovering from chronic illness or injury, this can mean noticing early signs of fatigue or discomfort, adjusting your posture at your desk, or choosing movements that conserve energy. In Private Practice Therapy, it empowers you to make conscious choices that support your well-being, leading to greater comfort and functional independence.
What is the difference between this Private Practice Therapy and physical therapy?
While both aim to improve physical well-being, Private Practice Therapy here emphasizes a deeper, integrative connection between your mind and body, particularly focusing on how past illness or injury impacts your postural habits and overall awareness. We complement traditional physical therapy by focusing on the subtle, often unconscious, patterns that affect your daily comfort, helping you to truly embody stable and efficient movement long-term.
