Understanding Your Postural Landscape Post-Recovery in Private Practice Therapy

Chronic illness or injury can subtly shift your body's alignment, sometimes leading to new aches or limitations. In Private Practice Therapy, we look beyond symptoms to understand how your unique history impacts your current posture and movement patterns. We'll work together to identify areas of tension or weakness, helping you re-establish a supportive foundation for your body as you continue to heal.
