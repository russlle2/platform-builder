A Private Practice Therapist Approach: Grounding Your Academic Experience

Our method is rooted in a clear understanding of the neurobiology of stress, yet delivered with profound empathy for your lived experience. We will collaboratively explore the specific triggers of your academic and social anxieties, then systematically build a personalized repertoire of regulation techniques. This is not about simply coping, but about constructing enduring internal stability.
