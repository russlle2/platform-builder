Frequently Asked Questions

How can Private Practice Therapy at Glass Effect specifically help my sleep as a college student?
As a college student, your sleep challenges are unique, often tied to academic stress, social demands, and anxiety. We offer targeted strategies that go beyond basic sleep hygiene, addressing the underlying mental chatter and pressure that keeps you awake. We'll help you develop personalized routines and coping mechanisms to improve both the quantity and quality of your rest, tailored to your college schedule.
I'm overwhelmed with academics; will adding therapy just be another thing on my plate?
We understand that your time is incredibly valuable. Our therapy is designed to be efficient and effective, focusing on practical tools you can integrate into your existing schedule. Improving your sleep will actually free up mental energy, making you more productive and less stressed, ultimately lightening your overall load rather than adding to it.
My anxiety about grades is a huge reason I can't sleep. How does therapy address this?
That's a very common experience for college students. In our Private Practice Therapy sessions, we'll explore the connection between your academic anxiety and sleep disruption. We'll work on techniques to manage performance pressure, reframe negative thoughts, and develop healthier coping strategies so that those worries don't follow you to bed, allowing for more peaceful and restorative rest.
What kind of 'integrative approaches' does Glass Effect use for student sleep improvement?
Our approach for college students blends evidence-based Western techniques like Cognitive Behavioral Therapy for Insomnia (CBT-I) with Eastern-inspired mindfulness and relaxation practices. This means we'll not only help you identify and change thoughts and behaviors that disrupt sleep, but also teach you ways to calm your mind and body through practices like guided meditation and breathwork, creating a comprehensive path to better rest.
