Notes:
- No external assets or CDNs are used. The page references local SVGs expected at assets/img/avatar.svg (and others across the project).
- Keep clinical language grounded: avoid guarantees and include confidentiality, crisis guidance, and scope notes as present in the page.
- Form action currently points to {{PRIMARY_CTA_URL}}; change to your form handler or booking endpoint.

How to preview:
1. Place this file alongside the rest of the site files.
2. Ensure assets/img/avatar.svg, assets/img/hero.svg, and assets/img/pattern.svg exist in the assets/img directory.
3. Open contact.html in a browser.

Accessibility & ethics:
- The form includes labels, required fields, and a consent checkbox. Do not accept clinical emergencies via the form; maintain the crisis notice in visible view.
