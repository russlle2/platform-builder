My practice: a blend of presence and practical skill

I combine evidence-informed techniques with a relational, attuned stance. The aim is not to rush healing but to support steady shifts: more flexibility, more self-trust, and healthier relationships.

Relational focus
Therapy as a corrective relational experience.
Skill building
Concrete tools for regulation and communication.

A concise plan I offer

Every plan begins with an intake and a negotiated set of goals. I orient work around three concentric aims below to keep therapy focused and humane.

Safety & stabilization

Regulation skills that help you feel present and less flooded.

Pattern awareness

Mapping relational and internal patterns that keep you stuck.

Integration

New choices and habits that carry into daily life.

Quick diagnostic cues I listen for

  • Where does tension live in the body when a trigger is present?
  • What relational messages repeat across relationships?
  • How do patterns show up in daily routines and decision-making?

Daily practices I recommend

  1. A brief nervous system check-in (1–3 minutes) twice daily.
  2. One small boundary practice each week to experiment with limits.
  3. Reflective journaling: a sentence of curiosity about a repeated reaction.

Pricing approach

I describe prices transparently during intake. My framing is relational rather than transactional: session fees reflect time, training, and the therapeutic container. There is the option for package pricing (short-term bundles) and a limited sliding scale for those with financial need.
