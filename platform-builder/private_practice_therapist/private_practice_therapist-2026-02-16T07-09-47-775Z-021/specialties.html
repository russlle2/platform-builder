Areas of focus

Where I offer steady support

I work with adults seeking more felt-centered regulation, clarity in relationships, and grounded choices in life transitions. Below are common areas I support — each entry names a practical diagnostic sign and a starter micro-practice you can try.

Anxiety & chronic worry

Diagnostic sign: persistent, anticipatory thoughts that limit action. Starter practice: 4-4-6 breathing to gently lengthen exhales.

Trauma-related patterns

Diagnostic sign: repeated flashbacks, avoidance, or hypervigilance. Starter practice: a brief grounding sequence focusing on senses.

Relationship strain

Diagnostic sign: recurring conflict, unmet needs, or cutting off. Starter practice: a short boundary script to name needs with clarity.

Life transitions & loss

Diagnostic sign: disorientation, numbness, or persistent sorrow. Starter practice: a 5-minute meaning-mapping reflection.

Burnout & exhaustion

Diagnostic sign: depleted motivation, cynicism, or steady fatigue. Starter practice: micro-rest breaks scheduled across the day.

Self-worth and self-criticism

Diagnostic sign: harsh inner voice and avoidance of praise. Starter practice: a daily factual affirmation (3 facts about you).

Micro-practices to begin

One-minute breath check
A short boundary sentence
Evening sense check (sight, sound, touch)
Two-minute compassion note to self
