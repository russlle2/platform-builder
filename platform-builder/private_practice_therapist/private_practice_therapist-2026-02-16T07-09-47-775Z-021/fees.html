Intake Snapshot

Before placement, every participant completes a brief diagnostic intake — a thoughtful snapshot of your current concerns, strengths, and goals. This helps me align you with the right cohort or private rhythm.

  • 30–45 minute intake questionnaire + optional 20-minute consult
  • Core focus selection (anxiety, trauma, relationships, transitions, burnout)
  • Clarifying goals and boundaries, accessibility needs

Path Roadmaps

Each offering follows an intentional syllabus: grounding practices, relational experiments, and integration weeks to translate insight into daily life.
