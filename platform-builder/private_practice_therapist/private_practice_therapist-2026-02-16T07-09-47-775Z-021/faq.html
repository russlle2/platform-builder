Curiosities & Clarifications

A gentle guide to common practical questions — honest answers about process, privacy, and what to expect.

What happens between sessions?

I offer short, practical micro-habits after each session — brief practices you can do daily. These are optional and meant to support integration rather than create extra pressure.

How do I know which path fits me?

We begin with a diagnostic intake (questionnaire + brief consult). This helps me recommend the right cohort or a private continuity. If you’re unsure, book the 20-minute consult and we’ll orient together.

How long will the work take?

Cohorts are designed for 8–12 weeks to allow for practice and integration. Private work varies; many clients start with weekly sessions and reassess at three months. Therapy is not time-limited; duration depends on your goals.

Do you accept insurance?

I am typically out-of-network. I provide a superbill you can submit to many insurers for possible reimbursement. I can support clients in exploring options during intake.

What if I need to cancel or reschedule?

Please provide 48 hours notice when possible. Late cancellations or no-shows may be charged a fee, except in documented emergencies. Cohort refunds handled per cohort policy; deposits are non-refundable but sometimes transferable with notice.

What about confidentiality and emergencies?

Sessions are confidential and notes are stored securely. Limits to confidentiality include imminent risk of harm to self or others, or situations required by law. I do not provide crisis services; if you are in immediate danger, call emergency services or a crisis line in {{CITY}}, {{STATE}}.

What approaches do you use?

My work blends several modalities: {{MODALITIES}}. I orient towards relational, somatic, and narrative techniques depending on your needs. This is collaborative and tailored, not prescriptive.

Can I join mid-cohort?

Occasionally I accept mid-cohort placements depending on group dynamics. Please inquire early; some cohorts are intentionally closed after week two to preserve trust and continuity.
