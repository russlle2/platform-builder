Frequently Asked Questions

How does Private Practice Therapy specifically help athletes manage physical stress?
Our Private Practice Therapy helps you by teaching you to identify the root causes of your physical stress, whether it's poor posture, inefficient movement patterns, or accumulated tension. You'll learn personalized techniques to release this tension, improve your recovery, and build resilience, allowing you to sustain your active lifestyle with greater ease.
What kind of body awareness can I expect to develop in your Private Practice Therapy?
You can expect to develop a more nuanced understanding of how your body moves and holds itself. This includes noticing subtle misalignments, recognizing early signs of fatigue or strain, and connecting your mental state to your physical sensations. This heightened awareness empowers you to make conscious adjustments for better performance and injury prevention.
Is this Private Practice Therapy only for professional athletes, or can active individuals benefit too?
Our Private Practice Therapy is designed for any active individual, from recreational enthusiasts to professional athletes, who wants to optimize their physical health and manage stress. If you engage in regular physical activity and want to move with more ease, prevent injuries, and enhance your body's capabilities, this is for you.
How is postural health addressed in your Private Practice Therapy sessions?
In our Private Practice Therapy sessions, we'll collaboratively assess your current postural habits and movement patterns. You'll gain insight into how these influence your performance and discomfort. We then work on practical strategies to improve your alignment, strengthen supportive muscles, and integrate healthier posture into your daily activities and training.
