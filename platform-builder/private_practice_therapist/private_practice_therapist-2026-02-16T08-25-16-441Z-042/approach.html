Approach

Calm, paced psychotherapy tailored to your life

I combine relational listening with practical interventions. We set modest experiments you can try between sessions and adjust from there.

Single Sessions

Drop-in focused work for pressing issues or a check-in on progress.
Good for immediate concerns or to maintain gains between packages.

Focus Package — 6 sessions

A short, goal-directed path for changes you can measure in a few months.
Often used for anxiety management, new relationship skills, or life transition planning.

Maintenance Plan

Periodic sessions to support long-term practice and prevent relapse into old patterns.
Flexible scheduling, we can build a cadence that suits your life.
Step 1 — Reach out
A short intake call to assess needs, clarify logistics, and ensure fit.
Step 2 — Plan
We decide on a short-term plan or a longer rhythm that aligns with your goals.
Step 3 — Practice
Between-session practices and small experiments help the work land into daily life.
Step 4 — Review
We review progress and adapt. Ending is collaborative and paced to your needs.

Frequently asked

How do you decide between short and long-term work?
We discuss urgency, complexity, and your capacity for change. Often a short package provides enough momentum, and we can extend if useful.
What is your cancellation policy?
Please provide 48 hours' notice when possible. Late cancellations or no-shows may be billed; we'll review policy in intake.
Do you provide letters or evaluations?
I provide routine documentation related to treatment as appropriate, within ethical and legal guidelines. Specific evaluations may require referral.
