Frequently asked

Do you accept insurance?

I am an out-of-network provider. I can provide a superbill you may submit to your insurer. Payment is due at the time of session.

How long is a typical session?

Standard sessions are 50 minutes. We can discuss longer or more frequent meetings depending on your needs.

Are sessions confidential?

Yes. I follow professional confidentiality standards. Exceptions include imminent risk of harm to self or others, or court-ordered disclosure.
