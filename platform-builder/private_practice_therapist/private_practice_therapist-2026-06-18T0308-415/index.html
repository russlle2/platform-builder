Frequently Asked Questions

What makes working with a Private Practice Therapist different?
Working with a Private Practice Therapist often means receiving more personalized attention and a tailored approach to your care. You typically engage directly with your therapist for scheduling and treatment decisions, fostering a deeper, more consistent therapeutic relationship focused on your individual progress.
How long does therapy typically last with a Private Practice Therapist?
The duration of therapy is highly individualized and depends on your specific goals and circumstances. Some clients find resolution in short-term therapy, while others benefit from longer-term support. Your Private Practice Therapist will discuss an estimated timeline with you, regularly reviewing your progress and adjusting as needed to ensure it aligns with your evolving needs.
What should I expect from my first session with a Private Practice Therapist?
Your first session with your Private Practice Therapist is an opportunity to discuss what brings you to therapy, your background, and your aspirations for treatment. It’s also a chance for you to ask any questions you have and determine if our approach feels like a good fit for you. The goal is to establish a comfortable and safe space for our work together.
Do you offer virtual sessions for Private Practice Therapy?
Yes, to ensure accessibility and convenience, I offer secure virtual sessions for Private Practice Therapy. This allows you to engage in therapy from the comfort and privacy of your own space, maintaining continuity of care regardless of your location within . We can discuss if virtual therapy is the right option for your needs.
