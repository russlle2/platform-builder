How does Private Practice Therapy specifically address the physical pain from my burnout?

Our Private Practice Therapy employs somatic techniques to help you recognize and release chronic tension patterns your body holds due to stress. We focus on improving your nervous system regulation, which can directly reduce symptoms like headaches, back pain, and digestive upset often linked to professional burnout.

I'm a busy professional; how will Private Practice Therapy fit into my schedule?

We understand your time is valuable. Our Private Practice Therapy offers flexible scheduling options. The goal is to provide effective, focused sessions that deliver tangible relief, integrating practical tools you can use in your daily life, minimizing the need for extensive time commitments outside of our sessions.

Is this different from just getting a massage or seeing a chiropractor for my physical pain?

Yes, our Private Practice Therapy goes beyond symptom management. While massage or chiropractic care can offer temporary relief, we address the underlying nervous system patterns and emotional factors contributing to your physical pain from burnout. We aim for lasting change by teaching you to self-regulate and process stress somatically.

What kind of commitment is required for Private Practice Therapy focusing on somatic healing?

The commitment for Private Practice Therapy varies based on your individual needs and goals. Typically, we recommend consistent weekly or bi-weekly sessions initially to build momentum. We'll work collaboratively to establish a frequency that supports your progress and fits realistically within your professional life, always aiming for sustainable relief.
