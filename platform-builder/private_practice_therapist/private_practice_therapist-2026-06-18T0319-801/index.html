Frequently Asked Questions

How can Private Practice Therapy help me specifically after chronic illness or injury?
Our Private Practice Therapy focuses on gently re-educating your body. We help you address compensatory patterns that may have developed, improve your postural stability, and deepen your awareness of how your body feels, moves, and needs support. This helps reduce discomfort and build confidence in your body's capabilities.
I'm worried about pain; how gentle is your Private Practice Therapy approach?
Your comfort and safety are paramount. Our Private Practice Therapy approach is incredibly gentle and always respects your body's current limits. We start where you are, using slow, mindful movements and tailored exercises. The goal is to reduce strain, not create it, fostering a sense of ease and trust in your body.
What kind of exercises or techniques will we use in Private Practice Therapy?
In our Private Practice Therapy, we utilize a blend of mindful movement, gentle stretching, breathwork, and targeted postural exercises. The specific techniques are chosen to meet your individual needs, focusing on improving flexibility, strength, balance, and proprioception – your body's sense of its position in space.
How long does it typically take to see results with your Private Practice Therapy?
Recovery timelines are unique to everyone. With our Private Practice Therapy, many clients report feeling a greater sense of body awareness and reduced discomfort within a few sessions. Lasting changes in postural health and movement patterns often develop over several weeks or months, as you consistently apply what you learn.
