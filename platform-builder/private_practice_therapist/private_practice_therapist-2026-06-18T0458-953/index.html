Frequently Asked Questions

Do you work with retirees already managing health conditions?
Yes. We collaborate with your primary care team and adjust strategies to fit your medications and routine—like pairing your morning coffee with mindfulness to support your existing health habits.
How often do you meet for preventive care?
Most clients meet quarterly, aligning with seasonal changes. We spot early shifts in your energy before they become noticeable—like planning for winter sluggishness before the holidays.
Is this like medication for focus?
No. We focus on sustainable daily habits—like nature exposure or simple movement routines—that support brain health without adding to your pill count or doctor visits.
What should I expect in my first session?
We’ll discuss your typical week (gardening, grandkids, walks) and identify one small, seasonal tweak—like moving your afternoon tea to sunlight—so you feel energized, not overwhelmed.
