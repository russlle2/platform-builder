Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Private Practice Therapist help with my sleep when my child still wakes up at night?
Even if your child’s sleep isn't perfect, we can focus on optimizing your personal rest. We’ll explore strategies like improving your sleep environment, managing your own anxiety around nighttime awakenings, and ensuring you maximize the sleep you do get. It's about finding resilience within your current family dynamics.
I feel too busy and tired for therapy. How will this fit into my already packed schedule?
I understand your time is precious. Our sessions are designed to be efficient and focused on practical, actionable steps you can implement right away. We'll prioritize the most impactful changes first, and even small shifts can make a big difference in your energy levels and overall well-being. Think of it as an investment in your capacity to parent.
My partner doesn't believe in therapy. Can I still benefit from Private Practice Therapy alone?
Absolutely. While it can be helpful for partners to engage together, your individual work in therapy can profoundly impact your household's dynamics and your own sense of calm and rest. You'll gain tools and insights that you can apply and share, positively influencing your family environment regardless of your partner's direct involvement.
What kind of strategies will we focus on to improve my rest?
We'll tailor strategies to your unique situation, but common areas include establishing consistent pre-sleep routines, addressing nighttime worries and intrusive thoughts, developing self-compassion practices, and setting realistic boundaries around your time and energy. The aim is to build a sustainable foundation for your rest.
