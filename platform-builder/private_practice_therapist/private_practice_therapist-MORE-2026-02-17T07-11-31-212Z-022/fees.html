Clinical boundaries & safety

Sessions are confidential within the limits of the law. I will review limits to confidentiality during intake. I do not provide emergency crisis services; if you are in crisis or at risk of harm, please call local emergency services or a crisis line immediately.

My scope is psychotherapy for adults. When other supports are needed (medical, legal, or emergency), I will help coordinate referrals.
