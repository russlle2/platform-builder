Process that names patterns and creates manageable change

My work mixes careful assessment, skill-building, and short experiments. The aim is steady, private progress — not quick fixes.

Typical timeline
Assessment → short plan → practice & review.

1. Initial conversation

A brief consult to clarify the concern, mutual fit, and possible options. This helps us pick an entry point — a short package, assessment, or ongoing work.

2. Assessment & planning

We identify patterns, resources, and a small set of objectives. The plan is practical: clear, measurable, and time-bound when helpful.

3. Practice and review

You try small experiments between sessions. We review what worked and adjust. I provide concise tools to practice moment-to-moment changes.

4. Decide next steps

After an initial block, we evaluate whether brief additional work, ongoing sessions, or wrapping up best serves your goals.

What I do not offer

This practice does not provide emergency services, court testimony, or forensic evaluations. I will discuss limits to confidentiality as needed and refer when specialist services are required.

If you are experiencing a crisis, call emergency services or your local crisis line immediately.

Micro-practices

Short actions between sessions help learning stick: a minute of paced breathing, a one-line journal note, or a planned behavioral test. These are optional but often helpful.
