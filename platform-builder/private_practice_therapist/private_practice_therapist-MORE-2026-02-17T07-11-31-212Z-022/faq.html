Common questions

Answers framed to help you choose whether to begin. If you still have concerns, booking a short consult can clarify fit.

What is the typical format?
Most work happens in 50-minute sessions. I also offer extended single sessions and structured short packages. We’ll agree on goals and cadence at intake.
Do you take insurance?
I am private-pay and can provide a superbill for you to submit to your insurer for possible reimbursement. If using insurance is essential, I can discuss referrals.
How confidential is therapy?
Sessions are confidential within legal limits. Exceptions include imminent risk of harm to self or others, abuse of a minor, elder abuse, or a court order. I review confidentiality and limits during the intake process.
What if I’m in crisis?
If you are in immediate danger or at risk of harming yourself or others, call emergency services or your local crisis hotline. I do not provide emergency services; for urgent mental health needs use local crisis resources or 988 (US).
What should I expect in the first session?
We review your concerns, your history relevant to the presenting problem, and collaboratively create a focused plan with clear next steps. You can expect a gentle but structured conversation aimed at clarity and safety.
How long will therapy take?
There is no fixed timeline. Some people find brief, targeted packages helpful; others choose ongoing work. We’ll revisit goals and pacing regularly and adjust as needed.
