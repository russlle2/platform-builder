My background

I bring years of clinical experience with adults and couples, training in evidence-informed approaches, and ongoing consultation. My aim is to create a private, compassionate space where we pay attention to what keeps you from the life you want.

I work within my professional scope: psychotherapy for non-crisis concerns. I do not provide emergency care. If you are in immediate danger or need urgent medical help, call your local emergency services or a crisis line immediately.
