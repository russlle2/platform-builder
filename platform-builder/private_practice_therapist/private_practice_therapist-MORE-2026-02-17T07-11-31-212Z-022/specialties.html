Common themes I support

A practical, reflective approach to the challenges people bring: I aim to help you notice patterns, reduce friction, and practice alternatives that actually fit into your routine.

Session types

Grief, career shifts, relationship changes — a brief enabling sequence helps clarify choices and reduce overwhelm.

Anxiety that limits action

Relationship patterns

When familiar cycles keep repeating, we track triggers and rehearse different moves in a contained setting.

Self-criticism & low mood

Gentle, evidence-informed strategies to soften harsh self-talk and build reliable daily practices.

Parenting stress

Short-term focused work to identify realistic steps and reduce chronic fatigue and worry.

Work-related overwhelm

Practical boundaries, decision tools, and brief behavioral experiments to test new patterns at work.

How I approach an area

We begin with an assessment: current patterns, what has helped or not helped before, and one or two practical changes to try. I provide observations, simple skills, and accountability — all within confidential clinical limits.
