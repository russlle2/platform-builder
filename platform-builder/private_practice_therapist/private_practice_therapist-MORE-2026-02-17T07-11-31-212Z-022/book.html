Scheduling

I offer telehealth and limited in-person availability. New clients begin with an intake session; follow-up appointments can be scheduled at the close of a session or via email.

Email directly
After you request time I will reply with next steps and available times. This is not an emergency line.

What happens after you request

  1. I reply by email to confirm availability and send intake paperwork.
  2. Complete paperwork before your intake so we can use session time for clinical work.
  3. At intake we review history, set initial goals, and make a short plan.

Before your intake

  • Have a quiet, private space for telehealth.
  • Be ready to discuss current concerns and any prior mental health care.
  • Think about what you hope to get from a few sessions or longer-term work.
