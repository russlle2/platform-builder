Confidentiality

Conversations within therapy are private and shared only with your consent, except when legal or safety limits apply (for example, imminent risk of harm, suspected abuse of a vulnerable person, or court-ordered disclosure). Please ask for clarification about specific scenarios — I’ll explain scope and limits during intake.

Session boundaries

Standard sessions are 50 minutes. The VIP Focus Day is an extended format with pre-brief and post-session summary. Emergency outreach outside scheduled sessions may be limited; phone contacts are for scheduling and urgent administrative needs only.

If you or someone is in immediate danger:

Call your local emergency number right away or go to the nearest emergency department. If you are within the United States, you may dial 988 for mental health crisis support. If you are elsewhere, use your local emergency resources. This practice is not an emergency service.
