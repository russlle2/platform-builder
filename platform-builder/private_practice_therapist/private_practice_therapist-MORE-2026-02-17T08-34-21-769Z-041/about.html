Confidentiality, boundaries, and limits
Confidentiality ▾

Sessions are confidential. I will not disclose your information without your written consent except where required by law (e.g., imminent risk of harm to self or others, suspected abuse). Notes are kept securely.

Scope & limits of practice ▾

My work is psychotherapy and coaching within a private practice setting. I do not provide medical diagnosis or emergency services. When needs exceed outpatient therapy, I will discuss appropriate referrals.

Session boundaries ▾

Standard sessions are 50 minutes. Scheduling, cancellations, and remote options are discussed at intake. I maintain clear boundaries around communication outside sessions to preserve clinical clarity.
