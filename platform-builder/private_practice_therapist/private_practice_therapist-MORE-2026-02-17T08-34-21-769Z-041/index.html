How sessions are structured

Below are common questions about sessions, confidentiality, and limits of practice. If you have concerns, mention them in your consultation.

Typical sessions are 45–55 minutes. We agree on timing at the outset. If you are late, the session will generally end at the scheduled time to respect others' appointments.

Clinical notes and session content are confidential. I follow state regulations and professional ethical standards. Exceptions to confidentiality include imminent harm to self or others, suspected abuse of a minor or vulnerable adult, or a court order.

Cancellation policies and fee structure are discussed at intake. Payment is expected unless other arrangements are agreed upon. There are no guarantees about progress — therapy is a collaborative effort.

Telehealth sessions use a HIPAA-compliant platform. If you have specific accessibility needs, tell me so I can try to accommodate them.

Crisis and immediate safety:

If you are in immediate danger, please call 911 or local emergency services. If you are thinking about harming yourself, contact your nearest crisis line or the national suicide & crisis lifeline by dialing 988 (U.S.). This practice is not an emergency service.

What issues do you treat?

What is your approach?
I combine evidence-informed techniques with relational reflection — practical tools plus thoughtful clinical listening to support steady change.
