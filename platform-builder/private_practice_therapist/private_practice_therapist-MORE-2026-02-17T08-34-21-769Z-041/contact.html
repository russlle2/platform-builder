Session boundaries & confidentiality
Session structure — what to expect
Typical appointments last 45–60 minutes. We will set a mutually agreed cadence for ongoing work. If you need more time occasionally, we can discuss options in advance.
Confidentiality & limits — privacy and scope
Conversations are confidential and kept in secure records. Exceptions include imminent risk of harm to self or others, suspected abuse of a minor or vulnerable adult, or court-ordered disclosure. I will always discuss limits of confidentiality with you and include this in the intake paperwork.
Cancellation & fees — brief policy
I ask for at least 48 hours' notice to avoid a cancellation fee. Insurance coverage varies; I can provide receipts for reimbursement where appropriate. Fees and payment options are reviewed before starting work.
Telehealth & technical notes — video sessions
Video sessions are offered; we will confirm platform and privacy practices. If a session cannot be completed due to technology, we will reschedule or switch to phone when appropriate.
Crisis & safety: If you are in immediate danger or need urgent medical help, please call emergency services or your local crisis number. If you are within the United States, the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline is available at 988. If you are outside the U.S., contact local emergency services or reach out to a trusted local resource. I cannot provide emergency services via email.
