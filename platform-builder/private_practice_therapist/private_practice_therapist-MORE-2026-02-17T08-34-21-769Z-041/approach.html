My clinical approach

I prioritize clear assessment, collaborative goal-setting, and iterative checks on progress. The aim is to reduce uncertainty while honoring your experience.

Session boundaries & confidentiality
I maintain confidentiality in accordance with professional standards. Required exceptions to confidentiality will be explained during intake.
I keep outside-session contact limited to scheduling and brief clarifications. For clinical questions, we use sessions to preserve treatment effectiveness.
If your needs require a different level of support, I will assist with referrals and transition planning.
If immediate help is needed, call emergency services or local crisis resources. For U.S. lifeline support, dial or text 988.
