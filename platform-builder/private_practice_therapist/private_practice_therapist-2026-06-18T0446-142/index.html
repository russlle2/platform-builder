Frequently Asked Questions

How can Private Practice Therapy specifically help my postpartum sleep?
Our Private Practice Therapy focuses on understanding the unique sleep disruptors new mothers face, from hormonal shifts to feeding schedules. We'll help you develop personalized coping mechanisms, manage anxieties around sleep, and implement practical strategies for improving the quality and duration of your rest, even with a newborn.
I feel so overwhelmed, how do I even start to prioritize sleep in my postpartum recovery?
It's completely normal to feel overwhelmed. Our Private Practice Therapy provides a dedicated, calm space for you to explore these feelings without judgment. We’ll break down large goals into small, manageable steps, helping you integrate restorative practices that fit your current capacity and family life, prioritizing your well-being gently.
Will Private Practice Therapy really make a difference if my baby still wakes up frequently?
Absolutely. While we can't control your baby's sleep patterns, Private Practice Therapy can significantly impact how you cope with frequent awakenings. We'll work on your mindset, reduce anxiety related to sleep interruptions, and help you maximize the quality of the sleep you do get, leading to greater resilience and a more rested feeling overall.
Is this Private Practice Therapy only for severe sleep issues, or can it help with general exhaustion during postpartum?
Our Private Practice Therapy is for any new mother struggling with sleep and exhaustion, whether it's a chronic issue or the general weariness of postpartum life. We aim to support you in finding more consistent, restorative rest, improving your mood, energy, and overall capacity to enjoy this special time with your little one.
