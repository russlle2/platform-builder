Frequently Asked Questions

Can seasonal care work if I'm not feeling well *right now*?
Absolutely. We begin where your body is today—your current energy, not an idealized version. Your first session focuses on what 'doable' means *for you* this week.
How is this different from general wellness advice?
We skip generic 'meditate more' tips. Instead, we use local seasonal triggers (like fall allergens or summer heat) to create practical, low-energy strategies for *your* condition. No fluff, just what works for your body.
Do we discuss medical treatments?
We focus on the lifestyle side you don't get at doctor appointments. Think: 'How can you move comfortably after your arthritis pain flares in wet weather?' not medical decisions.
What if I have to cancel last-minute?
We honor your energy levels. Cancellations never trigger guilt—it's part of the rhythm. We reschedule when you're ready, not when the calendar says.
