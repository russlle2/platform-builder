Frequently Asked Questions

I'm a parent of young children and feel constantly stressed. Can Private Practice Therapy really help me?
Yes, absolutely. Our Private Practice Therapist specializes in supporting parents like you. We understand the unique pressures of raising young children and the emotional toll it can take. We'll help you identify what's overwhelming you and develop personalized strategies to manage stress, process emotions, and find more calm in your daily life.
What does 'trauma-informed care' mean for me as a parent?
Trauma-informed care means we approach your experiences with an understanding of how past difficult events or ongoing stress can impact your current emotional and behavioral patterns. For parents, this might mean recognizing how past experiences resurface amidst the intensity of childcare, and working with you gently to process these feelings in a way that feels safe and supportive.
I've never been to therapy before. Is this a good fit for someone without 'wellness experience'?
Yes, this is an excellent fit! Our approach is designed to be accessible and gentle, especially for those new to therapy. There's no expectation for you to have any prior 'wellness experience.' We'll guide you every step of the way, creating a comfortable and understanding environment where you can explore your feelings at your own pace.
How can Private Practice Therapy help me specifically with household stress related to young children?
We'll delve into the specific aspects of household stress you're facing – whether it's the constant noise, managing tantrums, sleep deprivation, or juggling multiple demands. Our Private Practice Therapist will help you build emotional resilience, learn coping mechanisms, and process the feelings that arise from these challenges, leading to a more peaceful and manageable home environment.
