Clinician-led therapeutic work

About the clinician

I offer time-limited and ongoing therapeutic options for adults and older adolescents focused on clearer decision-making, more stable daily functioning, and skills you can use outside sessions.

Training, values, and blend

Licensed clinician with integrative, evidence-informed practice

I draw from relational frameworks, CBT-derived skills, and brief structured approaches to tailor work to the specific episode that brought you here. My attention is practical: mapping the few changes that create forward movement while keeping a careful eye on safety and privacy.

What to expect

  • An initial intake to clarify goals and routine
  • Collaborative plan: short series or weekly sessions depending on need
  • Options for telehealth and limited in-person availability

Confidentiality & scope

Sessions and records are private within legal and ethical limits. I do not provide emergency services. If you are in crisis or have any immediate safety concerns, contact local emergency services or your crisis line. Therapy is not a substitute for urgent medical care.

Insurance, length, and practical details

  • Insurance: I do not bill insurance directly in all cases. I can provide receipts for out-of-network reimbursement on request.
  • Session length: Standard sessions are 50 minutes; other formats available by arrangement.
  • Limits: Therapy may involve emotional discomfort while addressing difficult topics. We discuss progress and adjust the plan over time.

No guarantees are made about outcomes. For urgent matters call your local emergency number or seek immediate help. This site is for informational purposes and does not replace a full clinical evaluation.
