Common questions

How long will it take?

Length depends on the issue and goals. Some people find a short series enough; others appreciate ongoing check-ins. We'll set expectations together and review progress periodically.

What if I'm unsure about therapy?

Starting with a single session or a brief series is common. We can treat early sessions as investigative: decide whether the approach fits your needs without long commitments.

Do you accept insurance?

I do not bill insurance directly here. I provide documentation you can submit for potential reimbursement. We'll discuss practical options before scheduling.

Ready to take a practical step?

If you'd like to talk, choose a booking option that feels manageable. I keep scheduling simple and private.
