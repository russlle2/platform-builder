Frequently asked questions

Short, direct answers to practical questions. If I missed something, please get in touch at {{EMAIL}} or call {{PHONE}}.

How do I know if therapy is right for me?
Therapy can help when patterns, stress, or transitions make it hard to move forward. An initial conversation clarifies fit and expected work; there is no pressure to commit beyond that.
How long are sessions and how often?
Standard sessions are ~50 minutes. Frequency is collaborative — weekly, biweekly, or a short series depending on need.
What about privacy and records?
Clinical notes are kept according to professional standards. Communications by email/phone are used for scheduling and administrative details. Exceptions to confidentiality are limited by law (risk of harm, subpoena).
Do you take insurance?
I am currently a private-pay clinician. I can provide a superbill you may submit to your insurer; reimbursement depends on your plan.
What if I need urgent help?
This practice does not provide emergency services. If you are in immediate danger, please call local emergency services or your regional crisis line.

Short answers about meetings

If you are booking a short series, we will agree on spacing and intended focus. For ongoing work, we revisit goals periodically. Cancellation policies are discussed at first contact.
