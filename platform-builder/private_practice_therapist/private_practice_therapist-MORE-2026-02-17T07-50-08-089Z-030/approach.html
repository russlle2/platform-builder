How I organize clinical work

My practice emphasizes practical testing and measurable adjustments: we form a short plan, practice a few changes, and evaluate what helps. That keeps therapy focused and useful while remaining open to deeper work if needed.

Core components

1. Collaborative mapping
We outline the immediate problems, strengths, and what would look different if things shifted.
2. Short experiments
Small behavior changes or communication steps you try between sessions to test what produces relief.
3. Skill building
Simple tools—breathing, planning, boundary setting—practiced in context.
4. Review and adapt
Regular check-ins determine whether to continue a series, shift focus, or open a different pathway.

Typical commitments

  • Single session (50 minutes) or 4–8 session focused series
  • Weekly sessions for time-limited work; options for fortnightly or monthly for maintenance

Boundaries & safety

Confidentiality is upheld except where disclosure is required by law. I do not provide crisis management. If you are at immediate risk, call emergency services or a crisis hotline.

Practical logistics

Sessions are offered by telehealth and limited in-person meetings. I provide receipts for out-of-network reimbursement where applicable. Scheduling and questions are handled via email or phone.
