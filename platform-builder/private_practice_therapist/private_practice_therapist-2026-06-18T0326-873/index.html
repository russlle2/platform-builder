Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is 'postural health' for seniors?
For seniors, postural health means finding comfortable and balanced ways to hold and move your body. It's not about being rigid, but rather about reducing strain and increasing ease in daily activities like sitting, walking, or even just relaxing. As your Private Practice Therapist, we'll gently explore how your body carries itself.
Do I need any special experience or physical ability to benefit from this?
Absolutely not! Our approach as your Private Practice Therapist is designed for everyone, especially seniors with no prior wellness experience. We focus on gentle observation and small, easy adjustments. You don't need to be flexible or strong; just bring your willingness to explore how your body feels.
How is this different from physical therapy or chiropractic care?
While we complement those practices, our Private Practice Therapist focus is on gentle body awareness and education, rather than treating injuries or making physical adjustments. We help you notice your habits and make subtle shifts for long-term comfort, empowering you with knowledge about your own body, at your own pace.
What can I expect in a typical session with the Private Practice Therapist?
A typical session involves gentle conversation and guided, easy movements or observations. We might explore how you sit, stand, or reach for something, simply noticing what your body tells you. It's a relaxed, collaborative space where you learn to listen to your body's signals for greater comfort. There’s no homework or strenuous exercises.
