About the clinician

I offer concentrated, carefully-held sessions for people moving through turning points. My work pairs relational clinical listening with clear, small practices that support daily life.

A little about me
I’m a licensed clinician with experience in adult mood concerns, transitions, grief, and relationship patterns. My style is collaborative: we notice, name patterns, and try short experiments to see what changes. Sessions are private, clinically informed, and paced to what you bring.

Clinical scope & boundaries
I provide outpatient psychotherapy for adults. I do not provide emergency or inpatient care. If you are in crisis or risk immediate harm to yourself or others, contact local emergency services or your crisis hotline. Confidentiality is respected; limits to confidentiality include harm to self/others, abuse of minors/elderly, and court-ordered disclosures.
A short way to know the style
I tend toward calm curiosity: we slow down what’s urgent, outline what’s useful, and pick one small next step you can try between sessions.
