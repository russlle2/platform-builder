Common ideas — and a different way to look at them

Myth: I need to fix everything before starting

Truth: Small, deliberate work can create clearer options. We begin from where you are, not from perfection.

Myth: Therapy must be lengthy to help

Truth: Focused packages can move a specific concern forward; deeper work is available when wanted.

Myth: I should already know the right answer

Truth: The work often surfaces new questions that gently refocus choice and action.

How I organize the work

  • Clarify

    We map what matters now: relationships, roles, and inner experience to find leverage.

  • Try

    Small experiments and specific practices you can try between sessions, adjusted to your pace.

  • Reflect

    We look at what shifts, what feels different, and what comes next — without pressure to be perfect.
