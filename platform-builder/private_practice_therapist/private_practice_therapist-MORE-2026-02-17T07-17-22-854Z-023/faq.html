Frequently asked questions

Below are practical answers about what a typical engagement looks like, how I structure sessions, and limits to confidentiality. If your question isn’t here, please reach out.

What happens in a first session?

▸
We use the first visit to clarify your immediate concerns, outline goals, and discuss scheduling and fees. It’s a chance to see if the fit feels right. I aim to be practical and collaborative about next steps.

How long is each appointment?

▸
Standard sessions are 50 minutes. Some clients prefer a slightly longer session occasionally; we can plan that in advance and discuss the schedule and fee.

Do you use telehealth?

▸
Yes. I meet both in person and by secure video. We’ll agree on what feels safest and most useful for your work together.

Will you share my notes with anyone?

▸
Clinical notes are kept confidential within legal and ethical limits. I discuss how records are kept during intake. I will only release records with your written consent or when required by law.

What if I’m experiencing a crisis?

▸
I do not provide emergency crisis services. If you are at immediate risk, please call local emergency services or a crisis line. If you’re unsure where to start, contact your local health services for 24/7 support.

Can you help me choose between a short package and ongoing sessions?

▸
Yes — I’ll ask about what outcome you hope to see and how much time you can commit. Some situations are well-suited to a focused few sessions; others benefit from a monthly rhythm. We can map a plan together.
