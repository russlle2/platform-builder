Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Private Practice Therapist specifically help with my athletic recovery?
As an athlete, your recovery isn't just about training; it's about deep, restorative sleep. We help you identify specific sleep disruptors related to your physical stress and training, providing targeted strategies to optimize your sleep architecture for better muscle repair, hormone regulation, and mental clarity, directly impacting your performance and injury prevention.
I'm always active; how do I prioritize sleep when I feel like there's no time?
It's a common challenge for active individuals. We work with you to integrate effective sleep strategies into your demanding schedule. This isn't about adding more to your plate, but about optimizing existing routines and implementing efficient winding-down techniques that significantly improve sleep quality, even with limited time.
Is this just about getting more hours of sleep, or something more?
While sufficient sleep duration is important, our focus extends to the quality and restorative nature of your sleep. We delve into factors like sleep efficiency, deep sleep cycles, and reducing sleep disturbances, all of which are crucial for effective physical and mental recovery, particularly when managing significant physical stress.
What if my physical stress is making it impossible to relax and sleep?
When physical stress is high, the body's 'fight or flight' response can impede relaxation. Our Private Practice Therapist approach provides you with evidence-based techniques to calm your nervous system, reduce physiological arousal before bed, and develop a more conducive environment for restorative rest, even amidst demanding physical activity.
