How do you handle seasonal changes when I'm already managing a chronic condition?

We focus on *your* triggers, not generic seasonal advice. If you know winter brings joint stiffness, we'll map your medication schedule, heating habits, and activity pacing to avoid that flare-up. Your seasonal plan is built around *your* body, not the calendar.

What makes your preventive care different from general wellness coaching?

We're trained in chronic illness recovery, not just 'feeling good.' We integrate your medical team's guidance and focus on *preventing* setbacks (like avoiding the flu that could derail your healing), not just optimizing energy.

How often do we meet to adjust my seasonal plan?

You choose. Many clients meet monthly during high-risk seasons (like allergy season or flu season), then quarterly for maintenance. We never overload your schedule—just enough support to stay ahead of seasonal shifts.

Do you work with my doctor?

Yes, with your permission. We share your seasonal prevention plan with your healthcare team to ensure everyone's working together. Your care stays coordinated, not scattered across different providers.
