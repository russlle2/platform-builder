Frequently Asked Questions

What specific postpartum challenges does your Private Practice Therapist address?
We specialize in supporting new mothers through a range of postpartum emotional challenges, including anxiety, depression, intrusive thoughts, birth trauma processing, identity shifts, and navigating relationship changes after childbirth. Our goal is to help you feel more grounded and confident.
How is trauma-informed care integrated into your postpartum therapy?
Trauma-informed care means we always consider the potential impact of past and present experiences on your emotional well-being. Your sessions are designed to be a safe, predictable, and empowering environment where your autonomy is respected, and your pace is honored. We prioritize building trust and helping you regain a sense of control.
What if I feel overwhelmed and unsure where to start with therapy?
It's completely normal to feel overwhelmed when beginning therapy, especially during postpartum. We start with gentle, conversational intake sessions to understand your unique situation without pressure. Our aim is to create a clear, supportive path forward, focusing on small, manageable steps that lead to significant emotional relief.
Can your Private Practice Therapist help me with bonding issues with my baby?
Absolutely. Challenges with bonding or feelings of disconnect with your baby can be incredibly distressing. We provide a non-judgmental space to explore these feelings, understand their origins, and develop strategies to strengthen your connection. Our therapy helps you address underlying emotional blocks so you can experience the joy of attachment.
