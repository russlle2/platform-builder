Understanding Postpartum Sleep Deprivation

The early months with a newborn are profoundly demanding, often leading to significant sleep disruption. This isn't just about feeling tired; sustained sleep deprivation can impact your mood, cognitive function, and overall well-being. Our Private Practice Therapist approach helps you understand the specific physiological and psychological factors affecting your sleep during this delicate period, laying the groundwork for tailored interventions.
