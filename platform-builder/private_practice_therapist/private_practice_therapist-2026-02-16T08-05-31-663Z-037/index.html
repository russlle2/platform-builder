Common questions people have before starting

Is therapy confidential?

Yes — except when there is risk of harm to self or others, or as otherwise required by law. We discuss the limits of confidentiality at intake.

Will I have to talk about my childhood?

Only as it helps your present goals. We work at a pace you can tolerate and focus on practical, present-day change.

What if I only need a few sessions?

Short-term or goal-focused work is welcome. We co-design the length and frequency of care based on progress and need.

Can you help with work-related stress?

Yes. I offer strategies for stress management, boundaries, and communication — within the boundaries of clinical practice (not workplace mediation).
