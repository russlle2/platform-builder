How do I know if therapy is right for me?

You don't need certainty—curiosity is enough. In an initial consultation we clarify goals and practical steps. Therapy is collaborative and adapted to your needs.

What modalities do you use?

I blend evidence-informed approaches: CBT, somatic work, attachment-focused therapy, and mindfulness-based practices. I tailor methods to your goal. Modalities: {{MODALITIES}}.

Do you take insurance?

I do not bill insurers directly. I provide superbills on request so you may seek out-of-network reimbursement. We can discuss affordability and options during intake.

What happens in a first session?

We review concerns, clarify goals, and discuss confidentiality and logistics. There is room to share what feels most important; you set the pace. Expect a mix of listening, gentle assessment, and collaborative planning.

How long before I notice change?

Change is individual. Some clients feel relief within weeks; meaningful shifts often take months of consistent work. We set realistic milestones and review progress regularly.

What if I'm in crisis?

If you are in immediate danger, call emergency services or go to your nearest ER. This practice does not provide emergency crisis services. We will create a safety plan in therapy when appropriate.

What about confidentiality and limits?

Communication is confidential within the limits defined by law (e.g., harm to self or others, court orders, suspected abuse). I review privacy and consent with every client before care begins.
