How we’ll work together

An integrative, measurable process designed for clarity and sustainable change. Below is a typical flow; we adapt it to your needs.

1 — Connect & assess

A 50-minute intake to clarify priorities, history, and practical constraints. We set initial targets and decide whether a short Focus Plan or longer rhythm is best.

2 — Design the plan

We co-create a plan: the Intensive Reset (4–6 weeks), Sustained Integration (12+ weeks), or a custom rhythm. Each plan includes specific experiments and check-ins.

3 — Practice & calibrate

Short, practical exercises plus reflection. I provide coping strategies, cognitive work, and somatic techniques as needed. Progress is reviewed regularly.

4 — Review & next steps

At plan end we review outcomes and either close, taper, or design the next phase. We aim for clear decision points rather than open-ended uncertainty.

Typical tools

CBT-informed formulations, somatic regulation exercises, brief exposure tasks, values-oriented decision work, and relational experiments.

Common hesitations

"I’m not ready to commit" — Many start with the Intensive Reset to test fit.
