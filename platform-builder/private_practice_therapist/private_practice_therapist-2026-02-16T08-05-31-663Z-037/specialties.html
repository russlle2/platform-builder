Assessment & pathways

I begin with a focused intake to clarify goals. From there we choose a pathway: an eight-session Focus Pack, a Time-Limited Integration plan, or ongoing therapy. We’ll review what’s reasonable together.

Client notes

Sessions are confidential. For individuals in crisis, please seek immediate support—this practice cannot provide emergency care.

Objections I often hear

  • "I don’t have time" — I offer rhythm-focused work and flexible scheduling.
  • "Therapy feels vague" — We set concrete weekly experiments you can try between sessions.
  • "I’m not sure it will help" — We review progress and adjust or close if it isn’t right.
