How is seasonal prevention different from standard therapy for entrepreneurs?

Standard therapy treats symptoms after burnout hits. We map your business calendar to build prevention around actual stress points—like pre-launch or board meetings—and deliver tools before energy dips.

Can preventive care truly fit without adding to my schedule?

Absolutely. Our quarterly 45-minute sessions slot into natural lulls (e.g., post-quarterly review). We integrate with your existing systems, so you gain time by avoiding burnout-driven crises.

Why only 15 clients per year?

To ensure we deeply understand your business rhythm. You get strategic planning, not a template. Every recommendation aligns with your revenue cycles and leadership priorities—no off-the-shelf advice.

When will I notice reduced stress in my work?

Most clients see measurable energy gains within 4 weeks of their first seasonal plan. You'll have concrete tools for specific stress points (e.g., 'How to reset after a 10-hour sales call')—not vague suggestions.
