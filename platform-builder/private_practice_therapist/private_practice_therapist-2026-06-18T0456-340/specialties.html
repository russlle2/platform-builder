Private Practice Therapist

Targeted Somatic Support for Major Life Transitions

Major life events—career shifts, relationship changes, relocation—often manifest as physical symptoms: persistent aches, chronic tension, or unexplained fatigue. Our Private Practice Therapist approach focuses on the body's intelligent response to stress, providing you with practical tools to understand and release stored physical discomfort, making space for adaptation and resilience during your transition.
