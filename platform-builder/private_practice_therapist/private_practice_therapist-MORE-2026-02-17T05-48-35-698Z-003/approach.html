How I typically work with people

My work blends clear assessment, collaborative goal-setting, and small, measurable experiments. I aim for predictable sessions where we learn from practice and track what shifts.

1. Getting oriented

We clarify what brings you in, identify immediate safety concerns, and set one measurable short-term aim for the next 2–4 weeks.

2. Active work

Sessions focus on testing small changes, practicing skills, and reflecting on what worked. Between-session tasks are brief and targeted.

3. Consolidation

We review what has shifted, troubleshoot remaining barriers, and consider a maintenance rhythm—often the membership option for ongoing support.

4. Transition planning

If you choose to step back, we create a practical plan to maintain gains and identify when to return if needed.

A typical session

  1. Check-in on the past week and brief mood/behavior tracking.
  2. Target one or two items for focused practice.
  3. Plan a short experiment or concrete skill to try before the next meeting.

Sessions are time-limited and focused; they are intended to support practical changes rather than open-ended advice. If at any point your needs are outside this practice, I will help with referrals.
