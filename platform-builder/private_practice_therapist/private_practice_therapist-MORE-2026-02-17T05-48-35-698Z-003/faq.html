Questions you might have

Below are common questions about how I work, what to expect in sessions, and practical details. If you don't see what you need, reach out at {{EMAIL}}.

What can I expect in our first meeting?
The first session is an opportunity to share the concerns that brought you here, discuss goals, and outline a collaborative plan. We'll also review confidentiality, recordkeeping, and logistics so you know what to expect.
How long is a session and how often do people usually meet?
Standard sessions are 50 minutes. Frequency varies — some people meet weekly for a short period, others choose biweekly or a monthly maintenance rhythm. We discuss what fits your needs and schedule.
Do you accept insurance?
I am a private-pay clinician. I can provide a receipt (superbill) you may submit to your insurer for reimbursement; please check with your plan for coverage and out-of-network benefits.
What if I’m in crisis between sessions?
I do not provide crisis services. If you are at immediate risk of harm, please call local emergency services or go to the nearest emergency room. For urgent help, you can also reach local crisis hotlines. If you’re unsure, contact your primary care or emergency resources.
How is confidentiality handled?
Conversations are confidential within legal and ethical limits. I will review exceptions (e.g., risk of harm, legal requirements) in our first session and answer any questions about records and privacy.
What should I do if payment or scheduling becomes difficult?
If finances or scheduling are a challenge, please tell me as early as possible. I try to be transparent and work collaboratively to find a feasible plan; limited reduced-contribution spots may be available depending on current capacity.
