About the clinician

I’m a licensed therapist offering membership-based ongoing care and time-limited support, thoughtfully paced to fit busy lives. I work collaboratively to help you identify practical shifts that feel sustainable.

My approach to working together

I aim for clear, humane guidance. In sessions we ground your concerns in present patterns, set manageable aims, and practice small adaptations that build momentum. I draw from relational, cognitive, and behavioral techniques, recommending what seems useful while keeping your values central.

Clinical orientation

I hold a pragmatic stance: therapy is a process of understanding patterns and experimenting with new responses. I prioritize safety, collaboration, and measurable steps you can try between meetings. Interventions are selected with attention to your context—workload, relationships, sleep, and cultural background.

Confidentiality & scope

All sessions are confidential within legal and ethical limits. I do not provide emergency services. If you are in crisis or at risk of harming yourself or others, please contact local emergency services or a crisis line immediately.
