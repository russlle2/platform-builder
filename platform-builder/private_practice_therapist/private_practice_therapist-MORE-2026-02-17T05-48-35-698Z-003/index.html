Support offerings

I offer individual clinical sessions plus a membership pathway designed to keep practice simple and consistent.

Focused Sessions

Structured 50-minute clinical sessions to address immediate concerns, clarify priorities, and develop skills. Appropriate for short-term and longer-term work depending on goals.

Standard session — Insurance / self-pay options

Practice Membership

A month-to-month membership that centers on maintenance: short check-ins, guided exercises, and weekly practice prompts. Designed for steady support between sessions.

Membership tiers available — contact to find the fit.

Focused Skill Workshops

Small-group workshops that teach practical skills for pacing, boundaries, and managing transition. Not crisis services—educational and skills-focused.

How we typically work together

  1. Intro conversation

    A brief phone or video call to make sure we’re a good fit and to clarify current concerns and logistics.

  2. Initial sessions

  3. Ongoing care or membership

    From scheduled sessions to an active membership, we choose a rhythm that supports consistent practice and review progress regularly.
