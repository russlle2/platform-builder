Areas I commonly support

Below are broad themes where I help people find steady, practical changes—either through a focused series of sessions or ongoing membership support.

Stress & OverwhelmShort-term & membership

Tools to reduce reactivity, clarify priorities, and create simple rhythms that reduce decision fatigue. We often track one daily micro-habit.

Relationship PatternsCouples & individuals

Focus on communication cycles, boundaries, and identifying repeated responses that keep problems active. Work can be behavioral and reflective.

Transitions & Role ChangeCareer, parenthood, relocations

Support for reframing identity shifts and building practical routines during change. Emphasis on small, testable adjustments to daily structure.

Anxiety — performance & dailySkills-focused

Skill-building for momentary regulation and longer-term exposure to reduce avoidance. Collaborative planning and repeated practice between sessions.

Mood fluctuationsMonitoring & structure

Tracking mood patterns, sleep, and activity to create a stabilizing framework. Work emphasizes behavioral activation and routine supports.

Caregiver fatigueSustained support

Practical boundary-setting, values clarification, and micro-practices to reduce burnout while maintaining necessary responsibilities.

How I frame services

I offer a flexible membership for ongoing support and single-course engagements for focused needs. Membership is framed around the rhythm you want: frequent check-ins for active change, or monthly visits for maintenance and troubleshooting.
