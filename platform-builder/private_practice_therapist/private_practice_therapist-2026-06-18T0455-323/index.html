Frequently Asked Questions

How does this gentle approach help with physical pain for seniors?
Our methods for seniors focus on very gentle, body-centered awareness practices. We help you slowly re-educate your nervous system to release long-held tensions that contribute to chronic pain, without requiring strenuous movement. It’s about listening to your body’s wisdom to find lasting comfort and ease.
Is somatic healing suitable for someone with limited mobility?
Absolutely. Somatic healing is highly adaptable. We work with your body's current capabilities, using subtle movements and mindful attention that can be done seated, lying down, or even with very minimal physical effort. Your comfort and safety are always our priority, ensuring the process is accessible and beneficial for you.
What can I expect in a typical session for physical pain relief?
In a session, you'll engage in guided awareness exercises where we gently explore how your body holds tension or discomfort. We'll use verbal cues and sometimes very light touch to help you become more attuned to your inner sensations. The goal is to facilitate your body's natural capacity to unwind and find greater freedom from pain.
How is this different from traditional physical therapy or massage?
While traditional therapies focus on strengthening or manipulating muscles, our somatic approach for seniors emphasizes the mind-body connection. We help you learn to self-regulate your nervous system and release patterned tensions from within. It's about empowering you to find sustainable relief by understanding and responding to your body's signals, rather than just treating symptoms.
