Blending Traditions: Your Private Practice Therapist's Integrative Approach to Sleep

Your path to restorative sleep will be a collaborative one, drawing on both time-tested Eastern wisdom and evidence-based Western techniques. As your Private Practice Therapist, I believe in tailoring our work to your individual needs, rather than a one-size-fits-all solution. We’ll gently explore practices that calm your nervous system, address pain-related sleep disruptions, and foster an environment conducive to deep rest, all while integrating seamlessly with your ongoing medical care.
