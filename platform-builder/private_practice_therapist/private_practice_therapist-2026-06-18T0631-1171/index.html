Frequently Asked Questions

How does Private Practice Therapy specifically address sleep for someone recovering from a chronic illness or injury?
Our Private Practice Therapy understands that chronic conditions bring unique sleep disruptions. We don't just offer general sleep advice; we delve into how your specific illness or injury impacts your rest, whether it's pain, medication side effects, fatigue, or emotional distress. We then craft gentle, tailored strategies that acknowledge your physical limitations and emotional landscape, aiming for truly restorative sleep.
I've never done any 'wellness' work before. Will I feel comfortable in your Private Practice Therapy?
Absolutely. Our Private Practice Therapy is designed to be gentle and accessible, especially for those new to intentional self-care. There’s no prior experience needed, nor any expectation of perfection. We meet you exactly where you are, offering a warm, non-judgmental space where you can explore your sleep needs at your own pace, feeling fully supported every step of the way.
What kind of 'restorative rest' does this Private Practice Therapy help me achieve?
Restorative rest, in our Private Practice Therapy, means sleep that genuinely recharges your body and mind, helping you mend and regain strength after chronic illness or injury. It's not just about hours; it's about the quality of your sleep – deeper, less fragmented, and truly recuperative. We help you cultivate an environment and mindset that encourages profound, healing rest, allowing your body's innate repair mechanisms to flourish.
I feel very tired and overwhelmed. How much effort will be required from me in this Private Practice Therapy?
We deeply understand the exhaustion that comes with chronic recovery. In our Private Practice Therapy, we prioritize gentle, manageable steps. We won't ask you for anything that feels overwhelming. Our approach is designed to be low-effort and deeply supportive, integrating seamlessly into your current capacity. We build toward rest with small, compassionate adjustments, ensuring you never feel pressured or further drained.
