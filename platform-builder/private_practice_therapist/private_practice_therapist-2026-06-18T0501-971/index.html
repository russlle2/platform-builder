Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Private Practice Therapist help me if I'm recovering from a chronic illness or injury?
As your Private Practice Therapist, I can provide tailored support to address the emotional and practical challenges of recovery. We'll focus on gentle strategies to manage symptoms, process grief or frustration, and build sustainable habits that honor your body's needs, without adding extra pressure.
I've never done therapy before; is this suitable for me without prior wellness experience?
Absolutely. My Private Practice Therapist approach is designed to be gentle and accessible, especially if you're new to therapy or wellness practices. We'll start at your comfort level, focusing on practical, easy-to-integrate steps that support your recovery journey without requiring any prior experience.
What does 'seasonal wellness and preventive care' mean in the context of my recovery?
For your recovery, seasonal wellness means aligning your self-care with natural rhythms, like adjusting routines with changing daylight or temperatures. Preventive care involves proactively building habits that support your physical and emotional health, aiming to reduce future setbacks and enhance your overall well-being as you heal.
Will this Private Practice Therapist focus on my physical symptoms or my emotional state?
As your Private Practice Therapist, we'll address both, understanding they are interconnected during recovery. While I don't provide medical treatment for physical symptoms, we'll explore how your emotional state impacts your physical experience and vice versa, developing strategies to support your whole self gently.
