Understanding the Emotional Landscape of Recovery

Recovering from chronic illness or injury involves more than just physical healing. It often brings significant emotional challenges, including grief, anxiety, frustration, and changes in self-perception. Our Private Practice Therapist services focus on acknowledging and addressing these complex feelings in a supportive, trauma-informed environment, helping you navigate this new terrain with greater clarity and peace.
