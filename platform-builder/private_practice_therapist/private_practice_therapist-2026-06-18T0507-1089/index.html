Frequently Asked Questions

Why is sleep improvement crucial for me as an athlete, and how can a Private Practice Therapist help?
As an athlete, deep sleep is your most potent recovery tool, facilitating muscle repair, hormone regulation, and cognitive function. A Private Practice Therapist helps you address the psychological barriers – such as stress or performance anxiety – that disrupt your sleep, teaching you strategies to optimize your rest for peak physical and mental readiness.
My physical training schedule is demanding; how will Private Practice Therapy fit into my routine?
We understand the demands of your schedule. Our Private Practice Therapy sessions are designed to be flexible, focusing on efficient, impactful strategies you can integrate without adding further strain. We help you create mental space for rest, turning recovery into an integral, rather than an additional, part of your routine.
I feel constantly 'on' due to training and competition. Can a Private Practice Therapist truly help me switch off?
Feeling 'on' is a common experience for dedicated athletes. As your Private Practice Therapist, we provide techniques to calm your active mind and nervous system, helping you transition from high-intensity states to states of profound relaxation, making it easier for you to 'switch off' and enter restorative sleep.
What specific outcomes can I expect from working with a Private Practice Therapist on sleep for performance?
You can expect to experience more consistent, deeper sleep, leading to reduced physical fatigue, improved mood, sharper focus, and enhanced recovery from training. Our Private Practice Therapy aims to empower you with lasting mental tools for sustained restorative rest, directly contributing to your athletic longevity and excellence.
