Navigating Entrepreneurial Stress with Body Awareness in Private Practice Therapy

The drive to achieve can often lead to sustained periods of high stress, impacting not just your mental state but also your physical posture and overall body awareness. Our Private Practice Therapist approach helps you identify how stress manifests physically – perhaps as hunched shoulders, a tight jaw, or shallow breathing. By bringing conscious awareness to these patterns, you can begin to interrupt the stress cycle and foster a more resilient, calm presence, even amidst the demands of your ambitious life.
