Frequently Asked Questions

How can Private Practice Therapy specifically help with the physical pain I feel as a parent of young children?
As a parent, your body is under constant strain from lifting, carrying, and bending. Our Private Practice Therapist approach uses somatic techniques to target and release the specific muscular tension that accumulates from these demands, such as shoulder stiffness, lower back pain, or chronic headaches, which are common when managing household stress and young children.
I'm always pressed for time; how long are the Private Practice Therapy sessions?
We understand your schedule is demanding. Our Private Practice Therapy sessions are designed to be efficient yet comprehensive, typically lasting 50 minutes. This allows for focused, results-driven work without adding undue pressure to your already busy day, ensuring you receive maximum benefit in a manageable timeframe.
Will somatic healing truly alleviate my chronic tension, or is it just temporary relief?
Our Private Practice Therapist approach to somatic healing is designed for more than temporary relief. We work to help you identify and shift the underlying patterns of stress and tension held in your body, providing you with tools and awareness to sustain changes long-term. This leads to a deeper, more lasting sense of physical ease beyond the session.
Is this Private Practice Therapy appropriate if I feel overwhelmed and emotionally drained, not just physically sore?
Absolutely. Physical pain and emotional overwhelm are often deeply connected, especially when managing the stresses of parenting young children. Our Private Practice Therapist specializes in somatic healing, which addresses both the physical manifestations of stress and the emotional load. By releasing bodily tension, you often find greater emotional spaciousness and a renewed sense of calm.
