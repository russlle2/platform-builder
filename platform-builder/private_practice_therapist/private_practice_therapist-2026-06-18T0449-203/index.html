Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly does a Private Practice Therapist offer for someone my age?
As your Private Practice Therapist, I offer a confidential and supportive space specifically designed for seniors like you. We can discuss life transitions, feelings of loss, family dynamics, or simply finding a renewed sense of purpose and joy. It's about having a trusted, understanding listener who can help you navigate your emotional landscape with gentle guidance.
I'm not sure if my feelings are 'serious enough' for a Private Practice Therapist. How can I tell?
There's no need for your feelings to reach a certain level of 'seriousness' to seek support from a Private Practice Therapist. If you're experiencing persistent sadness, anxiety, difficulty coping with changes, or simply wish for a space to talk things through, that's reason enough. Your emotional comfort and peace of mind are always valid reasons to reach out.
What can I expect in my first few sessions with a Private Practice Therapist?
In our initial sessions, your Private Practice Therapist will focus on getting to know you and understanding what brings you here. We'll chat about your life experiences, your concerns, and what you hope to gain from our time together. It's a gentle, conversational start, designed to make you feel comfortable and respected, allowing us to build a foundation of trust at your own pace.
Is what I share with my Private Practice Therapist truly confidential?
Yes, absolutely. Confidentiality is a cornerstone of our work as Private Practice Therapists. What you share with me remains private, with very few legal exceptions, such as immediate danger to yourself or others. Your trust and privacy are paramount, creating a secure space for you to express yourself freely.
