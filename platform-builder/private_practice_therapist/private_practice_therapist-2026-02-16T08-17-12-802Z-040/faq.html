How do I know if we’re a good fit?
Therapy is a collaborative relationship. In the first session we’ll clarify goals and see whether my approach feels comfortable for you. If not, I’ll help with referrals. Fit matters as much as technique.
Do you accept insurance?
I do not bill insurance directly. Many clients choose out-of-network reimbursement and I provide a superbill you can submit to your insurer. I am happy to discuss financial options including limited sliding scale slots.
What issues do you work with?
I work with anxiety, trauma recovery, relationship concerns, life transitions, stress and burnout. If your needs require medical management, I coordinate care with prescribers when appropriate.
How often will we meet?
Frequency is flexible. Many clients begin weekly for 8–12 weeks then move to bi-weekly or monthly check-ins. We set a rhythm that supports your goals while respecting your schedule and budget.
What if I’m in crisis or need immediate help?
This practice does not provide crisis services. If you are in immediate danger, call 911 or your local emergency number. Crisis lines and local emergency resources are best for urgent safety needs.
How is confidentiality handled?
Your information is confidential within legal and ethical limits (e.g., imminent harm, abuse of a minor, court order). Records are stored securely. We discuss consent before sharing any information.

Therapeutic approach
