My clinical story

I blend evidence-informed approaches with a warm, playful stance to help you find footing during uncertain seasons. I trained in trauma-informed CBT, integrate somatic awareness, and emphasize collaborative goal setting. My work centers on practical skills you can use between sessions — I value transparency about scope, limits, and expected pace of change.

Confidentiality: sessions are private and protected under applicable laws. I do not provide emergency services. If you are in immediate danger, call local emergency services or a crisis line.

How I work: a three-track framework

Stabilize

Short-term tools to reduce distress: grounding, breath work, sleep habits, and behavioral scheduling.

Understand

Explore patterns, core beliefs, and relational cycles with curiosity — we map how past experiences shape present reactions.

Practice

Build skills into daily life: exposure, communication rehearsal, self-compassion practices, and relapse planning.

Support Paths

I offer short and longer-term pathways depending on need.

Compass: Brief Support

4–6 sessions focused on immediate stabilization and a clear action plan. Good for acute stress, decision points, or guidance.

  • Telehealth available
  • Simple homework & check-ins

Harbor: Ongoing Work

Open-ended weekly sessions for deeper processing around trauma, relationships, or persistent anxiety.

  • Integrated somatic & cognitive work
  • Periodic progress reviews

Bridge: Couples & Communication

Focused sessions to improve listening, boundary setting, and conflict patterns for partners.

  • Structured exercises in-session
  • Referral network for couples needing specialized care
