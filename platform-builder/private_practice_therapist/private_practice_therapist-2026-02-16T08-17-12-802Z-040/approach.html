Introducing The Bridge method

The Bridge is my working structure: support to cross from stuck patterns toward intentional action. It centers safety, curiosity, and rehearsal.

1. Anchor

Stabilize symptoms and make a safety plan if needed. Grounding, sleep, and coping resources come first.

2. Map

Understand triggers, patterns, and the stories that maintain them.

3. Practice

Try new responses in small, supported steps and adapt based on learning.

Tools & techniques I draw on

  • Mindfulness and distress-tolerance skills
  • Attachment-informed relational work
  • Phase-based approaches for trauma when indicated
