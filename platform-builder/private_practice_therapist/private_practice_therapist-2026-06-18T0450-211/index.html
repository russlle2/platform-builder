Frequently Asked Questions

How is this private practice therapy different for someone experiencing professional burnout?
Our approach specifically acknowledges the unique stressors of demanding careers. We focus on evidence-based methods tailored to address chronic stress, emotional depletion, and the feeling of being overwhelmed, rather than general anxiety or depression. Your therapy will integrate your professional context into your healing.
I'm hesitant to start private practice therapy; will it add more stress to my already busy schedule?
We understand your time is valuable and your energy is limited. Our process is designed to integrate seamlessly into your life, offering practical strategies and support without adding undue burden. We aim to be a source of relief, not another source of stress, helping you manage your emotional load more effectively.
What does 'trauma-informed care' mean in the context of professional burnout in private practice therapy?
It means we recognize that chronic stress and demanding environments can have a similar impact on your nervous system as more overt trauma. We approach your emotional state with sensitivity, understanding that burnout isn't just about being tired, but often involves deeper emotional responses to sustained pressure and unmet needs, guiding your healing gently and effectively.
Will private practice therapy help me learn to 'cope better' with my demanding job, or something more?
While coping strategies are part of our work, our private practice therapy goes beyond just 'coping.' We aim for deeper emotional healing, helping you understand the root causes of your burnout, process emotional residue, and develop sustainable ways to engage with your life and career that honor your well-being. It's about thriving, not just surviving.
