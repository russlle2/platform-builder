Frequently Asked Questions

How can Private Practice Therapy specifically support my recovery from chronic illness or injury?
Private Practice Therapy offers focused support in understanding and addressing the unique nervous system dysregulation that often accompanies chronic illness or injury. We prioritize practices that reduce physiological stress, improve your body's capacity for rest, and gently guide you toward a more regulated state, rather than just coping with symptoms.
What kind of techniques will we use to regulate my nervous system in Private Practice Therapy?
Our approach in Private Practice Therapy integrates evidence-based techniques such as somatic experiencing, breathwork, and mindful awareness practices. These methods are designed to help you gently attune to your body's signals, release stored tension, and re-pattern your nervous system responses, fostering a deeper sense of calm and safety.
Will Private Practice Therapy help with the fatigue and overwhelm I feel after my illness or injury?
Yes, a primary focus of Private Practice Therapy for those recovering from chronic illness or injury is to address the underlying nervous system stress that contributes to fatigue and overwhelm. By teaching your body to move out of 'fight or flight' and into 'rest and digest' more effectively, you can experience a reduction in these taxing sensations and cultivate more sustained energy.
Is Private Practice Therapy compatible with my medical treatment for chronic illness or injury?
Absolutely. Private Practice Therapy is designed to be a complementary support to your medical care. We work to enhance your body's natural capacity for healing and resilience, collaborating with your existing healthcare providers to ensure a holistic approach to your well-being. Our aim is to reduce the physiological burden of illness and support your overall recovery.
