Areas I commonly work with

I tend to work with adults experiencing anxiety, life transitions, relationship conflict, and the impacts of chronic stress. My approach centers safety, practical skills, and collaborative goals.

Request a consultation
Anxiety & Worry

I help people build sustainable ways to reduce rumination, manage panic moments, and regain confidence in daily functioning using behavioral tools and pacing.

Trauma-Informed Support

Trauma can show up as hypervigilance, avoidance, or numbness. I offer grounding, stabilization, and paced processing while prioritizing safety and consent.

Relationship Work

Whether it’s communication patterns, boundary-setting, or separation, I work with individuals and couples to clarify needs and practices that shift interactions toward safety.

Burnout & Work Stress

When work overwhelms, we explore systems-level thinking, rest strategies, and values-aligned changes to reduce exhaustion without shame.

Transitions & Life Shifts

Big life changes—relocation, parenting, job shifts—bring grief and growth. Therapy can be a navigational practice during these times.

Self-Compassion & Identity

Work that centers how you treat yourself and how you relate to identity-related stressors, including shame, cultural experiences, and belonging.

Which path fits you?

I offer a few ways to work together depending on goals:

  • Short-term Focus — 6–12 sessions to learn tools and tackle a specific problem.
  • Supportive Ongoing Therapy — weekly or biweekly sessions for deeper, steady work.
  • The Resilience Intensive — a single extended session for targeted work and planning. (Ask about availability.)
Pricing and sliding-scale options are discussed at intake; I provide superbills for insurance reimbursement.
