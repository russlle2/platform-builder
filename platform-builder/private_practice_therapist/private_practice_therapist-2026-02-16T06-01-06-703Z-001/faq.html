Questions about starting therapy

Below are practical answers I offer to common questions. If you don’t see what you need, reach out — I’m happy to clarify.

Who I work with

Adults navigating anxiety, relational shifts, trauma recovery, and life transitions. I use trauma-informed and attachment-aware approaches.

Therapeutic stance

Practical skills + relational reflection. We set realistic goals and check in together on progress.

How do I know if we’re a good fit?

I offer a brief 15-minute consult call. We’ll talk about your goals, logistics, and whether my approach feels like a good match. There’s no obligation to continue.

What if I need support between sessions?

I maintain boundaries around direct messaging outside sessions. If you need extra support, we’ll discuss safe options during the session and in crisis follow the emergency resources listed below.

Do you work with insurance?

I do not bill insurance directly. I can provide a superbill with necessary codes for out-of-network reimbursement. Insurance policies vary; reimbursement is not guaranteed.

How long before I see change?

Therapy timelines are individual. Some clients notice relief after a few sessions; others find deeper shifts over months. We’ll set short-term goals and review progress regularly.

Are sessions confidential?

Yes. I keep session content confidential within legal and ethical limits. Mandated reporting, court orders, or imminent safety concerns are exceptions. We’ll review confidentiality in your first session.

Do you offer telehealth?

I offer secure video sessions for clients in eligible regions. In-person availability may be limited; we’ll discuss your preference during the consult.
