FAQ — About contacting and beginning therapy

How quickly will I hear back?
I aim to respond to contact requests within 48 business hours. If you do not hear back, please try the phone number above.
Do you work with couples or families?
I primarily offer individual therapy; limited couples work may be available by arrangement. We can discuss fit during the brief intake conversation.
Notes on scope, privacy, and safety:
  • Confidentiality: Communications with me are confidential except where required by law (e.g., imminent risk of harm to self or others, or suspected abuse of a minor/elder). Please avoid sharing highly sensitive details via unsecured email or public devices.
  • Not an emergency service: If you are in immediate danger, call emergency services or your local crisis line. If you are in the U.S., the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline is available at 988.
  • Limits of care: Therapy involves exploring difficult emotions and memories; progress varies and cannot be guaranteed. We will collaborate on goals and review treatment plans regularly.
