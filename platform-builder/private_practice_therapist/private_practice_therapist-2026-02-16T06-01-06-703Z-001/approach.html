Programs & offerings

The Focused Series

A 6-8 session structured plan for a specific goal (e.g., reduce panic attacks, manage work stress). Homework and skill coaching included.

Format: Weekly 50-min sessions
Renewal Intensive

A single extended session (120–180 minutes) to jump-start a plan, stabilize symptoms, or map out concrete next steps. Best for people who need focused momentum.

Format: One extended appointment
Open Support

Ongoing weekly or biweekly therapy for deeper work on patterns, relationships, or identity development. Emphasis on long-term coping and meaning-making.

Consultation & Coaching

Short blocks for practical skill-building around sleep, boundaries, or workplace communication. Not a replacement for specialty treatment.

A pragmatic weekly rhythm

  1. Intake: We explore goals, history, safety, and scheduling preferences.
  2. Plan: We co-create a short plan with markers for progress and check-ins.
  3. Practice: Sessions include skill teaching, reflection, and real-world practice tasks.
  4. Review: Every 6–8 sessions we review progress and adjust the plan.

Common process questions

How quickly will I feel better?
Progress varies. Some notice relief in a few sessions with focused tools; deeper shifts often take months. We set short-term goals to measure change together.
Can I switch between in-person and telehealth?
Yes—where licensure allows, we will discuss logistics and preferences so care remains consistent.
