Questions people often have

Below are practical answers to common questions about sessions, membership, privacy, and next steps. If you still have a question, feel free to get in touch.

  • How do I know if this is a good fit?
    Fit often becomes clear after an initial session. I aim to provide a straightforward, professional space where we can explore what matters to you. If after a session it seems like another clinician would be a better match, I will say so and, when appropriate, help with referrals.
  • Do you take insurance?
    I work primarily out-of-network. I can provide a superbill you may submit to your insurer. Coverage varies widely — check benefits with your plan for out-of-network mental health reimbursement details.
  • What should I expect in the first session?
    The first session is a chance to share what brings you in, review confidentiality and limits, and collaboratively set goals. If you are considering ongoing work, we’ll talk about structure and a tentative plan.
  • How private are our conversations?
    Conversations are confidential within legal and ethical boundaries (for example, imminent safety concerns, court orders, or mandated reporting). We’ll review limits to confidentiality at intake and you can always ask questions about record-keeping and privacy.
  • What if I’m in crisis?
    I’m not able to provide emergency response through email or routine sessions. If you are in immediate danger, call local emergency services. You may also contact your local crisis line or the national hotline at 988 if you are in the United States.
