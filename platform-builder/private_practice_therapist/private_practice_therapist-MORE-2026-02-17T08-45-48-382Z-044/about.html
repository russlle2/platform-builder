A brief note from your clinician

I work with adults who are looking to build steadier ways of managing stress, difficult relationships, transitions, and recurring patterns that keep life feeling smaller than it could be. My practice is informed by evidence-based approaches and shaped around practical goals you set together with me.

I hold training in cognitive-behavioral and relational methods, with ongoing consultation and a focus on measurable, sustained change. My work is collaborative: I bring structure, and you bring the expert knowledge of your life.

Sessions are offered through a membership model that supports continuous work over time rather than one-off fixes.

I provide outpatient psychotherapy and not emergency services. If you are in immediate danger or are experiencing a life-threatening emergency, call local emergency services or go to the nearest emergency department.

How the membership is framed

Rather than hourly sessions only, the membership is a steady framework: an agreed-upon rhythm of sessions, brief check-ins, and a modest reserve of focused work time each month for planning and specific interventions. This helps keep momentum between sessions without pressure to ‘fix’ everything immediately.

If you'd like a quick sense of what a few months of work might look like, I invite you to book an introductory conversation. We’ll outline goals and a simple plan you can take away, no pressure.
