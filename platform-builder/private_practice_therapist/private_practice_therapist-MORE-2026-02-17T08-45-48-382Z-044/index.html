Common questions

        
How does membership billing work?
          
Membership is billed monthly. Each plan outlines frequency and included items. Additional services are described in the Investment page.

        

        
Is our work confidential?
          
Yes. Information shared in sessions is confidential within legal and ethical limits. Exceptions include imminent risk of harm to self or others, or when required by law — we discuss these boundaries during intake.

        

        
What if I need help right away?
          
This practice is not an emergency service. If you are in crisis or at immediate risk, contact local emergency services or a crisis line in your area. See contact page for local resources.
