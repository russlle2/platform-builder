Transparent rates and membership-style care

My practice centers on continuity and clarity. Below are the common pathways clients use to build steady momentum or focus on a particular concern. If cost is a barrier, please reach out — I try to offer a small number of sliding-fee slots each month.

Foundation Session

$165 — single visit

A 50–55 minute intake or topic-focused session. Useful for assessing fit or addressing an immediate concern.

Focused Series

$480 — 3-session bundle

A short, time-limited set of sessions designed to work through a specific challenge with clear goals and checkpoint conversations.

Continuity Membership

$290 / month

A monthly plan intended for ongoing therapeutic work. Includes 1 session per week or 4 sessions per month (standard length); flexible scheduling and priority booking. Memberships are month-to-month; details are discussed at intake.

Pricing reflects standard session lengths and administrative time. Additional service types (longer sessions, comprehensive paperwork, letters) are billed separately and discussed in advance. No refunds or guarantees; therapy is a collaborative process.
