The work, in plain terms

I view therapy like a workbench: we set a small number of measurable projects, try interventions that look feasible for your week-to-week life, and check what helps. The process is collaborative and paced to what you can realistically maintain.

What to expect in the first month

  • One intake to map immediate concerns and practical barriers.
  • Two-to-three focused sessions to try a strategy and refine it.
  • Short, agreed-upon steps between sessions (experiments, scheduling, brief exercises).
This is outpatient psychotherapy and not an emergency service. If you are in immediate danger, please contact local emergency services. Confidentiality is maintained within legal and ethical limits; I will review these limits with you.
