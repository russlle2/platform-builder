Understanding Postpartum Physical Stress in Private Practice Therapy

Your body has undergone significant changes, and it's natural to experience new or amplified physical discomforts. From persistent back pain and pelvic floor tension to overall bodily fatigue, these sensations are often connected to the profound shifts of childbirth and early motherhood. As your Private Practice Therapist, I focus on decoding these signals and developing precise, gentle somatic strategies to alleviate your specific pain points, promoting a more comfortable recovery.
