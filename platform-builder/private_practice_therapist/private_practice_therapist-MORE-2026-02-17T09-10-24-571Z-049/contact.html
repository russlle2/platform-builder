Guided moment

We'll breathe together. Follow the circle and the prompts. Sit comfortably. If at any moment you need to stop, simply close this window.

Ready

Set a gentle timer for five minutes and write without editing. If you prefer, speak out loud and notice the tone of your voice. When the prompt changes, keep writing.

  1. What is most present for you right now?
  2. What would you like to be different, even slightly?
  3. One small next step you could take.

Take two minutes to name an intention. Breathe three slow breaths, then repeat the intention silently. Keep it short and actionable.

Short practices are supportive and not a substitute for therapy when deeper work is needed.
