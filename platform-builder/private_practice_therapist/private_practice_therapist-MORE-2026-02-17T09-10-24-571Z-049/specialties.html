Focus Areas and short programs

I work with adults seeking practical ways to navigate transitions, relationships, and recurring stress. Below are common areas people bring. If something fits, we can design a short, guided engagement to suit your timeframe.

Transitions & change

Moving through job changes, relocations, or life milestones with attention to values and coping strategies that stick.

Anxiety and overwhelm

Skills for steadying attention, interrupting the worry loop, and creating manageable routines.

Relationship patterns

Exploring communication, boundaries, and recurrent dynamics that feel stuck or painful.

Tailored short engagements

We can build a focused series — typically 3–6 sessions — centered on a specific goal: making a decision, stabilizing routines, or preparing for a change. These are structured, collaborative, and practical.

How to choose

If you want time-limited work, tell me when you book. We’ll co-design the scope and review progress so you leave with clear next steps and resources.

Questions people often ask

How long is each session?

Most sessions are 45–55 minutes. Some focused check-ins can be shorter by arrangement.

Will this be ongoing therapy?

You choose. Some people prefer a short series; others continue with periodic check-ins. We discuss what fits your goals.
