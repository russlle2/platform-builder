Values alignment

Choices are weighed against what you want your days to feel like, not just what feels urgent now.

Session rhythm

Sessions are collaborative. Typical engagements are flexible: a single focused consultation, a short series for a specific goal, or ongoing meetings by agreement.

  1. Intake — identify priorities and timeline.
  2. Work — practice strategies and reflect on what helps.
  3. Review — evaluate progress and plan aftercare or follow-up.
