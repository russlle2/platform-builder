Questions I often hear

Below are practical answers that explain how I work, what to expect, and how to get started.

How do I know if we should start with a single session or an intensive?
If you need focused, short-term help with a decision or an immediate problem, a single 90-minute Focus Session or a Mini Tune can be enough to plan practical next steps. If you want to make measurable change quickly, a Bridge Intensive (a short series) can provide structure and accountability. We can decide together after an initial conversation.
Is therapy confidential?
Yes. I follow confidentiality standards consistent with clinical ethics and law. Some situations—like imminent risk of harm, suspected abuse of a dependent, or court orders—require breaking confidentiality; I will discuss limits with you during intake.
Do you work with insurance?
I do not bill insurance directly. I provide receipts (superbills) that you may submit to your insurer. I’m happy to help you understand this process if you plan to seek reimbursement.
What if I’m in crisis?
I am not a crisis service. If you are experiencing active suicidal thoughts, are in danger, or believe someone else is in immediate danger, please call your local emergency number or your local crisis line right now. If you are in {{STATE}}, you can also use your local resources for immediate help.
