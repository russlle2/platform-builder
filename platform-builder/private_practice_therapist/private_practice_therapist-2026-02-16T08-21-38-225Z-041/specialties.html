Primary areas of support

Below are common concerns I work with — each entry includes the kinds of steps we typically explore together.

Anxiety & panic

Psychoeducation, grounding and breathing skills, graded exposure to situations, and cognitive restructuring when helpful.

Trauma-related symptoms

Stabilization, titrated memory processing, and resources to manage physiological activation. Emphasis on safety and pacing.

Relationship challenges

Communication experiments, pattern recognition, and setting boundaries. Work may include individual sessions focused on relational skills.

Life transitions & grief

Meaning-focused conversations, problem solving, and values clarification to help orient next steps after loss or change.

Burnout & workplace strain

Assessing workload, pacing, compassionate self-direction, and small behavioral shifts to protect capacity.

Identity & developmental work

Exploring sense of self, belonging, and the transitions that accompany identity shifts across adulthood.
