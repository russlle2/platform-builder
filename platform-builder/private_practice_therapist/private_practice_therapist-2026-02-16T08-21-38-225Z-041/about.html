Core principles that guide my work

Safety first

Therapy is paced to match what feels tolerable. We prioritize regulation and predictability before deeper exploration.

Skillful clarity

Practical tools are taught with context — not as one-size-fits-all fixes, but as experiments you can tailor to your life.

Agency & partnership

You remain the author of change. My role is to reflect patterns, offer approaches, and co-design steps that feel meaningful.

Common misconceptions — and a different view

Myth: Therapy will tell me what to do.
Truth: Effective therapy helps you discover choices and practice them safely. I aim to expand options rather than prescribe a single path.
Myth: Progress should be steady.
Truth: Healing is often non-linear — that’s normal. We track signals and adjust pace so gains sustain over time.

Questions people often ask

What does a first session look like?
We spend time understanding your concerns, immediate stressors, and goals. I review confidentiality and typical logistics. We'll leave with a small experimental step.
How long will therapy take?
This varies. Some people benefit from short-term focused work; others prefer ongoing continuity. We'll discuss a plan that fits your needs and schedule.
Are sessions confidential?
Yes — with standard limits (risk of harm to self/others, court orders, mandated reporting). See the privacy note in the footer for scope and boundaries.
