Common beliefs and a clearer view

Myth

Therapy is only for severe problems.

Truth

Therapy is a space to develop skills and clarity for many life moments — from early stress to major transitions. It's proactive as well as restorative.

Myth

Everything will be fixed in a single session.

Truth

Meaningful change takes time and practice. I emphasize achievable steps and consistent support, and we track progress together.

How I structure care

Safety & Trust

Confidential, nonjudgmental space. We work at a pace that respects your boundaries and history.

Practical Skills

Evidence-informed tools you can use between sessions — emotion regulation, communication, and pacing strategies.

Collaborative Planning

We set concrete goals together and review what helps you move toward them.

Client examples (anonymized)

Managing recurring anxiety

Recovering from relationship rupture
