How I work — an overview of method and collaboration

My approach is integrative, pragmatic, and paced to what each person can tolerate. I combine cognitive-behavioral techniques, somatic awareness, and relational learning. Most importantly, we co-design the pace and goals.

Three practical pathways I offer

1) Foundation Track

Short-term, skills-forward work (6–10 sessions) to build regulation, communication tools, and clear behavioral experiments.

2) Stabilize & Process

For people who want pacing and intermittent processing of distressing material. Emphasizes safety, grounding, and consented memory work when ready.

3) Continuity Membership

Ongoing weekly/biweekly sessions for those who value relational continuity, deeper exploration, or complex difficulties. Includes priority scheduling and brief check-ins between sessions as appropriate.

How we choose a pathway

We discuss immediate needs, time availability, and preferences. Treatment is revisited regularly so plans stay responsive to change.

Tools & influences

I draw from evidence-informed practices: CBT, acceptance-based strategies, EMDR-informed thinking (where appropriate and consensual), somatic regulation, and relational frameworks. Interventions are chosen based on their fit for you rather than ideology.

What to expect in session

  1. We begin with what matters most to you today and your current coping capacity.
  2. We identify one or two experiments or skills to practice between sessions.
  3. We reflect on results and adjust — therapy is iterative and collaborative.

Questions about method

Will you use EMDR or memory processing?
If memory-processing approaches are clinically appropriate and you consent, we discuss the rationale and alternatives. Any such work is paced and preceded by stabilization.
Do you give homework?
Often. Assignments are collaborative, brief, and designed to provide information rather than to be performance tasks. We tailor them to your life and capacity.
