Finding Your Ground: Private Practice Therapy for Postural Alignment

Your physical performance is deeply intertwined with your body's structural integrity. We explore how sustained activity and past injuries can subtly shift your posture, creating imbalances that contribute to discomfort and limit your potential. Through gentle guidance, you'll learn to recognize these patterns and cultivate a more aligned, efficient way of being in your body, both on and off the field.
