Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Private Practice Therapist help me with physical pain after chronic illness or injury?
As your Private Practice Therapist, we don't just talk about pain; we work with your body's sensations directly. We use somatic techniques to help you understand where pain is held, release chronic tension, and teach you practices to calm your nervous system, which can significantly reduce physical discomfort and promote deeper healing.
Is this approach suitable if I'm still feeling very weak or fragile from my illness/injury?
Absolutely. Our Private Practice Therapist tailors every session to your current physical and emotional capacity. We emphasize gentle, slow movements and awareness practices that respect your body's boundaries, ensuring a safe and supportive environment for even the most delicate stages of recovery.
What makes somatic healing different from other therapies I've tried for pain?
Unlike therapies that might focus solely on thoughts or physical rehabilitation, our Private Practice Therapist integrates the mind-body connection. We help you explore the emotional and energetic components of your pain, recognizing that true healing often requires addressing the whole self, not just the physical symptoms in isolation.
Will I have to do difficult exercises or movements in these Private Practice Therapist sessions?
No, our focus is on gentle engagement and mindful awareness. Your Private Practice Therapist will guide you through subtle movements and internal practices designed to improve your body's self-regulation and reduce pain, without ever forcing or pushing past your comfort zone. It's about listening to your body, not overriding it.
