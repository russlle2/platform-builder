Cultivating Connection: Relationship Healing in Your College Years

From friendships and family ties to romantic partnerships, your relationships are a cornerstone of your college experience. When they falter, it can add significant stress. Our approach as a Private Practice Therapist focuses on heart-centered care, helping you understand relational dynamics, communicate effectively, and set healthy boundaries. We blend insights from both Eastern and Western perspectives to foster deeper connection and navigate conflict with greater ease, supporting all your vital relationships.
