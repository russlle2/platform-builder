How Posture Impacts Your College Anxiety & Focus

Ever notice how hunching over your laptop for hours leaves you feeling not just tired, but also more stressed? As a Private Practice Therapist, I guide you to understand the direct link between your physical posture and your mental state. We'll explore how specific body patterns contribute to feelings of anxiety and fatigue, and how small adjustments can significantly improve your mental clarity and emotional regulation during demanding academic periods.
