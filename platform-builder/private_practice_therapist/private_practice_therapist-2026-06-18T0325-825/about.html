Why is a Private Practice Therapist focusing on my posture for college anxiety?

Your body and mind are deeply connected. When you're stressed, your body often reflects that with tension or poor posture. As a Private Practice Therapist, I help you learn to use your physical self—your posture and body awareness—as a powerful tool to interrupt anxiety cycles and build mental resilience, which is incredibly useful for academic demands.

Will this approach feel like a science class or a gym session?

Neither, really! While my methods as a Private Practice Therapist are rooted in science, the sessions are conversational and focused on your personal experience. We'll explore practical, gentle techniques to help you notice and adjust your body's responses to stress, without needing to perform any 'exercises' in the traditional sense. It's about mindful awareness, not a workout.

How quickly can I expect to see improvements in my academic stress or anxiety?

Many college students report feeling a greater sense of calm and self-awareness after just a few sessions with a Private Practice Therapist. The timeline for significant change varies for everyone, but by consistently applying the body awareness techniques we explore, you can expect to develop more effective coping mechanisms and a clearer mind for your studies.

Is this a good fit if I'm already seeing other mental health professionals?

Absolutely. My work as a Private Practice Therapist often complements existing mental health support. We can coordinate with your other providers to ensure a cohesive and comprehensive approach to your well-being. The focus on postural health and body awareness offers a unique, tangible layer of support that integrates well with various therapeutic modalities.
