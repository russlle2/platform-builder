Therapeutic Frame

My work blends short-term, skills-focused coaching with trauma-informed therapy. Each plan is tailored: we typically begin with three standing aims and measure progress against them.

  • Assessment & stabilization (first 2–4 sessions)
  • Skills and relational experiments (next 6–10 sessions)
  • Integration and maintenance (transition planning)

Common Concerns and Honest Answers

"Will this feel like coaching or therapy?"
I adapt to your needs. Some sessions are action-oriented; others are exploratory. We decide together.
"What if I can't afford regular sessions?"
I offer a limited sliding scale and occasional low-cost openings. We talk frankly about sustainability.
"What if I'm in crisis?"
If you are in crisis, call local emergency services or a crisis hotline. Therapy is not a crisis response service.
