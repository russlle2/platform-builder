How I hold space for you

Therapy should feel safe, purposeful, and practical. These guiding values shape every session.

  • Clear goals. We identify 1–3 priorities and check progress collaboratively.
  • Warm directness. Honest feedback offered with compassion.
  • Practical tools. Skills you can use between sessions.

Approaches I draw from

I blend modalities to meet where you are. Common techniques include:

  • Cognitive-behavioral interventions for managing unhelpful thoughts and patterns.
  • Mindfulness and somatic practices to reduce stress and ground the nervous system.
  • Relational work to explore patterns in partnerships and family systems.
  • Strength-based planning to support realistic, steady change.

Common hesitations — and what I hear back

  • "I don’t have time." Even brief, regular sessions can shift momentum. We’ll plan with your schedule.
  • "I’m not sure therapy will help." You can treat the first several sessions as an assessment: if it’s not a good fit, we’ll discuss options.
  • "I’m worried about privacy." Confidentiality is central; exceptions are limited by law and safety concerns, which I will explain clearly.
