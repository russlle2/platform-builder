Therapeutic design

How I structure work so time is well spent

I combine assessment, skills practice, and relational experiments in a predictable rhythm so progress is visible. Below are common program frames — we always tailor to your priorities and pace.

Program frames (examples)

Momentum Program
8 sessions across 12 weeks. Focus: acute stress, decision clarity, and immediate coping strategies.
Single program pricing — see Fees
Stabilize Track
4–6 sessions to build regulation skills and safety planning before moving to longer work.
Flexible scheduling; limited sliding scale
Integration Sessions
Monthly follow-up sessions after active work to maintain gains and adapt plans.
Subscription-style scheduling available
Brief Consultation
A single 50-minute consult to orient and plan next steps.
Pay per session

How I measure progress

We set 2–3 objective goals and review them every 6 sessions. Progress is defined by function: sleep, relationships, work performance, and distress reduction — not by a promise of cure.

Session logistics
50-minute sessions. Telehealth available. Insurance not billed directly; I provide superbills on request.

Addressing doubts

"I tried therapy before — it didn’t help."
Different therapists use different frames. We'll clarify goals and agree on a measurable plan. If we don’t see progress, I’ll discuss referral options.
"Will sessions feel invasive?"
No. Pace, consent, and safety are central. You control what is shared and when.
"How long until I feel better?"
Timeline varies. We check milestones regularly and adjust the plan so work remains useful and efficient.
