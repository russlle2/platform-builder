Core practice principles

Respectful, evidence-informed care

I integrate empirically supported approaches while tailoring them to your context and pace. I won’t pathologize — I listen and collaborate.

Privacy & clear boundaries

Sessions are confidential. I maintain clear scope and limits; I will discuss confidentiality, record-keeping, and consent at intake.

Practical focus

We balance insight with practical strategies you can use between sessions, with an eye toward functional goals that matter to you.

How I work — methods I use

I draw from a range of approaches and choose the fit based on your goals and needs.

  • Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) — strategy-focused skill building for anxiety and mood.
  • Trauma-Informed Care — stabilizing, grounding, and paced trauma processing when appropriate.
  • Emotion-Focused Work — helping you locate and work with patterns in relationships and self-regulation.
  • Relational Therapy — attention to interpersonal patterns and communication in partnerships and families.

Common concerns I hear

"I’m not sure therapy will work for me."

That’s understandable. We start by clarifying what you want to change and choose practical, measurable steps. Therapy is collaborative: we try things, check how they land, and adjust.

"I don’t have time for weekly sessions."

We can discuss alternatives: short-term focused work, biweekly sessions, or a mix of sessions and structured between-session work tailored to your schedule.

"I’m worried about confidentiality."

I prioritize client privacy. I will explain limits of confidentiality, data handling, and your rights before we begin so you can decide with clarity.
