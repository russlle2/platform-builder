How does Private Practice Therapy address burnout specifically for working professionals aged 30-45?

Our Private Practice Therapy acknowledges the unique pressures you face. We focus on how burnout manifests physically—like persistent neck pain from screen time or general fatigue—and offer gentle, practical body-awareness techniques to help you ease these specific tensions, making your demanding work life more physically sustainable.

I have no experience with 'body awareness.' Is this still for me?

Absolutely. You don't need any prior experience. Our Private Practice Therapy approach is designed to be gentle and accessible. We'll start with very simple exercises and observations to help you gradually notice what your body is telling you, building comfort and awareness at your own pace.

How can focusing on posture help with feeling burnt out?

Poor posture often exacerbates physical discomfort, which adds to the overall fatigue of burnout. By gently improving your postural habits, you can reduce chronic aches and tension, freeing up mental and physical energy. Our Private Practice Therapy helps you ease this physical burden, contributing to a greater sense of well-being even when facing demanding work schedules.

Will this require a lot of extra time in my already busy schedule?

We understand your time is valuable. Our Private Practice Therapy focuses on integrating small, impactful changes into your existing routines rather than adding new, time-consuming tasks. We'll work together to find simple, effective ways to improve your body awareness and posture that fit seamlessly into your life as a busy professional.
