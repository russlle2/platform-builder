How I work — core commitments

I blend practical coaching-style clarity with evidence-informed psychotherapy. My commitments are simple: presence, directness, and collaboration.

  • We set measurable, realistic goals together.
  • I provide tailored tools—skills you can use between sessions.
  • I protect your privacy and bring a nonjudgmental stance to each meeting.

Approach & Methods

Sessions integrate brief cognitive-behavioral techniques, somatic regulation, and narrative work when helpful. Modalities: {{MODALITIES}}.

Typical session structure: check-in (10m), focused skill work or processing (30m), plan & experiment (5–10m).

Common concerns and real answers

  • "I can’t afford regular therapy." I keep several sliding-scale openings and offer focused packages to concentrate work when resources are limited.
  • "What if I don’t click with you?" Feel free to try one session; if we’re not a fit, I’ll help you find someone better suited to your needs.
  • "How long will this take?" That depends on goals. Many clients see meaningful change within 6–12 sessions; others use therapy as ongoing support.
