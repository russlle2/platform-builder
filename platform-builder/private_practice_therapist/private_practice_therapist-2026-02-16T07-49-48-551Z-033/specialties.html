Where most clients find meaningful change

Brief, targeted interventions and longer psychotherapeutic work depending on goals. Below are the common areas I support.

Anxiety & worry

Evidence-informed strategies for panic, generalized anxiety, and performance nerves—combining cognitive restructuring, exposure, and practical routines.

Trauma & complex stress

Stabilization, paced processing, and resource-building. Work is collaborative and respects your pacing and safety needs.

Relationship strain

Focus on patterns, boundaries, communication practices, and repairing ruptures. I do individual and co-therapy referral work.

Life transitions & identity

Career shifts, parenting changes, and late-life transitions—support to name values, experiment with choices, and reduce overwhelm.

Burnout & workplace stress

Assessment of role demands, emotion regulation, boundaries, and sustainable routines that protect energy and focus.

Self‑esteem & decision making

Practical work to interrupt self-criticism, increase clarity, and strengthen values-based action.

How I approach specialties

I tailor interventions to you: short-term skills for immediate relief and deeper relational work when needed. We set collaborative goals and review progress regularly.

Programs
"Renewal Path" — a 12-week guided plan for burnout and transition. "Stabilize & Grow" — a brief 6-session skills package for acute anxiety.

Not a fit
