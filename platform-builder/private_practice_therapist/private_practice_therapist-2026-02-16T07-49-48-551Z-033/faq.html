Common questions, answered plainly

Below are practical clarifications I share with people before their first visit. If something you wonder about isn’t here, please reach out.

How quickly can I get in for a first session?

Availability varies; I maintain a short waitlist for new clients and reserve a few slots for urgent needs. Email {{EMAIL}} or call {{PHONE}} and include a few times that generally work for you.

Do you accept insurance?

I do not bill insurance directly. Many clients use out-of-network benefits and I can provide a superbill. I also maintain a limited number of reduced-fee slots per month.

What modalities do you use in sessions?

I draw from cognitive-behavioral approaches, somatic regulation practices, and strengths-based coaching. Modalities: {{MODALITIES}}. We tailor work to your needs rather than follow a single manualized treatment.

How do you handle confidentiality and notes?

Notes are kept securely per professional standards. Confidentiality is protected except where disclosure is required by law (risk of harm, abuse). We can discuss how notes are used if you have concerns.

Can I switch to telehealth or in-person?

Yes. I offer both telehealth and limited in-person sessions depending on location and scheduling. We'll discuss what suits your goals and safety preferences.

What if I need crisis support?

I do not provide emergency services. If you are in immediate danger, call local emergency services. In the U.S. call 988 for the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. For urgent needs between sessions, contact your local crisis resources.

How do we decide goals?

We co-create short-term and longer-term goals in early sessions. I prioritize concrete, measurable aims so you can see progress in repeatable ways.

What are your boundaries around communication?

I typically respond to messages during business hours (M–F). For non-urgent matters, allow up to 48 hours for a reply. If you anticipate frequent between-session contact, we’ll discuss expectations and limits in advance.

Need a short consult?

I offer a 20-minute inquiry call to see if we're a good fit. It's a low-pressure way to get your questions answered before committing to a session.

A note on scope and ethics

I practice within an outpatient, non-emergency setting. If your needs require higher-level care (e.g., hospitalization, forensic evaluation), I will help you find appropriate resources. Your privacy is prioritized in all communications.
