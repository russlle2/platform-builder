Common concerns — and what helps

Will therapy take forever?

Not necessarily. Many clients pursue focused, 8–12 session pathways to target a specific issue; others choose ongoing work. We'll set expectations together.

What if I’m not ready to share everything?

You set the pace. Confidentiality and safety are prioritized; we'll build trust and move at a tempo that feels manageable.

Is this coaching or therapy?

It’s clinical therapy informed by coaching-style goal-setting. I maintain clinical scope, diagnosis where appropriate, and clear boundaries about what therapy can offer.
