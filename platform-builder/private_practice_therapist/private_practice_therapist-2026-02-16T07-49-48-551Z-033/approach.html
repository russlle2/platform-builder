Common questions about how I work

How are goals set?

Goals are collaborative. We define what success looks like in practical terms and check progress every 4–6 sessions.

What if I need more intensive support?

If your needs exceed outpatient therapy—e.g., safety concerns or intensive residential care—I will help coordinate referrals and a safe transition plan.

How long is treatment?

It varies. Some clients use a brief 6-session package; others engage in ongoing weekly work. We revisit length and frequency together.
