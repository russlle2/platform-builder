Session boundaries, confidentiality, and limits

I aim to maintain a clear, respectful practice framework. Below are the most important points; we will review them together when we meet.

Confidentiality:
Your communications are confidential, with legal exceptions. Disclosure may be required for imminent risk of harm to self or others, suspected abuse of a minor or vulnerable person, or if a court orders release of records. I will discuss these limits as part of informed consent.
Boundaries:
Phone and email are for scheduling and brief administrative exchanges. Lengthy clinical material is best covered in session. I will clarify response expectations and availability in our first conversation.
Fees & cancellations:
Please see the Fees & Plans page for specifics. I strive for fair policies and will discuss exceptions when needed.
If you are in immediate danger, call emergency services right away. In the U.S., you can dial or text 988 to reach crisis support. If you are elsewhere, please contact local emergency services or a local crisis line. These resources are available 24/7 for urgent situations.
