What I work with

Common themes people bring: relationship transitions, burnout and overwhelm, decision crossroads, grief and identity shifts, and patterns that keep repeating.

Focused Intensives

1–3 extended sessions designed to map the issue and try immediate, practical shifts. Good when you need momentum in a limited time.

Short Series

4–8 weekly meetings with clear goals and small between-session experiments. Intended for problem-focused work with measurable steps.

Maintenance & Care

Less frequent, ongoing sessions for people who want professional reflection and steady support without weekly scheduling pressure.

Techniques I blend

  • Practical cognitive strategies for clearer choices
  • Behavioral experiments and gentle exposure to reduce avoidance
  • Emotion-focused noticing and somatic grounding
  • Interpersonal pattern work focused on communication and boundaries
