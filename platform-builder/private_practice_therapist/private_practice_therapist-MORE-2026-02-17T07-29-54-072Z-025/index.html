Myth vs. Practical Care

Myth

Therapy must be long-term to be useful.

Truth

Focused, time-limited work can produce clearer choices and new tools for navigating decisions. We plan length and intensity around your needs.

Myth

You need to know every outcome before you begin.

Truth

Initial sessions are for assessment and co-creating a plan; clarity grows from a few intentional encounters.

What guides my work

  • Clinical clarity

    Interventions informed by training and decades of practice, tailored to you rather than a single technique.

  • Respect for boundaries

    Sessions emphasize clear roles, predictable structure, and agreed-upon goals.

  • Practical tools

    Concrete strategies you can use between sessions to manage emotions and test new choices.

Session boundaries & confidentiality

Therapeutic conversations here are confidential within legal and ethical limits. We discuss how records are kept, who may access them, and how long information is retained. I do not share session notes unless required by law or with your explicit written consent.

Boundaries and scheduling

Sessions begin and end on time. We agree on cancellation policies and communication channels during intake. Emergencies are addressed with clear limits; therapy is not an emergency service.

Scope

I provide clinical assessment and psychotherapy for common mental health and life concerns, and will recommend or collaborate with other providers when needs fall outside my scope.

If you are in immediate danger or facing an emergency, contact local emergency services right away. If you are within the United States and need urgent mental health support, you may call or text 988 for the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline; international resources vary by region. If you are not in crisis but need timely assistance, reach out to your primary care provider or local urgent care.

Common questions

Do you offer remote sessions?
Yes. Telehealth and in-person options are available depending on current scheduling and public health guidance.
How do I know which format fits me?
We can discuss a focused intensive (short bundle) or regular sessions during an intake conversation. The Mood-to-Method tool on this page suggests an option to explore.
What if I need support outside sessions?
I provide clear boundaries for contact between sessions; urgent matters will be triaged and, if needed, I will help connect you to appropriate crisis resources or referrals.
