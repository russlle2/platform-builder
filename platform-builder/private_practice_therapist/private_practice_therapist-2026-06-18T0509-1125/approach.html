The Architecture of Rest: A Science-Meets-Wellness Approach in Private Practice Therapy

My approach as a Private Practice Therapist is rooted in understanding the intricate relationship between your changing life circumstances and your sleep architecture. We'll meticulously examine your unique challenges, integrating insights from neuroscience and cognitive behavioral therapy for insomnia (CBT-I) with contemplative practices. This tailored framework helps you construct sustainable patterns for restorative sleep, empowering you to face your major life transitions with renewed clarity and resilience.
