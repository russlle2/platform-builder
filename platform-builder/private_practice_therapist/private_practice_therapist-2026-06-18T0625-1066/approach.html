Finding Your Breath in the Storm: A Private Practice Therapist's Approach to Burnout

Your professional life, once a source of drive, now feels like a constant drain, leaving you depleted. This private practice therapy focuses on the subtle art of stress reduction and nervous system regulation. We'll explore gentle, accessible techniques to soften the grip of chronic stress, helping you move from a state of constant 'on' to one of quiet resilience. You don't need prior wellness experience; just a willingness to find a different way to be.
