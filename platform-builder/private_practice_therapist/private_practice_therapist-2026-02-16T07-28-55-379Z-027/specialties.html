Common worries people raise

  • "I’m not the ‘therapy type’" — You don’t have to be. We begin with small, practical steps and measure their usefulness.
  • "It’ll take forever" — Some concerns respond to focused short-term plans; others benefit from longer exploration. We co-design the length.
  • "I can’t afford it" — I offer a short list of sliding-scale options and a resource pack to bridge gaps.
