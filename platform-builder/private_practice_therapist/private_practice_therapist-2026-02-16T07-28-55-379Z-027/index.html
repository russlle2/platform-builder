Core principles that guide our work

Practical clarity

We set clear, measurable goals and check progress each session—therapy that produces usable change, not just insight.

Collaborative rigor

You are the expert on your life; I bring tools, structure, and accountability so you can act on what matters.

Safety & privacy

Sessions are confidential. I follow legal and ethical standards. For emergencies, call local crisis services—I do not provide crisis response.

Approach & evidence-informed methods

I blend several modalities tailored to your needs. Each client gets an individualized plan drawn from:

  • Cognitive-Behavioral techniques — to shift patterns of thinking and behavior.
  • Trauma-informed work — stabilizing resources before exploring distressing memories.
  • Emotion-focused strategies — building tolerance and regulation for intense feelings.
  • Executive-coaching tools — habit design, values-aligned planning, and accountability structures.

Modality selection is collaborative. I’ll explain options and we’ll choose what fits your goals.

Common concerns — answered directly

"I’m not sure therapy will help me."

That’s a reasonable worry. We begin with a focused assessment and short-term objectives so you can evaluate progress before committing long-term.

"What if I can’t afford ongoing sessions?"

I offer time-limited packages and sliding-scale options when available. We’ll discuss priorities and design a plan that fits your situation.

"I’m worried about privacy."

Confidentiality is central. I explain how records, telehealth, and communication are protected. There are legal limits to confidentiality which I will review with you.

"What if I need crisis support?"

I do not provide emergency services. If you are in immediate danger or a life-threatening crisis, call emergency services or your local crisis line.
