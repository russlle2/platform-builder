Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Private Practice Therapist specifically help with my sleep when my child dictates the schedule?
As your Private Practice Therapist, I recognize that a parent's sleep is intricately linked to their child's. We won't try to force an unrealistic schedule. Instead, we'll focus on optimizing the sleep you do get, improving sleep efficiency, and strategically managing your energy during the day. We'll also address any anxieties or perfectionism around your child's sleep that might be impacting your own.
Is this just about sleep hygiene, or is there more to your Private Practice Therapist approach?
While foundational sleep hygiene is part of our work, my approach as a Private Practice Therapist goes much deeper. We explore the psychological and emotional components of parental sleep deprivation, including anxiety, guilt, and the unique stressors of household management. We use evidence-based therapeutic techniques to address these underlying factors, making your sleep improvements sustainable.
I'm already overwhelmed. Will adding therapy to my schedule just create more stress?
My goal as your Private Practice Therapist is to reduce your burden, not add to it. Our sessions are designed to be a supportive, structured space where you can offload stress and gain clarity. We will work at a pace that feels manageable for you, focusing on practical, incremental changes that lead to noticeable improvements without overwhelming your already full plate.
What kind of evidence-based strategies does a Private Practice Therapist use for sleep and stress?
As a Private Practice Therapist, I draw from scientifically validated methods such as Cognitive Behavioral Therapy for Insomnia (CBT-I), stress reduction techniques, and mindful parenting strategies. These approaches are not about quick fixes but about equipping you with concrete tools and understanding to improve your sleep patterns and manage daily stress effectively and sustainably.
