Can you really help with seasonal stress before it hits?

Absolutely. We look at your family's predictable cycles—like holiday demands or back-to-school transitions—and build simple, proactive habits *before* stress peaks. For example, planning a quiet Saturday morning in November to reset after the Halloween rush.

Do I need prior wellness experience?

Nope. We skip the jargon and focus on what you already do well: caring for your kids. We build from there using your existing routines—like tying breathing exercises to your daily coffee.

How does this differ from general therapy?

We don't fix 'problems.' We prepare for *your* family's specific seasonal patterns. If your kids get extra cranky after the first snow, we'll work on that *now* instead of waiting for the meltdown.

What if my schedule is chaotic?

We work *with* your reality. Sessions happen during school hours, over coffee, or with a quick call between errands. The goal isn't more time—it's making your existing time work harder for you.
