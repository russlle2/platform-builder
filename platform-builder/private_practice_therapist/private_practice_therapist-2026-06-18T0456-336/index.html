Frequently Asked Questions

How does Private Practice Therapy help with physical pain from chronic illness or injury?
Our Private Practice Therapy focuses on somatic techniques and pain education to help you understand and modify your body's pain responses. We'll work on identifying triggers, developing coping mechanisms, and gently retraining your nervous system to reduce chronic discomfort and enhance your physical capabilities.
Is this therapy only about mental health, or does it address physical symptoms directly?
While mental well-being is part of recovery, our Private Practice Therapy specifically addresses the physical manifestations of chronic illness and injury. We explore the mind-body connection, but our interventions are designed to directly impact your somatic symptoms, helping you find concrete relief from physical pain and tension.
What kind of techniques can I expect to learn to manage my pain?
In our Private Practice Therapy, you'll learn evidence-informed techniques such as somatic awareness exercises, targeted relaxation strategies, and gentle movement practices. We'll also explore methods for regulating your nervous system and reframing your relationship with pain, giving you practical tools to use daily.
How long does Private Practice Therapy typically take to see results for chronic pain or injury recovery?
The duration of Private Practice Therapy varies based on your unique situation and goals. Many clients report initial shifts in their pain experience and increased somatic awareness within the first few sessions. We'll regularly assess your progress and adjust your plan to ensure you're continuously moving towards your desired level of comfort and function.
