Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Private Practice Therapist help with my physical pain from burnout?
As your Private Practice Therapist, I'll help you recognize the connection between your emotional stress and physical symptoms. We'll use somatic techniques to gently release tension stored in your body, address pain patterns, and teach you practical tools to manage physical discomfort related to your demanding work life.
Is somatic healing suitable for someone with a very busy schedule?
Absolutely. Somatic healing with your Private Practice Therapist is designed to be integrated into your life. We focus on practical, gentle techniques that don't require extensive time commitments outside of our sessions, empowering you to find relief and build resilience even with a packed schedule.
I've tried other therapies; what makes this Private Practice Therapist approach different for burnout-related pain?
Unlike traditional talk therapy that might focus solely on thoughts and feelings, as your Private Practice Therapist, I specifically address the physical manifestations of your burnout. We'll work directly with your body's sensations to alleviate pain and tension, providing a more direct route to physical and emotional relief.
What should I expect during my first session with a Private Practice Therapist for physical pain?
In your first session with me, your Private Practice Therapist, we'll talk about your experiences with burnout and physical pain, and how they impact your daily life. We'll gently explore some initial body-centered awareness practices to begin understanding where your tension resides, all in a supportive and non-judgmental space.
