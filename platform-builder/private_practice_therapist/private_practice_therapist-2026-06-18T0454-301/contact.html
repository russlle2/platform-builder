Releasing the Physical Clench of Burnout with Your Private Practice Therapist

Your demanding career often means pushing through, even when your body screams for a break. That constant pressure can manifest as headaches, neck pain, tight shoulders, or a persistent knot in your stomach. At Private Practice Therapist Studio, we understand how burnout translates into physical discomfort. We'll work together to identify and release these stored tensions, helping your body find its way back to ease and comfort, so you can show up for your life with less physical burden.
