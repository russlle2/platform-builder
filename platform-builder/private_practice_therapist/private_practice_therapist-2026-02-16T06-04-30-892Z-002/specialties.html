Structured paths

Different needs call for different rhythms. Below are discreet offerings — we adapt length and focus during intake.

Bridge Program — 6 sessions
Fast-moving, skills-first plan for pressing problems or acute spikes in anxiety.
Stability Track — 10 sessions
A steady plan emphasizing pattern recognition, coping skills, and gradual exposure where helpful.
Relational Tune-Up — 8 sessions
Short-term couples work that centers communication practices and behavioral experiments between sessions.

Why these specialties?

My training spans cognitive and somatic-informed practices and I aim to tailor tools to the person. I do not offer emergency treatment or forensic assessments. If you have questions about scope or compatibility, I invite you to a brief consult.
