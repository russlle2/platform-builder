Three-part framework

1
Clarify

We identify immediate concerns, values, and small measurable goals. This makes sessions concrete and helps track progress.

2
Practice

Sessions include skill-building (breathing, cognitive reframes, communication scripts) and short experiments to try between meetings.

3
Reflect & Adjust

We review what worked, what didn’t, and adapt the plan. Therapy is iterative and personalized to your pace.

What a typical session looks like

50 minutes split between check-in, focused practice, and planning. Homework is collaborative and small — we only add tasks that feel useful and realistic.

Ethics, limits, and privacy

I follow professional ethics and maintain client confidentiality within legal limits. Therapy is not a replacement for medical care; I will collaborate with other providers when appropriate and refer when needs fall outside my scope.

If you are in crisis or immediate danger, please call emergency services or your local crisis line. Notes and records are kept securely and shared only when required by law.
