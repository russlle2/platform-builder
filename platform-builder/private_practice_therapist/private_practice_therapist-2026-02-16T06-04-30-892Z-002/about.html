Why I do this work

I trained in evidence-informed therapies and over the years I’ve learned that small, practical shifts often create meaningful change. I help people notice patterns that get in the way, practice skills that increase choice, and carry those skills into their daily lives. My style is warm and direct — we set collaborative goals and track progress with simple steps.

I work with adults and couples. I do not provide emergency services. If you are in crisis, please call your local emergency number or the crisis line in your area. Sessions are confidential within legal limits. For details, ask about my intake packet.
