Session logistics

Length
Standard work is 50 minutes. Longer intake sessions are 75–90 minutes.
Payment
Payment is collected at time of service by card or secure transfer. Packages require full payment at start.
Missed appointments
Late cancellations (<48 hours) or no‑shows may be charged the full session rate. If something comes up, please reach out early.

Boundaries, privacy, and scope

Confidentiality is central. Notes are kept securely. Exceptions to confidentiality include imminent risk of harm to self or others, child/elder abuse, or as required by law. I do not provide emergency services; if you are in crisis, call local emergency services or a crisis line immediately.
