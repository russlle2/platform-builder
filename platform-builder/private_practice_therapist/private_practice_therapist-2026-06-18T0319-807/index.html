Frequently Asked Questions

Why is postural health important for me as a new mother in my postpartum recovery?
Your body has undergone substantial changes during pregnancy and childbirth. Maintaining good postural health postpartum is crucial for preventing chronic pain, reducing strain from daily activities like feeding and carrying your baby, and supporting the healing of your core and pelvic floor. It helps you move with greater ease and comfort during this demanding period.
What specific aspects of body awareness will Private Practice Therapy help me with?
We'll focus on helping you become more attuned to how your body is positioned throughout the day, especially during tasks like nursing, lifting your baby, or simply sitting. This includes noticing tension patterns, understanding how your core muscles are engaging (or not), and recognizing signs of fatigue or strain, empowering you to adjust your movements proactively.
Is this Private Practice Therapy suitable if I'm experiencing pain, like back or neck pain, since giving birth?
Absolutely. Many new mothers experience back, neck, and shoulder pain due to new postures and repetitive movements. Our Private Practice Therapy provides a structured, supportive environment to explore the root causes of your discomfort, offering evidence-based strategies and exercises to alleviate pain and improve your body's functional mechanics.
How does Private Practice Therapy differ from physical therapy for postpartum recovery?
While both are beneficial, our Private Practice Therapy specifically emphasizes the mental and emotional connection to your physical self, focusing on cultivating internal body awareness and mindful movement patterns. We complement any physical therapy you might be receiving by integrating those physical insights with a compassionate approach to your overall postural well-being as a new mother.
