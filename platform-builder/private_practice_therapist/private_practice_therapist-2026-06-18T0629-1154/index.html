Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Private Practice Therapist specifically help with parental sleep challenges?
As a Private Practice Therapist, I provide a dedicated space to unpack the unique stressors impacting your rest as a parent. We'll explore personalized strategies, not just for improving sleep hygiene, but also for managing the underlying anxieties and demands that steal your peace, tailoring techniques to your family's specific rhythms.
Will therapy with a Private Practice Therapist add another burden to my already full schedule?
My aim as a Private Practice Therapist is to lighten your load, not add to it. Our sessions are designed to be a dedicated time for your restoration. We focus on practical, gentle adjustments that can be integrated into your existing life, offering support without overwhelming your already busy days as a parent.
My child's sleep is erratic. Can a Private Practice Therapist still help me find rest?
Absolutely. While I don't treat your child's sleep directly, as a Private Practice Therapist, I focus on your response to those erratic patterns. We will build your resilience, develop coping mechanisms for sleep interruptions, and cultivate a sense of calm that allows you to find restorative moments, even when continuous sleep feels elusive.
What if I feel too exhausted to even begin therapy with a Private Practice Therapist?
It's precisely when you feel most depleted that reaching out can be most beneficial. As your Private Practice Therapist, I offer a compassionate, low-pressure environment. Our initial conversations will be gentle, focusing on what feels manageable for you right now, and how we can slowly reintroduce moments of peace into your life.
