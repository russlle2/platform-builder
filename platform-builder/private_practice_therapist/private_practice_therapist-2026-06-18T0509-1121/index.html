Frequently Asked Questions

How does Private Practice Therapy specifically address sleep for someone with a chronic illness or injury?
Our Private Practice Therapy acknowledges that your sleep challenges are distinct. We delve into the direct impact of your specific condition, medication, and pain levels on your sleep architecture, devising bespoke interventions that go beyond general advice to target your unique physiological and psychological landscape for true restorative rest.
Will I need to take sleep medication as part of your Private Practice Therapy approach?
Our Private Practice Therapy prioritizes non-pharmacological interventions. While we can discuss medication in the context of your overall care with your medical team, our focus is on empowering you with sustainable behavioral and cognitive strategies to improve your sleep naturally, reducing reliance on external aids.
How long does it typically take to see improvements in sleep through Private Practice Therapy?
The timeline for improvement in Private Practice Therapy is highly individualized, depending on the nature of your chronic condition and your personal commitment. Many clients report initial shifts within a few weeks, with more profound and lasting changes developing over several months as new patterns become ingrained and your body re-learns restorative sleep.
What if my pain or discomfort prevents me from falling or staying asleep, even with Private Practice Therapy?
Managing pain and discomfort is an integral part of our Private Practice Therapy for sleep. We incorporate specific techniques, such as targeted relaxation, mindful body scanning, and cognitive reframing, to help you navigate these challenges. We also collaborate with your medical providers to ensure a cohesive approach to pain and sleep management.
