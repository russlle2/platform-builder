Frequently Asked Questions

I'm so busy, how will I find time for Private Practice Therapist sessions?
We get it – your schedule is packed. Our sessions are designed to be efficient and impactful, fitting into your life without adding more stress. We focus on practical steps you can implement right away, even in small increments, to start seeing improvements in your rest. Your time is valuable, and we respect that.
I've tried everything to get more sleep. How is this Private Practice Therapist different?
Many approaches focus solely on sleep hygiene. While that's important, as your Private Practice Therapist, we delve deeper into the unique mental and emotional burdens of parenthood that often interfere with restorative sleep. We'll explore strategies that address the underlying stress, not just the symptoms, in a gentle and accessible way.
Do I need to have prior experience with therapy or wellness to benefit from your Private Practice Therapist services?
Absolutely not. Our approach is designed to be welcoming and easy to understand for everyone, especially parents new to wellness support. There's no jargon, no complicated exercises – just straightforward, compassionate guidance to help you get the rest you deserve.
My child's sleep is the problem. Can a Private Practice Therapist help with that too?
While our primary focus is on your sleep, we understand how intertwined your child's sleep is with your own. As your Private Practice Therapist, we can discuss how to best manage your reactions and responses to your child's sleep patterns, helping you create a calmer environment for both of you and reduce the impact on your rest.
