Myth versus reality

Therapy is often surrounded by myths. Below I separate common assumptions from what is generally true, informed by clinical practice and research.

Myth: Therapy will give immediate answers.
Truth: Therapy is a process of gradual insight and skill-building. Some relief can be fast, but durable change often requires repeated practice.
Myth: A therapist will tell you what to do.
Truth: I help you explore options and test strategies; you retain agency over decisions.
