Common misconceptions about therapy

Myth: Therapy is only for crisis

Truth: Therapy helps both in crises and in quieter moments of growth. I help people prepare for transitions and develop tools before stress escalates.

Myth: You’ll be told what to do

Truth: My role is to guide and co-investigate, supporting your autonomy and practical decision-making.

Myth: Progress should be linear

Truth: Change is iterative. We track small markers and adapt approaches as needed.
