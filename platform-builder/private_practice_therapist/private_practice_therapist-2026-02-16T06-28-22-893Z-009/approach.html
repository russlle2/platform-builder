Frameworks and structure I use in sessions

I blend relational attunement with structured skill-building. Below I outline the typical stages of work and the membership-style offerings I currently maintain.

1. Intake and orientation

We assess immediate needs, risk, and priorities. We co-design an evaluation plan to track progress.

2. Stabilization & skill rehearsal

Practical tools are taught and practiced in-session with real-world assignments to test effectiveness.

3. Targeted interventions

We apply focused methods (cognitive, behavioral, somatic) chosen for your goals and revised as data emerges.

4. Consolidation & transition

We plan maintenance strategies and, if appropriate, a stepped-down schedule for continuing support.
