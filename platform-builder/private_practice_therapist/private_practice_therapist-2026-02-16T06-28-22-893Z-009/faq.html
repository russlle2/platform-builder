Frequently asked questions

Practical answers to help you decide if we’re a good fit. If you don’t see your question here, please reach out.

How do I know if therapy is right now?
People seek therapy at different moments — when coping feels harder than usual, patterns repeat, or a life change feels destabilizing. An initial session is a low-commitment way to explore suitability and priorities.
What approaches do you draw on?
I integrate trauma-informed relational work with pragmatic interventions from cognitive-behavioral and skills-based traditions. Modalities: {{MODALITIES}}. We tailor techniques to the client’s needs and pace.
Can I switch between telehealth and in-person?
Yes. Many clients use a hybrid model. Note: long-distance telehealth across state lines depends on licensing ({{LICENSE}}). We’ll review this when scheduling.
What if I’m in crisis?
If you are at immediate risk of harming yourself or others, call local emergency services or a crisis hotline. I am not available for emergencies via email or typical session channels.
How long does therapy typically take?
Duration varies. Some clients work through a single issue in a few sessions; others benefit from ongoing work over months. We set review points every 6–8 sessions to assess direction and progress.
Is what I share confidential?
Yes. Clinical notes and session content are confidential, except when disclosure is required by law (risk of harm, court order, child protection). We will discuss limits of confidentiality during intake.
