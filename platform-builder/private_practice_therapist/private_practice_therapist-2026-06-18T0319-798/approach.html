Cultivating Body Awareness: A Gentle Approach in Private Practice Therapy

It’s amazing what a difference a little awareness can make! Through our Private Practice Therapy, we'll guide you in noticing subtle sensations and patterns in your body. This isn't about strenuous exercises; it's about gentle observation and practical techniques you can easily integrate into your day. We'll help you develop a deeper connection to your body, leading to improved balance, reduced tension, and a greater sense of calm and control.
