  2. Regular meetings: Scheduled sessions tailored to your needs—week-to-week or blocks of focused work.

Common questions

Is this right if I need intensive daily support?

This membership is designed for outpatient psychotherapy and structured check-ins. It is not a substitute for intensive or inpatient services. We will discuss referrals if a different level of care is indicated.

How private is my work with you?

Sessions are confidential within legal and ethical limits. We will review confidentiality, limits, and documentation in your first meeting.

What happens if I need a cancellation or to pause?

Policies for cancellations and pausing membership are shared at intake and in the membership agreement to keep expectations clear and fair.

Reach out for an initial conversation to see if the membership structure fits your needs. We can clarify scope, scheduling, and how we’ll measure progress together.
