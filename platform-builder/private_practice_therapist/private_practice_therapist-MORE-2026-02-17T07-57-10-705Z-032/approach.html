Practical, collaborative care

I partner with you to develop clear steps and tools you can use between sessions. Therapy is a shared process: I bring clinical training and you bring lived experience.

{{PRIMARY_CTA_LABEL}}
1. Intake & priorities

We map current concerns, health context, and what you hope to shift. This sets the scope and frequency that make sense for you.

2. Collaborative plan

Together we identify achievable goals, short practices, and ways to notice progress even when change is gradual.

3. Review and adapt

Every few sessions we check in on how the plan is working and adapt—no rigid timelines, only shared decisions about next steps.

Therapeutic orientation

I draw from cognitive-behavioral strategies, acceptance-based work, and relational insights—selecting what fits a person’s situation. My approach is collaborative, curious, and oriented toward practical change rather than labels.

Membership and access

Membership offers predictable access and a clear way to plan support over time. Options include regular weekly sessions, a focused short plan, or a lighter check-in arrangement for ongoing members.

Pricing and details are discussed during an initial consultation.

Safety and limits

I keep confidentiality except where law or safety requires disclosure. Therapy by appointment is not emergency care—if you are in immediate danger, please call emergency services or a crisis line in your area.
