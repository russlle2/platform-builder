How membership works

A membership sets expectations: session frequency, billing cadence, and how we handle messages between sessions. Memberships include scheduling priority and administrative coordination so sessions can focus on clinical work.

  • We’ll start with a 30–50 minute orientation to identify goals and matches.
  • Each membership includes a set number of sessions per month; extra sessions can be added when available.
  • Messaging is available for brief check-ins; full clinical content is reserved for sessions.

Practical notes

Confidentiality is central. I follow professional ethical standards for record-keeping and privacy. There are legal limits to confidentiality (e.g., imminent risk, court orders). I do not provide crisis services; in emergencies, contact local emergency services or your crisis line.

Memberships are not insurance-based billing here; I provide receipts you may share with your insurer. We’ll discuss what fits your needs and boundaries of care during intake.
