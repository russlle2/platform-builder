About me

I am a licensed therapist working privately with adults navigating transitions, relationships, and persistent worry. My practice is informed by evidence-based techniques, clinical experience, and a collaborative stance—your priorities guide our work.

Work with me if you want steady support you can access on a membership basis, with clarity about goals and practical steps between sessions.

Membership model

Care Circle

Ongoing weekly or biweekly sessions, message access between visits, and periodic check-ins tailored to your pacing.

Focused Plan

A short series (6–10 sessions) for a concentrated focus: we clarify a goal, work practical steps, and set tools for after the plan ends.

Drop-in Support

For established members who prefer occasional sessions and brief check-ins when life shifts.

What to expect

  1. An initial intake to understand concerns, history, and current context.
  2. A collaborative plan outlining session frequency, areas of focus, and brief between-session strategies.
  3. Ongoing adjustments based on how you’re doing; steady, evidence-informed techniques rather than one-size-fits-all fixes.

Confidentiality, scope, and urgent care

All conversations are confidential within professional and legal limits. I will clarify scope at intake—therapy is not a replacement for emergency medical or crisis services. If you are in immediate danger or need urgent medical help, please call local emergency services or the appropriate crisis line. For non-urgent concerns about the limits of care, I will discuss referrals or coordination with other providers as needed.
