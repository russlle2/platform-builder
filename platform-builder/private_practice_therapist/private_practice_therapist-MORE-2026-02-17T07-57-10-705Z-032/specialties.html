How this is framed

Instead of fixed packages with rigid promises, I offer membership access with clearly described options. We pick a rhythm that fits your needs—regular weekly sessions, a focused plan, or occasional check-ins. The goal is to align sessions with your real life and priorities.

Boundaries and safety

Conversations are confidential within ethical and legal limits. I will clarify what I can and cannot provide; when concerns fall outside my scope I will discuss referrals. If you are in crisis or need immediate help, please contact emergency services or a crisis line; therapy by appointment does not replace urgent care.
