Frequently Asked Questions

How does Private Practice Therapy help athletes with sleep issues specifically related to physical stress?
We focus on the unique demands of an active lifestyle. This means addressing things like post-workout overstimulation, anxiety about performance affecting sleep, and the physiological impact of intense training on your circadian rhythm. We develop tailored strategies that consider your training schedule and recovery needs.
I'm always sore and tired. Can Private Practice Therapy really improve my physical recovery through better sleep?
Absolutely. Poor sleep directly impairs muscle repair, hormone regulation, and reduces your body's ability to recover from exertion. By improving your sleep quality and duration, we help your body perform its essential restorative processes more effectively, leading to reduced soreness and improved energy levels.
What if my busy training schedule makes it impossible to get enough sleep? How can Private Practice Therapy address this?
We understand the constraints of a demanding schedule. Our approach in Private Practice Therapy isn't about finding more hours in the day, but optimizing the hours you do have. We'll explore strategies like improving sleep efficiency, strategic napping, and structuring your day to support better sleep hygiene, even with tight timelines.
Is this just about sleep hygiene tips, or does Private Practice Therapy offer deeper support?
While sleep hygiene is a foundational element, our Private Practice Therapy goes much deeper. We explore the mental and emotional factors contributing to sleep disturbance, such as performance anxiety, mental fatigue, or difficulty switching off. We help you build resilience and mental tools to calm your mind, alongside practical sleep strategies.
