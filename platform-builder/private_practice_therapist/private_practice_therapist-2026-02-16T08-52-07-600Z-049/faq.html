Questions I often get

I aim for transparency. Below are practical answers about sessions, privacy, and what to expect. If you don’t see your question, reach out and I’ll respond within 48 hours.

How long is a typical session and what happens at the first meeting?
Most sessions are 50 minutes. The first session is assessment-focused: I gather history, understand your goals, and outline a collaborative plan. We will also cover confidentiality and administrative items.
Do you offer telehealth or in-person meetings?
I offer secure telehealth and limited in-person availability (where permitted). We choose a format based on your needs and treatment goals.
How quickly can I expect to see change?
Change is individualized. Some people notice relief from symptom-focused work within weeks; deeper relational patterns often require several months. I use measurable goals and periodic review to assess progress.
What is your cancellation and no-show policy?
Please provide 48 hours' notice to avoid a late-cancellation fee (50% of the session rate). Exceptions are considered in emergencies; communication is the key.
Can I use insurance?
I do not bill most plans directly. I provide a superbill you can submit to your insurer for potential reimbursement. I can discuss details during intake.
How do you handle confidentiality and records?
Clinical notes are stored securely and shared only with your written consent. Exceptions: imminent risk of harm to self/others, suspected abuse of a vulnerable person, or a valid court order. Your privacy is a priority; I will discuss limits during intake.
What should I do in a crisis?
I am not a crisis service. If you are in immediate danger, call emergency services or your local crisis line. If you are in the U.S., you can dial 988 for the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. For other urgent needs, use emergency resources first, then notify me when safe.
How do I know if therapy with you is the right fit?
Fit is both clinical and relational. We use an initial consult to clarify goals, approach, and rapport. If I am not the best match, I will offer referrals to other clinicians whose modality or availability may be a better fit.
