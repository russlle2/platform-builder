Common myths — and a clearer way forward

Myth: Therapy is only for crisis
Truth: Therapy can prevent escalation by building skills and insight earlier.
Myth: I should have answers before I start
Truth: Curiosity together produces practical next steps; you don’t need to be ready alone.
Myth: It will always feel worse before it gets better
Truth: Some changes feel gradual; we aim for manageable steps and notice incremental gains.
