Frequently Asked Questions

How is your seasonal focus different from other postpartum care?
We address specific seasonal impacts on postpartum recovery—like how cold weather affects your circulation or how spring allergens mimic postpartum fatigue. Other places offer generic advice; we help you navigate what's happening *right now* with your season and baby.
Do I need to have a wellness routine already?
No. We start where you are. If 'taking deep breaths' feels impossible with a newborn, we build tiny, practical steps that fit into your current routine—like syncing mindful moments with your baby's feeding schedule.
Can sessions happen while my baby naps?
Absolutely. We schedule around your baby's rhythm. Most new moms find 15-20 minute sessions fit easily into nap times or quiet moments between feedings. No need to arrange extra childcare.
How soon after birth can I start?
We welcome you as soon as your postpartum check-up confirms physical stability (typically 2-4 weeks). Your wellness plan begins where your body is, not where you think it 'should' be.
