Frequently Asked Questions

How does Private Practice Therapy specifically help athletes manage physical stress?
Our Private Practice Therapy focuses on identifying the root causes of physical stress, often related to postural imbalances or repetitive movements inherent in your sport. We help you develop greater body awareness, improve your biomechanics, and build mental resilience around your physical challenges, leading to more sustainable performance and reduced risk of injury.
I'm not injured, but I feel 'off' or stuck in my training. Can Private Practice Therapy help?
Absolutely. Many athletes experience plateaus or a general sense of being 'stuck' due to subtle postural issues or unconscious movement patterns. Our Private Practice Therapy provides a space to explore these areas, helping you fine-tune your body awareness and unlock new levels of performance and comfort, even without a specific injury.
What should I expect in a Private Practice Therapy session regarding postural health?
In a Private Practice Therapy session, we'll start by understanding your athletic background and current physical demands. We'll then collaboratively explore your movement patterns and postural habits. Through guided awareness exercises and targeted discussions, you'll gain practical insights into improving your alignment and reducing strain, both during activity and in daily life.
Will Private Practice Therapy interfere with my existing training or physical therapy?
Not at all. Our Private Practice Therapy complements your existing training, coaching, and any physical therapy you might be undergoing. We provide a distinct focus on the mental and emotional aspects of body awareness and postural health, helping you integrate physical strategies with a more conscious, empowered approach to your athletic well-being.
