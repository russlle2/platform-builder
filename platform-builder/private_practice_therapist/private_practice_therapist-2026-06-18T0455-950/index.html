Frequently Asked Questions

How is seasonal wellness different from traditional therapy?
It's about timing. We focus on the specific challenges of each academic season—like the transition after winter break or the energy dip in March. We help you prepare for these moments, not just react to them, using your semester calendar as a guide.
Do I need to be in crisis to start?
Absolutely not. In fact, that's when it's most helpful. Think of us as your mental health 'oil change' before the engine overheats. We help you prevent burnout, not just fix it, during the busy parts of your academic year.
How long are sessions, and how often do we meet?
We start with 50-minute sessions once a week. As your resilience grows, we might shift to every other week. The key is to find a rhythm that feels manageable for your busy schedule—no extra pressure to 'do more'.
Can you work with my school's counseling center?
Yes, we can collaborate if you're comfortable. We'll always get your permission first and only share what you want us to share. We see this as layering support, not replacing existing resources.
