Money questions I often hear

I try to be transparent: if cost is a barrier, please ask about limited sliding scale spots, group workshops, or a focused short-term plan designed to fit your budget.

  • Insurance: I do not bill insurance directly. I can provide a superbill for out-of-network reimbursement.
  • Packages: Prepaid packages are non-refundable and meant to ensure continuity; unused sessions can be discussed.
  • Reduced-fee options: Limited based on availability and clinical fit.
