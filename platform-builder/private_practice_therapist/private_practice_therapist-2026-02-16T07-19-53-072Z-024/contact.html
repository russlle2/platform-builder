Common concerns I hear

It's normal to have questions before starting. Here are a few I often address in early conversations:

  • Will this change feel like therapy forever? Most clients want focused change; I tailor pacing so you can meet short‑term goals and reassess regularly.
  • What if I'm not sure what to say? That's common. Sessions begin with orientation—what matters most to you—then we proceed together.
  • Is this confidential? Yes. Notes are kept in a secure system. Exceptions: imminent risk of harm to self/others, child or elder abuse, or court order.

Privacy, scope & boundaries
