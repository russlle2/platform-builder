How We Collaborate — The Map & Milestones

A clear, flexible structure helps therapy feel purposeful. Below is how I typically organize early work and ongoing care.

Typical First 8 Sessions

1. Intake & Priorities
Clarify concerns, set short-term goals, and identify immediate supports.
2. Stabilization
Teach grounding and coping strategies to ensure safety and reduce overwhelm.
3. Skill Integration
Practice cognitive and behavioral tools in context of your life.
4. Review & Plan
Assess progress and decide whether to continue, shift focus, or taper support.

What I Promise in Sessions

  • Clear session agendas and agreed goals.
  • Respectful challenge balanced with compassionate listening.
  • Confidentiality and transparent limits of care.

Worries people bring

"What if it doesn't help?" We track progress intentionally. If you and I don't see change, I will discuss adjustments or referrals.
