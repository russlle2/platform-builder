Questions I hear often

Short answers to common questions so you can decide if reaching out feels like the right next step.

Do you work with couples or individuals?
I primarily offer individual therapy but occasionally accept couples for targeted relational work when it aligns with my training and schedule.
How many sessions will I need?
It depends on goals. Some clients find relief in short-term, focused work (6–12 sessions); others continue weekly for longer to address deeper patterns. We'll co-create a plan and review regularly.
Can I use insurance?
I do not bill insurance directly. I provide a superbill you can submit to your provider for possible out-of-network reimbursement. Please check with your insurer about coverage.
What kinds of issues do you treat?
I work with anxiety, trauma recovery, burnout, life transitions, and relational concerns. I use evidence-informed modalities tailored to your needs. I do not give legal or medical advice.
