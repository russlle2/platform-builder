Primary Areas of Care

I specialize in clear, ethically grounded therapy for adults navigating stress, relationships, and transitions. Below are common presentations I work with and the pragmatic focus each path offers.

What guides my work

Safety, evidence-informed practice, and a collaborative stance. I aim to make therapy accessible, predictable, and useful.

Anxiety Management — "Calm Skills"

Short-term focus on worry cycles, exposure planning, and physiological regulation.

Trauma Stabilization — "Foundations"

Safety, grounding, and stepwise processing; we move at a pace you can maintain.

Relationship Repair — "Relational Repair"

Communication patterns, boundaries, and attachment-informed interventions for couples or individuals.

Life Transitions & Career Shifts — "Transition Labs"

Practical exploration, values alignment exercises, and decision-making frameworks.

Burnout & Stress Recovery — "Reset Module"

Behavioral activation, pacing strategies, and restorative routines to rebuild energy.

Parenting & Family Strain

Psychoeducation and emotion coaching techniques for caregivers navigating difficult family dynamics.
