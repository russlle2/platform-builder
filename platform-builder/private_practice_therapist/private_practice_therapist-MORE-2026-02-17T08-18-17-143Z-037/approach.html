What to expect in sessions

A short description that sets a practical tone

Sessions are collaborative. I listen for what you value and help translate that into small experiments and clearer choices. We use clinical methods suited to your situation—cognitive, behavioral, relational tools—chosen for clarity and real-world usefulness.

Confidentiality & limits

A necessary note

Conversations are protected by confidentiality in line with professional ethics and law. There are limits to confidentiality when safety concerns arise; we will discuss those explicitly. If you are experiencing a crisis or are in immediate danger, contact emergency services or local crisis resources right away.

Scope and fit

Who benefits most

People seeking steady, practical change—those who want to convert insight into action and who are comfortable trying small shifts between sessions. If you have complex legal, medical, or urgent safety needs, we will make sure you have appropriate supports in place.
