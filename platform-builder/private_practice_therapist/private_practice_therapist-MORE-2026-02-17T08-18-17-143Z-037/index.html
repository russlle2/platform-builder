How I structure our work

We begin with a practical map: what’s happening now, what you most want to change, and a few experiments you can try between sessions. Sessions are collaborative and focused on what moves you forward.

  • Map — understand the context
  • Try — small experiments you can do at home
  • Adjust — refine what helps

About your clinician

I practice from a grounded, collaborative stance. I aim to offer clear clinical thinking and practical plans tailored to your life. I maintain confidentiality within professional limits. I do not offer emergency crisis services; if you are in immediate danger, please contact local emergency services or a crisis line right away.
