Frequently asked questions

Clear answers to common logistical and clinical questions.

How long is each session?

Standard sessions run approximately 50–55 minutes. Some focused consultations or check-ins may be shorter; we’ll agree on the best format together.

Do you accept insurance?

I do not bill insurance directly. I can provide a detailed receipt (superbill) you may submit to your provider if your plan offers out-of-network reimbursement.

What should I expect at the first appointment?

The first meeting focuses on understanding your priorities, clinical history, and immediate concerns. We’ll discuss confidentiality, goals, and collaboratively design a plan rather than delivering directives.

How do cancellations and rescheduling work?

Please give at least 48 hours’ notice when possible. Late cancellations and no-shows may be charged a portion of the session fee, which we can discuss at intake. This policy helps keep the schedule sustainable for all clients.

Is therapy confidential?

Yes—within the limits of professional ethics and the law. Exceptions include imminent risk of harm to self or others, suspected abuse, or legal demands. If you have concerns, we’ll review confidentiality in the first session.

What if I need help right now?

I do not provide crisis services. If you are in immediate danger or thinking of harming yourself, call local emergency services or your local crisis line. In the United States you can call 988 for the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. If you are elsewhere, please contact local emergency resources.

Can I message between sessions?

Brief, clinically relevant messages are acceptable for scheduling and quick check-ins when included in a plan but are not a substitute for sessions. Long clinical discussions should be scheduled so we can maintain boundaries and adequate clinical care.
