Yes — sessions are confidential and records are maintained in line with professional standards. Exceptions include imminent risk of harm to yourself or others, suspicion of abuse of a minor or vulnerable adult, or if required by law. We review confidentiality at the start of care so you understand its boundaries.
If you are in immediate danger or experiencing a medical emergency, call emergency services or go to the nearest emergency department. For urgent behavioral health needs, please contact your local crisis line. This practice does not provide emergency crisis services.

Scope & limits:
Therapy focuses on emotional, relational, and behavioral concerns. I do not provide legal, financial, or medical advice. If your needs extend beyond the scope of psychotherapy, I will support referrals to appropriate professionals.
