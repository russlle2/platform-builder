My path to practice

I trained in community and outpatient settings, and now bring that perspective to private practice work. My approach is informed by relational therapy, skills-based interventions, and a steady interest in how small changes in routine can ease stress. In sessions we name what feels hard, track what’s in your control, and test new moves — all while honoring the realities of your life.

I aim to make my work pragmatic and humane: clear agreements about confidentiality, boundaries, and how we’ll measure progress. If you’re in crisis or feeling unsafe, please contact local emergency services or the crisis resources in your area; therapy here is not a substitute for immediate crisis care.
