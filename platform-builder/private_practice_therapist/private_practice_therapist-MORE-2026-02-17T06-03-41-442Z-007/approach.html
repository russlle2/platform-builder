How I work with people

An overview of the methods I blend and what that looks like in a first few sessions.

Practical framing

We begin by defining the immediate problem and what success will look like in concrete terms. Therapy here is organized around testing new behaviors and checking what actually changes.

  • Clarify goals and timelines
  • Identify manageable steps for daily life
  • Adjust as data comes in

Relational stance

I bring an attentive, collaborative presence. We notice how patterns show up in relationships and use that insight to shift interactions in real time.

  • Simple experiments in communication
  • Reflective feedback in session
  • Skill-building for boundaries

Skillful tools

Techniques from cognitive and behavioral approaches are offered alongside mindfulness and inter-personal practices, selected to fit your context.

  • Short practices to try between visits
  • Realistic adjustments for busy lives
  • Plans that can be scaled up or down

A typical early path

  1. Short intake conversation (15–20min) to orient and clarify fit.
  2. Two to four weekly sessions to set an initial plan.
  3. Review after four sessions; continue or switch formats as needed.

Boundaries & confidentiality

Confidentiality is respected within the limits of the law. Exceptions include imminent risk of harm to self or others, suspected abuse, or court-ordered disclosures. We review these limits before work begins.

If you are experiencing an emergency, contact local emergency services or crisis supports. This service is not for urgent medical issues.
