Frequently Asked Questions

What kind of 'postural health' does a Private Practice Therapist focus on for new mothers?
As your Private Practice Therapist, I'll help you become more aware of how you're holding your body throughout the day – when you're nursing, carrying your baby, or simply resting. We'll gently address common postpartum postural shifts, like rounded shoulders or lower back strain, and find ways to bring more comfort and balance to your everyday movements.
Do I need any special 'wellness experience' to work with a Private Practice Therapist on body awareness?
Absolutely not! This Private Practice Therapist approach is designed to be gentle and accessible, even if you've never thought about your posture before. We start exactly where you are, focusing on simple, everyday movements and building awareness at your own pace. There's no prior experience necessary, just your willingness to explore.
How can a Private Practice Therapist help me with body awareness when I'm so busy with my baby?
Our Private Practice Therapist sessions are practical and integrate into your real life. We'll look at how you naturally move and suggest small, achievable adjustments you can make during your daily routines. The goal isn't to add more to your plate, but to help you feel more comfortable and aligned in the movements you're already doing with your baby.
Is this Private Practice Therapist service suitable if I had a C-section or a difficult birth?
Yes, this Private Practice Therapist support is very much suited for you. We'll always work within your comfort and healing boundaries. Postural awareness and gentle movement are incredibly beneficial for all new mothers, including those recovering from C-sections or challenging births, as they can aid in a more comfortable and mindful recovery. We'll proceed at a pace that feels right for your body.
