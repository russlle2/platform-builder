Frequently Asked Questions

How can Private Practice Therapy specifically help with postpartum sleep when my baby dictates the schedule?
Our Private Practice Therapy doesn't aim to control your baby's sleep, but rather to help you optimize your rest within your current reality. We'll focus on strategies like maximizing sleep opportunities, improving sleep quality, managing sleep anxiety, and developing coping mechanisms for fragmented sleep, all tailored to your unique postpartum experience.
I feel too tired to even think about therapy. How will I manage sessions?
We understand that your energy is precious. Our Private Practice Therapy sessions are designed to be a supportive, low-stress environment. We can explore flexible scheduling options and ensure our discussions are practical and directly address your immediate needs, making the most of your time and energy.
Is 'restorative rest' different from just 'sleeping more'?
Yes, it is! While getting more sleep is often a goal, restorative rest in Private Practice Therapy encompasses more than just hours in bed. It's about truly replenishing your mental and physical energy. We'll explore techniques to help you feel genuinely refreshed, even if your sleep is still interrupted, focusing on quality over just quantity.
Will Private Practice Therapy really make a difference with my constant exhaustion?
Absolutely. Your exhaustion is valid, and our Private Practice Therapy is designed to equip you with concrete tools and a supportive framework to address it. We'll work on understanding the root causes of your fatigue beyond just lack of sleep, helping you build resilience and reclaim your energy, step by empowering step.
