Frequently Asked Questions

How is seasonal wellness different from traditional therapy for burnout?
Instead of focusing on 'why you burned out,' we build your wellness around your actual calendar seasons. For example, we might create a 'late summer renewal' routine that fits into your existing work rhythms instead of asking you to overhaul your entire life.
Do you handle acute burnout crises?
We're designed for prevention, not crisis care. If you're already overwhelmed, we'll help you access immediate support resources while building your long-term seasonal wellness plan for when your energy levels stabilize.
How does this fit into my 30-45 professional life?
We meet you where you are: during your morning coffee, after your kid's soccer game, or during your lunch break. We build wellness into your actual work cycles, not as an additional task.
Why do you emphasize 'preventive' instead of 'healing'?
Because burnout prevention isn't about fixing something broken—it's about supporting your natural energetic cycles. Like planting seeds before the rain, we help you build sustainable habits when you're still in energy reserves, not when you're depleted.
