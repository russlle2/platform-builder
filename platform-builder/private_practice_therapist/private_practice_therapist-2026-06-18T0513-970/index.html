Frequently Asked Questions

Can I just talk about my midterm stress without it being 'therapy'?
Absolutely. We focus on your immediate academic pressure, not 'healing' or 'your past.' If you're stressed about a paper deadline, that's exactly what we'll work on.
What if I'm stressed but don't want to 'talk about feelings'?
You don't have to. We use practical tools – like breathing techniques to use during a panic attack in the library or how to reset your focus after a bad class. It's about what works *now*.
Does seasonal wellness mean I need to change my whole routine?
No. It means noticing how your schedule affects you. For example: I help you see that 'I get anxious during the first week of class' isn't a personal flaw – it's a pattern to manage *before* it hits. We work with *your* actual days.
Is this for students who already feel 'fine' but want to prevent stress?
Yes. Many students come in before the pressure builds – like booking a session the week before finals to build a stress toolkit. Preventive care keeps you from hitting 'crisis mode' during midterms.
