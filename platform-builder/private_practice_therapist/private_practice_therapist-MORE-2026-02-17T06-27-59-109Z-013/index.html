How we typically begin

  1. Initial intake conversation — a 20–30 minute call to discuss fit, confidentiality, and practical questions.
  2. Plan co-creation — together we outline goals, frequency, and what counts as progress for you.
  3. Regular clinical sessions — held in a predictable frame with notes and agreed boundaries.
  4. Review moments — periodic check-ins to assess goals and adapt plans.

Confidentiality is central; limits include risk of harm to self/others, court-ordered disclosure, and mandated reporting. This practice does not provide emergency/crisis care.
