How I work — a clear, collaborative process

Clinical structure that emphasizes clarity: we name a limited set of aims, choose change experiments, and check progress at regular intervals.

1. Orientation

Brief intake to identify priorities, expectations, and whether my approach fits your needs. We clarify confidentiality and scope.

2. Goal design

We craft specific, behaviorally-focused aims with measurable indicators of progress.

3. Practice & review

Sessions include skill instruction, in-session practice, and brief, concrete tasks to use between sessions.

Typical sequence for short work

  1. One or two orientation sessions to clarify goals and fit.
  2. Three to six focused sessions with explicit practice and measurement.
  3. End review with maintenance plan or transition to ongoing care if helpful.

Clinical limits & crisis guidance

Therapy focuses on the concerns we agree upon; if care needs fall outside my scope, I will discuss referrals. This practice does not provide emergency or crisis services. If you are in immediate danger, call emergency services or your local crisis line.
