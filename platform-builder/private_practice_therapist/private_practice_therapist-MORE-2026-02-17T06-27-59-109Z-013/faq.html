Questions people often ask

Below are practical answers about how I work, what to expect, and how to book. If you don't see what you need, reach out directly.

What happens at the first session?
We clarify your concerns, review confidentiality and scope, and identify a short-term plan. We also talk about scheduling and whether a membership or package might suit your goals.
Do you take insurance?
I do not bill insurance directly here. I can provide a receipt you may submit to your carrier for possible reimbursement depending on your plan.
How long are sessions and where do they happen?
Standard sessions are 50 minutes. We meet via secure video or in person where offered; we confirm logistical details when you book.
What is the difference between membership and packages?
A membership provides regular weekly or biweekly support with flexibility for brief check-ins. Packages are a block of sessions focused on defined goals over a set period.
What if I’m in crisis?
This practice does not provide emergency services. If you are in immediate danger or having thoughts of harming yourself, call your local emergency number or a crisis line right away.
