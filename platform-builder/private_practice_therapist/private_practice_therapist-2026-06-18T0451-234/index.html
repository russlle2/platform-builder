Frequently Asked Questions

How does Private Practice Therapy specifically help athletes and active individuals manage physical stress?
Our Private Practice Therapy directly addresses the unique emotional challenges tied to physical stress, such as injury recovery, performance anxiety, and burnout. We help you process the psychological impact, develop coping strategies, and build emotional resilience, so you can maintain your mental edge and physical wellbeing.
What does 'trauma-informed care' mean in the context of my athletic experience?
For athletes, trauma-informed care acknowledges that intense physical experiences, like significant injuries or high-pressure situations, can create emotional distress. We understand that your body and mind might hold onto these experiences, affecting your performance or recovery. Our Private Practice Therapy provides a safe, supportive space to process these impacts without re-traumatizing you, focusing on healing and resilience.
I'm not injured, but I feel constant mental fatigue from training. Can Private Practice Therapy help?
Absolutely. Constant training and demanding physical routines can lead to mental and emotional exhaustion, even without a specific injury. Our Private Practice Therapy helps you identify the sources of this fatigue, develop strategies for mental recovery, regulate your nervous system, and improve your relationship with your training, preventing burnout and supporting sustained activity.
Will Private Practice Therapy interfere with my training or recovery schedule?
Our Private Practice Therapy is designed to complement your training and recovery. We understand the demands on your time and energy. Sessions are scheduled with your needs in mind, and the insights gained can actually enhance your recovery process and improve your focus, making your efforts more efficient and sustainable in the long run.
