Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Private Practice Therapist help with my physical pain as a parent?
As a parent, your body often bears the brunt of constant demands. We use somatic practices to help you recognize and release the physical tension that accumulates from daily stressors, like chronic back pain from carrying children or persistent neck stiffness from interrupted sleep. Our focus is on retraining your nervous system to find a more relaxed state, which directly reduces physical discomfort.
Is somatic healing really different from other types of pain relief for parents?
Yes, it’s quite distinct. While other methods might offer temporary relief, somatic healing with a Private Practice Therapist delves into the root causes of physical pain by addressing the stored stress and emotional patterns in your body. We don't just treat symptoms; we teach you how to attune to your body's wisdom, allowing you to sustainably reduce pain and prevent its return, especially relevant for the ongoing stresses of parenting.
I'm always busy with my kids; how can I fit Private Practice Therapist sessions into my schedule?
We understand that your time as a parent is incredibly precious. Our Private Practice Therapist sessions are designed to be efficient and impactful. We can discuss flexible scheduling options and even incorporate simple, body-centered practices you can do in short bursts throughout your day. The goal is to provide tools that integrate seamlessly into your life, not add another burden.
Will I have to talk a lot about my parenting struggles during a Private Practice Therapist session?
While we acknowledge that parenting is a significant source of stress, our focus is primarily on your physical experience and bodily sensations. You don't need to delve into every detail of your parenting challenges unless you feel it's directly impacting your physical state. Our aim is to help your body unwind and find ease, creating a calmer internal environment that can indirectly support you in managing external stressors.
