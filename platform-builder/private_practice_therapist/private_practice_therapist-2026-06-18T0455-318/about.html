How does Private Practice Therapy specifically address age-related physical pain?

Our Private Practice Therapy focuses on understanding the specific patterns of tension and discomfort that can arise with age. We use gentle, body-centered techniques to help your system release chronic holding patterns, often reducing pain and increasing your mobility, which supports your active retirement lifestyle.

Can somatic healing really improve my mental clarity and focus?

Yes, absolutely. Physical discomfort and stress often create a 'fog' that can impact your mental clarity. Through somatic healing in our Private Practice Therapy, we help you process and release physical tension. This often results in a clearer mind, improved concentration, and a greater sense of mental ease, allowing you to fully enjoy your retirement.

What should I expect during my first Private Practice Therapy session?

Your first session in Private Practice Therapy will involve a calm, conversational intake. We'll discuss your specific physical discomforts, how they impact your daily life, and your goals for maintaining vitality. We'll then introduce you to gentle somatic exercises, ensuring you feel comfortable and understood, laying the groundwork for your personalized plan.

Is this type of Private Practice Therapy suitable if I have limited mobility?

Yes, our Private Practice Therapy is designed to be highly adaptable. We work within your current physical capabilities, utilizing gentle movements and seated or reclined positions as needed. The focus is on subtle, internal awareness and release, making it effective and safe regardless of your current mobility levels.
