Map of our work

We move from understanding to practice in three steady phases — presence, insight, and living differently.

  1. Presence: Establish safety, boundaries, confidentiality, and a practical rhythm.
  2. Insight: Track patterns, language, relationships, and unprocessed material.
  3. Integration: Apply new habits to everyday life and reduce reactivity.

Sessions are confidential. If you are in crisis or at risk, please call emergency services. Therapy does not guarantee specific outcomes.

Daily practices you can try

Small, consistent acts expand capacity more than occasional heroics.

  • Two-breath check-in: Pause for two full breaths before answering your phone.
  • Boundary note: Choose one task to stop each day to protect attention.
  • Curiosity pause: When triggered, ask "What does my body need?" once before reacting.
