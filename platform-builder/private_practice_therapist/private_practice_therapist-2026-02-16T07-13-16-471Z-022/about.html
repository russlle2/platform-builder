How I frame evaluation — a steady diagnostic conversation

Initial sessions prioritize safety, history, and the patterns you most want to change. I look at symptoms in context: relationships, stress, sleep, substance use, and bodily responses. Assessment is descriptive — we avoid labels that shrink the person — and it informs clear, pragmatic next steps.

Plan & pacing

Therapy is paced to your tolerance. We may begin with stabilization (skill-building around grounding, sleep, and emotion regulation), then move into deeper processing as you feel safer. Frequency is negotiated — weekly, biweekly, or a tailored rhythm.

  • Short-term goals (4–8 sessions): symptom relief and crisis management.
  • Mid-term work (3–9 months): relational patterns and trauma processing.
  • Maintenance & relapse prevention: building everyday practices.

Everyday practices I offer — small habits that compound

These micro-habits are taught and practiced during sessions, then adapted to your life.

  • 2-minute grounding (breath + orientation) to interrupt overwhelm.
  • Evening check-in: one question to track shifts in mood and safety.
  • Micro-boundaries: experiments to strengthen relational clarity.
