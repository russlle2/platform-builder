Micro-practices you can start this week

Small, evidence-informed practices that support therapy between sessions. Gentle, consistent habits often have the biggest cumulative effect.

  1. 3-minute grounding each morning: notice breath, feet on floor, one intention.
  2. Pause and name an emotion once daily — "I notice anger/hope" — without judgment.
  3. Two brief self-checks on weekdays: energy and relational needs.
