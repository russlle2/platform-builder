Specialty areas

Where I typically work with people

I combine relational, somatic, and cognitive tools. Below are focused descriptions and the kinds of micro-practices I introduce early on.

{{PRIMARY_CTA_LABEL}}

Anxiety & chronic worry

We map triggers, cycle responses, and build micro-interventions that break escalation. Sessions include psychoeducation and short practices to use between meetings.

Early micro-habit

A 2-minute anchoring ritual to shift attention from threat to present-sensing.

Complex stress & burnout

For professionals and caregivers. Work focuses on pacing, boundary experiments, and restoring regulation. We scaffold return-to-work plans if needed.

Early micro-habit

A brief daily check-in to log energy and set a single restorative intention.

Attachment wounds & relationships

We explore relational patterns, communication experiments, and how safety is rebuilt in relationships. Sessions may include role-play and somatic tracking.

Early micro-habit

A clarity script to practice a boundary or request, used in low-stakes conversations first.

Trauma & post-traumatic responses

Care is paced and consent-driven. We begin with stabilization and resources, and only proceed to processing when safety and orienting capacities are strong.

Early micro-habit

A grounding anchor paired with a small movement to re-embed safety in the body.

Diagnostic approach

I orient to functioning rather than labels: how symptoms organize your life and relationships. Assessment is collaborative, with clarity about goals and expected pacing.

  • Initial intake focuses on safety and immediate needs.
  • We track measurable change and revise the plan every 6–8 sessions.

Safety & boundaries

Confidentiality is respected with standard legal exceptions (harm to self/others, abuse reporting, or court order). I am not an emergency service—call emergency services for imminent danger. For urgent mental health crises, use local crisis resources.
