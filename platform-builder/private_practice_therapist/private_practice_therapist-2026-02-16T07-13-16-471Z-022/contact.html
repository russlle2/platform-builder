Privacy, scope & safety

All communications are treated confidentially within legal and ethical limits. Confidentiality has limits (e.g., imminent risk of harm, child/elder abuse). Sharing information via online forms or email has inherent privacy risks; for highly sensitive details, we arrange secure intake procedures.

Scope, boundaries, and practical notes

I provide psychotherapy as a licensed clinician. I do not provide emergency crisis services, legal advice, or medical treatment. If you are currently in crisis or feel you may harm yourself or others, call your local emergency number or go to your nearest emergency room. For immediate mental health support in the U.S., you can call or text 988. If you are outside the U.S., please contact local emergency services.

Before beginning therapy I will confirm licensing jurisdiction ({{STATE}}) and relevant consent forms. If therapy is not an appropriate fit, I will offer referrals to community or higher level care.
