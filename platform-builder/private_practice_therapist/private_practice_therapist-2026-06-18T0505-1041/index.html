Frequently Asked Questions

How does private practice therapy specifically address chronic illness recovery?
Our private practice therapy specifically targets the physiological and psychological imprints left by chronic illness or injury. We focus on techniques that help your nervous system downregulate from a sustained state of stress, promoting a deeper sense of safety and calm, which is crucial for overall recovery and well-being.
What makes your approach to stress reduction unique for someone in recovery?
Our approach is unique because it acknowledges the distinct demands chronic recovery places on your internal resources. We don't just teach general stress techniques; we tailor interventions to gently re-pattern your nervous system's response to the unique challenges of your physical state, moving beyond mere coping to genuine regulation.
Will this private practice therapy help with the fatigue often associated with chronic conditions?
While private practice therapy does not directly treat physical fatigue, by significantly reducing chronic stress and regulating your nervous system, you may find an improvement in your energy reserves. A calmer nervous system expends less energy on vigilance, potentially freeing up vital resources your body needs for recovery.
What kind of commitment is required for this specialized private practice therapy?
Your commitment is primarily to yourself and your process. We recommend consistent, regular sessions initially to build momentum in nervous system regulation. The frequency and duration will be discussed and tailored to your individual needs and capacity during our initial consultations, ensuring it supports your recovery, not adds to your burden.
