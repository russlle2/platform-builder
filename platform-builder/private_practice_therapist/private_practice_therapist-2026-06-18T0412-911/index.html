Frequently Asked Questions

How do you help prevent chronic illness flares during seasonal changes?
We help you identify your unique seasonal triggers and create a simple, adaptable routine that prevents flares. For example, in autumn, we might focus on grounding practices to counteract energy dips. Your plan evolves with the seasons, so you're always supported.
Is this approach different from general wellness advice?
Yes. While general advice might suggest 'drink more water', we focus on how seasonal shifts impact your chronic condition and provide specific, ancient-informed practices. Your prevention plan is tailored to your body's response to the changing seasons, not one-size-fits-all.
Do I need to change my existing treatments?
No. We collaborate with your current healthcare team to complement your existing care. Our seasonal prevention practices work alongside your medication and therapies, helping you prevent flares without disrupting your routine.
How quickly can I expect to see results?
You'll start noticing smoother transitions through seasonal shifts within a few weeks as you integrate our practices. The key is consistency: small, regular adjustments prevent the larger flares that can disrupt your life. We focus on sustainable changes that build long-term resilience.
