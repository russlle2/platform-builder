How I structure sessions

Sessions are collaborative and goal-oriented. We typically follow a cycle:

  1. Brief check-in and measurement of current symptoms or challenges.
  2. Skill practice or intervention tailored to the immediate need.
  3. Review and planning: homework, experiments, or behavioral tasks.

I use a mixture of CBT, acceptance-based strategies, and somatic grounding depending on the presenting issue.

Practice principles

Three guiding principles shape my clinical work:

  • Transparency: We clarify goals and measures up front.
  • Collaboration: You remain the expert on your life; I provide structure and tools.
  • Gentle accountability: Small, achievable tasks anchored to values.

Barriers people worry about

Below are common barriers and typical responses I offer during intake.

"I’ve tried therapy before and it didn’t help."
We review what didn’t work and design a clearer, time‑limited plan that targets measurable change.
"I don’t want to relive trauma."
I prioritize stabilization and resource-building; trauma processing only happens with explicit consent and readiness.

Programs & intake

I offer several focused options depending on need:

  • Momentum Sessions — short-term weekly work (6 sessions) for specific targets.
  • Transition Path — 8-week plan for life changes and decision-making support.
  • Focused Intake — a single, structured consultation to map next steps.

Pricing is framed around packages to encourage measurable progress. Ask during intake for current fees and sliding-scale options.
