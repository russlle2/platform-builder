What people commonly worry about before starting

Below are common concerns and straightforward answers to help you decide.

Will this take forever?

Therapy is goal-oriented. Some goals are achieved in a few sessions; others take longer. We agree on metrics and revisit them regularly.

Is therapy confidential?

Yes—except where law requires disclosure (harm to self/others, abuse of a minor). I will always discuss limits of confidentiality before we begin.

I’m not sure if I’m “ill enough” for therapy.

Therapy is for growth and support, not only crisis. We will clarify whether short-term consultation or longer work fits your needs.

Do you take insurance?

Insurance policies vary. I can provide superbills for out-of-network reimbursement where appropriate; please ask during intake.
