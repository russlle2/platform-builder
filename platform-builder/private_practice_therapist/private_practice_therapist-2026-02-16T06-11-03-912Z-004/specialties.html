Areas of focus

Specialized, evidence-informed care for adults looking to reduce distress and build sustained coping capacity.

Common concerns I help with

  • Anxiety & worry: Panic, generalized worry, anticipatory stress.
  • Trauma reactions: Post-traumatic stress symptoms, intrusive memories, hypervigilance (trauma-informed, stabilization first).
  • Relationship strain: Communication patterns, attachment-related hurt, conflict navigation.
  • Life transitions: Career changes, relocation, parenting shifts.
  • Burnout & overwhelm: Work-related stress and chronic exhaustion.

Modalities & format

I blend approaches to match each person's needs and pace. Typical modalities include:

  • Cognitive-behavioral skills to shift unhelpful thinking and behavior.
  • Mindfulness and breath-based regulation for anxiety reduction.
  • Somatic grounding and resources for trauma-sensitive stabilization.

Sessions are private-pay; options include weekly therapy or short intensive packages (6–8 sessions).

Hesitations I commonly hear

Concerns are understandable. Here are practical responses.

  • Will this take too long? We set short-term goals and review progress every 4–6 sessions.
  • What if therapy feels intense? I prioritize stabilization and pacing; consent and safety are ongoing.
