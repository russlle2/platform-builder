Frequently Asked

How long is a typical session?

Most sessions are 50 minutes. Occasionally we arrange extended sessions for a careful review or planning session; we will schedule and confirm any extended appointment ahead of time.

Do you accept insurance?

I am a private-pay provider. I provide superbills that you may submit to your carrier for out-of-network reimbursement when applicable.

What should I expect at the first meeting?

We’ll review confidentiality, your goals, brief history, and agree on next steps. If immediate safety concerns emerge, I will address them and make appropriate referrals.

Can I pause or change my plan?

Yes. We review progress regularly and adjust the plan together. If a referral or higher level of care is needed, I will discuss that openly.

How do cancellations work?

Please provide 48 hours' notice when possible. Late cancellations or missed sessions may be billed at a reduced rate unless waived for emergencies.
