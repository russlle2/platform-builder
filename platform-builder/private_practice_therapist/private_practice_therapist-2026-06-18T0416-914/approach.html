Why Your Stress Peaks When Seasons Change

School starts. Leaves fall. Your toddler spills cereal at 7 a.m. while you're already fraying. This isn't 'just life'—it's your body reacting to seasonal shifts you didn't know to prepare for. We map how weather, routines, and communal rhythms (not just 'stress') impact your nervous system. Then we build your personal preventive toolkit for the next season.
