Frequently Asked Questions

How is your seasonal approach different from general therapy?
We don't treat 'anxiety.' We help you spot how summer heat or fall transitions spike your stress *before* it tanks your patience. You'll learn to adjust your family's routines using seasonal markers—like noticing when your coffee cup feels 'too hot' to hold (a physical sign your nervous system is overwhelmed).
What if I can't find 'time' for care?
We build your 'preventive care' around your existing moments. We'll find the 90 seconds when you're waiting for your kid to get their shoes on and turn it into a grounding practice. No extra hours needed—just reframing what you already do.
Do you use ancient traditions the way the wellness industry does?
No. We apply practical, non-mystical elements of traditional healing to your kitchen chaos. For example: using simple 'warmth rituals' (like a hot cup of tea at transition times) to signal safety to your nervous system, not 'spiritual energy.'
How quickly will I notice less stress?
You'll spot one specific stress point easing within 2 weeks. Not 'I'm calmer,' but 'I didn't snap when the milk spilled.' We track tangible shifts in your daily household moments, not vague 'progress.'
