Frequently Asked Questions

How can Private Practice Therapy specifically help with the stress of parenting young children?
Private Practice Therapy offers you a dedicated space to process the unique demands of raising young children. We focus on practical skills for stress reduction and nervous system regulation, helping you respond to daily challenges like meltdowns and sleep disruptions with more calm. You'll learn strategies to prevent burnout and foster a more peaceful family environment.
My schedule is already chaotic with young kids; how can I fit Private Practice Therapy in?
We understand that your time is incredibly valuable. We offer flexible scheduling options, including virtual sessions, to make it as convenient as possible for you to prioritize your well-being. Even a dedicated hour for yourself can make a significant difference in your capacity to manage everything else. You deserve this time to refuel.
Will Private Practice Therapy involve my children or my partner?
While the primary focus of your Private Practice Therapy will be on supporting your individual stress reduction and nervous system regulation, the positive changes you make will naturally benefit your entire family. We can discuss strategies for communicating with your partner about your needs, and how to implement new approaches within your family dynamic, without directly involving the children in your sessions unless specifically discussed and agreed upon for a particular goal.
What if I feel like my stress is 'normal' for a parent and I shouldn't need help?
It's common for parents of young children to normalize high levels of stress. However, 'normal' doesn't always mean 'healthy' or 'sustainable.' If you're feeling overwhelmed, constantly on edge, or finding it hard to enjoy parenting, that's a valid reason to seek support. Private Practice Therapy provides tools to manage these feelings effectively, so you can feel more present and resilient, not just 'getting through' each day.
