Common myths and what research shows

Myth: Therapy is just talking about the past.

Truth: While history matters, we focus on present patterns and practical strategies you can use now. We build skills and test changes between sessions.

Myth: You should wait until things are worse.

Truth: Early support often reduces suffering and prevents longer-term difficulties. Many people find short- to medium-term work helpful for transitions and stress.

Myth: One approach fits all.

Truth: I draw from evidence-based modalities ({{MODALITIES}}) and tailor what I use to your goals and values.

How I structure clinical work

Assessment & goals

We begin with a clear assessment of strengths, stressors, and goals so every session moves toward measurable progress.

Skills & experiments

Clinical tools are taught and practiced between sessions; we emphasize real-world experiments to test changes.

Integration & maintenance

Toward the end of work we consolidate gains and plan for relapse prevention and long-term resilience.
