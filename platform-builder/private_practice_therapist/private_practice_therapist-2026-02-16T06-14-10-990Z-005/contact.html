Common misunderstandings — clarified

Myth

Therapy always takes years.

Truth

Work can be short-term and focused; goals help shape duration. We collaborate on realistic timelines.

Myth

Therapy will fix everything.

Truth

Therapy supports skill-building and perspective shifts; it’s a process of change, not a one-time fix.

Myth

You must share every detail.

Truth

You control the pace and content. Safety and consent shape how we proceed.

Core therapeutic pillars

Evidence-informed practice

I draw on cognitive-behavioural, trauma-informed, and attachment-based approaches tailored to your needs.

Collaborative goal setting

We define concrete, measurable aims and check progress together regularly.

Practical skills

Sessions emphasize tools you can apply between meetings: regulation, communication, and behavioral experiments.

Respect for pace

Therapy adapts to readiness, cultural background, and life constraints.
