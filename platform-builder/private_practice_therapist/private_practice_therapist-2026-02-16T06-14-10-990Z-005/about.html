A clear, evidence-informed stance

My work blends cognitive-behavioral techniques, trauma-sensitive practices, and relational approaches. I track progress collaboratively and adjust methods based on what the client finds helpful. Therapy is a shared process: I bring frameworks; you bring the lived experience.

Common myth vs. measured truth

Myth: Therapy fixes you quickly if you try hard enough.
Truth: Change is gradual and depends on consistent practice, safety, and realistic goals. I emphasize measurable steps and skill-building rather than promises.
Myth: You must disclose everything immediately.
Truth: You set the pace. Disclosure happens when it feels safe; I prioritize stabilization and consent over forcing narrative recounting.

Clinical boundaries & what I can help with

I provide therapy for anxiety, trauma symptoms, relationship challenges, major life transitions, and professional burnout. I do not provide emergency/crisis services. If you are in crisis, call local emergency services or a crisis line.

Confidentiality & scope

Sessions are confidential within legal and ethical boundaries. I will discuss limits to confidentiality, informed consent, and record-keeping during our first meeting.
