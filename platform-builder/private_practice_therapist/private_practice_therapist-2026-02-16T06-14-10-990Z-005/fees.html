Common Misunderstandings

Myth

If I book a session, I’m committing to years of therapy.

Reality

Therapy is a collaborative decision. We plan together, and many clients choose brief focused work or intermittent check-ins after an initial block.

Myth

Sliding scale means lower-quality care.

Reality

Sliding scale helps me serve clients with limited means while keeping a balanced caseload. Clinical attention and boundaries are the same.

Myth

If I don’t use insurance, I can’t ever seek reimbursement.

Reality

I provide a superbill that many clients submit to insurers for partial reimbursement. Coverage varies by plan.

Short FAQ about Cost & Logistics

Do you accept insurance?

I am typically out-of-network. I provide superbills to submit to your insurer; reimbursement policies vary.

Is there a sliding scale?

Yes. A very limited number of reduced-fee appointments are available. Please contact me via email to inquire; requests are handled case-by-case.

What forms of payment do you take?

Credit card, bank transfer, and occasionally check. Payment is due at or before the session unless other arrangements are made.
