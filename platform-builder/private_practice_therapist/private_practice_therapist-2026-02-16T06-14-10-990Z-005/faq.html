Questions I Frequently Address

Below are practical answers rooted in clinical practice. If you don’t see what you need, email {{EMAIL}} or call {{PHONE}}.

What does an initial session involve?
The first visit is an assessment and collaborative planning conversation: we review your goals, history, and what has brought you in. We usually map priorities and agree on a timeframe for review.
How long do sessions last?
Standard sessions are 50 minutes. Extended intake appointments (75–90 minutes) are available when a deeper review is helpful; these appear as separate options when booking.
Is our work confidential?
Yes. Sessions are confidential with standard legal exceptions (imminent harm, abuse reporting, court orders). Confidentiality limits are discussed and documented during intake.
Do you offer telehealth?
Yes. Telehealth is provided via a secure platform. If in-person is preferred, I have limited office availability in {{CITY}}, {{STATE}}.
What if I’m in crisis?
I am not an emergency provider. If you are an immediate danger to yourself or others, call emergency services or go to the nearest emergency department. If you live in the U.S., you can also call or text 988 for the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline.
Will you share notes with my employer?
I do not share clinical records without your written permission, except when legally required. I will discuss limits to confidentiality during intake.
