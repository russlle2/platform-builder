Begin Care — Book an Appointment

Scheduling options: a short introductory consult or a full intake. If you’re unsure, choose the brief consult and we’ll clarify next steps together.

Available options
  • Introductory Consult — 20 minutes, complimentary for new clients to determine fit.
  • Initial Intake — 75–90 minutes, clinical intake and treatment planning (separate fee).
  • Regular Session — 50-minute ongoing therapy appointments.
Please note: The introductory consult is not a therapy session and is for assessing suitability and answering questions. If you are in crisis call emergency services immediately.
After you request a booking, I will follow up by email with available times and intake paperwork.

What to Expect After Booking

You will receive an email with: confirmation, secure intake forms, a short clinical questionnaire, and a note about fees and cancellation policy. Please complete paperwork before your intake to make our first session most useful.

How I Frame Early Sessions

Early work is assessment-focused: we identify immediate priorities, safety, supports, and practical steps. I use structured, evidence-informed approaches tailored to your situation—no one-size-fits-all prescriptions.

Booking-specific Questions

How quickly can I get an appointment?

Availability varies. Introductory consults are usually scheduled within 1-2 weeks; full intakes may have slightly longer wait times. If you need sooner care, I will suggest alternatives or resources.

What if I need to reschedule?

Please provide at least 48 hours' notice when possible. Late cancellations or missed sessions may incur a fee, which will be discussed during intake.

What technology do you use for telehealth?

I use a secure, HIPAA-compliant video platform. You will receive a link in your appointment confirmation email.

Can I change my session type after booking?

Yes. If you decide you need a full intake instead of a consult (or vice versa), let me know and we will adjust scheduling and fees as needed.
