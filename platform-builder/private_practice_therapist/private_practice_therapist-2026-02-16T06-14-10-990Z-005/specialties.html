Program formats — different frames for different goals

Focus Track (6–8 weeks)

Time-limited, targeted work for a specific problem (skill acquisition, behavioral experiments, in-session practice). Good for discrete goals and measurable change.

Foundations Program (12 weeks)

A block structured to build skills, deepen insight, and create sustainable routines. Includes periodic outcome checks and homework tailored to daily life.

Continuity Sessions (Ongoing)

Monthly or biweekly sessions for maintenance, life planning, or complex presentations. Flexible scheduling and periodic reviews.

Consult + Coaching Modules

Short consultations for clinicians or workplace referrals focusing on strategies to support wellbeing in team contexts (not therapy for all staff).
