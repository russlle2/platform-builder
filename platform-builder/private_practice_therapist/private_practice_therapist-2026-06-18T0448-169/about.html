How can Private Practice Therapy specifically help me with sleep when my young child dictates my schedule?

As a Private Practice Therapist, I understand the realities of parenting. We won't aim for unrealistic perfection. Instead, we'll focus on optimizing the sleep you do get and identifying small, achievable adjustments within your current family structure to improve your overall rest and energy levels. It's about working with your current reality, not against it.

My household feels chaotic; how will Private Practice Therapy address this stress?

In Private Practice Therapy, we'll gently unpack the specific sources of chaos and stress in your home. We'll explore strategies for managing expectations, setting boundaries, and cultivating a sense of calm within yourself that can ripple out to your family. It's about building your resilience and creating more space for peace, even in a busy household.

Is Private Practice Therapy going to involve a lot of extra 'homework' I don't have time for?

Absolutely not. As your Private Practice Therapist, I prioritize practical, manageable steps. Our work together is about integrating sustainable changes into your existing life, not adding more to your plate. We’ll focus on small, impactful shifts that feel natural and supportive, tailored to your capacity as a parent of young children.

I feel so disconnected from myself. Can Private Practice Therapy help me reconnect and feel more grounded?

Yes, deeply. Many parents of young children feel this sense of losing themselves amidst the demands. In Private Practice Therapy, we’ll create a dedicated space for you to reconnect with your inner resources, explore what truly nourishes you, and gently guide you back to feeling more grounded and authentically yourself, like roots reaching for the earth.
