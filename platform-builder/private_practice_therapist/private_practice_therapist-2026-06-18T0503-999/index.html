Frequently Asked Questions

How does private practice therapy specifically benefit athletes dealing with physical stress?
For athletes, physical stress isn't just muscle fatigue; it's a nervous system demand. Our private practice therapy focuses on providing you with concrete strategies to downregulate your sympathetic nervous system, enhance parasympathetic activity, and improve your body's capacity for recovery and adaptation. This directly supports your performance and longevity in sport.
What does 'nervous system regulation' mean for someone with an active lifestyle?
Nervous system regulation means cultivating a flexible, adaptive response to stress. For an active person, this translates to improved sleep quality, faster physical recovery, reduced susceptibility to injury, and enhanced mental clarity under pressure. We equip you with techniques to consciously shift your physiological state, moving from 'fight or flight' to 'rest and repair' with greater ease.
Will private practice therapy interfere with my training schedule?
Our private practice therapy is designed to integrate seamlessly into your life. We work with you to find session times that respect your training and competition demands. The skills you acquire are meant to complement and enhance your athletic endeavors, not compete with them, ultimately optimizing your overall performance and well-being.
How is your approach different from a general therapist for athletes?
While many therapists can support athletes, our private practice therapy is rooted in a deep understanding of the unique physiological and psychological stressors inherent in high-level activity. Our approach is evidence-based, focusing specifically on neurobiology and stress physiology as it pertains to training, recovery, and competition, offering you targeted, relevant support.
