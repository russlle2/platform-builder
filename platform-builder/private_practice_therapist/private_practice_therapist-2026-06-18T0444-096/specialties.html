Finding Your Anchor: Private Practice Therapist Guidance for Chronic Illness & Injury Stress

Living with chronic illness or recovering from injury often means your nervous system is working overtime. This constant state of alert can hinder progress and amplify discomfort. My approach as a Private Practice Therapist focuses on specific, gentle techniques to downshift your stress response, helping you feel more grounded and less reactive to daily challenges. We’ll build a toolkit tailored to your unique needs.
