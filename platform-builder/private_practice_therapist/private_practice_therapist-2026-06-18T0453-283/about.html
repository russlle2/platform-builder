What does 'trauma-informed care' mean for me as a senior in Private Practice Therapy?

For you, it means that your Private Practice Therapist, Dr. Morgan Ellis, understands that past difficult experiences can affect your present feelings. We create a safe and respectful environment, ensuring your comfort and autonomy are always prioritized. We won't push you to discuss anything you're not ready for, and our approach is always gentle and supportive, helping you feel more at ease.

I'm not sure if my feelings are 'serious enough' for Private Practice Therapy. Should I still reach out?

Absolutely. Your feelings are always valid, regardless of how 'serious' they might seem to you. Many seniors find comfort in having a private, confidential space to discuss life changes, worries, or simply to process emotions that arise. Private Practice Therapy with Dr. Morgan Ellis is for anyone seeking gentle support, and there's no problem too small to address.

What if I haven't done therapy before? Will I know what to do in Private Practice Therapy?

Many of our clients are new to Private Practice Therapy, and that's completely expected. You don't need to know anything beforehand. Dr. Morgan Ellis will gently guide you through the process, explaining everything clearly and ensuring you feel comfortable. Your sessions will be a conversation, and we'll help you find your voice and pace.

How can Private Practice Therapy help with feelings of loneliness or grief that often come with aging?

Private Practice Therapy offers a dedicated, compassionate space to explore and gently process feelings of loneliness or grief. Dr. Morgan Ellis will listen without judgment, helping you understand these emotions and develop personal strategies for coping. This support can help you feel less isolated and more connected to your own inner strength, fostering a sense of peace.
