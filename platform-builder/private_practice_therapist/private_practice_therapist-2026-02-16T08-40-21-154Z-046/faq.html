Practical FAQs

How do I start — what happens at the first appointment?

The first session is an intake: we review your goals, history, and immediate needs. It's also an opportunity to assess fit. Expect collaborative planning and clear next steps.

How long will therapy take?

There is no fixed timeline. Some people find relief in several sessions; others opt for ongoing work. We set measurable goals and periodically review progress.

Do you prescribe medication?

I do not prescribe medication. If you are interested in medication, I can coordinate with your prescriber or refer you to a psychiatrist.

What if I have a crisis between sessions?

I am not available for crisis care. If you are in immediate danger, call emergency services or a crisis line. We will establish a safety plan as needed in regular sessions.

Privacy & boundaries

I maintain clinical records and protect confidentiality according to professional and legal standards. Exceptions include imminent risk of harm, suspected abuse, or court-ordered disclosure.
