Common cost myths and clarifications

I aim to be clear about what therapy costs and what you're paying for. A few realities I discuss with clients:

  • Myth: "Therapy is a one-size-fits-all product."
    Truth: We co-design an approach. Packages are flexible.
  • Myth: "Lower cost = higher risk of poor care."
    Truth: Fit, clarity of goals, and therapeutic alliance matter more than price alone.
  • Myth: "Insurance covers everything."
    Truth: Coverage varies; I will give documentation to help with claims.
