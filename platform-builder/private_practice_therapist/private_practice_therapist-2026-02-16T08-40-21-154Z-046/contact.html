Common misconceptions about therapy

Myth: Therapy is only for crises

Truth: Many people start therapy to deepen self-understanding, navigate transitions, or build coping strategies before things reach a breaking point.

Myth: One style fits everyone

Truth: I integrate {{MODALITIES}} and tailor plans to your goals and needs rather than using a single method for everyone.

Myth: You must share everything immediately

Truth: Progress is collaborative and paced to your comfort. You set boundaries and depth over time.
