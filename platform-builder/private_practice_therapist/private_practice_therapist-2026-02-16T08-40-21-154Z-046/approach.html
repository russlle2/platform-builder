Therapy is only for "serious" problems.

Many people seek therapy for manageable but persistent problems: better sleep, calmer mornings, clearer boundaries. These are valid reasons to seek support.

A typical course: progressive exposure for social anxiety

Over 8 sessions, a client and I built a ladder of graded social steps, rehearsed skills in session, and reflected on results weekly. By the end of the block the client reported more choice in social settings and fewer avoidance patterns.
