A gentle diagnostic — where my work fits

Clients often come when their inner life feels crowded by worry, they find patterns repeating in relationships, or they are facing a change that hasn’t found its shape yet. Below are simple signs that therapy could help:

  • Persistent sleep disturbance or intrusive worry affecting daily tasks.
  • Recurring conflicts in relationships with difficulty breaking cycles.
  • Feeling stuck after a loss, career shift, or life transition.

How I typically plan the work

My practical structure balances safety-building, skill work, and meaning-making. A sample arc for new clients:

  1. Intake & trust-building — 1 to 4 sessions.
  2. Stabilization — anchoring practices for daily nervous-system regulation.
  3. Processing — focused work on patterns, attachment, or trauma memories when safe.
  4. Integration & relapse planning — consolidating insights and routines.

Daily micro-practices I share with clients

These are small, invitational habits that build resilience when practiced consistently.

  • 3-minute grounding: notice breath for three slow counts twice a day.
  • Evening reflection: name one thing that felt nourishing each day.
  • Boundary check: ask yourself what you need before saying yes.
