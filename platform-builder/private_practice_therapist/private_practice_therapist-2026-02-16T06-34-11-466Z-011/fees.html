Therapeutic Pathways

Different rhythms for different needs. Below are three common ways clients tend to engage; we’ll choose what feels right together.

  • Clarity Series — 6 sessions: Focused, short-term work to address a specific issue or transition.
  • Exploratory Work — 12 sessions: Deeper inquiry into patterns, relationship dynamics, or trauma-processing in a paced way.
  • Sustained Support — Ongoing: Weekly sessions for complex or chronic concerns and long-term growth.

Daily Practices I Recommend

Small, consistent habits move therapeutic work into daily life. These are practical and evidence-informed without replacing therapy.

  • Brief morning grounding (3–5 min)
  • Check-ins after triggering interactions
  • Compassionate journaling once or twice a week
