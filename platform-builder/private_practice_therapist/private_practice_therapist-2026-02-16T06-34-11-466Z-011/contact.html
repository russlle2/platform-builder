Where we begin — a focused intake

The first few sessions are an assessment: we notice what feels most urgent, clarify values and safety, and co-create a direction. Common domains I explore:

  • Anxiety, panic, and regulation
  • Trauma processing and stabilization
  • Relationship patterns and communication
  • Life transitions, grief, career & meaning
  • Burnout, attunement, and boundaries

This is an exploratory process; your pace guides the work. If care outside my scope is indicated, I will offer referrals.

Intake snapshot
65–90 minute first meeting. We review history, current supports, safety, and practical goals. A written plan follows on request.
A clear pathway — flexible, grounded

After assessment we map a treatment plan with measurable focuses: symptom reduction, skill-building, relational shifts, or meaning-making. Plans are revisited every 4–6 sessions.

  1. Stabilize: rhythm, safety, somatic regulation
  2. Skillwork: emotion regulation, communication
  3. Integration: narrative healing and new routines
  4. Transition: planning for long-term supports
Collaborative notes

You remain the author of change; I act as a witness and guide. Progress is often non-linear — that's expected and accommodated.

Daily practices you can begin today

Small, regular practices help translate session work into life. These are optional suggestions we can personalize.

  • 3-minute breathing check: notice inhale/exhale without judgement
  • Evening reflection: one thing that felt supportive today
  • Boundary rehearsal: a short, kind phrase to practice saying no
  • Movement pause: 5 minutes of gentle stretching between tasks
Micro-commitments

Consistency matters more than intensity. These practices are low-burden and build momentum between sessions.
