How to know if this fits

Quick check: therapy may be helpful when difficulties are persistent, when they reduce life satisfaction, or when social supports don’t feel enough. If you notice patterns repeating despite your best efforts, that’s an invitation for outside support.

Simple micro-habits by specialty

For anxiety

Name three possible outcomes instead of catastrophizing; practice a 4-6 breath box three times daily.

For burnout

Weekly time audit: note one activity that drained you and one that replenished you.

For relationships

Try a 3-minute check-in before hard conversations: what do you need? What do I imagine they need?

How sessions are organized

I accept individual sessions and a rolling seasonal cohort. Cohorts are named "Gathering Sessions" and emphasize psychoeducation, guided practice, and compassionate community. Individual work is tailored and may be short-term or long-term depending on goals.
