My Therapeutic Approach — Embodied Compass

I view therapy as a collaborative exploration: we find practical practices for daily life while attending to deeper felt experience. Sessions blend inquiry, somatic awareness, and meaning-making to support steady change.

Begin an intake

Core principles

  • Safety first: We co-create stabilizing practices before deep processing.
  • Embodiment: The body holds memory; gentle somatic practices are woven into talk work.
  • Skill + meaning: Skills reduce distress; reflective work helps life make sense.
  • Relational stance: Change happens in relationship; I aim to model curiosity and repair.

Session flow

A typical 50-minute session blends check-in, skill rehearsal, and focused work. Early sessions emphasize safety and values clarity; later sessions often move toward integration and relapse prevention.

  1. Check-in & stabilization (5–10 min)
  2. Skill practice or somatic exercise (5–15 min)
  3. Focused therapeutic work (20–30 min)
  4. Wrap-up & micro-habit assignment (5 min)

Micro-habits & rituals

Small practices that anchor the work between sessions.

  • Two-minute morning compass: set one intention for the day.
  • Midday grounding: place a hand on your chest and take five deep breaths.
  • Weekly reflection: write one paragraph about what shifted this week.

Offerings & practicalities

I offer individual sessions, time-limited packages, and seasonal cohort groups. For many clients, a 6- to 12-session package provides focused gains; longer-term therapy remains available when needed.
