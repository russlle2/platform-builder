Frequently Asked Questions

What does 'postural health for seniors' mean in your Private Practice Therapist approach?
For us, it means gently exploring how your body holds itself and moves through the world, focusing on comfort, balance, and reducing stiffness. It's about helping you feel more stable and less prone to aches, honoring the wisdom your body carries with age, rather than striving for an artificial ideal.
Will I have to do strenuous exercises during my sessions at Private Practice Therapist?
Absolutely not. Our approach is rooted in gentle, mindful movements and awareness practices. We focus on subtle shifts and understanding your body's language, not on strenuous exertion. Your comfort and safety are always our top priority, ensuring a nurturing and supportive experience.
How can 'body awareness' truly help me in my daily life as a senior?
By cultivating body awareness, you'll learn to notice how you move, sit, and stand throughout your day. This understanding allows you to make subtle, conscious adjustments that can reduce strain, improve balance, and even lessen discomfort. It’s about feeling more present and in tune with your physical self, leading to a greater sense of ease and independence.
Is your Private Practice Therapist service suitable for someone with existing physical limitations?
Yes, our approach is highly individualized and gentle, designed to accommodate various physical needs and limitations. We work with you where you are, always respecting your body's current capabilities and finding ways to support your well-being safely and effectively. We encourage you to discuss any concerns with .
