Areas of focus

Common pathways people seek: short, focused work for a specific concern, or a deeper series for layered change. Below are typical focal areas and the way I tend to frame the work.

Transitions & Life Shifts

When roles, places, or identities change

Practical reframing, prioritizing, and experiments to move through uncertainty. Useful for job transitions, moves, separation, or new caregiving roles.

Worry & Overwhelm

Reducing reactivity & regaining agency

Skills for noticing triggers, clarifying controllable steps, and practicing brief experiments that create new options.

Relationship Patterns

Repair, boundary, and communication work

Short-term work to clarify patterns, set practical boundaries, and rehearse conversations that feel safer and clearer.

Identity & Purpose

Questions of values, identity, and next steps

Exploratory work that honors complexity, with clear experiments to test possibilities in daily life.

Program pathways

Rather than fixed packages, I offer three pathways that can be tuned to your needs:

  • Navigation Pack — 4 focused sessions to define a problem and try concrete steps.
  • Intensive Series — 8 sessions for layered issues or faster momentum.
  • Maintenance Sessions — monthly check-ins to support ongoing changes.

Fees are presented as an investment in time-limited or ongoing work; we'll align on pacing during the first visit. No promises are made about outcomes.
