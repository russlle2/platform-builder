How I work

An integrative, pragmatic method — we name a question, design brief experiments, and test what moves you toward a clearer sense of choice. The work blends practical skills with reflective noticing.

Core commitments

  • Short-term clarity: make the next steps visible and testable.
  • Paced intensity: choose either tight, focused sessions or slower integration based on your life.
  • Transparency: I explain clinical thinking and next steps plainly.

What therapy here is not

This is not an emergency service or a medical substitute. I will suggest referrals when a different specialty or higher level of care would better meet your needs.

Practice boundaries

We will agree on goals and check progress. Notes are kept according to professional standards; we'll discuss any limits to confidentiality in our first session.

Values that guide the work

Practicality, compassion, and curiosity. I aim to make sessions feel like a clear check-in rather than a slow drift: what changed since last time and what small test do we set next?

Crisis note

If you are thinking about harming yourself or someone else, or if you are in danger, call emergency services or a crisis intervention line immediately.
