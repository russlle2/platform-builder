Questions, quick answers, and a self-check tool

Below are common questions about sessions and a short, non-diagnostic self-screening wizard you can complete to prepare for a first meeting. It produces suggested talking points to bring to consultation.

Frequently asked

How long are sessions?

Typical sessions are 50 minutes. Intensives are longer as specified on the Investment page. We’ll discuss what length feels useful at intake.

Do you take insurance?

Sometimes. If you plan to use insurance, let me know so we can discuss billing options and any necessary information.

What can I expect at the first appointment?

We’ll review confidentiality, your goals, current concerns, and any prior treatment history that feels relevant. You can bring the self-screening summary created below to help focus the conversation.
