What I do not treat here

I provide outpatient psychotherapy for adults. I do not provide emergency care, mandated forensic evaluations, or medication management. When appropriate, I will coordinate with or refer to psychiatrists and other specialists.

Confidentiality & safety

All sessions are confidential except in legally defined situations (danger to self/others, abuse reporting). If there is a safety concern, we will create a specific plan together.

If you are in immediate danger, call emergency services. For urgent mental health support, contact your local crisis resources.
