Why I do this work

I became a clinician because I value steady, trustworthy presence in hard moments. My training combines relational psychotherapy with pragmatic coping strategies—so therapy is both a place to be understood and a place to practice change.

I aim for sessions that feel honest, paced, and useful. We’ll attend to immediate concerns while also tracing patterns that keep you stuck. Change takes time; I’m here for the consistent work.
