Questions people frequently ask

Below are concise answers to practical questions about sessions, confidentiality, and what to expect. If you don’t see your question, contact me directly.

How long is each appointment and what happens in the first session?

The first meeting is an extended intake (60–75 minutes) where we review concerns, history, and goals. We’ll also discuss confidentiality, limits, and logistics so you can decide whether to continue.

Do you accept insurance or file claims?

I am a private-pay clinician. I can provide a superbill you may submit to your insurer for possible out-of-network reimbursement. Direct billing to insurance is not used to protect clinical privacy and to avoid limiting the focus of therapy.

What happens if I need to cancel?

Appointments canceled with at least 24 hours’ notice are typically not charged. Late cancellations or no-shows may incur a fee except in cases of emergency or sudden illness. We’ll go over this during intake.

How do you handle confidentiality and records?

Everything discussed in session is confidential with common legal exceptions (imminent risk of harm, court orders, mandated reporting). I maintain secure records and will explain limits of confidentiality before we begin.

Is telehealth available?

I offer video sessions for most clients. If you prefer in-person work we can discuss availability and location. Note that telehealth has technical and privacy considerations we’ll review as part of informed consent.

Do you work with couples or families?

I primarily see individuals but do take on relationship-focused work when appropriate. If specialized couples or family therapy is needed, I will discuss referral options.

Additional practical notes

Safety first
If you are in crisis or at immediate risk, call emergency services or your local crisis line. I do not provide emergency response through email or regular sessions.
Scope of care
My practice focuses on anxiety, trauma-informed therapy, relationship patterns, and life transitions. If your needs fall outside my scope, I will recommend trusted colleagues.
