Frequently Asked Questions

What kind of sleep issues can Private Practice Therapy help with?
Our Private Practice Therapy can gently support you if you're having trouble falling asleep, waking up frequently during the night, or not feeling refreshed in the morning. We focus on understanding the personal aspects of your sleep and developing calming approaches that fit your life.
Do I need any previous experience with therapy to benefit from your Private Practice Therapy?
Absolutely not. Our Private Practice Therapy is designed to be very accessible, especially for those new to therapy. We'll guide you through everything in a clear, straightforward way, ensuring you feel comfortable and understood every step of the way. No prior 'wellness' experience is necessary.
How is this Private Practice Therapy different for someone my age (60+)?
Our Private Practice Therapy specifically considers the unique changes and experiences that come with being over 60. We use gentle, supportive methods that respect your comfort and pace, focusing on sustainable changes that naturally encourage better rest, rather than quick fixes.
What if I'm not sure if Private Practice Therapy is right for my sleep concerns?
That's perfectly understandable. We encourage you to reach out for a brief, no-obligation chat. You can share what's on your mind, and we can discuss if our Private Practice Therapy approach seems like a good fit for your specific needs. There's no pressure, just an opportunity to explore.
