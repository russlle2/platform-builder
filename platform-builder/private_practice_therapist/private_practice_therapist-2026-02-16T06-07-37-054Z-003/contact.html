How I work — a brief diagnostic map

We begin by clarifying what matters to you: symptoms that interfere with daily life, relational patterns, and values you want to live by. From there I offer a clear plan with measurable checkpoints.

Step 1 — Listen

A focused intake to understand history, strengths, and immediate concerns.

Step 2 — Map

A shared formulation that links your patterns to practical, compassionate strategies.

Step 3 — Integrate

Small experiments and adjustments you can practice between sessions.

Step 4 — Evaluate

We recalibrate together and plan next steps or referrals as needed.

Session structure — a steady frame

Typical sessions are 50 minutes. The rhythm is practical and reflective: check-in, work on targeted interventions, and a closing plan. Telehealth and limited in-person availability. Cancellation policy: please provide 48 hours notice.

Micro-practices to try now

  • 1) Three breaths before responding to a hard message.
  • 2) A 5-minute end-of-day review: what went well, one adjustment for tomorrow.
  • 3) Grounding: name five things you can see, four you can touch.

These are gentle invitations — we adapt practices to your life rather than add obligations.
