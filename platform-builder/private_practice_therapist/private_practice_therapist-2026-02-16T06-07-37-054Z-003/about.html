Clinical compass: how I begin

An initial conversation maps current struggles, support systems, and safety. I listen for patterns — what keeps recurring, what makes you soften — and we co-create a short-term focus. This is not a one-size-fits-all intake; it is a practical planning conversation.

  • 18–50 minute intake that balances history with present concerns
  • Shared goal-setting for 6–12 weeks of work
  • Confidentiality & privacy discussed; limits to confidentiality explained

Therapeutic offerings & structure

My work is integrative and trauma-informed. Interventions are chosen to fit the person: somatic grounding, cognitive reframing, attachment-focused inquiry, and skills for emotion regulation.

Small practices that help between sessions

Change happens in the in-betweens. Below are micro-habits I often offer; they are simple, optional practices to support regulation and awareness.

  • 3-minute grounding: feet on floor, inhale 4 — exhale 6 x5
  • Evening noting: write one line about what felt true today
  • Curiosity pause: when reactive, ask "What am I fearing right now?" before reacting

Investment & options

I offer a tiered structure so care can meet you where you are. Please ask about sliding scale availability.

  • Compass Sessions — single 50-minute sessions for assessment and strategy. (Good for short-term planning.)
  • Pathway Package — recommended 8–12 sessions: structured work on a focused goal.
  • Open Care — ongoing weekly sessions for relational or complex trauma work.
