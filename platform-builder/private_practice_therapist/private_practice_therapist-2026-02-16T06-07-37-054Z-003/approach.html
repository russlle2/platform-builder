My clinical stance: curiosity + structure

Therapy is exploratory and practical. I combine empathic, culturally aware listening with structured interventions so you feel both seen and supported toward change.

Assessment & diagnostic frame

Early work is about safety and specificity. I assess current symptom patterns, relational context, and resources. Together we form a working diagnosis that guides treatment choices without defining you.

Therapeutic methods I draw from

Somatic-informed Regulation — grounding, orientation, and body-based resources to reduce reactivity.
Cognitive & Behavioral tools — practical skills for shifting unhelpful patterns of thought and action.
Attachment & Relational Repair — working with patterns that unfold in important relationships.
Mindful Integration — cultivating presence and compassionate noticing for difficult emotions.

Programs & investment (framed differently)

Below are program frames to help you choose. These are conversational starting points, not rigid packages.

  • Orientation Session — single session to assess fit and immediate priorities.
  • Stabilize & Skill — 6–10 sessions focused on symptom reduction and self-regulation skills.
  • Deep Integration — ongoing weekly work for complex trauma, relational patterns, or long-term growth.

Daily practices I suggest

2-minute breath anchor
Evening strengths note
Conflict script rehearsal
Grounding object technique

These micro-habits are optional and tailored to your preferences and safety. We select a few that actually feel doable in your life.

Intake flow

Ethical notes & limits of practice

I follow standards for confidentiality and informed consent. Therapy is not a substitute for emergency medical care. If you are an immediate danger to yourself or others, please call local emergency services. I will discuss limits to confidentiality in the first sessions. Referrals will be made when needs fall outside my scope.
