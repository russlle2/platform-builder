Small rhythms that help between sessions

Short practices I often suggest — not required, simply tools to try.

  • Two-minute grounding before meetings
  • Evening review: one thing that felt okay today
  • Anchor phrase: one sentence to reduce self-criticism

How to know if we might work together

I look for readiness to reflect, tolerate difficult feelings with support, and collaboration on practical steps. If you’re uncertain, the initial 20-minute consult helps clarify fit.

How do I start?

Use the scheduler on the Book page or email {{EMAIL}}. The 20-minute consult is an opportunity to discuss goals and logistics before committing.

Do you accept insurance?

I am typically out-of-network and provide superbills for reimbursement when possible. I will discuss documentation and limits of insurance during intake.

What if I'm in crisis?

For emergencies, call local emergency services or a crisis line. I do not provide crisis services. If there's imminent risk in a session, I will follow legal requirements to ensure safety.

Do you work with medication?

I do not prescribe. I will coordinate with prescribers when it supports treatment, with your consent.

What about privacy and records?

Notes are kept securely. Telehealth platforms used are HIPAA-aligned where possible. Confidentiality has legally required limits — harm to self/others, abuse of minors or dependent adults, and court-ordered disclosure.

How long is therapy?

It varies. Some people choose short-term work (6–12 sessions); others continue regularly for months. We collaborate on goals and revisit them often.
