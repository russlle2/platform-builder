Focused care for the patterns that keep returning

I work with adults facing persistent anxiety, the aftermath of trauma, relationship ruptures, burnout, and pivotal life transitions. Treatment is collaborative and paced to your current capacity.

How I assess needs

Initial sessions combine structured assessment with narrative — we track symptoms and lived experience. This diagnostic stage informs a clear, time-limited plan we both agree on.

What I commonly treat

Anxiety & Worry

Tools for panic, persistent worry, and physiological dysregulation using CBT-informed strategies and paced exposure.

Trauma Recovery

Stabilization, processing, and integration with somatic and relationally-focused interventions; safety prioritized throughout.

Relationship Strain

Communication skill-building, attachment-focused work, and repairing rupture in adult relationships.

Burnout & Transitions

Boundary-setting, values clarification, and pacing strategies for career and life transitions.

Programs (framing)

I describe my offerings as pathways rather than packages. Choose what fits your need: a focused consultation, a short therapeutic pathway for specific changes, or ongoing relational work.

Typical session

50 minutes, regular review of goals, optional between-session practices. Confidential, non-judgmental, and grounded in clinical ethics.

Practical supports I teach

Clients often ask for concrete practices. Below are examples integrated into therapy when appropriate:

  • Grounding & breath regulation for panic and overwhelm
  • Boundaries practice scripts for stressful interactions
  • Self-compassion exercises to work with shame or perfectionism

Values & limits

I maintain clear boundaries around session structure and confidentiality. If there are legal, safety, or medical concerns, I will discuss limits to confidentiality and coordinate care with your consent where needed.

If you are in crisis, please contact local emergency services or your crisis line; I do not provide emergency response.
