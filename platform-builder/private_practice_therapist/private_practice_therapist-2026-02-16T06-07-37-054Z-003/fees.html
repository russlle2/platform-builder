Paths & structures

Therapy is collaborative. Below are common pathways I offer; we adapt them to your needs.

Integration Path — weekly

Recommended for sustained work on patterns, trauma, or persistent anxiety. Typical commitment: 3–6 months initially.

  • 50–55 minute weekly sessions
  • Includes written reflections and brief between-session notes

Renewal Series — short-term

6-session focused plan for transitions, decision-making, or acute stress.

  • Personalized goal-setting and tools
  • Option to continue into the Integration Path

Daily micro-practices

Small rituals that support therapy between sessions.

  • 3-minute grounding: notice breath for three rounds before starting your day.
  • Boundary journal: one sentence noting a boundary you practiced today.
  • Compassion note: write one kind line to yourself each evening.
