Boundaries & confidentiality
What I keep private and what I must report
▾

I keep session content confidential within the limits of law and ethical practice. Exceptions include situations that indicate imminent risk to you or others, suspected abuse of a vulnerable person, or court-ordered disclosures.

I will always aim to explain the steps I take if a safety or legal concern arises. If coordination with another professional is helpful (e.g., for medication or medical needs), we will discuss how to consent to that sharing.

Session structure & cancellations
What to expect each visit
▾

Sessions typically run 45–55 minutes. We’ll check in, focus on an agreed area, and leave with one or two practical steps to try. If you need to cancel, please give 48 hours notice when possible; missed appointments and late cancellations may incur a fee.

If you are in immediate danger
Call 911 or your local emergency number. If you are in the United States and experiencing a suicidal crisis, you can dial or text 988 to reach the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. If you are outside the U.S., contact local emergency services or a crisis resource in your area.
