How I work — practical, relational, slow-cook change

My offerings combine direct therapeutic skills with group learning rhythms. Below are the guiding pillars and what to expect from a typical cycle.

1. Small experiments
We test one change at a time — short exercises between sessions so ideas land in your real life, not just in conversation.
2. Structure + flexibility
Each cohort or short plan has a predictable arc, but content bends to what the group or you actually need.
3. Practice with peers
Groups allow for role practice and perspective shifts; not therapy theater, but purposeful, supported rehearsal.

What a cycle looks like

  1. Initial intake and orientation — 30–50 minutes to set aims.
  2. Core meetings — weekly or biweekly sessions with clear goals.
  3. Between-session experiments and short check-ins.
  4. A concluding review and next-step planning.

Boundaries, confidentiality & scope

Privacy limits
What I keep private
▾

Conversations are confidential except when disclosure is required by law (imminent risk of harm, suspected abuse, or court order). I will be transparent about any steps I need to take if such issues arise.

Scope of practice
Where I can help and where we may refer
▾

I provide psychotherapy and facilitation for adults. I do not prescribe medication. If your care would benefit from psychiatric or medical evaluation, I will support referral and collaboration as needed.

If you are in immediate danger
Call 911 or local emergency services. If you are in the United States and are experiencing a suicidal crisis, call or text 988 to reach the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. For other urgent needs, contact local emergency resources.
