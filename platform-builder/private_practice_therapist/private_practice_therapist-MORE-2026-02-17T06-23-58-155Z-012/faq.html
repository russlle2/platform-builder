Questions people commonly ask

Short answers below. If something matters to you and isn’t listed, please reach out — I’m glad to clarify.

How do I know if we’re a fit?

Fit is often best judged after a first session. We’ll talk about your needs, goals, and what working together would look like. If I’m not the right clinician for your needs, I’ll offer referrals.

What happens in a first session?

We’ll spend time understanding what’s brought you here, identifying priorities, and co-creating a plan for next steps — whether that’s a few focused meetings or a longer pacing.

Insurance and payment

I am currently working with private pay. I can provide a superbill upon request that you may submit to your insurer for possible reimbursement. Payment methods and sliding-fee options can be discussed by email.

Telehealth or in-person?

I offer telehealth and selected in-person slots. If you have accessibility needs, tell me so I can accommodate or suggest alternatives.

Confidentiality and limits — short version

What you share in session stays private, with a few legal exceptions: imminent risk of harm to self or others, and mandatory reporting of abuse. I strive to be transparent; if there are concerns about involving others, we’ll talk about options first.

Need a quick next step?
