The Silent Strain: When Intensity Becomes Overload – A Private Practice Therapist's Focus

As an athlete or active individual, your drive is your strength. Yet, relentless physical demands can push your nervous system into overdrive, manifesting as persistent fatigue, stalled recovery, or performance anxiety. We'll delve into the subtle signals your body sends, teaching you to discern between productive challenge and detrimental stress, fostering a deeper connection to your physical and mental landscape.
