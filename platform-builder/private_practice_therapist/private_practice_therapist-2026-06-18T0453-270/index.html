Frequently Asked Questions

How does a Private Practice Therapist support someone going through a big life transition?
As your Private Practice Therapist, we provide a consistent, supportive presence as you navigate changes like career shifts, relationship endings, or moving to a new place. We help you explore the emotions that arise, process any past experiences that might be triggered, and develop practical strategies to feel more grounded and secure during this time.
What does 'trauma-informed care' mean in the context of a Private Practice Therapist?
Trauma-informed care means your Private Practice Therapist understands how past difficult experiences can shape your present reactions, especially during stressful life changes. We prioritize your safety and autonomy, working to prevent re-traumatization and helping you build resources to manage emotional responses with greater awareness and self-compassion.
I feel disconnected during this life change. Can a Private Practice Therapist help me find my way?
Feeling disconnected is a common experience during significant transitions. As your Private Practice Therapist, we can help you explore what might be causing this feeling. We'll work together to identify practices and perspectives that help you reconnect with your inner strengths, your values, and a sense of purpose, guiding you back to a feeling of being centered.
Is a Private Practice Therapist suitable for someone who hasn't experienced 'trauma' but is struggling with change?
Absolutely. While we specialize in trauma-informed care, our Private Practice Therapist approach is beneficial for anyone navigating major life changes, regardless of whether they identify with past trauma. The principles of safety, empowerment, and understanding emotional responses are universally helpful when you're feeling uncertain or overwhelmed by transitions.
