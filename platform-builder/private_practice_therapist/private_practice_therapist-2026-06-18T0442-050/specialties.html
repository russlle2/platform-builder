Regulating Your Nervous System Through Major Life Transitions with a Private Practice Therapist

Major life transitions, whether a new career, a move, or a relationship change, often bring an upheaval of emotions and heightened stress. Our focus as your Private Practice Therapist is on practical strategies to help your nervous system find its baseline again. We work together to identify triggers, build coping mechanisms, and cultivate a sense of inner calm, so you can move forward with greater ease and clarity.
