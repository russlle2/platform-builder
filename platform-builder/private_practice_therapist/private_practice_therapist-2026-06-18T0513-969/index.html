Frequently Asked Questions

What if my sport has no off-season?
We work with your current schedule. For year-round athletes, we layer preventive care into your existing routine—like adding a 5-minute mobility sequence during your post-workout cool-down.
How is this different from generic physical therapy?
No rehab-focused language. We focus purely on preventing strain before it happens—using your sport’s rhythm, not medical jargon. Think 'how to avoid hamstring pulls during fall soccer,' not 'restore function.'
Do I need to schedule around my coach or team?
Absolutely. Your training plan comes first. We’ll sync with your team’s schedule, book sessions after practice, or even do 15-minute check-ins during team travel days.
What if I’ve never done therapy before?
You won’t feel lost. We start with what you already do—like stretching after runs—and add just one small, seasonal habit to make it stick. No experience needed.
