How I work — a steady framework

Therapy can feel overwhelming. I use a clear, three-part framework so we both know where we are and what we’re working toward.

1. Map

We identify patterns, triggers, and goals. You’ll get a clear sense of what keeps things stuck and what’s possible.

2. Skills

Practical tools for managing distress: grounding, breathing, cognitive shifts, and boundary practice tailored to your life.

3. Integration

We build habits that fit your rhythms so change lasts — not quick fixes, but reliable progress you can trust.
