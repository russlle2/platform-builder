My therapeutic stance

I orient therapy around practical change and human connection: noticing what keeps you feeling stuck, creating experiments, and strengthening your capacity to be with difficult feelings without getting overwhelmed.

1. Mapping

We clarify your current patterns, priorities, and what matters most to you — so therapy is purposeful from the start.

2. Practicing

Small, repeated experiments help you learn what changes behavior and mood. We track what works and iterate together.

3. Integrating

The goal is to make new responses feel natural; that usually comes from steady practice and building supportive routines.
