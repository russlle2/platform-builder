Where I focus my work

I help adults who are feeling stuck — overwhelmed by anxiety, recovering from relational or developmental trauma, or navigating big life changes. Below are clarity-first descriptions so you can see if we'd be a fit.

Anxiety & Worry

Practical strategies for interrupting cycles of worry, tracking triggers, and building tolerance for uncertainty. Tools include cognitive reframing and interoceptive grounding.

Trauma-Informed Care

Gentle, paced work to increase safety in the body and relationships. We emphasize regulation skills, resource-building, and permission to move at a steady pace.

Relationship Repair & Communication

For people wanting clearer boundaries, healthier conflict styles, and more honest connection — whether in partnerships or family systems.

Burnout & Work Stress

Concrete steps to detect exhaustion, renegotiate expectations, and practice sustainable pacing. Small experiments that protect your energy.

Life Transitions

Support for career changes, parenting transitions, divorce, and moving — navigating loss, identity shifts, and the practical choices that follow.

Depression & Low Motivation

Compassionate, behavioral approaches to gently expand activity, improve sleep and routine, and notice small wins that lead to momentum.

How I organize care

I offer short-term focused plans for specific goals and open-ended therapy for deeper, longer work. We'll map out a proposed plan together in the first few sessions so you know what to expect.
