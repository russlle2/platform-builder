How do we start?+
We begin with brief intake questions in your first meeting to identify priorities. We'll agree on what to focus on and set realistic pacing. Therapy is collaborative; you set the goals and we track progress.
What if I'm unsure what I need?+
If you feel unsure, a single session is a low-effort way to explore what feels most urgent and consider next steps without committing long-term.
Do you work with couples or family members?+
I focus primarily on individual work. For couples or family therapy I can provide referrals or discuss whether a joint session would be appropriate within scope and consent.
How long until I notice change?+
Change depends on goals and consistency. Some people notice small shifts after one or two sessions; others benefit from a short sequence to practice new approaches. There are no guarantees, but we'll aim for measurable steps.
