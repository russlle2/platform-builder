A bit about my practice

I hold a private practice focused on short-term and ongoing options that are individualized to what feels useful for you. My work is informed by evidence-based approaches and adapted to the particular story you bring.

Context

Sessions are collaborative — we set aims together, try adjustments when something isn't helping, and take time to reflect on progress. I provide a supportive clinical relationship, not medical advice or emergency services.

Confidentiality & limits

Conversations are confidential within legal and ethical limits. I will discuss confidentiality in our first session. If there is imminent risk of harm, I must follow mandated reporting and emergency procedures.

My background

I am a licensed therapist with training in multiple therapeutic models. My clinical work focuses on helping people navigate transitions, reduce unhelpful patterns, and strengthen how they relate to themselves and others.

How I work

  • Short focused work for specific decisions or transitions
  • Longer-term collaboration when deeper exploration is useful
  • Practical tools—skill-building, communication practice, and reflection between sessions

Ethics & scope

I do not provide psychiatric prescribing. I can coordinate with other providers when medication or additional care is part of the plan.

Next steps

If you'd like to explore working together, the easiest step is a brief consult where we can see if there's a fit and plan a first session.
