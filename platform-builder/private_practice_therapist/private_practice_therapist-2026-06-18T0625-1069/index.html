Frequently Asked Questions

How can Private Practice Therapy specifically help athletes manage physical stress?
As a Private Practice Therapist, I understand that physical stress for athletes isn't just about sore muscles. It often translates to a constantly activated nervous system. We work to gently re-pattern your body's stress response, teaching techniques to downregulate and restore calm, which aids in deeper recovery and mental clarity, not just physical repair.
I don't have prior experience with therapy. Is Private Practice Therapy accessible for me?
Absolutely. My approach as a Private Practice Therapist is gentle and welcoming, specifically designed for those new to inner work. You don't need any prior wellness experience; we'll start exactly where you are, exploring simple, actionable ways to bring more ease and regulation into your active life, step by tender step.
Will Private Practice Therapy impact my performance or training regimen?
Engaging in Private Practice Therapy to regulate your nervous system is designed to enhance, not hinder, your performance and training. By reducing chronic stress and improving recovery, you may find yourself more resilient, focused, and less prone to burnout or injury. It’s about supporting your overall well-being as an active individual.
What does nervous system regulation mean in the context of Private Practice Therapy?
Nervous system regulation, in Private Practice Therapy, means learning to gently guide your body out of persistent 'fight or flight' states and into a more balanced, 'rest and digest' mode. For active people, this involves understanding how physical demands impact your internal state and cultivating practices that bring a sense of grounded calm, fostering deeper rest and recovery.
