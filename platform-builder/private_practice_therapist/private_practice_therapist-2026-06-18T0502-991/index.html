Frequently Asked Questions

How does nervous system regulation specifically help with chronic illness or injury recovery?
When you're recovering from chronic illness or injury, your nervous system can become stuck in a heightened state of alert. Regulating it helps to lower inflammation, improve sleep, reduce pain sensitivity, and gently encourage your body’s natural restorative processes, fostering a deeper sense of internal peace.
Will this type of Private Practice Therapy interfere with my medical treatments?
No, your Private Practice Therapist will always ensure that our work complements and supports your medical treatments. Our focus is on the emotional and physiological experience of recovery, providing a layer of care that enhances your overall well-being alongside your doctors' guidance.
I feel so exhausted by my condition; will Private Practice Therapy add to my burden?
We understand that your energy reserves are precious. Your Private Practice Therapist will tailor sessions to honor your capacity, focusing on gentle, restorative practices that aim to replenish rather than deplete you. Our goal is to lighten your load, not add to it.
How long does it take to feel the effects of stress reduction and nervous system regulation?
Just as recovery from chronic conditions unfolds over time, so too does the process of nervous system regulation. While many experience immediate moments of calm, sustained shifts are a gentle, ongoing process. Your Private Practice Therapist will support you every step of the way, celebrating each small step towards lasting tranquility.
