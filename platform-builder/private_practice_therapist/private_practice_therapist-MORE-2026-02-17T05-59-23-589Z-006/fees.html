Session boundaries & confidentiality

Confidentiality and limits
click to expand

Sessions are confidential within legal and ethical limits. Exceptions include reasonable suspicion of child/elder/dependent adult abuse, imminent risk of serious harm to self or others, and court-ordered disclosure. Telehealth sessions follow the same privacy practices; I use secure platforms and maintain clinical records per professional standards.

Scheduling & cancellation
click to expand

I ask for 48 hours notice for cancellations when possible. Late cancellations and no-shows may be billed at a portion of the session fee unless we agree otherwise. I will always work with you to find a fair solution when unexpected events arise.

Scope of practice
click to expand

I provide psychotherapy and short-term intensives. I do not provide legal, financial, or forensic services. If you require services outside my scope, I will help identify appropriate referrals.

If you are in immediate danger or believe you may harm yourself or others, call local emergency services (911 in the U.S.) or contact your nearest crisis line. For confidential help in the U.S., you can call or text 988 to reach the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. If outside the U.S., contact local emergency services or your national crisis resource.
