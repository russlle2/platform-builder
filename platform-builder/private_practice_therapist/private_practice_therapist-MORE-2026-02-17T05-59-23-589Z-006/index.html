Session boundaries & confidentiality

Sessions are scheduled and time-limited. Please arrive on time so we can make the most of our work together. If you need to reschedule, please let us know as soon as possible so that the session time can be offered to another client.

Therapy is a collaborative process focused on your goals. We will discuss what to expect at the outset and revisit boundaries and scope as your needs evolve.

Your privacy is treated with professional care. Notes and communications are stored securely. Confidentiality may be limited by law in situations such as clear risk of harm to self or others, or legal requirements; these limits will be discussed explicitly when relevant.

For detailed policies, ask for a copy of the practice agreement or see the intake materials before our first session.

If you are in immediate danger: contact local emergency services right away. If you are in the United States and experiencing a mental health crisis, you can call or text 988 for the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. If you are outside the U.S., please follow local emergency guidance.
