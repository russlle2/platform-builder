How I work with short intensives

My practice is organized around focused offerings that respect a clear time boundary. This helps keep therapy practical and oriented toward immediate change while holding space for reflection.

Intake
A brief intake conversation to make sure the intensive is the right fit, clarify goals, and cover consent and confidentiality details.
Work phase
One or a small set of focused sessions using somatic, narrative, and skills-based interventions chosen for your situation.
Integration
A short plan of practices and supports. Optional follow-up checks are available as a short bundle if helpful.
Assessment — we clarify the felt problem and your practical aims.
Intervention — targeted in-session work, practice, and immediate experiments.
Consolidation — a written integration plan with steps for the coming weeks.

Therapeutic stance

I position myself as a collaborative guide: I offer observations, experiments, and practices, while you choose what feels right and sustainable. I attend to both feeling and function — how you feel inside and what you want to do outside.

Session boundaries & confidentiality
Sessions are private and generally confidential. I keep brief clinical notes for continuity of care. Exceptions to confidentiality (such as imminent risk of harm, suspected abuse, or court-ordered disclosures) will be reviewed during intake.
Cancellation & session policy
I ask for at least 48 hours notice for cancellations of intensives when possible. Fees for late cancellations are discussed at intake. Courteous communication supports a respectful therapeutic arrangement.
If you are currently in crisis or feel unsafe, please call your local emergency number right away. In the United States, you can call or text 988 for immediate crisis support.

Practice ethics and referrals

I adhere to professional ethical standards. If an extended course of care, medication evaluation, or specialized trauma work is a better match, I will provide referrals and help coordinate with other clinicians when requested.

Coordination with other professionals
With your written consent, I can share brief summaries or consult with other professionals involved in your care to ensure alignment and safety.
Careful language
Therapy is collaborative work and outcomes vary. I do not promise specific results; instead, I offer clinical expertise, compassion, and pragmatic tools to support your goals.
