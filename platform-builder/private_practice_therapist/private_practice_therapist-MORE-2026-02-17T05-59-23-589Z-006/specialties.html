A short note on scope
I do not provide forensic evaluations, medication management, or emergency services. If your needs fall outside these offerings, I will help you find a clinician whose work is a closer match.
Crisis notice
If you are in immediate danger or are experiencing a psychiatric emergency, please call your local emergency number. In the U.S., dial or text 988 for the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. For other countries, contact local emergency services.
