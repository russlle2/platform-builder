Questions I hear most

Short answers to practical questions about sessions, access, and what to expect. These are meant to orient; we’ll discuss specifics during scheduling and intake.

How soon can I begin?
I aim to respond to booking requests within 48 business hours. Availability varies week-to-week; intensive formats can sometimes be scheduled more quickly when blocks align.
Do you accept insurance?
I do not bill insurance directly. I provide a superbill — an itemized receipt you may submit to your carrier for out-of-network reimbursement. Coverage varies by plan.
What is an intensive?
Intensives are a small set of longer or closely spaced sessions designed to address a particular problem or decision efficiently. They are not a guarantee of outcome, but a focused structure to clarify priorities and create a plan.
Do you offer sliding scale?
A limited number of sliding-scale openings are available. If finances are a barrier, please mention this when you inquire so we can explore options or referrals.
What if I need more than short-term work?
We will collaboratively assess needs and discuss ongoing care versus short-term work. I make referrals when longer-term or specialty services are a better fit.
Privacy & limits
Your conversations are confidential except where required by law: imminent risk, certain abuse disclosures, or court orders. If you have concerns, we’ll review them during intake.

Confidentiality & boundaries

Records are kept securely. Exceptions to confidentiality include harm to self or others, and suspicion of child/elder abuse. For emergencies, call local services or your regional crisis line.

Respectful crisis note

If you are thinking about harming yourself or someone else, please call local emergency services (911 in the U.S.) or contact your country’s emergency number. In the U.S., you can call or text 988 for the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. If you are outside the U.S., please contact local emergency services or your national crisis resource. If you share this during hours when I am not available, I will help connect you to immediate care when I next speak with you.
