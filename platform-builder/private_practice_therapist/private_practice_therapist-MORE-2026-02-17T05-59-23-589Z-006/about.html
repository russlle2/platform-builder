My approach

I combine attention to bodily experience, relational patterns, and practical behavior change. Sessions are collaborative; you remain the author of your experience. I aim to offer a gentle structure and clear practices rather than prolonged interpretation.

Session boundaries & confidentiality +
Conversations are confidential within legal and ethical limits. I will explain limits to confidentiality, record-keeping, and how I handle requests for records during intake. If coordination with other professionals is helpful, we will discuss consent explicitly.
Scope & referrals +
I work with adults on mood, transitions, relationship concerns, and stress. If you need medication management, emergency psychiatric care, or specialized services for complex trauma beyond short-term intensives, I will help connect you with appropriate clinicians.
If you are in crisis and there is immediate risk of harm to yourself or others, call your local emergency number or community crisis line. If you are in the United States, you can call or text 988 for the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. If you are outside the U.S., please use your local emergency resources.
Professional note
I hold a current clinical license and adhere to professional standards. Nothing here is a guarantee of outcome. Sessions do not replace emergency services.
Confidentiality reminder: If you disclose imminent intent to harm yourself or others or there is suspected abuse of a minor, I am legally obligated to act to protect safety.
