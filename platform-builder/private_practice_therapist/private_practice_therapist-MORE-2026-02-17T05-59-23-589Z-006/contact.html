Session boundaries & expectations +

Sessions begin and end on time. If we need to reschedule, I ask for 48 hours’ notice where possible. This practice aims to keep clear, predictable timeframes so clinical work can be focused and respectful of your life.

Confidentiality & limits +

Conversations are confidential within legal and ethical bounds. I will always discuss limits to confidentiality (for example, risk of harm to you or others) before we begin clinical work. I will also explain how records are kept and who may access them, if applicable.

Scope, referrals & continuity +

I aim to provide honest guidance about what I can offer. If your needs fall outside my scope, I will suggest referrals and community resources to support continuity of care.

Respectful crisis guidance:

If you are in immediate danger or need emergency help, please call your local emergency number. For urgent mental health crises, contact local crisis services or a trusted emergency contact. If you’re unsure who to call, dialing local emergency services is the fastest way to get immediate support.
