Frequently Asked Questions

Can I book an extra session during finals week?
Absolutely. We keep a few slots open specifically for term peaks—we schedule evenings and weekends so you don’t have to choose between therapy and studying.
How is this different from campus counseling?
Campus counseling is often for immediate crisis or short-term support. We focus *proactively* on how anxiety shows up during your academic year, with sessions designed around your class schedule—not just what's available.
What happens in the first session?
We’ll map out what’s actually on your plate right now: your classes, deadlines, and what ‘too much’ feels like for you. No homework, no jargon—just a clear plan for your next steps.
Do you work with student insurance?
Yes, we accept common student plans like Blue Cross Student Advantage. We’ll verify coverage before your first session—no surprise bills.
