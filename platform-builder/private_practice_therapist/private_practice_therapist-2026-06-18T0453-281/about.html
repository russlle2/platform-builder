I'm a working professional aged 30-45 dealing with burnout. How is your Private Practice Therapist approach different for me?

My approach is specifically tailored to the unique pressures faced by working professionals in your age group, acknowledging that burnout often comes with feelings of guilt, shame, and a sense of failure. We focus on gentle, trauma-informed strategies that fit into your busy life, helping you unpack the emotional load without adding more stress.

I've never been to therapy before. Will I understand what we're doing?

Absolutely. My style is very accessible and conversational. You don't need any prior wellness experience or 'therapy language' to benefit. We'll start exactly where you are, and I'll guide you through each step, ensuring you feel comfortable, understood, and in control of your process as your Private Practice Therapist.

I feel emotionally drained and fragile. Can your trauma-informed care really help with burnout?

Yes. Burnout can leave your emotional system feeling overtaxed and vulnerable. Trauma-informed care means we approach your experiences with deep respect for your capacity and resilience. We won't push you beyond what you're ready for, instead focusing on building a safe space to gently process the emotional impact of sustained stress, helping you feel more grounded.

I'm worried therapy will just add another item to my already overwhelming to-do list. How can this help?

I understand that concern completely. Therapy with me is designed to be a dedicated space for *you* to offload, not add. We'll explore strategies to simplify and create more breathing room in your life, rather than piling on more tasks. The goal is to reduce your burden, not increase it, helping you regain control and feel less overwhelmed.
