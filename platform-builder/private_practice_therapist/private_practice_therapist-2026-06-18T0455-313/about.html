How does Private Practice Therapy help with physical pain for seniors?

Our Private Practice Therapy focuses on somatic approaches, which recognize the deep connection between your body and your overall experience. We use gentle, evidence-based techniques to help you understand and alleviate physical discomfort, often by addressing underlying patterns of tension or sensation that contribute to pain. It's a non-invasive way to find more physical ease.

Is this approach suitable if I have existing medical conditions?

Absolutely. As your Private Practice Therapist, we prioritize your safety and well-being. Our approach is designed to complement any medical care you are already receiving. We will discuss your health history thoroughly to ensure our gentle methods are appropriate for your specific needs, and we can collaborate with your other healthcare providers if you wish.

What makes your Private Practice Therapy different for seniors aged 60+?

Our Private Practice Therapy is specifically tailored with the unique needs of seniors in mind. We emphasize a gentle, respectful pace, clear communication, and an understanding of age-related changes in the body. We focus on restoring comfort and function in ways that honor your life experiences, rather than pushing for intense or rapid changes.

How long will it take to feel relief through this Private Practice Therapy?

The experience of relief is very personal, and as your Private Practice Therapist, we respect your individual timeline. Some individuals notice subtle shifts in comfort within a few sessions, while others benefit from a more extended period of gentle exploration. Our focus is on sustainable, gradual improvement, and we'll regularly check in with you about your progress and comfort.
