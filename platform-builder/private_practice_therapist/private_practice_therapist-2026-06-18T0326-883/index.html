Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is 'postural health' for seniors in Private Practice Therapy?
Postural health for seniors in Private Practice Therapy means addressing how you hold your body when sitting, standing, and moving. We focus on gentle ways to improve your alignment to reduce discomfort, improve balance, and make everyday activities feel easier and more comfortable, without strenuous exercises.
How can 'body awareness' benefit me as an older adult in Private Practice Therapy?
Cultivating body awareness in Private Practice Therapy helps you notice how your body feels and moves. This skill is invaluable for older adults as it allows you to identify subtle signs of tension or imbalance early, adjust your movements to prevent falls, and move with greater confidence and less strain in your daily life.
Is Private Practice Therapy for posture suitable for someone with existing health conditions?
Yes, our Private Practice Therapy approach is designed to be gentle and adaptable. We always consider your individual health conditions and work collaboratively with you to ensure exercises are safe, comfortable, and beneficial. Your well-being and comfort are our top priorities.
What should I expect during a typical Private Practice Therapy session focused on posture?
In a Private Practice Therapy session, you can expect a warm, conversational environment. We'll start by discussing your concerns, then move into gentle, guided movements and observations. We might use props for support, and you'll learn simple practices you can do at home to continue improving your posture and body awareness between sessions.
