About the practice

I offer clear, humane psychotherapy for adults navigating life transitions, relationships, and the recurring patterns that make things feel stalled. Sessions blend clinical grounding with practical steps you can use between meetings.

I work with people who want steady, individualized support — not a rush to fix, but a careful map toward clearer decisions and gentler rhythms. My training is in evidence-informed therapies and relational practice; I aim to be straightforward, curious, and reflective in sessions.

Typical first session
We clarify your priorities, review history, and set an initial focus that feels manageable. You decide the pace from there.

Why a small, steady practice

Therapy is most useful when it fits your life. I design short, focused pathways and longer partnerships depending on what you need. We test ideas, observe what changes, and keep what helps. The work is collaborative: I bring structure and training; you bring lived experience and goals.

Notice

We name patterns and moments that recur.

Try

We design small experiments you can test between sessions.

Reflect

We explore what worked, what felt hard, and revise the plan.

Confidentiality + limits

Conversations are private to the extent permitted by law. Exceptions include imminent risk of harm to self or others, suspected abuse of a minor or vulnerable person, or court orders. If you have concerns about privacy, we will discuss them before beginning care.
