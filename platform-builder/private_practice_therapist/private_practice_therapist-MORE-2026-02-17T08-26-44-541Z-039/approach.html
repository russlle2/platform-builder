How I work with people

The practice combines empathic listening, hypothesis-driven experiments, and practical tools you can use between sessions. We name patterns, try small changes, and track what moves you forward.

Typical rhythm
Initial intake clarifies goals. Short blocks (4–8 sessions) test changes. Ongoing care available for sustained work.

Relational focus

We look at repeated interaction patterns and try small experiments that change the shape of conversations.

Behavioral experiments

Short, practical tasks designed to reveal what helps and what hinders — then we revise the plan.

Reflective stance

We track changes and make adjustments based on what you notice and report back between sessions.
