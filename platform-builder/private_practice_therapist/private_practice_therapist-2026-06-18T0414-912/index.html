Frequently Asked Questions

Can this fit around my investor calls and board meetings?
Absolutely. We schedule your seasonal check-ins when your energy is naturally shifting—not during crunch times. Your prevention plan works with your calendar, not against it.
How is this different from generic stress management?
We don't treat symptoms. We map your energy to actual seasons using ancestral wisdom. Your February dip isn't 'stress'—it's a seasonal shift. We build your prevention around *your* patterns, not a one-size-fits-all approach.
When should I reach out before a major deadline?
Before your next energy dip. If March is your fundraising season, schedule your reset *now*—not when you're already overwhelmed. We work with your seasonal timeline, not your panic timeline.
Do you work with people who hate 'wellness' jargon?
Yes. We speak your language: energy patterns, calendar timing, and prevention. No 'journeys' or 'healing.' Just your energy, stable, before the next crunch.
