Frequently Asked Questions

How does Private Practice Therapy help with the physical pain of burnout?
Burnout often manifests as physical symptoms like headaches, muscle tension, or fatigue. Our Private Practice Therapy uses somatic techniques to help you identify and release the stored stress and emotional patterns contributing to your physical discomfort, bringing you lasting relief.
I'm a busy professional; how can I fit Private Practice Therapy into my schedule?
We understand your demanding schedule. Our Private Practice Therapy offers flexible scheduling options to accommodate your work-life balance. We aim to make it as easy as possible for you to prioritize your well-being without adding more stress.
Is somatic healing suitable for everyone experiencing burnout and physical tension?
Somatic healing within Private Practice Therapy is a gentle and effective approach for many. It's particularly beneficial if you notice a connection between your stress levels and your physical sensations. We'll assess if it's the right fit for your unique needs during our initial consultation.
What makes your Private Practice Therapy different for professionals dealing with burnout?
Our Private Practice Therapy specifically targets the unique challenges of working professionals aged 30-45. We combine somatic healing for physical pain with a community-focused, inclusive approach, ensuring you feel truly seen and supported in addressing your burnout and its bodily impact.
