Services & specialties

I work primarily with adults on relationship shifts, anxiety that affects daily functioning, career transitions, and patterns that repeat from old wounds. Below are common areas people bring to therapy.

  • Relationship patterns
    Working with recurring relational dynamics — communication, boundaries, and repair.
  • Anxiety & performance pressure
    Practical strategies to reduce interference with daily tasks and values-driven action.
  • Life transitions
    Decision points such as career change, parenting shifts, or ending relationships.
  • Patterns from earlier experience
    Exploring how earlier attachments and habits show up now and influence choices.
If you are unsure what to expect, we can begin with a brief consultation to see fit and talk about pacing.
