About my practice

I work with adults navigating life shifts, persistent patterns that feel stuck, and moments when values and choices don't line up with living as you intend. My approach is rooted in evidence-informed methods blended with practical, present-focused work.

Membership model

Members receive regular sessions, a care plan we shape together, and flexible short blocks when life spikes. Membership is intended for ongoing therapeutic partnership rather than quick fixes.
