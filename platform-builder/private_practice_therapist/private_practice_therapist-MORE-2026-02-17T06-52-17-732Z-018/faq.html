Frequently asked questions

Below are practical answers about sessions, membership, and what confidentiality means in this practice. If your question isn’t listed, please reach out.

I offer ongoing supportive membership work, short focused series (4 sessions), and single sessions. We’ll choose a format that matches your goals and life demands; the structure is part of the clinical plan rather than a rigid promise.
Membership is a monthly arrangement for regular sessions and continuity of care. It includes priority scheduling, periodic brief check-ins, and the chance to build skills over time. Billing is monthly; you may transition out with 30 days’ notice. It is not emergency coverage.
Please give at least 48 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule. Late cancellations or missed sessions may be billed at the full session rate, except in cases of emergency or unexpected hardship where we can discuss accommodations.
Sessions are confidential. Information is not shared outside this practice without your permission except in the limited situations required by law: when there is an immediate risk of serious harm to yourself or others, suspected abuse of a child or vulnerable adult, or when ordered by a court. If such limits apply, I will tell you as soon as possible.
I do not bill insurance directly. I can provide documentation (a super-bill) you may submit to your insurer for possible reimbursement. Insurance companies may require clinical information as a condition of payment; if you intend to seek reimbursement, we can discuss what that may involve.
I offer telehealth sessions and limited in-person availability. We'll choose what works best for clinical needs and your comfort. Telehealth is not available for crisis situations; see crisis guidance below.
If a question touches on legal, medical, or urgent safety issues, we’ll discuss appropriate referrals and supports; I do not provide emergency services.

Session boundaries, confidentiality, and scope

Below is a concise outline of practice boundaries. These are shared to set clear expectations about what care here includes and does not include.

I keep therapy records secure and private. Exceptions include: imminent risk of serious harm, suspected abuse, court orders, or other legal obligations. If release of information is requested, I will explain what is being disclosed and why.
This practice is not a crisis line. I am available by scheduled appointment and respond to messages during regular hours. If you are in crisis or at immediate risk, contact local emergency services or a crisis hotline (see below). We can discuss emergency planning in a session.
I maintain clinical notes to support safe, effective care. If you use insurance, documentation about diagnosis and treatment may be required; I will review that process with you before submitting records.
If you are in immediate danger or at risk of harming yourself or others:
Call your local emergency number right away or go to the nearest emergency department. For non-urgent but pressing concerns, you may call your local crisis line. If you are in the United States you can call or text 988 for the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline; if you are elsewhere, contact your local emergency resources.
