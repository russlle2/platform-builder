What membership includes

  • Regular one-on-one sessions timed by your plan and availability.
  • Flexible short-series for focused work (4–6 sessions) when a single topic needs attention.
  • Secure messaging for scheduling and low-intensity check-ins within agreed boundaries.
  • Priority booking for members and a clear cancellation policy to protect time and progress.

How we move together

  1. Introductory consult to clarify needs and fit.
  2. Choose a rhythm: membership cadence or a short-series target.
  3. Clear boundaries discussed in writing; ongoing session planning with measurable steps.

Practical questions

Conversations are held to professional confidentiality standards. Limits include current risk of serious harm, court order, or otherwise mandated reporting. We will discuss limits and your preferences at intake.

Sessions run for the agreed time. Messaging is for scheduling and brief clarifications only; clinical content is best covered in sessions. We review scope and expectations early and periodically.

If you are at immediate risk of harm or need urgent medical help, please call your local emergency number or go to the nearest emergency department. For non-immediate but concerning needs, we will discuss local crisis resources during intake.

Interested in joining the membership?

We’ll match rhythm and scope to your current needs. Membership is arranged in written agreement and revisited over time.
