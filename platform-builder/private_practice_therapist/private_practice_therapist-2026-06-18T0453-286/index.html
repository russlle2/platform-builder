Frequently Asked Questions

I'm new to therapy, and my illness/injury makes me tired. Will this Private Practice Therapist be overwhelming?
Absolutely not. We understand that your energy levels might be low, and your experiences are complex. Our approach is gentle and flexible. We'll go at your pace, and our primary goal is to create a comforting, accessible space where you feel supported without any pressure.
How does this Private Practice Therapist specifically address the emotional challenges of chronic illness or injury?
We focus on the unique emotional landscape that comes with these conditions. This includes processing grief, managing anxiety about your health, coping with changes in your identity, and addressing any feelings of isolation or frustration. Our trauma-informed lens ensures we acknowledge the profound impact these experiences can have on your emotional well-being.
I'm worried about being judged or misunderstood because of my health condition. How will your Private Practice Therapist handle that?
We are dedicated to providing a non-judgmental and deeply empathetic space. We understand that chronic illness and injury often come with invisible struggles. Your experiences will be met with respect and understanding, and you'll never have to explain or justify your feelings here.
What if I don't know exactly what I need help with, just that I'm struggling emotionally with my recovery?
That's perfectly normal and a great place to start! You don't need to have all the answers. Our initial conversations will be about exploring what feels difficult for you right now, gently uncovering your needs, and figuring out together how this Private Practice Therapist can best support your emotional healing.
