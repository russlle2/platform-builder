Hesitations I hear — and how we handle them

"I don't know if therapy will help me."

We start with a brief roadmap and small, measurable tasks. If you don’t feel benefit in an agreed period, we revisit the plan together.

"I’m worried about confidentiality."

Confidentiality is fundamental. I’ll explain limits to confidentiality, record-keeping, and how I protect your privacy in our first meeting.

"I’m too busy for weekly sessions."

We can design shorter check-ins, a limited series, or a hybrid of in-person and remote meetings to fit your schedule.

"Will you judge me?"

My work is nonjudgmental and pragmatic. We focus on what’s effective for you, not on blame.
