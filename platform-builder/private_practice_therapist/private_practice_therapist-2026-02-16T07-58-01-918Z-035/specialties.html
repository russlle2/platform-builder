How work differs by need

Treatment is matched to the presenting problem. For example:

  • Anxiety: behavioral experiments and exposure in bite-sized steps
  • Trauma: pacing, grounding, and titrated memory work when appropriate
  • Relationships: communication exercises and role-based practice

Is my concern "big enough" for therapy?

Therapy is for persistent patterns, not just crises. If a problem repeatedly disrupts your life or drains energy, it’s worth exploring. We’ll define a target together and set realistic measures of improvement.
