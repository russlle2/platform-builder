Core Principles I Work From

Practical Compassion

Therapy that is warm and direct — we bring curiosity to hard feelings and translate insight into behavior you can test between sessions.

Integrity & Confidentiality

Sessions are private and held to professional confidentiality. I’ll clarify boundaries, recordkeeping, and what to expect about limits to confidentiality up front.

Goal-Focused Partnership

We co-create measurable goals and check progress. If short-term work fits better than long-term therapy, I’ll say so and offer a clear pathway.

Common Concerns — Addressed

Will therapy take forever?

It depends on your goals. Many clients see meaningful shifts in 8–20 sessions when we focus on clear outcomes. I offer short-term plans when appropriate.

I’m not sure I’ll be taken seriously — what if my problems seem small?

Nothing you bring is too small. We work with what matters to you; progress often comes from attending to the small, persistent stresses.

Can I afford this?

I offer transparent fee plans and limited sliding-scale slots. See the Investment page for program options and typical session fees.
