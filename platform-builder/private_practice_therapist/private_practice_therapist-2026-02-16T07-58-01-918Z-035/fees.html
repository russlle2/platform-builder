I accept card and bank transfer. If cost is a barrier, we can discuss limited sliding-scale availability during our first contact. Many clients find that a short package helps maintain momentum at a predictable monthly cost.

Common concerns

What if I can't commit long-term?
Short-term packages and pulse sessions are designed for flexible engagement. We define goals together and check in regularly to reassess.
Do you take insurance?
I do not bill insurance directly. I provide a superbill you may submit to your provider for possible reimbursement.
Is there a cancellation fee?
Cancellations within 48 hours may be subject to a fee; emergencies are handled with professional judgment. We review policy during intake.
