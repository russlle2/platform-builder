Practical questions — scheduling, pacing, and telehealth

How long before I notice a change?

People often report small improvements in weeks when they regularly practice skills. Meaningful, sustained change depends on the goal and consistency.

What if I can't commit weekly?

We design a cadence that fits your life — fortnightly sessions, brief check-ins, or intensive blocks are options.

Do you take insurance?
