Privacy, scope & safety

All communications and records are kept confidential within legal and ethical limits. I will not disclose information without your consent except where required by law (e.g., imminent risk of harm, mandated reporting). Intake includes a brief discussion of limits to confidentiality and how we handle records.

Scope: I work with adults on anxiety, trauma, relationship issues, life transitions and burnout. If your needs fall outside my scope (e.g., severe active psychosis, substance withdrawal needing medical oversight) I will provide referrals.

If you are in crisis or at risk of harming yourself or others, please call emergency services immediately or use your local crisis line. I do not provide emergency services through email or the standard therapy portal.
