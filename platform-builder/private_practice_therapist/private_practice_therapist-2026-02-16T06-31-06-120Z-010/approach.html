Therapeutic frame

I begin with safety and stabilization. From there we learn practical tools (breathing, grounding, thought experiments) and experiment with small behavioral changes. Over time we integrate those practices into daily routines so progress is sustainable.

Core influences

  • CBT and ACT for thinking and behavior shifts
  • Trauma-informed stabilization and somatic awareness
  • Relational repair and communication skills

How sessions typically flow

  1. Check-in & symptom tracking
  2. Brief skills practice or experiential work
  3. Review homework & plan next steps

Sessions are collaborative; you set the pace. Progress is tracked in concrete ways so we can see what helps.

Boundaries & safety

I am not available for crisis management. Confidentiality is prioritized; exceptions include imminent harm, abuse reporting requirements, or legal orders. If you are in immediate danger, call emergency services.

Therapy pathways — how we might work together

Discovery Session

Single consult to clarify goals and fit.

Stabilize & Skill

4–12 sessions focused on safety, regulation, and skill practice.

Continuous Support

Ongoing weekly or biweekly sessions for long-term or complex concerns.

Client commitments

Therapy is most helpful when sessions are regular, homework is attempted between sessions, and feedback is shared openly. If you need to pause or change frequency, we will plan that together.

A brief note on limits

I do not give medical or emergency services. If you are experiencing suicidal intent or immediate danger, please call emergency services or a crisis hotline. For medication questions, I can coordinate with prescribers but do not prescribe myself.
