Areas I commonly support

I work with adults navigating life transitions, persistent anxiety, and complex relational patterns. Below are common themes I support — each card notes what to expect and how we pace the work.

{{PRIMARY_CTA_LABEL}}

Anxiety & Worry

Practical pacing, skills for uncertainty tolerance, behavioral experiments. Often 6–12 sessions to build foundational practices.

Trauma-related distress

Safety first: stabilization, grounding, and gradual processing when appropriate. Work is collaborative and paced to your tolerance.

Relationship patterns

Explore attachment, communication patterns, and boundary-strengthening. Sessions include in-the-moment skill practice.

Burnout & work transitions

Practical strategies to rebuild energy, examine expectations, and design sustainable routines.

Life transitions

Starting/stopping relationships, moving cities, career shifts. We focus on values, meaning, and practical adjustments.

Grief & loss

Compassionate support for processing loss, finding ways to carry memories, and returning to daily functioning when ready.

How I tailor work

I integrate cognitive-behavioral strategies, somatic awareness, and relational repair. We co-create a plan that honors your pace and capacity; nothing is mandated. If work falls outside my scope I'll help with referrals.
