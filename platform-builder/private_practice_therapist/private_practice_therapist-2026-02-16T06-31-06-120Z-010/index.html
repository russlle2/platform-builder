Why I work this way

I trained in evidence-informed methods and blend that training with lived experience and practical tools. I aim to create a calm, collaborative space where we map what matters to you and experiment with changes that fit your life. I believe real change comes from small, steady shifts rather than dramatic fixes.

I maintain strict confidentiality. I will explain limits of confidentiality, my scope of practice, and what to expect in therapy during your first session. If you are in crisis, please call your local emergency number or a crisis line — this site is not for immediate crisis intervention.

The MAPS Framework

A practical structure to guide our work: Make sense, Act with choice, Practice skills, Strengthen routines.

Make sense

We clarify the patterns keeping you stuck and identify realistic goals together.

Act with choice

Try small experiments to build confidence and reshape responses to stress.

Practice skills

Use targeted tools — grounding, communication, boundaries — tailored to your needs.

Strengthen routines

Build dependable habits that support sustained change and well-being.
