My story — why I do this work

I came to therapy because relationships felt hard and deadlines felt relentless. Over the years I trained in trauma-informed care, cognitive-behavioral therapies, and somatic awareness. That mix helps me support people in both understanding patterns and developing practical, daily moves that create more ease.

I aim to hold sessions that are warm, clear, and grounded in real life. Sessions are collaborative: we set goals together, track small changes, and adjust based on what actually helps you feel safer and more capable.

Values in practice
Confidentiality, clarity, and consent are central. I will explain limits of confidentiality, document collaboratively, and invite your questions about treatment direction and boundaries.
Ethics & scope
I work with adults on mood, anxiety, trauma recovery, relationship concerns, and life transitions. I do not provide emergency services; if you are in crisis, please call local emergency services or a crisis line.

How I structure work — a simple framework

1. Map & Orient

We start by clarifying what matters most to you now and forming humane, measurable goals.

2. Learn & Practice

We introduce skills to shift patterns (emotion regulation, grounding, relational skills) and practice them in-session.

3. Integrate

We focus on everyday routines so gains hold when sessions end.

4. Review & Transition

We check progress, plan maintenance, and create a transition plan when you’re ready.
