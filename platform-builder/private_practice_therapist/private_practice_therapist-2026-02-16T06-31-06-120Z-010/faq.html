Common questions

What people usually want to know

Below are practical answers about sessions, privacy, and how to begin. If your question isn’t listed, email {{EMAIL}}.

How should I prepare for my first session?
You don’t need to prepare anything formal. I invite you to bring whatever feels most pressing—concerns, context, goals. Expect some intake questions about history and practicalities; we use the first meeting to co-create aims.
How long does a typical session last?
Most sessions are 50 minutes. Some modalities or couples work may be scheduled for 60 minutes; we’ll agree on the best length during booking.
Do you accept insurance?
I do not bill insurance directly. I can provide a SuperBill for out-of-network reimbursement. If you need an in-network provider, I can offer referrals.
Are virtual sessions available?
Yes. I offer secure teletherapy for people in {{STATE}} and in-state clients. In-person availability is limited; please ask about current options.
What does confidentiality cover?
Conversations are confidential within legal and ethical limits. Exceptions include imminent risk of harm to self or others, suspected abuse of a minor or dependent adult, and court-ordered disclosure. Electronic communications have additional privacy considerations; we can review these during intake.
How do cancellations and late arrivals work?
I request 48 hours notice for cancellations. Late arrivals shorten session time rather than extend it. Repeated short-notice cancellations may require scheduling changes.
