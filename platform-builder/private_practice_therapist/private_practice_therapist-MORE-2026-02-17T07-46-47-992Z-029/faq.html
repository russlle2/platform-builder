Common questions

Concise answers to practical concerns. If something isn't covered here, reach out directly.

How do I start?

You can reserve a first session via the booking page or send a quick message to confirm availability. After booking, you'll receive an intake form and a short welcome note.

Do you accept insurance?

I do not bill insurance directly for memberships. If you wish to seek reimbursement, I can provide a superbill upon request; this may require a diagnostic code and brief documentation of sessions.

What if I need to cancel?

Please provide at least 48 hours' notice when possible. Late cancellations and missed sessions may be subject to a fee; we'll review these terms in intake so expectations are clear.

Are sessions virtual?

Most work is offered by secure video; occasional in-person options are available when noted. We will align on a format that fits your needs.

What if I'm in crisis?

This practice is not an emergency service. If you are in immediate danger, please call local emergency services or a crisis line. If unsure, contact your nearest resources or call the national crisis line.

Can I change membership frequency?

Yes. Memberships are intended to be flexible. We will review timing, frequency, and payment adjustments during our sessions.

Clinical boundaries

Care is provided within professional scope; I maintain confidentiality except where disclosure is required by law. I document sessions in line with professional standards.
