Common questions

How does the membership work?
Members schedule at a regular cadence that we choose together. The membership includes priority booking and periodic plan reviews. It is not an emergency or crisis service.
Are sessions confidential?
Yes. Clinical notes and communications are held in confidence within the limits of law and ethical practice. I will explain the exact boundaries at intake.
What if I need immediate help?
This practice does not provide crisis or emergency services. If you are in imminent danger or need urgent help, contact local emergency services or a crisis line immediately.
