Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is postural health therapy for retirees?
It's a gentle approach within Private Practice Therapy that helps you become more aware of how you hold and move your body. We focus on simple, accessible techniques to improve your alignment and balance, which can reduce discomfort and support your overall vitality, without requiring strenuous exercises.
Do I need any prior experience with bodywork or movement classes?
Absolutely not! Our Private Practice Therapy is specifically designed for individuals with no prior wellness experience. We start with the basics, in a very gentle and conversational way, ensuring you feel comfortable and supported at every step.
How does improving my posture help with mental clarity?
When your body is in better alignment, it reduces physical strain and can create a sense of ease, which directly impacts your mental state. Many clients report feeling more focused, calmer, and generally more mentally agile as their postural awareness improves. It's about creating a better connection between your body and mind.
What can I expect in a typical Private Practice Therapy session?
Each session is tailored to your needs. We'll chat about your comfort and movement goals, and then gently explore simple movements and ways of noticing your body. It's a collaborative and relaxed environment where you're encouraged to move at your own pace, focusing on gentle adjustments and increased awareness, not on pushing yourself.
