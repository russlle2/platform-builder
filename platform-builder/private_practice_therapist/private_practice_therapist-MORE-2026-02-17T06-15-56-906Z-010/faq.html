Common Questions

How do I know if therapy is right now?
I can help you explore that. A single session can be a low-commitment way to get a sense of fit and next steps.
Do you accept insurance?
I am out-of-network but provide a receipt (superbill) you can submit to many insurers for reimbursement. We can discuss billing at intake.
What is the cohort experience?
A small group that meets regularly with focused learning prompts and optional individual check-ins. It’s structured for shared learning, not group therapy.
How long are sessions?
Standard sessions are 50 minutes. Some focused sessions or administrative check-ins may be shorter; we'll confirm in scheduling.
Is this confidential?
Yes. Notes and session content are confidential within professional and legal limits. We'll review limits of confidentiality during intake.
Need a quick sense of fit?

Book a single session to discuss priorities and options. No long-term commitment required.
