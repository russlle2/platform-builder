My approach: compass, experiments, and steady pacing

Think of therapy as a short series of experiments guided by a clinical compass. We name a direction, try an experiment, review, and revise. This keeps work concrete and humane.

1
Map what's most important
We clarify what truly matters to you and set achievable, observable aims rather than vague goals.
2
Try small experiments
Practical tasks you can do between sessions—short, measurable, reversible—so learning happens without overwhelm.
3
Reflect & adjust
We track the results together and adapt based on what helps, not what we hope will work.

Clinical boundaries & what to expect

I work from a relational, skills-oriented stance that integrates cognitive-behavioral tools, mindfulness, and real-world planning. Therapy here is collaborative: we set shared aims, check progress, and adjust together. I do not provide emergency services; for crisis, call local emergency lines.
