Frequently asked

Below are answers to practical questions about confidentiality, boundaries, and how sessions usually work. If something important is missing, please reach out.

How long is a session?
Standard meetings are 50 minutes. Occasionally a different length is arranged when appropriate; we will agree on that in advance.
Is therapy confidential?
Yes. I maintain client confidentiality within professional and legal limits. Exceptions include imminent risk of harm to self/others, suspected abuse of a minor, or as required by law. We'll review confidentiality details at intake.
Do you accept insurance?
I do not bill insurance directly here. I can provide a receipt (a superbill) that some clients use to request reimbursement. Handling insurance can affect confidentiality; we can talk through options.
What if I'm in crisis?
If you are in immediate danger or need urgent help, call emergency services or a local crisis line. This practice is not an emergency service. We will discuss crisis resources as part of care planning.
How do cancellations work?
I ask for 48 hours notice for cancellations when possible. Late cancellations or no-shows may be billed at a reduced fee depending on circumstances. We aim for clear, compassionate arrangements.
