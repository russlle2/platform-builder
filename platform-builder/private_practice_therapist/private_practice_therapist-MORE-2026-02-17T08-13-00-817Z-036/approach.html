How I help people find steadier ways of living

My method is relational and pragmatic: we notice patterns, try small experiments, and name practical steps that fit your life. I aim for clarity over jargon, and for plans you can actually keep.

Explore the steps
1. Map the moment

We begin by naming the immediate concern and how it plays out day-to-day.

2. Choose a small test

Rather than wholesale change, we pick an experiment you can try between sessions.

3. Review & adapt

We review what happened, refine the approach, and solidify useful shifts.

4. Build routines

Over time, practices and brief skills become more automatic and less effortful.

Clinical priorities

  • Respect for your values and pace
  • Clear boundaries about confidentiality and emergencies
  • Practical skills tested in everyday contexts

A note on limits

If your needs are outside my expertise, or if a medical or legal opinion is required, I will discuss alternatives and refer to appropriate services. If you are in immediate danger, please call emergency services.
