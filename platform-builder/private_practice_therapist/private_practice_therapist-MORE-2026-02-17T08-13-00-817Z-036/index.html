How we organize the work

Think of therapy here as a small map for the terrain you bring: short routes for pressing problems, steady trails for patterns, and occasional intensive waypoints when you need concentrated clarity.

Focused Path

4–8 sessions. A targeted plan to address a specific problem: relationship check-ins, sleep routines, or acute anxiety management.

Ongoing Collaboration

Open-ended weekly or biweekly sessions for continued exploration and skill-building—adapted to life’s shifts.

Concise Intensive

2–3 concentrated meetings over a few weeks to reframe a stuck decision, prepare for a transition, or regain momentum.

About the clinician

I’m a licensed clinician practicing with a relational, evidence-informed approach. I aim for clear goals, practical tools, and a steady, confidential space to reflect without judgement. We’ll set boundaries for safety and scope up front, and I’ll be direct about when work is outside my practice and a higher level of care is needed.

Confidentiality is respected within the limits of the law. If you are in crisis or thinking about harming yourself or others, call emergency services or your local crisis line immediately.
