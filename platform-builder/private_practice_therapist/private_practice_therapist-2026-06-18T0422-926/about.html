Can you help me without taking time off work?

Yes. We focus on micro-adjustments within your existing schedule—like shifting your afternoon meeting 15 minutes to recharge, or setting 'energy boundaries' for your workday. You don’t need to carve out extra hours; we work with the time you have.

What if I’m not sure what 'seasonal wellness' means?

It’s about noticing patterns: Your energy dips before tax season? Your focus wavers in March? We’ll identify *your* seasonal markers (not generic 'spring renewal') and build prevention tools around them. Think of it as scheduling your energy, not just your calendar.

How do you prevent burnout before it happens?

We track your 'warning signs' (like skipping lunch or snapping at emails) and create preemptive shifts. For example, if you know December is draining, we’ll plan small, non-negotiable reset moments *before* the chaos hits—not after you’re overwhelmed.

Do you work with people from all backgrounds?

Absolutely. Our work is rooted in your actual life—your job, your family, your commute, your city. We don’t assume ‘what your burnout looks like.’ We build solutions that fit *your* reality, not a one-size-fits-all ideal.
