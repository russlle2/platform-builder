The effects of chronic illness or injury can extend far beyond the initial physical event, leaving you with persistent pain, fatigue, or a sense of disconnection from your own body. In my Private Practice Therapist sessions, we create a sanctuary where you can process these experiences. My approach as a Private Practice Therapist focuses on somatic healing, which means we pay close attention to your body's sensations and responses. We'll use gentle techniques to help you reconnect with your body in a safe, non-judgmental way, reducing the grip of chronic pain and allowing for a more comfortable, present experience within yourself. Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Private Practice Therapist help with my physical pain from chronic illness or injury?
As your Private Practice Therapist, I don't treat the illness or injury directly. Instead, we focus on the emotional and somatic impact of living with persistent physical discomfort. We'll explore how your body holds stress and pain, using gentle techniques to reduce tension, improve your body awareness, and help you find new ways to relate to your physical sensations, ultimately aiming to alleviate your overall sense of suffering.
What does 'somatic healing' mean for someone recovering from chronic conditions?
For someone recovering from chronic illness or injury, somatic healing means we pay close attention to the sensations in your body. We'll explore how past experiences or ongoing discomfort might be stored physically, leading to tension or pain. As your Private Practice Therapist, I'll guide you through gentle practices to help your nervous system regulate, release chronic tension, and foster a safer, more comfortable relationship with your body's signals.
Is this therapy suitable if I'm still actively dealing with medical treatments for my condition?
Absolutely. This therapy complements your existing medical care by addressing the emotional and somatic aspects of your recovery. As your Private Practice Therapist, I can provide a supportive space to process the challenges of medical treatments, manage treatment-related stress, and gently work with any physical discomfort that persists. It's about supporting your well-being alongside your medical journey.
What if my pain levels fluctuate, or I have days where I feel particularly fragile?
Your comfort and well-being are always the priority. As your Private Practice Therapist, I'm here to meet you exactly where you are, even on your most challenging days. We'll adjust our sessions to match your energy and pain levels, using gentle, non-invasive techniques. The goal is to create a safe space where you can explore your experiences without feeling pressured or overwhelmed, ensuring the process feels supportive and nurturing for you.
