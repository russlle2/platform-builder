How can a Private Practice Therapist help me with postpartum stress?

As a new mother, your body and mind are navigating immense changes. We focus on understanding how stress impacts your nervous system and equip you with practical tools to reduce overwhelm, improve sleep, and feel more grounded in your day-to-day life.

I feel so tired. Will therapy add more to my plate?

Absolutely not. Our sessions are designed to be a supportive, calming space for you, not another task. We work at your pace, focusing on strategies that integrate seamlessly into your busy life, helping you conserve energy rather than expend more.

What does 'nervous system regulation' mean for a new mom?

It means learning to recognize when your body is in fight-or-flight mode and gently guiding it back to a state of rest and digest. For new mothers, this can involve techniques to manage anxiety, improve emotional resilience, and respond to your baby's needs without feeling constantly on edge.

Is this therapy only for mothers experiencing severe anxiety or depression?

Not at all. While we support those experiencing clinical symptoms, our services are also for any new mother feeling overwhelmed, exhausted, or simply wanting to feel more settled and less stressed during this significant life transition. You don't need a diagnosis to seek support.
