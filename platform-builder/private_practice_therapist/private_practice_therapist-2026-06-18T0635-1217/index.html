Frequently Asked Questions

Why is Private Practice Therapy specifically for new mothers important during postpartum recovery?
The postpartum period is a time of immense physiological and emotional change, often overlooked in its depth. Specialized Private Practice Therapy acknowledges the unique biological and psychological stressors you face, offering targeted support for mood disorders, birth trauma, and identity shifts that generic therapy might not fully address.
How does trauma-informed care apply to my postpartum experience in Private Practice Therapy?
Trauma-informed care in Private Practice Therapy recognizes that many birth and postpartum experiences, even those deemed 'normal,' can leave traumatic imprints. Your therapist will create a space of safety and predictability, focusing on your agency and choices, and working to integrate difficult memories without re-traumatization, fostering a sense of control and calm.
What if I feel overwhelmed and unsure where to begin in Private Practice Therapy?
It's entirely natural to feel overwhelmed. Your Private Practice Therapist understands this. We start exactly where you are, gently exploring your current feelings and concerns. There's no pressure to have all the answers; the initial steps in therapy are about building trust and creating a foundation for you to slowly unfold and process at your own pace.
Will Private Practice Therapy help me connect with my baby and navigate my new identity as a mother?
Absolutely. Our Private Practice Therapist supports you in processing any emotional barriers that might be impacting your connection with your baby or your comfort in your new maternal identity. By addressing your emotional well-being, we help you cultivate a more present and authentic relationship with yourself and your child, fostering a deep and meaningful bond.
