Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Private Practice Therapist help me manage my college anxiety?
We'll work with you to understand the specific triggers for your anxiety related to academics and social pressures. Our focus is on practical, body-centered techniques to regulate your nervous system, allowing you to respond to stress with more calm and clarity, rather than feeling overwhelmed.
I feel constantly stressed by academic pressure. Can therapy truly help with that?
Absolutely. Academic pressure often manifests as chronic stress, affecting your sleep, focus, and overall well-being. We'll explore strategies to reframe your relationship with academic demands and implement practices that reduce physiological stress responses, helping you feel more in control and less burdened.
What does 'nervous system regulation' mean for a college student?
For you, it means learning to shift out of the 'fight or flight' response that often accompanies academic anxiety. We'll practice techniques to activate your parasympathetic nervous system, promoting a state of rest and digest. This helps you think more clearly, sleep better, and approach challenges with a more grounded perspective.
Is Private Practice Therapy a long-term commitment, or can I get support for specific college challenges?
We tailor our approach to your needs. Some students benefit from short-term, focused support for immediate academic stressors or exam anxiety, while others prefer a more sustained space to explore deeper patterns. We'll discuss what feels right for you during our initial conversations.
