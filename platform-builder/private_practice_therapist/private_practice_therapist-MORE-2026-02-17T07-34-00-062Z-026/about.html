Session boundaries & confidentiality

Below are practical details about how sessions and communication are handled. If something here feels unclear, we can review it in your intake.

Standard sessions are 50 minutes. Occasionally, different lengths are offered for focused work; these will be described in the program details and during intake. Scheduling and cancellations are discussed at first contact.
Conversations are confidential within the limits of law: duty to protect from imminent harm, suspected abuse of a vulnerable person, or court-ordered disclosures. Administrative communication (scheduling, billing) is minimal and handled with care. If coordination with other providers is helpful, it will only occur with your informed consent.
Clinical notes summarize topics, agreed plans, and clinical impressions; they are retained in secure records. You may request a conversation about your records; legal exceptions may apply.

Scope & crisis note: Therapy focuses on emotional, relational, and behavioral concerns. This service is not an emergency resource.
