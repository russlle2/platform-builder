How I work

My work blends practical planning with clinically informed approaches — oriented to the question at hand, and to the rhythms of daily life.

Attentive assessment

An initial intake clarifies immediate concerns, context, supports, and safety. This shapes whether a short block or a longer plan fits best.

Practical tools

We identify a small set of practices (experiments, scheduling shifts, communication rehearsals) that are feasible between sessions.

Relational focus

Change happens in relationships and routines; sessions pay attention to how moves will land in your real life.

1. Brief intake
20–35 minute conversation to clarify needs, logistics, and immediate priorities.
2. Choose a frame
A focused block or a series — we decide together what's reasonable and useful.
3. Practical sessions
Sessions include reflection, strategy, and at least one concrete next action to try between meetings.
Case note — example

Client presented with persistent indecision about a career shift. Intake focused on values, constraints, and a 3-session plan. Over three sessions we clarified priorities, practiced communication scripts, and created a timeline for a trial project. This framed decision-making with less overwhelm.

Session and communication boundaries

Sessions are 50 minutes unless another length is agreed. Email and messaging are used for scheduling and brief coordination only. Clinical contact for urgent matters should be handled by local emergency services or crisis resources.

Confidentiality details
Information is confidential except for legal limits (risk of harm, abuse, or court orders). Releases for information exchange are written and time-limited.
If you feel at risk or are considering harming yourself or others, contact emergency services or a crisis line immediately (for example, 988 in the U.S.). If you are outside the U.S., use local emergency numbers.

Tools & measures

I may use brief standardized measures to track progress with your consent. These are tools to guide conversation, not labels or judgments.
