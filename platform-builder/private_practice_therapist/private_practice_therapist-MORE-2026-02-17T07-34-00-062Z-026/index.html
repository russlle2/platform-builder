How I work — session boundaries and confidentiality

Below are standard professional practices. If you have questions about how these apply to you, we can review them during intake.

Standard sessions are 50 minutes unless otherwise agreed. Cancellation policy: please notify within 48 hours so we can offer the time to another client; fees for late cancellations are discussed at intake.

Clinical notes and communications are kept private, with exceptions required by law (immediate risk of harm to self or others, or certain legal processes). I will discuss confidentiality and its limits before you share sensitive information.

Telehealth sessions are conducted with secure platforms when possible; notes are retained according to professional standards. We will review how records are stored during intake.

If you are in immediate danger or a medical emergency, call local emergency services right away. If you are in crisis and need urgent support but are not in immediate danger, consider your local crisis line or 988 (U.S.). This practice does not provide emergency services.

Questions people bring

Do you accept insurance?
Insurance policies vary. I provide superbills where appropriate; we can discuss specifics during contact.
How long is an intensive?
Intensives are typically a single focused intake plus 3–7 follow-up sessions depending on goals.
What are the topics you work with?
Transitions, decision-making, anxiety about change, relationship navigation, and aligning values with action. If your concern falls outside usual scope, I will help you find appropriate care.
