Program frames & investments

Work is offered in two common frames. Rather than guaranteeing outcomes, these are options for structuring time and focus.

  • Focused Block — 3 to 6 sessions with explicit goals and a simple plan for practice between meetings. Good for a clear decision or an immediate problem.
  • Foundational Series — 6 to 12 sessions for deeper pattern work and skill-building over time. Useful when changes require habit and support.
