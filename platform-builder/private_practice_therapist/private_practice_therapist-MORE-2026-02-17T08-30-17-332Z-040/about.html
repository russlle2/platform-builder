About the practice

I offer focused clinical support for individuals navigating transitions, relational shifts, and persistent patterns that feel stuck. My intention is to listen with clarity, help you name what's happening, and co-create practical steps to try between sessions.

Therapist statement

I approach therapy from an integrative, trauma-aware stance. Sessions emphasize collaboration: you'll get thoughtful clinical framing, and we will develop concrete practices and communication templates you can use outside of sessions. I aim to keep language plain and to prioritize safety, consent, and your values.

Confidentiality: sessions are confidential within clinical and legal limits. Scope: I provide psychotherapy and not emergency medical services. If you are in crisis or at immediate risk, please call your local emergency number or a crisis line in your area.

What to expect in a first consultation

  • A brief intake to understand presenting concerns and history
  • Collaborative goals for a short plan of work
  • A discussion of practical steps and scheduling

If you have questions in advance, use the self-screening tool on this page to gather thoughts to bring to the consultation.
