Therapeutic approach

My work blends evidence-informed techniques with a pragmatic lens. I emphasize safety, clarity, and experiments you can test between sessions. The goal is to help you gather information about what works for you, rather than to force a single path.

Core commitments

Collaborative framing

We co-create an understanding of how difficulties develop and what small steps might shift patterns.

Skill + structure

Therapy includes practical tools—communication templates, short behavioral experiments, and ways to track change.

Context-aware

We work with life realities—time limits, caregiving, work demands—so plans fit your everyday life.

Typical process

  1. Intake & focused formulation: a clear map of current concerns
  2. Initial plan: 3–6 steps to try between sessions
  3. Review and adjust: regular calibration based on what helps

This is a flexible sequence—some clients choose a single intensive, others prefer ongoing weekly work. We'll discuss fit in the first meeting.

Boundaries & crisis guidance

Confidentiality is upheld with attention to legal limits. I do not provide emergency services. If you are experiencing an emergency, please contact local emergency services or crisis resources immediately.
