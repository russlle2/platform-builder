Areas of focus

I work with adults in concentrated, evidence-informed cycles that emphasize clarity and usable steps. Below are common pathways clients choose to address specific life challenges.

Transition Support

For people navigating big life changes (career shifts, moves, family transitions). Sessions center on decision clarity, values, and practical rhythm-building.

Single consult: starting at $150

Focused Intensive

A concentrated session (extended time) to address a pressing topic with assessments and an immediate plan to try between sessions.

Half-day intensive: starting at $420

Short Series

A short course of 3–6 sessions to build momentum on a particular pattern (communication, boundaries, mood regulation).

Short series (3 sessions): starting at $420

Ongoing weekly work

Regular sessions for people seeking steady support and skill development over time.

Monthly cadence: custom, discussed in intake

Pricing is presented as a starting point and may vary with session length or complexity. There are no guarantees of outcome; therapy is collaborative and individualized.

Preparing for a focused session

  • Bring a brief timeline of the concern
  • Note recent attempts or strategies that have and haven't worked
  • Be ready to name small first steps you can try
