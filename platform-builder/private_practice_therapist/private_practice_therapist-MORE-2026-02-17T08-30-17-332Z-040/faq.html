Common questions, and a gentle self-check you can bring to a first meeting

Below are practical answers and a short intake wizard to help you notice priorities to discuss in your first session. This is not a diagnosis; it’s a way to prepare for focused conversation.

How do I know which format suits me?

Think about whether you want a short, goal-focused engagement, a single intensive start, or ongoing sessions for steady progress. We’ll pick a format together after an intake conversation.

Are sessions confidential?

Yes. Sessions are confidential within legal and ethical boundaries. I will review confidentiality, its limits, and mandatory reporting exceptions during our first meeting.

What if I’m in crisis?

If you are in immediate danger, please call local emergency services or your local crisis line. I do not provide emergency services and will help connect you to suitable resources when needed.
