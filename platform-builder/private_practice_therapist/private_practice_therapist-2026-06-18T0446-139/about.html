How is this Private Practice Therapy different from general stress management?

Our Private Practice Therapy is specifically tailored for parents of young children experiencing sleep deprivation and household stress. We don't offer generic advice; instead, we focus on the unique biological and emotional demands placed on you, providing targeted strategies that directly address parental burnout and sleep quality.

Will I have to drastically change my child's sleep routine for my own sleep to improve?

Our focus is primarily on your sleep and stress management as a parent. While we can discuss how your child's sleep impacts yours, the core of our work involves developing strategies for your own rest within the realities of your family's situation. We aim for sustainable improvements that respect your current family dynamics, not radical overhauls.

How quickly can I expect to see improvements in my sleep and stress levels through Private Practice Therapy?

Every individual's situation is unique, but many clients report feeling a greater sense of control and experiencing initial improvements in their ability to relax and fall asleep within a few weeks. Consistent application of the strategies we develop together will lead to more significant and lasting changes in your overall restorative rest.

Is this Private Practice Therapy just about getting more hours of sleep?

While increasing sleep duration is often a goal, our Private Practice Therapy goes beyond just hours. We prioritize improving the quality and restorativeness of your sleep. This includes strategies to help you fall asleep more easily, stay asleep, and wake up feeling more refreshed, even if your total sleep time is still impacted by your children's needs.
