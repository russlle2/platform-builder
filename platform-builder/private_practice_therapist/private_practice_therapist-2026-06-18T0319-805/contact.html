Private Practice Therapist

Understanding Your Body's Story During Transition

Major life changes, whether expected or sudden, often manifest in how you hold yourself. Stress and uncertainty can lead to new aches, tension, or a general feeling of being 'off-kilter.' As your Private Practice Therapist, we focus on helping you understand these physical cues, making sense of how your body is responding to the emotional landscape of your life transitions. This awareness is the first step toward reclaiming your sense of balance.
