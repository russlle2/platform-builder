A brief note about my practice

I offer confidential, time-bound therapy that prioritizes safety and collaboration. We will work together at a pace that feels manageable. I integrate mindfulness, relational attachment perspectives, and trauma-aware methods to support anxiety, life transitions, burnout, and relational concerns. I do not provide emergency services; if you are in crisis, contact local emergency services or a crisis line.

Sessions are private and protected by clinician-client confidentiality unless disclosure is required by law (harm to self/others, abuse). For details, see the privacy section on the Contact page.
