Questions I often hear

Below are practical responses about sessions, safety, and what to expect. If your question isn't here, please reach out — I welcome clarifying calls.

A calm container
"Practical tools and thoughtful listening." — client feedback
How do I know if therapy is right now?
People come when stress, relationships, or patterns feel overwhelming, or when a life change feels unclear. A brief consult can help us decide together if now is the best time.
What modalities do you use?
I blend trauma-informed approaches, relational work, and evidence-based skills (e.g., CBT-informed strategies, mindfulness practices). I center what fits your needs and pace. Modalities: {{MODALITIES}}.
Do you accept insurance?
I am out-of-network. I provide superbills for many carriers so you can seek reimbursement. I can help answer questions about that process during our first call.
How do cancellations or emergencies work?
I ask for 48 hours' notice for cancellations when possible. For urgent crises, I am not an emergency service. If you are in immediate danger, please contact local emergency services or your crisis line. In-session limits and boundaries will be discussed in intake.
How long before I notice change?
Change varies. Some people find practical relief after a few sessions; deeper patterns take more time. We'll set goals together and check progress regularly without promising specific outcomes.
Is telehealth available?
Yes. I offer secure video sessions and occasional phone appointments when appropriate. We’ll discuss privacy and technology needs before starting.
What are your boundaries around contact outside sessions?
I respond to scheduling and administrative messages; for clinical concerns I encourage bringing them to a session. Boundaries are explained clearly in the intake paperwork.
