My therapeutic stance

I offer evidence-informed care with an emphasis on safety, collaboration, and practical skills. Together we map patterns, highlight small experiments, and build greater capacity to manage distress and live according to your values. I draw from {{MODALITIES}} and relational approaches to hold both compassion and structure in our work.

I do not provide forensic evaluations or emergency services. If you are in acute crisis, please contact local emergency services or a crisis hotline.

Process — a gentle roadmap

1. Check-in & clarity

We start by identifying immediate concerns and values to form shared goals.

2. Stabilize & resource

Build tools to manage intensity and create a reliable base for deeper work.

3. Skill practice & integration

Experiment with small changes, reflect on outcomes, and adapt the plan.

4. Review & next steps

We check progress and decide whether to pause, continue, or shift focus.

Offering names & formats

I offer distinct ways to work depending on your needs — each framed to be transparent about scope and time commitment.

  • Compass Pathway — 6-session plan for clearer direction and immediate tools.
  • Stability Sessions — single sessions focused on regulation and coping strategies.
  • Transition Support — 10-week pathway for life changes and identity work.

These are starting templates; we tailor them to what you most need. Fees and scheduling details available on the Book page.

What therapy won’t promise

Therapy is a collaborative process. I cannot guarantee specific outcomes or elimination of distress. What I can promise is a consistent, confidential partnership and evidence-informed options to try together.

Questions clients raise

How soon can I expect to start?

Scheduling varies; new clients often begin within 1–3 weeks depending on availability. If you need faster support, ask about waitlist openings or referrals.

Will I get homework?

Sometimes. Tasks are collaborative, brief, and tied to what we practice in session — always optional and scaled to your capacity.

Do you take insurance?

Private-pay only. I provide superbills and can discuss sliding scale availability during intake.
