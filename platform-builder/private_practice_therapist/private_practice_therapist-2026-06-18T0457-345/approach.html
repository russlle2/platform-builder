Somatic Healing: A Private Practice Therapist's Approach to Stress Relief for Students

Somatic healing isn't just about talking; it's about listening to your body's signals. For college students dealing with anxiety and pressure, this means learning how to release stored tension, understand your physical reactions to stress, and build resilience from the inside out. We'll explore gentle techniques together to help you regulate your nervous system, leading to less physical pain and a greater sense of calm in your busy life.
