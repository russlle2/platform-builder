Regulating Your Nervous System as a Parent

The constant demands of parenting young children can keep your nervous system in overdrive. You might feel perpetually on edge, easily irritated, or completely exhausted. In our Private Practice Therapy sessions, we'll explore practical ways to bring your nervous system back into balance. This isn't about ignoring the mess or the tantrums, but about finding moments of internal peace so you can respond with more presence and less reactivity to your little ones and everyday household stress.

I'm a busy parent; how can I possibly fit Private Practice Therapy into my schedule?

We understand your time is precious! Our Private Practice Therapy offers flexible scheduling options, including virtual sessions, to make it as easy as possible for you to attend. We focus on practical, actionable strategies that integrate into your existing routine, rather than adding more tasks to your day. It's about finding small, impactful ways to care for yourself.

I've never done therapy before. What should I expect from Private Practice Therapy?

That's perfectly fine! Many parents are new to Private Practice Therapy. Our sessions are conversational and approachable. We'll start by getting to know you and what you're hoping to achieve. There's no pressure to have all the answers, and you don't need any special preparation. It's simply a safe space for you to talk, be heard, and learn new ways to manage the stresses of parenting young children.

How does Private Practice Therapy help with the specific stress of parenting young children?

Our Private Practice Therapy is specifically designed for parents like you. We focus on the unique challenges of raising young children, from sleep deprivation to emotional regulation for both you and your kids. We'll explore strategies for calming your own nervous system, managing household demands, and responding to your children with more patience, helping you feel more present and less overwhelmed in your daily life.

Will Private Practice Therapy teach me how to be a 'perfect' parent?

Absolutely not! There's no such thing as a perfect parent. Our Private Practice Therapy isn't about achieving an impossible ideal, but about helping you feel more grounded, resilient, and connected to yourself amidst the beautiful chaos of parenting. We'll help you develop tools to navigate stress and regulate your nervous system so you can show up as the best version of yourself, imperfectly, for your family.
