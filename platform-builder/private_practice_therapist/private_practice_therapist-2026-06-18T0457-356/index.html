Frequently Asked Questions

How does Private Practice Therapy help with physical pain from chronic illness or injury?
Our Private Practice Therapy focuses on somatic healing, which means we work with your body's physical sensations and responses. We help you identify and gently release patterns of tension and discomfort that are often stored in the body after chronic illness or injury, leading to a noticeable reduction in persistent physical pain and increased ease of movement. It's about retraining your nervous system for comfort.
Is this Private Practice Therapy suitable if my pain fluctuates daily?
Absolutely. We understand that chronic pain and recovery from injury often involve fluctuating symptoms. Our Private Practice Therapy approach is highly adaptable; we'll work with your body's capacity on any given day, always prioritizing gentle techniques that support your current state. The goal is to build resilience and consistent improvement over time, regardless of daily variations.
What makes this Private Practice Therapy different from physical therapy?
While both aim for physical improvement, our Private Practice Therapy delves into the emotional and nervous system aspects of pain and recovery. We complement the structural work of physical therapy by addressing how stress, trauma, and sustained illness create physical holding patterns. Our focus is on somatic awareness and release, giving you tools to manage pain and discomfort from a deeper, body-mind perspective.
How long does a typical Private Practice Therapy program last for somatic healing?
The duration of your Private Practice Therapy program is entirely personalized, depending on the complexity of your chronic illness or injury recovery and your specific goals for physical comfort. We'll discuss a recommended timeline during your initial consultation, but the pace is always set with your well-being in mind, ensuring a thorough and effective somatic healing experience tailored just for you.
