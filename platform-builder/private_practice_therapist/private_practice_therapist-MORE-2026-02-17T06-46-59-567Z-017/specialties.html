Ways I commonly help people

Below are focused offerings and topic areas I work with. We can shape these into a short series or an ongoing plan depending on what you need.

Short Clarity Series

A structured 4-6 session package designed to address one immediate issue — decision-making, a relationship concern, or managing burnout. We set a clear question and practical next steps each week.

Package pricing — discussed at booking

Transition Support

Planning and coping through life changes: career moves, relationship shifts, relocation, or parenting transitions. Practical planning and emotion regulation strategies.

Available as single sessions or ongoing work

Stress and Work Strain

Tools for managing chronic stress, setting boundaries at work, and preventing burnout. Skill-building that you can use between sessions.

Tailored short series or open scheduling

Low-level Anxiety & Mood

For persistent tension or low mood that affects daily life. Collaborative goal-setting and small experiments to shift patterns over time.

Starting points discussed during intake

How offerings are framed

I describe work in terms of focused series versus continuity. Focused series are time-limited and goal-oriented. Continuity is for people who want ongoing collaboration and a space to work through evolving issues.
