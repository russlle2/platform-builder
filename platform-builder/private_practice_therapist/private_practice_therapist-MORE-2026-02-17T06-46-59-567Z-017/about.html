About the practice

I offer short series and ongoing sessions for people navigating change, overwhelm, or wanting better day-to-day balance. I bring practical clinical training with a focus on steady, useful steps you can try between sessions.

Who I work with

Adults facing transitions, caregiving stress, workplace strain, or persistent low mood and anxiety. I aim to collaborate — we set goals together and track what helps.

What a session looks like

Focused conversation, brief skills practice, and a small plan to practice between sessions. Typical sessions are 45–55 minutes.

A bit about me

I trained in evidence-informed approaches and blend them with practical planning tailored to your life. I believe small experiments often reveal what changes are sustainable. My aim is to make therapy understandable and useful.
