Frequently asked questions

Short, plain answers. If something is still unclear, please reach out — I aim to be transparent about how I work.

What happens in a first session?
We review why you're coming, current concerns, and your preferences for therapy. We'll discuss confidentiality, set boundaries and develop an initial plan. There is space to ask questions about the process as well.
Do you take insurance?
I provide receipts for out-of-network reimbursement but do not bill insurance directly. I can offer a conversation about sliding scale options when appropriate.
Are sessions confidential?
Yes, conversations are confidential within legal and ethical limits (e.g., safety concerns, court orders, child or elder safety). I will review confidentiality and its limits at intake.
What if I am in crisis?
If you are in immediate danger or a medical emergency, call local emergency services. If you're feeling like you might harm yourself, please call your local crisis line or go to an emergency room. I do not provide crisis care via email or text.
How long are sessions?
Standard sessions are 50 minutes. Some programs use longer or shorter blocks; these will be discussed and agreed upon in advance.
Can I schedule remotely?
Yes. I offer telehealth appointments and, where available, in-person meetings. We'll discuss privacy and the logistics before starting telehealth.
What won't therapy here provide?
I do not offer medication management, forensic assessments, or emergency psychiatric care. If those are needed, I will help with referrals within appropriate professional channels.
How do you measure progress?
Progress is collaborative. We check in regularly, adjusting goals and tools together. If you prefer structured measures, we can build in brief questionnaires or tracking tools to guide our work.

Still have a question? Send a note or request a session.

A brief note on limits

Therapy is best thought of as a collaborative effort to build skills, understanding, and ways to cope. It does not guarantee outcomes; rather, it supports you with clinical tools and steady attention.
