A practical framework

Therapy is a collaborative process. I focus on short, testable changes that fit into your life and on clarifying what matters most to you.

Start the conversation

1. Orientation

First session is about listening and mapping the immediate concerns. We identify one or two practical aims to prioritize.

2. Small experiments

Between sessions we'll try short, measurable steps — a behavior to try, a thought experiment, or a communication strategy. We review what works and refine it.

3. Building routine

If helpful, we design small routines to sustain change: brief practices, check-ins, or planning anchors that fit your schedule.

4. Transition and maintenance

We plan how to keep gains alive and when to pause therapy or move to less frequent check-ins.

Session logistics

Typical length: 45–55 minutes. Remote and limited in-person availability. Fees and scheduling are handled on the booking page.

Confidentiality & scope

Conversations are confidential within legal limits. I cannot provide emergency care; if you are in immediate danger, contact local emergency services.

How I describe progress

Progress is tracked by what you feel able to do differently and by how consistently small changes fit into daily life. We also revisit goals and adjust the plan when needed.

Limits and referral

If a problem is outside my scope (e.g., urgent safety, severe substance withdrawal, complex medical overlap), I will suggest appropriate referrals and support the transition.
