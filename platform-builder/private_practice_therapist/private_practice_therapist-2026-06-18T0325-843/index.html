Frequently Asked Questions

What makes Private Practice Therapy at suitable for individuals aged 60+?
Our approach is specifically designed with the nuances of mature bodies in mind. We prioritize gentle, non-strenuous techniques that focus on restoring natural movement patterns and enhancing body awareness, rather than high-impact exercises. Your comfort and safety are paramount in every personalized session.
How does Private Practice Therapy address specific postural concerns common in seniors?
We carefully assess your individual posture and movement habits to identify areas of imbalance or strain. Our Private Practice Therapy then utilizes refined methods to strengthen supporting muscles, improve joint mobility, and encourage optimal spinal alignment, directly addressing issues like rounded shoulders, lower back discomfort, and balance challenges.
Is previous experience with body awareness practices required for Private Practice Therapy?
Absolutely not. Our Private Practice Therapy is accessible to everyone, regardless of prior experience. We begin by establishing a foundational understanding of your body and gradually introduce techniques that build your awareness and improve your postural health at a comfortable pace. You'll be guided every step of the way.
What can I expect from my first Private Practice Therapy session regarding postural health?
Your initial Private Practice Therapy session will involve a comprehensive discussion about your health history, daily routines, and any specific postural concerns you may have. We'll then conduct a gentle assessment of your movement and alignment. This allows us to create a bespoke plan that precisely targets your needs and sets the foundation for your improved comfort and poise.
