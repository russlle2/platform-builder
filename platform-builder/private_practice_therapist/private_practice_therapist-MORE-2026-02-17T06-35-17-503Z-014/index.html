Who these intensives fit for

  • One concern, clear focus

    If a single issue — a conversation, a decision, or recurring pattern — is taking up space, short intensives help clarify next steps.

  • Time-limited support

    Practical, actionable work over a few sessions rather than long-term weekly scheduling. We plan forward between meetings.

  • Blended formats

    Video sessions with follow-up notes and exercises tailored to your day-to-day life.

How an intensive usually unfolds

  1. Brief intake. We clarify what matters now and what a successful short course would look like.
  2. Focused sessions. We work with a tight question and practical experiments to try between meetings.
  3. Review & sustain. We assess what landed and plan small, durable steps forward.
