Where I typically help

People come with tangled demands — responsibilities, expectations, and internal pressure. These are common threads I help untangle in a focused, short-term way when possible.

Transitions & Reorientation

Career shifts, relationship changes, or moving to a new life chapter. We clarify priorities and test manageable next steps.

Decision Anxiety

When choices feel paralyzing, we narrow options, probe values, and design experiments to reduce uncertainty.

Grief & Ambivalence

Not all grief looks the same. We create space to name loss, tolerate uncomfortable feelings, and find rhythms for daily life.

Performance & Self-criticism

Skills for managing pressure, self-talk, and realistic goal-setting without losing well-being in the process.

Short, Focused Intensives

A condensed block of sessions aimed at clarifying a single question or decision. Blocks are co-designed and time-limited.

How I frame work

I begin with a short assessment and a shared plan. We pick concrete actions between sessions and review progress. If longer-term support is helpful, we discuss that together.
