About the practice

I bring clinical training, curiosity, and practical tools. My work is collaborative: we identify what feels most useful now, set a clear focus, and try targeted interventions that can be reviewed and adjusted. You’ll get a plan, not a script.

What I specialize in

  • Transition and life reorientation (career, relationships, relocations)
  • Anxiety that shows up around decisions and performance
  • Grief, ambivalence, and processing endings
  • Short-term intensives for focused clarity
