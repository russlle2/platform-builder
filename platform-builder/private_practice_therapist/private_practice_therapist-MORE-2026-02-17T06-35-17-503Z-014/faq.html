Frequently Asked Questions

Practical answers for how we work together and what to expect.

What happens in a first session?

We create a brief map: concerns that brought you here, what you hope to change, and some immediate steps you might try. We also cover confidentiality, logistics, and whether a short series or an intensive makes sense.

How long are sessions and how do payments work?

Standard sessions are 50–55 minutes unless otherwise arranged. Payment is collected at booking or at the time of service depending on the option chosen. For packaged work or intensives, full details are provided before scheduling.

Do you accept insurance?

I operate on a private-pay basis. I can provide a simple receipt (superbill) if you wish to submit for out-of-network reimbursement. Coverage and reimbursement depend on your plan.

Can I schedule an intensive instead of weekly sessions?

Yes — intensive formats are offered for focused work when you want a concentrated timeframe. They include intake and a summary with suggested next steps. We discuss suitability before booking.

What if I'm in crisis?

I am not an emergency service. If you are in immediate danger or need urgent help, please call your local emergency number. You may also contact crisis resources in {{CITY}}, {{STATE}} for immediate assistance.

How is confidentiality handled?

Confidentiality is foundational. Exceptions are limited to legal requirements such as imminent harm to self or others, or court orders. These limits are reviewed during intake so there are no surprises.
