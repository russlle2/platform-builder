How I work

My approach combines evidence-informed techniques with pragmatic, lived-experience focus. I tailor the cadence and tools to what you need in the moment.

1. Orient

A short assessment to name priorities and practical constraints. We agree on a measurable focus.

2. Plan

We co-create a time-limited plan — number of sessions, homework experiments, and ways to track progress.

3. Iterate

Review what’s working, adjust tools, and decide whether to extend, pause, or close the pathway.

Therapeutic stance

I practice with curiosity, respect, and a focus on tangible change. Therapy here is collaborative: I bring structure and clinical reasoning; you bring expertise about your life. Together we test small experiments.
