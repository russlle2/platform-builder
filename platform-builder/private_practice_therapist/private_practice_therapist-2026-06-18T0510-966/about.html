Your Work Rhythm Changes With the Seasons

How is seasonal wellness different from regular therapy?

We focus on *predictable* energy shifts tied to seasons—like why you feel sluggish in March after January deadlines—instead of processing past events. It’s about planning for what’s coming, not reliving what’s passed.

Do I need wellness experience to start?

Zero. We’ll explain concepts like 'seasonal energy mapping' with examples from your workday. Like connecting your 3 PM slump to summer heat—no prior knowledge needed.

How often would we meet?

Typically 1–2 sessions before key seasonal transitions (e.g., after tax season in April). Then we check in quarterly. Think of it like scheduling your annual physical for your energy.

What's the first step?

We’ll map your current work rhythm—when you feel energized vs. drained—over 3 weeks. Then we co-create your seasonal prevention plan. No forms, just real talk about your inbox and your coffee breaks.
