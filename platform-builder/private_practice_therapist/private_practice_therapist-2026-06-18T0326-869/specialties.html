From Daily Hunch to Empowered Posture: Your Body as a Stress Buffer

As a parent, your body often bears the brunt of constant lifting, carrying, and bending. This section of our Private Practice Therapist support focuses on identifying and releasing the physical tension that accumulates, contributing to your overall stress. We’ll explore how improving your body's alignment isn’t just about comfort, but about creating a physical resilience that helps you navigate chaotic moments with more ease and less strain on your nervous system. You'll learn practical, gentle movements to integrate into your busy schedule.
