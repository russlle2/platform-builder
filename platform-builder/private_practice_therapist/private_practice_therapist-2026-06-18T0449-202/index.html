Frequently Asked Questions

How is Private Practice Therapy different for new mothers?
Our Private Practice Therapy is specifically tailored to your postpartum experience. We focus on the unique emotional challenges of new motherhood, including sleep deprivation, identity shifts, relationship changes, and processing the birth experience. This isn't generic therapy; it's care designed to support you as you navigate this profound life transition.
I feel overwhelmed and guilty. Can Private Practice Therapy truly help me?
Absolutely. Those feelings are incredibly common for new mothers, and you are not alone in experiencing them. Our Private Practice Therapy provides a supportive, non-judgmental space for you to unpack these emotions, understand their roots, and develop strategies to manage them. We believe in your capacity for healing and finding balance.
What if I'm worried about discussing difficult birth experiences in Private Practice Therapy?
Your comfort and safety are paramount. Our Private Practice Therapy approach is trauma-informed, meaning we move at your pace and ensure you feel secure when discussing sensitive topics. We can help you process difficult birth experiences gently, without re-traumatization, helping you integrate these memories in a way that promotes healing, not further distress.
Is Private Practice Therapy going to take up too much of my already limited time?
We understand that your time is precious as a new mother. Private Practice Therapy sessions are scheduled to fit into your life as best as possible. The time you invest in your emotional well-being is incredibly valuable, benefiting not just you, but your baby and family. Even short, consistent sessions can make a significant positive impact on your postpartum recovery.
