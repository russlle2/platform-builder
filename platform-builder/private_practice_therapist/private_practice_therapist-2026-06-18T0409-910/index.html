Frequently Asked Questions

How does seasonal wellness actually help with my academic schedule?
We align your self-care with the academic calendar. For example, in the week before midterms, we focus on grounding rituals to prevent anxiety spikes. In the spring, we might adjust for longer days and more social events. It's about working *with* your season, not against it.
I’m busy with classes and exams—how can I fit therapy in?
We offer flexible scheduling around your academic demands. Most students find that a 30-minute session once a month actually saves time by preventing burnout. Plus, we offer virtual sessions so you can connect from your dorm or library.
What if I’m not into ‘spiritual’ stuff?
Our approach is practical, not preachy. We use ancient traditions as tools, not beliefs. You’ll get concrete strategies like mindful breathing techniques for test anxiety or time-blocking that honors your energy levels—no chanting required.
How is this different from just seeing a campus counselor?
Campus counselors often focus on immediate crisis care. We specialize in preventive, seasonal support tailored to your academic cycle. You’ll build long-term resilience, not just cope with today’s stress. And we’re a private practice, so your sessions are entirely confidential.
