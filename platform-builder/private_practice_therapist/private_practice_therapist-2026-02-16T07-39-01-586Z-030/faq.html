Questions people usually ask

Below are practical answers to help you decide if working together might be a fit. If your question isn’t listed, please reach out.

How long is a session and what happens in the first appointment?

Standard sessions are 50 minutes. The first session focuses on your immediate concerns, history relevant to current difficulties, and collaborative goal-setting.

How do you protect privacy and records?

All records are stored securely. Conversations are confidential within legal and ethical limits. I will discuss limits to confidentiality at intake (e.g., risk of harm, court orders).

Can I use insurance?

I am a private-pay clinician. I can provide a superbill you may submit to insurance; reimbursement depends on your plan. I can help you with that paperwork when requested.

What kinds of therapy do you use?

I draw from modalities such as cognitive-behavioral approaches, trauma-informed care, and relational therapy to tailor work to your needs ({{MODALITIES}}).

What if I'm in a crisis between sessions?

I am not available for emergencies. If you are at immediate risk, please call emergency services or the crisis hotline. We will plan safety steps in treatment when needed.

Still have a question? Reach out

I am licensed ({{LICENSE}}) and adhere to professional ethical standards. Therapy supports change but does not guarantee specific outcomes.

A short note on scope & limits

I offer psychotherapy for mood, anxiety, relationship concerns, trauma-informed coping, and life transitions. I do not provide forensic evaluations, prescribe medication, or offer emergency services. If specialized care is needed, I will support referrals.
