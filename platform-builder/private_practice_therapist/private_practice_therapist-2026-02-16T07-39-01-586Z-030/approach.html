Therapeutic frame and boundaries

Sessions typically last 50 minutes. Confidentiality is maintained within legal and ethical limits; there are exceptions related to safety and required reporting. I am not an emergency service. If you are at immediate risk, call local emergency services or crisis lines.

What I won’t do

  • Provide medical diagnoses or emergency intervention
  • Guarantee specific outcomes—therapy is collaborative

Evidence & orientation

I draw from cognitive-behavioral techniques, attachment-informed perspectives, and somatic awareness practices. My focus is to adapt evidence-informed methods to your unique situation and values.

Programs I offer

  • Short Skillset Path — 6 sessions focused on stabilization and core skills.
  • Exploration Track — 12 sessions for deeper pattern work and experiments.
  • Ongoing Support — Weekly or biweekly sessions for long-term goals.

Pricing is individualized; we discuss fit and resources during the consult. Sliding scale options may be available for those who qualify.

Questions people often have

How long until I feel better?

Progress varies. Many people notice practical improvements in weeks; deeper pattern change often takes months. We'll set realistic goals together.

Do you work with couples?

I primarily work with individuals, though I offer referrals for couple-focused therapy when appropriate.

Is therapy confidential?

Yes—within legal limits. We review these boundaries at intake.
