Navigating the Invisible Load: Emotional Healing for Athletes and Active Individuals with a Private Practice Therapist

Your dedication to physical excellence often means you’re adept at pushing through pain. But what about the emotional injuries that don’t show up on an X-ray? As a Private Practice Therapist, I specialize in recognizing how past experiences and current emotional stress manifest as physical tension, performance anxiety, or prolonged recovery. We'll work together to identify these patterns, allowing you to process and release them, so you can train and live with greater freedom and resilience.
