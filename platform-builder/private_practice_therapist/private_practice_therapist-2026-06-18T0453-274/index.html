Trauma-Informed Care: A Private Practice Therapist's Collaborative Approach to Your Well-being

My approach as a Private Practice Therapist is grounded in trauma-informed care, meaning I understand that your body's responses to stress, injury, or competition can be deeply connected to past experiences. We'll build a safe space where your experiences are validated, and together, we'll develop strategies to process complex emotions. This isn't about blaming; it's about understanding the roots of your reactions, so you can build new, healthier responses, both on and off the field.

Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Private Practice Therapist help me manage physical stress if my issues are emotional?
As a Private Practice Therapist, I understand that the mind and body are deeply connected. Emotional stress, unresolved trauma, or performance pressure can manifest physically as chronic pain, fatigue, or heightened injury risk. We will explore these connections, addressing the emotional roots to help you achieve better physical regulation, faster recovery, and more effective stress management.
I'm an athlete; will a Private Practice Therapist understand the demands of my lifestyle?
Absolutely. As your Private Practice Therapist, I recognize the unique pressures, discipline, and potential for injury and burnout inherent in an athletic lifestyle. My approach is tailored to acknowledge these specific demands, integrating your athletic identity into our work while focusing on emotional resilience and recovery strategies that complement your training.
What does 'trauma-informed care' mean for me as an active person seeing a Private Practice Therapist?
For you, 'trauma-informed care' with a Private Practice Therapist means we acknowledge that your body's responses to physical stress, injury, or competitive situations might be influenced by past emotional experiences. We create a safe, respectful environment where we address these connections, helping you understand and process emotions without re-traumatizing you, ultimately building your capacity to cope and thrive.
I feel stuck after an injury, not just physically. Can a Private Practice Therapist help with that?
Yes, absolutely. As a Private Practice Therapist, I often work with active individuals struggling with the emotional aftermath of injuries. Beyond the physical recovery, there can be significant grief, frustration, identity shifts, or fear of re-injury. We can process these complex feelings, develop coping mechanisms, and help you mentally prepare for your return to activity, empowering your complete recovery.
