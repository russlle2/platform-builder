Understanding Your Unique Emotional Landscape as a Senior

As a Private Practice Therapist, I recognize that emotional needs shift with age. You might be navigating grief, changes in health, family dynamics, or simply seeking a deeper sense of contentment. My approach is rooted in understanding these unique experiences, offering a safe space to process and build resilience. We focus on gentle, evidence-informed strategies to support your emotional health, helping you find stability and peace amidst life's transitions.
