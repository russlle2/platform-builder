Private Practice Therapist

Finding Your Anchor: A Grounded Approach in Private Practice Therapy

Our approach in Private Practice Therapy is akin to tending a garden – patient, observant, and deeply respectful of your unique growth. We integrate somatic practices and mindfulness, rooted in the understanding that your body holds wisdom. Together, we'll explore how academic stress impacts your nervous system and develop personalized strategies to bring it back into balance. This isn't about escaping your reality, but about building an inner sanctuary where you can return for strength and clarity, even in the busiest of semesters.
