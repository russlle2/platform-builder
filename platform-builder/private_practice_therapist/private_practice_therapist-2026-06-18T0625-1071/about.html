How can Private Practice Therapy help my nervous system after a long illness or injury?

After chronic illness or injury, your nervous system can become overactive, leading to persistent stress and fatigue. Our Private Practice Therapy offers gentle techniques to downregulate this activity, helping your body and mind find a more peaceful, regulated state. We focus on practices that soothe rather than push, honoring your body’s current capacity.

I'm new to therapy; will I feel overwhelmed with a Private Practice Therapist?

Absolutely not. Our approach in Private Practice Therapy is designed to be gentle and accessible, especially for those without prior wellness experience. We meet you exactly where you are, creating a supportive and understanding space where you can explore your feelings and experiences without pressure or judgment.

Is Private Practice Therapy only for emotional stress, or can it help with my physical recovery from chronic illness or injury?

While our focus is on stress reduction and nervous system regulation, these elements profoundly impact physical well-being. By calming your nervous system in Private Practice Therapy, you can support your body's natural healing processes, reduce perceived pain, and improve your overall capacity to cope with the demands of recovery from chronic illness or injury.

What if I feel too tired or sensitive to engage in Private Practice Therapy right now?

We understand that chronic illness and injury can leave you feeling depleted. Our Private Practice Therapy sessions are always tailored to your energy levels and sensitivities. There’s no expectation for you to push yourself. We can explore gentle, restorative practices that support you exactly as you are, even on your most sensitive days.
