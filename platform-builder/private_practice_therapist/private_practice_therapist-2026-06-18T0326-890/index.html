Frequently Asked Questions

How does Private Practice Therapy focusing on postural health differ from traditional talk therapy?
While traditional therapy often focuses on verbal processing, our approach integrates your physical experience. We explore how your body holds stress, tension, and emotions related to your life transitions, using this awareness as a powerful tool alongside verbal exploration. It's about a fuller understanding of your experience.
I'm not particularly 'flexible' or 'athletic'. Is this approach to body awareness in Private Practice Therapy still suitable for me?
Absolutely. This approach isn't about physical prowess, but about developing a deeper internal connection to your body. We meet you where you are, using gentle, accessible techniques. The focus is on your personal comfort and understanding, not on achieving specific physical feats.
How can improving my posture actually help me navigate a significant life change?
When you refine your postural habits, you're not just changing how you hold your body; you're often shifting how you hold yourself in the world. Improved posture can reduce physical discomfort, increase your sense of grounding, and even boost your confidence, providing a stable foundation as you adapt to new circumstances.
What can I expect in a typical Private Practice Therapy session focused on body awareness?
A typical session at involves a blend of conversation and gentle, guided attention to your physical sensations and posture. We might incorporate simple breathing exercises or mindful movements, tailored to your comfort and needs. The goal is to help you connect the dots between your mental and physical experiences during your transition.
