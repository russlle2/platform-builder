Finding Stability in Movement: Private Practice Therapy for Major Shifts

Major life transitions, whether career changes, relocations, or relationship shifts, can throw your entire system off balance. We explore how these shifts manifest physically, from subtle tension to changes in your posture. Understanding your body's responses offers a unique entry point for processing and adapting to new circumstances.
