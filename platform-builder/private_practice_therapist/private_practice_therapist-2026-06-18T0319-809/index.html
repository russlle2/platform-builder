Frequently Asked Questions

How does Private Practice Therapy specifically help with athletic performance and physical stress?
Our Private Practice Therapy focuses on identifying and refining your body's movement patterns and postural habits. By enhancing your body awareness, you can reduce inefficient movements that lead to stress and injury, ultimately improving your athletic performance and your body's resilience during demanding activities.
Is this Private Practice Therapy suitable if I'm not a professional athlete but stay very active?
Absolutely. Whether you're a professional athlete, an avid weekend warrior, or simply someone who maintains a very active lifestyle, our Private Practice Therapy is designed for anyone managing the physical demands and stresses that come with consistent activity. Your active life deserves specific, informed support.
What can I expect in terms of practical strategies for improving my posture and body awareness?
In Private Practice Therapy, you can expect practical, actionable strategies tailored to your specific needs. This might include guided awareness exercises, detailed feedback on your movement, and clear explanations of how your body mechanics impact your daily activities and athletic pursuits. Our goal is to empower you with tools you can use immediately.
How long does it typically take to see benefits from Private Practice Therapy focused on postural health?
The timeline for seeing benefits from Private Practice Therapy varies for each individual, depending on your specific goals and the nature of your physical stress. However, many active individuals report an improved sense of body awareness and reduced discomfort within a few sessions, as we work collaboratively to implement targeted adjustments to your movement and posture.
