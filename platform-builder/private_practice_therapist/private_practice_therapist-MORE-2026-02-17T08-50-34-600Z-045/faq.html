Confidentiality & safety

Conversations are private within legal limits. If you are in immediate danger or a life-threatening crisis, please call local emergency services or a crisis hotline. I am not an emergency service.

Scope: I provide outpatient psychotherapy and clinical consultation. If your needs require urgent medical or psychiatric care, I will help you find appropriate support.
