Areas of focus

I work with adults who are navigating sustained stress, relationship shifts, transitions (work, family, identity), and mood or worry that interrupts daily life. Below are common pathways clients ask for; each is shaped into tailored work rather than a fixed program.

Quick planner

Pick a focus and sketch a short plan.

Regulation & mood

Work here centers on routines, small behavioral experiments, and noticing patterns that sustain low mood. We prioritize manageable steps and ways to re-engage with what matters to you.

Anxiety & persistent worry

Practical tools, attention to avoidance, and gradual exposure to difficult situations help reduce the hold of worry. The pace is collaborative—experiments are tailored to your tolerance.

Relationships & communication

We explore patterns of giving and receiving, boundaries, and clear ways to test new approaches. Therapy focuses on what you can change and how to do so without abrupt risk.

Transitions & identity

When life shifts—career, family, or identity—the work is about orienting, defining priorities, and settling into new rhythms with practical supports.

Short-form offerings

Named packages: Anchor Series (4 sessions), Deep Check (single extended session), Rhythm Care (monthly maintenance). Pricing is discussed transparently; I offer a limited number of sliding-scale slots.

Coordination & referrals

I collaborate with other professionals when helpful and will discuss coordination when requested. I do not provide emergency or forensic services.
