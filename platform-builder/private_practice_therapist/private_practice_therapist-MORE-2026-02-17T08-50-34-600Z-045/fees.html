How pricing is framed

I present options rather than packages so we can match the work to your priorities. If cost is a barrier, please mention it when you reach out so we can discuss options.

Confidentiality note: Sessions are confidential within legal limits. I will explain boundaries of confidentiality and record-keeping when we meet. Scope and safety: I do not provide crisis services. If you are in immediate danger or need urgent help, call local emergency services or a crisis line. If you have questions about whether therapy is right for you, we can explore that together.
