How the work unfolds

A relational, skills-oriented practice: I combine collaborative problem solving with short, practical experiments. Your priorities shape the plan. We check in often and adjust what isn’t useful.

Collaborative Practical Boundaried

Framework

  1. Clarify: We name one or two concrete goals and what success looks like in small, observable terms.
  2. Experiment: We try short behavioral or cognitive experiments between sessions and look at results with curiosity.
  3. Refine: We keep what helps, adjust what doesn’t, and build skills into daily life so gains sustain.

Session rhythm

Most work starts weekly or biweekly. Some clients choose a short series (4–6 sessions) focused on a specific aim; others prefer ongoing rhythm. We discuss timing during a consult.

Boundaries & safety

I will discuss limits of confidentiality, expectations for session attendance, and procedures for cancellations. If safety concerns arise, we create a clear, immediate plan and connect with appropriate resources.

Who benefits

Adults motivated to try small, measurable changes and to reflect on how patterns operate in daily life. This work is not an emergency service and does not replace medical care when required.

What to bring

A concise list: a short description of the concern, when it began, what has helped or not, and any practical constraints. If you use medication or another clinician, bring that context.
