How I work

My approach favors clear steps, shared planning, and practical tools you can use between sessions. We notice patterns, test small changes, and keep the aim readable: what would feel different in daily life?

Confidentiality & scope

Notes are part of a confidential clinical record. Therapy is not emergency care. If you are in crisis or concerned for safety, please contact local emergency services or the crisis resources in your area.

Boundaries

I do not provide forensic or legal assessments in routine sessions. For questions about records or limits of confidentiality, we will review these directly in your intake.

Practice framework

A simple scaffold we return to: Clarify — Try — Reflect. It keeps work anchored, measurable, and adaptable.

Clarify

Find a clear question to focus on. We translate a broad concern into a practical aim for sessions.

Try

Experiment with small behavioral or relational steps between sessions, then gather what changed.

Reflect

Make sense of results and revise the plan. Therapy stays collaborative and revisable.
