About the practice

Clinical practice rooted in clear boundaries, steady attention, and small changes that add up. I work with adults navigating transitions, sustained low mood, anxiety, and relationships. Sessions are practical, reflective, and anchored in your life.

My orientation

I draw from evidence-informed approaches—brief cognitive techniques, relational work, and skill-building. The work is collaborative: we name priorities, try small experiments, and refine what’s useful. I bring clinical training, clear confidentiality practices, and a focus on functional changes you can notice week to week.

Think of our work as building a clearer path through a familiar landscape—the map changes as we walk.

About me

I am a licensed clinician offering individual therapy via secure telehealth and select in-person hours. I prioritize safety, informed consent, and transparent boundaries about scope of care. I do not provide emergency services; if you are in crisis, call local emergency services or a crisis line.

Confidentiality & scope

What you share is protected within the limits of the law. I will outline limits to confidentiality, informed consent, and coordination with other providers as needed. This practice does not provide legal or medical emergency services.
