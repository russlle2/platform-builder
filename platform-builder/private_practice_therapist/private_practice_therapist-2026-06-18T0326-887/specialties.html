The Entrepreneur's Posture: Addressing Stress in Your Body

The demands of high-achievement often manifest as physical tension. Learn how sustained stress impacts your posture, creating a cycle of discomfort and mental fog. We'll explore the subtle ways your body holds pressure, from rounded shoulders to a clenched jaw, and how these physical patterns directly influence your ability to concentrate and stay calm under pressure. Understanding these connections is the first step toward lasting relief and enhanced performance.
