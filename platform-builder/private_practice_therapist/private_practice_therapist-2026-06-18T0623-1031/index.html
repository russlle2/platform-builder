Frequently Asked Questions

How can Private Practice Therapy specifically help with chronic illness recovery?
Our Private Practice Therapy is tailored to acknowledge the unique stressors of chronic illness or injury. We focus on techniques that help down-regulate an overactive nervous system, reducing the physiological impact of chronic stress and fostering a sense of internal safety. This supports your body's innate capacity for repair and adaptation, offering a pathway to greater peace amidst your recovery.
Is Private Practice Therapy suitable if my energy levels are low due to my condition?
Absolutely. Our Private Practice Therapy sessions are designed to accommodate your energy levels. We prioritize gentle, restorative approaches that won't deplete you further. The goal is to create a nurturing environment where even small, consistent efforts can yield significant improvements in stress reduction and nervous system regulation, honoring your body's current capacity.
Will Private Practice Therapy clash with my medical treatments?
Our Private Practice Therapy complements medical treatments by focusing on your emotional well-being and nervous system health. We do not offer medical advice or replace your primary care. Instead, we work collaboratively to manage the psychological aspects of chronic illness or injury, such as anxiety, fatigue, and pain-related stress, enhancing your overall resilience alongside your medical care.
What if I feel overwhelmed by the thought of discussing my chronic condition in Private Practice Therapy?
It's completely understandable to feel overwhelmed. In Private Practice Therapy, you are in control of what you share and when. We begin gently, focusing on building a foundation of trust and safety. Our initial goal is to help you find moments of calm and regulation, creating a supportive container where you can gradually explore your experiences at your own pace, without pressure.
