Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Private Practice Therapist help with my physical pain when I’m so busy?
As a high-achiever, your time is incredibly valuable. We focus on efficient, body-centered techniques that directly address the physical impact of stress and overwork. Our sessions are designed to provide tangible relief and equip you with quick, effective strategies you can use between appointments, fitting seamlessly into your demanding schedule.
I've tried massages and chiropractic care; how is this different?
While those are valuable, my approach as a Private Practice Therapist delves deeper. We explore the underlying nervous system patterns and emotional stressors contributing to your physical discomfort. It’s not just about treating symptoms, but understanding the root cause, giving you a more profound and lasting sense of relief than temporary fixes.
Will somatic healing make me feel more overwhelmed by my emotions?
Absolutely not. My primary goal is your comfort and safety. We move at your pace, using gentle techniques that help you process physical sensations and emotions without feeling overwhelmed. You'll learn to build a stronger connection to your body's wisdom, which actually reduces feelings of overwhelm and enhances your capacity to cope.
I feel like I should just 'push through' my discomfort. Why should I invest in this?
Pushing through is often what got you to where you are, but it comes at a cost to your physical well-being and long-term performance. Investing in somatic healing is investing in your sustained success. By addressing physical pain and stress proactively, you're not just finding relief, you’re enhancing your clarity, focus, and resilience, allowing you to achieve even more, without the internal burden.
