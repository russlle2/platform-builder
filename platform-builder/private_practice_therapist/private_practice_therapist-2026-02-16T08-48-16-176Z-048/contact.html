Three small practices you can try this week

1
Label & Pause
When a strong emotion arises, silently name it for 5–10 seconds before reacting. Naming reduces reactivity and opens choice.
2
Two-minute grounding
Take two minutes to notice breath and the points of contact your body makes with a chair or the floor. Return to tasks with more clarity.
3
Curiosity journal
Write one line about something that surprised you emotionally each evening. Small observations build insight over time.
