Practical questions, answered plainly

Below are common questions I hear from prospective clients. If you don't see what you need, please reach out—I'll respond within 48 hours.

How do I know if short-term work is enough?
We begin with a focused intake to clarify goals. Short-term work is well-suited to targeted skill-building or coping with a specific stressor; longer care supports deeper patterns and trauma processing.
Do you take insurance?
I do not bill insurance directly in most cases. I provide documents (superbills) you can submit for possible reimbursement. We can discuss options during a consult.
What if I'm in crisis?
If you are in immediate danger or at risk of harming yourself or others, call emergency services or your local crisis line. Therapy sessions are not emergency services.
How do cancellations work?
Please notify me 48 hours before your appointment to avoid a cancellation fee. Late cancellations and no-shows may incur the full session rate.
Are telehealth sessions available?
Yes. I offer secure video sessions for clients in {{STATE}}. Some clients prefer a mix of in-person and telehealth to fit their schedule.
How long before I notice change?
People notice different shifts at different times. Many clients experience practical relief from stressors within a few sessions; deeper pattern change often takes more consistent work. We review progress regularly.
What are your specialties?
I work with anxiety, trauma-informed care, relationship transitions, burnout, and life-stage shifts. I draw from trauma-aware psychotherapies and mindfulness-based approaches. I do not provide medication management.
