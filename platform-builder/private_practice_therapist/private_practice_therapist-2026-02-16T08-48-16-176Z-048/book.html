What happens after you request?

  1. We schedule a brief consult to confirm fit and availability.
  2. If you choose to proceed, we'll set your initial session and share intake forms.
  3. We review goals in session and set a recommended rhythm for follow-up.

Intake snapshot

To make the most of our first session, please be prepared to share:

  • Current concerns and what you hope will change
  • Relevant mental health history
  • Any safety concerns

You'll receive secure intake forms and a consent packet after scheduling.

Small habits I invite clients to try

  • Daily 60-second breathing pause
  • Weekly reflection: one thing that felt different
  • Micro-boundary: 10 minutes of protected time each day

Notes on care & limits

Therapy provided is confidential within legal and ethical boundaries. I do not provide crisis response. If you're in immediate danger, call emergency services.
