Introductory diagnostic — a calm beginning

Our first step is an intentional diagnostic conversation. This is a 45–60 minute intake to understand where you are, what matters to you now, and how therapy can help. I listen for patterns of emotion, safety, capacity, and relationship dynamics rather than offering quick fixes.

Outcomes of this intake include a shared problem statement and a recommended path — whether short-term, open-ended, or cohort-based learning.

Care pathway: Foundations Cohort (example)

The Foundations Cohort is a focused, time-limited program for people navigating transitions or persistent anxiety. It blends clinical tools with contemplative practices and peer learning. Cohorts typically span 8 weeks with small group sessions plus individual check-ins.

  • Week 1: Orientation & safety planning
  • Week 3: Understanding patterns and triggers
  • Week 6: Building relational practices
  • Aftercare: consolidation and next steps

Micro-habits you can begin today

  • Two-minute grounding: notice breath for 2 minutes at the start of the day
  • Weekly check-in: one question to self — "What did I need this week?"
  • Boundary rehearsal: practice saying a short, clear boundary in a low-stakes context
