Areas of focus

I work with adults facing life transitions, persistent worry, relationship strain, and the aftermath of traumatic experiences. My approach is clinical, relational, and informed by contemplative practices.

Not a substitute for emergency services. If you are in immediate danger, call emergency services now.

Diagnostic & triage

Every new client begins with a structured diagnostic intake. This clarifies priorities, risk, and realistic goals. From there we choose whether individual sessions, brief focused work, or a cohort path best fits.

Specialties

Anxiety & excessive worry

Practical tools to manage physiological symptoms and cognitive patterns. We build tolerable exposure and habit-shaping strategies tailored to daily life.

Trauma-informed care

Stabilization first: safety, grounding, and increasing capacity. Therapy focuses on integration and increased agency rather than retraumatization.

Relationship patterns

Work on communication, boundaries, and repair strategies—useful for couples and individuals exploring relational patterns.

Life transitions & career shift

Support for identity work, decision-making, and the emotional complexity of major life changes.

Burnout & stress recovery

Assessing workload, values-aligned boundaries, restorative routines, and steps toward sustainable pacing.

Grief and meaning work

Holding space for loss while exploring values and practices that scaffold ongoing life and connection.

Program option: Resilience Pathway

The Resilience Pathway is a small cohort designed for people who want both clinical attention and peer learning. It mixes weekly group sessions, two individual sessions, and daily micro-practices for 10 weeks.

Includes: diagnostic intake, 8 group modules, 2 individual coaching sessions, and a private cohort portal.

Micro-habits and small changes

Micro-habits are intentionally small, evidence-aligned practices that are sustainable. My recommended suite includes:

  • 1–2 minute grounding morning routine
  • Evening inventory: three observations (gratitude, difficulty, learning)
  • Boundary check: one ask or decline per week

Pricing orientation

I offer session-based work and program pricing. Cohorts are priced as bundles with a clearer end date; individual therapy is billed per session. Sliding scale is available for limited slots.
