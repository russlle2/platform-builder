How can a Private Practice Therapist help me with postpartum sleep when I'm so exhausted?

Even small, consistent adjustments can significantly impact your sleep quality. As your Private Practice Therapist, I'll help you identify subtle barriers to rest, create realistic routines that support sleep within your new schedule, and offer techniques to calm your mind even when full nights are elusive. We focus on building restorative moments throughout your day and night.

Is this just about sleep training my baby, or is it for me?

This support from a Private Practice Therapist is primarily for *your* sleep and well-being during postpartum recovery. While we can discuss how your baby's sleep impacts yours, the focus is on your nervous system regulation, mental calm, and personal strategies to maximize the rest you do get, promoting your overall healing and resilience as a new mother.

What if I feel guilty asking for help with my own sleep while caring for my baby?

It's completely natural to feel that way, but prioritizing your own rest is one of the most important things you can do for your baby and yourself. As your Private Practice Therapist, I provide a judgment-free space to acknowledge these feelings. Caring for yourself is not selfish; it's essential for your capacity to care for your family.

What kind of techniques does your Private Practice Therapist practice use to improve sleep?

My Private Practice Therapist approach integrates gentle cognitive restructuring to quiet racing thoughts, guided relaxation and breathwork to soothe your nervous system, and practical environmental adjustments. We also explore mindful movement and self-compassion practices, all designed to help your body and mind prepare for and embrace restorative rest.
