How soon can I get an appointment?

Typical wait varies; first-time appointments are often within 2–4 weeks. If you need something sooner, note that in your message to {{EMAIL}} and I will do my best to accommodate.

What should I expect in the first session?

We review concerns, gather relevant history, and identify 1–2 practical steps. The first meeting is collaborative and helps us decide on next steps or a planning package.

Do you offer remote sessions?

Yes. I provide secure telehealth for clients in {{STATE}} (and sometimes neighboring states depending on licensure). We’ll confirm eligibility when you book.

Do you accept insurance or offer sliding scale?

I do not directly bill insurance. I provide a super-bill you can submit. Sliding scale openings are limited and prioritized for those with significant financial need.

How do you handle cancellation?

Appointments canceled with less than 48 hours' notice may incur a fee. If an emergency arises, we discuss options based on the circumstances.

What is your confidentiality policy?

Conversations are confidential except when disclosure is legally required (e.g., harm risk, subpoenas). I discuss limits of confidentiality at intake and in writing.

Additional topics

Working with trauma?

I use trauma-informed practices and collaborate on pacing and safety. Treatment never re-traumatizes and moves at your comfort level.

How do I know therapy is helping?

We set measurable goals and review them regularly. Progress can be steady, non-linear, or gradual; we attend to what is most meaningful to you.
