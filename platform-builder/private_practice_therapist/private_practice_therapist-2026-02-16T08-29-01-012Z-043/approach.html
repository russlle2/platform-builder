How sessions flow

  1. Check-in and symptom tracking (5–10 minutes).
  2. Work on a focused agenda item (30 minutes).
  3. Collaborative wrap: an experiment or homework and a clear goal for the next session (5–10 minutes).

Questions I get

"Will you tell me what to do?"

I offer recommendations and practical steps, but you always choose what fits within your life and values.

"How long until I see results?"

Many clients notice changes in daily coping within a few sessions; deeper patterns take time. We set review points to gauge progress.
