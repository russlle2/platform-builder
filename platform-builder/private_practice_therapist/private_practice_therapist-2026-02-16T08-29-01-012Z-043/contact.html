Common Hesitations — honest answers

"I don't know if therapy will help"

Therapy is a collaboration. Early sessions focus on concrete goals and practical steps; we check progress regularly and adapt the plan.

"I'm worried about confidentiality"

Your privacy is taken seriously. I will explain limits (harm, legal requirements) before we begin and document consent clearly.

"Can I afford this?"

I offer a variety of package structures, limited sliding scale slots, and brief consults to discuss fit before committing.

"What if I feel worse at first?"

It’s common for feelings to shift as we process material. We'll set safety plans and pace the work so you remain supported.
