When Academic Pressure Shows Up as Physical Pain

It's common for college students like you to feel anxiety and academic stress in your body. Maybe it's a constant knot in your shoulders before an exam, recurring headaches from late-night studying, or that persistent stomach ache when deadlines loom. As a Private Practice Therapist, I understand that these aren't just 'in your head' – they're real signals from your nervous system. We'll work together to understand these signals and teach your body how to respond differently, bringing you tangible relief.
