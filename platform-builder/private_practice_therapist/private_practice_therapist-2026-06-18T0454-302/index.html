Frequently Asked Questions

What kind of physical pain can Private Practice Therapy help with during postpartum recovery?
Our Private Practice Therapy specifically addresses common postpartum physical discomforts such as lower back pain from carrying or feeding, pelvic floor tension or soreness, neck and shoulder strain, and general bodily fatigue. We focus on gentle, somatic techniques to help your body find relief and restore balance.
Is this Private Practice Therapy only for physical symptoms, or does it cover emotional aspects too?
While our primary focus is on physical pain relief and somatic healing, we understand that physical discomfort is deeply intertwined with emotional well-being. In Private Practice Therapy, we create a space where you can also process the emotional impact of your physical experiences, helping you feel more integrated and supported as a new mother.
How is somatic healing different from traditional talk therapy for new mothers in Private Practice Therapy?
In Private Practice Therapy, somatic healing involves paying close attention to your body's sensations, movements, and responses, rather than just talking about your thoughts and feelings. We'll use gentle exercises, breathwork, and awareness practices to help you release physical tension and connect with your body's innate capacity for recovery, which can be incredibly helpful for postpartum mothers.
How many Private Practice Therapy sessions will I need to feel relief from my postpartum discomfort?
The number of Private Practice Therapy sessions needed varies for everyone, as every mother's postpartum recovery is unique. We'll work together to assess your specific needs and create a personalized plan. Our goal is to provide you with practical tools and support that help you feel noticeable relief and progress toward greater physical comfort and ease.
