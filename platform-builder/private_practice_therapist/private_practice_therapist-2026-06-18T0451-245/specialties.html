Private Practice Therapist

Releasing the Weight: Trauma-Informed Care for Academic Stress

College life can bring unexpected challenges, and sometimes past experiences, even subtle ones, can make current academic and social pressures feel overwhelming. Our Private Practice Therapist approach focuses on trauma-informed care, meaning we understand how difficult experiences can show up as anxiety, perfectionism, or burnout. We create a safe space for you to unpack these feelings without judgment, helping you build resilience and a stronger sense of self.
