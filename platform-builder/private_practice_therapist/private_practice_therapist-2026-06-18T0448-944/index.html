Frequently Asked Questions

Can you help when my child's in the middle of a meltdown?
Yes. Your therapist provides immediate, concrete strategies you can use *during* the moment—like a 30-second grounding technique you whisper while holding your child. We never say 'just breathe' when your toddler is screaming in the grocery aisle.
How is this different from regular therapy?
We don't discuss your childhood. We solve *your current season's* household stress with tools you implement tomorrow. No homework, no vague goals. Your first session ends with a 48-hour action plan: 'Add these 3 specific phrases to your morning routine during allergy season.'
What if I'm already overwhelmed with doctor appointments?
We coordinate directly with your pediatrician for seasonal health concerns. No extra scheduling. Your therapist sends them a concise, seasonal stress update: 'Family needs support managing nap disruptions during peak pollen season.'
How do you handle my partner's schedule?
Your seasonal plan includes a shared digital calendar with locked, private slots for both parents—no 'checking with your spouse' needed. We design the protocol around *your* family's actual rhythm, not a generic 'ideal.'
