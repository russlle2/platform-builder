Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is trauma-informed care in the context of postpartum recovery?
Trauma-informed care means that I recognize how past experiences or even the birth itself might be impacting your current emotional state. Our sessions prioritize your safety, choice, and control, ensuring you feel respected and understood. It's about providing support in a way that avoids re-traumatization and empowers you to heal gently, without needing to relive difficult moments unless you choose to.
I've never been to therapy before. Will I know what to say or do?
Absolutely. Many new mothers I work with are new to therapy, and that's perfectly okay. There's no right or wrong way to be in session. My role is to guide you, ask questions, and create a comfortable space where you can simply be yourself. We'll go at your pace, and you never have to share anything you're not ready for. Consider it a conversation with someone who genuinely cares about your well-being.
How can therapy help with the overwhelming feelings of new motherhood?
Therapy provides a dedicated, confidential space for you to unpack those overwhelming feelings without judgment. We can explore the roots of your anxiety, sadness, or frustration, develop coping strategies, and help you reconnect with your inner strength. It's about giving you tools to manage the emotional load and rediscover a sense of balance and joy in your new role as a mother.
Is it normal to feel sad or anxious after having a baby, even if everything seems fine?
Yes, it is completely normal and more common than many people realize. The postpartum period brings immense hormonal shifts, sleep deprivation, and significant life changes that can all contribute to feelings of sadness, anxiety, or irritability, even when you love your baby deeply. These feelings are valid, and exploring them in therapy can provide immense relief and clarity.
