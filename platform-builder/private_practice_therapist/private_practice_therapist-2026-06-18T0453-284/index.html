Frequently Asked Questions

Why should an athlete or active person consider a Private Practice Therapist for emotional healing?
Your dedication to physical activity often means you’re adept at pushing through pain, but emotional stress from injuries, performance pressure, or burnout can be isolating. A Private Practice Therapist understands these specific challenges and offers a safe, non-judgmental space to process these feelings, helping you find emotional balance without feeling like you have to 'tough it out' alone.
How does trauma-informed care from a Private Practice Therapist apply to physical stress?
Physical setbacks, intense training, or even the feeling of losing control over your body can sometimes be deeply impactful, even feeling traumatic. As your Private Practice Therapist, I approach our work with an understanding of how these experiences can affect your nervous system and emotional well-being, ensuring you feel safe, respected, and empowered throughout your healing process, without being re-traumatized.
I'm not sure if my emotional struggles are 'serious enough' for a Private Practice Therapist. What should I do?
If you're feeling overwhelmed, consistently low, or finding it hard to bounce back emotionally from physical demands, your feelings are absolutely valid and worth addressing. As a Private Practice Therapist, I'm here to support you whether you're navigating a significant crisis or simply seeking tools to better manage the day-to-day emotional load of your active life. There’s no need to wait until things feel 'serious enough' to seek support.
What kind of commitment is involved when working with a Private Practice Therapist?
Working with a Private Practice Therapist is a personal commitment to your own well-being. We'll typically start with weekly sessions to build momentum and rapport. The duration of therapy varies for everyone, depending on your goals and what feels right for you. My aim is to provide you with the support and tools you need to feel more resilient and at ease, at a pace that respects your active schedule and emotional capacity.
