How does a Private Practice Therapist help with physical pain for someone my age?

For seniors, our private practice therapist focuses on gentle, specialized techniques to address the unique ways pain manifests with age. We use somatic practices that help your body release tension and re-establish comfortable movement patterns, always with a deep respect for your body's current capabilities and without aggressive interventions.

Is this approach too gentle to make a real difference for chronic pain?

Absolutely not. Gentle doesn't mean ineffective. Our science-backed methods work with your body's natural capacity for healing and regulation. Many find that a gentler approach leads to more sustainable and profound relief from chronic physical pain, as it avoids causing further stress or resistance in the body.

What if my physical pain has been going on for a very long time?

Long-standing pain can often be effectively addressed through somatic work. Our private practice therapist is skilled at working with deeply ingrained patterns of tension and discomfort. We'll patiently guide you through practices that can gradually unravel these patterns, offering you new ways to experience your body with less pain and more ease, regardless of how long it's been present.

Will I need to do strenuous exercises or movements?

No, our approach emphasizes very gentle, mindful movements and body awareness. We never ask you to do anything that causes discomfort or feels strenuous. The focus is on small, precise movements and internal sensing, which are often more effective for lasting pain relief and improving body function than forceful exercises, especially for seniors.
