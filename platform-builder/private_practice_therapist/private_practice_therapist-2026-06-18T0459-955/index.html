Frequently Asked Questions

When should I reach out before my next big move?
When you notice your sleep pattern shifts with your new commute schedule or you skip your morning ritual. That's your body's early alert—before you're overwhelmed.
How is this different from 'general therapy'?
We don't ask you to 'find your purpose.' We ask: 'What's one small habit you can keep during your job transition?' Every session ties to your actual season—like preparing for winter when you're moving states.
Do you work with people experiencing grief?
Yes—but not as a 'journey.' We focus on the tangible: How do you handle grocery shopping on the first anniversary of a loss? How does your community's harvest festival affect your energy? Practical, not poetic.
What if I'm not 'ready' for therapy?
You don't need to be 'ready.' We start where you are: 'What does your coffee cup feel like in your hands right now?' We meet you in your current season, not some future 'ready' state.
