Common myths I hear — and a clearer view

Myth: "Therapy means digging endlessly into the past."
Truth: We use history when it helps; often the most useful work is about present patterns, skills, and relationships.
Myth: "If I struggle, therapy isn’t working."
Truth: Growth can feel destabilizing. We expect challenge and plan supports so change is sustainable.
