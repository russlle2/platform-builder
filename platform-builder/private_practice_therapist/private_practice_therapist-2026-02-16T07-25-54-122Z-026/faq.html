What people ask before starting

Below are common practical and clinical questions I hear. If your question isn't listed, please reach out — I usually respond within 48 hours.

How quickly can I schedule an appointment?
Availability varies. Typically new clients get an initial intake within 2–4 weeks; limited same-week openings may appear. If you need faster care, I will try to suggest local resources.
Do you accept insurance?
I am private-pay. Clients may request a superbill to submit to their insurer for out-of-network reimbursement. Reimbursement depends on your plan; please check with your provider.
What happens in the first session?
We review confidentiality, your current concerns, history, and goals. I’ll explain how I work and we’ll decide whether to proceed with an intake plan. You are welcome to ask about modalities like CBT, EMDR-informed care, or mindfulness-informed approaches.
Can I book virtual or in-person sessions?
I offer both telehealth and in-person appointments when safe and appropriate. I will confirm platform and location details at booking. Telehealth requires a private, secure space on your end.
How do you protect my privacy?
Clinical notes are stored securely. Electronic communications are encrypted, but email has limitations. I will provide a detailed privacy notice and obtain written consent for telehealth. For minors or court-ordered evaluations, different rules may apply.
What if I need to cancel?
Please give 48 hours' notice to avoid a fee. Exceptions exist for illness or emergencies. We can discuss mitigating circumstances with discretion.
Are there sliding scale options?
I reserve a small number of reduced-fee slots for qualifying clients. These are based on financial need and clinical fit. Please inquire via the Contact page.
Begin scheduling
Still unsure? A short consult helps.
If you want to discuss fit before committing, schedule a 30-minute consult. That session focuses on questions and alignment rather than therapy work.

Scope & boundaries

I work with adults on anxiety, trauma, relationship patterns, burnout, and life transitions. I do not provide emergency services, court testimony, or medication management. For crisis support, call your local emergency number.

For confidentiality: communications are part of clinical care and protected. Electronic methods have limits. Please request the full privacy policy for details.
