Common assumptions — and a clearer view

Myth: Therapy is only for crises

Truth: Therapy can be preventive and growth-oriented. Many people come to explore patterns, build skills, or navigate life changes before things escalate.

Myth: I need to be "broken" to seek support

Truth: Therapy is a relationship that helps people cultivate resilience, not a label for weakness. Seeking help is a pragmatic step.

Myth: Your therapist will tell you what to do

Truth: I collaborate with you to clarify values, options, and experiments that fit your life. I do not prescribe medication or give medical advice.
