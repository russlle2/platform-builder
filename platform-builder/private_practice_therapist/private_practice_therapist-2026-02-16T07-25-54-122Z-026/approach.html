Frequently asked about process

How do you decide which techniques to use?
Choice is guided by the problem formulation and by what the client finds tolerable and useful. I prefer brief trials of methods and then evaluate together.
Do you offer block programs?
Yes — I sometimes offer focused pathways named for their emphasis (e.g., "Short Reset" for 6 sessions; "Deep Stabilization" for 16 sessions). Pricing and commitments vary — see Fees for details.

Short Reset (example)

A six-session, structured plan for acute anxiety with skill practice, brief exposure, and relapse prevention steps.

Deep Stabilization (example)

A 12–16 session pathway for complex trauma where stabilization and pacing are prioritized before any memory-focused work.
