Common myths vs what actually helps

Myth: Therapy will fix me quickly.
Truth: Meaningful change typically unfolds over multiple sessions and requires practice between meetings.
Myth: You need to have everything figured out before starting.
Truth: Therapy is most useful when you are ready to explore uncertainty, not already healed.
Myth: Therapist tells you what to do.
Truth: I collaborate with you to surface patterns, skills, and decisions that fit your life.

Principles I bring to sessions

  • Curiosity-first listening that balances empathy with clarity
  • Skill-focused work: grounding, boundaries, communication
  • Practical experiments grounded in everyday life

Questions people often ask

How many sessions will I need?
It depends on goals. Some people find 8–12 sessions useful for targeted work; others continue longer for deeper change. We can plan collaboratively.
What if I'm in crisis?
If you are in immediate danger, please call your local emergency number or a crisis line. Therapy is not an emergency service; I will discuss crisis plans and resources during intake.
Is what I say private?
Sessions are confidential within the limits of the law (e.g., imminent risk of harm, court orders). I will review confidentiality in our first session.
