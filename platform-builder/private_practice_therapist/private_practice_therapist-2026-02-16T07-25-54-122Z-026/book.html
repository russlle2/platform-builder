Begin your first session — what to expect

Scheduling is simple. Below are steps from initial contact to intake. If you prefer, call {{PHONE}} or email {{EMAIL}}.

1. Reach out

Use the booking button, call, or email. Tell us if you prefer telehealth or in-person and share any immediate accessibility needs.

2. Brief consult

A 20–30 minute consult may be offered to clarify fit before a full intake. This is optional but helpful for setting shared goals.

3. Intake & orientation

The first full session includes consent, privacy review, and a collaborative plan. We discuss goals, timeframe, and coordination with other providers if relevant.
