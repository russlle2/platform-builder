How I frame cost

Instead of a one-size-fits-all hourly rate, I talk with clients about goals, likely duration, and coordination with other supports (primary care, psychiatry, or community resources). This helps us choose a package that aligns with needs and budget.

Cancellation & missed session policy

Please give 48 hours' notice to avoid a partial fee. Exceptions are made for illness and emergencies. Any payments are handled securely and confidentially.

Insurance & superbills

I am a private-pay clinician. Upon request, I can provide a monthly superbill you may submit to your insurer for out-of-network reimbursement. I cannot guarantee reimbursement rates.
