Focus areas and typical work

Below are common topics I support. Each card includes what to expect in a short focused series and when a longer plan is often helpful.

Transition and Life Change

Short series: 4–6 sessions to name priorities and set practical steps. Ongoing: monthly check-ins to maintain direction.

Workplace Stress & Boundaries

Short series: skill-building around communication and decision clarity. Longer work for complex workplace conflicts.

Parenting & Relationship Patterns

Focused modules address co-parenting strategies, emotion regulation, and small experiments to try between sessions.

Self-doubt, Perfectionism

Short structure: identify unhelpful routines and try 3 small behavioral shifts between sessions.

Life re-orientation (career, identity)

A collaborative roadmap for next steps—practical tools to test choices with low risk.

Session Planner

Build a short series personalized for your needs. Export a plain-text plan to copy into a message or bring to a first visit.

When a short series might not be enough

Some situations benefit from longer work or specialized services. If you have complex trauma, active suicidal thoughts, or require emergency care, those are outside the scope of this practice—I'll help connect you with appropriate services.
