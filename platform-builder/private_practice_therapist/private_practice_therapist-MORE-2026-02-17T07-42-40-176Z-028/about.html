Practice values

My offers aim to be clear and practical: shorter series for specific issues, and open slots for ongoing consultation. Below are the principles that guide how I work.

  • Collaborative planning — we build the plan together with your priorities front and center.
  • Transparency about limits — not a substitute for crisis care, and I will refer when specialized services are needed.
  • Confidential record-keeping and a focus on respect and privacy.

Bring this to a consultation

Use the intake tool to generate a short list of discussion prompts you can share at your first session. It’s meant to orient the conversation, not label or diagnose.

Sample prompts you might bring:
  • What feels hardest in daily life right now?
  • What would you like to see change in three months?
  • How do you usually cope when stressed?
Clinical scope: I provide short-term and longer-term psychotherapy for adults. I do not provide legal or emergency services. Confidentiality applies within ethical and legal limits. If you are in immediate danger or need urgent help, please call emergency services or your local crisis line.
