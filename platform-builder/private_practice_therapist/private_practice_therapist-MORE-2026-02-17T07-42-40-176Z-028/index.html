A clinical note about care

I am a licensed clinician offering evidence-informed psychotherapy. My work centers curiosity, practical hypothesis-testing, and clear session goals. I will attend to safety, privacy, and scope of practice. If you are in crisis or at risk of harm, please contact emergency services or a crisis line — I cannot provide emergency response through email or scheduling tools.

Sessions are confidential within legal and ethical boundaries; I will explain limits on confidentiality and documentation during intake.

Boundaries and Accessibility

I provide telehealth and limited in-person appointments. If you need accommodations, let me know when you book.
