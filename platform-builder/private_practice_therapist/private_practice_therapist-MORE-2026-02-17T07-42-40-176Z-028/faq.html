Common questions

How do I start?

Reach out by email or phone to share availability and a short note about what brings you here. We will schedule an initial orientation session to clarify goals.

How long are sessions?

Typical sessions are 50 minutes. There are also brief check-ins (25 min) and extended sessions by arrangement.

What should I expect in the first few meetings?

We’ll review the context, immediate concerns, and what you hope to change. Together we’ll outline a short-term plan and decide whether ongoing work is a good fit.

Insurance and billing

This practice primarily operates on a private-pay basis; receipts are available for out-of-network reimbursement. Please ask for specifics at intake.

Confidentiality and crisis: Communications are private within the bounds of law and ethics. If you are acutely unsafe, contact local emergency services immediately.
