Practical clinical sequence

How I typically work with people

My method is collaborative and time-conscious. Each series begins with clearer goals and ends with an actionable plan. Interventions are chosen to match your current priorities, and we review progress along the way.

Framework

  1. Clarify: set a shared, practical goal for this work.
  2. Try: practice one small change between sessions.
  3. Reflect: review what's helped and adjust the plan.
