Questions people often ask

Practical answers about scheduling, insurance, session format, and what to expect. If you don't find your question here, send a note via the contact page.

How long are sessions and how often do we meet?

Standard sessions are 50 minutes. Frequency varies by need — weekly or biweekly meetings are common, but some clients choose blocks or single sessions. We’ll decide together during intake.

Do you accept insurance?

I do not typically bill insurance directly. I can provide a receipt (superbill) with diagnosis codes that you may submit to your insurer for possible reimbursement. We can discuss paperwork during intake.

What if I need to cancel?

When possible, please give at least 48 hours' notice. Late cancellations or no-shows may be billed. If circumstances make fees a hardship, mention this and we can discuss options.

Is telehealth available?

Yes. I offer secure video sessions as well as in-person meetings when appropriate. We will agree on the format that best supports your needs.

How do you handle confidentiality?

Conversations are confidential with a few legal exceptions: risk of harm, suspected abuse of a vulnerable person, or court orders. We review limits to confidentiality during intake so you can decide with full information.

What kinds of issues do you work with?

I work with adults on concerns such as anxiety, depression, life transitions, relationship patterns, and stress management. If a concern is beyond my expertise, I will offer referrals to appropriate resources.
