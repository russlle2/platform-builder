Session boundaries & confidentiality
I work within clear ethical standards. Below are practical notes about confidentiality, record keeping, and situations that require limits to confidentiality.
Most conversations you have with me are private. I keep brief clinical notes to support care and continuity. I will only share information with others with your written permission, except in rare legal or safety situations described below.
There are specific circumstances where I may need to disclose information without your consent: reasonable belief of imminent harm to yourself or others, suspected abuse of a child or vulnerable adult, or if required by a court order. I will aim to discuss these steps with you when possible.
If we meet remotely, I will confirm your location and emergency contacts at the start of the first session. Telehealth is appropriate for many concerns, but I will discuss alternatives if in-person care is preferable.
I request 48 hours notice for cancellations when possible. Fees and rescheduling policies are outlined on the Investment page. If you have questions about affordability, let's talk; I keep a small number of reduced-rate spaces when available.
