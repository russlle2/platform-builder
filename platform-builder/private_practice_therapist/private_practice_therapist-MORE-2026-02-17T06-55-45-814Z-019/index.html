My Collaborative Framework

I orient our time around three practical commitments: clarity about goals, a working plan for sessions, and steady review of how things are changing. These guide decisions about pacing, homework, and the scope of work.

Clarify

We name what feels most strained and what would be most useful to focus on now.

Plan

Assess

We check how changes are sticking and adjust the plan accordingly.

About Your Therapist

I’m a licensed clinician offering thoughtful, evidence-informed care. My work focuses on helping people manage transitions, anxiety, grief, and relationship strain. I value realism and compassion: we attend to what’s achievable now and how to make steady progress.

Therapy is a collaborative process. I will not promise cures or guarantees — instead I offer my full attention, clinical judgment, and practical tools to try between sessions.

Session boundaries & confidentiality

Conversations in sessions are confidential with standard exceptions: imminent risk of harm to yourself or others, suspected abuse of a minor or vulnerable adult, or court-ordered disclosure. I will explain these limits clearly at intake.

Sessions are scheduled by appointment. If you need to cancel, please give as much notice as possible; cancellations within 24 hours may be subject to a fee depending on the agreement we make when you book.

I provide outpatient psychotherapy for adults. If your needs fall outside my scope (e.g., urgent medication management or intensive crisis services), I will assist with referrals to appropriate resources.

If you are in immediate danger or experiencing a medical or psychiatric emergency, please call your local emergency number or go to the nearest emergency department. If you are in the United States, the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline is available at dial 988.
