Session boundaries, confidentiality, and scope

Confidentiality — what stays between us▾
Conversations in therapy are treated with care and confidentiality. I follow the professional standards for client privacy and record-keeping. Exceptions include imminent risk of serious harm to you or others, concerns about abuse involving a minor or a vulnerable adult, or when required by law. If you have questions about limits to confidentiality, we can review them together before you book.
Scope of practice — what I offer and when I refer▾
My work focuses on psychotherapy and collaborative planning for life transitions, mood and anxiety concerns, relationship patterns, and practical coping strategies. If a concern appears outside of my training or scope, I will share that with you and, when appropriate, help connect you with additional resources or specialist support.
Cancellation, rescheduling, and insurance▾
Please provide at least 48 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule. Late cancellations or missed sessions may be billed in part. I do not bill insurance directly for sessions in most cases; I can provide a superbill that you may submit to your insurer. We will review fees and reimbursement options during intake.
If you are in immediate danger or a medical emergency: call your local emergency number right away. If you are experiencing intense distress, please contact your crisis line or go to the nearest emergency department. If you're within the United States, you can call or text 988 for the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline.
