A bit about how I work

I’m a licensed clinician offering focused, evidence-informed care that prioritizes your safety and autonomy. My work favors clear structure: we name the immediate challenge, try a practical step, and review how it landed. Over time, those small course corrections add up to meaningful change.

Session boundaries & confidentiality

Privacy basics ▾

Conversations are private and recorded only in clinical notes. There are legal limits to confidentiality, including situations that involve imminent harm to yourself or others, concerns about child or dependent safety, or court orders. We will review those limits during our first meeting.

Session policies ▾

Sessions are scheduled in advance; cancellations are requested with reasonable notice. I do not provide emergency services. If you are unable to keep an appointment, please contact me as soon as possible so we can reschedule.

Crisis resources

If you are in immediate danger, call 911 or your local emergency number. If you are experiencing a mental health crisis and need support right now, consider contacting your local crisis hotline or dialing 988 (US) where available. If you are outside the United States, please contact your local emergency services or nearest crisis resource.
