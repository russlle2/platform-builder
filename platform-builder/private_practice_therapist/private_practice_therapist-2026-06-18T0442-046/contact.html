Private Practice Therapist

Gentle Support for Your Nervous System During Recovery

When you're recovering from chronic illness or injury, your nervous system can become overactive, contributing to feelings of anxiety, fatigue, and difficulty sleeping. Our Private Practice Therapist focuses on techniques to soothe this heightened state, helping you find moments of peace and reduce the intensity of your physical and emotional experiences. We understand that your body's resources are precious, and our approach respects that, offering practical strategies that won't add to your burden.
