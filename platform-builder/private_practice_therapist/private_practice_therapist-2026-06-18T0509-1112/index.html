Frequently Asked Questions

How can Private Practice Therapy specifically address the sleep challenges unique to entrepreneurs and high-achievers?
For entrepreneurs and high-achievers, the mind often remains in 'on' mode, even when rest is needed. Our Private Practice Therapy goes beyond basic sleep hygiene, addressing the underlying mental and emotional patterns that disrupt your sleep. We help you create clear boundaries between work and rest, process high-stakes stress, and cultivate practices that signal safety and stillness to your nervous system, allowing you to truly disengage and find restorative sleep.
What 'ancient healing traditions' are incorporated into your Private Practice Therapy for sleep improvement?
Our Private Practice Therapy draws upon timeless wisdom, including mindful awareness practices, specific breathwork techniques from yogic traditions, and an understanding of energetic balance. These are not prescriptive rituals but adaptable tools designed to calm your overactive mind, release physical tension, and foster a deep connection to your body's innate rhythms, preparing you for profound and unbroken sleep.
I'm already overwhelmed. Will adding Private Practice Therapy to my schedule create more stress?
Absolutely not. Our Private Practice Therapy is designed to be a sanctuary, not another obligation. We tailor our sessions to fit your demanding schedule, and the practices we introduce are intended to reduce, not increase, your mental load. The goal is to provide you with simple, powerful tools that integrate seamlessly into your life, ultimately freeing up mental and energetic space by improving your sleep and reducing your overall stress burden.
How long does it typically take to see results in my sleep patterns with Private Practice Therapy?
While individual experiences vary, many high-achievers notice shifts in their sleep quality and overall sense of calm within a few weeks of beginning Private Practice Therapy. Consistency in applying the personalized practices is key. Our focus is on sustainable transformation, not just temporary fixes, guiding you to cultivate a lasting relationship with deep rest and inner peace.
