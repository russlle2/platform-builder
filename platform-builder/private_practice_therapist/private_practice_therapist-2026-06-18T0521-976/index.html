Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to reschedule my work hours to fit this in?
No. We work *around* your existing structure. Your practice is tied to your natural breaks—not adding more to your plate. Example: We’ll adjust your 15-min walk timing based on your meeting calendar, not ask you to cut it out.
How is this different from standard stress management?
Standard advice says ‘meditate more.’ We ask: When does your stress truly spike? (Hint: It’s not when you’re tired—it’s when your energy dips *before* your work peaks.) We give you precise, timed interventions that align with *your* biology, not general wisdom.
What if I’m already in burnout mode right now?
We don’t wait for ‘crisis mode.’ We help you stabilize *before* you need crisis care. Your first session includes a quick recovery window (like prioritizing sleep the week before a big presentation), not just talking about where you are now.
Can this work if I’m in a high-pressure job like finance or tech?
Absolutely. We’ve adapted practices for clients who can’t take 30-minute breaks. Example: For a financial analyst, we use lunchtime breathing patterns synced to their trade schedule—which actually improves focus during high-stakes meetings.
