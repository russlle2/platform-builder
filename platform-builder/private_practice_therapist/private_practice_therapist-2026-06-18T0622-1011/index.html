Frequently Asked Questions

How does Private Practice Therapy specifically address stress from chronic illness or injury?
Our approach within Private Practice Therapy focuses on calming the physiological responses to chronic stress and pain. We utilize techniques that help reset your nervous system's baseline, reducing the constant fight-or-flight activation often experienced during and after illness or injury. This includes somatic practices and mindful attunement to your body's signals, allowing for deeper rest and restoration.
What can I expect in terms of nervous system regulation in your Private Practice Therapy?
In Private Practice Therapy, we guide you through gentle practices designed to enhance your vagal tone and improve your body's ability to self-soothe. This means learning to recognize and respond to your body's cues for stress, slowly expanding your window of tolerance for discomfort, and cultivating states of calm and groundedness that can feel elusive during recovery.
Will this Private Practice Therapy be suitable if I'm still experiencing physical limitations from my chronic condition?
Absolutely. Our Private Practice Therapy is deeply adaptive and respects your current physical capacity. We emphasize gentle, non-strenuous techniques that can be modified for any level of mobility or energy. The focus is always on internal regulation and emotional processing, which can be done regardless of physical limitations, fostering a sense of agency and peace within your body.
How does 's spiritual and soulful approach inform the therapy for my recovery?
Our spiritual and soulful approach recognizes that recovery extends beyond the purely physical. In Private Practice Therapy, we honor your inner resilience and wisdom, providing a space where your spirit can find comfort and renewed purpose. We weave in mindful awareness and practices that connect you to your deeper self, fostering a sense of wholeness and meaning as you navigate your healing path, ultimately supporting nervous system calm.
