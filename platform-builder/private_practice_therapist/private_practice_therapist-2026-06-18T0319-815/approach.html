Understanding Your Body's Role in Major Life Shifts with a Private Practice Therapist

When facing major life transitions – be it career changes, relationship shifts, or new family dynamics – your body's response is often overlooked. Our private practice therapy approach helps you understand how these changes manifest physically, affecting your posture and overall body awareness. We provide tools to observe and adjust these physical responses, fostering a greater sense of control and calm as you adapt to new circumstances. This foundational understanding helps you remain centered.
