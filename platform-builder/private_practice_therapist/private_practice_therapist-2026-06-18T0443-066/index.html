Frequently Asked Questions

How does Private Practice Therapy help with chronic illness or injury recovery?
Our Private Practice Therapy focuses on the specific stress and nervous system dysregulation that often accompanies chronic illness or injury. We work with you to understand your unique experience, providing tools and practices to reduce overwhelm, manage chronic pain-related stress, and help your body find a more regulated, calm state, which is crucial for supporting your overall recovery.
What kind of 'stress reduction' will we focus on in Private Practice Therapy?
We won't just talk about stress; we'll focus on practical, embodied ways to reduce its physiological impact. This means exploring techniques that directly influence your nervous system's response, such as gentle breathwork, mindful movement, and sensory grounding. Our goal is to help you build a toolkit that feels natural and accessible, supporting your body's ability to rest and repair during chronic illness or injury recovery.
Is this Private Practice Therapy suitable if my mobility is limited due to injury or illness?
Absolutely. Our Private Practice Therapy is designed to be highly adaptable. We understand that your physical capacity may vary day by day, or be significantly impacted by your condition. Our stress reduction and nervous system regulation techniques are gentle and can be modified to suit your current physical comfort and limitations, ensuring you can participate fully and safely in your recovery journey.
How is this different from other types of therapy for chronic conditions?
While many therapies can be helpful, our Private Practice Therapy specifically centers on the direct impact of chronic illness and injury on your nervous system and your capacity for stress regulation. We don't just address the emotional toll; we provide concrete, somatic-informed strategies that help you gently re-pattern your body's stress responses, fostering a deeper sense of internal safety and calm as you navigate your recovery.
