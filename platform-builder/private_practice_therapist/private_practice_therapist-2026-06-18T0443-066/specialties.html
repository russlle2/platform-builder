Private Practice Therapist

Gentle Support for Bodies in Recovery: Private Practice Therapy

When your body is working hard to recover, your nervous system can feel perpetually on high alert. This Private Practice Therapy focuses on understanding how chronic illness or injury impacts your sense of safety and calm. We'll explore practical, nature-inspired tools to help your system find a more settled state, easing the often overwhelming stress that comes with long-term recovery.
