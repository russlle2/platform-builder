Frequently Asked Questions

How can Private Practice Therapy specifically help with my athletic performance?
We'll focus on optimizing your body mechanics and postural alignment, which directly translates to more efficient movement and reduced energy expenditure in your sport. By understanding and addressing underlying physical stressors, you can perform better and recover more effectively.
I'm not an elite athlete, but I'm very active. Is this still right for me?
Absolutely! If you engage in regular physical activity, whether it's weekend hiking, consistent gym workouts, or recreational sports, your body experiences similar stresses. We'll tailor our approach to your specific activity level and concerns, ensuring you maintain an active life comfortably.
What does 'postural health and body awareness' mean in a practical sense for someone active?
It means understanding how you hold your body during activity and rest, and how those patterns affect your comfort and performance. We'll work on identifying areas of tension or imbalance, then develop strategies to move more freely and efficiently, helping you avoid unnecessary strain.
How long does it typically take to see improvements in managing physical stress or posture?
The timeline varies for everyone, as it depends on your unique body and consistent practice. However, many clients report feeling a greater sense of body awareness and improved comfort within a few sessions, with more significant changes building over time as you integrate new habits.
