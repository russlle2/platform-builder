How can Private Practice Therapy specifically help me if I'm recovering from chronic illness or injury?

As a Private Practice Therapist, I understand that chronic illness and injury bring unique emotional challenges. We will address feelings of loss, grief, frustration, and anxiety directly related to your health, helping you process these emotions, adapt to changes, and develop coping mechanisms that empower you to live a fulfilling life despite your circumstances.

What does 'trauma-informed care' mean for someone in my situation in Private Practice Therapy?

Trauma-informed care, as provided by this Private Practice Therapist, means recognizing that your experiences with illness or injury might have been overwhelming or distressing. We create a therapeutic space where your autonomy is respected, and we move at a pace that feels safe for you. This approach helps you process difficult memories or feelings without re-traumatization, fostering a sense of control and safety.

Will Private Practice Therapy focus only on my illness/injury, or other aspects of my life?

While your chronic illness or injury will be a central theme, Private Practice Therapy with me extends beyond just the physical. We will explore how your health impacts your relationships, identity, career, and overall well-being. The goal is to support your emotional health comprehensively, helping you integrate this experience into your life narrative in a way that feels empowering, not diminishing.

How quickly can I expect to see results from Private Practice Therapy for my emotional recovery?

Emotional recovery is a very personal process, and its timeline varies for everyone. In Private Practice Therapy, we focus on building sustainable coping strategies and processing emotions at your own pace. While you might experience shifts in perspective or relief relatively early, true emotional healing is a journey. We will celebrate small victories and continually work towards your goals together.
