Frequently Asked Questions

How does a Private Practice Therapist address sleep specifically during life transitions?
When you're navigating a significant life shift, your Private Practice Therapist recognizes that stress and uncertainty can profoundly impact your sleep. We will explore the specific emotional, cognitive, and physiological disruptions unique to your transition, developing tailored strategies that re-establish your sleep hygiene and foster a sense of security even amidst change.
Will your Private Practice Therapist prescribe medication for sleep?
As a Private Practice Therapist, my approach is non-pharmacological. I focus on behavioral, cognitive, and integrative techniques to address sleep challenges. Should we determine that medication might be beneficial, I can collaborate with your medical doctor or provide referrals to professionals who can assess that aspect of your care.
What Eastern and Western approaches does your Private Practice Therapist incorporate for sleep?
Your Private Practice Therapist integrates evidence-based cognitive behavioral therapy for insomnia (CBT-I) and mindfulness techniques (Western) with principles from yoga nidra, breathing practices, and energetic attunement (Eastern). This blend allows for a comprehensive understanding of your sleep patterns and deeper restorative rest.
How long does it typically take to see improvements in sleep with a Private Practice Therapist?
The timeline for improvement varies for each individual, as every life transition and sleep pattern is unique. However, many clients begin to notice shifts in their sleep quality within a few weeks of consistent practice and engagement with the strategies developed with your Private Practice Therapist. Our work together is a gradual unfolding towards deeper rest.
