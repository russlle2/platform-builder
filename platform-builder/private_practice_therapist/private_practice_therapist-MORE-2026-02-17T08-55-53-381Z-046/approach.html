How I work with clients

My approach blends focused skills, collaborative formulation, and steady review. I aim for clarity in each session so you leave with one or two things to try between meetings.

Process overview

1. Orientation. We start by clarifying immediate concerns, what you’d like to change, and any constraints in your schedule or energy.
2. Formulation. A short, shared understanding of the patterns sustaining the difficulty—this is collaborative, not a label.
3. Plan. Practical, measurable steps and experiments to test between sessions. Plans are small and specific.
4. Review & adjust. We track what worked, refine strategies, and move the focus as things shift.

Methods I commonly use

  • Problem-focused CBT strategies when helpful for patterns and habits
  • Behavioral activation for low motivation and mood
  • Skills training for emotion regulation and distress tolerance
  • Reflective work to situate symptoms within life story and values

What to expect in the first session

We’ll review your intake, prioritize the first session goals, and agree on a short plan. I will explain confidentiality, session logistics, and the membership options if you want ongoing care.

Boundaries & crisis information

I provide scheduled clinical care and am not available for on-call crisis response. If you are in immediate danger, please contact emergency services or a local crisis line. Confidentiality follows standard clinical limits and will be explained at the first visit.
