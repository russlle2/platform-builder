About my work

My practice centers on calm, focused conversations that move toward clearer choices, more reliable coping, and improved day-to-day rhythm. I combine evidence-informed approaches with attention to personal story and what’s possible in your life now.

Who I typically work with

  • Adults navigating transitions: work changes, relationship shifts, parenting concerns
  • People with persistent anxiety, low mood, or patterns that interfere with goals
  • Those wanting regular support through a membership rather than sporadic blocks

How the membership works

Memberships are an ongoing model: a regular cadence of sessions, access to structured brief courses, and priority scheduling. Pricing is presented in transparent options—no surprise packages. If you prefer short series, I offer focused multi-session programs for specific goals.

Confidentiality & boundaries

Conversations are confidential within legal limits. I will explain confidentiality, record-keeping, and limits to confidentiality at our first meeting. I do not provide emergency services; if you are in immediate danger or a life-threatening emergency, please call local emergency services or your crisis line.

Scope: My work is therapy for adults. I do not provide forensic evaluations or on-call crisis management. If a different service is more appropriate I will help connect you with resources.
