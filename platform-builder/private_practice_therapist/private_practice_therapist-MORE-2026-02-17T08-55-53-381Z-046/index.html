Common questions

What is a membership?

A membership sets a regular rhythm for sessions and a small set of included touches such as brief check-ins and scheduling. It is designed to help with steady work rather than only one-off support.

Will you share my records?

Confidentiality is respected. Records are only shared with your permission except where required by law (e.g., risk of harm). We review confidentiality and limits at intake.

What if I am in crisis?

If you are in immediate danger, please call your local emergency number or go to the nearest emergency room. If you are in {{STATE}} and need urgent help, contact local crisis resources. This practice does not provide emergency services.
