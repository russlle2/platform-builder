Areas of focus

I offer steady clinical partnership across several common concerns for adults. Below are concise descriptions to help you decide if we might be a fit.

Carefully-paced anxiety work

Support for persistent worry, performance anxiety, or situational stresses. We co-create practices you can use between sessions and prioritize exposure that feels manageable.

Low mood & motivation

Practical, behavioral steps paired with reflective work on meaning and energy. I help you find small, dependable shifts rather than all-or-nothing goals.

Relationship patterns

From communication habits to boundary-setting, we look at interaction cycles and rehearse alternatives you can try outside sessions.

Transitions & role changes

Support through moves, career shifts, parenting changes, and other transitions. We focus on stabilizing routines and clarifying values.

Focused series for specific aims

Short, structured programs address sleep, emotion regulation, or decision clarity. These are time-limited and skill-oriented.

Membership options and framing

Rather than single blocks, many clients choose an ongoing plan. I offer clear tiers so you can pick the rhythm that fits your life.

Stabilize Circle

Biweekly sessions, access to short courses, messaging for scheduling.

Steady Circle

Weekly sessions, course access, periodic check-ins between sessions.

Focused Series

4–8 session concentrated programs for a specific goal; clear start and end.
