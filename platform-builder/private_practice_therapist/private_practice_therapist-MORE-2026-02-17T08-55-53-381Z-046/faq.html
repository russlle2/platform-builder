Frequently asked questions

How do I know if this is the right fit?
I offer an initial consultation to learn about your needs and see if the approach feels useful. You can also use the self-screening tool on the side to organize questions you want to discuss.
What does a membership include?
Memberships typically include regular session time, scheduling priority, and access to brief between-session check-ins as arranged. Specifics are agreed during intake and recorded in the care plan.
Insurance and billing
I provide invoices and can discuss receipts you can submit to insurance. I cannot guarantee reimbursement. For details, we will review billing during the consult.
Confidentiality and limits
All clinical notes are confidential except in cases required by law (e.g., imminent harm, abuse). If you have specific privacy concerns we can discuss steps to minimize risks.
How long before I notice change?
Therapy unfolds differently for everyone. Some people feel practical relief quickly; others appreciate steady progress over weeks or months. We'll set expectations together without promises of a specific outcome.
