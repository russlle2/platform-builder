Frequently Asked Questions

How is this different from general therapy for life changes?
We don't wait for your transition to feel chaotic. We address the *season* of your change (e.g., post-relocation winter, post-promotion spring) with concrete prevention steps—not just processing emotions after the fact.
Do I need to feel 'unwell' to start?
No. Think of this like a seasonal tune-up for your routine. If your energy dips during your new job’s busy season, we build habits *before* you feel overwhelmed. Prevention is proactive, not reactive.
What if my transition isn't tied to a season?
Your seasonal pattern still matters. Even if you moved in July, winter can strain your new routines. We match your change to the *current* seasonal energy (e.g., quiet winter vs. busy summer) to build sustainable habits.
How do sessions stay focused on my specific change?
Every session starts with your *actual* transition moment: 'What’s the weather like in your new job this month?' or 'How did travel affect your sleep last week?' We keep it real, not theoretical.
