Method metaphors

I use different metaphors to help make change tangible: a compass for navigation, a toolkit for skills practice, and a map for planning. Which image helps you get moving is personal.

Clinical orientation

My practice integrates evidence-informed techniques—behavioral experiments, brief cognitive strategies, and values-based planning—applied collaboratively. Work is shaped to your priorities and paced to your tolerance for change.

Session formats
  • Single session: focused problem or planning
  • Short package: 4–8 sessions with a concrete goal
  • Ongoing: weekly or biweekly for deeper work
Boundaries & crisis note

Conversations are confidential except when disclosure is legally required. This practice does not provide crisis response—if you are in immediate danger, contact emergency services.

A note on expectations

Therapy is a collaborative process without guaranteed outcomes. I aim to offer consistent, evidence-informed support and will work with you to set realistic, measurable goals.

Confidentiality & crisis guidance: Records are private except where disclosure is required by law. If you are at immediate risk, please contact emergency services or local crisis resources.
