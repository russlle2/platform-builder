Clinical stance

I work collaboratively, blending structured tools with reflective inquiry. Many people find progress comes from short-term focused work (6–12 sessions) or from a longer therapeutic frame when issues are layered and recurring. Sessions are confidential within legal and ethical limits; I will explain boundaries and coordination of care when relevant.

What to expect

  • A thoughtful first session to identify goals and priorities
  • Flexible pacing: weekly, biweekly, or a short structured package
  • Shared planning and brief skill-building between sessions when helpful

Notes on confidentiality & scope

Conversations are confidential except where disclosure is required by law (e.g., imminent risk of harm, mandatory reporting). I will clarify limits of confidentiality and consent at intake. I do not provide emergency services; if you are in immediate danger, please call local emergency services or a crisis line.

Clinical boundaries

This practice offers psychotherapy for adults. I do not provide legal, financial, or medical advice. If you have questions about medication, a primary care provider or psychiatrist can help coordinate care.

Confidentiality, scope, and crisis guidance: Records are kept securely and discussed with you; certain circumstances (harm to self/others, abuse of a minor) require disclosure. If you are in immediate danger, call emergency services or your local crisis line.
