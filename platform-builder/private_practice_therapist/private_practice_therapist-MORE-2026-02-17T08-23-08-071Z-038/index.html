How I work — four pragmatic pillars

Listen & map

We begin by mapping what matters now: stressors, patterns, and strengths you already have.

Targeted tools

Practical strategies you can test between sessions: experiments, routines, and boundary-setting.

Plan with evidence

Short cycles of review to see what worked and what to change next — efficient and humane.

Confidential partnership

A steady, discreet space for reflection that respects your privacy and life demands.

Illustrative notes (anonymized)

Turning uncertainty into a short plan

Client felt stuck deciding about a job change. We clarified values, tested a 30-day experiment, and reviewed outcomes. Result: clearer decision framework and reduced anxiety about making the "perfect" choice.

Managing flare-ups

Frequent mood surges were interrupting sleep. We prioritized coping skills and sleep hygiene, used brief behavioral experiments, and developed a relapse-prevention checklist.
