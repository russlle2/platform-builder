Questions people often ask

Straightforward answers about what to expect, how sessions are structured, and practicalities like fees and cancellations.

What happens in a first session?

+
We usually begin with what brings you now—goals, recent events, and immediate concerns. You'll get a chance to describe your priorities. We often leave time to plan next steps together. Sessions are collaborative and tailored to your pace.

How long is a session and how often should I come?

+
Standard sessions are 50–60 minutes. Frequency depends on need—some start weekly for momentum, others move to biweekly for steady maintenance. We'll discuss a rhythm that fits your life and goals.

Are sessions confidential?

+
Yes. Information shared in sessions is kept confidential within legal and ethical boundaries. Exceptions include imminent risk of harm, court orders, or where required by law. We'll go over limits of confidentiality on intake.

What if I need help between sessions or I'm in crisis?

+
I aim to respond to messages in a timely way, but I do not provide emergency services through email or text. If you are in immediate danger, please call local emergency services or a crisis line. We can discuss safety planning during sessions.

Payment, cancellations, and insurance

+
Payment is due at the time of the session. Cancellation policy is 48 hours notice for full refund or reschedule. I provide receipts you may submit to your insurer—check your plan for out-of-network coverage. No medical claims or guarantees are made about outcomes.
