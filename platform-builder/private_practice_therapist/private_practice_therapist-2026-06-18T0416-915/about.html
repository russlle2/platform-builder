How is seasonal wellness therapy different from standard therapy during transitions?

We focus on *preventing* overwhelm by working within your natural life rhythms. Instead of just processing past events, we help you align with your current season—like using spring renewal practices after a loss—to build resilience before the next shift.

Can this really help if my transition is chaotic and unplanned?

Absolutely. Your seasonal rhythm isn't about timing—it's about energy. We work with *your* reality: the chaotic move, the grief, the unexpected career pivot. You'll learn to gently reset your inner weather *during* the storm.

Do I need to believe in ancient traditions for this to work?

No. We share practical tools—like adjusting your routine with the seasons—without requiring spiritual belief. Ancient wisdom here means what's genuinely time-tested, not mystical. You'll use it, not debate it.

How do I know if I'm ready for preventive care instead of crisis therapy?

You're ready when you notice small ripples before the flood: feeling drained after meetings, skipping walks, or noticing your thoughts turning negative. That’s your signal—your seasonal alignment needs tending *before* it becomes overwhelming.
