Frequently Asked Questions

How does Private Practice Therapy specifically address the emotional impact of chronic illness or injury?
Our approach acknowledges that sustained physical distress, medical interventions, and changes to daily life can create emotional strain, grief, and even trauma. We focus on processing these specific experiences, providing tools to manage associated anxiety, depression, and identity shifts, rather than a generic mental health framework.
Is your Private Practice Therapy suitable if I'm still actively managing my physical condition?
Absolutely. Our Private Practice Therapy is designed to complement your medical care, not replace it. We work with you to understand the interplay between your physical symptoms and emotional well-being, providing support that respects your ongoing medical journey. Our focus is on the emotional resilience needed to navigate these complex circumstances.
What does 'trauma-informed care' mean in the context of my recovery from chronic illness or injury at your Private Practice?
Trauma-informed care in our Private Practice means we recognize that your experiences with illness or injury, including diagnostic processes, treatments, and pain, may have been overwhelming or distressing. We prioritize creating a safe, predictable, and empowering therapeutic space, ensuring you feel respected and in control of your healing process, without re-traumatizing you.
How can Private Practice Therapy help me reintegrate into my life after a significant physical setback?
Emotional reintegration after chronic illness or injury involves processing the losses, adapting to changes, and rebuilding a sense of self. Our Private Practice Therapy provides strategies to gently navigate social reentry, redefine personal goals, and cultivate a renewed sense of purpose and calm, acknowledging the profound shifts you've undergone.
