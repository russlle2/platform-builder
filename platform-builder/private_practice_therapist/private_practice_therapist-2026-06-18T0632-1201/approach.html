Private Practice Therapist

A Compassionate Framework: Trauma-Informed Private Practice Therapy

Your experience with chronic illness or injury may have been profoundly destabilizing. Our Private Practice Therapy is built on a trauma-informed foundation, acknowledging the potential for medical procedures, pain, and uncertainty to leave lasting emotional imprints. We cultivate a therapeutic environment of profound safety and respect, allowing you to articulate your experiences without judgment, fostering a gradual, gentle unwinding of internalized stress and fear.
