Therapeutic Specialties: Seasonal & Preventive Care

We focus on the seasons you're living through—not metaphorical ones. When you're navigating a career pivot in early spring, we help you build sustainable energy habits before burnout hits. For winter relocations, we address practical adjustment routines, not just emotional reflection.

What if my transition isn't dramatic—just a slow shift?

That's exactly where we excel. Seasonal wellness isn't about big events. We support the quiet shifts: adjusting routines after a promotion, finding energy patterns after a health change, or simply noticing when your usual habits no longer fit.

How is this different from general therapy?

We never ask you to fit your transition into a standard timeline. Your spring relocation might feel like late winter to you—we honor your personal pace and season, not a calendar.

Do I need to track my moods or do daily practices?

No. We focus on what already works in your life. If you check emails at 7 AM, we build on that—adding 5 minutes of breathing instead of adding new tasks.

What if I'm not ready to talk about my change yet?

We start where you are. Many begin by simply naming the shift ('This move has been heavy'), without diving deep. Your comfort level guides the pace.
