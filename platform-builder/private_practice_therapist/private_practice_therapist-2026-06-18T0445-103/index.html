Frequently Asked Questions

How is a Private Practice Therapist different from a doctor for sleep issues?
While your doctor focuses on physical health, as a Private Practice Therapist, I specialize in the emotional and behavioral aspects that greatly impact your sleep. We'll explore daily routines, thought patterns, and gentle techniques that contribute to a peaceful night's rest, working alongside any medical care you're receiving.
Will I need to take medication to improve my sleep with your Private Practice Therapist approach?
Not at all. My focus as a Private Practice Therapist is on non-pharmacological methods to improve your sleep. We'll concentrate on gentle, natural strategies and behavioral adjustments that help your body and mind prepare for sleep, without relying on medication unless it's part of a broader plan with your doctor.
How long does it typically take to see improvements in sleep with a Private Practice Therapist?
Everyone's journey to better sleep is unique. Some individuals notice small improvements fairly quickly, while for others, it's a gradual process. We'll work together at your comfortable pace, celebrating every step forward. My goal as your Private Practice Therapist is to equip you with lasting tools for consistent, restorative rest.
What if my sleep problems are linked to other worries or stress as a senior?
It's very common for sleep to be affected by life's worries. As your Private Practice Therapist, I'm here to gently explore those connections with you. We'll address not just the sleep challenges themselves, but also the underlying anxieties or stressors that might be keeping you awake, helping you find greater peace of mind overall.
