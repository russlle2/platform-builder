Private Practice Therapist

Targeted Private Practice Therapy: Rebuilding Postural Health After Setbacks

Navigating recovery from chronic illness or injury often impacts how you hold and move your body. Our Private Practice Therapy is designed to address these changes directly. We work with you to identify altered movement patterns and develop strategies to improve your alignment, reduce compensatory strain, and enhance your overall physical comfort. This isn't about quick fixes, but about establishing sustainable body awareness.
