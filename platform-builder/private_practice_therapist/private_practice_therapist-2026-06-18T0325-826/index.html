Frequently Asked Questions

How does Private Practice Therapy specifically address chronic illness or injury recovery?
Our Private Practice Therapy focuses on the physical and psychological impacts of chronic illness or injury on your posture and movement. We help you gently re-engage with your body, identify any ingrained compensatory patterns, and develop new, healthier ways of moving that support your healing process and reduce discomfort.
What can I expect in a session focused on postural health and body awareness?
In our Private Practice Therapy sessions, you can expect a collaborative approach. We'll start by discussing your current experiences and goals. Then, through gentle movement exploration and guided awareness exercises, we'll work on understanding your body's current state and gradually introduce practices to improve your alignment, balance, and proprioception.
Is this Private Practice Therapy suitable for all types of chronic conditions or injuries?
While our Private Practice Therapy is broadly beneficial for those recovering from various conditions, we encourage you to schedule a consultation to discuss your specific situation. We'll assess if our specialized focus on postural health and body awareness aligns with your needs and current recovery stage, ensuring we can provide effective support.
How long will it take to see improvements in my postural health and body awareness through Private Practice Therapy?
The timeline for seeing improvements varies for everyone, as it depends on your individual condition, consistency, and specific goals. Our Private Practice Therapy is designed for sustainable progress, not quick fixes. We'll work at your pace, celebrating small gains as you gradually re-establish greater comfort and control in your body.
