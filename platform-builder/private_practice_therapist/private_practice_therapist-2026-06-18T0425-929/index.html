Frequently Asked Questions

How does seasonal prevention differ from regular therapy?
Regular therapy often responds to injuries after they happen. We work ahead of time—mapping how your body adapts to seasonal changes (like colder air tightening muscles) and building small, daily actions to keep you moving.
Do I need to be a competitive athlete to get this?
Absolutely not. Our plans work for weekend hikers, gym enthusiasts, or team sport players. We tailor strategies to your actual activity level, not your competition status.
How many sessions do I need?
Most clients start with a seasonal assessment, then meet every 4-6 weeks to adjust. We sync with your training calendar so sessions fit without adding stress.
What if I’m not injured yet?
That’s exactly when preventive care shines. We focus on where your body is vulnerable now, so you don’t wait for pain to act. Think of it as maintenance for your active life.
