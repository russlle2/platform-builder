Begin here: scheduling a first meeting

If you're ready to take the next step, this page outlines how we schedule, what the intake involves, and what you can expect from the first month of work.

I aim to respond to scheduling requests within 3 business days. If you're in crisis, please contact local emergency services—I do not provide emergency care.

What to expect at intake

We review history, current concerns, safety, and logistical information (privacy, fees, and cancellations). This is also a chance to determine clinical fit.

Who I typically work with

Adults facing anxiety, relationship struggles, trauma recovery, life transitions, and burnout. If your needs are atypical, I'll recommend a colleague.

Documentation & privacy

Records are stored securely; a confidentiality statement and limits will be included in intake paperwork. I will discuss any questions you have before beginning care.

Brief examples of treatment frames

Eight-session plan

An option for focused goals: skill-building, phase-focused processing, and measurable steps.

Open-ended therapy

Weekly sessions with ongoing review of goals and progress; deeper relational work and trauma processing when indicated.

Check-ins

Shorter sessions for maintenance, skills rehearsal, and monitoring after more intensive therapy.

Practical matters

  • Fees and insurance are discussed at intake; superbills available for out-of-network reimbursement.
  • Cancellation policy: please provide 48 hours notice when possible; fees may apply for late cancellations.
  • If I am not able to meet your scheduling or clinical needs, I will provide referrals.

By scheduling, you agree to receive correspondence related to appointment logistics and practice policies.
