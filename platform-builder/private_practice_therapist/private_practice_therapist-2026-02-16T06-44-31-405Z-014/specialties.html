Clinical areas I commonly support

I work with adults experiencing emotional overwhelm, relationship strain, and the aftereffects of traumatic stress. Below are core areas and typical starting strategies; treatment is tailored to your needs.

Anxiety & Worry

Behavioral experiments, cognitive reframing, and focused exposure where appropriate to reduce avoidance and increase confidence.

Complex Stress & Burnout

Practical load-management, boundary setting, and emotion regulation to restore capacity without glossing over systemic factors.

Trauma-Related Distress

Stabilization, psychoeducation, and sensorimotor-informed techniques before deeper processing when ready.

Relationship Repair & Communication

Skill-based work to change interaction patterns, including reflective listening and behavioral shaping exercises.

Life Transitions

Decision-making frameworks and values clarification to navigate career, relational, or identity shifts.

Perinatal & Parenting Stress

Support for the emotional demands of parenting phases and perinatal mood concerns, with respect for medical collaboration when needed.

Obsessive Patterns

Strategy-driven interventions to reduce compulsive behaviors and intrusive thought distress.

Depression & Low Motivation

Activity scheduling, behavioral activation, and compassion-focused strategies to rebuild momentum.
