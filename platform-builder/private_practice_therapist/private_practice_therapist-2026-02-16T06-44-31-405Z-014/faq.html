Clarifying the process

What happens in the first session?

We review what brought you to therapy, your goals, clinical history, and practicalities (scheduling, fees, confidentiality). We collaboratively decide next steps; there's no obligation to continue after the first meeting.

How long does therapy usually take?

Duration varies: some clients find benefit in 8–12 focused sessions; others commit to longer-term work over months or years. We regularly evaluate progress and adjust pacing.

Is telehealth available?

Yes. I offer secure telehealth appointments and in-person slots when available. We'll discuss privacy and find the best format for your needs.

Do you work with couples and individuals?

I work with individuals, couples, and some family configurations. If you have a specific situation, contact me and I can advise on fit and structure.

Core practice principles

  • Evidence-informed approaches blended with relational attunement
  • Collaborative goal-setting and transparency about process
  • Attention to cultural context and identity

Confidentiality & boundaries

Your sessions are confidential except where disclosure is required by law (e.g., imminent risk, court orders). I will review limits of confidentiality in our intake paperwork.

Crisis information

I am not an emergency service. If you are in immediate danger, please call 911 or your local crisis line. If you are outside the United States, contact local emergency services.

Questions about fit and scope

How do I know if we're a good fit?

Fit is relational. In early sessions, we assess whether my style, expertise, and scheduling align with your needs. I may recommend another clinician if I believe different expertise would serve you better.

What issues do you commonly treat?

Typical concerns include anxiety, depression, trauma, relationship difficulties, caregiver stress, work-related burnout, and life transitions. I emphasize realistic coping strategies and relational patterns.

Are notes kept and who sees them?

I keep clinical notes for continuity of care. These are private and stored securely. If legal requests arise, I will discuss implications with you unless disclosure is legally mandated.

A few logistical FAQs

Can I cancel or reschedule?

Yes. Please provide at least 48 hours notice when possible. Cancellation policies are discussed at intake; fees may apply for late cancellations.

Do you provide letters or testing?

I can provide documentation for workplace or school accommodations in limited circumstances; more specialized evaluations or testing are referred to appropriate professionals.

What if I need a different kind of support?

If your needs fall outside my scope (e.g., forensic evaluations, medication management), I will offer referrals to colleagues or multidisciplinary teams.
