Questions people often ask

How long do people usually work together?

It varies — some aim for short-term focused work (8–12 sessions), others for longer exploratory therapy. We clarify goals early and revisit them regularly.

Is what we talk about private?

Yes. Sessions are protected by confidentiality with standard legal exceptions (e.g., imminent harm). I can provide a privacy statement before our first meeting.

What if I’m in crisis?

If you are at immediate risk, call your local emergency services or a crisis line. I do not provide emergency services via email or text.
