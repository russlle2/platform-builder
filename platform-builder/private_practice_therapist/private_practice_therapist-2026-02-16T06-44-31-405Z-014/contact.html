Scope

I provide short- and long-term psychotherapy for adults. I do not provide forensic evaluations, emergency services, or medication management. If you need immediate help, call local emergency services or a crisis line.

Common misunderstandings I hear

Myth

Therapy means talking about the past for years.

Truth: Many people work on current patterns and practical skills and notice change within a few months.

Myth

You must be in crisis to benefit.

Truth: Therapy can help with transitions, recurring patterns, and improving well-being before things become overwhelming.

Myth

The therapist will tell you what to do.

Truth: I collaborate with clients to explore options and develop workable steps tailored to your life.

Core professional principles

  • Evidence-informed practice: Interventions grounded in contemporary clinical research and adapted to you.
  • Collaborative planning: Goals are set together and reviewed regularly.
  • Practical focus: We combine insight with concrete skills for daily life.
