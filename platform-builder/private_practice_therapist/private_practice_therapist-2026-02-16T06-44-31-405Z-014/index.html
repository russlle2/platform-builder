Myths people often tell themselves about therapy

A scientist’s guide to what therapy is — and isn’t.

Myth: Therapy will fix me instantly

Therapy is rarely instantaneous. It’s a collaborative process informed by research and tailored to your goals. Progress can be steady but nonlinear.

Truth: Lasting change takes consistent, guided work

I use evidence-based practices and regular review of goals so we can measure what helps and adjust treatment as needed.

Myth: You have to talk about everything right away

There’s no pressure to disclose everything. Safety, pacing, and consent guide what we explore and when.

Truth: We set boundaries and pace together

We’ll co-create a plan that balances immediate coping strategies with deeper therapeutic work when you’re ready.

Questions I often get

How do membership plans work?

I offer ongoing membership plans that include a sliding number of sessions per month, messaging support during business hours, and periodic outcome reviews. Memberships provide continuity and priority scheduling without making clinical decisions for you.

What if I’m in crisis?

This practice does not provide emergency services. If you are at immediate risk of harming yourself or others, call 911 or go to your nearest emergency department. In the U.S. you can also call 988 for the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline.

Do you accept insurance?

I operate primarily on a private-pay basis and provide superbills to submit to insurance when applicable. We discuss fees and payment during intake so there are no surprises.

Ongoing care plans for steady progress

If you’re looking for continuity, the "Care Membership" offers scheduled sessions, urgent check-ins during business hours, and quarterly progress consultations. Flexible cancellation policies and transparent billing.
