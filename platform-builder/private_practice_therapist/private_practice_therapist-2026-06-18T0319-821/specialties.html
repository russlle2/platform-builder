Private Practice Therapist

Finding Stability: Private Practice Therapy for Postural Health After Illness

Chronic illness or injury can leave you feeling disconnected from your physical self. Our Private Practice Therapy gently guides you back to a place of inner stability. We explore foundational body mechanics, helping you understand how your posture influences your energy and overall well-being. This isn't about pushing through pain, but rather listening deeply to your body's unique language and needs.
