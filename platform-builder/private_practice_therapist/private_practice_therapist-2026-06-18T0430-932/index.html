Frequently Asked Questions

How do you prevent burnout without adding more to my schedule?
We build prevention into your existing rhythm. For example, we'll slot your 15-minute check-in between your morning meeting and lunch—no new hours, just smarter time use.
What if my business cycle is unpredictable?
Your seasonal stress patterns matter more than your business model. We'll track your unique peaks (like product launches or conference season) and create a flexible buffer plan you can adjust in real-time.
Is this just 'therapy' dressed up as prevention?
No. We focus on the 3-4 weeks before stress hits—like avoiding the holiday overwhelm before it starts. It's not talking about past stress; it's building your next seasonal edge.
How quickly do I see results?
Most clients notice smoother transitions within 1-2 weeks of implementing our seasonal tools. You'll catch stress before it becomes a crisis—like recognizing your summer energy dip before it affects your team's performance.
