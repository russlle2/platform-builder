Frequently Asked Questions

How do you address seasonal joint stiffness without medication?
We look at your daily life—like how you get in and out of your favorite rocking chair or walk to the post office. Then, we find small, easy tweaks: maybe a cushion for your chair, or a seated stretch you do while watching the news. No pills, just comfort that fits your routine.
What makes your preventive care different for seniors?
We focus on what you already do, not new habits. If you garden in spring, we discuss how to protect your hands. If you attend the community dinner in fall, we help you move comfortably. It’s about keeping your current activities joyful, not starting from scratch.
Do you work with people who are managing multiple health conditions?
Absolutely. We coordinate gently with your doctors and fit into your existing care. For example, if you see a cardiologist in , we’ll make sure our suggestions support your heart health without adding extra steps to your week.
How do I know if you’re the right fit?
Try a 20-minute check-in. We’ll ask about your morning coffee, whether your knees ache after walking the dog, or if you get chilly during winter movie nights. If our style feels warm and clear—like talking to a neighbor who knows your routines—you’ll know it’s right.
