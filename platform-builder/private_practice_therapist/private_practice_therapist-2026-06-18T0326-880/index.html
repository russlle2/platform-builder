Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly does 'postural health and body awareness' mean in Private Practice Therapy?
It means gently exploring how you hold your body, how you move, and how aware you are of your physical sensations. During major life changes, your posture can reflect your inner state. We'll work together to find simple ways to improve your physical comfort and presence, which can significantly impact how you navigate stress and change.
I've never done anything like this before. Do I need special knowledge or flexibility for this Private Practice Therapy?
Absolutely not! This approach is designed for everyone, especially those new to body-focused practices. There are no prerequisites, no complex movements, and no judgments. We meet you exactly where you are, offering accessible guidance to help you feel more at home in your body, regardless of your current experience or physical ability.
How can focusing on my body help me with a major life transition, like a career change or a move?
When you're going through big changes, it's easy to feel overwhelmed. By gently bringing your awareness to your body and posture, you can find a sense of stability and calm. This can help you feel more grounded, make clearer decisions, and adapt more smoothly to new circumstances, even when things feel uncertain around you.
What will a typical Private Practice Therapy session focused on body awareness look like?
Our sessions are conversational and gentle. We'll talk about what you're experiencing in your life transition and how your body is feeling. We might explore simple seated or standing postures, breathing techniques, or ways to notice physical sensations without judgment. It's always a collaborative and comfortable process, tailored to your pace and comfort.
