Frequently Asked Questions

What kind of issues does your private practice therapist typically address?
Our private practice therapist helps individuals with a wide range of concerns, including anxiety, depression, stress management, relationship challenges, grief, trauma, and personal growth. Your unique needs will always be at the forefront of our work together.
How long does a typical therapy session last at your private practice?
Generally, individual therapy sessions at our private practice are 50 minutes in duration. This allows ample time for in-depth discussion and therapeutic work, ensuring you feel heard and supported throughout your session.
Is what I share with my private practice therapist confidential?
Absolutely. Confidentiality is a cornerstone of our private practice. What you share in your sessions is held in the strictest confidence, with a few legally mandated exceptions which we will discuss during your initial consultation.
How can I schedule an appointment with your private practice therapist?
Scheduling an appointment is straightforward. You can easily reach out to us via email or phone, or use our online booking system. We will then connect to arrange your initial consultation at a time that suits your schedule.
