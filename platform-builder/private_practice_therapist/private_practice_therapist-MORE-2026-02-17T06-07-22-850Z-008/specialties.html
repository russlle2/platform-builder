Areas I support

Common themes people bring: decisions that shift daily life, patterns that keep returning, and interpersonal strain. Below are typical areas of focus and what we might notice in early sessions.

Transitions & Decisions

Sorting through career, relationship, or family changes with values-based clarity and practical steps.

Stress, Overwhelm & Burnout

Working on pacing, boundaries, and small routines to reduce constant activation and preserve energy.

Relationships & Communication

Clearer requests, boundary-setting, and reducing reactivity — whether in partnership, family, or work contexts.

Adjustment after Loss

Grief and life reorientation — creating ways to remember, to adapt, and to find steadying practices.
