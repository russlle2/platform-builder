Professional boundaries & confidentiality

Conversations in therapy are confidential, with important exceptions required by law (harm to self/others, abuse of a minor/elder). I can discuss limits of confidentiality during our first appointment. Therapy is not emergency care; if you are in crisis or danger, call local emergency services or a crisis line.

Scope & collaboration

I provide psychotherapy and brief coaching for adults. If your needs are outside my scope, I will recommend appropriate community or medical resources. Our work is collaborative; I aim to support you in making decisions that match your values and life context.
