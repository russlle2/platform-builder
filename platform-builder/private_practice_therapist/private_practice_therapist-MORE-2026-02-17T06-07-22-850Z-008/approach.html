How I work together with clients

Short-term focuses, longer-term partnership, and an emphasis on practical experiments. I blend structured strategies with moment-to-moment attention so sessions feel useful and grounded.

Process
  • Session 1: clarify concerns, set a short goal, and co-design a plan.
  • Weekly/biweekly work: a mix of skill practice, reflective exploration, and concrete steps.
  • Between-session experiments: small, measurable changes to test what helps.

What I ask of clients

  • Attend with a willingness to try small experiments.
  • Bring brief notes when helpful (things you notice, one recent example of the challenge).
  • Share any safety concerns so we can plan appropriate support.

Ethics & limits

Notes on confidentiality: I keep session content private except in legal exceptions (harm, abuse, court orders). Therapy is not a substitute for emergency services or urgent medical care.
