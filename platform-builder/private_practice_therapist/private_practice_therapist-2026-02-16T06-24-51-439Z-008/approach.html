A phased clinical map

Therapy often unfolds in phases. This is a simple map I share with clients to set expectations and collaborate on pacing.

1. Stabilize & orient
Create safety, build a contained plan, and introduce brief regulation practices.
2. Process & skill building
Address patterns and practice new ways of relating to thoughts, sensations, and people.
3. Integration & independence
Embed practices, prepare for setbacks, and transition out of regular therapy with a long-term plan.

Micro-habits I ask clients to try

Short, sustainable practices that support each phase.

  • Daily 2-minute grounding before important conversations
  • Evening reflection: one sentence about what helped today
  • Pre-emptive boundary note: rehearse a brief phrase before setting a limit

Diagnostic & intake framing

Initial work is assessment-focused: understanding patterns, supports, and risks. We co-create goals and a realistic timeline.

Therapeutic metaphors

I like simple metaphors: learning to steer a boat rather than trying to calm the ocean — practices change how you respond, not the presence of waves.
