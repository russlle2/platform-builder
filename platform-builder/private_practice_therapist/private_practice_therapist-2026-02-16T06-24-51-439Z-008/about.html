How we begin — an orientation

Our first sessions focus on creating a safe context: understanding what brought you here, clarifying priorities, and mapping supports. This is a collaborative diagnostic conversation, not a one-time test. Below is what to expect.

Session intake
  • Review of current concerns and goals
  • Brief history and relational patterns
  • Collaborative safety planning
Clinical scope
  • Evidence-informed approaches (CBT, somatic, EMDR-informed integration)
  • Focus on relationships, transitions, and stress
  • Not a substitute for emergency or inpatient care

The cohort pathway — an invitation

I run a small cohort-style program called the "Companion Cohort" designed for people who want structured support over 12 weeks. It blends short group check-ins with individual sessions and home practices.

What’s included
  • 12 weekly group sessions (90 minutes)
  • Two 50-minute individual sessions
  • Guided micro-practices and reflection prompts
Who it suits
  • People seeking community and structure
  • Those in life transition or experiencing chronic stress
  • Not appropriate for acute crisis or active suicidal intent

Micro-habits to steady the nervous system

Small practices that compound: brief breath work, two-minute body checks, writing one sentence of acknowledgment each evening.

  • Box breath — 4-4-4-4 for two minutes
  • Evening noticing: name one small win
  • Grounding: five senses check during moments of overwhelm
