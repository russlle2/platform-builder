Selected questions

Do you offer sliding scale or reduced-rate sessions?
I reserve a small number of reduced-rate spots. Please contact me with a brief description of need and financial range; these are evaluated on a case-by-case basis.
Will sessions be confidential?
Yes. Sessions are confidential within legal and ethical limits (e.g., imminent risk of harm, mandated reporting). I use secure record-keeping and HIPAA-compliant telehealth platforms.
How long will therapy take?
There is no fixed timeline. Some people find relief in a few months; others engage in longer-term work. We’ll review goals periodically and adjust the plan together.
What if I need to cancel?
I ask for 48 hours notice when possible. Late cancellations or missed sessions without notice may be charged at the session rate, except in emergencies.
Can I use insurance?
I am out-of-network. I provide superbills that some insurance plans may reimburse. Check with your insurer for details.
