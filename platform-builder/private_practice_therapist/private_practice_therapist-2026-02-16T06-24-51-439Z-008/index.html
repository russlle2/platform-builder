Micro-practices you can try today

Small, consistent acts support bigger change. Choose one and try it for a week.

3-Minute Ground

Slow inhalation for 4 counts, hold 2, exhale 6. Repeat three times with feet on the floor.

Boundary Whisper

Set a one-line limit today: "I can only do X until Y." Say it softly to a trusted person or write it down.

Gratitude Scan

List three small steady things that felt holding today — not grand, just true.

Micro-Compassion

When intensity rises, place a hand on your chest and say: "This is hard. I'm here."
