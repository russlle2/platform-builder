What kind of physical pain does Private Practice Therapy address for new mothers?

Our Private Practice Therapist services are specifically tailored for postpartum physical discomforts such as back pain from carrying or nursing, shoulder and neck tension, pelvic discomfort, wrist pain, and general bodily fatigue that impacts your daily functioning. We focus on how these physical sensations are experienced and held in your body.

How is somatic healing different from physical therapy for postpartum recovery?

While both are valuable, physical therapy often focuses on strengthening and rehabilitation exercises. Somatic healing in Private Practice Therapy, on the other hand, emphasizes tuning into your body's internal sensations and responses to release deep-seated tension and pain patterns, fostering a more intuitive and gentle approach to your physical recovery. It often works in conjunction with other care.

Will I have to do strenuous exercises during my Private Practice Therapist sessions?

No, our Private Practice Therapist sessions prioritize gentle, mindful practices. We understand your body is recovering. The focus is on subtle movements, breathwork, and guided attention to body sensations, rather than strenuous exercises. Your comfort and capacity are always respected and prioritized in every session.

How quickly can I expect to feel relief from my postpartum physical pain with Private Practice Therapy?

Every new mother's experience is unique, so the timeline for relief can vary. Many clients report feeling a greater sense of ease and reduced tension after just a few Private Practice Therapist sessions. Our goal is to help you build awareness and tools that provide sustained comfort and improve your overall well-being as you navigate postpartum life.
