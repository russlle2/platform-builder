Understanding Postpartum Physical Strain in Private Practice Therapy

The demands of new motherhood—lifting, feeding, comforting—can introduce unexpected aches and pains. From back strain to pelvic floor discomfort, these physical realities are often overlooked. Our Private Practice Therapist approach focuses specifically on identifying and addressing these somatic experiences, helping you navigate the physical demands of caring for your little one with greater comfort and resilience. You don't have to push through the pain.
