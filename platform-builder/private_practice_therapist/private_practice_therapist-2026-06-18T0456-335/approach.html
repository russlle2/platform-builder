From Cram Session Tension to Calm: Private Practice Therapy Addresses Your Body's Stress Signals

College life is intense, and your body often holds onto that stress. Headaches from late-night studying, jaw pain from clenching through exams, or persistent neck and back stiffness – these aren't just discomforts, they're signals. As a Private Practice Therapist, I work with you to understand these physical manifestations of academic pressure and anxiety, offering somatic techniques to release chronic tension and restore a sense of ease in your body.
