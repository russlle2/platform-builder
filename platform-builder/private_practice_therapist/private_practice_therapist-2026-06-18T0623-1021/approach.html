Private Practice Therapist

Re-calibrating Your Internal Landscape: Private Practice Therapy for Nervous System Regulation

Chronic illness and injury often leave an imprint of heightened vigilance on your nervous system. We work to gently re-pattern these responses, fostering a sense of safety within your own body. Through targeted techniques, your system learns to distinguish between past alarms and present peace, allowing for a more harmonious internal experience. This isn't about ignoring your past, but rather teaching your body new ways to be present and at ease.
