Realigning Your Frame: A Private Practice Therapist Focus on Postural Health for Parents

Daily life with young children often means contorting your body in ways that create tension. From carrying toddlers to bending over cribs, these movements impact your physical well-being. Here, we explore gentle, mindful approaches to release physical strain, improve your alignment, and cultivate a deeper connection to your body's innate wisdom, helping you move through your day with greater ease and less discomfort.
