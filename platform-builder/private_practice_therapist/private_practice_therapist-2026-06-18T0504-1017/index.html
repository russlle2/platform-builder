Frequently Asked Questions

How does Private Practice Therapy specifically address stress for new mothers?
Our therapy is tailored to the unique physiological and emotional demands of postpartum. We focus on regulating the autonomic nervous system, which is often dysregulated after birth and during the intense demands of newborn care. This means learning concrete strategies to shift out of fight-or-flight responses and into states of calm, specifically addressing sleep deprivation, hormonal fluctuations, and the emotional weight of new motherhood.
Can Private Practice Therapy truly help with my overwhelming feelings?
Yes, it can. Overwhelm often stems from an activated nervous system. We provide you with precise tools to down-regulate that activation. By understanding the neuroscience behind your feelings and practicing specific regulation techniques, you can learn to modulate intense emotions, find more mental clarity, and reclaim a sense of control amidst the beautiful chaos of new parenthood.
What if I'm too tired to even think about therapy right now?
We understand that exhaustion is a significant part of postpartum. Our sessions are designed to be supportive and low-energy for you. We can begin with very gentle, accessible practices that don't require immense mental effort, focusing on passive regulation techniques to gradually build your capacity. Your therapist will adapt to your energy levels, ensuring the process feels restorative, not draining.
How long does nervous system regulation typically take in Private Practice Therapy?
The timeline for nervous system regulation is unique to each individual, as it depends on your specific experiences and needs. However, you can expect to begin noticing subtle shifts and gaining practical tools within the first few sessions. Our goal is to empower you with lasting strategies, fostering sustained calm and resilience long after your therapy concludes.
