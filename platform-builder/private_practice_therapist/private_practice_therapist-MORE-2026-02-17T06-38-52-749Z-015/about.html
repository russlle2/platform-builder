Practice notes from a clinician who listens first

I work with adults who are navigating turning points — when choices feel heavy or the next step is unclear. Together we name what matters and find small, manageable ways forward. I blend evidence-informed methods with practical experiments you can try between sessions.

What a session looks like

We’ll start with what brought you in, explore how it shows up in daily life, and agree on 1–2 practical steps you can try before the next visit. Sessions are collaborative and paced to fit your needs.

Confidentiality & scope

Your care is confidential within legal and ethical limits. I am a licensed clinician; our work is supportive and not a substitute for emergency care. If you are in crisis or feel you may harm yourself or others, please call your local emergency number or a crisis line immediately.
