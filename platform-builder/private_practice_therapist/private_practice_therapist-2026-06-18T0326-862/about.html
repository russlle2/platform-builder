Why is postural health important for new mothers in Private Practice Therapy?

Postural health is crucial because pregnancy and childbirth significantly alter your body's alignment and core strength. Addressing this helps prevent common postpartum issues like back pain, pelvic floor dysfunction, and shoulder tension, allowing you to care for your baby without discomfort.

What kind of exercises will I do in Private Practice Therapy?

Our approach involves gentle, targeted movements designed to restore core and pelvic floor function, improve spinal alignment, and release tension. We focus on functional exercises that translate directly to your daily activities as a new mother, always respecting your body's current stage of recovery.

When can I start Private Practice Therapy after giving birth?

It's generally recommended to wait until you've received clearance from your healthcare provider, typically around 6-8 weeks postpartum. However, we can discuss your specific situation and tailor our approach to your individual recovery timeline during an initial consultation.

How does body awareness help me as a new mother?

Developing body awareness helps you recognize and respond to your body's needs more effectively. It empowers you to adjust your movements, identify early signs of strain, and understand how your body is adapting to motherhood, leading to greater comfort and confidence in your physical capabilities.
