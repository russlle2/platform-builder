Therapeutic approach

Grounded, relational, and oriented toward useful changes you can try between sessions.
Nest Who I Am Offerings How I Work
I combine relational understanding with behavioral and narrative techniques tailored to the person in front of me. Therapy is an experiment we run together — small, observable changes, reviewed and adjusted.

A brief outline of sessions

  1. Check-in and mood/context.
  2. Focused exploration of one target area.
  3. Co-created plan: 1–2 simple, testable steps to try before we meet again.
  4. Brief review and adaptation each session.

This structure is flexible; I tailor pacing and focus to what’s tolerable and useful for you.

Boundaries & confidentiality

Communication and records are confidential except in situations required by law (e.g., imminent risk of harm). We will discuss limits and expectations during the initial intake so you know how information is handled.

If you are in immediate danger or a medical/psychiatric emergency, please contact emergency services or a local crisis line.
