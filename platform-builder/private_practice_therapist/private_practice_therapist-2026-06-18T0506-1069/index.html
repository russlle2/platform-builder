Frequently Asked Questions

How does Private Practice Therapy help with the physical stress of being an athlete?
For athletes and active individuals, Private Practice Therapy offers specific techniques to downregulate your nervous system. This helps reduce the physiological impact of training, competition, and recovery demands, leading to less muscle tension, better sleep, and an improved sense of calm in your body.
I'm not familiar with wellness practices. Is this Private Practice Therapy right for me?
Absolutely. Our Private Practice Therapy is designed to be gentle and accessible, regardless of your prior experience. We'll guide you through simple, effective methods for nervous system regulation that you can easily integrate into your routine, building your inner resilience step by step.
Will Private Practice Therapy interfere with my training schedule or performance?
Quite the opposite. By focusing on stress reduction and nervous system regulation, Private Practice Therapy aims to enhance your recovery, improve mental clarity, and potentially optimize your performance by reducing internal friction. The practices we explore are designed to complement, not complicate, your athletic life.
What can I expect in a session with a Private Practice Therapist for nervous system regulation?
In a Private Practice Therapy session, you can expect a calm, supportive space. We'll gently explore the specific stresses you're experiencing and introduce practical, body-centered techniques to help your nervous system find balance. The focus is always on creating tangible relief and fostering a deeper connection to your body's inherent capacity for calm.
