Frequently Asked Questions

How does Private Practice Therapy help with chronic illness or injury recovery?
Our Private Practice Therapy focuses on the physiological and psychological impacts of chronic conditions. We employ specific techniques to reduce chronic stress, regulate your nervous system, and help your body and mind find a more stable, peaceful state, which is crucial for sustained recovery.
What kind of nervous system regulation techniques can I expect to learn in Private Practice Therapy?
In our Private Practice Therapy, you will learn practical, evidence-based tools designed to soothe an overactive nervous system. These may include specific breathing exercises, gentle movement practices, and guided visualization techniques, all aimed at restoring your body's natural capacity for calm and reducing sensations of unease.
Is this Private Practice Therapy suitable if my physical symptoms are still present?
Yes, absolutely. Our Private Practice Therapy is designed to complement your medical care. By addressing the nervous system's response to ongoing physical symptoms, we can help reduce their perceived intensity, improve your coping mechanisms, and enhance your overall quality of life, even when physical challenges persist.
How long does Private Practice Therapy for nervous system regulation typically last?
The duration of Private Practice Therapy varies for each individual, as recovery from chronic illness or injury is a personal process. We work at a pace that respects your unique needs and capacity, focusing on building sustainable skills for nervous system regulation that you can integrate into your daily life for long-term benefit.
