Frequently Asked Questions

Why is sleep improvement so crucial for college students with academic pressure and anxiety?
For college students, excellent sleep isn't just a luxury; it's a non-negotiable foundation for managing anxiety, improving focus, retaining information, and performing optimally on exams and assignments. When you're well-rested, you're more resilient to stress and your cognitive abilities are at their peak.

Absolutely. When your sleep is optimized, your brain functions more efficiently.
