Frequently Asked Questions

Why is sleep so crucial for athletes and active people managing physical stress?
For athletes and active individuals, sleep isn't just rest; it's active recovery. During deep sleep, your body repairs muscle tissue, consolidates memories, balances hormones essential for performance, and reduces inflammatory markers. Without adequate restorative sleep, your physical recovery is compromised, increasing injury risk and hindering athletic progress.
How does Private Practice Therapy specifically address sleep for athletes?
Our Private Practice Therapy goes beyond general sleep hygiene. We consider your training schedule, competition demands, and specific physical stressors. We utilize techniques that calm the nervous system, optimize circadian rhythms, and address performance anxiety that often interferes with sleep, creating a tailored plan that supports your athletic goals.
What kind of techniques can I expect to learn to improve my sleep?
You'll learn a range of practical techniques tailored to your needs. This might include specific breathwork to downregulate your nervous system post-training, mindfulness practices to quiet a busy mind, strategies for optimizing your pre-sleep routine, and identifying environmental factors impacting your rest. Our aim is to empower you with sustainable tools.
Is this approach suitable if I'm already seeing a coach or physical therapist?
Absolutely. Our Private Practice Therapy complements your existing support system. We work in tandem with your coaches, trainers, and physical therapists to ensure a holistic approach to your well-being. Improving your sleep will enhance the effectiveness of your other recovery and training efforts, creating synergistic benefits for your performance.
