What exactly does 'postural health' mean for a new mother in Private Practice Therapy?

For new mothers, postural health in Private Practice Therapy means understanding and gently adjusting how you hold and move your body after childbirth. It’s about creating stability and comfort while you feed, carry, and care for your baby, reducing common aches and pains, and feeling more aligned without rigid rules.

How is body awareness relevant to my postpartum recovery in Private Practice Therapy?

Body awareness in Private Practice Therapy for new mothers helps you reconnect with your body after the significant changes of pregnancy and birth. It allows you to notice tension, understand what movements feel supportive versus strained, and build a more intuitive sense of your own physical needs as you recover and adapt to motherhood.

Will Private Practice Therapy help with the physical demands of caring for a newborn?

Absolutely. Our Private Practice Therapy focuses on giving you practical tools and insights to manage the physical demands of caring for your newborn. We'll explore ergonomic ways to lift, hold, and feed your baby, aiming to minimize strain on your back, neck, and shoulders, making your daily interactions more comfortable and sustainable.

Is Private Practice Therapy suitable if I had a C-section or a complicated birth?

Yes, Private Practice Therapy is absolutely suitable. We tailor our approach to your individual birth experience and recovery needs. If you've had a C-section or a complicated birth, we'll focus on gentle, supportive strategies for scar tissue awareness, core re-engagement, and overall postural comfort, always respecting your body's unique healing timeline.
