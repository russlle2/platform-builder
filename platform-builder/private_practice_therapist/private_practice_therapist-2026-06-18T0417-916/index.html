Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I schedule sessions?
Most clients book quarterly check-ins synced with seasonal shifts (e.g., post-December holidays, pre-spring planning). We prevent burnout before it escalates.
What if my schedule is packed?
We offer 25-minute sessions designed to fit between meetings. No time for 'self-care'—just practical steps that integrate with your existing work rhythm.
How is this different from general therapy?
We focus exclusively on seasonal burnout triggers for professionals in your age group. No broad 'healing journeys'—just targeted interventions for your April deadlines or December travel demands.
Can you work with my company’s HR program?
Yes. We provide confidential preventive care that complements corporate programs without requiring you to disclose your sessions to HR.
