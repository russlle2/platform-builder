Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Private Practice Therapist specifically help me with college anxiety?
As your Private Practice Therapist, I understand the unique pressures of college life—deadlines, social adjustments, future worries. We'll work together to identify the root causes of your anxiety, develop coping strategies for academic stress, improve time management, and build resilience so you can navigate your studies and personal life more confidently.
What does 'seasonal wellness' mean for a college student and how does it relate to therapy?
Seasonal wellness for college students means understanding how things like daylight changes, academic cycles (like midterm season or finals), and even holiday breaks can impact your mood, energy, and stress levels. As your Private Practice Therapist, we'll explore these patterns and create proactive strategies to support your mental health throughout the year, preventing burnout before it starts.
I'm worried about fitting therapy into my busy college schedule. How does this work?
I get it, college schedules are packed! As a Private Practice Therapist, I offer flexible scheduling options to accommodate your classes, study time, and extracurriculars. The goal is to make therapy a supportive part of your routine, not another source of stress. We can discuss appointment times that genuinely work for you during our initial consultation.
Is seeing a Private Practice Therapist different from college counseling services?
Yes, while college counseling is valuable, a Private Practice Therapist often offers more personalized, long-term, and consistent care. You'll work with the same therapist throughout your time here, ensuring continuity and a deeper understanding of your specific needs, without the typical session limits sometimes found in campus services.
