Seasonal Shifts and Your Mental Health: A Proactive Private Practice Approach

Ever notice how the changing seasons can impact your mood and energy? From the autumn slump to spring fever, these shifts can amplify academic stress and anxiety. With a Private Practice Therapist, we'll explore how these seasonal changes affect you personally and develop proactive coping mechanisms. This isn't about just reacting; it's about building resilience so you can maintain your balance and focus, whether it's midterm season or a long winter break.
