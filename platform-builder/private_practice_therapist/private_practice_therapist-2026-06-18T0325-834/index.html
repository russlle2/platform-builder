Frequently Asked Questions

How can Private Practice Therapy specifically help me manage physical stress as an athlete?
Our Private Practice Therapy focuses on helping you recognize the subtle ways physical stress impacts your body and performance. We'll work together to identify tension patterns, develop effective relaxation techniques, and create strategies to integrate mindful recovery into your training, helping you perform better and feel more balanced.
What does 'postural health' mean in the context of Private Practice Therapy for active people?
For active people, postural health goes beyond just standing up straight. In Private Practice Therapy, it means understanding how your body aligns and moves during your sport or activity. We'll explore how minor misalignments can lead to inefficiencies or discomfort, and help you cultivate body awareness to optimize your movement patterns and prevent future issues.
Will Private Practice Therapy interfere with my training schedule?
Not at all. Our Private Practice Therapy sessions are designed to complement your active lifestyle. We'll collaborate with you to create a plan that fits your schedule and supports your training goals, focusing on techniques and insights you can integrate seamlessly into your routine to enhance your overall well-being and performance.
Is Private Practice Therapy only for athletes dealing with injuries?
While Private Practice Therapy can certainly support recovery from injuries, our focus extends to proactive well-being. We help active individuals prevent issues by improving body awareness, managing physical stress before it escalates, and optimizing postural health for sustained performance and long-term physical comfort, regardless of injury status.
