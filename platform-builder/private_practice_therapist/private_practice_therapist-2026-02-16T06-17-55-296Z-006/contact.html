Common questions about connecting

What happens during our first conversation?
We'll review confidential intake information, discuss your immediate concerns, and set collaborative goals. The focus is understanding needs and practical next steps, not labeling.
Do you accept insurance?
I currently offer private-pay services. I can provide a superbill you may submit to your insurer for out-of-network reimbursement. Please ask for details.
What if I’m in crisis?
If you are in immediate danger or are thinking of harming yourself, call local emergency services or 911 (USA). If you're in the U.S., you can also call or text 988 for the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. I am not available for crisis management via email—please use emergency resources.
