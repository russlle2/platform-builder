Questions people often ask

What do sessions feel like?

Sessions are collaborative and paced to your needs. I weave listening, gentle inquiry, and concrete skills. You set the focus each time.

Do you take insurance?

I offer private pay and can provide superbills for insurance reimbursement where permitted. We’ll discuss details during intake.

How long are sessions?

Standard sessions are 50 minutes. Extended initial or couples sessions can be arranged—ask when you book.

What if I’m in crisis?

If you are in immediate danger call emergency services. If you feel unsafe between sessions contact local crisis lines or go to the nearest emergency room.
