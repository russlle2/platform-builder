How scheduling and payment typically work

  1. Reach out via the booking page or phone to request an initial 15-minute consult.
  2. We confirm the session type and available times; you select what fits.
  3. Payment is collected securely prior to or at the time of the first session (cards accepted).
  4. If we agree on a package, sessions are scheduled to create momentum and continuity.
If you need documentation for insurance reimbursement, I provide a receipt (superbill) on request; please check with your carrier first.

Common questions about fees

Do you accept insurance?
I am not in-network with most carriers. I can provide a superbill for out-of-network reimbursement; policies vary by plan.
What if I can't afford the listed rate?
There are limited sliding scale slots. If finances are a barrier, please reach out and we can discuss options.
Are there cancellations fees?
I ask for 48 hours' notice for cancellations. Late cancellations or missed sessions are billed at the session rate except in emergencies.
