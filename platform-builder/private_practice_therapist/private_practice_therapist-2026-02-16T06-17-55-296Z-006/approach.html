Questions about the approach

How do you track progress?
We set measurable goals and review them regularly. Progress isn't linear; we adapt the work when needed.
Will I be asked to relive trauma?
No. Work is paced to your readiness. If processing feels unsafe, we focus on stabilization and resourcing first.
Can I bring my partner?
Sometimes. I offer individual and occasionally adjunctive sessions when clinically appropriate. We discuss boundaries and goals up front.
