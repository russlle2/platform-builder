Questions people often bring before our first session

Below are practical answers to common concerns about therapy logistics, what to expect, and how I work. If your question isn’t listed, please reach out.
How do I know if I should start now or wait?
If distress is affecting day-to-day functioning, relationships, or sleep, reaching out now can help. We can start with a brief consult to see if we’re a good fit.
What is a first session like?
We use the first session to understand what brings you, review brief history, set priorities, and co-create goals. It’s practical and collaborative — you set the pace.
How long does meaningful progress take?
Every person is different. Some find relief in a few targeted sessions; others benefit from ongoing weekly work. We track small goals and adjust as we go.
Do you work with couples or adolescents?
My practice focuses on adults in individual therapy. For couples or youth, I can provide referrals aligned with your needs.
How do you protect confidentiality?
Sessions are private. I use secure scheduling and billing systems. I will explain limits to confidentiality (e.g., safety, court orders) at intake.
Can I use insurance?
I am not in-network with most insurers. I provide superbills for possible out-of-network reimbursement. Check with your insurer for details.
