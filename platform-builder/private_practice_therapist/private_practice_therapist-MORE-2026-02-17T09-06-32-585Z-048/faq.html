Questions people often ask

Below are practical answers about sessions, privacy, and what happens if you need urgent help. If you don’t see your question, email {{EMAIL}}.

Recommendation: Focused set of sessions
What happens in a first visit?▾
The first visit is time to describe what brought you here, review practical goals, and consider options. We'll talk about scheduling, fees, and what to expect if you continue. You can ask about how I work and what paperwork is needed.
How private are the sessions?▾
I keep your records and conversations confidential. Exceptions are limited and defined by law: immediate risk to yourself or others, credible suspicion of abuse of a minor or vulnerable person, or a court order. If you have concerns about privacy, we can review them before beginning.
Am I required to continue?▾
No. Your care is voluntary. We review progress and goals periodically. If the fit isn’t right, I’ll offer referrals and help coordinate next steps.
What if I’m in crisis between sessions?▾
I am not available as an emergency service. If you are at immediate risk or feel you might harm yourself or someone else, please call local emergency services or your local crisis line right away. If you’re unsure of resources in this area, contact {{EMAIL}} and I will help locate options.
Billing and insurance?▾
I provide receipts that many clients submit to their insurers. Coverage varies, and I can provide the details you need to check with your plan. Payment questions can be discussed confidentially.
If you are in immediate danger
Call local emergency services or the crisis line in your region. I cannot provide immediate crisis response via email or outside scheduled appointments. If you’re unsure who to call, include your current location when contacting local emergency services so they can respond appropriately.

Clinical note: I work with adults on a range of concerns. I avoid definitive promises about outcomes — therapy is collaborative and results vary. If your needs are outside my scope, I will help connect you to appropriate care.
