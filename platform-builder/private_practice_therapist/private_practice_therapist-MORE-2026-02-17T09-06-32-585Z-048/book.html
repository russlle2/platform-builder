Confidentiality & limits

Sessions are confidential within legal limits. These exceptions are narrowly defined: if there is an imminent risk of harm, suspected abuse of a minor or vulnerable adult, or a court order, confidentiality may be breached to keep people safe. I will explain these limits and answer any questions before we begin.

Session boundaries

I am available during scheduled visits. Outside of sessions I respond to messages with reasonable timing but do not provide crisis response or continuous monitoring. If you need urgent help, contact local emergency services or your local crisis resources.

If you are in immediate danger:
Call local emergency services right now. I am not an emergency provider and cannot guarantee immediate responses outside of scheduled appointments.
