About the clinician

I work with adults who want practical tools and respectful reflection. My practice centers on noticing patterns, building tolerable change, and clarifying values as a way to move toward decisions that fit your life. I use evidence-informed techniques and tailor them to the problem at hand — whether that looks like a short block of sessions or a series over time.

I hold confidentiality seriously: sessions are private within the bounds of the law. I will review limits to confidentiality with you before we begin. I do not provide emergency services or crisis management in the moment.

How I structure work

I offer focused series (4–8 sessions) and single-session intensives for a targeted topic. Series are framed around an agreed goal and include collaborative homework when helpful. Pricing and scheduling details are on the Investment page.

Professional note

I maintain clear boundaries around session length, fees, and cancellations. I will present a written agreement that outlines scope of care, confidentiality, and contact expectations. If care needs fall outside my scope, I will discuss referrals and coordinate with other providers with your permission.

If you are in immediate danger, call emergency services. For urgent emotional support in the United States, consider dialing 988. Contact is for scheduling and questions only; clinical matters are handled in session.
