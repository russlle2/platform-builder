How we can work together

I match structure to the question: brief, skills-oriented plans for immediate relief; exploratory work to understand patterns; or a blend of both. Below you'll find the practical steps I usually follow and a live selector to help pick a starting point.

Typical flow

1
First contact
A brief call or message to clarify needs and logistics. We decide together whether a single session, a short series, or a referral is most appropriate.
2
Intake & plan
The first session includes a compact assessment and a co-created plan with measurable aims when helpful.
3
Work phase
Sessions may include skill practice, reflection, and small experiments between meetings. We review progress regularly and adjust pace.
4
Wrap & next steps
When goals are met or it's time to pause, we close a block intentionally and plan follow-up if needed.

I document details needed for continuity of care and billing. Confidentiality is maintained within legal limits; I will explain these limits during intake and in writing. I do not provide emergency intervention, and I will discuss referrals if urgent care is appropriate.
