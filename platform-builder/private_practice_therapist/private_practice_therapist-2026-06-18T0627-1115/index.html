Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Private Practice Therapist help with sleep when I'm experiencing major life changes?
As your Private Practice Therapist, I understand that significant life transitions often bring emotional turbulence that directly impacts your sleep. We will explore the specific anxieties, grief, or excitement tied to your transition, and develop personalized practices rooted in ancient wisdom to calm your nervous system and invite deeper, more consistent rest.
My mind races at night due to my life transition. How will this Private Practice Therapist approach help?
That racing mind is a common response to profound change. As your Private Practice Therapist, we will delve into techniques for quieting the internal dialogue that disrupts your sleep, drawing on meditative practices and mindful awareness to soothe your thoughts and gently guide your mind towards a state of peaceful repose, fostering inner stillness.
Is this Private Practice Therapist service only for those with severe sleep disorders during transitions?
Not at all. While I can support those with significant sleep disturbances, my Private Practice Therapist service is also for anyone navigating a major life transition who feels their rest is compromised, even subtly. If you desire more profound, restorative sleep and a deeper connection to your inner peace during these shifts, this space is for you.
What kind of ancient healing traditions does this Private Practice Therapist incorporate for sleep?
As your Private Practice Therapist, I draw from a rich tapestry of traditions emphasizing mind-body-spirit connection. This might include guided meditation, breathwork informed by ancient yogic principles, gentle energy practices, and ritualistic approaches to winding down, all tailored to your unique needs for restorative rest during your life's transitions.
