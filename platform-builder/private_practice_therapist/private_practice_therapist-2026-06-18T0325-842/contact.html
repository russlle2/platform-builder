Understanding Your Postpartum Body: A Private Practice Therapist Focus

Your body undergoes profound changes during pregnancy and childbirth. We specialize in addressing the nuanced postural shifts that can occur, such as pelvic floor tension, diastasis recti, and upper back discomfort from nursing and carrying. Our Private Practice Therapist approach offers targeted support to help you reconnect with your body's innate wisdom and restore its functional integrity, ensuring you feel strong and comfortable in your new role.
