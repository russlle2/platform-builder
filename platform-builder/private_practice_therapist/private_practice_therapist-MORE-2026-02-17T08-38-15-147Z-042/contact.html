Confidentiality & scope

Conversations with your clinician are kept private in line with professional standards, except where disclosure is required by law (e.g., imminent harm). Quick questions

How will the intake note be used?
The short summary you build is for you to share with the clinician to make the first session more focused. It is stored only in your browser unless you copy or save it.
Is this a diagnosis?
No. The intake tool is a preparation aid and not a substitution for a clinical evaluation.
Can I book from this page?
Yes — use the Schedule link at the top or the primary button above to request an appointment.
