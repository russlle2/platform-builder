Frequently asked

How confidential is this work?

Sessions are recorded in secure clinical notes. Confidentiality follows standard professional limits: if there is a safety risk to you or others, or a legal requirement, I must act accordingly. We can talk about how to keep notes and communication in ways that feel safe for you.

What if I am in crisis?

This practice does not provide emergency services. If you are in immediate danger or need urgent medical help, please call local emergency services or go to your nearest emergency department. For urgent mental health help in the U.S., you can call or text 988.

What framework do you use?

I draw from evidence-informed approaches and tailor them to your needs. Our work will be collaborative and pragmatic, focusing on what you say matters most.
