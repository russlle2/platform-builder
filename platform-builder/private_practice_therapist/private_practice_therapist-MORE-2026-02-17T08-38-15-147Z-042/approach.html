Clinical stance — clear, calm, collaborative

I aim for practical clarity: we notice patterns, pick a few small experiments, and review what changes. The work is collaborative — your priorities guide the plan.

1. Intake & goals

We start by listening closely to your current concerns and what you'd like to change. Together we clarify a handful of practical goals.

2. Tools & experiments

Sessions often mix reflective work with specific practices you can apply between visits. Progress is measured by small, meaningful shifts you notice in daily life.

3. Review & adjust

We revisit the plan regularly, refining what helps and setting new next steps when appropriate.
