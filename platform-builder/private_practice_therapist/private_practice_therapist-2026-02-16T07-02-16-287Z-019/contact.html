Common misconceptions — and what research-informed practice actually offers

Myth: Therapy fixes you quickly. Truth: Durable change usually requires experimentation, practice, and ongoing support. I use structured, evidence-informed methods and adjust based on what you report works.

Core supports I emphasize

Meaningful change

Quick questions

Do you accept insurance?
I am currently offering private-pay services. I can provide a superbill if you wish to seek partial reimbursement; coverage varies by plan.
How soon can I start?
Typically within 2–4 weeks for new clients. Membership or continuity options can provide faster scheduling for ongoing work.
Can I text you in between sessions?
For continuity-membership clients, brief check-ins are possible within agreed boundaries. For others, email is preferred unless urgent.
