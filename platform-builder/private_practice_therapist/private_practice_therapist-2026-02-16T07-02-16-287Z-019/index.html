Therapy: myths and what science-informed practice actually offers

Myth

Therapy will instantly fix every problem.

Truth

Therapy is a structured partnership; change often takes time, reflection, and repeated practice. I focus on small, sustainable shifts you can use outside the session.

Myth

Good therapy means the therapist tells you what to do.

Truth

I bring evidence-informed methods and guide you to choices that fit your values and context — you remain the expert on your life.

Rebuilding after relationship rupture
