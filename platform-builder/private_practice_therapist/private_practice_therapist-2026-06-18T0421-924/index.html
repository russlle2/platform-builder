Frequently Asked Questions

How is this different from 'therapy for parents'?
We don’t treat parenting as a problem to fix. Instead, we focus on the *predictable stress cycles* that come with seasons (school breaks, weather changes, holidays) and build concrete, small adjustments you can make *now*—not after you’ve crashed.
What if my child’s tantrums make me feel overwhelmed?
We’ll identify what in the tantrum cycle triggers *your* stress (e.g., timing, lack of sleep cues), then create a 2-sentence response plan. You’ll learn to recognize those early signs before they escalate—no 'calm down' advice that ignores your actual fatigue.
How do I fit this into my chaotic schedule?
Sessions fit around your existing routines—not the other way around. Most clients schedule during school drop-off or after bedtime. We also provide a 5-minute weekly check-in template you can do while making coffee.
Is this focused on my child or me?
It’s about *your* stress management. When you’re not in survival mode, you naturally respond better to your child. We use your child’s daily patterns (meals, sleep, school) to build your personal stress prevention system.
