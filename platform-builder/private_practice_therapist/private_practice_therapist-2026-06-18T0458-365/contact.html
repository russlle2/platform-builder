Private Practice Therapist

Releasing Physical Pain & Somatic Tension from College Stress in Private Practice Therapy

College life can really build up in your shoulders, neck, and gut. Here, we focus on how anxiety and academic pressure manifest physically. Through somatic techniques, you’ll learn to identify and gently release the knots of stress held in your body, offering tangible relief from tension headaches, digestive issues, and chronic discomfort. It's about understanding your body's signals and finding practical ways to soothe them.
