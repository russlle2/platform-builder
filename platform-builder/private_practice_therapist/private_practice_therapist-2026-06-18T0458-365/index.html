Frequently Asked Questions

How does Private Practice Therapy specifically address the physical symptoms of my college anxiety?
We'll explore how anxiety and academic pressure show up in your body—think tight shoulders, stomachaches, or restlessness. We then use somatic techniques, which are body-centered, to help you gently release that stored tension and create a sense of calm and ease within yourself.
I'm always stressed about my grades. Can Private Practice Therapy help with the physical impact of academic pressure?
Absolutely. Academic pressure can lead to chronic muscle tension, headaches, and sleep issues. In our Private Practice Therapy sessions, we'll work on recognizing these physical cues and developing grounding practices that help your nervous system regulate, allowing you to approach your studies with more calm and focus.
What kind of 'somatic healing' will we do in Private Practice Therapy? Is it like yoga or massage?
Somatic healing in Private Practice Therapy isn't physical exercise or massage. It involves gentle awareness practices, breathwork, and guided movements to help you feel and understand sensations in your body. It's about building a stronger mind-body connection to release stress and foster emotional regulation, all within a therapeutic conversation.
I'm a college student on a tight budget. Are your Private Practice Therapy services accessible?
We understand the financial considerations of college students. Please reach out to discuss our fee structure and any potential sliding scale options. Our goal is to make supportive Private Practice Therapy accessible so you can prioritize your well-being during your studies.
