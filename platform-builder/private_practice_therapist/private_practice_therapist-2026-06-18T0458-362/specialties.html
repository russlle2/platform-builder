Private Practice Therapist

Grounding Your Postpartum Recovery with Private Practice Therapy

Your body's landscape has shifted, and with it, new sensations, aches, and emotional currents. We focus on specific discomforts common after birth, such as pelvic floor tension, back strain from nursing, or general body fatigue. Our approach helps you reconnect with your inner strength, allowing you to find a calm center amidst the beautiful chaos of new motherhood. We address the physical manifestations of stress and the emotional weight often carried in your muscles and fascia.
