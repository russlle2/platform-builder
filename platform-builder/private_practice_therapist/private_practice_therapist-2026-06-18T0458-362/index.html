Frequently Asked Questions

What kind of physical pain can your Private Practice Therapy help with during postpartum?
We specifically address the physical discomforts new mothers often experience, such as persistent back pain from holding and feeding, shoulder and neck tension, pelvic floor discomfort, or general body aches and fatigue. Our somatic approach helps you understand and gently release these stored tensions, leading to tangible relief and greater ease in your daily movements.
Is this a replacement for physical therapy or other medical care for postpartum recovery?
Our Private Practice Therapy complements medical care and physical therapy. We focus on the emotional and nervous system aspects of physical pain, helping you build a deeper connection to your body's signals and release chronic tension that might not be fully addressed by other modalities. We work collaboratively to ensure you're receiving comprehensive support for your postpartum well-being.
How does somatic healing work for new mothers in their recovery period?
Somatic healing helps you tune into your body's sensations and release patterns of tension or emotional stress that are held physically. For new mothers, this means gently addressing the body's response to childbirth, hormonal shifts, and the demands of caring for an infant. We use gentle movements, breathwork, and mindful awareness to help you process these experiences and find a sense of grounded calm and comfort in your body again.
I feel completely overwhelmed as a new mom; can your Private Practice Therapy help with that emotional weight?
Absolutely. The emotional weight of new motherhood often manifests physically as tension, fatigue, or a sense of being 'stuck'. Our somatic Private Practice Therapy offers tools to gently process overwhelm, anxiety, and the profound shifts you're experiencing. By working with your body, you can find a greater sense of emotional regulation, inner calm, and a more grounded presence amidst the challenges of postpartum.
