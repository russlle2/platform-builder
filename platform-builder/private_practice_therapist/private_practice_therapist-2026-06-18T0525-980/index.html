Frequently Asked Questions

How is your approach different from general therapy for students?
We don't wait for crises. We schedule check-ins before predictable stress points—like a 20-minute session the Wednesday before midterms—to reset your mindset before your panic escalates.
Can you help during actual finals week?
Yes, but we prioritize preventing finals week overwhelm. If you're already struggling, we'll connect you with immediate resources while building your preventive toolkit for next semester.
Do you work with students who haven't had therapy before?
Absolutely. We start where you are—no prior experience needed. Your first session focuses on your current academic stressors, not your past, so you can get practical support fast.
How do you fit therapy into a packed class schedule?
Sessions are 25-45 minutes—shorter than your average class. We book them around your existing commitments (like right after a 10 a.m. lecture) and offer video options so you can connect from your dorm or library.
