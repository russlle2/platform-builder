Untangling Anxiety and Academic Pressure in Private Practice Therapy

College life, with its demanding coursework, social shifts, and future unknowns, often stirs up significant anxiety. As a Private Practice Therapist, I understand that this isn't just 'stress' – it's a deep-seated reaction. We’ll explore the roots of your academic overwhelm, social anxieties, and future worries, providing practical tools to help you stand firm amidst the academic currents and find your inner balance.
