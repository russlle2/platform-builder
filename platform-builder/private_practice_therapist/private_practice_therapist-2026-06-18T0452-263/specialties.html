Understanding Emotional Echoes: Private Practice Therapy for Seniors

Life brings many seasons, and sometimes earlier chapters leave lingering echoes. Our Private Practice Therapist specializes in helping individuals aged 60 and above gently process these emotional experiences. We create a safe space where you can explore feelings that may have been present for years, or new ones that have recently emerged, all with understanding and a focus on your inherent resilience. We believe in taking things at your pace, honoring your life's rich tapestry.
