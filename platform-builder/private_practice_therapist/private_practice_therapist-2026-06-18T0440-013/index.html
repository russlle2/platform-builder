Frequently Asked Questions

How does private practice therapy specifically help seniors with stress?
At , we understand that stress can manifest differently in later life. Our therapy for seniors focuses on gentle, practical techniques to address age-related stressors like health changes, social shifts, or finding new purpose, helping you regain a sense of control and calm in your nervous system.
What does 'nervous system regulation' mean for me?
Nervous system regulation simply means helping your body and mind find a more balanced and relaxed state. When your nervous system is regulated, you're less likely to feel overwhelmed by stress, can sleep better, and experience more overall peace. We provide gentle tools to help you achieve this.
Is this therapy suitable if I'm not familiar with mental health services?
Absolutely. Many seniors are new to therapy, and we welcome you. Our private practice therapy is designed to be approachable and easy to understand. We prioritize clear communication and a supportive environment, ensuring you feel comfortable and informed every step of the way.
How can I be sure this approach is evidence-based and effective?
Our methods at are grounded in established therapeutic principles shown to be effective for stress reduction and nervous system regulation. We stay informed by current research to ensure you receive care that is not only compassionate but also clinically sound and tailored to your specific needs.
