How can Private Practice Therapy specifically help seniors with posture?

Our therapy for seniors focuses on gentle, adaptive techniques to re-educate your body's alignment. We address common age-related postural changes, offering personalized exercises and awareness practices to strengthen core support and improve balance, helping you stand taller and move with less effort and more confidence.

What does 'body awareness' mean in the context of your Private Practice Therapy?

For you, body awareness means learning to consciously feel and understand how your body moves and holds itself. This helps you notice subtle tensions or imbalances before they become problems, allowing you to adjust your posture and movements more effectively for greater comfort and stability in your daily life.

Is this Private Practice Therapy suitable for someone with limited mobility?

Absolutely. Our Private Practice Therapy is designed to be gentle and adaptable to your unique needs and current mobility levels. We start where you are, using modifications and supportive techniques to ensure you can participate comfortably and safely, always respecting your body's capabilities.

How quickly can I expect to see improvements in my posture and body awareness?

Progress is personal and varies for everyone. Many seniors report feeling a greater sense of body awareness and subtle improvements in comfort within a few sessions. Consistent practice of the techniques you learn in Private Practice Therapy will lead to more noticeable and lasting changes over time, empowering you for the long run.
