Frequently Asked Questions

How does Private Practice Therapy specifically address the stress of new motherhood?
Our Private Practice Therapy is tailored to the unique stressors of postpartum, focusing on techniques like mindful breathing, somatic awareness, and gentle cognitive reframing to calm the physiological responses to sleep deprivation, hormonal shifts, and new responsibilities. We help you build a toolkit for self-regulation that fits seamlessly into your daily life as a new mother.
Can Private Practice Therapy truly help with nervous system dysregulation during postpartum?
Absolutely. Postpartum can often lead to a dysregulated nervous system, manifesting as anxiety, irritability, or feeling constantly on edge. Through our Private Practice Therapy, we introduce practices that encourage vagal nerve toning, cultivate interoception, and create a sense of safety within your body, gently guiding your nervous system back towards a state of balanced calm.
What is the blend of Eastern and Western approaches in your Private Practice Therapy for new mothers?
Our Private Practice Therapy integrates the contemplative depth of Eastern philosophies, such as mindfulness and compassion practices, with the structured, evidence-based interventions of Western psychotherapy. This allows us to address both the mental and embodied aspects of stress reduction and nervous system regulation, offering you a comprehensive path to well-being.
Is Private Practice Therapy suitable if I'm feeling overwhelmed but not experiencing severe postpartum depression?
Yes, completely. Our Private Practice Therapy is designed for any new mother seeking to manage the common overwhelm, anxiety, and emotional intensity of the postpartum period. You don't need a diagnosis to benefit from creating a more regulated nervous system and cultivating inner peace. We offer support for the broad spectrum of postpartum experiences.
