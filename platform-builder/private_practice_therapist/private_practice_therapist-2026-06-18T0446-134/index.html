Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Private Practice Therapist help with my athletic recovery?
As a Private Practice Therapist, we focus on the mental and behavioral aspects of sleep and stress management crucial for athletic recovery. We provide strategies to calm your nervous system, optimize sleep cycles, and build resilience against training-induced stress, directly enhancing your physical repair processes.
Is this only for elite athletes, or can active individuals benefit too?
Our services are designed for any active individual experiencing physical stress from their activities, whether you're a professional athlete, a dedicated weekend warrior, or someone with a highly active lifestyle. If you're pushing your body, you need quality rest, and we're here to help you get it.
What kind of techniques do you use to improve sleep and rest?
We utilize evidence-based techniques such as Cognitive Behavioral Therapy for Insomnia (CBT-I), mindfulness practices tailored for stress reduction, and behavioral adjustments to optimize your sleep hygiene and environment. Our methods are practical and designed to fit into your demanding schedule.
How quickly can I expect to see improvements in my sleep and recovery?
While individual results vary, many clients report noticing initial improvements in sleep quality and reduced stress within a few weeks of consistently applying the strategies we develop together. Sustained progress comes with ongoing practice and commitment to your personalized plan.
