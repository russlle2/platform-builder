Frequently Asked Questions

How do you handle my specific seasonal shift, like starting a new job in fall?
We build your plan *around* that fall timeline. For example, we'd focus on communication strategies for your first month at the new role—not generic 'stress reduction.' It's about matching prevention to your actual life rhythm.
What if I'm not in crisis but just adjusting to a move?
That's exactly why we exist. Preventive care means supporting you *during* the adjustment phase, not after it becomes overwhelming. We help you build resilience as you settle, not just when you're struggling.
How does this differ from therapy for 'big problems'?
We focus on the small, daily shifts that add up—not just major crises. For example, navigating a new school schedule for your child or adjusting to remote work. It's about building habits for smooth transitions, not fixing past issues.
Can I try this if I've never done therapy before?
Absolutely. We start where you are—no prior experience needed. Our approach feels familiar, not clinical, especially during life shifts. You'll learn practical tools you can use immediately, not just talk about your feelings.
