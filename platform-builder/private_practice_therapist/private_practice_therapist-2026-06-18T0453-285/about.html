I've never been to therapy before. What should I expect as a college student?

It's totally okay if this is your first time! Our sessions are designed to be a comfortable, confidential space for you. We'll start by getting to know each other, discussing what's bringing you in, and setting some goals together. There's no pressure, and you don't need any prior 'wellness experience' – just bring yourself, and we'll figure it out together.

How can therapy help me specifically with academic anxiety and pressure?

We'll work on understanding the roots of your academic anxiety – whether it's perfectionism, fear of failure, or time management struggles. You'll learn practical techniques like stress reduction, effective study habits, and boundary setting. Our aim is to help you feel more confident and less overwhelmed by your coursework and university expectations.

I'm worried about my privacy as a college student. Is everything confidential?

Absolutely. Confidentiality is a cornerstone of our private practice. What you share in therapy stays between us. There are very few legal exceptions, which we'll discuss during our first meeting, but rest assured, your privacy is paramount. This is your safe space to be open and honest without fear.

What if I'm dealing with past difficult experiences? Can you help with trauma-informed care?

Yes, definitely. Our practice is rooted in trauma-informed care, meaning we understand that past experiences can deeply impact your present. We approach your story with sensitivity and respect, creating a supportive environment where you can process these experiences gently and at your own pace. You'll learn tools to manage the impact of trauma and move towards healing.
