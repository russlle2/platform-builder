Frequently Asked Questions

How does Private Practice Therapy specifically address physical pain related to chronic illness?
Our Private Practice Therapy utilizes methods that target the nervous system's role in chronic pain perception. We help you identify and gently shift patterns of tension and reactivity in your body, which can significantly reduce the intensity and frequency of your physical discomfort. This is not about 'ignoring' pain, but rather re-educating your system for greater ease.
Is somatic healing in Private Practice Therapy suitable if my injury has a clear physical cause?
Yes, absolutely. Even with a clear physical cause, injury recovery often involves persistent pain, fear of movement, and emotional distress stored in the body. Somatic healing in Private Practice Therapy helps you process these bodily memories and sensations, facilitating a smoother recovery and preventing the perpetuation of pain cycles beyond the initial physical damage.
What if I feel uncomfortable talking about my body in Private Practice Therapy?
It is completely understandable to feel hesitant. Our Private Practice Therapy provides a clinical yet compassionate space where you are always in control. We start slowly, building trust and comfort at your pace. We focus on gentle awareness and tools you can use, never pushing you beyond what feels safe and manageable for your current state.
How long does it take to see results from Private Practice Therapy for physical pain or injury recovery?
The timeline for relief varies as each person's experience with chronic illness or injury is unique. However, many clients in our Private Practice Therapy report a reduction in pain intensity and an increased sense of bodily calm within a few weeks of consistent engagement. Our goal is to equip you with lasting strategies for managing your physical comfort.
