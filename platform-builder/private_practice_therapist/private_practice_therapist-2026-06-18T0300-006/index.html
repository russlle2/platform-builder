Frequently Asked Questions

What should I expect during my first session with a Private Practice Therapist?
During your initial session, you can expect a warm and confidential conversation. We'll discuss your reasons for seeking therapy, your goals, and any questions you may have. This is an opportunity for us to get to know each other and determine if our approach is a good fit for your needs. It's a low-pressure space for you to share openly.
How long will I need to see a Private Practice Therapist?
The duration of therapy is highly individual and depends on your unique goals and circumstances. Some clients find resolution in a few months, while others prefer longer-term support for ongoing personal growth. We will regularly assess your progress and adjust our plan to ensure it continues to meet your evolving needs effectively.
Is what I share with my Private Practice Therapist confidential?
Absolutely. Confidentiality is a cornerstone of our Private Practice Therapist services. All discussions are strictly private and protected by professional ethical guidelines and legal requirements. There are very few exceptions to confidentiality, which will be thoroughly explained during your first session, ensuring your comfort and trust.
What payment options are available for Private Practice Therapist services?
We strive to make therapy accessible. We accept various payment methods, including major credit cards, and can provide you with a super bill to submit to your insurance company for potential out-of-network reimbursement. Please contact us directly to discuss our current rates and payment policies, so you can plan accordingly.
