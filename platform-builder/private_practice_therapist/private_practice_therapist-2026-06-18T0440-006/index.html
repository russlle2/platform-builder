Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Private Practice Therapist help me manage the stress of chronic illness or injury?
As a Private Practice Therapist, I specialize in nervous system regulation. We'll work on techniques to shift your body out of its 'fight or flight' response, which is often heightened during chronic illness or injury. This can lead to reduced pain perception, better sleep, and an overall sense of calm, helping your body focus more on healing.
Is this approach suitable if I'm already seeing other medical professionals for my condition?
Absolutely. This therapeutic approach is designed to complement your existing medical care. We focus on the psychological and emotional impact of your condition, providing tools to manage stress and regulate your nervous system, which can often enhance the effectiveness of your other treatments and improve your quality of life.
What if I'm feeling too tired or overwhelmed to start therapy right now?
It's completely understandable to feel that way. Our sessions are always tailored to your energy levels and needs. We can start with very gentle, short engagements, focusing on simple, accessible techniques that won't add to your overwhelm. The goal is to create more ease, not more effort, in your recovery.
How long does it typically take to see results from nervous system regulation for chronic conditions?
The timeline for feeling shifts can vary greatly from person to person. Some individuals report feeling a subtle sense of calm and reduced tension after just a few sessions, while deeper, more lasting changes might unfold over several months. We prioritize sustainable, gentle progress, always attuned to your unique pace and needs.
