How does Private Practice Therapy specifically help with postural health after chronic illness or injury?

Our Private Practice Therapy focuses on re-educating your body's support system. After chronic illness or injury, your body might have adapted compensatory postures, leading to new aches or limitations. We guide you through gentle techniques to realign your spine, strengthen core muscles, and improve overall body mechanics, helping you move with more ease and less pain as you recover.

Will I need to perform strenuous exercises to improve my body awareness in Private Practice Therapy?

Not at all. Our Private Practice Therapy emphasizes mindful, gentle movements and heightened internal awareness rather than strenuous exercise. The goal is to help you feel what's happening within your body, identify areas of tension or imbalance, and learn to release them. It's about quality of movement and understanding, not intensity.

How long does it take to see improvements in body awareness and posture through Private Practice Therapy?

The timeline for improvement in Private Practice Therapy varies for everyone, as it depends on your specific condition, the duration of your chronic illness or injury, and your personal commitment. However, many clients report a noticeable shift in their body awareness and a reduction in discomfort within a few sessions, with consistent practice leading to more significant and lasting changes over time.

Can Private Practice Therapy integrate with other recovery treatments I'm receiving?

Absolutely. Our Private Practice Therapy is designed to complement and enhance other recovery treatments you may be undergoing, such as physical therapy or medical care. We can work collaboratively with your other healthcare providers to ensure a holistic and integrated approach to your recovery, focusing on how improved postural health and body awareness support your overall well-being.
