How can Private Practice Therapy specifically help my sleep during postpartum recovery?

Our Private Practice Therapy is tailored to the unique physiological and emotional shifts of postpartum recovery. We focus on identifying specific barriers to your sleep, such as anxiety, hormonal changes, or the demands of caring for a newborn. We then co-create gentle, sustainable strategies rooted in ancient wisdom and modern understanding to help you cultivate deeper, more restorative rest.

I'm a new mother and feel overwhelmed. Will Private Practice Therapy add more to my plate?

Absolutely not. Our Private Practice Therapy sessions are designed to be a calming, supportive space for you, not another task. We work at your pace, integrating practices that feel nourishing rather than burdensome. The goal is to reduce your overwhelm by improving your rest, which, in turn, helps you navigate the demands of new motherhood with greater ease and presence.

Do you only focus on sleep, or do you address other aspects of postpartum recovery in Private Practice Therapy?

While our primary focus is on restoring your sleep and rest, we understand that these are deeply intertwined with your overall postpartum recovery. In Private Practice Therapy, we gently touch upon related areas like emotional well-being, feelings of overwhelm, and creating supportive routines, always with the intention of enhancing your capacity for peaceful rest.

What if my baby's sleep patterns are unpredictable? Can Private Practice Therapy still help me?

Yes, even with unpredictable baby sleep, Private Practice Therapy can profoundly support you. We won't try to control your baby's sleep, but rather help you cultivate resilience, find moments of rest when they arise, and manage the mental and emotional impact of fragmented sleep. The aim is to strengthen your inner resources so you can feel more rested and grounded, regardless of external circumstances.
