Private Practice Therapist

Targeted Somatic Approaches for Senior Physical Pain

As we mature, unique physical experiences arise. Our Private Practice Therapist specializes in gentle, advanced somatic techniques designed specifically to address chronic aches, stiffness, and discomfort common in individuals over 60. We focus on nuanced body awareness and movement to ease your physical burdens, restoring a sense of lightness and fluidity. You deserve care that truly understands and respects your body's wisdom.
