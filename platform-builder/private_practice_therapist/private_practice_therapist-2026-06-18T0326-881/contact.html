The Overlooked Link: Posture, Burnout, and Your Professional Life

Your desk setup, long meetings, and constant screen time aren't just causing a stiff neck; they're contributing to your burnout. When your body is constantly in a state of physical tension or imbalance, it signals stress to your nervous system, exacerbating mental fatigue. Our Private Practice Therapist approach helps you identify these physical patterns of stress and offers tangible strategies to release them, fostering a greater sense of calm and clarity that extends beyond your workday.
