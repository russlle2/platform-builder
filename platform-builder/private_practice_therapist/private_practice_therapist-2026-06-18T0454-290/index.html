Frequently Asked Questions

I'm going through a big life change; how can Private Practice Therapy help me specifically?
When you're navigating a significant life transition, our private practice therapy offers a dedicated space to process the emotions that come with it. We help you understand the impact of these changes, develop coping strategies for stress or uncertainty, and gently work through any past experiences that might be resurfacing, all to help you find your footing again.
I don't have experience with therapy. Will your Private Practice Therapy be a good fit for me?
Absolutely. Our private practice therapy is designed to be accessible and gentle, even if you've never tried therapy before. We prioritize creating a comfortable and non-judgmental environment. You don't need any prior wellness knowledge; we'll guide you every step of the way, explaining our process and ensuring you feel supported and understood.
How does your Private Practice Therapy approach emotional healing and past trauma?
Our private practice therapy uses a trauma-informed approach, which means we understand that past experiences can deeply influence how you respond to current life transitions. We work gently and respectfully, focusing on creating a sense of safety. We help you explore and process difficult emotions or memories at your own pace, ensuring you feel in control throughout your healing journey.
What should I expect during my first Private Practice Therapy session?
Your first private practice therapy session is an opportunity for us to get to know each other. We'll discuss what brings you to therapy, what you're hoping to gain, and answer any questions you have. It's a low-pressure conversation aimed at understanding your situation and ensuring you feel comfortable. You won't be asked to delve into anything you're not ready to share.
