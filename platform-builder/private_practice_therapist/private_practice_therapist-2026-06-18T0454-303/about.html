How does a Private Practice Therapist help with physical pain for someone my age?

As your Private Practice Therapist, I focus on somatic techniques that gently explore the connection between your body and your feelings. This isn't about intense exercise, but rather about understanding where your body holds tension and pain, and then teaching you gentle ways to release it, often leading to significant relief and increased comfort in your daily activities.

Will I have to do strenuous exercises or uncomfortable movements with my Private Practice Therapist?

Absolutely not. Your comfort is our top priority. The approaches used by your Private Practice Therapist are always gentle and respectful of your body's current abilities. We work at your pace, using mindful movements, guided awareness, and breath work to help you find ease without any strain or discomfort.

What if my pain is due to a medical condition; can a Private Practice Therapist still help?

Yes, often a Private Practice Therapist can provide significant complementary support. While we don't replace medical treatment, somatic healing can help you manage the emotional impact of chronic pain, reduce muscle tension associated with your condition, and improve your overall sense of well-being and bodily awareness. We can work alongside your medical care team.

What can I expect during my first session with my Private Practice Therapist?

Your first session with your Private Practice Therapist is a gentle conversation where we get to know you. We'll discuss your specific physical discomforts, your health history, and what you hope to gain from our time together. It's a chance for you to ask questions and for us to understand how best to support you in finding greater comfort and ease in your body.
