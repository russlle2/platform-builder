Frequently Asked Questions

What kind of physical pain can a Private Practice Therapist address during postpartum?
As a Private Practice Therapist specializing in postpartum somatic healing, I frequently work with new mothers experiencing persistent lower back pain, hip discomfort, pelvic floor tension, neck and shoulder stiffness from feeding and holding, and overall body aches. My methods are designed to gently release these specific physical imprints.
Is somatic healing suitable if my physical pain started months after giving birth?
Absolutely. Postpartum physical discomfort can manifest at any stage, sometimes emerging months later as your body adjusts to new demands. My Private Practice Therapist approach is effective for both acute and chronic pain, helping you identify and resolve patterns of tension that may have developed over time since childbirth.
How is this different from traditional physical therapy for postpartum recovery?
While physical therapy focuses on strengthening and rehabilitation, my Private Practice Therapist approach emphasizes somatic release and nervous system regulation. We work with the body's felt sense to release deeply held tension and trauma, often at a pre-verbal level, which can complement and deepen the effects of physical therapy by addressing the emotional and energetic components of physical pain.
What should I expect in a session with a Private Practice Therapist focused on somatic healing?
In a session, you can expect a calm, private space where we'll discuss your specific physical experiences. I'll guide you through gentle, body-aware exercises and verbal processing, helping you tune into your internal sensations. The focus is on facilitating your body's natural capacity to release tension and restore comfort, always at your own pace and comfort level.
