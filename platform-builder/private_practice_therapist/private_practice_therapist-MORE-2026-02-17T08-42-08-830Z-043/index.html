Practical questions

What if I'm unsure therapy will help?
We begin with an assessment of concerns and values. If a particular approach doesn't fit your needs, we'll discuss alternatives or referrals. There are no guarantees; therapy is a collaborative exploration.
How is privacy handled?
Sessions are private within the bounds of confidentiality. I keep secure records and will explain legal limits to confidentiality during intake.
What about emergencies?
I do not provide crisis services. If you are actively at risk, call local emergency services or a trained crisis line immediately.

Ready to begin a focused plan?

When you're prepared, reach out to set a first meeting. We'll review fit, confidentiality, and next steps together.
