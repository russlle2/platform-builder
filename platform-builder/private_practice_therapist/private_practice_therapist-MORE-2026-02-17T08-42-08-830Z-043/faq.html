Frequently asked questions

Below are practical answers to common administrative and clinical questions. If you don't see what you need, reach out — I'm glad to clarify.

How do I start?
You can request a slot via the Reserve page or send a short message to {{EMAIL}}. If you prefer, use the Session Planner on the Investment page to shape a brief summary to include in your message.
What happens in the first session?
Initial sessions focus on understanding your concern, clinical history, current resources, and safety. We’ll agree on goals and a plan for next steps. The first meeting is also the time to cover consent, confidentiality, and administrative details.
Do you take insurance?
I operate primarily as an out-of-network provider. I provide a receipt that some clients submit to insurers for partial reimbursement. I can discuss documentation needs during intake.
Cancellation and scheduling
Appointments canceled with less than 48 hours' notice may be billed at the full session rate. The specifics are reviewed as part of informed consent so expectations are clear up front.
Confidentiality and limits
Confidentiality is respected within legal and ethical limits. I will discuss these limits (for example, situations involving harm to self/others or suspected abuse) during intake. If you have concerns about privacy, we can address them directly.
What if I am in crisis?
This practice does not provide emergency services. If you are in immediate danger or experiencing a medical or psychiatric emergency, please call your local emergency number or a crisis line right away (for example, in the U.S. call 988).
Is therapy guaranteed?
Therapy is collaborative work; I cannot guarantee specific outcomes. I aim to provide steady, evidence-informed clinical care and to tailor work to your stated goals.

Quick notes

Sessions typically last 45–55 minutes. Multi-hour options are available for focused planning. Fees and scheduling are flexible to accommodate thoughtful planning; see Investment for details.
