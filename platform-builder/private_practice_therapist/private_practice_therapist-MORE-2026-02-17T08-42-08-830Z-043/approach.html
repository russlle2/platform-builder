How I work

A clear, pragmatic method: assessment, focused practice, review, and adjustment. We keep the process measurable and humane.

Assessment and prioritized goals

Early sessions focus on clarifying what matters most—safety, immediate functioning, and the behaviours that most reliably move the needle. From there we set a small number of measurable targets to track progress.

Short experiments and skills

I emphasize short, testable experiments between sessions: concrete skills you can try, observe, and report back on. This keeps therapy active and oriented toward change.

Adaptive planning

We review what’s working and what’s not, and we adjust. Therapy is iterative—small course corrections are expected and useful.

Collaboration & scope

I aim to be transparent about methods, limits of practice, and what I can and cannot provide clinically. If specialized or immediate crisis care is needed, I will help connect you to appropriate services.

Tools I draw on

  • Brief, skills-focused interventions
  • Evidence-informed psychotherapy approaches
  • Values-based planning and behavioral activation
  • Collaborative problem solving and communication tools
