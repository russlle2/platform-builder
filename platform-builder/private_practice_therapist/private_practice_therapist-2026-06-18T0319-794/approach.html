Blending Eastern Wisdom with Western Approaches in Your Private Practice Therapy

My approach as a Private Practice Therapist integrates practical Western therapeutic techniques with contemplative Eastern practices. This means we'll explore both actionable strategies for managing daily stressors and heart-centered methods to foster inner peace and compassion. You'll learn how to bring more mindful awareness into your interactions, reducing reactivity and increasing patience, creating a more harmonious home environment for everyone.
