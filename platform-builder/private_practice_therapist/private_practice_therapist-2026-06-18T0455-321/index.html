Frequently Asked Questions

How does Private Practice Therapy address physical pain related to burnout?
Physical pain often acts as a messenger for deeper stress. In our sessions, we utilize somatic practices that help you connect with these bodily sensations, understand their origins, and learn to release them. This isn't just about managing symptoms, but about addressing the root causes of the tension your body is holding due to professional burnout.
I'm a working professional. Will Private Practice Therapy fit into my busy schedule?
We understand the demands of your professional life. Our Private Practice Therapy offers flexible scheduling options to accommodate your commitments. The aim is to create a supportive space that doesn't add to your stress, but rather provides a much-needed respite and effective tools for managing your well-being within your existing routine.
Is this a physical therapy clinic, or something different?
provides Private Practice Therapy, focusing on the emotional and energetic components that contribute to physical discomfort and burnout. While we don't offer traditional physical therapy, our somatic approach complements it by addressing the nervous system's role in pain and tension, helping you integrate mind-body awareness for lasting relief.
What if I feel disconnected from my body because of burnout?
It's common for professionals experiencing burnout to feel a sense of detachment. Our Private Practice Therapy is specifically designed to gently reintroduce you to your body's wisdom. Through guided awareness and gentle somatic techniques, you'll learn to safely reconnect, build resilience, and cultivate a more grounded presence, even amidst your busy schedule.
