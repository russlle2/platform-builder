My clinical approach — steady, skillful, human

I combine evidence-informed techniques with pragmatic coaching. The aim is to build resilience, reduce suffering, and improve daily functioning.

1
Intake & alignment
We clarify your priorities, review limits of confidentiality, and craft a short-term plan. This creates a shared map for our work.
2
Skills & stabilization
We introduce tools for emotion regulation, sleep, grounding, and managing reactivity so you have more capacity to engage with deeper material.
3
Pattern work & meaning
When you’re ready, we explore persistent patterns, relational themes, and decisions that keep you stuck. The pace is collaborative and trauma-informed.
4
Integration & next steps
We translate insights into practical steps and check-ins. If needed, we design brief follow-up sequences to sustain gains.

What if I’m unsure about therapy?

It’s normal to have doubts. Below are common questions I address early on.

Can therapy be brief and useful?
Yes. Short, focused series can produce meaningful change, especially when goals are concrete and actionable.
Will I be pushed into things I’m not ready for?
No. We move at a pace you consent to. I emphasize stabilization and choice throughout.
How do you decide which tools to use?
Tools are selected based on your goals, history, and current needs. I draw from CBT, somatic practices, EMDR-informed processing, and coaching methods when appropriate.
