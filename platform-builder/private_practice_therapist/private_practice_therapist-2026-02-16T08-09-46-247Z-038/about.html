How I typically work

My practice blends structured skill-building with space to reflect on meaning. Early sessions focus on practical goals and immediate coping. Over time we identify core patterns and develop tailored strategies—therapy is collaborative, paced to you.

  • Initial intake and values alignment (first 2 sessions)
  • Short-term goal setting and skill practice
  • Deeper pattern work and trauma-informed stabilizing if needed

People’s common hesitations

Starting therapy can feel vulnerable. Here are concerns I hear and how we approach them.

Will I be judged?
Therapy is a nonjudgmental space. My role is to understand, hold boundaries, and offer practical support.
What if it becomes overwhelming?
We prioritize stabilization. If intense material arises, we pace work and use grounding and safety planning.
What if I don’t see progress?
We review goals regularly and adjust methods. Therapy is iterative; collaboration helps keep it relevant to you.
