Focused pathways for common challenges

Below are the primary areas I work with. Each focus area includes practical skills, reflective work, and measurable steps we can take together.

Anxiety & Worry

Practical strategies for immediate relief, combined with longer-term pattern work. Techniques include CBT-based skill building and interoceptive grounding.

Good fit if:
  • You want tools for day-to-day calm
  • You notice avoidance or rumination

Trauma & Complex Stress

Trauma-informed stabilization, pacing, and resourcing are central. We integrate somatic awareness and EMDR-informed techniques when appropriate.

Approach:
  • Safety planning and grounding
  • Skill-driven, collaborative processing

Relationships & Communication

Focus on clear communication, boundaries, and repair. Work can be individual or couple-focused depending on goals.

Includes:
  • Conflict navigation skills
  • Attachment-awareness and patterns

Life Transitions

Career change, caregiving, relocation, and significant life shifts. I offer structure to help you make values-aligned choices without losing momentum.

Burnout & Work Stress

Practical boundary setting, energy management, and reframing work identity to restore sustainable functioning.

Decision-making & Clarity

Short-term, focused packages help clarify options and move toward decisive steps with reduced anxiety.

How a focused pathway works

We begin with a clear intake to identify what matters to you. From there I offer a recommended pathway—typically 6 to 12 sessions for focused work, with flexibility to continue at your pace.

Program frames
Pathways are framed as short, actionable series (e.g., "Clarity in 8") rather than open-ended commitments. Pricing and packages can be discussed during intake.
