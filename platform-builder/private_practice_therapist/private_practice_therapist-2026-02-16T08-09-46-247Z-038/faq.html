Practical FAQs — what people ask first

How do I know if therapy is right for me?
Therapy is a space to explore patterns, reduce distress, and try new ways of relating. If something in your life feels stuck or draining, a short conversation can clarify whether we’d be a good fit.
Do you accept insurance?
I work on a private-pay basis and provide superbills you can submit for potential reimbursement. If insurance is essential for you, I can provide referrals to clinicians who are in-network.
What methods do you use?
I integrate evidence-informed approaches: CBT for skill-building, EMDR-informed techniques for trauma processing, and coaching strategies to support applied change. Interventions are tailored to your goals.
What if I’m worried about privacy?
Confidentiality is central. Client records are stored securely; information is only shared with consent except when legally required (e.g., imminent risk). We review limits to confidentiality in our first session.
How long does work usually take?
Some people find relief in a few weeks of focused work; others benefit from months of exploration. I offer both time-limited intensives and ongoing care; we design a plan together.
Common concerns
Cost, time, and trust are the most common barriers. I offer an initial consult to clarify goals and fit, and I’m transparent about expected frequency and pacing so you can make an informed choice.
