What guides our work

Practical, measurable steps

I translate therapeutic insights into simple daily experiments that help you notice change and adjust as we go.

Warm nonjudgmental stance

Evidence-informed blend

How we’ll work together

Assessment & shared plan

We begin with focused intake to understand what matters most to you and build a clear, collaborative plan.

Skill practice between sessions

Ongoing review

Regular check-ins on what’s working so we can adapt pace and focus without unnecessary repetition.

Common concerns I hear

Is change possible when life feels so busy?

Yes. We prioritize small, high-impact practices you can integrate into an already-full schedule. Therapy needn’t double your workload.

Will you tell my employer what we talk about?

No. Sessions are confidential. I won’t share information without your written consent except in rare legal/ethical situations (harm to self/others, court order).

What if I’m not ready for deep trauma work?

We move at a pace you can tolerate. If trauma-focused interventions are appropriate later, we prepare carefully and ensure strong safety supports.
