Frequently Asked Questions

Can you help if I’m in the middle of a flare-up right now?
Absolutely. We focus on your *current* needs, not a future ‘ideal state.’ Your first session adapts to where you are—whether it’s managing a sudden winter joint flare-up or adjusting after a summer overexertion.
When should I schedule care for the best results?
Before you need it. We book check-ins 2-4 weeks before seasonal shifts (like late fall for winter stiffness or early spring for allergies) so you’re prepared, not reacting.
Do you work with my regular doctor or physical therapist?
Yes. We share a clear, simple update with your care team—no extra meetings. You’ll get a single, coordinated seasonal plan that matches your medical needs.
What if I don’t have a ‘perfect’ routine yet?
Your routine doesn’t need to be perfect—it needs to be *yours*. We start where you are, using one small adjustment at a time (like adding 5 minutes of seated stretching during dry winter air) to build sustainable habits.
