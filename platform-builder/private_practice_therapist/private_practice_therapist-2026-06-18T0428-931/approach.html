Adjusting Care Before Weather Hits

No more scrambling when pollen counts spike or temperatures drop. We teach you to spot early weather-related symptoms—like stiff joints in damp weather or fatigue during allergy season—and adjust your routine proactively. Your physical therapy schedule, meal timing, and rest periods get fine-tuned for *your* body’s seasonal rhythm, not generic advice.
