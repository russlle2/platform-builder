How we might work together

  1. 1. Connect

    We’ll start with a 20-minute consult call to clarify needs and fit. You can ask questions about modality, scheduling, and confidentiality.

  2. 2. Begin

    Initial sessions focus on safety, consent, and establishing goals. I blend evidence-based techniques with relational support.

  3. 3. Integrate

    We track progress, learn tools you can use between sessions, and adjust pacing so it feels manageable and respectful.

  4. 4. Transition

    When work concludes we plan a gradual step-down and offer resources for ongoing growth.

Confidentiality is foundational. For details about limits (e.g., imminent harm, mandated reporting) I provide clear written information at intake.
