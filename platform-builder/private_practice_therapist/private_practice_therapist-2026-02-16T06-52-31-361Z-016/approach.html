Frequently asked (different set)

What if therapy brings up strong emotions?

We approach intense material with regulation tools first. You control pacing; we only progress when you feel safe enough to do so.

How do we measure progress?

We set simple, observable goals (e.g., fewer avoidance behaviors, improved sleep, clearer conversations) and revisit them regularly.

What if I need a different therapist?

I offer referrals and help with warm handoffs when a different specialty or setting is a better fit.
