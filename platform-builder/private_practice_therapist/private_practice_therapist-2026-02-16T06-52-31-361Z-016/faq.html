Questions I often help answer

Below are practical answers about how I work, what therapy looks like, and what to expect when you reach out. If your question isn’t here, please contact me.

How quickly can I start?

I aim to respond to inquiries within 48 hours. New client availability varies; you may be offered an initial session or a 15-minute phone consult to see if we are a good fit.

What does a first session feel like?

The first visit is an intake-focused conversation: we review what brings you, your history, current stressors, and immediate goals. It’s collaborative and paced to what feels manageable for you.

Do you work with trauma and anxiety?

Yes. I provide evidence-informed approaches for anxiety, complex stress, and trauma-related concerns. Treatment plans are individualized and guided by your priorities and safety.

Can I use insurance?

I am private pay. I supply superbills you can submit to your insurer for potential reimbursement. Insurance coverage and reimbursement vary by plan.

What if I need help between sessions?

I encourage brief check-ins when clinically appropriate. For urgent crises, please use emergency services. Non-urgent messages are addressed during business hours; response times vary.

Do you offer couples or family sessions?

I offer couple-focused sessions in specific circumstances. Please contact me to discuss whether my approach is a good fit for your relational goals.

Practice notes

Confidentiality, informed consent, and limits to confidentiality are reviewed at intake. I maintain records in line with ethical guidelines.

When I might refer

There are times I will recommend additional support: active suicidality, unmanaged substance dependence, or medical issues requiring specialist care. In those situations, I collaborate on a safety and referral plan rather than trying to manage alone.
