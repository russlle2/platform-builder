Embodied Resilience: A Private Practice Therapist's Focus on Postural Health

Major life transitions, like career changes or new relationships, can disrupt your sense of self and stability. As a Private Practice Therapist, I help you reconnect with your body's innate wisdom. By exploring your postural health and body awareness, you can cultivate a foundation of resilience, allowing you to move through change with greater ease and confidence. This isn't about rigid posture, but about understanding how your physical self influences your emotional state during periods of flux.
