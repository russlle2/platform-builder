  • How long will I be in therapy?
    There is no set length—for some people a brief, goal-focused series of sessions is right; for others, open-ended work feels better. We will discuss estimated length and review progress regularly.
  • Do you accept insurance?
    I operate on a private-pay basis and can provide a receipt for out-of-network reimbursement. We can discuss billing during intake.
  • What if I need help between sessions?
    For short, non-urgent questions, the Care Continuity Add-on or brief secure messages may help. For urgent concerns, please follow crisis resources below.
  • How do you approach therapy?
    I blend evidence-informed techniques with practical planning, tailoring the approach to the issue at hand and your preferences.
  • Can I switch providers later?
    Yes. If you decide to transition, I can offer a summary and transfer notes with your consent.

What I keep private
Notes and communications are treated with confidentiality. I will only disclose information with your permission or when ethically or legally required (e.g., safety concerns).

Sessions are not recorded without explicit consent. For telehealth, choose a private space and let me know if you have privacy questions.

I am not a 24/7 crisis service. If you are in immediate danger, call local emergency services. See the crisis footer for resources.
