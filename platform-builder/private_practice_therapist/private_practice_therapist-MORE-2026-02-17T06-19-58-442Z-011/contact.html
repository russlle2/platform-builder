Session boundaries — what to expect ▼

Sessions typically run 50 minutes unless a different length is arranged. Please arrive on time; late starts shorten the session so other clients’ time is respected. If you need to cancel, I ask for 48 hours' notice when possible so the slot can be offered to others. Fees may apply for late cancellations.

Confidentiality & limits ▼

Your conversations are confidential and used to provide care, document progress, and coordinate treatment when needed. Exceptions include situations required by law: imminent risk of harm to self or others, suspected abuse of a vulnerable person, or court orders. If coordination with other professionals is helpful, I will ask for consent first.

Scope of practice ▼

I provide psychotherapy and clinical consultation. I do not provide emergency medical care or psychiatric medication management. If your needs extend beyond my scope, I will discuss appropriate referrals and supports.
