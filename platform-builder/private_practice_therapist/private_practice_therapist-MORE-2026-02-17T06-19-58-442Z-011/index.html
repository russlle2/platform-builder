What to expect in your first sessions

  1. Initial conversation

    We begin with a practical check-in: what brought you here, what you hope for, and what you need right now.

  2. Boundaries and confidentiality

    We review limits of confidentiality and how I handle records. This is a collaborative discussion — your privacy is central.

  3. Focused planning

    We co-create a brief plan with small, measurable steps you can try between sessions. If longer-term work is helpful, we discuss options.

Session boundaries & confidentiality

Typical sessions are 45–55 minutes. I ask for 48 hours notice for cancellations when possible. When notice isn't possible, a fee may apply; we discuss arrangements that aim to be fair and clear.

Your information is protected within ethical and legal limits. There are rare exceptions (e.g., imminent risk of serious harm). We review these together at intake so you can make an informed choice about care.

I use secure telehealth platforms and will discuss the safest ways to share information. Email or text are not secure — we agree on how we use these methods for scheduling only.

Questions people often have

Will therapy fix everything?

I don’t promise fixes. My aim is to help you build clarity and tools so you can make choices that fit your values and life.

What if I’m not sure therapy is for me?

A brief consult lets us try a short focus and decide together whether ongoing work would be helpful.

Do you take insurance?

I offer superbills and can discuss sliding scale; insurance participation varies and we’ll talk through practical options.

Ready to start a conversation?

If you’re unsure, a brief consultation can clarify next steps without pressure.
