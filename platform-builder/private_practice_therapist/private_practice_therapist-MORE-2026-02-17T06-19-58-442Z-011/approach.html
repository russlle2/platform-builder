How I work with people

My practice emphasizes an intentional rhythm: assess, try, reflect. We set a clear aim together, select tools that fit daily life, and check what changes when you try them.

Session format
Standard sessions are 50 minutes. Telehealth and in-person options may be available; we will confirm logistics at intake.

A simple sequence

1
Orient

We identify what feels most urgent and what you hope is different. I offer clinical observation and a collaborative plan.

2
Try

We pick one or two small, testable steps you can take between sessions — experiments that reveal what helps in your life.

3
Review

We notice what shifted and adjust the plan. The aim is steady forward movement, not perfection.

What to expect in the first month

  • Clear goals and a shared plan for the next 2–4 sessions.
  • Practical tools you can use between visits.
  • Respect for your time and boundaries; we talk schedule and communication preferences up front.

Boundaries, scope, and safety

While our time is focused and practical, there are limits I must follow for legal and ethical reasons. Confidentiality will be discussed during intake and summarized in the paperwork.

Confidentiality, limits, and emergency planning

I keep clinical notes and will maintain confidentiality except in the case of imminent risk, abuse reporting requirements, or legal orders. If safety concerns arise, we will create a plan together and I will involve appropriate services if needed.

If you are experiencing an urgent crisis, call your local emergency number immediately. For US callers, the 988 Lifeline is available for immediate support.
