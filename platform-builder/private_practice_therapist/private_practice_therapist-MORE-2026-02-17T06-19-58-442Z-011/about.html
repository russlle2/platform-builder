Session boundaries, confidentiality, and scope

Confidentiality and its limits

Conversations in session are treated as private clinical records within legal and ethical limits. Exceptions include risk of harm to self or others, suspected abuse of a minor or vulnerable adult, or when a court order requires disclosure. I will always aim to discuss these limits with you and plan for safety as needed.

Record keeping & secure communication

I maintain brief clinical notes to track progress and treatment planning. I use commonly accepted safeguards for electronic communications but avoid highly sensitive details over unencrypted channels. If you have questions about privacy or prefer minimal documentation, please raise this during intake.

Session logistics & cancellations

Standard appointments are 45–55 minutes. If you need to change or cancel, please notify me as soon as possible. Cancellation policies and any missed-appointment fees are discussed during intake and available in the intake packet.
