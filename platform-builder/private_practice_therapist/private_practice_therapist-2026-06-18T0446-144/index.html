Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Private Practice Therapist help with my athletic sleep challenges?
As an active individual, your sleep needs are unique. We can help you identify specific barriers to restorative sleep, such as overtraining, performance anxiety, or travel disruptions, and develop tailored strategies to improve your sleep quality and duration for optimal physical recovery.
I'm always sore and tired; can therapy really help with physical stress and sleep?
Absolutely. Chronic physical stress often impacts sleep, and poor sleep exacerbates physical stress. We'll work on techniques to calm your nervous system, improve your sleep environment, and create routines that promote deeper rest, directly aiding your body's recovery process.
What if my busy training schedule makes consistent sleep impossible?
We understand the demands of your schedule. Instead of aiming for 'perfect' sleep, we'll focus on optimizing what's possible within your routine. This might involve strategic napping, sleep hygiene adjustments around training, or techniques to quickly wind down, ensuring you get the most out of your rest periods.
Is this just about sleep hygiene, or something more comprehensive?
While sleep hygiene is a component, our approach as a Private Practice Therapist goes much deeper. We explore the psychological factors impacting your sleep, such as stress, anxiety, and mental recovery from intense activity, integrating these insights into a holistic strategy for truly restorative rest and overall well-being.
