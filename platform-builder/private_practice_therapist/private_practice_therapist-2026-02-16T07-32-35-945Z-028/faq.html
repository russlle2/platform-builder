Questions I often hear — brief, plain answers

Below are practical clarifications to help you decide whether to reach out. These are not exhaustive; if your question isn’t listed, contact me at {{EMAIL}}.

What happens in a first session?
We focus on safety, your immediate concerns, and a short assessment of goals and history. Expect to discuss what prompted you to seek support and any current risks. We conclude with practical next steps and a clear plan if we choose to continue.
How long will I need therapy?
There is no set timeline. Some clients work together for a few months to address a specific transition. Others engage in longer-term work. We set goals and review progress regularly; you decide when to scale back. If you seek short-term symptom relief, I may offer targeted interventions; for deeper relational patterns, longer work is often more effective.
Do you work with insurance?
I can provide a superbill that you may submit to your insurer for out-of-network reimbursement when allowed. I am not in-network with every plan. Payment is accepted by card or secure transfer. Sliding scale availability is limited — please ask.
Is online therapy available?
Yes, I offer telehealth sessions to clients in {{STATE}} and other jurisdictions where I am licensed. For out-of-state clients, I will confirm licensure rules before scheduling. If you need help with a technical setup, I will provide simple instructions.
What if I'm in crisis?
I do not provide emergency services. If you are in imminent danger or are a clear risk to yourself or others, call 911 or your local emergency number. If you’re having suicidal thoughts but are not in immediate danger, consider calling your local crisis line or the national crisis hotline in your country.
How do you protect my privacy?
Clinical notes and communications are stored securely and are disclosed only in accordance with law and ethical obligations (e.g., risk of harm, court order). For specifics about record requests and retention, contact {{EMAIL}}.
What modalities do you use?
I draw from {{MODALITIES}} and integrate somatic, relational, and mindfulness-informed practices. Treatment is individualized and collaborative.
Do you offer brief tools between sessions?
Yes. I often suggest micro-habits: short breathing practices, reflective prompts, or movement breaks tailored to your rhythm. These are optional and designed to support session work.

Scope, consent & limits
