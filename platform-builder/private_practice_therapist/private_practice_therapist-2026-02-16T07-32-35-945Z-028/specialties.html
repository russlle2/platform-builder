Specialty Portraits

Each focus includes a short diagnostic lens, suggested plan, and micro-habits to begin between sessions.

Anxiety & Worry

Diagnostic lens: recent escalation, avoidance patterns, physiological amplification. Plan: paced exposure, regulation skills, values-focused work. Micro-habit: 3-minute grounding practice after waking.

Trauma & Early Attachment

Diagnostic lens: relational patterns, triggers linked to attachment history. Plan: safety-building, sensorimotor techniques, relational re-patterning. Micro-habit: boundary notice — a brief daily check-in about needs.

Relationship Patterns

Diagnostic lens: repeating cycles, communication blocks, role confusion. Plan: skills-based work, relational experiments, guided reflections. Micro-habit: two weekly sentences reflecting a felt interaction.

Burnout & Life Transitions

Diagnostic lens: sustained stress, identity shifts, values drift. Plan: pacing, reprioritization, somatic regulation. Micro-habit: a 10-minute boundary ritual at day’s end.
