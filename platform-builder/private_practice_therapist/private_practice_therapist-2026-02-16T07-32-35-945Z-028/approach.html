Embodied Strategy

How I work — a frame for steady, kind progress

My approach blends somatic awareness, relational attunement, and reflective inquiry. I aim for clarity: compassionate assessment, collaborative planning, and daily micro-practices that feel doable.

Connect for a Diagnostic Conversation

Diagnostic intake — Listening as first practice

We begin with a 50-minute Diagnostic intake focused on safety, context, and the stories you're rotating through. I prioritize concrete details and felt experience: how symptoms appear in your life, what supports exist, and what feels most urgent.

A three-part plan

1. Stabilize
Build safety, basic regulation skills, and immediate supports.
2. Explore
Trace patterns, relational themes, and core narratives with gentle curiosity.
3. Integrate
Create lasting practices and name sustainable next steps.

Micro-habits — small practices, steady results

The practices I recommend are brief and repetitive; they cultivate capacity without overwhelming. Examples below can be adapted to your daily life.

  • Two-minute breath check at transitions
  • Single-sentence journaling about one felt moment per day
  • Short boundary experiments — one small ask each week

Boundaries & clinical limits

Confidentiality is maintained within the limits of law. If you are in crisis, call emergency services or a crisis line immediately. I do not provide emergency psychiatric services or prescribe medications; when needed, I coordinate care with other providers with your consent.
