Micro-habits you can try this week

Three-minute body scan

Sit, breathe, and notice sensation from feet to crown for three minutes. Name one sensation and let it be without trying to fix it.

Anchor phrase

Create a short, grounding phrase you can repeat when overwhelmed (eg. “I’m here, I can notice”). Say it with the exhale.

Micro-boundary

Choose one small boundary to practice—two minutes shorter meeting, a gentle no, or a limited email response window.
