A Gentle Approach: Your Private Practice Therapist for Body Awareness and Calm

No prior wellness experience? No problem. This Private Practice Therapist offers a kind, accessible space for you to connect with your body. We don't believe in quick fixes or complicated jargon. Instead, we'll work at your pace, introducing easy-to-understand body awareness exercises that can help you notice and release physical tension, ultimately reducing the grip of anxiety and academic pressure. It’s about building a friendly relationship with yourself.
