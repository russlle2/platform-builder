Frequently Asked Questions

How does private practice therapy specifically help with stress during life transitions?
My private practice therapy focuses on tangible ways to reduce stress by working directly with your nervous system. We'll explore techniques like mindful breathing, sensory grounding, and identifying your unique triggers to bring a sense of stability when your life circumstances are shifting. This helps you respond more calmly to change rather than reacting from a place of overwhelm.
Will this approach feel too 'woo-woo' if I'm looking for practical solutions?
While inspired by nature's grounding principles, my private practice therapy is rooted in evidence-informed practices for nervous system regulation. We'll use approachable, practical strategies that you can integrate into your daily life. The goal is always to provide you with concrete tools to manage stress, not abstract concepts.
I'm not sure what my 'major life transition' is, but I feel stressed. Can private practice therapy still help?
Absolutely. Often, stress during transitions can feel diffuse or hard to pinpoint. In private practice therapy, we can explore what might be contributing to your current feelings of unease, even if the specific 'transition' isn't immediately obvious. The core work remains the same: helping your nervous system find a more regulated state so you can feel more grounded.
What if I feel overwhelmed just thinking about starting private practice therapy?
It's completely understandable to feel that way, especially when navigating big changes. My priority is to make starting private practice therapy as gentle as possible. Our first conversation is simply a chance to connect, for you to ask questions, and for us to see if this feels right. There's no pressure, just an open space to explore how we might work together to support your well-being.
