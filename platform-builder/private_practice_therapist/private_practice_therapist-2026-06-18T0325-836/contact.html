Private Practice Therapist

Reclaiming Your Postural Comfort After Chronic Illness or Injury

Living with chronic illness or recovering from injury often means adapting to new ways of moving, or sometimes, not moving at all. This can significantly impact your posture and how you carry yourself, leading to discomfort or a feeling of disconnect. Our approach as a Private Practice Therapist centers on helping you identify these patterns and gently re-educate your body towards more supportive and comfortable alignment. We work with you to build strength and flexibility where you need it most, honoring your body's unique healing process.
