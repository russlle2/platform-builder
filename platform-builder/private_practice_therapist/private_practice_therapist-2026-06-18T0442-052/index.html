Frequently Asked Questions

How does Private Practice Therapy specifically address postpartum stress?
Our Private Practice Therapy focuses on the unique physiological and emotional shifts of postpartum. We address hormonal fluctuations, sleep deprivation's impact on mood, and the intense demands of caring for a newborn by offering specialized techniques to downregulate your nervous system and reduce the cumulative effects of stress.
Will Private Practice Therapy help me manage sleep deprivation and its effects?
While we cannot magically create more sleep, our Private Practice Therapy provides strategies to manage your nervous system's response to sleep deprivation. By reducing overall stress and reactivity, you can often experience more restful periods when sleep is available, and better cope with the moments when it's not.
Is this Private Practice Therapy suitable if I'm feeling overwhelmed or anxious?
Absolutely. Overwhelm and anxiety are common postpartum experiences. Our Private Practice Therapy is specifically designed to provide tools and support for nervous system regulation, which directly impacts feelings of anxiety and overwhelm, helping you regain a sense of control and calm.
What kind of results can I expect from Private Practice Therapy for nervous system regulation?
Clients typically report a significant reduction in stress, improved emotional regulation, and a greater sense of peace. You can expect to develop practical skills to calm your nervous system, leading to more resilience in daily life and a more enjoyable postpartum experience.
