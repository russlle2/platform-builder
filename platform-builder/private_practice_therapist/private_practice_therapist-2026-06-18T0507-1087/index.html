Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Private Practice Therapist help me with my postpartum sleep when my baby dictates the schedule?
As your Private Practice Therapist, I understand that your baby's needs are paramount. We won't try to force an unrealistic sleep schedule, but rather focus on optimizing the sleep you *can* get. This involves developing strategies for maximizing restorative rest during fragmented sleep, managing nighttime anxieties, and establishing gentle routines that support both your and your baby's well-being.
I feel too exhausted to even think about therapy. How will this help my sleep?
Feeling utterly drained is a common experience, and it's precisely why a Private Practice Therapist specializing in postpartum rest is so vital. Our sessions are designed to be a gentle, supportive space where you don't need to 'perform.' We'll focus on small, actionable steps that can make a significant difference to your rest, helping you reclaim precious moments of calm and replenish your reserves without adding to your overwhelm.
Is this just about sleep training my baby, or is it for me?
As a Private Practice Therapist, my focus is entirely on *your* postpartum recovery and restorative rest. While a baby's sleep patterns are part of the landscape, our work centers on your emotional well-being, your sleep quality, and developing coping mechanisms for the unique challenges of new motherhood. This is about nurturing you, the mother, so you can feel more rested and present.
What if I feel guilty taking time for myself to focus on my sleep?
It's incredibly common for new mothers to experience guilt, feeling that every moment should be dedicated to their baby. As your Private Practice Therapist, I want to assure you that prioritizing your rest is not selfish; it's a fundamental act of care that benefits your entire family. When you are rested, you are better able to connect, nurture, and enjoy this precious time. We'll explore these feelings together, gently reframing self-care as essential care.
