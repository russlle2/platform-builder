How can Private Practice Therapy help me specifically with postpartum stress?

Our therapy is specifically designed to address the unique stressors of postpartum recovery. We'll focus on techniques to down-regulate your nervous system, manage anxiety, and process the emotional shifts that come with new motherhood, helping you find more moments of calm in your day.

What does nervous system regulation mean for a new mother?

For new mothers, nervous system regulation means moving from a state of constant 'fight or flight' (common with sleep deprivation and new responsibilities) to a more balanced, settled state. We'll explore gentle practices that help your body and mind feel safer and more at ease, even when things are unpredictable.

Is this therapy suitable if I'm feeling overwhelmed but not necessarily depressed?

Absolutely. Many new mothers experience overwhelm, anxiety, and heightened stress without meeting criteria for depression. Our Private Practice Therapy is here to support you in navigating these common postpartum challenges, focusing on stress reduction and building coping skills before things escalate.

How can I fit Private Practice Therapy into my busy schedule as a new mother?

We understand that your time is incredibly precious right now. We offer flexible scheduling options and prioritize creating a supportive, efficient therapeutic space. Our goal is to make accessing support as easy as possible, so you can prioritize your well-being without adding more stress to your plate.
