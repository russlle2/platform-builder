How does seasonal wellness differ from general therapy?

We prevent burnout before it hits your peak season. Instead of reacting to fatigue, we align sessions with your work cycle — like booking a check-in before fiscal year-end.

How do I fit therapy into a demanding schedule?

We build appointments around your existing commitments. It's a non-negotiable personal meeting — just like a board meeting. Your schedule comes first, always.

What makes your approach exclusive?

Only 15 clients per quarter. We don't take more because we believe in depth over volume. Every session is tailored to your current work pressures — not a one-size-fits-all plan.

How quickly can I expect to feel less overwhelmed?

Many clients notice less reactive stress within 3 sessions. We focus on concrete tools for your immediate work cycle, not just 'feeling better' — because you need results that last through your next season.
