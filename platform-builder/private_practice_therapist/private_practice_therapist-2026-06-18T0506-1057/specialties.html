Embracing Stillness: Your Nervous System as a New Mother

The postpartum period asks immense physical and emotional energy from you. Your nervous system, constantly responding to infant cues and sleep deprivation, can become dysregulated, leading to heightened anxiety, irritability, and a feeling of being 'on edge.' As your Private Practice Therapist, I guide you to understand these internal shifts, offering techniques to downregulate your stress response and cultivate a sense of inner peace amidst the beautiful chaos of new motherhood.
