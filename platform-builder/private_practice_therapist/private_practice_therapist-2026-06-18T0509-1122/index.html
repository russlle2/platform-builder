Frequently Asked Questions

How does Private Practice Therapy specifically address sleep for entrepreneurs?
As an entrepreneur, your sleep is often intertwined with relentless problem-solving and future-forward thinking. Our Private Practice Therapy goes beyond general sleep advice, targeting the specific cognitive and emotional patterns that prevent high-achievers from disengaging and achieving restorative rest, such as performance anxiety and the inability to 'switch off'.
Will Private Practice Therapy impact my demanding schedule?
We understand your time is a critical asset. Our Private Practice Therapy sessions are structured for efficiency and impact, integrating seamlessly into your high-demand lifestyle. The strategies you learn are designed to be practical and implementable, ultimately saving you time by optimizing your mental and physical recovery.
How is 'restorative rest' defined in your Private Practice Therapy approach?
Restorative rest, in our Private Practice Therapy, is not just about hours slept, but about the quality of that sleep – allowing for deep cellular repair, cognitive consolidation, and emotional regulation. It's the profound renewal that fuels sustained innovation, sharp decision-making, and resilience against burnout, moving beyond mere fatigue reduction.
What scientific principles guide your Private Practice Therapy for sleep improvement?
Our Private Practice Therapy is rooted in advanced neuroscience and evidence-based psychological frameworks. We integrate principles from cognitive behavioral therapy for insomnia (CBT-I), neurobiology of sleep, and stress physiology to create a precise, data-informed approach to enhancing your sleep and restorative capacity.
