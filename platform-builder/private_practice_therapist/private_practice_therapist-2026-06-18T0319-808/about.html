What exactly is 'postural health' for seniors, as a Private Practice Therapist defines it?

For seniors, postural health isn't about standing perfectly rigid. It's about finding a comfortable, balanced alignment that supports your spine, reduces strain on your joints, and allows for easier movement in your daily activities. As your Private Practice Therapist, we focus on helping you achieve this through gentle awareness and specific, low-impact guidance.

How can a Private Practice Therapist help me improve my body awareness without intense exercise?

Improving body awareness doesn't require strenuous activity. As your Private Practice Therapist, we use gentle, guided explorations of movement and sensation. You'll learn to notice subtle cues from your body – how your feet meet the ground, how your shoulders rest, or how your breath flows. This mindful attention gradually builds a stronger connection between your mind and body, without any pressure to perform.

Is this approach suitable if I have existing age-related physical limitations or discomfort?

Absolutely. As your Private Practice Therapist, our approach is specifically designed to be gentle and adaptable. We prioritize your comfort and safety, and all guidance is tailored to your individual capabilities and any existing physical limitations. Our goal is to alleviate discomfort and support your body's natural abilities, never to push beyond what feels appropriate for you.

What can I expect during my first session with your Private Practice Therapist?

During your first session with your Private Practice Therapist, we'll have a calm, confidential conversation about your current experiences, any areas of discomfort, and your goals for postural comfort. We'll also do some gentle, guided movements to assess your current body awareness and movement patterns. It's a relaxed, collaborative beginning to understand how we can best support you.
