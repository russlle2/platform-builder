Private Practice Therapist

Release the Physical Strain of Academic Stress in Private Practice Therapy

Your body often holds the unspoken stress of looming deadlines and social pressures. In Private Practice Therapy, we explore how prolonged study sessions, screen time, and academic anxiety can manifest as slumped shoulders, neck pain, or shallow breathing. We'll connect these physical sensations to your emotional state, developing practical strategies to improve your posture and reduce the physical burden of your college experience.
