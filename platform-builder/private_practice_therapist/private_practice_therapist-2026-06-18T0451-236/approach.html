Private Practice Therapist

Navigating the Emotional Landscape of Chronic Illness and Injury Recovery in Private Practice Therapy

Chronic illness and injury don't just affect your body; they profoundly reshape your emotional world. Feelings of loss, frustration, anxiety, and even grief are common, yet often unaddressed. As your Private Practice Therapist, we provide a safe space to explore these complex emotions without judgment. We'll work together to identify the specific emotional challenges you're facing, developing strategies to manage them and build a stronger emotional foundation for your ongoing recovery.
