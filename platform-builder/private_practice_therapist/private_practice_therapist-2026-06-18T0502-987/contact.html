Private Practice Therapist

Tending to Your Delicate Nervous System in Postpartum Recovery

The profound shifts of motherhood ripple through every cell, often leaving your nervous system humming with an unfamiliar intensity. Our Private Practice Therapy provides a compassionate anchor, guiding you through tailored practices that gently soothe overwhelm, reduce the persistent hum of anxiety, and invite a deeper sense of peace into your daily rhythm. We focus on practical, grounding techniques to help your body and mind reconnect after the beautiful upheaval of birth and new parenthood.
