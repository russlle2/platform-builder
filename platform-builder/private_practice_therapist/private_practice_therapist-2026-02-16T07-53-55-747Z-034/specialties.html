Areas I commonly support

Therapy is tailored to specific struggles. Below are common presenting concerns I work with—each card describes how we typically approach the issue and what a few sessions might focus on.

Anxiety & Panic

Short-term strategies for panic and generalized anxiety, including behavioral experiments, exposure exercises, and skills to reduce physiological arousal.

Trauma Recovery

Stabilization, grounding, and gradual processing using somatic-informed and evidence-based approaches to support integration and safety.

Relationship Strain

Work on communication, boundaries, and attachment patterns—either in individual work or collaborative couple sessions when appropriate.

Career & Burnout

Strategies to manage overwhelm, set sustainable limits, and realign work with values to prevent burnout.
