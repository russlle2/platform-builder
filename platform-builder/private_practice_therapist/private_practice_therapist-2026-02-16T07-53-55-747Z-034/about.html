Common Concerns People Share

Will therapy take forever?

Some goals are short-term and solution-focused; others need more time. We decide pacing together and periodically review progress.

Is this confidential?

Yes—except in specific legal or safety situations. I will explain limits of confidentiality before intake.

What if I don’t feel better right away?

Early sessions are about building trust and direction. We’ll set realistic milestones and adjust methods as we go.
