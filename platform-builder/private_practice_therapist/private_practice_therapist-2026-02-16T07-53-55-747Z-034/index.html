Common hesitations — and honest answers

"I don’t know if therapy will help me."

That’s a reasonable concern. We begin with clear goals and short-term check-ins so you can assess benefit early on. Therapy is a collaboration with measurable steps.

"I’m too busy for weekly sessions."

Many clients start with every-other-week or brief check-ins while building skills. We design a rhythm that fits your life.

"Won’t therapy just rehash my past?"

We aim to make the past understandable in service of present change — focusing on patterns that matter now and pragmatic tools for the future.
