Frequently Asked Questions

How does Private Practice Therapy specifically address stress for new mothers in postpartum recovery?
Our Private Practice Therapy acknowledges the unique stressors of postpartum, from sleep deprivation to identity shifts. We utilize targeted techniques such as somatic awareness and mindfulness to help you recognize and gently release tension, fostering a more regulated emotional landscape amidst the demands of new motherhood.
What kind of nervous system regulation techniques can I expect in your Private Practice Therapy?
In our Private Practice Therapy, you can expect to learn and practice gentle techniques that calm an overstimulated nervous system. This includes breathwork tailored for maternal well-being, grounding exercises, and sensory soothing methods, all designed to help you feel more centered and less reactive.
Is your Private Practice Therapy inclusive of all backgrounds and birthing experiences?
Absolutely. Our Private Practice Therapy is built on a foundation of inclusivity and community. We welcome new mothers from all backgrounds, family structures, and birthing experiences, providing a safe and respectful space for everyone to explore their postpartum journey.
How can Private Practice Therapy help me feel more grounded when everything feels overwhelming as a new mother?
Our Private Practice Therapy focuses on bringing you back to your body and the present moment. We'll explore practices that anchor you, such as guided visualizations and mindful movement, helping you to find pockets of stillness and strength when the world around you feels overwhelming, allowing you to respond rather than react.
