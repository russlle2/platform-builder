Frequently Asked Questions

How can Private Practice Therapy specifically help with my professional burnout?
We focus on the unique pressures faced by working professionals aged 30-45, addressing the root causes of your burnout. You'll gain practical techniques to manage work-related stress, regulate your nervous system, and set sustainable boundaries, helping you rebuild your capacity and energy.
What does 'nervous system regulation' mean, and how will it help me with stress?
Nervous system regulation means learning to shift your body and mind out of a constant state of fight-or-flight. We'll equip you with techniques to calm your physiological responses to stress, reduce anxiety, and improve your ability to cope with daily demands, leading to a more stable and resilient you.
I'm worried about adding another commitment to my busy schedule. How flexible is Private Practice Therapy?
We understand your time is valuable. We offer flexible scheduling options to accommodate your work commitments. Our goal is to make therapy accessible and supportive, not another source of stress. We can discuss what works best for your schedule during our initial consultation.
Will this therapy just teach me to 'cope' with a stressful job, or will it help me make real changes?
Our approach is about much more than just coping. While you'll learn effective coping mechanisms, we also empower you to identify and implement meaningful changes in your life and work patterns. This could involve improving boundaries, refining communication, or exploring sustainable career strategies, ultimately reducing the sources of your stress.
