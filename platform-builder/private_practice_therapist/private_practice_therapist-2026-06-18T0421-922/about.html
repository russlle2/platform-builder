Seasonal Stress Management, Not Just SAD

Your energy dips with the seasons—not just in winter. As your business cycles through launches and fiscal quarters, your stress patterns shift. We map yours using evidence-based seasonal resilience frameworks, so you’re never caught off-guard. Your calendar stays full—you just stay steady.

How is this different from regular therapy?

We focus exclusively on how seasonal shifts and business cycles impact your cognitive bandwidth. No ‘journey’ talk—just practical strategies to maintain performance during your busiest phases.

Can we fit this into my existing schedule?

Absolutely. Most sessions occur during natural breaks in your calendar—like before team meetings or during lunch. We optimize for your rhythm, not ours.

What if I’m not burnt out yet?

Preventative care works best when you’re operating at capacity. We help you build resilience before stress becomes a blocker, so you stay ahead of the curve.

How do you measure results?

We track your ability to maintain focus during high-pressure periods and reduce reactive stress responses. Think of it like monitoring your business KPIs—but for your mental stamina.
