Therapeutic approach

A collaborative, paced method that balances symptom relief with deeper personal understanding. I emphasize safety, clarity, and practical steps you can try between sessions.

Modality blend: {{MODALITIES}}

Three guiding principles

  1. Start with stabilization — practical tools to feel safer in the present.
  2. Build insight — understand patterns without blame.
  3. Create sustainable change — small, consistent shifts that fit your life.

A typical short-term process

  • Intake & priority setting.
  • Skill introduction: grounding, emotion regulation, communication tools.
  • Practice and review with small, measurable goals.
  • Planning for transition or ongoing work based on progress.

What to expect in session

Sessions are collaborative. I bring structured prompts, gentle reflection, and clear takeaways. You bring your experience — we move at a pace that feels tolerable and helpful.

Session styles

  • Skill-focused (short-term goals)
  • Exploratory (understanding patterns)
  • Integrated (skills + insight)
Fees & program framing: I offer single sessions, short bundles, and the Renewal Intensive (VIP Day) for concentrated work. Payment and insurance details are discussed at intake.

Questions about fit and logistics

How long does therapy usually last?

Some clients find relief in a handful of focused sessions. Others choose longer-term work. We set review points so you can decide whether to continue.

Will sessions be recorded or shared?

No. Sessions are not recorded without your explicit written consent. Clinical notes follow professional confidentiality standards.

What if I am unsure about therapy?

An initial consult is a low-commitment way to ask questions and get a sense of fit. We can outline a short plan and decide next steps together.
