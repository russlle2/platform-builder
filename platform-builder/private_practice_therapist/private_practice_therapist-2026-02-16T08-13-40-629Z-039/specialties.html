Areas of clinical focus

I provide focused therapeutic support for common, painful patterns. Each pathway is structured yet flexible to your pace and needs.

Anxiety & Panic

Practical strategies to reduce panic cycles, paired with exploration of underlying patterns that keep anxiety active.

Suitable for work-related anxiety, health anxiety, social anxiety.

Trauma-Informed Healing

Safe, paced approaches to process difficult experiences. We prioritize stabilization and present-moment resources before deeper processing.

Includes grounding, somatic presence, and narrative work.

Relationship Patterns

Work on communication, attachment patterns, and setting boundaries. Suitable for individuals or couples (when appropriate).

Burnout & Transitions

Support for career shifts, caregiving fatigue, and reclaiming balance. We create realistic steps for rest and sustainable change.

Life Stage Work

Navigating parenting changes, midlife shifts, empty nest, or retirement decisions with attention to values and meaning.

Focused Intensives

Short, concentrated packages for people seeking accelerated clarity and tools (see program options below).

Program options — choose what fits

  • Starter Bundle: 4 weekly sessions for focused skill-building and planning.
  • Care Pathway: Ongoing sessions with monthly reviews — for deeper relational work and steady progress.
  • Renewal Intensive (VIP Day): A single extended session to map goals, practice tools, and leave with a concise plan.

A word about confidentiality & scope

All clinical work is private and documented according to professional standards. I do not provide emergency services; if you are experiencing a crisis, please call your local emergency number or a crisis hotline. I will always discuss limits of confidentiality at intake.
