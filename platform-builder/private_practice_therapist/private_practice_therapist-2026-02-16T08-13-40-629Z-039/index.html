Questions people often ask

Do you offer insurance billing?
I do not bill insurance directly. I provide receipts you can submit to your provider. We can discuss sliding scale options when needed.
How long are sessions?
Standard sessions are 50 minutes. Extended sessions are available by arrangement for certain needs.
What if I’m in crisis?
If you are an immediate danger to yourself or others, please call your local emergency number or go to the nearest emergency room. If you are in {{STATE}}, you can also contact local crisis lines for support. I do not provide crisis services outside scheduled sessions.
