Questions I often hear

How do I know if therapy is the right step now?
Many people come when they notice patterns that feel stuck — increased anxiety, relationship tension, life transitions, or burnout. We can start with a single session to explore fit and goals without committing to a long plan.
What do sessions typically focus on?
Sessions are collaborative. We build from your concerns and values, blending practical tools with deeper exploration. Modalities I use include {{MODALITIES}}. Each plan is personalized to your needs.
Can I use insurance?
I do not bill insurance directly. I can provide a superbill for clients who wish to seek reimbursement. Insurance coverage, limits, and copays vary — please check with your provider.
Is telehealth available?
Yes. I offer secure video sessions and in-person options when available. We'll discuss what best supports your privacy and accessibility needs.
How long does therapy usually last?
There is no one-size-fits-all timeline. Some people seek brief support for a season; others prefer ongoing weekly work. We set collaborative goals and review progress regularly.
