Frequently Asked Questions

How is this Private Practice Therapy different from general stress management for burnout?
While stress management is part of it, our Private Practice Therapy specifically zeroes in on how your burnout manifests as sleep disturbances. We go beyond general advice to pinpoint the unique factors impacting your restorative rest, offering targeted strategies for sleep improvement that are relevant to your professional life.
I'm a working professional with a demanding schedule; can I really make time for Private Practice Therapy and improve my sleep?
Absolutely. We understand the constraints of a busy professional life. Our Private Practice Therapy offers flexible scheduling options to accommodate your commitments. Investing time in improving your sleep now will ultimately boost your productivity and well-being, making it a worthwhile use of your time.
Will Private Practice Therapy involve medication for sleep, or is it purely talk-based?
Our Private Practice Therapy primarily focuses on evidence-based behavioral and cognitive strategies to improve your sleep and address burnout. We'll explore techniques like CBT-I (Cognitive Behavioral Therapy for Insomnia) and lifestyle adjustments. While we don't prescribe medication, we can discuss it and, if appropriate, coordinate care with your medical doctor.
I've tried everything for my sleep and nothing works. How can your Private Practice Therapy help?
It's understandable to feel discouraged. In our Private Practice Therapy, we look deeper than surface-level solutions. We'll work together to identify underlying psychological factors, behavioral patterns, and environmental contributors to your poor sleep and burnout, creating a comprehensive and personalized plan that addresses the root causes, not just the symptoms.
