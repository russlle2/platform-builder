How work usually looks

I mix direct assessment, simple tools, and reflective questions. Sessions are held with clarity and openness—less layering, more pruning. Below is a sketch of common steps.

1
Clarify
We name the immediate problem and what success would look like.
2
Map
Identify patterns and small levers you can try between sessions.
3
Try
Put a short experiment in place, with a concrete observation plan.
4
Reflect
Review what changed, and refine the next step.
I aim for sessions that leave you with one clear, achievable next move. If long-term work is needed, we'll discuss a rhythm that supports growth without overwhelm.
