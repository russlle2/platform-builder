Questions people bring

Practical answers to common logistics, boundaries, and what to expect in the room.

How do I start?
You can request a short orientation by using the Reserve link. We'll clarify what you want from our time together and find the right first step—single session, a short series, or a concentrated day.
Do you accept insurance?
I provide superbills on request and can work with many major carriers where appropriate. Many clients pay privately and use insurance elsewhere; we'll discuss options during scheduling.
What is your cancellation policy?
Sessions canceled with at least 48 hours notice are rescheduled without charge when possible. Late cancellations or missed sessions may be billed; we'll review arrangements before we begin.
What kinds of concerns do you work with?
I work with anxiety, relationship transitions, life-stage stress, grief, and trauma-informed care, among other concerns. If your needs are outside my scope, I will provide referrals to other clinicians or services.
Is our work confidential?
Yes. I keep your information private except when disclosure is required by law (e.g., imminent harm) or when you provide written consent. We will discuss confidentiality and limits at intake.
What if I'm in crisis?
If you are in immediate danger or in a medical emergency, call emergency services. For urgent mental health crises in the U.S., dial 988 for the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. I do not provide crisis services via email—if you are in crisis, please call local emergency resources first.
