Common misunderstandings, clarified

  • Myth:

    Therapy must be long-term to help.

    Grounded view:

    Short, focused work can shift patterns; some needs do benefit from ongoing collaboration. We choose the scope together.

  • Myth:

    Change requires perfect discipline.

    Grounded view:

    Small, repeatable practices are more sustainable. We design steps that match your life.

  • Myth:

    Therapy is only for crisis.

    Grounded view:

    Therapy can be preventive, supportive, and skills-oriented, not only reactive care.

Questions people ask

Do you take insurance?
I do not bill insurance directly in most cases; I can provide a superbill for clients to submit to their insurer. We can discuss options during booking.
What happens in a membership?
Members receive one scheduled session per month, secure messaging check-ins, and curated practice materials. Membership is intended as ongoing supportive care rather than crisis-only access.
How is confidentiality handled?
Clinical records are maintained in a secure manner. Communications may have limitations depending on platform; we will review boundaries at intake.

Ready to start a steady path?

If you're curious about working together, schedule a connection call. We’ll clarify concerns, scope, and a plan aligned with your life.
