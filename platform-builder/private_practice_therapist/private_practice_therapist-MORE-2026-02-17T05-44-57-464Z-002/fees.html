Confidentiality, boundaries, and safety

Sessions are confidential within legal and ethical limits. I will explain what confidentiality includes and any limits (such as concerns about safety). If you are experiencing a crisis or are in danger, please contact local emergency services or go to the nearest emergency department. This practice does not provide emergency services.

Therapy is a collaborative process. I do not promise outcomes; instead we work together to clarify goals and try approaches that may help. If a different level of care would be more appropriate, I will discuss referrals.
