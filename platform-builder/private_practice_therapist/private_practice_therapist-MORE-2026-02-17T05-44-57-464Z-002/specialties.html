Focus areas

I work with adults to make transportable changes that honor how you live and who you care for. Below are some common reasons people seek support here.

Anxiety and worry

Short- and long-term support for patterns of persistent worry, panic symptoms, and avoidance. We use exposure planning, cognitive tools, and habit redesign to increase livability.

Mood regulation

Work on consistent routines, activity planning, and mood-tracking to create steadier days. Interventions are matched to your schedule and resources.

Life transitions & relationship work

Support through role changes, parenting shifts, separation, or relationship navigation. We aim for clarity in values and practical decisions aligned with them.

Attention & executive habit support

If organizing tasks or keeping a routine feels difficult, we consider structure adjustments, environmental design, and brief behavioral experiments.
How I present care
I generally offer ongoing care through a membership: regular check-ins, easy scheduling, and flexible session lengths for people balancing work and life demands. Pricing and specifics are shared during a consult.
