Clinical approach — pragmatic and collaborative

I blend accessible therapeutic frameworks with short experiments you can try between sessions. The aim is steady, usable change rather than quick fixes.

1. Shared formulation

We start by mapping the problem together: what maintains it, what matters to you, and what realistic changes might look like in your life context.

2. Small experiments

We design brief, testable steps — a behavioral experiment, a small schedule change, a short mindful practice — and review what helps and what doesn't.

3. Ongoing support

Through a membership model you have predictable access: regular sessions, planning time, and slots for shorter check-ins when helpful.

4. Adjustment and maintenance

We adapt interventions to fit life shifts and focus on ways you can sustain helpful changes without overwhelming your schedule.

Case notes — a composite vignette

A person came in feeling stuck by low motivation and near-daily worry. Together we clarified a single week-long experiment: a brief morning routine and a 15-minute evening mood log. Over four weeks, the person reported clearer structure and selected adjustments that fit their schedule.
