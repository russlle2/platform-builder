About the practice

Psychotherapy shaped around how you actually live

I offer ongoing clinical support through a membership model — a steady place for reflection, planning, and small adjustments that build over time. Sessions blend evidence-informed approaches with realistic, daily practices we can adapt to your life.

How I work

My work is collaborative and pragmatic. We clarify goals, map small habits that support those aims, and test what actually fits your week. I bring clinical training and an emphasis on safety, confidentiality, and practical steps you can try between sessions.

Common misconceptions

Myth: Therapy must always be intensive to matter.
Truth: Consistent, steady attention often makes change more manageable; the pace is matched to you.
Myth: There's one right method for everyone.
Truth: We tailor methods to your needs — what feels coherent and doable for you.
