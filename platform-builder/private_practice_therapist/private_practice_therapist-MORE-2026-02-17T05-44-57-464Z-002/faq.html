How do I pay and what methods are accepted?
Card payments and invoicing are accepted. Membership plans are billed monthly when selected. If you need a different arrangement, please mention it in a private message and we will discuss options.
Is telehealth available?
Yes—sessions over a secure, HIPAA-conscious platform are available for most clients. In-person availability is noted on the booking page for those local to {{CITY}}, {{STATE}}.
What if I need to cancel or reschedule?
Please notify me as early as possible. Cancellation policies vary by plan; membership agreements will include specific terms. If an emergency arises, reach out and we will work to reschedule fairly.
Do you provide documentation for insurance?
I can provide a superbill if you wish to seek out-of-network reimbursement from your insurer. Some documents may require additional time and a fee—please ask in advance.
How is confidentiality handled?
Sessions are confidential within legal and ethical limits. Information is only shared with consent except in situations such as imminent risk of harm, suspected abuse, or as otherwise required by law. I will always discuss these limits with you.
What should I do in a crisis?
If you are in immediate danger or need urgent medical help, contact local emergency services or go to the nearest emergency department. This practice is not an emergency service and cannot guarantee immediate responses outside scheduled sessions.
How long does therapy typically last?
The length of work varies based on your goals and needs. Some people find short-term, focused support is enough; others prefer ongoing, periodic work. We’ll discuss a plan that fits your priorities during early sessions.
