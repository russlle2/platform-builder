Understanding Postpartum Emotional Recovery in Private Practice Therapy

Your emotional landscape after childbirth can feel overwhelming. In our Private Practice Therapy sessions, we acknowledge that postpartum recovery extends far beyond the physical. We'll explore the complex feelings, hormonal changes, and new identities that emerge, providing a safe space to process your experiences without judgment. This isn't about 'getting back to normal,' but about discovering your new normal with resilience and support.
