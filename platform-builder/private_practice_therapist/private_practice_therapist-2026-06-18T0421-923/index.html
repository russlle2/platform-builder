Frequently Asked Questions

How soon will I notice changes in my focus?
Most clients see clearer planning within 2-3 weeks of implementing small, personalized adjustments. We track metrics like task completion time, not vague 'feelings.'
Do you work with Medicare or supplemental plans?
Yes. We offer transparent billing for preventive care sessions, with clear breakdowns of covered services. We’ll help you navigate your plan’s specifics.
Can sessions adjust around seasonal weather changes?
Absolutely. If a heatwave disrupts your morning routine, we’ll shift exercises to your afternoon window. Flexibility is built into every plan.
What if I’m not ready for 'deep work'?
We never pressure you. Your focus is on practical life moments—like playing bridge with friends without forgetting the rules—using tools that fit your current energy.
