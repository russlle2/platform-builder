Frequently Asked Questions

How can Private Practice Therapy help me manage the emotional impact of physical stress?
As an athlete or active individual, your body is often pushed to its limits. Our Private Practice Therapy helps you explore how physical stressors, injuries, or performance pressures might be affecting your emotional state. We provide strategies to process these experiences, regulate your nervous system, and build resilience, so you can address the emotional side of your physical life.
What does 'trauma-informed care' mean for an athlete seeing a Private Practice Therapist?
Trauma-informed care means your Private Practice Therapist recognizes that intense physical or competitive experiences can sometimes have lasting emotional effects, even if they aren't traditionally labeled as 'trauma.' We approach your story with sensitivity, understanding, and a focus on creating safety, helping you process difficult memories or feelings without re-traumatization, and fostering a sense of empowerment.
Will a Private Practice Therapist understand the unique pressures I face as an active person?
Absolutely. Your Private Practice Therapist is specifically attuned to the challenges faced by athletes and active individuals. We understand the drive for performance, the disappointment of setbacks, and the unique mental fortitude required. Our approach is tailored to acknowledge and support these specific aspects of your experience, rather than treating them as generic stress.

As your Private Practice Therapist, we create a balanced and integrative path to emotional healing, helping you connect mind and body for more complete well-being, specifically for your active lifestyle.
