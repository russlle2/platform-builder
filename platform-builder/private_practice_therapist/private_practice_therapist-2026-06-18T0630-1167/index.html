Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Private Practice Therapist truly help with my postpartum sleep when my baby dictates the schedule?
As your Private Practice Therapist, I understand that your baby's needs are paramount. Our focus isn't about forcing an unrealistic sleep schedule, but rather about cultivating restorative rest within your unique circumstances. We explore ways to maximize the sleep you do get, manage sleep fragmentation, reduce anxiety around sleep, and create a calming environment that supports your body's natural inclination to rest, even in short bursts.
I feel so overwhelmed and tired; will therapy add another burden to my plate?
It's completely understandable to feel that way. My role as a Private Practice Therapist is to lighten your load, not add to it. Sessions are a dedicated space for you to be heard and supported, without judgment. We will move at your pace, focusing on gentle, practical steps that can be woven into your life without creating additional stress, always prioritizing your well-being and seeking to bring ease to your days and nights.
Is this just about sleep hygiene, or does a Private Practice Therapist offer something deeper?
While sleep hygiene is a component, my work as your Private Practice Therapist delves much deeper. We explore the emotional, psychological, and physiological factors that impact postpartum sleep. This includes processing the intense emotions of new motherhood, addressing anxiety, exploring your identity shifts, and understanding how these contribute to your ability to find restorative rest. It's about nurturing the whole you, so sleep can naturally follow.
I'm worried about feeling judged for my sleep struggles; how can I feel safe sharing with a Private Practice Therapist?
My private practice is built on a foundation of profound empathy and non-judgment. As your Private Practice Therapist, I recognize that struggling with sleep during postpartum is incredibly common and nothing to be ashamed of. This is a sanctuary where your vulnerability is met with compassion, and your experiences are honored. You will find a safe, confidential space to explore your challenges without fear of criticism, fostering a sense of trust and understanding.
