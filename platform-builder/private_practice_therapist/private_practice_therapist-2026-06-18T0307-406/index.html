Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Private Practice Therapist?
A Private Practice Therapist operates independently, providing personalized mental health services directly to you. This structure often allows for more tailored care, greater flexibility in scheduling, and a consistent therapeutic relationship with your chosen practitioner, .
How long does therapy typically last?
The duration of therapy varies greatly depending on your individual needs and goals. Some clients find resolution in a few months, while others benefit from longer-term support. We will regularly assess your progress together to ensure your therapy remains effective and aligned with your objectives.
Is what I share confidential?
Absolutely. Confidentiality is a cornerstone of our Private Practice Therapist commitment. What you share in your sessions is held in strict confidence, with very few legal exceptions (e.g., imminent harm to self or others). We will discuss these limits thoroughly during your first session to ensure you feel secure.
Do you offer virtual therapy sessions?
Yes, to provide greater accessibility and flexibility, offers secure virtual therapy sessions. This allows you to engage in effective therapy from the comfort and privacy of your own space, maintaining continuity of care regardless of your location within .
