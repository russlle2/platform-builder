How is this different from standard therapy?

We focus on prevention, not just reaction. While other therapists wait for burnout symptoms, we work with you before deadlines or seasons take a toll—like planning your 'recovery blocks' for the upcoming holiday rush.

What if my company expects me to be always available?

We help you build sustainable boundaries that work within your role. You’ll learn to say 'I’ll handle this after my 3 PM recovery block' without guilt, keeping your productivity high and your energy stable.

Do you work with people who need to 'fix' burnout?

Not yet. We work with you *before* you’re overwhelmed. Think of it like an annual physical—catching energy dips early prevents the need for major resets later.

How quickly will I see results?

You’ll start noticing smaller shifts within weeks: less 3 PM fatigue, fewer reactive decisions, and clearer focus during meetings. The real win? Preventing that deep burnout that derails your career trajectory.
