Frequently Asked Questions

How can Private Practice Therapy focused on postural health help with my college anxiety?
When you're anxious, your body often tenses up, leading to a hunched posture or shallow breathing. Our Private Practice Therapy helps you recognize these physical signs and learn to gently release that tension. By becoming more aware of your body and adjusting your posture, you can send signals to your nervous system that it's safe to relax, helping to reduce feelings of anxiety and improve your focus on academics.
Will I be doing yoga or physical exercises in Private Practice Therapy?
While we won't be doing a full yoga class, our Private Practice Therapy incorporates gentle, mindful movements and body awareness practices. The focus is on understanding your unique physical responses to stress and learning how to use your posture and breath as tools for self-regulation, rather than intense physical exercise. It's about practical, everyday changes you can integrate seamlessly.
I'm not very 'in tune' with my body. Can Private Practice Therapy still help me?
Absolutely! Many college students feel disconnected from their bodies, especially when overwhelmed by academics. Our Private Practice Therapy is designed to meet you where you are. We'll start with simple, accessible ways to notice your body's signals, building your awareness gradually. You don't need prior experience; just a willingness to explore how your physical self impacts your mental well-being.
How quickly can I expect to see changes in my anxiety and posture through Private Practice Therapy?
The pace of change varies for everyone, but many clients in Private Practice Therapy report feeling a greater sense of calm and improved body awareness within a few sessions. By consistently practicing the tools we explore, you'll begin to notice shifts in how you carry yourself and how you respond to stressful situations. It's about cultivating sustainable habits that support your well-being long-term.
