Starting questions

How do I know if therapy with you is right for me?
I offer a short diagnostic conversation to clarify needs and goals. We’ll explore safety, immediate concerns, and the kind of container that feels supportive. It’s normal to be unsure — the diagnostic call is precisely for that uncertainty.
What should I expect during the first few sessions?
Early sessions typically focus on assessment, co-creating a plan, and introducing small practices you can try between sessions. I aim for a steady rhythm that balances insight with actionable steps.
Do you provide teletherapy?
Yes. I meet with clients by secure telehealth and in-person when available. We’ll discuss what feels most grounding for you during the intake.
Is therapy confidential?
Yes — within legal and ethical limits (e.g., imminent harm, abuse, court orders). Confidentiality details are provided in our initial paperwork and I’m happy to review them with you.

Support between sessions

I encourage micro-habits that support embodiment and steadiness: short grounding practices, 3-minute journaling, breath checks, and consistent sleep routines. These are not replacements for therapy, but they often accelerate progress.

If you need urgent support between sessions, we’ll discuss a plan together. I do not provide crisis services; for immediate distress, contact emergency services.

Questions clients often ask

How will we measure progress?
We set collaborative goals and periodically review them. Progress is both measurable (reduced panic frequency, improved sleep) and qualitative (feeling steadier, more agency).
What if my needs are outside your scope?
I will be honest about scope. If your needs require medication management, acute inpatient care, or specialized services, I will provide referrals and coordinate transitions with your consent.
How do cancellations work?
I ask for 48 hours notice. Late cancellations may be billed; exceptions are made for emergencies and safety concerns.
