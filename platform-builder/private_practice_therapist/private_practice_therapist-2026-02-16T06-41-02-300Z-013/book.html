What the diagnostic looks like

The diagnostic call is a listening conversation: I’ll check in about safety, immediate needs, past therapy, and what change would look like for you. We usually end with a clear next step — single session, short series, or an invitation to a cohort.

Typical flow
  • 10 minutes — present concerns & safety
  • 6 minutes — history & supports
  • 4 minutes — proposed plan and next steps

Micro-practices to try before our call

If it feels helpful, try these brief practices to build grounding: a 3-minute breath practice (inhale 4, exhale 6), a short body scan, or a single-line journaling prompt each evening. These are gentle experiments — not requirements.
