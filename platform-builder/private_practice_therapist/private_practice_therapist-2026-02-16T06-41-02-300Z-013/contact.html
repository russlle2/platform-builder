Small practices you can try today

Box breathing — 4 in, hold 4, 4 out, hold 4. 1 minute can calm the nervous system.
Sensory check-in — name three things you see, two you can touch, one you hear.
Gentle boundary — practice saying a brief, kind no to something small to notice how you feel.

These are supportive practices; they are not a substitute for therapy when deeper work is needed.
