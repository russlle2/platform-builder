Session boundaries & confidentiality

What is kept private?−
Clinical conversations are treated as confidential. Limits include imminent risk of harm, suspected abuse, or legal orders. I will aim to discuss any limits with you whenever possible.
Records and notes+
I keep brief clinical notes that support continuity of care. If you request copies, I will guide you through the process and discuss any privacy concerns.
Emergency & crisis guidance+
If you are in immediate danger, call local emergency services. For urgent mental health concerns outside sessions, visit the nearest emergency department or call the national crisis line. For non-urgent safety planning, we will address it together during the first meeting or by phone if needed.
If you are thinking about harming yourself or someone else, contact emergency services immediately or use your local crisis line. If you are located in {{STATE}} and need help locating resources, email {{EMAIL}} and I will assist with referrals.
