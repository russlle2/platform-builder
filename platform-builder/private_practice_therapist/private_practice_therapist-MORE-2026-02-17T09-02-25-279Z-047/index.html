How I notice what's most helpful

I begin with a short diagnostic arc: two sessions to clarify what is most burdensome, what tends to help, and how we’ll track meaningful change. This is clinical work — not quick fixes. We keep goals tangible and scope bounded.

Sessions are standardly 45–55 minutes. Telehealth and in-person options depend on scheduling. Notes are kept in a secure practice record. Where safety concerns arise (risk to self or others), those limits to confidentiality are reviewed and handled in line with legal/ethical standards.

If you are currently at risk of harm, call local emergency services or your crisis line immediately.

Small practices that matter

Between sessions, small shifts root clinical work in daily life. Below are simple experiments I often suggest.

  • Three-minute grounding: name five things you see, four you feel, three you hear.
  • Boundary rehearsal: a two-line script for a difficult request.
  • End-of-day check: one thing that went okay, one small next step.
