Session boundaries & confidentiality

Sessions are typically 45–55 minutes. I’ll set expectations for cancellations, rescheduling, and contact. If you join a cohort, attendance norms will be reviewed in advance.
Your notes are private. Exceptions include imminent risk of harm, mandated reporting, or court orders. We’ll discuss documentation and limits before we begin.
If you are in immediate danger or a medical emergency, call local emergency services. For urgent emotional crisis in the U.S., call 988 or your local crisis number. I cannot provide crisis management via email.
