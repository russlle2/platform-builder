How I tend to work with people

I borrow from several well-researched approaches and adapt them to each person or cohort. The emphasis is on practices you can use, clear expectations, and safety. Below are the key commitments I bring to therapy.

Practical orientation

We identify small, testable steps rather than only talking. Goals are revisited and adjusted—therapy should connect to daily life.

Collaborative clarity

We set agreements about focus, confidentiality, communication, and what happens between sessions.

Safety & pacing

I track safety concerns explicitly and slow down when material is intense. I’ll offer referrals when the work is outside my scope.
