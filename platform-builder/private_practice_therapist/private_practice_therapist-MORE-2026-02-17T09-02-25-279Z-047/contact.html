Session guidelines

I value clear session boundaries to keep our work focused and safe. Typical sessions are 45–55 minutes; we start and end on time. I request notice for cancellations and have a limited scheduling policy to preserve continuity for all clients.

If a different rhythm is needed we can discuss options that fit your life and clinical needs.

Confidentiality & limits

I protect what you share within legal and ethical limits. Exceptions include situations where there is risk of harm to you or others, suspected abuse of a vulnerable person, or legal requirements such as court orders.

Clinical notes are kept for professional use and storage follows standard privacy practices. If you have questions about records, let’s talk about what you want to keep private and how I document sessions.

If you are in immediate danger or thinking about harming yourself or someone else, please call local emergency services right away. For non‑emergency crisis lines in the U.S., you can call 988. If you are elsewhere, contact local emergency resources. I am not a crisis service and cannot promise immediate response through email or form submissions.
