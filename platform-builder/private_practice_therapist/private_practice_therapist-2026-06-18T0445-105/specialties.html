Understanding Sleep Challenges for College Students: A Private Practice Therapist's View

College life is a whirlwind, and it's easy for sleep to take a backseat. Between late-night study sessions, social pressures, and the constant hum of anxiety about grades and the future, your natural sleep rhythms can get completely thrown off. As your Private Practice Therapist, I understand these unique challenges aren't just about 'sleeping more,' but about addressing the underlying pressures that make rest feel impossible. We'll explore what's keeping you awake.
