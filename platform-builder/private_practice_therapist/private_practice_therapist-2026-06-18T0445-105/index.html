Frequently Asked Questions

How can a Private Practice Therapist specifically help with my college sleep problems?
As your Private Practice Therapist, I'll help you identify the specific anxieties and academic pressures that are disrupting your sleep. We'll then work together on targeted strategies, like cognitive restructuring for racing thoughts or stress reduction techniques, to help you calm your mind and create a conducive environment for restorative rest, instead of just general sleep advice.
I'm so busy with classes and extracurriculars; how can I fit therapy for sleep into my college schedule?
I totally get that your time is precious. As your Private Practice Therapist, I offer flexible scheduling options to accommodate your demanding college life. Our sessions are designed to be efficient and focused, providing you with practical tools you can implement right away, minimizing the time commitment while maximizing your progress towards better sleep.
Will addressing my sleep issues really help with my academic anxiety and pressure?
Absolutely! There's a strong link between sleep, anxiety, and academic performance. When you're well-rested, your ability to focus, retain information, and manage stress significantly improves. As your Private Practice Therapist, we'll address your sleep not just as an isolated issue, but as a crucial foundation for reducing your overall academic anxiety and pressure.
What if I've tried everything for my sleep and nothing has worked?
It's understandable to feel frustrated if you've tried various solutions without success. As your Private Practice Therapist, I approach each individual with fresh eyes, delving deeper into the root causes of your sleep difficulties beyond surface-level fixes. We'll explore personalized, evidence-based techniques together to find what genuinely works for your unique situation and helps you achieve restorative rest.
