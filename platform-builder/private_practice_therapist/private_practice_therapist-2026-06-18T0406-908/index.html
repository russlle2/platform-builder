Frequently Asked Questions

Do you use ancient healing terms or just ‘wellness’ jargon?
We honor traditions like moon-phase rhythms or seasonal herbal wisdom—but only when it feels meaningful *to you*. No vague ‘spiritual energy’ talk. We’ll talk about adjusting your pillow height in winter for better sleep or choosing soothing teas as temperatures shift.
How is this different from a regular doctor’s preventive visit?
Your doctor checks your blood pressure. We check how the *season* affects your body’s daily feel. We won’t replace medical care, but we’ll help you align your daily habits with nature’s flow—like warming your hands before bed when nights grow long—to ease tension before it starts.
What if I’m not ‘into’ spirituality?
This is about practical, grounded care—no beliefs required. We focus on tangible things: how your joints feel in humid weather, or which gentle movements make you feel steadier. Spirituality stays personal—your comfort is our only focus.
Can this help even if I’m already comfortable?
Absolutely. We’ll notice subtle shifts before they become noticeable—like when you start needing less help opening jars in spring. Small, timely adjustments keep you feeling your best long-term, not just today.
