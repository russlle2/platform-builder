Why I do this work

I trained in clinical psychology and have supported people through grief, burnout, and major life shifts for over a decade. My practice is calm, pragmatic, and deeply attuned to each person’s values. I aim to help clients build practical skills while honoring the complexity of their histories.

Outside the office I keep a simple routine — reading, walking by the water, and remembering that steady change is often slow and relational. I bring that same steadiness into sessions with compassion and clear focus.

How I Work — A Practical Framework

I use a stepwise, collaborative framework that keeps you engaged in setting goals and noticing change. Each step is adaptable to your pace.

1. Build Safety & Stabilization

Focus on practical skills for emotional regulation and grounding.

2. Identify Patterns

Explore recurring thoughts, behaviors, and relationship patterns with curiosity.

3. Skill Development

Practice tools (behavioral experiments, mindful awareness, communication skills).

4. Meaning & Next Steps

Shift toward values-led living and sustainable changes.
