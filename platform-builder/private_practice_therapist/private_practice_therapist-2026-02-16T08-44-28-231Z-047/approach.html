My Map for Therapy — What Guides the Work

I view therapy as a map-making process. We name the terrain (what’s happening), mark the landmarks (patterns), and chart paths forward that fit your life and values.

I integrate modalities and tailor them to what actually helps you feel more capable and connected.

Core Process

Orientation
We clarify what matters most and build immediate safety.
Assess & Track
We notice patterns and set measurable, concrete goals.
Intervention
Skill practice, exposure when appropriate, relational work, and processing.

Pilot Series

A short, focused series (6–8 sessions) to test approaches and build momentum toward change.

Continuing Work

Open-ended therapy for deeper exploration and long-term support.

Skills Lab

Practical sessions targeting specific skills (sleep, worry management, communication).

My Therapeutic Stance

I aim to be direct, humane, and collaborative. I offer structure when it helps and hold reflection when the work asks for it. Therapy is not a quick fix; it is relational and iterative.
