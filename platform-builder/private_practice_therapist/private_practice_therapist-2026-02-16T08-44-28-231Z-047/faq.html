My work centers on building safety, then moving toward change. Below are answers shaped by common concerns I hear in the first few sessions.

Therapeutic frame

Sessions are collaborative and confidential. We will co-create goals and check in on progress. I integrate evidence-informed approaches without promising specific outcomes.

How I typically structure work

  • Short-term focus (4–8 sessions) for a defined issue.
  • Open-ended weekly therapy for ongoing relational or trauma work.
  • Occasional drop-in sessions for check-ins and strategy.

How do I know if therapy is right for me?

Therapy can help when everyday coping feels strained or when patterns cause repeated pain. We can start with a single intake to assess fit and goals—there is no obligation to continue.

What happens in the first session?

The first session is an intake: we review your history, current concerns, safety, and what you hope to change. We’ll also discuss confidentiality and create an initial plan together.

Do you accept insurance?

I provide superbills for out-of-network reimbursement but do not bill insurance directly. Contact your insurer to confirm out-of-network benefits and mental health coverage.

Can I schedule a virtual or in-person session?

I offer telehealth and limited in-person availability. We’ll decide what’s safest and most effective for your needs during intake.

Are sessions confidential?

Yes—conversations are confidential except in limited legal or safety situations (e.g., risk of harm to self or others, child or elder abuse). If you have concerns we will discuss them before sharing information.

What if I’m in crisis?

This site and my practice are not crisis services. If you are in immediate danger, call local emergency services or your crisis hotline. If you disclose imminent risk during contact, I may need to involve emergency resources.

How long should I expect to attend?

Therapy length varies. Some people work for a handful of sessions to address a specific issue; others continue for months to build deeper change. We’ll set periodic review points.

Rates & policies (brief)

Standard sessions are listed on the Work With Me / Fees page. Cancellation requires 48 hours notice. I strive for transparent billing—no hidden fees.
