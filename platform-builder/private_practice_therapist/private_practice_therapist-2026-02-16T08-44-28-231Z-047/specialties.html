Areas of Focus — What I Usually Work With

I support adults through a range of concerns. Below are common areas I address with a blend of skills training, relational exploration, and careful processing when needed.

Anxiety & Worry

Techniques to reduce avoidance, manage intense thoughts, and rebuild confidence in daily routines.

Common presentations: generalized worry, panic, health-related anxiety.

Trauma-Informed Care

A gentle, stabilizing approach that centers safety first. Pacing and consent guide the work.

Includes skills for grounding, regulation, and integrating experiences.

Relationship & Communication

Help with patterns that get in the way of connection — assertive communication, boundaries, and repair.

Life Transitions & Identity

Career changes, caregiving shifts, becoming a parent, and other transitions explored with practical planning and emotional support.

Burnout & Workplace Stress

Strategies for pacing, boundaries, and recalibrating expectations to protect wellbeing.

Sleep & Behavioral Patterns

Targeted interventions for sleep hygiene, routines, and reinforcing helpful behavior change.

Compass of Care

I use a compass metaphor in sessions: safety, skills, exploration, and integration. We map where you are and choose practical next steps together.

Core elements
  • Stabilization & regulation
  • Experiential noticing
  • Skills-based rehearsal
  • Values-focused change
