Finding Ground Again: Private Practice Therapy for Persistent Pain

When your body feels like a stranger after chronic illness or injury, it can be disorienting. Our Private Practice Therapy specializes in gentle techniques to address the physical and emotional residue of these experiences, helping you re-establish a sense of safety and comfort within your own skin. We focus on somatic practices that honor your body's wisdom.
