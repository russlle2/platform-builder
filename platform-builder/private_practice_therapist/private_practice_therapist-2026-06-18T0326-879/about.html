Easing the Physical Load of Parenting with Private Practice Therapy

Parenting often means constant lifting, bending, and carrying. Over time, these movements can lead to aches and a feeling of being 'out of alignment.' Our Private Practice Therapy focuses on helping you notice these patterns and learn simple adjustments for better posture and less strain, so you can move through your day with greater ease and less physical stress.
