Private Practice Therapist

Specialized Emotional Healing in Private Practice Therapy

Navigating life after chronic illness or injury brings unique emotional landscapes. Our private practice therapy focuses on addressing the specific grief, anxiety, and frustration that often accompany physical recovery. We provide a tailored, trauma-informed approach to gently guide you through these complex feelings, fostering resilience and a renewed sense of self beyond your physical experience.
