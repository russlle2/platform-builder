Frequently Asked Questions

How can Private Practice Therapy specifically help me if I'm recovering from a chronic illness or injury?
Our Private Practice Therapy focuses on the unique emotional landscape that accompanies recovery from chronic illness or injury. We help you process grief, frustration, anxiety, and the sense of identity shift that can occur, providing tools to navigate these complex feelings and rebuild your sense of self.
Is trauma-informed care relevant even if I don't feel my illness or injury was 'traumatic'?
Yes, absolutely. Trauma-informed care in Private Practice Therapy means we understand that experiences of chronic illness or injury can be deeply impactful, regardless of whether you label them as 'traumatic.' We approach your story with sensitivity, recognizing the potential for emotional distress and ensuring your comfort and safety throughout our work together.
What if my physical condition limits my ability to attend in-person Private Practice Therapy sessions?
We understand that physical limitations are a significant consideration. Our Private Practice Therapy offers flexible options, including virtual sessions, to accommodate your needs. We want to ensure that accessing support for your emotional healing is as convenient and comfortable as possible for you.
How long does emotional healing typically take when recovering from chronic illness or injury in Private Practice Therapy?
The timeline for emotional healing is unique to each individual, especially when navigating recovery from chronic illness or injury. Our Private Practice Therapy provides ongoing support, working at your pace. We focus on building your resilience and coping strategies, understanding that healing is a process, not a destination.
