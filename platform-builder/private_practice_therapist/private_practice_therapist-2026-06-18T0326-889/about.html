How can a Private Practice Therapist help me manage the physical demands of parenting?

As your Private Practice Therapist, I'll guide you in recognizing how daily parenting activities affect your body. We'll explore gentle techniques from both Eastern and Western traditions to improve your posture, reduce tension, and build body awareness, helping you move through your day with more comfort and less strain.

I'm always rushing; how can I realistically incorporate body awareness into my busy schedule with young children?

That's a common concern! We'll focus on small, actionable steps that fit into your existing routine, rather than adding extra burdens. It might be a mindful moment while you're holding your child, or a brief check-in with your breath during a quiet minute. My approach as a Private Practice Therapist is about integrating awareness, not creating new tasks.

Will addressing my posture really help with my overall stress as a parent?

Absolutely. Your physical state and emotional well-being are deeply interconnected. When your body is tense and misaligned, it can exacerbate feelings of stress and overwhelm. By improving your postural health and body awareness, you'll often find a corresponding reduction in mental and emotional tension, helping you feel more resilient as a parent.

What makes your Private Practice Therapist approach different for parents of young children?

My Private Practice Therapist approach specifically acknowledges the unique physical and emotional stressors of parenting young children. We don't just talk about stress; we explore its impact on your body and offer practical, body-centered tools to help you respond to those demands with greater calm and physical ease, rather than pushing through them.
