Frequently Asked Questions

What can I expect during my first session with a Private Practice Therapist?
Your first session is an opportunity for us to get to know each other. You'll share what brings you to therapy, and we'll discuss your goals and how we can best support you. It's a low-pressure environment for you to feel comfortable and ask any questions you have.
How long does therapy typically last when working with a Private Practice Therapist?
The duration of therapy is highly individualized. Some individuals find clarity and resolution in a few sessions, while others benefit from longer-term support. We will regularly assess your progress and adjust our approach to best suit your evolving needs.
Is what I share with my Private Practice Therapist confidential?
Yes, confidentiality is a cornerstone of our practice. What you share in your sessions is protected by strict ethical guidelines and legal requirements, with a few specific exceptions related to safety, which will be thoroughly explained to you.
How do I know if a Private Practice Therapist is the right fit for me?
Finding the right fit is crucial. We encourage an initial consultation to see if our approach aligns with your needs and personality. It's important that you feel comfortable and understood with your Private Practice Therapist, and we're happy to discuss this with you.
