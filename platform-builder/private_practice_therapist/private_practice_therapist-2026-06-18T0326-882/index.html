Frequently Asked Questions

How does Private Practice Therapy specifically address postpartum postural health?
Our Private Practice Therapy focuses on the unique changes your body experiences after childbirth. We look at how carrying, feeding, and bending for your baby impact your spine, shoulders, and pelvis, and then provide tailored strategies and exercises to restore balance and reduce strain. It's about retraining your body for its new demands.
Is this Private Practice Therapy suitable for someone several months postpartum?
Absolutely! There's no specific timeline for addressing postpartum postural health. Whether you're a few weeks or several months postpartum, our Private Practice Therapy can help you identify and correct compensatory patterns that may have developed, improving your comfort and functional movement at any stage.
What can I expect in a session focusing on body awareness in Private Practice Therapy?
In our Private Practice Therapy sessions, we often begin with gentle guided movements and observational exercises to help you tune into your body's current state. We'll explore how different positions feel and how small adjustments can create significant shifts in comfort, fostering a deeper connection to your physical self. It's a very collaborative process.
How does your integrative approach to Private Practice Therapy benefit new mothers?
Our integrative Private Practice Therapy approach recognizes that your physical posture is intimately linked to your emotional and mental state, especially postpartum. By blending Eastern principles of mindful movement with Western understanding of biomechanics, we offer a comprehensive path to not just physical recovery, but also a renewed sense of calm and control in your body.
