Recalibrating Your Internal Compass: Private Practice Therapy Focus

Major life transitions — be it career changes, relational shifts, or new beginnings — can dysregulate the nervous system, leading to heightened stress. Our focus in Private Practice Therapy is to provide you with targeted strategies to re-establish physiological equilibrium. We delve into the science of stress response, offering practical techniques to down-regulate activation and cultivate internal coherence, moving you from reactivity to considered response.
