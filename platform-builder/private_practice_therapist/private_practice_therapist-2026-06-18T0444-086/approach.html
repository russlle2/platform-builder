Private Practice Therapist

A Gentle, Accessible Approach to Feeling Better

We understand that your energy levels might be fluctuating, and you might feel sensitive to new inputs. That's why our Private Practice Therapy approach is always gentle and tailored to your specific needs. There's no pressure to perform or push yourself. Instead, we focus on small, manageable steps that can make a big difference in how you feel day-to-day. Our goal is to help you build comfort and safety within your own body, at your own pace.
