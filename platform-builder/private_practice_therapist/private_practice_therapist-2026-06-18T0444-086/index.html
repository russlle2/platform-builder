Understanding Your Nervous System in Recovery

When you're dealing with chronic illness or injury, your body and mind are often working overtime. This can leave your nervous system in a constant state of alert, making rest and recovery feel impossible. Our Private Practice Therapy focuses on simple, accessible ways to help your nervous system find a more balanced rhythm, without needing any prior wellness experience. We’ll explore gentle techniques to reduce that 'on-edge' feeling, helping you feel more grounded as you heal.

Frequently Asked Questions

I'm recovering from a chronic illness/injury and often feel exhausted. Is Private Practice Therapy still right for me?
Absolutely. Our Private Practice Therapy is designed specifically for people like you. We understand that your energy levels may be limited, and our sessions are structured to be gentle and low-demand. We focus on simple, accessible techniques for stress reduction and nervous system regulation that won't add to your fatigue. We meet you where you are, always.
I've never done any 'wellness' practices before. Will I be able to understand and benefit from this type of Private Practice Therapy?
Yes, completely! You don't need any prior wellness experience to benefit from our Private Practice Therapy. Our approach is gentle and accessible, explaining everything in straightforward language. We'll guide you step-by-step through techniques for stress reduction and nervous system regulation, ensuring you feel comfortable and understand how they can help your recovery.
How does Private Practice Therapy help with the physical symptoms of chronic illness or injury?
While Private Practice Therapy doesn't directly treat physical symptoms, it significantly supports your body's ability to cope and recover. When your nervous system is regulated and stress is reduced, your body can dedicate more resources to healing. This can lead to a reduction in pain perception, improved sleep, and a greater sense of calm, all of which are vital for physical recovery from chronic illness or injury.
What if I have a bad day and can't make it to my Private Practice Therapy session?
We understand that recovery from chronic illness or injury can mean unpredictable days. We have a flexible cancellation policy and encourage you to communicate with us as soon as possible if you need to reschedule. Your well-being is our priority, and we'll work with you to find a time that fits your fluctuating needs. There's no judgment here.
