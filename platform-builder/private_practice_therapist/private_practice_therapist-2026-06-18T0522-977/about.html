How does seasonal care apply to my postpartum recovery?

Seasonal care means adapting your support to the time of year. In cold months, we focus on warmth and circulation to help your body recover from the physical stress of birth. In warmer months, we emphasize hydration and gentle movement to prevent overheating and fatigue. It's about meeting your body where it is, season by season.

Can you help if I'm still experiencing physical symptoms months after birth?

Absolutely. Postpartum recovery is a process, and we don't expect you to bounce back by six weeks. Whether you're dealing with pelvic floor issues, back pain, or fatigue, we'll assess your symptoms and create a preventive plan tailored to your body's current needs and the season.

How do your sessions integrate with my baby's schedule?

Our sessions are designed around your reality. We offer flexible times that fit with your baby's sleep patterns. You might have a 30-minute session during naptime, or we can do quick check-ins over video. And we always focus on exercises you can do while holding your baby or during feeding.

What makes your preventive care different from general wellness advice?

We don't just tell you to 'drink more water'. We give you specific, seasonal actions: like adding ginger to your tea in winter for circulation, or doing a 5-minute stretch routine during morning cuddles. It's practical, immediate, and built for new moms who don't have hours to spare.
