Frequently Asked Questions

How is seasonal wellness different from regular therapy?
It’s proactive, not reactive. We focus on preventing exhaustion before you’re overwhelmed by your new role, move, or life change—using data from seasonal patterns in stress research.
Can you help me before I make a big life change?
Absolutely. We design your wellness plan before the transition hits. For example, if you’re relocating to , we’ll layer in support for the 3 months before your move to avoid the typical ‘adjustment slump.’
Do I need to be struggling to start?
No—this is for when you’re *anticipating* change. Think of it like scheduling a car service before a big trip, not waiting for the engine to fail.
How does your approach use science?
We reference seasonal affective disorder research and longitudinal studies on career transitions to tailor your support. You get data-driven steps, not vague advice.
