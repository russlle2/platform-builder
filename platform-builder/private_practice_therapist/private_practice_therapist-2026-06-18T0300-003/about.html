What can I expect from my first session with a Private Practice Therapist?

Your first session is a chance for us to get to know each other. You'll have the opportunity to share what brings you to therapy, and we'll discuss your goals and how we might work together. It's also an excellent time for you to ask any questions you have about the process or our approach.

How long does Private Practice Therapy typically last?

The duration of therapy is highly individual and depends on your unique needs and goals. Some individuals find short-term therapy helpful for specific concerns, while others benefit from longer-term support. We'll regularly review your progress and adjust our approach as needed to best support you.

Is what I share with my Private Practice Therapist confidential?

Absolutely. Confidentiality is a cornerstone of effective therapy. What you share in your sessions is kept private, with a few legal exceptions, such as if there's a risk of harm to yourself or others. We will thoroughly explain our confidentiality policy during your initial consultation.

How can Private Practice Therapy help me manage stress and anxiety?

As your Private Practice Therapist, Dr. Morgan Ellis can provide you with effective strategies and tools to understand and manage stress and anxiety. We'll explore the root causes, develop coping mechanisms, and help you build resilience to navigate life's challenges more calmly and confidently.
