Frequently Asked Questions

How does Private Practice Therapy specifically address sleep issues linked to chronic illness or injury?
Our approach acknowledges that sleep disruptions in recovery are often multifaceted, involving physiological, psychological, and environmental factors unique to your health condition. We utilize evidence-based cognitive and behavioral strategies adapted for the sensitivities and demands of a body in healing, focusing on restoring your natural sleep architecture without adding undue strain.
What if my pain or discomfort makes it impossible to sleep soundly?
We understand that pain is a significant barrier to restorative sleep. While we do not manage pain directly, our Private Practice Therapy helps you develop strategies to mitigate its impact on sleep, such as relaxation techniques, cognitive reframing, and optimizing your sleep environment. The goal is to create conditions where your body can find moments of deeper rest, even amidst discomfort.
Is this approach clinical, or more about general relaxation?
Our Private Practice Therapy is deeply clinical and evidence-based, rooted in established psychological science. While relaxation techniques are part of the toolkit, our work goes beyond general calming. We apply structured therapeutic interventions, such as adapted Cognitive Behavioral Therapy for Insomnia (CBT-I) principles, to address the specific thoughts, behaviors, and environmental factors that impede your sleep and recovery.
How long does it typically take to see improvements in sleep through Private Practice Therapy?
The timeline for improvement varies for each individual, as chronic illness and injury recovery are highly personal processes. However, clients often report initial shifts in sleep patterns and a reduction in sleep-related anxiety within a few weeks of consistent engagement with Private Practice Therapy. Sustainable change is built through ongoing practice and adjustment.
