Our Process

A disciplined path from first call to final resolution, tailored to your life and your recovery.

What to expect

  1. 1

    Free consultation

    We listen to your story, assess fault, and identify all available coverage. You’ll leave with a clear game plan and next steps.

  2. 2

    Immediate investigation

    We send spoliation letters, preserve scene evidence, capture witness statements, and retain the right experts early.

  3. 3

    Medical and damages mapping

    We coordinate medical care and meticulously document treatment, wage loss, and future needs to build full-value demands.

  4. 4

    Demand and negotiation

    Your case is presented as a compelling narrative, supported by expert reports and visuals that insurers can’t ignore.

  5. 5

    Litigation and trial

    If the carrier won’t be reasonable, we file suit. Our trial preparation starts day one, so we’re always ready.
