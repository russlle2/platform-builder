Practice Areas

From impact to outcome — {{BUSINESS_NAME}} guides you through every stage with focus and tenacity.

How we can help

Car Accidents

Rear-end, head-on, T-bone, hit-and-run, rideshare, and uninsured motorist claims. We secure medical payments, wage loss, pain and suffering, and vehicle diminution.

  • Evidence preservation and scene investigation
  • UM/UIM coverage analysis
  • Medical record curation and narrative reports

Truck & 18-Wheeler

We expose violations of FMCSA rules, negligent hiring, improper cargo securement, and driver fatigue to drive high-value resolutions.

  • Rapid response team for spoliation letters
  • ECM/telematics and dash-cam downloads
  • Industry experts in safety and logistics

Motorcycle Accidents

Bias against riders is real. We counter with physics-based reconstructions and medical experts to establish mechanism of injury.

  • Helmet law and comparative negligence strategy
  • Visibility and reaction-time analyses

Work Injuries

Beyond workers’ comp — we pursue third-party negligence for full damages when contractors and property owners cut corners.

  • OSHA and site safety violations
  • Third-party and product liability angles

Premises Liability

Slip, trip, and negligent security claims stemming from unsafe conditions in stores, apartments, and public spaces.

  • Preservation of surveillance video
  • Notice and inspection policy audits

Catastrophic Injury & Wrongful Death

Life-changing injuries deserve life-changing advocacy. We build comprehensive damages models for the future you face.

  • Life care plans and vocational assessments
  • Economic loss and household services valuations

Not sure where your case fits? We’ll map the strategy with you.
