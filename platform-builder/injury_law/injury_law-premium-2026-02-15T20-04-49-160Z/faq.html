Frequently Asked Questions

No-nonsense answers to common concerns after an injury.

Your questions, answered

How much does it cost to hire {{BUSINESS_NAME}}?

We work on a contingency fee. You pay nothing upfront, and no attorney’s fee unless we recover money for you. We’ll explain the percentage and case costs during your free consultation.

Do I have a case?

If someone else’s negligence caused your injury, you may have a claim. We look at liability, damages, and coverage. Start with a free review at {{PHONE}} or {{EMAIL}}.

How long will my case take?

Many cases resolve within 3–9 months, depending on medical treatment and insurer cooperation. Litigation can extend timelines. We’ll give you a tailored estimate after learning the facts.

Who pays my medical bills?

Options include MedPay, health insurance, and the at-fault party’s insurer. We coordinate benefits and negotiate medical liens to maximize your net recovery.

Will I have to go to court?

Most cases settle before trial, but we prepare every case as if it will be tried. That preparation leads to larger settlements — and confidence in the courtroom if needed.

What is my case worth?

Value depends on liability, injuries, treatment, wage loss, and insurance limits. After your consultation, we’ll explain a likely range and the strategy to reach the top of it.
