How do contingency fees work?

You pay no upfront fees. We cover case costs. If we recover money, our fee is a percentage of the recovery plus reimbursed costs. If we don’t recover, you owe nothing for our work.

Do I have a case?

If someone’s negligence caused your injuries and damages, you may have a case. The fastest way to know is to request a free review. We’ll assess liability, damages, and insurance coverage.

How long will my case take?

Timelines vary. Many claims resolve within a few months of completing medical treatment. Litigated cases can take longer. We push for efficient, maximum‑value resolutions.

Should I talk to the insurance company?

Not before speaking with a lawyer. Insurers may record statements and use them against you. We’ll handle communications and protect your rights.

What is my case worth?

Value depends on liability, injuries, treatment, wage loss, future care, and insurance limits. We build medical and economic evidence to support full compensation.
