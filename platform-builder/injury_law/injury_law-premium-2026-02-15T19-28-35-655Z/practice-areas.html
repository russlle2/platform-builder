Motor Vehicle Accidents

Crashes change lives in seconds. We handle car, motorcycle, trucking, pedestrian, cyclist, and rideshare collisions. Our team reconstructs crashes, pulls black‑box data, and pursues every liable party to maximize recovery for medical bills, wage loss, pain and suffering, and future care.

  • Commercial truck and 18‑wheeler collisions
  • Hit‑and‑run and uninsured motorists
  • Drunk, distracted, or reckless driving

Premises Liability

Property owners must keep spaces reasonably safe. When they don’t, falls and other incidents cause disabling harm. We secure surveillance footage, maintenance logs, and code records to prove negligence and hold businesses accountable.

  • Slip/trip and fall injuries
  • Negligent security and assaults
  • Unsafe stairs, railings, walkways

Workplace Injuries

Beyond workers’ compensation, many job‑site injuries involve negligent third parties. We coordinate comp benefits with civil claims to pursue full damages not covered by comp, including pain and suffering.

  • Construction and industrial accidents
  • Subcontractor negligence
  • Equipment failures and falls from height

Medical Negligence

When healthcare providers violate standards of care, the harm can be permanent. We partner with board‑certified experts to evaluate records and prove breaches that caused injury.

  • Diagnostic errors and delays
  • Surgical mistakes and anesthesia injuries
  • Birth injuries and medication errors

Products Liability

Defective designs, manufacturing flaws, and inadequate warnings can make everyday products dangerous. We litigate against manufacturers and distributors to improve safety and compensate victims.

  • Consumer and workplace equipment
  • Medical devices and implants
  • Household and children’s products

Wrongful Death

Families deserve answers and accountability. We pursue funeral expenses, lost support, and loss of companionship claims with compassion and resolve.

  • Catastrophic crashes
  • Worksite and industrial incidents
  • Dangerous premises and products
