Our Process

A disciplined approach tailored to your recovery and results.

  1. 1

    Listen & Advise

    Your story matters. We gather facts, answer questions, and advise on medical documentation and claim preservation.

    Tip: Keep a recovery journal and save all bills and receipts.
  2. 2

    Investigate & Preserve

    We send preservation letters, obtain reports, canvas for video, download vehicle EDR data, and hire experts when needed.

  3. 3

    Build Damages

    We quantify full losses: medical expenses, wage loss, loss of earning capacity, pain and suffering, and future care costs.

  4. 4

    Negotiate Hard

    We present a trial‑ready demand and press insurers to pay fair value—fast.

  5. 5

    Litigate & Try

    If they refuse, we file suit, take depositions, argue motions, and try the case. We’re built for court.

  6. 6

    Resolve & Protect

    We finalize settlement or verdict, resolve liens, and ensure funds are disbursed quickly and securely.

You owe nothing unless we recover compensation for you.
