FAQ

Safety & use

Do oils have side effects? Some people can be sensitive. We suggest small dilutions and a patch test before sustained use.

Pregnancy & nursing Some botanicals are discouraged. Please mention pregnancy or nursing during booking; we will adapt recommendations.

Pets Certain oils may be irritating to animals. We offer pet-safe options and usage guidance.

Allergies We ask about skin and inhalant allergies and omit any suspect botanicals.
