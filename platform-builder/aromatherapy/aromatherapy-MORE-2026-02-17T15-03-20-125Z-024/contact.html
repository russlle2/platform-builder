Quick safety

  • Guidance provided here aims to be safety-forward and may support well-being; not a medical diagnosis.

FAQ — safety & practice

Will aromatherapy help my sleep?

Individual responses vary. Scents and routines we suggest may support a calmer bedtime routine; they are adjunct, not medical treatments.

Patch test & dilution

All sessions include dilution guidance and patch-test steps. We recommend a 1%–3% dilution for home blends unless otherwise advised.

Pets & pregnancy

We use a safety-first stance. Please disclose pregnancy, breastfeeding, and pets before a session so we can tailor safer suggestions.

Allergies & sensitivities

If you have known sensitivities, list them when you contact us. We avoid citrus and strong oxidizers for sensitive skin when needed.
