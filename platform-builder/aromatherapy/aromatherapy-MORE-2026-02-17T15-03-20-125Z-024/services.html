FAQ — Safety & use

How should I dilute oils?
Dilution depends on purpose. A common starting point is 1–2% for topical use (6–12 drops per 30 mL carrier). Always follow practitioner guidance and patch test.
What about patch tests?
Apply a diluted drop to a small area of skin and wait 24 hours; if irritation occurs, discontinue. Patch testing is recommended for new blends.
I have pets or pregnancy concerns.
Some oils can affect animals or be unsuitable in pregnancy. We prioritize safety: always consult your healthcare provider and inform your practitioner during intake.
Do scents cure anything?
No medical claims are made. Aromatherapy here is framed as a possible support for attention, routine, and comfort, not as medical treatment.
