Questions & safe practices

Are these medical treatments?

No. Aromatherapy may support subjective experiences such as relaxation or focus; it is not a replacement for medical or mental health care.

How do you handle dilution and patch tests?

Every blend includes recommended dilutions and a patch test protocol. Start low, wait 24 hours, and discontinue if irritation occurs.

Pets, pregnancy, and children?

Many essential oils can affect pets and pregnant people. We provide alternative blends and specific safety guidance. Consult your health provider; these suggestions may support comfort but are not medical advice.

Do you guarantee results?

Outcomes vary. Scent practices may support well-being for some people; they're best understood as supportive, not curative.
