Frequently Asked Questions

How does Aromatherapy specifically address the stress of high-achievers?
Our Aromatherapy protocols are not generic. We focus on essential oils scientifically recognized for their impact on the autonomic nervous system, directly addressing the cortisol spikes and sympathetic nervous system overdrive common in demanding professions. This helps mitigate burnout and supports sustained cognitive performance.
Will these Aromatherapy blends interfere with my focus or make me drowsy during work hours?
Absolutely not. We craft blends with specific intentions. For daytime use, we select oils known to enhance alertness, focus, and mental clarity, while reducing anxiety without inducing sedation. Your customized blend will be designed to complement, not hinder, your productivity.
What kind of commitment is required to see results with Aromatherapy?
Our approach emphasizes integration and efficiency. While consistency yields the best results, even brief, targeted scent rituals can significantly impact your nervous system. We provide clear, actionable guidance on how to weave Aromatherapy into your existing routine without adding complexity to your already packed schedule.
How is your Aromatherapy different from simply buying essential oils online?
Our practice goes beyond retail. We provide personalized assessment, expert formulation, and guidance on safe and effective application, tailored to your unique physiological and professional demands. We source only the highest-grade, ethically produced essential oils, ensuring purity and potency for optimal therapeutic effect, which is crucial for nervous system regulation.
