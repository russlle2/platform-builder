Frequently Asked Questions

How does Aromatherapy specifically help seniors with seasonal changes?
Aromatherapy offers gentle support for common seasonal shifts that seniors may experience. For example, certain aromas can help uplift spirits during shorter, darker days, or promote a sense of coolness and calm during summer heat. We focus on blends that encourage restful sleep regardless of the season, support respiratory comfort, and help maintain a balanced mood throughout the year, complementing your body's natural adjustments to environmental changes.
Is Aromatherapy safe to use alongside my current medications or health conditions?
Your safety and well-being are our top priority. We always conduct a thorough consultation to understand your complete health profile, including any medications you are taking and existing health conditions. This allows us to select and recommend aromas that are appropriate and safe for you, avoiding any potential interactions or contraindications. We encourage you to discuss any new complementary practices, like Aromatherapy, with your primary care physician.
What kind of preventative benefits can I expect from Aromatherapy in my senior years?
Our preventative Aromatherapy for seniors focuses on supporting your overall well-being. This can include encouraging consistent, restful sleep, promoting a sense of calm to help manage daily stresses, supporting mental clarity, and maintaining feelings of physical comfort. While Aromatherapy does not cure diseases, it can be a gentle tool to support your body's natural resilience and contribute to your quality of life as a proactive measure.
How do you ensure the Aromatherapy blends are gentle and not overwhelming for older adults?
We prioritize gentle application and carefully diluted blends specifically for seniors. We use only high-quality, pure essential oils and always start with very low concentrations, gradually adjusting based on your comfort and response. Application methods are typically gentle, such as diffusion or topical application with a carrier oil, avoiding strong or stimulating scents unless specifically indicated and tolerated by you. Your feedback is crucial in ensuring the experience is always pleasant and beneficial.
