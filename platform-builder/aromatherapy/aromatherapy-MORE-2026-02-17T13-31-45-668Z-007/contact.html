Safety & FAQ

Aromatherapy is safety-forward. Please review:

  • Always dilute essential oils before skin application; typical topicals range from 1–3% for daily adult use.
  • Do a patch test before first topical use; stop if irritation occurs.
  • Keep oils away from eyes and mucous membranes.
  • If pregnant, nursing, or under medical care, consult a provider before use.
  • Many essential oils are not safe for cats; research pet safety before diffusing around sensitive animals.
