Safety essentials

  • Always dilute essential oils for topical use — typical adult dilutions range 1–5% depending on purpose.
  • Do a patch test before broader topical use and start with lower dilutions when sensitive skin is a concern.
  • Extra caution around pets: some oils are not suitable for animals; avoid heavy diffusion in small, shared spaces without ventilation.
  • If pregnant, breastfeeding or taking medications, seek individual professional guidance before starting new aromatic practices.
