Safety & common questions

Are these medical treatments?

No. Our practices and products are intended to be supportive; they may support wellbeing but are not medical interventions.

Dilution & patch tests

We recommend proper dilution and a patch test before topical use. We'll provide dilution guidance in every session.

Pets & pregnancy

Certain essential oils can affect pets and may not be appropriate during pregnancy. We offer alternatives and will adapt recommendations.
