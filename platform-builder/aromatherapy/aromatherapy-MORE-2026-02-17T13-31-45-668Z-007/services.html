Frequently asked safety notes

  • We recommend a patch test and clear dilution instructions; always dilute essential oils before topical use.
  • If you have pets, please check species-specific guidance before using diffusers in shared spaces.
  • If pregnant, breastfeeding, or on medication, consult a qualified practitioner before using new aromatics.
  • Sessions are educational and supportive — not medical advice.
