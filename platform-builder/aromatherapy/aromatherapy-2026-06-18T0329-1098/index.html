Frequently Asked Questions

How can Aromatherapy specifically help with age-related sleep changes?
As you age, your sleep architecture can shift, often leading to lighter sleep and more awakenings. Aromatherapy, using specific essential oils, can gently influence your body's natural sleep cycles, encouraging deeper REM and non-REM sleep phases. We focus on essences known to promote GABA activity and parasympathetic nervous system engagement, guiding your body back to its inherent rhythm for restorative rest.
Is Aromatherapy safe to use alongside my current medications for sleep or other health concerns?
Your safety is paramount. We conduct a thorough intake process, reviewing your health history and any medications you are currently taking. Our approach is evidence-based and clinical, ensuring that any recommended essential oils are compatible and will not interfere with your prescribed treatments. We always advise consulting with your primary care physician regarding any new therapeutic modalities.
How does Aromatherapy support mental clarity and cognitive function in retirement?
Beyond sleep, certain essential oils possess properties that can support neurocognitive function. By incorporating specific aromatic cues into your daily routine, we can help to mitigate brain fog, enhance concentration, and foster a sense of mental alertness without stimulants. This mindful engagement with scent can be a profound tool for maintaining your intellectual vigor.
What kind of commitment is required to see benefits from Aromatherapy for sleep improvement?
Consistency is key for gentle, sustained change. We recommend integrating your personalized aromatic rituals into your daily and nightly routine. While many clients report improvements within a few weeks, the most profound and lasting benefits emerge with dedicated, mindful practice. We provide clear, simple instructions to make this integration seamless and enjoyable for you.
