FAQ — safe use highlights

Dilution: For general adult topical use, 1–3% is common for roll-ons; custom concentrations are provided during consultations. Lower concentrations for older adults and children. These are general guidelines — consult during intake for personalized advice.

Patch test: Apply a small diluted amount to forearm, wait 24–48 hours. If irritation or redness occurs, discontinue. Do not apply to damaged skin.

Pets: Some essential oils are harmful to animals (notably cats). Avoid close-proximity undiluted oils and consult a veterinarian for household-specific guidance.

Pregnancy & nursing: Many oils have restrictions. Do not use without clinician approval; book a safety review before use in pregnancy or while nursing.
