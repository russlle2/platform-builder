Signature blends — composition & notes

Below are curated blends with top/middle/base notes, an aroma profile, and a concise safety-forward "supports" statement. These are traditional aromatic uses and not medical claims.

Coastal Calm

Top: bergamot, lime; Middle: lavender, neroli; Base: cedarwood, vetiver
A soft, saline-tinged citrus floral with anchoring woody depth.
Traditionally used to support a sense of relaxation and grounding when inhaled. Use dilute topically; avoid during pregnancy without clinician approval.

Morning Clarity

Top: grapefruit, peppermint; Middle: rosemary; Base: light musk accord
Bright, brisk citrus-herbal with a clean, focused lift.
May support alertness and a clear-headed atmosphere when diffused. Not for use with children under 6 or pets in close proximity without guidance.

Evening Unwind

Top: mandarin; Middle: chamomile, clary sage; Base: sandalwood
Warm, gently sweet floral with calming resinous base notes.
Traditionally used for creating a calming bedtime routine environment. Avoid undiluted skin contact; patch test prior to topical use.

Clear Path

Top: lemon, eucalyptus; Middle: tea tree; Base: cedar
Crisp, herbaceous, and bright — a clarifying herbal bouquet.
Often used in diffusers to refresh indoor air and support a sense of clarity. Keep away from cats; adhere to safe diffusion durations.

Soft Focus

Top: bergamot; Middle: ylang-ylang, jasmine; Base: vanilla, benzoin
Creamy floral with a comforting sweet balsamic base.
May support an inviting atmosphere and emotional ease during mindful moments. Not intended as medical treatment.

Forest Tether

Top: fir needle; Middle: pine; Base: cypress, patchouli
Green, resinous, with deep earthy anchors.
Traditionally used to foster grounding sensations when inhaled. Use caution with blood pressure conditions and consult a clinician as needed.

Gentle Ease

Top: sweet orange; Middle: lavender, marjoram; Base: clove bud (low)
Warm citrus with soft herbal heart and a low-spice base.
Selected for soothing ritual use. Contains low levels of spicy notes; dilute carefully and avoid in infants.

Quiet Mind

Top: frankincense; Middle: lavender, hinoki; Base: amber accord
Incensed, contemplative, clean woody resins.
Traditionally chosen for contemplative practice. Avoid during pregnancy without clinical approval and do not ingest.

Pocket Lift

Top: lemon; Middle: lavender; Base: fractionated coconut oil base recommended for roll-on
Bright and portable — formulated for gentle topical application when diluted properly.
Designed for topical roll-on use at safe dilutions (typically 1–3% for adults). Perform patch test; discontinue if irritation occurs.

Safety and use notes

These blends are created with a safety-focused perspective. Always follow dilution guidance, perform patch tests, and consult a clinician before use during pregnancy, nursing, or in the presence of children and companion animals. Descriptions are aromatic profiles and supportive language is traditional/usual use, not medical advice.
