FAQ — safety essentials

Dilution: Use conservative dilutions. Typical topical dilutions range 1–3% unless otherwise advised after intake.
Patch test: A small patch-test is recommended for new topical blends. Instructions will be provided before application.
Pets: Inform us about pets at intake. Some essential oils can be irritating to animals; recommendations will avoid risky compounds and suggest safer practices.
Pregnancy & breastfeeding: Several oils are contraindicated. Always disclose pregnancy or breastfeeding so that guidance is strictly conservative.
