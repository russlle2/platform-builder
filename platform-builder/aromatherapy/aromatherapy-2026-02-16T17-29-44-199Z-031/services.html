How we work — practical clinical framework

A clear, staged approach that centers safety, informed choice, and measurable routine. We use a hybrid model mixing remote assessment with hands-on blending when desired.

  1. Remote intake — health history, environmental review, scent preferences.
  2. Safety check — interactions, pregnancy considerations, pet-safe guidance.
  3. Protocol design — dilution, application method, timing and storage.
  4. Follow-up — adjust concentrations, refine scent profile, education.
