Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is Aromatherapy?
Aromatherapy is a holistic healing modality that uses natural plant extracts, known as essential oils, to promote health and well-being. It involves inhaling the aromatic compounds or applying diluted oils topically to affect your mood, alleviate discomfort, and support physiological functions.
How can Aromatherapy benefit my daily life?
Aromatherapy offers numerous benefits, from reducing stress and improving sleep to boosting energy and enhancing focus. Your personalized blends can assist with emotional balance, promote relaxation, support respiratory health, and even help soothe minor aches, integrating seamlessly into your wellness routine.
Are essential oils safe for everyone?
While generally safe, essential oils are potent and should be used with care. We always conduct a thorough intake to understand your health history, sensitivities, and any medications you are taking. We provide clear guidance on proper dilution and application methods to ensure your safe and beneficial experience.
What should I expect during an Aromatherapy consultation?
During your consultation, you will discuss your current health, lifestyle, and wellness aspirations with . Based on this, a unique essential oil blend will be formulated specifically for you, along with clear instructions on how to use it effectively to achieve your desired results.
