Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is Aromatherapy and how can it benefit me?
Aromatherapy is a holistic healing modality that uses natural plant extracts, known as essential oils, to promote health and well-being. You can experience benefits such as stress reduction, improved sleep, enhanced mood, pain relief, and immune support, all tailored to your individual needs.
Are the essential oils used in your Aromatherapy practice safe?
Yes, your safety is our top priority. We use only pure, therapeutic-grade essential oils and provide clear, comprehensive guidance on their safe and effective application. We consider your health history and any sensitivities during your consultation to ensure appropriate usage.
How long does a typical Aromatherapy consultation last?
A typical initial Aromatherapy consultation lasts approximately 60-90 minutes. This allows ample time to discuss your wellness goals, health history, and for us to formulate a personalized blend and usage plan tailored specifically for your needs.
Can Aromatherapy help with specific conditions like anxiety or sleep issues?
Absolutely. Many individuals find significant relief from anxiety, stress, and sleep disturbances through personalized Aromatherapy. We will craft blends specifically designed to address these concerns, helping you cultivate a greater sense of calm and restorative rest.
