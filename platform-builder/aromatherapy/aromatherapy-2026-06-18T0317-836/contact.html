Aromatherapy

Aromatherapy for Your Postural Foundation

When you're recovering from chronic illness or injury, your body's natural alignment can shift. Our Aromatherapy sessions are designed to help you reconnect with your core, encouraging a subtle yet profound awareness of your posture. We focus on blends that promote a sense of grounding and stability, assisting your body in remembering its innate balance, without imposing aggressive or overwhelming sensations. We listen to your body's unique needs.
