How can Aromatherapy specifically help with postural health after chronic illness or injury?

Aromatherapy, through carefully chosen essential oils, can help your body relax muscular tension that might be contributing to poor posture. Certain scents can also enhance your body's proprioception, helping you become more aware of how you hold your body and where adjustments are needed, all in a gentle, non-invasive way.

Is Aromatherapy safe to use if I have sensitivities due to my recovery from illness or injury?

Absolutely. We prioritize your comfort and safety above all else. During your initial consultation, we'll discuss your specific sensitivities, current medications, and any concerns you may have. We then create highly personalized, diluted blends that are gentle and tailored precisely to your unique needs and recovery stage.

What kind of body awareness can I expect to develop through Aromatherapy?

Our Aromatherapy approach aims to help you develop a deeper, more intuitive connection with your body. You might find yourself more attuned to subtle shifts in your posture, how different movements feel, and areas where you hold tension. This increased awareness supports you in making conscious choices for better alignment and comfort in your daily activities.

How does Sage Garden Aromatherapy create an inclusive environment for people in recovery?

We foster an atmosphere of understanding and respect. Our practitioners are trained to listen attentively to your unique experiences and challenges. We ensure our space is accessible and that our approach is always gentle, supportive, and free from judgment, creating a safe haven for you to focus on your well-being and postural re-engagement.
