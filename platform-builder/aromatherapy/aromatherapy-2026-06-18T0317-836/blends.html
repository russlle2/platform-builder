Aromatherapy

Cultivating Body Awareness with Custom Aromatherapy Blends

Understanding how your body feels and moves is a cornerstone of recovery. Our custom Aromatherapy blends are formulated to enhance your proprioception – your body's sense of its position in space. By engaging your olfactory senses, we help you draw attention to areas that need support, encouraging a gentle re-patterning of movement and posture. This nuanced approach supports you in building confidence in your physical self, at your own pace.
