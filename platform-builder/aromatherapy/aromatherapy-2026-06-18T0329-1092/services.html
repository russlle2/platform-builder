Aromatherapy for Postpartum Sleep Architecture

Postpartum sleep is often fragmented, leaving new mothers in a state of perpetual exhaustion. Our Aromatherapy protocols are meticulously designed to influence your sleep architecture, promoting longer periods of deep, undisturbed rest. We select specific essential oils known for their neuro-modulating properties, aiding in the transition from anxious wakefulness to profound slumber, always with your unique postpartum physiology in mind.
