Frequently Asked Questions

How can Aromatherapy specifically help with postpartum sleep deprivation?
Aromatherapy utilizes specific essential oils, like lavender or frankincense, known for their ability to influence the nervous system. For postpartum sleep, these oils can help reduce anxiety, calm an overactive mind, and promote a deeper, more sustained sleep cycle, addressing the common fragmentation you experience.
Is Aromatherapy safe for new mothers, especially when breastfeeding?
Yes, when practiced with informed guidance. We select essential oils that are recognized as safe for use during postpartum and lactation, ensuring appropriate dilution and application methods. Your safety and your baby's well-being are our foremost considerations, and we will always discuss specific concerns you may have.
What kind of Aromatherapy practices will you recommend for my nightly routine?
We focus on creating simple, yet profound, rituals that fit into your new routine. This might include gentle diffusion in your bedroom before sleep, a calming aromatic bath, or a diluted essential oil blend for a soothing foot or neck massage. The aim is to signal to your body that it is time for rest, without adding complexity.
How quickly can I expect to see improvements in my sleep with Aromatherapy?
While individual responses vary, many new mothers report feeling a sense of immediate calm and an improved ability to fall asleep within the first few sessions. Consistent practice of your personalized Aromatherapy routine typically leads to more sustained and restorative sleep patterns over a few weeks, as your body and mind adapt.
