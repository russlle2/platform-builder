How is your Aromatherapy different from retail essential oil stores?

We provide clinical-grade formulations based on your physiological responses, not pre-mixed products. Our blends undergo safety testing for your specific health profile, unlike generic retail options.

Can Aromatherapy help with medical conditions?

We work alongside your healthcare providers for complementary support. While not a substitute for treatment, our scent protocols can support symptom management (e.g., nausea reduction during therapy) with protocols documented in peer-reviewed journals.

Do I need prior knowledge of essential oils?

No—our process eliminates confusion. We handle oil selection, dilution ratios, and application methods tailored to your skill level and needs, with clear safety guidelines.

How soon will I notice results?

Most clients report subtle shifts within 24-72 hours for stress/anxiety relief. For chronic conditions, consistent use over 2-4 weeks with adjusted protocols yields measurable improvements, supported by your progress tracking.
