Micro-habits for daily scenting

Tiny rituals that make scent approachable and safe.

  1. 2–3 drops in a diffuser for 20–30 minutes; ventilate the room.
  2. Use 1–3% dilution in a carrier for skin roll-ons; always patch-test.
  3. Store oils away from heat and direct light in amber bottles.
  4. Pause scent use around animals or vulnerable people; consult safety guides.

Tip: keep a scent journal to track responses and affinities.
