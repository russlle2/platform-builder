Notes on safety & guidance

Patch test & dilution: Always dilute essential oils according to guidance. For topical use, a patch test is recommended: apply diluted oil to a small area and wait 24 hours for sensitivity.

Pregnancy & medical: If pregnant, nursing, or under medical care, consult a licensed provider before using essential oils. Sessions avoid contraindicated oils and prioritize safety.

Pets: Many oils affect animals differently. Notify me about household pets; we'll choose pet-safe approaches for shared spaces.

Allergies & sensitivities: Tell me about fragrance allergies or chemical sensitivities. I will adapt blends and delivery methods accordingly.

These notes are guidance and do not replace professional medical advice.
