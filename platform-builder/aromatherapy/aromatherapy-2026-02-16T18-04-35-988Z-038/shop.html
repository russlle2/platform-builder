FAQ

Dilution: We recommend 1–3% for most adults on skin; children and elders typically need lower dilutions.
Patch test: Apply a small amount of diluted product behind the ear or on the inner arm and wait 24 hours.
Pets: Avoid using certain oils (e.g., tea tree, eucalyptus) around cats and small mammals. Consult us if you have pets.
Pregnancy & breastfeeding: Some oils are contraindicated. Please tell us if you are pregnant, trying to conceive, or breastfeeding.
