Safety & FAQ

Dilution: Adhere to recommended dilutions (commonly 1–3% for topical blends) depending on age, sensitivity and intended use.
Patch test: Always patch test a new blend on a small area of skin and wait 24 hours for reactions.
Pets: Some essential oils are not pet-safe. Inform your practitioner if you share space with animals for tailored recommendations.
Pregnancy: Certain oils are contraindicated in pregnancy. Please disclose pregnancy or breastfeeding before sessions.
