FAQs — gentle guidance

What about dilution? We favour conservative dilutions and clear labelling. Topical products are prepared to safe percentages for adult skin unless otherwise advised.

Patch test? Always recommended — apply a small amount to an inner forearm and observe for 24 hours.

Pets? Many essential oils can be problematic for animals. Tell us about pets so we can avoid risky aromatics and recommend safer alternatives.

Pregnancy? Some oils are contraindicated. Disclose pregnancy before sessions so we can adapt recommendations.

Tiny rituals to start

  • Two mindful inhales — breathe in for four, out for six, notice the aroma.
  • Bedtime roller: low dilution, calming notes (try a lavender-forward mix) applied to wrists or chest — patch test first.
