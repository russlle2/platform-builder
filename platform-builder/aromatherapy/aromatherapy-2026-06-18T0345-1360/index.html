Frequently Asked Questions

How can Aromatherapy specifically help with postpartum physical pain?
Aromatherapy uses specific essential oils known for their analgesic, anti-inflammatory, and muscle-relaxing properties. We create personalized blends that can be applied topically to soothe sore muscles, ease abdominal discomfort, and reduce tension in your neck and shoulders, directly addressing the physical strains unique to your postpartum recovery.
I'm sensitive to smells; will Aromatherapy be too overwhelming for me during this delicate time?
Your comfort is paramount. We always start with very gentle, low dilutions and carefully selected oils known for their mild and nurturing profiles. We will work closely with you to find scents that feel soothing and supportive, never overpowering, ensuring a comfortable and beneficial experience tailored to your sensitivity and preferences.
Is Aromatherapy safe to use while I'm breastfeeding or caring for my newborn?
Yes, when practiced with expertise and caution. We are trained in safe essential oil use for postpartum mothers and infants. We avoid certain oils, use appropriate dilutions, and guide you on application methods that prioritize the safety of both you and your baby, ensuring peace of mind during your recovery.
I don't have any experience with wellness practices. How accessible is Aromatherapy for someone like me?
Aromatherapy is wonderfully accessible and requires no prior wellness experience. We guide you gently through every step, explaining how each scent works with your body and how to easily incorporate simple aromatic rituals into your daily routine. Our approach is designed to be intuitive and comforting, supporting you exactly where you are.
