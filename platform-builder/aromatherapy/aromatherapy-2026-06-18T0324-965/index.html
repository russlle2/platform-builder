Frequently Asked Questions

How can Aromatherapy specifically help me through a major life transition?
Aromatherapy supports you by providing targeted aromatic tools to manage the emotional and physical demands of change. We focus on blends that can help reduce stress, improve sleep quality, enhance focus during new challenges, and stabilize mood, allowing you to adapt more smoothly and maintain your well-being as your life evolves.
Is 's approach to Aromatherapy different for seasonal wellness?
Absolutely. Our seasonal wellness Aromatherapy adapts to the unique challenges each time of year presents, especially when you're also navigating a personal transition. We'll select essential oils and practices that support your body's natural rhythms, offering preventative care for seasonal shifts in energy, immunity, and mood, helping you stay balanced year-round.
What kind of preventative care can I expect from your Aromatherapy services?
Our preventative care focuses on empowering you with daily aromatic rituals to fortify your health. This includes creating custom blends for immune support, stress reduction, and better sleep, all tailored to your specific needs during a life transition. We aim to help you proactively manage potential imbalances before they impact your progress.
Will I learn how to use essential oils safely and effectively at home?
Yes, a core part of our service is educating you on the safe and effective use of essential oils. You'll receive clear guidance on dilution, application methods, and storage, ensuring you feel confident integrating Aromatherapy into your daily routine for ongoing support during your transition and beyond.
