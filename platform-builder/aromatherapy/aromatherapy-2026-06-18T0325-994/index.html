Frequently Asked Questions

How can Aromatherapy specifically help me manage the stress of parenting young children?
Aromatherapy offers gentle tools to signal safety to your nervous system. Specific essential oils can help reduce feelings of overwhelm, promote a sense of calm, and even support more restful sleep, allowing you to feel more present and resilient when facing the daily demands of parenting.
Are Aromatherapy blends safe to use around my young children?
Yes, when properly diluted and diffused, many Aromatherapy blends are safe for use around young children. We will guide you on appropriate essential oil selections, safe diffusion practices, and proper dilution ratios to ensure the well-being of your entire family, prioritizing gentle and effective support.
Will using Aromatherapy require a lot of extra time in my already busy schedule?
Absolutely not. Our approach to Aromatherapy is about integrating small, impactful moments of calm into your existing routine. This might mean a quick inhale from a personal inhaler, a few drops in a diffuser while you prepare dinner, or a relaxing evening bath. The focus is on ease and accessibility for busy parents.
How does Aromatherapy help with nervous system regulation when I feel constantly on edge?
Certain aromatic compounds in essential oils interact with your limbic system, the part of your brain responsible for emotions and memory. This interaction can help to calm the amygdala, reducing the 'fight or flight' response and promoting a parasympathetic (rest and digest) state, helping your nervous system find its rhythm again.
