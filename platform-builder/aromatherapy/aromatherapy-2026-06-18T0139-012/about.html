The Precision of Professional Aromatherapy

Unlike generic essential oil kits, our Aromatherapy practice begins with a comprehensive consultation to understand your physiology, lifestyle, and wellness goals. We blend only certified organic oils using evidence-based ratios, avoiding the pitfalls of over-scented or diluted products. Your blend becomes a personalized wellness tool, not just another scent in a bottle.

How soon will I notice results from my Aromatherapy blend?

Results vary by individual, but most clients report subtle shifts within 3-5 days of consistent use. We provide a 30-day adjustment period to refine your blend based on your feedback.

Are your Aromatherapy blends safe for children?

Yes, with proper dilution. We create separate pediatric formulations using child-safe oils like chamomile and lavender, with reduced concentrations appropriate for their physiology.

How long does a typical Aromatherapy consultation take?

Initial consultations last 45 minutes to deeply understand your needs, followed by a 15-minute refinement session. All blending is done on-site with you present to ensure perfect personalization.

Do you ship Aromatherapy blends internationally?

We ship within the contiguous US, Canada, and select EU countries. International shipping is available upon request with additional handling and customs fees.
