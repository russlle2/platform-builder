Frequently Asked Questions

How can Aromatherapy specifically address the emotional impact of intense physical training or competition?
Aromatherapy, through specific essential oils, can directly influence the limbic system, the brain's emotional center. For athletes, this means we can craft blends to soothe performance-related anxiety, mitigate feelings of frustration or disappointment after a challenging event, and foster emotional equilibrium needed for sustained focus and recovery from physical and mental exertion.
Is Aromatherapy safe to use alongside my existing recovery protocols or medical treatments?
Yes, when practiced by a knowledgeable professional, Aromatherapy is generally very safe and complementary. We always consider your full health picture, including any existing recovery routines, medications, or injuries, to ensure that the essential oils chosen are appropriate and supportive of your overall well-being and emotional stability.
What does a trauma-informed Aromatherapy session entail for someone managing physical stress?
A trauma-informed Aromatherapy session prioritizes your sense of safety and agency. We proceed gently, allowing you to guide the pace and choice of scents. The focus is on creating a supportive environment where you can explore how scents can help regulate your nervous system, release stored tension from your body's experiences, and gently process emotional echoes of physical demands without re-traumatization.
How quickly can I expect to feel the emotional benefits of Aromatherapy in my active lifestyle?
Many individuals report an immediate sense of calm and grounding during and after their first Aromatherapy session. However, the deeper emotional work – building resilience, processing complex feelings related to physical challenges, and integrating new emotional patterns – unfolds over time with consistent engagement. Your personalized blends are designed to support this ongoing process, becoming an integral part of your emotional recovery toolkit.
