What exactly is Aromatherapy?

Aromatherapy is a holistic healing treatment that uses natural plant extracts to promote health and well-being. Sometimes called essential oil therapy, Aromatherapy uses aromatic essential oils medicinally to improve the health of the body, mind, and spirit. It enhances both physical and emotional health.

How can Aromatherapy benefit my specific concerns?

Aromatherapy can offer support for a wide range of concerns, including stress reduction, improved sleep quality, mood enhancement, pain management, and boosted energy levels. During your consultation, we'll discuss your individual needs to determine how specific essential oils can best support your unique wellness goals.

Are essential oils safe to use?

When used correctly and with professional guidance, essential oils are generally safe. However, they are highly concentrated and require careful handling. We prioritize your safety and will provide clear instructions on proper dilution, application methods, and any necessary precautions tailored to your health profile and the specific oils in your blend.

What should I expect during an Aromatherapy consultation?

During your consultation, you can expect a comprehensive discussion about your health history, lifestyle, and wellness aspirations. We'll explore scent preferences and any sensitivities you may have. Based on this information, Dr. Morgan Ellis will formulate a personalized essential oil blend and provide guidance on how to effectively integrate it into your daily life for optimal results.
