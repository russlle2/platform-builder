Frequently Asked Questions

How do you ensure essential oil purity?
We require full GC/MS testing reports from all suppliers, verifying no dilution, synthetic additives, or pesticide residues. We only use therapeutic-grade oils verified by independent labs.
What's the typical session length?
Initial consultations last 75 minutes to develop your personalized formula. Follow-up sessions are 50 minutes. We never rush the blending process—you’ll receive your custom blend and usage instructions before leaving.
Are your blends safe for pets?
We offer specialized pet-safe blends (diluted to meet animal physiology) and will always discuss specific concerns about your companion animals during consultation. Never use human blends without professional guidance.
How soon will I notice benefits?
Many notice subtle shifts within 48 hours of consistent use. For deeper emotional patterns, we recommend 3-4 sessions over 4-6 weeks. We provide ongoing adjustment support to optimize results as your needs evolve.
