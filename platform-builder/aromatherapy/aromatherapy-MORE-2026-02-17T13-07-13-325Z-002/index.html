Myths & Practical Notes

Myth: Aromas fix medical problems

Truth: Essential oils can be supportive and comforting. They are not a substitute for medical care and may support wellbeing in small, intentional ways.

Myth: More is better

Truth: Less is often more. Dilution and patch testing reduce irritation and increase safety for long-term use.

Myth: All oils are safe for everyone

Truth: Some oils are not recommended in pregnancy, around pets, or for children. Check guidance and ask before using.

    Frequently asked — safety & use

    How do I dilute safely?

    General starter guide: For adults, a 1% dilution is about 6 drops per 30 ml carrier; 2% is about 12 drops per 30 ml. For facial application or sensitive skin, use 0.5–1%. For children or pregnancy, use lower dilutions and avoid certain oils. These guidelines are conservative and not medical advice.

    What about patch testing?

    Apply a small diluted amount to inner forearm, cover, and wait 24 hours. If redness, itching, or irritation occurs, do not use.

    Are essential oils safe around pets?

    Some oils may be harmful to animals. Diffuse with caution, ensure pets can leave the room, and consult a vet for species-specific guidance.

    Can I use oils in pregnancy?

    Some oils are not recommended during pregnancy or breastfeeding. Use lower dilutions and consult a qualified prenatal healthcare provider before use.
