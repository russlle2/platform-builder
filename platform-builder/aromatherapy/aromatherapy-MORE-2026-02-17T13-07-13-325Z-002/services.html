How I work

Sessions combine scent curation, breath-guided presence, and practical aftercare you can use at home. Sessions are skillful, non-prescriptive, and never make medical claims.

Programs

  • Focus Session — 45 min tailored scenting for attention and ritual prompts.
  • Ease & Sleep Session — 60 min practices and nighttime blend planning.
  • Seasonal Reset Series — 4 sessions across 6 weeks for habit support.

Pricing and availability are presented on the Investment page. Group and sliding scale options are sometimes available.

What to expect

Brief intake, scent selection, guided practice, and a practical take-home plan. Sessions may support mood and routine; they are not medical treatment.
