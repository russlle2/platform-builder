FAQ — Safety & practice notes

Dilution: Suggested dilution ranges are general aromatherapy practice notes and not medical instructions. Typical topical dilutions used in our non-medical blends are around 0.5%–3% for most adults for routine use; certain situations call for lower dilutions. The Blend builder gives conservative guidance based on client type.

Patch test: Always perform a patch test: apply 1 drop of diluted blend to inner forearm and observe 24 hours. Avoid if irritation occurs.

Pregnancy & breastfeeding: Some essentials are commonly avoided or used at lower dilutions during pregnancy. If client is pregnant or breastfeeding, consult their healthcare provider before topical use. We default to lower suggested dilutions for these cases.

Pets: Many essential oils can affect pets, especially cats. Avoid applying strong oils directly to pets, and keep diffusers or applied blends away from animal sleeping areas. Consult a veterinarian familiar with essential oils if you are unsure.

Allergies & medications: Aromas may interact with sensitivities. We suggest discussing ongoing medications or severe allergies prior to using a blend. Our suggestions are not a substitute for professional medical advice.
