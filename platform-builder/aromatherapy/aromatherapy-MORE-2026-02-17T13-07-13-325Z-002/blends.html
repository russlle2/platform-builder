Common ingredient notes

  • Lavender: gentle, broadly tolerated.
  • Chamomile: calming; may be sensitizing for some.
  • Citrus (sweet orange, bergamot): uplifting; avoid skin sun exposure after use if phototoxic oils were chosen.
  • Peppermint: strong; avoid in young children and during pregnancy.
Pet & pregnancy safety

Some essential oils can affect pets or be contraindicated in pregnancy. Choose low dilutions and discuss specifics at booking.

Patch test

Apply a small amount of diluted blend to inner forearm and wait 24 hours for irritation signs before regular use.
