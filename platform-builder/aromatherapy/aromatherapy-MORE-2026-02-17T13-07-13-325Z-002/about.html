Helpful Safety Notes

        

          
Patch test: Apply a small dab of a diluted sample to inner forearm; wait 24 hours. Discontinue if irritation occurs.

          
Pregnancy & breastfeeding: Some essential oils are not recommended. Consult a qualified provider before use.

          
Pets: Many aromatics affect animals differently. Diffuse in short bursts and provide a pet-free escape. Avoid direct application on animals.
