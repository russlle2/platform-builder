Frequently Asked Questions

What is Aromatherapy and how can it benefit me?
Aromatherapy is the therapeutic use of aromatic plant extracts and essential oils for psychological and physical well-being. It can help you manage stress, improve sleep, boost mood, alleviate discomfort, and support your overall health through natural means.
Are essential oils safe to use, and how do you ensure their quality?
When used correctly, essential oils are safe. We prioritize your safety by using only high-quality, ethically sourced essential oils from reputable suppliers. During your consultation, we'll discuss proper dilution, application methods, and any contraindications relevant to your health.
How do you determine which essential oils are right for my individual needs?
We begin with a comprehensive consultation to understand your health history, current concerns, and wellness goals. Based on this, we carefully select essential oils known for their specific therapeutic properties that align with your unique physiological and emotional landscape, creating a truly personalized blend.
What can I expect during an Aromatherapy consultation with ?
During your consultation, you can expect a warm, confidential conversation about your well-being. We'll explore your needs, educate you on relevant essential oils, and together, we'll formulate a bespoke Aromatherapy plan and blend designed specifically to support your journey towards balance and vitality.
