How to use blends

Blends may be used in a diffuser, inhaler stick, or diluted for topical application. Recommended dilutions are provided. Perform a patch test before topical use.

Patch test

Mix the intended dilution, apply a small amount to the inner forearm, wait 24 hours. If any irritation occurs, discontinue use.

Safety & FAQs

Can children use these blends?
Some blends may be appropriate at lower dilutions; disclose age and health during booking. We will advise child-safe options.
What about pets?
We avoid certain oils near pets or recommend restricted use; always ventilate spaces when diffusing around animals.
Pregnancy?
Certain botanicals are avoided. Please inform us; we will provide pregnancy-safe alternatives.
