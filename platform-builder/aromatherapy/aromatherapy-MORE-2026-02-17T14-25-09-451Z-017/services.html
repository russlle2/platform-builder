What happens in an intensive

Each session is crafted around aromatic notes selected for clarity of effect and safety. We center on respiration, scent focus, and aftercare. All practices are non-invasive and intended to complement your routine.

Aroma Wheel

Notes

  • Top notes — bright, ephemeral; perceived first. Examples: citrus, herbaceous lifts.
  • Middle notes — the heart of a blend; floral, spicy, or green impressions.
  • Base notes — grounding, long-lasting; resinous, woody, or amber facets.

Session format

Intake & safety check, guided scent experience (30–50 min), integration suggestions for follow-up. All blends come with dilution notes and patch-test guidance.

Quick safety

  • Always dilute essential oils as directed; topical use requires patch testing.
  • Communicate pregnancy, medications, or pet exposure concerns during booking.
  • Sessions are supportive and are not medical treatment.

Questions & cautions

Will scents cause reactions?
Some people are sensitive. A patch test and low dilution are advised. If you react, stop use and consult a healthcare provider.
Is it safe around pets?
Some essential oils can be irritating to animals. We will discuss pet exposure and select pet-safer blends when needed.
Pregnancy & medications?
Tell us at booking. Certain botanicals are avoided in pregnancy or with specific medications; the session will be adapted.
