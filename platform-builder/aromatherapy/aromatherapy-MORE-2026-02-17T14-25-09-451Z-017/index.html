    Questions & safety notes

    Is aromatherapy safe for everyone?

    We prioritise safety. We will review pregnancy, medication, and pet exposure. If you are pregnant, nursing, or on medication, consult a provider and share that in intake.

    How should I dilute oils?

    We recommend dilution guides specific to adult topical use, typically 0.5–3% for home blends unless directed otherwise. Always perform a patch test before regular use.

    What about pets?

    Many essential oils can be irritants to animals. Keep oils away from pets, avoid direct topical application to animals, and use diffusers with caution in shared spaces.
