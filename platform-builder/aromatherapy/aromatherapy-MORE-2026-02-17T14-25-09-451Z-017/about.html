FAQs — safety essentials

Patch tests: Always patch-test at home before full skin application. Wait 24 hours and watch for irritation.

Dilution: You will receive clear dilution charts for topical use; facial and mucous contact is avoided unless specified.

Pregnancy & medication: Mention pregnancy, breastfeeding, and medications during intake so blends can be adapted.

Pets: Some oils are not pet-safe; alternatives and ventilation guidance are provided for shared spaces.
