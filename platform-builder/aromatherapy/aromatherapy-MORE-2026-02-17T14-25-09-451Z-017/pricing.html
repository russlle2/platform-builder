Common questions & safety notes

Do these sessions treat medical conditions? — No. Services are not a substitute for medical care. Language such as "may support" is used to reflect possible outcomes, not clinical claims.

Patch testing & dilution — You will receive dilution guidance and a recommended patch-test before introducing new blends to skin. For facial or mucous contact we advise extra caution.

Pregnancy & nursing — Some oils are not recommended during pregnancy. Discuss pregnancy, breastfeeding, and medication during intake so we can adapt blends safely.

Pets — Certain essential oils can affect animals. We provide pet-safe options and alternatives if you share space with sensitive pets.

Refunds & rescheduling — See booking terms on the Reserve page. Deposits apply to hold appointments.
