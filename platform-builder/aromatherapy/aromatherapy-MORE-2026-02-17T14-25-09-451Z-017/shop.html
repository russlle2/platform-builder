Usage & safety

How should I dilute for topical use?
We include dilution notes on each product. Typical topical dilutions range 0.5–2% depending on use and sensitivity. Patch test before use.
Pets & home use?
Diffuse with ventilation and limit direct exposure around pets. Some oils may not be pet-safe; consult the product notes.
Pregnancy & medication interactions?
If pregnant or on medication, consult your healthcare provider. We can recommend pregnancy-safe alternatives.
