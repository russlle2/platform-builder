Questions and common hesitations

Is aromatherapy safe during pregnancy?
We avoid suggestive language. Many essential oils are contraindicated in pregnancy. If you are pregnant or nursing, please note this in your intake and we will adapt sessions to use pregnancy-safe options or postpone blending. Always check with your healthcare provider.
Can pets be affected?
Yes. Some oils may irritate or impact animals. We provide pet-safe recommendations and dilution guidance and will advise when to avoid certain botanicals around sensitive species.
Do I need a patch test?
Yes—before using a new topical blend, perform a patch test. We always provide dilution instructions and recommend testing 24 hours prior to regular use.
Are these sessions medical care?
No. Our sessions are sensory and supportive in nature; language will emphasize may-support outcomes, and we do not diagnose or treat medical conditions.
