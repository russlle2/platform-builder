Safety & care notes

Safe use is a priority. Essential oils may support mood and wellbeing for some people but are not a substitute for medical care. Please consult your provider with health questions.

Dilution & patch testing

We recommend diluting concentrated oils before skin use and performing a patch test on a small area 24–48 hours before wider application.

Pets & pregnancy

Some oils can affect pets and are not recommended during pregnancy. Contact us for pet-friendly or pregnancy-safe options; we'll suggest alternatives and guidance.
