Safety & common questions

Patch test and dilution: Always do a skin patch test before topical use. Suggested dilutions are 0.5–3% for adults unless otherwise advised. Keep blends out of reach of children.

Pregnancy & nursing: Some essential oils are not recommended in pregnancy or while nursing. If you are pregnant or breastfeeding, mention this during booking so we can tailor recommendations.

Pets and scent: Many common essential oils can affect pets. We will provide pet-safe usage guidance where relevant. Diffuse cautiously and monitor behavior.

Disclaimer: Aromatherapy may support daily well-being but is not a medical treatment. If you have health conditions or take medications, consult a healthcare provider first.
