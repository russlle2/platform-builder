Why we do this

We blend simple rituals with plant aromatics so you have gentle practices to return to. Each offering is designed to be practical, safe, and supportive — not a replacement for medical care but a companion for everyday rhythms.

How it works

  1. Consult: A short conversation to learn preferences, sensitivities, and safety considerations (pets, pregnancy, medications).
  2. Design: A suggested ritual or blend that may support your aim — wording uses "may support" and includes a dilution guide.
  3. Practice: Micro-exercises and journaling prompts you can use at home.

Safety: We advise patch testing, careful dilution, and avoiding certain oils during pregnancy or with pets. See the FAQ for details.
