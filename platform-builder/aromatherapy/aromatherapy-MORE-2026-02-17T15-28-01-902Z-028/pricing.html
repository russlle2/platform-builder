Frequently asked safety notes

Patch test

Always test blends on a small skin area before regular use. If irritation occurs, discontinue use.

Dilution

We recommend dilutions appropriate for topical use; ask about safe percentages for your needs.

Pregnancy & medical

Some oils are not recommended during pregnancy or certain health conditions — consult your healthcare provider before use.

Pets

Certain essential oils may affect animals. Keep blends away from pets unless confirmed safe by a veterinary professional.
