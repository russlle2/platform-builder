Safety notes & common questions

Do you make medical claims?
We do not offer medical advice. Blends and practices may support a sense of well-being; consult a licensed professional for medical concerns.
How about pets and pregnancy?
Certain oils can affect pets and should be used with caution. Some oils are not recommended in pregnancy. Always ask if you have concerns.
Patch testing & dilution
Patch testing and appropriate dilution are part of our standard guidance. We'll provide percentages for topical use and clear steps for testing at home.
