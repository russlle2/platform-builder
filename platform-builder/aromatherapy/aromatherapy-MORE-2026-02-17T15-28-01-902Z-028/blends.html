About our formulations

Blends are curated for safe everyday use where possible. We highlight dilution levels, application routes, and any special precautions on each product. If you are pregnant, nursing, or live with pets, reach out so we can recommend the safest options.

FAQ — safety notes

Patch test: Apply a tiny dab of diluted product to inner forearm for 24 hours to check for irritation.

Children & pets: Many essential oils can affect young children and animals. Use lower dilution, keep diffusing sessions short, and consult before using around babies or pets.

Pregnancy: Some oils are typically avoided in pregnancy. If pregnant or nursing, tell us before purchasing so we can guide you.

Allergies: Read ingredient lists carefully. We cannot guarantee the absence of cross-reactive allergens in natural materials.
