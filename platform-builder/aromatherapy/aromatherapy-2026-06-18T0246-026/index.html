Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is Aromatherapy, and how can it benefit me?
Aromatherapy is a holistic healing modality utilizing aromatic essential oils from plants to promote well-being. It can support your emotional balance, alleviate stress, improve sleep, and enhance your overall sense of calm and vitality. We tailor each blend to your specific needs, ensuring a personalized and effective experience.
Are the essential oils used in your Aromatherapy practice safe for everyone?
We prioritize your safety and well-being above all else. During your consultation, we carefully review your health history, sensitivities, and any medications you may be taking to ensure the essential oils chosen are appropriate and safe for your individual use. We always recommend proper dilution and application methods.
How long does a typical Aromatherapy consultation last, and what should I expect?
Your initial Aromatherapy consultation typically lasts 60-90 minutes. During this time, we engage in a comprehensive discussion about your health goals, lifestyle, and preferences. You'll learn about different essential oils, and we'll collaboratively create a custom blend and usage plan tailored just for you.
Can Aromatherapy replace conventional medical treatments?
Aromatherapy is a complementary wellness practice intended to support your overall health and well-being. It is not a substitute for conventional medical diagnosis or treatment. We always encourage you to consult with your healthcare provider for any health concerns and to inform them about any complementary therapies you are using.
