What exactly is Aromatherapy?

Aromatherapy is a holistic healing modality that uses natural plant extracts, known as essential oils, to promote health and well-being. It harnesses the aromatic compounds of plants to improve physical, mental, and emotional health, offering a gentle yet powerful approach to self-care.

How can Aromatherapy benefit my specific concerns?

Aromatherapy can address a wide range of concerns, from alleviating stress and improving sleep to boosting mood and supporting respiratory health. During your consultation, we'll discuss your unique needs and identify specific essential oils and applications that can best support your well-being goals.

Are the essential oils used in Aromatherapy safe?

Yes, when used correctly and with professional guidance, essential oils are safe and highly effective. We prioritize using only pure, therapeutic-grade essential oils and provide clear instructions on proper dilution and application methods tailored to your individual profile to ensure your safety and maximize benefits.

What should I expect during an Aromatherapy consultation?

During your consultation, you can expect a warm and thorough discussion about your health history, lifestyle, and wellness aspirations. Dr. Morgan Ellis will then guide you through an olfactory experience, helping you choose essential oils that resonate with you, and formulate a personalized blend with clear usage instructions.
