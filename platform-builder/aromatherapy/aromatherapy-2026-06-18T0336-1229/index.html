Frequently Asked Questions

How can Aromatherapy specifically help me, as a parent managing daily household stress?
Aromatherapy offers a gentle, non-invasive way to support your nervous system. We craft specific blends to address the emotional weight of parenting, helping to calm overwhelm, ease anxiety, and foster moments of peace, allowing you to respond to daily demands with more presence and less reactivity.
Is the Aromatherapy at suitable for sensitive individuals or those with a history of trauma?
Absolutely. Our approach is deeply trauma-informed, meaning we prioritize safety, choice, and gentle guidance. We work at your pace, always ensuring comfort and agency. Your sensitivities are honored, and blends are meticulously chosen to be soothing and supportive, never overwhelming.
What does a typical Aromatherapy session look like for a busy parent?
We understand your time is precious. Sessions are designed to be grounding and efficient, often involving a gentle conversation about your current emotional landscape, followed by the co-creation of a personalized aromatic blend. We focus on practical application, ensuring you leave with tools that seamlessly integrate into your daily routine, offering immediate and ongoing support.
How can I integrate these aromatic practices into my already full schedule without adding more stress?
Our goal is to simplify, not complicate. We guide you toward simple, potent scent rituals that fit into the quiet moments you already have—a conscious breath before the school run, a calming aroma during bath time, or a grounding scent as you prepare for sleep. These small, consistent practices become powerful anchors for your emotional well-being.
