Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is Aromatherapy and how can it benefit me?
Aromatherapy is the therapeutic use of aromatic essential oils to promote physical and psychological well-being. It can help you in numerous ways, such as reducing stress, improving sleep quality, boosting mood, alleviating discomfort, and enhancing focus. Your specific benefits will depend on your individual needs and the tailored blends we create together.
Are essential oils safe to use, and how do you ensure quality?
When used correctly and with proper guidance, essential oils are very safe. We prioritize your safety by using only high-quality, ethically sourced, and pure essential oils from reputable suppliers. We will always discuss appropriate dilution methods, application techniques, and any potential contraindications with you to ensure a safe and effective experience.
Do I need to have prior knowledge of essential oils before a session?
Absolutely not! Our sessions are designed for everyone, regardless of your prior experience with essential oils. will gently guide you through the world of aromatherapy, explaining everything you need to know and answering all your questions. Your comfort and understanding are our top priorities.
What can I expect during my first Aromatherapy consultation?
During your initial consultation, you can expect a warm, confidential conversation about your health goals, lifestyle, and preferences. We'll explore various aromas, discuss how they make you feel, and collaboratively design a personalized aromatherapy plan, which may include custom essential oil blends and practical usage tips for your daily life. It's a journey of discovery focused entirely on your well-being.
