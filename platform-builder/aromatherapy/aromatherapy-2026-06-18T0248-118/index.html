Frequently Asked Questions

What is Aromatherapy and how can it benefit me?
Aromatherapy is a holistic healing treatment that uses natural plant extracts to promote health and well-being. It can benefit you by supporting emotional balance, easing stress, improving sleep quality, and enhancing physical comfort through the therapeutic properties of essential oils.
Are your Aromatherapy products safe for sensitive skin?
Your safety is paramount. We carefully consider your skin type and any sensitivities during your consultation. All blends are created with high-quality, pure essential oils and appropriate carrier oils, and we provide clear instructions for safe application and patch testing.
How do I choose the right Aromatherapy blend for my needs?
Choosing the right blend begins with a detailed consultation. Lora, your practitioner, will guide you through a process of understanding your current well-being goals and preferences. Based on this, a bespoke blend will be formulated to precisely meet your unique requirements.
What should I expect during an initial Aromatherapy consultation?
During your initial consultation, you can expect a warm and attentive conversation with Lora. We will discuss your health history, lifestyle, emotional state, and any specific concerns you wish to address. This comprehensive discussion allows us to create a truly personalized Aromatherapy plan for you.
