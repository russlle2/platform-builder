Frequently asked safety questions

Do you recommend patch tests?

Yes — before regular topical use, we recommend a small patch test with the suggested dilution. If irritation occurs, discontinue use and consult a clinician.

What about pregnancy?

Some oils are avoided in pregnancy. Notify us when booking so protocols can be adapted. We will not recommend oils known to be contraindicated in pregnancy.

Are essential oils safe for pets?

Certain oils can affect animals. We provide guidance on diffusion times, ventilation, and avoidance of topical use on or near pets.
