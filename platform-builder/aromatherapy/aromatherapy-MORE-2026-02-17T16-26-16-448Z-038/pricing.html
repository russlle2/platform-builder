Safety & common questions

Dilution and patch tests

All topical suggestions are given with dilution ratios. We always recommend a patch test prior to regular topical use.

Pregnancy & medical conditions

Some essential oils are not recommended during pregnancy or certain medical situations. Inform the practitioner when booking so work can be adapted to your needs.

Pets

We provide guidance on safe use around pets. Some oils can be irritating to animals; diffusion and topical use recommendations reflect that caution.
