Safety & FAQ

Do I need to dilute oils?
Yes. All topical suggestions include dilution guidance by weight/volume. We recommend a patch test before regular topical use.
Are your sessions medical?
No. Guidance is wellness-focused and may support everyday comfort. We avoid medical claims and refer to appropriate care when needed.
Pregnancy, pets, or children?
Special considerations apply. Please disclose pregnancy, breastfeeding, or animal companions during intake so we can adjust suggestions. Safety is prioritized.
Patch testing
We include simple patch test steps with topical recommendations. Stop use if irritation occurs and consult a clinician.
