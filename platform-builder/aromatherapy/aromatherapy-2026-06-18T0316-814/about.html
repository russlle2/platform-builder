How can Aromatherapy specifically help my postural health as an athlete?

Aromatherapy supports postural health by encouraging deep relaxation in stressed muscles and enhancing your proprioception – your body's awareness of its position in space. Specific essential oils can help calm the nervous system, allowing for better muscle release and improved mental focus on alignment, making it easier for you to maintain a strong, balanced posture even after strenuous activity.

Will Aromatherapy interfere with my training or recovery routine?

Not at all. Our Aromatherapy blends are designed to complement and enhance your existing training and recovery. They are crafted to be supportive, aiding in muscle recovery, reducing mental stress, and promoting restorative rest, all of which contribute positively to your athletic performance and overall well-being. We ensure our recommendations integrate seamlessly into your daily life.

How are the Aromatherapy blends personalized for my active lifestyle?

Your blends are entirely unique to you. We begin with a detailed consultation about your specific athletic demands, areas of physical stress, and postural goals. From there, Dr. Morgan Ellis selects and combines essential oils known for their properties in muscle support, inflammation reduction, mental clarity, and grounding, creating a formula that directly addresses your individual needs and supports your active body.

What should I expect during an Aromatherapy session for physical stress management?

During a session, you'll discuss your current physical state, any areas of tension, and your goals for better body awareness and stress reduction. Dr. Morgan Ellis will guide you through the selection of specific essential oils, explaining their benefits. You might experience the aromas through inhalation or gentle topical application, designed to bring a sense of calm, support muscle release, and reconnect you with your physical center.
