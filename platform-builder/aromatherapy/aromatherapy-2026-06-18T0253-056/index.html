Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is Aromatherapy and how can it benefit me?
Aromatherapy is a holistic healing modality that utilizes natural plant extracts, known as essential oils, to promote health and well-being. These oils are inhaled or applied topically to influence your mood, alleviate discomfort, improve sleep, and support overall balance. Your personalized blends will target your specific needs.
Are the essential oils you use safe for everyone?
We prioritize your safety above all else. During your consultation, we conduct a thorough assessment of your health history, sensitivities, and any medications you may be taking. This allows us to select and dilute essential oils appropriately, ensuring your blends are safe and effective for your unique constitution.
How long does it take to experience the benefits of Aromatherapy?
The experience of Aromatherapy is highly individual. While some individuals may feel immediate effects, consistent and mindful use of your personalized blends will deepen their impact over time. We will guide you on the best practices for integrating Aromatherapy into your daily routine to maximize its long-term benefits for you.
What does a typical Aromatherapy consultation involve?
Your initial consultation is a comprehensive discussion about your health goals, lifestyle, and preferences. We'll explore your current well-being, and based on this, we will formulate a customized essential oil blend. You'll also receive guidance on how to use your blend effectively and safely, empowering you to integrate Aromatherapy into your self-care practices.
