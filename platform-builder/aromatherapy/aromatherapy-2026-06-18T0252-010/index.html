Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is Aromatherapy and how does it work?
Aromatherapy is a holistic healing treatment that uses natural plant extracts to promote health and well-being. It primarily utilizes aromatic essential oils for therapeutic benefits. These oils are inhaled or applied topically, stimulating your olfactory system and entering your bloodstream to influence your mood, alleviate discomfort, and support your body's natural healing processes.
Is Aromatherapy safe for everyone, including children or those with sensitivities?
While aromatherapy is natural, safety is paramount. We always conduct a thorough intake to understand your health history, sensitivities, and any medications you may be taking. For children, pregnant individuals, or those with specific health conditions, we use highly diluted blends and specific oils deemed safe. Your well-being is our utmost concern, and we ensure all recommendations are appropriate for your unique circumstances.
How long does it take to see results from personalized Aromatherapy blends?
The experience of aromatherapy is highly individual. Some people report an immediate sense of calm or upliftment upon first use, while more chronic concerns may require consistent application over several weeks to notice significant shifts. We encourage regular use as part of your self-care routine and are available to adjust your blend or application methods to optimize your results and experience.
What should I expect during an Aromatherapy consultation?
Your consultation is a collaborative conversation. We'll discuss your health goals, current lifestyle, emotional state, and any specific concerns you wish to address. We'll explore your scent preferences and sensitivities. Based on this comprehensive understanding, I will formulate a custom essential oil blend tailored precisely to your needs, along with clear guidance on its safe and effective use. You'll leave with a personalized plan and your unique aromatic creation.
