Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is Aromatherapy, and how can it help me?
Aromatherapy is the therapeutic use of aromatic essential oils to promote physical and psychological well-being. It can support you in various ways, such as reducing stress, improving sleep quality, boosting mood, alleviating discomfort, and enhancing mental clarity, all through the natural power of plant essences. Your personalized session will address your specific concerns.
Are essential oils safe to use, and how do I know which ones are right for me?
When used correctly, essential oils are generally safe. However, purity, dilution, and individual sensitivities are crucial considerations. will guide you on safe usage practices, proper dilution, and appropriate application methods, ensuring your custom blends are perfectly suited for your unique needs and health profile. You'll receive clear, personalized instructions.
What can I expect during my first Aromatherapy consultation?
Your initial consultation is a comprehensive conversation where will listen to your health history, lifestyle, and well-being goals. Based on this, a bespoke Aromatherapy plan will be developed, including custom essential oil blends and guidance on their integration into your daily life. You'll leave with clear next steps and a deeper understanding of your aromatic journey.
How long does it take to see results from Aromatherapy?
The experience of Aromatherapy is highly individual. Some individuals report immediate shifts in mood or relaxation, while others observe more gradual changes over time with consistent use. Your commitment to incorporating the suggested practices will significantly influence your results. will set realistic expectations with you and support you throughout your process.
