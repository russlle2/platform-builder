Aromatherapy: Reestablishing Intimacy with Self and Others

Chronic illness or injury often creates a chasm, separating you from your former self and impacting your closest relationships. Our specialized Aromatherapy practices are crafted to bridge this gap. We focus on gentle, evocative scents that support emotional regulation, foster self-compassion, and create a tender space for you to reconnect with your inner landscape, and subsequently, with those who matter most.
