Questions people ask

\n
\n

Are these medical treatments?

\n

No. Aromatherapy practices described here are not medical care and may support wellbeing in gentle ways. They are educational and experiential.

\n

What about dilution and patch testing?

\n

Dilution guidance is provided for topical use. We recommend a patch test before regular topical application and using pre-diluted retail roll-ons for convenience.

\n

Can I use these around pets or during pregnancy?

\n

Not all oils are pet-safe or pregnancy-safe. Please consult your healthcare provider and avoid certain botanicals. We include pet and pregnancy notes on product pages and in sessions.
