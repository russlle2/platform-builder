Frequently asked — safety-forward

Do I need to dilute essential oils?
Yes. Essential oils should be diluted for topical use. Typical dilution ranges are 1–3% for adults for roll-ons/skin applications. For children or sensitive skin start lower. We will always provide dilution guidance when suggesting blends.
What about patch tests?
A patch test is recommended before applying a new blend to larger skin areas. Apply a small amount on inner forearm and wait 24 hours to check for sensitivity.
Pets and diffusion
Some essential oils can affect pets. Avoid uncontrolled diffusion where animals can inhale directly. Mention household pets in your message so we can suggest safer options.
Pregnancy & medical cautions
If you are pregnant, nursing, or have a medical condition, consult your care provider before using essential oils. We do not provide medical advice; we offer scent practices that may support comfort.
