Frequently asked

Are these medically validated?
Scent work is not a substitute for medical care. Our suggestions may support subjective wellness but are not medical advice.
Safety & dilution
We recommend dilutions for topical products and a patch test before skin use. Typical dilutions are 1–3% for adults depending on product type. For children, pregnancy, or serious sensitivities, consult a physician before use.
Pets & home
Keep concentrated oils out of reach of pets. Avoid applying essential oils directly to an animal or spraying in enclosed pet spaces without veterinary guidance.
Pregnancy & conditions
Some essential oils are not recommended during pregnancy or for people with certain medical conditions. Please consult a qualified professional.
