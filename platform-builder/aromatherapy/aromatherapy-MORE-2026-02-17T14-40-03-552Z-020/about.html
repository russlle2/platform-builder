Safety & common questions

Do your blends make medical claims?
No. Blends are intended to be supportive sensory tools and may support wellbeing, but they are not medical treatments.
What about dilution and patch tests?
All ready-to-use blends are properly diluted for typical topical use. We recommend performing a patch test before full use — place a small dab on inner forearm and wait 24 hours for irritation. Guidance is included with each product.
Pets, pregnancy and children?
Some essential oils are not recommended around pets, infants, or during pregnancy. We provide notes and alternatives; consult your care provider for individual guidance.
How long are blends good for?
Stored away from heat and light, most blends remain good for many months. We label each product with a best-by indication.
