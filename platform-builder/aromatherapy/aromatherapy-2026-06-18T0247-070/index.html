Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is Aromatherapy?
Aromatherapy is a holistic healing treatment that uses natural plant extracts to promote health and well-being. Sometimes called essential oil therapy, Aromatherapy uses aromatic essential oils medicinally to improve the health of the body, mind, and spirit. It enhances both physical and emotional health.
How can Aromatherapy benefit me personally?
Aromatherapy can offer numerous benefits tailored to your unique needs. It can help reduce stress and anxiety, improve sleep quality, alleviate headaches, boost mood, support respiratory health, and even ease certain types of discomfort. We focus on creating personalized blends to address your specific wellness goals.
Are essential oils safe to use?
When used correctly and with professional guidance, essential oils are generally safe. We prioritize your safety and educate you on proper dilution, application methods, and potential contraindications. We only use high-quality, pure essential oils and ensure your blends are appropriate for your health profile and lifestyle.
What should I expect during an Aromatherapy consultation?
During your consultation, you can expect a comprehensive discussion about your health history, current well-being, and personal goals.
