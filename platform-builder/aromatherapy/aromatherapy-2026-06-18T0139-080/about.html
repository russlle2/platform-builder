The Science of Scent

Aromatherapy isn't just about pleasant scents—it's about how specific aromatic compounds interact with your olfactory system to influence cortisol levels, sleep cycles, and cognitive function. Our formulations are developed using current research on molecular interactions, avoiding generic blends in favor of precise, evidence-supported combinations tailored to your physiological responses.

How is your Aromatherapy approach different from other practitioners?

We use a bio-scent assessment process based on current research in olfactory neurobiology, not just preference-based blending. Each protocol is supported by scientific literature on specific compound interactions rather than generalized 'calming' or 'energizing' claims.

Do I need to be a professional to use your blends?

No—we design for accessibility. All formulations include usage instructions based on your scent assessment, with clear guidance on duration and application. We also provide troubleshooting for common user responses to specific compounds.

Can I use Aromatherapy alongside my current medications?

Absolutely, but always consult your physician first. Our protocols avoid compounds known to interact with common medications, and we'll discuss potential considerations during your consultation based on your health regimen.

How soon will I notice effects from the blends?

You'll typically notice subtle shifts within 3-5 uses as your system adapts to the molecular pattern. For targeted support like sleep or focus, we recommend consistent use over 14 days to allow your nervous system to recalibrate to the scent profile.
