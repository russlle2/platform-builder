How can Aromatherapy gently support my relationships as I age?

Our Aromatherapy focuses on emotional well-being. Specific aromas can evoke feelings of calm, compassion, or joy, which can gently influence your interactions and help you approach relationship dynamics with greater patience and understanding. It's a subtle, loving way to foster deeper connections and mend any frayed edges.

Is Aromatherapy safe and appropriate for seniors, especially if I have health considerations?

Absolutely. We specialize in gentle, mindful Aromatherapy tailored for seniors. We always conduct a thorough consultation to understand your health history and any sensitivities you may have. We use only the highest quality, therapeutic-grade essential oils, diluted appropriately, ensuring your comfort and safety are our utmost priority.

What kind of relationship challenges can Aromatherapy help me with?

Aromatherapy can offer a comforting presence when navigating the complexities of family dynamics, loss, loneliness, or simply the evolving nature of long-term partnerships. It can help soothe anxiety, promote emotional openness, and support a sense of inner peace, which in turn, positively influences how you connect with others.

What does a first Aromatherapy session entail at Sage Garden?

Your first session is a gentle conversation, much like sharing a cup of tea with a trusted friend. We'll listen to your heart's desires and concerns regarding your relationships and well-being. Then, we'll guide you through experiencing various aromas, crafting a personalized blend that resonates with your spirit and supports your unique journey towards relational harmony.
