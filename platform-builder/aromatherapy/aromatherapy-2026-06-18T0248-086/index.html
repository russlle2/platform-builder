Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is Aromatherapy?
Aromatherapy is a holistic healing treatment that uses natural plant extracts to promote health and well-being. It utilizes aromatic essential oils medicinally to improve the health of the body, mind, and spirit. Your practitioner will explain how specific oils can support your unique needs.
How can Aromatherapy benefit my well-being?
Aromatherapy can offer numerous benefits, including stress reduction, improved sleep quality, mood enhancement, pain relief, and immune support. Your personalized blends will be formulated to address your specific concerns, helping you achieve a greater sense of balance and vitality in your daily life.
Are the essential oils you use safe and pure?
Yes, your safety and well-being are our top priority. We source only high-quality, ethically produced essential oils from reputable suppliers. We ensure that our oils are pure, unadulterated, and appropriate for therapeutic use, always guiding you on proper and safe application methods for your custom blends.
What should I expect during an Aromatherapy consultation?
During your consultation, you'll have a warm and confidential discussion with your practitioner about your health history, lifestyle, and wellness goals. This allows us to understand your unique needs and preferences, leading to the creation of a custom Aromatherapy plan and bespoke essential oil blends tailored just for you.
