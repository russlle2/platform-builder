Quiet Evening

Top: Sweet orange; Middle: Lavender; Base: Cedarwood.
Aroma: soft, sweetMethod: diffuser/mist
Supports winding down and evening ritual. Avoid direct skin use without dilution. Patch test recommended.

Forest Walk

Aroma: resinous, groundingMethod: diffuser
Creates a grounding atmosphere for reflective moments. Keep away from pets with known sensitivity to phenols.

Citrus Lift

Top: Bergamot; Middle: Sweet orange; Base: Petitgrain.
Aroma: bright, effervescentMethod: diffuser/roller

Top: Rosemary; Middle: Clary sage; Base: Benzoin.
Aroma: warm, herbalMethod: diffuser
Offers an anchor for focused evenings. Not recommended for individuals with high blood pressure without consult.

Moonwater Mist

Top: Roman chamomile; Middle: Neroli; Base: Sandalwood.
Aroma: floral, softMethod: mist, topical (low dilution)
Designed for gentle bedtime routines. Use conservative dilution for topical use and patch-test.

Sunlit Herb Garden

Top: Lemon verbena; Middle: Basil; Base: Cypress.
Aroma: citrus-herbaceousMethod: diffuser
Invites clarity for creative tasks. Avoid concentrated topical use on sensitive skin.

Warm Hearth

Top: Cardamom; Middle: Ginger; Base: Vanilla absolute.
Aroma: spicy, cozyMethod: diffuser, inhalation
Comfort-oriented scent for shared spaces. Keep out of reach of children and always air the room well after use.

Stillness Resin

Top: Frankincense; Middle: Myrrh; Base: Labdanum.
Aroma: resinous, contemplativeMethod: diffuser, resin incense
Suitable for meditation practices. Avoid heavy use in small, unventilated rooms.

Garden Dew

Top: Green mandarin; Middle: Geranium; Base: Patchouli.
Aroma: fresh floral-earthyMethod: roller, diffuser
Lightly grounding while brightening mood; for topical use follow dilution guidelines and test if pregnant or nursing.
Safety note: These blends are aromatic formulations and are not medical treatments. We provide dilution, patch test, and pregnancy/pet guidance with every custom or in-person offering. Consult a licensed medical professional for health concerns.
