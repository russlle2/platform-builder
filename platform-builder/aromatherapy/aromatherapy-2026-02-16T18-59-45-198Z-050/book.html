Quick FAQ

Do I need to patch test before attending?
We recommend doing a patch test at least 24 hours before any hands-on topical activity. You can also perform onsite guidance for first-timers.
Can I bring my pet?
Pets are not recommended in workshop spaces. Diffusions and some oils can be stressful or unsafe for animals; we provide guidance on pet-safe scheduling and choices.
I'm pregnant — can I attend?
Yes, but please notify us when booking. We will suggest pregnancy-safe blends and avoid topical applications unless cleared with a care provider.
