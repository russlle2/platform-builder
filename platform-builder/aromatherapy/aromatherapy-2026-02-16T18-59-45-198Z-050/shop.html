Product FAQ — safety highlights

Dilution guidance included?
Yes. Every topical product is labeled with dilution percentage and recommended frequency. Custom dilutions are available via consultation.
Do you recommend patch testing?
Always. Apply a small amount to the inside of the forearm and check for 24–48 hours for irritation.
What about pets?
Certain oils are unsafe for cats and some dogs. Do not apply undiluted oils to pets. When diffusing, ensure the animal can leave the room and air the space afterwards. Consult your vet for specifics.
Pregnancy and nursing?
We mark products that are not recommended during pregnancy or breastfeeding. Contact us for pregnancy-safe alternatives; we offer tailored kits with adjusted formulations.
