Myth & Truth

Myth

Essential oils are a cure-all — they fix everything instantly.

Truth

Essential oils are supportive tools for ritual, mood, and environment. They are not medical treatments. Practice safely: dilute when needed, patch test, and consult health professionals for medical advice.

Frequently Asked

We recommend 1%–3% dilution for most topical adult use in roll-ons and creams. For general guidance: 1% ~ 6 drops per 30ml; 2% ~ 12 drops per 30ml. Adjust by age, skin sensitivity, and purpose. Always follow workshop instructions.

A patch test is a small application of a diluted blend to an inconspicuous skin area to observe sensitivity over 24 hours. We practice patch tests during hands-on sessions.

Some aromas can affect animals. Use pet-safe practices: avoid direct topical application to pets, keep diffused oils light and well-ventilated, and consult a veterinarian for specific concerns.

Pregnancy requires extra caution. Some oils are discouraged in pregnancy and postpartum. We provide pregnancy-safe alternatives and recommend consulting your healthcare provider before participation.
