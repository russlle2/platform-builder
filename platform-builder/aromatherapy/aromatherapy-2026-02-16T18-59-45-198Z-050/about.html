Questions I hear often

How do I keep blends safe for family use?
Label blends, keep concentrations modest, avoid direct application to children without guidance, and store oils out of reach.
What about pets and diffusers?
Use low diffuser times in well-ventilated areas and provide pet-free spaces while diffusing. Some oils are better avoided around birds and small mammals — we list those in class notes.
Are there pregnancy considerations?
Yes. We provide pregnancy-safe recipes and recommend private consultations for topical use. We do not provide medical advice.
Why teach patch testing?
Patch testing helps spot sensitivity before larger topical use. It’s a simple, low-risk measure that we practice in workshops.
