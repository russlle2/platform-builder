Questions often asked

How do you approach dilution?
We default to conservative dilutions (0.5–3%) for topical products depending on age and context. For atmospheric uses we offer clear ppm guidance. Individual factors change recommendations; consultation required for tailored advice.
Do I need a patch test?
Yes — for any new topical blend, we advise a 24–48 hour patch test on a small area. We coach you through the process during sessions.
Are blends safe for pets?
Pets are more sensitive. We do not recommend undirected topical application to animals. Diffusion in common areas should be brief and well-ventilated; consult with your veterinarian for specific concerns.
Pregnancy & breastfeeding — what then?
Some botanicals are contraindicated. We perform pre-session checks and provide pregnancy-safe options or defer topical blends when appropriate.
