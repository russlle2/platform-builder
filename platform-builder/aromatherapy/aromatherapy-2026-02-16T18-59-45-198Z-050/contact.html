Questions I often answer

Dilution — how concentrated should blends be?
For topical use I recommend conservative dilutions: 0.5–2.5% for adults depending on application. For events we provide clear labeling and sample sizes so you can make an informed choice.
Patch testing
A patch test on a small skin area is an essential step. We guide attendees through a simple patch protocol before topical use. If irritation occurs, discontinue use.
Pets & aromatic safety
Many oils are potent around animals. In shared spaces we prioritize ventilation, lower concentrations, and opt for pet-friendly alternatives when appropriate. If you are bringing animals, let me know in advance.
Pregnancy and nursing
Certain oils are contraindicated during pregnancy or lactation. We adapt blends and provide alternatives; always consult a licensed healthcare provider with specific medical questions.
Can oils replace medical care?
No. Aromatherapy supports well-being through scent and ritual but is not a replacement for professional medical treatment.
