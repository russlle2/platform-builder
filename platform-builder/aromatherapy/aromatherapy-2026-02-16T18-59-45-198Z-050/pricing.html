Frequently Asked (safety-forward)

How do you recommend diluting essential oils?
We model conservative dilutions: 1–2% for daily topical use for most adults, lower for children and sensitive people. Specific recipes are shared during workshops.
What is a patch test and how do I do one?
Apply a 1% dilution to a small skin patch on the forearm, cover for 24 hours, and watch for redness or irritation. If irritation appears, do not use the oil topically.
Are essential oils safe around pets?
Some oils are not pet-friendly. We recommend ventilation, low diffuser times, pet-free spaces while diffusing, and consulting a veterinarian for specific concerns.
Can pregnant people attend events or use blends?
Pregnancy brings special considerations. We provide pregnancy-safe alternatives and encourage one-on-one consultation before topical use.
