How can Aromatherapy specifically help with my postural health during recovery?

Aromatherapy can support your postural health by encouraging relaxation in tight muscles, reducing inflammation, and enhancing your mental focus to improve body awareness. Specific essential oils can help you feel more grounded and present, making it easier to notice and adjust your posture throughout your day without strain.

Is Aromatherapy safe to use if I'm managing a chronic illness or injury?

Yes, when administered by a trained practitioner, Aromatherapy is generally very safe. We take a thorough health history to ensure all blends are customized to your specific needs, sensitivities, and any medications you may be taking, always prioritizing your comfort and safety during your recovery.

What kind of Aromatherapy blends would be beneficial for improving my body awareness?

Blends for body awareness often include oils that promote mental clarity and a sense of calm, such as frankincense, cedarwood, or bergamot. These aromas can help you tune into your physical sensations more effectively, making it easier to identify areas of tension or imbalance and gently guide your body toward better alignment.

How often should I use Aromatherapy to support my recovery and postural comfort?

The frequency of Aromatherapy use is highly individualized, depending on your specific recovery needs and comfort levels. We’ll discuss a personalized plan during your consultation, which might include daily gentle applications or specific use during activities that challenge your posture, always aligning with what feels right for your body.
