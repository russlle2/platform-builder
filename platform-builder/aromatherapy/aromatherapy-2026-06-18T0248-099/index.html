Frequently Asked Questions

What is Aromatherapy and how can it benefit me?
Aromatherapy is a holistic healing modality that uses natural plant extracts, known as essential oils, to promote health and well-being. It can support your emotional balance, enhance physical comfort, improve sleep quality, and even boost cognitive function, depending on your specific needs and the oils chosen for you.
Are essential oils safe for everyone?
While essential oils are natural, they are highly concentrated and should be used with care. During your consultation, we'll discuss your health history, medications, and any sensitivities to ensure your custom blends are formulated safely and appropriately for you. Your well-being is our utmost priority.
How quickly can I expect to see results from Aromatherapy?
The experience of Aromatherapy is deeply personal. Some individuals feel an immediate shift in their mood or comfort, while others notice more gradual, subtle changes over time. Consistency in using your custom blends as directed is key to experiencing their full potential and supporting your body's natural healing processes.
Do you offer virtual Aromatherapy consultations?
Yes, we offer convenient virtual consultations, allowing you to access personalized Aromatherapy guidance from the comfort of your home. Following your virtual session, your custom essential oil blends will be carefully prepared and shipped directly to you, ensuring a seamless and supportive experience wherever you are.
