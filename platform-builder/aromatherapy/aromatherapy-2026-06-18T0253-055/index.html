Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is clinical aromatherapy?
Clinical aromatherapy utilizes therapeutic-grade essential oils, derived from plants, to support your physical, mental, and emotional health. It's a holistic approach, where we consider your entire well-being to create personalized blends and applications designed to help your body's natural healing processes.
How can aromatherapy benefit my daily life?
Aromatherapy can offer a multitude of benefits, from reducing stress and improving sleep to boosting mood and supporting respiratory health. By incorporating essential oils into your routine, you can create a more balanced and harmonious environment for yourself, enhancing your overall sense of peace and vitality.
Is aromatherapy safe for everyone, including children or pregnant individuals?
While generally safe, essential oils are potent and require careful consideration. We always prioritize your safety. If you are pregnant, nursing, have specific health conditions, or are considering aromatherapy for children, we will discuss appropriate dilutions and specific oils during your consultation to ensure a safe and beneficial experience for you.
What should I expect during my first aromatherapy consultation?
Your initial consultation with will be a comfortable, in-depth conversation. We'll explore your health history, current lifestyle, and wellness goals. Based on this, we'll discuss essential oil properties and potential applications, ultimately formulating a personalized aromatherapy plan and a custom blend designed to support your unique needs.
