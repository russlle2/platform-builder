Frequently Asked Questions

What is Aromatherapy and how can it benefit me?
Aromatherapy is a holistic healing treatment that uses natural plant extracts to promote health and well-being. By inhaling essential oils, or applying them topically, you can experience benefits ranging from stress reduction and improved sleep to pain relief and mood enhancement, all tailored to your specific needs.
Are the essential oils used in your Aromatherapy blends safe?
Absolutely. We prioritize your safety and well-being. We use only therapeutic-grade essential oils from reputable suppliers, and each blend is carefully formulated with appropriate dilutions for your individual use, considering any sensitivities or health conditions you may have. Your safety is our utmost concern.
How do I choose the right Aromatherapy blend for my needs?
That's precisely what our personalized consultation is for! During your session, we will discuss your health goals, lifestyle, and preferences in detail. Based on this comprehensive understanding, we will guide you in selecting or creating the perfect Aromatherapy blend to support your unique journey.
Can Aromatherapy replace conventional medicine?
While Aromatherapy offers significant benefits for your well-being, it is intended to complement, not replace, conventional medical treatments. We always recommend consulting with your healthcare provider for any medical conditions. Aromatherapy serves as a powerful support system for your holistic health.
