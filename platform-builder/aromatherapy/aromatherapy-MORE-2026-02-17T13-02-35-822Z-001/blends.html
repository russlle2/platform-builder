How dilution is calculated

Dilution is given as percent concentration and approximate drops per volume. We choose conservative ranges when sensitivity, pregnancy, or pets are noted. Use mild dilutions for rollers and topical use; inhalation in a diffuser typically uses fewer drops.

Export and use

Safety corner

This tool provides guidance only. If pregnant, nursing, or under clinical care, consult a qualified clinician before topical or internal use. Keep blends out of reach of children and avoid use on pets without veterinary advice.
