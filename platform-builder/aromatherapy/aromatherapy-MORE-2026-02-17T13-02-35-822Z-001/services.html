Quick FAQ

Are these medical treatments?

No. Aromatherapy may support well-being but is not a substitute for medical care.

Safety and dilution

Every plan recommends safe dilution ranges and a patch test. We avoid high-risk oils for pregnancy and certain household pets.

Pets at home?

Tell us about pets in intake. Some oils should not be used around cats or birds; we will suggest alternatives.

Pregnancy and breastfeeding

Certain oils are not recommended during pregnancy or breastfeeding. We will offer safe possibilities and always err on caution.
