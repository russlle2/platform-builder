FAQ — Safety first

      

        
Are these medical treatments?

        
No. Our offerings are informational and may support wellbeing. We do not make medical claims. Consult a healthcare professional for medical conditions.


        
What about dilution and patch tests?

        
We provide clear dilution guidance in blend cards. Always perform a patch test for topical use: apply a small diluted amount to an inconspicuous area and wait 24 hours. Discontinue if irritation occurs.


        
Pets and essential oils?

        
Some oils can affect animals. Avoid exposing pets to concentrated vapors and consult a veterinarian if unsure. We include pet-safe notes when relevant.


        
Pregnancy and nursing

        
Caution is advised. Some oils are not recommended in pregnancy or lactation. Please disclose pregnancy/nursing status before any personalized guidance.
