Questions commonly answered

How strong should topical blends be?
Typical starting dilutions: 0.5–1% for facial areas, 1–2% for general adult topical use, and 0.25–0.5% for older adults or sensitive skin. This tool gives drop-per-bottle guidance; always patch test before widespread use.
What about pets?
Many essential oils affect animals differently. Keep blends out of reach, avoid concentrated topical use on pets, and avoid diffusing strong oils in small enclosed spaces with pets present. Consult a vet for specific guidance.
Pregnancy and nursing
Some oils are contraindicated in pregnancy. If you are pregnant or nursing, discuss blends with a qualified clinician before use. We will always recommend extremely conservative dilutions and prioritise diffusion over topical use when appropriate.
