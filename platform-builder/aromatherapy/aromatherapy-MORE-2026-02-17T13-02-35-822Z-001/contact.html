FAQ highlights

Is aromatherapy safe?
Aromatherapy may support comfort and focus for some people. It is not a substitute for medical care. Follow dilution guidance, perform a patch test, and stop use if irritation occurs.
What about pregnancy & medications?
Pregnancy, breastfeeding, and some medications may require extra caution. Consult a qualified health provider before using essential oils in these circumstances.
Pet safety
Some essential oils can be toxic to pets, especially cats and small mammals. Avoid close contact, keep oils out of reach, and consult a veterinarian for guidance.
