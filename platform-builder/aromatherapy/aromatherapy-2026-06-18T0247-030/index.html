Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is Aromatherapy?
Aromatherapy is a holistic healing treatment that uses natural plant extracts to promote health and well-being. Sometimes called essential oil therapy, it utilizes aromatic essential oils medicinally to improve the health of the body, mind, and spirit. It enhances both physical and emotional health, offering you a gentle path to balance.
How can Aromatherapy benefit me?
Aromatherapy offers a wide range of benefits for your well-being. It can help reduce stress and anxiety, improve sleep quality, alleviate headaches and muscle aches, boost mood, and enhance your overall sense of calm and focus. Your personalized blends will be designed to address your specific needs and support your personal wellness goals.
Are essential oils safe to use?
When used correctly and with proper guidance, essential oils are generally safe. We prioritize your safety and educate you on appropriate dilution, application methods, and any potential contraindications. It's crucial to use high-quality, pure essential oils, and will ensure you receive only the best for your personalized Aromatherapy experience.
What should I expect during an Aromatherapy consultation?
During your consultation, will engage in a warm and thorough discussion to understand your health history, lifestyle, and specific wellness concerns. This allows us to craft a truly bespoke Aromatherapy plan for you, including custom essential oil blends and guidance on how to integrate them into your daily routine for optimal benefit.
