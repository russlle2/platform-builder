Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is Aromatherapy?
Aromatherapy is a holistic healing modality that utilizes natural plant extracts, known as essential oils, to promote health and well-being. It leverages the aromatic compounds of plants to support your physical, mental, and emotional health through inhalation or topical application. It's a gentle yet powerful approach to wellness that focuses on your body's innate ability to heal.
Is Aromatherapy safe for everyone?
While Aromatherapy is generally safe, essential oils are potent and should be used with care. During your consultation, we will discuss your health conditions, medications, and sensitivities to ensure every recommendation is appropriate and safe for your specific needs. We always prioritize your safety and well-being, providing clear instructions for proper use.
How can Aromatherapy help with stress and anxiety?
Many essential oils possess calming and uplifting properties that can significantly help manage stress and anxiety. Oils like lavender, bergamot, and frankincense can promote relaxation, reduce feelings of tension, and improve mood. We will help you identify the most effective oils and application methods to create a personalized regimen for your stress and anxiety relief.
What should I expect during my first Aromatherapy consultation?
During your initial consultation, you can expect a warm and thorough discussion about your health goals, lifestyle, and any concerns you may have. We will explore various essential oils, explain their properties, and collaborate with you to formulate a personalized plan and custom blend designed to support your unique path to well-being. It's a collaborative and informative experience focused entirely on you.
