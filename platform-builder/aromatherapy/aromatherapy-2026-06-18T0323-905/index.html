Frequently Asked Questions

How can Aromatherapy specifically help me during a major life transition?
During significant life changes, Aromatherapy can offer a structured approach to managing stress and supporting emotional balance. We focus on specific aromatic compounds that have been shown to influence mood and physiological responses, providing you with tools to navigate new routines and adapt to your evolving circumstances more smoothly.
Is your Aromatherapy approach suitable for preventative care against seasonal shifts?
Yes, absolutely. Our consultations emphasize proactive strategies. We will discuss your individual needs in relation to upcoming seasonal changes—such as immune support for winter or promoting mental clarity in spring—and craft a tailored Aromatherapy plan to help you prepare and maintain your well-being throughout the year.
What makes your Aromatherapy practice 'evidence-based'?
Our practice relies on current scientific research regarding the therapeutic properties of essential oils. We select and recommend specific oils based on their documented effects on the body and mind, ensuring that the recommendations you receive are supported by clinical understanding rather than anecdotal claims, providing you with reliable and effective support.
How do you ensure my Aromatherapy plan is personalized for my specific life changes?
Your initial consultation involves a detailed discussion of your current life circumstances, any transitions you are experiencing, and your specific wellness goals. We then combine this information with our clinical knowledge to formulate a personalized Aromatherapy plan, including specific blends and application methods, designed to address your unique needs and support your journey.
