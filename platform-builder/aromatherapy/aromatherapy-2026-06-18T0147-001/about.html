How do you choose my personalized Aromatherapy blend?

We begin with a detailed wellness consultation covering your daily routines, stress triggers, and goals. We never prescribe based on symptoms alone—your unique biochemistry and lifestyle determine the precise oil and carrier oil combination for your optimal results.

Can Aromatherapy really help with anxiety?

Yes. Research shows certain essential oil combinations (like lavender and bergamot) can reduce perceived anxiety within 20 minutes when used correctly. We design protocols that address your specific anxiety patterns—not just general 'calming' effects—to give you practical, immediate tools for your day-to-day.

How soon will I notice benefits?

Many clients report feeling more grounded within the first 3-5 uses. For deeper shifts like improved sleep quality or reduced stress reactivity, consistent use over 2-4 weeks is ideal. We provide clear usage instructions and check-ins to track your progress toward your goals.

Do you work with medical conditions?

We always recommend consulting your healthcare provider first. However, our Aromatherapy safely complements your existing care. We collaborate with practitioners to ensure your scent protocol aligns with your health plan, offering non-invasive support for your well-being.
