Frequently Asked Questions

What is Aromatherapy and how can it benefit me?
Aromatherapy is a holistic healing treatment that uses natural plant extracts to promote health and wellbeing. It can benefit you by reducing stress, improving sleep quality, easing discomfort, boosting mood, and enhancing overall emotional and physical balance through the therapeutic properties of essential oils.
Are essential oils safe to use, and how do you ensure their quality?
Yes, essential oils are safe when used correctly under professional guidance. We source only the highest quality, pure essential oils from reputable distillers. We provide you with clear instructions for safe dilution and application, ensuring your comfort and efficacy with every blend.
How does a consultation work, and what should I expect?
During your initial consultation, we'll have a detailed conversation about your health goals, lifestyle, and preferences. You can expect a warm and attentive discussion where gathers all necessary information to create a truly personalized Aromatherapy plan and custom essential oil blends just for you.
Can Aromatherapy address specific health concerns I have?
While Aromatherapy is not a substitute for medical treatment, it can be a wonderful complementary therapy to support your overall wellbeing and help manage symptoms associated with various concerns like stress, anxiety, sleep disturbances, and mild discomfort. We focus on enhancing your body's natural ability to find balance.
