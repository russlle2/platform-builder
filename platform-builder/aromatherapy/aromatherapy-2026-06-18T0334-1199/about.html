How can Aromatherapy specifically help me manage the stress of parenting young children?

Our Aromatherapy approach focuses on helping your nervous system achieve a more balanced state. By using specific essential oils known to influence mood and stress responses, we can create custom blends that help reduce feelings of overwhelm, improve sleep quality, and foster greater emotional resilience, allowing you to respond to daily challenges with more presence and calm.

Is Aromatherapy safe to use around my young children?

Yes, safety is paramount. We prioritize the safe and appropriate use of essential oils, especially in households with young children. Dr. Morgan Ellis will provide clear, evidence-based guidelines on dilution, diffusion methods, and specific oils that are safe for your family's environment, ensuring therapeutic benefits without compromise.

What does a trauma-informed Aromatherapy session involve?

A trauma-informed Aromatherapy session begins with creating a secure and understanding space. We explore your emotional landscape with sensitivity, identifying specific stressors and emotional triggers. Dr. Morgan Ellis then guides you in selecting and experiencing essential oils that can gently support nervous system regulation and emotional processing, always respecting your pace and comfort level.

How quickly can I expect to feel the effects of Aromatherapy on my stress levels?

The experience of Aromatherapy is highly individual. Many parents report feeling a sense of immediate calm or grounding during and after initial sessions. Consistent, personalized use of your custom blends can lead to more sustained improvements in emotional regulation, reduced anxiety, and an overall greater sense of well-being over time, building your internal resources.
