Frequently Asked Questions

How can Aromatherapy specifically help my postpartum stress?
Aromatherapy utilizes the powerful connection between scent and your limbic system, which governs emotions and memory. For new mothers, specific essential oils can signal to your brain that you are safe and supported, actively reducing cortisol levels and mitigating feelings of anxiety and overwhelm that are common in postpartum.
Is Aromatherapy safe for me and my baby during postpartum?
Absolutely. Your safety and your baby's well-being are our utmost priority. We use only therapeutic-grade essential oils and, more importantly, create highly diluted and carefully selected blends suitable for mothers in postpartum, with specific considerations for breastfeeding and proximity to infants. We will discuss all considerations during your consultation.
What kind of Aromatherapy practices will I learn for nervous system regulation?
You will learn practical and simple rituals that fit seamlessly into your day. This might include creating a calming room spray for your sanctuary, using pulse point rollers for immediate relief, or incorporating aromatic baths to unwind. The goal is to empower you with accessible tools to consciously regulate your nervous system on demand.
How quickly can I expect to feel a difference with postpartum Aromatherapy?
While individual experiences vary, many new mothers report an immediate sense of calm and a subtle shift in their emotional state upon incorporating intentional Aromatherapy. Consistent practice amplifies these effects, helping you build resilience and a more stable nervous system over time. Think of it as nurturing a garden; consistent care yields beautiful results.
