FAQ — safety & common questions

      

        
How do you calculate dilution?

        
We use percent-based dilution (volume percent). The calculator above converts that into approximate drops for 10 ml and 30 ml bottles using a standard drop estimate. These are general aromatherapy guidelines and not medical directions.


        
Is a patch test necessary?

        
Yes. Apply a small diluted drop to an inner forearm, cover for 24 hours, and watch for redness, itching, or irritation. Stop use if you notice a reaction. Patch testing is a simple precaution.


        
What about pregnancy and nursing?

        
Some essential oils can be more active during pregnancy. If you are pregnant, breastfeeding, or have underlying conditions, please consult a qualified healthcare provider before topical or concentrated use.


        
Are essential oils safe around pets?

        
Many pets, especially cats and some small animals, are sensitive to essential oils. Keep oils out of reach, avoid diffusing in enclosed spaces without ventilation, and consult a veterinarian if you have concerns.
