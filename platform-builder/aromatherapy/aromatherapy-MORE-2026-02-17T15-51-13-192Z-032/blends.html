Notes on safety

General adult topical dilutions often range from about 1–3%. Pregnant people, young children, and certain health contexts usually use lower dilutions (for example 0.25–1%). Diffuser or room use avoids topical dilution but still requires careful choice of oils. This tool gives general guidance — we review specifics during consultations.
