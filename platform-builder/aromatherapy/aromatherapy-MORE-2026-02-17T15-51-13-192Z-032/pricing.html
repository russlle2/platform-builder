FAQ — safety first

What does dilution mean here?

Dilution is the percentage of essential oil in a carrier (like jojoba or fractionated coconut). It is presented as a guide. For 10 mL, 1% is roughly 2 drops; 2% ~4 drops; 3% ~6 drops. For 30 mL, 1% ~6 drops; 2% ~12 drops; 3% ~18 drops. These are approximations based on an average of ~20 drops per mL.

Do I need a patch test?

Yes — test any new topical blend on a small area of skin and wait 24 hours. Discontinue if irritation occurs. Avoid applying undiluted essential oils directly to skin.

Any considerations for pets or pregnancy?

Many oils can affect pets or are not recommended during pregnancy. If pregnant, nursing, or keeping pets, consult your care provider or a veterinarian before using essential oils. Our guidance is precautionary and not medical advice.
