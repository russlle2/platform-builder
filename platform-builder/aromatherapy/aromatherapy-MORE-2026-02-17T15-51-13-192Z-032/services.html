Practical notes

Sessions emphasize safety. We include dilution guidance, patch testing, and household considerations, especially around pets and pregnancy.

FAQ — Safety & preparation

Dilution: Topical blends typically use low dilutions. Common safe adult ranges are 1–3% for general use; lower rates (0.25–1%) are advised for pregnant people and young children. We include clear drop-based recipes in every plan.

Patch test: Always patch test a new blend on a small area of skin for 24 hours. Discontinue if irritation occurs.

Pets: Some oils can be harmful to pets. We will flag pet-sensitive scenarios and suggest room-only or pet-safe options.

Not medical: Our work is supportive and non-medical. We do not diagnose, treat, or claim cures. Scent work may support wellbeing for some people.
