Safety & FAQs

      
Are these medical treatments?

      
No — information and sessions are educational and may support wellbeing. We do not diagnose or treat medical conditions.

      
What about dilution and patch testing?

      
All blend cards show safe dilution ranges and recommend a patch test: apply a small diluted amount to an inner forearm and wait 24 hours. Discontinue if irritation occurs.

      
Pets, pregnancy, and special situations

      
Essential oils can affect pets and people differently. Use caution around cats, dogs, young children, and during pregnancy. Consult a qualified provider for special conditions before use.
