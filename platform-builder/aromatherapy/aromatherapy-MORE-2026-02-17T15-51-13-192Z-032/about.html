FAQ

Will this replace medical or veterinary care?

No. Our guidance is informational; it may support wellbeing but is not a substitute for professional medical or veterinary advice.

How to do a patch test

Dilute the recommended recipe, apply a small amount to an inner forearm, and observe for 24 hours. Stop if any irritation occurs.

Pregnancy, nursing, and pets

Certain oils are not advised during pregnancy or around pets. Please consult a relevant professional if you fall into these categories. Our suggested recipes come with notes about these considerations.
