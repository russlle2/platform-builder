What exactly is Aromatherapy and how can it benefit me?

Aromatherapy is a holistic healing treatment that uses natural plant extracts to promote health and well-being. It can benefit you by improving mood, reducing stress, alleviating pain, and supporting various physical and emotional needs through the therapeutic properties of essential oils.

Are the essential oils you use safe for everyone, including children and pets?

Your safety is our priority. We use high-quality, pure essential oils and always provide guidance on appropriate dilution and application methods. For children, pets, or individuals with specific health conditions, we conduct a thorough consultation to ensure that any suggested aromatherapy practices are safe and beneficial for your unique circumstances.

How long does an Aromatherapy consultation typically last and what should I expect?

A typical initial aromatherapy consultation lasts around 60-90 minutes. During this time, we will discuss your health history, lifestyle, and wellness goals. You can expect a personalized recommendation for essential oils and blends, along with practical advice on how to integrate them into your daily routine.

Can Aromatherapy replace my current medical treatments?

Aromatherapy is intended to complement, not replace, conventional medical treatments. We encourage you to continue consulting with your healthcare provider for any medical conditions. Our role is to support your overall well-being and work collaboratively with your existing health regimen.
