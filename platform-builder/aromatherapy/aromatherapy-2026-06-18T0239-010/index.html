Frequently Asked Questions

How do you personalize an Aromatherapy session for me?
We begin with a detailed wellness conversation to understand your physical sensations, emotional landscape, and daily routines. Your Aromatherapy experience reflects these insights—not just your 'favorite' scent but the botanical properties that address your specific needs.
What happens during my first Aromatherapy session?
You'll share your wellness goals and preferences in a comfortable consultation. We'll explore your current sensory habits and identify subtle stress patterns. Then, you'll experience a tailored scent application with guidance on integrating it into your daily rhythm for maximum benefit.
How long do the effects of a custom Aromatherapy blend last?
Benefits are immediate with each use, but the true value comes from consistent ritual. Most clients experience sustained emotional balance within 2-3 weeks of regular application. We provide usage guidance and follow-up to ensure your blend continues to support your evolving needs.
Why are your blends more effective than store-bought products?
Store products prioritize scent longevity over therapeutic purpose. Our blends balance botanical efficacy with your personal resonance. We never compromise on ingredient purity, and every formula is calibrated for your unique physiological response—not just for the 'average' user.
