Frequently Asked Questions

How can Aromatherapy specifically support my emotional well-being during postpartum recovery?
Aromatherapy can gently support your emotional well-being by influencing your limbic system, which is tied to emotions and memory. Specific essential oils can help mitigate feelings of anxiety, uplift your mood, aid in restful sleep, and create a sense of calm and grounding, all crucial for your emotional recovery after childbirth.
Is Aromatherapy safe to use while I am breastfeeding or recovering from childbirth?
Yes, with careful and informed guidance, Aromatherapy can be safely integrated into your postpartum routine. We prioritize your safety and will select only the most gentle, appropriate essential oils, diluted properly, and applied in ways that respect your body's delicate state and your baby's proximity, especially if you are breastfeeding.
How does your trauma-informed approach to Aromatherapy benefit new mothers?
Our trauma-informed approach recognizes that childbirth and early motherhood can be emotionally complex. We create a compassionate and non-judgmental space, ensuring you feel heard and respected. Our Aromatherapy practices are designed to be empowering, helping you to gently process emotions and foster a sense of safety and agency in your healing, rather than re-traumatizing or overwhelming you.
What kind of Aromatherapy practices would be personalized for my unique postpartum experience?
We begin with a thorough conversation to understand your specific emotional landscape, your birth experience, and your current needs. Based on this, we'll craft personalized essential oil blends for diffusion, topical application, or aromatic baths, along with mindful ritual suggestions, all tailored to support your individual emotional healing and recovery during this tender time.
