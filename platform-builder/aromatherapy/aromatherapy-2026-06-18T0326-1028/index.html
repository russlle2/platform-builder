Frequently Asked Questions

How does Aromatherapy specifically help with stress reduction for seniors?
Aromatherapy utilizes the olfactory system to influence the limbic system, which governs emotions and stress responses. For seniors, gentle aromas like lavender, frankincense, or chamomile can signal to your nervous system to downshift, reducing the sensation of overwhelm and fostering a state of relaxation. We focus on mild, supportive essences to avoid overstimulation, ensuring a comforting experience tailored to your sensitivity.
Is Aromatherapy safe for someone taking medications or with existing health conditions?
Your safety and well-being are paramount. Before any aromatherapy session or blend creation, we conduct a thorough consultation to understand your health history, current medications, and any sensitivities. This allows us to select essential oils that are gentle, non-reactive, and supportive, always ensuring your comfort and avoiding any potential contraindications. We prioritize your health and tailor every aspect of our care to your individual needs.
What can I expect during my first Aromatherapy session?
Your first session will begin with a warm conversation where we listen to your experiences, your rhythms, and your aspirations for calm. We'll explore scents together, guiding you to discover aromas that resonate with your sense of peace. This gentle exploration leads to a personalized approach, whether it's through a diffused essence, a gentle topical application, or a custom blend designed just for you to use at home. Our aim is to create an atmosphere of quiet trust and understanding.
How often should I engage with Aromatherapy to maintain nervous system regulation?
The rhythm of aromatherapy for nervous system regulation is deeply personal. Some find weekly sessions a comforting anchor, while others prefer monthly engagements complemented by daily use of custom blends at home. We will work with you to establish a gentle, consistent approach that fits seamlessly into your life, ensuring the calming effects become a sustained part of your well-being, fostering a continuous sense of inner quiet.
