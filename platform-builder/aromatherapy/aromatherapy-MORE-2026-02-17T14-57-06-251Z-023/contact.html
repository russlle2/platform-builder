FAQ — safety & common questions

Do these blends treat ailments?
No. Scents and essential oils are not a substitute for medical care. Our suggestions may support certain sensations or moods, but they are not medical advice.
How should I dilute?
We recommend dilute-for-purpose ranges and a patch test for skin sensitivity. Typical starting dilution for adults is 1–3% for topical roll-ons. For sensitive skin, use less. Consult guidance for children and older adults.
What about pets and pregnancy?
Some oils are not recommended around pets, and pregnancy/ breastfeeding calls for specific guidance. Please share if you are pregnant, nursing, or have pets so we can make safer suggestions.
Do I need a patch test?
Yes. Try a diluted patch test on forearm skin and wait 24 hours. Discontinue if irritation occurs.
