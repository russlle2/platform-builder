Frequently asked safety questions

Do these scents replace medical care?

They do not. Aromatherapy may support well-being but is not a substitute for professional medical advice or treatment.

How do you recommend diluting oils?

We offer conservative dilution guidance (typically 1-2% for blends intended for topical use). Patch testing is advised before wider use.

What about pets and pregnancy?

Some essential oils can affect pets or be inadvisable during pregnancy. We always provide alternative options; consult your healthcare provider when in doubt.
