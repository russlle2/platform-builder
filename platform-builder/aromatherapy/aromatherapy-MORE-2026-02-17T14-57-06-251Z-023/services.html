FAQ & safety notes
Do I need to dilute oils?
Yes. We include recommended dilutions with every custom or retail blend and suggest a patch test before skin use.
Are aromatics safe around pets?
Some essential oils may not be suitable for animals. We provide pet-safe guidance on request and include alternatives when needed.
Pregnancy or health conditions?
Consult a qualified health provider for medical advice. Our notes avoid medical claims; aromatherapy may support comfort but is not a substitute for medical care.
Patch test?
Apply a small diluted amount to the forearm and wait 24 hours. Stop use if irritation occurs.
