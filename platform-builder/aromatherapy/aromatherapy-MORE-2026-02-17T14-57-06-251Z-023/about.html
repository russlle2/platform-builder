FAQ

Are these medical treatments?
No. Aromatherapy may support everyday comfort and mood for some people. It is not medical advice.
How do you ensure safety?
Dilution guidelines and a patch test are always provided. If you have specific health concerns, consult a knowledgeable provider.
Pets and oils?
Some essential oils can be harmful to pets. Keep oils away from drinking water and ask about pet-safe alternatives during sessions.
