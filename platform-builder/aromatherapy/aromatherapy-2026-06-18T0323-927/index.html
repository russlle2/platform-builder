Frequently Asked Questions

How can Aromatherapy specifically help me during my postpartum recovery?
Aromatherapy can offer gentle support for common postpartum experiences like sleep disruption, emotional shifts, and fatigue. We create personalized blends to help soothe your nervous system, encourage relaxation, and uplift your mood, all tailored to your unique recovery phase and seasonal needs.
Is Aromatherapy safe for me if I'm breastfeeding my baby?
Yes, when properly chosen and diluted, Aromatherapy can be safe while breastfeeding. is trained to select essential oils that are appropriate for nursing mothers and will guide you on safe application methods to ensure the well-being of both you and your baby.
Can Aromatherapy help with the emotional ups and downs often experienced after birth?
Absolutely. Essential oils have profound effects on our emotions. We'll work together to identify scents that can help balance your mood, reduce feelings of overwhelm, and promote a sense of calm and well-being during this tender time. Think of it as gentle emotional support through scent.
How does consider seasonal wellness in its Aromatherapy approach for new mothers?
We recognize that the demands on new mothers can feel different throughout the year. For instance, in winter, we might focus on uplifting blends to combat seasonal blues, while in summer, we might prioritize cooling and refreshing scents. Your personalized Aromatherapy adjusts with the seasons to best support your evolving needs.
