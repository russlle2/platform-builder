What exactly is Aromatherapy?

Aromatherapy is a holistic healing treatment that uses natural plant extracts to promote health and well-being. Sometimes called essential oil therapy, Aromatherapy uses aromatic essential oils medicinally to improve the health of the body, mind, and spirit. It enhances both physical and emotional health.

How can Aromatherapy benefit my well-being?

Aromatherapy can offer a wide range of benefits, including stress reduction, improved sleep, enhanced mood, pain relief, and immune support. Your personalized blend will be formulated to address your specific concerns, helping you achieve greater balance and vitality in your daily life.

Are the essential oils used in Aromatherapy safe?

Yes, when used correctly, essential oils are generally safe. At Aromatherapy Studio, we prioritize your safety. Dr. Morgan Ellis uses only high-quality, pure essential oils and provides detailed guidance on proper dilution and application methods to ensure your experience is both effective and safe.

What should I expect during an Aromatherapy consultation?

During your consultation, Dr. Morgan Ellis will engage in a thorough discussion about your health history, current wellness goals, and lifestyle. This allows us to understand your unique needs. Based on this information, we will then develop and prepare a custom essential oil blend tailored specifically for you, along with clear usage instructions.
