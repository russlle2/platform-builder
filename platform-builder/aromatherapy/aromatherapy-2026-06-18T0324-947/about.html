How can Aromatherapy specifically help me during postpartum recovery?

Aromatherapy provides gentle, natural support for many common postpartum experiences. We focus on blends that can aid in relaxation for better sleep, uplift your mood during hormonal shifts, soothe discomfort, and create a calming atmosphere for both you and your baby. Our approach is tailored to your unique recovery needs, honoring the seasonal changes within and around you.

Is Aromatherapy safe to use when I'm breastfeeding or around my baby?

Yes, when used correctly, Aromatherapy can be very safe. We prioritize your and your baby's well-being by using only pure, high-quality essential oils and carefully formulating blends at appropriate dilutions. We will guide you on safe application methods, such as diffusion or topical use with carriers, ensuring your comfort and peace of mind as you nurture your little one.

What does 'seasonal wellness' in Aromatherapy mean for a new mother?

'Seasonal wellness' for new mothers means aligning your self-care with the natural rhythms of the year. For example, during cooler months, we might focus on warming and comforting blends to support feelings of coziness and inner strength. In warmer seasons, refreshing and clarifying oils can help invigorate your spirit. This approach helps you feel more connected to nature and your own innate wisdom as you recover.

How do I get started with a personalized Aromatherapy plan for my postpartum period?

Starting your personalized Aromatherapy plan is simple. You can book an initial consultation with Dr. Morgan Ellis. During this session, we'll discuss your current needs, your recovery phase, and your lifestyle. From there, we'll co-create a bespoke Aromatherapy ritual and suggest specific blends to support your unique postpartum journey, helping you feel more grounded and cared for.
