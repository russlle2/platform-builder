How does Aromatherapy specifically address postpartum posture concerns?

Our Aromatherapy blends are formulated with essential oils known to support muscle relaxation, reduce inflammation, and calm the nervous system, all of which contribute to easing tension that can affect your posture. We focus on areas commonly strained postpartum, helping your body gently realign and find a more comfortable, upright stance.

Can Aromatherapy truly help with my body awareness after childbirth?

Yes, absolutely. Aromatic cues can create powerful anchors for mindful attention. By associating specific scents with gentle movements and breathwork, you can enhance your ability to sense your body's position, engage your core muscles, and re-establish a deeper, more intuitive connection to your physical self during your recovery.

Are the Aromatherapy blends safe for me as a breastfeeding mother?

Your safety and the well-being of your baby are our utmost priority. All Aromatherapy blends are meticulously crafted with your unique postpartum needs in mind, including breastfeeding. We use only the purest, therapeutic-grade essential oils and provide clear guidance on safe application methods and dilution ratios to ensure comfort and efficacy.

What kind of Aromatherapy support can I expect for my physical recovery?

You can expect personalized Aromatherapy support tailored to your specific physical recovery needs. This includes custom topical blends for gentle massage to ease muscle soreness, aromatic inhalations to promote relaxation and deeper breathing for core engagement, and guidance on integrating these scents into your daily routine to support ongoing postural health and body awareness.
