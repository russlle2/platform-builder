Frequently Asked Questions

How does aromatherapy specifically address postpartum posture concerns?
Our aromatherapy blends are formulated with essential oils known for their muscle-relaxing and anti-inflammatory properties. When applied topically or diffused, they can help reduce tension in muscles often strained by new mothering demands, such as those in the neck, shoulders, and back, thereby supporting improved postural alignment and comfort as you care for your baby.
Can aromatherapy truly help me reconnect with my body after childbirth?
Yes. The olfactory system has a direct link to the limbic system, which is involved in emotion and memory. By using specific aromas, we can create sensory cues that help you focus inward, becoming more attuned to your body's sensations and needs. This guided approach helps re-establish a conscious connection to your physical self during postpartum recovery.
Are the aromatherapy products safe to use while breastfeeding?
Absolutely. Safety is our utmost priority. All our aromatherapy blends are carefully formulated with new mothers and breastfeeding in mind. We use only high-quality, pure essential oils at appropriate dilutions, and we will discuss any specific concerns or sensitivities you may have during your personalized consultation to ensure your comfort and well-being.
What kind of results can I expect from postpartum aromatherapy for my posture?
You can expect to experience reduced muscle tension, increased comfort in daily movements, and a greater awareness of your body's positioning. While aromatherapy supports your physical recovery, it's not a substitute for physical therapy. It works synergistically to enhance your overall sense of physical ease and mental clarity as you navigate motherhood.
