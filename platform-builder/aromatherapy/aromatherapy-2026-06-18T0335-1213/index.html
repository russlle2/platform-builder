Frequently Asked Questions

How does Aromatherapy specifically support emotional well-being for seniors?
Aromatherapy, when thoughtfully applied, can gently influence mood and memory. For seniors, specific aromas can evoke feelings of calm, reduce agitation, or even provide a sense of connection to comforting memories, all without intrusive methods. Our focus is on fostering emotional safety and easing anxieties.
Is Aromatherapy safe if I have existing health conditions or take medications?
Your safety is paramount. We conduct a thorough consultation to understand your health history, current medications, and any sensitivities. This allows us to select and dilute essential oils appropriately, ensuring that your Aromatherapy experience is both effective and completely safe for your unique circumstances.
What does a trauma-informed Aromatherapy session entail?
A trauma-informed session begins with creating an environment of deep respect and choice. We move at your pace, offering gentle guidance rather than direction. The focus is on subtle aromatic suggestions and creating a sense of internal resourcefulness, allowing you to experience emotional solace without any feeling of pressure or re-enactment.
How can Aromatherapy help with feelings of isolation or lingering sadness?
While not a substitute for professional mental health care, Aromatherapy can offer a tangible, comforting presence. Certain aromas can provide a gentle lift to the spirit, promote relaxation, and even foster a sense of connection to the natural world. Our blends are designed to be a soft companion, offering a moment of quiet solace.
