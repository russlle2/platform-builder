Practical safety & FAQ

Dilution: Typical topical dilution for adults is 1–3% for blends (6–18 drops per 30ml carrier). For children, elderly, and sensitive skin use lower dilutions. These are educational suggestions, not medical instructions.

Patch test: Apply a small diluted amount to inner forearm and wait 24 hours. If irritation occurs, discontinue use.

Pets: Many essential oils can affect pets — avoid direct application to animals and strongly ventilate diffused spaces; never leave diffusers running in small pet enclosures. Birds and reptiles are particularly sensitive.

Pregnancy & medication: Some oils are not advised during pregnancy or with certain medications. If pregnant, nursing, or taking medication, consult a qualified healthcare provider before topical or internal use.
