Safety & care — brief FAQ

Dilution: All topical products are prepared at safe dilutions for adults. If you are pregnant, nursing, or have a health condition, check with a qualified clinician before use.

Patch test: Carry out a patch test for any topical product: apply a small amount to inner forearm and wait 24 hours for reaction.

Pets: Some oils are not safe for animals. Avoid direct application to animals and ensure diffusers are in well-ventilated areas; do not leave diffusers running continuously around birds or small pets.

Pregnancy & medication: Certain oils may be contraindicated during pregnancy or with specific medications. These products are educational and aromatic — they do not replace medical advice.
