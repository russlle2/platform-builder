Events series — how it works

Structure: Each series runs 4–6 sessions, paced weekly. Sessions combine education, hands-on blending, and a shared diffuser ritual. Safety reminders and dilution guidance are provided at each meeting.
Capacity: Small groups keep the experience intimate; attendance is limited for clarity and comfort. Masks are optional; we ventilate and limit direct inhalation samples.

Notes on safety

Dilution guidelines are provided for every blend; we recommend patch testing and avoiding undiluted application. If you are pregnant, nursing, or caring for pets, please mention this when booking — we adapt blends and delivery methods accordingly. This practice does not provide medical advice.
