Why scent? A gentle introduction

Essential oils are concentrated plant expressions used historically for ritual, care, and home comfort. Our offerings present aromatherapy as a sensory companion — not a cure. We prioritize dilution, patch testing, and clear guidance so practices feel safe and sustained.

Our approach

Minimalist blends, transparent sourcing, and group formats that center education. Each session includes demonstration, hands-on blending, and take-home guidance so you leave with both scent and confidence.

Practice & safety

Practical notes we share in every event — short, clear, and precautionary.

Dilution

We recommend conservative dilution rates for topical use (often 1–3% depending on age and sensitivity). Workshop materials include exact recipes and measuring guidance.

Patch testing

Perform a small patch test before topical use — apply diluted blend to forearm and wait 24 hours for any irritation. If redness or discomfort occurs, discontinue use.

Pets

Some essential oils are not safe for animals. We review pet-friendly practices and alternatives, and recommend avoiding direct topical or diffused exposure around sensitive animals.

Pregnancy & nursing

We take a conservative stance: certain oils are avoided during pregnancy and breastfeeding. Please consult with a healthcare provider before using new products; classes include adaptations and non-aromatic options.
