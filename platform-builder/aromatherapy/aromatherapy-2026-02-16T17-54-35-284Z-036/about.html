A short story

I began experimenting with botanical distillates and cold-pressed essences while curious about ritual and habit. Over time, what mattered most was safety: clear instructions, evidence-informed precautions, and honoring sensitivities. {{BUSINESS_NAME}} grew from that care.

Foundations — Study of aromatics, safe dilution, and plant chemistry basics.
Practice — Shared classes and small-group series, refined for accessibility and safety.

Framework & values

  • Clarity: no medical claims; scent is invited, not prescribed.
  • Accessibility: small groups and written takeaways.

Common guidance

Always perform a patch test before topical use. For pregnancy, nursing, or cardiac/respiratory concerns, mention this when you book so I can suggest adapted blends or diffusion-only approaches. Keep products away from curious pets — some essential oils are not pet-safe.
