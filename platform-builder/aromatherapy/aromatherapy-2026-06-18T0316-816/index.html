Frequently Asked Questions

How can Aromatherapy specifically help my posture after chronic illness or injury?
Aromatherapy can support your posture by influencing your nervous system to reduce tension that may pull your body out of alignment. Certain essences can also heighten your proprioception, which is your body's sense of its position in space, helping you become more aware of your postural habits and make gentle adjustments as you recover.
Will Aromatherapy directly address my pain related to poor posture?
While aromatherapy doesn't directly 'fix' structural issues, it can profoundly assist in managing the discomfort and tension often associated with poor posture, especially during recovery. By promoting relaxation and reducing inflammation, it creates a more conducive internal environment for your body to naturally find its optimal alignment and ease, lessening the sensation of pain.
Is Aromatherapy safe to use alongside my current recovery treatments for postural health?
Absolutely. Aromatherapy is a complementary practice designed to work in harmony with your existing recovery plan, including physical therapy or chiropractic care. We always encourage you to discuss any new modalities with your primary healthcare provider. Our blends are crafted with your specific health needs in mind, ensuring a gentle and supportive addition to your regimen.
What does a typical Aromatherapy session involve for someone focusing on body awareness?
A session for body awareness typically begins with a thoughtful conversation about your current physical sensations and postural goals. We then guide you through experiencing specific aromatic essences, noting your body's subtle responses. This might involve gentle topical application with a carrier oil or inhalation, all designed to deepen your connection to your body and its innate wisdom for balance and comfort.
