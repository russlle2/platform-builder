Frequently Asked Questions

How does Aromatherapy specifically aid athletic recovery and sleep?
Aromatherapy targets your body's limbic system through scent, influencing hormone regulation, reducing cortisol (stress hormone), and promoting neurotransmitters like GABA and serotonin. This biological shift helps you fall asleep faster, achieve deeper REM and slow-wave sleep, and therefore accelerate physical repair and mental rejuvenation after intense training.
Are the Aromatherapy blends safe for all athletes, including those with sensitivities?
Your safety is paramount. All our Aromatherapy blends are formulated with pure, therapeutic-grade essential oils. We conduct a thorough intake to understand your individual sensitivities, health conditions, and specific training demands, ensuring your custom blend is both effective and non-irritating for your unique physiology.
What kind of results can I expect for my sleep and physical stress from Aromatherapy?
You can anticipate a noticeable improvement in sleep onset, sleep duration, and sleep quality. Many clients report waking feeling more refreshed, experiencing reduced muscle soreness, and a greater sense of mental calm, which collectively contributes to enhanced daily performance and better stress management.
How do I integrate Aromatherapy into my existing recovery routine?
We design your Aromatherapy protocol to seamlessly fit into your current routine. This might involve pre-sleep diffusion, targeted topical application to tired muscles, or inhalation techniques during cool-downs. We provide clear, simple instructions for optimal use, making it an effortless yet powerful addition to your recovery strategy.
