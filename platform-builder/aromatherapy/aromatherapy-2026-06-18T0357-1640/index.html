Frequently Asked Questions

How can Aromatherapy specifically aid my postpartum immune system?
Our Aromatherapy focuses on specific essential oils known for their immune-modulating and adaptogenic properties. We select essences like frankincense, tea tree, and lemon to gently support your body's natural defenses, helping to ward off common imbalances during this delicate recovery phase, without overwhelming your system.
Will Aromatherapy help me regain my energy and vitality after childbirth?
Absolutely. We craft custom Aromatherapy blends designed to address the profound fatigue often experienced postpartum. Uplifting and restorative oils such as bergamot, grapefruit, and peppermint are incorporated to gently stimulate your energy, clear mental fog, and help you feel more invigorated and present for yourself and your baby.
Are the Aromatherapy blends safe for me and my baby during breastfeeding?
Safety is our paramount concern, especially for new mothers and their infants. We utilize only the purest, therapeutic-grade essential oils and always dilute them appropriately. During your consultation, we discuss your breastfeeding status and any sensitivities to ensure all Aromatherapy recommendations are gentle, effective, and safe for both you and your little one.
How do I integrate Aromatherapy into my busy postpartum routine for maximum benefit?
We understand that time is precious. Our Aromatherapy approaches are designed for ease of integration into your daily life. This could include simple rituals like a morning diffusion, a gentle roll-on pulse point application, or a calming evening bath blend. We'll guide you in creating manageable practices that deeply support your immune health and vitality without adding stress.
