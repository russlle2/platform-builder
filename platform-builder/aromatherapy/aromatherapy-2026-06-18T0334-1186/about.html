Re-Weaving Your Sense of Self with Trauma-Informed Aromatherapy

Chronic illness and injury can leave behind emotional imprints long after physical recovery begins. Our approach to Aromatherapy acknowledges these tender spaces, offering custom aromatic compositions that support your nervous system. We work with you to create a sanctuary of scent, helping to gently re-establish feelings of safety and stability when your world has felt shaken.

How does Aromatherapy specifically address the emotional impact of chronic illness or injury?

Our trauma-informed Aromatherapy focuses on selecting essential oils known for their capacity to support emotional regulation, soothe the nervous system, and gently address feelings of anxiety, grief, or frustration that often accompany long-term health challenges. We create personalized blends that acknowledge your specific experiences and needs.

Will Aromatherapy interfere with my current medications or recovery protocols?

Your safety is paramount. We conduct a thorough intake to understand your medical history, current treatments, and any sensitivities. We carefully select essential oils and dilution rates to ensure compatibility and will always advise you to consult with your primary healthcare provider regarding any new complementary therapies.

How do you ensure a trauma-informed approach in your Aromatherapy practice?

Our practice prioritizes creating a safe, respectful, and empowering space for you. We focus on choice, collaboration, and building trust. We will never push you to engage with scents that feel uncomfortable, and we tailor our suggestions to honor your individual boundaries and emotional responses, gently guiding you toward comfort and ease.

What kind of results can I expect from using Aromatherapy for emotional healing during my recovery?

While individual experiences vary, many clients report a greater sense of calm, improved sleep quality, reduced feelings of overwhelm, and a gentle reconnecting with their inner resilience. Our aim is to provide you with tools and support to navigate the emotional landscape of your recovery with greater ease and self-compassion.
