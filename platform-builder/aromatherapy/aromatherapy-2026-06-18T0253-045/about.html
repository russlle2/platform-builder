What can I expect during my first Aromatherapy consultation?

During your initial consultation, we will engage in a comprehensive discussion about your health history, lifestyle, and specific wellness goals. This allows us to gain a full understanding of your needs before we begin crafting a personalized Aromatherapy plan tailored just for you.

How long does it take to see results from Aromatherapy?

The time it takes to experience the benefits of Aromatherapy can vary depending on your individual circumstances and the nature of your concerns. Many clients report feeling positive shifts within a few days or weeks of consistent use, while deeper, more chronic issues may require a longer period of support. We encourage patience and consistency for optimal results.

Is Aromatherapy safe for everyone?

While Aromatherapy is generally safe, it's crucial to use essential oils correctly and under professional guidance, especially if you are pregnant, nursing, have a medical condition, or are taking medication. We prioritize your safety and will always ensure that your personalized blends and recommendations are appropriate for your unique health profile.

Can I use my existing essential oils with your Aromatherapy guidance?

We welcome discussing any essential oils you currently own during your consultation. We can provide guidance on their safe and effective use, and help you integrate them into your personalized Aromatherapy plan if they align with your wellness goals. Our aim is to empower you with knowledge and confidence in your aromatic journey.
