Safety & common questions

Are these therapeutic or medical treatments?
Our work uses aromatic botanicals and is not a substitute for medical treatment. Aromatherapy may support well-being for some individuals; consult healthcare for medical conditions.
How do you handle dilution and patch tests?
All custom blends include clear dilution instructions. We recommend a patch test when using a new blend: apply a small diluted amount to a forearm and observe for 24 hours.
What about pets and pregnancy?
Some oils can affect pets or are not recommended during pregnancy. We include alternative suggestions and never recommend use that could risk safety. Please share if you are pregnant or have pets.
