FAQ — safety & notes
Patch test: Always do a small patch test. Apply a diluted amount to the inner forearm and wait 24 hours.
Dilution guidance: Blends are crafted for topical use at recommended dilutions; follow the label. For face or sensitive skin, reduce dilution further.
Pets: Some essential oils may be irritating to pets; keep blends and diffusers out of reach and consult your veterinarian for specific concerns.
Pregnancy & nursing: Certain oils are not recommended during pregnancy or breastfeeding. Please disclose pregnancy when booking; we will recommend safe alternatives.
