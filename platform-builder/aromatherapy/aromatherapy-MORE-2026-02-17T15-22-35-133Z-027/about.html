FAQ — Safety & Use

How should I dilute essential oils?
We provide concentration suggestions for topical, room-spray, and diffuser use. A common guideline is 1–3% for adults in topical blends; less for sensitive skin. Always perform a patch test before regular topical use.
Do you offer pet-safe recommendations?
Yes. Certain oils are not recommended around pets; our product pages and consults include explicit pet-safety notes and ventilation suggestions. If your pet shows sensitivity, discontinue use and consult a veterinarian.
What about pregnancy, nursing, or medical conditions?
We avoid recommending particular oils during pregnancy or nursing in a generic way; please reach out before use so we can provide a personalized plan. Our guidance is non-medical—consult your healthcare provider for medical advice.
How do I do a patch test?
Dilute the blend to the recommended concentration and apply a small amount to the inner forearm. Cover and observe for 24–48 hours for redness, itching, or irritation. If reactions occur, discontinue and contact us for a tailored solution.
