Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is Aromatherapy and how does it work?
Aromatherapy is a holistic healing modality that utilizes natural plant extracts, known as essential oils, to promote health and well-being. These oils are typically inhaled or applied topically, stimulating your olfactory system and absorbing into your bloodstream to exert various therapeutic effects on your body and mind.
Is Aromatherapy safe for everyone, including children and pregnant individuals?
While Aromatherapy is generally safe, certain essential oils may not be suitable for everyone. will conduct a thorough consultation to understand your health history and any specific considerations, such as pregnancy, nursing, or underlying medical conditions, ensuring your personalized blends and recommendations are entirely safe for you.
How can Aromatherapy specifically help with stress and anxiety?
Many essential oils possess anxiolytic and calming properties. Through guided Aromatherapy, you can learn to incorporate specific scents like lavender, frankincense, or bergamot into your routine to help quiet your mind, reduce feelings of overwhelm, and promote a sense of tranquility and emotional balance, helping you navigate daily stressors more effectively.
What should I expect during my first Aromatherapy consultation?
During your initial consultation, you'll have a detailed discussion with about your health goals, lifestyle, and preferences. You'll explore various essential oils and their properties, and together, you'll develop a personalized Aromatherapy plan tailored to your unique needs, which may include custom blend formulations and usage recommendations.
