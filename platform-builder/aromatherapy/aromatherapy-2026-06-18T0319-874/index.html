Frequently Asked Questions

How can Aromatherapy specifically help my postural health as an athlete?
Aromatherapy can enhance your body awareness by creating subtle scent associations with good posture. Certain aromas can cue your nervous system to relax tension, which in turn allows for better alignment and reduces the unconscious bracing that can hinder your flexibility and stability.
I'm always active; how does Aromatherapy fit into managing my physical stress?
Our Aromatherapy blends are designed to help your body and mind down-regulate after exertion. By introducing specific calming or uplifting scents, we help shift your physiological state, promoting relaxation and aiding recovery, which can reduce overall physical stress and support your body's natural healing processes.
Do I need any prior experience with essential oils or wellness practices to benefit from Aromatherapy for body awareness?
Absolutely not! We meet you where you are. Our approach is gentle and accessible, designed for anyone curious about supporting their body. We'll guide you through everything, ensuring you feel comfortable and understand how to integrate these aromatic tools into your active life.
What kind of Aromatherapy blends can I expect for improving body awareness and reducing stress from activity?
We create custom blends tailored to your specific needs. These often include essential oils known for their muscle-relaxing, anti-inflammatory, or grounding properties, such as marjoram, frankincense, or vetiver. The goal is to support your physical comfort and mental clarity, helping you tune into your body's signals more effectively.
