What exactly is Aromatherapy and how can it benefit me?

Aromatherapy is a holistic healing treatment that uses natural plant extracts to promote health and well-being. It leverages the aromatic compounds of essential oils to improve physical and psychological health. For you, this can translate into reduced stress, better sleep, enhanced mood, pain relief, and even support for respiratory issues, all tailored to your specific needs.
