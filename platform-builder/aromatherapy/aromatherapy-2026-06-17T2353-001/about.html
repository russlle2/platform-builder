Personalized Aromatherapy Consultations

Forget one-size-fits-all scent recommendations. At Harmony Aromatherapy, we begin with a detailed wellness assessment—exploring your physical needs, emotional landscape, and lifestyle. Our certified aromatherapist creates custom protocols using only therapeutic-grade essential oils, avoiding fillers and synthetic fragrances. Each session evolves with your progress, ensuring your aromatherapy practice grows alongside you.

How is your aromatherapy approach different from general wellness centers?

We integrate clinical assessment with scent science rather than offering generic scent experiences. Every blend and recommendation is documented in your personalized wellness journal, tracking how each protocol affects your specific patterns over time. This creates a measurable path forward, not just a momentary sensory experience.

How long do initial aromatherapy consultations take?

Your first appointment is 75 minutes, including a thorough wellness assessment and blend formulation. Follow-up sessions are 50 minutes focused on adjusting protocols. We never rush the process—your scent journey deserves time to develop.

Can aromatherapy work with my current medications?

Absolutely. We screen all medications during your consultation and collaborate with your healthcare team when needed. Certain oils interact with medications (like blood thinners with clove oil), but we proactively avoid these and provide documentation for your physician.

What if I don't like the scent of essential oils?

Scent preference is deeply personal. We begin with your most comfortable aromas and gradually introduce supportive botanicals. If you dislike a scent, we'll adjust immediately. Your comfort is our priority—aromatherapy shouldn't feel overwhelming.
