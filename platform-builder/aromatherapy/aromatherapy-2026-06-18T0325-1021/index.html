Frequently Asked Questions

How does Aromatherapy specifically help with recovery from chronic illness or injury?
Aromatherapy can directly influence your limbic system, the part of your brain involved in emotion and memory, as well as your autonomic nervous system. For those recovering, specific aromatic compounds can help downregulate stress hormones, encourage deeper breathing, and promote a sense of safety and calm, which are crucial for cellular repair and reduced pain perception.
Will Aromatherapy interfere with my current medical treatments or medications?
Our approach to Aromatherapy is always complementary. We encourage you to discuss any new therapies, including Aromatherapy, with your primary care provider. takes a detailed intake to ensure that any blends or practices recommended are safe and supportive of your current health regimen, avoiding contraindications with medications or existing conditions.
What kind of results can I expect from incorporating Aromatherapy into my recovery plan?
Clients often report a noticeable reduction in perceived stress, improved sleep quality, and a greater sense of emotional resilience. While Aromatherapy doesn't replace medical treatment, it provides a powerful, non-invasive tool to help manage symptoms like anxiety, fatigue, and discomfort, creating a more supportive internal environment for your overall recovery.
How do you ensure the quality and safety of the essential oils used in Aromatherapy?
We source our essential oils from reputable suppliers who provide comprehensive GC/MS (Gas Chromatography/Mass Spectrometry) reports, ensuring their purity and authenticity. Our blends are formulated by , who is trained in safe and effective dilution ratios, considering your specific health status and sensitivities to prevent adverse reactions.
