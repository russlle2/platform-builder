Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is Aromatherapy, and how can it benefit me?
Aromatherapy is a holistic healing treatment that uses natural plant extracts to promote health and well-being. It can benefit you by enhancing your mood, improving sleep quality, reducing stress, alleviating minor aches, and supporting overall emotional balance through the therapeutic properties of essential oils.
Are the essential oils you use safe for everyone?
Your safety is paramount. We use only high-quality, pure essential oils and provide thorough guidance on their proper use. During your consultation, we'll discuss your health history, sensitivities, and any medications to ensure your personalized aromatherapy plan is safe and effective for your unique needs.
How long does it take to see results from Aromatherapy?
The experience of aromatherapy is highly individual. While some clients report immediate shifts in mood or relaxation, sustained benefits often emerge with consistent, mindful use of your personalized blends. We will guide you on incorporating these practices into your routine for optimal, long-term well-being.
