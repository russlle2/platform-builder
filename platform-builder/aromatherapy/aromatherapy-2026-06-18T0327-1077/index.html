Frequently Asked Questions

How can Aromatherapy specifically help my postpartum stress and nervous system?
Aromatherapy, when expertly applied, can directly influence your limbic system, the part of your brain responsible for emotions and memory. During postpartum, specific essential oils can signal relaxation to your nervous system, reducing cortisol levels, fostering a sense of safety, and helping you transition from a 'fight-or-flight' state to one of 'rest-and-digest', which is crucial for recovery.
Are the essential oils safe for me and my baby during this sensitive time?
Absolutely. Your safety and your baby's well-being are our utmost priority. We use only the highest quality, therapeutic-grade essential oils and adhere to very conservative dilution rates, especially for new mothers and those who are breastfeeding. We will discuss your specific circumstances and any sensitivities to create a completely safe and effective aromatic plan for you.
What kind of Aromatherapy rituals can I incorporate into my busy postpartum routine?
We understand that time is a precious commodity for new mothers. Our rituals are designed to be brief yet potent. This might include a few drops of a custom blend on a diffuser at your changing station, a calming pulse point application before a feed, or a gentle aromatic mist before rest. The focus is on creating small, consistent moments of peace that accumulate to significant nervous system support.
How long will it take to feel the effects of Aromatherapy on my stress levels?
While individual experiences vary, many mothers report feeling a subtle shift towards calm and a greater sense of equilibrium almost immediately with consistent use. Aromatherapy works cumulatively; regular, intentional application of your personalized blends will build resilience in your nervous system over time, leading to more sustained emotional balance and reduced stress.
