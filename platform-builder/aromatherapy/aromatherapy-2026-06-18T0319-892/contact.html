Seasonal Aromatherapy for Postpartum Wellness

Your body's needs shift with the seasons, especially during postpartum recovery. We’ll explore how specific essential oils can help you adapt to seasonal changes, from boosting your mood in winter months to supporting energy during spring and summer, always focusing on your unique phase of recovery. This isn't just about feeling good; it's about building resilience for the long term.
