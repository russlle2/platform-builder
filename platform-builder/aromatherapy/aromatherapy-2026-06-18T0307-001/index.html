Frequently Asked Questions

How do you ensure my blend is truly custom?
We create your blend after understanding your unique health journey, not just asking about preferences. Our consultation examines your daily patterns and wellness goals to develop a scent profile that functions as a targeted wellness tool for your needs.
Can Aromatherapy address my specific health challenges?
While not a medical treatment, our custom blends support your wellness journey by promoting relaxation, focus, or restorative sleep—complementing your existing care. We focus on how scent enhances your body's natural balance for measurable benefits.
What makes your oils different from store-bought blends?
We use exclusively therapeutic-grade, single-origin essential oils verified for purity. Every batch is hand-blended by our certified practitioner, avoiding synthetic fixatives or dilution that compromise effectiveness. You receive only the highest quality botanical care.
How quickly will I notice benefits from my blend?
Many clients experience immediate calm during their first use through intentional scent rituals. For cumulative effects, consistent daily use as part of your routine typically shows noticeable shifts in mood or sleep patterns within 2-3 weeks of regular application.
