Frequently Asked Questions

How can Aromatherapy directly address the physical pain I experience from work-related stress?
Your persistent physical pain, often stemming from long hours and mental strain, can be deeply eased by specific essential oils. We use oils known for their analgesic and anti-inflammatory properties, applied topically and inhaled, to directly target muscle tension, headaches, and general bodily stiffness, helping your body to unwind its chronic holding patterns.
I feel perpetually drained and disconnected from my body. How does Aromatherapy support somatic healing for burnout?
When you're experiencing profound exhaustion and a sense of detachment, Aromatherapy acts as a gentle anchor. We select grounding and uplifting aromas that help re-establish your connection to your physical sensations, encouraging a release of stored tension and promoting a sense of presence and calm within your own body, moving you away from that 'disconnected' feeling.
Will Aromatherapy interfere with my demanding work schedule or require a significant time commitment?
Our Aromatherapy practices are designed to integrate seamlessly into your life. We focus on creating simple, yet powerful, daily aromatic rituals that require minimal time. These rituals are meant to provide pockets of profound calm and physical relief, acting as vital anchors during your busy day, rather than adding another demand to your schedule.
I'm concerned about the sustainability and sourcing of essential oils. How do you ensure ethical practices?
Your concern for sustainability aligns with our core values. We meticulously source our essential oils from ethical producers who prioritize ecological stewardship and fair trade practices. We believe that true healing begins with respect for the earth and its resources, ensuring the purity and integrity of every botanical essence we use to support your well-being.
