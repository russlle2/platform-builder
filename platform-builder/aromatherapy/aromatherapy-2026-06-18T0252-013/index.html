Frequently Asked Questions

What is Aromatherapy and how can it benefit your well-being?
Aromatherapy is a holistic healing modality that utilizes natural plant extracts, known as essential oils, to promote health and well-being. It can support your emotional balance, enhance relaxation, improve sleep quality, and even assist with discomfort, all through the power of scent and topical application.
Are the essential oils used in your Aromatherapy practice safe?
Absolutely. We prioritize your safety and use only high-quality, therapeutic-grade essential oils. During your consultation, we discuss your health history, sensitivities, and any medications to ensure that all blends and recommendations are safe and appropriate for your specific needs.
How long does an Aromatherapy consultation typically last?
An initial Aromatherapy consultation typically lasts 60-75 minutes. This allows ample time to understand your wellness goals, discuss your health profile, and create a truly personalized essential oil blend and usage plan tailored just for you. Follow-up sessions are usually shorter, around 30-45 minutes.
What should you expect during your first Aromatherapy session?
During your first Aromatherapy session, you'll engage in a detailed discussion about your health concerns, lifestyle, and preferences. You'll learn about various essential oils and their benefits, and together, we'll formulate a custom blend. You'll leave with your personalized blend and clear instructions on how to integrate it into your daily routine.
