What exactly is Aromatherapy and how can it help me?

Aromatherapy is a holistic healing treatment that uses natural plant extracts to promote health and well-being. It harnesses the therapeutic properties of essential oils to enhance physical and psychological health, helping you with everything from stress reduction and sleep improvement to boosting mood and supporting respiratory function.

Are the essential oils used in your Aromatherapy practice safe?

Absolutely. We prioritize your safety and well-being above all else. We use only pure, therapeutic-grade essential oils from reputable suppliers. Before any application or recommendation, we conduct a thorough consultation to understand your health history and sensitivities, ensuring all blends are safe and appropriate for your individual needs.

How do you personalize Aromatherapy blends for clients?

Our personalization process begins with a detailed consultation to understand your specific health concerns, lifestyle, emotional state, and scent preferences. Based on this information, Dr. Morgan Ellis carefully selects and blends essential oils known for their synergistic effects, creating a unique formulation that resonates with your body and mind.

What can I expect during my first Aromatherapy consultation?

During your initial consultation, you can expect a warm and supportive conversation with Dr. Morgan Ellis. We'll discuss your wellness goals, health history, and any questions you may have. This collaborative session allows us to gain a comprehensive understanding of your needs, forming the foundation for your personalized Aromatherapy plan and custom blend.
