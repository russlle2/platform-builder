Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is Aromatherapy and how can it benefit you?
Aromatherapy is a holistic healing modality utilizing natural plant extracts, known as essential oils, to promote health and wellbeing. It can support your emotional state, alleviate discomfort, enhance relaxation, and improve focus, all through the therapeutic properties of scents.
How do you determine which essential oils are right for my needs?
During your consultation, we'll discuss your health history, current concerns, and wellness goals. Based on this comprehensive understanding, we carefully select essential oils with properties that align perfectly with your individual requirements, ensuring a blend that is both effective and enjoyable for you.
Are the essential oils you use safe for everyone, including children or pets?
Safety is paramount in Aromatherapy. We prioritize using only pure, high-quality essential oils. During your consultation, we'll discuss any specific sensitivities, health conditions, or if you have children or pets, to ensure your blends are formulated and recommended for safe and appropriate use for you and your household.
What can you expect during an Aromatherapy consultation?
Your consultation is a personalized conversation where we explore your wellness objectives and preferences. You'll share your experiences, and we'll collaboratively design a unique Aromatherapy plan, including custom blend formulations and guidance on how to integrate them into your daily life for optimal benefit.
