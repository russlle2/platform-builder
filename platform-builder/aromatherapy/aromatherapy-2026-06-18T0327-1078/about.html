How can Aromatherapy specifically help with nervous system regulation for seniors?

Aromatherapy utilizes the olfactory system to directly influence the limbic system, which plays a key role in emotion, memory, and stress responses. For seniors, carefully selected essential oils can gently encourage the parasympathetic nervous system – responsible for 'rest and digest' – to activate, thereby reducing feelings of agitation, supporting deeper relaxation, and promoting a more balanced mood.

Are there any considerations for seniors regarding the safety of Aromatherapy?

Absolutely. Your safety is paramount. We always use essential oils at very low dilutions, focusing on gentle and well-tolerated options. We take a thorough health history to ensure no contraindications with any medications or existing conditions. We prioritize methods like diffusion and gentle topical application, avoiding internal use, to ensure a comfortable and beneficial experience tailored to your unique needs.

What does a typical Aromatherapy session entail for someone seeking stress reduction?

A session begins with a conversation about your current well-being and specific areas where you seek calm. We then explore various aromatic profiles, helping you identify scents that resonate deeply with you. Based on this, a personalized blend is created for diffusion during your session or for gentle topical application, focusing on areas like the pulse points or temples, encouraging profound relaxation and nervous system support.

How frequently should I consider Aromatherapy to support my nervous system?

The frequency depends on your individual needs and desired outcomes. For initial support in cultivating nervous system balance, a series of sessions may be beneficial. Many seniors find a consistent practice, whether weekly or bi-weekly, helps maintain a sustained sense of calm and well-being. We will work together to create a rhythm that feels right and supportive for you.
