Frequently Asked Questions

How can Aromatherapy specifically help my postpartum posture and body awareness?
Our aromatherapy blends are carefully formulated with essential oils known to promote relaxation, mental clarity, and a deeper connection to your physical self. When used consistently, these scents can act as cues, gently reminding you to check in with your posture, engage your core, and breathe deeply, supporting your body's natural alignment as you recover from childbirth.
Are your Aromatherapy products safe for new mothers and around babies?
Absolutely. Safety is our top priority. All our aromatherapy blends are created with the specific needs of new mothers in mind, using only high-quality, pure essential oils at appropriate dilutions. We provide clear instructions for safe use, ensuring they are gentle enough for you and your home environment, especially around your little one. We always recommend consulting with your healthcare provider.
What kind of scents are used to support pelvic floor and core recovery?
We lean into earthy and grounding scents like frankincense, cedarwood, and vetiver, which are known for their calming and centering properties. These are often blended with gentle florals or citrus notes to uplift the spirit without overstimulating. The intention is to create an olfactory anchor that encourages a sense of stability and mindful presence within your body.
How do I integrate Aromatherapy into my daily postpartum routine for best results?
We'll guide you on simple, effective ways to incorporate aromatherapy, such as diffusing during quiet moments, applying diluted blends to pulse points or the lower abdomen, or adding drops to a warm bath. The key is consistency and creating small, mindful rituals that support your body awareness and postural health throughout your day, even with a newborn.
