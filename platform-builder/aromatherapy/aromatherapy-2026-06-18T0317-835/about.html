How can Aromatherapy help with my posture when I'm always studying?

Aromatherapy can assist by helping you relax tense muscles that contribute to poor posture. Certain scents encourage a sense of openness and ease, making it easier for you to consciously adjust your sitting or standing habits. It's about bringing more awareness to your body so you can make those postural corrections more naturally.

I feel a lot of anxiety about exams. Can Aromatherapy really make a difference?

Yes, many students find Aromatherapy helpful for managing exam anxiety. Specific essential oils are known for their calming properties, which can help to reduce feelings of nervousness and promote a more focused, steady mindset. We can create a personalized blend to use before or during study sessions and exams to support your emotional well-being.

Is Aromatherapy safe to use while I'm on campus or in my dorm room?

Absolutely. We prioritize safety and will guide you on appropriate and discreet ways to use your custom Aromatherapy blends on campus or in your dorm. This might include personal inhalers, roll-ons, or room sprays, ensuring your practice is respectful of shared spaces and effective for your individual needs.

What if I don't know anything about essential oils? Is this for me?

No prior knowledge is needed! Our practice is designed to be accessible and welcoming. Dr. Morgan Ellis will gently introduce you to the world of Aromatherapy, explaining how different scents work and helping you choose what feels right for you. We're here to educate and empower you, step by step, in a supportive and inclusive environment.
