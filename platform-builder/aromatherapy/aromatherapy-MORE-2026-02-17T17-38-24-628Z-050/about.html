Safety & common questions

Dilution: topical products are formulated to safe dilution ranges; you will receive clear instructions. Always perform a patch test on a small area and wait 24 hours before broader use.

Pets: certain essential oils can affect animals. We provide pet-safe alternatives and guidance when needed.

Pregnancy: some oils are not recommended during pregnancy. Notify your practitioner and we will design pregnancy-appropriate options.

How we name methods

Methods are framed by delivery (inhalation, topical, ambient) and intention. No promises — phrasing centers on what the approach may support.
