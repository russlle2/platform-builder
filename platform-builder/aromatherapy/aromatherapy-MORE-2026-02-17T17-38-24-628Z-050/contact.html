FAQ — safety highlights

Dilution & patch test: We recommend diluting essential oils for topical use and a patch test 24 hours prior to skin use. Results vary; stop use if irritation occurs.

Pregnancy & medical: If pregnant, breastfeeding, or under medical care, consult a healthcare provider before starting aromatherapy. We avoid strong topical applications without prior clearance.

Pets: We advise caution using airborne blends around small or sensitive pets. Share pet sensitivities with us so we can recommend safer alternatives.

Children: Use diluted formulas and follow child-safe guidance. Keep oils out of reach.
