Questions and safety notes

Are sessions medical care?

These practices are not medical treatment. Aromatherapy approaches here may support wellbeing but are not a substitute for medical advice.

Patch tests & dilution

We will guide dilution levels and recommend a patch test before topical use. Dilution and application instructions are provided with every blend.

Pregnancy & pets

Some essential oils are not advised during pregnancy and around animals. We always review contraindications and suggest pet-safe alternates when needed.
