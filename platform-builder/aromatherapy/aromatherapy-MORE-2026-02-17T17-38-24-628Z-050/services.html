Questions & safety notes

Are these medical treatments?
No. Sessions and blends are wellness-focused and may support emotional or sensory balance. They are not medical advice.
Patch testing & dilution
All personalized blends include dilution ratios and a recommended patch-test protocol. If you are prone to sensitivities, always test on a small skin area and wait 24 hours.
Pregnancy, pets & safety
Certain essential oils are best avoided during pregnancy and around some pets. We will review contraindications as part of the intake and tailor recommendations accordingly.
