Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is Aromatherapy?
Aromatherapy is a complementary therapy that uses natural plant extracts (essential oils) to promote health and well-being. It leverages the aromatic compounds to positively influence your mood, cognitive function, and even physical health, supporting your body's natural healing processes.
How can Aromatherapy benefit me?
Aromatherapy can offer a wide range of benefits, including stress reduction, improved sleep quality, pain relief, enhanced mood, increased focus, and support for respiratory health. Your specific benefits will depend on the essential oils used and your individual needs, which we explore during your consultation.
Are essential oils safe to use?
When used correctly and with professional guidance, essential oils are generally very safe. At , we prioritize your safety by ensuring proper dilution, advising on suitable application methods, and considering any personal sensitivities or health conditions you may have during blend creation.
What does a consultation with involve?
Your initial consultation is a collaborative conversation where we delve into your health history, lifestyle, and specific well-being goals. This allows to gain a comprehensive understanding of your needs before formulating a truly personalized aromatherapy plan and custom essential oil blend just for you.
