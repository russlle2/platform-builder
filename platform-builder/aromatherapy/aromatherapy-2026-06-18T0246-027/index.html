Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is Aromatherapy and how can it help me?
Aromatherapy is a holistic practice that uses natural plant extracts, known as essential oils, to promote well-being. It can support your journey by helping to reduce stress, improve sleep quality, uplift your mood, and even alleviate minor physical discomforts, all through the therapeutic properties of scent.
Are essential oils safe for everyone to use?
When used correctly and with professional guidance, essential oils are generally safe. However, safety varies depending on the oil, your health status, and how it's applied. We prioritize your safety by providing personalized recommendations and clear instructions, especially if you have sensitivities, are pregnant, or have specific health conditions.
How is a personalized Aromatherapy blend created for me?
Your personalized blend begins with a detailed consultation where we discuss your health history, current concerns, and wellness goals. Based on this, we carefully select specific essential oils known for their therapeutic properties and synergistic effects, then formulate a unique blend tailored precisely to your individual needs and preferences.
What should I expect during my first Aromatherapy consultation?
During your initial consultation, we'll have an in-depth conversation about your health, lifestyle, and what you hope to achieve with Aromatherapy. This allows us to understand your unique profile and create a customized plan, including specific essential oil recommendations and usage guidelines to start your journey effectively.
