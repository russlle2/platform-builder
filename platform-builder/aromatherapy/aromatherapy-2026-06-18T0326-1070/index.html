Frequently Asked Questions

How can Aromatherapy specifically help with college anxiety?
Aromatherapy offers a gentle, non-invasive way to signal safety to your nervous system. Specific scents can help reduce feelings of overwhelm, calm racing thoughts, and promote a sense of groundedness, supporting you in moments of academic pressure or social stress.
Do I need any prior experience with wellness or essential oils to benefit from Aromatherapy?
Absolutely not. Our approach is designed to be accessible and gentle, requiring no prior knowledge. We guide you through each step, explaining how specific aromatic practices can support your well-being in a clear and easy-to-understand way, making it simple to integrate into your busy student life.
Will using Aromatherapy interfere with my studies or make me sleepy?
Our Aromatherapy practices are carefully tailored to support your academic success. We focus on scents and methods that promote mental clarity, reduce stress-induced fatigue, and enhance focus, rather than inducing drowsiness. The aim is to help you feel more regulated and present, not less engaged.
How can I incorporate Aromatherapy into my daily college routine?
We'll work together to find simple, manageable ways to weave Aromatherapy into your day. This might involve a quick scent ritual before a lecture, a calming blend for your study space, or a soothing aroma to unwind before sleep. The goal is to create small, impactful moments of calm that fit seamlessly into your schedule.
