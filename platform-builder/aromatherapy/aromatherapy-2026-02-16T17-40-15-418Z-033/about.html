Philosophy

How a typical consult works

  1. We cover preferences, known sensitivities, pregnancy status, and household pets.

  3. You get written directions for use, patch-test steps, and gentle micro-habits to try at home.

Frequently asked (safety-forward)

How do I dilute oils?
I provide dilution charts with every blend: typical ranges are 0.5–3% for topical adult use; less for children. We'll discuss specifics in your consult.
What about patch testing?
Patch-test on a small area of inner forearm with your diluted blend for 24 hours. Avoid if irritation occurs.
Pets in the home?
Some essential oils can affect animals. I always recommend pet-safe alternatives and diffusion practices that prioritize animal comfort.
Pregnancy or medication?
Please mention pregnancy, nursing, or medications before booking. We avoid certain botanicals and focus on very low dilution or aromatic-only approaches when needed.
