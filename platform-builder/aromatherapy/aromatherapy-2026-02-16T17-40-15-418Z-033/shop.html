Product FAQ
Recommended dilution? Typical topical dilutions for adults are 1–3% unless directed otherwise. Children, elderly, or sensitive skin require lower dilutions.
Patch test? Always perform a patch test: apply diluted product to inner forearm and observe for 24–48 hours.
Pets? Many oils affect animals differently. Use low diffusion levels, keep pets able to leave the space, and avoid highly toxic oils around pets.
Pregnancy? Some oils are not recommended in pregnancy. Consult a qualified healthcare provider before use and book a tailored consultation for pregnancy-safe options.
