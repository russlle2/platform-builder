Safety & FAQ

How do you dilute blends?
I provide dilution guidance for each product. Typical topical blends range 1–3% for adults unless otherwise advised by a qualified clinician. Children or sensitive skin require lower dilution and explicit consent.
Do I need a patch test?
Yes. I recommend a small patch test on the inner forearm for new topical products and 24–48 hour observation before extended use.
What about pets?
Some essential oils can be irritating to animals. I advise keeping diffusers on low, using pet-safe blends, and ensuring animals can leave the room if needed.
Pregnancy & medical notes
I avoid suggesting certain oils during pregnancy and advise consulting your healthcare provider. All guidance here is non-medical and safety-forward.
