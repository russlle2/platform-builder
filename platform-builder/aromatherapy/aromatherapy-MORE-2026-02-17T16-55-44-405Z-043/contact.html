FAQ — safe use & tests

Patch test
We always recommend a small patch test (diluted oil applied to inner forearm) for 24 hours to check for sensitivity.
Dilution
Topical blends are offered at safe dilutions (commonly 1%–3% for general use). If you have a specific sensitivity, we will reduce strength.
Pregnancy & nursing
We adapt recommendations — some essential oils are avoided in pregnancy. Please disclose if you are pregnant or nursing so we can provide safety-conscious options.
Pets
Certain oils can be harmful to pets. Tell us about household animals and we will suggest pet-safe approaches for diffusing or topical use.
