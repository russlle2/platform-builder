Shop FAQ — safe use, clearly explained

Dilution: Bottles intended for topical use are supplied at safe dilutions; always follow the label. For DIY, adult topical dilutions generally range 1–3% depending on sensitivity. Children and elders often require lower dilutions; consult the practitioner.

Patch Test: Before topical use, apply a small amount of diluted product to the inner forearm and wait 24 hours. If irritation, redness, or discomfort occurs, discontinue use.

Pets: Many essential oils can affect animals differently. Avoid direct application to pets and consult before diffusing in shared spaces. Notify us of pets in your home for tailored product guidance.

Pregnancy & Nursing: Some essential oils are not recommended during pregnancy or nursing, and some blends will be adapted accordingly. If pregnant or nursing, select products labeled pregnancy-safe or contact {{PRACTITIONER_NAME}} for recommendations.
