Safety & Usage

Each blend is intended for aromatic use or proper topical dilution. Always perform a patch test and follow directions provided at purchase. For pregnant persons, young children, or households with pets, consult with the practitioner to adapt blends and dilution. Avoid ingesting essential oils.

FAQ — gentle answers

Patch test? Apply diluted sample (2–3% in carrier) to inner forearm and wait 24 hours. If irritation occurs, discontinue use.

Dilution guidance? Typical aromatic inhalation blends are 1–3% for room diffusion; topical blends depend on age and sensitivity. Membership notes include exact dilution recipes.

Pets? Some oils are not pet-safe (e.g., high concentrations of phenolic compounds). Always notify the practitioner about animals in the home; we’ll tailor choices accordingly.

Pregnancy? Certain oils and high concentrations are not recommended during pregnancy. We prioritize safety-forward options and will provide pregnancy-safe alternatives upon request.
