Why scent? A warm invitation

My path to aromatherapy began with a single lavender sachet and a desire to craft calm moments. I learned to blend thoughtfully, to prioritize safety, and to listen—first to plants, then to people. Today I offer a membership that centers education, ethical sourcing, and ritual-making.

Every blend shared here includes notes, recommended dilutions, and guidance for patch testing. We avoid medical claims and focus on supportive experiences.

A S.A.F.E. Framework for scent

A simple checklist we teach to keep aromatic practice pleasant and considerate.

Sanitize & Source

Use clean vessels and ethically sourced essential oils. Know purity and supplier practices.

Always Dilute

We recommend conservative dilution ranges and provide specific ratios. Never apply undiluted essential oils to skin.

Feel-Test (Patch)

Patch-test blends on a small area; wait 24 hours. Discontinue if irritation occurs.

Ear & Environmental Care

Be mindful around children, pets, pregnant people, and those with respiratory sensitivities. Consult your healthcare provider for specific concerns.
