A few safety notes

Dilution & use
I recommend gentle dilutions for topical work. Specific ratios depend on age, sensitivity, and context. Exact dilution guidance will be offered during consults—never apply neat essential oils to skin.
Patch test
Always patch-test a diluted blend on a small skin area and wait 24–48 hours to check for reactions.
Pets & diffusing
Use caution diffusing around pets. Keep diffusing sessions short, use pet-safe blends, and ensure animals can leave the room.
Pregnancy & medical conditions
If pregnant or under medical care, consult with your healthcare provider before using essential oils. I will always prioritize safety and avoid contraindicated oils.

These notes are general guidance, not a substitute for personalised medical advice.
