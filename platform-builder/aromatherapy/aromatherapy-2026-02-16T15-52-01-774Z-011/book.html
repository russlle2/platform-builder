FAQ — safety focused

Dilution? Typical topical dilutions are 1–3% for adults; lower for sensitive skin and children. Exact guidance provided per session.

Patch test? Always perform a patch test on inner forearm 24 hours before regular topical use.

Pets? Some essential oils can affect animals. We provide pet-safe alternatives and dosing guidance for household use.

Pregnancy & nursing? We avoid certain oils and will offer tailored, safer choices. Consult your care provider as well.
