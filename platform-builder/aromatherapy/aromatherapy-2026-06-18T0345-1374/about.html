How can Aromatherapy specifically address the physical pain from chronic illness or injury?

Our Aromatherapy focuses on plant essences known for their anti-inflammatory, analgesic, and muscle-relaxing properties. By creating personalized blends, we target the specific types of pain you experience, whether it's neuropathic, musculoskeletal, or inflammatory, helping to soothe the affected areas and quiet the persistent signals of discomfort your body may be sending.

Will Aromatherapy interfere with my current medications or other recovery protocols?

When working with Sage Garden Aromatherapy, your safety is paramount. We conduct a thorough intake to understand your medical history, current medications, and ongoing treatments. We carefully select essential oils that complement your existing regimen, ensuring no adverse interactions. Our goal is to support and enhance your recovery, not to replace professional medical advice or prescribed therapies.

I'm sensitive to smells due to my condition. How do you approach Aromatherapy for individuals with heightened sensitivities?

We understand that sensitivity to scent can be a concern, especially during recovery. Our initial consultations include a detailed discussion about your scent preferences and any sensitivities. We begin with extremely diluted concentrations and gentle, often subtle aromas, carefully observing your response. The process is always guided by your comfort, ensuring a gentle and supportive experience that never overwhelms your senses.

How does Aromatherapy contribute to somatic healing when the body feels 'stuck' in pain?

Aromatherapy works on multiple levels to encourage somatic healing. Specific aromas can directly influence the limbic system, impacting emotional states and the body's stress response, which often perpetuates pain cycles. By gently shifting these internal states, we help to release held tension and trauma within the physical tissues. This process allows your body to gradually unwind from ingrained patterns of discomfort, inviting a new sense of freedom and fluidity.
