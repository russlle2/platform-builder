Frequently Asked Questions

How can Aromatherapy specifically help me improve my sleep as a parent?
Aromatherapy for parents focuses on creating a predictable sensory cue for sleep, easing the mental load that often keeps you awake. We'll craft bespoke blends to soothe an overactive mind, reduce anxiety, and signal to your body that it's time for rest, transforming your evening routine into a peaceful ritual.
What kind of Aromatherapy blends are best for managing household stress?
For household stress, we prioritize essential oils known for their calming and grounding properties. Think gentle florals to soften tension, citrus notes to uplift without overstimulating, and earthy aromas to promote a sense of stability, all tailored to your personal preferences and the specific rhythms of your family.
Will Aromatherapy be safe to use around my young children?
Absolutely, safety is paramount. We meticulously select and dilute essential oils appropriate for environments with young children, focusing on gentle methods like diffusion and indirect application. During your consultation, we'll discuss the specific ages of your children and ensure every recommendation is safe and beneficial for your entire household.
How quickly can I expect to see results from using Aromatherapy for sleep?
While individual experiences vary, many parents report feeling a noticeable shift in their evening calm and sleep quality within a few days to a couple of weeks of consistent use. The subtle yet powerful nature of Aromatherapy works with your body's natural rhythms, building towards deeper, more restorative sleep over time.
