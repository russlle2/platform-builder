Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is Aromatherapy and how can it benefit me?
Aromatherapy is a holistic healing treatment that uses natural plant extracts to promote health and well-being. It leverages the therapeutic properties of essential oils to improve your physical and emotional health. You might experience benefits like reduced stress, improved sleep, enhanced mood, and relief from minor discomforts, tailored to your specific needs.
Are the essential oils used in your Aromatherapy practice safe?
Absolutely. Your safety is our top priority. We use only high-quality, pure essential oils from reputable suppliers. During your consultation, we'll discuss your health history, sensitivities, and any medications you're taking to ensure the blends are safe and appropriate for your unique constitution. You will receive clear instructions on how to use your custom blends.
How do I know which Aromatherapy blend is right for my concerns?
That's exactly what our personalized consultation is for! will take the time to understand your specific concerns, health goals, and preferences. Based on this comprehensive assessment, a custom Aromatherapy blend will be formulated just for you, designed to address your individual needs effectively and naturally.
Can Aromatherapy replace my conventional medical treatments?
Aromatherapy is a complementary therapy intended to support your overall well-being, not to replace conventional medical treatments. It works wonderfully alongside your existing healthcare plan. We always recommend consulting with your primary healthcare provider for any health concerns and before making significant changes to your medical routine.
