FAQ & Safety

How do I dilute safely?

Guidance in this site is educational and not medical. Typical adult daily dilutions are 1% for many blends; 0.5% for sensitive adults; 0.25–0.5% for young children. We'll show exact drops for your chosen volume. Do a patch test before topical use.

Can pets be affected?

Some oils may bother pets. Keep blends away from enclosed pet spaces, introduce scents slowly, and consult a veterinarian for concerns.

Pregnancy and nursing

If pregnant, nursing, or under medical care, speak with your healthcare provider before using essential oils. This program keeps safety conservative and may suggest alternatives.
