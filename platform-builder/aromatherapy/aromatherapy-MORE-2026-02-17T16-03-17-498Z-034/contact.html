FAQ — Safety & notes

Is this medical advice?
No. Content here is informational and non-medical. Aromatherapy may support comfort or routine but is not a replacement for medical care.
Dilution and patch tests
Begin with a small dilution and patch test on inner forearm. Typical adult topical ranges are shown in the blend builder (0.5%–3%). For sensitive people, children, pregnant or nursing people, or pets in the home, use lower dilutions or avoid topical application. Consult a qualified provider for personal guidance.
Pets
Some essential oils can affect animals. Use diffusers and topical products cautiously; keep oils away from pets and their bedding. If unsure, avoid use around sensitive species.
Pregnancy & medical conditions
Some oils are not recommended during pregnancy or with certain medical conditions. Please seek personalised guidance from a knowledgeable practitioner before use.
