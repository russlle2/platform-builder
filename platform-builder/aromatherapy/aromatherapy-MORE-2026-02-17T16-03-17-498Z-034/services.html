Common questions

Are these medical sessions?
No. Sessions and blends are for wellness-support practices and are not medical advice. Aromas may support mood or calm in some people, but results are individual.
Safety: dilution, patch test, pregnancy, pets
We always provide dilution recommendations and a patch-test step for skin application. Please avoid topical essential oils if you are pregnant or breastfeeding unless cleared by a healthcare provider; do not use certain oils around young children or pets. Check each blend card for safety notes. If in doubt, consult a qualified clinician.
How long are the cohorts?
Typically six-week cycles with weekly short practices and two live group check-ins. Each cycle is limited in size to keep guidance personal.

Three-breath grounding & mini-journal

Step 1 — Settle. Close your eyes if comfortable. Inhale 4 counts, hold 2, exhale 6. Repeat three times.

Step 2 — Notice. Open your eyes. Write one short line: how do you want the next hour to feel?

Step 3 — Anchor. Smell a safe, familiar scent (tea, citrus peel, or a cotton cloth). Whisper your intention aloud or to yourself.
