Origins

Quiet craft, deliberate scent

We work with plant distillations and botanical absolutes to create small, intentional practices. Sessions and blends aim to support daily rhythm and presence — not medical treatment.

Our approach

We combine sensory listening with simple, repeatable rituals. Each plan is safety-forward: we discuss sensitivities, dilution, and environmental considerations before creating a blend. Our work may support relaxation, focus, or sleep when paired with consistent practice.

How a session flows

  1. Brief intake and safety check
  2. Custom blend or inhalation strategy
  3. Guided in-session practice (breath, awareness, gentle movement)
  4. Take-home notes and product suggestions

"Small, steady rituals that fit into your day."

Values

  • Clarity over complication
  • Safety and consent first
  • Plant humility
  • Accessible practices for everyday life

Practical notes

- Patch testing is recommended for topical use.
- Keep concentrations modest; we often use 1–3% for adults.
- Inform us of pregnancy, nursing, medical conditions, or household pets so we can adapt safely.

Meet the practitioner

A longtime student of plant materials and somatic practices, the practitioner blends practical botany with plain language coaching. Sessions are invitational and paced to your needs.
