Safety & preparations

  • Always perform a patch test for new blends.
  • Use 1–3% dilution for most topical blends unless advised otherwise.
  • Keep essential oils away from eyes and mucous membranes; avoid undiluted application.
  • If you have pets, especially cats, inform us — some oils may be harmful to animals.
  • If pregnant or nursing, consult your healthcare provider before use and notify us.
