Safety & practical notes

Dilution & patch test
All blends are shared with clear dilution guidance. A small patch test is recommended for topical use before regular application.
Pets and plants
Essential oils can affect animals differently. If you have pets, we will recommend low-risk approaches and ventilation practices.
Pregnancy & nursing
Some oils are best avoided during pregnancy and lactation. Please tell us in the planner and we will adapt recommendations.
Not medical advice
Sessions and blends are supportive and educational. They are not a substitute for medical or mental health care.
