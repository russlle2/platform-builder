Safety & basic guidance

  • All suggestions use nondirective language — aromas may support a gentle change in mood but are not a substitute for medical care.
  • Always perform a patch test before topical use and consult a clinician when in doubt.
  • Keep essential oils away from eyes and mucous membranes; store safely out of reach of children and pets.

FAQs

Dilution

Typical dilution suggestions are included in the plan. For daily at-home topical use, a conservative 1%–2% (6–12 drops per 30ml carrier) may be appropriate; lower concentrations are advised for sensitive skin. These are guidelines, not prescriptions.

Patch test

Apply a small diluted amount to the inner forearm and wait 24 hours. If irritation occurs, discontinue use.

Pets

Some oils can be harmful to animals. If you have pets, avoid nebulizing near them and consult your veterinarian about specific oils.

Pregnancy & breastfeeding

If pregnant or nursing, consult a qualified clinician before using essential oils. Some oils are not recommended during pregnancy.

Final note
