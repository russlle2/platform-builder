Aromatherapy

The Gentle Art of Aromatherapy in Chronic Recovery

Chronic illness and injury can sever the delicate threads of connection to your body, your spirit, and the world around you. Tender Aromatherapy provides a clinically informed, deeply compassionate approach to mend these relationships. We utilize precise botanical essences to create a supportive internal environment, fostering a sense of calm and re-establishing a loving dialogue with your physical and emotional self during recovery.
