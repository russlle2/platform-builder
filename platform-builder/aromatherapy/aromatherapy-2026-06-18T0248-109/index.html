Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is Aromatherapy and how can it benefit me?
Aromatherapy is the therapeutic use of essential oils to enhance physical and psychological well-being. It can support your mood, reduce stress, improve sleep, alleviate discomfort, and boost energy. We work with you to identify specific benefits relevant to your personal goals.
Are essential oils safe to use, and how will I know which ones are right for me?
When used correctly, essential oils are generally safe. We prioritize your safety and well-being by providing expert guidance on proper dilution, application methods, and contraindications. Your personalized consultation ensures you receive recommendations perfectly suited to your health profile.
How long does it take to see results from Aromatherapy?
The experience of Aromatherapy is highly individual. Some people notice immediate effects, especially with mood and stress reduction, while others experience more gradual changes over time. Consistency in your personalized ritual is key to achieving optimal and lasting benefits.
Do I need to have a specific health concern to benefit from Aromatherapy?
Not at all! Aromatherapy is wonderful for proactive wellness and general well-being, not just for addressing specific concerns. Many clients seek our services to enhance relaxation, improve focus, support emotional balance, or simply to enrich their daily self-care practices.
