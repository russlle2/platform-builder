Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is Aromatherapy and how can it benefit me?
Aromatherapy is the therapeutic use of aromatic plant extracts and essential oils for psychological and physical well-being. It can support you in managing stress, improving sleep, boosting mood, alleviating discomfort, and enhancing overall vitality. Your benefits will be unique to your needs and the specific blends we create for you.
Are the essential oils used in your Aromatherapy practice safe for everyone?
Safety is paramount in our Aromatherapy practice. We use only high-quality, pure essential oils and always consider your individual health history, sensitivities, and any medications you may be taking. We will guide you on safe application methods and appropriate dilutions, ensuring your experience is both effective and secure.
How long does a typical Aromatherapy consultation take?
A typical initial Aromatherapy consultation lasts approximately 60-90 minutes. This allows us ample time to thoroughly discuss your wellness goals, health history, and preferences to craft the most effective and personalized essential oil blends for you. Follow-up sessions are usually shorter, around 30-45 minutes.
Can Aromatherapy help with specific emotional or physical challenges I'm facing?
Yes, Aromatherapy can be a wonderful complementary support for a wide range of emotional and physical challenges. While it doesn't replace conventional medical treatment, many find it helpful for issues like anxiety, fatigue, headaches, digestive discomfort, and mood imbalances. We’ll discuss your specific concerns to determine how aromatherapy can best support your well-being journey.
