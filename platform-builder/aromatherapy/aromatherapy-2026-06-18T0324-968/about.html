What exactly is Aromatherapy and how can it help seniors like me?

Aromatherapy uses natural plant extracts, called essential oils, for well-being. For seniors, it can offer gentle support for common concerns like occasional sleep difficulties, muscle discomfort, or simply enhancing your mood. We use very diluted and safe applications, focusing on comfort and relaxation without any harsh effects.

Do I need any prior experience with essential oils or wellness practices to start?

Absolutely not! Many of our clients are entirely new to Aromatherapy. We specialize in making it accessible and easy to understand. Dr. Morgan Ellis will explain everything clearly, ensuring you feel comfortable and informed every step of the way. Your comfort and understanding are our top priorities.

How does Aromatherapy support seasonal wellness for someone in their 60s and beyond?

Seasonal wellness in Aromatherapy means selecting scents that complement the time of year. For example, warming scents might be suggested in winter to promote comfort, while refreshing aromas could be used in summer to invigorate. It's about gently supporting your body's natural rhythms to help you feel more balanced and adaptable as seasons change.

Is Aromatherapy safe for seniors, especially if I'm taking medications?

Yes, when practiced responsibly and gently, Aromatherapy can be very safe for seniors. We always begin with a thorough chat about your health history and any medications you're taking to ensure we choose the most appropriate and gentle essential oils and application methods. Your safety and well-being are paramount.
