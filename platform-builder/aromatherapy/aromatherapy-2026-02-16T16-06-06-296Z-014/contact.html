Truth

FAQ — practical safety answers

How do I dilute essential oils for topical use?
Start low. A common conservative guideline: 0.5–2% for adults depending on context. Use a carrier oil and measure drops precisely. I provide exact dilution plans during consultations.
What is a patch test and why do I need one?
A patch test checks for skin sensitivity: apply a tiny diluted amount to a small area and observe 24–48 hours. Stop use if you notice redness, itching, or burning.
Are essential oils safe around pets?
Many oils can affect animals differently. Use well-ventilated spaces, keep diffusers out of reach, and consult a veterinarian before regular use around sensitive species. I provide pet-safety notes for blends.
What about pregnancy and breastfeeding?
Pregnancy requires extra caution. Some oils are not recommended. Tell me if you are pregnant or breastfeeding so I can tailor safe options and avoid any restricted substances.
Can aromatherapy treat medical conditions?
No. Aromatherapy offers supportive sensory and ritual tools. It does not replace medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Seek healthcare professionals for medical concerns.
