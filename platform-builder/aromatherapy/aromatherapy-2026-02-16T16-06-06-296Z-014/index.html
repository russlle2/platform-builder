Myth vs. Truth: Aromatherapy, clearly

Myth

Essential oils cure diseases and replace clinical care.

Truth

Essential oils can support wellbeing when used safely as part of a broader, evidence-informed self-care plan. We do not make medical claims; we focus on symptom support, comfort, and sensory regulation.
