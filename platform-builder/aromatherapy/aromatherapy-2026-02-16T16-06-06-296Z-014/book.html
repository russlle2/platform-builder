Booking FAQ

How soon can I book?

Typical lead time is 3–10 business days depending on service and season.

What if I am pregnant?

Please inform us during booking. We will recommend avoidance of certain oils and adapt to pregnancy-safe alternatives.

Can I bring my pet?

For in-person consults, pets should generally stay at home due to scent sensitivity risks. If your pet will be present, disclose when booking.

Do I need a patch test?

Yes for topical applications: apply diluted sample to forearm and observe for 24–48 hours. We will guide you on concentration.
