FAQ — safety essentials

How do you recommend dilutions?

Dilution depends on age, skin sensitivity, and intended application. Standard adult topical ranges are conservative; we never advise medical dosing.

Should I do a patch test?

Yes. Apply a small amount of the diluted product to a discreet area and observe for 24–48 hours.

What about pets?

Avoid leaving strong diffusions in poorly ventilated spaces with pets; some compounds can irritate. We provide pet-safety notes and recommend veterinary advice for concerns.

Pregnancy guidance

Many oils are best avoided in pregnancy or used with modified dilutions. Please disclose pregnancy during intake so we can adapt recommendations.
