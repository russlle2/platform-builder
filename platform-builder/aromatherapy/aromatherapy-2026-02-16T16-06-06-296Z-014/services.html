Frequently asked — safety highlights

Dilution and topical use
We provide dilution ranges in consultations. Typical topical roll-ons are 1–5% depending on age and context. We avoid recommending anything beyond general aromatherapy practice; consult your healthcare provider for medical conditions.
Patch testing
Patch testing on a small skin area is advised prior to regular topical use. Discontinue if irritation occurs.
Pets and diffusion
Not all oils are pet-safe. We assess household pets and recommend low-concentration, well-ventilated protocols or alternate approaches if needed.
Pregnancy and breastfeeding
Some oils are contraindicated. We screen for pregnancy status and recommend consultation with a care provider; safety is prioritized over any single approach.
