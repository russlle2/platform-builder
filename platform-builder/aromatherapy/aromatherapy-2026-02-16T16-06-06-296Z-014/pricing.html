Common questions — safety first

How do you set dilutions?

We provide conservative dilution ranges based on intended use (aromatherapy, topical, mild sensory support). We never recommend therapeutic dosing or clinical treatment levels.

What about patch tests?

Always do a patch test for topical blends: apply a small amount (diluted) to forearm, observe 24–48 hours for irritation.

Are your products pet-safe?

Certain oils can be irritating to pets; we provide pet-safety notes and recommend avoiding direct topical use on animals and ensuring ventilation when diffusing. Consult your veterinarian for animal health concerns.

Pregnancy and nursing note?

Many oils are contraindicated in pregnancy or require modified dilutions. Always inform your practitioner; we will recommend avoidance and safer alternatives.
