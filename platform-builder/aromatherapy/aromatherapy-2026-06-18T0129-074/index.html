Frequently Asked Questions

How is your Aromatherapy different from spa or retail products?
We use pharmaceutical-grade botanical essences at neurologically effective concentrations, never diluted for scent. Blends are formulated based on clinical research—not marketing trends—to directly influence specific nervous system responses.
Do I need to commit to a long-term program?
We offer both targeted protocols (3-6 sessions for specific goals like sleep optimization) and ongoing sensory maintenance. Most clients achieve measurable results within 4 sessions, with optional follow-ups to refine protocols as your needs evolve.
What if I have sensitivities or don't like certain scents?
We use single-note botanicals (like cypress for calm or frankincense for focus) and adjust molecular concentrations. If you're sensitive to citrus, we'll create protocols using vetiver or myrrh instead—your preferences guide the scent architecture.
Can it complement my current mental health treatment?
Absolutely. We coordinate with your healthcare team to create protocols that support—not replace—existing treatments. Our work has been integrated with CBT and neurofeedback programs for enhanced outcomes in anxiety and PTSD management.
