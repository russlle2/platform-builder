How can Aromatherapy specifically help my nervous system during postpartum recovery?

Aromatherapy utilizes the olfactory system to directly influence the limbic system, which governs emotions and stress responses. Certain essential oils can promote parasympathetic nervous system activity, helping you shift from a 'fight or flight' state to one of 'rest and digest,' crucial for recovery, sleep, and emotional regulation after childbirth.

Are the essential oils used safe for me as a new mother, especially if I am breastfeeding?

Absolutely. Safety is our paramount concern. Dr. Morgan Ellis is extensively trained in the nuanced application of essential oils during sensitive life stages. We meticulously select oils and dilution ratios that are clinically appropriate for postpartum mothers, including those who are breastfeeding, ensuring efficacy without compromise to your well-being or your baby's.

What kind of stress reduction can I expect from Aromatherapy sessions?

You can anticipate a reduction in feelings of overwhelm, improved sleep quality, a greater sense of emotional stability, and an enhanced ability to cope with daily stressors. Our aim is to provide a gentle, consistent support that helps your nervous system find its baseline calm, allowing you to engage more fully and peacefully with your new role.

How often should I engage with Aromatherapy to experience its benefits postpartum?

The frequency is tailored to your individual needs and the intensity of your postpartum experience. Initially, more regular engagement, either through sessions or consistent home rituals with your custom blends, can establish a foundation of calm. As you progress, we'll adjust the plan to support sustained well-being, empowering you to integrate these practices into your long-term self-care.
