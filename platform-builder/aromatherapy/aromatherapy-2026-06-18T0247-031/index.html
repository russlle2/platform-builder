Frequently Asked Questions

What is Aromatherapy and how can it benefit me?
Aromatherapy is a complementary therapy that uses natural plant extracts, known as essential oils, to promote health and well-being. It can benefit you by supporting emotional balance, alleviating stress, improving sleep quality, easing discomfort, and enhancing your overall sense of vitality. We'll discuss your specific needs to determine the most beneficial approach for you.
Are essential oils safe for everyone, and how are they used in Aromatherapy?
When used correctly, essential oils are generally safe. However, their potency requires careful handling. In Aromatherapy, they can be diffused into the air, inhaled directly, or applied topically to the skin, always diluted in a carrier oil. We prioritize your safety and will guide you on the appropriate and safe usage methods tailored to your health profile.
What can I expect during my first Aromatherapy consultation?
During your initial Aromatherapy consultation, you can expect a warm and thorough discussion about your health history, current well-being goals, and any concerns you may have. We'll explore scent preferences and lifestyle factors to understand your unique needs. This allows us to craft a truly personalized Aromatherapy plan and essential oil blend for you.
How long does it take to see results from Aromatherapy?
The experience of Aromatherapy is highly individual. Some people report feeling immediate effects, such as relaxation or upliftment, while for deeper or more chronic concerns, consistent use over several weeks may be necessary to notice significant changes. We encourage you to be patient and observe how your body and mind respond over time.
