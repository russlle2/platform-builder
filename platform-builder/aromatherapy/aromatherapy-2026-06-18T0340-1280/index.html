Frequently Asked Questions

How can Aromatherapy specifically help with postpartum physical pain?
Aromatherapy offers targeted support for your postpartum body by utilizing essential oils known for their analgesic, anti-inflammatory, and muscle-relaxing properties. We craft bespoke blends to alleviate specific discomforts like uterine cramping, perineal tenderness, back pain from nursing, and shoulder tension, helping your body find comfort and accelerate its natural healing processes.
Is Aromatherapy safe to use during breastfeeding and around my baby?
Yes, with expert guidance, Aromatherapy can be safely integrated into your postpartum routine. We prioritize your and your baby's well-being by selecting only the safest essential oils, recommending appropriate dilution ratios, and suggesting application methods that are gentle and effective, ensuring peace of mind while you nurture yourself.
What does somatic healing through Aromatherapy mean for a new mother?
Somatic healing in Aromatherapy for new mothers means gently addressing the physical and emotional imprints of birth held within your body. We use specific aromas and guided practices to help you release physical tension, process lingering sensations, and integrate the profound experience of childbirth, fostering a sense of grounding and embodied peace during your recovery.
How long will it take to feel relief from postpartum discomforts with Aromatherapy?
While individual experiences vary, many mothers report feeling immediate soothing effects from their personalized Aromatherapy blends. Consistent, gentle application as guided by your practitioner supports your body's ongoing healing, with progressive relief from discomforts and a growing sense of physical ease and emotional balance over time.
