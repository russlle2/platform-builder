How does trauma-informed Aromatherapy specifically address recovery from chronic illness or injury?

Trauma-informed Aromatherapy acknowledges the profound emotional and physiological impact of chronic illness or injury. We prioritize creating a safe, empowering space, using carefully selected essential oils to gently support nervous system regulation, reduce anxiety, and help you process the emotional residue of your experiences, rather than bypassing them. Your comfort and agency are paramount in every step.

Will Aromatherapy interfere with my ongoing medical treatments or medications for chronic conditions?

Your safety is our highest priority. We always recommend you consult with your primary healthcare provider regarding any new complementary therapies, including Aromatherapy, especially if you are on medications or undergoing ongoing treatments. Our approach is designed to complement, not replace, conventional medical care, and we will discuss any potential interactions or contraindications thoroughly during your consultation to ensure your well-being.

How can Aromatherapy help me manage the emotional fatigue often accompanying chronic recovery?

Emotional fatigue is a common, yet often overlooked, aspect of chronic recovery. Aromatherapy can offer gentle support by utilizing essential oils known for their uplifting, calming, or restorative properties. We focus on creating personalized aromatic practices that can help replenish your emotional reserves, foster mental clarity, and provide moments of respite, helping you navigate your day with renewed emotional resilience.

What if I have sensitivities or allergies, especially after a period of illness or injury?

Your unique physiological landscape is always respected. We conduct a thorough intake to understand any sensitivities, allergies, or health concerns you may have, especially those arising from your chronic illness or injury. We use only high-quality, pure essential oils and always begin with extremely diluted applications, often through passive diffusion or patch testing, to ensure your comfort and safety. Your comfort guides our choices.
