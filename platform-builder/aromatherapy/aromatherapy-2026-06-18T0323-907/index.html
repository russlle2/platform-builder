Frequently Asked Questions

How can Aromatherapy specifically help my postpartum recovery?
Aromatherapy can offer gentle support for common postpartum experiences like sleep disruption, emotional fluctuations, and physical discomfort. We focus on specific essential oils known to promote calm, reduce feelings of overwhelm, support gentle energy, and aid in relaxation, all tailored to your individual needs as a new mother.
Is Aromatherapy safe for new mothers and around infants?
Yes, when practiced with proper knowledge and dilution, Aromatherapy can be safe. We prioritize your safety and your baby's, using only appropriate essential oils at safe concentrations. During your consultation, we'll discuss your specific circumstances and any concerns to ensure your blends are beneficial and nurturing for your entire family.
How does seasonal wellness factor into my postpartum Aromatherapy plan?
Our approach acknowledges that your body's needs are influenced by the natural rhythms of the seasons. For example, during colder months, we might focus on grounding and immune-supporting blends, while warmer seasons might call for uplifting and refreshing aromas. This seasonal alignment helps your body adapt and thrive, offering preventive care tailored to nature's flow.
What kind of commitment is required for Aromatherapy sessions?
Our service is designed to fit into your life as a new mother. You can choose single sessions for immediate support or opt for a series of consultations to develop a comprehensive seasonal wellness plan. We aim for gentle, consistent support rather than rigid commitments, allowing you to prioritize your recovery at your own pace.
