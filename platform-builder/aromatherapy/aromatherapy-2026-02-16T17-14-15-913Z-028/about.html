What to expect in a session

  1. Quick intake and safety check (allergies, medications, pregnancy, pets).
  2. Scent sampling and preference mapping (top/middle/base notes explained).
  3. Blend design with dilution guidance and labeled samples.
  4. Short ritual practice and written at-home notes.

We avoid therapeutic claims. Sessions are educational and supportive; if you have medical concerns, consult a licensed healthcare provider.

Safety-first choices

We include patch test instructions, dilution charts, and pet-safe diffusion methods. If you are pregnant or nursing, we will use pregnancy-safe oils and tailor recommendations. If you have cats, we will discuss species-specific cautions and suggest safe alternatives.
