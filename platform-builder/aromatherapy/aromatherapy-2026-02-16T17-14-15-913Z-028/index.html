The guided ritual: simple, repeatable care

We design an easy-to-follow ritual you can do at home. The goal is consistency and safety: proper dilution, storage, and application practices instead of one-off "miracle" solutions.

Breathe

Short breathing practices paired with a curated inhalation blend to support calm and focus (non-medical). Use inhaler or diffused mist.

Apply

Topical applications in a carrier oil at safe dilution rates. We always recommend patch testing first and following dilution guidance for skin safety.

Reflect

Short check-ins post-ritual to note effects, scent memory, and adjustments. We provide journaling prompts and practical prompts for tracking results.

Frequently asked safety questions

We recommend specific dilution ratios by age and purpose. Typical topical dilutions range from 0.5%–5% depending on use and client profile. Your plan will include exact percentages. Always do a patch test before wider use.
A patch test is applying a small amount of a diluted blend behind the ear or on the forearm and observing for 24 hours. It helps identify sensitivity before regular use.
Some oils can affect pets. We will advise on pet-safe options and recommend avoiding direct topical application to animals. Keep diffusers and storage out of reach and ventilate spaces.
Pregnancy and breastfeeding entail extra precautions. We screen intake forms for pregnancy status and follow conservative guidance; many oils are contraindicated. Always consult your healthcare provider alongside our recommendations.
