Pricing notes & safety

All prices are for aromatherapy consultation and product blending. We avoid medical promises: aromatherapy can support well-being when used responsibly but is not a medical treatment. Dilution, patch-testing, and pet-safety briefing are part of every session. Travel, custom formulas, and private group workshops are quoted separately.

Common questions

Do you offer refunds or cancellations?

Cancellations made more than 48 hours in advance receive a full refund. Within 48 hours, a partial credit may be offered. VIP Day bookings require a 25% deposit; see the booking page for full terms.

What about allergies and skin sensitivity?

We always conduct a brief ingredient review and recommend a patch test for new topicals. All blends come with dilution guidance and clear labeling. If you have a known botanical allergy, tell us at booking.

Are your sessions safe for pets and pregnancy?

Safety is prioritized. Some essential oils are not recommended around cats, dogs, or during pregnancy. We will adapt blends and diffusion recommendations. If you are pregnant or have pets, please disclose this on booking so we can prepare appropriate alternatives.
