FAQ — safety first

Dilution and topical safety
Essential oils are potent. I recommend safe dilution ratios for adults and detailed guidance for children/older adults. Never apply undiluted essential oils to skin. Instructions are provided for each blend.
Patch testing
Always patch-test a new blend on forearm skin for 24 hours before regular use. If redness, itching, or irritation occurs, discontinue and consult a licensed medical professional.
Pets & household members
Some essential oils can be unsafe for pets. I will provide pet-friendly alternatives and clear usage notes to avoid harm. Please inform me of any pets during consultation.
Pregnancy & medical cautions
I avoid recommending certain oils during pregnancy and for specific medical conditions. Aromatherapy is not a substitute for medical care. Always consult your healthcare provider about oil use if pregnant or managing a health condition.
Allergens & sourcing
I source from transparent suppliers and can list botanical names and origins. If you have known sensitivities or allergies, please share them before your session.
