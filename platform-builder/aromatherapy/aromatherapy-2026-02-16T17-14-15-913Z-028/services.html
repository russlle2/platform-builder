Frequently asked — safety & practical

What dilution do you recommend?
I provide dilution charts and will calculate dilutions per client. Typical topical blends range 1–3% for adults; lower for sensitive skin and older adults.
Do I need a patch test?
Yes — for topical use, apply a small diluted amount to the inner forearm for 24 hours and watch for irritation. If redness or irritation occurs, stop use and consult a clinician.
Are oils safe for pets?
Some essential oils affect animals differently. I offer pet-safe alternatives and ventilation guidance; never apply oils directly to pets without veterinary advice.
What about pregnancy?
Pregnancy requires extra caution. I use pregnancy-safe guidelines and will avoid contraindicated notes. Always check with a prenatal provider for medical concerns.
