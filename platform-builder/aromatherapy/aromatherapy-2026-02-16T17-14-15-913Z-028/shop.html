How to use

Products are labelled with suggested uses. For topical roll-ons: follow dilution guidance, do a patch test, and avoid ingestion. Diffuse where ventilation is available; keep away from pets' breathing spaces and avoid direct contact with animals.

FAQ — Shop safety

Do roll-ons need a patch test?
Yes. Apply a tiny amount of the product to the inner arm and observe for 24 hours. Discontinue if irritation occurs.
Are any items pregnancy-safe?
We label pregnancy-safe options clearly. If unsure, consult your prenatal provider and select blends marked as pregnancy-friendly.
What about pets?
Keep diffusers and oils out of reach of pets and avoid diffusing near litter boxes or small pets. We recommend pet-safe alternatives — check product details for guidance.
