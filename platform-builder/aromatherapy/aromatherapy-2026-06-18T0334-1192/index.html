Frequently Asked Questions

How can Aromatherapy specifically support my emotional well-being during postpartum recovery?
Aromatherapy, when carefully applied during postpartum, can offer profound emotional support by influencing the limbic system, which governs emotions and memory. Specific essential oils can help quiet an overactive nervous system, reduce feelings of anxiety or sadness, and encourage a sense of grounding and emotional stability, assisting your body and mind in recalibrating after birth.
Is Aromatherapy safe to use while I am breastfeeding or recovering from birth?
Yes, with proper guidance. Our practice is dedicated to evidence-based, safety-conscious Aromatherapy. We meticulously select and dilute essential oils appropriate for postpartum mothers, including those who are breastfeeding or recovering from various birth experiences. We will always discuss any health considerations to ensure your comfort and safety with every blend.
What does 'trauma-informed' mean in the context of your Aromatherapy practice?
Being 'trauma-informed' means we recognize that past experiences, including birth, can impact your emotional and physical state. Our approach respects your boundaries, promotes a sense of control, and avoids any practices that might re-trigger distress. We focus on creating a safe, supportive environment where you feel empowered to engage with Aromatherapy at your own pace, prioritizing your emotional comfort above all else.
How will you personalize Aromatherapy for my unique postpartum experience?
Your initial consultation involves a compassionate and thorough discussion about your specific emotional landscape, recovery needs, and any sensitivities. Based on this, we will hand-select and blend essential oils known for their therapeutic properties, creating a personalized aromatic formula designed to address your individual challenges—whether that's promoting restful sleep, easing overwhelm, or fostering gentle emotional release.
