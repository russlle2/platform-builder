Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is Aromatherapy?
Aromatherapy is a holistic healing modality that uses natural plant extracts, known as essential oils, to promote health and well-being. It harnesses the power of scent to influence your mood, alleviate discomfort, and support your body's natural healing processes.
How can Aromatherapy benefit me?
Aromatherapy can offer a wide range of benefits, including stress reduction, improved sleep quality, enhanced mood, pain relief, and increased focus. Your specific benefits will depend on your individual needs and the essential oils chosen for your personalized blends.
Are essential oils safe to use?
When used correctly and with proper guidance, essential oils are generally safe. We prioritize your safety by ensuring our essential oils are pure and high-quality, and we provide clear instructions for their safe and effective application, always considering your unique health profile.
What should I expect during an Aromatherapy consultation?
During an Aromatherapy consultation, we'll discuss your health history, lifestyle, and wellness goals. This comprehensive conversation allows us to understand your specific needs and preferences, enabling us to create a truly personalized essential oil blend and recommend the most effective application methods for you.
