Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is Aromatherapy and how can it benefit me?
Aromatherapy is a holistic healing modality that utilizes natural plant extracts, known as essential oils, to promote well-being. It can benefit you by reducing stress, improving sleep, boosting mood, alleviating discomfort, and supporting overall physical and emotional balance, all tailored to your specific needs.
Are the essential oils used in your Aromatherapy practice safe?
Absolutely. Your safety is paramount. We use only high-quality, ethically sourced essential oils and provide clear guidance on their safe and appropriate use. During your consultation, we'll discuss your health history to ensure every recommendation is perfectly suited for you.
How long does an Aromatherapy consultation typically last?
A typical initial Aromatherapy consultation at lasts approximately 60-75 minutes. This allows ample time for us to thoroughly understand your needs, discuss your wellness goals, and create a truly personalized essential oil blend and application strategy for you.
Can Aromatherapy replace my current medical treatments?
Aromatherapy is a complementary therapy and is not intended to replace conventional medical treatment. It works wonderfully alongside your existing healthcare regimen to enhance your well-being. We always advise you to consult with your primary healthcare provider regarding any medical conditions.
