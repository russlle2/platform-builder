Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is Aromatherapy and how can it benefit me?
Aromatherapy is a holistic healing treatment that uses natural plant extracts to promote health and well-being. By inhaling essential oils or applying them topically, you can experience benefits such as reduced stress, improved sleep, enhanced mood, pain relief, and immune support. It's a natural way to support your body's innate healing processes.
Are your Aromatherapy essential oils safe for everyone?
We prioritize your safety and well-being. All our essential oils are therapeutic grade and carefully selected. During your consultation, Practitioner Name will conduct a thorough intake to understand your health history, sensitivities, and any medications you may be taking, ensuring that your personalized aromatherapy blends are safe and appropriate for your unique circumstances.
How long does an Aromatherapy consultation typically last?
A comprehensive initial aromatherapy consultation at Golden Hour Aromatherapy typically lasts between 60 to 90 minutes. This allows ample time for a detailed discussion about your health goals, lifestyle, and preferences, ensuring that Practitioner Name can create a truly bespoke and effective plan tailored just for you.
Can I use Aromatherapy to address specific health concerns?
Yes, aromatherapy can be a powerful complementary approach for addressing a wide range of health concerns, from managing anxiety and chronic pain to improving respiratory function and skin conditions. While not a substitute for medical advice, your personalized aromatherapy plan can offer significant support in achieving your wellness objectives.
