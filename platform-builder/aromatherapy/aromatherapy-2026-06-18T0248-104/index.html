Frequently Asked Questions

What is Aromatherapy and how can it benefit me?
Aromatherapy is a holistic healing treatment that uses natural plant extracts to promote health and well-being. It can benefit you by supporting emotional balance, reducing stress, improving sleep quality, easing discomfort, and enhancing mental clarity through the therapeutic properties of essential oils.
Are essential oils safe for everyone to use?
While essential oils are natural, they are potent and require careful use. During your consultation, we will assess your health history, sensitivities, and any medications you are taking to ensure your personalized aromatherapy recommendations are safe and appropriate for your individual needs. We prioritize your well-being with every blend.
How long does an Aromatherapy consultation typically last?
Your initial Aromatherapy consultation is typically 60-75 minutes, allowing ample time for a comprehensive discussion about your health goals, lifestyle, and preferences. Subsequent follow-up sessions, if needed, are generally shorter, focusing on progress and adjustments to your personalized plan.
Can I purchase pre-made Aromatherapy blends, or are they all custom?
While our signature offering is custom-blended aromatherapy for personalized results, we also provide a curated selection of thoughtfully crafted, ready-to-use blends designed for common needs. We can guide you to the best option, whether it's a bespoke creation or a carefully selected pre-made blend, to support your well-being.
