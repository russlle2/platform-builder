Frequently Asked Questions

How does Aromatherapy specifically address postpartum physical pain?
Aromatherapy utilizes the therapeutic properties of essential oils to interact with your body's systems. For postpartum pain, we select oils known for their analgesic, anti-inflammatory, and antispasmodic effects. These are carefully blended and applied topically or diffused, targeting areas like uterine cramping, perineal discomfort, breast tenderness, or muscle aches, offering a gentle yet effective layer of relief.
Is Aromatherapy safe to use during breastfeeding and around my newborn?
Your safety and your baby's well-being are paramount. We adhere to rigorous safety protocols, ensuring all essential oil selections and dilutions are appropriate for your postpartum phase and compatible with breastfeeding. We prioritize gentle, low-concentration applications and provide clear guidance on diffusion practices to ensure a nurturing and safe environment for both you and your infant.
What kind of somatic healing can I expect from Aromatherapy in postpartum?
Somatic healing in postpartum Aromatherapy focuses on reconnecting you with your body and releasing any physical or energetic tension. Specific aromas can help to down-regulate the nervous system, reduce feelings of overwhelm, and encourage a sense of safety and calm within your physical form. This gentle grounding aids in processing birth experiences and integrating your new identity as a mother, fostering a deeper sense of embodiment.
How are the Aromatherapy blends personalized for my postpartum needs?
Each blend is meticulously crafted after a thorough consultation about your specific postpartum symptoms, birth experience, and current physical and emotional state. We consider any existing health conditions, preferences, and sensitivities. This evidence-based, individualized approach ensures that the Aromatherapy support you receive is precisely tailored to your unique recovery journey, optimizing its therapeutic impact.
