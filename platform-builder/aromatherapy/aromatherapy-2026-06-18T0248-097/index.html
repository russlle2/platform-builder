Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is Aromatherapy?
Aromatherapy is a holistic healing treatment that uses natural plant extracts to promote health and well-being. It utilizes aromatic essential oils medicinally to improve the health of the body, mind, and spirit. It enhances both physical and emotional health through inhalation or topical application.
Is Aromatherapy safe for everyone?
While generally safe, the suitability of Aromatherapy depends on various factors including your health conditions, medications, and age. We conduct a thorough consultation to ensure that any essential oils or blends recommended are appropriate and safe for your specific circumstances. Your well-being is our utmost priority.
How long does it take to see results from Aromatherapy?
The time it takes to experience benefits from Aromatherapy can vary. Some individuals notice improvements almost immediately, while for others, it may take consistent use over several days or weeks. Your commitment to the recommended protocols and the nature of your concerns will influence your unique timeline.
Can Aromatherapy help with stress and anxiety?
Absolutely. Many essential oils possess properties that can significantly help manage stress and anxiety. Oils like lavender, bergamot, and frankincense are frequently used for their calming and mood-balancing effects. We will guide you in selecting the most effective oils and application methods for your stress relief.
