Frequently Asked Questions

How does Aromatherapy specifically support my postpartum recovery?
Our Aromatherapy focuses on regulating your nervous system, promoting restful sleep, and easing emotional fluctuations common in postpartum. We craft blends to support your energetic realignment, helping you feel more centered and connected to your intuition during this profound period of change.
Can these Aromatherapy blends be used safely around my newborn?
Absolutely. Safety is paramount. We exclusively use highly diluted, gentle essential oils and appropriate application methods, always considering your baby's delicate system. Our approach prioritizes passive diffusion and topical applications on your person, ensuring a safe and comforting aromatic environment for both you and your little one.
How can Aromatherapy help with the emotional shifts of new motherhood?
Essential oils possess unique vibrational qualities that interact with your limbic system, influencing mood and emotion. We select oils known for their ability to soothe anxiety, uplift the spirit, and promote emotional resilience, helping you navigate the tender emotional landscape of postpartum with greater grace and stability, fostering a deeper connection to your emotional self.
What kind of commitment is involved in working with your Aromatherapy practice?
Our practice is designed to fit into your new rhythm. We offer initial consultations to understand your unique needs, followed by personalized blend creation and guidance. The commitment is flexible, from single sessions for immediate support to ongoing care as you move through your postpartum journey, always centered on your evolving needs and comfort.
