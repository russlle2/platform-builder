Frequently Asked Questions

How can Aromatherapy specifically help with athletic recovery?
Our Aromatherapy focuses on specific essential oils known to support muscle comfort, reduce the feeling of fatigue, and promote restful sleep, all crucial for an athlete's recovery. We aim to help your body naturally restore itself more efficiently after exertion, preparing you for your next training session or competition.
Is Aromatherapy safe to use alongside my existing training regimen and supplements?
Yes, when used correctly, Aromatherapy can safely complement your current regimen. We always take a detailed intake to understand your unique health profile, existing practices, and any supplements you're using to ensure our recommendations are integrated safely and effectively with your overall wellness plan.
How does seasonal wellness Aromatherapy benefit an active person?
Seasonal wellness Aromatherapy helps your body adapt to environmental changes. For active individuals, this means supporting respiratory health during allergy seasons, promoting immune function in colder months, and aiding hydration and skin health during warmer periods. It's about maintaining consistent performance and comfort throughout the year.
What should I expect during a consultation for preventive Aromatherapy?
During your consultation, we'll discuss your specific athletic goals, current physical stressors, and any seasonal challenges you face. We'll assess your needs and preferences to formulate personalized essential oil blends and application methods designed to proactively support your body and mind, helping you maintain your active lifestyle.
