A little backstory

I began exploring plant aromatics as a way to anchor daily habits — small scent-led checks that help mark transitions and offer moments of calm. Over time that led to formal training and careful study of safety and application. My work centers on building practical rituals and blends that may support steadiness in everyday life.

"The session gave me clear steps I could try at home. The blends were thoughtful and gentle — I appreciated the attention to safety." — A client

Notes on safety

I will always recommend a patch test for topical use, clear dilution instructions (often 1-2% for topical products unless otherwise noted), and specific guidance when pets or pregnancy are present. If you have a medical condition, please consult a healthcare provider alongside sessions here.
