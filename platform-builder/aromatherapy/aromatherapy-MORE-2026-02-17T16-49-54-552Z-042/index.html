Common questions & answers

Are these treatments safe around pets?

We consider animals in every recommendation. Some essential oils can be irritating to pets; we'll adapt blends and discuss safe storage and use. Always keep oils out of reach and consult your vet for specific concerns.

Pregnancy and sensitive skin — what to expect?

We avoid potentially problematic oils for pregnancy and will suggest gentle dilution and a patch test for sensitive skin. Your comfort and safety guide every suggestion.

Do you make blends I can buy?

Yes — a small boutique of ready blends, travel-rolls, and ritual kits are available in the shop. Blends are retail add-ons and come with usage and dilution guidance.
