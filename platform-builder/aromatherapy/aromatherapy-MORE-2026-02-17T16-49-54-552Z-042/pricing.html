Safety & best practices

We prioritize gentle, evidence-informed approaches. Recommendations will always include dilution instructions, a patch test suggestion, and considerations for pets and pregnancy. Essential oils are powerful botanicals; please follow guidance closely.

FAQ

Do you make medical claims?
No. Sessions and blends are intended to support wellbeing and may support calm, focus, or sleep. They are not medical treatments.
What about pets?
We provide guidance to reduce risk around animals. Tell us about pets during your intake.
Pregnancy?
Some oils are not recommended in pregnancy. We'll avoid those and offer safe alternatives.
Do I need a patch test?
A patch test is recommended for topical use—especially for those with sensitive skin or known allergies.
