What to expect in a session

A welcoming conversation to identify preferences and safety considerations, guided sensory practice, and a simple take-home ritual or inhaler. Time is held for questions and practical care ideas. Sessions are non-medical and may support emotional ease.

Safety notes

  • We always recommend a patch test before topical use and gentle dilution guidance.
  • If you are pregnant, breastfeeding, or have a serious medical condition, disclose so we can tailor recommendations.
  • We advise care around pets — some essential oils can affect animals.

Common concerns

Unsure whether aromatherapy is right for you? Start with a short session or a single inhaler. If you have sensitive skin, we’ll provide dilution steps and options for scent-free practices.
