FAQ & safety notes

Dilution & patch testing: Topical blends are shared with suggested dilutions. We always encourage a small patch test (on the forearm) before first topical use.

Pregnancy & nursing: If you are pregnant or nursing, please let us know — we will tailor recommendations and may advise avoiding certain oils.

Pets: Many essential oils can affect pets. We provide pet-safe guidance for at-home use and diffusion choices.

Medical concerns: Aromatherapy is supportive and not a substitute for professional medical care. Our suggestions may support well-being but are not medical advice.
