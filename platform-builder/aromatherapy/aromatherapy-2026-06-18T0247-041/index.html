Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is Aromatherapy and how can it help me?
Aromatherapy is the therapeutic use of aromatic plant extracts, known as essential oils, to promote health and well-being. It can help you by supporting emotional balance, reducing stress, improving sleep, alleviating discomfort, and enhancing your overall sense of vitality, all through their natural chemical properties and aromatic influence.
Are the essential oils used in Aromatherapy safe?
Yes, when used correctly, essential oils in Aromatherapy are generally safe. We prioritize the use of high-quality, pure essential oils and provide you with clear, personalized instructions for safe dilution and application methods. Your safety and well-being are always our top concern, and we will guide you on appropriate usage for your specific needs.
How is a personalized Aromatherapy blend created for me?
Creating your personalized Aromatherapy blend involves a detailed consultation where we discuss your health goals, lifestyle, preferences, and any concerns you may have. Based on this information, we carefully select and combine specific essential oils known for their therapeutic properties that align perfectly with your unique needs, creating a custom formula just for you.
What can I expect during an Aromatherapy consultation?
During an Aromatherapy consultation, you can expect a warm and supportive environment. We will explore your current well-being, discuss your desired outcomes, and identify any areas where you seek support. This collaborative process allows us to understand your individual profile and formulate the most effective and pleasant aromatic solutions for your journey.
