Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is Aromatherapy and how does it work?
Aromatherapy is a holistic healing treatment that uses natural plant extracts to promote health and well-being. It involves inhaling essential oils or applying them topically. The aromatic molecules travel to your brain's limbic system, influencing emotions, memory, and hormone release, while topical application allows for absorption into your bloodstream for systemic benefits.
Is Aromatherapy safe for everyone?
While Aromatherapy is generally safe, it's crucial to use essential oils correctly. Your safety is our priority, and we always recommend a personalized consultation to discuss any health conditions, medications you're taking, or sensitivities you might have. We will guide you on appropriate dilutions and application methods suitable for your individual circumstances, including during pregnancy or for children.
How can Aromatherapy help with stress and anxiety?
Many essential oils possess calming and grounding properties that can significantly help manage stress and anxiety. Oils like lavender, bergamot, and frankincense can promote relaxation, reduce cortisol levels, and encourage a sense of peace. We can create a custom blend and ritual to help you alleviate tension and foster emotional balance in your daily life.
What should I expect during an Aromatherapy consultation?
During your consultation, we'll have a warm, in-depth conversation about your health history, current well-being goals, lifestyle, and any concerns you wish to address. This allows us to understand your unique needs comprehensively. Based on this, we'll formulate a personalized essential oil blend and provide clear guidance on its safe and effective use, ensuring you feel confident and supported.
