Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is Aromatherapy and how can it benefit me?
Aromatherapy is a holistic healing treatment that uses natural plant extracts to promote health and well-being. It can benefit you by alleviating stress, improving mood, enhancing sleep quality, and even supporting physical comfort, depending on the essential oils and methods used.
Are essential oils safe to use, and how do you ensure my safety?
When used correctly and with proper guidance, essential oils are generally safe. We prioritize your safety by using only high-quality, pure essential oils and providing clear instructions for their use, including proper dilution and application methods. We also consider your health history to avoid any contraindications.
How does a personalized Aromatherapy consultation work?
During your personalized Aromatherapy consultation, we will discuss your health goals, lifestyle, and preferences. Based on this comprehensive understanding, we will then formulate a unique essential oil blend and provide recommendations on how to best incorporate Aromatherapy into your daily routine to support your specific needs.
Can Aromatherapy replace my current medical treatments?
Aromatherapy is a complementary therapy and is not intended to replace conventional medical treatments. It can work beautifully alongside your existing healthcare plan to support your overall well-being. We always recommend consulting with your primary healthcare provider for any medical concerns.
