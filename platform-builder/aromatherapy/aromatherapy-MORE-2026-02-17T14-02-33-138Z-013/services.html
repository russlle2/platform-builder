What people often misunderstand

Myth: Aromas fix medical conditions. Truth: Thoughtful scent work may support wellbeing and habit. Sessions always emphasize safety, and orders include dilution and patch-test instructions.

FAQ — safety and use

Are these medical treatments? No. Aromatic practices described here are non-medical and described as practices that may support certain states. They are not a substitute for medical care.

Safety notes: Always perform a patch test. Dilute essential oils appropriately before topical use. Keep essential oils away from eyes. If pregnant, nursing, or under medical care, consult your healthcare provider before use. If you have pets, select oils that are considered pet-safe and use diffusers in ventilated spaces.

Patch test: Apply diluted oil to a small skin area and wait 24 hours for reactions.
