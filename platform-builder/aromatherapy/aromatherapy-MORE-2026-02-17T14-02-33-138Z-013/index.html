Myths and craft

Aromatherapy isn't magic nor a substitute for care — it's a supportive practice that may complement wellbeing.

  • Myth: Essential oils cure illness. Truth: Oils may support comfort and mood when used safely.
  • Myth: More oil equals better results. Truth: Dilution is essential; small amounts are preferred.
  • Myth: All oils are safe for everyone. Truth: Some botanical extracts are contraindicated in pregnancy, for pets, or with sensitivity.

Frequently asked

How do I use oils safely?

Always dilute essential oils in a carrier (typical range 0.5–3% for topical use unless advised). Do a patch test: apply a small diluted amount to the inner forearm and wait 24 hours. Avoid using undiluted on skin.

Are essential oils safe around pets or pregnancy?

Some oils are not recommended during pregnancy or around animals. Share details with your practitioner; we will suggest safe alternatives and protocols that may support your household.

Do you make blends for home?

Yes — personalised blends and small-batch products are available in the shop. Guidance on dilution and use is provided with each blend.
