Common questions

Are aromatics safe for everyone?
We follow a safety-first framework. Some essential oils are not recommended during pregnancy, and others may affect pets; such topics are part of intake so we can adapt recommendations.
What about dilution and patch tests?
Blends are given with dilution guidelines. We suggest a small patch test on the forearm before broader use when introducing a new blend.
Do you make blends for children or pets?
We do design gentle options for older children with parental guidance. We do not recommend direct use on pets — we will suggest pet-safe alternatives and precautions.
Can you make a blend for medication or serious conditions?
We do not make medical claims. Aromatics may support comfort in daily life; we encourage clients to coordinate with healthcare providers for complex or clinical concerns.
