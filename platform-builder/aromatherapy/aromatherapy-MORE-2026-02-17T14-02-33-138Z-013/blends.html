How blends are framed

Blends are described as practices that may support certain subjective states. Labels include dilution ratios, suggested patch-test steps, and notes for households with pets or pregnant persons.

Case notes — what to expect

Scenario

Short inhalation sessions paired with breathwork often feel like a small reset — clients may notice a subtle shift in tone or mood rather than an immediate cure.

Scenario

Topical blends applied with proper dilution serve as ritual support rather than a treatment.

Safety

Patch test: Apply a diluted drop to inner forearm and wait 24 hours. Pregnancy & health: Consult care providers if pregnant, nursing, or immunocompromised. Pets: Some essential oils may be harmful to pets; see full guidance in product notes.
