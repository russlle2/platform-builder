FAQ & safety notes

Are aromatherapy sessions safe?

Sessions are guided with safety-first attention. Recommendations are intended to be supportive and are not medical advice. Please tell the practitioner about allergies, pregnancy, nursing, medications, or respiratory conditions before any in-person or at-home use.

Dilution & patch tests

Topical blends are recommended at conservative dilutions. A patch test is suggested for new blends: apply a small diluted amount to an inner forearm and observe for 24 hours.

Pets & essential oils

Some essential oils can be sensitive for animals. Keep strong diffusions and topical applications away from pets, and consult a veterinarian if in doubt.

Pregnancy & special populations

Certain oils and approaches are not recommended during pregnancy or for infants. Please share pregnancy or breastfeeding status during booking so sessions and blends can be safely tailored.
