Frequently Asked Questions

How does Aromatherapy address the emotional impact of chronic illness or injury?
Our Aromatherapy uses specific essential oils, chosen for their scientifically recognized effects on the limbic system, to help regulate emotions. We focus on compounds that can gently reduce anxiety, mitigate feelings of trauma, and promote a sense of calm and stability, aiding your emotional healing in the context of your physical recovery.
Is Aromatherapy safe for someone with a chronic illness or sensitive health condition?
Yes, your safety is paramount. We conduct a thorough intake to understand your medical history, current medications, and sensitivities. Our approach is always trauma-informed, using very dilute, high-quality essential oils and carefully selected methods of application to ensure comfort and efficacy without exacerbating your condition.
What kind of emotional benefits can I expect from Aromatherapy during my recovery?
You can anticipate support in managing stress, reducing feelings of overwhelm, and fostering a sense of emotional security. Our Aromatherapy aims to help you cultivate internal resources for coping, encouraging moments of peace and aiding in the gentle processing of the emotional experiences tied to your chronic illness or injury.
How long will it take to see emotional improvements with Aromatherapy?
Emotional healing is a process, not an event, especially during recovery from chronic illness or injury. While some individuals report immediate sensations of calm, sustained emotional recalibration typically unfolds over several sessions. We focus on consistent, gentle support to help you gradually build emotional resilience and comfort.
