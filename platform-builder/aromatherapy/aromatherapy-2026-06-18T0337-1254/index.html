Frequently Asked Questions

How does Aromatherapy specifically address the emotional stress faced by athletes?
Aromatherapy directly impacts the limbic system, the brain's emotional center. For athletes, this means we can target specific emotional responses to physical stress, such as performance anxiety, recovery frustration, or the psychological impact of injury. We utilize precise aromatic compounds to help regulate stress hormones and cultivate emotional resilience, supporting your mental game as much as your physical.
Is trauma-informed Aromatherapy different from general Aromatherapy for active individuals?
Yes, profoundly. Trauma-informed Aromatherapy recognizes that physical demands and injuries can be deeply impactful, sometimes even traumatic. We approach your emotional landscape with sensitivity, ensuring that our aromatic interventions do not trigger or overwhelm. Our focus is on creating a sense of safety and agency, allowing you to process and integrate your experiences gently, rather than just providing symptomatic relief.
Can Aromatherapy truly help with the mental blocks or 'burnout' common in high-performance environments?
Absolutely. Mental blocks and burnout often stem from accumulated emotional and physiological stress. Aromatherapy provides a non-invasive pathway to calm an overactive nervous system, reduce mental fatigue, and re-establish focus. By carefully selecting and applying essential oils, we help you clear mental clutter, rekindle your intrinsic motivation, and find renewed clarity to excel without depletion.
How will I integrate Aromatherapy into my demanding training or competition schedule?
We understand your schedule is rigorous. Our approach focuses on seamless integration. We'll co-create simple, effective aromatic rituals that fit into your existing routine – perhaps a pre-training focus blend, a post-competition recovery ritual, or a restorative evening scent. The goal is to provide powerful emotional support without adding complexity, empowering you to maintain your edge.
