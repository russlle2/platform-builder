Frequently Asked Questions

What is Aromatherapy and how can it benefit me?
Aromatherapy is the therapeutic use of aromatic essential oils to promote physical and psychological well-being. It can help you manage stress, improve sleep, uplift your mood, and support various health concerns naturally through inhalation or topical application.
Are your Aromatherapy blends safe for everyone?
We prioritize your safety. During your consultation, we meticulously assess your health history, sensitivities, and any existing conditions to ensure your custom aromatherapy blends are appropriate and safe for your individual use. We always provide clear usage instructions.
How long does an Aromatherapy consultation take?
Your initial Aromatherapy consultation typically lasts between 60 to 90 minutes. This allows us ample time to discuss your wellness goals in depth, understand your preferences, and formulate a truly personalized plan for your aromatic journey.
Can Aromatherapy replace my conventional medication?
Aromatherapy is a complementary therapy and is not intended to replace conventional medical treatment. We encourage you to always consult with your primary healthcare provider regarding any medical conditions or before making changes to your medication regimen. We work alongside your existing healthcare plan to enhance your well-being.
