Frequently Asked Questions

How can Aromatherapy specifically help with my posture as a senior?
Our Aromatherapy focuses on using essential oils known for their ability to soothe muscles and reduce feelings of stiffness. By applying these custom blends, we aim to encourage relaxation in areas that might be pulling your body out of alignment, helping you achieve a more comfortable and upright posture naturally.
Will Aromatherapy make me feel more aware of my body's position?
Absolutely. Certain aromas can enhance your mind-body connection, helping you become more attuned to how you are holding yourself throughout the day. This increased body awareness empowers you to make subtle, conscious adjustments to your posture, fostering greater comfort and preventing common aches.
Is Aromatherapy safe for seniors with various health considerations?
Your safety and well-being are our top priorities. We conduct a thorough consultation to understand your health history and any medications you are taking. All our Aromatherapy blends are carefully diluted and selected to be gentle and effective, ensuring they complement your existing health regimen without adverse effects.
How often should I use Aromatherapy to see improvements in my postural health?
Consistency is key for gentle, lasting change. We recommend a personalized routine that might involve daily application of a custom blend or regular sessions. We'll work together to create a plan that fits comfortably into your lifestyle, supporting your postural health goals at a pace that feels right for you.
