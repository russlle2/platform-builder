Frequently Asked Questions

How can Aromatherapy support my recovery from a chronic illness or injury?
Aromatherapy, when tailored specifically for your recovery journey, can offer gentle support for managing discomfort, promoting relaxation, and encouraging restful sleep. Our approach focuses on specific seasonal influences and your individual physical state to help you feel more balanced and resilient during your healing process.
Is Aromatherapy safe to use with my current medications or therapies?
Absolutely. Your safety is our priority. We always conduct a thorough intake to understand your chronic illness or injury, current medications, and any sensitivities. We then carefully select and blend essential oils that will complement, not conflict with, your existing treatment plan, ensuring a supportive and safe experience.
What kind of Aromatherapy approaches do you use for seasonal wellness?
We integrate both traditional Eastern principles of seasonal harmony and Western scientific understanding of botanical properties. This allows us to craft Aromatherapy strategies that proactively address common seasonal challenges, like changes in energy or mood, which can be particularly impactful when you're managing a chronic condition or injury recovery.
Will I learn how to use Aromatherapy at home for ongoing support?
Yes, a key part of our practice is empowering you with knowledge. We'll guide you on safe and effective ways to incorporate personalized Aromatherapy into your daily life at home. This includes teaching you how to use your custom blends, diffusers, and other tools to maintain your seasonal wellness and support your continuous recovery.
