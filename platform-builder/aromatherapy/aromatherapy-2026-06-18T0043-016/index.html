Frequently Asked Questions

How is your Aromatherapy approach different from typical spa treatments?
We prioritize clinical relevance over sensory experience. Our protocols are based on evidence of how plant compounds interact with human biology—not just scent preferences. Every recommendation considers your health history, medications, and specific wellness objectives for targeted results.
Do I need to be healthy to benefit from Aromatherapy?
Absolutely not. We adapt formulas for all wellness stages—from acute discomfort management to preventative care. Our approach is inclusive, considering chronic conditions, medication interactions, and wellness goals at any point in your journey.
How do you ensure oil quality and safety?
We source only third-party lab-tested, therapeutic-grade essential oils from ethical suppliers. All blends undergo safety verification for recommended dilutions and usage frequencies, with clear guidelines provided for home use. No synthetic additives ever.
How soon will I notice results?
Results vary based on individual physiology and consistency. Many clients report noticeable changes in sleep quality or stress response within 1-3 weeks of consistent use. We track progress through personalized wellness check-ins to adjust formulas as needed for optimal outcomes.
