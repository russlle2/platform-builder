How can Aromatherapy specifically support my postpartum relationship with my partner?

Our Aromatherapy focuses on essences known to reduce stress, enhance emotional connection, and promote restful sleep. By creating a calm atmosphere and offering gentle support for your nervous system, these scents can help you approach your partner with more patience and presence, fostering deeper understanding and rekindling intimacy during this unique phase.

Are the Aromatherapy blends safe to use around my newborn and during breastfeeding?

Yes, your safety and your baby's are paramount. We use only the purest, therapeutic-grade essential oils and create highly diluted, gentle blends specifically formulated with new mothers and infants in mind. We will discuss specific applications and oils to ensure they are appropriate for your individual circumstances and breastfeeding journey.

How does Aromatherapy help me reconnect with myself after giving birth?

The profound changes of postpartum can sometimes make you feel disconnected from your former self. Our bespoke Aromatherapy blends are designed to gently call you back to your inner core, offering scents that evoke self-compassion, emotional balance, and a sense of grounding. This encourages a loving re-acquaintance with your new, powerful self.

What kind of commitment is required for Aromatherapy sessions?

Your postpartum period is unique, and our approach is flexible. We recommend an initial consultation to understand your needs, followed by a personalized plan. This might involve a series of sessions or simply custom blends for home use, tailored to fit seamlessly into your life without adding undue pressure, focusing on gentle, consistent support.
