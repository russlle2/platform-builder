How long until I notice results from my blend?

Most clients feel subtle shifts within 24-48 hours of consistent use. For deeper physiological changes (like improved sleep patterns), 2-4 weeks of targeted application is typical. We track progress through your personalized wellness journal.

Are essential oils safe for children or seniors?

Yes, but safety depends on formulation. We use age-specific dilution protocols and avoid certain oils (like peppermint for under 3s). All blends are vetted for age-appropriate safety—no 'natural' assumption.

Do you use carrier oils in your blends?

Always. We select cold-pressed, unrefined carriers (like jojoba or sweet almond) that complement your skin type and the essential oils’ properties. Never mineral oil or synthetic bases—this isn’t perfume, it’s therapeutic delivery.

How is your approach different from a spa?

Spas treat symptoms; we address root causes through science-based protocols. All our blends are documented, dosage-tailored, and evaluated for efficacy. No 'one size fits all' lavender-sleep routine—only what your body actually needs.
