Safety FAQs

How do you recommend diluting oils?
Dilution is individualized. Common guidelines: 0.5–1% for sensitive adults or older adults, 1–3% for general adult use, and lower for children. Recommendations are part of consults and product labels.
What is a patch test and why do one?
A patch test identifies sensitivity. Apply diluted oil to a small skin patch, wait 24 hours, and monitor. If irritation occurs, discontinue use.
Are oils safe around pets?
Pet safety depends on the animal and exposure. I provide guidance for minimal-risk diffusion and recommend consultation for any concerns about senior or medically fragile animals.
Pregnancy or breastfeeding considerations?
I follow conservative guidance and avoid recommending certain oils during pregnancy or breastfeeding. Please mention pregnancy during intake so I can tailor recommendations.
