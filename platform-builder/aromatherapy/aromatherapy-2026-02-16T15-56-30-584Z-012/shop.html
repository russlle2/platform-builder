Common shopping questions

Dilution guidance

Each topical product lists the dilution on the label. Our roll-ons are crafted for adult use. If you prefer different concentrations, book the custom blend service.

Patch tests

Use a 24–48 hour patch test with small amounts of product on inner forearm. Discontinue if redness or irritation occurs.
