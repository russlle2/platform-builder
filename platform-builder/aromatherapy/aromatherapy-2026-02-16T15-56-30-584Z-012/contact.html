Common questions

How should I dilute essential oils?
Dilution depends on purpose and age. Typical adults: 1–2% for daily body application and topical roll-ons; for children or sensitive skin we use lower dilutions. At your session you'll receive a clear dilution chart and recommended carrier oils. This is educational guidance, not medical advice.
What is a patch test and why is it needed?
A patch test checks for skin sensitivity. Apply a small diluted sample to an area of inner forearm and observe for 24–48 hours. If irritation occurs, discontinue. We will walk you through this during blending.
Are essential oils safe around pets?
Some oils can be problematic for pets. We always discuss pet safety and recommend pet-friendly blends or alternative use methods (diffusion schedules, closed containers, or avoidance) depending on your household.
Can I use aromatherapy during pregnancy?
Pregnancy requires extra caution. We offer pregnancy-aware recommendations and avoid certain oils. Any guidance provided is educational; consult your healthcare provider for medical advice.
