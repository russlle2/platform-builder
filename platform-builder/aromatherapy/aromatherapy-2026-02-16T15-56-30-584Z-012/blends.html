Signature Blends — Notes & safe use tips

A curated library of blends with top/middle/base notes, aroma profiles, and non-medical "supports" statements. All formulas include dilution and safety prompts.

Safety reminder:
These are non-medical suggestions. Always patch test, follow dilution guidance, avoid use with contraindications, and keep essential oils away from children and pets unless specifically indicated as safe.

Blend library

Morning Clarity

Top: Lemon, Bergamot — Middle: Rosemary — Base: Vetiver

A bright citrus-herbal profile. Supports alertness for morning routines. Suggested dilution: 2–3% for adults.

Gentle Focus

Top: Sweet Orange — Middle: Clementine, Lavender — Base: Cedarwood

Sweet-citrus with soft floral heart. Supports steady attention without overstimulation. Avoid direct use for cats; use diffused briefly.

Evening Unwind

Top: Mandarin — Middle: Lavender — Base: Chamomile Roman

Calming, soft floral-citrus. Supports an evening ritual. Use 1–2% dilution for topical sleep roll-ons.

Ground & Center

Top: Bergamot — Middle: Frankincense — Base: Patchouli

Resinous and earthy. Supports moments of grounding and intentional breathwork. Not recommended for pregnancy without tailored substitution.

Breath Ease (diffuse)

Top: Eucalyptus Radiata — Middle: Tea Tree — Base: Ravensara

Camphorous fresh profile for clear, open aroma in air. Use short 30-60 minute diffusions and avoid near pets unless pet-safe alternatives are advised.

Joyful Lift

Top: Grapefruit — Middle: Ylang Ylang — Base: Benzoin

Bright, floral-sweet. Supports mood-boosting moments. Safe topical use at low dilution; avoid during pregnancy unless approved.

Calm Belly

Top: Ginger — Middle: Cardamom — Base: Fennel

Warm-spicy aromatic profile. Supports digestive comfort rituals; apply topically with low dilution to abdomen only if comfortable and after patch testing.

Evening Ritual Roll-on

Top: Sweet Marjoram — Middle: Lavender — Base: Copaiba

A gentle balm-like aroma for pre-bed routines. Designed as a topical roll-on at ~2% dilution for adults.

Refresh & Reset

Top: Peppermint — Middle: Spearmint — Base: Basil

Crisp, minty profile for a midday reset. Keep away from young children and use diluted for topical application.

Cozy Hearth

Top: Orange Peel — Middle: Cinnamon Leaf — Base: Clove

Warm and spicy, reminiscent of cozy kitchens. Use in diffuser intermittently; reduce intensity if sensitive.

How to use these recipes safely

  • Always perform a patch test for topical use.
  • Standard adult dilution guidance is included with each blend; reduce concentrations for sensitive skin and older adults.
  • When diffusing, limit sessions to 30–60 minutes and ventilate the space.
  • For pregnancy, breastfeeding, medical conditions, or pets, consult before use — personalized substitutions are recommended.
