Common questions — safety-focused answers

How should I dilute essential oils?
Dilution depends on age, sensitivity, and intended use. Typical adult topical dilutions range from 1–3% for daily wear. For a 1% dilution, mix 6 drops of essential oil into 30 ml (1 oz) of carrier oil. I provide personalized dilution guidance during consults.
What is a patch test?
A patch test helps identify skin sensitivity. Apply a small amount of diluted blend to an inner forearm, cover for 24 hours, and observe for irritation. Stop use immediately if significant redness or discomfort occurs.
Are essential oils safe with pets?
Some oils can affect animals. Safety depends on species, concentration, and exposure. During consultations I provide pet-safe suggestions and diffusion practices to minimize risk.
What about pregnancy and breastfeeding?
Some oils are not recommended during pregnancy or breastfeeding. I follow conservative, safety-first guidance and will tailor recommendations if you are pregnant, breastfeeding, or planning pregnancy.
