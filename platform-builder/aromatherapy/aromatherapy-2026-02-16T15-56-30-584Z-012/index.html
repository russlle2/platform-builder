Questions people ask

Dilution depends on product type and intended use. A common guideline is 1-3% for adults in roll-ons and topical blends; lower for children, older adults, and during pregnancy. Exact recommendations are provided during sessions. This is educational information, not medical advice.
A patch test checks for skin sensitivity. Apply a small diluted sample to inner forearm, cover for 24 hours, and observe for irritation. If redness, itching, or burning occurs, discontinue use and discuss alternatives.
Many oils can be irritating to animals. We cover pet-safe options and avoid recommending topical or diffuser use that might harm sensitive species. Always consult your veterinarian for pet-specific concerns.
Some oils are not recommended during pregnancy. We provide pregnancy-aware recommendations and will offer alternatives. This guidance is precautionary and educational — consult your healthcare provider for medical advice.
