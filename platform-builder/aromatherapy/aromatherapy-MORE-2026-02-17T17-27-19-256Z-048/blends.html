Dilution quickchart

Common conversions we use:

  • 1% ≈ 6 drops per 30 ml
  • 2% ≈ 12 drops per 30 ml
  • 3% ≈ 18 drops per 30 ml

Adjust down for children, older adults, or sensitive skin. Always include a written patch-test reminder with blends.

Safety notes

If pregnant, nursing, or under medical care consult a qualified provider. Some oils may be hazardous to pets—seek veterinary guidance. This information is educational and not a substitution for medical advice.
