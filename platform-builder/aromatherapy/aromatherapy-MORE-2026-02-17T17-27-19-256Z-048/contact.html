FAQ — Safety & common questions

How should I dilute oils?

Dilution depends on application. Common guides: 1% for facial/near-sensitive skin, 2-3% for general topical use, 5% for short-term spot application. For diffuser/inhalation use only a few drops in water or inhaler wick. Always follow a patch test and keep blends away from eyes. This is not medical advice; it may support safe practice.

Pets & pregnancy?

Some essential oils are not recommended for pregnancy or around pets. We review these contexts before recommending blends and will offer safe substitutes.

Patch test?

Always test a small diluted amount on inner forearm for 24 hours. If irritation occurs, discontinue and consult a qualified practitioner.

Are these medical treatments?

No. Aromatherapy suggestions may support wellbeing but are not medical treatments. Consult your healthcare provider for medical concerns.
