FAQ — safety and common questions

Do these sessions treat medical conditions?

No. Aromatherapy in these sessions is educational and supportive and may support wellbeing. It is not medical care. Seek licensed medical advice for diagnosable conditions.

What about dilution and patch testing?

All blend cards include recommended dilution ranges and a simple patch test: apply a small amount of diluted blend to an inner forearm and wait 24 hours. If irritation occurs, discontinue use.

Are there pet or pregnancy cautions?

Yes. Some oils may affect pets or be contraindicated during pregnancy. Any blend advice is safety-forward. Inform me if you are pregnant, breastfeeding, or have pets so recommendations can be adjusted.

How should I store blends?

Store blends upright in dark glass containers, away from heat and sunlight, and label with the dilution date. Use within a recommended timeframe included on each card.
