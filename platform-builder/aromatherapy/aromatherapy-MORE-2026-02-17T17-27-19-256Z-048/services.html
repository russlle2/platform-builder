Frequently asked — safety first

Dilution guidance
Typical guidance we use: 1% = ~6 drops per 30ml carrier; 2% = ~12 drops per 30ml; 3% = ~18 drops per 30ml. We always provide a written dilution chart for your take-away blend.

Patch test
We recommend a patch test before topical use: dilute recommended amount and test a small skin area for 24 hours. Discontinue if any irritation occurs.

Pregnancy & nursing
If you are pregnant, nursing, or under medical care, consult your healthcare provider. We avoid certain oils during pregnancy and will recommend appropriate alternatives.

Pets
Some essential oils can be problematic for pets. We provide pet-aware guidance and suggest veterinary consultation for household safety.
