Common questions — safety notes

Are these medical services?

No. Aromatherapy offerings here are educational and supportive in nature. Language such as "may support" is used because these are not diagnoses or medical treatments.

What about dilution and skin safety?

All blend recommendations include conservative dilution guidance and a patch test suggestion. Typical adult topical dilutions shown will be 0.5%–3% depending on age and context; during pregnancy, for infants, or with significant medical conditions, dilution guidance will be more conservative and some oils may be avoided.

Pets and essential oils?

Some oils are not recommended around pets. If you live with companion animals, we review safe use and ventilation, and provide pet-safe alternatives.

Cancelation and refunds

Appointments may be canceled up to 48 hours in advance for a full refund. Kits and shop items have a separate return policy available on the shop page.
