Why I work with scent

I blend botanical tradition with modern safety practices. My approach is curious and evidence-minded: we co-create scent guides that may support relaxation, focus, or sleep. Everything begins with screening and a cautious dilution framework so your at-home use is practical and safe.

The process I follow

  1. Discovery: intake + safety screening.
  2. Design: I craft a small set of blends and a simple ritual card you can follow at home.
  3. Practice: we check in and adapt the blends across visits.

Values

Safety-first, gentle experimentation, transparency, and a focus on usable tools for daily life. Sessions are supportive, not medical.

FAQ — safety highlights

Patch testing: Always test a small diluted amount on inner forearm and wait 24 hours for irritation signs before regular use.

Pregnancy & nursing: We avoid certain botanicals and use markedly lower dilutions. Please disclose pregnancy or nursing during intake.

Pets: Some essential oils are not recommended around cats and some dogs. We provide alternatives and ventilation guidelines.
