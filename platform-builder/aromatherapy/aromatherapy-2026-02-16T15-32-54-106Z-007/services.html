Frequently asked — practice safety

How do you handle dilution?

I provide dilution tables and prep guidance for each blend. For topical blends I typically recommend 1–3% for adults unless otherwise discussed.

Do I need a patch test?

Yes — patch testing is recommended for new blends. Instructions are provided with every bespoke order.

What about pets and pregnancy?

I offer pet-aware and pregnancy-aware alternatives. We avoid certain notes and delivery methods; full safety guidance given in consults.
