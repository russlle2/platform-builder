Curated blends — recipes for ritual

Safety & guidance

Dilution: Typical topical dilutions range from 0.5–3% for adults depending on sensitivity and use. Children, pregnancy, and reactive skin require tailored, lower dilutions.

Patch test: Always perform a patch test for topical applications. Apply a diluted amount to the inner forearm and wait 24 hours for any reaction.

Pregnancy & pets: Certain oils are avoided during pregnancy and some can be harmful to animals. If you are pregnant or have pets at home, book a consult so we can choose pet- and pregnancy-safe alternatives.

I do not make medical claims. These blends are offered to support rituals and sensory comfort; they are not medical treatments.
