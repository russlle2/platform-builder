FAQ highlights

How should I dilute oils?
We recommend safe dilution ranges in each recipe—typically 1–3% for adults for topical use unless otherwise advised. Always do a patch test first.
What is a patch test?
Apply a diluted drop on inner forearm and wait 24 hours to watch for irritation. If redness or discomfort occurs, discontinue use.
Are oils safe for pets?
Some essential oils are not pet-safe. We always provide pet-specific guidance; never diffuse or apply without consultation if pets are present.
Pregnancy and nursing?
Certain oils are not recommended during pregnancy or breastfeeding. Please consult your care provider and mention pregnancy when contacting us.
