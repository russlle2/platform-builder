FAQ — safety reminders

Dilution: You will receive recommended dilution ratios for topical blends; follow them closely and use appropriate carrier oils.

Patch test: Always patch-test a new blend on a small skin area 24–48 hours before regular use.

Pregnancy & nursing: Some oils are not recommended. Please disclose pregnancy or nursing in your booking notes so we can adjust suggestions.

Pets: Scented products can affect animals. Share details about household pets and we will suggest pet-aware alternatives and ventilation guidance.

Before your session

  • Please avoid wearing strong perfumes to in-person appointments.
  • Bring a list of current medications and any known allergies.
  • If you are pregnant or nursing, note this on the booking form—no oils will be recommended without a tailored conversation.
