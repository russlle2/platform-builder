Myths unraveled, with care

Aromatherapy carries stories. Here we sort folklore from safe practice—gentle, clear, and client-centered.

Myth: Essential oils cure illnesses

Truth: Oils are supportive tools for comfort, ritual, and wellbeing. They are not a replacement for medical care; we stay safety-first and evidence-informed.

Myth: Natural always means safe

Truth: "Natural" is not a guarantee. Proper dilution, patch-testing, and attention to pregnancy, meds, and pets make use safer.

Myth: More makes it stronger

Truth: With scent, less is more. We prioritize mindful dosing and guided application over heavy fragrance.

Frequently asked (safety-focused)

What dilution should I use?

We recommend 0.5–2% for general use with adults depending on application. Children and sensitive skin require lower dilutions. As a membership benefit, we provide dilution charts for common applications.

How do I patch test?

Apply one drop of a 1% dilution on the inside forearm, cover, and observe for 24–48 hours. If redness, itching, or irritation appears, discontinue and consult your practitioner.

Are essential oils safe around pets?

Many oils can affect pets—especially cats and birds. We give pet-safe diffusion protocols, limit exposure time, and list oils to avoid. Always monitor your animal and consult a veterinarian for specific concerns.

What about pregnancy and breastfeeding?

Some oils are contraindicated in pregnancy or require specialist approval. We ask pregnant or breastfeeding clients to share status before any recommendation and rely on up-to-date safety references.

Can I use oils with medications?

Certain oils may interact or be inadvisable with some medications. We take a medication history during intake and advise consulting a prescriber when in doubt.

Safety note: The information here is educational and not medical advice. If you have a health condition, please consult a healthcare professional.
