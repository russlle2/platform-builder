FAQ — safety notes for shoppers

Patch testing: Recommended for topical products. Apply a dab of diluted product to inner arm and wait 24 hours.

Pets & pregnancy: Some oils are not recommended around pets or during pregnancy. Product pages will flag common cautions; contact us for specific guidance.
