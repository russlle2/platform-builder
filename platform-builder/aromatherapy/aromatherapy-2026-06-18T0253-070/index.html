Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is Aromatherapy and how can it benefit me?
Aromatherapy is a holistic healing modality that utilizes natural plant extracts, known as essential oils, to promote well-being. It can support your emotional balance, physical comfort, and mental clarity. We use these aromatic compounds to create personalized experiences that resonate with your body's innate ability to heal and restore.
Are the essential oils you use safe for sensitive skin or allergies?
Your safety is our utmost priority. We exclusively use high-quality, therapeutic-grade essential oils and always conduct a thorough intake to understand your sensitivities and allergies. Custom blends are formulated with appropriate dilution ratios, and we will guide you on patch testing to ensure complete comfort and safety with your personalized products.
How long does it take to experience the effects of Aromatherapy?
The experience of aromatherapy is unique to each individual. Some clients report feeling immediate shifts in mood or comfort, while others notice more subtle, cumulative benefits over time. Consistency in using your custom blends as recommended is key to fully experiencing the profound and lasting effects on your well-being.
Can Aromatherapy complement my existing wellness practices?
Absolutely. Aromatherapy is wonderfully complementary to many wellness practices, including meditation, yoga, massage, and even traditional therapies. It can enhance relaxation, deepen focus, and provide additional support for your overall health journey. We can discuss how our blends can seamlessly integrate with your current routine.
