What exactly is Aromatherapy and how can it help me?

Aromatherapy is a holistic healing modality that uses natural plant extracts, known as essential oils, to promote health and well-being. It can help you address a wide range of concerns, from stress reduction and sleep improvement to boosting mood and supporting physical comfort, by engaging your sense of smell and the physiological effects of these powerful botanicals.

Are the essential oils used in your Aromatherapy practice safe?

Absolutely. We are committed to your safety and well-being. We use only high-quality, therapeutic-grade essential oils from trusted suppliers. During your consultation, we'll discuss your health history and any sensitivities to ensure that the blends created for you are safe and appropriate for your individual needs.

How long does a typical Aromatherapy consultation last?

A typical initial Aromatherapy consultation lasts approximately 60-90 minutes. This allows us ample time to thoroughly understand your health goals, lifestyle, and preferences, ensuring we can develop the most effective and personalized aromatic strategy for your well-being journey.

Can Aromatherapy be combined with other wellness practices I already use?

Yes, Aromatherapy is wonderfully complementary to many other wellness practices. It can enhance the benefits of massage, meditation, yoga, and even conventional therapies by promoting relaxation, reducing stress, and supporting emotional balance. We can discuss how to best integrate aromatherapy into your current routine during your session.
