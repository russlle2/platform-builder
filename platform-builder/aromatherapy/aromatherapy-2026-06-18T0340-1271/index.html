Frequently Asked Questions

How does Aromatherapy specifically address the emotional needs of seniors 60+?
Our Aromatherapy approach for those in their seasoned years focuses on gentle, non-invasive methods. We use specific essential oils known for their calming and uplifting properties, carefully chosen to support emotional balance, ease anxieties, and gently encourage the release of past burdens without overwhelming your senses.
Is Aromatherapy safe if I have existing health conditions or take medications?
Your well-being is our utmost priority. We conduct a thorough intake process to understand your health history, current medications, and sensitivities. This allows us to select and dilute essential oils appropriately, ensuring that your Aromatherapy experience is both safe and beneficial for your unique constitution.
What can I expect during an initial Aromatherapy consultation for emotional healing?
During your first consultation, we will engage in a compassionate conversation about your emotional landscape, life experiences, and any specific areas where you seek support. This unhurried discussion helps us to understand your needs deeply, allowing us to co-create a personalized Aromatherapy plan that honors your individual journey.
How long does it take to feel the effects of Aromatherapy for emotional well-being?
The experience of Aromatherapy is deeply personal and unfolds at its own pace. While some individuals report an immediate sense of calm and comfort, the deeper emotional shifts often emerge gradually with consistent, gentle engagement. We encourage patience and mindful observation as your inner world responds to the aromatic support.
