Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I use my custom Aromatherapy blend?
Frequency depends on your specific formulation. Most daily support blends are used 1-2 times per day, while seasonal immunity blends may be applied more frequently during high-risk periods. We provide clear usage guidelines during your consultation.
Are your essential oils suitable for sensitive skin?
Yes, all oils are properly diluted to skin-safe concentrations (typically 2-5% in carrier oils). We also assess your skin sensitivity during consultations and recommend non-irritating options when needed.
Can I use Aromatherapy during pregnancy?
We create pregnancy-safe blends after thorough assessment. Certain oils are avoided entirely during specific trimesters, while others provide safe support for common pregnancy discomforts. All pregnancy-related formulations undergo additional safety review.
How should I store my essential oil blends?
Keep in dark glass bottles away from direct sunlight and heat. Store upright in a cool, dry place (not in the bathroom). Most blends maintain efficacy for 12-18 months when stored properly, with specific guidance provided for each formulation.
