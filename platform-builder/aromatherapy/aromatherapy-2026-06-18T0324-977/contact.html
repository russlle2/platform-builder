Aromatherapy

Embracing Seasonal Aromatherapy for New Mothers

As a new mother, your body is undergoing immense changes. Our aromatherapy approach acknowledges that your needs shift with the seasons, just as nature does. We focus on natural plant essences to support your energy, sleep, and emotional equilibrium during postpartum, helping you align with the rhythms of spring, summer, autumn, and winter for sustained well-being. This isn't about quick fixes, but about building resilience.
