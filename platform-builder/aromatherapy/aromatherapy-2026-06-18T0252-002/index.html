Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is Aromatherapy and how can it benefit me?
Aromatherapy is the therapeutic use of aromatic plant extracts and essential oils for psychological and physical well-being. It can support your mood, reduce stress, improve sleep, alleviate discomfort, and generally enhance your holistic health by working with your body's natural systems.
Are essential oils safe to use, and how do you ensure quality?
When used correctly, essential oils are safe. We prioritize your safety by using only 100% pure, high-quality essential oils sourced from reputable suppliers. During your consultation, we'll discuss proper dilution, application methods, and any contraindications relevant to your health profile to ensure safe and effective use.
What should I expect during my first Aromatherapy consultation?
Your initial consultation is a comprehensive conversation where we discuss your health history, current concerns, and wellness aspirations. We'll explore your preferences and sensitivities to scents, and together, we'll develop a personalized Aromatherapy strategy, which often includes recommendations for custom blends and usage guidelines.
How long does it take to see results from Aromatherapy?
The experience of Aromatherapy is highly individual. Some people notice immediate effects, such as a shift in mood or a sense of calm, while others experience more gradual changes over time. Consistency in applying your personalized recommendations is key to achieving the best and most lasting benefits.
