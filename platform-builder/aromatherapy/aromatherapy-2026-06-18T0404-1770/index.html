Frequently Asked Questions

How can Aromatherapy specifically support my postpartum emotional well-being?
Our Aromatherapy uses carefully selected essential oils known for their profound emotional support, helping to ease feelings of overwhelm, anxiety, or sadness common in the postpartum period. These scents create a fragrant anchor, gently guiding your nervous system towards calm and fostering a sense of inner peace, allowing you to connect more deeply with yourself and your baby.
Can Aromatherapy help rekindle connection with my partner during this new phase?
Absolutely. We craft bespoke Aromatherapy blends and suggest gentle rituals designed to create an inviting atmosphere for connection. Certain aromas can reduce stress, enhance mood, and foster a sense of intimacy and presence, helping you and your partner navigate the shifts of new parenthood with greater understanding and tenderness.
Is Aromatherapy safe for me and my baby during postpartum and breastfeeding?
Your safety and your baby's well-being are paramount. We adhere to the highest safety standards in Aromatherapy, using only pure, therapeutic-grade essential oils and providing clear guidance on appropriate dilution and application methods suitable for new mothers, including those who are breastfeeding. We prioritize gentle, supportive approaches.
What kind of Aromatherapy services do you offer for new mothers?
We offer personalized consultations to understand your unique postpartum needs, custom-blended aromatic remedies for emotional support and relationship nurturing, and guidance on incorporating daily scent rituals into your routine. Our focus is on providing gentle, heart-centered care to support your recovery and strengthen your familial bonds.
