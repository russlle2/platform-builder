Frequently Asked Questions

How does Aromatherapy specifically address the physical stress experienced by athletes?
For athletes, Aromatherapy targets physical stress by utilizing essential oils with documented properties that reduce inflammation, alleviate muscle soreness, and support tissue repair. We formulate blends to penetrate deeply, easing the strain on your musculoskeletal system and promoting efficient recovery from training and competition.
Can Aromatherapy help with chronic pain or recurring issues from athletic activity?
Yes, Aromatherapy offers a complementary approach to managing chronic pain and recurring issues. By addressing underlying inflammatory responses and calming nerve pathways, our specialized blends can provide consistent relief and assist your body in breaking cycles of discomfort, promoting a more stable state of physical well-being.
What does 'somatic healing' mean in the context of Aromatherapy for active individuals?
Somatic healing, in our practice, refers to addressing the body's stored emotional and physical tensions that often accumulate with intense physical demands. Aromatherapy, through its profound influence on the limbic system, helps you access deeper states of relaxation, allowing for the gentle release of these ingrained stressors and fostering a sense of integrated physical and mental ease.
How are Aromatherapy blends customized for my specific physical needs and activity level?
Your Aromatherapy blends are meticulously customized after a thorough consultation about your specific athletic endeavors, areas of physical stress, and recovery goals. We carefully select and combine essential oils based on their scientific properties and synergistic effects, ensuring the formulation directly addresses your unique physiological and somatic requirements.
