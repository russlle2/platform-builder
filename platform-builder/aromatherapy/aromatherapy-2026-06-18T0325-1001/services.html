Aromatherapy

The Deliberate Art of Aromatherapy in Recovery

Chronic conditions and recovery periods often bring heightened states of alert, fatiguing the body and mind. Our approach to Aromatherapy is not about masking, but about providing targeted, subtle cues to your nervous system. Each aromatic selection is made with careful consideration of its physiological impact, aiming to reduce hyper-vigilance and encourage a state of grounded repose, fostering an environment conducive to healing.
