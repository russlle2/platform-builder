Frequently Asked Questions

How does Aromatherapy specifically help with chronic illness or injury recovery?
Aromatherapy, when precisely applied, can influence the limbic system, which is deeply connected to emotion, memory, and physiological responses like heart rate and stress. For those in recovery, certain aromas can signal safety and calm to the nervous system, helping to reduce pain perception, mitigate anxiety, and promote a more restorative state, which is crucial for healing.
Are the Aromatherapy blends safe for sensitive individuals or those on medication?
Yes, safety is paramount. always conducts a thorough intake to understand your medical history, current medications, and any known sensitivities. All aromatherapy blends are formulated with appropriate dilutions and specific essential oils known for their gentle profiles. We prioritize your well-being and ensure compatibility, avoiding any contraindications.
What kind of results can I expect from using Aromatherapy for nervous system regulation?
While individual experiences vary, clients often report a noticeable reduction in perceived stress, improved sleep quality, a greater sense of calm, and enhanced ability to cope with discomfort. The goal is to gently guide your nervous system towards a more regulated state, fostering an internal environment that supports your recovery and overall sense of peace.
