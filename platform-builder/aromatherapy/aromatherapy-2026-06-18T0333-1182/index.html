Frequently Asked Questions

How does trauma-informed Aromatherapy specifically help new mothers in postpartum recovery?
Trauma-informed Aromatherapy acknowledges that birth can be a profound, sometimes overwhelming experience. We select essential oils known for their ability to calm the nervous system, reduce hypervigilance, and foster a sense of safety and grounding. Our approach gently supports emotional integration and helps mitigate the lingering effects of stress or discomfort, recognizing the delicate emotional state of new mothers.
Are the essential oils used in Aromatherapy safe for me while I'm recovering postpartum, especially if I'm breastfeeding?
Absolutely. Your safety and your baby's well-being are paramount. We meticulously choose essential oils that are generally considered safe for postpartum use and breastfeeding, always diluting them appropriately. We will discuss your individual health history and any concerns during our consultation to ensure every blend is tailored with the utmost care and caution.
Can Aromatherapy help with the emotional fluctuations and 'baby blues' I'm experiencing?
Yes, many new mothers find profound comfort and balance through Aromatherapy during this period. We utilize essential oils known for their mood-balancing and uplifting properties, helping to gently ease feelings of sadness, anxiety, and irritability often associated with the 'baby blues.' Our aim is to provide aromatic anchors that support emotional equilibrium and foster a sense of peace.
What can I expect from my first Aromatherapy consultation for postpartum emotional healing?
Your first consultation is a warm, gentle conversation where we listen deeply to your postpartum experience. We'll explore your emotional landscape, any physical discomforts, and your hopes for well-being. Based on this understanding, we'll begin to craft a personalized Aromatherapy plan, discussing specific essential oils and ways to integrate them into your daily routine to support your emotional healing and comfort.
