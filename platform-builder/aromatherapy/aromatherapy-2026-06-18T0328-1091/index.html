Frequently Asked Questions

How does Aromatherapy specifically address burnout-related sleep issues?
Aromatherapy for burnout-related sleep issues focuses on calming an overactive nervous system, often in a state of 'fight or flight.' We use specific essential oils known for their neurochemical effects, such as reducing cortisol and promoting GABA activity, which helps to slow down racing thoughts and prepare your body for deep, restorative sleep, rather than just superficial rest.
Is the Aromatherapy approach at evidence-based for sleep improvement?
Yes, our approach is rigorously evidence-based. We select essential oils and application methods that have demonstrated efficacy in clinical studies for their impact on sleep physiology, anxiety reduction, and nervous system regulation. We provide you with clear, scientific explanations for why certain botanicals are chosen for your specific sleep challenges, ensuring a data-informed path to better rest.
How long does it typically take to see improvements in sleep from your Aromatherapy protocols?
The timeline for improvement varies, as each individual's sleep challenges and response to botanicals are unique. However, many clients report noticing subtle shifts in sleep onset and quality within 1-2 weeks of consistently implementing their personalized Aromatherapy protocol. Significant, sustained improvements in restorative rest often emerge over 4-6 weeks as your body and mind adapt to the new sleep patterns.
Will Aromatherapy interfere with any medications I currently take for sleep or stress?
As a responsible practice, your safety is paramount. We conduct a thorough intake process, including a detailed review of your current medications and health conditions. While Aromatherapy is generally safe, certain essential oils can interact with medications. We will always discuss potential interactions and advise you to consult with your physician. Our goal is to complement, not conflict with, your existing healthcare regimen, ensuring a holistic and safe approach to your sleep improvement.
