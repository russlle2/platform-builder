FAQ — Safety & guidance

Dilution & patch testing

Always dilute essential oils for topical use. We recommend a patch test: dilute to suggested proportions and wait 24 hours. Our sessions provide clear dilution guidance that may support gentle use.

Pets

Many oils are not recommended around companion animals. Mention the species and sensitivity and we will suggest pet-aware options that place safety first.

Pregnancy & nursing

If pregnant or nursing, tell us before receiving guidance. We will offer safety-first ideas and will avoid any strong or contraindicated suggestions.

Medical disclaimers

Aromatherapy may support ordinary wellbeing for some people, but it is not a substitute for medical care. We do not make therapeutic claims.
