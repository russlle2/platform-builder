Frequently Asked Questions

How can Aromatherapy specifically help my nervous system during recovery from illness or injury?
Aromatherapy, through specific essential oils, can interact with your limbic system, the part of your brain responsible for emotions and memory. This can help modulate stress responses, reduce feelings of anxiety, and encourage a sense of calm and safety, all vital for a nervous system that's been under strain during your recovery.
I'm sensitive to many things since my illness/injury. Is Aromatherapy safe for me?
Your sensitivity is precisely why our approach is gentle and personalized. We use highly diluted, therapeutic-grade essential oils and always start with very subtle applications. We will discuss your sensitivities and any medical considerations thoroughly to ensure your comfort and safety, crafting blends that are truly nurturing for your delicate system.
Do I need to 'believe' in Aromatherapy for it to work for my stress reduction?
No, you don't need to 'believe' in anything. The physiological responses to scent are often subconscious. Our focus is on creating an environment and introducing aromas that scientifically support nervous system regulation and stress reduction. Your only requirement is a willingness to experience the gentle support we offer.
How can Aromatherapy be integrated into my daily routine without feeling overwhelming during my recovery?
We understand that your energy might be limited. We focus on simple, achievable methods for integrating Aromatherapy, such as a few drops in a diffuser during quiet time, a gentle roll-on for pulse points before sleep, or an aromatic mist for your pillow. The goal is to create supportive rituals that feel like a soft embrace, not another task.
