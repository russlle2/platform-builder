Aromatherapy

Aromatherapy for a Quiet Nervous System

After the demands of illness or injury, your nervous system can feel perpetually on edge. Our Aromatherapy approach focuses on offering soft, natural cues that invite deep relaxation. We use carefully selected botanical essences to help quiet the internal chatter, allowing your body and mind to gently release tension and find a more settled rhythm. This isn't about overcoming; it's about gently coaxing your system into a state of ease.
