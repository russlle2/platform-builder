What exactly is Aromatherapy and how can it benefit me?

Aromatherapy is a holistic healing modality that uses natural plant extracts, called essential oils, to promote health and well-being. It can benefit you by reducing stress, improving sleep, easing discomfort, boosting mood, and supporting various physical and emotional needs through their unique therapeutic properties.

Are the essential oils used in Aromatherapy safe?

Yes, when sourced from reputable suppliers and used correctly, essential oils are safe. At Aromatherapy Studio, Dr. Morgan Ellis uses only high-quality, pure essential oils and provides personalized guidance on their safe application and dilution, ensuring your well-being is always prioritized.

How long does it take to see results from Aromatherapy?

The time it takes to see results from Aromatherapy can vary depending on your individual needs and consistency of use. Some individuals experience immediate relief, while for others, benefits accrue over time with regular integration into their wellness routine. We will discuss realistic expectations during your consultation.

Can I use Aromatherapy alongside my current medical treatments?

Absolutely. Aromatherapy is a complementary therapy and can often be used to support your existing medical treatments by helping to manage symptoms, reduce stress, and enhance your overall quality of life. It's always wise to discuss any new wellness practices with your primary healthcare provider.
