FAQ — safety & practice

How do you handle dilution?

I follow recommended dilution ranges for topical use and never advise medicinal treatments. Typical adult dilutions for roll-ons and body work are between 1–3% unless otherwise discussed and with consent.

What about patch testing?

Patch testing is always recommended. We'll provide a small sample area and observe for a minimum of 24 hours before topical applications at events or private sessions.

Are essential oils safe for pets?

Many essential oils can affect animals. Pets are considered in every plan — if you share a home with animals, mention it so blends and methods are adjusted for their safety.

Pregnancy and nursing

I approach pregnancy with heightened caution. Certain oils are avoided. Please disclose pregnancy or nursing status before attendance; consult your healthcare provider for personalized guidance.
