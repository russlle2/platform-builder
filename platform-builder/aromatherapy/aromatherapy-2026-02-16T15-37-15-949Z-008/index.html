Questions people ask

How should I dilute essential oils?
Dilution guidance is provided for every blend. Typical topical dilutions for adults in non-medical aromatherapy are 1–3% for roll-ons. We show exact drops-to-carrier ratios during sessions. Always follow age- and sensitivity-specific guidance.
Should I patch test first?
Yes. We recommend a patch test on the forearm with your final diluted blend and waiting 24 hours for any reaction. If irritation occurs, discontinue use.
Are essential oils safe around pets?
Many essential oils can affect animals differently. We provide pet-safety notes and alternatives. Do not apply strong blends to pet fur, and consult a veterinarian for concerns.
What about pregnancy and breastfeeding?
We exercise extra caution. Some oils are not recommended during pregnancy or breastfeeding. Please inform the practitioner if you are pregnant or nursing so we can adapt blends and offer appropriate alternatives.
