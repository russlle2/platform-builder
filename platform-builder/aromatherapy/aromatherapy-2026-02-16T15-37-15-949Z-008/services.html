Questions (safety-first)

How is dilution handled?
All topical blends are provided at safe dilution ranges and labeled. For group take-homes we use conservative dilutions and include clear instructions.
What about a patch test?
We recommend a 24-hour patch test for any topical product: apply a small amount to the forearm and observe. If irritation occurs, discontinue use.
Are essential oils safe with pets?
Some oils can be irritating to animals. We keep pets in mind and avoid direct application on animals. If you share space with pets, mention this during booking so we can adapt use.
Pregnancy and nursing notes?
Certain oils are best avoided during pregnancy and nursing. Please disclose pregnancy or nursing status at booking so we can recommend safe options or alternatives.

Services are educational and not a substitute for medical care.
