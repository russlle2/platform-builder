Safety & common questions

Dilution guidance
I demonstrate recommended dilutions and provide labeled blends at safe concentrations for adults. Children and sensitive skin require further modifications—ask for guidance.
Patch testing
Before applying any new blend, we perform a small patch test. This is part of every hands-on session.
Pets
Some essential oils can be unpleasant or unsafe for animals. We cover pet-safe practices and storage so your blends remain companion-friendly.
Pregnancy & special conditions
Certain oils are avoided in pregnancy. If pregnant or breastfeeding, please contact me ahead of booking to discuss safe alternatives.
