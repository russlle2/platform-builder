Product safety FAQ

Dilution: Every topical product includes a recommended dilution range. If in doubt, choose the lower concentration.
Patch testing: We recommend a patch test for any topical product to check for sensitivity, even if pre-diluted.
Pets: Some products may not be suitable around cats or small mammals. Check the product notes or reach out before use.
Pregnancy & nursing: Several common oils are avoided during pregnancy; consult before purchasing if pregnant or nursing.
