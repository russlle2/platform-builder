Why these blends?

  • Designed with top/mid/base balance for layered aromatic complexity.
  • Paired with precise, safety-focused usage notes.
  • Recipes are adaptable — swap oils per sensitivity or availability.

How to use a blend safely

  1. Decide delivery: diffuser, roller, or inhaler.
  2. Apply recommended dilution for age & skin type.
  3. Perform a patch test 24–48 hours before regular topical use.

Safety FAQs

Why dilute? Pure essential oils are potent; dilution reduces irritation risk and supports safe topical use.
Patch test details Apply a diluted drop to the inner forearm, cover, and check for any reaction for up to 48 hours.
Pets Some oils can affect animals. Diffuse cautiously and avoid oils known to be harmful to cats and small mammals.
Pregnancy/nursing Many common oils are not recommended during pregnancy; contact me for pregnancy-safe alternatives.
