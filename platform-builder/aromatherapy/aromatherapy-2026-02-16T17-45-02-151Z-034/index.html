Frequently asked (safety first)

Always follow dilution guidance given in your session. Typical adult dilutions: 1% for sensitive skin (6 drops per 30 mL), 2–3% for general use. For children, elderly, or sensitive skin use lower dilutions. We provide exact recipes with each blend.
A patch test checks skin sensitivity. Apply recommended dilution to inner forearm, cover for 24 hours, then check for redness or irritation. Avoid use if reaction occurs.
Some oils can be harmful to pets. We provide clear pet-safety notes and diffuser guidelines; always consult your veterinarian for specific concerns and avoid direct application to animals.
Many oils are safe in low dilutions, but some are contraindicated. Tell us if you’re pregnant or nursing; we will use pregnancy-safe protocols and offer alternatives where needed.
