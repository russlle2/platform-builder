Common questions — safety first

How should I dilute essential oils?
Dilution depends on age, skin sensitivity, and intended use. Typical adult topical dilutions range 1–3% for daily use; we recommend following provided formulae and avoiding direct application of undiluted essential oils. For personalized dilution guidance, request a consult.
What about patch testing?
Patch testing is simple and recommended: apply a 1% diluted sample to a small skin area, wait 24 hours, and look for irritation. If you notice redness, swelling, or discomfort, discontinue use and let us know so we can adjust blends.
Are essential oils safe around pets?
Some oils can be harmful to animals. We provide pet-safe options and diffuser practices that keep pets comfortable. Always notify us about pets in your home so we can design suitable approaches.
I’m pregnant or breastfeeding — is aromatherapy okay?
Many aromatic practices can be adapted for pregnancy, but safety varies by oil and stage. We avoid certain oils and supply pregnancy-safe alternatives and dilution guidance. Always discuss pregnancy status when booking.
