Safety & FAQ

How do you handle dilution?

I recommend safe dilution ranges based on age, skin sensitivity, and intended use. All recommendations avoid medical claims and focus on safe aromatic use.

What is a patch test?

A small application on the forearm to check for skin reactions over 24–48 hours. I guide you through it step-by-step.

Are oils safe around pets?

Some essential oils can affect animals. I include pet-safety advice and alternatives in every consultation.

Pregnancy & nursing notes?

I avoid suggesting oils contraindicated in pregnancy. If you are pregnant or nursing, we discuss safe options and referral when needed.
