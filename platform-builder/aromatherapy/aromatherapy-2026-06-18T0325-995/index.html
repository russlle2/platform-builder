Frequently Asked Questions

How can Aromatherapy specifically support me during a major life transition?
Aromatherapy offers a profound way to address the stress and nervous system dysregulation that often accompany significant life changes. Specific essential oils can help quiet an overactive mind, ease emotional turbulence, and promote feelings of security, providing a gentle foundation as you adapt to new circumstances.
Will Aromatherapy truly help reduce my stress and regulate my nervous system?
Absolutely. The olfactory system has a direct link to the limbic system of your brain, which governs emotions and memory. By inhaling therapeutic aromas, you can directly influence your nervous system, encouraging a shift from fight-or-flight to rest-and-digest, thereby reducing stress and fostering a sense of calm.
What kind of Aromatherapy approaches do you use for individuals navigating change?
We focus on a personalized, gentle approach. This includes custom essential oil blends for diffusion, topical application, and aromatic baths, all designed to meet your specific emotional and physiological needs as you move through your transition. Our aim is to create rituals that bring you comfort and stability.
Is Aromatherapy a good fit if I'm feeling overwhelmed by life's changes?
Yes, it is an excellent fit. When overwhelmed, your nervous system can feel perpetually on edge. Aromatherapy provides a non-invasive, yet powerful method to gently guide your body and mind back to a state of balance, helping you to feel more grounded and capable of processing the changes you're experiencing.
