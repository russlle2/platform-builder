Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is Aromatherapy and how can it benefit me?
Aromatherapy is a holistic healing modality that uses natural plant extracts, known as essential oils, to promote health and well-being. It can benefit you by supporting emotional balance, reducing stress, improving sleep, alleviating discomfort, and enhancing overall vitality through carefully selected aromatic compounds.
Are the essential oils used in your Aromatherapy practice safe?
Yes, your safety is our utmost priority. We use only high-quality, ethically sourced essential oils and provide clear guidance on their appropriate and safe application. During your consultation, will review your health history to ensure all recommendations are tailored to your specific needs and avoid any potential contraindications.
How long does a typical Aromatherapy consultation last?
A typical initial aromatherapy consultation at usually lasts between 60 to 90 minutes. This allows ample time for to understand your unique needs, discuss your goals, and formulate a personalized essential oil blend and usage recommendations for you.
Can Aromatherapy help with chronic conditions?
While aromatherapy is not a substitute for medical treatment, it can be a valuable complementary therapy. It may help manage symptoms, reduce stress associated with chronic conditions, and enhance your overall quality of life. We always recommend consulting with your primary healthcare provider alongside any aromatherapy practices.
