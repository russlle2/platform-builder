FAQ — safety essentials

How should I dilute essential oils?
Dilution recommendations are conservative and individualized. Typical topical dilutions are 0.5–3% depending on age, skin sensitivity, and context. A practitioner will recommend a starting dilution.
What is a patch test and why do it?
A patch test is a small topical trial to observe skin response over 24–72 hours. It reduces the chance of irritation and helps guide safe topical use.
Are oils safe around pets?
Some essential oils can affect animals. We include pet-safe guidance and alternatives; never apply undiluted oils near pets or use diffusers in confined spaces without supervision.
What about pregnancy?
Pregnancy requires care. We do not make medical claims; pregnant clients receive a tailored review with recommended safe options and referral to their healthcare provider when appropriate.
