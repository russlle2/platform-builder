Brief safety FAQs

  • Dilution: We recommend conservative dilution ranges in writing; follow label instructions and consult for individualized ratios.
  • Patch test: Always perform a patch test before regular topical use—apply a diluted sample to inner forearm and observe 24–48 hours.
  • Pets: Some essential oils can irritate animals. Discuss pet exposure during consults; do not apply oils to pets unless advised by a veterinary professional.
  • Pregnancy & breastfeeding: Extra caution is warranted. Mention pregnancy or breastfeeding in your message; we will tailor recommendations and avoid contraindicated oils.
