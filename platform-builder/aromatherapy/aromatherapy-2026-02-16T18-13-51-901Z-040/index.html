FAQ — Safety notes

  • Dilution: Recommendations always start low (1–3% for adults) and are adjusted conservatively.
  • Patch test: Apply diluted product to a small skin area for 24 hours before regular use.
  • Pets: Some essential oils can affect pets; we tailor blends and use diffusion best practices.
  • Pregnancy: Many oils are avoided; we provide clear alternatives and physician collaboration.

Micro-habits: small scent practices

Short, repeatable rituals to integrate aromatherapy safely into daily life.

  • Morning inhale: One slow inhale of a citrus or light floral roll-on with a 2% dilution to support alertness.
  • Pause reset: A 60-second breathing break with a diluted lavender inhaler after a stressful task.
  • Bedtime cue: A linen mist spritz (very light dilution) 20 minutes before bed for ritualized wind-down.

All micro-habits include step-by-step dilution and patch-test guidance in your plan.
