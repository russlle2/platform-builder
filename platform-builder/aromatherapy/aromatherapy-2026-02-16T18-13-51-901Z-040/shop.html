Product safety FAQ

Dilution: Product pages and labels list dilution percentages. For topical products, use only as directed and avoid undiluted oil on skin.

Patch test: We recommend a 24–72 hour patch test for new topical products. See the services page for guided instructions.

Pets: Keep diffusers and strong volatile oils away from animals; consult your practitioner about pet-safe options.

Pregnancy & medications: Certain products are not appropriate during pregnancy or with some medications. Seek guidance in a consultation prior to use.
