Blend safety notes

Dilution: We use conservative dilution ranges and list them on each formulation card. Never apply undiluted essential oils to skin.

Patch-test: Conduct a 24–72 hour patch test on a small skin area before extended topical use.

Pets: Some oils are not pet-safe. Keep diffusers in well-ventilated rooms and consult prior to using near animals.

Pregnancy: Certain oils and higher dilutions are not recommended. Pregnant clients receive an adjusted set of options.
