FAQ — common safety topics

How do you recommend dilution?
We use conservative dilution guidelines tailored to age and exposure. Typical topical dilutions start low (0.5–2.5% in a carrier) and are adjusted only with client consent.
What is a patch test?
A patch test is a small, supervised application to skin using the intended dilution, observed for 24–48 hours to check for irritation or sensitivity.
Are blends safe for pets?
Some essential oils are not pet-safe. We review your household and provide alternatives or avoidance strategies to minimize risk for animals.
Pregnancy and nursing?
We take a precautionary stance. Certain oils are avoided in pregnancy; we discuss options and coordinate with healthcare providers when appropriate.
