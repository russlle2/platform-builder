How does Aromatherapy specifically help with chronic physical pain for seniors?

Aromatherapy utilizes concentrated plant extracts to interact with your body's systems, promoting pain reduction and inflammation relief. For seniors, specific essential oils can be chosen to address joint discomfort, muscle stiffness, and nerve pain, offering a gentle yet potent avenue for comfort without harsh side effects. We carefully select blends tailored to your unique needs and sensitivities.

Is Aromatherapy safe for seniors, especially if I have other health conditions?

Your safety is paramount. We conduct a thorough intake to understand your complete health profile, including any medications or existing conditions. Aromatherapy, when applied by a trained practitioner, is very safe. We use diluted, high-quality essential oils and apply them with care, ensuring the methods are appropriate and beneficial for your specific circumstances.

What can I expect during an Aromatherapy session focused on somatic healing?

During a somatic healing session, we begin with a conversation about your current physical sensations and emotional landscape. Gentle aromatic blends are then applied through mindful massage or diffused, encouraging your body to release stored tension. The focus is on subtle shifts in sensation, inviting a deeper awareness and fostering a sense of profound relaxation and ease.

How can I continue to benefit from Aromatherapy for pain relief at home?

We provide personalized recommendations for home use, including custom aromatic blends and simple application techniques. These may involve gentle self-massage with a diluted rollerball, aromatic baths, or diffusion to create a soothing environment. Our aim is to equip you with practical tools to maintain your comfort and well-being between sessions.
