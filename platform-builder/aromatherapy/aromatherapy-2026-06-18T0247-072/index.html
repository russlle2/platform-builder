Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is Aromatherapy and how can it benefit me?
Aromatherapy is the therapeutic use of aromatic plant extracts and essential oils for psychological and physical well-being. It can benefit you by promoting relaxation, reducing stress, improving sleep quality, easing discomfort, and enhancing mood, depending on the specific oils and their application.
Are the essential oils used in Aromatherapy safe?
When used correctly and with professional guidance, essential oils are generally safe. We prioritize therapeutic-grade, pure essential oils and will always provide clear instructions for safe dilution and application methods tailored to your individual needs and sensitivities.
What can I expect during my first Aromatherapy consultation?
During your initial consultation, we will have an in-depth discussion about your health history, current concerns, lifestyle, and wellness goals. This allows us to understand your unique needs and craft a personalized Aromatherapy plan, including custom blends and application recommendations, specifically for you.
How quickly will I notice results from Aromatherapy?
The experience of Aromatherapy is highly individual. Some people may notice immediate shifts in mood or comfort, while others experience more gradual changes over time with consistent use. We encourage you to be patient and observant of your body's responses, as Aromatherapy supports your body's natural healing processes.
