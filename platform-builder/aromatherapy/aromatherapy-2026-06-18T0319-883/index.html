Frequently Asked Questions

How can Aromatherapy specifically help with my posture as a senior?
Aromatherapy can support your posture by promoting relaxation in tight muscles often associated with poor alignment, encouraging deeper, more open breathing which affects spinal posture, and fostering a greater sense of body awareness. When you're more aware of your body, you can consciously adjust your stance and movements for better comfort and alignment.
Are the essential oils used in your Aromatherapy safe for seniors with various health considerations?
Absolutely. Your safety and well-being are our top priority. We always conduct a thorough intake to understand your individual health profile, including any medications or sensitivities. All our essential oils are therapeutic grade and carefully diluted to ensure they are gentle and effective for your specific needs, focusing on non-irritating applications that support your comfort.
What does a typical Aromatherapy session for postural health involve?
A typical session begins with a conversation about your current postural concerns and comfort levels. Based on this, we'll create a personalized essential oil blend. This might involve gentle aromatic diffusion during a guided body awareness exercise, or a custom roll-on blend for localized application to areas needing support, like the neck or lower back, always in a way that feels comfortable and empowering for you.
Can Aromatherapy help with balance, which is important for seniors?
Yes, Aromatherapy can play a supportive role in enhancing your sense of balance indirectly. By reducing stress, improving focus, and fostering a more grounded feeling, essential oils can contribute to improved body awareness. This heightened awareness allows you to better sense your position in space, which can naturally lead to more confident and steady movements, supporting your overall balance.
