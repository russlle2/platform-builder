Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is Aromatherapy and how can it benefit me?
Aromatherapy is a holistic healing modality that utilizes natural plant extracts, known as essential oils, to promote well-being. It can benefit you by supporting emotional balance, easing tension, improving sleep quality, enhancing focus, and uplifting your mood, all through the powerful influence of scent on your mind and body.
Are the essential oils you use safe and pure?
Absolutely. Your safety and well-being are our top priority. We source only the highest quality, therapeutic-grade essential oils from reputable suppliers, ensuring they are pure, unadulterated, and rigorously tested. We will always guide you on their proper and safe application for your specific needs.
How long does an Aromatherapy consultation typically last?
A typical initial Aromatherapy consultation lasts approximately 60 to 75 minutes. This allows us ample time to understand your health history, current concerns, and wellness goals, ensuring we create the most effective and personalized aromatic plan for you.
Can Aromatherapy help with specific health concerns I have?
While Aromatherapy is not a substitute for medical treatment, it can be a wonderful complementary therapy to support your overall well-being and address various concerns. We can discuss how specific essential oils may assist you with stress management, sleep disturbances, fatigue, or mood enhancement during your consultation.
