Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is Aromatherapy and how can it benefit me?
Aromatherapy is a holistic healing modality that utilizes natural plant extracts, called essential oils, to promote health and well-being. It can benefit you by supporting emotional balance, improving sleep, reducing stress, alleviating minor discomforts, and enhancing overall vitality through various application methods.
Are essential oils safe to use, and how do you ensure their quality in Aromatherapy?
When used correctly, essential oils are generally safe. We prioritize your safety by using only high-quality, ethically sourced essential oils from trusted distillers. During your consultation, we discuss proper dilution, application methods, and any contraindications to ensure your experience is both effective and secure.
How long does an Aromatherapy consultation take, and what should I expect?
A typical initial Aromatherapy consultation lasts between 60 to 75 minutes. During this time, we will discuss your health history, current wellness goals, and lifestyle. You can expect a collaborative conversation that leads to a personalized essential oil blend recommendation and guidance on how to best integrate it into your routine.
Can Aromatherapy replace my current medical treatments?
Aromatherapy is intended to complement, not replace, conventional medical treatments. It works wonderfully as an adjunct therapy to support your well-being. We always recommend consulting with your primary healthcare provider for any medical conditions and before making changes to your current treatment plan.
