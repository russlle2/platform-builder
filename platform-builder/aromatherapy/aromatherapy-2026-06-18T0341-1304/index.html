Frequently Asked Questions

How can Aromatherapy specifically address the physical pain from my chronic illness or injury?
Aromatherapy utilizes the therapeutic properties of essential oils to interact with your body's systems. Certain oils are renowned for their anti-inflammatory, analgesic, and muscle-relaxing qualities, which can directly help alleviate persistent discomfort and tension arising from chronic conditions or injuries. We carefully select and blend these to meet your unique needs.
Will Aromatherapy help with the somatic aspects of my recovery?
Absolutely. Somatic healing acknowledges the deep connection between your body and mind. Aromatherapy, through its profound effect on the limbic system, can help release emotional and energetic imprints held within your physical form. This can lead to a softening of habitual tension patterns and a greater sense of ease in your body as you recover.
Is Aromatherapy safe to use alongside my current medical treatments for chronic conditions?
Yes, when guided by a trained practitioner, Aromatherapy can be a complementary support to your medical care. We always encourage open communication with your healthcare providers to ensure a cohesive and safe approach to your well-being, tailoring our suggestions to avoid any contraindications with your existing treatments.
What kind of Aromatherapy experience can I expect for my recovery?
You can expect a deeply personalized experience. We begin with a thorough understanding of your specific pain points and recovery goals. Your Aromatherapy will involve custom-blended essential oils for topical application, inhalation, or diffusion, designed to soothe your physical discomfort, calm your nervous system, and support your body's inherent wisdom in its journey back to strength.
