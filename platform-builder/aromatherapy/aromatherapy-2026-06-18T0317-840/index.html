Frequently Asked Questions

How can Aromatherapy specifically help with my posture as a college student?
Aromatherapy can help in a couple of ways. Certain scents can promote relaxation, easing the muscle tension that often leads to poor posture from prolonged sitting or stress. Other blends can enhance mental clarity and focus, allowing you to be more present and aware of your body's alignment, rather than hunching over without realizing it. It's about creating a subtle sensory cue to support better physical habits.
I'm always stressed about exams. Can Aromatherapy really make a difference for academic anxiety?
Absolutely. Specific essential oils have properties known to calm the nervous system, reduce feelings of anxiousness, and improve concentration. We can create a personalized blend for you to use during study sessions or before exams. This isn't a magic fix, but a supportive tool to help you feel more centered, focused, and less overwhelmed by academic pressure, allowing you to approach your work with a clearer head.
Is Aromatherapy safe to use in my dorm room or shared living spaces?
Yes, when used mindfully. We primarily recommend personal inhalers, roll-ons, or small, water-based diffusers that are safe and considerate for shared spaces. We'll discuss the best application methods for your living situation to ensure your aromatherapy practice is effective without impacting roommates or violating housing rules. Your safety and comfort are our priority.
