Finding Your Center with Aromatherapy Amidst Academic Demands

College life can feel like a constant balancing act. Long hours at a desk, late-night study sessions, and the pressure of exams often lead to slumped shoulders and a tight neck. Aromatherapy isn't just about pleasant smells; it's about harnessing specific plant essences to gently encourage your body and mind to relax, helping you maintain better posture and navigate academic stress more smoothly. We'll explore blends that help you feel more grounded and less overwhelmed.
