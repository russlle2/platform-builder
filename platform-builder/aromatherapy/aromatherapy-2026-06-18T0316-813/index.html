Frequently Asked Questions

How can Aromatherapy specifically help with my posture as I age?
Aromatherapy offers a gentle way to encourage muscle relaxation and reduce discomfort that can contribute to stooping or stiffness. Specific aromas can help you become more attuned to your body's alignment, gently reminding you to stand taller and move with greater ease without conscious effort.
Is Aromatherapy safe to use with other medications I might be taking?
Yes, when used mindfully and with professional guidance, Aromatherapy is generally safe. We always conduct a thorough intake to understand your health history and any medications you are taking, ensuring our blends are chosen carefully and applied in ways that complement your existing care.
What does a typical Aromatherapy session for postural health involve?
A session begins with a conversation about your current experiences and goals for comfort and posture. We then explore various aromas together, selecting those that resonate most with you. You might receive a custom blend for home use, gentle aromatic compresses, or guidance on mindful scent inhalation to support your body awareness throughout your day.
Will Aromatherapy make me feel dizzy or lightheaded, especially with sensitive senses?
Our approach to Aromatherapy is always gentle and tailored to your comfort. We use very diluted blends and introduce scents gradually, ensuring you feel completely at ease. Many find the experience grounding and calming, rather than overwhelming, and we prioritize your comfort above all else.
