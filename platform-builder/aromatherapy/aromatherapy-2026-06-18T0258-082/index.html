Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is Aromatherapy and how does it work?
Aromatherapy is a holistic healing treatment that uses natural plant extracts to promote health and well-being. Essential oils, derived from plants, are inhaled or applied topically to influence mood, alleviate discomfort, and support the body's natural healing processes through their aromatic and chemical properties.
Is Aromatherapy safe for everyone?
While Aromatherapy is generally safe, individual sensitivities and health conditions must be considered. During your consultation, will thoroughly assess your medical history and any potential contraindications to ensure the recommended oils and application methods are safe and appropriate for your specific situation.
How quickly can I expect to see results from Aromatherapy?
The experience of Aromatherapy is highly individual. Some individuals report immediate shifts in mood or comfort, while others notice changes over a period of consistent use. We encourage you to observe your body's responses and maintain open communication with for optimal results.
Can Aromatherapy replace conventional medical treatments?
Aromatherapy is intended to complement, not replace, conventional medical care. It serves as a powerful supportive therapy to enhance your overall well-being. We always recommend consulting with your primary healthcare provider for any medical concerns you may have.
