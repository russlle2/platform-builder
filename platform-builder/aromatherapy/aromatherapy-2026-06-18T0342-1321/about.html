How can Aromatherapy specifically help with age-related physical discomfort?

Our specialized Aromatherapy employs botanical essences known for their anti-inflammatory, analgesic, and muscle-relaxing properties. We carefully select and blend these to address common senior concerns like joint stiffness, muscle soreness, and nerve discomfort, helping you experience greater ease and mobility in your daily life.

Is Aromatherapy safe for someone taking multiple medications?

Your safety is our utmost priority. During your initial consultation, we conduct a thorough review of your health history and current medications. This allows us to craft bespoke aromatic blends that are safe, effective, and free from contraindications, ensuring your well-being is always protected. We work with you thoughtfully and considerately.

What does a typical Aromatherapy session for physical pain relief involve?

A session with Dr. Morgan Ellis begins with a gentle conversation about your specific discomforts and preferences. We then create a personalized aromatic blend, which may be applied topically with a soothing massage, diffused gently in the air, or offered for inhalation. The experience is designed to be calming, restorative, and deeply comforting for your body and spirit.

How often might I need Aromatherapy to see benefits for somatic healing?

The frequency of sessions for somatic healing varies for each individual, as every body's journey is unique. We typically recommend beginning with a series of sessions to establish a foundational sense of calm and relief. From there, we work with you to determine a personalized rhythm that best supports your ongoing comfort and well-being, gently guiding your body back to balance.
