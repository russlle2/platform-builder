FAQ




Are essential oils safe for everyone?


Individual sensitivity varies. Perform a patch test and follow dilution guidelines. Consult a health provider for pregnancy, breastfeeding, or medical concerns.


What dilution do you recommend?


Typical topical dilution suggestions range from 0.5–3% depending on age and sensitivity. Use lower concentrations for children or sensitive skin.


What about pets?


Some oils can affect animals. Keep blends away from pets, ventilate spaces, and consult a veterinarian when in doubt.
