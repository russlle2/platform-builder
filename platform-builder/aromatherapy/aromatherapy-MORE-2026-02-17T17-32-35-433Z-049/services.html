Practitioner notes & approach

Each plan is personalized, safety-forward, and practical. We emphasize low-concentration protocols and stepwise introduction to new aromas. Sessions may support relaxation, gentle focus, or an uplift in mood; they are not medical therapies.

Safety & FAQs

  • Are sessions medical? No. Aromatherapy sessions are supportive and complementary and may support well-being, but they are not medical treatments.
  • Dilution guidance: Home blends typically start 0.5–2% for adults. For sensitive skin, pregnancy, children, or pets at home, we advise lower dilutions and specific ingredient avoidance.
  • Patch test: Always perform a small skin patch test before topical use: dilute, apply a small amount behind the ear or inner forearm, and wait 24 hours.
  • Pregnancy & medications: Some aromas are contraindicated in pregnancy or certain conditions. Disclose pregnancy, medications, and major health considerations before sessions.
  • Pets: Many essential oils can be harmful to pets. We provide pet-safe alternatives and diffusion guidelines when animals share the space.
