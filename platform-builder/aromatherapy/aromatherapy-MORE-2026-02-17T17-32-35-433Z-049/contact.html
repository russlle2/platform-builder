FAQ — safety and practical notes

Patch tests & dilution

Do a patch test for topical blends: dilute essential oils in a carrier oil (commonly 1–3% for most adults) and apply a small amount behind the ear or on inner forearm. Higher sensitivity, older adults, and children will often use lower dilution (0.5–1%).

Pets

Some essential oils can be hazardous to animals. Inform us if you have pets and their species; we will recommend pet-safe approaches (diffusion timing, ventilation, or alternative formulas) that may reduce risk.

Pregnancy & medical considerations

If you are pregnant, breastfeeding, or on medication, tell us before any session. Certain oils are typically avoided in pregnancy; we will choose safer alternatives and follow best-practice guidance. Our work is not a substitute for medical care.
