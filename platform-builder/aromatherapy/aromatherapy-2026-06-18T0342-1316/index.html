Frequently Asked Questions

How does Aromatherapy specifically address the physical aches often experienced in retirement?
Our Aromatherapy protocols are designed to target common discomforts through specific essential oils known for their analgesic and anti-inflammatory effects. We create personalized blends that you can apply topically or diffuse, aiming to soothe joint stiffness, muscle soreness, and neuropathic sensations, helping you move with greater comfort and freedom.
Can Aromatherapy genuinely support mental clarity and focus as I age?
Yes, certain essential oils have demonstrated properties that support cognitive function. We utilize these specific aromas to formulate blends that can enhance your alertness, improve concentration, and aid in memory retention. Regular use can contribute to a more focused and mentally agile state, helping to maintain your sharp mind.
What is 'somatic healing' in the context of Aromatherapy and how does it relate to physical pain?
Somatic healing, with Aromatherapy, involves using scent to help your body release stored physical and emotional tension. When pain is chronic, it often holds a somatic component. Our aromatic blends, combined with gentle guidance, help you connect with your body's sensations, facilitating the release of deep-seated muscular and energetic blockages that contribute to persistent discomfort, fostering a profound sense of ease.
Is Aromatherapy safe for individuals who may be on multiple medications or have sensitive skin?
Your safety is paramount. During your initial consultation, we conduct a thorough review of your health history, including any medications and skin sensitivities. We then select and dilute essential oils appropriately, ensuring the blends are gentle and safe for your specific needs. We will always guide you on patch testing and proper application methods to ensure your comfort and well-being.
