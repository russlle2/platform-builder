What is Aromatherapy and how can it benefit me?

Aromatherapy is the therapeutic use of aromatic plant extracts and essential oils for psychological and physical well-being. It can help you manage stress, improve sleep quality, uplift your mood, support respiratory health, and even soothe minor discomforts naturally.

Are essential oils safe to use directly on my skin?

While some essential oils can be applied topically, it's crucial to dilute them properly with a carrier oil to prevent skin irritation. We will always provide personalized guidance on safe and effective application methods tailored to your specific needs and the oils you are using.

How long does an Aromatherapy consultation typically last?

Your initial Aromatherapy consultation is typically 60-90 minutes, allowing us ample time to discuss your health history, current concerns, and wellness goals thoroughly. Follow-up sessions are usually shorter, focusing on progress and adjustments to your blends.

Can Aromatherapy replace traditional medical treatments?

Aromatherapy is a complementary therapy and should not replace conventional medical advice or treatment. It works wonderfully alongside other health practices to support your overall well-being. We encourage you to always consult with your healthcare provider for any medical concerns.
