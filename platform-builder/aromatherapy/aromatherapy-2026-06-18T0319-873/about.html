What is gentle aromatherapy, and how can it help my posture?

Gentle aromatherapy uses specific essential oils, often diluted and applied topically or diffused, to encourage a sense of calm and relaxation in your muscles. By easing tension, especially in your back and shoulders, it can indirectly support your body in finding a more natural and comfortable posture. We focus on blends that promote a feeling of lightness and ease.

Do I need any special knowledge or experience with essential oils to begin?

Absolutely not! Our approach is designed for everyone, especially if you're new to aromatherapy. We guide you every step of the way, explaining everything clearly and simply. You don't need any prior experience; just an openness to gentle, natural ways of supporting your body's comfort.

How does aromatherapy help me become more aware of my body's movements?

Certain aromas can have a grounding or centering effect, which helps you pay closer attention to your physical sensations. By gently focusing your mind with a specific scent, you can become more attuned to how you're moving, standing, or sitting. This increased awareness can subtly guide you towards more comfortable and balanced positions throughout your day.

Is aromatherapy safe for someone over 60, especially if I have other health considerations?

Yes, when practiced gently and thoughtfully, aromatherapy is very safe. We always use highly diluted essential oils and consider any health conditions you may have, ensuring the blends are appropriate for you. We'll discuss your health history thoroughly to create a personalized and safe experience that supports your well-being.
