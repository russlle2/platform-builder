What exactly is Aromatherapy?

Aromatherapy is a holistic healing modality that uses natural plant extracts, known as essential oils, to promote health and well-being. These oils are carefully distilled from various parts of plants and can be inhaled or applied topically to positively influence your mood, alleviate discomfort, and support your body's natural healing processes.

Is Aromatherapy safe for everyone?

When used correctly and with professional guidance, Aromatherapy is generally very safe. However, individual sensitivities vary, and certain essential oils may not be suitable for pregnant women, young children, or individuals with specific health conditions. Your practitioner will conduct a thorough consultation to ensure all recommendations are tailored safely to your unique profile.

How can Aromatherapy help with stress and anxiety?

Many essential oils possess properties that can significantly impact your nervous system, promoting relaxation and reducing feelings of stress and anxiety. For example, lavender is renowned for its calming effects, while bergamot can uplift your mood. Your personalized Aromatherapy plan will often include oils specifically chosen to help you find greater tranquility and emotional balance.

What should I expect during an Aromatherapy consultation?

During your consultation, you'll have an in-depth conversation with your practitioner about your health history, current well-being goals, and any concerns you may have. Based on this information, your practitioner will recommend specific essential oils or custom blends, along with guidance on how to use them effectively and safely to best support your journey to wellness.
