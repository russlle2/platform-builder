FAQ — Safety and use

Are these medical treatments?
No. Our sessions and blends are not medical treatments. Aromas may support wellbeing in non-medical ways; always seek professional care for health concerns.
What about dilution and patch testing?
We provide explicit dilution guidelines and recommend a patch test. Begin with low concentrations and increase cautiously if no irritation occurs.
Pets & pregnancy
We always screen for pets and pregnancy. Some oils are not recommended for cats, dogs, pregnancy, or nursing. We will adjust blends accordingly.
Can I use blends on skin?
Topical use is possible with proper dilution. We give percent-based guidance and examples (drops per 10ml/30ml) so you can prepare safely.
