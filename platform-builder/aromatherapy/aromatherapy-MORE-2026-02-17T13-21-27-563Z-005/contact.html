FAQ — Safety first

Dilution and patch testing

We recommend conservative dilutions. Typical adult dilutions: 2% for routine topical (approximately 12 drops in 30 ml), 1% for sensitive skin, 0.5% for children. Always do a patch test on a small skin area and wait 24 hours before regular topical use.

Pregnancy and breastfeeding

Some essential oils are not recommended during pregnancy or breastfeeding. If you are pregnant or nursing, mention it in notes and seek individualized guidance before topical use.

Pets in the home

Many essential oils can affect pets. Diffuse briefly and in well-ventilated spaces; do not diffuse around birds. Share pet details so blends avoid problematic compounds.
