FAQ — safety highlights

Patch test: Apply a small diluted amount to the inner arm, wait 24 hours for irritation. Pets: Some oils may be harmful to pets; avoid diffuse near small animals and consult veterinarian. Pregnancy & nursing: Many oils are contraindicated; please disclose pregnancy status before using topical or internal strategies. This practice is supportive, not medical.
