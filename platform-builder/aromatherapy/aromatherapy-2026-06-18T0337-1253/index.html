Frequently Asked Questions

How does trauma-informed Aromatherapy specifically support seniors?
Trauma-informed Aromatherapy acknowledges that past experiences can profoundly influence present well-being. For seniors, this means recognizing how life's accumulated experiences, both joyful and challenging, shape your emotional landscape. We use gentle, non-invasive aromatic interventions to support your nervous system, promoting a sense of safety and calm without requiring you to re-engage with past difficult memories directly. Your comfort and emotional security are our utmost priority.
Will Aromatherapy interfere with my current medications or health conditions?
Your safety and well-being are paramount. During your initial consultation, we conduct a thorough review of your health history, including any medications you are taking and pre-existing conditions. We carefully select essential oils and application methods that are appropriate and safe for you, always prioritizing gentle, diluted approaches. We also encourage open communication with your primary healthcare provider, ensuring a truly integrated approach to your care.
I've never tried Aromatherapy before. What can I expect during a session?
You can expect a calm and supportive environment where your comfort is central. A session begins with a gentle conversation about your emotional goals and preferences. We then introduce you to various essential oil aromas, guiding you to choose those that resonate most deeply with you. This might involve gentle diffusion, topical application to pulse points, or simple inhalation techniques. The experience is designed to be deeply relaxing and empowering, giving you agency in your own healing process.
How can Aromatherapy help me reclaim a sense of agency and empowerment?
Aromatherapy, when approached with intention, can be a powerful tool for self-empowerment. By consciously choosing aromas that support your emotional state, you actively participate in your own well-being. Learning simple aromatic rituals gives you practical tools to manage stress, soothe anxieties, and uplift your spirit independently. This process fosters a renewed sense of control and confidence, reminding you of your inherent capacity for resilience and inner peace, even amidst life's challenges.
