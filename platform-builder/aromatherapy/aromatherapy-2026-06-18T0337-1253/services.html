Aromatherapy: Gently Unfurling Burdens of the Past

Your life has been a rich journey, and with it, perhaps, indelible imprints. Our trauma-informed Aromatherapy sessions are crafted with profound reverence for your experiences. We use specific essential oils, chosen for their gentle yet potent ability to soothe the nervous system, helping to soften the edges of past discomfort without re-traumatization. This isn't about revisiting pain, but rather, inviting a quiet release, allowing you to breathe more freely within your own story.
