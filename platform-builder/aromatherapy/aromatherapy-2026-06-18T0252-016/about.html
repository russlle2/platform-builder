What exactly is Aromatherapy and how can it help me?

Aromatherapy is a holistic healing modality that uses natural plant extracts, called essential oils, to promote health and well-being. It can support your physical and emotional health by helping with stress relief, improving sleep, uplifting mood, and alleviating discomfort, all tailored to your specific needs.

Are the essential oils used in Aromatherapy safe?

Yes, when used correctly and with professional guidance, essential oils are safe. At Aromatherapy Studio, we prioritize your safety by using only high-quality, therapeutic-grade oils and providing clear instructions for their proper application, considering any sensitivities or health conditions you may have.

What should I expect during my first Aromatherapy consultation?

During your initial consultation, you'll have a detailed conversation with Dr. Morgan Ellis about your health history, lifestyle, and wellness goals. This allows us to understand your unique needs and collaboratively develop a personalized Aromatherapy plan, often including a custom essential oil blend just for you.

How quickly will I see results from Aromatherapy?

The experience of Aromatherapy is highly individual. While some people notice positive shifts immediately, others may experience changes gradually over time. Consistency in using your personalized blends and following guidance from Dr. Morgan Ellis will enhance your results and support your journey towards well-being.
