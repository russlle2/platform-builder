Credentials and approach

Clinically informed training, plant chemistry knowledge, and years of practice in crafting household-safe blends. Sessions emphasize personalization and conservative dilution.

How this practice began

A desire to translate botanical complexity into short, usable practices brought the studio into being. Instead of large rituals, we design tiny, repeatable scent-based practices that fit a morning coffee, a mid-afternoon pause, or a bedtime transition.

The aim is never to promise cure; rather, each blend and practice is proposed as something that may support an intended shift — a prompt for attention, a scaffold for breath, a familiar touchstone.

Safety and usage notes

Patch tests, dilution, and sensitive populations

We advise a patch test for topical blends and conservative dilutions. For pregnant people, infants, or households with animals, we offer specific formulations and always avoid certain oils. Please disclose pregnancy or pets during intake so we can plan safely.

How do you frame your guidance?

All guidance is practical and conservative; we do not make medical claims. Our work aims to offer sensory structure that may support everyday rhythms rather than replace clinical care.
