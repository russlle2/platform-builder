Frequently asked safety and practical questions

How do you recommend diluting blends for at-home use?

Common adult dilution guidance is 1–3% for daily use (1% = about 6 drops per 30ml carrier). For sensitive skin, older adults, or first-time users, start at 0.5–1% and do a patch test. We include a dilution card with every custom blend.

What about patch tests and skin safety?

A patch test means applying a small amount of the diluted blend to a small area (inner forearm) and waiting 24 hours. If any redness, itching, or irritation occurs, discontinue use and consult a clinician. We never make medical claims; this guidance is safety-forward.

Are essential oils safe around pets, children, or pregnancy?

Some oils may not be suitable around pets or during pregnancy. We always confirm household composition on intake and create pet- and pregnancy-aware options. If you have concerns, mention them when booking.
