Notes on safety

This tool offers general dilution suggestions and is not medical advice. Always patch test and adapt for sensitive people, pregnancy, or pets. See FAQ for details.

Quick reference: dilution guide

The builder uses a simple assumption: 1 ml ≈ 20 drops. Dilution examples are rounded to whole drops. For topical use we recommend conservative dilutions (1–3%) depending on sensitivity.

FAQ

How are percentages chosen? The default percentages are conservative: diffuser blends use higher aromatic proportions for scent presence, topical blends default to 1–2% for safety. Adjust downward for sensitive skin.

Patch testing — Apply a small diluted amount to the inner forearm and wait 24 hours for irritation. Discontinue if redness or discomfort occurs.

Pets & pregnancy — Some essential oils can affect animals or pregnancy. The builder will flag oils that commonly require caution; consult your practitioner or healthcare provider for individual guidance.
