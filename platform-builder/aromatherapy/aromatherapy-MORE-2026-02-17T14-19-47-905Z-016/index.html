Common questions

Are these services medical?

These offerings are not medical treatments. Aromatherapy may support relaxation, focus, or sleep for some people; it is not a substitute for medical care.

Is dilution guidance provided?

Yes. Every custom blend includes a safe dilution guide for topical use and instructions for inhalation. If you are pregnant, nursing, taking medication, or have serious health concerns, consult a medical professional before use.

Any notes about pets?

Certain essential oils can affect pets. We provide pet-safe options and recommendations for safe diffusion; always keep concentrated oils out of reach and avoid direct application to animals.

How do I patch test?

A patch test is recommended for any topical use. Apply a small diluted amount to inner forearm, wait 24 hours, and discontinue if irritation appears.
