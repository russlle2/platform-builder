Dilution & patch test
Suggested dilutions are provided as safe-start guidance only. For adults, typical topical dilutions range from 1%–3% for daily use; for sensitive skin or older adults consider 0.5%–1%. For children and babies, reduce more substantially or avoid topical use; always consult a qualified provider. A patch test (small amount on inner forearm) is recommended before extended topical use.
Pregnancy, medications, and pets
Aromas can be powerful. If pregnant, breastfeeding, or on regular medications, we recommend consulting your healthcare provider before using essential oils. Some oils are not safe for pets; do not diffuse heavily around small animals and always research individual oil safety.
Non-medical advisory
Information provided here and in consultations is educational and not medical advice. Essential oils may support feelings like calm or focus for some people; results vary. For health concerns, seek professional medical guidance.

A brief guided practice
A short 6-minute breathing, journaling, and intention-setting for a quiet reset.
Ground — 30 seconds
Find a comfortable seated position. Sense your feet and the seat beneath you.
Breathing — 2 minutes
Follow the on-screen cycle: inhale 4s, hold 2s, exhale 6s. A gentle rhythm to slow the nervous system.
Journal — 90 seconds
Write one sentence: what would make today feel lighter? Keep it short.
Set intention — 2 minutes
Choose a single, clear intention. Repeat it silently on the out-breath.
