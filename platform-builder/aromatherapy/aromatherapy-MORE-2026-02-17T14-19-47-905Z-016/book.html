Safety snapshot

  • Patch test: apply a small amount of diluted blend to inner forearm; wait 24 hours.
  • Pregnancy: some oils are avoided; disclose pregnancy at booking.
  • Pets: certain strong oils can be risky around animals; we create pet-aware mixes when needed.
  • Children: use reduced dilutions and keep oils out of reach.
