How does aromatherapy specifically benefit seniors for seasonal wellness?

For seniors, seasonal aromatherapy focuses on gentle support for common changes. We might use uplifting citrus in winter to counter low mood, or calming lavender in spring to soothe seasonal sensitivities. It's about proactively supporting your body and mind through each shift, helping you maintain comfort and a sense of balance as the year progresses.

Is aromatherapy safe for me, considering my current health conditions or medications?

Your safety is our top priority. We conduct a thorough consultation to understand your health history and any medications you're taking. We only use pure, therapeutic-grade essential oils, carefully diluted and applied using methods that are gentle and appropriate for your individual needs, ensuring no adverse interactions or side effects.

What kind of preventive care can I expect from aromatherapy?

Preventive aromatherapy focuses on supporting your long-term well-being. This might include blends to encourage deeper, more restorative sleep, aid in mental clarity, or promote feelings of calm and relaxation to help manage daily stress. It's about integrating supportive aromas into your routine to help maintain your health and vitality over time, rather than waiting for issues to arise.

What does a typical aromatherapy session involve for someone my age?

A session begins with a relaxed conversation about your current well-being and goals. We then craft a custom aromatic blend tailored specifically for you. This might involve a gentle topical application, an inhaler, or a diffuser blend for your home. The focus is always on comfort, gentle efficacy, and providing you with tools for ongoing self-care.
