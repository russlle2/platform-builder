Frequently Asked Questions

How does Aromatherapy specifically aid an athlete's sleep and recovery?
Aromatherapy, through carefully selected essential oils, can significantly reduce the physiological stress response accumulated during intense training. Certain botanicals calm the nervous system, lower heart rate, and prepare your body for deeper REM and slow-wave sleep, which are vital for muscle repair, hormone regulation, and cognitive restoration—all critical for athletic performance.
Are the Aromatherapy blends suitable for sensitive skin or allergies?
Your safety and comfort are paramount. We conduct a thorough intake to understand your unique sensitivities, allergies, and existing conditions. All blends are custom-formulated, and we prioritize hypoallergenic, pure essential oils, carefully diluting them in premium carrier oils to ensure gentle yet effective application, minimizing any risk of irritation.
What kind of commitment is required to see results with Aromatherapy for sleep?
Consistent integration of your personalized aromatherapy ritual is key. While many clients experience immediate calming effects, profound shifts in sleep patterns and recovery often emerge within a few weeks of dedicated use. We guide you on simple, effective daily practices that seamlessly fit into your pre-sleep routine, ensuring sustained benefits.
Can Aromatherapy help with the mental stress associated with high-performance demands?
Absolutely. The same botanical compounds that promote physical relaxation also have a powerful impact on the limbic system, which governs emotions and memory. Our tailored aromatherapy can alleviate performance anxiety, quiet a racing mind, and foster a sense of mental tranquility, allowing you to transition into restful sleep unburdened by the day's demands.
