A little about the practice

We blend traditional aromatic wisdom with contemporary safety best practices. Every consultation begins with questions about sensitivities, medications, pregnancy, and pet exposure to ensure blends are safe and appropriate.

1. Intake

Gentle conversation to learn scent history, allergies, and preferences. Patch-test guidance included.

2. Exploration

Smelling station with single-note samples: top, heart, base. We observe preferences and tolerance.

3. Ritual

A tailored at-home ritual and safe dilution instructions for topical use. Non-medical supportive language is used throughout.

Custom Blend — Starter

5 ml roll-on, curated to your preferences. Retail add-on option available during sessions.

Deep Aroma Session

90-minute immersive experience with guided inhalation and bespoke diffuse protocols.

Virtual Consultation

Video-based scent guidance and DIY blend formulas. Includes dilution chart and patch test instructions.

Safety-first notes

Aromatherapy here is complementary and non-diagnostic. We avoid medical claims and prioritize safe dilution, patch testing, and considerations for pregnancy and pets. Always consult your healthcare provider for medical concerns.
