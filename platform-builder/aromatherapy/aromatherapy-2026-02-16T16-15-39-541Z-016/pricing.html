Small print & safety

Dilution and patch-test guidance included with every blend. We recommend patch testing new oils on a small skin area 24 hours prior to wider use. Avoid ingesting essential oils. Keep oils out of reach of children. For pregnancy, infancy, and certain health conditions, we emphasize conservative choices and encourage consultation with your healthcare provider.
