From practice to perfume — a short story

{{PRACTITIONER_NAME}} began blending as a daily ritual: small experiences that shape presence. We combine sustainably sourced essential oils, careful dilution guidance, and clear safety notes so you can enjoy scent thoughtfully.

We do not make medical claims. Aromatherapy here is offered as a complementary sensory practice for comfort and routine, not a replacement for professional medical care.

Quick safety & FAQ

  • Dilution: Recommended dilution ratios are provided with blends and products — always follow the label.
  • Patch test: Try a small diluted patch on forearm before regular use.
  • Pets: Use caution; many essential oils are concentrated and may affect animals. Keep diffusers in well-ventilated spaces and monitor pets for sensitivity.
  • Pregnancy: Consult a qualified care provider before introducing new essential oils during pregnancy.

Our method — SENSE

A simple framework to choose and use blends: safe, sensory, and repeatable.

S — Select

Choose a blend by aroma profile and supporting intention. We describe top/middle/base notes to help you decide.

E — Evaluate

Check dilution, do a patch test, and consider pets or pregnancy before use.

N — Notice

Use mindfully for a few minutes and observe how scent fits into your routine.

S — Sustain

Store blends away from heat and light. Revisit blends seasonally or as your needs shift.
