Frequently asked

How should I dilute oils?

We recommend dilution percentages for adults, children, and sensitive skin during sessions. Typical adult blends are commonly 1–3% for daily use; guidance varies by oil and context.

Should I do a patch test?

Yes. Apply a tiny diluted amount to the inner forearm and wait 24 hours. If irritation occurs, discontinue use.

What about pets and pregnancy?

We include pet-safe and pregnancy-conscious recommendations. Tell us at booking so we can tailor suggestions; some oils are best avoided around cats, birds, and during certain stages of pregnancy.
