FAQ — Safety & Use

Dilution: Please follow dilution charts provided with each product. For topical application, 1–3% is common for adults; always reference practitioner guidance for individual circumstances.
Patch test: Patch testing is recommended before topical use. Apply diluted product to a small area of skin and wait 24 hours to check for sensitivity.
Pets: Some essential oils can be harmful to pets. Inform us of any animal exposures during consultations so we can recommend pet-safe options and diffusion practices.
Pregnancy & nursing: Certain oils are not recommended during pregnancy and breastfeeding. Consult your healthcare provider and inform the practitioner during intake.
