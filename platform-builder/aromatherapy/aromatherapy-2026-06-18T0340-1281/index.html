Frequently Asked Questions

How does Aromatherapy specifically help with age-related aches and stiffness?
Aromatherapy utilizes essential oils known for their anti-inflammatory, analgesic, and muscle-relaxing properties. For age-related discomfort, these oils can be carefully blended and applied topically to soothe sore joints, ease muscle tension, and improve circulation, offering a gentle yet effective path to greater physical comfort without harsh interventions.
Is Aromatherapy safe for someone taking multiple medications?
Absolutely. Your safety is our utmost priority. We conduct a thorough intake to understand your health history and any medications you are taking. This allows us to select essential oils that are safe, avoid any potential interactions, and ensure your Aromatherapy experience is entirely beneficial and without concern.
What can I expect during a typical Aromatherapy session for physical pain relief?
A session begins with a gentle conversation about your current discomforts and preferences. We then craft a personalized aromatic blend. This blend might be diffused, or carefully applied to specific areas of your body through a gentle touch, or provided for home use. The focus is always on creating a deeply soothing and comforting experience for your body.
Will I need to disrobe for an Aromatherapy session focused on pain relief?
Not at all, unless you prefer. Most of our Aromatherapy applications for physical discomfort can be done with you fully clothed and comfortably seated or reclined. The essential oils are applied topically or diffused, meaning you can remain completely at ease and covered throughout your session.
