Frequently Asked Questions

How can Aromatherapy specifically address my chronic neck and shoulder tension from working at a desk?
We use specific essential oils known for their muscle-relaxing and anti-inflammatory properties, like marjoram or lavender, blended with invigorating oils such as peppermint or rosemary to improve circulation. Your personalized blend will be designed for targeted application, helping to ease tension and encourage better postural habits throughout your workday.
I'm always feeling drained by the end of the week. Can Aromatherapy help with the physical fatigue associated with burnout?
Absolutely. Certain essential oils, like citrus or frankincense, are known to uplift and ground, counteracting the feeling of depletion. We'll create a routine that includes invigorating blends for morning focus and calming blends for evening unwinding, helping to regulate your energy and support physical recovery from prolonged stress.
I'm skeptical about 'mind-body' approaches. How does Aromatherapy actually improve my body awareness?
Aromatherapy provides a direct sensory input that bypasses cognitive thought. By associating specific scents with moments of conscious posture correction or tension release, you create powerful sensory cues. This trains your nervous system to become more attuned to your body's needs, making it easier to notice and adjust your posture before discomfort sets in.
My schedule is packed. How can I realistically incorporate Aromatherapy into my busy professional life?
We focus on simple, micro-interventions that fit seamlessly into your day. This could be a quick roll-on application at your desk, a few deep breaths from an inhaler during a break, or a five-minute foot bath in the evening. Our goal is to provide practical, accessible tools that support your well-being without adding to your overwhelm.
