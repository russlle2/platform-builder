Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is Aromatherapy?
Aromatherapy is a holistic healing treatment that uses natural plant extracts, called essential oils, to promote health and well-being. It harnesses the power of scent to influence your mood, alleviate discomfort, and support your body's natural healing processes.
Are the essential oils you use safe?
Absolutely. We are committed to your safety and well-being. We use only 100% pure, therapeutic-grade essential oils from reputable suppliers. During your consultation, we discuss any sensitivities or health conditions to ensure your custom blend is safe and appropriate for your individual needs.
How can Aromatherapy help me personally?
Aromatherapy can address a wide range of concerns, from stress and anxiety to sleep disturbances, low energy, and even support for seasonal changes. By understanding your specific goals, will create a custom blend tailored to support your unique physical and emotional well-being.
What does a typical Aromatherapy consultation involve?
Your initial consultation is a comprehensive conversation where we discuss your health history, current concerns, and wellness aspirations. We explore your preferences and lifestyle, allowing us to formulate a personalized essential oil blend and provide guidance on how to best incorporate it into your daily routine for maximum benefit.
