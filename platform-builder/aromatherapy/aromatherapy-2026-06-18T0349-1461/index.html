Frequently Asked Questions

What is Aromatherapy and how does it support mental clarity for seniors?
Aromatherapy uses natural plant extracts, called essential oils, for their aromatic and therapeutic benefits. For seniors, specific aromas can gently stimulate areas of the brain associated with memory and focus, helping to reduce feelings of fogginess and promote a sense of calm alertness. It's a subtle way to support your mind's natural functions without needing prior experience.
Do I need to have any prior experience with essential oils to benefit from your Aromatherapy services?
Absolutely not. Our approach at is designed for everyone, especially those new to Aromatherapy. We provide gentle, clear guidance on how to safely and effectively use essential oils to support your cognitive wellness. You'll learn simple, accessible practices that fit comfortably into your routine.
How will Aromatherapy help with my memory and concentration?
Certain essential oils are known for their properties that can gently support cognitive functions. For example, some aromas can help you feel more alert and focused, while others can encourage relaxation, which in turn can improve concentration. We focus on creating custom blends that aim to stimulate your senses in a way that encourages clearer thought and better recall, always with your comfort in mind.
Is Aromatherapy safe for seniors, especially if I'm taking medications?
Your safety and well-being are our top priorities. All our Aromatherapy practices are gentle and non-invasive. During your initial consultation, we will carefully discuss your health history and any medications you are taking to ensure that any essential oils suggested are suitable and safe for you. We always prioritize your comfort and peace of mind.
