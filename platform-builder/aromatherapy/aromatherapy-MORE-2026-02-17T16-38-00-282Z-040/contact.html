Frequently asked — safety & guidance

How do you handle dilution and patch tests?
All topical blends include dilution guidance based on age and context. We recommend a small area patch test 24 hours before topical use; discontinue if irritation occurs.
Are aromatics safe around pets?
Some essential oils can be irritating to pets. We discuss species-specific safety and offer pet-friendly alternatives. Always consult your veterinarian for specific health concerns.
Pregnancy and breastfeeding notes
We follow conservative, safety-first guidelines and avoid certain oils during pregnancy. If pregnant or breastfeeding, please mention this in your message so we can tailor recommendations.
Do you make medical claims?
Our services are supportive and informational; statements use language like "may support." Aromatherapy is not a substitute for medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.
