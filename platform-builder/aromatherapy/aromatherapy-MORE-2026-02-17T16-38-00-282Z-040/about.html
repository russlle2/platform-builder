Frequently asked

Do I need a patch test?

Yes — we recommend a small patch test before applying a new blend topically. We include dilution guidance for all topical products.

Are essential oils safe around pets?

Some oils are not safe for animals. We provide pet-safety guidance and alternatives where needed.

What about pregnancy?

Pregnancy and breastfeeding require special consideration. Please disclose pregnancy status before booking so we can recommend safe options or adapt the session.

Do you make therapeutic claims?

No medical claims are made. We describe possible supportive effects using careful language such as "may support" and recommend medical consultation when appropriate.
