FAQ — safety and use

Are these medical treatments?

No. Aromatic practices are supportive lifestyle tools and may support wellbeing; they are not medical treatments.

Do you include dilution guidance?

Yes. Every blend and kit includes recommended dilution ratios and clear patch-test steps to help reduce risk.

What about pets?

Certain oils can affect animals. We include pet-safety notes and alternatives — always check with your veterinarian for specific concerns.

Pregnancy or medical conditions?

If you are pregnant or have medical concerns, consult your healthcare provider before using essential oils. We avoid making medical claims and provide safety-forward options.
