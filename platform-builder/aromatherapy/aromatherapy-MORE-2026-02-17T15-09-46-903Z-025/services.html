Read the FAQ

FAQ — safety highlights

Patch test: Apply 1 drop diluted to a fingertip area and wait 24 hours. Discontinue if irritation occurs.
Dilution: Typical adult topical dilution ranges from 1% (sensitive) to 3% (standard); we provide exact calculations during sessions.
Pregnancy & children: Some oils are not recommended; please disclose pregnancy or child exposure during booking so we can adapt recommendations.
Pets: Use caution — some essential oils may be unsafe for pets. We'll provide guidance when pets are present at home.
