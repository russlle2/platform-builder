Quick reference — dilution guide

Typical adult topical recommendations (educational):
  • 0.5% — very gentle (infants, very sensitive, some pregnancy situations)
  • 1% — gentle daily support
  • 2% — common for most adults for short-term use
  • 3% — stronger, occasional use; use with care
Drop math: ~20 drops = 1 ml. We show approximate drops for the bottle size you choose.

Safe use reminders

Patch test before topical use. Not all oils are safe in pregnancy or with pets — if sensitive, leave the "Sensitive" checkbox on. This page offers guidance, not diagnosis or treatment.

FAQ — blends

Patch test: Always dilute before topical application and test on a small skin area for 24 hours.
Children & pregnancy: Some oils should be avoided. Tell us during booking so we can adapt blends and dilution.
Pets: Certain oils can be harmful to animals. Use minimal exposure, avoid direct topical use on pets, and consult a vet if concerned.
