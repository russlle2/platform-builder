FAQ & Safety notes

        

          
Are these sessions medical treatments?

          
No. Sessions and blends are designed to be supportive and are not medical advice. Oils and practices "may support" certain states, but results vary. Consult a qualified health provider for medical conditions.


          
What about dilution and patch testing?

          
We recommend conservative dilutions for topical use (often 0.5–2% for adults). Always perform a patch test before broader topical application: apply one drop of diluted blend to a small area of skin and wait 24 hours for any reaction.


          
Pregnancy, nursing, and children

          
If pregnant, nursing, or working with children, follow specialist guidance. Some oils are not recommended; err on the side of caution and seek tailored advice.


          
Pets in the home

          
Many essential oils can affect animals. Keep diffusing subtle, allow animals to leave the room, and consult a veterinarian for any concerns.


          
Can I use blends in shared workspaces?

          
In shared areas keep scent light and ask consent where possible. Use personal inhalers or discreet diffusion when appropriate.
