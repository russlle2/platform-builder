This is an educational, non-medical guide. Always patch test and consult a care provider for health concerns. For pet environments, avoid diffusing certain oils and never apply oils directly to animals.

Frequently asked safety notes

  • Patch test: Apply 1 drop of a 1% dilution to inner forearm; wait 24 hours. If irritation occurs, discontinue.
  • Dilution: Adults commonly use 1–2% for topical blends; children and sensitive skin often use 0.25–1%. These are guidelines and may support safer use but are not medical advice.
  • Pregnancy: Some oils are best avoided in pregnancy. Please tell your practitioner and we will adapt choices.
  • Pets: Many oils can affect animals. Use diffusers with fresh air, avoid prolonged exposure, and never apply oils to pets without veterinary guidance.
