Common misunderstandings

Myth: Strong scent equals better results.
Truth: Subtle, intentional doses often produce the most reliable responses. We favor measured, safe use over intensity.
Myth: One blend fits everyone.
Truth: Personal history, sensitivities, and context change how a blend may affect someone. We create flexible pathways, not one-size-fits-all formulas.
Myth: Aromatherapy replaces medical care.
Truth: Aromatherapy may support wellbeing but is not a substitute for medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

FAQ — safety & guidance

How do you decide dilution?
We use conservative dilutions by default. Typical adult short-term topical blends range from 1–3% depending on use and skin sensitivity. Child, pregnant, and pet-related guidance is more cautious; see the blend builder and always patch test.
What is a patch test?
You apply a small diluted drop to an inconspicuous area (inside forearm) and watch for 24 hours. No redness, itching, or swelling should occur. If irritation appears, discontinue use.
Pets and essential oils
Certain essential oils can be harmful to animals. We recommend consulting your veterinarian before diffuse or topical use near pets. For household scent, use very low concentrations and good ventilation.
Pregnancy and breastfeeding
Some oils are not recommended during pregnancy. If you are pregnant or breastfeeding, consult a qualified health provider before beginning aromatherapy. We provide lower concentration options and alternative approaches.
