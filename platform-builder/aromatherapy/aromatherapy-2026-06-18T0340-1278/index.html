Frequently Asked Questions

How can Aromatherapy specifically help me when I'm going through a big life change?
Aromatherapy provides a profound, non-verbal pathway to the emotional centers of your brain. During significant transitions, this can help gently soothe frayed nerves, reduce feelings of overwhelm, and support the release of emotional tension often held in the body. It helps you find a sense of inner calm and clarity when external circumstances feel turbulent.
Is your approach to Aromatherapy different because of its trauma-informed focus?
Absolutely. Our trauma-informed approach means we prioritize safety, choice, and empowerment. We understand that past experiences can shape your present reactions. Our Aromatherapy practices are introduced with utmost care, never forcing, always inviting, ensuring that the scent experiences support your nervous system without triggering distress, allowing for genuine healing and integration.
What if I'm sensitive to smells? Can I still benefit from Aromatherapy?
Yes, many individuals with sensitivities still find profound benefit. We begin with very gentle applications and carefully selected oils, always respecting your comfort levels. We can explore various methods, such as indirect diffusion or even energetic imprints, to ensure a supportive experience without overwhelming your senses. Your comfort and agency are always paramount.
How quickly can I expect to feel a difference with Aromatherapy during a life transition?
The experience of Aromatherapy is deeply personal. Some individuals report an immediate sense of calm or upliftment. For deeper emotional integration during a major life transition, effects are often cumulative. Consistent, gentle engagement with your personalized blends supports a gradual, yet profound, shift in your emotional landscape and overall resilience over time.
