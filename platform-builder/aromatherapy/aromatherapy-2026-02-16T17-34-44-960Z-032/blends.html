Frequently asked safety notes

Dilution basics
We recommend conservative dilutions. For topical products a 1–2% dilution is common for adults; consult directly if you have sensitivities. Children and older adults often use lower dilutions.
Patch testing steps
Apply 1–2 drops of a diluted sample to the inside forearm, cover, and check at 24 and 48 hours for responses.
Pets and essential oils
Not all oils are safe around animals. We list pet-safe options and diffuser placement guidelines; always consult a vet for concerns.
Pregnancy & nursing note
Certain oils are avoided in pregnancy. If you are pregnant or nursing, choose a session labelled 'pregnancy-aware' and follow the practitioner's conservative guidance.
All descriptions are supportive and not medical advice. For medical concerns consult a healthcare professional.
