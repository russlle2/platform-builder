Frequently asked, answered with care

What dilution should I use?

Recommended dilutions vary by application and age. Typical roller dilutions for adults are 2–5% essential oils in a carrier. We include exact ratios for each blend and purpose, and we never recommend undiluted application to skin.

How do I perform a patch test?

Apply a small amount of a diluted blend to an inner forearm and observe for 24 hours. If irritation occurs, discontinue use and consult the practitioner. Patch tests are part of every new topical recommendation.

Are essential oils safe for pets?

Some oils can be harmful to animals. We provide explicit pet-safety guidance and alternative blends when animals are present. Never apply essential oils directly to a pet without veterinary approval.

Can I use oils during pregnancy?

Pregnancy requires special consideration. We use conservative, evidence-aware guidance and will avoid or modify recommendations for pregnant or breastfeeding clients. Always consult your healthcare provider alongside our guidance.
