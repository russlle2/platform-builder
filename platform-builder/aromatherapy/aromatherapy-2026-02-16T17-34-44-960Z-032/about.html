Quick safety notes

  • Dilution: I recommend dilution guidelines for topical blends based on age and sensitivity. Never apply undiluted essential oils to skin.
  • Patch tests: A small skin patch test is advised before longer-term topical use.
  • Pets: Some botanicals can affect animals. Tell me about household pets so I can make safe suggestions.
