Common safety questions

What dilution should I use?
Typical topical dilutions range 0.5–3% for adults depending on use; consult during a session for personalised guidance. Never use undiluted essential oils neat on skin unless directed by a qualified professional.
How to patch test?
Apply a small diluted amount to the inner forearm, cover with a plaster, and observe for 24 hours. Discontinue if irritation occurs.
Are essential oils safe around pets?
Some oils can be harmful to animals. We provide pet-aware alternatives and diffuser placement recommendations during consultations.
What about pregnancy and nursing?
Certain oils are avoided in pregnancy. Pregnant or breastfeeding clients will receive specific, conservative guidance and will not be offered contraindicated oils.
