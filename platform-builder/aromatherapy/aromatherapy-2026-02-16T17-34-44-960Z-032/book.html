FAQ — quick guidance

Dilution: Typical adult topical dilutions range 1–3% for routine use; lower for sensitive individuals and children.

Patch test: Apply a small diluted sample to inner forearm and observe for 24 hours before broader use.

Pets: Keep diffusers elevated and rooms ventilated; inform us of species and size for tailored advice.

Pregnancy & medication: We will default to extra caution and avoid certain botanicals; always coordinate with your healthcare provider.
