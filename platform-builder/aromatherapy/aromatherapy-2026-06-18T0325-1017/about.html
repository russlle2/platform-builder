How can Aromatherapy specifically help my postpartum stress and nervous system?

Aromatherapy for postpartum mothers focuses on specific essential oils known to downregulate sympathetic nervous system activity, reducing the 'fight or flight' response. This can help alleviate feelings of overwhelm, promote relaxation, and encourage more restful sleep, supporting your body's natural recovery processes.

Is Aromatherapy safe for me and my baby during the postpartum period?

Absolutely. Your safety and your baby's well-being are our utmost priority. We use only high-quality, ethically sourced essential oils and provide clear, gentle guidelines for their use, often recommending diffusion or topical application diluted in carrier oils. We'll discuss any concerns and tailor recommendations to your unique circumstances, especially if you are breastfeeding.

What kind of Aromatherapy blends can I expect for nervous system regulation?

You can expect bespoke blends featuring calming and grounding essential oils such as lavender, frankincense, bergamot, and Roman chamomile, among others. Each blend is formulated after a thorough consultation to address your individual emotional state, sleep patterns, and stress levels, designed to gently guide your nervous system back to balance.

How do I integrate Aromatherapy into my already busy schedule as a new mother?

We understand your time is precious. Our approach emphasizes simple, impactful rituals that seamlessly fit into your day. This could be a few drops on a pulse point, a calming diffuser blend during nap time, or a soothing bath ritual after your baby is asleep. We'll help you create sustainable practices that nurture you without adding overwhelm.
