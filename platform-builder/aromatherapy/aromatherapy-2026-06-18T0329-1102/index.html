Frequently Asked Questions

How can Aromatherapy specifically support my sleep during postpartum recovery?
Aromatherapy, when thoughtfully applied, can profoundly impact your postpartum sleep by calming an overactive mind, easing physical discomforts, and promoting a deep sense of peace. We select specific essences that support hormonal balance and nervous system regulation, guiding your body back to its natural rhythm of rest.
Are your Aromatherapy blends safe to use while I am breastfeeding or caring for my newborn?
Absolutely. Your safety and your baby's well-being are paramount. We meticulously formulate all blends with utmost care, using only pure, therapeutic-grade essential oils and always considering dilution rates appropriate for mothers who are breastfeeding or in close contact with their infants. We will discuss all considerations during your consultation.
What kind of results can I expect from integrating Aromatherapy into my nightly routine?
Many new mothers report experiencing a deeper sense of calm, a reduction in the time it takes to fall asleep, and more sustained periods of rest. Our goal is to help you feel more replenished and less overwhelmed, allowing your body the vital sleep it needs to recover and thrive during your postpartum period.
