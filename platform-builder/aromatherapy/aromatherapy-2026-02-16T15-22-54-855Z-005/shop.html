Shop FAQ — Safety highlights

Dilution & safe topical use

Products that are for topical use arrive pre-diluted with labeled dilution percentages. If you make your own blends, follow provided dilution charts and consult before applying near sensitive skin.

Patch test

Patch test new topical products: apply a small amount to inner forearm and wait 24–48 hours to check for reactions.

Pets & diffusion

Diffusion guidance is included. Some oils are not recommended around cats or small animals; check product details or ask before purchase.

Pregnancy notes

Certain oils are contraindicated in pregnancy. Pregnant or nursing customers should consult with the practitioner before buying or using products.
