Common questions — safety first

What dilution should I use for topical blends?
Dilution depends on age, sensitivity, and purpose. A typical starting point for adults is 1–3% for general use and up to 5% for short-term targeted application. I always provide exact recipes and encourage patch testing. This is educational guidance, not medical advice.
How do I do a patch test?
Apply a small amount of the diluted blend (a drop) to the inside of the forearm, cover with a bandage, and wait 24 hours. If irritation, redness, or itching occurs, discontinue use. We’ll walk through this in sessions.
Are essential oils safe for pets?
Some oils can be harmful to pets. We prioritize pet-safe recommendations and will avoid or substitute oils known to be problematic. Always consult your veterinarian for specific medical concerns.
Pregnancy and breastfeeding considerations?
Several oils are contraindicated during pregnancy or breastfeeding. I provide conservative, research-informed options and will refer to qualified medical professionals when appropriate. Your safety is the priority.
