Usage notes & safety reminders

Dilution guidance

We provide specific dilution recommendations for adults and for when to reduce dilution for sensitive skin. Seek personalized guidance during a consult.

Patch testing

Patch test any new topical blend: apply a small diluted amount to inner forearm and observe 24–48 hours for irritation.

Pets

Some essential oils can affect pets. We list alternatives and recommend brief, supervised diffusion sessions or pet-free zones.

Pregnancy & nursing

Certain oils are not recommended during pregnancy or breastfeeding. We select safe options when needed and document exclusions.
