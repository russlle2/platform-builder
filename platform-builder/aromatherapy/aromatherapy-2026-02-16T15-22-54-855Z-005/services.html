Frequently asked — safety first

Dilution & measurements

Every blend includes a recommended dilution percentage and exact drops per carrier volume. Adults and children have different guidance; we always personalize.

Patch testing

A patch test is recommended when trying a new blend. Apply diluted product to a small skin area and wait 24–48 hours; if irritation occurs, discontinue use.

Pets & diffuser use

Some oils may be irritating to pets. We provide pet-safe diffusion practices and list oils to avoid around cats and dogs.

Pregnancy & special conditions

Certain oils are not recommended during pregnancy or for people with specific conditions. We screen during intake and select safe alternatives.
