Common questions

Dilution depends on the application, age, and condition. Typical guidelines: 1% for children/elderly, 1.5–3% for daily adult topical use. Your session includes a clear dilution chart tailored to your needs. These are general recommendations and not medical advice.
A patch test applies a small diluted amount to a discreet skin area and observes for 24–48 hours. We walk you through safe patch testing during your appointment to check for sensitivities.
Some oils are not safe near pets. We always ask about animals in the household and provide pet-safe alternatives and usage notes — including diffusion duration limits and ventilation practices.
Certain oils and high concentrations are best avoided during pregnancy. We adhere to conservative, safety-first guidance and will create pregnancy-appropriate blends or recommend alternatives.
