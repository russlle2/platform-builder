Booking FAQs

Dilution & safety
We provide clear dilution ranges for topical use and discuss carrier oils, patch testing, and age-appropriate suggestions. No medical promises are made.
Patch testing
Patch testing is recommended prior to topical use. We will guide you through a simple at-home patch test during and after the session.
Pets & home diffusion
We ask about pets in your household and provide pet-safe diffusion recommendations. Certain oils should be avoided or used with extra caution around animals.
Pregnancy & breastfeeding
If you are pregnant or breastfeeding, we take extra precautions and provide options that prioritize safety. We will not suggest products that are contraindicated; always consult your healthcare provider for pregnancy-specific medical advice.
