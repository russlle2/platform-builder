A simple ritual to begin

We prioritize clear, repeatable steps you can bring home. Each session includes a consultation, an aroma profile review, and a guided blending or diffusion ritual you can adapt for daily use.

  1. Ground: 2–3 deep breaths to orient the senses.
  2. Observe: note top, middle, base impressions without judgement.
  3. Blend: combine a safe dilution in a carrier and test on patch area.
  4. Practice: diffuse for 10–20 minutes in a well-ventilated room.

Why ritual matters

Ritual creates structure—helping scent associations form reliably. We keep instructions practical and backed by safety best practices (no medical claims).

What to expect in a session

  • 30–90 minutes depending on depth — consultation and aroma sampling.
  • Safety review including skin sensitivity, medication considerations, and household pets.
  • Takeaway personalized notes, a labeled sample, and a follow-up plan.

FAQ

Recommended dilutions are given per-session and are typically 1–3% for adults. We discuss carrier oils and low-volume testing; do not apply undiluted essential oils to skin. This is general guidance, not medical advice.
A patch test checks skin sensitivity: apply a diluted amount to the inner forearm and wait 24 hours for any reaction. We perform supervised patch tests when creating a topical blend.
Some oils are not safe for pets. We review household animals and recommend pet-safe usage, low concentrations, and avoiding closed-room exposure. Always consult your veterinarian for specific concerns.
Pregnancy requires extra caution. We follow conservative guidelines and may avoid certain oils or recommend modifications. We do not provide medical advice—consult your healthcare provider if pregnant.
