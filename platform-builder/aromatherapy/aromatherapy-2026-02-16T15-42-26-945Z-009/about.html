Daily ritual to ground the senses

A simple, repeatable ritual can help you connect to scent with intention. Here’s a gentle practice I offer clients to adapt to their routine.

Morning Inhale (2–3 min)
Choose a single uplifting note like sweet orange or rosemary. Inhale gently for three mindful breaths to set intention for the day.
Midday Reset
Diffuse a low dilution of a calming blend for 20–40 minutes if needed. Keep windows or ventilation in mind.
Evening Ritual
A light topical roll-on (1–2% dilution) applied to wrists or chest as part of wind-down. Patch-test first.
