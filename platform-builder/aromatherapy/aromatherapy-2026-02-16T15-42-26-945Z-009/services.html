The ritual — how a session flows

We design a simple, repeatable ritual so aromatic practice fits your day. Each session follows a predictable, safety-forward sequence:

1. Intake — aromatic preferences, scent history, medications, pregnancy/pet exposure notes.
2. Sensory mapping — short smell test  (no inhalation beyond comfort), identify evocative notes.
3. Blend design — choose top/middle/base notes, determine dilution and carrier.
4. Safety plan — patch testing, dilution instructions, storage/label guidance.
5. Ritual coaching — how to use diffusers, inhalers, roll-ons, and integrate into a daily pause.

What to expect

Sessions are educational and experiential. Expect neutral, non-invasive guidance: no medical diagnosis, no replacement for healthcare. We emphasize gentle practices, clear dilution math, and pet/pregnancy considerations.

Frequently asked — safety & best practice

What dilution should I use?
Dilution depends on age, sensitivity, and application. Typical topical roll-ons range 1–5% essential oil in a carrier. We provide precise ml/percentage instructions in each session.
Do I need to patch test?
Yes — we always recommend a small patch test on inner forearm for 24 hours before regular topical use.
Are blends safe around pets?
Some oils can irritate or harm pets. We design pet-aware options and teach safe diffuser placement and ventilation. Always disclose household pets during intake.
Pregnancy & breastfeeding
We take a safety-first approach. Certain oils are avoided in pregnancy and breastfeeding. Bring your care team questions and we'll advise with conservative, evidence-aware guidance.
