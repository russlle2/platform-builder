Common questions

How are essential oils diluted?
Dilutions follow safety guidelines: typically 1–3% for adults in topical blends unless otherwise directed. Specific dilution is set per client, accounting for age, skin sensitivity, and context.
Should I patch-test new blends?
Yes. We recommend a patch test on a small skin area 24–48 hours prior to regular topical use, especially for sensitive skin or new ingredients.
What about pets and essential oils?
Some oils can be irritating to animals. Sessions will include guidance on safe diffusion and storage. If you have pets, please mention species and any known sensitivities during booking.
Pregnancy and aromatherapy
Pregnancy requires cautious selection and dilution. Notify the practitioner if you are pregnant or breastfeeding; we will tailor recommendations and avoid contraindicated oils.
