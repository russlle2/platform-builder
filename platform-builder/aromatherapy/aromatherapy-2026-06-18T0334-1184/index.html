Frequently Asked Questions

How does Aromatherapy address the emotional stress unique to athletes?
Aromatherapy offers direct pathways to your limbic system, the brain's emotional center. For athletes, this means using specific aromas to help regulate the nervous system, process performance anxiety, ease the emotional impact of injuries, and cultivate a sense of calm resilience, rather than just masking discomfort.
Can Aromatherapy truly aid in processing the emotional aftermath of sports injuries?
Absolutely. Injuries often carry a profound emotional toll – grief for lost ability, frustration, and fear. Trauma-informed Aromatherapy provides gentle, non-invasive support for these complex feelings. By creating a safe sensory experience, it can help you release tension, reduce feelings of overwhelm, and foster a sense of hope and self-compassion during recovery.
Is Aromatherapy safe for someone managing chronic physical stress and fatigue?
Yes, when practiced by a knowledgeable practitioner, Aromatherapy can be a comforting support. We carefully select essential oils known for their gentle calming and restorative properties. Our approach is always trauma-informed, ensuring that the aromas and applications feel safe and supportive for your unique nervous system, without adding to your fatigue or overwhelm.
How is trauma-informed Aromatherapy different for active individuals?
Trauma-informed Aromatherapy for active individuals recognizes that intense physical demands can create stress responses in the body that mirror trauma. We focus on creating a sense of safety and agency, using scents that ground and stabilize, rather than stimulating. The goal is to help you gently release stored tension, rebuild a sense of inner security, and integrate your experiences with a deepened sense of self-awareness and strength.
