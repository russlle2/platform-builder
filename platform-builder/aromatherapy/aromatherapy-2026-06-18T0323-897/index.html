Frequently Asked Questions

How can Aromatherapy specifically help me during postpartum recovery?
Aromatherapy can offer targeted support for common postpartum experiences like occasional emotional shifts, sleep disturbances, and skin care. We focus on gentle, evidence-based applications designed to soothe and support your body and mind as you navigate this unique phase, always prioritizing your safety and well-being.
Is Aromatherapy safe to use while I am breastfeeding my baby?
Yes, with proper guidance. We provide detailed, evidence-informed recommendations on essential oils that are safe for use during breastfeeding, including appropriate dilutions and application methods. Your safety and your baby's health are paramount, and we will ensure you have all the necessary information to use Aromatherapy responsibly.
Can Aromatherapy assist with seasonal wellness concerns during my postpartum period?
Absolutely. Your body is undergoing significant changes postpartum, and seasonal shifts can add another layer of adjustment. We can craft specific aromatic blends to help support your immune system, manage seasonal skin dryness, or uplift your mood, providing gentle, preventive care tailored to both your postpartum status and the time of year.
What kind of scientific evidence supports the use of Aromatherapy for new mothers?
Our practice is built on a foundation of scientific research. We draw upon studies examining the effects of essential oils on mood, sleep quality, and skin health. We share this evidence with you, explaining how specific aromatic compounds interact with your body to provide support during your postpartum recovery, ensuring a clinical yet compassionate approach.
