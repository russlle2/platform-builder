Questions & safety notes

How should I dilute essential oils?

We teach safe dilution rates commonly used in aromatherapy (e.g., 1–3% for adults for topical use). Your session includes a tailored dilution chart. Always avoid undiluted application unless instructed for very specific non-skin uses by a qualified practitioner.

What is a patch test?

A patch test checks sensitivity: apply a small diluted amount to an inner forearm and wait 24 hours. If irritation occurs, discontinue. We guide you through this step-by-step during sessions.

Are essential oils safe around pets?

Many oils can affect animals. We provide pet-safe guidance and alternative suggestions during consults. Always keep diffusers and concentrated oils away from pets and consult a veterinarian for concerns.

Pregnancy and nursing considerations

Certain oils are not recommended during pregnancy or breastfeeding. We follow conservative, safety-first guidance and will adjust recommendations accordingly.
