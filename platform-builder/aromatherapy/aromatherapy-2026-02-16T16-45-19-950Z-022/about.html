Common questions

Are oils safe during pregnancy?

We take a conservative approach. If pregnant or nursing, some oils are avoided; we provide alternatives and always advise consulting your healthcare provider.

Can I use blends around pets?

Not all oils are animal-safe. We'll share pet-specific recommendations and non-topical options when needed.

Do you make custom blends?

Yes — custom blends are part of intensives and bundles. Each comes with dilution percentages and storage tips.
