Safety & usage notes

These are suggested formula ideas. For topical blends, always follow recommended dilutions, perform a patch test, and avoid oils that are contraindicated for pregnancy or unsafe for pets. For any personalized blend, we will review your sensitivities and situation before creating it.

Common questions

What dilution do you use?

Dilution is tailored. Typical topical dilutions are conservative (1–3% for daily wear), and we'll include application instructions and labeling with every custom blend.

How do I patch test?

Apply a small amount of diluted product to an inner forearm and wait 24 hours. Discontinue if irritation occurs. Reach out if you have a history of sensitivities.

Are these blends safe around pets?

Some oils may be irritating to pets. We'll recommend diffusion practices, distance, timing, or pet-safe alternatives when needed. Consult your vet for specific concerns.

Can pregnant people use these?

Pregnancy-specific safety matters. We avoid certain oils and opt for pregnancy-safe substitutes; please disclose pregnancy during intake so we can adapt blends.
