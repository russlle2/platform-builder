Frequently asked — safety first

How do you handle dilution?

Dilution is personalized. We use conservative, recommended dilutions (typically 1–5% for topical blends unless advised otherwise) and provide clear application instructions. We never suggest exceeding safe rates.

Do I need a patch test?

Yes — for topical blends, a patch test is encouraged before regular use, especially if you have sensitive skin.

Are essential oils safe around pets?

We prioritize pet safety: some oils are best avoided around animals and we'll suggest diffusion practices and placement to reduce exposure. Always consult your veterinarian if unsure.

What about pregnancy?

Pregnancy needs extra caution. We avoid certain oils and use pregnancy-safe alternatives. Inform us early in the intake so formulations are appropriate.
