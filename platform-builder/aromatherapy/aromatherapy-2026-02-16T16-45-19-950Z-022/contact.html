A few safety-first FAQs

How should I dilute essential oils?
Dilution depends on age, sensitivity, and intended use. Typical rates: 1% for facial blends, 1–3% for daily topical mixes, and up to 5% for short-term spot use. Your intensive includes a bespoke dilution chart. Always use a carrier oil and follow patch-test guidance.
What is a patch test?
A patch test helps check skin sensitivity: apply a small diluted sample to the inner forearm for 24 hours. If irritation occurs, discontinue use.
Are essential oils safe around pets?
Some oils can be harmful to animals. During consultations we review household pets and provide safe-use guidelines or pet-friendly alternatives.
What about pregnancy or breastfeeding?
Pregnancy and breastfeeding require extra caution. We follow conservative, evidence-aligned guidance and will suggest appropriate safe options or defer when needed.
