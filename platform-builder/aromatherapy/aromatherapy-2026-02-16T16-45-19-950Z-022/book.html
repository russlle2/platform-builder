Booking FAQs

Do you require a patch test?

We recommend a patch test before consistent topical use. During the session we'll guide you through performing one safely.

What if I have pets?

Please mention pets in your notes. We'll offer pet-safe alternatives and advice on ventilation, placement, and which oils to avoid.

Pregnancy & nursing guidance

We proceed conservatively for pregnancy and nursing, avoiding certain essential oils and sharing non-topical options. Always consult your healthcare provider too.
