FAQ — session & safety basics

How is dilution handled?
Dilution recommendations are provided for skin safety (commonly 1–3% for adults depending on use). Exact dilutions will be clear on your label.
Do I need a patch test?
Yes — a small patch test is recommended before regular topical use. We'll guide you through it during your session.
What about pets?
We prioritise pet safety. Certain oils are not suitable around animals; we'll tailor guidance and suggest pet-friendly approaches.
Pregnancy & nursing note
If pregnant or nursing, we proceed conservatively and may avoid certain chemotypes. Always consult your healthcare provider as well.
