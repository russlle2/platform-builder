Aromatherapy

Finding Stability: Aromatherapy for Postural Support During Transition

Major life changes, whether expected or sudden, can subtly alter how you hold yourself, creating tension or discomfort. Our Aromatherapy sessions are designed to gently guide you back to an awareness of your posture and how your body moves through space. We’ll explore bespoke essential oil blends that encourage relaxation in specific muscle groups, helping you release habitual bracing and find a more comfortable, aligned way of being in your own skin, even amidst external shifts.
