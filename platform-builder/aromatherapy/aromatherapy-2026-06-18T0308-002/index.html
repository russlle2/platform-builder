Frequently Asked Questions

How long until I notice benefits from my custom blend?
Most clients experience subtle shifts within 7-10 days of consistent use. Deep physiological effects like improved sleep patterns or reduced anxiety typically emerge in 3-4 weeks. Your personal journey is unique, but we provide clear milestones to track your progress.
Can Aromatherapy help with specific stress triggers?
Absolutely. We design blends targeting your exact stressors—whether work deadlines, family dynamics, or chronic discomfort. For example, a morning blend might contain rosemary for focus and bergamot for tension relief, while an evening formula uses chamomile and vetiver for calm. Your triggers guide the formulation.
Are your oils safe for daily use on sensitive skin?
Every carrier oil we use is cold-pressed and skin-tested. We avoid common irritants like synthetic fragrances. All blends come with a patch test guide and usage instructions tailored to your skin type—ensuring safety without compromising efficacy.
How do you choose which botanicals to include?
We prioritize clinical research over trends. Each oil is selected for evidence-based benefits: lavender for sleep efficacy (per JAMA studies), frankincense for skin barrier support, or neroli for cortisol reduction. We never include oils without documented wellness impact.
