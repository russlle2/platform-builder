Frequently Asked Questions

How can Aromatherapy specifically support my nervous system as a senior?
Aromatherapy for seniors focuses on gentle, calming essential oils that help to signal relaxation to your nervous system. This can lead to a reduction in feelings of anxiousness, promote more restful sleep, and foster an overall sense of serenity, aiding in a more balanced daily rhythm.
Do I need any prior knowledge or experience with essential oils to benefit from Aromatherapy?
Absolutely not. Our approach is designed to be accessible and gentle, requiring no prior experience. We will guide you through every step, explaining the benefits of each aroma and ensuring your comfort and understanding throughout your Aromatherapy experience.
Is Aromatherapy safe for me, especially if I have existing health considerations?
Your safety and well-being are paramount. We conduct a thorough, gentle consultation to understand your health profile and any sensitivities you may have. We then carefully select and dilute essential oils to ensure a safe and beneficial Aromatherapy experience tailored specifically for you.
What kind of changes might I notice in my daily life after experiencing Aromatherapy?
Many seniors find that regular, gentle Aromatherapy helps to soften daily stresses, leading to moments of deeper calm and greater ease. You might notice an improved sense of peacefulness, more balanced moods, and a gentle support for your overall emotional and physical comfort.
