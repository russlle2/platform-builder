Frequently Asked Questions

How can Aromatherapy specifically help seniors with seasonal changes?
Aromatherapy offers gentle support for seniors by addressing common seasonal shifts. For example, specific aromas can help uplift spirits during darker winter months, ease discomfort from seasonal allergies in spring, or promote restful sleep when summer evenings are long. We create custom blends to help your body and mind adapt gracefully to each season.
Is Aromatherapy safe for someone taking other medications or with health conditions?
Your safety and well-being are our top priority. We always recommend discussing any new regimen, including Aromatherapy, with your primary healthcare provider. During your consultation, we'll carefully review your health history and current medications to ensure that any aromatic blends we suggest are gentle, appropriate, and won't interfere with your existing care.
What kind of preventive care does Aromatherapy offer for seniors?
Our preventive care approach uses Aromatherapy to support overall well-being and maintain comfort. This might include blends to encourage deeper relaxation for better sleep, aromas that support a sense of calm and reduce everyday stresses, or gentle applications to soothe muscles after light activity. We focus on fostering your natural resilience and comfort throughout the year.
How often should I use Aromatherapy for seasonal wellness?
The frequency of use for your Aromatherapy blends is always tailored to your individual needs and preferences. Some blends might be ideal for daily use, like a comforting aroma diffused in your living space, while others could be applied as needed for specific seasonal concerns. We'll provide clear, gentle guidance during your consultation to ensure you get the most benefit.
