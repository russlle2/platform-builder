FAQ — Safety & practical notes

Dilution & patch test

We recommend dilute topical blends (commonly 1-3% for adults) and a small patch-test on the forearm when using a new oil. We discuss safe dilution in sessions.

Pregnancy & medical conditions

Some oils are best avoided during pregnancy or with certain conditions. Please tell us about pregnancy, medication, or health concerns before a consult so we can adapt suggestions. No medical treatments are provided.

Pets

Many essential oils can affect animals. We'll cover pet-safe options and diffusion practices to keep companions safe.

Allergies & sensitivities

If you have known allergies, inform us and we will avoid those materials. Patch-testing is always encouraged.
