Frequently asked practical questions

Are these medical therapies?

No. Sessions are experience-based and educational. They may support wellbeing but are not a substitute for medical treatment.

How should I dilute oils and test for sensitivity?

Safe dilution is covered in every workshop. A common home guideline is 1–3% in a carrier for topical use; always do a patch test and reduce concentration for children, pregnancy, or if you have skin concerns.

What about pets and pregnancy?

Some aromatics are not appropriate for pregnancy or for pets. If pregnant or caring for animals, share that up front so we can suggest safe alternatives.
