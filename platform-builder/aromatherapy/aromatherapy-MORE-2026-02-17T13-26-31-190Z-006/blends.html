Starter blends

Calm Corner — Diffuser

3 drops lavender, 2 drops bergamot, 1 drop frankincense. May support a restful space. Avoid near cats; reduce citrus around young children.

Notes: 3–5 drops total for a small room.

Wake-up Focus — Inhaler or Diffuser

2 drops rosemary, 2 drops sweet orange, 1 drop peppermint. Short inhalations may assist alertness for some people. Skip peppermint with small children.

Evening Ease — Roll-on (3% dilution)

In 10ml jojoba: 5 drops chamomile, 4 drops sandalwood, 1 drop mandarin. Patch test on inner forearm first.

Grounding Mini — Personal inhaler

2 drops vetiver, 1 drop bergamot. Keep for desk use; inhale gently twice when you need a clear break.

Safety primer

Always dilute essential oils for topical use and perform a patch test. Be mindful of pregnancy, medications, and animals—some oils should be avoided. If in doubt, choose milder plant aromatics and speak with a health professional before use.
