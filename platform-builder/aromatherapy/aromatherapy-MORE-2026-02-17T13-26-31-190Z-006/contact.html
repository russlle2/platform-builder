Frequently asked safety notes

What about dilution?
Topical use should follow safe dilution guidance. We recommend low concentrations for general use; always follow product labels and professional resources.
Should I do a patch test?
Yes. Patch testing is recommended before skin application to check for sensitivity.
What about pets?
Essential oils can affect animals differently. Keep products away from pets and consult a veterinarian familiar with aromatherapy before using near animals.
Pregnancy & nursing
Certain oils are not recommended during pregnancy or while breastfeeding. Please consult a qualified health professional or a medical provider before use.
