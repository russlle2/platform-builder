Our focus

Practical guidance on scent safety, easy blends, and rituals you can repeat. No medical promises — just reliable, safety-forward education.

How sessions are structured

  • Welcome & safety window: We review any preferences, pregnancy notes, and pet considerations.
  • Scent introduction: Learning simple profiles and choosing materials to test.
  • Practice: Guided breathing or journaling with scent support (optional) to anchor the learning.
  • Takeaway: A short plan and dilution guidance you can use at home.
