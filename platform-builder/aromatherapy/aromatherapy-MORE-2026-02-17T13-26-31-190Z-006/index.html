Frequently asked — safety-first

Are essential oils safe during pregnancy?
Some oils are best avoided in pregnancy. We recommend consulting your healthcare provider and following session guidance; individual considerations are discussed in private consults.
How should I dilute oils?
We teach conservative dilution guidelines during workshops (e.g., 1% for facial use); always patch test and follow safety steps provided in sessions.
What about pets?
Certain oils can affect animals differently. We cover pet-safe choices and diffusion practices so home use considers all household members.
Do you make medical claims?
No. We do not claim to treat or diagnose conditions. Language used is that practices may support comfort, routine, or attention.
