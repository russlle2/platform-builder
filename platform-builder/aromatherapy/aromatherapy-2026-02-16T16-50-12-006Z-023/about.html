A scent-led story

My path began with curiosity and a small bottle of lavender. Over years I studied extraction notes, safe dilution, and how scent sits with memory. I craft blends with an emphasis on honest sourcing and clear guidance so you can use aroma in ways that are intentional and well-informed.

I prioritize safety: dilution charts, patch testing, and tailored notes if you live with pets, are pregnant, or caring for infants. If you're ever unsure, we begin with a brief consultation to make a plan together.

Philosophy
Listen: Tune into how scent affects your body and mood.
Layer: Create moments through small rituals — a diffuser, a roll-on, a linen mist.
Learn: Practice safe dilution, store oils carefully, and patch test.
