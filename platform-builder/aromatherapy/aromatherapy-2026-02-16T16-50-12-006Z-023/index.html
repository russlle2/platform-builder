The tale behind the bottles

I started blending in a cramped kitchen and learning to listen — to neighbours, to seasons, to my own breath. Today I share approachable, safety-first aromatherapy for people who want ritual without fuss.

I never make medical claims; instead I offer practices that invite relaxation, focus, or ritual. Every recommendation includes safety guidance, dilution guidance, and options for pets and pregnancy where relevant.

My simple four-step framework

  1. Listen: Share what you're hoping to invite into your day without promising cures.
  2. Personalize: A bespoke blend idea with dilution and safety notes.
  3. Practice: Short rituals and use-cases suited to your life and home.
  4. Reflect: Gentle check-ins so scenting becomes sustainable.

Safety-first: I do not provide medical advice. Consult your healthcare provider for health concerns. Follow dilution and patch test guidance provided with each blend.

Featured blends (teaser)

Below are ten thoughtfully composed blends — each lists top/middle/base notes, aroma profile, and supportive intentions. Always follow dilution and patch-test guidance.

  • 1. Quiet Morning — Top: Bergamot; Middle: Lavender; Base: Cedarwood. Profile: bright-herbal, calm. Supports: gentle focus and a grounded start.
  • 2. Evening Hearth — Top: Sweet Orange; Middle: Clary Sage; Base: Vanilla CO2. Profile: warm-spicy, cozy. Supports: evening ritual, winding down.
  • 3. Forest Walk — Top: Pine; Middle: Rosemary; Base: Vetiver. Profile: green-woody. Supports: clear-mindedness and connection to nature.
  • 4. Citrus Lift — Top: Grapefruit; Middle: Neroli; Base: Sandalwood. Profile: effervescent-floral. Supports: mood refresh and light energy.
  • 5. Cozy Study — Top: Cardamom; Middle: Clove; Base: Benzoin. Profile: spicy-amber. Supports: gentle concentration for reading or creative work.
  • 6. Gentle Clarity — Top: Lemon; Middle: Peppermint; Base: Light Wood Blend. Profile: crisp-herbal. Supports: clear thinking without overstimulation.
  • 7. Rose Afternoon — Top: Geranium; Middle: Rose; Base: Cedar. Profile: soft-floral. Supports: self-care pauses and mood-softening.
  • 8. Night Anchor — Top: Marjoram; Middle: Chamomile; Base: Patchouli. Profile: herbal-earthy. Supports: relaxation cues for sleep routines.
  • 9. Breeze Blend — Top: Eucalyptus; Middle: Blue Tansy; Base: Cypress. Profile: fresh-camphorous. Supports: clearing the air (use caution around pets; see notes).
  • 10. Soft Courage — Top: Black Pepper; Middle: Lavender; Base: Frankincense. Profile: warm-resinous. Supports: steadying moments before presentations or interviews.

Each blend includes safety notes: recommended dilution ranges, patch-test instructions, and pet/pregnancy alternatives as appropriate.
