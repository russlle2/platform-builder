Frequently asked safety questions

How should I dilute oils?
Dilution depends on age, sensitivity, and use. A common guideline is 1–3% for adults in topical blends; lower for children and sensitive skin. I provide explicit dilution for each custom blend.
What is a patch test?
Apply a small diluted drop to the inner forearm, cover for 24 hours, and check for irritation. If redness or discomfort occurs, discontinue use.
How do I use blends around pets?
Some essential oils can be toxic to animals. Avoid leaving diffusers running unattended; keep animals in a separate room while diffusing and consult a veterinarian for specific concerns.
Are blends safe during pregnancy?
Certain oils are not recommended in pregnancy. If you are pregnant or nursing, please consult your healthcare provider and disclose this in any intake so blends can be adjusted accordingly.
Note: These blends are aromatic suggestions and learning tools. They are not medical treatments. For medical advice, please consult a licensed professional. Every blend includes guidance on dilution and a reminder to patch test.
