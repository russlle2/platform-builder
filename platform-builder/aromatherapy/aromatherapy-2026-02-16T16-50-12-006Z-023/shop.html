Product safety & FAQ

Dilution: Each topical product is labeled with dilution. If you have sensitive skin, start lower than the suggested dilution and patch test.

Patch test: Apply a small amount on inner forearm for 24 hours to check for irritation before regular use.

Pets: Keep diffusers in a well-ventilated space and avoid prolonged exposure when pets are present. Some oils are unsafe for animals—consult us if you have concerns.

Pregnancy & medications: If pregnant, nursing, or on medication, consult your healthcare provider before using essential oils. We can tailor blends with safety in mind.

Shipping & returns: Local pickup and standard shipping available. Opened products may not be returnable for hygiene reasons—contact us if there’s an issue.
