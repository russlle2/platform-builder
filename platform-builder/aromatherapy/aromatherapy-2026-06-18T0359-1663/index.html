Frequently Asked Questions

How can Aromatherapy specifically help with college stress and immune support?
Aromatherapy uses plant essences to gently influence your mood and body. For stress, certain aromas can signal your nervous system to relax, helping to calm racing thoughts. For immune support, essential oils possess properties that can encourage your body's natural defenses, helping you feel more resilient to the everyday demands of campus life.
I'm new to wellness practices. Is Aromatherapy too complicated for a busy college student?
Not at all! Our approach to Aromatherapy is designed to be accessible and easy to integrate into your busy schedule. We start with simple, gentle practices and guide you step-by-step. You don't need any prior experience; just an openness to exploring how scent can support your well-being. We focus on practical applications that fit your life.
Are the essential oils safe to use, especially if I have allergies or sensitivities?
Your safety is our priority. We always discuss any allergies, sensitivities, or health conditions you may have before recommending any essential oils. We use high-quality, pure essential oils and advise on proper dilution and application methods to ensure a safe and positive experience for you. We aim for gentle, effective support.
How often do I need to use Aromatherapy to see benefits for my immune system or stress levels?
The beauty of Aromatherapy lies in its gentle, consistent nature. While some benefits can be felt quickly, like a calming aroma during a stressful moment, sustained immune and vitality support often comes with regular, mindful use. We'll help you create simple routines that you can easily maintain, allowing the plant essences to work their subtle magic over time.
