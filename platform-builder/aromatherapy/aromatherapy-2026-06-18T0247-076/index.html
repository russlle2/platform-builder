Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is Aromatherapy, and how can it benefit me?
Aromatherapy is a holistic healing modality that uses natural plant extracts, known as essential oils, to promote health and well-being. It can benefit you by reducing stress, improving sleep quality, easing discomfort, boosting mood, and enhancing overall emotional and physical balance through various application methods.
Are the essential oils used in your Aromatherapy practice safe?
Yes, safety is paramount in our Aromatherapy practice. We utilize only high-quality, pure essential oils and provide thorough guidance on their safe and appropriate use. During your consultation, we will discuss any sensitivities or health conditions to ensure your personalized blends are safe and effective for you.
How do you determine which essential oils are right for my specific needs?
During your initial consultation, will conduct a detailed intake to understand your health history, current concerns, and wellness goals. Based on this comprehensive assessment, we will thoughtfully select and blend essential oils known for their therapeutic properties that align with your unique requirements.
What can I expect during an Aromatherapy consultation with ?
An Aromatherapy consultation with is a personalized and collaborative experience. You can expect a deep dive into your well-being, where we discuss your lifestyle, emotional state, and any physical concerns. You will receive a custom essential oil blend, along with clear instructions and ongoing support for integrating Aromatherapy into your life.
