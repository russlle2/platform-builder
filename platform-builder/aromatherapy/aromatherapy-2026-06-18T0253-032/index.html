Frequently Asked Questions

What is Aromatherapy and how can it benefit me?
Aromatherapy is a holistic healing modality that uses natural plant extracts, known as essential oils, to promote health and well-being. It can benefit you by supporting emotional balance, enhancing relaxation, improving sleep quality, easing discomfort, and uplifting your mood, all through the therapeutic properties of scents.
Are essential oils safe to use, and how do you ensure their quality?
When used correctly, essential oils are generally safe. We prioritize your safety by using only 100% pure, therapeutic-grade essential oils from reputable suppliers who provide comprehensive testing results. During your consultation, we will discuss proper dilution, application methods, and any contraindications relevant to your health.
What can I expect during an initial Aromatherapy consultation?
During your initial Aromatherapy consultation, you can expect a comprehensive discussion about your health history, lifestyle, and specific wellness goals. We will explore your preferences for scents and discuss how essential oils can be integrated into your routine, culminating in a personalized plan and custom blend recommendation tailored just for you.
Can Aromatherapy help with stress and anxiety?
Yes, Aromatherapy can be a powerful tool for managing stress and anxiety. Certain essential oils, like lavender, bergamot, and frankincense, are known for their calming and mood-balancing properties. We can help you create custom blends and practices that support relaxation and emotional resilience, helping you navigate daily stressors more effectively.
