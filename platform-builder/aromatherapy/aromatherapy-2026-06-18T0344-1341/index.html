Frequently Asked Questions

How does Aromatherapy specifically address physical pain for individuals aged 60+?
Our Aromatherapy uses carefully selected pure essential oils known for their anti-inflammatory, analgesic, and muscle-relaxing properties. These are blended and applied gently, often through massage or inhalation, to reduce sensations of discomfort, improve circulation, and encourage a feeling of warmth and ease in your joints and muscles.
Can Aromatherapy help with the stiffness or limited mobility often experienced with age?
Yes, it can. Our bespoke Aromatherapy blends are designed to soothe and relax tense muscles, reduce inflammation, and enhance circulation. By gently supporting your body's natural processes, these aromatic essences can contribute to greater flexibility and a more comfortable range of motion, helping you move with more fluidity.
Is Aromatherapy safe for someone who takes multiple medications?
Your safety is our utmost priority. Before any session, we conduct a thorough consultation to understand your health history and current medications. This allows us to select essential oils that are safe and beneficial for your specific needs, avoiding any known contraindications and ensuring a gentle, supportive experience.
What can I expect during an Aromatherapy session focused on somatic healing?
During a somatic-focused Aromatherapy session, you can expect a tranquil environment where you feel deeply supported. We might use gentle touch, guided breathing, and specific aromatic blends to help you gently release long-held tension patterns in your body. The goal is to foster a deeper awareness of your bodily sensations and promote a profound sense of inner calm and physical relief.
