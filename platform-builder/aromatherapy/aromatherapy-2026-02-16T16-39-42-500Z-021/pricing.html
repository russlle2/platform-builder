Practical safety FAQ

What dilution do you recommend?
Dilution varies by age, skin sensitivity, and intended use. Typical topical dilutions range from 0.5–3% for adults; we will provide tailored dilution guidance during consultations. Never apply undiluted essential oils to skin.
How do I perform a patch test?
Apply the recommended diluted blend to a small forearm area and observe for 24 hours. If redness, itching, or irritation occurs, discontinue use and consult your healthcare provider.
Are essential oils safe around pets?
Some oils can be harmful to animals. We provide pet-safe alternatives and usage recommendations. Diffuse cautiously, ensure ventilation, and never force exposure on an animal.
Pregnancy and nursing considerations?
Certain oils are contraindicated in pregnancy. If pregnant or breastfeeding, let us know during booking so we can recommend appropriate, safety-focused options.
