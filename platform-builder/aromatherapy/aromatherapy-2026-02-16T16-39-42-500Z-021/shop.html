Common product questions

Dilution: Bottled roll-ons are pre-diluted and labeled. For DIY, use 1%–3% for adults. Lower concentrations for older adults and children.

Patch test: Always patch test new products before widespread topical use. Apply a small amount to inner forearm and check at 24 hours.

Pets: Many essential oils can cause irritation in animals. Use diffusers sparingly in pet areas, keep products out of reach, and never apply human blends directly to animals.

Pregnancy & breastfeeding: Some oils are contraindicated. If pregnant or breastfeeding, consult your healthcare provider before use and share ingredient lists with them.
