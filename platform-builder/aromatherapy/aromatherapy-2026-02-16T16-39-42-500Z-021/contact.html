Consultation framework

A short, reproducible structure guides each session so outcomes are clear and safe.

1 — Intake & safety
Document exposures, pregnancy/pediatric status, medication interactions, and household animals. Recommend patch test and dilution limits when applicable.
2 — Profile & goals
Explore scent preferences, daily routines, and desired aromatic experiences (sleep, focus, ritual). No medical claims are made.
3 — Formulation
Create a conservative blend with top/middle/base notes; provide dilution ratios and application guidance for safe topical or diffusion use.
4 — Follow-up
Short check-in to confirm tolerability, adjust dilution, and troubleshoot scent fatigue.
FAQ highlights: Always dilute essential oils before skin contact; perform a patch test on a small area; avoid certain oils during pregnancy and around some pets; consult with your medical provider for health-related questions.
