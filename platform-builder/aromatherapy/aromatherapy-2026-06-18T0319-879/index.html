Frequently Asked Questions

How can Aromatherapy help with my posture as a parent?
As parents, you often find yourselves in positions that strain your back and shoulders. Aromatherapy can help by creating a sensory cue that reminds you to check in with your body throughout the day. Certain scents can encourage relaxation in tense areas and gently bring your awareness to your alignment, helping you make small, impactful adjustments to your posture.
Do I need any prior wellness experience to benefit from this Aromatherapy?
Absolutely not! Our approach is designed to be accessible and gentle, even if you’ve never tried Aromatherapy or focused on body awareness before. We’ll guide you through simple ways to incorporate scents into your routine that support your physical comfort and help you feel more grounded.
What kind of Aromatherapy blends are used for postural health?
We focus on crafting blends with essential oils known for their calming, muscle-relaxing, and grounding properties. Think scents like frankincense, lavender, and marjoram, tailored specifically to your unique areas of tension and how they relate to your daily activities as a parent. These are used in easy-to-apply formats like rollerballs or simple diffusions.
How can I incorporate Aromatherapy into my busy parenting schedule?
We understand your time is precious! Our recommendations are designed to be quick and seamless. This might involve a quick inhale from a personal inhaler before lifting your child, a few drops in a diffuser during quiet play, or a calming spritz on your pillow at night. We'll help you find practical ways that fit into your existing routine without adding extra pressure.
