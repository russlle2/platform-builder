FAQ & Safety

        

          Are these medical treatments?
          
Sessions are supportive practices and are not medical care. Oils may support well-being but are not a substitute for medical advice.

        

        

          What about dilution and patch tests?
          
We recommend dilution guidelines in every plan and suggest a patch test for topical use: dilute, apply a small amount to a patch of skin, wait 24 hours. If irritation occurs, discontinue use.

        

        

          Pets and essential oils
          
Certain oils can be harmful to animals. If you have pets, we will choose pet-safe options and recommend ventilation and distance.

        

        

          Pregnancy & nursing
          
If you are pregnant or nursing, we take extra precautions. Some oils are avoided; we may request clearance from your healthcare provider for particular applications.
