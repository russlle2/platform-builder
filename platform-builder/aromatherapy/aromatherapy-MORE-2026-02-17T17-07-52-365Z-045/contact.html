Practical FAQs

Is aromatherapy medical care?

No. Aromatherapy may support comfort and wellbeing but is not a substitute for medical care. Sessions are intended to support personal routines.

How to dilute for topical use?

Dilution guidance varies; a common starting guideline is 1–2% for adults for short-term topical use. Patch test a small area and wait 24 hours for reactions.

Pets and pregnancy?

Some essential oils are best avoided around pets or during pregnancy. Note these details in your planner so recommendations steer clear of concerning substances.
