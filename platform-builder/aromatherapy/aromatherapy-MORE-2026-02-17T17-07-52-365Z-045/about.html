Listening first, then scent

The sessions are guided by listening and safety. Blends are crafted to be gentle, purposeful, and practical — suggestions that may support better rest, steadier focus, or calmer moments for some people.

Origin story

I began exploring aromatic plants as an avenue for attention and care — creating small, repeatable scent supports that feel quietly helpful in an everyday life. Over time the work evolved into a practice focused on clarity: clear intake, clear dilution, clear aftercare. Each recommendation centers safety and usability.

Approach & training

My training includes clinical aromatherapy principles, plant chemistry basics, and depth listening methods. Sessions avoid medical claims; I share evidence-aware guidance and always encourage collaboration with your healthcare providers when relevant.

How I work

Sessions combine a measured intake, scent sampling, and co-creation of a single practical blend. You’ll leave with a plain-text plan (and, if desired, a small take-home vial) plus dilution and patch-test steps.

Practitioner notes

I prioritize:

  • Safety-forward formulations and conservative dilutions
  • Options that consider pets, pregnancy, and sensitivities
  • Simple, repeatable practices rather than complex routines

What to expect

A thoughtful session, clear written follow-up, and straightforward steps you can try at home. If you touch base ahead of time with any medical or pregnancy notes, I will adapt recommendations.
