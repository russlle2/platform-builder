FAQ — safety and common questions

Are these treatments medical?

No. Aromatherapy is complementary and may support wellbeing for some people. It is not a substitute for medical care.

What about dilution and patch testing?

You will receive a clear dilution percentage and a step-by-step patch test for any new blend we suggest. Always perform a patch test before broader use.

Pets, pregnancy, or allergies?

We will adapt recommendations for pregnancy, breastfeeding, and households with pets or allergies. Please disclose these during intake; some essential oils should be avoided or used with extra caution.
