Frequently Asked Questions

How can Aromatherapy specifically address the physical pain from my chronic illness or injury?
Our Aromatherapy protocols are built on a scientific understanding of how specific botanical compounds interact with physiological pain pathways. We select essential oils with documented anti-inflammatory, antispasmodic, or analgesic properties, applying them through precise methods to target areas of discomfort and encourage your body's natural restorative processes.
Is Aromatherapy safe to use alongside my existing medical treatments for chronic conditions?
Absolutely. Our practice prioritizes your safety and well-being. We conduct thorough consultations to understand your complete medical history and current treatments. All Aromatherapy recommendations are carefully considered to complement your existing healthcare regimen, never to replace it, ensuring there are no contraindications or interactions with your medications.
What does 'somatic healing' mean in the context of Aromatherapy for chronic pain?
Somatic healing, in this context, refers to re-establishing a harmonious connection with your physical body, particularly after experiencing chronic pain or injury. Aromatherapy aids this by calming the nervous system, reducing tension, and encouraging a gentle awareness of bodily sensations, helping your body release stored stress and return to a more comfortable, integrated state.
How long will it take to experience relief from my chronic pain through Aromatherapy?
Responses to Aromatherapy can vary based on individual circumstances and the nature of your chronic condition. While some individuals report subtle shifts and a sense of calm relatively quickly, sustained relief and somatic integration typically unfold over a series of sessions and consistent application of personalized blends. We focus on gradual, compassionate progress.
