What exactly is Aromatherapy?

Aromatherapy is a holistic healing treatment that uses natural plant extracts to promote health and well-being. Sometimes called essential oil therapy, Aromatherapy uses aromatic essential oils medicinally to improve the health of the body, mind, and spirit. It enhances both physical and emotional health.

How can Aromatherapy benefit me?

Aromatherapy offers a wide range of benefits, including stress reduction, improved sleep quality, pain relief, mood enhancement, and support for various health concerns. Your personalized blend will be designed to address your specific needs and help you achieve your wellness goals naturally.

Are essential oils safe to use?

When used correctly and with proper guidance, essential oils are very safe. At Ocean Breeze Aromatherapy, we prioritize your safety by using only high-quality, pure essential oils and providing clear instructions on dilution and application methods suitable for your individual needs. We'll discuss any sensitivities you may have.

What should I expect during an Aromatherapy consultation?

During your consultation, Dr. Morgan Ellis will engage in a thorough discussion about your health history, lifestyle, and wellness aspirations. This allows for a deep understanding of your needs, ensuring the creation of a truly bespoke essential oil blend and a personalized Aromatherapy plan tailored specifically for you.
