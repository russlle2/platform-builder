Frequently Asked Questions

How can Aromatherapy specifically help with my posture as a senior?
Aromatherapy, when thoughtfully applied, can gently cue your body to relax areas of tension that might be hindering good posture. Certain aromas can also enhance your body awareness, helping you to consciously align yourself and stand with more ease and balance throughout your day. It’s about creating a subtle, supportive framework for your physical comfort.
Will the aromatic blends be too strong or overwhelming for my senses?
Absolutely not. Your comfort is paramount. We create personalized blends with a very gentle hand, using carefully diluted essential oils that are suited to your individual sensitivities and preferences. The goal is a subtle, supportive presence, not an overpowering one, ensuring a pleasant and beneficial experience for you.
What kind of commitment is required to see benefits for my body awareness?
Our approach focuses on gentle integration into your daily rhythms. While consistency brings the most profound benefits, even small, mindful moments with your personalized aromatic blends can begin to cultivate a deeper connection with your body. We'll work together to find a simple, enjoyable routine that fits seamlessly into your life.
Is Aromatherapy safe to use alongside my current medications or health conditions?
Your safety and well-being are our highest priority. Before crafting any blends, we conduct a thorough consultation to understand your complete health picture, including any medications or conditions. This ensures that the essential oils chosen are perfectly safe and complementary to your unique needs, and we always recommend consulting with your primary healthcare provider.
