Aromatherapy

Reconnecting with Your Core Through Aromatherapy

After childbirth, your body goes through so many changes. It's common to feel disconnected from your core and notice new aches from holding, feeding, and caring for your little one. Our Aromatherapy blends are crafted to encourage subtle body awareness, helping you gently re-engage those muscles that support your spine and pelvis. We focus on scents that invite calm and a sense of grounding, making it easier to notice how you're holding yourself throughout the day.
