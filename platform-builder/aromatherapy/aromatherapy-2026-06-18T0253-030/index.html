Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is Aromatherapy and how does it work?
Aromatherapy is a holistic healing treatment that uses natural plant extracts to promote health and well-being. It works by stimulating your sense of smell and, when applied topically, by absorption through your skin. These plant essences can influence your mood, alleviate stress, and support various physiological functions through their unique chemical compositions.
Is Aromatherapy safe for everyone, including children and pets?
While Aromatherapy is generally safe, the use of essential oils requires careful consideration, especially for children, pregnant individuals, and pets. Your specific health conditions and sensitivities are always taken into account. We prioritize your safety and provide tailored recommendations, ensuring appropriate dilutions and methods of application for optimal well-being.

What kind of conditions can Aromatherapy help with?
Aromatherapy can offer significant support for a wide range of concerns. Many clients find relief from stress, anxiety, and sleep disturbances. It can also aid in pain management, boost energy levels, enhance focus, and improve overall emotional balance. Your individual needs will be thoroughly discussed to determine how Aromatherapy can best benefit you.
