Frequently Asked Questions

How can Aromatherapy specifically help my postpartum posture?
Aromatherapy can assist by using essential oils known for their muscle-relaxing and anti-inflammatory properties. These can help alleviate tension that contributes to poor posture, making it easier for you to adjust and maintain a more aligned position. We focus on blends that support your core and back muscles.
Is Aromatherapy safe to use while breastfeeding or around my baby?
Yes, when properly diluted and carefully chosen, Aromatherapy can be safe. We prioritize your and your baby's well-being, selecting specific essential oils and application methods that are appropriate for new mothers, including options for diffusion or topical application away from direct contact with your infant.
What does 'body awareness' mean in the context of postpartum Aromatherapy?
Body awareness refers to your conscious perception of your body's position, movement, and internal state. In postpartum, this can be crucial for recognizing and correcting postural habits. Our Aromatherapy supports this by helping you feel more grounded and connected to your physical self, allowing you to identify areas of strain and make subtle adjustments.
Will Aromatherapy replace physical therapy for postural issues?
Aromatherapy is a complementary support and does not replace professional medical or physical therapy advice. We work alongside your existing healthcare plan to enhance your comfort and awareness, making it easier for you to engage with and benefit from any recommended exercises or treatments for your postural health.
