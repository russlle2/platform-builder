Common questions

How do you avoid skin reactions?
We favor low dilutions and recommend patch tests. If you have a history of allergies, let us know during intake; we’ll steer toward safe options.
My home has pets — is diffusing okay?
Diffusing in an open area with access for pets to leave is important. We include pet-safety notes with blends and can suggest pet-friendly alternatives.
Are your suggestions safe during pregnancy?
I include pregnancy-safe alternatives when needed. For medical advice, please consult your care provider; our guidance is educational.
Will you tell me how much to use?
Yes — every blend comes with a dilution percentage and a simple method to make it (e.g., x drops per 10ml carrier). Follow the instructions provided.
