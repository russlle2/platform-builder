Blends — tasting notes & gentle uses

Below are 10 safe, non-medical blend descriptions with top/middle/base notes, overall aroma profile, and suggested "supports" language you can share with clients or use at home. Always patch-test and use advised dilution.
Citrus Morning — Bright Wake
Top: Bergamot, Lemon · Middle: Sweet Orange · Base: Vetiver
Aroma profile: bright, uplifting citrus with an earthy anchor. Supports gentle energizing and focussed starts to the day.
Green Garden — Fresh & Herbal
Top: Basil · Middle: Rosemary, Geranium · Base: Cedarwood
Aroma profile: crisp herbal clarity. Supports a sense of grounded alertness during tasks.
Quiet Brook — Soft Floral Calm
Top: Neroli · Middle: Lavender · Base: Sandalwood
Aroma profile: tranquil floral woods. Supports gentle evening wind-down rituals.
Warm Hearth — Spiced Embrace
Top: Sweet Orange · Middle: Cinnamon Cassia (low %) · Base: Benzoin
Aroma profile: cozy spice and resin. Use low-cassia amounts; supports feelings of comfort and homey warmth.
Forest Walk — Earth & Resin
Top: Pine · Middle: Juniper · Base: Patchouli
Aroma profile: resinous, woodsy grounding. Supports centering and a nature-immersive scent experience.
Soft Lullaby — Gentle Night
Top: Chamomile · Middle: Lavender · Base: Sweet Marjoram
Aroma profile: subtle, floral-herbal ease. Supports pre-sleep rituals when used lightly and with attention to tolerance.
Citrus Grove — Clarity + Lift
Top: Grapefruit · Middle: Basil · Base: Cypress
Aroma profile: tart, uplifting with green facets. Supports mental clarity and a fresh atmosphere.
Moon Root — Deep Comfort
Top: Bergamot · Middle: Rosewood · Base: Vanilla absolute (or CO2)
Aroma profile: soft, balsamic with faint florals. Supports intimate ritual moments and contemplative pause.
Peppermint Lift — Cool Focus
Top: Peppermint (low %) · Middle: Spearmint · Base: Lime
Aroma profile: cooling, minty brightness. Supports short bursts of alertness—use small amounts and avoid around pets that are sensitive to menthols.
Evening Resin — Slow Down
Top: Petitgrain · Middle: Frankincense · Base: Myrrh (minimal)
Aroma profile: resinous, meditative. Supports contemplative breathing practices and quiet evenings when used sparingly.

Blend safety reminders

These are not medical treatments. I recommend patch-testing all blends, following dilution guidelines, and avoiding direct use around infants, young pets, or during pregnancy without professional clearance. In sessions I will adapt blends for individual sensitivities.
