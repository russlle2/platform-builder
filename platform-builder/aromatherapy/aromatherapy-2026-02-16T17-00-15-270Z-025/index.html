Frequently asked — safety & practice

How should I dilute essential oils?

We recommend following our dilution charts tailored to age and application area. Commonly used topical dilutions are 1%–3% for adults for short-term use. Members get printable dilution guides. If you have specific concerns, consult a qualified healthcare provider.

What is a patch test and how do I do one?

A patch test helps identify sensitivity. Mix your diluted oil, apply a pea-size amount to the inner forearm, cover with a bandage, and check after 24 hours for redness or irritation. If irritation occurs, discontinue use.

Are essential oils safe around pets?

Some oils can be harmful to pets. We provide pet-safety guidelines and alternatives for pet-friendly homes. When using diffusers, ensure pets can leave the room and monitor for signs of distress. When in doubt, choose scent-free options or consult a veterinarian.

What about pregnancy, breastfeeding, or children?

Certain oils and applications are not recommended during pregnancy or for young children. Our resources include a pregnancy-safe reference and low-dose guidance, but we do not provide medical advice. Always consult your medical provider regarding pregnancy, breastfeeding, or pediatric use.
