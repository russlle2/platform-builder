Short FAQ

How do I patch test?
Apply a small diluted drop on the inner forearm and wait 24 hours. If irritation appears, stop use.
What about pets?
We provide pet-safety notes. Avoid diffusing in closed spaces with pets that cannot leave.
Pregnancy?
Report pregnancy in the notes; we recommend specific pregnancy-safe alternatives and will avoid certain oils.
Dilution guidance?
We include dilution percentages for topical use with each blend (e.g., 1% = 6 drops per 30ml carrier).
