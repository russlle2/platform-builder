Frequently asked — safety first

How do I dilute essential oils?
We provide dilution guidance for each blend. Typical adult dilution is 1–3% for daily wear. For children or sensitive skin, lower dilutions are recommended. Always follow the written ratio included with blends.
What about patch testing?
Patch testing is recommended before topical use. Apply a diluted small amount to an inner forearm, wait 24 hours, and look for irritation. If irritation occurs, discontinue use.
Are blends safe for pets?
Some essential oils can affect animals. We include pet-safety notes with blends. Never diffuse in a closed room where pets cannot leave; consult your veterinarian if unsure.
Pregnancy and breastfeeding?
Certain oils are not recommended during pregnancy or breastfeeding. We include pregnancy notes and will suggest alternatives. Always consult your healthcare provider for medical guidance.
