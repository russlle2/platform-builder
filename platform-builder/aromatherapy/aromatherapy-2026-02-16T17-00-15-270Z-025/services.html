FAQ — safety first

How should I dilute essential oils?
Topical dilution typically ranges from 1% (very gentle) to 3% for most adults. I recommend 0.5–1% for sensitive skin or new users. Always follow guidance given during your session.
Do I need to patch-test?
Yes — a small patch-test on the inner forearm with your chosen dilution for 24 hours helps rule out irritation before wider use.
What about pets and pregnancy?
Some oils are not appropriate around pets or during pregnancy. I provide specific safety guidance and alternatives in sessions. If you are pregnant, breastfeeding, or caring for animals, inform me so I can tailor recommendations.
