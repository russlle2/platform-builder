Frequently asked questions (safety-forward)

How do I dilute essential oils safely?
Dilution depends on the user and use-case. For adults, typical topical dilutions range from 0.5%–3% for daily wear; consult directly to tailor this. Never apply undiluted essential oils to skin. This is general guidance, not medical advice.
Should I do a patch test?
Yes. Apply a small diluted amount to a forearm patch and observe for 24–48 hours. If redness, itching, or irritation occurs, discontinue use.
Are essential oils safe around pets?
Some oils can be irritating or toxic to animals. Always disclose pets when we consult. I prioritize pet-safe alternatives and diffusion practices that reduce exposure. Never force exposure on an animal.
What about pregnancy, breastfeeding, or children?
Certain oils and dilutions are contraindicated in pregnancy and for infants/young children. If pregnant, breastfeeding, or working with children, please mention this when you contact me so we can prioritize safe choices. This is precautionary, not medical advice.
Do you make custom blends?
Yes. Custom blends are offered within membership tiers and as one-off purchases. Customization always begins with safety screening for medications, sensitivities, and other considerations.
