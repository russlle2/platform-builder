Frequently Asked Questions

How can Aromatherapy specifically benefit my postural health as an athlete?
Our specialized aromatherapy focuses on engaging your nervous system through scent, which can influence muscle tone and body perception. This helps you develop a more acute awareness of your postural alignment, allowing for subtle adjustments that reduce strain and improve biomechanics during activity and rest.
Will Aromatherapy interfere with my training or competition schedule?
Absolutely not. Our aromatherapy protocols are designed to seamlessly integrate into your existing routine. We create blends for specific times of day – pre-workout for focus and warm-up, post-workout for recovery and stress reduction, and evening blends for deep restorative sleep – all without hindering your performance or schedule.
What makes your Aromatherapy different for active individuals managing physical stress?
We move beyond general relaxation. Our approach is highly targeted, using specific essential oil profiles known for their analgesic, anti-inflammatory, and neuro-modulating properties. We craft custom blends that directly address the physiological stressors unique to athletes, aiding in muscle recovery, reducing nerve impingement, and enhancing your body's natural repair processes.
How does Aromatherapy enhance my body awareness for improved performance?
By using precise aromatic cues, we help you tune into your body's subtle signals. This heightened interoception allows you to detect early signs of fatigue or imbalance, enabling you to make proactive adjustments to your form or intensity. This refined self-awareness ultimately leads to more efficient movements, reduced injury risk, and sustained peak performance.
