Frequently Asked Questions

How can Aromatherapy specifically help my postpartum posture?
Aromatherapy can support your postpartum posture by addressing key areas. Certain essential oils can help relax tense muscles that contribute to poor alignment, while others can invigorate and improve circulation, encouraging better core engagement. We also use aromas to enhance your proprioception, helping you become more aware of your body's position in space and how to adjust for optimal comfort and strength.
Is Aromatherapy safe for new mothers, especially if I'm breastfeeding?
Yes, when used correctly, Aromatherapy can be very safe and beneficial for new mothers. We use only high-quality, pure essential oils and apply them in appropriate dilutions and methods. We always take into account if you are breastfeeding, and will select oils that are safe for both you and your baby, avoiding any that are contraindicated. Your safety and well-being are our top priority.
What kind of results can I expect from focusing on postural health with Aromatherapy?
You can expect to feel more grounded and connected to your body. Many new mothers report reduced back and neck tension, improved balance, and a greater sense of confidence in their movements. By incorporating Aromatherapy, you'll also gain tools to manage daily physical stressors, allowing you to move through your day with greater ease and less discomfort while caring for your baby.
Do I need any prior experience with Aromatherapy or body awareness practices?
Absolutely not! Our sessions are designed for all new mothers, regardless of their prior experience. We will guide you step-by-step through understanding your body's changes, how Aromatherapy can assist, and simple practices you can integrate into your daily life. Our goal is to make these tools accessible and empowering for your unique postpartum journey.
