Frequently asked (safety & use)

Dilution and patch testing

Recommended dilution depends on age, context, and application. The planner and blend builder below provide suggested dilution ranges. Always perform a small patch test before topical use and discontinue if irritation appears.

Pregnancy, medications, and health

Some essential oils are not recommended during pregnancy or with certain medications. Please disclose pregnancy, nursing, or relevant medications during booking so recommendations can be tailored safely.

Pets

Many oils can affect companion animals. Share information about pets so any at-home suggestions avoid potential harm; diffusion in shared spaces should be minimal and supervised.
