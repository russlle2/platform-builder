FAQ — Safety & practical notes

How do you recommend diluting blends?
Typical adult topical dilutions range from 1–3%. For children, pregnancy, or sensitive people we recommend much lower dilutions (0.25–1%). We always provide a drops-based guide on blend cards and encourage patch testing.
What about pets?
Some essential oils can be harmful to pets. We avoid recommending direct application to animals and provide pet-safe alternatives and ambient guidance when relevant. Always consult your veterinarian for specific concerns.
Is aromatherapy a medical treatment?
No. Our guidance is non-medical and meant to support simple day-to-day practices. If you have health concerns, consult a medical professional.
