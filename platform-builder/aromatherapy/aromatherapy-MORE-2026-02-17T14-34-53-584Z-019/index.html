Frequently asked questions
\n        
\n          
Are these sessions medical?
\n          
No. Sessions and blends are educational and supportive only and are not a substitute for medical care. Language used is intentionally cautious: scents may support comfort, routines, or focus but are not intended to diagnose or treat conditions.
\n        
\n        
\n          
What about dilution and safety?
\n          
All topical recommendations include dilution guidance and a patch test suggestion. For topical use we recommend low dilution for sensitive people and children; always do a patch test and consult a qualified clinician for pregnancy or complex medical situations.
\n        
\n        
\n          
Pets and pregnancy
\n          
If you live with pets or are pregnant/breastfeeding, we will adapt suggestions and avoid contraindicated oils. Please mention these in your intake.
