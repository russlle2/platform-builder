Frequently Asked Questions

How can Aromatherapy specifically support my sleep as a senior?
For seniors, sleep patterns often shift, leading to lighter sleep or difficulty falling asleep. Aromatherapy offers a gentle, non-invasive method using specific essential oils known to calm the nervous system, reduce anxious thoughts, and prepare your body for a deeper, more sustained period of rest without the side effects often associated with other interventions.
Are the essential oils used in Aromatherapy safe for seniors?
Yes, safety is paramount. We use only the highest quality, pure essential oils and always dilute them appropriately. conducts a thorough consultation to understand your health history and any sensitivities, ensuring that the chosen oils and their application methods are perfectly safe and beneficial for your individual needs.
What does a typical Aromatherapy session for sleep improvement involve?
Your first session begins with a gentle conversation about your sleep habits, health, and preferences. Based on this, will guide you through selecting specific aromas, demonstrate safe application methods for your home use, and provide you with a personalized blend and clear instructions to seamlessly integrate this peaceful ritual into your evening routine.
How long will it take to experience better sleep with Aromatherapy?
The response to Aromatherapy is individual, but many seniors report a noticeable improvement in their ability to relax and fall asleep more easily within a few days to a few weeks of consistent use. True restorative sleep is a gentle process, and our aim is to support your body in re-establishing its natural sleep rhythms over time.
