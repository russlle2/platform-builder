Frequently asked safety questions

Dilution and patch testing
We provide dilution ranges for topical use and always recommend a small patch test on the inner forearm 24–48 hours before broader use.
Pregnancy and breastfeeding
Some essential oils are not recommended during pregnancy or breastfeeding. Tell us during booking so we can adapt recommendations. We avoid making medical claims and focus on safety-conscious suggestions.
Pets in the home
Many oils can affect animals; we will discuss safe approaches and alternatives for households with pets and offer non-aerosol options where possible.
