Questions & Care Notes

Is aromatherapy safe?

When prepared and used with guidance, essential oil blends may support wellbeing. We always use conservative dilutions and advise patch testing. This practice is not a replacement for medical care.

Do I need to test before use?

Yes. A patch test protocol is provided with every blend: dilute according to instructions and test on a small area to check for sensitivity.

What about pets and pregnancy?

Some oils are not recommended for pregnant people or certain pets. You will receive a tailored guide with restrictions and safer options if needed.

How do sessions work?

Sessions include intake, scent selection, and a personalized plan. You may receive sample blends, home-use instructions, and follow-up recommendations.
