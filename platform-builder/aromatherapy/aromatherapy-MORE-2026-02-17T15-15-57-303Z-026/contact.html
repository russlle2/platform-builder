Safety & Dilution

All topical products are provided with recommended dilution ranges and instructions. Always patch-test any topical blend 24–48 hours prior to regular use. Avoid undiluted application.

Pets & Pregnant Clients

We prioritize safety: share household pets and pregnancy or nursing status during intake so we can adapt blends and session methods. Some essential oils should be avoided around pets and during pregnancy.

Patch Test

A small patch test on the inner forearm is recommended; discontinue if irritation occurs.

Non-medical Scope

Aromatherapy is supportive and may support relaxation or focus. It is not medical treatment. For health conditions, consult a health care professional.
