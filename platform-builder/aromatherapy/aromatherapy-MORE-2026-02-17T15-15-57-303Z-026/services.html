Is aromatherapy safe?

When used with appropriate dilution and attention to contraindications, essential oils may support wellbeing. This is not medical advice. We prioritize safety and will review any medications, pregnancy status, and pet exposure risks before suggesting blends.

Do I need to patch-test?

Yes. Patch testing is recommended for topical applications. We provide step-by-step dilution instructions and a gentle patch test protocol during consultation.

Pet & home safety

Some oils can be harmful to animals. We will avoid or adapt suggestions if you share your home with pets and offer alternative delivery formats.

Pregnancy & medical notes

If you are pregnant, breastfeeding, or under medical care, we follow conservative guidance and will defer or adapt recommendations. Please discuss these details during booking.
