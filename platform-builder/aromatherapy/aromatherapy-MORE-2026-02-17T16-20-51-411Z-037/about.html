Common questions & safety notes

Are essential oils safe?

When used responsibly and diluted, essential oils may support comfort for many people. Always dilute for topical use and perform a patch test. Avoid concentrated undiluted oils on skin. Keep oils away from eyes and mucous membranes.

What about pets?

Some oils can be irritating to animals. We provide pet-aware suggestions and can adjust recommendations for multi-species homes.

Pregnancy & medical considerations

If you are pregnant, nursing, or have a serious medical condition, please consult your healthcare provider before using essential oils. Our sessions are not a substitute for medical care.

Dilution & patch testing

We include dilution ratios and clear patch-test steps with all topical suggestions. If any irritation occurs, discontinue use and consult a provider.

Our method
