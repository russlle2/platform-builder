Simple Daily Practices

1–2 deep breaths: three slow inhales through the nose, exhale gently. Repeat once or twice.
Mini-roll: A single drop diluted on a carrier applied to wrists for brief re-orientation (perform a patch test first).
Tray pause: Warm a mug and sit with a tuned blend at room temperature for 3–5 minutes to reset between tasks.

FAQ — safety notes

Dilution & patch testing: For topical use, start at low dilution (1–2%). Apply a small amount to inner forearm and wait 24 hours to check for irritation.

Pregnancy & nursing: Some oils are not recommended. Please check with your healthcare provider before using essential oils if pregnant or breastfeeding.

Pets: Keep oils away from birds, reptiles, and other sensitive animals; ensure good ventilation and consult a qualified vet for guidance.

Medical boundaries: Our offerings focus on scent-based strategies that may support wellbeing; they are not medical treatments.
