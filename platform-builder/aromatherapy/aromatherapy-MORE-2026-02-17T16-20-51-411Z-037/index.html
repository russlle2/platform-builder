Micro practices to fold into routine

  • Three-breath inhale: Inhale a subtle scent for three simple breaths before starting work.
  • Doorway spritz: A quick, low-dilution spray on your scarf or wrist to mark transitions.
  • Night veil: A small diffuser or cotton pad with a mild sleepy-support blend near your pillow (low concentration).

Always patch-test new blends and follow dilution guidance. Avoid certain oils during pregnancy or around sensitive pets — see FAQ.

FAQ — safety notes

  • Always dilute essential oils for topical use; follow provided dilution percentages.
  • Patch test new blends on a small skin area and wait 24 hrs.
  • Some oils are not recommended during pregnancy; consult a qualified provider.
  • Keep blends away from pets and follow species-safe guidance; avoid diffusing certain oils around cats.
