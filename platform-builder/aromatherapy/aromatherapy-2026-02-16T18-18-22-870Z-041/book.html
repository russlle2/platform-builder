FAQ — practical safety

How should I do a patch test?
Apply one drop of the diluted blend to the inside of your forearm and cover with a bandage. Check for redness or irritation at 24 and 48 hours.
What dilution is typical?
We normally recommend 1–3% for adults for most topical blends; for sensitive skin or elders we may recommend lower dilutions. We will write the exact percentage on your label.
Are essential oils safe around my dog or cat?
Certain oils are not recommended near pets. We build pet-safe plans and advise on diffuser placement to reduce animal exposure.
What if I am pregnant or breastfeeding?
We use conservative alternatives and avoid specific botanicals contraindicated in pregnancy. We do not provide medical advice — discuss clinical concerns with your provider.
