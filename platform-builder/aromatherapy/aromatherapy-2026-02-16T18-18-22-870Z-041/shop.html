Shopping & safety notes

All products ship with a label containing dilution and patch-test instructions. If you are pregnant, breastfeeding, or have pets, reach out before ordering so we can recommend appropriate products and usage.

FAQ (short)

How do I dilute roll-ons? Use the provided pre-diluted rollers, or dilute essential oils into a carrier (fractionated coconut oil) to around 1–3% for personal roll-ons.
Patch test? Apply a small diluted amount behind the ear or inner forearm and observe for 24–48 hours.
Pets? Some oils (e.g., tea tree, large quantities of eucalyptus) may be unsafe for pets—consult before diffusing or placing oils where animals frequent.
Pregnancy? Certain oils should be avoided. We recommend a consult before purchase if pregnant or breastfeeding.
