Frequently asked — safety first

How do you determine dilution?
We use conservative dilution guidelines tailored to age, skin sensitivity, and intended use. Typical adult topical dilutions range from 1–3% depending on context; we document your personalized dilution on every blend label.
Is a patch test recommended?
Yes — we always recommend a small patch test (inner forearm) 24–48 hours before wider topical use to check for irritation or allergy.
Are essential oils safe around pets?
Some oils are poorly tolerated by animals. During your consultation we will review household pets and recommend pet-safe scent strategies and diffuser placement to avoid exposure.
What if I’m pregnant?
Pregnancy involves special considerations. We follow conservative practice and will avoid certain botanicals and provide pregnancy-safe alternatives. Always consult your care provider for clinical guidance.
