Rituals that anchor your day

Our sessions center around small, repeatable rituals you can use at home. We favor low-risk dilution and simple inhalation practices—no medical promises, only mindful support for everyday wellbeing.

  • Breath-centered inhalation techniques
  • Short roll-on recipes for on-the-go
  • Diffuser protocols for safe home use

Quick ritual: 3-min Reset

Carry a personal roll-on. Pause, breathe for three slow counts, dab the blend to wrists, cup your hands and inhale gently. Repeat once or twice.

Always patch test new blends and follow recommended dilutions.

FAQ — safety & practice

We follow conservative dilution guidelines: typical topical dilutions are 0.5–2.5% depending on age and sensitivity. Your practitioner will recommend a specific ratio for your blend.

Yes — a patch test is recommended before regular topical use. Apply a small, diluted amount to the inner forearm and wait 24 hours to check for reactions.

Some oils can be harmful to animals. We provide pet-safe guidance and alternatives; always use diffusers in well-ventilated areas and avoid direct application on pets.

We take pregnancy into account and avoid certain oils. If pregnant or nursing, disclose this on intake so we can offer safe options. This is not a substitute for medical advice.
