Frequently asked — safety & practical

How should I dilute essential oils?
I provide dilution guidelines based on age, sensitivity, and intended application. Typical topical dilutions range from 0.5–5% depending on use; always follow the specific recipe and perform a patch test first.
What is a patch test?
A patch test involves applying a small diluted amount to a discreet area of skin and observing for 24–48 hours. It's a key safety step, and I will walk you through it.
Are your blends safe around pets?
Some oils can be irritating to animals. I will provide guidance specific to your household and recommend pet-safe options and dispersal methods.
Pregnancy and breastfeeding notes?
Certain oils are best avoided during pregnancy and lactation. I do not provide medical advice; I will offer conservative, safety-first recommendations and encourage consultation with your healthcare provider.
