Frequently asked safety questions

How should I dilute essential oils?
Dilution depends on age, sensitivity, and intended use. Typical topical dilutions: adults 1–3% for short-term use; for children or sensitive skin practitioners recommend lower concentrations or aromatherapy via diffusion. Your practitioner will advise a safe ratio for your context.
What is a patch test?
A patch test applies a diluted drop of a blend to a small area of skin to check for irritation over 24–48 hours. We always recommend patch testing for new blends if you plan to apply them topically.
Are essential oils safe around pets?
Some oils can affect animals. We avoid recommending direct topical application for pets and will suggest pet-safe alternatives and diffusion practices that minimize exposure. Always mention household pets during intake.
What about pregnancy or medication interactions?
Pregnancy and certain medications require special precautions. We do not provide medical advice; we will defer and suggest consultation with your healthcare provider when needed, and will propose pregnancy-safe approaches when appropriate.
