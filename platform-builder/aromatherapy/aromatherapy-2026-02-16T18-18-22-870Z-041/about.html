A simple ritual we teach

  1. Set intention for the scent session (1–2 minutes).
  2. Explore 6–8 single-note smells in small vials.
  3. Co-create a 1–3 oil blend and label dilution instructions.
  4. Bring the blend into a daily 60-second ritual for 2 weeks and note reactions.

We prioritize patch testing, clear dilution labels, and household safety, particularly around children and pets.

How I work

Sessions begin with a structured intake focused on sensitivities, exposures, and lifestyle. We explore aroma profiles in an ordered way so any reactions are easy to spot. If you have pregnancy, chronic conditions, or pets, we adjust blends conservatively — this is about comfort and safe use, not medical treatment.

What to expect in your first appointment

  • 30–45 minute intake and scent exploration
  • Selection of safe top-notes and base-notes to build blends
  • Written dilution guidance and storage instructions

FAQ — safety highlights

How do you set dilution percentages?
We follow conservative, age-appropriate guidelines and record the percent on every label so you can replicate safe home use.
Do I need a patch test?
Yes — performing a patch test 24–48 hours before extended topical use is standard practice and reduces risk of irritation.
What about pets?
We present pet-safe scent plans and diffusion strategies. Certain oils can be harmful to animals — we will avoid those entirely if pets live in the home.
Can pregnant clients participate?
Pregnancy requires extra caution. We use pregnancy-safe options and will avoid a set of oils known to be contraindicated in pregnancy; consult your prenatal provider about any clinical questions.
