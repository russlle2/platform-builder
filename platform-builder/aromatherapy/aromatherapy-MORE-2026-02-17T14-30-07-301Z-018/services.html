FAQ — Safety & Use

Dilution and dosing

We provide dilution instructions for each blend and recommend conservative starting points. If uncertain, choose the lowest dilution and increase only with guidance.

Patch testing

Patch tests are advised for topical use. Apply a small amount on the inner forearm and observe for 24–48 hours for irritation.

Pets

Some oils can be problematic for animals. We always flag pet-safe blends and offer alternatives when households include animals.

Pregnancy & medical conditions

We avoid certain botanicals in pregnancy and advise consulting a healthcare provider. Our guidance does not replace medical advice.
