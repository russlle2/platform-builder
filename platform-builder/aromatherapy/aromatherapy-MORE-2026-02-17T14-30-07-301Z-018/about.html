Frequently asked safety questions

How should I do a patch test?
Dilute a drop of blend in at least 4–10 ml of carrier oil. Apply a small amount to the inner forearm and wait 24 hours for any reaction. Avoid if you see redness or itch.
What about dilution and concentrations?
We publish dilution for each context. Typical single-session topical use is often between 0.5%–3% depending on the application and participant profile.
Are essential oils safe around pets or pregnancy?
Some oils are contraindicated for pregnancy and some are risky for pets. We include explicit notes and ask clients to disclose pregnancy, nursing, and household pets before sessions.
Do you make clinical claims?
No medical claims are made. We describe how blends are intended to be used and what responses people commonly report; outcomes are individual and may vary.
