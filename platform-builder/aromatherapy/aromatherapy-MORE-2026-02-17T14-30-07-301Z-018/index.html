Frequently asked

Are essential oils safe?

When used thoughtfully and at appropriate dilutions, essential oils may support comfort for many people. We always include dilution guidance and encourage patch testing. We do not make medical claims.

What about pets or pregnancy?

We avoid certain oils around pregnancy and specific conditions, and we advise caution with pets. Please mention pregnancy, nursing, medical conditions, or pets when booking so we can adapt sessions and blends.

Do I need prior experience?

No. Workshops are entry-friendly and focus on safety, scent literacy, and simple practices you can try at home.

How do I test for sensitivity?

We recommend a small patch test with a low dilution and waiting 24 hours to check for reactions. If you react, discontinue use and consult a healthcare professional.
