Frequently Asked Questions

How can Aromatherapy specifically help with the physical strain of parenting?
Aromatherapy can be a powerful tool for parents by providing sensory cues that support improved posture and reduce physical tension. Certain essential oils can help calm the nervous system, which in turn allows muscles to relax and encourages a more aligned body. We focus on blends that remind your body to release common areas of parental strain, like the shoulders, neck, and lower back.
I'm always rushing. How do I fit Aromatherapy into my busy day as a parent?
We understand that time is precious. Our approach to Aromatherapy for parents is about integrating simple, impactful moments. This could be a quick roll-on blend applied while waiting for the kettle to boil, a gentle room spray during naptime, or a few deep inhalations from an essential oil diffuser during a quiet moment. We'll help you find practical ways to weave these beneficial practices into your existing routine, rather than adding another 'to-do'.
Is Aromatherapy safe to use around young children?
Yes, when used correctly and with proper guidance. We prioritize safety and will educate you on appropriate dilution ratios, diffusion methods, and essential oils that are safe for use in a household with young children. Our recommendations are always tailored to your specific family environment, ensuring a beneficial experience for you without compromising the well-being of your little ones.
How does Aromatherapy connect to postural health and body awareness?
Aromatherapy directly impacts your nervous system and can influence your physical state. By using specific scents, we can help bring your awareness back to your body, prompting you to notice and release tension. For instance, grounding scents can help you feel more rooted, encouraging an upright stance, while relaxing aromas can signal muscles to soften, improving flexibility and reducing strain that often leads to poor posture.
