Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is clinical Aromatherapy?
Clinical Aromatherapy is the therapeutic application of essential oils for holistic health. It involves using highly concentrated plant extracts to support physical, mental, and emotional well-being. Your practitioner uses evidence-based knowledge to select and blend oils safely and effectively for your specific needs.
How can Aromatherapy benefit my everyday life?
Aromatherapy can offer numerous benefits, from reducing stress and improving sleep quality to boosting mood and supporting respiratory health. By incorporating custom essential oil blends into your routine, you can experience enhanced focus, emotional balance, and a greater sense of calm, tailored to your lifestyle.
Are the essential oils you use safe and pure?
Absolutely. We are committed to using only the highest quality, therapeutic-grade essential oils from reputable suppliers. Your safety is paramount, and we carefully select pure, unadulterated oils, ensuring they are free from synthetics or harmful additives, and always consider your sensitivities and health profile.
What should I expect during an initial Aromatherapy consultation?
During your initial consultation, you'll engage in a comprehensive discussion about your health history, lifestyle, and wellness goals. Your practitioner will listen attentively to understand your unique needs. This allows us to formulate a personalized Aromatherapy plan and custom essential oil blends specifically designed to support your journey.
