Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is Aromatherapy and how can it benefit me?
Aromatherapy is a holistic healing modality that utilizes natural plant extracts, known as essential oils, to promote health and well-being. It can support you in various ways, including stress reduction, improved sleep, enhanced mood, pain management, and boosted energy levels, all tailored to your individual needs.
Are the essential oils used in your Aromatherapy practice safe?
Absolutely. Your safety is our utmost priority. We use only high-quality, ethically sourced essential oils and provide clear guidance on their proper and safe application. During your consultation, we take a detailed health history to ensure all recommendations are appropriate for your specific circumstances.
How long does a typical Aromatherapy consultation last?
A typical initial Aromatherapy consultation lasts approximately 60-75 minutes. This allows ample time for us to thoroughly discuss your health history, current concerns, and wellness goals, ensuring a comprehensive and personalized approach to your aromatic journey.
Can Aromatherapy help with specific emotional challenges?
Yes, Aromatherapy can be particularly supportive for emotional well-being. Certain essential oils are known for their calming, uplifting, or balancing properties, which can help you navigate feelings of anxiety, stress, or low mood. We will work with you to identify the most beneficial aromas for your emotional landscape.
