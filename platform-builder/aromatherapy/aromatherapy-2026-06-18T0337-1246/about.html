How can Aromatherapy specifically address the emotional impact of chronic illness or injury?

Aromatherapy utilizes the olfactory system's direct link to the limbic system, the brain's emotional center. For those recovering from chronic illness or injury, specific scents can gently modulate stress responses, reduce feelings of overwhelm, and foster a sense of security, helping to re-regulate emotional states without forcing them.

Is your Aromatherapy approach suitable for someone experiencing trauma related to their chronic condition?

Yes, our practice is deeply rooted in trauma-informed principles. We create a safe, gentle, and non-invasive space, using aromatherapy to support your nervous system's capacity to self-regulate. We focus on empowering you with tools to feel more grounded and present, respecting your pace and boundaries throughout your healing process.

What kind of commitment is required for an Aromatherapy program focused on chronic recovery?

The commitment is tailored to your capacity and needs. We often begin with an initial consultation to understand your specific circumstances. From there, we might suggest a series of gentle sessions or provide personalized blends and guidance for daily home use, ensuring the approach supports rather than overwhelms your recovery.

How do you ensure the safety of essential oils, especially for someone with a delicate health profile due to chronic illness?

Your safety is paramount. We conduct a thorough intake to understand your health history, medications, and sensitivities. We use only therapeutic-grade essential oils, often recommending gentle dilutions or diffusion rather than direct skin application, and always educate you on proper, safe usage tailored to your unique health landscape.
