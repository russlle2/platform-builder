Micro habits to integrate

Small, consistent practices support safe enjoyment of essential oils.

  • Patch test new blends on a small skin area and wait 24 hours.
  • Use a diffuser for 15–30 minute intervals; ventilate the room.
  • Store oils away from heat and sunlight in labeled containers.
  • Note reactions in a simple log for future reference.
