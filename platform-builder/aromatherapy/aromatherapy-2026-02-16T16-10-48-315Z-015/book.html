Frequently asked safety questions

Dilution & application: Your practitioner will provide dilution recommendations (e.g., 1–3% typical for topical blends) and safe application methods. Always follow instructions and discontinue if irritation occurs.

Patch test: A patch test is advised for topical blends: apply a small diluted amount to inner forearm and observe for 24 hours before wider use.

Pets: Many essential oils are potent for animals. Inform us if you have pets — we will adapt diffusion and topical suggestions to reduce risk and avoid certain oils.

Pregnancy & nursing: Some oils are contra-indicated. If you are pregnant or nursing, note this on intake; recommendations will be conservative and safety-forward.

Note: The practitioner provides non-medical aromatic guidance. For medical questions, consult your healthcare provider.
