Common safety questions

How do you recommend dilutions?
We recommend conservative dilutions by age and sensitivity. Typical topical dilutions are 0.5–2% for adults for acute use; we provide precise ratios in each plan. Always perform a patch test before regular topical use.
What is a patch test?
A patch test uses a small diluted amount applied to forearm skin for 24–48 hours to check for irritation or reaction. We supply step-by-step guidance when recommending topical blends.
Are essential oils safe around pets?
Many oils can affect animals differently. We assess pet exposure during intake and recommend pet-safe alternatives and diffusion practices to minimize risk.
Pregnancy & oils — what to know?
Pregnancy requires extra caution. We avoid recommending certain botanicals and will default to the safest inhalation or non-use when appropriate. Consult your healthcare provider for any clinical questions.
