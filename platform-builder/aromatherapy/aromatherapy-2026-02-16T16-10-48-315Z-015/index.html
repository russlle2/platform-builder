Micro-habits for scent harmony

Small, reproducible practices support safe and enjoyable aromatherapy use.

  1. Patch test a diluted sample on inner forearm for 24 hours before regular use.
  2. Start with low dilution (0.5–2%) for daily topical support; increase cautiously if appropriate.
  3. Prefer short diffusing sessions (10–30 minutes) and ventilate the room.
  4. Store oils in a cool, dark place away from pets and children.

If you have pets or pregnancy considerations, see our guidance or book a consultation.
