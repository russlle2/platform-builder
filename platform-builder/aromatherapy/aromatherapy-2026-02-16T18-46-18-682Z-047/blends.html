Safety notes

Always dilute essential oils for topical use. We provide dilution guidance during sessions. Keep blends out of reach of children and label clearly. If pregnant or breastfeeding, mention it so we can adjust suggestions. Pets may react—skip eucalyptus, wintergreen, and high-phenol oils around cats and small animals.

Patch testing

Apply a small amount of diluted blend to a small skin area and observe for 24–48 hours. Discontinue if irritation occurs. For sensitive skin, start at very low dilutions.

FAQ

Dilution: Typical topical dilutions range from 0.5%–3% depending on use and age. We recommend precise percentages during consults.

Pets: Some oils are unsafe for cats and small mammals. Diffuse with caution and consult us for pet-safe options.

Pregnancy: Avoid certain botanicals during pregnancy. Always tell us if you are pregnant—our guidance will be safety-forward.
