Aromatherapy

Aromatherapy to Realign Your Core During Change

Major life transitions, whether career changes, relocating, or new relationships, can subtly impact your physical presence. Our Aromatherapy approach focuses on how specific scents can encourage relaxation in tense areas, allowing for more conscious postural adjustments. You'll learn to use scent as a cue to notice and gently correct habits that might be compounding physical discomfort during times of stress and uncertainty.
