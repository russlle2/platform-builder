Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is Aromatherapy and how can it benefit me?
Aromatherapy is a holistic healing modality that utilizes natural plant extracts, known as essential oils, to promote health and well-being. It can support your physical, emotional, and mental health by influencing your mood, reducing stress, improving sleep, easing discomfort, and much more. Your personalized Aromatherapy plan will address your specific needs.
Are the essential oils used in your Aromatherapy practice safe?
Absolutely. Your safety is our top priority. We use only high-quality, ethically sourced essential oils and adhere to strict safety guidelines. During your consultation, we meticulously review your health history, allergies, and sensitivities to ensure every blend is safe and appropriate for your individual use. We always provide clear usage instructions.
How is a personalized Aromatherapy blend created for me?
Your personalized Aromatherapy blend is created after a comprehensive consultation where we discuss your health goals, lifestyle, and preferences. Based on this information, selects specific essential oils known for their therapeutic properties and formulates a unique blend tailored just for you. This ensures maximum effectiveness and a delightful aromatic experience.
What should I expect during my first Aromatherapy consultation?
During your initial Aromatherapy consultation, you can expect a warm and supportive conversation about your current health, wellness aspirations, and any concerns you might have. We'll explore your preferences for scents and discuss how essential oils can best support your journey. This session is designed to understand your unique needs and lay the groundwork for your personalized wellness plan.
