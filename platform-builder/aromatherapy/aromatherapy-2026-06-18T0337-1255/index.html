Frequently Asked Questions

How can Aromatherapy specifically help with college anxiety and academic pressure?
Our Aromatherapy approach is trauma-informed, meaning we understand the deep impact stress has on your nervous system. We use specific essential oils to modulate your body's stress response, reduce racing thoughts, and create a sense of safety, allowing you to face academic challenges with greater emotional stability and focus.
Is Aromatherapy safe to use while I'm taking medications or managing other health concerns?
Your safety is paramount. We conduct a thorough intake process to understand your complete health profile, including any medications or existing conditions. We will guide you on safe and appropriate Aromatherapy practices, ensuring that any blends or recommendations complement your current care without interference.
What does a typical Aromatherapy session look like for a college student?
A session begins with an open conversation about your current emotional state, academic stressors, and areas where you seek support. We then explore various aromatic profiles, noting your responses, to create a personalized plan and custom blend that resonates deeply with your needs, empowering you with tools for emotional self-regulation.
Will Aromatherapy make me feel drowsy, impacting my studies or daily activities?
Our goal is to enhance your well-being and academic performance, not hinder it. We carefully select essential oils and concentrations that promote calm and mental clarity without inducing drowsiness, unless deep relaxation is a specific, desired outcome for restorative sleep. We prioritize blends that support your active, engaged college life.
