What exactly is Aromatherapy?

Aromatherapy is a holistic healing treatment that uses natural plant extracts to promote health and well-being. Sometimes called essential oil therapy, Aromatherapy uses aromatic essential oils medicinally to improve the health of the body, mind, and spirit. It enhances both physical and emotional health.

How can Aromatherapy benefit me?

Aromatherapy can offer a wide range of benefits tailored to your specific needs. It can help reduce stress and anxiety, improve sleep quality, alleviate headaches, boost mood, support respiratory health, and even enhance skin vitality. Your personalized plan will focus on addressing your unique concerns effectively.

Are essential oils safe to use?

When used correctly under professional guidance, essential oils are generally safe. However, they are potent and require careful handling. Dr. Morgan Ellis will educate you on proper dilution, application methods, and any contraindications specific to your health profile, ensuring your safety and maximizing benefits.

What does a typical Aromatherapy consultation involve?

Your initial consultation begins with a comprehensive discussion about your health history, lifestyle, and wellness goals. We'll explore your preferences and concerns to craft a personalized Aromatherapy plan, which may include custom essential oil blends, usage instructions, and ongoing support for your journey.
