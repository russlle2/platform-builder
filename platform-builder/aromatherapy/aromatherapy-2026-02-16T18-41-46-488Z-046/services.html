Frequently asked questions

How should I dilute essential oils for topical use?
Dilution depends on age and sensitivity. Typical adult dilutions range from 1% (10 drops per 30ml) to 3% for short-term targeted use. Always follow practitioner guidance for children, elders, and specific conditions.
What is a patch test and why do I need one?
A patch test applies a small diluted amount to the forearm and monitors for 24–48 hours. It helps identify skin reactions before broader use.
Are essential oils safe around pets?
Some oils can be irritating to animals. We provide pet-specific guidance and diffusion limits; when in doubt, keep pets away from diffusers and ventilate spaces.
What about pregnancy and breastfeeding?
Certain oils are best avoided or used only under practitioner supervision during pregnancy and breastfeeding. Safety-first planning is part of our intake process.
