Booking policies
A confirmation email will include an intake form and safety checklist. Cancellations less than 24 hours may be charged a fee. Sessions are educational and supportive, not medical care.

Pre-booking checklist

  • Note any allergies or skin sensitivities
  • Mention pregnancy or breastfeeding status
  • Inform about household pets
If you have complex health concerns, please consult your healthcare provider alongside booking a consult.

Why a guided consult?

Personalised guidance reduces risk: appropriate dilution, patch testing, and guidance about home use are part of every consult. We focus on practical, safe steps you can follow independently.

Short FAQ

Do I need a patch test?
Yes, when topical use is planned we recommend a patch test at a conservative dilution before regular application.
Are smells safe around cats and dogs?
Some oils are hazardous to pets. We'll discuss safer alternatives and diffusion limits during your consult.
What about pregnancy?
Pregnancy changes safety recommendations. We tailor guidance and often recommend seeking approval from your healthcare provider.

Practice safeguards & truths

Myth: oils are harmless if natural
Responsible practice recognises concentrated plant extracts can cause irritation, interact with medications, or be unsafe in pregnancy and around certain pets. Guidance reduces those risks.
Core pillars
