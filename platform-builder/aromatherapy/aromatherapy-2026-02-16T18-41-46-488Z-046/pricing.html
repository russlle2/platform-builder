Myths & grounded truths

Myth: Essential oils cure illnesses
Truth: Oils can support comfort, mood, and routine when used safely. They are not medical treatments. Work with licensed providers for health conditions.
Myth: If it is natural it's always safe
Truth: 'Natural' does not equal risk-free. Concentration, route of use, interactions, and age matter.

Frequently asked questions

How should I dilute essential oils?
Dilution depends on age, use, and oil potency. Common safe at-home ranges: 0.5–1% for infants/older adults in sensitive contexts, 1–3% for daily adult blends, and up to 5% for targeted short-term topical use. Practitioner will advise specific ratios.
What is a patch test?
A patch test applies a small diluted amount to a forearm and checks for reaction over 24–48 hours. It’s a simple precaution before regular topical use.
Are essential oils safe for pets?
Some oils are unsafe for animals. Avoid diffusing for long periods around pets, and consult a veterinarian or practitioner for pet-safe options and concentrations.
Can I use oils during pregnancy?
Pregnancy requires a cautious, personalised approach. Some oils and uses are contraindicated; consult a practitioner and your healthcare provider before use.
