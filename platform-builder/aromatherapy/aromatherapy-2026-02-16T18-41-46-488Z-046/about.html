Common questions we answer

What if I'm pregnant?
Pregnancy alters recommended oils and concentrations. We review options carefully; some sessions may be deferred or adjusted pending medical advice.
How are blends created?
Blends are curated from safety-screened oils and always shared with dilution instructions and suggested use cases (topical vs inhalation).
Are oils safe around my cat or dog?
Not always. Safety depends on the oil and exposure. We discuss pet-safe practices and alternatives.
