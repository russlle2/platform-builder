FAQs — practical safety notes

How should I dilute essential oils?
We recommend conservative dilutions. Typical general topical dilutions are 1–3% for adults for daily use; children, older adults, and sensitive skin require lower concentrations. Your personalized plan will follow established aromatherapy guidelines.
What is a patch test and why do it?
A patch test (small amount on inner forearm, covered 24 hours) helps identify local skin sensitivity before broader topical use. Wait 24–48 hours and observe for redness or irritation.
What about pets in the home?
Some essential oils can be irritating to animals. We provide pet-safe recommendations and usage limits; never diffuse strongly in closed spaces with pets present without professional advice.
Can I use essential oils during pregnancy?
Pregnancy requires special caution. Certain oils and higher concentrations are not recommended. We will advise pregnancy-safe approaches and always coordinate with your prenatal care provider when appropriate.
