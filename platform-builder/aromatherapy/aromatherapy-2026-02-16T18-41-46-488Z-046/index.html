Frequently asked — safety first

How should I dilute essential oils?
Dilution depends on age, route, and sensitivity. We typically recommend conservative dilutions (e.g., 0.5–2% for topical adult use in roll-ons) and provide exact recipes during consults. Never apply undiluted essential oils to skin.
What is a patch test and why do it?
A patch test applies a small amount of diluted blend to a discrete skin area and checks for reactions after 24–48 hours. It helps identify sensitivities before wider use.
Are essential oils safe around pets?
Some essential oils can be irritating to animals. We provide species-specific guidance and recommend diffusing in well-ventilated areas with options to exit the room; always check before use and consult a veterinarian for concerns.
What about pregnancy and breastfeeding?
We take a conservative approach. Certain oils are avoided during pregnancy and lactation. During consults we discuss up-to-date safety guidance and tailor recommendations accordingly. We do not provide medical advice.
