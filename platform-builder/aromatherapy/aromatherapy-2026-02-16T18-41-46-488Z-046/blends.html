Common questions about blends

How much of an essential oil is in a blend?
Each finished product includes a recommended dilution. For custom blends we typically specify 1%–3% for adults, and lower for sensitive individuals. Exact percentages are listed per product or provided in consultation.
Do I need to do a patch test?
Yes. Apply a small diluted amount on the inner forearm and observe for 24–48 hours. If irritation occurs, discontinue.
Are these blends pet-safe?
Not all blends are pet-friendly. Keep diffusers out of reach, ventilate rooms, and consult the specific product notes; we provide pet-specific guidance with purchases.
What about pregnancy?
Several oils are best avoided in pregnancy. We flag such oils and provide alternative formulations for pregnancy or breastfeeding clients.
