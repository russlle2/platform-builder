Safety & usage notes

  • Dilution instructions included with each topical product. For adults, common dilutions are 1%–3% unless otherwise specified.
  • Perform a patch test for topical products: apply a small amount to the inner forearm and monitor for 24–48 hours.
  • Keep essential oils out of reach of children. Some oils can be harmful to pets; see product pages for pet guidance.
  • Pregnant and breastfeeding clients should consult with the practitioner before using new oils.
