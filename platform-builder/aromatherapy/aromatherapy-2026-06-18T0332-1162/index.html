Frequently Asked Questions

How can Aromatherapy specifically help my postpartum sleep?
Aromatherapy can offer a gentle embrace during your postpartum period. Certain essential oils are known to calm the nervous system, reduce feelings of overwhelm, and prepare your body for rest, even when sleep is fragmented. We focus on blends that encourage relaxation and deeper, more restorative moments of rest for you.
Is Aromatherapy safe to use around my baby during this sensitive time?
Your baby's well-being is paramount. We prioritize using essential oils with the utmost care, recommending gentle dilution and safe diffusion practices. Our consultations will guide you on appropriate usage, ensuring your Aromatherapy practices are not only beneficial for your rest but also safe and harmonious for your entire family's environment.
I'm overwhelmed and don't know where to start. How easy is it to incorporate Aromatherapy into my routine?
We understand that your time and energy are precious right now. Our approach to Aromatherapy is designed to be wonderfully accessible and simple to integrate into your existing routine. We will guide you to create small, manageable rituals that don't add to your plate but instead offer moments of peace and preparation for rest.
What if I'm not familiar with essential oils or Aromatherapy at all?
That's perfectly fine! No prior experience with essential oils or Aromatherapy is needed. We are here to gently introduce you to this beautiful practice, explaining everything in an easy-to-understand way. Our goal is to empower you with simple, effective tools to nurture your rest during this tender postpartum time.
