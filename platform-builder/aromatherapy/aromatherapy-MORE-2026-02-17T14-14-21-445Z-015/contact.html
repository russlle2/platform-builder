FAQ — Safety & practice
How do you recommend dilution and patch tests?
Dilution level depends on context and client profile. For topical use we frequently recommend 1–3% for most adults; lower for sensitive skin. A patch test is advised: apply the diluted blend to a small area for 24 hours and watch for irritation.
I have pets. Are aromatherapy blends safe at home?
Many essential oils can affect animals differently. We discuss household composition and adapt recommendations (diffuser placement, ventilation, and oil selection) to reduce risk. Please tell us about any pets during intake.
I'm pregnant or breastfeeding. Can I participate?
Pregnancy and nursing require adjusted choices. Some oils are contraindicated; we design alternatives and consult safety references. Always mention pregnancy or nursing at first contact.
Are these medical treatments?
No. Aromatherapy here is offered as supportive practice that may support wellbeing and daily rhythms, not as a replacement for medical care. We encourage coordination with your healthcare providers when relevant.
