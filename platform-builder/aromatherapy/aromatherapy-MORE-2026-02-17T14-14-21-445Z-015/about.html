Frequently asked safety questions

Dilution: Essential oils should be diluted. Typical topical dilutions for adults range from 0.5–2% for daily use depending on the context. Please follow provided dilution instructions and check sensitivities.

Patch test: Always perform a small patch test on skin before regular topical use. Discontinue if irritation occurs.

Pregnancy & medical conditions: Some aromatic notes may be contraindicated in pregnancy or certain conditions. Consult your healthcare provider and mention such factors during intake.

Pets: Some essential oils are unsafe for pets. Keep concentrated materials away and follow pet-safe guidance included with blends.
