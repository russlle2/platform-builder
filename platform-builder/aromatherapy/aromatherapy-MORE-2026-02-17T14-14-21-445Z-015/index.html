Frequently asked safety questions

How do I dilute oils safely?
We provide dilution ratios for topical use (commonly 1–3% for adults) and clear instructions. Always perform a patch test before broader use.
Do essential oils affect pets?
Some oils can be toxic to animals. We give pet-safe guidance and alternatives. Always consult a veterinarian for specific concerns.
Can I use these scents during pregnancy?
Certain oils are contraindicated in pregnancy. Pregnant or breastfeeding clients receive tailored protocols that avoid restricted botanicals.
What is a patch test?
A patch test applies a diluted sample to a small skin area and waits 24 hours to check for irritation. If redness or burning occurs, discontinue use.
