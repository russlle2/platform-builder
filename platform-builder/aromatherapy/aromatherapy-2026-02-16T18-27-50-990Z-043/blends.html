Safety & FAQ

Dilution: Recommended dilutions are provided with every blend. A common guideline is 1–3% for room diffusion and 0.5–2% for topical roll-ons for adults. Lower for sensitive skin.
Patch test: Apply a small diluted amount to an inner forearm and wait 24 hours to check for irritation before wider use.
Pets: Some oils can affect animals. Mention pets when booking; we will advise safe blends and placement. Avoid direct application to animals.
Pregnancy & nursing: If you are pregnant or nursing, mention it before booking. Certain oils are discouraged in pregnancy; guidance will be tailored and conservative.
