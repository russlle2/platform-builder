Safety notes: we use gentle, evidence-informed dilution ranges and recommend a patch test. If you have specific medical concerns, consult a healthcare professional. Pets: we use animal-friendly practices and will note any substances to avoid around pets.

How the series flows

An intimate events series over six gatherings — each evening explores a theme, a blend, and a short ritual to practice at home. Designed for curious people, not as medical treatment.

  1. Meet & notice: gentle guided smelling and sharing.
  2. Learn: scent notes, safety, and simple dilution.
  3. Make: blend together a small personal roll-on or inhaler.
  4. Practice: a micro-habit to carry home.

Groups limited to 8. Materials provided. Accessibility considerations are welcome — contact to discuss.

Three small practices

Tiny rituals you can try between gatherings.

  • Mornings: One inhalation of a citrus-forward blend to greet the day (1–2 gentle sniffs).
  • Midday reset: Dab a diluted roll-on at the base of the throat and breathe for 30 seconds.
  • Evening close: A single drop on a handkerchief while you settle; slow breaths for calm.

Always dilute appropriately. Perform a patch test before topical use. Keep oils away from eyes; store out of reach of children and pets.
