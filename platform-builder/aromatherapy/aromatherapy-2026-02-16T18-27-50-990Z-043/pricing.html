Safety & common questions

  • Always dilute essential oils before applying to skin; typical dilution suggestions provided with blends.
  • Perform a patch test 24–48 hours before regular topical use.
  • Avoid certain oils during pregnancy and consult a care provider; each event includes pregnancy-safe alternatives.
  • If you have pets, we discuss safe choices and diffusion practices to reduce exposure.
