My approach

I frame scent work as a series of tiny, sustainable changes — micro-habits — that invite presence and sensory awareness. Each offering pairs careful sourcing with dilution guidance and patch-test encouragement. I never make medical claims; instead, I offer practices and options that fit into everyday life.

Signature plan — Event series

The events series is a small-group sequence over four weeks where participants learn simple diagnostics, build a personal blend, and practice safe application. Every participant receives a kit and a concise safety guide.

Micro-habits

Morning inhale
Two mindful breaths from a personal inhaler each morning — 1–2 drops diluted.
Patch-test routine
Test new blends on a small skin area 24–48 hours before regular use.
Diffusion windows
Diffuse in short 15–20 minute windows, especially with pets or sensitive people present.
Safety snapshot
  • Dilute before topical use; full dilution guidelines provided with blends.
  • Patch test new preparations; discontinue if irritation occurs.
  • Some oils are not recommended during pregnancy — ask for alternatives.
  • Pets: we offer pet-safe blend options; avoid direct application without vet consult.
