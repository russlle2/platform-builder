Product FAQ

Dilution: All topical products are labelled with recommended dilution. For at-home blending, typical adult dilutions range 0.5–3% depending on use.
Patch test: Apply a small diluted quantity to inner forearm and observe up to 24 hours for irritation before wider use.
Pets: Some essential oils can be bothersome to animals. Keep diffusers in separate spaces and avoid direct application near pets. Ask for pet-safe options.
Pregnancy & nursing: If pregnant or nursing, consult with the practitioner before purchasing. We offer pregnancy-safe alternatives and conservative guidance.
