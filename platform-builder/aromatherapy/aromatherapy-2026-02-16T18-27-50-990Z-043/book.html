Quick FAQ

How to dilute?

Dilution varies by age and use; events include clear charts (eg. 1–2% for everyday adult topical use). Ask during your session.

Patch test?

Apply diluted blend to a small area and wait 24–48 hours. Discontinue if irritation occurs.

Pregnancy & pets

Some oils are not recommended during pregnancy and some are pet-sensitive. We provide safe alternatives; always discuss in notes.
