Micro practices I teach

Three simple habits I guide clients to adopt—rooted in repetition, not perfection.

  • Create a bedside inhaler for sleep transitions.
  • Set a 10-minute mid-afternoon diffuser pause (no direct prolonged exposure for pets).
  • Monthly blending session—mix 5ml and label it with dilution date.
