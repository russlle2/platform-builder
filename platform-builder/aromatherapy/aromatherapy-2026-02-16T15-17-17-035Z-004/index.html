Micro-habits for scenting your day

Small, safe gestures to integrate aroma without overwhelm.

Morning inhale

One gentle inhale from a personal inhaler or cloth — choose a bright top-note like lemon or sweet orange. Keep dilution low for inhalation-only use.

Work pause

Diffuse for 10–15 minutes only; avoid continuous exposure. If others are present, check comfort and respiratory sensitivity first.

Evening wind-down

Use a soft base-note blend in a small room diffuser 30 minutes before bed. Avoid strong stimulants close to bedtime.

Patch-test ritual

Always test diluted oils on a small skin patch for 24 hours before continued topical use.
