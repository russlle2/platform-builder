FAQ — Safety highlights

Dilution: We provide dilution recommendations for each blend. Most topical blends are 1–3% for everyday use unless otherwise specified.

Patch test: Apply a small diluted amount to the inner forearm and wait 24 hours to check for reactions.

Pets: Some essential oils are not pet-safe. We'll share ventilated diffuser protocols and pet-safe alternatives.

Pregnancy: Many oils are contraindicated during pregnancy; we'll use only pregnancy-safe options when needed and recommend consulting your provider.
