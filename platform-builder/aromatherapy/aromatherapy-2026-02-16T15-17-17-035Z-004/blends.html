FAQ — safe use notes

How do I dilute a blend for topical use?
Common guidelines: 1–2% for adults for general topical use (roughly 6–12 drops per 30ml carrier). Lower concentrations for children and older adults. When in doubt, book a consult for personalized guidance.
What is a patch test?
Apply a small amount of diluted blend to the inner forearm, cover, and check after 24 hours. If irritation or redness occurs, discontinue use.
Are blends safe around pets?
Many oils affect animals differently. Use diffusers in well-ventilated spaces, avoid concentrated sprays near animals, and consult about specific oils if you have pets.
Pregnancy & nursing considerations
Some essential oils are not recommended during pregnancy or breastfeeding. Please consult with your healthcare provider and a practitioner before use. Book a tailored consultation for safe alternatives.
