Small daily aromatherapy habits

Tiny practices build steady change—three micro-habits to try this week:

  • Morning: 1–2 deep inhales from a inhaler or cloth (diluted roll-on for skin use only in recommended spots).
  • Midday: 10-minute diffuser pause while hydrating.
  • Night: A lavender-centred linen spritz (patch-test and dilute as instructed).

Safety: Always follow dilution guides and perform patch tests. When in doubt, consult your healthcare provider.
