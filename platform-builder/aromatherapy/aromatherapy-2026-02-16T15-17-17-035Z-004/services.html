Safety-first micro-habits

Patch test
Apply diluted blend to inner forearm, wait 24 hours. Stop if irritation occurs.
Simple dilution
Follow recommended dilutions for topical use; children, older adults, and pregnancy require tailored guidance.
Pet-safe practices
Use diffusers in open spaces, avoid prolonged exposure for sensitive animals, and consult before introducing new blends.
