Quick notes on safety & next steps:
  • Always dilute essential oils appropriately; typical adult dilutions are 1–3% for leave-on blends. For children, older adults, and pregnancy, use lower dilutions and consult a trusted healthcare provider.
  • Perform a patch test before regular topical use — apply diluted blend to a small area and observe 24 hours for sensitivity.
  • Keep oils away from pets' noses and food bowls; some essential oils are not safe for animals. Mention any pets when we speak.
  • For pregnancy, breastfeeding, or complex medical conditions, please consult your clinician before use. I will provide safety-forward recommendations during our consultation.
