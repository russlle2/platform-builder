Aromatherapy is a holistic healing modality that uses natural plant extracts, known as essential oils, to promote health and well-being. These aromatic compounds, distilled from flowers, leaves, bark, and roots, can be inhaled or applied topically to positively influence your mood, alleviate discomfort, and support your body's natural healing processes.
During your initial Aromatherapy consultation, will engage in a warm, detailed conversation to understand your health history, lifestyle, and specific wellness goals.
