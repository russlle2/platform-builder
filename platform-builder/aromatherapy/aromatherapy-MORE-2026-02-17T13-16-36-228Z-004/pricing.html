Program details & safety notes

Session rhythm

Each session includes a gentle guided practice, a short teaching segment, and time for group reflection. Practices are optional — adapt what feels safe. Sessions may include topical discussion about dilution, patch testing, and pet-safety.

Patch testing & dilution

We emphasize safe dilution and recommend doing a patch test for new oils. Sample dilution guidelines and a patch-test protocol are shared in the cohort workbook. If you have specific health considerations, consult your healthcare provider.

Pregnancy & pets

Some essential oils are not recommended in pregnancy or around certain animals. We provide lists of commonly cautioned oils and alternative practices. These notes are informational and do not replace professional guidance.
