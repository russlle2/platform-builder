Choose below whether you’re joining a small-group series or booking a one-on-one consult. Please share any safety notes so we can prepare appropriate materials.

What happens next?

After you request a booking you receive a short intake and an invoice. Seats are held for 48 hours pending payment. For private sessions we offer a 30-minute intake call before scheduling.

Safety highlights

  • We provide dilution guidance and a patch-test protocol.
  • We list commonly cautioned oils for pregnancy and pets.
  • Practices are optional; you may observe without participation.

Helpful resources

Before your session we recommend reviewing: a basic dilution chart, a short patch-test guide, and a list of oils commonly avoided around animals or in pregnancy. These are shared in the intake materials.
