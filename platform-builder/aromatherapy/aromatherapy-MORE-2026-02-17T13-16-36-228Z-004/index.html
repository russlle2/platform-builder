Myth vs. Truth

Aromatherapy gets many claims. Here’s a sober, safety-first way to think about it.

  • Myth: Essential oils are harmless — use any amount. Truth: They are concentrated. Dilution and patch testing are important and may reduce risk of irritation.
  • Myth: A smell can cure health conditions. Truth: Scents may support mood or focused attention for some people but are not medical treatments.
  • Myth: All oils are safe around pets. Truth: Certain oils can be harmful to animals. Always check safety notes before using around pets.

Questions we answer

Are these medical treatments?

No. Aromatherapy practices we teach may support well-being for some people but are not a substitute for medical care.

What about dilution and patch tests?

We always teach safe dilution ratios and how to do a patch test. Small doses are usually enough to notice effects and reduce risk.

Can I use oils around pets?

Exercise caution. Some oils may be unsafe for animals. We review pet-safe options and strategies to avoid exposure.

Pregnancy and nursing

If you are pregnant or breastfeeding, please consult your healthcare provider. We offer alternative practices that do not rely on topical or inhaled essential oils.
