About the guide

I work with scent as a way to organize attention and to invite a small, repeatable ritual into busy days. My teaching centers on safety, simple practices, and group learning where people can try things and reflect together.

Roots: Years of studying aromatic plants and safe use, with an emphasis on dilution and sensible application.
Practice: A mix of small-group workshops and private consultations — practical tools you can take home.
Approach: Experimentation without pressure; practices that may support steadiness and clearer noticing.

My work is not medical care. We stay within safety guidelines and offer resources for patch tests, dilution, and pet/pregnancy considerations. If you have specific health conditions, please consult a licensed healthcare provider.

How I structure cohorts

Groups are small. Sessions combine a short teaching, a guided practice, and time for sharing. Each cohort gets a workbook and a clear safety brief. There is space to adapt: you never need to perform an exercise you’re not comfortable with.

Sample session

  1. Opening check-in
  2. 10-minute scent exploration
  3. Shared practice + journaling prompt
  4. Group reflection (15 minutes)
