A few notes about aromatherapy

Myth: Essential oils fix feelings instantly

Reality: Scents may support shifts in attention and mood for some people, especially when paired with a mindful routine. They complement — not replace — other supports.

Myth: More is better

Reality: Dilution is essential. Proper dilution and a patch test help reduce sensitivity and irritation.

Frequently asked — safety & use

How should I dilute oils?

For topical blends, common dilutions range from 0.5–3% for everyday adult use. Children, older adults, and sensitive skin often do well with lower dilutions. We always encourage a patch test before wider skin use.

Do I need to patch test?

Yes. Apply a properly diluted single drop on your inner forearm and observe for 24 hours. Discontinue if irritation occurs.

What about pets & pregnancy?

Some oils may be irritating to pets or contraindicated in pregnancy. We will counsel safer choices in cohort sessions; always consult your healthcare provider for individual medical guidance.
