Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is Aromatherapy and how can it benefit me?
Aromatherapy is a holistic healing modality that uses natural plant extracts, called essential oils, to promote health and well-being. It can benefit you by supporting emotional balance, easing physical discomfort, enhancing mental clarity, and promoting relaxation through the powerful effects of scent and topical application.
Are the essential oils you use safe and ethically sourced?
Absolutely. Your safety and well-being are our top priority. We meticulously source our essential oils from reputable suppliers who provide detailed GC/MS reports, ensuring purity, potency, and ethical harvesting practices. We guide you on proper dilution and application for safe and effective use.
What should I expect during my first Aromatherapy consultation?
During your initial Aromatherapy consultation, you can expect a comprehensive discussion about your health history, lifestyle, and specific wellness goals. Lora will listen attentively to understand your needs, answer any questions you have, and together you'll begin to craft a personalized plan that may include custom essential oil blends and usage recommendations.
How can Aromatherapy complement my existing wellness practices?
Aromatherapy is a wonderful complementary therapy that can seamlessly integrate with many existing wellness practices, such as yoga, meditation, massage, or even your daily routine. It can deepen your relaxation, enhance focus, and provide additional support for your overall health goals, creating a more holistic approach to your well-being.
