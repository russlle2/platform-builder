Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is Aromatherapy and how can it benefit me?
Aromatherapy is a holistic healing modality that uses natural plant extracts, known as essential oils, to promote health and well-being. It can benefit you by helping to alleviate stress, improve sleep quality, boost mood, support respiratory health, and even provide pain relief, depending on your individual needs and the oils used.
Are the essential oils you use safe for everyone?
We prioritize your safety and well-being above all else. During your consultation, we conduct a thorough assessment of your health history, sensitivities, and any medications you may be taking. This allows us to select and dilute essential oils appropriately, ensuring that any blends we create are safe and beneficial for your specific circumstances. We always use high-quality, pure essential oils.
How long does a typical Aromatherapy consultation last?
A typical initial Aromatherapy consultation lasts approximately 60-75 minutes. This allows ample time for a comprehensive discussion about your health goals, lifestyle, and any concerns you have. Subsequent follow-up sessions, if needed, are generally shorter, around 30-45 minutes, to review progress and adjust your personalized blends.
Can Aromatherapy replace my conventional medical treatments?
Aromatherapy is a complementary therapy intended to support your overall well-being and should not replace conventional medical treatments or advice from your healthcare provider. It works wonderfully alongside traditional medicine to enhance your healing journey. We encourage you to discuss any complementary therapies with your doctor.
