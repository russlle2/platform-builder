Quick safety FAQs

  • Dilution: Pre-diluted products are labeled. If blending at home, follow 1–3% guidance for adults.
  • Patch test: Always patch test roll-ons before regular skin use.
  • Pets: Avoid heavy diffusion in small, enclosed spaces with pets; consult if unsure.
  • Pregnancy: Some products are not appropriate during pregnancy — check product notes and consult with the practitioner.
