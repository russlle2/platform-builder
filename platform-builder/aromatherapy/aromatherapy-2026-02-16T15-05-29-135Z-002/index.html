Common questions — safety first

Dilution depends on age, sensitivity, and intended use. Typical adult topical dilutions are 1–3% for roll-ons and 0.5–2% for facial products. Exact percentages will be provided with each blend. Avoid self-treating serious conditions; this is not medical advice.
Apply a small diluted amount to the inner forearm and wait 24 hours. If irritation, redness, or discomfort appears, discontinue use and contact the practitioner.
Many oils can be irritating or harmful to animals. I provide pet-safe recommendations and diffuser guidelines; never apply human blends directly to pets and avoid diffusing continuously in enclosed spaces.
Pregnancy requires extra caution. Some oils and higher dilutions are not appropriate. If pregnant or nursing, we use only oils and dilutions considered acceptable by current aromatherapy safety resources and will refer to medical providers when needed.
