Common questions

How should I dilute essential oils?
Dilution depends on age, skin sensitivity, and intended use. Typical topical dilutions range from 0.5% (children, sensitive skin) to 5% (adults, short-term use). Every session includes a personalized dilution plan. This is not medical advice.
Do I need to do a patch test?
Yes — we recommend a small patch test on the forearm with your intended dilution for 24 hours. If irritation occurs, discontinue use. Patch testing info is included in every care note.
Are essential oils safe for pets?
Some oils may be irritating to pets. We provide pet-safety guidance and list oils to avoid around cats and small mammals. Consult your veterinarian for medical concerns; our guidance is precautionary and safety-focused.
What about pregnancy and breastfeeding?
Pregnancy and breastfeeding require special caution. We offer pregnancy-safe options and will avoid contraindicated oils, but this is not a substitute for medical care. Always share pregnancy status before a session.
