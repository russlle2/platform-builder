Booking FAQs

How do you handle pregnancy or breastfeeding?
Please indicate pregnancy or breastfeeding at booking. We provide pregnancy-safe alternatives and conservative dilutions. This is precautionary, not medical advice.
What about pets?
Tell us about household pets. We will advise on diffusion, avoidance of certain oils, and safe ventilation practices.
Why do you recommend patch testing?
Patch testing reduces the chance of topical irritation. We include patch-test instructions and appropriate dilutions with each blend.
