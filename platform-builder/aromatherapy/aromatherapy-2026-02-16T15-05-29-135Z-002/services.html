Frequently asked safety questions

What dilution do you recommend?

General guidelines: 1% for children/older adults or sensitive skin, 2–3% for typical adult topical use. Your practitioner will advise a safe ratio for your case.

How do I perform a patch test?

Apply a diluted drop to the inner forearm and wait 24 hours. If irritation occurs, discontinue use and consult a healthcare practitioner if needed.

Are essential oils safe for pets?

Many oils can be irritating to animals. Keep diffusers in well-ventilated areas, monitor pets, and inform your practitioner about household animals.

What about pregnancy?

Some oils are contraindicated in pregnancy. Always disclose pregnancy or breastfeeding status during intake so we can choose safe alternatives.
