Frequently asked — safety highlights

How should I dilute essential oils?

Dilution depends on age, sensitivity, and application. Typical topical dilutions range from 0.5–5% for adults; children, elders, and sensitive skin require lower percentages. I will recommend exact ratios during consultation. Avoid undiluted application unless guided by a licensed professional.

Do I need to patch test?

Yes. A patch test helps identify skin sensitivity. We’ll go over a simple protocol during intake: apply a diluted amount to a small area, wait 24 hours, and observe for irritation.

Are essential oils safe around pets?

Some oils can affect animals. We follow pet-safe guidelines and will tailor recommendations to your household. If you have cats, birds, or other sensitive animals, mention them at booking so we can suggest safe alternatives and diffusion practices.

What about pregnancy and nursing?

Pregnancy and lactation require extra caution. Certain oils and practices are not recommended. I provide pregnancy-safe guidance and a referral path when appropriate; we never make medical claims.
