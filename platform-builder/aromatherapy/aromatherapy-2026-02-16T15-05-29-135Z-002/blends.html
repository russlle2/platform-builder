Safety notes & FAQ

Dilution, patch testing, pregnancy, and pet-safe guidance are essential. These blends include suggested uses; discuss individual sensitivities during a consultation.

  • Dilution: Typical topical dilutions are 1–3% depending on age and sensitivity.
  • Patch test: Apply diluted sample to inner forearm and observe for 24 hours.
  • Pets: Some oils are not suitable for animals; ventilate rooms and consult if pets show discomfort.
  • Pregnancy: Disclose pregnancy or breastfeeding; some oils are contraindicated.
