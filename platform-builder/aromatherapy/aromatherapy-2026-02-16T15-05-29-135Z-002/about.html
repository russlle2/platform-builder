Questions about practice & safety

Dilution guidance — how is it chosen?
Dilution is chosen based on age, skin sensitivity, and intended application. Recommended percentages are shared during the session and recorded for your use. If you're pregnant or nursing, dilutions are more conservative and certain oils are avoided.
Patch testing instructions
We walk you through a standardized patch test to check for irritation. Patch testing on the forearm for 24 hours is the usual approach.
Pet safety
We flag oils that can be problematic around cats and small animals and recommend safe diffusion practices and alternatives.
Pregnancy & breastfeeding considerations
Certain essential oils are avoided in pregnancy. We will provide pregnancy-safe alternatives and clear usage limits. This is precautionary guidance, not medical counsel.
