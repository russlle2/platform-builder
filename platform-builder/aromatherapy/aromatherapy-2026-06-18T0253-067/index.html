Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is Aromatherapy?
Aromatherapy is a holistic healing modality that uses natural plant extracts, known as essential oils, to promote health and well-being. It harnesses the therapeutic properties of these aromatic compounds to support your physical, mental, and emotional balance.
How can Aromatherapy benefit my unique needs?
Your unique needs are carefully considered. Aromatherapy can help address a wide range of concerns, from stress reduction and sleep improvement to boosting energy and enhancing focus. We tailor blends specifically for your individual goals, ensuring the most effective support.
Are the essential oils used in your Aromatherapy practice safe?
Absolutely. We prioritize your safety and well-being. We use only high-quality, ethically sourced essential oils and provide clear guidance on their safe and appropriate use, including proper dilution and application methods for your specific blend.
What does a consultation for personalized Aromatherapy involve?
During your consultation, we'll have an in-depth conversation about your health history, lifestyle, and specific wellness objectives. This allows us to understand your needs comprehensively and craft a personalized Aromatherapy plan and custom blends that are perfectly suited for you.
