Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is Aromatherapy and how can it benefit me?
Aromatherapy is a holistic healing treatment that uses natural plant extracts, known as essential oils, to promote health and well-being. It can benefit you by supporting emotional balance, reducing stress, improving sleep, easing discomfort, and enhancing mental clarity. We tailor specific blends to address your individual concerns and goals.
Are the essential oils used in your Aromatherapy practice safe?
Absolutely. We prioritize your safety and well-being above all else. We use only the highest quality, therapeutic-grade essential oils, carefully sourced and ethically produced. During your consultation, we discuss any sensitivities or health conditions you may have to ensure your personalized blends are both safe and effective for your unique constitution.
How long does an Aromatherapy consultation typically last?
A typical initial Aromatherapy consultation lasts between 60 to 90 minutes. This allows ample time for us to thoroughly understand your needs, discuss your health history, and craft a personalized essential oil blend and application strategy tailored specifically for you. Follow-up sessions are usually shorter, lasting around 30-45 minutes.
Can Aromatherapy replace conventional medical treatments?
No, Aromatherapy is a complementary therapy and should not replace conventional medical treatments or advice. It works wonderfully alongside traditional healthcare to support your overall well-being. We encourage you to always consult with your primary healthcare provider for any medical conditions and to inform them about your use of Aromatherapy.
