Frequently Asked Questions

How can Aromatherapy specifically help me during a major life transition?
During significant life changes, your emotional landscape can feel turbulent. Aromatherapy, when approached with trauma-informed care, offers gentle support by working directly with your limbic system, the brain's emotional center. Specific scents can help soothe anxiety, promote a sense of grounding, or gently uplift your spirit, providing a consistent emotional anchor as you navigate uncertainty.
Is trauma-informed Aromatherapy different from regular Aromatherapy?
Yes, profoundly so. Trauma-informed Aromatherapy recognizes that past experiences can influence current emotional responses, especially during stressful transitions. It prioritizes creating a sense of safety and agency, avoiding scents or methods that might be triggering. Your practitioner, , is trained to listen deeply and tailor approaches that respect your individual history and current emotional capacity, ensuring a nurturing and non-invasive experience.
What if I'm sensitive to smells, or unsure which essential oils are right for me?
Your comfort and safety are paramount. We begin every consultation with a careful exploration of your sensitivities and preferences. We use only the highest quality, ethically sourced essential oils and introduce them gently. Our process is collaborative; you are always in control, and we will guide you to scents that feel safe, supportive, and resonant with your unique emotional needs, even with heightened sensitivities.
How quickly can I expect to feel emotional relief from Aromatherapy?
While individual experiences vary, many clients report a sense of immediate calm or gentle shift in their emotional state during and after an Aromatherapy session. Consistent engagement with your personalized scent rituals over time supports deeper, more sustainable emotional re-patterning. Our approach focuses on building resilience and fostering a gentle, ongoing process of emotional integration rather than instant, fleeting fixes.
