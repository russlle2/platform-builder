Frequently Asked Questions

What is Aromatherapy and how can it benefit me?
Aromatherapy is a holistic healing modality that uses natural plant extracts, known as essential oils, to promote health and well-being. It can positively influence your mood, alleviate stress, improve sleep quality, and support physical comfort through various application methods. Your specific benefits will depend on your individual needs and the personalized approach we develop together.
Are the essential oils used in Aromatherapy safe?
Yes, when used correctly and with professional guidance, essential oils are generally safe. We prioritize high-quality, ethically sourced essential oils and provide clear instructions for their safe dilution and application. During your consultation, we'll discuss any sensitivities or health conditions you may have to ensure your personalized blends are suitable for you.
How long does an Aromatherapy consultation typically last?
An initial Aromatherapy consultation typically lasts between 60 to 90 minutes. This allows ample time for a thorough discussion of your health history, lifestyle, and wellness goals. Subsequent follow-up sessions, if needed, are generally shorter, focusing on progress and adjustments to your personalized blends.
Do you offer custom Aromatherapy blends for specific concerns?
Absolutely. Our core service is crafting custom Aromatherapy blends tailored specifically to your unique concerns and preferences. Whether you're seeking support for stress, sleep, focus, or emotional balance, will formulate a blend designed to address your individual needs effectively and safely.
