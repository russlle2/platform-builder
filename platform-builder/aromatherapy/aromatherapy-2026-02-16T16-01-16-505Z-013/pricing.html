Frequently asked — session & safety

How should I dilute essential oils?
Dilution depends on age, skin sensitivity, and intended use. Typical adult topical dilutions are 1–3% for daily blends; counseling provided during the session. We never recommend undiluted skin use unless specifically guided for tiny test spots.
What is a patch test?
A patch test involves applying a small diluted amount to the forearm and waiting 24–48 hours to check for irritation. We demonstrate and record results for your safety.
Are essential oils safe around pets?
Some oils can be irritating to pets. We include pet-safe guidance and identify oils to avoid around cats and dogs; always consult your veterinarian if concerned.
Pregnancy or breastfeeding?
We follow conservative, safety-first guidance for pregnancy and lactation. Certain oils are avoided or only used with medical clearance; we will modify recommendations accordingly.
