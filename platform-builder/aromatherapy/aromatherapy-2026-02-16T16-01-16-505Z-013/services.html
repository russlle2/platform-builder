Common questions

How do you handle dilution?

Dilution is calculated by weight/volume and provided with each custom blend. For topical use we give safe percentage ranges and instructions for caregivers when applicable.

Do I need a patch test?

Yes — we recommend a small patch test for new blends, with step-by-step guidance included in your session notes.

Are essential oils safe for pets?

Some oils can be irritating to animals. We discuss household pets during intake and provide pet-safe alternatives and diffusing practices.

Pregnancy and nursing notes

Certain botanicals are contraindicated in pregnancy. If you are pregnant or breastfeeding, please mention this during booking so recommendations avoid restricted oils.
