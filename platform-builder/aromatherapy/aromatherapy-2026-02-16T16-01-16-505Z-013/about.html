Frequently asked — safety topics

How do you keep sessions safe?
We begin with a full intake, discuss medications and sensitivities, and provide a conservative dilution plan. No medical promises are made; aromatherapy supports sensory experience and mindfulness when used responsibly.
Can I bring my pet?
Pets should generally remain at home; certain oils are best kept away from animals. If you're unsure, we will plan pet-safe options during your consult.
Pregnancy notes
I follow conservative guidance for pregnancy and lactation. Some oils are avoided; alternatives are offered.
