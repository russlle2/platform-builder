Frequently asked — safety & common questions

How should I dilute essential oils?

Dilution depends on use and user. Typical topical dilutions range from 0.5–5% for adults. Your personalized guidance will include exact drop counts and carrier recommendations. Avoid undiluted application.

What is a patch test?

A patch test uses a small diluted sample on the forearm for 24–48 hours to check sensitivity before broader use. We include patch-test instructions with every new topical blend.

Are essential oils safe around pets?

Some oils can be harmful to animals in concentrated form. We provide pet-safe alternatives, diffuser placement guidance, and avoidance lists when animals are in the home.

Can I use aromatherapy during pregnancy?

Pregnancy needs special precautions. We avoid certain constituents and always recommend discussing options with your healthcare provider. Safe alternatives are available for most needs.
