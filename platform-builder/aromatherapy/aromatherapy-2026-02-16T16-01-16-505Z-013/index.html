Questions people ask

What dilution ratios do you recommend?
Dilution depends on the product and use. Typical topical dilutions range from 0.5–3% for roll-ons and short-term topical use. Your practitioner will suggest a conservative ratio based on age, sensitivity, and context.
How do I do a patch test?
Apply a small diluted amount to an inner forearm and wait 24 hours. If redness, itching, or irritation appears, discontinue use and contact your practitioner.
Are essential oils safe around pets?
Many oils can affect animals. We use pet-aware guidance and recommend keeping diffusers on low, ensuring ventilation, and avoiding direct topical application to pets. Always consult a veterinarian for concerns.
What about pregnancy and breastfeeding?
We take pregnancy and breastfeeding very seriously. Some oils are not recommended; we select options and dilutions that prioritize safety and may defer use when appropriate.
