Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is Aromatherapy and how can it benefit me?
Aromatherapy is the therapeutic use of aromatic plant extracts and essential oils for psychological and physical well-being. It can support you in various ways, from reducing stress and improving sleep to easing discomfort and enhancing mood, all through the natural properties of pure botanicals.
Are the essential oils used in your Aromatherapy practice safe?
Yes, your safety is our utmost priority. We use only high-quality, pure essential oils from reputable suppliers, and is trained in their safe and effective application. During your consultation, we'll discuss your health history to ensure all recommendations are appropriate and safe for your unique needs.
How long does it take to see results from Aromatherapy?
The experience of Aromatherapy is highly personal. Some individuals report feeling benefits almost immediately, while for others, a more consistent application over time yields the desired results. We encourage you to engage with your custom blends regularly and observe the subtle, positive shifts in your well-being.
Do I need any prior experience with essential oils to try Aromatherapy?
Absolutely not. Whether you're new to the world of essential oils or have some experience, our Aromatherapy services are designed to meet you where you are. will guide you through every step, ensuring you feel comfortable and confident in using your personalized blends and understanding their benefits.
