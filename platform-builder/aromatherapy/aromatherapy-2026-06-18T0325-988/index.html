Frequently Asked Questions

How can Aromatherapy specifically support my nervous system as a senior?
Aromatherapy for seniors focuses on gentle, calming essential oils that interact with your limbic system, the brain's emotional center. This can help to quiet an overactive nervous system, reduce feelings of worry, and promote a sense of deep relaxation, aiding in better sleep and overall emotional balance.
Are the essential oils used in your Aromatherapy safe for sensitive skin or existing health conditions?
Absolutely. Your safety is our utmost priority. We conduct a thorough consultation to understand your health history and any sensitivities. We then select and dilute essential oils appropriate for seniors, ensuring a gentle and beneficial experience without irritation. We always recommend patch testing and careful application.
What kind of Aromatherapy practices might I experience to reduce stress?
We explore various gentle methods tailored to your comfort, such as diffusion, topical application with gentle massage, or aromatic inhalers. These practices are designed to integrate easily into your daily life, providing moments of calm and helping you to consciously regulate your stress responses throughout the day.
Will Aromatherapy interfere with my current medications or other care routines?
It is always wise to inform your healthcare provider about any complementary therapies you are considering. During your consultation, we will discuss your current medications and health regimen to ensure that our Aromatherapy recommendations complement your existing care, providing support without interference.
