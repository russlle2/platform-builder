Safety FAQ

Dilution: Typical adult dilution ranges 1–3% for daily topical use; short-term applications may be higher under supervision.

Patch test: Apply a 1% diluted test spot to the inner forearm; wait 24 hours and observe any reaction.

Pets: Some blends contain oils unsuitable for cats or dogs. Keep diffusion short and provide pet-free zones. Ask for pet-safe alternatives.

Pregnancy & nursing: Many oils are contraindicated. We recommend consulting before using any essential oils during pregnancy or while breastfeeding.
