Quick guidance — common questions

What dilution is safe?
Tap to expand
Typical topical dilutions for adults range 1–3% for daily use and up to 5% for short-term targeted support, depending on the essential oil. Children, elderly, and those with sensitivity often require lower dilutions. We will recommend a dilution based on a personal consultation and a safety review. Never apply neat (undiluted) essential oils to skin.
Should I do a patch test?
Tap to expand
Yes. A simple patch test helps identify sensitivity: apply a small 1% dilution to a discreet area and monitor for 24 hours. If irritation occurs, discontinue use and contact the practitioner.
Are essential oils safe around pets?
Tap to expand
Many essential oils can affect pets differently than humans. Cats and small animals are particularly sensitive. We recommend avoiding direct topical use on pets and ensuring diffusion is brief and with good ventilation. Always consult your veterinarian before using oils around animals.
Pregnancy & nursing considerations?
Tap to expand
Some aromatic compounds are contraindicated in pregnancy or nursing. Inform us if you are pregnant, planning pregnancy, or breastfeeding. We prioritize non-invasive, low-risk options and will tailor recommendations accordingly.
