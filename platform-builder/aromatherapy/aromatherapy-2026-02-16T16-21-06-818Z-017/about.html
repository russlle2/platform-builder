Safety FAQ

Dilution guidance

We provide dilution recommendations by age and use-case (inhalation vs topical). Typical topical dilutions range from 0.5–5% depending on context; practitioner guidance is always tailored.

Patch test

A patch test is recommended for new topical blends: apply a small amount to the forearm for 24 hours and monitor for skin changes.

Pets

We advise caution. Some essential oils are not safe for pets. Guidance will be tailored if pets share your space.

Pregnancy & breast-feeding

We take a conservative approach. Certain oils are avoided; always disclose pregnancy or breastfeeding at intake.
