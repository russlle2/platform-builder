Common myths—and what’s true

Myth: Essential oils cure disease.
Truth: Oils can support wellbeing and sensory balances but are not substitutes for medical care. We emphasize safe, evidence-informed use.
Myth: Undiluted is better.
Truth: Most topical uses require dilution. We provide precise dilution guidelines tailored to age and sensitivity.
Myth: If it’s natural it’s safe for pets.
Truth: Some oils can be harmful to pets. We always advise pet-safe application methods and diffusion routines.
Myth: More scent equals better results.
Truth: Subtlety is key. We often recommend low-concentration blends to support comfort and habituation.

Frequently asked — safety highlights

Dilution
We recommend age- and use-specific dilutions. Typical adults: 1–3% for topical formulas unless otherwise instructed.
Patch testing
Always perform a patch test on a small area for 24 hours before wider topical use.
Pets & diffusion
Some oils are not pet-safe. Use short diffusion bursts and provide pet-free zones.
Pregnancy & nursing
Caution advised; many oils are contraindicated in pregnancy. Consult a qualified practitioner before use.
