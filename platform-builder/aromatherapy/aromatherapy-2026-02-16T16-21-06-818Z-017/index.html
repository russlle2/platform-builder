Myth vs Truth: Aromatherapy with a safety-first lens

Myth

Essential oils are always "natural and safe" because they are plant-based.

Truth

Plant-derived does not equal risk-free. We prioritize dilution, patch testing and contraindication checks for every client.

Myth

More drops equals better results.

Truth

Appropriate potency and delivery matter. Less can be safer and more effective for daily use.

Frequently asked — careful, practical answers

Dilution is set per client based on age, skin sensitivity, intended use and frequency. We typically start with conservative percentages and adjust only if tolerated. Always do a patch test before regular use.
A patch test applies a small amount of diluted product to a discreet skin area and monitors for reaction over 24–48 hours. It helps identify sensitivities before wider use.
Some oils can be irritating to pets. We review household animals and recommend diffusion practices, restricted access, and pet-safe alternatives when necessary.
Pregnancy requires tailored guidance. We avoid certain constituents and work with you to choose safe, low-concentration approaches. Always consult your healthcare provider for clinical advice.
