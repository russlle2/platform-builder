Common questions

How do you handle dilution?
All blending in workshops uses clear dilution tables and measured carrier oils. Participants are shown how to calculate safe dilutions for topical use (suggested ranges only). We never give medical advice.
Should I patch test?
Yes. A brief patch-test protocol is demonstrated and encouraged for all topical blends. If you have known sensitivities, please inform the host before the session.
What about pets?
Some aromas can affect animals. Sessions aim for pet-friendly practices: low airborne concentration, ventilation, and separate storage. Contact us with specifics about your pet if you plan to bring them.
Can I attend while pregnant?
Safety during pregnancy requires individual consideration. We encourage you to consult your care provider and to let the host know — we can adapt blends and offer fragrance-free options.
