Frequently asked — safety notes

How do you dilute?
I recommend dilutions by context—topical roll‑ons are commonly 2–5% for adults; lower for sensitive skin and elderly. Exact ratios are provided with each blend.
Patch test?
Yes. A 24–48 hour patch test is advised before regular topical use.
Pets?
Many essential oils affect animals differently. I provide pet‑safe alternatives and diffusing guidance to minimize exposure.
Pregnancy & nursing?
I avoid recommending certain oils for prenatal clients and offer pregnancy‑appropriate formulas. Always consult with your healthcare provider for personal guidance.
