Practicals & safety

What's a patch test?
A small, controlled test to check for skin sensitivity; we provide clear steps so you can test safely at home.
How do you recommend dilution?
We provide conservative dilution guidelines (commonly 1–3% for topical blends depending on age and sensitivity) and carrier suggestions; never apply undiluted essential oils to skin.
Are essential oils safe around pets?
Some oils can affect animals. We advise keeping blends out of reach, avoiding direct application to pets, and opting for simple ventilation rather than continuous diffusing when animals are present.
Pregnancy considerations?
We don\'t offer medical advice. If pregnant, consult your healthcare professional; we can offer unscented or pregnancy-conscious options instead of certain oils.
