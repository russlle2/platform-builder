Common questions

How are prices quoted?
Fees are published per session or event. Full details appear before checkout so there are no surprises.
What about dilution and safety?
Guidance on dilution ratios and carrier use is included with consultations; we always recommend patch testing and conservative dilutions.
Can pets attend events?
Due to sensitivity and safety, pets are generally not allowed unless explicitly stated for an approved outdoor event.
Pregnancy and essential oils?
We avoid making medical claims. If you are pregnant, please consult your healthcare provider; we provide non-scented or pregnancy-safe options when requested.
