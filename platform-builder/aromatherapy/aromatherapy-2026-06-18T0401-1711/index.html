Frequently Asked Questions

How can Aromatherapy specifically aid relationship healing for seniors?
Aromatherapy, when thoughtfully applied, can help soothe anxieties or resentments that sometimes strain connections. Specific essences can encourage empathy, promote open communication, and foster a sense of calm, creating a more nurturing emotional environment for your relationships to flourish. It’s about gentle emotional support, not a quick fix.
Are the essential oils used in your Aromatherapy safe for those aged 60+?
Absolutely. Your well-being is our utmost priority. We use only the highest quality, pure essential oils and employ conservative dilution rates and application methods specifically tailored for sensitive skin and mature systems. We always conduct a thorough consultation to ensure your comfort and safety, considering any current health considerations or medications.
What does 'heart-centered care' mean within your Aromatherapy practice?
'Heart-centered care' signifies an approach that prioritizes emotional well-being, compassion, and authentic connection. In Aromatherapy, this translates to selecting scents that resonate with the heart chakra, fostering feelings of love, forgiveness, and interconnectedness, thereby supporting the emotional foundations of your relationships with yourself and others.
Will I need to change my daily routine to incorporate Aromatherapy for relationship support?
Not at all. Our bespoke Aromatherapy blends are designed to integrate seamlessly into your existing routine. Whether it's a gentle diffusion during quiet reflection, a personal inhaler for moments of emotional comfort, or a subtle topical application, we help you find methods that feel natural and supportive without demanding significant changes to your daily rhythm.
