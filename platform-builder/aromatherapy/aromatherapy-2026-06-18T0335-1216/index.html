Frequently Asked Questions

How does trauma-informed aromatherapy specifically support emotional healing after chronic illness or injury?
Our approach acknowledges the profound impact chronic illness or injury can have on your nervous system and emotional well-being. We select specific essential oils that research indicates can help modulate stress responses, reduce feelings of overwhelm, and gently encourage emotional processing, all within a framework that respects your history and current capacity.
Will aromatherapy interfere with my existing medical treatments or medications for chronic conditions?
Your safety and well-being are paramount. We conduct thorough consultations to understand your complete health profile, including any existing medical treatments or medications. Our aromatherapy protocols are designed to be complementary and supportive, always prioritizing non-interference and working in harmony with your care team. We encourage you to discuss this with your primary healthcare provider.
What kind of emotional shifts can I realistically expect from incorporating aromatherapy into my recovery from injury or illness?
While individual experiences vary, many clients report a greater sense of calm, improved emotional regulation, and a reduction in feelings of anxiety or despair often associated with chronic health challenges. Aromatherapy can help cultivate a more positive emotional climate, supporting your resilience as you continue your physical recovery and adapt to new realities.
How are the aromatherapy blends tailored to my specific emotional needs during my recovery journey?
Through a detailed intake process, we explore your unique emotional landscape, current sensitivities, and desired outcomes. This allows us to select essential oils with precision, creating a personalized blend that resonates with your specific needs for emotional support, whether that's for grounding, gentle upliftment, or fostering a sense of peace.
