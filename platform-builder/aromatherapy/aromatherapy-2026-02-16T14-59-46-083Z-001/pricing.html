Common questions — safety & use

What dilution should I use?

Dilution depends on product type, age, and sensitivity. Typical topical dilutions range 1–3% for adults; sensitive users and children warrant lower dilutions. Specific ratios are given with each blend.

Should I patch test first?

Yes. Always patch test a new topical blend on a small area for 24 hours before regular use.

Are essential oils safe around pets?

Some essential oils can be irritating to animals. We always include pet-safe recommendations and will adapt blends if you live with pets.

What about pregnancy?

Pregnancy requires extra caution. We provide guidance and will avoid oils that are commonly discouraged during pregnancy. Always consult your healthcare provider for personal medical concerns.
