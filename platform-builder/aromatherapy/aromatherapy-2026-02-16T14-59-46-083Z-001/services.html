Frequently asked safety questions

How should I dilute essential oils?
Dilution depends on age, sensitivity, and intended use. Typical adult topical dilutions range 1–3% for roll-ons and 0.5–2% for children (with medical guidance). Your consult includes a customized dilution guide. This is educational information, not medical advice.
Do I need to patch test?
Yes. We recommend a patch test on the inner forearm 24 hours before regular topical use. If you notice redness or irritation, discontinue use and consult a healthcare provider.
Are essential oils safe around pets?
Some oils can be irritating to animals. We review pet-safe options and dilution practices. Always avoid applying concentrated oil directly on pets and ensure good ventilation when diffusing. If in doubt, consult your veterinarian.
What about pregnancy?
During pregnancy certain oils are often avoided. We provide pregnancy-aware recommendations and will tailor blends. Consult your healthcare provider for medical guidance.
