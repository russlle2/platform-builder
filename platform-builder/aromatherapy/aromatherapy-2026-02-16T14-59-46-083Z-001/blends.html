Safety Highlights

  • Dilution: follow recommended % for age and context.
  • Patch test before regular topical use.
  • Keep oils away from eyes and open wounds.

Quick FAQ

Can I use blends around my pet?
Some oils are irritating to animals. Use low amounts, short diffusion bursts, and consult your vet for specific species guidance.
What's the shelf life?
Most blends are best within 12–18 months if stored cool and dark. Carrier oils may shorten shelf life.
