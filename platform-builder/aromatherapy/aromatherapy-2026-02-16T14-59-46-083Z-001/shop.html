Buying guidance & safety

We include dilution templates and a patch-test card with every order. If you're pregnant, nursing, or have a chronic condition, consult a healthcare professional before using products. Keep essential oils away from children and pets, and avoid undiluted application.

FAQ — practical answers

How do I patch-test?

Apply a small amount of diluted product to the inner forearm. Wait 24 hours. If irritation occurs, discontinue use.

What dilution is safe for roll-ons?

We typically use 1–3% for adult roll-ons depending on intent; children's dilutions are lower and require guidance.

Can I diffuse near my pet?

Some pets are sensitive. Use short bouts, good ventilation, and avoid oils known to be problematic for animals.
