Philosophy: scent as a practical tool

I view aroma as an expressive tool that can shape environment and routine when used with respect and clarity. Sessions emphasize reproducible routines — labeled blends, clear dilution notes, and safe storage recommendations.

What I don't do

I do not diagnose, treat, or claim cures for medical conditions. I provide sensory and lifestyle support, and when health conditions are relevant I encourage collaboration with your healthcare provider.

Safety Q&A

Dilution guidance

We provide dilution percentages for every topical product and explain adjustments for age and sensitivity.

Patch testing

A patch test is strongly recommended for new topical blends and will be covered during the session.

Pets & oils

We use pet-aware guidelines and will adapt blends if animals are in your home. Some oils are avoided where animal exposure is likely.

Pregnancy note

Pregnancy warrants extra caution. We provide pregnancy-safe alternatives and will omit oils commonly discouraged in prenatal care.
