Frequently asked — concise answers

How should I dilute essential oils?
Dilution depends on age, skin sensitivity, and purpose. Typical topical dilutions range from 1% (general) to 2.5% for short-term targeted use. For children, older adults, or sensitive skin we use lower dilutions. I'll provide exact recipes if you book a consult.
What is a patch test and why does it matter?
A patch test applies a diluted blend to a small skin area and observes for 24–48 hours. It's the simplest step to check for irritation or allergic reactions before wider use.
Are essential oils safe around pets?
Some oils can be risky for animals. We take a conservative approach: I provide pet-safe alternatives and guidance for diffusion times and ventilation. Never apply oils directly to pets without professional vet advice.
What about pregnancy and nursing?
Pregnancy requires special precautions. Many oils are avoided or used in very low dilutions. If you're pregnant or nursing, we'll review trusted guidance and choose only well-studied, conservative options or postpone aromatherapy when appropriate.
