Common questions

How should I dilute essential oils?
Dilution depends on age, use, and oil. Typical adult topical dilutions range from 1%–3% for daily creams/roll-ons. We cover exact math during the VIP Day and provide dilution cards. Always follow safety data for each oil.
What is a patch test?
A patch test applies a small diluted amount to skin for 24 hours to check for irritation. We'll show the correct method and what to watch for.
Are essential oils safe around pets?
Some oils can be harmful to pets. We emphasize pet-safe choices and diffusion patterns to minimize risk. Consult your veterinarian for concerns.
What about pregnancy?
Pregnancy requires extra caution. Certain oils and higher concentrations are not recommended. We provide pregnancy-safe alternatives and refer to medical guidance when appropriate.
