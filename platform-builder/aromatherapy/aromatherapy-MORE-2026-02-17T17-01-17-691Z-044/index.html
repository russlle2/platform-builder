Safety & FAQ

Are these remedies medical advice?

No. Content here is informational and supportive. If you have health concerns, seek professional medical advice.

Dilution and patch testing

Recommended dilution depends on volume and purpose. Our builder suggests conservative dilutions (0.5–2%). Always perform a patch test: apply a small drop of diluted oil on the forearm and wait 24 hours for irritation.

Pregnancy & nursing

Certain essential oils are not recommended during pregnancy or nursing. Consult your provider before use. Use low dilutions and avoid internal ingestion.

Pets

Diffusing near animals requires care. Use low concentrations, ventilate the space, and observe your pet. Some oils are unsafe for pets; consult your veterinarian.
