FAQ — safety first

Are these remedies medical?
No. Our scent guides and blends are thoughtfully composed and may support mood or relaxation, but they are not medical treatments.
How should I dilute oils for topical use?
We list dilution ranges on each suggested blend. A common low-risk range for adults is 1-2% for leave-on skin application. For children, pregnant people, or sensitive skin choose lower dilutions and consult a provider. Always perform a patch test.
What about pets?
Some essential oils can be unsafe for pets. Use caution near animals, avoid applying oils directly to them, and ensure shared spaces have good ventilation.
Pregnancy & nursing
If pregnant or nursing, consult a qualified healthcare provider before using essential oils.
