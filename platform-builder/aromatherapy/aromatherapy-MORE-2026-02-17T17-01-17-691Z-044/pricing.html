FAQs — safety quick notes

  • Patch test: Apply a small diluted sample to forearm for 24 hours; stop if irritation develops.
  • Pregnancy & medications: Some oils are best avoided; consult a qualified practitioner before use.
  • Pets: Certain oils (e.g., tea tree, pennyroyal) can be harmful to animals; keep blends out of reach and avoid direct application to pets.
  • Storage: Keep blends in dark glass, cool and away from direct heat or sunlight.
