Safety, dilution & common notes

  • Dilution: Guidance is suggestive. Typical topical dilutions might be 0.5–2% depending on sensitivity and purpose — see the blend builder for drops-per-bottle examples.
  • Patch test: Always do a patch test when trying a new topical blend: apply a small amount to the inner forearm and check after 24 hours.
  • Pets & pregnancy: Essential oils can affect pets and pregnancy. Consult a professional before topical use if pregnant, nursing, or caring for animals.
