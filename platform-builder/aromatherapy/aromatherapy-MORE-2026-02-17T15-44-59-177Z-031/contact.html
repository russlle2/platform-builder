Common safety steps

  • Always dilute essential oils; follow recommended ratios and store safely.
  • Perform a patch test on skin before regular topical use.
  • Inform us about pregnancy, nursing, medications, or medical conditions.
  • We provide pet-safe alternatives on request; some oils are toxic to animals.
