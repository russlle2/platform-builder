Safety & guidance

All guidance is supportive and educational; essential oils may support wellbeing but are not a substitute for medical care.

Patch test: We recommend a small patch test before regular use.

Dilution: Follow dilution guidelines for topical use; full-strength essential oils are not applied neat.

Pets & pregnancy: Some oils can affect animals or pregnancy—consult a qualified practitioner before use.
