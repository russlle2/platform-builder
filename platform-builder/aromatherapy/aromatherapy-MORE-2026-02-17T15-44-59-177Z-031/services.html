Safety & brief FAQ

Do I need a patch test or dilution guidance?

Yes. For topical use we provide dilution guidelines and ask for a small patch test. We recommend conservative dilutions (1–3%) for most daily blends. Avoid applying undiluted essential oils to skin.

What about pets and pregnancy?

Some essential oils are contraindicated in pregnancy and around sensitive animals. We will discuss household members, including pets and pregnancy status, when creating a plan. No medical advice is given; consult your care provider for medical concerns.

Are these medical treatments?

No. Aromatherapy may support well-being and routines but is not a substitute for medical care. We focus on safe, sensory-based practices that may support relaxation, focus, or sleep.
