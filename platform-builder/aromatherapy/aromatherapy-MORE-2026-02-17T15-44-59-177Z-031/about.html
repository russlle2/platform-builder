Philosophy

We believe small, repeatable scent practices can be accessible. Guidance prioritizes safety and clarity: dilution, testing, and consideration of household factors are standard parts of our care.

FAQ
Patch test: Apply a diluted spot to the inside of your arm and observe for 24 hours.
Dilution: We provide dilution ratios — particularly for topical use — and do not recommend undiluted application.
Pets & pregnancy: Discuss oils and use with us before using around pets or in pregnancy; some oils may be contraindicated.
