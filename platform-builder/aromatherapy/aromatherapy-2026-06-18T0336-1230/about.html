How can Aromatherapy specifically help me during a major life transition?

Aromatherapy, when approached with trauma-informed care, offers a non-verbal pathway to emotional regulation. Specific scents can help soothe your nervous system, reduce feelings of anxiety or overwhelm, and create anchors of comfort and safety as you adapt to new circumstances. We focus on blends that support your emotional processing and resilience.

Is Aromatherapy suitable if I'm dealing with past trauma alongside current changes?

Absolutely. Our practice is built on trauma-informed principles. We understand that current life changes can sometimes re-activate past experiences. Aromatherapy can be a gentle, non-invasive tool to help you feel more grounded and present, allowing you to engage with your emotions safely and at your own pace, without feeling overwhelmed.

What makes your Aromatherapy approach different for someone experiencing significant life shifts?

Our approach prioritizes your emotional safety and autonomy. We don't just offer oils; we offer a supportive relationship that acknowledges the depth and complexity of your experience. We co-create scent rituals that honor your personal history and current needs, focusing on building inner resources and promoting a sense of agency as you navigate change.

Will I feel immediate relief from using Aromatherapy during a difficult period?

While many individuals report an immediate sense of calm or comfort, the deeper work of emotional healing and integration through Aromatherapy is often a gradual process. Our aim is to provide you with tools and support that accumulate over time, building sustained resilience and a more stable emotional foundation as you move through your transition. Consistency is key.
