Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is Aromatherapy?
Aromatherapy is a holistic healing treatment that uses natural plant extracts to promote health and well-being. Sometimes called essential oil therapy, it utilizes aromatic essential oils medicinally to improve the health of the body, mind, and spirit. Your practitioner guides you on how to incorporate these powerful essences safely and effectively.
Are essential oils safe to use?
When used correctly and under professional guidance, essential oils are very safe. Your practitioner will provide detailed instructions on proper dilution, application methods, and any contraindications specific to your health profile. It's crucial to use high-quality, pure essential oils and never to ingest them unless specifically advised by a qualified professional.
How can Aromatherapy benefit my daily life?
Aromatherapy can offer a myriad of benefits to your daily life, from reducing stress and anxiety to improving sleep quality and boosting mood. Your personalized blends can support your focus during work, soothe sore muscles after exercise, or create a calming atmosphere for relaxation, enhancing your overall sense of well-being consistently.
What should I expect during my first Aromatherapy consultation?
During your initial consultation, you can expect a comprehensive discussion about your current health, lifestyle, and wellness goals. Your practitioner will listen attentively to your needs and collaboratively design a personalized Aromatherapy plan, which may include custom essential oil blends and recommendations for their safe and effective use at home. It's a supportive and informative experience tailored just for you.
