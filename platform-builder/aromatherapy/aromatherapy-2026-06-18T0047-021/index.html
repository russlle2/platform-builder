Frequently Asked Questions

How soon will I notice results from my blend?
Most clients experience subtle shifts within 24-48 hours of consistent use, with deeper benefits emerging over 2-4 weeks. This depends on your specific goals and how you incorporate the blend into your routine.
Can I use your blends alongside other wellness treatments?
Absolutely. We coordinate with your existing healthcare team to ensure compatibility. Our blends complement practices like massage therapy, meditation, and even medical treatments without interference.
How often should I replace my custom blend?
Essential oils maintain potency for 6-8 months when stored properly. We recommend refreshing your formula every 6 months or when you notice diminished effects, as your needs may have evolved.
Are your essential oils certified organic?
Yes, every oil undergoes rigorous third-party testing for purity and organic certification. We source directly from ethical farms, avoiding synthetic additives or diluents.
