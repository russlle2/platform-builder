Frequently Asked Questions

How do you choose oils for my specific needs?
We discuss your wellness goals and lifestyle to select oils with scientifically proven effects for your unique physiology—no generic recommendations.
Can aromatherapy help with chronic stress?
Absolutely. We formulate blends targeting cortisol regulation and nervous system balance, supported by clinical data on specific oil combinations.
How soon will I notice results?
Most clients feel subtle shifts within 24-72 hours of consistent use, with deeper effects emerging over 2-4 weeks of integration.
Are your blends safe with medications?
Yes. We provide full ingredient transparency and consult on any potential interactions—your safety is our priority.
