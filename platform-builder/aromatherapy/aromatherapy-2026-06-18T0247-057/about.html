What exactly is Aromatherapy and how can it benefit me?

Aromatherapy is a holistic practice that uses aromatic essential oils extracted from plants to promote physical and psychological well-being. It can support your mood, alleviate stress, enhance relaxation, and even assist with minor physical discomforts, all tailored to your specific needs.

Are the essential oils used in Aromatherapy safe?

Yes, when used correctly and under professional guidance, essential oils are safe. We prioritize your safety and will only recommend high-quality, pure essential oils, providing you with detailed instructions on proper dilution and application methods for your individual circumstances.

Do I need any prior knowledge of essential oils before my Aromatherapy session?

Absolutely not. Your session is designed to be accessible whether you're new to aromatherapy or have some experience. We will guide you through every step, explaining the properties and uses of different essential oils in a way that is clear and easy to understand.

How long does it take to see results from Aromatherapy?

The experience of aromatherapy is highly individual. Some people notice benefits immediately, while for others, positive changes unfold gradually with consistent use. We will discuss realistic expectations and how to best integrate your custom blends for optimal results in your daily life.
