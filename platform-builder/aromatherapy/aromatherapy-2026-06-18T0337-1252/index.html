Frequently Asked Questions

How can Aromatherapy specifically support my postpartum emotional recovery?
Aromatherapy provides a gentle yet powerful way to address the emotional shifts and potential trauma experienced during postpartum. We utilize specific essential oils known for their calming, uplifting, and grounding properties, carefully chosen to support your nervous system, reduce anxiety, and foster a sense of emotional balance. Your personalized blends will be crafted to address your unique needs, helping you process your birth experience and nurture your emotional well-being.
Is Aromatherapy safe for me and my baby during the postpartum period?
Yes, when practiced by a trained professional, Aromatherapy is safe and highly beneficial. We prioritize your and your baby's well-being above all else. We use only the highest quality, therapeutic-grade essential oils and apply them with dilution and methods appropriate for new mothers and those around infants. We will always discuss application methods and safety precautions in detail to ensure your comfort and peace of mind.
What does a trauma-informed Aromatherapy session involve for new mothers?
A trauma-informed session begins with a deep, compassionate conversation where you can share your experiences without judgment. We focus on creating a safe and empowering space. We then carefully select essential oils based on your emotional landscape and preferences, guiding you through gentle aromatic practices that honor your body's wisdom. The goal is to facilitate emotional release and cultivate a sense of inner safety and resilience, always at your pace.
How long until I might feel the emotional benefits of Aromatherapy postpartum?
The experience is unique for everyone, but many new mothers report feeling a sense of immediate calm and gentle upliftment during and after their first session. Consistent, personalized use of your aromatic blends can lead to deeper, more sustained emotional regulation, reduced stress, and an increased sense of well-being over time. We encourage you to honor your process and trust the subtle yet profound shifts that unfold.
