Frequently Asked Questions

How can aromatherapy support me during a big life transition?
Aromatherapy offers a gentle way to support your emotional and physical well-being when you're navigating significant life changes. We focus on specific essential oils that can help soothe stress, promote clarity, and encourage a sense of stability, helping you feel more grounded through it all.
Is aromatherapy only for when I'm feeling overwhelmed during a transition?
Not at all. Our approach emphasizes preventive care. By incorporating aromatherapy into your routine before or during a transition, you can build resilience, maintain emotional balance, and support your body's natural adaptive capabilities, rather than just reacting to stress.
How do you tailor aromatherapy to the changing seasons and my personal needs?
We consider both the current natural season and your individual 'life season' – the phase of transition you're experiencing. We'll discuss your specific needs and current environment to craft personalized blends and practices that resonate with both, ensuring truly relevant and effective support.
What kind of natural products do you use in your aromatherapy practice?
We are committed to using pure, high-quality essential oils sourced with sustainability in mind. Our blends incorporate botanical extracts and natural carriers, reflecting our earthy and grounded ethos. You can trust that our products are crafted with integrity and respect for nature.
