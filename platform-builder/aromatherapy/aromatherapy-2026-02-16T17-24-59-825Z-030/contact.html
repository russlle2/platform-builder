Common safety questions

How should I dilute essential oils?
Safe dilution guidance
General guidance: adults often use 1–3% dilution for blends applied to skin (6–18 drops per 30 mL carrier). Lower dilutions are recommended for older adults, children, and sensitive skin. Specific guidance is personalized during consultation.
What is a patch test and why do one?
Simple precaution
A patch test applies a small amount of diluted oil to the forearm for 24 hours to check for irritation or sensitization. It is the simplest way to reduce risk of adverse skin reactions.
Are essential oils safe around pets?
Pet-aware guidance
Some essential oils can be toxic to pets at certain exposures. During intake I ask about household animals and provide specific, conservative guidance for diffusing and topical use to reduce risk.
Can I use essential oils during pregnancy?
Precautionary note
Pregnancy raises special considerations. I follow safety-forward protocols and will discuss which oils and dilutions are appropriate. Always disclose pregnancy status during booking.
