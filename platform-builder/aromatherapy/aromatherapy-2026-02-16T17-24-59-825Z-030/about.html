FAQ highlights

Dilution best practices

We recommend adult dilutions around 1–3% for topical blends. Specifics depend on purpose and individual sensitivity.

Patch testing

Patch testing is recommended for all new topical blends prior to regular use.

Pets and essential oils

We provide pet-safe alternatives and diffusion guidance; consult your vet when in doubt.

Pregnancy considerations

We exercise added caution in pregnancy; full disclosure of status at booking is requested and will guide recommendations.
