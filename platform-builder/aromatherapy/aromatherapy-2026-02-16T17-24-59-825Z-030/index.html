Frequently asked questions

Dilution — how concentrated are blends?
Dilution is individualized. I recommend conservative dilutions and will provide precise percentages and vehicle recommendations (carrier oils, inhalers, etc.).
Patch testing — do I need one?
Yes. A simple patch test is recommended for topical use. I will guide you through a step-by-step patch test and alternatives if you have sensitive skin.
Pets — are essential oils safe around animals?
Some essential oils can be harmful to pets. I will review your household (species, size, health) and recommend pet-safe choices and diffusion practices to reduce risk.
Pregnancy & breastfeeding
Pregnancy and lactation require special consideration. I avoid recommending certain oils and will work within evidence-informed restraint. Consult your healthcare provider for medical questions.
Do you make medical claims?
No. Sessions focus on safety, sensory support, and lifestyle rituals. I do not diagnose or treat medical conditions.
