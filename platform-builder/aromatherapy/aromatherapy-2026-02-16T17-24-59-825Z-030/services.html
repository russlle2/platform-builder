Common questions

How is safety handled?
I screen for allergies, medications, pregnancy, and pet exposure. I provide dilution percentages and recommended application methods — no medical claims are made.
What about dilution?
Dilution is individualized. Typical topical dilutions range from 0.5–5% depending on age, skin sensitivity, and context. You will always get written dilution guidance.
Patch test?
Yes — a small patch test is recommended for topical use. Instructions are provided with each topical blend.
Pets & pregnancy?
Specific guidance is provided. Some essential oils are not recommended around certain pets or during pregnancy. We adjust blends accordingly.
