Common questions

How should I dilute essential oils?

Dilution depends on purpose, age, and skin sensitivity. Typical topical dilutions for adults are 1–3% for regular use; for children or sensitive skin, lower dilutions apply. We provide clear dilution guidance with every blend.

What is a patch test?

A patch test is applying a small amount of diluted blend to a discrete area of skin for 24 hours to check for irritation. We recommend patch testing new blends before regular topical use.

Are essential oils safe around pets?

Some oils can be irritating to pets. We avoid suggesting topical use of known risky oils for animals and provide guidance for diffusing safely (short intervals, leaving the room available to your pet). Always consult your veterinarian for pet-specific concerns.

Pregnancy and aromatherapy

Pregnancy involves special considerations. We use a safety-forward approach and will adapt or avoid certain oils. Always share pregnancy status during intake; we will not make medical claims.
