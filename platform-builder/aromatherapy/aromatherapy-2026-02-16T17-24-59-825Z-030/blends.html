Signature blends

1. Morning Clarity

Aroma profile: bright, herbaceous, light piney base.

2. Calm Harbor
Top: Sweet Orange • Middle: Lavender • Base: Cedarwood
Aroma profile: soft citrus folded into floral-woody warmth.

Aroma profile: herbaceous citrus sharpened by earthy base notes.

4. Gentle Reset

Blend safety & FAQs

Dilution: I provide concentration guidance for topical and personal inhalers. Typical ranges are listed but adjusted for age, pregnancy, medication, and skin sensitivity.

Patch test: For any topical use, a patch test is recommended 24–48 hours prior to regular application.

Pets: Some oils are not pet-safe. We will offer an alternative or diffusion plan if pets share the environment.

Pregnancy: Certain oils and higher concentrations are avoided; consult your care provider and disclose pregnancy during intake.
