What exactly is Aromatherapy?

Aromatherapy is a holistic healing modality that harnesses the therapeutic properties of aromatic essential oils to promote physical, mental, and emotional well-being. It involves using these concentrated plant extracts for inhalation or topical application to support your body's natural healing processes and enhance overall harmony.

How can Aromatherapy benefit my daily life?

Aromatherapy can offer a wide array of benefits, depending on your individual needs. It can help alleviate stress and anxiety, improve sleep quality, boost mood, support respiratory health, ease minor aches and discomforts, and enhance focus and energy. Your personalized blend will target your specific wellness goals.

Are essential oils safe to use?

When used correctly and with guidance from a qualified Aromatherapist, essential oils are generally safe. We prioritize your safety by using only high-quality, pure oils and providing clear instructions on dilution and application methods. During your consultation, we'll discuss any sensitivities or health conditions you may have to ensure your blends are appropriate for you.

What should I expect during an Aromatherapy consultation?

During your consultation, you'll have a warm, in-depth conversation with Dr. Morgan Ellis about your health history, lifestyle, and wellness goals. We'll explore which essential oils might best support you, and then she will custom-blend a unique formulation tailored to your specific needs. You'll also receive guidance on how to use your blend effectively at home.
