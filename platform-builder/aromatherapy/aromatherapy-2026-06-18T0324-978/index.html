Frequently Asked Questions

How can Aromatherapy specifically benefit seniors aged 60+?
For seniors, Aromatherapy offers gentle, non-invasive support for a range of needs. We focus on enhancing your comfort, promoting relaxation for better sleep, supporting cognitive clarity, and providing natural ways to address seasonal challenges, all without harsh chemicals or invasive procedures. Our goal is to augment your current wellness practices with botanical wisdom.
Is Aromatherapy safe to use if I am taking other medications?
Your safety is our top priority. We always conduct a thorough consultation to understand your health history and any medications you are currently taking. This allows us to select essential oils and application methods that are gentle, effective, and won't interfere with your existing health regimen. We emphasize dilution and careful application to ensure your well-being.
What does a typical Aromatherapy session involve for preventive care?
A session begins with a relaxed conversation about your current well-being and seasonal concerns. Based on this, we'll suggest specific essential oils or blends and demonstrate simple application techniques you can use at home, such as gentle inhalation or diluted topical application. Our focus is on empowering you with practical, easy-to-integrate tools for ongoing preventive care.
How can Aromatherapy help me with seasonal transitions and preventive wellness?
Aromatherapy excels at supporting your body's natural resilience through seasonal changes. For instance, specific aromas can help uplift spirits during darker months, support clear breathing during allergy season, or promote a sense of calm during periods of change. We guide you in using these natural tools to proactively maintain your balance and comfort throughout the year.
