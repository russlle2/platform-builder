Frequently Asked Questions

How can Aromatherapy specifically help me, as a parent managing daily household stress?
Aromatherapy provides gentle, sensory inputs that directly influence your limbic system, which is tied to emotion and memory. For parents, this means a subtle yet powerful tool to signal safety and calm to your nervous system, reducing the feeling of being constantly 'on alert' and helping you navigate the demands of childcare with more resilience and a quieter inner landscape.
I have no prior experience with wellness practices. Is Aromatherapy still accessible for me?
Absolutely. Aromatherapy is wonderfully accessible and requires no prior wellness experience. We meet you exactly where you are, offering simple, gentle ways to incorporate scents into your daily life. Our approach is about finding what feels good and supportive to you, without any pressure or complicated routines, easing you into a sense of calm.
Will using Aromatherapy add another task to my already overflowing schedule?
Our focus is on integration, not addition. We help you find incredibly simple ways to weave Aromatherapy into your existing routines – a drop in the shower, a quick sniff from a roller bottle, or diffusing while you prepare dinner. These are small, potent moments designed to give back to you, rather than demand more of your precious time.
Are the Aromatherapy products safe to use around my young children?
Your family's safety is our highest priority. We meticulously select and recommend essential oils and blends that are appropriate for use around young children, always considering their delicate systems. We provide clear guidance on dilution, diffusion methods, and age-appropriate application, ensuring your Aromatherapy practices are both effective and safe for everyone in your home.
