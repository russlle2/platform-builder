Frequently Asked Questions

I'm always so busy, how can Aromatherapy genuinely help with my work-related physical pain and burnout?
Aromatherapy provides a direct, yet gentle, pathway to your nervous system. Even a few minutes of intentional aromatic practice can signal to your body that it's safe to relax, helping to unwind the chronic tension that accumulates from demanding work schedules and the mental fatigue of burnout. We focus on practical, accessible ways to integrate this relief into your routine.
I have no experience with 'wellness' or 'somatic healing.' Is Aromatherapy still for me?
Absolutely. You don't need any prior knowledge or experience. Our approach to Aromatherapy is gentle and accessible, focusing simply on how specific scents can help your body feel better and more at ease. We guide you every step of the way, making it easy to understand and integrate these practices into your life, regardless of your background.
My physical pain feels so ingrained from years of work stress. Can Aromatherapy really make a difference?
While Aromatherapy isn't a quick fix, it offers a powerful, complementary tool for addressing chronic physical pain linked to stress and burnout. By targeting the nervous system and encouraging deep relaxation, specific essential oils can help reduce inflammation, ease muscle spasms, and shift your perception of discomfort, offering a gentle but profound sense of relief over time.
I'm concerned about adding another 'to-do' to my already packed schedule. How can I fit Aromatherapy in?
We understand completely. Our goal is to simplify, not complicate. We'll work with you to find small, manageable ways to incorporate Aromatherapy into your day – perhaps a calming scent at your desk, a soothing blend before bed, or a targeted roll-on for tension. The aim is gentle integration that supports you, not overwhelms you.
