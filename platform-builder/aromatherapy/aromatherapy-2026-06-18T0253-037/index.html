Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is Aromatherapy and how can it benefit me?
Aromatherapy is a holistic healing treatment that uses natural plant extracts to promote health and well-being. It leverages the aromatic compounds of essential oils to improve physical and psychological health. For you, this can mean anything from reducing stress and improving sleep to easing discomfort and boosting your mood, all tailored to your specific needs.
Are the essential oils used in your Aromatherapy blends safe?
Yes, your safety is our utmost priority. We use only high-quality, ethically sourced essential oils and conduct thorough consultations to understand your health history and sensitivities. All blends are formulated with appropriate dilution ratios for safe use, and we provide clear instructions on how to apply and enjoy your custom aromatherapy products.
How long does a typical Aromatherapy consultation last?
A typical initial aromatherapy consultation at lasts approximately 60-75 minutes. This allows ample time for us to discuss your wellness goals, health background, and lifestyle, ensuring that we create the most effective and personalized plan and blend for your unique needs. Follow-up sessions are usually shorter, around 30-45 minutes.
Can Aromatherapy help with specific conditions I might be experiencing?
While aromatherapy is not a substitute for medical treatment, it can be a valuable complementary therapy. Many individuals find it supportive for managing stress, anxiety, improving sleep quality, alleviating mild pain, and boosting overall emotional well-being. During your consultation, we'll explore how aromatherapy can specifically address your concerns and integrate with your current health regimen.
