FAQ — quick safety notes

  • Dilution: Recipes below use approximate drops assuming ~20 drops/ml. Follow the table given for your bottle size.
  • Patch test: Apply 1 drop of diluted blend to inner forearm, wait 24 hours—if irritation appears, discontinue use.
  • Pets: Some oils may affect animals. Keep blends stored safely; avoid direct application near pets' faces. If in doubt, consult a vet.
  • Pregnancy & medical: If pregnant, nursing, or under medical care, seek professional guidance. This guidance is not medical advice and is safety-forward in tone.
