Hi — I guide scent with intention

I blend notes and practices to help you shape moments across the day. My work frames scent as a directional tool — a way to orient attention, create breaks, and signal transitions.

I trained in botanical aromatics and sensory coaching, bringing clinical safety practices together with bespoke ritual design. My offerings are designed to be practical: small aromatic interventions that fit into real lives. They may support calm, focus, or clearer transitions between day parts.

Quick facts

I do not diagnose or treat medical conditions. Language like "may support" is used intentionally. For pregnancy, veterinary concerns, or complex health conditions, I provide conservative guidance and referral suggestions.
Ask a safety question
Do you make medical claims?

No. All language is supportive in tone: blends and routines may support a sense of calm or focus, but are not medical treatments.

Pets and essential oils

Pets can be more sensitive. I provide pet-aware dilution recommendations and alternatives. Consult your veterinarian for specific illnesses.

Pregnancy and nursing

If you are pregnant or nursing, I will default to low or no-essential-oil options and recommend consultation with your healthcare provider.

Patch testing

Always patch test diluted blends before topical use. Apply a small amount on inner forearm and observe for 24 hours.
