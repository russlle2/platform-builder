Planner details & safety

The planner creates a plain-text summary you can copy into an email or include with your booking. It is not medical advice. For pregnancy, nursing, children, or pets we will recommend conservative or alternative scent choices and lower dilutions.

Patch test

We always recommend patch testing any new diluted topical product for 24 hours before regular use.

Labeling and storage

Label blends with contents, dilution, and date. Store out of sunlight and away from heat sources.
