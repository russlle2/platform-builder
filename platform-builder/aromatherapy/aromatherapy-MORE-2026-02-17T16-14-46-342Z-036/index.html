FAQ & Safety

Are these medical therapies?

These practices are non-medical and designed for wellbeing support. Language like "may support" is used intentionally; they are not a diagnosis or medical treatment.

Patch test and topical safety

Always patch test a topical blend before regular use: apply 1 drop of diluted blend to inner forearm and wait 24 hours. Follow dilution guidance provided by the blend builder.

Pets & pregnancy

Certain essential oils can affect pets or be contraindicated during pregnancy. Consult your veterinarian or healthcare provider. If pregnant, breastfeeding, or caring for infants, check guidance before use.
