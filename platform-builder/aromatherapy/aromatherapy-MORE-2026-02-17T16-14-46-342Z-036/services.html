Quick safety notes

  • Start with low dilutions (0.5–2%).
  • Always patch-test on the inner forearm before topical use.
  • Essential oils should be kept away from eyes and mucous membranes.
  • If pregnant or nursing, consult with your health care provider about specific oils.
  • Some oils can be hazardous to pets — review notes before use around animals.
