About the dilution guide

We calculate drops using ~20 drops per mL. The guide will show how many drops of essential oil to add to your chosen bottle size for the dilution you selected. These are conservative numbers meant for safe topical and inhalation use for most adults.

Frequently asked safety questions

Patch test

Apply a small, diluted amount to inner forearm. Wait 24 hours. If irritation occurs, discontinue use.

Pregnancy & nursing

Some oils are not recommended. Please consult your healthcare provider before use.

Pets

Certain oils (e.g., tea tree, citrus in high amounts) can be harmful to small animals. Avoid topical or diffused exposure around sensitive pets without vet advice.
