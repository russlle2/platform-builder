Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is Aromatherapy?
Aromatherapy is a holistic healing modality that uses natural plant extracts, known as essential oils, to promote health and well-being. These oils are extracted from flowers, leaves, bark, and roots, and are used for their therapeutic benefits through inhalation or topical application.
Are essential oils safe to use?
When used correctly and with guidance, essential oils are generally safe. However, they are highly concentrated and should always be diluted before topical application. We prioritize your safety and provide clear instructions on proper usage, including considerations for skin sensitivity, allergies, and specific health conditions.
How can Aromatherapy help me?
Aromatherapy can support various aspects of your well-being. It can help with stress reduction, improving sleep quality, easing discomfort, boosting mood, enhancing focus, and supporting respiratory health. Your personalized blend will be designed to address your unique needs and goals.
What does a consultation entail at ?
During your consultation, will engage in a detailed discussion about your health history, current concerns, and wellness aspirations. Based on this comprehensive understanding, a personalized Aromatherapy plan and custom essential oil blend will be formulated specifically for you, accompanied by usage guidelines.
