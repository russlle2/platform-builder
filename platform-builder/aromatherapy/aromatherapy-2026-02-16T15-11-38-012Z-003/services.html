Frequently asked safety questions

How do you handle dilution?
All topical blends include specific dilution ratios; the practitioner will calculate a safe dilution for adults. For infants, children, pregnancy, or pets, guidance is conservative and restrictive.
Do I need a patch test?
Yes — patch testing is recommended for new topical blends. Instructions will be provided with each session.
What about pets?
We discuss household animals and advise on safe diffusion, ventilation, and placement to minimize exposure.
Is this medical treatment?
No. Sessions offer aromatic support, education, and rituals; they do not diagnose or treat medical conditions.
