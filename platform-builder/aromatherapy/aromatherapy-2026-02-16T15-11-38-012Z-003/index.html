A simple 10-minute scent ritual

A brief, repeatable practice designed to anchor breath and attention. Safe for adult use — follow dilution and patch-test guidance before topical application.

  1. Clean focus: Find a quiet chair and wash your hands.
  2. Choose a blend: Select a 1–2 drop inhalation or a pre-diluted roll-on.
  3. Three breaths: Inhale gently for 3 counts, exhale for 5. Repeat three times.
  4. Reflect: Note any shifts, then record aroma and effect for future sessions.
This description is educational. Aromatherapy complements wellbeing practices but is not a substitute for medical care.

Frequently asked questions

Dilution guidance depends on the oil, user age, and application. Typical adult topical dilutions are 1–2% for short-term use. Your consultation includes specific dilution instructions. Always avoid undiluted application unless directed by a trained clinician.
A patch test reduces risk of skin reaction: apply a small diluted amount to a forearm area, cover, and observe for 24 hours. If redness or irritation occurs, discontinue use and consult your practitioner.
Many oils affect animals differently. We provide pet-safety guidance during consultations. Use low concentrations, ensure ventilation, and keep topical applications away from animal fur and faces.
Pregnancy requires extra caution. Some oils are contraindicated. Consult before using blends; your practitioner will recommend pregnancy-safe options and conservative dilution.
