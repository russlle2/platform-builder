Frequently asked — safety-forward reminders

How should I dilute essential oils?

Dilution depends on the oil and use-case. Typical topical dilutions range from 0.5–5% for adults. We will recommend conservative dilutions and provide clear step-by-step instructions. For children or sensitive skin, even lower dilutions are used. Always consult with your healthcare provider when relevant.

Do I need a patch test?

Yes — we encourage a small patch test before extended topical use. We will describe the simple patch test protocol during your session.

What about pets and aromas?

Many essential oils affect animals differently. We ask about household pets and provide pet-safe alternatives and strategies for diffusing or storing blends to minimize exposure.

Pregnancy & breastfeeding

Certain oils are contraindicated during pregnancy or breastfeeding. We take a conservative approach and will avoid oils that are not considered safe; your safety and that of your infant are prioritized.
