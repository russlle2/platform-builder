Practical safety FAQs

What about dilution and patch testing?
Dilution ratios are provided for each topical blend. A patch test is recommended for new topical products: apply a small amount to the inner arm and observe 24–48 hours.
Are oils safe around pets?
Some essential oils pose risks to animals. We ask about household pets during intake and recommend pet-safe options and application methods.
PREGNANCY & breastfeeding considerations
Certain oils are contraindicated in pregnancy. If you are pregnant or breastfeeding, note this in your booking and we will prepare pregnancy-appropriate alternatives. We do not provide medical advice — consult your healthcare provider for medical decisions.
