Safety notes and FAQ

Dilution: All topical preparations are given with clear dilution ratios. Recommended dilutions vary by age, pregnancy status, and other factors.
Patch test: Always perform a patch test for new topical blends; instructions are provided with products and sessions.
Pets: Some essential oils are not appropriate around animals; we provide pet-friendly guidance tailored to species and household routines.
Pregnancy & breastfeeding: Certain oils are avoided or used only at very low concentrations. Disclose pregnancy or breastfeeding during consultations.
