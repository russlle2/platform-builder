Common questions — prudent safety

How is dilution handled?
Dilution is provided as part of the session. For topical use we provide clear ratios and small-quantity samples. Higher-concentration products are not offered without consultation.
Should I do a patch test?
Yes. A patch test is recommended for topical blends: apply a small amount to the inside forearm and observe 24–48 hours for irritation.
Are blends safe around pets and pregnancy?
Safety notes are discussed during consultation. Some essential oils are contraindicated for pets and pregnancy — we provide alternatives and usage limits. Always consult your healthcare provider for pregnancy-specific guidance.
