Unfurling Your Worries: Trauma-Informed Aromatherapy Care

College life often brings unseen burdens. Our Aromatherapy approach recognizes the deep impact of stress and anxiety, offering a compassionate space where your experiences are honored. We gently co-create blends that speak to your specific needs, helping to quiet the internal storm and foster a sense of security, without ever feeling overwhelmed or rushed. This isn't just about pleasant smells; it's about creating a safe emotional anchor for your busy mind.
