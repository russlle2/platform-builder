Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is Aromatherapy and how can it help me?
Aromatherapy is a holistic healing treatment that uses natural plant extracts to promote health and well-being. It can support you in various ways, from alleviating stress and improving sleep to boosting your mood and supporting respiratory health. Your personalized consultation will determine the best approach for your unique needs.
Are essential oils safe to use, and how will you ensure my safety?
When used correctly, essential oils are generally safe. As a certified aromatherapist, I prioritize your safety. During your consultation, we will discuss your health history, sensitivities, and any medications you are taking to create a blend that is appropriate and safe for your use. I will also provide clear instructions on proper dilution and application.
How long does an Aromatherapy consultation take, and what can I expect?
A typical initial Aromatherapy consultation lasts between 60 to 90 minutes. You can expect a warm, confidential conversation where we explore your health goals, lifestyle, and preferences. This allows me to understand your unique needs and formulate a custom essential oil blend specifically for you, along with guidance on its use.
Can Aromatherapy replace conventional medical treatments?
Aromatherapy is a complementary therapy and is not intended to replace conventional medical treatments or advice. It works wonderfully alongside your existing healthcare regimen to support your overall well-being. Always consult with your doctor for any health concerns and before making changes to your medical care.
