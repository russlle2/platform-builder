What exactly is Aromatherapy?

Aromatherapy is a holistic healing modality that utilizes natural plant extracts, known as essential oils, to promote health and well-being. It harnesses the therapeutic properties of these aromatic compounds to support your physical, mental, and emotional balance.

How can Aromatherapy benefit my specific needs?

Your benefits from Aromatherapy are uniquely tailored to you. During your consultation, we'll discuss your health goals and concerns. We then create a personalized plan and custom essential oil blends designed to address areas like stress reduction, sleep improvement, pain management, or emotional support.

Are essential oils safe to use?

When used correctly and with professional guidance, essential oils are very safe. We prioritize your safety by using only high-quality, pure essential oils and providing clear instructions on proper dilution and application methods for your custom blends. We will discuss any potential sensitivities during your consultation.

What should I expect during an Aromatherapy consultation?

During your consultation, you'll have a warm and open discussion about your health history, current lifestyle, and wellness aspirations. We'll explore your preferences and sensitivities to scents. This comprehensive approach allows us to formulate the most effective and pleasant Aromatherapy protocol specifically for you.
