Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is clinical Aromatherapy?
Clinical Aromatherapy is the therapeutic application of essential oils for holistic health. It involves a detailed assessment of your individual needs, followed by the careful selection and blending of essential oils to support your physical, mental, and emotional well-being. It's a personalized approach, ensuring safety and efficacy.
How can Aromatherapy benefit my daily life?
Aromatherapy can offer numerous benefits, from reducing feelings of stress and anxiety to promoting more restful sleep, boosting your mood, and supporting respiratory health. By incorporating custom blends into your routine, you can create a more balanced and supportive environment for your overall well-being and enhance your daily rituals.
Are the essential oils you use safe for everyone?
Safety is paramount in Aromatherapy. We exclusively use high-quality, ethically sourced essential oils and conduct thorough consultations to understand your health history, sensitivities, and any medications you may be taking. This allows us to formulate blends that are appropriate and safe for your unique circumstances, always prioritizing your well-being.
What should I expect during an initial Aromatherapy consultation?
During your initial consultation, we'll have an in-depth conversation about your health goals, lifestyle, and any concerns you may have. We'll explore your preferences for scents and discuss how essential oils can best support you. This collaborative process allows us to create a tailored Aromatherapy plan and custom blend that aligns perfectly with your individual needs.
