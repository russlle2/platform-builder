What exactly is Aromatherapy?

Aromatherapy is a holistic healing modality that uses natural plant extracts, known as essential oils, to promote health and well-being. It leverages the aromatic compounds of plants to support physical, emotional, and spiritual balance within your body and mind.

How can personalized Aromatherapy benefit me?

Personalized Aromatherapy focuses on your unique needs and goals. Instead of generic solutions, your custom blends are formulated to address your specific concerns, whether it's stress reduction, improved sleep, mood enhancement, or physical comfort, leading to more effective and profound results for you.

Are the essential oils used in Aromatherapy safe?

Yes, when used correctly and sourced responsibly. We prioritize the use of high-quality, pure essential oils from reputable suppliers. During your consultation, we discuss proper dilution, application methods, and any potential sensitivities to ensure your Aromatherapy experience is both safe and beneficial for you.

What should I expect during an Aromatherapy consultation?

Your consultation is a conversation about your current well-being, lifestyle, and any health goals. Dr. Morgan Ellis will listen attentively to your needs, answer your questions, and then propose an Aromatherapy strategy and custom blend recommendations tailored specifically for you.
