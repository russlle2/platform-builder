Frequently Asked Questions

How does Aromatherapy specifically aid an athlete's physical stress management?
Aromatherapy, through specific volatile compounds, can directly influence the limbic system, impacting your body's stress hormones and autonomic nervous system. For athletes, this means a scientifically supported method to reduce cortisol, mitigate inflammation, and accelerate the shift from 'fight or flight' to 'rest and digest,' crucial for effective recovery and preventing overtraining.
Can Aromatherapy really help with sleep quality for active individuals?
Absolutely. Quality sleep is paramount for athletic recovery. Certain aromatic compounds are known to promote alpha brain wave activity and increase neurotransmitters associated with relaxation, like GABA. Lora Aromatherapy focuses on blends that encourage deeper, more restorative sleep cycles, helping your body repair and consolidate memories from training, without relying on sedatives.
Is Aromatherapy a proven method for nervous system regulation?
Yes, a growing body of research supports the physiological effects of aromatherapy on the nervous system. Studies indicate that specific essential oils can modulate heart rate variability, blood pressure, and brain wave patterns, all markers of nervous system activity. Lora utilizes this evidence-based understanding to create targeted interventions for stress reduction and optimal physiological balance.
How will Lora Aromatherapy integrate with my existing training and recovery protocols?
Lora's approach is designed to complement your current regimen. We focus on non-invasive, accessible applications that fit seamlessly into your day – before training, during cool-downs, or as part of your evening wind-down. Our goal is to enhance your existing efforts by providing a powerful, natural tool for nervous system support, optimizing your body's innate ability to adapt and perform.
