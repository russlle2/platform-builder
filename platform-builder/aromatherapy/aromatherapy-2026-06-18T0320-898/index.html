Frequently Asked Questions

How does Aromatherapy specifically help maintain mental clarity as you age?
Aromatherapy uses specific essential oils known for their neuro-supportive properties. We'll select blends that can gently stimulate cognitive function, improve focus, and reduce feelings of mental fogginess, helping you feel more alert and engaged in your daily activities.
Can Aromatherapy really support my physical vitality through different seasons?
Absolutely. Certain essential oils have properties that can support your immune system, soothe tired muscles, and promote better sleep, which are all crucial for maintaining physical vitality. We tailor seasonal blends to address the unique challenges and opportunities each time of year brings for your body.
Is Aromatherapy safe to use if I'm already taking other medications?
Your safety is our top priority. We always conduct a thorough intake to understand your health history and any medications you're taking. We'll choose essential oils that are safe and complementary to your current health regimen, and we'll advise on proper usage to ensure your well-being.
What can I expect during my first Aromatherapy consultation with ?
During your first visit, we'll have a warm, unhurried conversation about your current well-being, your lifestyle, and your goals for maintaining vitality and mental clarity. We'll then guide you through selecting aromas and discuss practical ways to incorporate personalized Aromatherapy into your daily routine at home, creating a plan that feels right for you.
