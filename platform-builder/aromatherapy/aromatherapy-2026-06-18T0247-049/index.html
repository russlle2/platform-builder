Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is Aromatherapy and how can it help me?
Aromatherapy is a holistic healing modality that uses natural plant extracts, known as essential oils, to promote health and well-being. It can help you by supporting emotional balance, reducing stress, improving sleep quality, easing discomfort, and enhancing overall vitality, tailored to your specific needs.
Are essential oils safe to use, and how do you ensure my safety?
When used correctly, essential oils are very safe. Your safety is our utmost priority. We conduct a thorough intake to understand your health history and sensitivities. We then formulate blends with appropriate dilutions and provide clear instructions for safe application, ensuring a positive and beneficial experience for you.
What can I expect during my first Aromatherapy consultation?
During your initial consultation, we'll discuss your health goals, lifestyle, and any concerns you may have. This allows us to understand your unique needs. We'll then explain how specific essential oils can support you and begin to formulate a personalized aromatic blend and usage plan tailored just for you.
How long does it take to see results from Aromatherapy?
The time it takes to experience results from aromatherapy can vary based on your individual constitution and the concerns you are addressing. Many clients report feeling shifts within a few days or weeks of consistent use, especially with emotional and stress-related issues. We focus on sustainable, long-term well-being for you.
