Frequently Asked Questions

How does Aromatherapy specifically address stress from chronic illness or injury?
Our approach is not generalized. We focus on specific botanical compounds known for their neuro-modulating properties, carefully selected to soothe an overstimulated nervous system. Your bespoke Aromatherapy session targets the physiological markers of stress, guiding your body towards a parasympathetic dominance essential for true recovery.
Will Aromatherapy interfere with my ongoing medical treatments or medications?
Your safety and well-being are paramount. We conduct a thorough intake to understand your complete health profile, including all current treatments and medications. We then formulate your Aromatherapy protocol with meticulous care, ensuring compatibility and synergy, and we encourage you to discuss any concerns with your primary healthcare provider.
What sensations can I expect during an Aromatherapy session for nervous system regulation?
You can anticipate a profound sense of quietude and gentle release. Many clients report a noticeable shift in their breathing, a softening of muscle tension, and a mental clarity that replaces persistent worry. The experience is designed to be deeply calming, facilitating a tangible reduction in internal distress signals.
How quickly can I expect to feel the benefits of Aromatherapy for stress reduction?
While individual experiences vary, many clients report an immediate sense of relief and calm during their first Aromatherapy session. Consistent engagement with your personalized botanical regimen amplifies these effects, building sustained resilience and helping your nervous system maintain a regulated state over time.
