Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly will I notice benefits?
Most clients feel subtle shifts within 2 sessions, with deeper effects by week 4 as blends align with your circadian rhythm. We track progress through tailored check-ins, not guesswork.
Are your blends safe with medications?
Our plant-based approach minimizes interaction risks compared to synthetic alternatives. Always consult your physician first, but our blends avoid common pharmaceutical conflict points like citrus oils with blood thinners.
What's the custom blend process?
Three steps: 1) Personalized consultation, 2) Initial blend testing, 3) Refinement based on your feedback. We never rush—your comfort comes before convenience.
How subtle are the scents?
Our blends are designed to be nuanced, not overwhelming. Think of a forest breeze rather than perfume—supportive in background, never in your face.
