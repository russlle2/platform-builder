FAQ snapshot

Do you make medical claims? No. Aromatherapy may support subjective well-being but is not a medical treatment.

What about pets and pregnancy? We screen for these, avoid certain oils around cats, and provide safe alternatives. Always consult a provider when pregnant.

How do you approach dilution? We provide clear dilution ratios and a patch-test protocol for every blend.
