Frequently asked

Is aromatherapy safe?

When used with appropriate dilution and care, essential oils can be integrated safely into daily routines. We include explicit dilution guidance and recommend a patch test before topical use. Avoid undiluted skin application.

What about pets?

Certain essential oils can be hazardous to pets. If you have animals in the home, we'll choose pet-safer options and delivery methods. Always ventilate spaces and avoid direct exposure to fur and faces.

Pregnancy and breastfeeding

Some oils are not recommended during pregnancy or breastfeeding. Please disclose pregnancy or nursing during intake so we can provide safe alternatives and modify dilution guidelines.

Patch tests and sensitivity

We provide a simple patch-test protocol with every plan. If you have known sensitivities, we select lower concentrations and perform incremental introductions.
