Frequently asked — safety, pets, pregnancy

Is aromatherapy safe?
We prioritize safety. Recommendations will include dilution rates and patch-test steps. Statements are not medical advice; aromatherapy may support comfort but is not a substitute for medical care.
What about pets?
Some essential oils can affect animals. We will provide pet-safe alternatives and room-specific guidance if you have pets at home.
Pregnancy and breastfeeding
Certain oils are not recommended in pregnancy or breastfeeding. Disclose pregnancy or lactation at intake so we can adapt plans and blends.
Patch test & dilution
Every topical suggestion includes dilution percentages and a simple patch-test protocol before wider use.
