Frequently asked — safety and logistics

Do you make medical claims?
We do not make medical claims. Suggestions and practices we provide may support wellbeing in non-medical ways.
How do I dilute essential oils?
We include clear dilution guidance in every plan (e.g., 1–3% for topical blends for adults). We recommend testing small amounts and following patch-test instructions we provide.
What about pets?
Some oils can affect animals. We always ask about pets and include pet-safe usage notes and ventilation recommendations if needed.
Pregnancy and sensitive conditions
If you are pregnant, nursing, or have a serious medical condition, please note that in your intake. We will avoid contraindicated materials and include cautious alternatives; always confirm with your care provider.
Do I need a patch test?
Yes — we include a clear patch-test protocol in plans: small dilution on the inner forearm, wait 24 hours, discontinue if irritation occurs.
