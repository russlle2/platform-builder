What exactly is Aromatherapy and how can it benefit me?

Aromatherapy is a holistic healing modality that uses natural plant extracts, known as essential oils, to promote health and well-being. It can benefit you by improving mood, reducing stress, alleviating discomfort, enhancing sleep quality, and supporting your overall emotional and physical balance through various application methods.

Are the essential oils you use safe and high quality?

Absolutely. Your safety and well-being are our top priority. We source only 100% pure, therapeutic-grade essential oils from reputable suppliers who adhere to strict quality control standards. We discuss proper dilution and application methods extensively during your consultation to ensure safe and effective use.

Do I need any prior knowledge of essential oils before a consultation?

Not at all. You don't need any prior knowledge. Our consultations are designed to be informative and accessible for everyone, from beginners to those already familiar with essential oils. Dr. Morgan Ellis will gently guide you through the process, explaining everything you need to know in a clear and understandable way.

What can I expect during my first Aromatherapy consultation?

During your first consultation, you can expect a warm and confidential discussion about your health history, current well-being goals, and any concerns you may have. Dr. Morgan Ellis will then create a personalized Aromatherapy plan and custom essential oil blend tailored specifically for your needs, providing clear instructions for use and ongoing support.
