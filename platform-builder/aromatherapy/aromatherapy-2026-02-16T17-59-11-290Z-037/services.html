FAQ — safety & basics

How do you handle dilution?
Every blend includes a recommended dilution ratio and usage instructions. For topical use, I always advise conservative dilutions and labeled bottles.
What is a patch test?
A patch test is a small application of diluted oil behind the ear or on the inner forearm to check for sensitivity before regular use.
Are blends safe around pets?
Some essential oils can be irritating to animals. We custom-design blends with household pets in mind and recommend diffusing with ventilation and supervision.
Can pregnant or nursing people use these blends?
Pregnancy and breastfeeding require extra caution. I always recommend consulting your healthcare provider; we will avoid certain oils and tailor safer options.
