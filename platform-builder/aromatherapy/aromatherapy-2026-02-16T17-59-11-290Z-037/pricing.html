FAQ — safety essentials
What dilution should I use?
Dilution depends on age, skin sensitivity, and intended use. Typical topical dilutions range from 0.5% (sensitive) to 5% (adult spot treatments). Your session includes a tailored dilution guide.
How do I do a patch test?
Apply a small diluted amount to the inner forearm, cover for 24 hours, and watch for redness or irritation. If any reaction occurs, discontinue use and consult the session notes.
Are essential oils safe around pets?
Some oils can affect pets. We take pet-safety seriously: we'll review any animals in your household and tailor blends and usage recommendations accordingly.
What about pregnancy?
Pregnancy requires special precautions. If you are pregnant or breastfeeding we will adapt recommendations and avoid oils known to be contraindicated. No medical advice is given; we focus on safety and referral when needed.
