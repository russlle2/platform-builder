Safety-first FAQ
How is dilation explained?
Dilution is provided as a percentage with examples (eg 1% = 6 drops in 30ml carrier). We always favor conservative dilutions unless otherwise agreed.
Do you recommend patch tests?
Yes — every topical recommendation includes a patch test protocol and an alternative if you have sensitivities.
Will you consider my pet(s)?
Yes. We ask about household animals and adapt blends and usage to reduce risk. We avoid strong, persistent diffusion when pets are present.
Pregnancy & essential oils
Pregnancy involves modifications. We avoid oils commonly recommended against in pregnancy and provide clear, conservative options. This is safety guidance, not medical advice.
