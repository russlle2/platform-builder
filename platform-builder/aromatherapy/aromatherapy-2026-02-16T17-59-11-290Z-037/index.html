Common questions (safety & use)

How do I dilute essential oils for safe home use?

We recommend dilution guidelines by experience and age. Typical adult topical dilutions are 1–3% for daily use. During a session you'll receive exact drops-to-ml ratios tailored to your blend. Never apply undiluted essential oil to skin.

What is a patch test and why do I need one?

A patch test checks for skin sensitivity. Apply a diluted drop to the inner forearm, cover, and wait 24 hours. If irritation occurs, stop use and consult your practitioner about alternatives.

Are essential oils safe around pets?

Many oils are irritating to animals. We give pet-safe alternatives and diffuser placement guidance. Always consult with your vet if your pet has health issues; never force an animal to inhale an oil.

Can pregnant or nursing people use aromatherapy?

We take a conservative approach: certain oils are avoided during pregnancy and nursing. You will receive pregnancy-safe recommendations and options during intake.
