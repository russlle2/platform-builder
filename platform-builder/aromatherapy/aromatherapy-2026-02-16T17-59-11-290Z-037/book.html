Quick safety Qs
Dilution guidance?
You will receive a specific dilution plan. Typical topicals are 0.5–5% depending on use and sensitivity.
Patch test instructions
Apply a small diluted sample to the inner forearm and observe for 24 hours. If irritation occurs, discontinue and contact us.
Pets & essential oils
Tell us about any pets. We'll avoid or adapt diffusing recommendations when animals are present and select pet-safer options.
Pregnancy considerations
Pregnancy requires conservative selections. We will avoid oils typically contraindicated and prioritize neutral, gentle options.
Before your session
  • Complete intake above with any health or pet notes.
  • Avoid strong perfumes on the day.
  • Have a list of current medications and supplements ready.
