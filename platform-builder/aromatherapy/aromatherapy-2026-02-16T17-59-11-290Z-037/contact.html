FAQ — practical safety

Dilution: how to use essential oils safely?
Dilution depends on use case. For topical daily blends we commonly recommend 1–2% for adults; lower for facial use and lower still for children. Your VIP Day includes exact dilution guidance tailored to your blends.
Patch test — what and why?
A patch test checks for skin sensitivity. Apply a diluted drop to a small skin area, wait 24–48 hours, and look for redness or irritation. We walk you through this during the session.
Pets — are essential oils safe around animals?
Many oils concentrate strongly and can affect pets; we review pet-safe choices and diffusion practices, and include specific recommendations if you have cats or small animals.
Pregnancy & nursing considerations?
Some oils are not recommended during pregnancy. We'll review current safety guidance and develop alternatives tailored to comfort and safety. This is not medical advice; always consult a medical provider for clinical concerns.
