Practical FAQ — blending safety

Dilution: Every blend has recommended dilution ranges. For adult topical use, we typically suggest conservative dilutions (1–2% for daily use) unless otherwise advised.
Patch test: Before using a new topical blend, apply a small amount of diluted oil to an inner forearm and wait 24 hours to check for sensitivity.
Pets: Not all oils are pet-safe. If you share your home with animals, we will create blends with that in mind and recommend cautious diffusion practices.
Pregnancy & nursing: Some oils are not recommended. If pregnant or breastfeeding, please mention this during consults so blends can be tailored to safer alternatives.
Note: These blends are not medical treatments. They are sensory tools and lifestyle supports; consult healthcare professionals for medical concerns.
