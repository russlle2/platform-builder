Frequently Asked Questions

How can Aromatherapy specifically help my sleep during significant life changes?
When you're navigating major life transitions, your sleep often bears the brunt of emotional and mental shifts. Our Aromatherapy uses carefully selected essential oils known for their calming and sedative properties, helping to quiet your overactive mind, ease anxiety, and signal to your body that it's safe to enter a deep, restorative sleep state, even amidst upheaval.
Will the Aromatherapy blends be tailored to my unique sleep challenges?
Absolutely. Your sleep experience during a life transition is deeply personal. We conduct a thorough consultation to understand your specific disruptions, emotional landscape, and preferences. From this, we custom-blend essential oils to create an Aromatherapy protocol uniquely designed to address your individual needs for more consistent and restorative rest.
What does 'restorative rest' truly mean in the context of Aromatherapy?
Restorative rest, for you, means sleep that genuinely rejuvenates your mind and body, allowing you to wake feeling refreshed and prepared for the day's demands, especially during a transition. Our Aromatherapy aims to optimize both the duration and quality of your sleep, helping you achieve deeper sleep cycles that are crucial for emotional processing, physical repair, and mental clarity.
How quickly can I expect to see improvements in my sleep with Aromatherapy?
While individual experiences vary, many clients report a noticeable difference in their ability to relax and fall asleep more easily within the first few nights of consistently using their personalized Aromatherapy blends. Sustained, deeper restorative sleep often develops over a few weeks as your body and mind adapt to the new sleep-supportive routine you establish.
