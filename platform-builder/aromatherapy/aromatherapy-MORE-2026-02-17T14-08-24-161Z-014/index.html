Frequently asked safety questions

How should I dilute essential oils?

We recommend starting at conservative dilutions: 0.5–2% for topical blends depending on area and sensitivity. For household diffusing, follow short session durations and ventilate the room.

What is a patch test?

A patch test means applying one diluted drop to a small area of skin and observing 24 hours for irritation. If you notice redness or discomfort, discontinue use.

Are essential oils safe around pets?

Some oils may affect pets. Keep blends and diffusing periods limited, avoid direct contact with animals, and share pet details during intake so we can adapt recommendations.

What about pregnancy or nursing?

Pregnancy and breastfeeding involve unique considerations. Always disclose pregnancy/nursing during intake so suggestions emphasize safety and avoid certain botanicals.

Do these practices treat medical conditions?

No. These are supportive scent practices that may support well-being. They are not a substitute for medical care. Always consult your healthcare provider for medical concerns.

Ready to begin a steady scent practice?

Membership welcomes newcomers and experienced users. We emphasize safety, clear dilution, and practical blends you can actually use.
