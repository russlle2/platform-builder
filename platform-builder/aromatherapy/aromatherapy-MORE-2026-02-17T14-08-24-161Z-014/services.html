FAQ & Safety

Do you make medical claims?

No. Aromatherapy offered here is supportive and may support wellbeing. It is not a medical treatment.

Dilution & patch tests

Topical products are prepared at conservative dilutions. We recommend a patch test for new blends.

Pets & pregnancy

Tell us about household pets and pregnancy status during intake so we can adapt or avoid specific oils.
