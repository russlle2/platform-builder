Common questions

Are your blends safe?

Blends are prepared with safety-forward dilution guidance. Topical suggestions will include a conservative dilution (often 1–3%) and a patch test is recommended before regular use.

What about pets and pregnancy?

We adapt suggestions when pets live in the home and avoid certain oils during pregnancy or breastfeeding. Please disclose these in your booking so we plan safely.

Do you make claims?

We do not make medical claims. Our practices and blends may support relaxation, focus, or a sense of ease; they are not treatments.
