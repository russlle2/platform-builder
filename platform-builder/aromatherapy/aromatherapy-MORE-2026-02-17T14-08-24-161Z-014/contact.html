FAQs — Safety-forward notes

How do you handle dilution and topical use?
Recommendations are conservative: adult topical dilutions commonly start around 1–2% for short-term use unless otherwise directed. Specific dilutions are determined during intake. Always perform a 24-hour patch test before broader topical use.
Are essential oils safe around pets?
Some volatile oils can affect animals differently. We ask about pets in the intake and provide alternative suggestions. Diffusion should be limited in time and space, and animals should have access to leave the area.
Pregnancy and nursing
If you are pregnant, trying to conceive, or breastfeeding, disclose this on intake. Some oils are avoided in pregnancy. We use evidence-aware guidance and will adapt recommendations accordingly.
Do you make medical claims?
No medical claims are made. Aromatic practices may support feelings of calm, focus, or comfort; they are not a substitute for medical advice or treatment.
