How can Aromatherapy specifically help with my posture and body awareness as a senior?

Aromatherapy uses specific plant essences that can gently relax muscles, calm your nervous system, and improve your focus. This allows you to become more attuned to how your body is positioned and held, making it easier to make subtle adjustments for better posture and greater comfort. It's about a gentle, sensory approach to understanding your body.

Are the essential oils used safe for seniors, especially if I have sensitive skin or other health considerations?

Yes, your safety and comfort are paramount. We use only high-quality, pure essential oils and always dilute them appropriately. Before any application, we will discuss your health history, any sensitivities you may have, and current medications to ensure the blends are perfectly suited and safe for your unique needs. Your well-being is our priority.

What does a typical Aromatherapy session for postural support look like?

A session begins with a conversation about your current comfort levels and goals. Then, we select and blend specific aromas together. You'll experience the oils through gentle inhalation or light, targeted application (with your permission). The focus is on encouraging relaxation and awareness, guiding you to notice your body's alignment and inherent wisdom in a calm, supportive environment.

Will Aromatherapy replace my existing physical therapy or medical treatments for posture?

Aromatherapy is a complementary practice, designed to work alongside and enhance your current health and wellness routines. It is not intended to replace medical advice or treatments from your doctor or physical therapist. We encourage you to discuss any new complementary therapies with your healthcare providers to ensure a holistic approach to your well-being.
