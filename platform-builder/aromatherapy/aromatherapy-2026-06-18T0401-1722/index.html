Frequently Asked Questions

How can Aromatherapy specifically address the physical stress I experience as an athlete?
Aromatherapy utilizes essential oils known for their anti-inflammatory, analgesic, and muscle-relaxing properties. We create specific blends that target areas of physical strain, helping to alleviate discomfort, reduce swelling, and promote faster recovery, allowing you to maintain a healthy relationship with your active body.
Beyond physical recovery, how does Aromatherapy at support my emotional well-being?
Our heart-centered approach acknowledges the emotional toll of intense physical activity. Aromatherapy can help balance your mood, reduce performance anxiety, improve sleep quality, and foster a sense of inner calm, strengthening your emotional resilience and your connection to your purpose as an athlete.
What should I expect during my first Aromatherapy session with ?
Your first session will involve a compassionate conversation with about your athletic pursuits, physical discomforts, and emotional needs. This allows us to understand your unique story. From there, we'll introduce you to specific essential oils and discuss how they can be blended to create a personalized Aromatherapy ritual that supports your complete well-being.
Is Aromatherapy compatible with my existing training and recovery routines?
Absolutely. Aromatherapy at is designed to complement your current routines, not replace them. We can integrate essential oil applications into your pre-workout warm-ups, post-workout recovery, or evening wind-down rituals, enhancing your existing self-care practices and deepening your relationship with your body's recovery process.
