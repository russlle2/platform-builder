Frequently asked: safety & practice

How do I dilute essential oils safely?
Dilution guidelines are provided with every blend. Typical topical dilutions are 1–3% for adults unless otherwise advised. Children, older adults, and people with sensitive skin require lower dilutions. Patch testing is recommended.
What is a patch test?
Apply a small amount of diluted blend to the inner forearm, cover with a bandage, and observe for 24 hours. If redness, irritation, or discomfort occurs, discontinue use.
Are essential oils safe around pets?
Some oils can be harmful to animals. Avoid diffusing or applying oils in shared spaces without confirming pet-safe choices. Always consult qualified veterinary guidance for specific concerns.
Pregnancy & nursing guidance
Some oils are contraindicated during pregnancy or breastfeeding. Tell us if you are pregnant, breastfeeding, or planning pregnancy so we can select appropriate, safety-first options.
