Safety notes & FAQs

Dilution and topical safety
We use conservative dilution recommendations in-session and on product labels (commonly 1–2% for adults). Never use undiluted essential oils on skin. Seek practitioner guidance for special cases.
Patch test protocol
Apply a small drop of diluted blend to the inner forearm, cover for 24 hours if possible, and monitor for redness, itching, or irritation before broader topical use.
Pets & essential oils
Many oils can be harmful to pets. Keep diffusers in well-ventilated areas and avoid prolonged exposure. Inform us of household animals for tailored guidance.
Pregnancy, nursing, and children
Some oils are avoided during pregnancy or with infants. Notify us at booking; we will adapt blends or recommend alternatives to keep your practice safety-first.
