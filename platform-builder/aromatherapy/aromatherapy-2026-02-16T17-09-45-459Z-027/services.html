Frequently asked — safety first

How do you dilute essential oils?
We recommend conservative dilutions: typically 1–2% for adults for short-term topical use. We never advise home use at undiluted strengths. Specific dilution guidance provided in-session and on product labels.
What is a patch test?
A patch test is a small topical test on the inner forearm using diluted oil to check for skin sensitivity prior to broader topical use.
Are essential oils safe around pets?
Many oils can be irritating to animals. We provide guidance on pet-safe choices, ventilation, and avoidance strategies. If you have pets, tell us at booking.
What about pregnancy or nursing?
Certain oils are avoided in pregnancy and nursing. If you are pregnant, nursing, or seeking fertility support, please inform us before booking. We will adapt or decline topical/aromatherapy approaches as needed to prioritize safety.
