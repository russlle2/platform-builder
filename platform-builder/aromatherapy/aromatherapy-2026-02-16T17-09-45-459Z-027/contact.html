What should I expect after reaching out?

We aim to respond within 48 hours. Initial replies cover availability, a short intake about sensitivities or allergies, and safe options for retail or a consult. Follow-up messages will include next steps and any preparation notes.

Do you offer in-person visits?

Yes — in-person sessions are available by appointment. Virtual consultations are also supported for custom blends and product guidance. Travel details are discussed when booking.

Dilution & safe use

We always recommend proper dilution for topical application and clear labeling for products. Typical dilution ranges are discussed during consultations; please share any skin sensitivities.

Patch test & skin safety

Patch testing is encouraged for new topical blends. Apply a small amount to the forearm and observe for 24 hours. If irritation occurs, discontinue use and contact us.

Pets & essential oils

Essential oils can affect animals differently. We provide pet-safe guidance and alternatives; never apply oils directly to an animal unless advised by a qualified veterinary professional.

Pregnancy & lactation

Pregnancy and breastfeeding require extra caution. We provide evidence-informed suggestions and will tailor recommendations; always consult your healthcare provider if you have concerns.

Payment & retail
