Frequently Asked Questions

We recommend safe dilution guidelines: common adult topical dilutions are 1–3% for regular use (6–18 drops per 30ml/1oz carrier). For sensitive skin, use lower dilutions. You will always receive clear dilution instructions with retail blends.

A patch test checks for skin sensitivity: apply a diluted drop to a small area (inner forearm), wait 24 hours, and observe for irritation. We do patch tests for topical blends to keep you safe.

Some oils can affect animals. We avoid recommending topical or diffused use around pets without a careful, species‑specific review. Always inform us about pets during intake so we can offer pet‑safe alternatives.

Pregnancy calls for extra caution. Some oils are not recommended in pregnancy. We provide pregnancy‑appropriate guidance and will avoid contraindicated oils. Consultation with your healthcare provider is advised for medical questions.
