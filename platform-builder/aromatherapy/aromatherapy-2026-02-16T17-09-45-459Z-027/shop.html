Shop FAQs — safety and guidance

How should I dilute products for topical use?
Many shop products are sold pre-diluted. For concentrated oils, follow the included dilution card (commonly 1–2% for adults). If in doubt, consult with {{PRACTITIONER_NAME}}.
What is a patch test?
Apply a small amount of diluted product to the inner forearm and wait up to 24 hours for reaction. Discontinue if irritation occurs.
Are these products pet-safe?
Not necessarily. Check blend notes and avoid diffusing or applying products where pets cannot move away. Contact us for pet-safe recommendations.
Pregnancy & special populations
If pregnant, nursing, or buying for children, consult with your healthcare provider and notify us prior to purchase. Some oils are contraindicated during pregnancy.
