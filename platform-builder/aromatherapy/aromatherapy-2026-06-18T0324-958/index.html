Frequently Asked Questions

How can Aromatherapy specifically help me in my senior years?
Aromatherapy for seniors focuses on gentle support for age-related changes, like promoting restful sleep, easing occasional discomfort, or uplifting your mood. We select essential oils known for their mild, supportive properties, ensuring your experience is safe and beneficial as you navigate different seasons of life.
Is Aromatherapy safe if I'm taking other medications?
Absolutely. Your safety is our top priority. We conduct a thorough intake to understand your current health and any medications you're taking. We then carefully select essential oils and application methods that are known to be safe and complementary, avoiding any potential interactions. We always recommend discussing your interest in Aromatherapy with your primary care physician as well.
What does a typical Aromatherapy session involve for seasonal wellness?
A session begins with a conversation about your current seasonal experiences and any specific areas where you'd like support. Based on this, we might create a custom inhaler blend for energy during short winter days, or a gentle roll-on for comfort during humid summer evenings. You'll leave with clear instructions and an understanding of how to integrate these simple practices into your daily routine for ongoing benefit.
How quickly will I notice benefits from Aromatherapy for preventive care?
Many clients report feeling a sense of calm or upliftment shortly after incorporating Aromatherapy. For more sustained preventive benefits, like improved sleep or resilience during seasonal transitions, consistency is key. We'll guide you on a simple, enjoyable routine, and you'll likely notice gradual, positive shifts in your overall well-being as you continue your practice.
