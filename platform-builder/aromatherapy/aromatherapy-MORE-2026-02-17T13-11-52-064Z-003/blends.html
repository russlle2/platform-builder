FAQ

Safety and common questions

  • How to perform a patch test: dilute the blend and place a drop on inner forearm; monitor 24 hours.
  • Pregnancy and nursing: some oils are contraindicated. Seek specialized advice before using.
  • Pets: many essential oils affect animals; use diffusers with caution and consult your vet.
