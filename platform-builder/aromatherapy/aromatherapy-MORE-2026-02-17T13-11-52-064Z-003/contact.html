Safety notes & FAQ

  • Dilution: suggested ranges are listed with the Blend Builder. These are conservative, approximate guides for adults and intended for non-medical aromatic support.
  • Patch test: always patch test a blend on a small area of skin and wait 24 hours before broader use.
  • Pregnancy & nursing: some essential oils are best avoided or used at reduced dilutions. Please mention pregnancy when booking; we will recommend pregnancy-safe options and suggest you consult your healthcare provider.
  • Pets: many essential oils are not safe for companion animals. Keep blends out of reach, avoid direct application to pets, and let us help choose pet-safe approaches.
  • Children: use much lower dilutions for children and consult a pediatric provider if unsure.
