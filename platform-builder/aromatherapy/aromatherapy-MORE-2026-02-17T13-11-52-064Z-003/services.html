Micro practices

Small repeated gestures that fit into daily life — scent paired with an action. Try one for 7 days and journal the effect.

  • Three-breath ritual: inhale chosen blend for two slow breaths before sleep.
  • Focus anchor: scent on a cloth beside workspace for 10 minutes of focused work.
  • Reset pause: a single drop on wrists then a minute of eyes-closed breathing.

FAQ & safety notes

Safety-forward guidance:

  • Always dilute essential oils; never apply undiluted to skin.
  • Perform a patch test: dilute the blend and apply a small spot on the inner forearm; wait 24 hours.
  • If pregnant, nursing, or managing health conditions, consult a qualified provider before use.
  • Keep essential oils away from eyes, mucous membranes, and reach of children and pets.
