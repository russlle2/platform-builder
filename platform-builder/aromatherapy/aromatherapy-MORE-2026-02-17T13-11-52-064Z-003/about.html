FAQ

What about dilution?

Dilution depends on age, sensitivity, and intended use. Typical adult topical dilutions are 1–2% for daily use; lower (0.5%) for sensitivity and pregnancy. For small bottles: 1% of 30 ml ≈ 6 drops total. We always recommend starting low.

Do I need a patch test?

Yes. Apply a small diluted amount to an inner forearm, wait 24 hours. If irritation occurs, discontinue. This is not a medical test but a simple safety step.

Pets and essential oils?

Many essential oils affect animals differently. Use diffusers in well-ventilated areas, allow animals to leave the room, and avoid direct application on pets. For specific animals and oils consult a veterinarian experienced with aromatherapy.

Pregnancy and medications

Some oils are not recommended in pregnancy or with certain medications. Please speak with your healthcare provider before using new aromatic preparations. Our guidance is informational, not medical.

A note on language

I avoid promises. Words like "may support" and "suggest" reflect the subtle and individual nature of aromatic work.
