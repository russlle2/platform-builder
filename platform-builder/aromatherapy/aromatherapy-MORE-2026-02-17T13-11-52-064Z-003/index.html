Safety note: Aromatherapy may support wellbeing but is not a medical treatment. Always patch test diluted blends on inner forearm 24 hours before wider use; consult a professional for pregnancy, nursing, or severe medical conditions. Keep essential oils away from pets and young children.

      

    


    

      

        
Micro-rituals

        
Tiny practices that accumulate: carry a roll-on, scent a pillowcase, or add a drop to a diffuser pad.

        
    
          
  • Two-breath reset
    A single inhale from a personal inhaler between meetings.
    • 
          
  • Pre-sleep mist
    A light spritz to the top of your pillowcase (avoid direct skin contact).
    • 
          
  • Focus strip
    A small cloth with a steady citrus-wood blend kept at your desk.
