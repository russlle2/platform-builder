Safety & Responsible Use

All blends are offered with suggested dilution ranges and patch test guidance. Please note: essential oils are potent. Avoid undiluted skin application, keep blends away from young children and eyes, and use caution around pets. If you are pregnant, nursing, or under medical care, discuss any new aromatic practice with your healthcare provider before use.

Frequently asked questions

Dilution: how should I dilute a blend?

Typical dilution recommendations are 1–2% for general topical use (about 6–12 drops of essential oil per 30ml carrier). For sensitive skin or older adults, start at 0.5–1%. I provide exact dilution guidance with every custom blend.

Patch testing: do I need one?

Yes — always patch test a new blend. Apply a small amount of diluted blend to the inner forearm, wait 24 hours, and watch for irritation. Stop use if redness or discomfort appears.

Pets: are these blends safe around animals?

Some oils can be irritating to pets. Avoid direct application to animals, and use diffusers cautiously with good ventilation. Tell me about your pets during consults so I can adapt recommendations.

Pregnancy & nursing notes

Certain essential oils are not recommended during pregnancy. If you are pregnant or breastfeeding, we will choose pregnancy-safe options and conservative dilutions. Consultation with your healthcare provider is encouraged.
