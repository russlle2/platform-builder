My story

My path to aromatherapy began as a curiosity about scent and ritual, and matured into formal study and hours of careful practice. I became a practitioner to help people bring intention and sensory kindness into daily life. I do not offer medical treatment; my work focuses on wellbeing, rituals, and how scent can gently support routines.

Training: clinical aromatherapy modules, dilution and safety coursework, and supervised client hours. I prioritize informed consent, clear dilution guidance, and attention to contraindications such as pregnancy and pet safety.

Practice framework

  1. Careful intake — questions about history, environment, pets, and preferences.
  2. Safety review — dilution tables, patch testing, and documented contraindications.
  3. Blend crafting — top/middle/base notes with aromatic personality and use suggestions.
  4. Follow-up — evaluate how the blend fits into daily life and adjust as needed.

I keep records and offer simple, printable blend cards so you can use aromatherapy safely at home.

Signature offerings

  • Intake & personalized blend creation
  • Roller blends & inhaler recipes
  • Short follow-ups and plan packages

If you have specific concerns about pregnancy, pets, or medications, please mention them during intake so we can discuss safety and refer you as needed.
