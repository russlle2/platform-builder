Before your intake

To keep sessions safe and useful, note these practice points:

Patch test

Always do a small patch test for topical blends. Use a 0.5–2% dilution for most roll-ons unless otherwise advised.

Dilution guidance

You will receive clear dilution tables and step-by-step instructions for any topical product.

Pets

Certain essential oils are not pet-safe. Please mention household pets during booking so blends can be chosen accordingly.

Pregnancy & breastfeeding

Some oils are contraindicated during pregnancy. If pregnant or breastfeeding, include that in your notes so safe alternatives can be used.
