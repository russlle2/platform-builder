Frequently Asked Questions

How can Aromatherapy specifically help my postpartum stress and nervous system?
Our Aromatherapy uses specific plant essences known to influence mood and physiological responses. For new mothers, we focus on botanicals that promote relaxation, reduce anxiety, and encourage a sense of calm, directly aiding in the regulation of an often overstimulated postpartum nervous system. We help you find your steady rhythm again.
Are your Aromatherapy blends safe for new mothers, especially if I'm breastfeeding?
Yes, your safety and your baby's well-being are paramount. We meticulously select and dilute essential oils, choosing only those considered safe for postpartum use, including during breastfeeding. We will discuss any concerns you have and ensure all blends are tailored to your unique circumstances and sensitivities.
What does a typical Aromatherapy session for a new mother involve?
A session begins with a gentle conversation about your current state, sleep patterns, emotional landscape, and any specific stressors. Based on this, we'll guide you through scent experiences and create personalized Aromatherapy blends for home use, focusing on inhalation and gentle topical application to support your recovery and stress reduction.
How quickly can I expect to feel the effects of Aromatherapy on my stress levels?
Many new mothers report feeling a subtle shift towards calm and groundedness within moments of using their personalized Aromatherapy blends. Consistent, intentional use over time deepens these effects, building a resilient foundation for your nervous system and significantly reducing chronic stress. It's a gentle, cumulative support.
