Frequently Asked Questions

How does Aromatherapy specifically support postpartum sleep for you?
Our Aromatherapy focuses on regulating your nervous system, which is often heightened during postpartum. We utilize specific essential oils known to reduce anxiety, promote relaxation, and signal to your brain that it's safe to rest deeply, helping you achieve more consolidated and restorative sleep cycles.
Are the Aromatherapy blends safe for you while you're nursing?
Yes, your safety and your baby's well-being are paramount. We create customized, low-dilution blends with essential oils carefully selected for their safety profile during lactation. We'll discuss any specific concerns you have and ensure your personal blend is perfectly suited for you.
What kind of Aromatherapy rituals can you expect for better rest?
You can expect personalized, easy-to-integrate rituals such as gentle diffusion during your evening wind-down, localized topical application of diluted blends to pulse points or the soles of your feet, or aromatic bath soaks designed to ease tension and prepare your body for sleep.
How quickly can you expect to see improvements in your sleep with Aromatherapy?
While individual experiences vary, many new mothers report a noticeable difference in their ability to relax and fall asleep more easily within a few nights of consistently using their personalized aromatherapy ritual. Deeper, more sustained restorative sleep often develops over several weeks as your body responds to the consistent cues.
