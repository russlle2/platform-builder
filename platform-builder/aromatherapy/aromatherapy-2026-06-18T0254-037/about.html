Personalized Aromatherapy Consultations

We begin by listening deeply to your unique needs—whether you seek calm, clarity, or deeper rest. Your Dr. Morgan Ellis collaborates with you to craft a blend using only ethically sourced, therapeutic-grade essential oils. How do you create my custom blend?

We discuss your goals, lifestyle, and scent preferences in our consultation. Dr. Morgan Ellis selects oils based on their therapeutic properties and your unique response to scent, crafting a blend that aligns with your needs.

How soon will I notice results from my blend?

Many clients report feeling calmer or more focused within days of consistent use. The ritual itself builds a habit of intentional self-care, deepening the benefits over time through daily engagement.

Can you work with my existing health concerns?

Yes. We collaborate with your healthcare team when needed, ensuring our Aromatherapy complements your overall wellness plan while respecting all sensitivities.

What makes your consultations different?

We prioritize deep listening over quick fixes. Our process focuses on understanding your holistic needs, not just surface-level symptoms, to create solutions that truly resonate with your life.
