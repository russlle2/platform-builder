Frequently Asked Questions

How does trauma-informed Aromatherapy specifically benefit athletes and active people?
For athletes, physical stress can trigger or exacerbate emotional dysregulation. Trauma-informed Aromatherapy recognizes this connection, using specific aromatic profiles to gently support your nervous system, help process lingering emotional impacts from exertion or injury, and cultivate a sense of safety and calm, rather than merely masking symptoms.
Will Aromatherapy interfere with my training or recovery protocols?
No, our Aromatherapy practices are designed to complement your existing training and recovery. We focus on non-invasive, supportive scent applications that can enhance your sleep, reduce emotional stress, and promote mental clarity, all of which are crucial for optimal athletic performance and recovery, without any counter-indications to your physical regimen.
Can Aromatherapy help me manage the emotional impact of an injury or setback?
Absolutely. Injuries and setbacks can carry significant emotional weight. Our trauma-informed Aromatherapy provides a gentle, yet powerful, avenue to address feelings of frustration, fear, or grief. We craft blends that offer emotional comfort, promote acceptance, and support your mental resilience during challenging recovery periods, helping you reconnect with your inner strength.
How do you ensure the Aromatherapy approach is inclusive for all backgrounds?
Our practice is built on a foundation of inclusivity and respect for every individual's unique experience. We engage in open, empathetic dialogue to understand your personal history and preferences, ensuring that all scent choices and applications are culturally sensitive, personally meaningful, and contribute to an environment where you feel seen, heard, and supported.
