What is Aromatherapy and how can it specifically help seniors with emotional healing?

Aromatherapy uses natural plant extracts, called essential oils, to promote well-being. For seniors, we focus on specific aromas that can gently calm anxieties, ease feelings of sadness, and support peaceful rest, without requiring verbal processing of difficult memories. It's a non-invasive way to offer subtle emotional support and comfort.

I've never tried any 'wellness' practices before. Is Aromatherapy suitable for someone like me?

Absolutely. Our Aromatherapy approach is designed to be gentle and accessible for everyone, especially if you're new to wellness practices. There's no pressure or expectation. We simply introduce you to pleasant and beneficial scents in a relaxed, supportive setting, ensuring you feel completely at ease and comfortable.

How does your trauma-informed approach to Aromatherapy ensure my comfort and safety?

Our trauma-informed approach means we prioritize your sense of safety, choice, and control above all else. We explain everything clearly, ask for your consent at every step, and respect your boundaries. We never push you to engage with scents or topics that cause discomfort, creating a truly supportive and gentle experience tailored to your pace.

Will Aromatherapy interfere with my current medications or health conditions?

Your health and safety are paramount. Before creating any custom Aromatherapy blends, we conduct a thorough, confidential consultation to understand your health history and any medications you are taking. We use only therapeutic-grade essential oils and ensure all recommendations are safe and complementary to your existing care, always advising you to consult with your doctor.
