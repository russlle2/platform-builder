Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is Aromatherapy and how can it benefit me?
Aromatherapy is a holistic healing modality that utilizes natural plant extracts, known as essential oils, to promote health and well-being. It can benefit you by addressing a wide range of concerns, from reducing stress and improving sleep to easing discomfort and uplifting your mood, all through the therapeutic power of scent and topical application.
Are the essential oils used in your Aromatherapy practice safe?
Yes, your safety is our utmost priority. We use only high-quality, pure essential oils from reputable suppliers. During your personalized consultation, we carefully assess your health history, sensitivities, and any medications you may be taking to ensure that all recommendations and blends are safe and appropriate for your unique needs.
How long does it take to experience the effects of Aromatherapy?
The experience of Aromatherapy is highly individual. Some people notice immediate effects, such as a shift in mood or a sense of relaxation, while for others, benefits may unfold more gradually with consistent use over time. We'll discuss realistic expectations during your consultation and guide you on the best way to incorporate your blends for optimal results.
Can Aromatherapy replace traditional medical treatments?
Aromatherapy is a complementary therapy and is not intended to replace conventional medical treatment. It works wonderfully alongside your existing healthcare regimen to support your overall well-being. We encourage you to always consult with your primary healthcare provider for any medical conditions or concerns you may have.
