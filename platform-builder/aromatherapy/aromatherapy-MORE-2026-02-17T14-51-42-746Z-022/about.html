FAQ — safety notes

Patch test: We recommend a patch test for topical blends (apply a small diluted amount to the inner forearm and observe for 24 hours).

Dilution: Full dilution instructions accompany any topical suggestion — common guidance: 1–2% for general use, lower for sensitive skin or older adults.

Pets & pregnancy: If you have pets, are pregnant, or nursing, mention this during booking so we can suggest pet-safe and pregnancy-conscious options. Recommendations may support comfort but are not medical advice.
