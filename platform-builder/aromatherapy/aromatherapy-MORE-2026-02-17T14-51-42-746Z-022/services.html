Frequently asked safety notes

  • Dilution: We recommend carrier oils and dilution percentages appropriate for topical or aromatic use. Typical roll-ons are 3–5% for adults unless otherwise advised.
  • Patch test: Always perform a patch test on forearm skin when using a new blend; discontinue if irritation occurs.
  • Pets: Some essential oils can affect animals. Tell us about household pets so we can give pet-safe suggestions.
  • Pregnancy & nursing: If pregnant or nursing, consult your healthcare provider. We avoid certain botanicals and offer alternatives.
