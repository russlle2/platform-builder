FAQ & safety notes

Do these blends treat conditions?

No. Recommendations are not medical advice. Aromatherapy suggestions may support wellbeing but are not substitutes for medical care.

How should I dilute oils?

We provide dilution guidance with every recipe. Typical topical dilutions for adults are 1–3% for general use; always follow specific guidance and test a small patch first.

What about patch tests?

Apply a diluted sample to a small skin area and wait 24 hours. If irritation occurs, discontinue use and consult a professional.

Pets & pregnancy

Some oils are not appropriate for pets or during pregnancy. Mention pets, pregnancy, breastfeeding, or medications during intake so we can tailor guidance.
