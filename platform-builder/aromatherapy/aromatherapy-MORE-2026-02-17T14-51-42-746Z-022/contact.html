Quick FAQ

Dilution and safety
All blends include dilution guidance. You will receive clear percentages and instructions; follow a patch test for topical use.
Patch test
Apply a small diluted amount to inner forearm and wait 24 hours to check for sensitivity.
Pets & pregnancy
If you have pets or are pregnant, disclose this early. Some essential oils are not advised around animals or during pregnancy — I will suggest alternatives.
No medical claims
Sessions are educational and supportive; language will be framed as what scents "may support." Not a substitute for medical advice.
