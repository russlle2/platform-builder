How do you create a blend that's truly personal?

We begin by listening deeply to your wellness story, stress patterns, and preferred scents. Then we craft your blend using only botanicals that align with your body's needs—no generic formulas. Your journey is co-created with your input at every step.

Can I use my blend during pregnancy or with sensitive skin?

Absolutely. During your consultation, we'll discuss your health context and create a gentle, diluted formula specifically for you. We provide clear usage guidelines and avoid oils that may interact with your unique physiology.

How often should I use my aromatherapy blend?

Your practitioner will suggest a personalized rhythm based on your goals. Some use it daily for routine grounding, others for targeted moments. We focus on quality over quantity—your blend is designed to support you when you need it most, not as a constant expectation.

Do you offer samples before committing?

Yes. We provide a curated sample set of natural botanicals to explore scents in your environment. This helps us understand what resonates with your senses before creating your final blend, ensuring no surprises when you receive your bottle.
