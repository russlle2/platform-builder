Aromatherapy for Postural Health: Beyond the Desk.

Your daily routine often involves hours at a screen, leading to slumped shoulders and a stiff neck. Our Aromatherapy approach targets these common issues by integrating specific scents that encourage muscle relaxation and proprioception. We're not just masking discomfort; we're providing sensory cues to help you consciously adjust and maintain better alignment throughout your day, reducing the cumulative strain that leads to burnout.
