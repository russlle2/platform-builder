Frequently Asked Questions

How can Aromatherapy specifically help with my college-related anxiety?
Aromatherapy works by signaling the limbic system in your brain, which is responsible for emotions and memory. Specific essential oils can promote relaxation, reduce cortisol levels, and improve sleep quality, all crucial for managing academic stress and anxiety effectively.
Are the essential oil blends safe to use in a dorm or shared living space?
Yes, we prioritize safety. We'll guide you on appropriate diffusion methods and dilution rates. Many essential oils can be safely diffused or applied topically (diluted) without impacting others, but we'll discuss your specific living situation to ensure comfortable and considerate use.
How often should I use Aromatherapy for preventive care during the semester?
For preventive care and seasonal wellness, consistency is key. We'll work with you to create a personalized routine, which might involve daily diffusion, topical application during study breaks, or specific blends to use before exams. The goal is to integrate it seamlessly into your schedule.
Will Aromatherapy interfere with my medications or other wellness practices?
It's always important to discuss any existing medications or health conditions with your practitioner. While Aromatherapy is generally safe, we'll ensure our recommendations complement your current health regimen and do not interfere with other treatments you may be receiving.
