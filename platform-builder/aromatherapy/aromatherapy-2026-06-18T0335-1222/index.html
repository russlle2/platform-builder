Frequently Asked Questions

How can Aromatherapy specifically support my emotional healing during postpartum recovery?
Aromatherapy, when applied with trauma-informed care, can gently influence your limbic system, which is connected to emotions and memory. We use specific essential oils to help calm the nervous system, reduce feelings of anxiety or overwhelm, and promote a sense of grounding and emotional safety as you navigate the tender process of postpartum healing.
Is Aromatherapy safe to use while I am breastfeeding or recovering from birth?
Yes, with expert guidance, Aromatherapy can be very safe and beneficial. Your practitioner, , is trained in safe essential oil use for new mothers and will carefully select and dilute blends appropriate for your individual recovery, taking into account breastfeeding, birth type, and any sensitivities you may have, ensuring your utmost well-being.
What does a trauma-informed Aromatherapy session involve for new mothers?
A trauma-informed Aromatherapy session centers on creating a space of deep respect and agency. We begin with a gentle conversation to understand your current emotional landscape and comfort levels. Your practitioner will then introduce carefully chosen aromatic scents, inviting you to experience them at your own pace, always prioritizing your comfort and choice. The focus is on fostering a sense of safety and empowering your innate capacity for healing.
How quickly can I expect to feel the effects of Aromatherapy for my postpartum emotions?
The experience of Aromatherapy is unique to each individual. Many mothers report feeling a sense of immediate calm or gentle upliftment during or shortly after a session. Emotional healing is often a subtle, layered process, and consistent, gentle engagement with your personalized aromatic blends can deepen these effects over time, supporting sustained emotional regulation and well-being.
