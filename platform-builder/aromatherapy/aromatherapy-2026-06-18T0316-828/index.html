Frequently Asked Questions

How does Aromatherapy specifically address postural health?
Aromatherapy can support postural health by using essential oils that encourage relaxation in tight muscles or stimulate alertness for improved body awareness. Certain aromas can cue your nervous system to release tension, making it easier to maintain an upright, comfortable posture throughout your day, contributing to your overall vitality.
Can Aromatherapy truly help with mental clarity as I age?
Yes, specific essential oils have properties that can support cognitive function. We carefully select and blend oils known to enhance focus, reduce mental fatigue, and promote a sense of calm alertness. This can assist you in maintaining mental sharpness and engaging fully in your daily activities.
Is Aromatherapy safe for someone with existing health conditions?
Your safety is paramount. We conduct a detailed consultation to understand your complete health profile, including any existing conditions or medications. This allows us to craft Aromatherapy blends that are not only effective but also entirely safe and appropriate for your individual needs. We always recommend consulting with your primary care physician as well.
What can I expect during an initial Aromatherapy consultation?
During your initial consultation, we'll discuss your specific goals regarding postural comfort, mental acuity, and overall well-being. We'll explore your lifestyle, health history, and any preferences you may have. This detailed conversation allows us to design a personalized Aromatherapy plan and custom blend tailored precisely to your needs for sustained vitality.
