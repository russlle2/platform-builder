Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is Aromatherapy?
Aromatherapy is a holistic healing treatment that uses natural plant extracts, known as essential oils, to promote health and well-being. It's often used for mood improvement, pain relief, and to promote relaxation, helping your body's natural healing processes.
Is Aromatherapy safe for everyone?
While generally safe, the responsible and effective use of essential oils requires professional guidance. We always conduct a thorough intake to understand your health history and any sensitivities, ensuring your personalized aromatherapy blends are safe and beneficial for you.
How can Aromatherapy help with stress and anxiety?
Many essential oils possess calming and grounding properties that can significantly reduce feelings of stress and anxiety. Through carefully selected blends, we help you create aromatic anchors that promote relaxation, mental clarity, and emotional balance, supporting your nervous system.
What should I expect during an Aromatherapy consultation?
During your consultation, we'll discuss your health goals, lifestyle, and preferences in detail. Based on this, will formulate a bespoke essential oil blend specifically for you, providing clear instructions on how to use it safely and effectively to achieve your desired outcomes.
