What exactly is Aromatherapy?

Aromatherapy is a holistic healing modality that utilizes natural plant extracts, known as essential oils, to promote health and well-being. These aromatic compounds can be inhaled or applied topically to positively influence your mood, alleviate physical discomfort, and support your overall vitality.

How can Aromatherapy benefit my specific needs?

Your benefits from Aromatherapy are highly personalized. During our consultation, we'll discuss your individual goals, whether it's managing stress, improving sleep quality, boosting energy, or supporting emotional balance. We then craft a custom blend and application strategy specifically designed to address your unique requirements effectively.

Are essential oils safe to use?

When used correctly and with professional guidance, essential oils are generally safe. However, their potency requires careful consideration. Dr. Morgan Ellis will educate you on proper dilution, application methods, and any contraindications specific to your health profile, ensuring your safety and optimal results.

What can I expect during an Aromatherapy consultation?

Your initial consultation is a comprehensive conversation where we explore your health history, current concerns, and wellness aspirations. We'll discuss your sensory preferences and lifestyle. Based on this, Dr. Morgan Ellis will propose a personalized aromatherapy plan, including custom blends and practical usage recommendations for you to integrate into your daily routine.
