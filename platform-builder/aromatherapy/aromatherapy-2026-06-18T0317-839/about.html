How can Aromatherapy specifically help my postural health as an athlete?

Aromatherapy can enhance your body awareness, making you more attuned to your alignment and muscle engagement. Specific essential oils can also help relax overused muscles that might be pulling your body out of alignment, supporting better posture and reducing compensatory patterns from physical stress.

I experience a lot of physical stress from my training. Can Aromatherapy really make a difference?

Absolutely. Targeted aromatherapy can be incredibly effective in mitigating physical stress. Certain essential oils have properties that promote muscle relaxation and soothe tension, which in turn can prevent stress from accumulating and negatively impacting your body's structural integrity and overall posture.

Is Aromatherapy safe to use alongside my existing training and recovery routines?

Yes, when used correctly, aromatherapy is a complementary practice. We'll work with you to integrate it seamlessly into your current regimen, ensuring the blends and application methods support your training goals without interference. It's about enhancing what you already do to support your body.

How do I choose the right Aromatherapy blends for my specific needs as an active person?

That's where we come in! We'll have a conversation about your specific activities, any areas of physical stress or postural concerns, and your personal scent preferences. Based on this, we'll guide you in selecting or creating custom aromatherapy blends that directly address your unique athletic and body awareness goals.
