Aromatherapy

Aromatherapy's Embrace for Emotional Reconnection

When the body bears the weight of chronic illness or injury, the spirit often carries unseen burdens. Our trauma-informed Aromatherapy practices are crafted to honor this delicate landscape, offering a pathway to reclaim emotional equilibrium. We use carefully selected botanical essences to gently address the lingering anxieties, grief, and disembodiment that can arise during prolonged recovery, helping you re-establish a sense of safety within yourself.
