Frequently Asked Questions

How does Aromatherapy specifically support emotional well-being for individuals over 60?
Aromatherapy, when approached with a trauma-informed lens, offers a non-invasive way to soothe the nervous system. For you, this means gentle support for feelings of anxiety, grief, or loneliness that may arise with age, encouraging a sense of peace and emotional regulation through carefully selected, comforting scents. We focus on essences known for their calming and uplifting properties.
Is Aromatherapy safe for someone taking multiple medications or with sensitive skin?
Absolutely. Your safety is our utmost priority. We conduct a thorough, compassionate intake process to understand your health history, current medications, and any sensitivities. This allows us to select and dilute essential oils appropriately, ensuring that your Aromatherapy experience is both effective and completely safe, with no adverse interactions or irritations.
What can I expect during my first Aromatherapy session with ?
Your first session is a gentle conversation, a space where you can share your emotional landscape and what you hope to gain. We will explore various aromatic profiles together, identifying scents that resonate deeply with you. There’s no pressure, just an open, welcoming environment to understand how Aromatherapy can softly support your unique needs.
How can Aromatherapy help with past difficult experiences or trauma?
While Aromatherapy is not a substitute for therapy, it can be a supportive companion on your path. Certain aromas can help create a sense of safety and calm, which can be invaluable when processing difficult memories. Our trauma-informed approach means we move at your pace, using scents to gently ground and comfort you, fostering a feeling of security and resilience without forcing confrontation.
