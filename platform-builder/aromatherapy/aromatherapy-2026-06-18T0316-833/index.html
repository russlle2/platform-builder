Frequently Asked Questions

How does Aromatherapy specifically help with postural health for retirees?
Our Aromatherapy focuses on essences that can help you become more attuned to your body's position and alignment. This increased proprioception supports better posture by making you more aware of how you're standing or sitting, helping you make subtle adjustments to maintain comfort and stability throughout your day.
Can Aromatherapy truly improve mental clarity and focus?
Yes, certain aromatic compounds have been studied for their effects on cognitive function. We carefully select essential oils known to promote alertness, concentration, and memory recall. When used consistently, these blends can support your mental acuity, helping you maintain a sharp and focused mind.
What kind of Aromatherapy practices are involved?
We offer personalized consultations to create custom essential oil blends for diffusion, topical application, or inhalation. These practices are designed to be simple and integrate easily into your daily routine, promoting both physical comfort and mental engagement without requiring complex steps.
Is Aromatherapy safe to use if I have other health conditions or medications?
Your safety is our priority. During your initial consultation, we thoroughly review your health history and any medications you are taking. We then formulate blends that are safe and appropriate for your individual circumstances, always recommending patch testing and proper dilution. We encourage you to discuss Aromatherapy with your healthcare provider as well.
