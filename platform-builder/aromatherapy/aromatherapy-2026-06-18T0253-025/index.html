Frequently Asked Questions

What is Aromatherapy and how can it benefit me?
Aromatherapy is the therapeutic use of aromatic plant extracts and essential oils for psychological and physical well-being. It can help you manage stress, improve sleep, uplift your mood, and support various bodily systems. We focus on creating blends tailored to your specific goals and preferences.
Are essential oils safe to use, and how will I know which ones are right for me?
When used correctly, essential oils are safe and effective. During your consultation, will assess your health history and sensitivities to recommend appropriate oils and safe usage methods. We prioritize your well-being and provide clear guidance for every blend.
What can I expect during an initial Aromatherapy consultation?
Your first consultation is an opportunity for us to understand your wellness journey. We'll discuss your health concerns, lifestyle, and preferences. This allows to develop a truly personalized Aromatherapy plan and custom essential oil blend just for you, ensuring it aligns perfectly with your needs.
How often should I use my custom Aromatherapy blends for best results?
The frequency of use for your custom Aromatherapy blends depends on your individual needs and goals. will provide clear instructions and recommendations during your consultation, guiding you on how to integrate your blends effectively into your daily routine for optimal benefit.
