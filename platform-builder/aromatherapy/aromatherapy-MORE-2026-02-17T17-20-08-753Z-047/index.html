FAQ & safety

Are these medical treatments?
No. Aromatherapy may support well-being but is not a medical treatment. We avoid medical claims and encourage professional care when needed.
How do I apply oils safely?
Always dilute essential oils for topical use (commonly 1–3% for adults), and perform a patch test. Avoid undiluted application. We provide dilution guidance with each blend.
What about pets and pregnancy?
Some essential oils are not suggested around cats, dogs, or during pregnancy. We always screen for pets and pregnancy and provide safe alternatives when relevant.
