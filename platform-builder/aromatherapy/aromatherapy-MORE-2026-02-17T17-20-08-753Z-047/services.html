3. Plan: a two-week micro-practice with dilution guidance and pet/pregnancy notes if applicable.

FAQ — Safety highlights
Dilution: Every blend comes with a recommended dilution for topical use. Follow the plan; do not apply neat essentials to skin.
Patch test: A patch test is suggested before continued topical use. I will walk you through it.
Pets: Blends can be made with pet-safety in mind; some oils are inadvisable around animals — we will adapt.
Pregnancy & nursing: Certain oils are avoided; tell me if you are pregnant, trying, or breastfeeding so I can recommend safe choices.
Not medical care: Aromas and routines are offered as supportive practices and should not replace medical advice.
