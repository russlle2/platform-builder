Reserve a short orientation

A 20-minute session to review preferences, sensitivities, and a safe introduction to simple blends and practices. Sessions are private and safety-first.

Step 1 — Choose a slot. If you are pregnant or have significant health concerns, select the "safety consult" option and note that on the form.
Step 2 — Fill the intake below. We will ask about allergies, pets in the home, and any medication that may be relevant to scent sensitivity.
Step 3 — Bring a notepad. The session includes practical dilution notes and a short at-home practice you can repeat.
By requesting a reservation you agree to our safety-first approach. If pregnant or nursing, please book a safety consult. We provide dilution & patch test instructions before any topical recommendations.

FAQ — Safety highlights

Patch test: dilute recommended blends and apply a small amount on the inner forearm; wait 24 hours to check for irritation. Pets: certain oils affect animals; we provide pet-safe alternatives. Pregnancy: avoid certain oil types; we will always recommend a pregnancy-safe plan when asked.
