Frequently asked safety questions

Dilution and patch tests

I recommend simple dilution guidelines and a patch test before applying any blend topically. Guidance will be tailored and safety-forward — no medical promises, only supportive language.

Pets

Essential oils can affect animals differently. If you have pets, include information in your message so I can offer pet-safe options and diffusion suggestions.

Pregnancy & medications

Some oils are not recommended in pregnancy or with certain medications. Always mention pregnancy status and current medications during booking so I can ensure safety-focused alternatives.

Allergies & sensitivities

We always prioritize patch tests and low-concentration introductions. If you have known sensitivities, please tell me up front.
