Practical notes & safety

We prioritize safe, gradual scent practice. Members receive guidance on dilution, storage, and patch testing. Avoid direct skin application without proper dilution; perform a patch test and consult your health provider for pregnancy or medical concerns.

Pets: some essential oils can affect animals. Keep blends out of reach and consult with a veterinarian for specific guidance.
