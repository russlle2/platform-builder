Frequently asked safety questions

Dilution & patch testing

All blends include recommended dilutions (e.g., 1–2% for adults) and a simple patch-test procedure. Always test on a small skin area and wait 24 hours before wider use.

Pets & shared spaces

Essential oils can affect animals differently. We offer pet-safe alternatives and guidance to avoid prolonged exposures in shared areas.

Pregnancy & health conditions

Some oils are not recommended during pregnancy or certain conditions. We review contraindications in intake and make non-scent strategies available. This service is not medical advice.
