Our approach

We center sensory practice, education, and safety. Sessions combine scent, breath, and short reflective prompts. Nothing we offer is medical — our language is careful: practices may support regulation, attention, and sleep when used responsibly.

How sessions are structured

  1. Intake & safety: We review sensitivities, pregnancy/pets/dilution notes, and a patch-test approach.
  2. Focus & intention: Set a small aim for the session — attention, unwind, or clarity.
  3. Sensory practice: Guided smelling, brief breathwork, journaling prompts as desired.
  4. Home guidance: Clear dilution and storage steps — practical, safety-forward notes for home use.

Common questions & safety notes

Are these medical treatments?
No — practices and blends are intended to be supportive and may support wellbeing. We do not make medical claims.
How should I dilute essential oils?
We provide dilution guidelines and recommend a patch test before regular topical use. Typical starting dilution is 0.5–2% depending on use case; specific ratios are provided per blend.
What about pets and pregnancy?
We include explicit guidance for pets and pregnancy in intake and blend notes. If you are pregnant or caring for animals, please mention this during intake so we can suggest safe alternatives.
