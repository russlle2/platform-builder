Guides & safe use

Simple dilution guide

A quick frame: 1% ≈ 6 drops per 30ml carrier; 2% ≈ 12 drops per 30ml. Use lower strengths for children, older adults, pregnancy, and households with pets.

Patch-test

Apply a diluted drop to inner forearm and observe 24 hours. If irritation occurs, discontinue and consult a clinician.
