Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is Aromatherapy and how can it benefit me?
Aromatherapy is a holistic healing modality that uses naturally extracted aromatic essences from plants to promote and enhance health and well-being. It can benefit you by helping to alleviate stress, improve sleep, boost mood, support respiratory function, and provide natural pain relief, among many other applications. Your specific benefits will depend on your individual needs and the essential oils chosen.
Are the essential oils used in Aromatherapy safe?
Yes, when used correctly, essential oils are safe and highly beneficial. We exclusively use therapeutic-grade, pure essential oils and adhere to strict safety guidelines. During your consultation, we'll discuss your health history, sensitivities, and any medications you're taking to ensure your personalized Aromatherapy blends are safe and effective for your unique constitution.
How long does an Aromatherapy consultation typically last?
An initial Aromatherapy consultation typically lasts between 60 to 90 minutes. This allows ample time for us to understand your health goals, lifestyle, and preferences, and to formulate a truly bespoke aromatic plan tailored just for you. Follow-up sessions are usually shorter, focusing on progress and adjustments to your blends.
What can I expect during an Aromatherapy session with ?
During your session, will engage in a compassionate and thorough discussion about your current well-being, concerns, and desired outcomes. You'll experience various essential oil aromas, helping to identify those that resonate most with you. Based on this, will formulate a custom Aromatherapy blend and provide clear instructions on how to incorporate it into your daily routine for optimal results.
