How can Aromatherapy specifically help parents managing household stress and emotional overwhelm?

Aromatherapy, when approached with a trauma-informed lens, offers a gentle yet powerful pathway to re-regulate your nervous system. Specific aromas can help soothe fight-or-flight responses, foster a sense of safety, and create moments of emotional recalibration, helping you respond to daily parenting challenges with more presence and less reactivity.

Is Aromatherapy safe to use around young children in the home?

Your family's safety is paramount. We prioritize educating you on the safest and most effective ways to incorporate Aromatherapy into your home environment, especially with young children present. This includes guidance on dilution, diffusion methods, and selecting essential oils that are appropriate for their age and sensitivities, ensuring a nurturing space for everyone.

What does a trauma-informed Aromatherapy session involve for a parent like me?

A session with Dr. Morgan Ellis begins with a compassionate conversation about your unique experiences with stress and emotional regulation as a parent. We gently explore the imprints of these experiences, and then, together, select specific aromas that resonate with your inner landscape, fostering a sense of grounding and emotional release in a safe, supported space.

How quickly can I expect to feel the effects of Aromatherapy for my emotional well-being?

While individual experiences vary, many clients report an immediate sense of calm and a subtle shift in their emotional state during and after an Aromatherapy session. Consistent practice with your personalized blends at home can deepen these effects, supporting a more sustained sense of emotional balance and resilience over time.
