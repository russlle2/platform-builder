Common safety & practice questions

Are these sessions medical treatment?
No. Sessions are educational and supportive. Aromatherapy may support comfort for some people but is not a medical intervention.
What about dilution & patch testing?
Dilution guidance is provided for each blend. Patch testing is recommended before regular use and instructions are given for how to do a safe patch test.
Pets & pregnancy
Certain oils may not be safe for pets or during pregnancy. We always plan blends with these precautions in mind and offer alternatives where needed.
