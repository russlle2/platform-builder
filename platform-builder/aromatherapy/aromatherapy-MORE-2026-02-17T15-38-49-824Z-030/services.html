How an intensive flows

We begin with a short intake, followed by a scent exploration and a guided extract. You'll leave with a labeled sample and a simple ritual to practice. No medical promises — only supportive sensory work.

What we tailor

Blends are chosen for aroma profile, safety (including flight risk for pets), and dilution that aligns with your context. Expect fragrance notes categorized by brightness and depth.

Safety first

Patch-tests, dilution guidance, and clear pregnancy/pet precautions are standard. The work may support emotional ease or ritual structure; it is not medical treatment.

Follow-up

Short check-ins and a note about adjustments are available. We encourage incremental changes and careful observation.

Common hesitations

Will I be overwhelmed by scent? — We choose subtle dilutions and test first. Is it safe with pets or pregnancy? — We screen and adjust; see our FAQ on the Boutique page for specifics.
