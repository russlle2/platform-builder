Safety & usage notes

  • Patch test: Apply a diluted dot to inner forearm. Wait 24 hours for irritation.
  • Dilution: We publish recommended dilutions per product. Children, pregnancy, and nursing require bespoke guidance.
  • Pets: Many essential oils can irritate animals; keep products well out of reach and use ventilation. Consult a veterinarian when in doubt.
