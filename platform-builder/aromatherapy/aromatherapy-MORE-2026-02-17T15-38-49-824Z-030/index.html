What to expect in an intensive session

Personal intake

We begin with a conversation about routine, sensitivities, and desired feeling. Safety guidance and patch test steps are covered early on.

Scent exploration

Guided sniffing and the aroma wheel help you identify impressions. No medical promises — only experience-based suggestions that may support your goals.

Blending & protocol

A tailored blend is created with dilution guidance, storage tips, and use-when directions. Advice for pets and pregnancy considerations is included where relevant.

Common questions

Is aromatherapy safe for everyone?

We emphasize safety-first practices. Please inform us of allergies, pregnancy, or medication. Customized dilution and patch testing are recommended. This work is not medical treatment.

What about pets?

Certain essential oils can affect animals. We will offer pet-safe alternatives and instructions to minimize risk.

Can blends be used daily?

Guidance includes frequency and dilution so blends may be integrated safely into routines.
