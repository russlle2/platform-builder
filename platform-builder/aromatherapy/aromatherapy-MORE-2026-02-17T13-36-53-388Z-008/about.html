How this practice began

I came to scent through kitchens and windowsills: ginger simmer pots, sun-warmed lavender, and conversations about small rituals that held a day together. Over time those simple encounters became a practice of selecting and pairing plants that may support attention, calm, or restorative pause. Sessions and blends are designed to be approachable, safety-forward, and easy to fold into daily life.

Frequently asked safety notes

Dilution: Suggested dilution is provided with retail blends. For topical use, typical recommendations are conservative; follow provided guidance.

Patch test: Always patch test a new blend before widespread topical use.

Pets: Keep oils out of reach. Some botanicals can be irritating to animals — consult a veterinarian knowledgeable about essential oils.

Pregnancy & medication: If pregnant, breastfeeding, or taking medications, consult a qualified healthcare provider before use.
