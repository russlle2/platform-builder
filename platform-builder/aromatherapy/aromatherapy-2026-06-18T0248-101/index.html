Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is Aromatherapy and how can it benefit me?
Aromatherapy is a holistic healing treatment that uses natural plant extracts to promote health and well-being. It leverages the therapeutic properties of essential oils to enhance physical and psychological health. For you, this can mean reduced stress, improved sleep, boosted mood, pain relief, and enhanced cognitive function, all tailored to your specific needs.
Are the essential oils used in your Aromatherapy practice safe?
Absolutely. Your safety is our paramount concern. We exclusively use high-quality, pure therapeutic-grade essential oils from reputable suppliers. During your consultation, we'll discuss your health history, any sensitivities, and current medications to ensure your custom blends are formulated safely and appropriately for your unique constitution.
How long does a typical Aromatherapy consultation take?
A typical initial Aromatherapy consultation lasts approximately 60-75 minutes. This allows ample time for to understand your health goals, discuss your lifestyle, and formulate a personalized essential oil blend and usage recommendations tailored specifically for you.
Can Aromatherapy help with stress and anxiety?
Yes, Aromatherapy is widely recognized for its efficacy in supporting emotional balance, particularly with stress and anxiety. Specific essential oils are known for their calming, uplifting, or grounding properties. We will work with you to create a blend designed to soothe your nervous system and promote a sense of tranquility and emotional well-being.
