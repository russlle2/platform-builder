Frequently Asked Questions

How does Aromatherapy specifically support immune health for seniors?
Our Aromatherapy focuses on essential oils with recognized immune-supporting properties, like frankincense or lemon, which can help strengthen your body's natural defenses and resilience. We use gentle, diluted applications tailored to your sensitivity, aiming to fortify your system from within.
Will Aromatherapy help me feel more energetic and vibrant?
Absolutely. We craft specific Aromatherapy blends to uplift your spirit and stimulate your senses, using oils known for their revitalizing qualities. These can help reduce feelings of lethargy and promote a sense of refreshed vitality, encouraging a more active and engaged daily life.
Is Aromatherapy safe for someone with existing health conditions?
Your safety is our utmost priority. We conduct a thorough consultation to understand your medical history and any medications you may be taking. We then select essential oils and application methods that are safe and beneficial for your specific circumstances, always recommending you consult your primary healthcare provider.
What kind of commitment is involved with Aromatherapy sessions?
Our approach to Aromatherapy is flexible and designed to integrate seamlessly into your life. We offer individual sessions to create personalized blends and rituals, and ongoing support can be tailored to your preference, whether it's a monthly check-in or as needed. The commitment is entirely yours, focused on your comfort and well-being.
