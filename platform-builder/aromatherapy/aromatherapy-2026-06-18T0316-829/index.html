Frequently Asked Questions

How can Aromatherapy directly support my postural health as an athlete?
Our Aromatherapy protocols use specific essential oils known for their ability to influence muscle tension, focus, and body perception. By integrating these scents, we aim to enhance your proprioception, making you more attuned to your spinal alignment and overall body positioning, which is crucial for injury prevention and efficient movement.
I experience chronic physical stress from my sport; how does Aromatherapy help?
For athletes managing ongoing physical stress, Aromatherapy can be a powerful tool. We select blends that help calm your nervous system, reduce perceived exertion, and support recovery processes. This can alleviate the systemic stress response, contributing to better sleep, reduced muscle tightness, and an improved capacity for training and competition.
Is Aromatherapy safe to use alongside my existing training and recovery routine?
Absolutely. Our Aromatherapy approaches are designed to complement your current training, physical therapy, or chiropractic care. We focus on non-invasive, topical, or aromatic applications that support your body's natural systems without interfering with other modalities. We'll discuss your current regimen to ensure a synergistic approach.
What kind of Aromatherapy practices will help me improve my body awareness?
We'll guide you through practices that pair specific aromatic blends with mindful breathing and gentle movement cues. This helps create a stronger neural pathway between scent, sensation, and your body's position in space. The goal is to cultivate a deeper, more intuitive understanding of your own biomechanics, allowing for more conscious and effective movement.
