Micro-habits — 2–5 minute scent rituals

Tiny, repeatable practices to fold aromatherapy into everyday life. Always follow dilution and safety guidance.

  • Morning Clarify

    2–3 drops of citrus blend in diffuser for 10 minutes to refresh a room.

  • Calm Pause

    Breathwork with a lavender roll-on applied to wrists (1% dilution).

  • Work Reset

    Swipe a citrus-herb inhaler before deep-focus work sessions.

  • Sleep Wind-down

    Diffuse a low-intensity chamomile-woody blend 30 minutes before bed.
