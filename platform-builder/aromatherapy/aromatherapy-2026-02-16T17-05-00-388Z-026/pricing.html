Why retail add-ons?

Add-ons are curated for safe home use and include dilution and storage notes. We avoid recommending anything unsafe for children, pets, or pregnancy without individual assessment.

Planning your session

Sessions include a gentle intake, safety checklist, and a collaborative plan. Expect education on safe dilution and patch testing — we do not diagnose or treat conditions.

What to bring

A list of scents you like/dislike, any known allergies, and questions about home use with pets or pregnancy.

Safety first

We discuss dilution percentages, patch testing, safe storage, and child/pet-safe choices. If you are pregnant or have serious health concerns, please mention it — we will collaborate on safe aromatic options or recommend deferring to your care team if needed.
