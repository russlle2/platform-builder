Small rituals to begin

Morning inhale
2–3 mindful breaths from a personal inhaler or diffuser for a gentle mood reset.
Patch test routine
Try a 0.5–1% dilution on forearm for 24 hours—no itching or redness before topical use.
Diffuser etiquette
Diffuse in short bursts (10–30 minutes) and ventilate the space; keep pets and people with sensitivities in mind.
Storage
Keep blends cool, dark, and capped to preserve aroma and efficacy.
