Safety & usage notes

Blends are not medical treatments. We emphasize conservative dilution, patch testing, and pet/pregnancy considerations. Contact for specific safety guidance.

Quick FAQ

Dilution basics
We provide dilution percentages for each blend depending on use: diffusion, topical roll-on, or facial inhalation. We favor lower concentrations.
Patch testing
Patch test a small 1–2% sample on the inner arm for 24 hours before regular topical use.
Pets & pregnancy
Certain oils are contraindicated for cats and some are avoided in pregnancy; consult us during intake for safe alternatives.
