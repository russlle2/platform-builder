Shop FAQ

Dilution & application
Each product card includes suggested dilutions. For topical use, we recommend a patch test and conservative percentages. Contact us for personalized dilution calculations.
Patch testing
Apply a small (1–2%) sample to the inner arm and wait 24 hours. If irritation occurs, discontinue use.
Pets & diffusers
Diffusion should be done in short bursts and in spaces where pets can leave the area. Some oils are not recommended for cats—see product notes.
Pregnancy considerations
If pregnant or nursing, consult during booking and use only oils we explicitly recommend. We follow conservative safety guidance.
