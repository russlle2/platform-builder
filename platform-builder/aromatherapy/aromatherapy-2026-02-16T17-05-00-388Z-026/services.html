Frequently asked safety questions

Dilution — how much is safe?
We recommend starting at 0.5–1% for facial or sensitive-area use, 2–3% for roll-ons, and 1–2% for general diffusion. Your consult will include precise dilution suggestions. Always err on the side of lower concentrations.
Patch test guidance
Apply a 1% blend to a small area of inner forearm for 24 hours. Discontinue if irritation occurs. Patch testing is especially important with citrus or cinnamon-type oils.
Pets and aromatics
Many essential oils can be overwhelming to pets. We give pet-safe alternative suggestions and diffusion practices to minimize exposure. Always consult your veterinarian for species-specific concerns.
Pregnancy & nursing notes
Some oils are avoided during pregnancy. We follow conservative safety guidelines and will adjust recommendations during an intake. If pregnant or nursing, please disclose during booking.
