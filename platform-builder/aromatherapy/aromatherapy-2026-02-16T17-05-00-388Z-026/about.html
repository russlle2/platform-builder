Common questions

How do you advise on dilution?
We use conservative dilution ranges and document exact percentages. Your session includes a written guide so you can safely replicate blends at home.
What is a patch test?
A small, diluted application on inner forearm to check for irritation before regular use. We always recommend doing one with new materials.
Pets and aromatherapy?
Some essential oils can be irritating to animals. We'll tailor options and dilution if you share your pet species during intake.
Pregnancy notes
Certain oils are contraindicated in pregnancy. If you are pregnant or planning to become pregnant, please disclose this so we can choose suitable, conservative options.
