Frequently Asked Questions

How does Aromatherapy specifically help with postpartum postural health?
Aromatherapy can support your postural health by using essential oils that relax tense muscles, reduce inflammation, and encourage deeper, more supportive breathing. Certain aromas also enhance body awareness, helping you to consciously realign your spine and engage core muscles, which is vital after childbirth.
Are the Aromatherapy blends safe for new mothers and around infants?
Yes, our Aromatherapy blends are carefully formulated with the safety of new mothers and their babies in mind. We use gentle, appropriate dilutions and select essential oils known to be safe during postpartum. We'll discuss your unique circumstances and sensitivities to ensure your comfort and well-being.
Can Aromatherapy address specific areas like back pain or shoulder tension from nursing?
Absolutely. Many new mothers experience discomfort in their lower back, shoulders, and neck due to the demands of caring for an infant. Our personalized Aromatherapy blends can target these specific areas, providing soothing relief and encouraging release of tension, which in turn supports better posture.
How do I incorporate Aromatherapy into my daily postpartum routine for body awareness?
We'll guide you in creating simple, ritualistic ways to use your custom Aromatherapy blends. This might include applying oils to specific points before or after feeding, using an aroma diffuser during gentle stretching, or incorporating a mindful breathing practice with your chosen scent to reconnect with your body's alignment throughout your day.
