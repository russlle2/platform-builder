Alleviating Postpartum Musculoskeletal Discomfort Through Aromatherapy

The physical demands of childbirth and infant care can leave your body feeling strained and unfamiliar. Our bespoke Aromatherapy blends are meticulously crafted to address specific musculoskeletal aches, from pelvic floor tenderness to shoulder tension from feeding. We use carefully selected essential oils, known for their anti-inflammatory and analgesic properties, to offer targeted, soothing relief, helping your body soften and release stored tension.
