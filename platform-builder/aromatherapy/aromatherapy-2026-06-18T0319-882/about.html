How can Aromatherapy specifically help with my postpartum posture?

Aromatherapy can support your postpartum posture by offering blends that ease muscle tension, reduce stress that often contributes to poor posture, and enhance your body awareness. This allows you to consciously adjust and strengthen the muscles needed for optimal alignment as your body recovers and adapts to new demands.

Are the essential oil blends safe to use while breastfeeding and around my baby?

Yes, your safety and your baby's safety are our top priority. We use only high-quality, therapeutic-grade essential oils and create blends specifically formulated for new mothers. We will guide you on safe dilution rates and application methods, ensuring the blends are gentle and effective for use while breastfeeding and around your little one.

What kind of body awareness practices will you recommend with Aromatherapy?

We recommend simple, actionable practices that fit into your busy life as a new mother. This might include mindful breathing exercises with specific aromatic cues, gentle stretching routines paired with certain scents to deepen relaxation, or integrating aromatic compresses to specific areas of your body to increase sensory feedback and release tension.

How quickly can I expect to feel a difference in my body awareness and posture?

The experience is individual, but many mothers report feeling a greater sense of calm and increased body awareness within a few sessions. Consistent use of your personalized aromatic blends and integrating the recommended practices into your daily routine will gradually support improvements in your postural comfort and overall physical well-being.
