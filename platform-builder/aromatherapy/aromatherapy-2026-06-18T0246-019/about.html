What exactly is Aromatherapy?

Aromatherapy is a holistic healing modality that utilizes natural plant extracts, known as essential oils, to promote health and well-being. It involves inhaling or topically applying these aromatic compounds to positively impact your mood, cognitive function, and physical health.

How can Aromatherapy benefit me?

Aromatherapy can offer numerous benefits, such as reducing stress and anxiety, improving sleep quality, alleviating discomfort, boosting energy levels, and supporting emotional balance. Your specific benefits will depend on the essential oils and methods tailored to your individual needs.

Are essential oils safe to use?

When used correctly and with professional guidance, essential oils are generally safe. We prioritize your safety by ensuring proper dilution, advising on appropriate application methods, and considering any personal sensitivities or health conditions you may have.

What can I expect during an Aromatherapy consultation?

During your consultation, you'll discuss your health goals, lifestyle, and preferences. Dr. Morgan Ellis will listen attentively and then recommend a personalized Aromatherapy plan, which may include custom essential oil blends, usage instructions, and practical tips for integrating them into your daily life.
