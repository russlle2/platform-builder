Frequently Asked Questions

How can Aromatherapy specifically help with college anxiety?
Aromatherapy uses plant-derived essential oils to influence your mood and physiological responses. For college anxiety, specific aromas can signal your brain to reduce stress hormones, improve focus, or promote relaxation, helping you manage test jitters, presentation nerves, and general overwhelm more effectively.
Are the Aromatherapy blends safe to use in a dorm room or campus apartment?
Yes, we prioritize safety. We'll discuss diffuser types and proper ventilation during your consultation. Our blends are crafted with appropriate dilutions for general use, and we'll guide you on how to enjoy the benefits of aromatherapy responsibly in your living space, respecting any campus guidelines.
Can Aromatherapy help me sleep better when my academic schedule is intense?
Absolutely. Restful sleep is crucial, especially during demanding academic periods. We can develop specific nighttime aromatherapy rituals and blends using calming essential oils that encourage relaxation and deeper sleep, helping your mind and body recover, even when your study load is heavy.
How does 'seasonal wellness' in Aromatherapy apply to a college student's year?
Seasonal wellness in Aromatherapy for students means aligning your aromatic support with the changing demands of the academic calendar. For instance, invigorating scents for autumn study, immune-boosting blends for winter cold season, and calming aromas for spring exam periods, helping you proactively adapt and thrive.
