Frequently Asked Questions

How do you choose scents for my blend?
We analyze your lifestyle, stress patterns, and physiological responses to botanical compounds. For example, if you need focus, we prioritize rosemary and peppermint, not just 'energetic' scents.
Are your blends safe for sensitive skin?
Absolutely. All base oils are diluted to skin-safe concentrations, and we avoid common irritants like cinnamon or clove. We’ll test a patch before finalizing your custom blend.
How soon will I notice effects?
Many clients feel subtle shifts within 24 hours of consistent use. For deeper benefits like reduced anxiety, we recommend 2–4 weeks of daily application with your tailored routine.
Can you accommodate medical conditions?
Yes. We coordinate with your healthcare provider for conditions like migraines or chronic pain. Blends are formulated to complement—not replace—your existing care plan.
