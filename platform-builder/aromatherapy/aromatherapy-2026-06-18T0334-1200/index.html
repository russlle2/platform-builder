Frequently Asked Questions

How does Aromatherapy specifically address the emotional impact of major life transitions?
Aromatherapy works by interacting with your limbic system, the brain's emotional center. During transitions, this system can become dysregulated. We use specific essential oils to modulate these responses, helping to reduce feelings of anxiety or grief, and promoting emotional stability and clarity as you adjust to your new circumstances. It's about providing a subtle, yet powerful, physiological support.
Is this approach to Aromatherapy suitable if I've experienced past trauma?
Absolutely. Our practice is deeply trauma-informed. This means we prioritize your safety and comfort above all else. We select essential oils and application methods that are gentle and non-triggering, always working within your window of tolerance. The goal is to create a supportive environment where your nervous system can feel calm enough to process and integrate new experiences without re-traumatization.
What can I expect during an initial Aromatherapy consultation focused on life transitions?
Your first consultation is a compassionate space for understanding your specific experiences and emotional landscape. We'll discuss your unique transition, any emotional challenges you're facing, and your preferences. Based on this, we'll begin to craft a personalized aromatic strategy, explaining the evidence-based rationale behind each oil choice, ensuring you feel informed and empowered in your care.
How long does it take to see emotional benefits from Aromatherapy during a transition?
The experience is highly individual, as each person processes change and healing at their own pace. Many individuals report feeling a sense of calm or gentle upliftment relatively quickly, often within days of consistent use. However, sustained emotional recalibration is a process, and we recommend a commitment to your personalized aromatic practice to experience the deeper, more lasting benefits.
