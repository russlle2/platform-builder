Frequently Asked Questions

How can Aromatherapy specifically help with my academic anxiety?
Aromatherapy uses plant essences to influence your limbic system, which is linked to emotions and memory. Specific essential oils can calm your nervous system, reduce feelings of overwhelm, and promote a sense of groundedness, helping you approach your studies with a clearer, more focused mind.
I spend hours at my desk. How does Aromatherapy relate to postural health?
Prolonged sitting and stress often lead to tension in your neck, shoulders, and back. Certain aromas can act as cues to gently bring your awareness back to your body, reminding you to adjust your posture, take a deep breath, and release tension. It's about using scent as a mindful anchor to your physical presence.
Will using Aromatherapy make me sleepy during class or studying?
Not at all! We carefully select and blend essential oils based on your needs. While some oils are calming, others are uplifting and promote focus and mental clarity, which can be highly beneficial for studying and staying alert in lectures. Your custom blend will be designed to support your academic performance, not hinder it.
Is Aromatherapy a replacement for traditional methods of managing stress or physical discomfort?
Aromatherapy is a complementary practice that supports your overall well-being. It works wonderfully alongside other self-care strategies like exercise, proper nutrition, and mindfulness. It's not intended to replace medical advice or treatment for persistent anxiety or pain, but rather to enhance your ability to cope and thrive.
