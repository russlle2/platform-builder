Frequently Asked Questions

What is Aromatherapy and how can it benefit me?
Aromatherapy utilizes aromatic plant extracts and essential oils for therapeutic purposes. It can support your well-being by reducing stress, improving sleep, uplifting your mood, and even alleviating certain physical discomforts, all through natural and gentle methods.
Are the essential oils you use safe and ethically sourced?
Absolutely. Your safety and ethical practices are paramount. We exclusively use high-quality, pure essential oils sourced from reputable suppliers who adhere to strict ethical and sustainable harvesting standards. We will also guide you on proper dilution and application for your specific needs.
How do I choose the right essential oils for my concerns?
Choosing the right essential oils can feel overwhelming, but that's where our expertise comes in. During your consultation, we'll discuss your unique health goals and preferences to recommend and create blends that are perfectly suited to support your individual journey.
What should I expect during an Aromatherapy consultation?
During your consultation, you can expect a warm and attentive discussion about your health history, lifestyle, and wellness aspirations. We'll explore various aromatic options and educate you on their properties, culminating in a personalized aromatherapy plan tailored just for you.
