Frequently asked about safety & use

How should I dilute essential oils?
General guidance: for adults, common topical dilution is 1–3% for roll-ons and blends unless otherwise recommended. We'll go over exact math during a consult. Always include a patch test.
What's a patch test?
A patch test is applying a small drop of diluted blend to your inner forearm for 24 hours to check for irritation before broader use.
Are essential oils safe around pets?
Certain oils can be irritating to animals. We'll review pet safety and tailor suggestions to avoid risky compounds when animals are in the home.
Pregnancy & breastfeeding?
Some oils are not recommended in pregnancy. I follow safety-conscious guidance and will avoid or adjust oils and dilutions for pregnant or nursing clients. Always consult your healthcare provider.
