Common questions — safety and preparation

Do aromatherapy sessions make medical claims?
Sessions are non-medical, supportive sensory practices. We do not diagnose or treat medical conditions. Always consult a licensed healthcare provider for medical concerns.
Dilution & patch testing
All topical blends are prepared at safe dilutions. A patch test is offered on request and recommended if you have sensitive skin or multiple allergies.
Pets and essential oils
Essential oils can affect pets. Sessions are pet-aware: we avoid strong diffusions if animals are present and will advise on safe home use.
Pregnancy & nursing notes
Certain oils are avoided during pregnancy and nursing. Please let us know if you are pregnant or breastfeeding so blends are adapted accordingly.
