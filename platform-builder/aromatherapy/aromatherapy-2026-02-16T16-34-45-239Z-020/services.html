Safety & FAQ

Dilution — how concentrated are blends?

All blends are created with client safety in mind; typical topical dilutions range between 1–3% unless otherwise discussed. Specific dilution instructions are provided with every blend.

Patch test — do I need one?

Yes — a simple patch test on inner forearm is recommended for new essential oils or if you have sensitive skin. Guidance will be given during your session.

Pets — are essential oils safe around animals?

Essential oils can affect pets differently. We'll discuss pet-safe diffusing practices and recommend oils to avoid. If you live with pets, inform us before blends are created.

Pregnancy & nursing?

Certain oils are not recommended during pregnancy or breastfeeding. Inform your practitioner and your session will be tailored with safety-first alternatives. This service is informational and not medical advice.
