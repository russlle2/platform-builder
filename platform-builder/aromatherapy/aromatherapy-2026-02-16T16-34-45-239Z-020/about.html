Philosophy & approach

Each blend is created with respect for individual sensitivities, allergy screening, and evidence-aware sourcing. I avoid high-risk oils for pregnant or nursing clients and tailor dilutions to skin sensitivity.

Intake & Listening

A thorough intake identifies fragrances, preferences, and safety notes. We ask about medications, pregnancy, and pets to keep practice safe.

Scent Ritual
