FAQ — safety-forward guidance

How do you dilute essential oils?
We provide conservative dilution recommendations (usually 1–3% for adults for leave-on products). We never prescribe stronger concentrations and will adapt based on age, sensitivities, and pregnancy status. Please follow written instructions included with your blends.
What about patch testing?
A patch test is recommended for leave-on blends. We'll demonstrate how to perform one using a small amount on the inner forearm and observe for 24 hours. If irritation occurs, discontinue use and consult a medical professional.
Are essential oils safe around pets?
Some oils can be problematic for animals. I will review pet exposure and provide pet-safe options and diffuser practices. Always keep topical products away from pets and avoid strong diffusions in small, enclosed spaces with animals.
Pregnancy & aromatherapy — is it allowed?
Pregnancy is a contraindication for some oils. We follow conservative safety protocols and will avoid or substitute oils as needed. If pregnant, please disclose during booking so we can tailor a safe session.
Do you make claims about treating conditions?
No. Sessions are educational and experiential. We do not diagnose or offer medical treatment. Please consult a licensed healthcare provider for medical concerns.
