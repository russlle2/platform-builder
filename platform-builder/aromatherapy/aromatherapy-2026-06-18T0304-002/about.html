Personalized Wellness Mapping

We begin with a detailed wellness mapping session to understand your stress patterns, sleep habits, and emotional needs. No two clients receive the same blend. Your unique physiology, lifestyle, and goals guide every botanical selection, ensuring your aromatherapy experience is as individual as your fingerprint.

How long until I notice results from my custom blend?

Most clients observe subtle shifts in stress response within 24 hours. Deeper changes like improved sleep quality or emotional stability typically appear within 1-2 weeks of consistent use. Your blend is designed to work with your body's natural rhythms.

Do you use essential oils for medical treatment?

We focus on wellness enhancement and prevention, not medical treatment. Our blends support your body's innate healing capacity. Always consult your physician for health conditions—aromatherapy complements, but doesn't replace, medical care.

How often should I refresh my blend?

Your wellness profile evolves. We recommend a wellness mapping review every 3-4 months. Your current blend may need adjustment for new stressors, seasonal changes, or shifting wellness goals—ensuring your aromatherapy remains optimally aligned with your life.

Can you accommodate scent sensitivities?

Absolutely. We conduct a comprehensive sensitivity assessment during your mapping session. Your blend will avoid any trigger scents while maximizing therapeutic benefits. All oils used are 100% pure, unadulterated, and verified for safety.
