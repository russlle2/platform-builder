Frequently Asked Questions

What is Aromatherapy and how can it help my sleep after 60?
Aromatherapy uses natural plant extracts, called essential oils, to promote well-being. For those over 60, specific gentle aromas can help signal your body to relax, quiet your mind, and make it easier to drift into a peaceful sleep, supporting your body's natural rhythms without harsh interventions.
Do I need any special knowledge or equipment to benefit from your Aromatherapy for sleep?
Absolutely not. Our approach is designed to be gentle and accessible. We guide you through simple ways to incorporate these beneficial scents into your evening routine, often just with a diffuser or a gentle application. You don't need any prior wellness experience; we'll show you everything you need to know.
Are the essential oils used in Aromatherapy safe for seniors?
Your safety and comfort are our highest priority. We use only high-quality, pure essential oils and always recommend gentle dilution and application methods specifically tailored for sensitive skin and systems, ensuring a safe and comforting experience for you.
How quickly can I expect to see improvements in my sleep with Aromatherapy?
The experience of aromatherapy is personal, but many individuals notice a subtle shift towards greater calm and ease in their evenings within a few days or weeks. Consistency is key, and we're here to gently support you as your body reacquaints itself with more restful sleep patterns.
