Small, safe scent practices

60-second inhale

Roll one drop of a diluted roll-on on your wrist, cup your hands, inhale gently for a count of four. Best used seated and with eyes closed.

Diffuser reset

Diffuse 5–15 minutes in a well-ventilated room. Keep sessions brief and observe household sensitivities.

Patch-test practice

Always do a 24-hour patch test with a 2% dilution on forearm skin before regular topical use.
