FAQ — safe use essentials

Q: What dilution should I use?
A: For general adult topical use, 1–3% is common (6–18 drops per 30ml carrier). For children, elderly, or sensitive skin use lower dilutions and consult a professional.

Q: How do I do a patch test?
A: Apply a small diluted amount to the inside of the forearm. Wait 24 hours. If redness or irritation appears, discontinue use.

Q: Are essential oils safe for pets?
A: Some essential oils can be toxic to animals. Keep oils away from pets, avoid diffusing in enclosed spaces with animals, and consult a veterinarian before use around pets.

Q: Pregnancy and nursing notes?
A: Some oils are not recommended during pregnancy. Please consult your healthcare provider before using essential oils while pregnant or nursing. We provide pregnancy-safe options upon request.
