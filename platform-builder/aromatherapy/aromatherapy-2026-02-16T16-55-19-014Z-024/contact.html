Micro-habits

Small rituals that stitch scent into your day
Morning inhale

A 3-breath ritual with a single drop on a tissue to anchor intention. Patch-test first if topical use is new.

Desk diffuser

Low-dilution diffusion bursts (10–15 min) to refresh focus; avoid continuous long runs and consider pets' sensitivity.

Evening roll-on

A pre-diluted roll-on applied to inner wrists for a calming scent shorthand. Pregnancy and pets: consult guidelines in FAQs.

Mini-check

Daily 60-second pause: one inhale, one exhale, note the scent and how it lands. Adjust as needed.
