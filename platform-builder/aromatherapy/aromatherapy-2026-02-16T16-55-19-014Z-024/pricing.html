Safety highlights

  • Always dilute essential oils into a carrier. Typical dilution ranges are provided in member guides.
  • Patch test new blends on a small area of skin 24–48 hours prior to use.
  • Use care around pets — some oils are not pet-safe. We include pet-safe alternatives.
  • If pregnant or nursing, consult your healthcare provider before use.
