Safety notes & gentle guidance

Always use small dilutions for topical application (commonly 1–3% for adults unless advised). Perform a patch test for new topical use. Avoid direct application to eyes. Consult your healthcare provider regarding use during pregnancy or with young children. Keep essential oils away from pets — some oils are toxic to animals. These blends are aromatic tools intended to support daily routines; they are not medical treatments.
