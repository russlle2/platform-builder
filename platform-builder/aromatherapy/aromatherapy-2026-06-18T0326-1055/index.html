Frequently Asked Questions

How can Aromatherapy specifically help me when I'm going through a big life change?
Aromatherapy, with its direct impact on the limbic system, can profoundly influence your emotional state and nervous system. During major life changes, your body often perceives stress as a threat. Specific essential oils can help downregulate this stress response, encouraging your body to shift into a 'rest and digest' state, which is crucial for coping and adapting effectively.
Will Aromatherapy make me feel drowsy or affect my ability to function during my transition?
Our approach to Aromatherapy is centered on balance and regulation, not sedation. While some oils are calming, others are uplifting or help with mental clarity. Your personalized blend will be carefully crafted to support your nervous system without impairing your daily functions, allowing you to remain present and engaged as you navigate your new path.
I'm new to Aromatherapy. What should I expect from a consultation with [PRACTITIONER_NAME]?
A consultation with [PRACTITIONER_NAME] is a gentle, collaborative process. We'll discuss your specific life transition, how it's impacting your well-being, and your preferences for scent. You'll learn about the botanical properties of various essential oils and how they can support your nervous system. From this conversation, a personalized Aromatherapy plan, including custom blends and usage rituals, will be developed just for you.
Is Aromatherapy a long-term commitment, or can it provide immediate relief during a challenging period?
Aromatherapy offers both immediate and cumulative benefits. You may experience a sense of calm and relief from the first use of a well-chosen blend. For sustained nervous system regulation and support through your entire transition, consistent, mindful use over time yields the most profound and lasting effects, helping you build resilience that extends beyond the immediate challenge.
