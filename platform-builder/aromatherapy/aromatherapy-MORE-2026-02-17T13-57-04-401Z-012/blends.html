Safety & dilution primer

Typical safe topical dilutions for adults: 1% for daily use, 2% for short-term targeted support, and up to 3% when used briefly and with awareness of sensitivity. For children, older adults, pregnancy, or pets, choose lower dilutions (0.25–0.5% or consult a specialist).
