FAQ — Safety essentials
Dilution guidance
General guidance is conservative: 1–2% for everyday adult use, lower for sensitive skin and children. For pregnancy or breastfeeding, consult a healthcare provider. See your practitioner for tailored dilution plans.
Patch testing
Before using a new blend, apply a small amount diluted on the inner forearm and wait 24 hours. Discontinue if irritation appears.
Pets
Keep essential oils and diffusers out of reach of pets. Some oils can be harmful to animals; consult a veterinarian if in doubt.
Pregnancy & children
Use extra caution. Some botanicals are best avoided in pregnancy. If pregnant, breastfeeding, or caring for young children, check with your healthcare provider before use.
