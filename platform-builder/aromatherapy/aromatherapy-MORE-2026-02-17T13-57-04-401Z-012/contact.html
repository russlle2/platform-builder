Safety & quick FAQ

Are these sessions medical care?
Sessions are aroma-focused consultations and supportive practices. They are not a substitute for medical, psychiatric, or emergency care.
How about dilution and patch testing?
Topical blends should be diluted. A typical general-use dilution is 1–3% for adults (6–18 drops per 10 ml carrier for 1–3% ranges). Always test a small patch and wait 24 hours for sensitivity.
Pets and essential oils?
Some oils can be sensitizing to animals. If you have pets, mention species and size before applying or diffusing. We provide pet-friendly alternatives.
Pregnancy and breastfeeding
Certain oils are best avoided during pregnancy or breastfeeding. If this applies, let us know up front so we can recommend safe options and adjustments.
