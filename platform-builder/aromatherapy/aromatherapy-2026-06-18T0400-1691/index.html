Frequently Asked Questions

How does Aromatherapy specifically support relationship healing for seniors?
Aromatherapy, when thoughtfully applied, can gently influence emotional states. For seniors, specific aromas can help reduce stress and anxiety often associated with relational changes or past experiences, encouraging a more open and compassionate mindset. It can create an atmosphere of calm for difficult conversations, or evoke feelings of warmth and nostalgia, strengthening bonds and fostering understanding within your relationships.
Are the essential oils safe for me, given I might have sensitivities or medications?
Your well-being is our utmost priority. During your initial consultation, we conduct a thorough review of your health history, including any sensitivities, allergies, or medications you may be taking. We then select and dilute essential oils with great care, ensuring that any custom blends are gentle, effective, and safe for your unique physiological needs. We always advocate for a cautious, evidence-based approach tailored specifically for you.
What can I expect during an initial Aromatherapy consultation focused on heart-centered care?
Your first consultation is a gentle conversation where we listen intently to your specific needs regarding relationships, emotional comfort, and overall well-being. We'll discuss your life experiences, current emotional landscape, and any relational concerns. Based on this, we will propose a personalized Aromatherapy approach, explaining the chosen essential oils and their gentle actions, ensuring you feel completely heard and understood.
How can Aromatherapy help me connect with memories or loved ones who are no longer present?
Aromatherapy can be a profound tool for gentle remembrance and emotional connection. Certain aromas have a unique ability to evoke memories and feelings, offering a comforting anchor when reflecting on cherished relationships. We can craft blends designed to support feelings of peace, gratitude, and enduring love, creating a fragrant space for you to honor and connect with the spirit of those important to you, fostering a sense of continued closeness.
