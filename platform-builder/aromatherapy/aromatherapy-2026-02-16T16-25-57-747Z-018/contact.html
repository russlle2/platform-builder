Questions we often hear

How should I dilute essential oils?
We present standard dilution ranges during sessions. For topical use we typically suggest low dilutions (1–3%) for adults; we include written guidance and pre-diluted samples. Always follow the provided instructions.
Should I patch test?
Yes. A small patch test is recommended for topical use. We encourage testing on inner forearm and waiting at least 24 hours to check for sensitivity.
Are scents safe for pets?
Some essential oils can affect pets. If you share your home with animals, tell us in advance so we can recommend pet-safe options and avoid scheduling home-based diffusions without consent.
What about pregnancy?
Pregnancy requires extra caution. Some oils are not recommended. Please disclose pregnancy when booking; we will adapt blends and offer pregnancy-safe alternatives. We do not provide medical advice.

Reserve a place

Seats are small to keep the space calm. If you prefer, request a private consult or ask about corporate/wellness bookings.
