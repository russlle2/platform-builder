Common questions — safe practice first

Dilution: what is recommended?

Suggested dilutions are included with each session. Typical adult topical dilutions range 1–3% for blends in roller bottles. For children or sensitive skin, lower dilutions are advised. Always perform a patch test prior to extended use.

Do you do patch tests?

Yes. We guide a simple skin patch test during private sessions and recommend it for all new blends before regular topical use.

What about pets?

Certain essential oils are not pet-friendly. We avoid diffuse-heavy use of known toxic oils in mixed settings and will advise pet-safe options. Inform us at booking if animals will be present.

Pregnancy & nursing

If you are pregnant, planning pregnancy, or nursing, please mention this before booking. We will adapt blends and practices; sessions avoid strong topical or inhalation techniques contraindicated during pregnancy. This is guidance, not medical advice.
