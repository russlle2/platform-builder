Frequently asked — safety & practical

How should I dilute essential oils?
Dilution depends on use. For topical roll-ons, 1–5% is typical (about 6–30 drops per 10ml carrier). We demonstrate exact measures at events. Always consider age, skin sensitivity, and pregnancy. No medical advice is given.
Do I need to patch test?
Yes. A small patch test behind the ear or on the forearm with the final diluted blend is recommended and shown during sessions.
Are essential oils safe with pets?
Some oils can affect animals. We review pet-safe handling and advise airing spaces and keeping concentrated oils away from pets. Ask about specific oils if you have concerns.
What about pregnancy or nursing?
Certain oils are not recommended during pregnancy or breastfeeding. We cover contraindications and suggest consulting a healthcare professional. Sessions are educational, not clinical.
