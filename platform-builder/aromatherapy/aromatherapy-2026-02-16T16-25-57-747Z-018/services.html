Common questions

How do you dilute oils during a session?
We teach safe dilution ratios for topical use (e.g., 1–3% for adults in leave-on applications) and always provide carrier oils. Ratios are adapted for age, skin sensitivity, and the planned use. This is educational, not medical advice.
What about patch testing?
A patch test is demonstrated and encouraged. We show steps: small mix, apply to inner forearm, observe 24 hours for irritation.
Pets in the home?
We review species-safe practices; many essential oils can affect pets. Diffuse cautiously, avoid direct application, and ask about your pet before taking blends into shared spaces.
Pregnancy & nursing
Certain oils are best avoided or used with caution during pregnancy and while nursing. We always provide guidance and recommend consultation with your care provider for any health concerns.
