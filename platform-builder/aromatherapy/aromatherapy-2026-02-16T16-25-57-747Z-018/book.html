Quick FAQ

Dilution: Typical adult dilution references are provided for each session. Use lower concentrations for sensitive skin and children.
Patch test: Recommended before applying new blends topically.
Pets: Some oils are unsafe for animals; keep diffusers and roll-ons away from pets and ask for pet-safe options.
Pregnancy: Tell us if you're pregnant or nursing; we will adapt blends and techniques. This is safety guidance, not medical advice.
