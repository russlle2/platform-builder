Notes on safety (please read)

Dilution — We recommend specific carrier ratios for adult topical use (commonly 1–3% for leave-on). Lower percentages for sensitive skin and children; always follow the label provided with blends.

Patch test — A patch test is simple: dilute as directed, apply a small amount to inner forearm, wait 24 hours. Discontinue if irritation occurs.

Pets — Many essential oils can affect animals. We list caution notes and suggest consulting your veterinarian before using diffusers or topical products in homes with pets.

Pregnancy & nursing — Some oils are contraindicated in pregnancy or best used only with professional guidance. We provide pregnancy notes and suggest discussing use with your healthcare provider.
