Frequently Asked Questions

How can Aromatherapy specifically help my posture during a life transition?
During significant life changes, stress can cause unconscious muscle tension and poor postural habits. Aromatherapy uses specific essential oils to relax tense muscles, reduce inflammation, and calm the nervous system, which in turn encourages your body to release bracing and return to a more natural, aligned posture. It helps you become more aware of how you hold your body.
Is Aromatherapy a physical therapy substitute for postural issues?
Aromatherapy is a complementary practice. It works beautifully alongside physical therapy or chiropractic care by addressing the energetic and emotional components that often contribute to postural imbalances. It helps create a receptive environment in your body for other therapies to be more effective, rather than replacing them entirely.
How does Aromatherapy foster body awareness when I'm feeling disconnected?
Certain essential oils have properties that enhance sensory perception and ground your consciousness in your physical body. By engaging your sense of smell, Aromatherapy can create a direct pathway to your nervous system, promoting a mindful connection to your physical sensations and helping you feel more present and aware of your body's signals during times of disconnection.
What kind of Aromatherapy session should I expect for postural support?
Your session will involve a discussion about your specific transition and any postural concerns you have. We'll then create a custom essential oil blend, often applied to specific areas of the body to encourage relaxation and alignment. You'll learn simple self-application techniques to continue supporting your posture and body awareness at home.
