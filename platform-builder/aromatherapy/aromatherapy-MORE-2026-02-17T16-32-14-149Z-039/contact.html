Safety notes & FAQ

Dilution & patch tests: Always dilute essential oils before skin application; consider a small patch test. Typical dilution ranges for adults are 0.5–3% depending on use. We can provide blend-specific guidance.

Pets & pregnancy: Some oils are not recommended for pregnancy or for use around pets. Please tell us if you are pregnant, nursing, or have animals in the home so we can offer safer alternatives.

Not a replacement: Services and suggestions are not medical treatment. They may support comfort, relaxation, or routine but are not medical advice.
