Small practices you can try in five minutes

  1. Two-breath inhalation: 1–2 drops on a cloth, inhale slowly. Patch-test first for sensitivity.
  2. Desk mist: 2–3 drops in a small spray bottle with water for a brief, shared uplift. Avoid direct spray near pets.
  3. Pulse roll-on: Dilute in fractionated coconut (2–3% dilution). Apply to wrists for a subtle, portable scent.

These are practices that may support presence and routine. Keep products away from children and animals; store away from heat.
