Frequently Asked Questions

How can Aromatherapy specifically help seniors aged 60+?
Aromatherapy offers gentle support for common experiences in later life, such as encouraging more restful sleep, easing occasional stiffness, or simply uplifting spirits. We focus on natural, non-invasive methods that work with your body, not against it, to enhance your comfort and overall sense of well-being.
Are the essential oils used in Aromatherapy safe for older adults?
Yes, your safety is our top priority. We use only high-quality, pure essential oils and always dilute them appropriately for sensitive skin and mature bodies. We'll discuss any medications or health conditions you have to ensure every blend is tailored safely and effectively for your individual needs.
What does a seasonal wellness Aromatherapy session involve?
During a seasonal wellness session, we'll chat about how you're feeling with the current season and any specific areas where you'd like support. will then suggest and blend essential oils known to harmonize with that season's energies, helping you feel more balanced and comfortable, whether it's for energy in spring or calm in autumn.
Will Aromatherapy interfere with my current medications or health conditions?
We always recommend you inform your primary healthcare provider about any complementary therapies you're considering, including Aromatherapy. During your consultation, please share all current medications and health conditions. will carefully review this information to ensure that any suggested Aromatherapy approaches are safe and complementary to your existing care.
