Questions & safety notes

Are these medical treatments?

Yes. Dilution is provided with clear ratios and written guidance so you can re-create blends safely at home.

Patch testing is recommended. I provide alternative notes for pregnancy and pet households; please disclose so we can adapt blends safely.
