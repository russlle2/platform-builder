FAQ — safety & practices
Short answers for common safety concerns. Always patch test and check personal risks.

Dilution & patch tests
We recommend dilutions for topical use and a small patch test before first topical application. Suggested dilution rates are provided during consultations. This is general guidance and not medical advice.
Pregnancy & medication notes
Certain essential oils may be avoided in pregnancy, while others require extra care. Always consult your healthcare provider about medications and pregnancy; we provide conservative suggestions and safety-minded alternatives.
Pets & home environments
Some oils can be irritating to animals. We guide on pet-friendly usage and ventilation strategies to reduce risks. If you have concerns, share details in your message.
What to expect during a session
Sessions include a conversation about history and preferences, sensory exploration, and a safety checklist. Recommendations may support a ritual or routine but are not a substitute for medical care.
