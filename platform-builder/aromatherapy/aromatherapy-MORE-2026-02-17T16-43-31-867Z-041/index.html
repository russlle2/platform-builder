How an Intensive Unfolds

  1. Arrival & Intake. A focused intake to set goals and review safety (allergies, medications, pregnancy, pets).
  2. Scent Mapping. We explore notes together using the Aroma Wheel; you guide emphasis.
  3. Blend & Brief. I prepare a tailored concentrate with clear dilution guidance and a patch test plan.
  4. Integration. Steps to use, store, and adapt the blend; an optional recorded summary is provided.

Safety: Always patch-test new blends; dilute essential oils per guidance; consult prenatal or veterinary professionals when needed. No medical claims are made — oils may support comfort or calm.

Common Questions

Are these sessions medical?
No. Sessions are educational and may support well-being; they are not medical care.
What about dilution and patch testing?
All blends come with dilution ratios and a recommended patch test area with timing. We prioritize lowest effective dilution for safety.
Can I use oils around pets or during pregnancy?
Essential oils can affect pets and pregnancy differently. We discuss alternatives and consult guidance; avoid using near animals without professional advice.
How long is a session?
Intensives typically run 45–75 minutes depending on objectives.
