Frequently Asked Questions

What is Aromatherapy and how can it benefit me?
Aromatherapy is a holistic healing treatment that uses natural plant extracts to promote health and well-being. It can benefit you by reducing stress, improving sleep, boosting mood, alleviating discomfort, and supporting overall emotional and physical balance through the therapeutic properties of essential oils.
Are the essential oils used in your Aromatherapy practice safe?
Yes, your safety is our top priority. We use only high-quality, ethically sourced, and pure essential oils. During your consultation, we will discuss any sensitivities or health conditions you may have to ensure your custom aromatherapy blends are safe and appropriate for your individual needs.
How do I use my custom Aromatherapy blend once I receive it?
We provide clear, personalized instructions for each custom blend. Typically, you might use your blend via diffusion, topical application (diluted in a carrier oil), or through inhalation. We will guide you on the best methods and dosages to achieve your desired therapeutic effects safely and effectively.
What can I expect during an Aromatherapy consultation?
During your consultation, you can expect a warm and confidential discussion about your health goals, lifestyle, and preferences. We'll explore your current well-being, any concerns you have, and how aromatherapy can support you. This allows us to craft a truly personalized and effective essential oil blend just for you.
