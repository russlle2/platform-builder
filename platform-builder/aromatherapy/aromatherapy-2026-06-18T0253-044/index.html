Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is Aromatherapy?
Aromatherapy is a holistic healing treatment that uses natural plant extracts to promote health and well-being. It leverages the aromatic compounds of essential oils to enhance physical and psychological health, offering a gentle yet powerful approach to self-care.
How can Aromatherapy benefit my specific concerns?
Aromatherapy can be tailored to support a wide range of concerns, including stress, anxiety, sleep disturbances, pain management, and mood imbalances. By understanding your individual needs, we can create custom blends designed to foster your desired outcomes.
Are the essential oils used in Aromatherapy safe?
The safety of essential oils in Aromatherapy depends on their quality, proper dilution, and appropriate application. We exclusively use therapeutic-grade essential oils and provide clear guidance on their safe and effective use to ensure your well-being.
What should I expect during an initial Aromatherapy consultation?
During your initial Aromatherapy consultation, you'll engage in a detailed discussion about your health history, current concerns, and wellness goals. This allows us to understand your unique profile and collaboratively develop a personalized aromatic strategy just for you.
