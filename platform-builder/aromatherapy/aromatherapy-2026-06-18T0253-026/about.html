What exactly is Aromatherapy?

Aromatherapy is a complementary health modality that uses naturally extracted aromatic essences from plants to promote physical and psychological well-being. It involves the use of essential oils for therapeutic purposes, carefully selected and applied to support your body's innate healing processes and enhance your overall state.

Is Aromatherapy safe for everyone?

While Aromatherapy is generally safe, individual sensitivities and health conditions must be considered. We conduct a thorough intake to understand your unique profile, including any allergies, medications, or health concerns, to ensure that the essential oils and methods recommended are appropriate and safe for you. Your well-being is our priority.

How can Aromatherapy help me?

Aromatherapy offers diverse benefits, supporting various aspects of your well-being. It can assist with stress reduction, improve sleep quality, uplift mood, alleviate discomfort, and enhance focus. By working with your specific needs, we create custom blends designed to target your particular concerns and promote a greater sense of balance and vitality in your life.

What does a typical Aromatherapy consultation involve?

A consultation typically involves a detailed discussion about your health history, current lifestyle, and specific wellness goals. We listen carefully to your needs and preferences. Based on this information, we will formulate a personalized essential oil blend and provide clear instructions on its safe and effective use, ensuring you feel confident and informed throughout your journey.
