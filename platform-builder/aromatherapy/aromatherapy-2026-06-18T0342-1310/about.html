How does Aromatherapy specifically help with postpartum physical pain?

Our Aromatherapy focuses on specific physiological mechanisms. Essential oils are chosen for their documented anti-inflammatory, analgesic, and muscle-relaxing properties, which can be absorbed through the skin to target localized pain in areas like your pelvic floor, abdomen, back, and shoulders, common after childbirth. This provides direct physical relief and supports your body's recovery.

Is Aromatherapy safe for new mothers who are breastfeeding?

Yes, when practiced by a trained professional like Dr. Morgan Ellis, Aromatherapy is safe for breastfeeding mothers. We carefully select essential oils that are non-toxic and used in appropriate dilutions, avoiding any that could be harmful or pass into breast milk. Your safety and your baby's well-being are our utmost priority, and we discuss all considerations during your initial consultation.

What kind of somatic relief can I expect from your Aromatherapy sessions?

You can expect a reduction in deep-seated physical tension and an increased sense of bodily ease. Our Aromatherapy helps to calm the nervous system, which in turn can release chronic muscle contraction and improve your ability to feel present and comfortable in your body. This somatic release can alleviate feelings of stiffness, heaviness, and persistent aches, aiding your physical and emotional recovery.

How quickly might I feel the effects of postpartum Aromatherapy?

Many new mothers report feeling immediate relief and a sense of calm during and shortly after their first Aromatherapy session. For more chronic or deep-seated pain, consistent application and a series of sessions may be recommended to achieve lasting comfort and somatic rebalancing. We work with you to create a personalized plan that honors your body's unique recovery timeline.
