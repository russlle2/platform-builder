Frequently Asked Questions

What is Aromatherapy and how can it benefit your well-being?
Aromatherapy is the therapeutic use of aromatic plant extracts and essential oils to promote holistic health. It can benefit your well-being by supporting emotional balance, enhancing relaxation, improving sleep quality, easing discomfort, and uplifting your mood, all through natural means.
Are the essential oils used in your Aromatherapy practice safe?
Absolutely. We prioritize your safety and well-being. All essential oils used in our Aromatherapy practice are high-quality, ethically sourced, and undergo rigorous purity testing. We will always guide you on proper dilution and application methods suitable for your individual needs.
How does a personalized Aromatherapy consultation work?
During your personalized Aromatherapy consultation, we'll discuss your health history, lifestyle, and specific wellness goals. Based on this comprehensive understanding, will craft custom essential oil blends and provide tailored recommendations for their safe and effective use to support your unique journey.
Can Aromatherapy complement your existing health regimen?
Yes, Aromatherapy can be a wonderful complementary therapy. It works synergistically with many health regimens to support your overall well-being. We encourage you to discuss your current health practices with us so we can ensure our Aromatherapy recommendations align seamlessly with your existing care.
