Aromatherapy for the Weary Body of a Caregiver

How can Aromatherapy specifically help with the physical pain I experience as a parent?

Aromatherapy uses concentrated plant essences to address various physical discomforts. For parents, this can mean using specific oils to soothe muscle tension in your neck and shoulders from carrying children, alleviate stress-induced headaches, or calm digestive unease often linked to household stress. Your session will be tailored to target your particular areas of physical strain.

I feel constantly overwhelmed. Can Aromatherapy help with the 'heavy' feeling in my body?

Absolutely. That 'heavy' feeling is often a somatic manifestation of accumulated stress. Aromatherapy can gently support your nervous system, encouraging a state of deeper relaxation. By working with specific scents that promote calm and grounding, we help your body release that stored tension, allowing you to feel lighter and more present, even amidst the daily demands.

Is Aromatherapy safe for someone who is breastfeeding or has young children at home?

Your safety and the well-being of your family are paramount. During your consultation, we will thoroughly discuss your personal circumstances, including if you are breastfeeding, pregnant, or if there are any sensitivities within your household. We will select and dilute essential oils appropriately, ensuring a safe and effective experience for you.

What does a typical Aromatherapy session involve for someone seeking physical pain relief?

A session begins with a conversation about your current physical discomforts, stress levels, and daily routines. Based on this, Dr. Morgan Ellis will custom-blend essential oils, often applied topically through gentle massage or diffused for inhalation. The focus is on creating a calming, supportive environment where your body can begin to release tension and find comfort, addressing the root causes of your physical unease.
