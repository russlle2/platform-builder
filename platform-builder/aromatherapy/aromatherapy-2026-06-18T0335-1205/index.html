How can Aromatherapy specifically help with college anxiety and academic stress?
Our Aromatherapy sessions utilize specific essential oils known to downregulate the sympathetic nervous system, which is often overactive during periods of stress and anxiety. Is Aromatherapy a safe and effective complement to other support I might be receiving for my emotional well-being?
Yes, Aromatherapy can be a powerful and gentle complement to other forms of support, including therapy or counseling. We always encourage open communication about any other care you are receiving. Our trauma-informed approach ensures that your Aromatherapy experience is supportive and integrates seamlessly into your overall emotional healing path.
