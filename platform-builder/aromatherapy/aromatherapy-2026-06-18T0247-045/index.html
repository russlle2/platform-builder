Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is Aromatherapy and how can it help me?
Aromatherapy is a holistic healing modality that uses natural plant extracts, known as essential oils, to promote health and well-being. It can help you manage stress, improve sleep, boost mood, alleviate discomfort, and support emotional balance by interacting with your body's systems through scent and topical application.
Are the essential oils used in your Aromatherapy practice safe?
Yes, we exclusively use therapeutic-grade, ethically sourced essential oils from trusted suppliers. During your consultation, we carefully consider your health history and sensitivities to ensure every blend is safe and appropriate for your individual needs. Your safety and well-being are our highest priorities.
How long does an Aromatherapy consultation typically last?
An initial Aromatherapy consultation usually lasts between 60 to 75 minutes. This allows ample time for us to thoroughly discuss your health goals, assess your needs, and begin the process of formulating your personalized essential oil blend. Follow-up sessions are generally shorter, around 30-45 minutes.
Can Aromatherapy replace my current medical treatments?
Aromatherapy is intended to complement, not replace, conventional medical treatments. We encourage you to continue consulting with your healthcare provider for any health concerns. Our practice aims to support your overall well-being and can be a wonderful adjunct to your existing health regimen.
