What exactly is Aromatherapy and how can it help me?

Aromatherapy is a holistic healing treatment that uses natural plant extracts to promote health and well-being. It can support your emotional, physical, and mental states by utilizing the therapeutic properties of essential oils, helping with stress, sleep, energy, and more.

Are the essential oils used in your Aromatherapy blends safe?

Yes, we prioritize your safety and well-being. We use only high-quality, pure essential oils from reputable suppliers. During your consultation, we discuss your health history and any sensitivities to ensure your custom blends are formulated safely and appropriately for your unique needs.

How do I use my personalized Aromatherapy blends?

The usage depends on your specific blend and its intended purpose. We provide clear, personalized instructions for each custom blend, which may include diffusing, topical application (diluted in a carrier oil), or inhalation methods. We ensure you feel confident and comfortable with your new aromatic tools.

What makes Golden Hour Aromatherapy different from buying oils online?

Unlike mass-produced oils, Golden Hour Aromatherapy offers a deeply personalized experience. Your blends are not generic; they are meticulously crafted after a thorough consultation to address your individual concerns and preferences, ensuring maximum efficacy and a truly bespoke wellness solution tailored just for you.
