Aromatherapy

Scent Rituals to Support Your Posture Awareness

When you're recovering, it can be challenging to notice how you're holding your body. Our Aromatherapy practices use carefully chosen scents to gently bring your attention to your posture. These simple rituals can help you become more aware of tension and find subtle ways to release it, fostering a sense of grounding and stability in your everyday movements. No prior experience with essential oils is needed – we'll guide you every step of the way.
