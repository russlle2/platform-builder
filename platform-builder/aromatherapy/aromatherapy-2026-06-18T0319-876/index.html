Frequently Asked Questions

How can Aromatherapy help with postural health when I'm recovering?
Aromatherapy can gently support your postural health by engaging your sense of smell to bring awareness to your body. Certain scents can help you notice where you might be holding tension, encourage more relaxed breathing, or simply provide a calming presence that allows you to tune into your physical sensations more easily. It's about building a gentle connection to your body's signals.
I'm new to Aromatherapy. Do I need any special knowledge?
Absolutely not! You don't need any prior experience with essential oils or wellness practices. is designed to be gentle and accessible. will guide you through everything, explaining how to use the scents safely and effectively, and helping you find what feels most comfortable and supportive for your body.
Is Aromatherapy safe to use during my recovery from illness or injury?
Your safety and comfort are our top priorities. We always take into account your specific health situation, current medications, and sensitivities. All essential oils are diluted appropriately and selected for their gentle properties. We'll have a detailed conversation about your health history before recommending any blends or practices to ensure everything is supportive and safe for you.
What does a typical Aromatherapy session for postural comfort look like?
A session with is a relaxed conversation where we discuss your current comfort levels, any challenges you're experiencing with your posture, and your recovery goals. Based on this, will introduce you to a selection of gentle essential oils, helping you discover scents that resonate with you. We'll then create a personalized plan, which might include custom blends or simple scent rituals you can easily do at home to foster greater body awareness.
