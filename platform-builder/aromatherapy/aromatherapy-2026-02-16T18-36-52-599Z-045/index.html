Myths unbottled, truths diffused

Aromatherapy is often misunderstood. Here’s what to actually expect.

Myth: Essential oils are a cure-all.

Some marketing suggests essential oils can treat medical conditions. We avoid clinical claims and focus on sensory practice, wellbeing, and supportive rituals.

Truth: Oils can support routines safely.

When used mindfully—diluted properly, patch-tested, and used with pet and pregnancy considerations—essential oils can enrich mood and ritual without replacing medical care.

Questions people ask

Short, safety-forward answers to common concerns.

We recommend dilution guidelines by product type and use: typical topical blends are 1–3% for adults (6–18 drops per 30ml carrier). Children, older adults, and sensitive skin often need lower dilutions. Specific guidance will be provided during consults. These are safety-oriented best practices, not medical prescriptions.

A patch test helps identify skin sensitivity. Apply a diluted drop to a small area and wait 24 hours; if irritation occurs, avoid that oil topically. Patch testing is an easy, common-sense step to reduce risk.

Some oils can affect animals differently. We provide pet-safety notes and alternatives. When diffusing, ensure pets can leave the space and consult your veterinarian for specific concerns. We never recommend practices that may harm animals.

Pregnancy changes physiology and sensitivity. We avoid certain oils and concentrations during pregnancy and always provide pregnancy-specific guidance. Always consult your healthcare provider if you have questions.
