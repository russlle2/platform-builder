FAQ — practical notes

How do you decide a dilution?
Dilution is set by age, skin sensitivity, pregnancy status, and intended use. Typical topical blends range 0.5–3% for adults; roll-ons often 1–2%.
Do you require a patch test?
Yes—patch tests are recommended for topical applications. We guide you through a safe protocol during the session.
Are your sessions pet-friendly?
We always assume pets are in the home and will propose pet-safe alternatives; certain oils are avoided for cats and small animals.
Pregnancy & nursing?
Specific oils are avoided in pregnancy and breastfeeding. We tailor sessions with that safety lens—always consult your healthcare provider for medical questions.
