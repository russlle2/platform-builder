Safety notes & FAQ

Dilution: Typical aromatic use is between 0.5–3% for adults depending on application and oil potency. We always provide exact percentages for topical blends.

Patch test: Recommended for any topical product—apply a small diluted amount to forearm and observe for 24 hours.

Pets: Certain oils can be harmful to cats and other animals; we offer pet-safe substitutions and diffusing guidelines.

Pregnancy & nursing: Some oils are avoided. We adapt blends and always flag contraindicated ingredients.
