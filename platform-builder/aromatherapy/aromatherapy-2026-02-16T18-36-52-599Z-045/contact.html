Myths & Truths — straight talk

Aromatherapy is rich in folklore. Here’s a clear, safety-focused take.
Myth:
Essential oils cure disease.
Truth:
They may support comfort and a positive sensory environment but are not a replacement for medical care. Always consult medical professionals for health issues.
Myth:
If it’s natural, it’s always safe.
Truth:
Many oils are potent. Dilution, patch testing, and context (pets, pregnancy, medications) matter.

FAQ — quick guidance

How do I dilute oils safely?
We recommend 0.5–3% for topical blends for adults depending on use-case. I provide clear dilution tables with every custom blend.
Should I patch test?
Yes. Apply a tiny amount of the diluted blend to the inner forearm and wait 24–48 hours. Stop use if irritation occurs.
Are essential oils safe around pets?
Some oils are not safe for pets. Diffusion in short, supervised bursts and avoiding direct topical use on animals helps reduce risk. Share your pet’s species and health notes before use.
What about pregnancy?
Certain oils are not recommended during pregnancy. If you are pregnant or breastfeeding, we’ll use pregnancy-safe protocols and coordinate with your care team as needed.
