Frequently asked — safety first

How do I dilute essential oils?

Dilution depends on age, skin sensitivity, and purpose. Typical aromatherapy practice uses carrier oils (e.g., jojoba, sweet almond). General starting points: 0.5–1% for facial applications, 1–3% for short-term topical use. Your practitioner will advise specific ratios; never apply undiluted essential oil to skin.

What is a patch test?

A patch test helps identify skin sensitivity. Apply a diluted sample to a small skin area and wait 24–48 hours. If irritation appears, discontinue use and consult a clinician.

Are essential oils safe around pets?

Some oils can be irritating to animals. When using diffusers, ensure animals can leave the area, ventilate spaces well, and avoid diffusing in enclosed spaces frequented by pets. We provide pet-safe suggestions during sessions.

Pregnancy, nursing, and health conditions?

Certain oils are not recommended during pregnancy or for people with specific health conditions. Always inform your practitioner of pregnancy, nursing, medications, or medical diagnoses. Aromatherapy is complementary and not a substitute for professional medical advice.
