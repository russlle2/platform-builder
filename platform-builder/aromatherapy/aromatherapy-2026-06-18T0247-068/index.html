Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is Aromatherapy and how can it benefit me?
Aromatherapy is a holistic healing treatment that uses natural plant extracts to promote health and well-being. It can benefit you by helping to reduce stress, improve sleep, uplift mood, alleviate discomfort, and support overall emotional and physical balance. We'll explore how specific essential oils can address your individual concerns.
Are the essential oils you use in your Aromatherapy practice safe?
Yes, your safety is our top priority. We use only high-quality, ethically sourced, and pure essential oils. During your consultation, we'll discuss any sensitivities or health conditions you may have to ensure that all blends and applications are tailored to be safe and effective for your specific needs.
How long does an Aromatherapy consultation typically last?
A typical initial Aromatherapy consultation lasts approximately 60-90 minutes. This allows us ample time to understand your health history, discuss your well-being goals, and collaboratively create a personalized essential oil blend and usage plan that truly supports you.
Can Aromatherapy replace my current medical treatments?
Aromatherapy is intended to complement, not replace, conventional medical treatments. We encourage you to continue any prescribed medical care and always consult with your healthcare provider regarding any health concerns. Our practice aims to support your well-being holistically alongside your existing health regimen.
