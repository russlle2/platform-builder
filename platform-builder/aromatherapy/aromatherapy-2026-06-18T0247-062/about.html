What exactly is Aromatherapy and how can it benefit me?

Aromatherapy uses aromatic plant extracts (essential oils) to promote health and well-being. It can help you with stress reduction, improved sleep, mood enhancement, pain relief, and much more, all through natural, plant-based remedies.

Are essential oils safe to use, especially if I have sensitivities?

When used correctly and with professional guidance, essential oils are generally safe. We prioritize your safety by conducting a thorough consultation to understand any sensitivities or health conditions you may have, ensuring your blends are perfectly suited for you.

How do you determine which essential oils are right for my specific needs?

Your personalized Aromatherapy journey begins with a detailed discussion about your health goals, lifestyle, and preferences. Based on this, Dr. Morgan Ellis carefully selects and blends essential oils known for their therapeutic properties that align with your individual requirements.

What can I expect during an Aromatherapy consultation?

During your consultation, you'll have a warm and open conversation with Dr. Morgan Ellis. We'll discuss your current well-being, any concerns you have, and your aspirations for health. This collaborative process allows us to craft a personalized Aromatherapy plan and custom blend just for you.
