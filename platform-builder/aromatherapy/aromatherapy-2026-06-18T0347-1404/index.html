Frequently Asked Questions

How can Aromatherapy specifically address the mental fatigue I experience after my chronic illness?
After chronic illness, mental fatigue often stems from systemic inflammation and nervous system dysregulation. Specific essential oils, like certain citruses or evergreens, can stimulate alertness and support healthy brain circulation, helping to gently lift the veil of weariness and restore a sense of mental stamina. Your personalized blend will focus on these restorative properties, tailored to your unique needs.
Will Aromatherapy help me regain my focus and concentration, which have suffered since my injury?
Yes, Aromatherapy can be a potent ally in restoring focus and concentration. Certain essential oils are known for their neuro-stimulative qualities, encouraging clearer thought processes and sustained attention. We will select essences that help to calm mental chatter and anchor your awareness, allowing you to re-engage with tasks and conversations with greater presence and mental acuity.
I'm sensitive to strong smells. How will my Aromatherapy blend be formulated to avoid overwhelming me in my recovery?
Your comfort and well-being are paramount, especially during recovery. We will begin with very gentle dilutions and mild, well-tolerated essential oils, carefully chosen based on your sensitivities and preferences. The goal is subtle, supportive aromatic presence, not an overpowering scent. We'll work collaboratively to find the perfect, delicate balance that nurtures your system.
Beyond just feeling calmer, what tangible cognitive benefits can I expect from consistent Aromatherapy practice?
With consistent and tailored Aromatherapy practice, you can anticipate tangible improvements in several cognitive areas. Many clients report enhanced mental clarity, reduced 'brain fog', improved short-term recall, and a greater capacity for sustained concentration. The subtle yet profound influence of these botanicals aims to support your brain's natural ability to restore function and maintain a more stable, alert state.
