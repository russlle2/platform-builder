How does Aromatherapy specifically address the stress experienced by entrepreneurs and high-achievers?

Your unique pressures often lead to chronic sympathetic nervous system activation. Our Aromatherapy protocols are precision-engineered to engage the parasympathetic system, promoting neurochemical balance and reducing the physiological markers of stress, without hindering your focus or drive. We select specific botanicals known for their evidence-based effects on cortisol reduction and amygdala modulation.

Is the efficacy of Aromatherapy for nervous system regulation scientifically supported?

Absolutely. We rely on a foundation of scientific literature, examining studies on the impact of specific essential oil constituents on neurochemistry, heart rate variability, and stress hormone levels. Your treatment plan will incorporate botanicals with documented efficacy in promoting relaxation and balanced nervous system function, ensuring a clinical yet compassionate approach.
