Safety & common questions

Below are short answers to frequently raised topics. These are general safety recommendations, not medical advice. If you are pregnant, nursing, under a physician's care, or managing a medical condition, please consult your healthcare provider before using essential oils.

Dilution & application

We recommend topical dilution for adults commonly at 1–3% for daily use. For sensitive skin or new oils, start lower. Always follow blend-specific recommendations and perform a patch test first.

Patch test

Apply a small diluted drop on inner forearm, cover for 24 hours, and observe for irritation. Discontinue if irritation occurs. Patch testing is a simple and effective precaution.

Pregnancy & nursing

Some oils are contraindicated or require caution in pregnancy and lactation. Consult your healthcare provider and mention pregnancy during any consultation. We will provide pregnancy-safe options when applicable.

Pets in the home

Certain essential oils can be harmful to pets, especially cats and some small mammals. Use diffusers with care, avoid direct application to pets, and ensure separate, well-ventilated spaces when diffusing. Ask about pet-safe alternatives during a consult.

Common myths — clarified

M
Myth: Natural always means safe.
Truth: Natural substances can be potent. Dose, route, and quality matter. We emphasize dosing, testing, and contraindications.
M
Myth: One blend fits everyone.
Truth: We tailor blends to individual context, sensitivity, and goals. Customization reduces risk and increases satisfaction.
