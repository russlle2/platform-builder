Client preferred warm citrus notes but had sensitive skin. Offered a very low dilution inhalation strategy and a 0.5% topical blend for limited-area use. Frequently asked

How do you stay current?

Regular continuing education, reading recent safety summaries, and peer consultation inform my practice.

Can I purchase blends directly?

Yes — bespoke blends are available through the shop and by consult. Pre-made kits are also offered for safe at-home use.

What if I have pets at home?

I provide alternatives and diffusion protocols that minimize exposure to vulnerable animals; consult your vet if you have specific concerns.

Pregnancy considerations?

Pregnancy requires tailored avoidance lists and often conservative or alternative approaches; we always screen and adapt.
