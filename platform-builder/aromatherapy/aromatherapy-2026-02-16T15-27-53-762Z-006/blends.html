Safety reminder

Blends are tailored to preferences and safety. Always share pregnancy status, medications, and pet ownership before a custom formulation. I provide dilution guidance and recommend patch testing.

Common questions

What does “supports” mean?
A non-medical term indicating that a blend may help foster a sensory state (e.g., relaxation, focus) as part of a larger routine.
Are these safe for children?
Conservative dilutions and child-safe alternatives are available; avoid certain oils and strong concentrations. Always consult before applying to children.
Interested in a custom blend?
I will tailor notes and dilution to your needs and provide step-by-step safety guidance.
