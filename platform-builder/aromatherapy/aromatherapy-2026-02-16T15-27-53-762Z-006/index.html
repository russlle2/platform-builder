Frequently asked — safe use reminders

How do I dilute correctly?

Dilution depends on age, sensitivity, and application site. Typical adult topical dilutions range from 0.5%–5%. Your practitioner will recommend a safe percentage and show how to measure it. Never apply neat (undiluted) to skin.

What is a patch test?

Apply a small diluted amount to the inner forearm, cover, and observe for 24 hours. If redness, itching, or irritation appears, discontinue use and consult your practitioner.

Are essential oils safe around pets?

Some oils affect animals differently; avoid direct application and diffuse with caution. Veterinary consultation is recommended before introducing oils into a household with pets.

Can pregnant or breastfeeding clients use oils?

Pregnancy and breastfeeding have unique safety considerations. Consult before use; certain oils and exposures are contraindicated. Your practitioner screens and recommends alternatives.

How long until I notice effects?

Aroma preferences and perceived outcomes are subjective. We prioritize gradual, trackable changes and avoid promises of clinical cure.

Emergency contact

For acute reactions, stop use, wash skin with mild soap, and contact local medical services. Keep {{PHONE}} and your practitioner contact accessible for non-emergent follow-up.
