Safety-first shopping
All products include dilution guidance and patch-test instructions. Avoid applying concentrated oils directly to skin. Keep essential oils away from eyes and mucous membranes. For pregnancy, pets, or medical concerns, consult a qualified provider before use.
FAQ — common safety topics
Dilution: Most topical products are 1–3% for adults; child-safe dilutions are lower. Exact percentages are listed per product.
Patch test: Apply a small diluted amount on inner forearm for 24 hours; discontinue if irritation appears.
Pets: Some oils can be harmful to animals. Use low concentrations, diffuse in well-ventilated spaces, and allow pets to leave the room.
Pregnancy & breastfeeding: Many oils are used cautiously or avoided; contact me before ordering if you are pregnant or nursing.
