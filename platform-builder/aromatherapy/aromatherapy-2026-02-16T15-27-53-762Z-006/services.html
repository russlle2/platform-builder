Frequently asked safety questions

Dilution — how should I dilute essential oils?
Common practice: 1–3% for adults in topical roll-ons and creams. For sensitive skin or older adults, consider 0.5–1%. I provide exact dilution for each product and recommend a patch test.
Patch testing — why and how?
Apply a small diluted amount to the inner forearm and wait 24 hours. If irritation appears, discontinue use. Always patch test before new blends.
Pets — are essential oils safe around animals?
Some oils can be irritants to pets, especially cats. Use low concentration, provide escape routes, and consult a veterinarian for concerns. I avoid recommending direct application for pets.
Pregnancy & breastfeeding — any cautions?
Certain oils are best avoided or used at very low dilution during pregnancy. I follow conservative safety lists and will tailor all recommendations if you are pregnant or nursing.
