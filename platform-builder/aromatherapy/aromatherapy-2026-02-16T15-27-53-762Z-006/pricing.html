Practical questions

How do you recommend dilutions?

Dilutions are set by age and use-case. Typical adult topical dilutions are 1–3% for roll-ons; inhalation and diffusion use tiny amounts. You will receive explicit dilution guidance with each blend.

What about patch testing?

A patch test is recommended for all topical applications: 24–48 hours of observation on a small skin area before regular use.

Are essential oils safe around pets?

Some compounds affect animals differently. We include pet-safe guidance and alternatives; always consult your veterinarian for specific concerns.

Pregnancy and breastfeeding?

Certain oils are avoided in pregnancy and postpartum. I screen for pregnancy and nursing status and provide pregnancy-safe recommendations when relevant.
