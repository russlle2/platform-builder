Frequently Asked Questions

Are your blends safe with prescription medications?
Absolutely. We conduct comprehensive medication interaction checks during consultation. All blends use single-herb or evidence-based botanical combinations with known safety profiles, avoiding high-risk interactions. We prioritize open communication with your healthcare team.
How long is a typical session?
Initial consultations last 60 minutes for in-depth assessment. Subsequent sessions are 45 minutes. We structure time to ensure thorough understanding of your needs before formulating your unique blend, with follow-up check-ins to optimize results.
Can you create blends for sensitive skin?
Yes. We use carrier oil dilution protocols based on your skin's tolerance. All blends undergo patch testing before full application. We prioritize gentle, non-irritating bases like jojoba or fractionated coconut oil for sensitive skin types.
How do you determine which botanicals to use?
Based on your physiological markers, not just preferences. We analyze your symptom patterns, environmental factors, and bio-individual needs. For example, digestive issues may require ginger and peppermint (with specific ratios), while sleep disruption might use passionflower with specific terpene profiles.
