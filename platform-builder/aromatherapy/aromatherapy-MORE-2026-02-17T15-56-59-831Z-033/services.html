Common questions

Are these sessions safe?
Safety is central. We review allergies, medications, pregnancy, and pets. Suggestions are framed as supportive and non-medical.
What about dilution and patch testing?
Every blend comes with a recommended dilution and simple patch-test instructions. Always try a small patch and wait 24 hours before regular use.
Do you work with clients who have pets or are pregnant?
Yes, but recommendations will be adjusted. We avoid advising on medical conditions and instead offer scent choices and practical cautions tailored to your context.
How do I prepare for a session?
Bring notes on any fragrance sensitivities, pets, and current routines. There's a short intake form after booking.
