Common Questions

Are these approaches medical treatments?

No. Aromatherapy approaches described here may support wellbeing but are not medical care. Always consult a healthcare professional for medical concerns.

How do you handle dilution and patch testing?

We recommend safe dilutions for topical use and demonstrate patch testing before regular application. Typical carrier ratios and stepwise testing are provided for home use.

What about pets and pregnancy?

Some essential oils are not appropriate for pets or pregnancy. We review alternatives and avoidance strategies as part of every session.
