Common questions

Are there refunds or rescheduling rules?

We ask for 48 hours notice for reschedules. Refund policies vary by program; full details are provided at booking.

Safety notes — dilution, patch tests, pregnancy and pets

All recommendations are safety-forward. We outline dilution ratios for topical products and suggest patch testing on a small skin area before regular use. If you are pregnant, breastfeeding, or managing health conditions, we advise consulting your care provider before using essential oils. Some oils are not recommended around pets; we will provide pet-safe guidance and alternatives.

What does "may support" mean?

We do not make medical claims. Language such as "may support" points to traditional or experiential uses rather than guaranteed outcomes.
