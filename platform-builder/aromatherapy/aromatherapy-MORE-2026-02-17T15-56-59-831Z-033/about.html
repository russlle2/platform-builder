Why we craft scent with care

We combine plant knowledge and practical, safe guidance so scent can be an accessible, everyday tool. Our work centers on clear instructions, thoughtful ingredient choices, and respect for diverse bodies and households.

ME

Meet the practitioner

Aromatic practice built on study and lived experience. Training in safe dilution, allergy awareness, and practical blending informs every offering.

Our method

1. Gentle intake
We listen to your priorities and any sensitivities, then map options that are low-risk and practical.
2. Tailored options
Choices are concrete: suggested dilutions, how to store, how to use around pets, and what to avoid during pregnancy or with certain medications.
3. Take-home clarity
You leave with written notes and labels that make safe use straightforward.
