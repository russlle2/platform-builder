FAQ & safety notes

Is aromatherapy safe for everyone?

Essential oils are potent. They may support sensory routines for many people but are not a substitute for medical care. Avoid ingestion unless under qualified supervision. If pregnant, nursing, or managing serious health conditions, consult a healthcare provider before use.

Do I need to dilute oils?

Yes — dilute essential oils appropriately before topical use. Typical dilution ranges from 0.5%–3% for daily use for most adults; lower concentrations for delicate populations. We recommend a patch test and always keep oils out of eyes and mucous membranes.

What about pets?

Many animals, especially cats and small mammals, can be sensitive to concentrated essential oils. Use diffusers sparingly, ventilate spaces, and consult a veterinarian if you have concerns about interactions with pets.

How do I patch-test?

Mix a small dilution on carrier oil and apply a pea-sized amount to a forearm. Wait 24 hours. If irritation, redness, or discomfort appears, discontinue use. When in doubt, choose lower concentrations or select oils known for gentler profiles.
