Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is Aromatherapy and how can it help you?
Aromatherapy is a holistic healing modality that uses natural plant extracts, known as essential oils, to promote health and well-being. It can support your emotional state, alleviate discomfort, improve sleep, and boost your overall vitality by interacting with your body's systems through scent and topical application.
Are the essential oils used in your Aromatherapy practice safe for you?
Absolutely. We prioritize your safety and well-being above all else. We use only high-quality, pure essential oils from reputable suppliers. During your consultation, we'll thoroughly review your health history and any sensitivities to ensure that all recommendations are perfectly safe and appropriate for your unique circumstances.
How is a personalized Aromatherapy blend created for your needs?
Your personalized blend begins with a detailed consultation where we discuss your current health goals, lifestyle, and preferences. Based on this, we carefully select specific essential oils known for their therapeutic properties that align with your needs. These are then expertly combined in a safe and effective carrier, ensuring a custom solution just for you.
What can you expect during your first Aromatherapy consultation?
During your initial consultation, you can expect a warm and attentive discussion about your wellness aspirations and any challenges you're facing. We'll explore your health history, explain the principles of Aromatherapy, and answer all your questions. Our goal is to understand you fully so we can collaboratively create a meaningful and effective aromatic path forward.
