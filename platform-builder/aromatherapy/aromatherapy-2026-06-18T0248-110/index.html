Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is Aromatherapy?
Aromatherapy is a holistic healing treatment that uses natural plant extracts, known as essential oils, to promote health and well-being. It is a complementary therapy designed to enhance your physical and emotional health through the therapeutic use of aromatic compounds.
How can Aromatherapy benefit me?
Aromatherapy can offer a wide range of benefits, including stress reduction, improved sleep quality, pain management, mood enhancement, and support for respiratory health. Your specific benefits will depend on your individual needs and the essential oils chosen for your personalized blend.
Are essential oils safe to use?
When used correctly and with professional guidance, essential oils are generally safe. We prioritize your safety and will always provide clear instructions on proper dilution and application methods for your personalized Aromatherapy blends, ensuring you use them effectively and without risk.
What should I expect during an Aromatherapy consultation?
During your consultation, you will discuss your health history, current well-being goals, and any concerns you wish to address. This allows us to understand your unique needs and create a bespoke Aromatherapy plan and custom essential oil blend tailored specifically for you.
