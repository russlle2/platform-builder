Frequently asked — pricing & safety

Do prices include blends and materials?
Starter consultations are session-only; Essential and VIP include a small take-home kit detailed on booking pages.
What about dilution and patch tests?
Every formula includes a dilution recommendation and a step-by-step patch-test. Always test on a small area and wait 24 hours for reactions.
Are blends safe around pets?
Many oils can affect pets. I provide pet-safety notes and alternatives; do not diffuse near animals without prior guidance.
Pregnancy and sensitive populations?
I avoid recommending oils contraindicated in pregnancy and will adapt blends for sensitive cases. Consult your healthcare provider for medical concerns.
