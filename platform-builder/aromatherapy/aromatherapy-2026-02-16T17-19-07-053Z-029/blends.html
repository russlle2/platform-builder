FAQ — safety essentials

Dilution guidelines?
Standard adult dilutions for roll-ons and topical blends are typically 1–3% depending on use and sensitivity. We provide exact drop counts per bottle size during consultations. Children and sensitive adults need lower concentrations.
How to patch test?
Apply a small amount of diluted oil on the inner forearm, cover if needed, and observe for 24 hours. If redness or irritation occurs, discontinue use.
What about pets?
Some essential oils can affect animals. We recommend careful diffuser use, short sessions, room ventilation, and avoiding oils known to be unsafe for specific species. Ask during intake for pet-safe alternatives.
Pregnancy & nursing considerations?
Certain oils and high-concentration protocols are contraindicated in pregnancy or breastfeeding. If pregnant or nursing, consult with your healthcare provider and inform your practitioner to receive an adapted plan.
