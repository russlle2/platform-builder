Frequently asked safety questions

How should I dilute essential oils?
Dilution guidance is individualized, but a common safe starting point is 1–2% for adults in topical blends. We always review age, medical history, and skin sensitivity before recommending dilution. This is educational guidance and not medical advice.
Do I need to do a patch test?
Yes. We recommend a patch test on forearm skin (diluted blend) and observing for 24 hours. Stop if irritation occurs. We will walk you through patch testing during sessions.
What about pets and essential oils?
Many oils can affect animals differently. We prioritize pet-safe alternatives and diffusion practices that keep pets safe: short diffusion windows, pet-free rooms, and ventilation. Always consult your veterinarian for pet medical concerns.
Is aromatherapy safe during pregnancy?
Pregnancy requires extra caution. Some oils and doses are not recommended. We provide pregnancy-safe alternatives and will adapt protocols; we do not provide medical advice and recommend consulting your care provider for specific health questions.
How should I store oils?
Store in dark glass, away from heat and direct light, out of reach of children. Label blends with dilution and date; best practice is to refresh blends every 6–12 months depending on oil chemistry.
