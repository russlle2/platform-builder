FAQs — safety-first notes

How do I dilute essential oils for topical use?
We recommend starting at 1–2% for most adults (6–12 drops of essential oil per 30 mL carrier oil) unless otherwise directed during a session. Children, older adults, and sensitive skin require lower dilutions. Always follow your practitioner guidance.
What is a patch test and why do it?
Apply a diluted drop to inner forearm, cover for 24 hours, and watch for redness or irritation. Discontinue if any sensitivity appears.
Are essential oils safe around pets?
Some oils can be harmful to pets. We review pet exposure during intake and provide pet-safe options and diffuser use guidance. Keep concentrated oils out of reach of animals.
What about pregnancy & medical conditions?
Certain oils and protocols are contraindicated in pregnancy or specific health conditions. We always screen for pregnancy and refer to primary care or certified healthcare professionals when needed.
