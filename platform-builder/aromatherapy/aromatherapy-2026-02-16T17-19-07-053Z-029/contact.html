Frequently asked safety questions

Dilution guidance
General dilution recommendations are provided with every formula. For topical blends we often suggest 1–3% for adults as a starting point; children and sensitive skin require lower rates. Always follow the provided label.
Patch testing
Patch test new blends on a small skin area for 24 hours to check for irritation. Discontinue use if redness or discomfort occurs.
Pets and essential oils
Some oils can be sensitive to pets. We provide pet-safe recommendations and diffusing guidance; avoid applying oils directly to pets. Consult your veterinarian for medical concerns.
Pregnancy and breastfeeding
Certain oils are not recommended during pregnancy or lactation. We screen requests and will suggest alternatives or refer to trusted clinical resources. We do not provide medical advice.
