Frequently asked (safety essentials)

How do you handle dilution recommendations?
I provide clear dilution charts with each formula and explain how to measure and label blends at home.
What's your patch test approach?
Patch tests are always recommended: apply a small amount diluted on the inner forearm and monitor for 24 hours.
Are essential oils safe around pets?
Some oils are not recommended near animals. I offer pet-friendly alternatives and use diffusion practices that minimize exposure.
Can you work with pregnancy?
I adapt recommendations for pregnancy and sensitive clients and avoid oils with known contraindications; I do not provide medical advice.
