Shop FAQs

Dilution and product strength
Pre-diluted roll-ons are created at practitioner-safe concentrations. If you need a lower dilution, request a custom blend during booking. Always patch test.
Patch testing
Apply a small amount on your inner forearm for 24 hours. If any redness or irritation appears, discontinue use and wash with a mild cleanser.
Pets and scent products
Pets can be sensitive to many oils. We label pet-safe options and recommend short diffuser sessions, fresh air, and avoiding direct contact. Ask for a pet-safe list if you share your home with animals.
Pregnancy & nursing notes
Certain essential oils and strong concentrations are not recommended. If pregnant or nursing, please consult your healthcare provider and inform us prior to purchasing a custom product.
