Frequently Asked Questions

How can Aromatherapy specifically help me during a major life transition?
Aromatherapy offers a gentle way to engage with your emotions during significant life changes. Specific botanical scents can help to calm an overwhelmed nervous system, promote a sense of stability, and create new, supportive emotional anchors as you adapt to your new circumstances. It's about finding subtle, aromatic allies to steady your emotional core.
I'm new to wellness practices and Aromatherapy. Is this suitable for me?
Absolutely. Our approach is designed to be accessible and gentle, requiring no prior wellness experience. We guide you through each step, ensuring you feel comfortable and understood. The focus is on finding what feels right and supportive for you, making Aromatherapy an approachable tool for emotional care during any transition.
What does 'trauma-informed' Aromatherapy mean in practice?
Trauma-informed Aromatherapy means we prioritize your emotional safety and autonomy. We move at your pace, offering choices and respecting your boundaries. Our recommendations are always gentle, never overwhelming, and focus on supporting your nervous system and promoting a sense of security, particularly when navigating sensitive emotional landscapes.
Can Aromatherapy help with feelings of grief or anxiety related to change?
Yes, Aromatherapy can be a comforting presence when experiencing feelings of grief, anxiety, or unease that often accompany major life transitions. We select essential oils known for their soothing and grounding properties, creating blends that offer a sense of solace and support your emotional regulation during these tender times. It's a way to offer your system gentle, aromatic comfort.
