Frequently Asked Questions

How can Aromatherapy specifically support my emotional well-being during postpartum recovery?
Aromatherapy can offer a gentle, non-invasive way to support your emotional landscape during postpartum recovery. We focus on blends that can help quiet an overactive mind, provide a sense of calm amidst overwhelm, or gently uplift your spirit when feelings of sadness arise. Our approach is trauma-informed, meaning we prioritize your comfort and safety, crafting experiences that feel supportive rather than overwhelming, acknowledging the unique sensitivities of this time.
Do I need any prior experience with essential oils or 'wellness' practices to benefit from your Aromatherapy?
Absolutely not. Our practice is designed to be accessible and gentle for everyone, especially those new to exploring aromatic support. We guide you every step of the way, explaining the qualities of each botanical and ensuring you feel comfortable and understood. There's no pressure to have any background knowledge; just an openness to receiving gentle support is all that's needed.
Is your Aromatherapy approach suitable for someone experiencing the emotional tender spots of childbirth or early motherhood?
Yes, our Aromatherapy practice is rooted in trauma-informed care, specifically acknowledging the emotional sensitivities that can arise from childbirth and early motherhood. We create a safe and nurturing space, using gentle botanicals to help soothe and process complex emotions. Your comfort and emotional safety are our highest priorities, and we work at your pace, always.
How do you ensure the Aromatherapy blends are safe for me as a new mother and for my baby?
Safety is paramount, especially during the postpartum period. We use only high-quality, pure essential oils and carrier oils, always diluting them appropriately. We take a detailed intake to understand your unique health profile, any sensitivities, and your baby's presence. Our recommendations are carefully considered to ensure they are gentle and supportive for both you and your little one, avoiding any potentially irritating or contraindicated oils.
