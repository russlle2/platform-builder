Safety & common questions

How do I dilute these blends?

Typical topical dilutions vary by age and intended use. We provide specific dilution guidance per blend during consultations. For general reference: adults often use 1–3% for daily topical blends; reduce for children and sensitive skin. This information is educational, not medical advice.

Do I need to patch test?

Yes: patch testing is a simple, recommended step before regular topical use. Apply a diluted small amount to an inconspicuous area and wait 24–48 hours for reactions.

Are these blends pet friendly?

Some plants and oils can be problematic for pets. We suggest using diffusers in well-ventilated spaces, keeping them away from sleeping areas, and selecting pet-compatible options when needed. Discuss your household pets during a session.

Pregnancy & breastfeeding — what should I know?

Certain oils should be avoided or used with caution in pregnancy and breastfeeding. We screen for this and will customize blends accordingly. Always consult your healthcare provider in conjunction with aromatherapy guidance.
