Common Questions

How do you handle dilution?
Dilution is tailored to the client and intended use. For topical products we provide clear percentages and patch-test instructions. We never suggest internal use.
Do I need a patch test?
Yes — for topical blends we recommend a patch test 24–48 hours before regular use. We'll walk you through the steps during your session.
Are these safe around pets?
Some essential oils are not pet-friendly. We'll provide pet-safe alternatives and advice if you share your home with animals.
What about pregnancy?
Safety during pregnancy is important. We offer pregnancy-friendly options and avoid oils contraindicated in pregnancy. Always consult your healthcare provider on medical concerns.
