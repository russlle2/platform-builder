FAQ — practical notes

What is dilution and why does it matter?
Dilution is the percent of essential oil in a carrier. It's how we keep topical blends safe and reduce irritation. During sessions you receive exact percentages for your blend and its intended use.
How to do a patch test
Apply a small diluted drop to the inner forearm and wait 24–48 hours. If redness or itchiness occurs, discontinue use. We'll demonstrate during your session.
Are blends pet-safe?
Some oils are not recommended around pets. Tell me about your household and I will suggest pet-friendly formulations and diffusing practices.
Pregnancy & essential oils
Pregnancy safety is nuanced. I offer pregnancy-safe alternatives and avoid oils that are commonly contraindicated. For medical advice, consult your healthcare practitioner.
