How an intensive flows

  1. Intro & intake. Discuss history, sensitivities, and goals — we document allergies and medications for safety.
  2. Sensory session. Guided sniffing and micro-blending with non-contact sampling techniques.
  3. Formulate & plan. We create a blend with clear dilution %, patch test reminders, and labelled takeaways.

Common questions

Dilution depends on age and skin sensitivity. We recommend a standard 1–3% dilution for adults for daily wearable blends and lower (0.5–1%) for sensitive skin or older adults. Specific guidance is provided with every formula.
A patch test places a small diluted amount of a blend on the inner forearm for 24 hours to check for irritation. We always include simple patch-test instructions with new blends.
Some oils can be irritating to animals. We avoid recommending diffusion near unsupervised pets and will advise pet-safe alternatives when requested. Never apply oils directly to animals.
Pregnancy requires extra care. We offer pregnancy-appropriate options and will only suggest oils recognized as commonly accepted by safety resources. Always consult your healthcare provider when pregnant or breastfeeding.
