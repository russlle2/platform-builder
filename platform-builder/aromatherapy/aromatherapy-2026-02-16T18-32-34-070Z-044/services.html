Questions I hear most

Dilution — what do you recommend?

We follow safe dilution guidelines (typical topical blends range from 1–5% depending on use, age, and sensitivity). Your session includes a personalized dilution plan. This is educational and not medical advice.

Do I need a patch test?

Patch testing is recommended for topical blends, especially if you have sensitive skin. We will demonstrate and document patch test instructions during the session.

What about pets?

We take pets seriously. Certain oils can be irritating; we’ll discuss safe placement of diffusers and pet-friendly choices. If you have severe pet sensitivities, we’ll adapt recommendations.

Pregnancy & breastfeeding notes

Some oils are best avoided in pregnancy or breastfeeding. We screen for pregnancy and will adjust blends accordingly. Always consult your healthcare provider alongside aromatherapy guidance.
