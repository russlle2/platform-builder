Shop FAQs — safety first

How should I dilute if I want to use oils topically?

We include basic dilution guidance with kits. Typical adult topical dilutions are 1–3% for everyday blends, reduced for sensitive skin, children, or pregnancy. Always follow the product leaflet and perform a patch test.

What’s a patch test and how do I do it?

Apply a diluted small amount to the inside of the forearm and monitor for 24–48 hours for redness or irritation. Discontinue if irritation occurs.

Are these products safe around pets?

Some oils may affect animals. Use diffusers in well-ventilated rooms, avoid prolonged exposure in small enclosed areas, and consult us if you have concerns about specific household pets.

Pregnancy & breastfeeding — are any products restricted?

Certain products contain oils we advise avoiding during pregnancy. Product notes will flag those oils; we recommend consulting your healthcare provider and contacting us for alternative suggestions.
