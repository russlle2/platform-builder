Frequently asked — contact edition
Do you take pets into account?
Yes. I ask about species, size, and sensitivities before suggesting blends. Many oils are strong for animals; I suggest pet-safe diffusion practices and strict separation when needed.
What about pregnancy & breastfeeding?
I use pregnancy-safe guidance and prefer to consult with your healthcare provider when in doubt. I will not recommend contraindicated oils during pregnancy or lactation.
How do I patch test?
A simple patch test: dilute a small amount of the blend (per guidance) and apply to a forearm patch. Watch for 24 hours for irritation. If in doubt, consult me before using.
