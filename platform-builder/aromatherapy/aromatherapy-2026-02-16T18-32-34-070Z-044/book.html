Booking FAQ

Dilution guidance — will I receive exact ratios?
Yes. For every topical product you receive, we include precise dilution percentages and instructions for safe application.
Patch test — when should I do one?
Perform a patch test 24–48 hours before regular use of a topical blend. We'll include a step-by-step in your confirmation email.
Pets in the home — what should I tell you?
Let us know species and typical behaviors. We avoid certain oils for households with dogs, cats, or birds and suggest safer diffusion and cleaning practices.
Pregnancy & sessions
If you are pregnant or nursing, mention this in the notes field. We will offer pregnancy-safe alternatives and avoid oils that are commonly contraindicated.
