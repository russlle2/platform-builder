How this practice works

Blending sensory attention with small, repeatable prompts that may support everyday shifts. Sessions center on scent education, personal mapping, and a take-home element you can use without fuss.

Foundational approach

I listen for patterns in what you notice, test gentle pairings, and offer a compact practice to try between sessions. Every plan includes dilution guidance and a patch test to keep things safe and comfortable.

What to expect in a session

  • Brief intake and safety check (incl. pregnancy, meds, pets).
  • Note exploration with inhalation sampling and mapping.
  • Creation of a simple, portable support (e.g., inhaler, roll-on).
  • Guided short ritual and micro-habits for practice.

Ethos & training

Work is informed by scent composition practices and clinical safety. Recommendations are crafted to be gentle and non-invasive — framed as practices that may support mood or focus, never medical advice.

Quick safety
Always perform a patch test. Keep oils diluted. If pregnant or caring for animals, we’ll adapt options. Ask about a pet-safe plan when booking.

Micro-habits to try

Small invitations that can slot into a day: a single scent inhale at transitions, a wrist-roll for ten seconds before a meeting, a quiet spritz on linens before rest. These are practices that may support a shift in attention.

FAQ

Patch test & dilution: I provide clear dilution ratios and we perform a patch test during sessions. If you have sensitive skin we will tailor the approach.
Pregnancy & meds: If you are pregnant, nursing, or taking medication, please mention this on booking; we will select appropriate options.
Pets: Some oils are unsafe for animals. Keep blends stored securely and avoid diffusing near pets unless cleared as pet-safe.
