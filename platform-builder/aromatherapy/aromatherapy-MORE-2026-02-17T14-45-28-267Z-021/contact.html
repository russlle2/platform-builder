Safety & dilution
All blend suggestions include recommended dilution ranges and a patch test. If you are pregnant, nursing, or have pets, mention that in your message — we’ll tailor options that emphasize low-volatility, skin-safe choices.
Pets & pregnancy
Some essential oils may be irritating to animals or contraindicated in pregnancy. We prioritize safety: always consult your care provider for personal medical questions and disclose pets/pregnancy in consults.
Patch test
Before using any topical blend, dilute as directed and perform a 24-hour patch test on a small area of skin. If irritation occurs, discontinue use.
