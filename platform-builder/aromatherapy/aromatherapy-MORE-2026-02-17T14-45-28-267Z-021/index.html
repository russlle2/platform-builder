Three micro-habits to try today

  1. One inhale pause — 3 deep breaths with a drop on a tissue. Patch test first; dilute appropriately.
  2. Desk mist — A light spritz of a water-based room atomizer away from pets and people with sensitivities.
  3. Evening bowl — A warmed ceramic bowl with an essential-oil-dampened cloth beside your pillow (avoid direct contact).

Safety: Patch test new blends, dilute for topical use, consult with a qualified clinician if you are pregnant or have serious health concerns. Keep oils away from pets' faces and food.

FAQ — safety & common questions

Is aromatherapy safe with pets?

Some oils can be irritating to animals. Keep blended products away from areas where pets eat or sleep, avoid direct application to pets, and consult a veterinarian for specific concerns.

What about pregnancy and nursing?

Some essential oils are not recommended during pregnancy or breastfeeding. We provide pregnancy-safe alternatives and advise consulting your health provider.

How should I dilute oils for topical use?

We recommend a gentle dilution: typically 1–2% for adults in daily-use blends (~6–12 drops per 30ml carrier). Always patch test and stop use if irritation occurs.
