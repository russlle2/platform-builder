Frequently Asked Questions

How does Aromatherapy specifically support cognitive function in seniors aged 60+?
Our Aromatherapy focuses on specific essential oils with documented neuroprotective and cognitive-enhancing properties. These botanicals can gently stimulate neural pathways, improve blood flow to the brain, and mitigate factors that contribute to mental fog, helping you maintain sharper memory and clearer thought processes.
Are there any contraindications or safety concerns for seniors using Aromatherapy?
Your safety is paramount. We conduct a thorough consultation to review your health history and any medications. We then formulate blends using highly diluted, gentle essential oils, avoiding any known contraindications for seniors, ensuring a safe and beneficial experience tailored to your unique needs.
What kind of results can I expect from consistent Aromatherapy for mental clarity?
With consistent and personalized Aromatherapy, many individuals report an improved sense of focus, reduced mental fatigue, enhanced memory recall, and a greater feeling of calm and presence. While results vary, the aim is to gently support and enhance your natural cognitive vitality.
How can I integrate Aromatherapy into my daily routine for optimal cognitive benefits?
We provide clear, simple guidance on incorporating your bespoke aromatherapy blend into your daily life. This might include gentle diffusion in your living space, a calming pulse point application, or an aromatic ritual designed to anchor your mind and enhance moments of focus throughout your day.
