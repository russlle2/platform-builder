Frequently Asked Questions

How does Aromatherapy specifically aid postural health after a chronic illness or injury?
Our aromatherapy approach uses specific essential oil blends to address the unique challenges of recovery. Certain oils can help relax over-tightened muscles that pull your body out of alignment, while others gently stimulate circulation to areas needing support. This helps your body remember and re-establish a more balanced and comfortable posture, reducing strain from compensatory patterns developed during illness or injury.
Will Aromatherapy help me become more aware of my body's alignment and sensations?
Absolutely. By engaging your sense of smell, aromatherapy creates a powerful, non-verbal connection to your internal state. We design specific aromatic 'cues' that, when inhaled or applied, can draw your attention to areas of tension or weakness. This enhanced body awareness is crucial for you to consciously adjust your posture and understand what your body needs to feel supported and aligned.
Is Aromatherapy safe to use if I'm still sensitive or fragile from my recovery?
Your safety and comfort are our utmost priority. We use only pure, therapeutic-grade essential oils and always dilute them appropriately. During your initial consultation, we meticulously review your health history, current medications, and sensitivities. All blends are custom-formulated with your unique recovery phase and individual needs in mind, ensuring a gentle yet effective experience tailored specifically for you.
How can Aromatherapy complement my existing physical therapy or rehabilitation program?
Aromatherapy can be a beautiful complement to your current rehabilitation efforts. While your physical therapy focuses on strengthening and mobility, our aromatic blends can help manage discomfort, reduce inflammation, and enhance relaxation, making your therapeutic exercises more effective and comfortable. The calming and uplifting properties of essential oils can also support your emotional well-being throughout your recovery, fostering a more positive and consistent engagement with your program.
