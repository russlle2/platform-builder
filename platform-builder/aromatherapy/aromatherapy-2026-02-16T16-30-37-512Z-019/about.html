A small story

I learned to listen in the kitchen and the garden. Scent became a language for mornings, grief, and celebration. Over years of study and thoughtful practice I offer blends that invite ritual and presence. Safety is threaded through everything I do — dilution guidance, patch testing, and plain-speech instructions for home use.

This practice is not a replacement for healthcare. If you have medical concerns or are pregnant, please consult your licensed healthcare professional before beginning any aromatherapy routine. My work focuses on education, experience, and slow transformation through scent.
