Curated blends — crafted with intention

Each blend is created with scent harmony and safety in mind. Below are ready-made blends you can request in sessions or buy as roll-ons and kits. No medical claims — just thoughtful sensory support.

Safety reminder

Always follow dilution instructions. Avoid applying concentrated oils directly to skin. Consult your practitioner for pregnancy, pet, or medical-sensitivity notes. Perform a patch test before regular topical use.

Sunrise Calm
Top: Sweet Orange, Bergamot • Middle: Lavender • Base: Cedarwood
Aroma: Bright & comforting
Type: Uplifting

Supports gentle wakefulness and a peaceful morning ritual. Use as a diffuser blend at low concentrations for shared spaces.

Quiet Evening
Top: Petitgrain • Middle: Roman Chamomile • Base: Vetiver
Aroma: Herbaceous, soft
Type: Grounding

Supports relaxation and a calming bedtime routine. Diffuse briefly 30–60 minutes before sleep. Not a sleep cure; complements restful habits.

Focus Grove
Top: Rosemary • Middle: Lemon • Base: Frankincense
Aroma: Crisp & resinous
Type: Clarity

Supports concentration for short work sprints. Use sparingly and ventilate well. Avoid prolonged exposure for children and pets.

Warm Hearth
Top: Sweet Orange • Middle: Cinnamon leaf (very low) • Base: Sandalwood
Aroma: Cozy & warm
Type: Comforting

Supports companionable gatherings. Cinnamon leaf is used at low dilution only — check for sensitivities and avoid direct skin contact.

Mist of Meadow
Top: Bergamot • Middle: Neroli • Base: Ambrette
Aroma: Floral & airy
Type: Light, uplifting

Supports moments when you want a gentle, flower-like presence. Use in small diffuser doses for short intervals.

Quiet Strength
Top: Black Pepper (tiny) • Middle: Geranium • Base: Patchouli
Aroma: Earthy & floral
Type: Centering

Supports feeling steady in transitions. Pepper is included minimally; topical use only when fully diluted and patch-tested.

Breathe Easy (gentle)
Top: Eucalyptus (low) • Middle: Lavender • Base: Myrrh
Aroma: Clean & resinous
Type: Respiratory-friendly (mild)

Supports comfortable breathing when used with caution. Keep exposure brief and avoid around infants; consult practitioner for specific health concerns.

Gentle Bloom
Top: Ylang Ylang (very low) • Middle: Rose otto (low) • Base: Jojoba carrier
Aroma: Soft floral
Type: Soothing

Supports emotional ease and self-care rituals. Floral absolutes are used in low concentrations—ideal as a roll-on blend.
