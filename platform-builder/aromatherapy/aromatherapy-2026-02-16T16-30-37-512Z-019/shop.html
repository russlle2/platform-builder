FAQ — usage & safety

Dilution: Most topical products are delivered at safe dilutions for adults. Children, sensitive skin, and pregnancy require lower dilutions—ask before use.

Patch test: Always patch-test new topical products on a small area and wait 24 hours for reactions.

Pets: Some oils can affect animals. Our pet-safe products avoid known problematic oils, but please consult your veterinarian for specific pet needs.

Pregnancy & medical conditions: We provide guidance but are not medical professionals. Check with your healthcare provider for personalized medical advice.
