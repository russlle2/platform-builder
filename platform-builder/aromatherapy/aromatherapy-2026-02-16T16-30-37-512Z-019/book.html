Safety notes (please read)
  • Dilution: We provide clear dilution guidance for topical use. Never apply undiluted essential oils to skin.
  • Patch test: Test new blends on a small skin area and wait 24 hours to check for reactions.
  • Pets: Some oils are unsafe for pets. Mention pets in your notes so we can recommend pet-safe blends.
  • Pregnancy & children: Certain oils are contraindicated. If you are pregnant, nursing, or planning for pregnancy, consult your healthcare provider before use and note this in your booking.
  • Interactions: If you are taking medications or have serious health conditions, please check with your clinician before aromatherapy routines.
