Frequently asked — safety & practical notes

How should I dilute essential oils?
I provide dilution guidelines per blend and per use-case. For topical use a common guidance is 1–3% for adults; children and sensitive people use lower dilutions. Always follow your practitioner's notes.
Do I need a patch test?
Yes. We recommend a small patch test for topical blends: apply diluted product to a tiny area and wait 24 hours to check for reactions.
What about pets and pregnancy?
Some oils are not recommended for pets or during pregnancy. We'll review safety considerations during intake and offer alternatives with milder profiles.
Are these medical treatments?
No. Aromatherapy supports well-being through scent-based practices and is not a substitute for medical care.
