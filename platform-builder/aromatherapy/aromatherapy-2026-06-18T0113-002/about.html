How quickly will I notice benefits from Aromatherapy?

Many clients report reduced tension within the first session (via inhalation techniques). For deeper shifts like improved sleep patterns or anxiety management, consistent use over 2-4 weeks is typical, supported by our follow-up check-ins.

Are there side effects with professional Aromatherapy?

When guided by certified practice, side effects are rare. We screen for allergies, test dilutions, and use only therapeutic-grade oils. You'll receive clear usage instructions—no 'just add to bath' vague advice.

How does your Aromatherapy differ from massage or essential oil stores?

We focus on scent as a physiological tool, not just a sensory experience. Unlike store mixes, our blends are created for specific outcomes (e.g., 'Focus for Remote Work' versus generic 'Energize'). And unlike massage, we avoid manual manipulation unless specifically requested.

Can I use Aromatherapy while taking medication?

Yes, safely. We review all medications during consultation. For example, citrus oils are avoided with certain antidepressants, but we'll recommend compatible alternatives like chamomile or vetiver. Always transparent and collaborative.
