Myths vs. Safety-first Truths

We separate common misconceptions from guidance rooted in safety and best practice.

Myth

Essential oils are always safe because they're natural.

Truth

Natural does not equal risk-free. We apply dilution guidelines, patch testing, and context-specific advice for each client.

Myth

Inhalation is always harmless for everyone.

Truth

Some individuals have respiratory sensitivities and there are specific precautions for children, pregnancy, and pets. We never substitute aromatherapy for medical care.

FAQ — Practical safety & use

We typically recommend 0.5–2% for adults depending on context. For children or sensitive skin, lower dilutions are advisable. Your practitioner will advise a dilution based on age, skin condition, and intended use.

Yes — patch tests are a simple, low-risk step. Apply a small diluted amount to a discreet skin area and observe for 24–48 hours. If irritation occurs, discontinue use and consult your practitioner.

Some oils can be toxic to animals at certain exposures. We design pet-aware protocols and recommend ventilation, limited diffusion time, and keeping concentrated preparations away from pets. Notify your practitioner if you have animals.

Pregnancy and postpartum periods require extra caution. Certain oils and routes can be contraindicated. We follow conservative, specialist-informed guidance and will tailor recommendations or defer as appropriate.

No — aromatherapy is complementary and not a substitute for medical diagnosis or treatment. We emphasize collaboration with healthcare providers where relevant.
