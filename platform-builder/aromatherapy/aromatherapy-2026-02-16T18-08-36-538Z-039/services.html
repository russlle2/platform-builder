Common myths and clear realities

Myth: Essential oils are harmless natural fixes.
Truth: Natural does not equal risk-free. Essential oils can cause irritation, sensitization and interactions. We prioritize dilution and testing.
Myth: More scent equals better results.
Truth: Optimal dosing is often subtle. Excessive exposure increases risk of adverse effects and reduces therapeutic usefulness.
Myth: Any oil is safe around pets.
Truth: Many oils are toxic to animals at certain doses or via inhalation. We give specific pet-safe guidance when relevant.

FAQ — practical safety notes

How should I dilute essential oils?

Dilution depends on age, condition and application. Typical adult topical dilutions range 0.5–3% for most daily-use blends. We always provide a dilution chart in your plan.

What is a patch test?

A patch test is applying a small diluted sample to an area of skin and observing for 24–48 hours for irritation or sensitivity before regular use.

Are essential oils safe around pets?

Some oils are not safe for animals. We ask about household pets during assessment and will tailor advice or recommend pet-free zones and ventilation plans.

Pregnancy and breastfeeding precautions

During pregnancy or breastfeeding, certain oils and dosages are contraindicated. We will use pregnancy-safe guidance and coordinate with your healthcare provider as needed.
