Frequently asked safety questions

How do you handle dilution?
Dilution is chosen based on application (diffuser vs. topical), client age, and sensitivity. Typical topical dilutions are conservative; I guide you on exact ratios for home blending.
Do I need a patch test?
Yes. Patch testing is recommended before regular topical use of a new blend. I provide a simple step-by-step patch test protocol during consults.
Are essential oils safe around pets?
Some oils can affect animals. I assess household pets and suggest pet-safe alternatives and diffusion strategies to minimize exposure. If you have pets, please mention them when booking.
What about pregnancy and breastfeeding?
Pregnancy and lactation require extra caution. I use established safety resources to recommend only oils and practices considered low-risk and always advise consultation with your healthcare provider.
