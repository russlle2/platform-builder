Why a safety-first practice matters

Essential oils are potent. My role is to help you understand concentration, interactions, and when to consult your primary care provider. I provide clear dilution charts and patch-test protocols with every plan.

Misconceptions — quick clarifications

Myth: More oil = better results.
Truth: Use the minimum effective dose. Higher concentrations increase risk of irritation.
Myth: Natural always means safe.
Truth: Natural extracts can be sensitizing. Context, dose, and individual history matter.
