How can Aromatherapy specifically help me during my postpartum recovery?

Aromatherapy provides gentle, non-invasive support for your nervous system during this tender time. Specific aromas can help reduce feelings of anxiety, support more restful sleep, and encourage moments of calm, helping your body and mind regulate after the profound experience of childbirth and early motherhood.

I'm new to Aromatherapy; will I understand how to use these blends?

Absolutely. Our approach to Aromatherapy for new mothers is designed to be accessible and gentle. You'll receive clear, simple guidance on how to safely incorporate these beautiful scents into your daily rhythm, whether through gentle diffusion or topical application, with no prior wellness experience needed.

Are the Aromatherapy blends safe for me and my baby?

Your safety and your baby's safety are our utmost priority. All our postpartum Aromatherapy blends are crafted with carefully selected, high-quality essential oils and are diluted to appropriate concentrations. We'll always discuss safe usage, including considerations for nursing mothers and infants, ensuring peace of mind.

How often should I use Aromatherapy to support my nervous system postpartum?

The beauty of Aromatherapy is its flexibility. You can use your blends as often as you feel drawn to them, whether it's a few intentional breaths throughout the day, a calming diffusion before sleep, or a gentle pulse point application when you need a moment of stillness. Listen to your body; it will guide you.
