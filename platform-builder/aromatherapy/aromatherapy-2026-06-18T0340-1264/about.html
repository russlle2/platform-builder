How does Aromatherapy specifically help with emotional recovery from chronic illness or injury?

Aromatherapy, when approached with a trauma-informed lens, can offer a profound sense of grounding and emotional regulation. Certain scents can help to calm an overactive nervous system, gently process feelings of loss or frustration, and create a comforting sensory environment that supports your emotional healing process, respecting your body's current state.

I've never tried Aromatherapy. Is this practice gentle enough for someone new to wellness after a difficult experience?

Absolutely. Our approach to Aromatherapy is centered on gentleness and accessibility. We understand that your body and mind may be delicate, and we introduce scents with careful consideration, always checking in with your comfort. No prior wellness experience is needed; we meet you exactly where you are, with compassion and support.

Will Aromatherapy interfere with my current medical treatments or medications?

Your safety is paramount. We always recommend consulting with your primary healthcare provider before integrating Aromatherapy, especially if you are undergoing medical treatments or taking medications. We are happy to collaborate with your care team to ensure our support complements your existing health plan safely and effectively.

What if I have sensitivities or allergies to certain scents? Can I still benefit from Aromatherapy?

Yes, your sensitivities are important considerations in our practice. We conduct a thorough intake to understand any allergies or sensitivities you may have. We work with a wide range of pure essential oils and can tailor blends very specifically, often starting with extremely diluted concentrations or just a single, well-tolerated oil to ensure your comfort and well-being.
