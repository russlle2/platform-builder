What exactly is Aromatherapy, and how can it benefit me?

Aromatherapy is the therapeutic use of aromatic plant extracts and essential oils for psychological and physical well-being. It can support your mood, improve sleep quality, ease tension, and enhance overall balance by interacting with your body's systems through inhalation and topical application.

Are the essential oils used in your Aromatherapy practice safe?

Absolutely. We exclusively use high-quality, pure essential oils from reputable suppliers. During your consultation, Dr. Morgan Ellis will discuss any sensitivities or health conditions you may have to ensure your personalized blends are safe and effective for your unique constitution.

How long does an Aromatherapy consultation typically last, and what should I expect?

An initial consultation typically lasts 60 to 90 minutes. You can expect a detailed discussion about your health history, lifestyle, and wellness goals. Dr. Morgan Ellis will then create a custom essential oil blend tailored to your needs and provide guidance on its proper use.

Can Aromatherapy replace traditional medical treatments?

Aromatherapy is a complementary therapy and is not intended to replace conventional medical treatment. It works wonderfully alongside your existing healthcare regimen to support your well-being. We always recommend consulting with your doctor for any health concerns.
