Shopping FAQs — safety-first

Dilution: Our topical roll-ons are pre-diluted as noted. For custom blends, typical adult dilutions are 1–3% depending on sensitivity. Patch test: always recommended—apply a small amount of diluted product to the inner forearm and wait 24 hours. Pets: avoid heavy diffusion around cats and consult before use. Pregnancy & nursing: some oils are not advised; book a consult for pregnancy-safe recommendations.
