Micro-habits: small scent rituals that add up

Simple, repeatable steps you can adopt. Each habit is paired with safety notes and alternatives for sensitivity or pregnancy.

  • 3-breath reset: One discreet spritz of a diffuser blend into the air, inhale gently for three breaths. Avoid direct inhalation near the face.
  • Roller ritual: Apply diluted roller to inner wrists and behind ears (patch test first). Replace if irritation occurs; avoid undiluted essential oils on skin.
  • Bedtime buffer: Diffuse a low-concentration sleep blend for 15–30 minutes before bed. Keep diffuser away from pets and never diffuse essential oils near birds.
  • Carry a scent token: A small felt pad in a locket with a single drop of a safe blend—great for public spaces and travel.

Safety note: Always follow dilution and patch-test guidance. These suggestions are educational and not medical advice.
