FAQ — safe practice highlights

How do I dilute essential oils?
Dilution depends on age, skin sensitivity and use. Typical adult topical dilutions range from 0.5–5% depending on purpose. Your practitioner will recommend a safe ratio during sessions.
Should I do a patch test?
Yes. Apply a small diluted amount to the inner forearm and observe 24 hours for irritation. Stop use if redness, itching, or discomfort occur.
Are essential oils safe around pets?
Some essential oils can be harmful to animals, especially cats and small dogs. We'll provide pet-safe guidance and alternatives during planning.
What about pregnancy or medical conditions?
Pregnancy, nursing, and serious medical conditions require extra caution. Aromatherapy can support comfort, but recommendations will avoid contraindicated oils and always advise consulting your healthcare provider.
