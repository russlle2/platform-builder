Frequently asked safety questions

Dilution — how to dilute essential oils?
Use carrier oils (fractionated coconut, jojoba). Common topical dilutions: 1% for sensitive users, 2–3% for general adult use. Always follow the guidance provided with your blends.
Patch test — do I need one?
Yes. Apply diluted blend to a small area on the inner arm for 24 hours to check irritation. Stop use if redness or discomfort occurs.
Pets — are essential oils safe around animals?
Some oils can be harmful to pets, especially cats. Use low, well-ventilated diffuser settings and consult before using strong oils if you have pets at home.
Pregnancy & nursing — any notes?
Some oils are not recommended during pregnancy and while nursing. We always review pregnancy status during intake and provide pregnancy-safe alternatives.
