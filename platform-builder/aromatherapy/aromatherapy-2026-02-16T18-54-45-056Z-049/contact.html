FAQ — safe use and common questions

How should I dilute essential oils?
Dilution depends on age, sensitivity, and use. I recommend conservative dilution (1–3%) for topical blends unless otherwise advised. We review dilution during consultations.
What is a patch test and why is it important?
A patch test checks for skin sensitivity. Apply a small diluted amount to the inner forearm and wait 24–48 hours. If irritation occurs, do not continue use and consult me for alternatives.
Are essential oils safe around pets?
Some oils can be harmful to animals. I always ask about pets and tailor recommendations. Keep diffusers in well-ventilated areas and observe animals' behavior; remove scents if any sign of distress appears.
What about pregnancy or medication?
Certain oils are contraindicated in pregnancy or with some medications. Disclose pregnancy, healthcare treatments, and medications during intake so I can provide safe options. I do not provide medical advice.
