Safety & frequently asked

Are these treatments?
We do not provide medical treatment. Aromatherapy practices and blends may support general wellbeing and daily routines but are not a substitute for medical care.
Patch test and dilution guidance
Every blend includes a dilution recommendation and a patch-test protocol. Start with a small amount and wait 24 hours to check for irritation.
Pregnancy, children, and pets
Some essential oils are not recommended during pregnancy or around young children and certain animals. We include clear notes and alternatives. Consult your healthcare provider for specific medical advice.
How to store blends
Store blends in a cool, dark place and label clearly. Keep out of reach of children and pets.
