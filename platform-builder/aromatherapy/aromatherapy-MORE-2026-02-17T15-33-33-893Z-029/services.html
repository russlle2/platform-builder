Safety notes
Blends provided are guidance. Always perform a patch test and dilute according to instructions. If pregnant, nursing, managing chronic conditions, or living with sensitive pets, consult with your healthcare provider or vet before use. No medical claims are made; aromatics may support comfort for some individuals.
Patch test
Apply a diluted amount to inner forearm and wait 24 hours. Discontinue if irritation occurs. Keep blends away from eyes and mucous membranes.
