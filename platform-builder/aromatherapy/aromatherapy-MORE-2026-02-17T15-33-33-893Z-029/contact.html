Frequently asked safety questions

Do aromatic approaches replace medical care? No. Aromatherapy may support comfort or relaxation for some people but is not a substitute for medical treatment.

How should I test a blend? We recommend a 24-hour patch test at a conservative dilution and to avoid sensitive areas. We provide step-by-step guidance with consultations.

What about pets and pregnancy? Certain essential oils are not recommended for pregnant people or animals. We will advise alternatives and safer practices during sessions.
