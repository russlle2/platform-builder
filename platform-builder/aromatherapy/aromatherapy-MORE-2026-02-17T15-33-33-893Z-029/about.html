A short practitioner note

I design small, repeatable scent supports — not treatments. Many clients tell me these simple practices bring back a sense of steadiness. If you have health concerns or are pregnant, please consult your healthcare provider before using essential oils.

Common safety guidance
  • Always do an external patch test before extended topical use.
  • Use recommended dilution ratios; children and pets require extra precautions.
  • Avoid undiluted essential oils on skin; store oils securely away from children.
