Frequently Asked Questions

How does Aromatherapy specifically support emotional well-being for those in their golden years?
Our Aromatherapy focuses on the unique emotional landscape of seniors, gently addressing feelings of grief, loneliness, anxiety, or past experiences. We select essences known for their calming and uplifting properties, fostering emotional balance and providing a sense of comforting presence. It’s about creating a fragrant haven for your spirit to find peace.
Is Aromatherapy safe if I have existing health considerations or take medications?
Your safety is paramount. We conduct a thorough intake to understand your health history, current medications, and any sensitivities. We then select and dilute essential oils judiciously, ensuring a gentle and safe experience. Our approach is always trauma-informed and mindful of your complete well-being, consulting with you every step of the way.
How does your trauma-informed approach to Aromatherapy differ from typical wellness practices?
Our trauma-informed approach prioritizes your sense of safety and agency above all. We avoid any practices that might re-trigger past experiences and instead create a deeply respectful and empowering environment. We focus on gentle, consensual engagement with scents, allowing you to guide the process and find comfort at your own pace, honoring your emotional boundaries.
What can I expect during my first Aromatherapy consultation for emotional healing?
Your first consultation is a gentle conversation where we listen deeply to your life story and emotional experiences, without judgment. We'll discuss your intentions for healing and introduce you to various aromatic essences in a slow, guided manner. Together, we'll begin to craft a personalized aromatic pathway designed to gently support your emotional landscape and bring solace to your spirit.
