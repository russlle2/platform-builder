Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is Aromatherapy and how can it benefit me?
Aromatherapy is a holistic healing treatment that uses natural plant extracts to promote health and well-being. It can benefit you by enhancing physical and psychological health, offering support for stress reduction, improved sleep, pain management, and emotional balance, tailored to your specific needs.
Are essential oils safe for everyone to use?
While generally safe, essential oils are potent and should be used with care. We always conduct a thorough consultation to understand your health history and sensitivities. We provide clear guidance on proper dilution, application methods, and precautions to ensure your safe and effective use of essential oils.
How do you determine which essential oils are right for my individual needs?
Your personalized Aromatherapy journey begins with a comprehensive consultation where we discuss your wellness goals, lifestyle, and preferences. Based on this, thoughtfully selects specific essential oils known for their therapeutic properties that align perfectly with your unique requirements, ensuring an effective and pleasant experience for you.
What can I expect during my first Aromatherapy consultation?
During your initial consultation, you can expect a warm and engaging conversation where we explore your current health, emotional state, and any specific concerns you wish to address. We'll discuss your preferences and educate you on how Aromatherapy can support your well-being, leading to the creation of your custom aromatic plan.
