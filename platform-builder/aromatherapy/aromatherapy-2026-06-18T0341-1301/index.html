Frequently Asked Questions

How can Aromatherapy specifically help with age-related physical discomfort?
Aromatherapy utilizes the therapeutic properties of essential oils to gently address concerns like joint stiffness, muscle soreness, and circulation challenges often experienced in later years. Specific oils can offer anti-inflammatory or analgesic effects, providing a natural complement to your existing care, helping your body feel more comfortable and mobile.
Is Aromatherapy safe for seniors, especially those with sensitivities or existing conditions?
Your safety and comfort are paramount. We conduct a thorough intake to understand your health history, medications, and sensitivities. We use only the highest quality, diluted essential oils, applied gently through diffusion, inhalation, or topical application, ensuring a safe and beneficial experience tailored precisely to your unique needs.
What does a typical Aromatherapy session for physical pain relief involve?
A session begins with a heartfelt conversation about your specific discomforts and aspirations for relief. Based on this, we'll guide you through a selection of aromas, creating a personalized blend. This blend may be diffused for inhalation, or gently applied topically (with your consent) to areas of tension, inviting your body to relax and release.
How often might I need Aromatherapy to experience a lasting sense of ease?
The frequency of sessions is as individual as your needs. Some find profound relief and sustained comfort with regular, perhaps monthly, sessions, while others prefer support during periods of increased discomfort. We will discuss a personalized plan that honors your body's rhythms and supports your continued well-being.
