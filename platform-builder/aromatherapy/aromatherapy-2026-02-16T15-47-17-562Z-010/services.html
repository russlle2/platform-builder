Safety first — short guide

All sessions include dilution charts, patch-test instructions, and plain language notes for pets, pregnancy, and age. No medical diagnosis or treatment is offered.

Common questions

Patch test: Apply small diluted amount on inner forearm, wait 24 hours. Stop if irritation occurs.

Dilution: We provide clear percent-based dilution tables tailored to body area and method.

Pets & pregnancy: We'll advise specific oils to avoid and safer alternatives; always consult your healthcare provider for pregnancy questions.
