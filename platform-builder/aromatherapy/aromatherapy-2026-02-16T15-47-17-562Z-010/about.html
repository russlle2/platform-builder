My story

I started with a curiosity for plant aroma and a respect for safety. Over years of study and hands-on practice, I developed a method that favors simple, effective rituals you can keep and reuse. My focus is on education and practical blends, not medical treatment.

Framework I use

I use a three-layer framework: identification, safety, and practice.

  • Identify: choose notes that suit your preference and environment.
  • Safety: check sensitivities, dilution, and interactions.
  • Practice: integrate short rituals into daily life for consistent benefit.

What I offer

One-on-one sessions, VIP Days, small-group workshops, and bespoke blend creation. I partner with makers for ethically sourced oils.

Safety & FAQ

Dilution: I always provide safe dilution ratios based on age and use-case. Typical topical dilutions are 0.5–3% for adults unless otherwise discussed.
Patch test: I recommend a patch test for topical blends — apply diluted blend to a small area and wait 24 hours.
Pets: Many essential oils can affect pets. I advise avoiding direct application on animals and using diffusers with caution; check species-specific guidance.
Pregnancy & nursing: Some oils are best avoided. If pregnant or nursing, we will review safe options together. I do not offer medical advice; consult your healthcare provider when relevant.
