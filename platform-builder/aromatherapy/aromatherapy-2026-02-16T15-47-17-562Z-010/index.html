Why aromatherapy, practically speaking

I blend aromatic skill with a safety-first approach. Whether you want a calming bedside roll-on or a focused ritual blend, I meet you where you are and make a clear, usable plan you can follow at home.

Sessions and blends avoid medical claims; we focus on scent profile, safe dilution, and daily ritual that fits your life.

How a session flows

  1. Intake: environment, preferences, safety (allergies, pregnancy, pets).
  2. Explore notes: sampling top/middle/base aromas with guided scent mapping.
  3. Create: a personalized blend, dilution plan, and printable instructions.

My framework: S.A.F.E. ritual design

A clear process so you leave confident and comfortable.

S — Survey

Assess space, sensitivities, and goals (family, pets, pregnancy considerations).

A — Assemble

Choose safe, high-quality oils and blends with clear top/middle/base notes and usage notes.

F — Formulate

Mix at recommended dilutions and document a patch-test routine for home use.

E — Educate

Provide written care, storage, and usage guidance so you can rely on the routine long-term.
