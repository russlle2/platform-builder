How blends are crafted

We follow a repeatable method: safety screening, scent trials, and dilution planning. Blends list recommended delivery methods — diffusion, roll-on, or diluted topical use. Always follow patch tests and dilution guidance.

FAQs — safe use

Dilution: We give percent-based dilution charts. Typical roll-ons are 2–5% for adults; children and sensitive skin require lower dilutions. We provide exact recipes in consultations.

Patch test: Apply a small diluted amount to the inner forearm and wait 24 hours. Discontinue if you see irritation.

Pets: Some oils are not pet-safe. We recommend ventilation, low concentrations, and specific avoidance lists; always follow veterinarian guidance.

Pregnancy & nursing: Extra precautions apply. We will advise alternative oils and recommend you check with your healthcare provider before using oils in pregnancy.
