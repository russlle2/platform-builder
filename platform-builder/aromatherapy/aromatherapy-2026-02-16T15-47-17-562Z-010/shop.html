Product Safety Notes

Dilution: Bottled products list recommended dilutions. We advise lower concentrations for children and sensitive skin; consult the included dilution card.

Patch test: Always patch test new topical products. Apply diluted drop to inner forearm and monitor for 24 hours.

Pets: Use discretion around pets. Keep diffused spaces well-ventilated and avoid direct application to animals.

Pregnancy: Some oils are contraindicated in pregnancy. Check product notes and consult your healthcare provider before use.
