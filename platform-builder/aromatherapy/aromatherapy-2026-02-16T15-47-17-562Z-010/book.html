FAQ — safety-focused

How should I dilute oils for topical use?

Typical adult dilutions are 0.5–3% depending on purpose and sensitivity. I provide specific dilution instructions for each custom blend.

Should I patch test?

Yes — for topical use, apply a small amount of the diluted blend to an inner forearm and wait 24 hours to check for reactions.

Are essential oils safe around pets?

Some oils can be harmful to animals. We will discuss pet-safe options and diffusion practices that reduce risk. Avoid direct application to pets.

What about pregnancy or nursing?

Certain oils are best avoided during pregnancy or nursing. Let me know during booking so I can recommend safe alternatives. This is educational guidance, not medical advice.
