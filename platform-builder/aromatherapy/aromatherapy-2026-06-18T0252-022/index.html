Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is Aromatherapy and how can it help me?
Aromatherapy is the therapeutic use of aromatic plant extracts, known as essential oils, to promote physical and psychological well-being. It can help you by supporting emotional balance, reducing stress, improving sleep quality, easing discomfort, and boosting overall vitality through inhalation or topical application of carefully selected oils.
Are the essential oils used in Aromatherapy safe?
Yes, when used correctly and with proper guidance, essential oils are safe. At , we prioritize your safety by using only high-quality, pure essential oils and providing clear instructions for their dilution and application. We always discuss any sensitivities or health conditions you may have to ensure a safe and beneficial experience.
How does a personalized Aromatherapy consultation work?
Your personalized Aromatherapy consultation begins with a thorough discussion about your health goals, lifestyle, and preferences. Based on this information, will recommend and blend specific essential oils tailored to your unique needs. You'll receive a custom blend and clear instructions on how to use it effectively to achieve your desired outcomes.
