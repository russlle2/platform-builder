Frequently Asked Questions

What is Aromatherapy and how can it help me maintain vitality?
Aromatherapy uses natural plant extracts, called essential oils, to promote well-being. We focus on gentle, specific scents that can help keep your mind clear and your body feeling lively. For example, certain aromas can help you feel more alert during the day or more relaxed at night, supporting your overall vitality.
I've never tried Aromatherapy before. Is it complicated?
Not at all! Sage Garden Aromatherapy is designed to be very accessible and gentle. We'll walk you through simple, safe ways to use essential oils, focusing on what feels comfortable and beneficial for you. There's no prior experience needed to enjoy the benefits.
How does Sage Garden Aromatherapy address seasonal changes for someone my age?
We craft personalized aromatherapy approaches that consider how different seasons can affect your energy and mood. For instance, we might suggest invigorating scents for shorter, darker winter days or calming blends to help you adjust to warmer summer nights, always with your vitality and comfort in mind.
Can Aromatherapy really help with mental clarity?
Many people find that certain essential oils can be very supportive for maintaining focus and a sense of mental brightness. We'll explore specific aromas and gentle application methods that may help you feel more clear-headed and engaged in your daily activities, supporting your cognitive well-being.
