Frequently Asked Questions

What can I expect during my first Aromatherapy consultation?
During your initial consultation, we'll discuss your health history, lifestyle, and specific well-being goals. This comprehensive conversation allows to understand your unique needs and preferences before formulating personalized Aromatherapy recommendations and blends tailored just for you.
Are the essential oils used in your Aromatherapy practice safe?
Your safety is our top priority. We use only high-quality, ethically sourced essential oils from trusted suppliers. provides clear guidance on proper dilution, application methods, and any contraindications, ensuring your Aromatherapy experience is both effective and safe for your individual circumstances.
How can Aromatherapy support my emotional well-being?
Aromatherapy can profoundly impact emotional well-being by influencing the limbic system, the brain's emotional center. Specific essential oils can help uplift your mood, reduce feelings of anxiety, promote relaxation, or enhance mental clarity. We'll explore which essences best resonate with your emotional landscape to foster greater balance.
Do you offer custom essential oil blends for specific concerns?
Absolutely. A core aspect of our Aromatherapy service is the creation of custom essential oil blends. Based on your consultation, will handcraft a unique blend designed to address your particular concerns, whether they relate to stress, sleep, energy, or other aspects of your well-being.
