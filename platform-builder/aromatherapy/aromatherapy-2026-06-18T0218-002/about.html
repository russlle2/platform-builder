How long does it take to create my custom blend?

Your first consultation takes 60 minutes. We'll discuss your needs, test oils with you, and give you a blend to trial for one week. After a follow-up call, we'll finalize your signature formula without rushing your process.

Can aromatherapy help with specific conditions?

We collaborate with your healthcare providers for conditions like chronic pain or anxiety. Your blends complement your existing care—never replace it. We focus on creating supportive scents that enhance your well-being without making medical claims.

Where do you source your essential oils?

All oils come from certified organic farms with strict quality control. We provide batch numbers and lab reports upon request. No synthetic additives or dilution—we only use pure, therapeutic-grade oils tested for safety and efficacy.

How often should I adjust my blend?

Seasonal shifts and life changes affect your scent needs. We recommend quarterly check-ins. For example, you might need stronger grounding oils during work deadlines or lighter floral blends during spring. We never make you feel pressured to buy more—just refine what already works for you.
