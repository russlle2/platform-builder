Frequently Asked Questions

How can Aromatherapy specifically aid my immune system during postpartum recovery?
Certain pure essential oils possess remarkable properties that can support your body's natural defenses. We select oils known for their immune-modulating and protective qualities, integrating them into gentle yet potent blends designed to help fortify your system as you recover and adapt to new motherhood.
Will Aromatherapy help me regain my energy and vitality after childbirth?
Absolutely. Postpartum can often feel like an ebb of vital energy. Our Aromatherapy approaches focus on essences that are renowned for their revitalizing and uplifting properties. We aim to help you gently reclaim your inner strength and sense of well-being, fostering a renewed sense of vitality.
Is Aromatherapy safe to use while I am breastfeeding my baby?
Your safety and your baby's well-being are paramount. We meticulously choose essential oils and dilution ratios that are considered safe for breastfeeding mothers. We will always discuss your individual circumstances and any concerns you have to ensure our recommendations are gentle and appropriate for you.
How does Aromatherapy integrate Eastern and Western philosophies for postpartum care?
We blend the intuitive wisdom of Eastern energy work and botanical traditions with Western scientific understanding of essential oil chemistry. This integrative approach allows us to create Aromatherapy protocols that address both the energetic balance and physiological needs unique to postpartum recovery, supporting your entire being.
