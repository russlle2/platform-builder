Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is Aromatherapy and how can it benefit me?
Aromatherapy is a holistic healing modality that uses natural plant extracts, known as essential oils, to promote well-being. These oils are inhaled or applied topically to influence your mood, alleviate physical discomfort, and support emotional balance. It can help you with stress reduction, improved sleep, pain management, and enhancing your overall sense of vitality.
Are the essential oils used in your Aromatherapy practice safe?
Absolutely. We prioritize your safety and well-being above all else. We use only therapeutic-grade essential oils from reputable suppliers, and during your consultation, we carefully assess your health history and sensitivities to ensure every blend is tailored and safe for your individual use. We will always guide you on proper dilution and application methods.
How long does an Aromatherapy consultation typically last?
A typical initial Aromatherapy consultation lasts approximately 60-75 minutes. This allows ample time for a comprehensive discussion about your health goals, lifestyle, and preferences, ensuring we can create a truly personalized and effective plan for you. Follow-up sessions are usually shorter, around 30-45 minutes.
What can I expect during my first Aromatherapy session with ?
During your first session, you will engage in a thorough conversation with about your health history, current concerns, and desired outcomes. You'll learn about various essential oils and their properties, and together, you'll co-create a custom aromatic blend. You'll also receive clear instructions on how to incorporate your personalized Aromatherapy ritual into your daily routine.
