What exactly is Aromatherapy and how can it benefit me?

Aromatherapy is a holistic healing modality that uses natural plant extracts, known as essential oils, to promote health and well-being. By inhaling or topically applying these oils, you can experience benefits ranging from stress reduction and improved sleep to enhanced mood and physical comfort. Your personalized session will address your specific needs.

Are essential oils safe to use, and how will you ensure my safety?

Yes, when used correctly, essential oils are safe and highly effective. Dr. Morgan Ellis is a certified aromatherapist who prioritizes your safety. We conduct a thorough intake to understand your health history, sensitivities, and any medications you may be taking. We will guide you on proper dilution, application methods, and storage to ensure your experience is both beneficial and safe.

How is a personalized Aromatherapy blend created for me?

Creating your personalized blend involves a comprehensive consultation where we discuss your wellness goals, emotional state, and any physical concerns. Based on this, Dr. Morgan Ellis selects specific essential oils known for their therapeutic properties and synergistic effects. Your unique blend will be formulated to support your individual needs and preferences, creating a truly tailored experience.

What should I expect during my first Aromatherapy consultation?

During your initial consultation, we'll have an in-depth conversation about your health, lifestyle, and what you hope to achieve through aromatherapy. This allows us to gain a holistic understanding of your needs. You can expect a warm, confidential environment where you'll feel comfortable sharing. We'll then discuss potential essential oils and a personalized plan for your well-being.
