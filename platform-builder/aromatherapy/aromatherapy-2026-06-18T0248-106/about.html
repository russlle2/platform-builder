What exactly is Aromatherapy?

Aromatherapy is a holistic healing modality that uses natural plant extracts, known as essential oils, to promote health and well-being. These oils are carefully extracted from botanicals and can be inhaled or applied topically to support physical, emotional, and mental balance. We focus on therapeutic-grade oils for their purity and efficacy.

How can Aromatherapy benefit me?

Aromatherapy offers a wide range of benefits. It can help alleviate stress and anxiety, improve sleep quality, boost your mood, support pain management, enhance concentration, and promote overall relaxation. Your specific blend will be formulated to target your unique health goals and personal preferences.

Is Aromatherapy safe for everyone?

When used correctly and with professional guidance, Aromatherapy is generally safe. However, essential oils are potent, and certain conditions (like pregnancy, specific medical issues, or sensitivities) require careful consideration. We always conduct a thorough consultation to ensure your personalized blends are safe and appropriate for your individual health profile.

What should I expect during an Aromatherapy consultation?

During your consultation, we will discuss your health history, lifestyle, and specific wellness goals. This in-depth conversation allows us to understand your needs comprehensively. Based on this, Dr. Morgan Ellis will create a custom essential oil blend and provide clear instructions on how to use it safely and effectively to support your well-being.
