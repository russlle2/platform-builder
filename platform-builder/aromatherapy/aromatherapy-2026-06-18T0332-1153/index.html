Frequently Asked Questions

How does Aromatherapy specifically help seniors improve their sleep?
Aromatherapy for seniors focuses on gentle, non-invasive methods to encourage relaxation and sleep. We select specific essential oils known for their calming effects, which can help quiet an overactive mind, ease muscle tension, and promote a sense of security before bed. This approach is designed to support your body's natural sleep cycles without harsh interventions, helping you achieve more consistent and restorative rest.
Is Aromatherapy safe for someone over 60, especially if I'm taking medications?
Your safety is our utmost priority. We conduct a thorough consultation to understand your health history, current medications, and any sensitivities. We then select essential oils that are gentle, dilute them appropriately, and guide you on safe application methods. Our approach is always personalized and cautious, ensuring that your Aromatherapy experience complements your overall well-being without interference.
What kind of results can I realistically expect from Aromatherapy for my sleep?
While individual experiences vary, many clients report a noticeable improvement in their ability to fall asleep more easily, experience fewer nighttime awakenings, and wake feeling more refreshed. Our aim is to help you establish a consistent, peaceful sleep routine, empowering you to experience deeper, more restorative rest, and thus, more vibrant days.
How long does it take to see improvements in my sleep using Aromatherapy?
The path to better sleep is a personal one. Some individuals notice subtle improvements within the first few weeks, while for others, it may take a bit longer to fully integrate the new routines and for your body to respond. We emphasize consistency and patience, guiding you step-by-step to build lasting habits that support profound, restful sleep over time.
