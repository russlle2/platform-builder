Frequently Asked Questions

How does Aromatherapy specifically help seniors improve their sleep?
For those in their golden years, Aromatherapy offers a gentle, non-invasive approach to sleep. We use carefully selected essential oils known for their calming and sedative properties, applied through inhalation or gentle topical use. These ancient remedies help to quiet an active mind, ease tension, and establish a serene environment, signaling to your body that it's time to rest deeply and restore.
Are the essential oils used in your Aromatherapy safe for sensitive skin or other health conditions common in seniors?
Absolutely. Your safety and comfort are paramount. We always conduct a thorough consultation to understand your unique health profile, including any sensitivities or existing conditions. We then select and dilute essential oils with the utmost care, using only the gentlest and purest botanical essences, ensuring a safe and nurturing Aromatherapy experience tailored just for you.
What kind of Aromatherapy experience can I expect for restorative rest?
You can expect a deeply personal and soothing experience. We often begin with a quiet conversation to understand your needs. Then, gentle aromatic blends might be offered for diffusion in your space, or as a component in a calming massage. The intention is always to create a tranquil atmosphere, inviting your spirit to unwind and prepare for a night of profoundly restorative, peaceful sleep.
How long does it take to see improvements in sleep from Aromatherapy?
The path to deeper rest is unique for everyone. Many find immediate solace and a greater sense of calm from their first Aromatherapy experience. Consistent, gentle application over a few weeks often deepens these effects, helping to re-establish a more regular and restorative sleep pattern. We walk with you at your own pace, honoring your body's natural rhythm.
