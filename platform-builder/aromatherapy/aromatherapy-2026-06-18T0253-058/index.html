Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is Aromatherapy and how can it benefit me?
Aromatherapy is the therapeutic use of aromatic plant extracts, known as essential oils, to promote physical and psychological well-being. It can benefit you by supporting relaxation, improving mood, alleviating discomfort, enhancing sleep, and boosting energy, all through natural, plant-derived compounds.
Are essential oils safe to use, and how do I know which ones are right for me?
When used correctly and with professional guidance, essential oils are generally safe. We conduct a thorough intake to understand your health history and sensitivities, then recommend specific oils and application methods tailored to your unique needs, ensuring safety and efficacy. We prioritize pure, high-quality oils.
What should I expect during an Aromatherapy consultation with Sarah Jenkins?
During your consultation, you can expect a warm, confidential conversation where we discuss your wellness goals, health concerns, and preferences. Sarah will then create a personalized Aromatherapy plan, which may include custom essential oil blends, usage instructions, and lifestyle recommendations to support your journey.
How long does it take to see results from Aromatherapy?
The time it takes to experience results from Aromatherapy can vary depending on your individual needs and consistency of use. Some individuals notice benefits almost immediately, such as improved mood or relaxation, while others may observe more gradual changes over several weeks as their body responds to the therapeutic properties of the oils.
