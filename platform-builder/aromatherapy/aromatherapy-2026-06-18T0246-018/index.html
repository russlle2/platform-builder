Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is Aromatherapy and how can it benefit me?
Aromatherapy is the therapeutic use of aromatic plant extracts and essential oils for psychological and physical well-being. It can benefit you by reducing stress, improving sleep, boosting mood, alleviating discomfort, and enhancing focus, all through the natural power of scent.
Are the essential oils used in your Aromatherapy practice safe?
Yes, we prioritize your safety above all else. uses only high-quality, pure, and ethically sourced essential oils. During your consultation, we discuss your health history and any sensitivities to ensure that all recommendations are safe and appropriate for your unique needs.
How does a personalized Aromatherapy consultation work?
During a personalized Aromatherapy consultation, you'll have a one-on-one discussion with about your health goals, lifestyle, and preferences. Based on this, a custom blend and specific application methods will be recommended, empowering you with tools for self-care tailored just for you.
Can Aromatherapy help with specific emotional or physical challenges?
While Aromatherapy is not a substitute for medical treatment, it can be a wonderful complementary therapy. Many find essential oils supportive in managing stress, anxiety, improving sleep quality, easing muscle tension, and uplifting spirits. We focus on enhancing your overall well-being and supporting your body's natural healing capabilities.
