Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is Aromatherapy?
Aromatherapy is a holistic healing treatment that uses natural plant extracts to promote health and well-being. Sometimes called essential oil therapy, Aromatherapy uses aromatic essential oils medicinally to improve the health of the body, mind, and spirit. It enhances both physical and emotional health.
How can Aromatherapy benefit me?
Aromatherapy can offer a wide range of benefits tailored to your individual needs. It can help reduce stress and anxiety, improve sleep quality, alleviate headaches, boost mood, support respiratory health, and even soothe minor aches. Your personalized session will focus on addressing your specific wellness goals.
Are essential oils safe to use?
When used correctly and with proper guidance, essential oils are generally safe. However, they are highly concentrated and potent, requiring careful dilution and application. will provide you with clear, personalized instructions for safe usage, including any necessary precautions based on your health profile.
What should I expect during an Aromatherapy consultation?
During your consultation, will engage in a detailed discussion about your health history, lifestyle, and wellness objectives. This comprehensive approach allows for a deep understanding of your needs. Based on this, a personalized Aromatherapy plan, including custom essential oil blends and application methods, will be developed just for you.
