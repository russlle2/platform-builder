Frequently asked safety questions

How do you calculate dilution?
We estimate drops using a standard ~20 drops per mL rule. Recommended dilution is percent × bottle size. Example: a 30 mL bottle at 2% ≈ 12 drops total. Always round thoughtfully and use lower dilutions for sensitive populations.
Patch test
Apply a single diluted drop to skin under the forearm, cover, and wait 24 hours. If irritation occurs, discontinue use.
Pregnancy, nursing, and children
Some oils are not recommended during pregnancy or for young children. Use lower dilutions and consult a qualified clinician for individualized guidance.
Pets
Use caution around animals. Many essential oils may be strong for pets; avoid direct application without veterinary approval and keep diffusions gentle and brief.
