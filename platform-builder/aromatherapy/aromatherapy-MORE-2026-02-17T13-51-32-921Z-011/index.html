Safety & notes
  • All suggestions are educational and not medical advice. Individual reactions vary; consult a professional if needed.
  • Patch test: apply one diluted drop to a small skin area and wait 24 hours for irritation.
  • Pregnancy & breastfeeding: some oils are not recommended. Please check with a qualified practitioner before using.
  • Pets: many essential oils are harmful to animals. Keep blends away from pets and consult a veterinarian for guidance.
