Session details & safety

Sessions are non-medical and centered on sensory practices. Any guidance uses language such as “may support.” If you are pregnant, nursing, have a chronic condition, or are taking medication, let your practitioner know so we can adapt recommendations.

Patch test & dilution

We recommend a patch test before using a new blend and will suggest dilution ranges during sessions. See the Mix Lab for a clear dilution guide when you create a blend.
