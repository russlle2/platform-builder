Practitioner philosophy

We work with aroma as a context for small, repeatable practices that people can fold into daily life. Our offerings aim to support presence and simple routines, not to treat medical conditions.

Sessions and resources emphasize informed, safety-first choices. Every recommendation is accompanied by dilution guidance, advice about pets and pregnancy, and a simple patch test procedure. We invite curiosity and care.

Common questions

How do I do a patch test?
Apply a small diluted drop to the inside of the forearm, cover for 24 hours, and check for irritation. If redness or discomfort occurs, discontinue use.
What dilution is safe?
Typical home dilutions are 0.5%–2% for adults depending on sensitivity and purpose. Our materials always include a clear dilution guide for each suggested blend size.
What about pets or pregnancy?
Some essential oils may pose risks to pets or during pregnancy. We include warnings and suggest consulting a provider in pregnancy or when pets are in the home. Never apply oils directly to a pet without veterinary guidance.
Are these medical treatments?
No — our guidance uses phrasing such as “may support” and is not a substitute for medical care.
