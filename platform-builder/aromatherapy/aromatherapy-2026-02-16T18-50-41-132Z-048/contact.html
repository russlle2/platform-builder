FAQ

safety & simple guidance
How do you dilute essential oils?

I recommend starting with low dilutions (0.5–3% for most adults) depending on application. Specific percentages are provided with each blend so you can replicate the ritual safely at home.

Should I patch test?

Yes. I guide you through a simple patch test on the inner forearm before suggesting full use. If irritation occurs, discontinue use and consult your healthcare provider.

What about pets?

Some essential oils can be irritating to pets. I’ll review your household (cats, dogs, other animals) and suggest pet-safe options and diffusion practices to reduce exposure.

Pregnancy & breastfeeding

If you are pregnant or breastfeeding we take extra precautions. Some oils are avoided or used only with medical approval; I always advise consulting your care provider alongside our conversation.
