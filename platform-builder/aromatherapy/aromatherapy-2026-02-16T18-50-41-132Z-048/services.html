Common questions

How do you handle dilution?
We provide clearly labeled blends with suggested dilution ratios and safe carrier suggestions. For roll-ons we typically use 2–5% for adults; lower for sensitive users.
Should I do a patch test?
Yes — we recommend applying a small amount behind the ear or on the forearm for 24 hours before regular use.
Are blends pet-safe?
Essential oils can affect animals differently. We will flag blends that are not recommended around cats or small mammals. Always keep diffusers and applied oils away from pet-facing areas.
Pregnancy and nursing?
We follow conservative safety practices: many oils are avoided during pregnancy. Please share status during intake so we can tailor options.
