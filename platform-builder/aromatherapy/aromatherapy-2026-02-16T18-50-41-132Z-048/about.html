The ritual I offer

I arrange scent like a sentence: top notes to greet, middle notes to converse, base notes to settle. Sessions begin with a gentle consultation — we listen to your preferences, household context (pets? pregnancy?), and any skin sensitivities. Then we craft a small, safety-minded blend and invite it into the space with care. This is intentional scenting, not medical therapy.

A typical session

  1. Welcome & brief intake (allergies, meds, pregnancy, pets)
  2. Patch test & dilution guidance if topical use is requested
  3. Scent exploration & curated inhalation time
  4. Blend to-go and home-use notes

What to expect

You’ll receive clear guidance on safe use, recommended dilutions, and storage. If topical application is of interest, a patch test is part of the visit. We avoid oils contraindicated for pregnancy and certain conditions — please share those details when booking.

Safety-first reminders
  • Not a replacement for medical care
  • Patches & dilutions provided
  • Pet-friendly guidance available
