FAQ — safe practice first

We provide dilution guidelines for adults and for common topical uses. Typical dilution ranges: 0.5–2% for daily wear, higher under practitioner direction. Always follow recipe notes and avoid direct undiluted application.
A patch test is recommended for new oils or blends: apply a diluted drop behind the ear or on the inner forearm for 24 hours and observe for irritation before regular use.
Some oils can be harmful to animals. We include specific pet-safety notes and recommend consulting your veterinarian. Use diffusers with care and never apply oils to pets without professional guidance.
Certain oils and high-dose aromatherapy are not recommended in pregnancy or while nursing. Members receive pregnancy-safe alternatives and guidance, but this is not medical advice—consult your healthcare provider for personal recommendations.
