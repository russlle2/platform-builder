Safety & usage notes

All blends list top/middle/base notes, suggested dilution, and patch-test guidance. We avoid recommending use around young children or certain pets for some oils. If pregnant, nursing, or managing a medical condition, please consult a licensed health professional before use and inform us during intake.

FAQ

What dilution is used?
Our ready-to-use products include recommended dilution ratios on the label. For adult topical roll-ons we typically keep between 1–5% depending on intention and sensitivity.
How to patch test?
Apply a small dot of diluted oil to an inner forearm or behind the ear. Wait 24 hours to check for irritation.
Are your blends safe for pets?
Some oils can affect pets differently. We advise not diffusing continuously in occupied pet spaces and will flag any blend not recommended around cats or small mammals.
What about pregnancy?
We take a conservative approach — many oils are avoided or reduced. Always disclose pregnancy or breastfeeding before use.
