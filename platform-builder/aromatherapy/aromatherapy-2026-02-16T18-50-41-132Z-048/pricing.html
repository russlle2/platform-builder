Questions we commonly hear

How should I dilute blends?

Dilution depends on the blend and your skin sensitivity. We provide clear dilution ratios and instructions to use with carrier oils. Always follow the practitioner’s guidance.

Do I need a patch test?

Yes — patch testing is encouraged for topical use. It helps identify sensitivity before wider application.

Are the blends safe for pets?

Some essential oils are not pet-friendly. We will review your household composition and provide guidance on safe use and storage.

What about pregnancy?

If you are pregnant or breastfeeding, please mention this when booking. We will avoid oils that are contraindicated and tailor blends for safety. This is advisory, not medical advice.
