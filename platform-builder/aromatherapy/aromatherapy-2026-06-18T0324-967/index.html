Frequently Asked Questions

How can Aromatherapy specifically help me during my postpartum recovery?
Aromatherapy can offer gentle support for common postpartum experiences like interrupted sleep, feelings of overwhelm, and low energy. We focus on natural scents that can help calm your nervous system, promote a sense of well-being, and support your body's recovery during this delicate time.
Do I need any prior experience with essential oils to try your Aromatherapy services?
Absolutely not! Our services are designed to be accessible for everyone, especially new mothers. We’ll gently introduce you to the world of Aromatherapy, explaining everything in simple terms and guiding you every step of the way, ensuring you feel comfortable and confident.
How does seasonal wellness factor into your Aromatherapy for new mothers?
Just as your body adjusts to motherhood, it also responds to the changing seasons. We tailor our Aromatherapy blends and recommendations to align with the current season, offering specific support for concerns like winter blues or summer fatigue, helping you maintain balance year-round.
Are the Aromatherapy blends safe for me and my baby during this postpartum period?
Your safety and your baby's well-being are our top priority. We use only high-quality, pure essential oils and provide clear, gentle guidelines for use, focusing on safe dilution and application methods suitable for new mothers and their immediate environment. We'll always discuss any concerns you have.
