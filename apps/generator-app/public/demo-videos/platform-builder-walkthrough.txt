Let's build a website for Willow and Coast Wellness. Then follow the customer experience from the first preview into the customer portal. We'll choose a design, make it our own, add a custom photo and explore publishing and inquiries. This demonstration uses a test business and a test checkout. Start with the business details, the name, what you offer and the contact information you want people to see. Use an email address you control for checkout and website access. Keep the description specific. A few clear sentences help you see how the design will introduce the business to potential clients. Next, choose the mood and style that fit the business. The matching step brings up template options to explore. Open a design, look at its layout, and compare another if you need to. Choose a starting point you like, then use the preview to see where your own content belongs. Now make the preview sound like Willow and Coast. Double-click an editable text area, replace the headline, and check how it sits in the design. Explore the available colors and fonts, too. Keep the wording clear and the page easy to scan so visitors can quickly understand what the business offers. The selected design and business details carry into the order. Review the plan, price, and terms, then continue to stripe. This demonstration uses a test checkout with no real charge. After checkout completes, the website is provisioned and the portal shows published. Now we can start managing the site. The delivery email includes a private link to the customer portal. Open that link to manage your website and keep it somewhere safe. The portal brings together your business details, website status, images, and inquiries. It's the place to return whenever you want to update something or check a new message. Inside the portal, let's update Willow and Coast's tagline and choose Save and Publish. The update completes successfully and the new wording appears on the hosted website. The portal also provides the supported business and service fields. Review your changes carefully, publish them, and check the page your visitors will see.

Now let's add a custom photo. In the portal, open Images and choose Upload. Select your photo from your computer and wait for its thumbnail to appear. The image library keeps website-ready images available for this site. Use clear, well-lit photos that represent your business and keep each source file under four megabytes. To put the photo into the design, open the visual editor and continue to the preview. Click the image you want to replace, then choose the photo from your computer. Here, our coaching room photo replaces the original picture. Check its crop and the surrounding text so the section still feels balanced and easy to read. Finally, choose Publish to Live Site and wait for the confirmation. Then, open the actual website and refresh it. There is the new photo, alongside the updated business wording and headline. This final check closes the loop. The changes are visible to visitors, and you can return to the portal whenever your content needs another update.

Let's test what happens when a visitor gets in touch. On the published website, Jordan sends a sample enquiry. Back in the portal, the message appears with the sender's details and the notification status. The email notification also arrived in our inbox. To respond, use Reply in your email app. Your saved enquiry stays available here, so you can keep track of the conversation's starting point. Your account dashboard brings the website and its management tools together. Manage Billing opens Stripe, where you can review the subscription, payment method, and invoices. The website already has a hosted address. If you own a domain, the portal provides connection instructions. Website records, image storage, and email notifications are managed for you. From here, the routine is simple. Update your content, publish, check the live result, and respond to enquiries.
